# Terms dropped after the link pass

A term note that no note links to is a graph island: retrievable by name,
unreachable by traversal, and therefore invisible to the graph arm the
experiment measures. Dropping such a term is the point of the list, not a gap
in it.

| Term | Why |
|---|---|
| `regulatory_approval` | Its useful surface forms (`approval`, `approved by`) were removed as polysemous — they name different things in finance, pharma and sport. Only `cleared by regulators` survived, which no note's source contains, so the term links to nothing. |

Terms reused rather than captured are recorded separately in `term_reuse.json`:
those are not absences but dedup decisions, where an existing note already
carries the concept and a second note would split its evidence.
