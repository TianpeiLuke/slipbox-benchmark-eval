# FTX Founding and Growth

[Sam Bankman-Fried](sam_bankman_fried.md) co-founded the crypto-trading firm [Alameda Research](alameda_research.md) in 2017, then co-founded [FTX](ftx.md) in 2019 as a complement to Alameda, intended to bring in revenue and liquidity for the trading arm.

Within two years more than 80 investors provided about $2 billion in capital, including $1 billion during 2021, from backers that included Sequoia, Temasek and Tiger Global. In January 2022 FTX raised $400 million in a Series C round that boosted its valuation to $32 billion; this was its last round of public funding. At its peak Bankman-Fried was worth $22.5 billion and was the richest person in the world under 30, roughly eighteen months after having nothing. Trades on FTX climbed to $15 billion per day.
