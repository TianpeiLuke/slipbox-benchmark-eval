# X Content Moderation Capacity After the Musk Takeover

X's internal ability to respond to reports of content problems was drastically reduced after Elon Musk's takeover in late 2022, through major layoffs that included content moderation and human rights teams, undertaken to improve profitability.

Several changes compound the reduction. Legacy account verification ended and the Blue Check became pay-to-play, so a reader can no longer tell at a glance whether a notable account is authentic — which matters precisely when a crisis makes verification urgent. Legacy content moderation policies were removed and in-house enforcement teams cut, while a crowdsourced alternative, Community Notes, was promoted as the main response to disinformation risk. Musk also withdrew X from the EU's voluntary Code of Practice on Disinformation, prompting Breton's response: "Obligations remain. You can run but you can't hide."

The structural consequence is that responsibility for adjudicating contested claims shifts from staff to users, at the moment when volume is highest and verification hardest. A September 2023 study commissioned via the EU's Code of Practice found X the worst of the major platforms for spreading disinformation.
