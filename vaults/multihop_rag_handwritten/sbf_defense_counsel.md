# SBF Defense Counsel

[Sam Bankman-Fried](sam_bankman_fried.md) is represented by the firm Cohen & Gresser, with Mark Cohen — a high-profile defense attorney and former federal prosecutor — as lead attorney. Cohen also represented Ghislaine Maxwell in her sex trafficking trial related to Jeffrey Epstein. He requested early release for Bankman-Fried and was denied.
