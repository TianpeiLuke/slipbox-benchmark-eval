# Meta Arabic Mistranslation Incident

In October 2023 Instagram users found that the common Arabic phrase "Alhamdulillah" — "Praise be to God" — was being automatically mistranslated when it appeared alongside Palestinian identifiers.

In screen recordings posted online, users whose bios contained "Palestinian" and the Palestinian flag emoji together with the Arabic phrase had their bios translated as "Palestinian terrorists – Praise be to Allah" or "Praise be to God, Palestinian terrorists are fighting for their freedom." Removing "Palestinian" and the flag emoji produced the correct translation, "Thank God." The mistranslation was active for hours before Meta corrected it.

Meta told 7amleh a "bug" caused it. A spokesperson told 404 Media: "We fixed a problem that briefly caused inappropriate Arabic translations in some of our products. We sincerely apologize that this happened." Meta did not respond to TechCrunch's request for comment.

Shayaan Khan, a TikTok creator who posted a viral video about it, argued the failure is dangerous rather than merely offensive, because it fuels Islamophobic and racist rhetoric already exacerbated by the war. He pointed to the fatal stabbing of Wadea Al-Fayoume, a Palestinian-American child whose death is being investigated as a hate crime.
