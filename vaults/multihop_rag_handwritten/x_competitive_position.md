# X Competitive Position and User Decline

X introduced sign-up friction at a time of increased competition. A report from market intelligence firm Similarweb found that X's web traffic and monthly active users on mobile had declined after the acquisition, as Musk's product and policy changes took root and some users left or posted less.

Competing microblogging products include Spill, Bluesky, Pebble, Countersocial, Spoutible, Hive, the open-source platform Mastodon, and Meta's Threads. Few charge a fee except for premium features.

Musk's broader plan is to turn X into an "everything app" spanning text, live and recorded audio and video calling, and payments.
