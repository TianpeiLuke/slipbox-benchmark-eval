# FTX to Alameda Customer Fund Flow

Testimony in the FTX trial described a consistent structure by which customer deposits at [FTX](ftx.md) were routed to [Alameda Research](alameda_research.md) and then deployed elsewhere. FTX co-founder and CTO Gary Wang testified that customer transactions were funnelled toward Alameda and directed onward, and that Alameda drew funding directly from FTX customers.

Peter Easton, a University of Notre Dame professor who assisted the government prosecutions of Enron and WorldCom, traced the flow of billions of dollars between the two firms. He testified that much of the funding originated with customers, and that user deposits were used by both FTX and Alameda to purchase real estate, make investments, and direct funding toward political causes and charities.

The relation holds specifically for the period covered by the charges and rests on witness testimony and Easton's tracing rather than on any admission by the defense.
