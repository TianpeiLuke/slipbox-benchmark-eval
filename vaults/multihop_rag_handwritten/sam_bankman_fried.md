# Sam Bankman-Fried

Sam Bankman-Fried, referred to as SBF, is the former CEO and co-founder of the crypto exchange [FTX](ftx.md) and the defendant in a criminal trial on seven counts tied to fraud and money laundering. The charges concern his conduct while leading FTX and its sister company [Alameda Research](alameda_research.md).

Prosecutors characterised him as having built an empire "on lies" and displayed photographs of him alongside wealthy and powerful figures to illustrate his influence before FTX's collapse. His defense characterised him instead as overwhelmed rather than ill-intentioned.
