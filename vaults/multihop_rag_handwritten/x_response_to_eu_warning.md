# X Response to the EU Warning

Responding to the EU's warning, X CEO Linda Yaccarino released a letter stating that a leadership group had been convened to consider X's response, that "tens of thousands" of pieces of content had been removed, and that "thousands" of posts and "hundreds" of accounts linked to terrorist groups, violence or extremism had been actioned.

She said X was responding to law enforcement requests but claimed it had received no requests from Europol at that time.

X has promoted Community Notes — a feature crowdsourcing additional context onto questionable posts — as its main response to disinformation risk.
