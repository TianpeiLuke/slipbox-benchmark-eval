# Taking the Stand as a Defense Gamble

Former federal prosecutor Josh Naftalis, speaking to TechCrunch, characterised [Sam Bankman-Fried](sam_bankman_fried.md)'s decision to testify in his own trial as a "Hail Mary" ahead of a verdict and potential sentencing on seven counts.

The grounds are that testifying is irreversible once begun: "Once it goes to cross[-examination], he doesn't get to say, 'I'm done,'" Naftalis said. "He can't just walk out if he doesn't like how it's going." A defendant who takes the stand therefore surrenders control over how long the examination continues and what ground it covers, which is why the choice reads as a last resort rather than a routine tactic.

This is the assessment of an outside former prosecutor, not a statement by the defense.
