# Intent Versus Incompetence in the FTX Failure

A recurring reading of the [FTX](ftx.md) collapse holds that [Sam Bankman-Fried](sam_bankman_fried.md) failed to manage risk rather than set out to defraud. On this account he became one of many who "disastrously failed to manage the risk inherent in crypto speculation," as financial discrepancies accumulated between the exchange and its sister company [Alameda Research](alameda_research.md), itself a trader on the platform.

Ayelet Noff of Sliced Brand put the same reading in terms of capability: "He had no experience in management or handling such big undertakings. I think he was in way over his head."

The failure itself is not in dispute. What the trial must decide is how criminal his intentions were, and how far they extended — which is why intent, not outcome, is the contested question.
