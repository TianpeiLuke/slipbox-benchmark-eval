# User Workarounds for Suspected Shadowbanning

Instagram users who believed their Palestine-related content was being suppressed adopted several evasion tactics.

- **Misspell trigger words** — posting "p@lestine" rather than "Palestine" to bypass content filters.
- **Superimpose text on unrelated images** — placing information about Gaza over a neutral photo, such as a cat, so it is not flagged as graphic or violent.
- **Interleave unrelated posts** — posting a random image between Palestine posts, which users reported restored their view counts. One user described posting "intermittent non-Gaza content in order to 'release' my stories to be viewed again."
- **Add counter-signals** — including an Israeli flag emoji or tagging posts #istandwithisrael, even without supporting the Israeli government, in an attempt to game engagement.

These are user-inferred workarounds against an unpublished ranking system, not documented platform behaviour; their apparent effectiveness is the users' own evidence for the suppression they describe.
