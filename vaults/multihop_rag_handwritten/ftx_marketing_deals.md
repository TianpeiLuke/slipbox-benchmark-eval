# FTX Celebrity and Sports Marketing Deals

[FTX](ftx.md) gained mainstream recognition through branding deals and partnerships. In 2021 it bought the naming rights to the Miami Heat's home arena, and its name was branded on Major League Baseball umpires' polos.

It partnered with celebrities including Tom Brady and his ex-wife Gisele Bündchen, Steph Curry, Shaquille O'Neal and Naomi Osaka. Brady was paid $55 million and Curry $35 million to, in Michael Lewis's characterisation, "lend FTX legitimacy and edge." Anna Wintour approached [Sam Bankman-Fried](sam_bankman_fried.md) to sponsor the Met Costume Ball.
