# FTX

FTX is a crypto exchange co-founded and led by [Sam Bankman-Fried](sam_bankman_fried.md) that reached a peak valuation of $32 billion as the third-largest crypto exchange before filing for bankruptcy. Its sister company was the trading firm [Alameda Research](alameda_research.md).

Its collapse is the subject of a criminal fraud and money-laundering trial, and of a class-action lawsuit accusing investors including Paradigm of defrauding customers by promoting the exchange.
