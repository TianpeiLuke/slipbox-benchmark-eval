# SBF Trial Proceedings and Schedule

Sam Bankman-Fried, former CEO and co-founder of the collapsed crypto exchange FTX, stood trial on seven counts tied to fraud and money laundering committed while leading FTX and its sister company [Alameda Research](alameda_research.md). Jury selection began on October 3, 2023. The trial was then paused from October 20 until proceedings resumed on a Thursday, at which point the case entered its defense phase after the prosecution concluded its case.

Bankman-Fried confirmed during a Wednesday teleconference that he would take the stand himself, alongside a handful of other defense witnesses.

Access to the proceedings is limited: no devices are permitted in the courtroom, so reports are physically assembled inside the courtroom or relayed to overflow rooms. The jury ranged from a train conductor to a retired investment banker who attended Stanford University.
