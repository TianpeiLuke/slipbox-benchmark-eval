# Week Three Testimony in the SBF Trial

The third week of the FTX trial featured three prosecution witnesses.

Can Sun, FTX's former general counsel, cooperated with authorities and testified that [Sam Bankman-Fried](sam_bankman_fried.md) asked him to create a theoretical "legal justification" for the use of billions in consumer funds just days before [FTX](ftx.md) filed for bankruptcy.

Robert Boroujerdi, managing director of the hedge fund Third Point, testified about a partnership that resulted in a $60 million investment, and about his lack of awareness of the special relationship between [Alameda Research](alameda_research.md) and FTX. He testified that Third Point would not have invested had it known.

Peter Easton, a University of Notre Dame professor who helped the government prosecute Enron and WorldCom, testified to tracing billions of dollars flowing between Alameda and FTX.
