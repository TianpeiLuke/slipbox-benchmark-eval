# EU Urgent Warning to X

On October 10, 2023, EU internal market commissioner Thierry Breton sent an "urgent" letter to Elon Musk, and shared it on X, warning that the Commission had indications X was being used to disseminate illegal content and disinformation in the EU following the October 7 Hamas attacks on Israel.

Breton reminded X of the [Digital Services Act](digital_services_act.md)'s "very precise obligations": "When you receive notices of illegal content in the EU, you must be timely, diligent and objective in taking action and removing the relevant content when warranted. We have, from qualified sources, reports about potentially illegal content circulating on your service despite flags from relevant authorities."

He objected specifically to a change X had made the previous night to its public-interest policy governing newsworthiness judgments, which he said had left "many European users uncertain" about how X applies its own rules. He also called for "proportionate and effective mitigation measures" against risks to public security and civic discourse from disinformation, and asked Musk to contact law enforcement and Europol.

The Commission gave Musk 24 hours to respond, stating the answer would be added to its assessment file on X's DSA compliance.
