# The U.S. Justice Department's Parallel Antitrust Suits Against Google

The Helena World Chronicle class action arrives alongside the filing of the U.S. Justice Department's lawsuit against Google for monopolizing digital ad technologies, and the complaint also references the Justice Department's 2020 civil antitrust suit over search and search advertising.

The two government cases concern different markets: search and search advertising in the 2020 suit are distinct from the digital ad technologies at issue in the more recent one.
