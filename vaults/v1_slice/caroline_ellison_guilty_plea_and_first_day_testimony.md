# Caroline Ellison's Guilty Plea And First Day Of Testimony

Caroline Ellison, head of the hedge fund Alameda Research during its implosion along with FTX, took the stand on October 10th, 2023 having already pleaded guilty to criminal charges — fraud, conspiracy to commit fraud, and money laundering — and told the jury that Bankman-Fried "directed me to commit these crimes." She quantified the taking: "We ultimately took about $14 billion, some of which we were not able to pay back."

Her long-anticipated first day took the form of walking through a damning spreadsheet she had prepared for her ex and boss, now the defendant in a criminal fraud trial. She entered hunched in on herself, wearing a dusty rose dress with a gray blazer over it, looking less like an executive than like a girl who had borrowed her boyfriend's coat because she was cold. Asked by the prosecution to identify Bankman-Fried, she had trouble finding him and gazed around the courtroom for more than 20 seconds — apparently he was incognito with his new haircut — and when she did spot him she identified him as the one wearing a suit, which got chuckles from the rest of the defense table, also all in suits. She tilted her head down to answer questions and lifted it when she had finished each answer.

The defendant's own reaction registered the shift: Bankman-Fried had been vibrating slightly during Wang's testimony, and during Ellison's his bouncing became more noticeable. By the end of the day, The Verge's reporter wrote, the spreadsheet seemed like a sideshow.
