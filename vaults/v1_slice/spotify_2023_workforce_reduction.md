# Spotify's 2023 Workforce Reduction

Spotify is eliminating about 1,500 jobs, or roughly 17% of its workforce, in its third round of layoffs of 2023, as the music streaming giant looks to become "both productive and efficient."

In a note to employees on Monday 4 December 2023, Spotify founder and chief executive Daniel Ek — citing slow economic growth and rising capital costs — said right-sizing the workforce is crucial for the company to face the "challenges ahead."
