# Google's Defense That Safari Succeeded On Product Integration

Google lawyer John Schmidtlein's rebuttal attacked the DOJ's premise that Google's position on Apple devices was bought rather than earned: his overall point was that Google helped Safari succeed not by forcing Apple's hand, but by being a great product that integrated seamlessly with Apple's own stuff.

He developed it after a closed session and the lunch break by leading Eddy Cue through a history of the Google/Apple partnership and of the Safari browser. Cue testified that Safari's combination of URL and search bar was a user interface innovation and that the seamless Google integration was part of what made it work; Schmidtlein pointed out that in early promotional materials for Safari, the Google integration was nearly always mentioned. Cue supplied the origin story: "Before 2003, the way that you searched the web was you had to go in and you had to type in google.com in the URL field, or you could type in another URL. We came up with the idea that if you type anything in the URL field that's not a URL, it just goes to search."
