# The Four Recommended Sportsbook Apps: DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, Caesars

Sporting News recommends the same four sportsbook apps — DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM and Caesars — for both NBA Rookie of the Year futures and golf betting, describing what each offers.

For NBA Rookie of the Year betting: DraftKings, known as a daily fantasy sports operator before becoming a renowned sportsbook, offers a nice variety of futures bets including the ROTY award, with an intuitive online sportsbook and app and a competitive welcome bonus for new players. FanDuel, also known from the DFS market, has one of the largest followings in the country, offers competitive ROTY odds and one of the best betting apps, with a quality welcome bonus and attractive promotions for existing customers. BetMGM, one of the most recognizable names in the gambling industry, attracts and keeps customers with competitive odds for all bet types including futures and ROTY, many deposit and withdrawal options, 24/7 customer service and generous bonuses and promotions. Caesars offers favorable odds for almost every bet type including ROTY, a nice variety of deposit and withdrawal options, a competitive welcome bonus and NBA betting promos for existing players.

For golf betting: DraftKings offers markets and props for PGA Tour events and major championships including tournament winners, top 5, 10 and 20 finishes and matchup betting, and allows live betting on individual holes and shots during a tournament. FanDuel offers a wide range of markets and props including top-20 finishes, head-to-head matchups and first-round leaders, and covers international events such as the DP World Tour and the Ryder Cup. BetMGM provides event winners, top finishers and hole-in-one props, and like DraftKings and FanDuel offers live betting for golf. Caesars offers a user-friendly platform with a wide range of bets for the PGA Tour and other major events, such as winning margin, top finishes and live betting props, and is known for its strong selection of bets and live betting interface.
