# Prediction: OpenAI Becomes A Product Company

TechCrunch predicts that after the leadership shake-up in November 2023, OpenAI will be a changed company — perhaps not outwardly, but the trickle-down effect of Sam Altman being more fully in charge will be felt at every level, manifesting above all in a "ship it" mindset.

The concrete test named is the GPT store, originally planned for launch in December 2023 but understandably delayed due to the C-suite fracas. TechCrunch expects the "app store for AI" to be pushed hard as the platform to get your AI toys and tools from, never mind Hugging Face or any open source models, on the reasoning that OpenAI has an excellent model to work from in Apple's and will follow it all the way to the bank.

More broadly, the prediction is that the caution and academic reserve the previous board exerted will give way to an unseemly lust for markets and customers. Other major companies with AI efforts are expected to follow the same trend — for instance, Gemini/Bard horning in on a ton of Google products — but the writer suspects it will be more pronounced at OpenAI.
