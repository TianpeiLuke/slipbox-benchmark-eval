# Wang's Cooperation Agreement With Prosecutors

Gary Wang testified under a six-page cooperation agreement with the government that requires him to meet with prosecutors, answer their questions truthfully and turn over evidence; he faces up to 50 years in prison when he goes before a judge for sentencing following the trial.

Wang said he returned to the U.S. and met with prosecutors the next day. For months Bankman-Fried has known that Wang and Ellison, integral members of his personal and professional inner circles, had turned on him: both pleaded guilty in December and have since been cooperating with the U.S. attorney's office in Manhattan. Wang's testimony, which stretched into Friday, was given under that cooperation agreement, and Ellison was expected to take the stand under a similar arrangement. U.S. District Judge Lewis Kaplan presided.
