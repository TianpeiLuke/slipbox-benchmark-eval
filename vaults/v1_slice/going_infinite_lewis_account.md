# Going Infinite: Michael Lewis's Account Of SBF's Political Dealings

Michael Lewis's book "Going Infinite" debuted on the first day of Sam Bankman-Fried's trial, and details about Bankman-Fried's political dealings emerging from it include his alleged attempt to buy off Donald Trump to get him not to run again for president.

TechCrunch's team was focused on the Manhattan Federal Court for the trial of Bankman-Fried, the disgraced entrepreneur accused of orchestrating the collapse of the cryptocurrency exchange FTX; the Lewis book is the second channel through which the week's revelations arrived. The reporting is framed as allegation rather than established fact, and it comes from the book rather than from courtroom testimony. The item appeared in TechCrunch's Week in Review of October 7, 2023.
