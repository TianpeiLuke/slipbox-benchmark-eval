# The Taylor Swift And Travis Kelce Romance Overview

Rumours that singer Taylor Swift and Chiefs tight end Travis Kelce were dating ran for weeks and only intensified as Swift attended five Chiefs games during the season, according to CBS Sports' timeline of the rumoured romance published 12 October 2023.

The report gives the pattern of appearances. At the first game she attended the previous month, the Eras Tour singer was seen chatting with the tight end's mother, Donna Kelce, and cheering emphatically when No. 87 scored a touchdown. The week before publication she was in attendance to watch the Chiefs defeat the Denver Broncos in Week 6 on "Thursday Night Football." She was once again at Arrowhead Stadium, this time to watch the Chiefs take on another AFC West opponent, the Los Angeles Chargers.
