# Epic v. Google Is A Jury Trial

Epic and Google have to convince a jury, not the judge, which is totally different from how the Apple case played out — that one was a "bench trial."

The consequence is that persuasion cuts both ways and is hard to predict. All the evidence of tricky deals inside Google might sway a jury against the company; alternatively, Google scaremongering that sideloaded apps equal gaping security holes might sway a jury against Epic. Who knows.

The jury is also sealed off from coverage: Judge Donato explicitly told jurors they are in a "news-free bubble" through mid-December.
