# Shadowban Workarounds

As the war continued, social media users tried to find ways around the alleged shadowbanning on Instagram, using three supposed loopholes: misspelling flagged words, hiding text over unrelated images, and adopting pro-Israel signals they do not endorse. TechCrunch recorded the tactics on 19 October 2023, and describes them as supposed loopholes — user hypotheses about what evades the filters, not verified mechanics of Meta's systems.

The first tactic is orthographic: misspelling certain words, such as writing "p@lestine" instead of "Palestine," in hopes of bypassing any content filters. The second is visual: sharing information about Gaza as text superimposed over unrelated images, like a cat photo, so it will not be flagged as graphic or violent content. The third is signalling: creators have tried including an emoji of the Israeli flag, or tagging their posts and Stories with #istandwithisrael even when they do not support the Israeli government, in hopes of gaming engagement.

The precondition for all three is the belief that the platform is throttling Palestine-related posts, and the frustration behind them is widespread. Leen Al Saadi said her frustration with Meta is common among Palestinians, both in occupied territories and across the diaspora. "All we're asking for is to give us the exact same rights," she said. "We're not asking for more. We're literally just asking Meta, Instagram, every single broadcast channel, every single media outlet, to just give us the respect that we deserve."
