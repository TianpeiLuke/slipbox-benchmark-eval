# Epic's Rebuttal Of Google's Leaked Email Summary

Pressed with the suggestion that the email Epic accused Google of leaking showed Epic was itself interested in a special deal, Tim Sweeney attacked the premise of the characterisation: Google did not leak the email, he said — it leaked "their ridiculously biased summary of it" to Abner Li at 9to5Google.

He supported the leak claim with trial testimony: Don Harrison testified that he didn't think Google leaked to the press, and was then presented with a document including Google's press team and Sameer Samat, head of all Android, summarising the articles that appeared as a result of Google's leaking of Epic's plans to the press. On the substance of the email, Sweeney said it is in evidence and is the same one he had already described: right before Christmas-ish 2019, with the new Marvel season and a lineup of Star Wars content planned, he asked Google's executives to let Fortnite come to the Google Play Store using Epic's own payment method and to open up the use of their own payment methods to all developers, not just Epic. The interviewer conceded the point, saying he had read that email in court and had not realised it was the same one, because it was so far off from what he had heard described as a "special billing exception."
