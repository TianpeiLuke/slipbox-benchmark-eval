# MasterClass Founder David Rogier's Seed Round

On TechCrunch's Found podcast, the crew talked to David Rogier, the CEO and founder of MasterClass, a streaming platform where you can learn from the world's experts on a range of topics.

Before Rogier launched MasterClass he worked as a VC, and through his connections he received a $500,000 seed round before he even had an idea for a company.
