# TikTok's Ad-Free Subscription Tier Test

TikTok is testing an ad-free subscription tier for some users, priced at $4.99 for an ad-free experience with no other strings attached, TechCrunch reported in its Week in Review of October 7, 2023.

The test is narrow, and TechCrunch cautioned readers not to expect the option to arrive anytime soon: TikTok said it was piloting the plan in a single, English-speaking market outside the United States for now.

A week later, in the issue of October 13, 2023, TechCrunch noted a parallel experiment at a different platform: X, formerly Twitter, appeared to be testing three tiers of its Premium service according to its CEO, and code in the app showed that one of those tiers may be ad-free.
