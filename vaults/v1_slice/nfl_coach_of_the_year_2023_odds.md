# 2023 NFL Coach Of The Year: Campbell Favored, Payton The Longshot Case

Dan Campbell was the current favorite for 2023 NFL Coach of the Year at +200 via DraftKings, with Sean Payton the longshot at +2200, and Sporting News argued on Dec. 5, 2023 that Payton should be at the forefront of the conversation.

The case against Campbell as favorite is about which season is being rewarded. Campbell had done a tremendous job with the Lions, but Sporting News thought it fair to wonder whether he was the betting favorite because of how well he had done his job that season or over the last two: that year the team benefited from the groundwork laid the previous season. If there were a reward for the best coach over two seasons, he would deserve it; as for that season, not so much.

The case for Payton is that in-season turnarounds are harder than offseason ones. It is one thing to turn around a team that was dreadful last season, but Payton had taken one that got off to a horrible start and turned things around in-season — and he had resurrected Russell Wilson's career. Sporting News argued that alone should put him at the top of the list, with the scope condition that he may need to get Denver into the playoffs to convince voters.
