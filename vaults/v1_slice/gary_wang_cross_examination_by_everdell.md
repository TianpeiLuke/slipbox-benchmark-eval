# Gary Wang's Cross-Examination By Everdell

Defense attorney Christian Everdell's cross-examination of Gary Wang on October 10th, 2023 attacked the premise that Alameda's ability to run a negative balance was unique to Alameda. Wang had testified the week before that Alameda got access to a special credit line and an option to take its balance into the negative without triggering liquidation, something he alleged no other FTX account received; Everdell countered by pointing to FTX's spot margin program, which let users lend each other assets for margin trading and in which it was possible to have a negative balance in a specific coin.

The Verge's reporter judged the counter incomplete rather than false: in spot margin cases it was not possible for those accounts to avoid liquidation, as Wang testified Alameda could, or to have an overall negative balance. Her read was that the defense was betting jurors would throw up their hands in confusion — Everdell could not undo the damage of the previous week's code review, but he shook the rust off long enough to make Wang sound less reliable by drowning the jury in confusing technicalities.

A second line of attack went to Wang's consistency. What Wang said in court apparently contradicted something he had told the government in earlier interviews about market making — "apparently," because Everdell was probably handing him his previous testimony to refresh his recollection while Wang insisted he did not remember, and whatever Wang was shown was not submitted as evidence or shown to the court. The reporter got the gist and bet the jury did too, calling it probably the strongest work the defense had done so far.
