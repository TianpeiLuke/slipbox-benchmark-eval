# The Scope Of The 23andMe Data Breach

The 23andMe breach turned out to reach 6.9 million people, far beyond the number the genetic testing company first quantified. On Friday 1 December 2023, 23andMe announced that hackers managed to access the personal data of 0.1% of customers, or about 14,000 individuals, but the company did not initially say how many other users might have been impacted by the breach, which it first disclosed in October.

The wider group had their names, birth years, relationship labels, the percentage of DNA they share with relatives, ancestry reports and self-reported locations exposed.
