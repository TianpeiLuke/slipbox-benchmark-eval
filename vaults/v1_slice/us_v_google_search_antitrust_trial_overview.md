# US v. Google: Two Months Into The Search Antitrust Trial

As of TechCrunch's November 13, 2023 roundup, the Justice Department's landmark antitrust case against Google was nearly two months old, and the trial record was still producing fresh revelations. TechCrunch framed it as one of the biggest fights in tech antitrust since the U.S. took Microsoft to trial in the 1990s.

An earlier roundup had already surfaced that Google spent $26.3 billion in 2021 making itself the default search engine across platforms, and that Google tried to have Chrome preinstalled on iPhones. Over the couple of weeks preceding this roundup, more of Google's inner workings came to light, including some of the search engine's most lucrative search queries, the shape of the revenue-share agreements between Google and Android OEMs, and why Expedia has a bone to pick with Google.
