# 2023 NFL Defensive Player Of The Year: Danielle Hunter As The Longshot

Micah Parsons, Myles Garrett and T.J. Watt were the current favorites for 2023 NFL Defensive Player of the Year, with Danielle Hunter the longshot Sporting News argued at least deserved to be in the conversation, per its Dec. 5, 2023 awards-odds piece.

The grounds are Hunter's production and its effect on his team. Hunter led the league in tackles for a loss and was third in sacks with 13.5, behind Khalil Mack and Watt. Not only had he produced at a high level, but his play had been instrumental in the Vikings turning things around after a 1-4 start.

The warrant Sporting News offers is that name recognition, not performance, explains the gap in odds: Hunter may not have had the name recognition that Parsons, Garrett and Watt had, but he had played as well, if not better, that season. If he could continue to produce, he at least deserved to be in the conversation, if not to win.
