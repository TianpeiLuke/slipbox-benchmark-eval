# The Claim That 80 Percent Of SEOs Are Scammers

Peter Kent — author of several dozen explanatory tech books including SEO for Dummies and Bitcoin for Dummies — has for years been telling people that part of the SEO industry's reputation problem is that 80 percent of SEOs are scammers.

His grounds come from consulting work: "A lot of companies and individuals out there selling their services as SEO gurus don't know what they're doing or don't really give a damn," he explained. Businesses often ask him to vet the work of other SEOs, and "I would take a look at their site and determine the firm had done next to nothing and had been charging thousands a month for years on end." Kent has done his fair share of SEO jobs but also has something of an outsider's perspective, and he draws a distinction with an adjacent industry: "The SEO people are just trying to make money. The cryptocurrency people are trying to make money, but they're also trying to overthrow, you know, the existing system."

When the 80 percent figure was put to other SEOs, most agreed it sounded accurate, though people were divided about what to ascribe to greed and what was just stupidity — which is where the warrant is contested. "It isn't because they have a scammer's heart," said Bruce Clay. "It's because they don't have the real expertise." Clay is an avuncular man with a mustache often credited with coining the phrase "search engine optimization," and is therefore called "the father of SEO"; he said his agency never hires an SEO with less than a decade of experience.
