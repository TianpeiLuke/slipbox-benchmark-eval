# Matt Huang And Paradigm's FTX Investment

Matt Huang, co-founder and managing partner of Paradigm, a crypto venture capital firm that invested over $275 million in FTX — a stake that was wiped out — was the third witness to take the stand, and testified about his firm's due diligence.

Huang told the court that Bankman-Fried assured him funds would be used for FTX and not Alameda, and that he was promised Alameda had no preferential treatment on the FTX platform, even though the hedge fund was one of its top traders. Huang said he was concerned about FTX's lack of a board of directors but eventually invested anyway. During cross-examination he said Paradigm pressed Bankman-Fried on the board issue and was told he did not want investors as directors but did plan on having a board with experts.
