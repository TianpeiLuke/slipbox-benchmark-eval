# Sam Bankman-Fried's Federal Fraud Trial

Sam Bankman-Fried, the founder of bankrupt cryptocurrency exchange FTX, is charged with seven counts of fraud and money laundering following the collapse of his cryptocurrency empire the previous year, and was on trial in New York in October 2023.

Two fixed points anchor the proceeding as CNBC described it on 2023-10-06. A U.S. judge revoked Bankman-Fried's bail at a hearing in a New York courthouse on August 11, 2023. And on Thursday, Oct. 5, 2023, Matt Huang, co-founder of Paradigm Operations LP, arrived at the New York court to appear in the case.
