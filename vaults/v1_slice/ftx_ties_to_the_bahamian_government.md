# FTX's Ties To The Bahamian Government

On cross-examination on October 31st, 2023, prosecutor Danielle Sassoon drew out evidence that Bankman-Fried had a cozy — perhaps even inappropriately cozy — relationship with the Bahamian government, which is not what he was on trial for but probably did not make him look any better to a jury. She asked whether he had made comments about paying off the Bahamian national debt, which he did not recall.

The court then saw an internal FTX group chat called "Project Chinchilla Chatter," in which another member asked who in the Bahamian government they needed to talk to for the project. "His name is Ryan Salame :p," Bankman-Fried replied; Salame was one of FTX's executives. In another part of the chat, Bankman-Fried noted that the Bahamian prime minister was at the FTX arena in FTX seats with his wife.

The jury was also shown a November 9th email from Bankman-Fried to Ryan Pinder, attorney general of the Bahamas, saying "we are deeply grateful" for what the Bahamas had done for FTX, and offering as a token of that gratitude: "We would be more than happy to open up withdrawals for all Bahamian customers on FTX, so that they can, tomorrow, fully withdraw all of their assets, making them fully whole. It's your call whether you want us to do this, but we are more than happy to and would consider it the very least of our duty to the country, and could open it up immediately if you reply saying you want us to. If we don't hear back from you, we are going to go ahead and do it tomorrow." Bankman-Fried did indeed open withdrawals for Bahamian customers.
