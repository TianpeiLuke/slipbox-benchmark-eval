# SEO Is Now Baked Into Everything

The apparent paradox — that people complain SEO is ruining the internet while practitioners say the SEO business is in decline — dissolves once you see that SEO is now baked into everything. Jairo Bastilla, a tall, charming man who described himself multiple times as "a nerd," pointed out that even though working directly with search engine rankings is "no longer monetizing at the highest payout," the same "core knowledge of SEO" remains relevant for everything from native advertising to social media.

The boundary case is Bastilla's own specialty: email campaigns, which he called "deliverability." To a person who militantly unsubscribes from any and all marketing emails, the realisation was claustrophobic — surrounded by people who annoy the rest of us for a living. Why does it always seem to surprise us, even after all these years, that the way we behave on the internet is often quite different from how we act in real life?
