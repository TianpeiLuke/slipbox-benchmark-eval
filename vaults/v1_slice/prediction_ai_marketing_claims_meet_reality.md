# Prediction: AI Marketing Claims Meet Reality

TechCrunch predicts a considerable customer withdrawal from AI tools in 2024 as the benefits fail to justify the costs and risks. The simple fact behind the forecast is that the hype built up in 2023 is going to be very hard for companies to follow through on: marketing claims made for machine learning systems that companies adopted in order to not fall behind will receive their quarterly and yearly reviews, and it is very likely they will be found wanting.

At the far end of that spectrum, the prediction is that we will see lawsuits and regulatory action against AI service providers that failed to back up their claims.

The forecast is bounded rather than blanket: capabilities will continue to grow and advance, but 2023's products will not all survive by a long shot, and there will be a round of consolidation as the wobblier riders of the wave fall and are consumed.
