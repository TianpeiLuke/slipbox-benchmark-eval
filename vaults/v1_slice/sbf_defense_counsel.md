# SBF's Defense Counsel

Bankman-Fried will be represented by the firm Cohen & Gresser, with Mark Cohen — a high-profile defense attorney and former federal prosecutor — as lead attorney. That was the state of play in TechCrunch's "where we are today" account of the case on 2023-10-01.

Cohen's name was already familiar from another trial: he also represented Ghislaine Maxwell in her sex trafficking trial related to Jeffrey Epstein. He requested an early release for Bankman-Fried, but was denied.
