# Sweeney And Pichai's Settlement Talks

Asked about his settlement talks with Google CEO Sundar Pichai, Tim Sweeney said he could not discuss their content, but that the two met for an hour and had a professional discussion in which they did not reach a settlement — they were, in his words, "rather far apart."

He explained the gap in terms of what Epic was asking for: free and fair competition for everybody, removal of the payments tie, and removal of the anticompetitive measures, which he said obviously leads to far better deals for consumers and developers. What Epic does not want is a special deal just for itself. Sweeney characterised Google's strategy so far, as visible from all these deals, as taking out potential competitors one at a time while they are still weak and small, before they build up or unite into a formidable force — and said Epic is not going to go along with something like that.
