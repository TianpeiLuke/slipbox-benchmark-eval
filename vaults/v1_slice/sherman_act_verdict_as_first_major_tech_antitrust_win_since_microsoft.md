# The Sherman Act Verdict As The First Major Tech Antitrust Win Since Microsoft

Tim Sweeney argued that the jury's finding for Epic showed "the Sherman Antitrust Act works in the new era of tech monopolies" — his grounds being that no major antitrust verdict against a tech company that meant change and benefits for everybody had landed since the 1990s and US v. Microsoft, back in the early days of the internet.

The warrant joining claim and grounds is his account of present-day harm: the industry, he said, is "being strangled by a few gatekeepers imposing insane amounts of control and extracting huge taxes," which not only raise prices for consumers but also make a lot of kinds of products simply unviable. If the statute can still reach that conduct after a three-decade gap, then it remains a working remedy, which is why he called the verdict "a great day for all developers" and "much needed by the industry." Sweeney was speaking about a trial four years in the making, tracing back to a September 2019 email produced in discovery about a plan to draw Google into a legal battle over antitrust.
