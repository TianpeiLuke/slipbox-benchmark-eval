# ZestMoney's Shutdown

ZestMoney is shutting down following unsuccessful efforts to find a buyer, TechCrunch reported on 9 December 2023. The buy now, pay later startup's ability to underwrite small-ticket loans to first-time internet customers had attracted many high-profile investors, including Goldman Sachs.

The Bengaluru-headquartered startup employed about 150 people at peak and raised more than $130 million over its eight-year journey.
