# Epic Games' 2023 Layoffs And Overspending

Epic Games fired roughly 830 people in 2023, about 16 percent of its staff, including significant job cuts at Mediatonic, the Fall Guys studio Epic purchased in 2021.

CEO Tim Sweeney gave the reason in his note about the layoffs: "For a while now, we've been spending way more money than we earn," adding, "I had long been optimistic that we could power through this transition without layoffs, but in retrospect I see that this was unrealistic." Asked separately by The Verge whether app store restrictions had shaped Epic's plans, Sweeney said not directly, describing a really broad strategy of building games and technology and huge success in recent years with Unreal Engine gaining adoption among all kinds of industries even beyond games. He also acknowledged the company's own financial limitations: "we expanded the company to nine times the size we were before Fortnite took off," and Epic is "just trying to forge the strategy where we're living within our means and doing everything we absolutely can."
