# Alameda's missing risk team as an active governance choice, not a neutral fact

In The Verge's FTX trial coverage ("The jury finally hears from Sam Bankman-Fried," 2023-10-28), the reporter treats Alameda Research's lack of a risk team as an indictment rather than a neutral detail: "Not having a risk team, when you are any kind of financial anything, is certainly a choice." The logic given is that risk is inherent to a futures exchange, which she calls "even more like a casino than regular cryptocurrency," so the absence is something a person decided, not an oversight of scale. Sam Bankman-Fried, Alameda's CEO and majority owner, testified he did not create one and has since called it a "big mistake" — a choice reclassified as an accident.
