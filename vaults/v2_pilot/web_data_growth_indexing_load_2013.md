# Google's cited 2013 data volume: around 9 zettabytes a year

In The Age's 22 October 2023 report on Google Search, people generated around 9 zettabytes — nine trillion gigabytes — of data per year in 2013, all of which Google had to index continuously. The matching 2023 figure is cut off; it is absent from the source text held here.
