# Manchester United and Newcastle United Out of Europe After Finishing Bottom of Their 2023/24 Champions League Groups

Manchester United and Newcastle United will play no further part in European competition in 2023/24, having been eliminated by finishing bottom of their UEFA Champions League groups, Sporting News reported on 12 December 2023. Manchester United ended Group A last on 4 points from six matches, with one win, one draw and four defeats.
