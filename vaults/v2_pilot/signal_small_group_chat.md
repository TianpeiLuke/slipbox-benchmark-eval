# The "small group chat": FTX's inner-circle Signal group in the collapse

The "small group chat" is the Signal conversation in which FTX's inner circle coordinated strategy during the exchange's final closing days — a private messaging thread, not any public FTX channel or forum. Joe Bankman, father of Sam Bankman-Fried, is among the people included in it, according to a 2023-10-26 Verge account of his son's fraud trial, where his membership featured in the evidence against him. The chat sits apart from a separate Signal group, Donations Processing, described by Nishad Singh as routing FTX and Alameda money to political causes.
