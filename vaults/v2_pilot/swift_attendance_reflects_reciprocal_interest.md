# Swift's five Chiefs games after Kelce's invite suggest mutual interest, not publicity

Hypothesis: Taylor Swift's attendance at five Chiefs games this 2023 season — reported in CBSSports.com's Oct 12, 2023 timeline, spanning from the Week 3 Chicago Bears game on Sept 24 through the Sunday Los Angeles Chargers game at Arrowhead Stadium — reflects reciprocal interest following Kelce's public Sept 21, 2023 invitation on the Pat McAfee Show, rather than a one-sided publicity cycle. It is falsified if the appearances were arranged through the club or her entourage, or if she attended games Kelce was not playing in.
