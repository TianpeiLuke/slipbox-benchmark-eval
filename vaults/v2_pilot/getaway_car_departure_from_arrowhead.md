# Swifties Photographed Swift and Kelce Leaving Arrowhead Stadium in a "Getaway Car"

After Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce attended the Chiefs' game at Arrowhead Stadium on Monday, 25 September 2023, Swifties were "immediately on high alert", with some capturing the pair leaving the stadium together. Their vehicle was characterised at once as a "getaway car" — phrasing borrowed from "...Ready For It?", a track on Swift's 2017 album Reputation.
