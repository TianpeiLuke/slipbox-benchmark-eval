# Daron Babin entered SEO in 1994, before Google, and recalled "the air of manipulation was insane"

SEO practitioner Daron Babin told The Verge (November 1, 2023) that his career in search engine optimization began in 1994, before Google existed. Looking back at those earliest years of the trade, Babin recalled that "the air of manipulation was insane." His account places SEO's manipulative instincts at the origin of the practice, rather than treating them as a later reaction to Google's algorithm.
