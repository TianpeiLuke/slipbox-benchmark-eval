# Google Maps replied "Very relatable" to the viral Thai Food Near Me tweet

In 2023, a pre-opening photo of Thai Food Near Me, a New York restaurant named after a Google search query, went viral on Twitter, drawing replies from brands seeking attention. The official Google Maps account — the platform the restaurant's name is designed to outsmart — replied, "Very relatable," with a crown emoji. Reaction to Google Maps' presence in the thread was mixed; one person shot back, "This is about how your products don't even work anymore."
