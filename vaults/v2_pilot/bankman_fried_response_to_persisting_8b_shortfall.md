# After the $8 billion shortfall persisted, Bankman-Fried directed repayment of third-party loans rather than escalation

In the FTX trial testimony recounted by The Verge on 2023-10-31, Sam Bankman-Fried did not escalate or halt activity upon learning that the roughly $8 billion shortfall remained after the software bug was fixed. Instead, Bankman-Fried directed alleged co-conspirator Caroline Ellison to repay third-party loans and went on making investments.
