# Deriving Qualification Permutations Before a Final Matchday

A Champions League group permutation is worked out by taking a club's position in the table before a matchday is played and enumerating the results — in that club's own fixture and in the other fixture of the same matchday — that would leave it inside the qualifying places. The output is a checkable list, not a prediction: each combination can be compared against the actual scores once the matches finish.

Sporting News applied this on 2023-12-12, reviewing the UEFA Champions League group stage ahead of its final match night and examining what teams from each group needed to qualify for the Round of 16, of which 16 places were at stake.

Group A shows the method at its simplest. Bayern Munich had already qualified, but Copenhagen, Galatasaray and Manchester United all entered the final matchweek with a chance of joining them. Galatasaray knew that if they beat Copenhagen at Parken they would go through. Copenhagen won 1-0 through Lukas Lerager's only goal, which took Copenhagen into the knockout rounds and left Galatasaray on 5 points from 6 games. Manchester United were eliminated after a 1-0 defeat to Bayern at Old Trafford, Kingsley Coman scoring, leaving them bottom of the group on 4 points.
