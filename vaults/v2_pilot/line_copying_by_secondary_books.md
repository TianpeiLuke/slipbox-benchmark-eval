# Some Sports Betting Apps Copy Lines Rather Than Set Their Own

As of Sporting News' line-shopping guide dated 2023-11-06, sportsbook pricing is not uniform across the market. The best sportsbooks employ oddsmakers and oddsmaking services, which use computer models and algorithms weighing analytics, statistics, injury and roster news, performance, and matchup data, to set their lines and odds. Other sports betting apps, by contrast, simply copy those lines rather than produce prices of their own.
