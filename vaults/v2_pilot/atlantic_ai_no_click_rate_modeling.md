# The Atlantic modelled AI-integrated Google search and found 75% of queries needed no click-through

Online magazine The Atlantic modelled what would happen if Google integrated AI into search, and found that 75% of the time the AI would answer the user's query without requiring a click-through to its website, costing it traffic. The finding was reported by The Wall Street Journal on Thursday, in a piece cited by Helena World Chronicle's antitrust lawsuit against Google, filed in the U.S. District Court in D.C. this week, as of 2023-12-15.
