# Epic lost to Apple but won against Google — a split verdict the Verge called a surprise

A federal jury sided with Epic Games on Monday, 2023-12-12, finding Google an illegal monopolist over Android app distribution and in-app billing. The Verge framed that as a surprise: a "walled garden" rival, Apple, "almost entirely won a similar case two years ago." Sweeney drew the parallel himself in the interview, noting that Apple's Phil Schiller "sat throughout the entire Epic v. Apple trial, as did I."
