# Load management, not talent, is the objection to a Wembanyama Rookie of the Year lock

Sporting News (2023-10-04) hedges its Victor Wembanyama favorite with the qualifier "even with a young roster and potential load management" at the San Antonio Spurs. That concession is the sharpest objection to a Wembanyama lock: fewer games would shrink the full-season accumulation of stats the NBA Rookie of the Year award depends on, however undeniable his impact is.
