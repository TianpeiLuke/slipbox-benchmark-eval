# PSV's comeback win at Sevilla and Lens' defeat sealed Group B qualification in November 2023

PSV came from behind to win 3-2 at Sevilla on 29 November 2023, taking their UEFA Champions League Group B tally to 9 points. The same matchweek confirmed PSV's place in the Round of 16, because Lens lost 6-0 to Arsenal on 29 November 2023 and could reach only 8 points, finishing third with a goal difference of -5. Arsenal topped the group on 13 points; bottom club Sevilla had 2.
