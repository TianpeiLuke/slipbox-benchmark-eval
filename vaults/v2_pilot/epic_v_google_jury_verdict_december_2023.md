# Epic v. Google: nine-person federal jury finds Android monopolisation and illegal tying, Monday 11 December 2023

On Monday 11 December 2023, a nine-person federal jury found for Epic Games, the Fortnite maker, after a four-week antitrust trial. The jury decided that Google has an illegal monopoly over Android app distribution and over Android in-app payment systems, and that Google illegally tied its Google Play billing system to its app store, the Play Store. The verdict surprised the world, particularly because "walled garden" rival Apple had almost entirely won a similar Epic case two years earlier.
