# Epic's Android App Distribution Monopoly Claim Against Google

In Epic Games' antitrust suit against Google, whose trial began on November 6th, 2023, Epic's core argument concerns Android app distribution. Epic argues that Google makes it so difficult for developers and users to get around its Android app store, and that app store's standard fees, that Google has created an illegal monopoly. The claimed harms are twofold: the monopoly unjustly enriches Google, and it artificially drives up app prices, because other app stores can't compete. Epic calls the standard in-app payment fees the "Google tax". The remedy Epic seeks is to break up Google's alleged monopoly on Android app stores and payment methods, so that developers aren't stuck paying that tax or passing the fee along to players.

Source: The Verge, "Epic v. Google, explained" (2023-11-05).
