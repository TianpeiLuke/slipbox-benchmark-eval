# Big Tech will push AI into existing product lines in 2024, but less aggressively than OpenAI

Hypothesis (TechCrunch, 2023-12-19): the trend of embedding AI directly into established product lines, expected at OpenAI after Sam Altman took fuller control following the November leadership shake-up, will also spread to other major companies running AI programs in 2024. The cited example: Gemini/Bard predicted to "horn in on a ton of Google products."

The prediction carries a bound — the author suspects the AI-into-products push "will be more pronounced in this case," meaning OpenAI, whose caution and academic reserve, worn down by the previous board, give way to an unseemly lust for markets and customers.

Falsified if major vendors keep AI features separated from core products through 2024.
