# Alameda's balance sheet listed more FTT than existed, 193% above total market cap

CoinDesk's publication of Alameda Research's balance sheet — showing $14.6 billion in assets and $8 billion in liabilities as of June 30, 2022 — revealed an anomaly. Alameda's largest asset was $3.66 billion of "unlocked FTT" plus $2.16 billion of "FTT collateral": $5.82 billion of FTT, the token behind FTX. That was 193% higher than the total FTT market cap, then about $3 billion, meaning Alameda purported to hold more FTT than existed. Reported by TechCrunch, 2023-10-01.
