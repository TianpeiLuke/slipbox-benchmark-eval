# Line Shopping: Checking Odds Across Multiple Sportsbooks

Line shopping is the practice of checking the lines and odds for a prospective bet at multiple online sports betting sites. Sporting News' guide to line shopping, published 6 Nov 2023, defines the process this way and describes its result as "quite often better odds and improved lines." The aim is to book a wager at improved odds or a more favourable line than a single sportsbook would offer, since betting is rarely the same at every sportsbook.
