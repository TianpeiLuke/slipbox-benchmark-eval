# Rehn's Opening Portrait of Bankman-Fried as "On the Top of the World"

In his opening statement at Sam Bankman-Fried's FTX trial, government prosecutor Thane Rehn told the jury: "One year ago, it looked like the defendant was on the top of the world." Bankman-Fried ran a supposedly thriving crypto exchange, jetted between international locales, hobnobbed with celebrities like Tom Brady and Larry David, and repeatedly emphasized to customers that their money was safe and secure. Fortune reported this on 2023-10-04, day two of the trial.
