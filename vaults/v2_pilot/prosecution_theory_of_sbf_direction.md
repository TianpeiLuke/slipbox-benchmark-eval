# The Verge's Assessment: Prosecution Built a Strong Case of Orchestration and Direct Participation by Bankman-Fried

In a column published 2023-10-26, as the government's case against Sam Bankman-Fried neared its end, The Verge argued that the prosecution "has made a strong case" that Bankman-Fried presided over the various forms of fraud played out at his trading firm Alameda Research and at the FTX exchange — orchestrating some of that conduct and engaging in some of it directly. Bankman-Fried then faced seven counts: two counts of wire fraud and five conspiracy charges. The claim's scope is explicitly limited to the trial evidence presented up to that date, and it is a journalist's assessment of the government's persuasiveness rather than any verdict — testimony still to come included from former colleagues who had already pleaded guilty and were felons.
