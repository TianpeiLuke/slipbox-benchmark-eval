# Ford and GM shares slip as UAW expands strike, 29 September 2023

On 29 September 2023, shares of Ford and General Motors slipped after the United Auto Workers said it would expand its limited strike to include one additional facility at each company. Ford fell 1.1 per cent, and GM dipped 0.6 per cent. The moves came as Wall Street closed out its worst month and quarter of the year, with the S&P 500 slipping 0.3 per cent.
