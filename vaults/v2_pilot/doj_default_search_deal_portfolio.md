# DOJ's theory in *US v. Google*: default deals capture search entry points

In *US v. Google*, the antitrust trial over Google's search business heard before Judge Amit Mehta in Washington, DC, the US Department of Justice's case turns on the agreements Google signs to be the default search engine. Reporting on Apple executive Eddy Cue's testimony on 2023-09-26, The Verge states that the Justice Department is focused on the deals Google makes — with Apple but also with Samsung and Mozilla and many others — to ensure it is the default search engine on practically every platform. The Apple contract, the Information Services Agreement (ISA), has been in place since 2002 and pays Apple billions of dollars a year. DOJ lawyer Meagan Bellshaw pressed that Apple offers its users a choice about lots of things, big and small, while the ISA did not allow Apple to offer users a choice of search engines during setup.
