# SEO manufactures an organic ranking rather than buying a "Sponsored" slot

In The Verge's article "The people who ruined the internet" (published 2023-11-01), the distinction between SEO and paid search placement is the core of the model. Buying the advertising space labelled "Sponsored" above organic search results is a straightforward transaction: money for a known, explicitly marked slot. SEO is not that. In the author's phrasing, it is "more like the Wizard of Oz projecting his voice to magnify his authority" — the practitioner's goal is to tell the algorithm whatever it needs to hear for a site to appear as high up as possible in search results. The output looks organic, so it borrows Google's supposed objectivity to lure people in, and only then do they usually get shown some kind of advertising. That borrowed credibility, not the placement fee, is what makes the ranking worth having: "Voilà: a business model!"
