# Navigation index: The Verge's "The people who ruined the internet" (2023-11-01)

This note indexes how The Verge report "The people who ruined the internet" (2023-11-01) is built. It opens at a rooftop bar in South Florida where a 10-foot alligator was advertised for a party of SEO practitioners; the animal arrived at five and a half feet. It then defines SEO as telling Google's algorithm whatever it needs to hear for a site to rank high and then show ads, distinct from buying the space labeled "Sponsored" above organic results. Blame for unusable search results splits between Google, with its 90 percent market share for online search, and the practitioners. Cade Lee, with over a decade in SEO, supplies the stereotype: pirates, ego, "You've outdone Google." Among the 200 digital marketers, interviews with Missy Ward and Babin complicate it.
