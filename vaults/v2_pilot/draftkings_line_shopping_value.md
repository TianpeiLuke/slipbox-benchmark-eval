# DraftKings Runs More Open Betting Markets Than Most Online Sportsbooks

In its guide to line shopping, Sporting News (2023-11-06) reports that DraftKings Sportsbook "has more open betting markets than most online sportsbooks, and the lines and odds are always competitive." The observation appears among four brands Sporting News names as the biggest US online sportsbooks, alongside Caesars, BetMGM, and FanDuel, each described as offering competitive lines in dozens of states.
