# Lewis's unverified claim that SBF considered paying Trump ~$5B to skip a re-run

Michael Lewis, author of "Going Infinite" (debuting 2023-10-02, day one of Sam Bankman-Fried's Manhattan Federal Court trial), claimed in a 60 Minutes interview broadcast 2023-10-01 that Sam Bankman-Fried had concocted a plan to buy off Donald Trump to get him not to run again for president. Lewis recounted the reasoning as: "Like how much would it take to get an answer? There was a number that was kicking around… this was $5 billion."

The hypothesis remains unproven on two counts Lewis himself supplied. First, Bankman-Fried was never sure whether the $5 billion figure came directly from Trump. Second, TechCrunch notes no report of whether anything was ever wired to the Trump camp — a detail the trial, beginning Tuesday, could establish either way.
