# The Pixel 8 Pro's upgrades may justify a purchase despite a familiar shell

In TechCrunch's Week in Review of 2023-10-07, Darrell's hands-on assessment of the newly unveiled Google Pixel 8 Pro argues that its improvements over the previous year's Pixel 7 Pro are substantial enough to potentially warrant buying the phone. The warranting evidence he cites is a set of three concrete gains: improved cameras, a brighter screen, and enhanced AI-powered features. The claim is scoped by what those gains do not cover. The Pixel 8 Pro remains very similar in design to the Pixel 7 Pro, and the temperature sensor it adds is underutilized, so it contributes nothing to the case. This is a first-impressions judgment from limited time with the device, not a verdict; the full review was still pending.
