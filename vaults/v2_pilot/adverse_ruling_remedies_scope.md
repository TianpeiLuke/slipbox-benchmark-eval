# Remedies floated against Google if Judge Mehta finds liability

In TechCrunch's November 13, 2023 roundup of the US v. Google antitrust trial, the remedies sketched for Google all hinge on one condition: if Judge Amit Mehta rules against Google. The article names three. First, Google may have to change its behavior and share its APIs with third-party developers. Second, it may be banned from making anticompetitive and exclusive deals with smartphone and computer manufacturers and with wireless carriers. Third, Google might end up having to turn over all or most of the data it has collected to other search engines, so those rivals can improve their products and attract more users. The DOJ has said that Google gets 16 times more data than Bing does everyday, and the government's case is that Google uses its platforms and partner deals to block competition in search and advertising, cutting rivals off from the data they would need to improve their products.
