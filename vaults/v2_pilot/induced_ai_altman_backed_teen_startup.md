# Sam Altman Among Backers of Teen-Founded AI Startup Induced AI

Sam Altman is among the backers of Induced AI, an AI startup founded by two teenagers earlier in 2023, according to Manish's reporting in TechCrunch's Week in Review digest published on 2023-10-07. Induced AI lets businesses input their back-office tasks in plain English and converts those instructions into pseudo-code in real time. The company's stated aim is to assist businesses in automating workflows in "previously unexplored" ways. The item appeared in TechCrunch's roundup of the week's most-read stories.
