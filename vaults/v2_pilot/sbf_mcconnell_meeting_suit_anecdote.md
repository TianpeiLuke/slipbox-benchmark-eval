# Lewis watched SBF arrive at the McConnell meeting with his suit balled under his arm

Michael Lewis told 60 Minutes, in the segment aired 2023-10-01 and reported by TechCrunch on 2023-10-02, that he met Sam Bankman-Fried ahead of SBF's own meeting with Senate leader Mitch McConnell. That McConnell discussion concerned how SBF could fund political candidates seeking to offset Donald Trump's influence inside the Republican Party. Lewis's durable image of it: the suit SBF had brought to wear was balled up tightly under one arm, with his dress shoes falling out of the middle of the bundle — SBF's preferred attire otherwise being t-shirts and cargo shorts.
