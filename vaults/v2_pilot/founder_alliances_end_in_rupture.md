# TwoSilence: Musk's broken friendships as evidence that ego, not engineering, splits AI's leaders

The argument advanced in "How ego and fear fuelled the rise of artificial intelligence" (The Age, 2023-12-09) rests on a repeated pattern of silence. Elon Musk and Google's Larry Page stopped speaking soon after the 2015 summer party in Napa Valley where they had debated AI's future. A few weeks later Musk dined with Sam Altman — then running a tech incubator — at the Rosewood hotel in Menlo Park, California, a dinner that produced OpenAI later in 2015. Musk and Altman have since also stopped speaking, though OpenAI is now valued at more than $US80 billion ($A123 billion). Two friendships ending after the closest alignment suggests the fractures are personal, not technical. Altman's own term for it: "disagreement, mistrust, egos."
