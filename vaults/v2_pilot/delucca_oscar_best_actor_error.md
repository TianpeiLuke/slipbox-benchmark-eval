# Gemini Pro in Bard Named the Wrong Best Actor Oscar Winner, 2023-12-07

On 2023-12-07, Vitor de Lucca posted on X (formerly Twitter), under the handle @vitor_dlucca, a screenshot of Gemini Pro running in Bard answering a question about the previous year's Best Actor Oscar winner. The model named Brendan Gleeson; the actual winner was Brendan Fraser. De Lucca called the outcome "very, very bad results to questions that shouldn't be hard anymore with RAG," and TechCrunch's Devin Coldewey cited the exchange as the first of several user-reported basic factual failures after Gemini Pro began rolling out to Bard the day before.
