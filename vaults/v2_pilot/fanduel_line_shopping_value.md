# FanDuel's competitive odds and heavy promo volume favor line shoppers (Sporting News, 2023-11-06)

In its guide to line shopping, Sporting News (2023-11-06) reports that FanDuel is committed to posting competitive odds and runs so many generous promos and odds boosts that it is always worth a look. The observation appears among FanDuel Sportsbook, alongside Caesars, BetMGM, and DraftKings.
