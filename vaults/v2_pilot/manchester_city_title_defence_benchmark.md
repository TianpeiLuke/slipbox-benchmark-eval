# City's back-to-back bid would be first by an English club since Nottingham Forest in 1980

An unusual record sits behind Manchester City looking to go back-to-back after their triumph in last season's Champions League: they would be the first English club since Nottingham Forest in 1980 to successfully defend the trophy, per Sporting News' group-stage review dated 2023-12-12. The claim's scope is only English clubs, so it says nothing about the competition's wider history — Bayern Munich, Real Madrid and Barcelona, whom the same review credits with "strong group-stage campaigns" and places "in the mix towards the sharp end of the competition," fall outside it. The record is contested, not settled: City still face the Round of 16, while Manchester United and Newcastle United were eliminated by finishing bottom of their groups.
