# Caroline Ellison: Alameda's former head and cooperating witness against Bankman-Fried

Caroline Ellison led Alameda Research, the hedge fund at the center of the collapse of the cryptocurrency exchange FTX, and is the former girlfriend of its founder, Sam Bankman-Fried. She has already pleaded guilty to criminal charges in the case: fraud, conspiracy to commit fraud, and money laundering. Testifying for the prosecution on 2023-10-10, the first day she took the stand in Bankman-Fried's criminal trial, she listed those crimes and said that Bankman-Fried "directed me to commit these crimes," adding, "We ultimately took about $14 billion, some of which we were not able to pay back." Bankman-Fried is the defendant on seven counts of wire fraud and conspiracy.
