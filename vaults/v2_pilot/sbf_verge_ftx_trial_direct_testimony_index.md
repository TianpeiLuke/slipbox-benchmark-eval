# The Verge live blog: SBF's first day of direct testimony, FTX trial, 2023-10-28

The Verge's FTX trial live-blog post covering Sam Bankman-Fried's first day of direct testimony on 2023-10-28. It runs from Judge Lewis Kaplan's instruction to answer counsel Mark Cohen's questions and three sustained "Objection, narrative!" calls, through the morning vocabulary detour ("Amazon Web Services," "database," "market manipulation"), to Alameda Research's 2017 founding, its Alameda County naming and internal label "Wireless Mouse," the hires of Gary Wang, Nishad Singh and Caroline Ellison, and Bankman-Fried's claim he did not supervise Wang. It also looks ahead to prosecutor Danielle Sassoon's cross-examination, and cuts off mid-figure on the arbitrage result he described — 50 percent to 100 percent annualized returns — before the FTX founding and his 20 percent chance-of-success estimate.
