# ChatGPT's Launch Reset Competitor Roadmaps: Bard, Amazon Q and Gemini

As ChatGPT became OpenAI's top priority, its success propagated through rivals' product roadmaps in what TechCrunch described on 2023-11-30 as a competition-driven marketplace. Google scrambled to answer it, releasing Bard, "a more or less comparable AI chatbot," in February 2023. Countless other ChatGPT rivals and derivatives reached market afterward, the most recent being Amazon Q, a more business-oriented take on ChatGPT. DeepMind, Google's premier AI research lab, was expected to debut a next-gen chatbot, Gemini, before the end of 2023. The pattern set by these three responses is that a breakout consumer product supplies the template competitors copy and reposition rather than a market they vacate.
