# 2023/24 Champions League Group A final standings after six matchdays

After six matchdays of 2023/24 UEFA Champions League Group A, Bayern Munich led on 16 points from 6 games (5 wins, 0 draws, 0 losses, goal difference +6). FC Copenhagen, also qualified, had 8 points (2 wins, 2 draws, 2 losses, GD 0), ahead of Galatasaray on 5 points (1 win, 2 draws, 3 losses, GD -3) and Manchester United on 4 points (1 win, 1 draw, 4 losses, GD -3). Figures are from Sporting News' Group A table of 12 December 2023; Bayern and Copenhagen were marked as having qualified, with Galatasaray and Manchester United eliminated.
