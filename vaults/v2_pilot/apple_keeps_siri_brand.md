# Hypothesis: Apple ships its AI under the Siri brand, not a new one

Testable claim drawn from a TechCrunch predictions piece dated 2023-12-19: Apple will release its AI in 2024 under the existing Siri brand rather than launching a separately named service such as "Apple AI", because the incumbency of "hey Siri" voice habits outweighs that assistant's reputation for mediocrity. The reasoning sits on the article's observation that Apple has an established pattern of waiting, watching and learning from other companies' failures, then arriving with a refined and polished take that puts others to shame, and that the timing is right partly because waiting too long risks competitors eating up the market. Falsified if Apple introduces a distinct AI brand.
