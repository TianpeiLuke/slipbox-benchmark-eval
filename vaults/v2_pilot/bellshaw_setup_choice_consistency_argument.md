# Bellshaw's consistency objection to Apple's "setup is just critical stuff" defence

In *US v. Google* on September 26, 2023, Meagan Bellshaw, a Justice Department lawyer, pressed Eddy Cue on Apple's defence that iPhone setup covers "just critical stuff" and that showing people a bunch of search engines would be a bad user experience. She put three screenshots in front of him: the Appearance screen that appears when an iPhone is first booted so users can pick font sizes; the location-tracking prompt that appears when a user opens Maps; and the App Tracking Transparency pop-up that tells a user when an app wants to collect their data. Cue objected to all three being counted as part of setup. Bellshaw's counter was one of consistency: Apple already offers its users a choice about lots of things, big and small, so search could be one of those choices rather than a fixed default.
