# A Felony Conviction Outlasts Any Prison Sentence — the FTX Defendants' Case

In the FTX trial coverage The Verge ran on 2023-10-26, the argument is that Gary Wang, Nishad Singh and Caroline Ellison — FTX co-founder, longtime family friend who pleaded guilty to six charges, and Sam Bankman-Fried's ex-girlfriend respectively — are now felons whose punishment is not primarily custodial. Even if they never do time, though they may well be imprisoned or put under house arrest, their lives are irrevocably changed, because three consequences run independently of any sentence: whatever restitution requirements they are subjected to, bars on taking certain kinds of jobs, and the publicity of the proceedings themselves. The same reasoning extends the harm to Joe Bankman, Barbara Fried and Gabe Bankman-Fried, whose reputations are tarnished and who became undesirable in the political arenas where they once operated.
