# Michael Lewis's "Going Infinite" Alleges SBF Tried to Buy Off Trump

In a book by Michael Lewis titled "Going Infinite," which debuted on the first day of the trial of Sam Bankman-Fried at the Manhattan Federal Court, details of SBF's political dealings emerge, including an attempt to buy off Donald Trump to get him to not run again for president. Bankman-Fried, the disgraced entrepreneur, stands accused of orchestrating the collapse of the cryptocurrency exchange FTX. TechCrunch reported this observation in its Week in Review digest dated 7 October 2023.
