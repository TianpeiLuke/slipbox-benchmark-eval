# FTX sued Bankman-Fried's parents for millions just before his criminal trial began

Days before Sam Bankman-Fried's criminal trial opened in late September 2023, lawyers for FTX — the cryptocurrency exchange he founded in 2019 and which has since collapsed — filed suit against Bankman-Fried's parents, saying they should give back millions of dollars they had received from their son. Reported in The Verge's trial coverage on 2023-09-28.
