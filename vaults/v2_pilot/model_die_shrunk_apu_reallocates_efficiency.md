# Steam Deck OLED's die-shrunk AMD APU buys battery and cooling, not speed

The Steam Deck OLED, reviewed by Engadget on 2023-11-09, uses a die-shrunk version of the custom AMD APU in the original Steam Deck — the CPU and GPU are the same, just more efficient. Engadget compares the approach to the process Sony uses for the PS4 Slim and PS5 Slim. Instead of collapsing the chassis around the smaller silicon, Valve spent the savings on a bigger battery and a larger fan in the existing body. So there is no extra performance: developers keep the same performance targets and need no games built specifically for the new hardware. The device ends up about 30 grams lighter than the original, the equivalent of five quarters.
