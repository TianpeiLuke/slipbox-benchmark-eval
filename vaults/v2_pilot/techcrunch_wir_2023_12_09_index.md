# TechCrunch Week in Review, 9 December 2023: a ten-story index

TechCrunch's Week in Review of 9 December 2023 — its Saturday recap of the past few days in tech, headed "Google fakes an AI demo, Grand Theft Auto VI goes viral and Spotify cuts jobs" — indexes ten stories. The "Most read" list runs from Google's faked Gemini Pro demo and the insensitive notebooks given to attendees of its K&I Black Summit in August, through defense company Anduril's Roadrunner air vehicle, the 23andMe breach exposing 6.9 million people, the Grand Theft Auto VI trailer's 85 million views in 22 hours, medical-image leaks in the DICOM standard found by Germany-based Aplite, Meta's Imagine with Meta AI generator, and Spotify cutting about 1,500 jobs, or roughly 17% of its workforce. Framing it, the newsletter says AI "stole the headlines once again", with tech giants from Google to X (formerly Twitter) heading off against OpenAI for chatbot supremacy, alongside an autonomous truck startup leaving the U.S.
