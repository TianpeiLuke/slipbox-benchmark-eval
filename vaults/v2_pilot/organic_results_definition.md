# Organic results: Google's definition of the non-sponsored blue links

In October 2023, Google's official search liaison Danny Sullivan defined "organic results" in a post on X as the list of blue links in search results that are not sponsored. Responding to allegations that Google alters queries for ad revenue, he said ad systems do not affect the organic results, and that "a separate process, which has nothing to do with ads, is used to match organic results to a query."
