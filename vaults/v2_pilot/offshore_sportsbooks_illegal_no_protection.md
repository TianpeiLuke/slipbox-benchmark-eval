# Offshore Sportsbooks Are Illegal and Fraud-Exposed, So They Cannot Enter the ROTY Odds Comparison

Comparing NBA Rookie of the Year odds across every "online sportsbook" reachable on the open internet is unsound. Sporting News' NBA ROTY guide of 2023-10-04 concedes that offshore sportsbooks "advertise widely on the internet, but they are illegal and offer no protection against fraud and other criminal activities." The obligation it states is to use "only legal online sportsbooks for any type of betting," so the permissible set for ROTY futures is the licensed, regulated books — for that piece, DraftKings and FanDuel — not the cheapest quoted price wherever it appears.
