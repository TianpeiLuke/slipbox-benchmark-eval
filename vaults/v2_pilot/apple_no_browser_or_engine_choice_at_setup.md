# Apple Never Switched Search Providers or Prompted Browser Choice Under Its Google Deal

TechCrunch's 2023-10-31 account of the U.S. antitrust trial against Google records the observed effect of the Apple search deal in place since 2003: Google agreed not to promote Chrome to Safari users, and during that agreement Apple never switched to a Google search competitor and never let iPhone users choose a browser when setting up their iPhones.
