# Google's changing AdSense rates cited as a further publisher harm in the Helena World Chronicle antitrust suit

Helena World Chronicle's class action against Google and parent company Alphabet, filed in the U.S. District Court in D.C. and reported by TechCrunch on 2023-12-15, alleges that Google's changing AdSense rates have economically harmed news publishers. The Arkansas publisher — which owns and publishes two weekly newspapers — pleads that rate change alongside its better-documented claims about Google's AI search products as anticompetitive conduct under the Sherman Act, in the market its complaint calls the "digital news and publishing line of commerce." TechCrunch's account of the AdSense allegation is one clause: no rate figures, effective dates, or mechanism are given.
