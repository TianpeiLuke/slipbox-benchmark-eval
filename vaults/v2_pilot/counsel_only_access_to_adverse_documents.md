# Sweeney Saw Google's Exhibits When the Jury Did

In The Verge's post-verdict interview of 2023-12-12, Epic CEO and controlling shareholder Tim Sweeney described an evidentiary position inside the Epic v. Google courtroom unlike his outside lawyers'. He sat through nearly all of the four-week trial from "the best seat in the house," with a clear view of the jury, the judge, each witness and Google's lawyers — yet as an Epic employee rather than counsel, he could not read Google's produced internal documents beforehand and came to the key exhibits at the same moment as the nine-person jury. Asked what surprised him in the courtroom, he said he had suspected Google's practices "since 2018 or so": leaking Epic's conversations to reporters, paying developers not to launch competing stores, and secret deals keeping carriers and OEMs from carrying them.
