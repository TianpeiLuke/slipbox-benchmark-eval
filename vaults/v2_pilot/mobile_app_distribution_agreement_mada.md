# Mobile App Distribution Agreement (MADA): Google's 11-App Pre-Load Requirement

A mobile app distribution agreement (MADA) is the contract Google requires Android smartphone makers to sign. Jamie Rosenberg, a Google employee focused on Android and Google Play, described it in testimony given in Google's defense at the US v Google antitrust trial on November 8, 2023. The MADA obliges makers such as Samsung and Oppo to pre-load a bundle of 11 Google apps on the device, including Search, Chrome and Play. It does not require those pre-loaded apps to be the user's default choices, Rosenberg testified, a distinction TechCrunch reported on November 13, 2023.
