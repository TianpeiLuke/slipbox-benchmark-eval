# Vendor capability claims and user outcomes are weakly coupled — Gemini Pro, December 2023

The weakness of benchmark-derived vendor claims as evidence about real use shows up sharply in the Gemini Pro launch. In blog posts and press materials released the week of 2023-12-07, according to TechCrunch's "Early impressions of Google's Gemini aren't great," Google touted Gemini's superior architecture and capabilities and claimed the model meets or exceeds the performance of leading generative AI models such as OpenAI's GPT-4. The end-user evidence that a "lite" version, Gemini Pro, began rolling out to Bard on 2023-12-06 does not refute those claims, and that is precisely the problem. Users posting on X reported wrong 2023 Oscar winners, a seven-letter answer to a request for a six-letter French word, stale Ukraine summaries, and failed Python and HTML coding attempts. Blog-asserted capability and observed outcome are only weakly related.
