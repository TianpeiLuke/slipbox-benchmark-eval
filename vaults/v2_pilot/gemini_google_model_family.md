# Gemini: Google's flagship AI family launched with only its "lite" member

Gemini is Google's flagship AI model family, unveiled in the week of 8 December 2023 and covered in TechCrunch's Week in Review of 9 December 2023. Only its "lite" member, Gemini Pro, was released; the full model, Gemini Ultra, was withheld. In a press briefing and blog posts, Google touted Gemini's coding capabilities and multimodal prowess, claiming the model can understand images, audio and videos just as well as text. Gemini Pro is strictly text-in, text-out and has proved mistake-prone. Google was also caught faking a Gemini demo, tuning text prompts with still images off camera.
