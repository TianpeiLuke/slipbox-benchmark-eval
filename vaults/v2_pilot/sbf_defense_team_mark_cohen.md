# Bankman-Fried's defense led by ex-prosecutor Mark Cohen; early-release request denied

Sam Bankman-Fried, the former FTX CEO facing seven counts of fraud and conspiracy, is represented by Cohen & Gresser, with lead attorney Mark Cohen — a former federal prosecutor who also defended Ghislaine Maxwell in her sex-trafficking trial. Cohen's request for Bankman-Fried's early release was denied, per TechCrunch, 2023-10-01.
