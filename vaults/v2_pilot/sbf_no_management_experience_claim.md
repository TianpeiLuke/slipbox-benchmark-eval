# Ayelet Noff attributes Sam Bankman-Fried's poor on-camera performance to inexperience at scale

In TechCrunch's 2023-10-02 report on Michael Lewis and *Going Infinite*, the closing assessment comes from Ayelet Noff, CEO and founder of the PR firm Sliced Brand: Sam Bankman-Fried performed badly on camera because he had never managed an undertaking of that size and was in over his head. The warrant she leans on is behaviour visible in the 60 Minutes footage Lewis appeared on — Bankman-Fried shuffling cards with a nervously twitching leg behind his desk, and his eyes going back and forth during his first television appearance because he was trying to win a video game on air. Noff's claim sets its own scope: it is about the impression of competence Bankman-Fried projected, not about his legal culpability on the roughly seven fraud and conspiracy charges he pleaded not-guilty to.
