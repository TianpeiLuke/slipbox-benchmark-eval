# Reach of the retracted Wired piece on Google query manipulation, October 2023

In October 2023, Wired published, then removed for not meeting its standards, an opinion article by lawyer and privacy advocate Megan Gray alleging that Google had revealed during the US antitrust trial that it manipulates search queries for ad revenue — for example replacing "children's clothing" with "NIKOLAI-brand kidswear". The Age reports that the extent of its sharing on social media and boosting in write-ups at other outlets shows how willing people are to accept foul play by Google, amid a standing suspicion of privacy invasion and inappropriate data handling.

SOURCE: The Age, "Is Google Search better than the rest? And is that fair?", 2023-10-22.
