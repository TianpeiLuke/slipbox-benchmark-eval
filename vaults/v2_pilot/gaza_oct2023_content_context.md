# Gaza's devastation and Meta's stated Hamas content rules after October 2023

In the aftermath of Hamas' attack on Israelis, Israel's retaliatory airstrikes and total blockade — cutting access to electricity, water and vital supplies — have devastated Gaza, according to a TechCrunch article published 2023-10-19. In response to the escalating violence, Meta said it is closely monitoring its platforms for violations and may inadvertently flag certain content, but never intends to "suppress a particular community or point of view." Content praising or supporting Hamas, which governs Gaza and is designated as a terrorist organization by the United States and the European Union, is expressly forbidden on Meta's platforms.
