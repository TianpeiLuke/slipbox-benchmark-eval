# TechCrunch's Framing: 2024 as the Year AI Hype Meets Reality

TechCrunch's "8 predictions for AI in 2024" (2023-12-19) opens with a claim about timing rather than about any single product. Its argument: 2023 was "a banger for AI," with the technology moving from niche to mainstream "about as fast as anything ever has," and that speed is precisely what makes 2024 the year the hype "runs full-steam into reality." The reasoning is that publics who adopted AI quickly have not yet had to judge it. Reckoning with what large-scale AI can and cannot do requires products already shipped and already reviewed, which is why the article expects 2024, not 2023, to be the confrontation. The remaining predictions are offered as specific instances of that collision.
