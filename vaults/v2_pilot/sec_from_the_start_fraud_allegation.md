# SEC Alleges FTX Was a Fraud "From the Start," Caused by Bankman-Fried's Own Misappropriation

The SEC's allegation against Sam Bankman-Fried, the founder of the failed cryptocurrency exchange FTX, goes further than the criminal charges he was facing at trial — seven counts of wire fraud and conspiracy. The Securities and Exchange Commission alleges that FTX was a fraud “from the start,” which frames the misstatement as running through the exchange's whole existence rather than arising only as it collapsed. The element that separates the SEC's theory from a plain story of a failed exchange is the source of the shortfall: it alleges a “multi-billion-dollar deficiency caused by his own misappropriation of customer funds,” naming Bankman-Fried personally and specifying customer money as what went missing.
