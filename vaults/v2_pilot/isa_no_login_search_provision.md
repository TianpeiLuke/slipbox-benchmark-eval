# Eddy Cue's 2023-09-26 testimony on the ISA's no-login search clause and Apple's Safari tracking defenses

On 2023-09-26, testifying for Apple in US v. Google before Judge Amit Mehta in Washington, DC, Eddy Cue described the privacy terms built into the Information Services Agreement (ISA) — the deal in place since 2002, renegotiated with Google CEO Sundar Pichai in 2016, that makes Google the default search engine on all Apple devices. According to The Verge's account of that day's testimony, one ISA provision required Google to let people search without logging in. Separately, Cue said, Apple had hardened Safari and its other platforms against tracking by Google or anyone else.
