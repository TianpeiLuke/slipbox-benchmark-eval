# The "Llamacorn" Gambit: Epic's Provoked-Takedown Lawsuit Strategy

The "llamacorn" gambit names Epic Games' deliberately provocative legal strategy, executed on August 13th, 2020. Epic secretly deployed a hotfix to Fortnite — its free-to-play hit, monetised through the virtual currency V-Bucks — letting players buy V-Bucks directly through Epic's own payment processing at a discount, bypassing the in-app payment fee (critics' "Google tax" or "Apple tax") charged to players on Android and iOS devices who installed Fortnite through an official app store. That breach of the developer agreements got Fortnite removed from both stores, as Epic anticipated: it filed suit against Apple and Google the same day and released an attack ad showing a Fortnite hero swinging a unicorn-llama hammer at a giant screen echoing Apple's "1984" Macintosh commercial. The removals and the ad were the publicity engine, not setbacks. The label comes from The Verge's explainer of Epic v. Google, published 2023-11-05.
