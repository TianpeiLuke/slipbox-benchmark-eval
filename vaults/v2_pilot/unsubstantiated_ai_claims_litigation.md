# Hypothesis: Unsubstantiated AI marketing claims trigger lawsuits and regulatory action in 2024

TechCrunch's "8 predictions for AI in 2024" (published 2023-12-19) forecasts, in its "Marketing meets reality" section, that the far end of the 2024 AI backlash will be legal rather than commercial. After machine learning marketing claims meet quarterly and yearly reviews and are found wanting — and after customers withdraw from AI tools whose benefits fail to justify costs and risks — AI service providers "that failed to back up their claims" are likely to face lawsuits and regulatory action during 2024.

Falsified if no litigation or regulatory enforcement against such vendors follows unsubstantiated 2023 AI claims.
