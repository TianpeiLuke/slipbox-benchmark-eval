# Anti-Fragmentation Agreements (AFAs): Google's contract term restricting Android modification

Anti-Fragmentation Agreements (AFAs) are contract terms between Google and Android original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) that prevent those manufacturers from modifying Android in ways that would allow frictionless downloading of apps outside Google Play. The term entered public courtroom discussion in Epic Games' antitrust trial against Google, which began November 6, 2023, as reported by TechCrunch. Epic also pointed to one AFA-related outcome: a deal Epic had made with the OEM OnePlus to offer Epic games on OnePlus devices through the Epic Games app was cancelled over concerns the arrangement would bypass the Google Play Store.
