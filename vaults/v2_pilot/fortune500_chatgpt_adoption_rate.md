# More than 92% of Fortune 500 Companies Use ChatGPT (TechCrunch, 2023)

According to TechCrunch's ChatGPT explainer, dated 2023-09-28, more than 92% of Fortune 500 companies use ChatGPT, OpenAI's text-generating AI chatbot. The figure describes how far the product had spread beyond its origins: TechCrunch characterises ChatGPT as having started as a tool to hyper-charge productivity through writing essays and code with short text prompts, then evolving into a behemoth that those large employers deploy for more wide-ranging needs. The article was last updated through December 2023.
