# Sam Bankman-Fried charged with seven federal counts: two wire fraud, five conspiracy

Sam Bankman-Fried, founder of the failed cryptocurrency exchange FTX and of the crypto trading firm Alameda Research, faced seven counts of criminal charges: two counts of wire fraud and five counts of conspiracy, The Verge reported on 2023-09-28. FTX, founded in 2019, was a fraud "from the start," per the SEC, which cited customer funds diverted to Alameda.
