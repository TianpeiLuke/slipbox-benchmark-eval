# Facets covered in TechCrunch's one-year ChatGPT retrospective

TechCrunch's "One year later, ChatGPT is still alive and kicking" (2023-11-30) covers ChatGPT's first year across eight facets: OpenAI's launch of it as a "low-key research preview" and the data-gathering rationale leadership gave; the adoption record plus third-party traffic figures from Similarweb and Data.ai; the capability expansion into web search, document analysis, DALL-E 3 image creation, and hearing, speaking and seeing; ChatGPT becoming priority number one inside OpenAI; competitor responses including Google's Bard, Amazon Q and DeepMind's Gemini; Stella Biderman's reframing of it as a "user experience breakthrough" rather than an AI one; the psychology of conversational interfaces per Ruoxi Shang and Adam Hyland; and measured productivity effects.
