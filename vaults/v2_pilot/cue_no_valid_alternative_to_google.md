# Cue's 2023-09-26 "No Valid Alternative" Argument for Apple's Google Default

Testifying on 2023-09-26 in US v. Google, Apple's Eddy Cue argued that Apple never seriously considered switching to another search provider or building its own search product, because "Certainly there wasn't a valid alternative to Google at the time." He said there still isn't one.

The claim's scope is the 2016 renegotiation period, when Cue negotiated the current iteration of the Information Services Agreement (ISA) with Google CEO Sundar Pichai — the deal, in place since 2002, that makes Google the default search engine on Apple devices and pays Apple billions of dollars a year.

Cue's warrant was that the choice was product-driven rather than purely economic: the ISA is Apple's own evaluation, and he said "We make Google be the default search engine because we've always thought it was the best. We pick the best one and let users easily change it." Supporting that, he told DOJ lawyer Meagan Bellshaw he'd never really considered walking away from the deal.
