# Taylor Swift at the Chiefs–Broncos Week 6 "Thursday Night Football" Game (CBSSports.com Timeline)

CBSSports.com's 2023-10-12 timeline of the rumored Taylor Swift–Travis Kelce romance gives, as its second dated attendance record, that "last week, she was in attendance to watch the Chiefs as they defeated the Denver Broncos in Week 6 on 'Thursday Night Football.'" This follows the first, her appearance at the Chiefs' Week 3 game against the Chicago Bears on Sept. 24. The same article's lede counts five Chiefs games Swift attended that season, including the Sunday matchup against the Los Angeles Chargers at Arrowhead Stadium.
