# Al Saadi: Palestinians fight two wars, the second against the Western media landscape

Al Saadi argues that Palestinians fight two wars at once, quoted in the TechCrunch report of 19 October 2023: "The first is with their legal occupier. The second war is with the entire Western media landscape … I mean social media." Her warrant is her own repeated experience of platform restrictions — a pattern the article documents in Khitam Jabr's 24-hour blocks on Palestine travel Reels, and in the third-party report Meta commissioned after the 2021 violent outbreak between Hamas and Israel, which found an "adverse human rights impact" on Palestinian users' freedom of expression and political participation. The claim is scoped to the information dimension of occupation, not its military or legal questions.
