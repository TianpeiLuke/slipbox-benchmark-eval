# Google's "a billion people" sideloading figure, as clarified to The Verge

In Epic v. Google opening arguments, reported by The Verge on 2023-11-07, Google lead attorney Glenn Pomerantz told the jury that over a billion people have gone through the process Epic portrays as needlessly onerous for installing apps outside the Google Play Store. Google later corrected the meaning of that figure in an email to The Verge: the number counts people who have turned on Android's setting that permits apps from outside Play, after being shown a warning about the risks — not people who necessarily completed such an install.
