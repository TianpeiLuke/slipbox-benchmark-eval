# Benfica beat Salzburg 3-1 in Austria to take Group D's Europa League spot

Salzburg went into the final matchday of UEFA Champions League Group D 2023/24 holding their own Europa League destiny, but on 12 December 2023 an Angel Di Maria-inspired Benfica won 3-1 in Austria. Benfica and RB Salzburg both finished the six-game group on 4 points with a goal difference of -4, so Roger Schmidt's Benfica took third place and the Europa League berth on goals scored, with Arthur Cabral's stoppage-time strike proving decisive.
