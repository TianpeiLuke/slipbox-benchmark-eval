# The SEO practitioner stereotype: hustler, content goblin, nihilist prankster

The stereotype of the stock SEO practitioner, as described in The Verge's 2023-11-01 article "The people who ruined the internet", is that of a hustler: a "content goblin" willing to eschew rules, morals, and good taste in exchange for eyeballs and mountains of cash. The same figure is a nihilist in it for the thrills, a prankster gleeful about getting away with something. Cade Lee, who spent over a decade working in SEO, characterized the practice at conferences as "modern-day pirate shit" and noted an arrogance among practitioners: "You succeed, and now you're a genius. Now you've outdone Google."
