# Bankman-Fried pleaded not guilty in January to fraud charges carrying up to 115 years

In January, Sam Bankman-Fried pleaded not guilty to all counts against him, which include wire fraud, conspiracy to commit money laundering and conspiracy to misuse customer funds, according to TechCrunch's 2023-10-01 account of the FTX collapse. He could face up to 115 years in jail if convicted on all charges.
