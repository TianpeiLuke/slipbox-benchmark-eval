# Taylor Swift Cheering Travis Kelce at Arrowhead Stadium, September 25, 2023

On the Monday covered in The Age's September 26, 2023 timeline of Taylor Swift and the NFL, the 12-time Grammy winner Swift attended a Kansas City Chiefs game at Arrowhead Stadium. She cheered on Kelce, the Chiefs tight end wearing No. 87, with gusto, and appeared to shout "let's f—ing go" after Kelce scored a touchdown, before chest-bumping other Chiefs fans. Swifties were immediately on high alert, with some capturing Swift and Kelce leaving the stadium together.
