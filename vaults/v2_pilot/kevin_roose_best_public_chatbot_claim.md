# Kevin Roose's ChatGPT Superlative as Concession from a Skeptical Press

The argument from credibility: when a columnist trained to distrust hype calls a product the best of its kind, the praise carries more weight. On TechCrunch's report of November 30, 2023, in "One year later, ChatGPT is still alive and kicking," The New York Times' Kevin Roose is named among the "cynics" that ChatGPT "impressed," having called it "the best AI chatbot ever released to the general public." The force of that superlative lies in who withheld it beforehand.
