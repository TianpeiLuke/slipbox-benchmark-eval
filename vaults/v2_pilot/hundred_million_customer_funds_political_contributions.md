# FTX indictment alleges $100M+ in customer funds went to political contributions, uncharged but admissible

The August 14th superseding indictment against Sam Bankman-Fried alleges he used more than $100 million of misappropriated FTX customer funds to make political contributions — a use of customer money distinct from the real estate (more than $200 million), investments, and Alameda lender repayments also cited. Those contributions are not among the seven counts of wire fraud and conspiracy charged in his trial, which began in late September 2023, but prosecutors may still introduce evidence of them; a second trial with additional charges was scheduled for March 2024 (The Verge, 2023-09-28).
