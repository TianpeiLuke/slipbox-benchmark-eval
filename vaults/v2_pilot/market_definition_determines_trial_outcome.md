# Epic v. Google's outcome hinges on which market the court defines

In Epic v. Google, which went to trial on November 6th, 2023, the trial's outcome turns on the market definition the court adopts — the line of argument The Verge names "market definition." If the court decides the relevant market is phones and app stores, not specifically Android ones, then Google's on the road to victory, since Google argues the real competition is Apple and buyers can choose an iPhone instead. If it accepts that Android apps are their own market, Epic will be in better shape; Epic claims illegal monopolies in "Android app distribution" and "Android in-app payment processing." The hypothesis is falsified by whichever definition the judge records, including a different one entirely, as the judge in the Apple case ended up picking.
