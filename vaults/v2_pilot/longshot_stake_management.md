# Stake Sizing for NFL Award Longshot Bets: Keep It Small or Use a Bonus Bet

Sporting News' December 5, 2023 guidance on NFL award longshots (MVP, OPOY, DPOY, Coach of the Year, Comeback Player of the Year) is to keep the stake small, or to use a promotional bonus bet if one is available at the sportsbook. The reasoning is that a player or several players have relatively short odds because they have been the best at their position all season, and there is just as good a reason why longshots carry odds as long as the ones they do. So the longshot's price reflects a genuine, not merely overlooked, case against it.

Sizing down, or staking money the bettor did not deposit, caps the loss on a low-probability outcome while leaving the payout upside intact: if the longshot goes on to win, the bettor collects the full odds on the original price rather than a shorter one obtained by waiting, since with five weeks left in the 2023 regular season a longshot can still become a contender, and the payout falls the longer the bet is deferred.
