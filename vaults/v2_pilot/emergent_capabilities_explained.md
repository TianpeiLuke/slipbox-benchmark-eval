# 2024 research on "emergent" LLM capabilities will explain their irregular profile, including inability to multiply large numbers

Prediction made in TechCrunch's "8 predictions for AI in 2024" (published 2023-12-19): the seemingly magical "emergent" capabilities of large language models (LLMs) will be better studied and understood in 2024, and anomalies in their capability profile — including their inability to multiply large numbers — will "make more sense." The stated backdrop is that LLMs have proved more capable than anyone expected and have grown more so as more compute is added, but 2024 "will be the year something gives." Falsified if emergent behavior remains unanalyzed at the research frontier, where the article itself concedes activity is still unresolved and "where exactly it is impossible to predict."
