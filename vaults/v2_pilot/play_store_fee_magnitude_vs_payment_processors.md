# The Verge: Google's Play fees run up to ten times a PayPal or credit card transaction

In its explainer "Epic v. Google, explained" (The Verge, Nov. 5, 2023), the author leaves the "market definition" argument and offers a plainer yardstick: Google charges up to ten times more per transaction than a consumer pays with PayPal or a credit card. The Verge presents that multiple as a lay comparison supporting Epic's case against the "Google tax," not as evidence entered at trial.
