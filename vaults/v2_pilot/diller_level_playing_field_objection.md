# Diller's Counter: Google's Ad Load Punishes the Organic Results It Depends On

In US v Google, IAC/Expedia chairman Barry Diller testified on November 1, 2023, and his 2019 letter to Google quoted in the same section objects to a different practice than the default deals: Google's growing ad load in search results, which he argues degrades the organic listings and the players dependent on them that need a level playing field.

The target of that attack is Google's own defense — that its results are best-in-class and so deserve their defaults. Expedia's dependence is visible in the record: Judge Amit Mehta made public a list of 20 terms that were lucrative for the week of September 22, 2018, and among them sits "Cheap flights," one of the perennial commercial queries the article says shows "how much Google dominates vertical search," search in very specific market categories.
