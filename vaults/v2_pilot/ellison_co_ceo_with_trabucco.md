# Ellison became Alameda Research co-CEO in 2021, during her breakup with Bankman-Fried

Caroline Ellison joined Alameda Research in 2018 and later ran the hedge fund; she was also in a romantic relationship with its founder, FTX's Sam Bankman-Fried. In 2021, during the interval when Ellison and Bankman-Fried were broken up, she was named co-CEO of Alameda Research alongside Sam Trabucco. She had pleaded guilty to fraud, conspiracy to commit fraud, and money laundering before testifying against him, as The Verge reported on 2023-10-10.
