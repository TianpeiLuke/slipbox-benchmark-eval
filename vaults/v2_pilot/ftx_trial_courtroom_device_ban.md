# No Devices Allowed in the SBF Trial Courtroom Limits How the Public Follows It

Coverage of the trial of Sam Bankman-Fried, former CEO and co-founder of crypto exchange FTX, is constrained by the terms of access inside the courtroom. The proceedings began with jury selection on October 3, 2023, and no devices are allowed, so reporters’ dispatches are physically put together within the courtroom or sent out to overflow rooms.
