# Juror Awake Again Once Caroline Ellison Took the Stand

Court coverage of the Sam Bankman-Fried fraud trial on 2023-10-10 records a juror who had dozed off while testimony turned to the FTT token snapping awake when Caroline Ellison — head of Alameda Research, already pleaded guilty to fraud, conspiracy to commit fraud and money laundering — began testifying about her relationship with Bankman-Fried, the defendant facing seven counts of wire fraud and conspiracy.
