# The Rationalists: Yudkowsky's community, later called effective altruists

Eliezer Yudkowsky — an internet philosopher and self-taught AI researcher — led a community whose members called themselves the Rationalists and, in later years, effective altruists, as described in *The Age* on 9 December 2023. They were bound by a double belief: that AI could find a cure for cancer or solve climate change, and that AI bots might do things their creators had not intended. If the machines became more intelligent than humans, the Rationalists argued, the machines could turn on their creators. That outlook reached the British founders of DeepMind — Demis Hassabis and two colleagues who, in 2010, were looking for money to start building "artificial general intelligence," or AGI — many of whom had a connection to Yudkowsky.
