# NFL Award Longshot Payouts Shrink With Five Weeks Left in the 2023 Season

As of December 5, 2023, with five weeks remaining in the NFL regular season, a bettor's potential payout on an award longshot falls the longer the wait, according to Sporting News: waiting until the favorite "locks it in" means "your payout will be a lot less than if you were to roll the dice on a longshot right now." After Week 13, Brock Purdy, Dak Prescott and Jalen Hurts led MVP boards and Tyreek Hill was the heavy Offensive Player of the Year favorite, yet five weeks is still "plenty of time for a longshot to become a contender."
