# Epic's Antitrust-Sensitive Chats Went Silent and Were Destroyed, Sweeney Says

Tim Sweeney told The Verge on 2023-12-12, after the Epic v. Google jury verdict, that during the trial it was called out in detail how message threads on antitrust-sensitive topics in Epic's chat systems fell silent the moment a participant pointed out that chat history was being recorded. After that, according to Sweeney, retention was switched off and the messages were destroyed. He added that many of Google's practices he had suspected since roughly 2018, including leaking Epic's conversations to reporters, proved true in discovery.
