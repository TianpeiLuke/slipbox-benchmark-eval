# Lutke's Objection: Sympathetic Coverage of SBF, Hostility Toward Value-Creating Founders

An objection to the framing of Michael Lewis's account of Sam Bankman-Fried came from Shopify founder tobi lutke, posting on October 2, 2023 about a Lewis appearance on 60 Minutes discussing "Going Infinite," the book debuting on the first day of SBF's trial. Not disputing the profile's facts, lutke wrote: "60 Minutes got rightly community noted here. How is it that SBF is _still_ getting super positive media coverage when the media has nothing good to say about the founders that have actually created things and brought value into the world?" The counter-argument is that the press keeps extending a fraud defendant sympathetic, close-access treatment while withholding equivalent coverage from founders who build things.
