# Singh's testimony on the "Donations Processing" Signal chat and blank checks for FTX donations

In The Verge's coverage of the Sam Bankman-Fried trial on 2023-10-26, Nishad Singh, a longtime family friend of Bankman-Fried who pleaded guilty to six charges including wire fraud, money laundering, and conspiracy counts around his political donations, identified a Signal group chat named "Donations Processing." Gabe Bankman-Fried, co-founder of the nonprofit Guarding Against Pandemics, which received millions from FTX, was in that chat, which Singh said was used to coordinate the transfer of FTX and Alameda Research money to various political causes. In the chat, Gabe Bankman-Fried can be seen recommending that some donations be made "in the dark" to avoid public relations headaches. Singh also said Gabe Bankman-Fried had an assistant fly to the Bahamas with a stack of blank checks for Singh to sign.
