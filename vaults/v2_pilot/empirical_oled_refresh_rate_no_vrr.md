# Steam Deck OLED Display Hits 90 Hz but Lacks Variable Refresh Rate

The HDR OLED display on the Steam Deck OLED tops out at 90Hz, whereas the original LCD Steam Deck could only reach 60Hz, per Engadget's review dated 2023-11-09. However, the newer display does not support variable refresh rates (VRR). Engadget describes the updated display as "smooth, crisp and bright."
