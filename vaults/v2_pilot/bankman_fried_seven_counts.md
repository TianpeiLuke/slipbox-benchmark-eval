# Bankman-Fried Faced Seven Counts at Close of Prosecution's Case, October 2023

At the close of the prosecution's case in October 2023, Sam Bankman-Fried faced seven counts: two counts of wire fraud and five conspiracy charges, according to The Verge's reporting on 2023-10-26. The trial concerned fraud at the FTX exchange and his trading firm Alameda Research.
