# Engadget's verdict: the Steam Deck OLED is a refinement, not a successor

In its 2023-11-09 review, Engadget scored the Steam Deck OLED 89 and concluded it "isn't the Steam Deck 2 some are waiting for, it's just a welcome improvement on an already good device" — a mid-cycle refresh from Valve, comparable to the Switch OLED. The evidence it marshals points one way: a 7.4-inch OLED at 1,000 nits peak and 90Hz replacing the original's 7-inch, 400-nit, 60Hz LCD; roughly 30 grams less weight; better thermals; and a Hades run of 4 hours 23 minutes against 2 hours 42 minutes on the LCD model, a 62 percent gain exceeding Valve's own 30 to 50 percent estimate. The single counterweight is the unchanged chassis — both listed cons, "Still ridiculously large" and "Seriously it's so big," say the same thing. Priced at $549 on Steam, the device reads as better, not new.
