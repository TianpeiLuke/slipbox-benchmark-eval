# Megan Gray: DuckDuckGo ties and her standing position on Google Search (October 2023)

In October 2023, per a report published 2023-10-22 in The Age, Megan Gray is a former vice-president at DuckDuckGo, the privacy-focused company founded explicitly to counter giants such as Google. She admits that she may have misinterpreted the evidence, but she maintains that Google manipulates Search to maximise ad revenue.
