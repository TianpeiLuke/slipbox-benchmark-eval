# Epic's Core Argument: Google's OEM and Developer Agreements Are Unlawful Restraints

Epic Games' claim against Google, tried from November 6, 2023, rests on one argument: Google's agreements with Android device manufacturers and app developers are anticompetitive restraints under Sections 1 and 2 of the Sherman Act and under California's Cartwright Act and Unfair Competition Law, regardless of whether Android permits sideloading.

That sideloading point matters because it distinguishes this case from Epic's suit against Apple, where Epic alleged there was no other way to load apps. Here Android does allow sideloading, so Epic contends those agreements — not the absence of alternatives — are what restrain competition in two markets: distribution of apps to Android users, and payment processing solutions for content inside Android apps. Epic cites Anti-Fragmentation Agreements blocking OEMs from modifying Android for frictionless downloading outside Google Play, through which 90% of apps are still downloaded.
