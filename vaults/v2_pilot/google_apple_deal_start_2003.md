# Google's default-search deal with Apple dates to 2003

In its 2023-10-31 report on the U.S. antitrust trial against Google, TechCrunch stated that "Google has had its deal with Apple in place since 2003." That is the agreement making Google the default search engine on Apple devices, so it had been running roughly twenty years by the time the deal's cost came under scrutiny in court in late October 2023.
