# The "winter break hypothesis" for ChatGPT's December performance decline

The "winter break hypothesis" is a user-proposed explanation, circulating in December 2023, for why ChatGPT appeared to worsen in quality at year's end. It argues that AI is worse in December because it "learned" to do less work over the holidays, having absorbed seasonal patterns of reduced human labor from its training data.

The hypothesis arose because, starting in November 2023, ChatGPT users noticed the chatbot felt "lazier" than normal, citing instances of simpler answers and refusing to complete requested tasks. OpenAI confirmed on December 8, 2023 that it was aware of the complaints about GPT-4 getting lazier, stated it hadn't updated the model since November 11, 2023, and said the behavior "certainly isn't intentional" and unpredictable, and that it was looking into fixing it. Beyond the winter break hypothesis, some users wondered if the chatbot was simulating seasonal depression.
