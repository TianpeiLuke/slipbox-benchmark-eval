# The Singularity as the Trope Orienting Peter Thiel's AI Giving

The singularity is a trope of science fiction describing the moment at which intelligent technology can no longer be controlled by humanity. In 2010, the tech investor Peter Thiel had developed a fascination with it, according to an article in The Age published on 2023-12-09, and that fascination oriented his involvement in artificial intelligence: he was courting founders building "artificial general intelligence," or AGI, a machine that could do anything the brain could do. The same fear ran through the community of "Rationalists" — later called effective altruists — clustered around the internet philosopher and self-taught AI researcher Eliezer Yudkowsky, who argued that machines more intelligent than humans could turn on their creators.

Thiel made his fortune through an early investment in Facebook and his work with Elon Musk in the early days of PayPal.
