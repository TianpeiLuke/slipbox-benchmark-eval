# Epic's opening tied a $360M payment to Activision staying out of app stores

In Epic Games v. Google opening arguments on 2023-11-07, as reported by The Verge, Epic lead attorney Gary Bornstein told jurors, "It's like Google saying, 'Here's $360 million' — that's an actual number you'll hear about — why don't you sit this one out and let me win?" That $360 million refers to an alleged payment that kept Activision from opening an app store that could compete with Google Play.
