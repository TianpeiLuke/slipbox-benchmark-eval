# Order of Alameda Research Co-Conspirator Recruitment per Bankman-Fried's Testimony

In his federal trial testimony, reported by The Verge on 2023-10-28, Sam Bankman-Fried described a hiring spree at Alameda Research, the cryptocurrency trading firm he started in 2017 in the arbitrage business, that brought on his alleged co-conspirators in sequence: first Gary Wang, to program the computers; then Nishad Singh, about a month after founding Alameda Research; and finally Caroline Ellison.

Bankman-Fried also testified that although he was Alameda's CEO and majority owner, he did not supervise Wang's direct work.
