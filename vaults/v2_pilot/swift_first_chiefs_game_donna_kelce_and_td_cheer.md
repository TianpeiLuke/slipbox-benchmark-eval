# At Swift's first Chiefs game, only observed behavior — no statement from her

CBSSports.com's Oct. 12, 2023 timeline documents observable behavior at the first Kansas City Chiefs game Taylor Swift attended, the Week 3 game against the Chicago Bears on Sept. 24, 2023 — roughly a month before that article, and not any statement by Swift herself. There she was seen chatting with Donna Kelce, tight end Travis Kelce's mother, and cheering emphatically when No. 87 scored. That touchdown, a three-yard catch, put Kansas City up 41-0.
