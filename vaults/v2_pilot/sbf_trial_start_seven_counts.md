# SBF Criminal Trial Began 2023-10-03 on Seven Counts of Fraud and Conspiracy

The highly anticipated criminal trial of Sam Bankman-Fried, former CEO of the bankrupt crypto exchange FTX, began on Tuesday, 2023-10-03, according to a TechCrunch article dated 2023-10-01. The trial was to determine whether he is guilty of seven counts of fraud and conspiracy. A former federal prosecutor quoted in the article said: "The odds seem to be stacked against him at this point."
