# The Fed's transmission mechanism: high rates slow the economy and depress asset prices

As of October 1, 2023, the Federal Reserve is trying to push still-high inflation down to its target. Its main tool, high interest rates, does that by trying to slow the economy and hurting prices for investments. The Fed's main interest rate is at its highest level since 2001, and the central bank indicated in the week before October 1, 2023 that it may cut interest rates next year by less than it earlier expected. The associated rate-holding stance is described as "higher for longer."
