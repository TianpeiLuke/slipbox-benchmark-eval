# FTX peaked at a $32 billion valuation as the third-largest crypto exchange before bankruptcy

In TechCrunch's coverage of the Sam Bankman-Fried trial, dated 2023-10-06, FTX is described as having gone from "the third-largest crypto exchange valued at a peak of $32 billion" to bankruptcy. That fall frames the trial of Bankman-Fried, formerly FTX's CEO and co-founder, then standing trial on seven counts tied to fraud and money laundering.
