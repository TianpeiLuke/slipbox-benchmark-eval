# Yedidia's Padel Court Testimony on the $8 Billion Hole

Adam Yedidia — Sam Bankman-Fried's college friend, Bahamas roommate, and an employee at FTX — testified that he had asked Bankman-Fried about the $8 billion hole on a padel tennis court in their luxury complex in June or July. Bankman-Fried, testifying in his own defense at his fraud trial, appeared to deny that conversation had ever taken place. It was not until Judge Lewis Kaplan intervened to ask whether Bankman-Fried had ever been told by Yedidia about that money, in words or in substance, that Bankman-Fried admitted he had been told. Reported by The Verge on 2023-10-31.
