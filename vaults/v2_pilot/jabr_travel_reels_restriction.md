# Filmmaker Khitam Jabr Locked Out of Instagram for 24 Hours After Anti-Palestine Comments on Non-Political West Bank Reels

In 2023, Palestinian-American filmmaker Khitam Jabr posted a handful of Reels about her family's trip to the West Bank — her first visit in a decade — short travel vlogs sharing snippets of Palestinian culture, from eating decadent meals to dancing at her niece's wedding. The anti-Palestine comments those Reels drew left her unable to respond to them or use her account for 24 hours. "I wasn't even posting anything about the occupation," Jabr told TechCrunch in its report of 2023-10-19. She suspects Instagram restricted her because other users reported her Palestine travel videos.
