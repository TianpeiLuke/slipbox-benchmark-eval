# Grand Theft Auto V Is the Second-Best-Selling Video Game of All Time, Behind Minecraft

As of TechCrunch's Week in Review dated 2023-12-09, Grand Theft Auto V — the previous entry in Rockstar Games' long-running franchise — remains the second-best-selling video game of all time, falling short only of Minecraft. TechCrunch cites that standing as context for excitement "a decade in the making" around Grand Theft Auto VI, whose first trailer drew 85 million YouTube views in 22 hours.
