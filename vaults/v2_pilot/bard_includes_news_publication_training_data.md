# Google Bard trained on news publisher content, Helena World Chronicle complaint alleges

In the class action antitrust complaint Helena World Chronicle filed in the U.S. District Court in D.C. in December 2023, the Arkansas publisher alleges that Google's Bard AI chatbot was trained on a dataset that included "news, magazine and digital publications." In support, the complaint cites a 2023 report from the News Media Alliance and a Washington Post article on AI training data. That Post reporting, done with researchers at the Allen Institute for AI, found that News and Media sites were the third largest category of AI training data, according to a TechCrunch account published 2023-12-15.
