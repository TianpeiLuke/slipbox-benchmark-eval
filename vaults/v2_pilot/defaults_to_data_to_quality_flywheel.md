# Pichai's Loop: Default Payments, Data Intake, Search Quality

In the U.S. antitrust trial over Google's search dominance, CEO Sundar Pichai defended what Google spends on default placement with a self-reinforcing account of why the money is justified. Being the default on other companies' browsers, phones and platforms produces more searches; more searches mean more data taken in; more data make Google the best engine across those devices; and being the best explains why partners choose it and why further default payments are reasonable. TechCrunch reported this on 2023-10-31, describing Pichai's testimony that Monday, two days after search head Prabhakar Raghavan testified on 2023-10-28 that Google had paid $26.3 billion in 2021 for default placement. Pichai added that the decision was made with the consumer in mind. The loop is Google's own testimony about its motives, not an independently established causal finding, and the government contests its premise.
