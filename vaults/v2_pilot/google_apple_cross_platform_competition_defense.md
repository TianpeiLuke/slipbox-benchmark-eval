# Google's counter-argument: iPhone substitution, not Android app stores, defines the market

Google rebutted Epic's market definition in Epic v. Google — which went to trial on Nov. 6, 2023, some 1,180 days after Epic first sued — by arguing the real competition is Apple, because people can choose to buy an iPhone instead. On that view, Google's app store competes with Apple's across platforms, so it is "pretty tough to argue Google has a monopoly on app stores in general." Epic's contrary position: Android app distribution and Android in-app payment processing are each their own market.
