# Ian Eagle's "Blank Space" touchdown call, September 17, 2023

On September 17, 2023, Travis Kelce scored a touchdown against the Jaguars, prompting CBS announcer Ian Eagle to say "Kelce finds a blank space for the score" — a reference to Taylor Swift's 2014 hit *Blank Space*, poking fun at Kelce's newfound Swiftie status. The Age (2023-09-26) places this as the first Swift lyric to cross into NFL game broadcast commentary, ahead of NFL Network's Rich Eisen's preshow lyric run.
