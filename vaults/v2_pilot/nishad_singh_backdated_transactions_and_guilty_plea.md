# Nishad Singh's Guilty Plea: Backdated FTX Transactions and Doctored Balance Sheets

Nishad Singh, described as a longtime family friend of Sam Bankman-Fried, admitted to conduct that sounded like classic embezzlement while testifying during Bankman-Fried's trial. Singh said he backdated transactions at FTX in order to help produce doctored balance sheets to show to investors. He pleaded guilty to six charges: wire fraud, money laundering, and conspiracy counts concerning his political donations. This testimony comes from reporting by The Verge dated 2023-10-26, written as the prosecution's case against Bankman-Fried — charged with seven counts, two of wire fraud and five of conspiracy — was closing. Singh had already become a felon by that time.
