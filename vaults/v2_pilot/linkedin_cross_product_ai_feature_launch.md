# LinkedIn Announced a Suite of AI Features Across Job Hunting, Marketing and Sales Products, Week of 7 October 2023

In the week of 7 October 2023, LinkedIn unveiled a string of new AI features spanning its job hunting, marketing and sales products, according to Ingrid's reporting in TechCrunch's Week in Review digest published 2023-10-07. The announcements comprised three named additions: a big update to LinkedIn's Recruiter talent sourcing platform, with AI assistance built into it throughout; an AI-powered LinkedIn Learning coach; and a new AI-powered tool for marketing campaigns. The item was one of several in that week's roundup, which also covered Google's hardware event and a TikTok ad-free tier test.
