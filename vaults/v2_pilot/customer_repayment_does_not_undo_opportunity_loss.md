# Full FTX Restitution Cannot Undo the Harm of Trapped Customer Funds

When the FTX exchange collapsed in late 2022 because Sam Bankman-Fried's trading firm Alameda Research had purloined its customers' funds, everyone with money trapped on the platform had their life altered, The Verge argued on 2023-10-26 during Bankman-Fried's trial. The courtroom reporting's central claim: even if the bankruptcy proceedings make those customers financially whole, the entitlement already destroyed was access — money that should have been available at any time they wanted it. That lost liquidity translates into concrete harms no restitution reverses: houses that weren't purchased, medical procedures that were left undone, and life savings lost. Compensation restores a balance, not the life that delay forecloses.
