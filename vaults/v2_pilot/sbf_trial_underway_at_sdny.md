# Sam Bankman-Fried's FTX Trial in Session at Manhattan Federal Court, Week Ending 7 October 2023

As of the week ending 7 October 2023, the trial of Sam Bankman-Fried — the disgraced entrepreneur accused of orchestrating the collapse of cryptocurrency exchange FTX — was in session at the Manhattan Federal Court, and TechCrunch reported that its team had been stationed there to cover the proceedings. The observation appears in TechCrunch's Week in Review digest of that week, in the item headlined "SBF allegedly tried to buy off Trump," which described the trial as the setting for emerging details about Bankman-Fried's political dealings.
