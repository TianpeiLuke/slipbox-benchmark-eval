# High-Profile Spectators in Court on Day Two of the Bankman-Fried Trial

Day two of Sam Bankman-Fried's FTX trial, reported by Fortune on 2023-10-04, drew an audience "replete with big names": Bankman-Fried's professorial parents and Damian Williams, the U.S. attorney for the Southern District of New York. The article cites that line-up as evidence of the case's prominence.
