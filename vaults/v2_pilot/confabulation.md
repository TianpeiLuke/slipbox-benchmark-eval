# Confabulation in generative AI: fluent statements that are untrue (Gemini Pro, Dec 2023)

Confabulation, as applied to a generative AI model, means the model producing fluent, confident statements that are simply untrue — as distinct from refusing to answer or acknowledging uncertainty. Science fiction author Charlie Stross found many examples of confabulation in a recent blog post about Gemini Pro, the "lite" version of Google's flagship generative AI model that began rolling out to Bard (Google's ChatGPT competitor) the day before TechCrunch's report of 2023-12-07. Among other mistruths recorded in that post, Gemini Pro said that Stross contributed to the Linux kernel; he never has.
