# Google's November 2023 Canadian Agreement: $73.5 Million a Year to News Organisations, Allocated by Headcount

Under an agreement Google reached with the Canadian government in November 2023 — the month before the Helena World Chronicle class action was filed in the U.S. District Court in D.C. on 2023-12-15 — the search giant will pay Canadian media for use of their content. The terms set Google's contribution at $73.5 million (100 million Canadian dollars) every year to news organisations in the country, with the funds distributed according to each news outlet's headcount. The same reporting notes that negotiations between the Canadian government and Meta remained unresolved at that point; Meta had begun blocking news in Canada in August.
