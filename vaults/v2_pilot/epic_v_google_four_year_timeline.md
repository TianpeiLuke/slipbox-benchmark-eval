# Epic v. Google as a Four-Year Sequence, Originating in a September 2019 Plan Email

The Epic Games v. Google antitrust proceeding reads as a dated four-year chain rather than a single lawsuit. Its causal origin is a September 2019 email surfaced in discovery, describing Epic's plan to draw Google into a legal battle over antitrust — the document that predates and sets up everything after it. That plan became a four-week federal antitrust trial before a nine-person jury, which found on Monday, December 11, 2023 that Google holds an illegal monopoly over Android app distribution and in-app payment systems and illegally tied Google Play billing to its app store. The Verge's December 12, 2023 interview with Epic CEO Tim Sweeney frames the verdict as "four years in the making." Sweeney dates his own suspicion of Google's practices to 2018, one year earlier than the email.
