# Group B final standings in the 2023/24 UEFA Champions League group stage

The Group B table at the close of the 2023/24 UEFA Champions League group stage, reported by Sporting News on 2023-12-12 after matchday six, put Arsenal top on 13 points from 6 games (4 wins, 1 draw, 1 loss, goal difference +12) and PSV second on 9 points (2 wins, 3 draws, 1 loss, goal difference -2), both qualifying for the Round of 16. Lens, on 8 points, took the Europa League place; Sevilla finished last with 2 points.
