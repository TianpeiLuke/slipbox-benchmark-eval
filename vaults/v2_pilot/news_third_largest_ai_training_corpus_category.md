# Post/Allen Institute finding: News and Media sites are the third-largest category of AI training data

A Washington Post analysis conducted with researchers at the Allen Institute for AI found that News and Media sites form the third-largest category of material in AI training datasets. The Helena World Chronicle's class action antitrust suit against Google and parent Alphabet — reported by TechCrunch on 2023-12-15 — cited that finding, together with a 2023 News Media Alliance report, in arguing that Google's Bard was trained on "news, magazine and digital publications."
