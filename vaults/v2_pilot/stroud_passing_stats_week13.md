# C.J. Stroud's League-Leading Passing Stats Through Week 13 of the 2023 NFL Season

Through Week 13 of the 2023 NFL regular season, Houston Texans quarterback C.J. Stroud led the league in passing yards, according to a Sporting News awards-odds piece published 2023-12-05. He was also one of only four quarterbacks to have started the whole season with a passer rating above 100, and his touchdown-to-interception ratio of 4 to 1 was the best in the NFL. The article cited those three figures as the reason bettors should consider Stroud an MVP longshot behind Brock Purdy, Dak Prescott, and Jalen Hurts.
