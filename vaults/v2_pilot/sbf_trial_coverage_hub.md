# TechCrunch's Guide to Following Its SBF Trial Coverage

TechCrunch's running hub for the Sam Bankman-Fried trial, dated 2023-10-06, gathers the outlet's coverage in one place. Jacquelyn Melinek, TechCrunch's crypto expert, has reported on the ground since the trial began with jury selection on October 3 — access being limited because no devices are allowed and reports are physically put together inside the courtroom or sent to overflow rooms. The hub links Melinek's breakdown of how FTX went from the third-largest crypto exchange, valued at a peak of $32 billion, to bankruptcy. It also points to TechCrunch+ analysis of what to expect from both the prosecution's and the defense's arguments and the takeaways from the opening arguments, the Chain Reaction newsletter (Thursdays at 12 p.m. PT), and the Chain Reaction podcast, which hits listeners' feeds every Thursday.
