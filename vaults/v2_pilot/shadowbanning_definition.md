# Shadowbanning: Visibility Limitation Without Notice

Shadowbanning is the platform-side limitation of a post's visibility or reach, imposed without notice to the account holder — the sense in which TechCrunch's Oct 19, 2023 report on Meta glosses the term as limiting "the visibility of" content. Because nothing announces it, the effect is indistinguishable from ordinary fluctuation in algorithmic distribution: Instagram users who "accused Meta of shadowbanning their Stories about Palestine" in October 2023 observed only falling reach, while Meta attributed a Suppression to a "bug impacting all Stories that re-shared Reels and Feed posts" and blamed technical errors for removals during the 2021 conflict. Bella Hadid reported her Story views dropping by "almost 1 million."
