# 60 Minutes left Michael Lewis's FTX introducer unnamed and his investment unconfirmed

In the TechCrunch account (2023-10-02) of the 60 Minutes interview broadcast the day before, author Michael Lewis said a friend who lived in Berkeley introduced him to Sam Bankman-Fried in early 2021 and asked him to meet SBF to "evaluate his character." That friend's identity is never disclosed, nor whether he ever backed FTX — a exchange that raised some $2 billion, including $1 billion in 2021, from Sequoia, Temasek, Tiger Global and dozens more.
