# Rodriguez-Villegas's diagnosis-gap argument as the warrant for founding Acurable

In TechCrunch's Week in Review of 2023-10-07, the account of Rosario Rodriguez-Villegas becoming a founder rests on a single diagnostic claim: the medical devices currently available make conditions such as sleep apnea and epilepsy extremely difficult to detect and treat. That claim is the warrant for her move out of academia and into founding Acurable. Its force depends on the setting it targets — the argument is scoped to catching these conditions at home, not inside a clinical facility, where the machinery she finds inadequate is already installed and operated by specialists.
