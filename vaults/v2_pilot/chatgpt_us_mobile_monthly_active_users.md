# ChatGPT's iOS and Android apps at 4.9 million U.S. monthly active users, per Similarweb

Similarweb, the web metrics company, reported in TechCrunch's ChatGPT first-anniversary coverage of November 30, 2023 that OpenAI's ChatGPT iOS and Android applications had 4.9 million monthly active users in the United States alone. The figure covers only the U.S. market and only the mobile apps, one month before the release of the October usage data that Similarweb cited.
