# The Rosewood dinner and the founding of OpenAI (2015)

OpenAI was the laboratory created later in 2015 out of a private dinner in a room at the Rosewood hotel in Menlo Park, California, a favoured deal-making spot close to the venture capital offices of Sand Hill Road. There Elon Musk dined with Sam Altman, who was then running a tech incubator, and several researchers. The dinner came a few weeks after Musk and Google's Larry Page stopped speaking following a summer party in Napa Valley. Backed by hundreds of millions of dollars from Musk and other funders, the new lab promised to protect the world from Page's vision. Per The Age, 9 Dec 2023, OpenAI later became valued at more than US$80 billion (A$123 billion), though the Musk-Altman partnership ended and the two stopped speaking.
