# The Age's Own Caveat: Arrowhead Sighting Does Not Confirm a Romance

The Age concedes, in its own words, that "none of this actually confirms Swift and Kelce are actually dating." Every datapoint the timeline marshals in its favour of romance is equally consistent with friendship. Swift's presence at Arrowhead Stadium on Monday September 2023, her cheering No. 87, the stadium's 1989-era references, Kelce's post-game outfit allegedly labelled the "1989 bedroom painting set", and Swifties capturing the two "leaving the stadium together" — each has a plainer reading: a supporter at a football game, a wardrobe coincidence, two people sharing a ride. The observations are compatible with dating; none of them requires it.
