# Epic's Tying Claim: Google Play Payments Bundled With the Play Store

Alongside its core monopoly allegation, Epic claims in Epic v. Google (trial beginning November 6, 2023, per The Verge, November 5, 2023) that Google illegally ties its Google Play payments platform to the Google Play app store. The consequence Epic draws is that rival in-app payment mechanisms are kept from competing, so the tying claim extends the suit beyond app distribution alone into Android in-app payment processing. This is the second of the two practices Epic says make developers and users "stuck paying the Google tax."
