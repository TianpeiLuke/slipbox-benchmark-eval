# Group-stage dominance predicts a deep 2023/24 Champions League knockout run

Sporting News on 12 December 2023 wrote that Bayern Munich, Real Madrid and Barcelona "will surely be in the mix towards the sharp end" of the 2023/24 UEFA Champions League. Generalised, that editorial claim is a hypothesis: a dominant group stage predicts a deep knockout run.

The observed grounds. Real Madrid, the 14-time European champions, won all six Group C games for 18 points and +9 goal difference. Bayern Munich topped Group A on 16 points from five wins, +6 goal difference.

The test is each club's exit round this season. If any of the three falls in the Round of 16 or the quarter-finals, group-stage form carried no signal about knockout form and the hypothesis is falsified.
