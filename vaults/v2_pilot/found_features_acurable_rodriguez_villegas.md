# Found episode this week featured Acurable's Esther Rodriguez-Villegas

TechCrunch's Week in Review digest (published 2023-10-07) includes in its Audio section a conversation on the Found podcast with Esther Rodriguez-Villegas of Acurable, which the column describes as a medical device company making patient-friendly wearable devices that diagnose and manage respiratory conditions such as sleep apnea at home. The review frames that conversation around Rodriguez-Villegas's status as a career-long academic.
