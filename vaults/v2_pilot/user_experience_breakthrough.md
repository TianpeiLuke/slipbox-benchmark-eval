# Biderman's "User Experience Breakthrough" vs. an AI Breakthrough

In an interview published by TechCrunch on 2023-11-30, Stella Biderman, an AI researcher at Booz Allen Hamilton and the open research group EleutherAI, separated an "AI breakthrough" from what she called a bonafide "user experience breakthrough." The second is a change in how already-existing capability is reached and consumed by ordinary people, not a leap in the capability itself. ChatGPT, released a year earlier as a "low-key research preview," was that: a free, web-based, chat-focused interface on top of OpenAI's existing model GPT-3.5, taking generative AI mainstream. Previously, she said, "you needed to have some skill, albeit not be an expert, to consistently get usable stuff out of [text-generating models]." OpenAI has released dozens of research papers on its models but never one on ChatGPT.
