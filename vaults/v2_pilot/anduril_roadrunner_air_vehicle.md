# Roadrunner: Anduril's reusable autonomous VTOL interceptor

Roadrunner is a product unveiled in December 2023 by Anduril, the controversial defense company co-founded by Oculus founder Palmer Luckey. It is a modular, twin-jet-powered autonomous vertical take-off and landing air vehicle built to counter the proliferation of low-cost, high-powered aerial threats. One version is capable of carrying a warhead.

Operationally, Roadrunner can take off, follow and destroy targets. If there is no need to intercept the target, it autonomously maneuvers back to base for refueling and reuse, making it a reusable interceptor rather than a single-use munition.
