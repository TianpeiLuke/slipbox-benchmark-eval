# Four Spanish Clubs in the 2023/24 Champions League Round of 16

Spain put four clubs into the UEFA Champions League 2023/24 Round of 16, according to Sporting News (2023-12-12). Real Madrid, the 14-time European champions, won Group C with six wins out of six and 18 points, and Barcelona likewise enjoyed a strong group-stage campaign. Atletico Madrid and Real Sociedad completed the Spanish contingent.
