# Gemini Pro: the "lite" tier of Google's Gemini model family

Gemini Pro is the "lite" tier of Google's Gemini generative AI model family, the variant that began rolling out to Bard — Google's ChatGPT competitor — on 2023-12-06. It is not the most capable version of Gemini: Google's own launch materials, described by TechCrunch on 2023-12-07, present Gemini as a family of models, with Pro the lightweight one. The December 2023 complaints about wrong facts, translation errors, and weak coding all concern Gemini Pro specifically, not Gemini's top tier.
