# The SEO Business Model: Ranking High to Convert Google's Objectivity Into Ad Traffic

According to a 2023-11-01 Verge report on search engine optimization, the SEO business model works by borrowing the appearance of neutrality that Google lends to organic results. The practitioner's goal is to tell the algorithm whatever it needs to hear for a site to appear as high up as possible in search results, then use that placement — Google's supposed objectivity — to lure people in and, usually, show them some kind of advertising. The article distinguishes this from simply buying the advertising space labeled "Sponsored" above organic results, comparing SEO instead to the Wizard of Oz projecting his voice to magnify his authority. The scheme is contingent on ranking: it pays only insofar as the site actually reaches the top of results, against a backdrop in which Google holds a 90 percent market share for online search.
