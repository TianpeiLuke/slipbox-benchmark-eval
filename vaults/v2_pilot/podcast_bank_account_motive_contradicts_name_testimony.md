# Podcast remark contradicts Bankman-Fried's "under the radar" account of naming Alameda

On 2023-10-28, at Sam Bankman-Fried's FTX trial before Judge Lewis Kaplan, Bankman-Fried testified that Alameda Research was named for Alameda County in California, site of its first office, and that "effectively, we wanted to be under the radar at that point in time" — he said he did not want to call it "Sam's Crypto Trading Firm," that competitors would race to copy what he was building, and that the earlier internal name was "Wireless Mouse."

The Verge's reporter contests that framing: a video exists of Bankman-Fried explaining on a podcast that the name made it easier to get a bank account. That podcast statement surfaced during the first day of Gary Wang's testimony, an event Bankman-Fried attended — and the jury attended too.
