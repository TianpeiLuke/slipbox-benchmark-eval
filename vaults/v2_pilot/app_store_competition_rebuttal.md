# Google's Competition Defense: App Stores Rivaling Google Play

Google plans to push back against Epic's claim that it faces no competition in the app store business. It will argue that Google Play competes with Apple's App Store — a rivalry that the Ninth Circuit ruling in the Apple case agreed upon, too. Beyond that, Google will point out that Android also supports competitive app stores, specifically Samsung's Galaxy Store and Amazon's Appstore. This counters Epic's assertion that Google restrains competition in the market for distributing apps to Android users. Epic's anticipated response is that Google makes it hard for those alternative app stores to succeed and reach consumers.
