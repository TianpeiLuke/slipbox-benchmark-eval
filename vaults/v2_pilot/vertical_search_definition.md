# Vertical Search: Search Within One Very Specific Market Category

Vertical search is search confined to one very specific commercial market category — car insurance, cheap flights, credit reports or pay-TV — as distinct from general web search. TechCrunch gave that gloss on 2023-11-13, in a roundup of the US v Google antitrust trial, writing that terms like "car insurance," "cheap flights" and "credit report" are "perennial favorites" and "speak to how much Google dominates vertical search — that is, search in very specific market categories." The revenue-ordered query list it was reading from covered the week of September 22, 2018 and also contained "Car insurance quotes," "Cheap car insurance," "Insurance quotes" and the pay-TV service "DirecTV".
