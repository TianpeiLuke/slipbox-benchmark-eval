# Real Madrid finish Group C with a perfect six from six, eliminating Union Berlin

On 12 December 2023, Real Madrid, the 14-time European champions, completed six wins out of six in the UEFA Champions League 2023/24 group stage, beating Union Berlin 3-2 away in a topsy-turvy Group C fixture to finish on 18 points and a goal difference of +9. The defeat also ruled the Bundesliga side, who ended the group with just 2 points, out of the Europa League.
