# Google's annual hardware event announcements, week ending 7 October 2023

In the week ending 7 October 2023, Google held its annual hardware event, at which the search and consumer tech giant showed off what it had been working on. TechCrunch's Week in Review roundup of that event listed announced updates covering the Pixel 8 and Pixel 8 Pro, the Pixel Fold, Android 14, Pixel Buds, Google Assistant, Bard, and Pixel Watch 2, plus "other goodies." TechCrunch's report of the announcements was published on 2023-10-07.
