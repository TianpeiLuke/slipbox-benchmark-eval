# Agent-Based AI Stays Sub-Scale in 2024 but Lands Clutch Use Cases Like Insurance Claims

Hypothesis from TechCrunch's 2023-12-19 piece "8 predictions for AI in 2024": agent-based AI will not take off at scale in 2024, yet will perform "a little more convincingly than they did last year," and a few clutch use cases will surface in famously tedious processes such as submitting insurance claims.

The reasoning: 2023 produced a few tentative attempts at the "agent" approach and none really caught on. Useful agents require access to a user's spreadsheets, ticket buying interfaces, transportation apps and so on — a plumbing problem beyond summarization and list-making. The prediction places agents alongside generated video and generated music as niche applications graduating "from quaint to experimental" in 2024.

Falsified if agents register no credible workflow wins during 2024.
