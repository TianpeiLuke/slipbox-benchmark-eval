# Similarweb data: Google drives nearly 40% of news publishers' web traffic (December 2023)

Data from the analytics firm Similarweb, cited in a Wall Street Journal report that TechCrunch referenced on 2023-12-15, showed Google driving nearly 40% of news publishers' website traffic at that time. TechCrunch quoted the figure while covering the Helena World Chronicle class action antitrust lawsuit against Google and Alphabet.
