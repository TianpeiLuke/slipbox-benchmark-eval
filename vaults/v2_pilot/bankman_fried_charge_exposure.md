# Sam Bankman-Fried Faces Seven Federal Charges Carrying Life Exposure, October 2023

As his fraud trial over the collapsed crypto exchange FTX proceeded on 6 October 2023, Sam Bankman-Fried, 31, faced seven federal charges, including wire fraud, securities fraud and money laundering — charges that could put him in prison for the rest of his life.
