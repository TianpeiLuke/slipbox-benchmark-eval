# Will FTX's creditors ever be made whole as its trial begins?

FTX's criminal trial for Sam Bankman-Fried began Tuesday, per TechCrunch on 2023-10-01, but an unresolved question lingers: will investors and creditors be compensated, and what becomes of the billions in crypto assets tied up in legal proceedings? FTX is "trying to claw back any funds to distribute to creditors." The bankruptcy's eventual creditor distributions test this.
