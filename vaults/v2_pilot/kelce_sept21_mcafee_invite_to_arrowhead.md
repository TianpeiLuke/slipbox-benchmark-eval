# Kelce's Sept 21, 2023 On-Air Response to Swift Rumors: Telephone Analogy and Arrowhead Challenge

On the Pat McAfee show on Sept 21, 2023, Travis Kelce addressed romance speculation with Taylor Swift: "It is hilarious how much traction this has actually got," comparing it to "that old game in school called telephone where everybody is just whispering in each others ear." He then disclosed he had invited Swift to a game: "I threw the ball in her court," telling her he had seen her "rock the stage in Arrowhead" and she "might have to come see me rock the stage in Arrowhead and see which one is a little more lit" (CBSSports.com, 2023-10-12).
