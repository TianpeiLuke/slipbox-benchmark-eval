# September 2023 media reports of Swift and Kelce "quietly hanging out"

In September 2023, it was reported that Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce had been "quietly hanging out" for months in New York. Per The Age's 26 September 2023 timeline of Swift's NFL takeover, this was the first, albeit tentative, confirmation carried by professional media rather than by amateur Swiftie sleuths on social media. The Age notes that, having crossed that threshold, it was only a matter of time before the pairing entered the cultural zeitgeist.
