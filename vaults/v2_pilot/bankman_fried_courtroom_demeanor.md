# Bankman-Fried Visibly Upset During Wang's Testimony, October 2023

CNBC's courtroom report of 5–6 October 2023 records that Sam Bankman-Fried, 31, stayed mostly quiet at his fraud trial, listening intently to witnesses and at times writing notes to his attorneys. But as Gary Wang testified against him, Bankman-Fried looked visibly upset, shifting his gaze from his former friend to the ground and at one point putting his head in his hands.
