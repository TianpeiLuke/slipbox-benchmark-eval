# Everdell's Undisclosed Prior-Statement Exchange with Gary Wang — FTX Trial, 2023-10-10

During Gary Wang's cross-examination at Sam Bankman-Fried's FTX fraud trial on 2023-10-10, defense attorney Christian Everdell put alleged contradictions to Wang — the FTX chief technology officer and co-owner of FTX and Alameda — between his courtroom account of market making and what he had said in earlier interviews with the government. Wang insisted he did not remember those earlier statements. The author notes that whatever Wang was shown was neither submitted as evidence nor shown to the court, and infers, qualifying it with "probably," that Everdell was handing Wang his own previous testimony to refresh his recollection. The author judged it the strongest work the defense had done so far.
