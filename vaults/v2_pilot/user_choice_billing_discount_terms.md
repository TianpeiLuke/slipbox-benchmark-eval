# Google's User Choice Billing cuts Play commission by 4% in 35 pilot markets

Google's User Choice Billing, first introduced in November 2022 and still in pilot testing as the Epic Games v. Google trial opened on November 6, 2023, is open to all developers selling apps in the 35 markets where it is available, including the U.S., and reduces the standard Google Play commission by 4% for companies that choose to use their own payment processing solution. Spotify and Bumble were the initial testers; Match took advantage of the option under its settlement with Google, while Epic Games declined to use it.
