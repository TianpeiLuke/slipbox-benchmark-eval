# Epic's Twin Antitrust Suits Diverged: Apple Settled in 2021, Google Waited to 2023

Epic Games sued Apple and Google on the same day, August 13th, 2020, both over in-app payment fees critics call the "Apple tax" and "Google tax," but the two cases then moved at wildly different speeds. The Apple lawsuit went to court in 2021, and a ruling came down that September mostly in Apple's favor, with both parties still waiting for the Supreme Court to potentially weigh in. The Google suit never got its day in court during those years, delayed again and again, so that Epic v. Google trial beginning November 6th, 2023 arrived "a mere 1,180 days" after Epic filed, per The Verge's article dated 2023-11-05.
