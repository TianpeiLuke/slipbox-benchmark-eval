# SGE rides GoogleBot, so publishers have no clean opt-out

Search Generative Experience (SGE) draws on the same crawler feed that powers Google's ordinary search, which means a publisher cannot refuse SGE's use of its content without also vanishing from regular search results. The class action antitrust suit Helena World Chronicle — an Arkansas publisher of two weekly newspapers — filed against Google and parent Alphabet in the U.S. District Court in D.C. in December 2023 puts it bluntly: publishers "can't block SGE because it uses the same web crawler as Google's general search service, GoogleBot." The complaint's injunction asks Google to let publishers who opt out of SGE still appear in Google search results.
