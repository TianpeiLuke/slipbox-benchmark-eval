# Everdell's spot-margin cross of Gary Wang was confusion strategy, not rebuttal — The Verge, 2023-10-10

Writing about the FTX trial on 2023-10-10 for The Verge, the author argues that defense attorney Christian Everdell's cross-examination of Gary Wang — chief technology officer of FTX and co-owner of both FTX and Alameda — was a credibility play, not a substantive rebuttal. Last week Wang testified that Alameda received a special credit line plus an option to take its balance into the negative without triggering liquidation, which he alleged other FTX accounts did not get. Everdell's response was to steer into the spot margin program, which let users lend each other assets for margin trading and could produce a negative balance in a specific coin. That comparison fails on its own terms, the author notes: spot-margin accounts could neither avoid liquidation nor hold an overall negative balance. So the questioning's only real function was drowning the jury in confusing technicalities — and even that held as a sideshow, because it "couldn't undo the damage of last week's code review."
