# Elden Ring maxed out drained a Steam Deck OLED in 2h 20min, about 40 percent longer than the original

In Engadget's Steam Deck OLED review, published 2023-11-09, the hardest battery test was Elden Ring with settings maxed out. Played that way, Valve's new Steam Deck OLED ran 2h 20min before its battery died — roughly 40 percent longer than the original LCD Steam Deck lasted under the same maxed-out load. The figures come from Engadget's own couch testing of both machines side by side, swapping between the LCD and OLED models rather than relying on Valve's stated 30 to 50 percent battery claim.
