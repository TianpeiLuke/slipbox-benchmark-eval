# Index: CNBC's 6 October 2023 report on Gary Wang's FTX trial testimony

This collection indexes CNBC's 6 October 2023 report on Gary Wang's courtroom testimony in Sam Bankman-Fried's FTX fraud trial. Entry points cover the MIT-background witnesses Wang and Adam Yedidia, Wang's six-page cooperation agreement, the roughly $14 billion hole in Alameda's books, and FTX's final days in November 2022, including the Nov. 12 Bahamas trip.
