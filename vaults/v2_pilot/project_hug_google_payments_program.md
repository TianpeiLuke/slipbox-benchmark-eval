# Source text breaks off before Sweeney's "Project Hug" characterization is stated

In The Verge's December 12, 2023 interview with Epic CEO Tim Sweeney after the jury verdict in Epic v. Google, the published text stops mid-sentence — "You'd think a trillion-dollar compa" — so Sweeney's characterization of Google's "Project Hug" as a corrupt payment programme at massive scale, and the deal structure he is said to compare it with, are not stated in this source and cannot be attributed from it. The verifiable content at that point is Sweeney describing Google "paying other developers off to convince them not to launch their own stores" and "paying carriers and OEMs secretly not to carry competing stores," with OnePlus and carriers telling Epic they could not bundle Fortnite because of a secret Google deal.
