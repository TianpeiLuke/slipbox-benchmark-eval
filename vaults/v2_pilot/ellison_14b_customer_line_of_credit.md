# Caroline Ellison's $14 Billion Testimony, Week Two of the SBF Trial

In week two of Sam Bankman-Fried's fraud and money laundering trial, reported by TechCrunch's Jacquelyn Melinek on 2023-10-06, the standout testimony came from Alameda Research's former CEO Caroline Ellison. She claimed she took $14 billion from customers to repay debts to lenders, using them as a line of credit under the instruction of SBF. The case against Bankman-Fried covers seven counts tied to fraud and money laundering.
