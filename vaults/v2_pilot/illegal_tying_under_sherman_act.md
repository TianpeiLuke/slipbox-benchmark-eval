# "Illegally tied": a Sherman Act wrong in Epic v. Google, not a contract or regulatory breach

In Epic Games v. Google, the nine-person federal jury whose verdict The Verge reported on 2023-12-12 found that Google "illegally tied" its own Google Play billing system to its Android app store — an illegal monopoly over app distribution and in-app payments, so Play Store access came with Google's payment system attached. Epic CEO Tim Sweeney invoked the statute, calling it proof that "the Sherman Antitrust Act works in the new era of tech monopolies," the first such verdict since US v. Microsoft in the 1990s. The wrong is therefore Sherman Act–based, not a contractual or regulatory breach.
