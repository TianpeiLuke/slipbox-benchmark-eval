# FTX co-founder Gary Wang faces up to 50 years at sentencing after trial

Gary Wang, the 30-year-old FTX technology chief and cooperating witness, testified on Friday, October 6, 2023, that he faces up to 50 years in prison when sentenced following Sam Bankman-Fried's fraud trial, per CNBC's report.
