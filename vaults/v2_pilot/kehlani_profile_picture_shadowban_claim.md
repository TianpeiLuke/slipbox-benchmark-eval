# @whoisitbad's 14 October 2023 Post Alleging Kehlani Was Shadowbanned Over Palestine Profile Picture

In a post on X dated 14 October 2023, Sabby Targaryen (@whoisitbad) claimed that singer Kehlani was "shadowbanned" as soon as she changed her profile picture to "i stand with Palestine." The post cited supermodel Bella Hadid's prior allegations of Instagram suppressing her Palestine-related content as precedent, and characterized the alleged action as coordinated silencing, adding "THEY TRY TO SILENCE US. DON'T STOP!!"
