# Naftalis: SBF testifying in his own fraud trial is a "Hail Mary"

Speaking to TechCrunch on 2023-10-06, former federal prosecutor Josh Naftalis characterized Sam Bankman-Fried's decision to take the stand in his own trial as a "Hail Mary" ahead of a verdict and potential sentencing on seven counts tied to fraud and money laundering. Naftalis's reasoning: the defense cannot control the witness once he is finished with direct testimony. "Once it goes to cross[-examination], he doesn't get to say, 'I'm done,'" Naftalis said. "He can't just walk out if he doesn't like how it's going." The comment came as proceedings resumed and the onus shifted to the defense after the prosecution concluded its case.
