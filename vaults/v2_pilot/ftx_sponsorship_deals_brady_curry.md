# FTX paid Tom Brady $55 million and Steph Curry $35 million in sports endorsement deals

As FTX's fame and cash grew, the crypto exchange bought legitimacy through star athletes. Tom Brady was paid $55 million and Steph Curry $35 million in sports marketing deals that the Michael Lewis interview on 60 Minutes described as meant to "lend FTX legitimacy and edge." TechCrunch reported the figures on 2023-10-02, ahead of Sam Bankman-Fried's Manhattan Federal Court trial.
