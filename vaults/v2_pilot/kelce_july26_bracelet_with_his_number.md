# Kelce's "my number" friendship bracelet for Swift — New Heights, July 26, 2023

On the July 26, 2023 episode of the "New Heights" podcast, which Travis Kelce hosts with his brother, Philadelphia Eagles center Jason Kelce, Travis Kelce said he made a friendship bracelet — a fan tradition at Taylor Swift's concerts — bearing "my number" to give to Swift. "I wanted to give Taylor Swift one with my number on it," he said. When Jason asked whether he meant his jersey number or his phone number, Kelce replied only, "you know which one," per CBSSports.com (2023-10-12).
