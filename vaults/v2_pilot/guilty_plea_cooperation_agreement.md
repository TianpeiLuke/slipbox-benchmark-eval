# Cooperation Agreement at the Bankman-Fried Trial

A cooperation agreement, in the sense used at Sam Bankman-Fried's FTX fraud trial, is a written deal with the U.S. attorney's office in Manhattan under which a defendant who has pleaded guilty testifies for the government. Gary Wang, 30, FTX's technology chief, told jurors on Friday, Oct. 6, 2023 that his six-page cooperation agreement obliges him to meet with prosecutors, answer their questions truthfully and turn over evidence. He and Alameda Research's Caroline Ellison had pleaded guilty in December and have since been cooperating with that office.
