# The Apple–Google ISA revenue-share: a percentage of Google's search ad revenue from Apple users

Under the Information Services Agreement (ISA) — the deal that makes Google the default search engine on Apple's products, in place since 2002 and renegotiated in its current form in 2016 by Eddy Cue with Google CEO Sundar Pichai — Google pays Apple a share of the advertising revenue Google earns from Apple users it directs toward Google Search. The model is a percentage, not a fixed sum. At the 2016 renegotiation, Apple pressed for a higher percentage of that revenue; Pichai wanted to keep the deal as it was. They compromised on a figure neither disclosed in open court: on 26 September 2023, in US v. Google before Judge Amit Mehta in Washington, DC, Cue testified that discussion of specific numbers was reserved for closed court sessions. Google has paid Apple that percentage ever since, a sum the reporting describes as billions of dollars a year.
