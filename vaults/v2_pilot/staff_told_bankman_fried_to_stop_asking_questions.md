# Bankman-Fried testified his staff told him to stop asking questions

At his fraud trial, Sam Bankman-Fried testified that when he probed the fiat@ftx account — the account tracking how much Alameda owed FTX — and the missing billions, his employees told him "they were busy and I should stop asking questions because it was distracting." The Verge's trial reporter, writing on 2023-10-31, stressed that she heard those words "uttered aloud in a court of law this morning," adding she was "not creative enough to make this kind of thing up."
