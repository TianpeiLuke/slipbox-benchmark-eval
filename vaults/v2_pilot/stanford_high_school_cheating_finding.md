# Stanford Survey of More Than 40 U.S. High Schools Found No ChatGPT-Driven Surge in Cheating

New research from Stanford University, reported in TechCrunch's ChatGPT coverage (article dated 2023-09-28), found that the popularization of chatbots like ChatGPT did not produce an influx in cheating across U.S. high schools. In a survey of more than 40 U.S. high schools, researchers found cheating rates "similar across the board this year." The observation matters because it runs against the intuitive expectation that a tool able to write essays and code from short text prompts would show up directly in student dishonesty rates within its first year of wide use: in this sample, the baseline held.
