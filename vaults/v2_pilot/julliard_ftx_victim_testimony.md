# FTX customer Marc-Antoine Julliard could not withdraw almost $100,000

Marc-Antoine Julliard, a Paris-born cocoa trader living in London who chose FTX as his exchange in 2021 and traded Bitcoin and Dogecoin, testified on 2023-10-04 as the prosecution's first victim witness. On Nov. 8, in the exchange's final few days, he tried to pull out his cash and crypto — almost $100,000 — and, asked whether he was ever able to, answered "Never."
