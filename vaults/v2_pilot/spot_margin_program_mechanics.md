# Spot margin (FTX program)

Spot margin was a program at the cryptocurrency exchange FTX that let users lend each other assets for margin trading. Its distinguishing feature: in those cases, it was possible to have a negative balance in a specific coin. It was not, however, possible for spot margin accounts to avoid liquidation, nor to hold an overall negative balance. The term surfaced on 2023-10-10 at Sam Bankman-Fried's criminal trial, when defense attorney Christian Everdell raised spot margin in cross-examining FTX chief technology officer Gary Wang. Wang had testified that Alameda Research could take its balance negative without triggering liquidation, an option he alleged other FTX accounts did not get.
