# SBF Faces Seven Counts of Fraud and Money Laundering from FTX and Alameda Leadership

Sam Bankman-Fried, former CEO and co-founder of the collapsed crypto exchange FTX, faces seven counts tied to fraud and money laundering allegedly committed while leading FTX and its sister trading firm Alameda Research. As of October 6, 2023, the trial was entering the defense phase after the prosecution had concluded its case and Bankman-Fried was set to testify in his own defense.
