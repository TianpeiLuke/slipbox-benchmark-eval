# The "Google tax": critics' name for Google's in-app payment fee

"Google tax" is the informal name critics use for the in-app payment fee Google collects when a purchase made inside an Android app installed through the official Google Play store is processed through Google's billing system — paired with the parallel term "Apple tax" for the same mechanism on iOS. The Verge, in a November 5, 2023 explainer on Epic v. Google, illustrated it with Fortnite: buying the game's virtual currency V-Bucks on an Android or iOS device installed via an official app store triggered the fee until August 13th, 2020, when Epic bypassed it. The source notes Google charges up to ten times more per transaction than PayPal.
