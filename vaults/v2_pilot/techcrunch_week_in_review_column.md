# Week in Review (WiR): TechCrunch's Weekly Tech News Digest

Week in Review, abbreviated WiR, is TechCrunch's weekly email digest of the past week in tech news. TechCrunch describes it as its column that highlights the major stories over the past few days, and pitches it as "a darn useful resource for folks on the go." The newsletter arrives in subscribers' inboxes every Saturday, and readers not yet subscribed are invited to sign up through a link in the email. The edition dated 2023-10-07, for example, gathered Sam Altman backing the teen founders' AI startup Induced AI, Google's hardware event and the Pixel 8, and TikTok's $4.99 ad-free tier test.
