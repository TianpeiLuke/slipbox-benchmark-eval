# Prosecutors' intent theory: Bankman-Fried's tweets and interviews vs. the record

In its August 14th superseding indictment, prosecutors called Sam Bankman-Fried's November 2022 tweets "false and misleading." In The Verge's September 28, 2023 trial coverage, the government's plan is to use Bankman-Fried's own public statements — on Twitter and in the many interviews he sat for — to show a gap between what he said and what actually happened at FTX, supplying the evidence needed to prove intent.
