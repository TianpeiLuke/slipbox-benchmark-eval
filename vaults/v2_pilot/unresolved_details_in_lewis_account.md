# The three gaps TechCrunch leaves open in the Lewis account of FTX

TechCrunch's 2023-10-02 report on Michael Lewis's "Going Infinite" keeps flagging what the account omits rather than contradicting it: "No, we don't find out." Lewis says a Berkeley friend who wanted to back FTX introduced him to Sam Bankman-Fried in early 2021 to "evaluate his character" — neither the friend's name nor whether that investment happened is given, though FTX raised some $2 billion in 2021 from Sequoia, Temasek, Tiger Global and others. Nor do we learn why SBF shuffles cards with his leg twitching during the 60 Minutes segment, nor whether any of the reported $5 billion ever reached Trump's camp.
