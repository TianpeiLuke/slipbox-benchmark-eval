# SBF Denied Bail Awaiting His FTX Fraud Trial (October 2023)

As of the TechCrunch article dated 2023-10-02, Sam Bankman-Fried — the founder and head of the collapsed crypto exchange FTX, accused of orchestrating "one of the biggest financial frauds in American history" — had been denied bail while awaiting his trial. That trial was set to begin the following Tuesday in the Manhattan Federal Court, with SBF having pleaded not guilty to some seven charges of fraud and conspiracy.
