# Kelce's July 26, 2023 New Heights account of failing to meet Swift at her concert

On July 26, 2023, Travis Kelce announced on the "New Heights" podcast — which he hosts with his brother, Philadelphia Eagles center Jason Kelce — that he had attended Taylor Swift's highly anticipated concert but did not get the chance to meet the 12-time Grammy winner. "I was disappointed that she doesn't talk before or after her shows because she has to save her voice for the 44 songs she sings ... She doesn't meet anybody, or at least she didn't want to meet me, so I took it personal," Kelce said. (CBSSports.com, 2023-10-12)
