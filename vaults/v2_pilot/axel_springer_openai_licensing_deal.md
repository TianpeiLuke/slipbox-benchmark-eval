# Axel Springer licenses its news content to OpenAI, week of 15 December 2023

In the week of 15 December 2023, German publisher Axel Springer signed a deal with OpenAI to license its news for AI model training, a TechCrunch article on that date reported. The agreement was cited as one example of publishers "trying to get ahead of" the threat that AI-powered search poses to their traffic. Overall, publishers expected to lose 20-40% of website traffic once Google's AI products fully rolled out, per The WSJ, and Google then drove nearly 40% of publisher traffic (Similarweb).
