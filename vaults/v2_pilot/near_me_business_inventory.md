# A 2023 sweep for Google-style "Near Me" business names across US cities

In a search sweep reported by The Verge on 2023-10-26, a reporter catalogued US businesses named after literal "near me" Google queries, following New York's Thai Food Near Me. The finds: a chain of half a dozen Affordable Dentist Near Me's in Texas; an Antiques Near Me two hours outside of New York City; seven Plumber Near Me businesses; a Phone Repair Near Me in Cape Cod, Massachusetts; a Psychic Near Me in Chicago; and more than 20 iterations of "Notary Near Me" across the US. The counts describe only businesses the reporter was able to find, so the naming convention is likely more common still.
