# Missy Ward entered SEO in 1998 when it was "five girls and all dudes"

At a South Florida rooftop party for digital marketers built around the release of an alligator — billed as 10 feet, actually five and a half — held among roughly 200 attendees, SEO professional Missy Ward, wearing an orange bandage dress, described the field she entered in 1998 as "five girls and all dudes." Ward, whom The Verge met while reporting on search engine optimization, later sold her company for $40 million, a trajectory running from a near-empty niche to a nine-figure exit. The Verge, 2023-11-01.
