# Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) as the Benchmark in Early Gemini Criticism

Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) is the technique of grounding a language model's answer in documents fetched from outside the model at question time, so factual look-ups depend on search rather than on what the model memorized during training.

In a TechCrunch report published 2023-12-07, "Early impressions of Google's Gemini aren't great," Reddit user Vitor de Lucca (@vitor_dlucca) invoked that standard in a post dated December 7, 2023: he said he was "extremely disappointed with Gemini Pro on Bard" for giving "very, very bad results to questions that shouldn't be hard anymore with RAG."

His example was a look-up of the 2023 Oscar winners, which Gemini Pro answered wrongly, naming Brendan Gleeson as Best Actor rather than the actual winner, Brendan Fraser. RAG, in this framing, is what should have made such a question trivial.
