# Similarweb counted 140.7 million unique visitors to the ChatGPT web portal in October 2023

For October 2023, Similarweb, the web metrics company, recorded 140.7 million unique visitors to OpenAI's ChatGPT web portal. The figure appeared in TechCrunch's reporting of November 30, 2023, marking one year since OpenAI released ChatGPT as a free "low-key research preview" built on its GPT-3.5 model. TechCrunch presented the count as third-party evidence that ChatGPT still attracted heavy traffic a year after launch.
