# Oil prices at a one-year-plus high in early October 2023 despite a 92-cent US crude fall

Oil prices jumped to their highest level in more than a year as of 1 October 2023, according to The Sydney Morning Herald, pressuring the US economy by raising fuel costs for everyone. A barrel of US crude sank 92¢ on Friday 29 September 2023 to settle at $US90.79, but that price remained up sharply from about $US70 in June 2023. Brent crude, the international standard, also weakened on 29 September 2023. S&P 500 energy stocks fell 2 per cent that day, more than double the loss of any other sector.
