# Swift Back at Arrowhead for Chiefs–Chargers, the Fifth Attendance of the Season

On the Sunday of the week in which the CBSSports.com timeline was published, 2023-10-12, Taylor Swift was again at Arrowhead Stadium to watch the Kansas City Chiefs take on the Los Angeles Chargers, another AFC West opponent. That fourth appearance is what made the article's tally of "five games so far this season" current as of writing: Swift had attended the Chiefs' Week 3 game against the Chicago Bears on Sept. 24, was present again last month, and returned the prior week for Kansas City's victory over the Denver Broncos in Week 6 on "Thursday Night Football."
