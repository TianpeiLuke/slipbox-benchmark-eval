# Barber Shop Near Me's only pre-opening footprint was a logo on Instagram — clients assumed Google was coming to town

Felix Silva's Coral Springs, Florida, store Barber Shop Near Me existed online before it existed physically, and nothing else. "Before we opened, we just had a logo [on an Instagram account]," Silva says. "And we had some clients then tell us, 'Oh, man, before you guys opened we thought Google was coming to town.'" Reported in The Verge on 2023-10-26, four years after Silva chose the name in 2019.
