# Jason Kelce Neither Confirmed Nor Denied Swift-Kelce Rumors, Sept 14, 2023

In CBSSports.com's Taylor Swift–Travis Kelce timeline (published 2023-10-12), the Sept. 14, 2023 entry is titled "Rumors aren't denied." Jason Kelce, the Philadelphia Eagles center who is Travis Kelce's brother and podcast partner on "New Heights" and whom the article describes as very close with his brother, was asked whether rumors of the two hanging out were true. He did not confirm them, and he did not deny them. The entry's stated substance is that non-denial — the source records no statement from Swift or from Travis Kelce at that date.
