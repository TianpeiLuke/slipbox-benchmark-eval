# AI's Central Debate Framed as Control or Freedom

In "How ego and fear fuelled the rise of artificial intelligence" (The Age, 9 December 2023), the argument about AI is framed as a single either/or question: whether the technology "should be controlled or set free." That choice rests on a prior uncertainty — whether AI "will elevate the world or destroy it – or at least inflict grave damage." The framing organises an ongoing debate among Silicon Valley founders, chatbot users, academics, legislators and regulators, and pits some of the world's richest men against one another: Musk, Page, Mark Zuckerberg of Meta, tech investor Peter Thiel, Satya Nadella of Microsoft and Sam Altman of OpenAI.
