# Kelce Asked to Name a Celebrity Crush, Aug 5, 2023 — Answer Supplied Before He Spoke

On Aug 5, 2023, well before the dating rumors that CBSSports.com later tracked starting Sept 14, 2023, Kansas City Chiefs tight end Travis Kelce was asked to name his celebrity crush. According to CBSSports.com's timeline of Oct 12, 2023, before Kelce could answer, "the answer was given for him." That source records no name for the questioner and no exact wording of what was said, so the exchange cannot be verified beyond the fact that it occurred and that Kelce never got to supply his own reply.
