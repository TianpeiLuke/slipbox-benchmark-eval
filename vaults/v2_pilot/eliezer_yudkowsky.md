# Eliezer Yudkowsky: internet philosopher behind the Rationalist AI-safety milieu

The 2023 article *How ego and fear fuelled the rise of artificial intelligence* (The Age, 2023-12-09) describes Eliezer Yudkowsky as an internet philosopher and self-taught AI researcher rather than a credentialed computer scientist, and as a leader of a community who called themselves Rationalists and, in later years, effective altruists. They believed AI could cure cancer or solve climate change but worried bots might do things their creators had not intended, arguing that machines more intelligent than humans could turn on their creators. Yudkowsky ran a Thiel-funded AI lab and an annual conference on the singularity, and Demis Hassabis's two British co-founders were among those connected to him.
