# Adam Yedidia, MIT friend of Bankman-Fried, testified under a grant of immunity

Adam Yedidia, a fast-talking MIT graduate and close college friend of Sam Bankman-Fried, testified for the prosecution on the second day of the FTX founder's New York trial, reported Fortune on 2023-10-04. He said that after Bankman-Fried left Jane Street he recruited him first as a trader at Alameda Research and then as a developer at FTX. Prosecutor Danielle Sassoon disclosed that Yedidia was giving evidence under legal immunity.
