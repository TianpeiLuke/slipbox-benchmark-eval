# FTX's mainstream reach came from arena naming rights, MLB branding and celebrity partnerships

FTX gained mainstream recognition through branding and celebrity deals, per TechCrunch on 2023-10-01. In 2021 the crypto exchange bought the naming rights to the Miami Heat's home arena, and FTX also had its name branded on Major League Baseball umpires' polos. It partnered with celebrities including Tom Brady and his ex-wife, Gisele Bündchen, as well as Steph Curry, Shaquille O'Neal and Naomi Osaka, among others.
