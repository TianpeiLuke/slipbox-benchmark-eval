# Meta-Commissioned Third-Party Report Found Adverse Human Rights Impact on Palestinian Users in 2021

During the 2021 Hamas-Israel escalation, a third-party report commissioned by Meta concluded that the company's own actions had produced an "adverse human rights impact" on Palestinian users' right to freedom of expression and political participation. The finding, cited in a TechCrunch article published Oct. 19, 2023, is significant because Meta itself paid for the assessment, making it an internally sourced acknowledgment rather than an external allegation. Meta separately attributed its 2021 content removals to technical errors.
