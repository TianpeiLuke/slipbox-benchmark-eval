# Project Liberty: Epic's covert plan to provoke app-store removal and sue

Project Liberty was Epic Games' covert plan, described in evidence Google presented during its opening statement in the Epic v. Google trial. Epic patched Fortnite with code bypassing app-store payment fees, invited Apple and Google to remove the game, and then sued; the litigation over Google Play's in-app purchase fees was filed in 2020. Epic's claim: Android's Google Play store is an unlawful monopoly. Google's rebuttal: Epic's demands would damage Android's ability to offer a secure user experience and to compete with Apple's iOS.
