# Shang: Conversational Interface Research Explains ChatGPT's "Convincingly Real" Dialogue

Ruoxi Shang, a third-year PhD student at the University of Washington studying human-AI interaction, named ChatGPT's ability to conduct conversations that are "convincingly real" as one reason for the chatbot's enduring popularity, in TechCrunch's coverage of its first anniversary on 2023-11-30.

Shang grounded that explanation in earlier work: "Human-computer interaction researchers have studied how conversational interfaces can improve understandability of information, and the socialization aspects of chatbots bring increased engagement." She continued: "Now, AI models have enabled conversational agents to conduct conversations nearly indistinguishable from human dialogues."

The contrast Shang drew is with chatbots that had existed for decades but ran on models much less sophisticated than those powering ChatGPT, so the same two interface properties now operate at far higher fidelity.
