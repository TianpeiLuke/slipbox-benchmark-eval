# The judge, not the witness, defines legal terms like "market manipulation"

During Sam Bankman-Fried's direct testimony at his FTX trial on 2023-10-28, reported by The Verge, Bankman-Fried offered his own definition of "market manipulation." Judge Lewis Kaplan cut in and told the jury that he, Kaplan, was the final authority on that term. The morning had already been spent largely explaining vocabulary, including "Amazon Web Services" and "database," which the reporter judged too detail-oriented. The exchange illustrated a standing allocation of roles in the courtroom: the witness supplies facts within his experience, and the court supplies the legal meaning of charged terms such as market manipulation.
