# Nike shares jumped 6.7 per cent on better-than-expected quarterly profit

On 29 September 2023, on Wall Street, Nike jumped 6.7 per cent after reporting better profit for the latest quarter than analysts expected. Strength overseas helped it make up for some declines in North America. The rise came on a day the S&P 500 slipped 0.3 per cent as Wall Street closed out its worst month and quarter of the year.
