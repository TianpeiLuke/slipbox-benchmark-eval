# Sean Payton Deserves to Lead the Coach of the Year Conversation, Sporting News Argues (December 2023)

In a Sporting News piece on NFL award longshots dated 2023-12-05, the argument made is that Sean Payton — listed at +2200 via DraftKings — "should be at the forefront" of the Coach of the Year conversation rather than betting favorite Dan Campbell (+200 via DraftKings). The reasoning: turning around a team that was dreadful the previous season is one thing, but Payton took a Broncos team that got off "a horrible start" and reversed it in-season. The article adds that Payton has "resurrected Russell Wilson's career," calling that achievement alone enough to put him at the top of the list. The stated caveat is that this case may not satisfy voters: Payton "may need to get Denver into the playoffs to convince voters." The same piece also questions whether Campbell's favorite status reflects this season's work or the groundwork from the prior season.
