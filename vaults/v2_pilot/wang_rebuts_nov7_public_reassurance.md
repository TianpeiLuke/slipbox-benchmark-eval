# Wang's "FTX Was Not Fine" Rebuts the Nov. 7, 2022 Reassurance Tweets

At Sam Bankman-Fried's Manhattan fraud trial on Oct. 6, 2023, FTX co-founder Gary Wang rebutted the reassurance record prosecutors put before the jury: a series of tweets beginning Nov. 7, 2022, in which FTX blamed bank hours for slow withdrawals while Bankman-Fried assured customers from his personal account that all was fine. "FTX was not fine and assets were not fine," Wang testified.
