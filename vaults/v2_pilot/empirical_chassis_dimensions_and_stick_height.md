# Steam Deck OLED keeps the original's dimensions, with 1 mm taller thumbsticks

In its Engadget review of Nov 9, 2023, the Steam Deck OLED measured 11.7 inches long, 4.5 inches tall and nearly 2 inches thick — the same size as the original Steam Deck LCD. The only dimensional change noted: the OLED's thumbsticks stand 1 mm taller than the first version's. The device does weigh about 30 grams less than the original.
