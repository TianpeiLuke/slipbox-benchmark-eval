# Google paid $26.3 billion in 2021 for default search status, per US v Google disclosures

In the previous TechCrunch roundup from the US v Google antitrust trial, disclosures showed Google spent $26.3 billion in 2021 making itself the default search engine across platforms. That same roundup reported Google had tried to have Chrome preinstalled on iPhones. Both facts were carried into TechCrunch's roundup published on November 13, 2023.
