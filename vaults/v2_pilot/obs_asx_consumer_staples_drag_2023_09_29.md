# ASX consumer staples fell 1.1 per cent on 29 September 2023, led by Treasury Wine Estates and Woolworths

On Monday 29 September 2023, Treasury Wine Estates fell 1.9 per cent and Woolworths fell 1.2 per cent, dragging the ASX consumer staples sector down 1.1 per cent. The declines came as most sectors traded lower, with interest-rate sensitive sectors including healthcare and consumer companies among the weakest amid expectations the Reserve Bank of Australia would keep rates on hold at its Tuesday October meeting. Pilbara Minerals (down 2.1 per cent) and Qantas (down 1.5 per cent) were also among the biggest large-cap decliners that session.
