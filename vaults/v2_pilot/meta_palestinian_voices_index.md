# Index: Meta's Moderation of Palestinian Voices (TechCrunch, 19 October 2023)

This document (doc_0106) is TechCrunch's 19 October 2023 report that Instagram users suspect Meta of shadowbanning — that is, limiting the visibility of — content about Palestine. Its definitional material covers shadowbanning and black-and-white policy design. Its structural-bias mechanisms include Meta's thin Arabic language resources (a language with over 25 dialects) and partnerships with government agencies such as the Israeli Cyber Unit, set against Meta's ban on content supporting Hamas, designated a terrorist organization by the United States and the European Union. Case material covers filmmaker Khitam Jabr's 24-hour restrictions and Bella Hadid's Story views dropping by "almost 1 million." Counter-argument material carries Meta communications director Andy Stone's rebuttals and the Oct. 18 newsroom statement, plus the 2021 third-party human rights report behind the bias-versus-bug dispute.
