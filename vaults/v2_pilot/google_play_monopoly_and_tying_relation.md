# The store–billing relation: how Epic v. Google's two findings form one loop

Read as a single structure rather than two verdicts, the findings a federal jury returned against Google — reported by Tim Sweeney of Epic Games in The Verge, 2023-12-12 — describe one loop with two ends.

One end is the monopolised product: Google holds an illegal monopoly over Android app distribution and in-app payment systems. The other end is the tied product: Google illegally tied its Google Play billing system to its app store.

The relation between the ends is the model. Whichever product a developer must accept to reach Android users also becomes the channel through which that developer's money moves, so control of distribution converts into control of payment — and the payment share Sweeney calls "huge taxes" paid to a few gatekeepers reinforces the distribution hold. Two holdings, one circuit.
