# V-Bucks: Fortnite's in-game virtual currency

V-Bucks are the virtual currency of Fortnite, the "extraordinarily popular free-to-play game" from Epic Games, and the means by which the game makes money: players buy in-game items with them. Players typically purchase V-Bucks in the same place they play Fortnite. Until August 13th, 2020, an Android or iOS player who installed Fortnite through an official app store triggered an in-app payment fee to Google or Apple on that purchase.

(The Verge, "Epic v. Google, explained," November 5, 2023)
