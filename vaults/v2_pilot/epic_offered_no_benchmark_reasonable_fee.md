# Epic's case against Google is weakened by never naming a reasonable store fee

Epic Games' antitrust case against Google, which went to trial on November 6th, 2023 — 1,180 days after Epic originally sued — argues that Google holds illegal monopolies in "Android app distribution" and "Android in-app payment processing," and that developers buying for or publishing on Android are stuck paying the "Google tax." One criticism aimed at Epic's position is that it wants something for nothing: Epic has never stated what fee it would consider reasonable for Google to charge for placement in the Google Play app store. Rather than naming a defensible figure, Epic CEO Tim Sweeney instead implies that a developer who uses its own payment system should pay Google nothing at all for store listing. That silence leaves Epic's target vague: it opposes the current fee without ever describing the competitive price a court could order.
