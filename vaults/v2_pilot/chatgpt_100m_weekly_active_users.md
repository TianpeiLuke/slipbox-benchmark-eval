# ChatGPT Hit 100 Million Weekly Active Users About a Year After Launch

As of TechCrunch's coverage dated 2023-09-28, ChatGPT had reached 100 million weekly active users, roughly one year after its initial launch — the timeline described as "nearly a year ago." TechCrunch attributed this user milestone to OpenAI's heavy continued investment in the chatbot, which the outlet reported was by then used by more than 92% of Fortune 500 companies.
