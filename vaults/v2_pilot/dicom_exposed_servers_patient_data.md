# Thousands of Internet-Exposed DICOM Servers Leaked Millions of Patients' Records (2023)

In December 2023, TechCrunch reported that Aplite, a Germany-based cybersecurity consultancy, had discovered thousands of exposed servers spilling the medical records and personal health information of millions of patients. The exposure traced to security weaknesses in Digital Imaging and Communications in Medicine (DICOM), a decades-old, internationally recognized industry standard for storing and sharing medical images. According to Aplite's finding, those DICOM security shortcomings meant many medical facilities had unintentionally made private patient data accessible to the open web.
