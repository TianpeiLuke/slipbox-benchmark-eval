# 2007 Google homepage data: +15% searches when set, −27% when removed

On 30 October 2023, U.S. Justice Department lawyer Meagan Bellshaw showed Google CEO Sundar Pichai a 2007 email from a Google product strategy meeting containing homepage experiment data: when people changed their browser homepage to Google, they performed 15% more Google searches; when they switched away, their Google searches dropped 27%. The email, which was sent to Pichai and other Google leaders, summarized an argument from Nitin that focusing on homepage market share was one of the most effective things Google could do to make gains in search market share. TechCrunch reported this on 31 October 2023, citing The Verge.
