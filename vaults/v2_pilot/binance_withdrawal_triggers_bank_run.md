# Binance's $2.1 billion redemption triggered the bank run on FTX

Around the time CoinDesk exposed Alameda Research's balance sheet in early November 2022, Binance — then the world's largest crypto exchange — began pulling out its remaining $2.1 billion equivalent of cash held in BUSD and FTT. Per TechCrunch (2023-10-01), this essentially triggered a bank run on FTX. Binance had held an equity position in FTX from 2019 to 2021.
