# Epic Told OnePlus and Carriers Could Not Bundle Fortnite Due to Secret Google Deals

In a 2023-12-12 interview with The Verge after the federal jury verdict in Epic's antitrust case against Google, Epic CEO Tim Sweeney recounted a concrete corroboration of his long-held suspicions: when Epic tried to bundle Fortnite with other smartphone manufacturers such as OnePlus and with "carriers of all sorts," those partners told Epic they could not do a deal because Google had already done a secret deal with them. Sweeney cited this alongside his claim that Google was paying carriers and OEMs secretly not to carry competing stores, and paying other developers not to launch their own stores.
