# Sullivan: Google's ad systems do not touch organic search ranking

In a post on X, Google's official search liaison Danny Sullivan stated that Google's ad systems do not affect the organic results, which he defines as the list of blue links in search results that are not sponsored. His account keeps two processes apart: "Ad keyword matching is a long-standing and well-known process that is designed to connect people to relevant ads. A separate process, which has nothing to do with ads, is used to match organic results to a query," he said (reported in The Age, "Is Google Search better than the rest? And is that fair?", 2023-10-22).
