# 2023 users called Google search results "absolutely unusable", "garbage" and "a nightmare"

In 2023, people describing their ability to find information on the internet said the links that pop up when they go looking for answers online — which for almost everyone means the quality of Google Search results — are "absolutely unusable", "garbage" and "a nightmare", because "a lot of the content doesn't feel authentic." The observation is reported in "The people who ruined the internet", published by The Verge on 2023-11-01, and is attributed to users of search rather than to a measured survey.
