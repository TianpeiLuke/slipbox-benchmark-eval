# Habit as switching cost: the Google search bar as a bookmark list

In The Age's search-engine comparison (2023-10-22), the author draws one structural lesson from testing Google, Bing and DuckDuckGo: the Google Search bar functions as much as a way of returning to familiar sites as of looking up information. A rival provider that has not recorded those prior destinations therefore raises a switching cost that is behavioural rather than qualitative — moving away would be "temporarily annoying simply because it wouldn't remember all the places I've been".
