# Altman: Ego and Conflict Grow With Shared Purpose, Not Opposition

Sam Altman's argument is that disagreement, mistrust and ego intensify with proximity of purpose rather than fading as people agree more. "There is disagreement, mistrust, egos," Altman said in The Age's survey of AI's founders published 9 December 2023. "The closer people are to being pointed in the same direction, the more contentious the disagreements are. You see this in sects and religious orders. There are bitter fights between the closest people." The consequence for artificial intelligence: its sharpest disputes should be expected among collaborators pursuing the same goal, not between the camps that openly oppose each other. That claim's own test case arrived in November 2023, when rebel OpenAI board members tried to force Altman out over trust, and the fight lasted five chaotic days at a company valued at more than $US80 billion ($A123 billion).
