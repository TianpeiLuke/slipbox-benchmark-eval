# Wembanyama as the early favourite for 2023-24 NBA Rookie of the Year

Sporting News on 2023-10-04 rated Victor Wembanyama a strong early contender for the 2023-24 NBA Rookie of the Year award, and the case rests on a trait no other rookie that season could match. Wembanyama's elite defence, highlighted by his 7-foot-4 height and 8-foot wingspan, allows him to dominate where others can't, while his agility and skill set are unparalleled for his size. The supporting argument is upside rather than proven production: refining his shooting and continuing to adapt to the Spurs' style, as seen in his impressive NBA preseason performance, would make him a force to reckon with. Coach Gregg Popovich's young roster and potential load management are the acknowledged risks to that impact.
