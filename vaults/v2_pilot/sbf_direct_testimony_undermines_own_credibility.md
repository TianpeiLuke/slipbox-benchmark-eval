# Bankman-Fried undercut himself on direct, before the cross

Sam Bankman-Fried damaged his own credibility on direct examination at his FTX trial, before cross-examination even began, by repeatedly failing to answer the questions put to him by his own counsel, Mark Cohen. Writing in The Verge on 2023-10-28, the trial correspondent noted that Judge Lewis Kaplan had already instructed Bankman-Fried to answer the question he was being asked by his own lawyer. That came one day after Kaplan had scolded him for not answering prosecutor Danielle Sassoon's questions on cross-examination at an evidentiary hearing. The running count of "Objection, narrative!" followed by "Sustained" stood at three. The jury was watching all of it intently. The argument is that a defendant waives nothing by testifying badly for himself: the damage was visible to the jury in the prosecution's absence, with no hostile questions required to produce it.
