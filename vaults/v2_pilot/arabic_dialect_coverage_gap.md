# Linguistic Under-Coverage in Platform Moderation of Arabic

Linguistic under-coverage is a moderation capacity condition in which a platform's language resources treat Arabic as a single variety, even though it has over 25 dialects, so dialectal and cultural nuance falls outside the reach of both automated classifiers and human reviewers. TechCrunch described this on 2023-10-19 in "Meta has a moderation bias problem, not just a 'bug,' that's suppressing Palestinian voices" (publisher: TechCrunch, dated 2023-10-19), noting that Meta struggles to navigate the cultural and linguistic nuances of Arabic and has been criticized for neglecting to adequately diversify its language resources.

The concept names a capability gap rather than an intent: content in a given variety sits beyond what the resources can parse.
