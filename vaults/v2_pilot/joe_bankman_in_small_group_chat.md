# Joe Bankman Identified in FTX Inner Circle's Signal "Small Group Chat"

Joe Bankman, the father of FTX founder Sam Bankman-Fried, is among the people included in a Signal group called the "small group chat," where the FTX inner circle strategized during the exchange's last days. The identification appears in The Verge's October 26, 2023 account of Sam Bankman-Fried's fraud trial, which lists Joe Bankman among the family and friends drawn into the case.
