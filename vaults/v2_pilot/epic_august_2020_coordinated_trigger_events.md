# Epic's August 13, 2020 Fortnite hotfix triggered app-store removals and two antitrust suits

On August 13th, 2020, Epic Games, the studio behind Fortnite — a free-to-play game that sells in-game items with its virtual currency V-Bucks — publicly announced it was bypassing Apple and Google's app store fees. It deployed a hotfix update to Fortnite without either company's knowledge, letting players purchase V-Bucks directly through Epic's own payment processing option at a discount. Until that date, such purchases on Android or iOS devices installed through an official app store triggered an in-app payment fee to Google or Apple. Apple and Google almost immediately reacted by kicking Fortnite off their app stores for breaking the rules. Then Epic, per The Verge's November 5th, 2023 account, sprung a pre-planned response: two lawsuits and an attack ad depicting a Fortnite hero throwing a unicorn-llama hammer into a giant screen reminiscent of Apple's famous "1984" Macintosh ad.
