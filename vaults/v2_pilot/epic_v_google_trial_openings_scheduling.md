# Epic v. Google after openings: what the evidence phase must settle

Following both sides' opening statements to the jury in early November 2023 — Epic Games' on Monday, 2023-11-06, and Google's later the same week, lasting 45 minutes — the Epic v. Google antitrust trial moves to its evidence phase, with several disputes deferred to later weeks. Testimony must first settle Epic lead attorney Gary Bornstein's claim that "Google pays actual and potential competitors not to compete," including the alleged $360 million payment that kept Activision from opening an app store competing with Google Play; Activision told The Verge in 2022 it "never entered into an agreement that Activision would not open its own app store." The trial must also resolve whether Android is open: Bornstein promised to "show that Google has closed off each and every other option," while Google lead attorney Glenn Pomerantz points to over a billion users installing outside Play.
