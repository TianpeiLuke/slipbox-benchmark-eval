# OpenAI connected ChatGPT to the internet for all users

OpenAI connected ChatGPT to the live internet for all users, according to TechCrunch's running explainer on the chatbot (dated 2023-09-28). The rollout is notable for who it covered. Unlike GPT-4, which the article says "remains largely exclusive to paying ChatGPT users" — reachable free of charge only through Microsoft's Bing Chat in the Edge, Google Chrome and Safari browsers — the internet connection went to the entire user base rather than to subscribers alone. It arrived alongside a slew of updates announced around OpenAI DevDay, the company's first developer conference, held November 6.
