# Know what "epistemology" means? Don't testify in your own defense

The Verge's FTX trial reporter (31 Oct 2023) draws one rule from Sam Bankman-Fried's cross-examination by Danielle Sassoon: "if you know the meaning of the word 'epistemology,' you absolutely should not testify in your own defense." Wire-fraud liability turns on what a defendant knew and when he asked — so Bankman-Fried's quarrels over precision ("hole isn't really the word I would use"; wanting "a few more qualifiers and scoping on it") wrote the jury the record of his own inquiry. He conceded he knew of the fiat@ftx account in June 2022 but not its purpose until October, and admitted Judge Lewis Kaplan pressed him into admitting Adam Yedidia had told him about the missing $8 billion.
