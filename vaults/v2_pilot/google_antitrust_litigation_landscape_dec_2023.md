# Google antitrust actions surrounding the Helena World Chronicle publisher suit, December 2023

Helena World Chronicle's class action against Google and Alphabet, filed in the U.S. District Court in D.C. and covered by TechCrunch on 2023-12-15, arrived while several other Google proceedings were live. This note indexes that surrounding docket.

- U.S. Department of Justice v. Google (digital ad technology): the DOJ's monopoly suit over Google's ad-tech stack, pending alongside the publisher case in late 2023.
- U.S. Department of Justice v. Google (2020): the earlier civil antitrust suit over search and search advertising.
- Epic Games v. Google: the app-store antitrust case, which Epic won; the Helena complaint borrows its finding of improper spoliation of evidence — Google's destruction of chat messages.
- Canada settlement (November 2023): Google agreed to pay Canadian media $73.5 million (100 million Canadian dollars) a year, distributed by newsroom headcount. Meta's negotiations remained unresolved; Meta had begun blocking news in Canada in August.
