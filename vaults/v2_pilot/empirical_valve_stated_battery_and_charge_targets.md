# Valve's stated Steam Deck OLED battery figures: 30–50 percent longer life, 20–80 percent charge in 45 minutes

Valve's published figures for the Steam Deck OLED, reported by Engadget on 2023-11-09, claim battery life 30 to 50 percent longer than the original Steam Deck, depending on how the user plays. The original Steam Deck's battery maxed out at eight hours, and that only under ideal conditions — a noted downside of the first model. Valve further claimed faster charging: 20 percent to 80 percent in 45 minutes. All three numbers are Valve's own manufacturer estimates as relayed in the review, not independent measurements.
