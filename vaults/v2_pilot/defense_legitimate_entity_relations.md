# Cohen's Competing Model: Alameda and FTX as Lawful, Ordinary Businesses

In his opening statement on Oct 4, 2023, as reported by Fortune, defence lawyer Mark Cohen offered a model of the FTX–Alameda relationship at odds with the prosecution's two-entity structure. Alameda was not subterranean or shady but a successful hedge fund, he said; FTX was no Ponzi scheme but a "very innovative, successful company." The business practices between the two were reasonable, Cohen argued: Alameda acted legally as an FTX customer, payment processor and market maker, a financial entity acting as a trading partner for customers buying and selling cryptocurrencies. "Sam didn't defraud anyone," he said.
