# Black-and-White Moderation Policy

A black-and-white moderation policy is a rule set that classifies content in binary terms — permitted or forbidden — with no graded middle category. Because of this binary design, such policies cannot effectively moderate nuanced speech, as illustrated by Meta Platforms, whose content rules are described in a TechCrunch article published 2023-10-19: "The company's black-and-white policies often preclude it from effectively moderating any nuanced topic, like content that discusses violence without condoning it." Content that describes violence without endorsing it fits neither the "allowed" nor the "banned" category cleanly, so it tends to be mis-flagged or removed. Meta was additionally criticized in the article for struggling with Arabic's cultural and linguistic nuances, a language with over 25 dialects.
