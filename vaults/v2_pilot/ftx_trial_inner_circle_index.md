# Index: The Seven People Closest to Sam Bankman-Fried in the October 2023 FTX Trial Record

This index scopes the individuals named in a The Verge article dated 2023-10-26 as the people who loved or worked with Sam Bankman-Fried and appeared in the FTX trial record. The source lists them in order of testimony and relationship: Adam Yedidia and Gary Wang (college roommates), Nishad Singh (longtime family friend), Caroline Ellison (ex-girlfriend), his parents Joe Bankman and Barbara Fried, and his brother Gabe Bankman-Fried. Wang, Singh, and Ellison are felons; the article's closing paragraph covers their sentencing exposure, restitution, job bars, and reputational damage.
