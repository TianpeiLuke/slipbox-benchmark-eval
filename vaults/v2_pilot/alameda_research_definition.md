# Alameda Research: FTX's sister hedge fund, run by Caroline Ellison

Alameda Research was the hedge fund operating as the sister firm to the bankrupt cryptocurrency exchange FTX, run by Caroline Ellison, who had been Sam Bankman-Fried's girlfriend. Media reporting in November 2022 detailed Alameda's business practices and its troubling ties to FTX, which Bankman-Fried's co-founder Gary Wang later described in October 2023 trial testimony. Alameda had a roughly $14 billion hole in its books.
