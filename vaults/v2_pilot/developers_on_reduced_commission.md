# Google: 99% of Play developers qualify for 15% or lower commission

In its defense ahead of the Epic Games trial that began November 6, 2023, Google stated that 99% of its developers qualify for a commission of 15% or less, a figure determined by their revenues on the Google Play store. Google's standard commission on sales it processes is 15% to 30%. Reported by TechCrunch.
