# Mind the Gap PAC Listed Among Recipients of FTX Customer Money

Barbara Fried's political action committee, Mind the Gap, is listed among the entities that received FTX customer money as political donations, according to a The Verge article on the trial published 2023-10-26. Nishad Singh pleaded guilty to six charges, including conspiracy counts around his political donations. Fried and Bankman lived together in a Bahamas house purchased using FTX investor funds, and in a lawsuit filed by FTX's bankruptcy estate are alleged to have been given $10 million of FTX customer funds.
