# Iron ore price as a driver of ASX iron ore heavyweight share prices

The model relation recorded in The Sydney Morning Herald's ASX market report of 2023-10-01: a rise in the iron ore price lifts the share prices of the iron ore heavyweights on the Australian Securities Exchange, holding other factors constant. That date's evidence is that iron ore heavyweights advanced after the iron ore price increased 1.4 per cent overnight.

The relation is partial, not sufficient: on the same Monday the boost from stronger iron ore prices was not enough to lift the local bourse, which was held down by the prospect of higher-for-longer interest rates ahead of the Reserve Bank of Australia's meeting on Tuesday.
