# FTX and Alameda Research filed for Chapter 11 in mid-November 2022

FTX's collapse came after CoinDesk published Alameda Research's balance sheet showing $14.6 billion in assets and $8 billion in liabilities as of June 30, 2022, and after Binance pulled out its remaining $2.1 billion equivalent of cash in BUSD and FTT, triggering a bank run. FTX and Alameda filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in the U.S. in mid-November 2022 (TechCrunch, 2023-10-01).
