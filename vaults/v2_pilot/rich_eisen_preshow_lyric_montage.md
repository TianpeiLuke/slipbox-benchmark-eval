# Rich Eisen's Swift-lyric preshow, acknowledged by Kelce

During NFL game preshow coverage, NFL Network's Rich Eisen worked a slew of Taylor Swift lyric references into a montage, including "shake it off", "anti-hero" and "all too well". Tight end Travis Kelce acknowledged the tongue-in-cheek banter by commenting on the preshow montage video Eisen posted to Instagram: "Well played Rich … Well played." Per The Age, 26 September 2023.
