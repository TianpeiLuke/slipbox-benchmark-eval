# Cartwright Act and California Unfair Competition Law in Epic v. Google

In its trial against Google over the Google Play store and its commission structure, which began November 6, 2023, Epic Games plans to plead two California anticompetitive statutes — the Cartwright Act and the Unfair Competition Law — alongside federal claims under Sections 1 and 2 of the Sherman Act. The state-law claims are the Californian legal basis for Epic's argument that Google restrains competition in Android app distribution and in payment processing for content inside Android apps.
