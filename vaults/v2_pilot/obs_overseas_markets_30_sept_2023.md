# European indexes modestly higher as Asian exchanges closed, 29 September 2023

In overseas sharemarkets on 29 September 2023, indexes finished modestly higher in Europe, while exchanges were closed across much of Asia. The European gain sat in contrast to Wall Street, which had closed the month with further losses after its worst month and quarter of the year. The report was published by The Sydney Morning Herald on 1 October 2023.
