# DOJ exhibits showed Apple and Cue criticising Google's privacy policies

In US v. Google on 2023-09-26, Justice Department lawyer Meagan Bellshaw put a series of emails and slides before Apple senior vice president Eddy Cue in which Cue and Apple had railed against Google's privacy policies. Cue agreed with her that Apple has "always thought we had better privacy than Google." Bellshaw was pressing on how Apple had concluded Google was the best search product, having earlier asked Cue whether Apple believed user privacy was important — a question he answered "Absolutely." The Verge covered the exchange.
