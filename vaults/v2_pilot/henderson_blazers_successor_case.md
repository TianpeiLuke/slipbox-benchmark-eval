# Scoot Henderson's ROTY Case Rests on Portland's Rebuild Around Him

Sporting News reported on 2023-10-04 that Scoot Henderson's NBA Rookie of the Year chances run through Portland's restructuring: the Trail Blazers placed Henderson as Damian Lillard's successor, with talents such as Anfernee Simons and Jerami Grant beside him while much of the Blazers' fortunes hinge on Henderson. A stellar season with the G League Ignite sent expectations for his rookie year soaring, backed by his strength, agility and promising defense.
