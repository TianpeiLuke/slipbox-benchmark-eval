# Sam Bankman-Fried's path from Alameda Research to FTX, per TechCrunch

Before his criminal trial began in October 2023, Sam Bankman-Fried — aged 31 at the time, facing seven counts of fraud and conspiracy — had built the crypto firm FTX on the back of an earlier venture. Per TechCrunch's reporting on 2023-10-01, he co-founded the crypto-trading firm Alameda Research in 2017, then co-founded FTX in 2019 as a complement to it, to bring in revenue and liquidity for the trading arm. FTX became the third-largest crypto exchange, reaching a peak valuation of $32 billion.
