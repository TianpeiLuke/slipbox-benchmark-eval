# Hypothesis: mixtures of experts rival monolithic LLMs in 2024

A TechCrunch 2024 prediction published 2023-12-19 ("8 predictions for AI in 2024", section "The limits of monolithic LLMs") hypothesizes that in 2024 diminishing returns on parameter counts will reach the point where training a 500-billion-parameter model may technically produce better results but the compute required could provably be deployed more effectively. On that view, a single monolithic model is unwieldy and expensive, while a mixture of experts — a collection of smaller, more specific and likely multimodal models — will prove almost as effective while being much easier to update piecemeal. It fails if monolithic models retain a decisive quality or cost advantage.
