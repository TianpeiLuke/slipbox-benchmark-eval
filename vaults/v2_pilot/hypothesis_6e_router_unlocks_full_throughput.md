# Steam Deck OLED's Wi-Fi 6E gain is conditional on a compatible 6E network, not yet proven

Proposed by the wireless section of Engadget's Steam Deck OLED review (2023-11-09) and left untested: the Steam Deck OLED's Wi-Fi 6E module and improved antennas will push throughput beyond 562 mbps, and widen its lead over the original Steam Deck, only when the handheld is paired with a Wi-Fi 6E router and multi-gigabit internet. Against a Wi-Fi 6 router, the comparison showed a gain of about 10 percent. That 10 percent is the falsifier: supply a compatible 6E network and, if throughput shows no further improvement over it, the hypothesis fails — the 6E radio would be contributing nothing beyond the antenna upgrade, rather than being capped by the host network.
