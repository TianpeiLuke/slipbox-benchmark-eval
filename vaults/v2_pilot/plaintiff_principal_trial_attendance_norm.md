# Why Tim Sweeney Sat in Court Every Day of Epic v. Google but One

Asked by The Verge on 2023-12-12 why he attended Epic Games' antitrust trial against Google every day but one, Tim Sweeney — Epic's CEO, co-founder and controlling shareholder — gave an argument from obligation rather than strategy. Epic was "asking a lot of the court system and the jury," spending four weeks on a major antitrust trial full of complicated facts and evidence; in his words, "It wouldn't be right to start something like this and not show up." He cited precedent: Phil Schiller, Apple's senior vice president, sat throughout the entire Epic v. Apple trial, as Sweeney did. His conclusion is that daily personal attendance by the executive who brought the case is necessary to show respect for the legal process. The one day he missed, he declined to explain, calling it "non-Epic issues."
