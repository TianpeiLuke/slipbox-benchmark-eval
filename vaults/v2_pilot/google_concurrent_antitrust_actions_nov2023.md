# Google faced three search and app-store antitrust trials at once in mid-November 2023

As of mid-November 2023, Google was simultaneously litigating three antitrust matters touching search and app distribution, according to a TechCrunch report dated November 13, 2023. First, the Justice Department's landmark case against Google over search was roughly two months in, before Judge Amit Mehta. Second, Google had settled a separate antitrust lawsuit with dating site Match Group the previous week. Third, on November 6, 2023 Google went to trial against Fortnite maker Epic Games, which sought to prove Google engages in anticompetitive behavior regarding its Android app store, Google Play, and its commission structure.
