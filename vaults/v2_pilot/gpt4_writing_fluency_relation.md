# GPT-4 writes more naturally and fluently than OpenAI's earlier models

TechCrunch described GPT-4, in a guide dated 2023-09-28, as a model that "can write more naturally and fluently than previous models" — a comparison between GPT-4 and OpenAI's earlier generations of language models. TechCrunch elsewhere calls GPT-4 "its latest language-writing model," situating that claim as a generational improvement rather than a benchmark result. No score, test, or evaluation backs the description; it is a qualitative judgment about writing style. The stated access conditions give the claim its practical reach: GPT-4 remained largely exclusive to paying ChatGPT users, though available free through Microsoft's Bing Chat.
