# Where this TechCrunch Gemini piece compares Gemini Pro head-to-head with ChatGPT/GPT-4

TechCrunch's "Early impressions of Google's Gemini aren't great" (2023-12-07) benchmarks Gemini Pro against OpenAI models at three spots: news summarization, where ChatGPT returns a cited bullet-list recap and Gemini Pro refuses; the HTML analog clock Mohsen Azimi built with ChatGPT, which Gemini Pro reproduces "off by a few ms"; and Edison Ade's Tic Tac Toe game on 2023-12-06 — ChatGPT first try, Gemini Pro 3 tries.
