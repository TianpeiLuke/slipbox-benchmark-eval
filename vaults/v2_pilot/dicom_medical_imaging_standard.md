# DICOM: the internationally recognised standard for medical images

Digital Imaging and Communications in Medicine (DICOM) is a decades-old industry standard designed for storing and sharing medical images, and is the internationally recognised format for medical imaging. Its scope is the exchange of imaging data across industry systems. Security weaknesses in DICOM, documented in TechCrunch's Week in Review of 2023-12-09 following findings by Aplite, a Germany-based cybersecurity consultancy, left thousands of exposed servers spilling the medical records and personal health information of millions of patients.
