# DOJ's Chat-Deletion Accusation Counters Pichai's Litigation-Hold Compliance Claim

Pichai's assertion that he took "great care" to comply with the government's litigation hold in Google's antitrust suit collides with the DOJ's own account of Google's deletion practices. In February 2023, the DOJ accused Google of systematically destroying internal chats through its history-off option, which deleted messages every 24 hours unless a user manually changed the setting — a policy applied while Google was already subject to the hold. Pichai testified on October 30, 2023 that he had acted against the history-off default in February 2023 to comply with the DOJ's litigation hold, per CNBC. DOJ lawyer Meagan Bellshaw also pulled up a 2021 message exchange in which Pichai asked for history to be turned off in a group chat. TechCrunch reported both on 2023-10-31.
