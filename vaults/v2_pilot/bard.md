# Bard: Google's ChatGPT competitor and the consumer surface for Gemini Pro

Bard is Google's consumer chatbot, which TechCrunch on 2023-12-07 describes as "Google's ChatGPT competitor," the counterpart to OpenAI's ChatGPT. It is one of the range of products and services that Gemini — Google's new flagship generative AI model, unveiled the same week and pitched as meeting or exceeding models like OpenAI's GPT-4 — was meant to power. A "lite" version of that model, Gemini Pro, began rolling out to Bard the day before publication, on 2023-12-06, making Bard the surface through which ordinary users first reached Gemini Pro.
