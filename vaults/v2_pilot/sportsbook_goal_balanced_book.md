# The Sportsbook Business Model: Balanced Action Wins Either Way

Per Sporting News (2023-11-06), a sportsbook's objective on each market is balanced action, not picking winners. "Every sportsbook's goal is to even out the betting on both sides of every bet," which lets it earn the profit already built into the odds and lines — the margin commonly called the vig — without worrying about which side wins. Lines move when the public bets heavily on one side; the originals come from oddsmakers and oddsmaking services using computer models and algorithms fed by analytics, statistics, injury and roster news, performance, and matchup data.
