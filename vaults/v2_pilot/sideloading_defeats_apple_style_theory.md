# Epic's Sideloading Problem: The Obvious Weakness in Its Google Antitrust Case

The most obvious objection to the case Epic Games brought against Google, tried from November 6, 2023, is structural: Android permits sideloading apps, so Epic cannot allege there is no alternative path to install apps on the platform — the claim it made against Apple, where no such route exists. That distinction is why the Google trial "will differ slightly" from the Apple fight. Epic's fallback, that Google makes sideloading cumbersome, is a weaker position than a closed door, and it leaves the bulk of the case resting on agreements with device manufacturers and developers rather than on any outright installation ban.
