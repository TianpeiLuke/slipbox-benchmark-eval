# US 10-year Treasury yield ends 29 September 2023 at 4.58 per cent, near its highest since 2007

On Friday 29 September 2023, the yield on the 10-year US Treasury returned to 4.58 per cent, the level it had touched late Thursday, after dipping earlier in the session to 4.52 per cent on encouraging signals about inflation. Reported by The Sydney Morning Herald on 1 October 2023, that finish left the benchmark again near its highest level since 2007, as solid early gains in US stocks faded under bond-market pressure.
