# Spotify cuts about 1,500 jobs, roughly 17% of staff, on 4 December 2023

Spotify announced on Monday 4 December 2023 that it is eliminating about 1,500 jobs, or roughly 17% of its workforce — its third round of layoffs that year. In a note to employees, founder and chief executive Daniel Ek cited slow economic growth and rising capital costs, saying the music streaming giant was aiming to become "both productive and efficient," per TechCrunch's Week in Review newsletter dated 2023-12-09.
