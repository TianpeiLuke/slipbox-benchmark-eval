# Against the Accumulation Argument: One-Way Signals and Silence Are Not Statements by Swift

Every entry in CBSSports.com's Oct. 12, 2023 timeline is a signal sent by one party or a gesture read in a crowd — Kelce's friendship bracelet on July 26, his on-air invitation on the Pat McAfee show Sept. 21, his "Vigilante Sh--" bracelet Sept. 22 — plus Swift's attendance at five Chiefs games starting Sept. 24. None is a statement by Swift. Jason Kelce's Sept. 14 non-denial is compatible with no relationship existing, so treating the aggregate as proof over-reads silence.
