# Epic frames two separate markets in its Google antitrust case

In its antitrust trial against Google, which began November 6, 2023, Epic Games — the Fortnite maker — frames Google as restraining competition in two separate markets rather than one: the distribution of apps to Android users, and payment processing solutions for content inside Android apps. Epic brings the case under Sections 1 and 2 of the Sherman Act, the U.S. antitrust law, and under California's Cartwright Act and Unfair Competition Law. The split lets Epic attack two distinct sets of contracts: Google's agreements with Android device manufacturers and developers, and Google's requirement that apps use its own first-party billing system, which charges a 15% to 30% commission on the sales it processes.
