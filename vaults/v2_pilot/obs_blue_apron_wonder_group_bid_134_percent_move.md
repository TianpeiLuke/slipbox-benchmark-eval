# Blue Apron shares surged 134.5 per cent on 29 September 2023 after Wonder Group buyout announcement

Blue Apron shares soared 134.5 per cent after the meal kit company said it was being bought by Wonder Group for $US13 per share in cash, in a deal valued at $US103 million ($160.2 million). The move was recorded in The Sydney Morning Herald on 2023-10-01, covering Wall Street's trading on Friday 29 September 2023 — a session that capped the stock market's worst month and quarter of the year, with the S&P 500 slipping 0.3 per cent.
