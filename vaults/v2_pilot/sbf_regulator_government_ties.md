# Sam Bankman-Fried's ties to U.S. regulators and officials he donated to

Per TechCrunch's 2023-10-01 account of FTX's collapse, former FTX CEO Sam Bankman-Fried "had close ties to U.S. regulators and government officials, many of whom he donated to" — listed among the mainstream reach of the crypto exchange, which peaked at a $32 billion valuation.
