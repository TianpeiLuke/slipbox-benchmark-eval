# John J. Ray III, the Enron liquidator now running FTX, calls its collapse worse and BFF's acts "old-fashioned embezzlement"

John J. Ray III, the bankruptcy attorney known for cleaning up Enron, was by September 2028 the person leading what remained of FTX. In that role he had publicly said FTX was worse than Enron, and he described founder Sam Bankman-Fried's conduct as "really just old-fashioned embezzlement." The characterizations were reported by The Verge on 2023-09-28, as Bankman-Fried's fraud trial opened.
