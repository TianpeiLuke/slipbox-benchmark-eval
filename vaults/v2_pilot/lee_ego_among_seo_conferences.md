# SEO's defining arrogance: success measured as having outdone Google

Cade Lee, speaking to The Verge's Rachel Botsford on 2023-11-01 after more than a decade working in SEO, offered the argument that the profession's defining trait isn't technique but temperament: an arrogance born of treating search rankings as a personal victory over Google. He described what he prepared the reporter to expect at the alligator party for South Florida digital marketers — "This is modern-day pirate shit, as close as you can get" — and named what he'd noticed most at SEO conferences and SEO networking events: "There's definitely an ego among all of them. You succeed, and now you're a genius. Now you've outdone Google." The claim reframes SEO not as marketing discipline but as adversarial triumph, which is why the reporter found the megalomaniacal framing — vanquishing the almighty algorithm, technically many algorithms — so compelling going in.
