# ChatGPT Summarized the News Prompt Gemini Pro Deflected

In a news-summary test described in a TechCrunch article published on 2023-12-07, the author asked Bard, running Google's Gemini Pro, to recap a topical story, and got a response matching Gemini Pro's pattern, as the author characterizes it, of declining to comment on potentially controversial news topics and telling users to Google it themselves instead. As a control, the author ran the same prompt through ChatGPT, which returned a bullet-list summary with citations to news articles.
