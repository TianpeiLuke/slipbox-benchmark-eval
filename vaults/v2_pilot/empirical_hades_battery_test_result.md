# Hades battery test: Steam Deck OLED vs LCD (Engadget, 2023)

In Engadget's November 9, 2023 review of the Steam Deck OLED, the reviewer used Hades, her most-played title on the original Steam Deck, to test Valve's battery life claims, running both machines at the same settings: 800p with a 60 fps cap and similar brightness. The Steam Deck OLED lasted four hours and 23 minutes before dying; the original LCD model died after two hours and 42 minutes. That is a 62 percent improvement, surpassing Valve's own estimate of 30 to 50 percent longer battery life. She notes her LCD battery has had time to degrade.
