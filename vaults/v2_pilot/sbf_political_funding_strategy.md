# SBF's cross-party political money: McConnell, Republicans and Democrats

Sam Bankman-Fried put money behind candidates on both sides of the aisle, per TechCrunch's 2023-10-02 report on Michael Lewis's "Going Infinite". In a 60 Minutes interview broadcast the day before, Lewis said he met SBF ahead of SBF's meeting with Mitch McConnell to discuss how the FTX founder could fund candidates seeking to offset Donald Trump's influence in the Republican Party; SBF backed Democrats as well. The article recounts the suit SBF brought to that McConnell meeting, tightly balled under his arm with his dress shoes falling out of the bundle. The logic it ascribes to him: you never know when you might need a friend.
