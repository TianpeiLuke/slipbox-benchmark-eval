# Arsenal's 6-0 win over Lens topped Group B on 29 November 2023

Arsenal's 6-0 thrashing of Lens on matchday five of the 2023/24 UEFA Champions League group stage, on 29 November 2023, confirmed them as winners of Group B. Going into the final matchnight they sat top with 13 points from six games, four wins, one draw and one loss, a goal difference of +12.
