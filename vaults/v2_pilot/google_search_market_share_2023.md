# Google described in 2023 as a trillion-dollar firm with ~90% of online search

In reporting published by The Verge on 2023-11-01, Google was characterized as "an all-powerful, all-seeing, trillion-dollar corporation with a 90 percent market share for online search." That figure surfaced inside a blame-attribution argument: some users unhappy with 2023 Google Search results blamed the corporation itself rather than the search engine optimization (SEO) practitioners who game its rankings.
