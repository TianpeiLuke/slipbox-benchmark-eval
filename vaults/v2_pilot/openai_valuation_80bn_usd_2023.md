# OpenAI valued at more than US$80 billion in its latest funding round

OpenAI is valued at more than US$80 billion (A$123 billion), according to two people familiar with the company's latest funding round, as reported by The Age on 2023-12-09. That valuation attaches to the startup created in 2015 from a dinner between Elon Musk and Sam Altman at the Rosewood hotel in Menlo Park, California, and backed by hundreds of millions of dollars from Musk and other funders.
