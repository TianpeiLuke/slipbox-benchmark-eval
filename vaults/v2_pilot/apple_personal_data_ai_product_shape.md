# Prediction (2023-12-19): Apple's 2024 AI exploits the user's own data rather than generating media

TechCrunch's "8 predictions for AI in 2024" (2023-12-19) expects Apple's 2024 AI to follow the company's established pattern — waiting, watching and learning from other companies' failures, then arriving with a refined and polished take. Concretely: an AI centered on practical uses of the user's own data across Apple's ecosystems, with elegant handling of dangerous prompts, multimodal understanding limited mainly to images, and no media generation. That hypothesis is falsified if Apple ships generative image, video or music capabilities in 2024, or if its assistant draws primarily on cloud data rather than the user's local context. The prediction assumes Apple's characteristic timing: wait too long and "its competition may eat up the market," but the tech is ripe for Apple's kind of improvement.
