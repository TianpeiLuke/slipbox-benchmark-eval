# An SEO-driven name buys the search moment, not the visit

The Verge's 2023-10-26 report on Google-first business names lands on one limit: "the most powerful thing an SEO-driven name might be able to do is get customers in the door." The argument's warrant is that the name wins the search result, not the experience inside — Thai Food Near Me in Murray Hill, Manhattan still owes pad see ew, and Barber Shop Near Me in Coral Springs, Florida still owes a haircut. That scope condition matters: the name works as an acquisition device only, and retention belongs to the business itself. The source makes the loop the opposite way too: happy customers leave good reviews, good reviews raise a Google Maps profile's rank, so Felix Silva posts high-quality photos, videos and updates, since some experts say active profiles improve rankings.
