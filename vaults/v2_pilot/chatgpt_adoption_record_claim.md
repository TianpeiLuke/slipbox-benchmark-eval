# TechCrunch contrasts ChatGPT's minimal launch with an unsourced "fastest-growing consumer app" claim

TechCrunch's case for ChatGPT's significance on its first birthday (November 30, 2023) rests on a contrast between an unpromising origin and a superlative outcome. The chatbot shipped a year earlier as a "low-key research preview" — initially only a basic free-to-use, web-based, chat-focused interface on top of GPT-3.5, one of OpenAI's existing models. From that, the article asserts, it became OpenAI's most popular product ever and "the fastest-growing consumer app in history," a record stated without naming the data behind it. Sam Altman's retrospective tweet that evening — "a year ago tonight we were probably just sitting around the office putting the finishing touches on chatgpt … what a year it's been…" (@sama, November 30, 2023) — supplies the humble-origin half of the argument.
