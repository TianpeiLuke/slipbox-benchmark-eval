# Lens Beat Sevilla 2-1 on 12 December 2023 to Lock Up Group B's Europa League Berth

On the final matchday of UEFA Champions League Group B on 12 December 2023, Lens beat Sevilla 2-1 to make certain of the Europa League berth that third place carried, while PSV and Arsenal played out a 1-1 draw. Lens, from Ligue 1, finished on 8 points behind qualifiers Arsenal (13) and PSV (9); LaLiga's Sevilla ended the group with 2 points from six games.
