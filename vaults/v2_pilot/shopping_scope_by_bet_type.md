# Line Shopping Extends to Every Priced Market, and How to Execute It

Line shopping — checking the posted lines and odds for the same prospective bet at multiple sportsbooks — is not confined to one market. Sporting News' line-shopping guide (2023-11-06) states that you can shop around for moneylines, point spreads, totals bets, prop bets, and more, adding that finding the best line or better odds on any bet "is like finding money." The workflow it prescribes: pick a side, then use the drop-down menus and tabs — on a live odds feed or on several sportsbook apps installed on your phone — to reveal that bet's odds and lines across books, and bet when one shows better odds or an improved line than the competition.
