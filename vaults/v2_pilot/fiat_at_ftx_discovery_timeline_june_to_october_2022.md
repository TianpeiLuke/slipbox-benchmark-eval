# Bankman-Fried's Account of When He Learned About fiat@ftx, October 2022

Testifying at his own trial on 2023-10-31, reported by The Verge, Sam Bankman-Fried said he found out about the fiat@ftx account — the account tracking how much Alameda owed FTX — in June 2022, while his senior staff was focused on fixing the software bug. He said he did not discover what the account was for until October.
