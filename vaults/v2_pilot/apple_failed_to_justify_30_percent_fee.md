# Apple Could Not Justify Its 30 Percent Fee — an Argument Epic Cannot Reuse Against Google

Ahead of Epic v. Google, Sean writes in The Verge on 2023-11-05 and inserts an editorial aside: during Epic v. Apple, Apple was unable to explicitly justify its 30 percent in-app payment fee to the judge. He flags that failure as a point Epic cannot raise in the Google case, where the fees at issue are Google's own.
