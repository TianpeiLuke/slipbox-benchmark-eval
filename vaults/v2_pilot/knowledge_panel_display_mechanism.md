# Google's Knowledge Panel Draws on the Knowledge Graph, Per the 2023 Helena World Chronicle Complaint

In the class action antitrust suit filed in the U.S. District Court in D.C. and reported by TechCrunch on 2023-12-15, Arkansas publisher Helena World Chronicle quoted the complaint's description of a long-standing Google search feature: "When a user searches for information on a topic, Google displays a 'Knowledge Panel' to the right of the search results. This panel contains a summary of content drawn from the Knowledge Graph database." The complaint adds that "Google compiled this massive database by extracting information from Publishers' websites — what Google calls 'materials shared across the web' — and from 'open source and licensed databases.'" The effect the suit alleges: the panel answers the searcher's question from publisher-sourced facts on Google's own page, so the reader never visits the publisher.
