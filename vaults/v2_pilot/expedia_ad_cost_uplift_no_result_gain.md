# Expedia Told Google Ad Prices Rose Without Better Search Results

In the section on Expedia's complaints in TechCrunch's 2023-11-13 roundup of the US v Google trial, Expedia executives testified that Google's increases in ad prices were not matched by any corresponding improvement in search results. The testimony surfaced during the Justice Department's antitrust case against Google, heard before Judge Amit Mehta, in coverage that also turned to vertical search — categories such as "cheap flights."
