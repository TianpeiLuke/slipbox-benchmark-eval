# Caroline Ellison testified Alameda Research was already failing when she joined in 2018

Caroline Ellison testified at Sam Bankman-Fried's criminal fraud trial, as reported by The Verge on October 10, 2023, that Alameda Research — the hedge fund she ran — was troubled from her earliest time there in 2018. "Shortly after I started, I learned the company was in worse shape than I realized," Ellison said. Alameda had initially been funded with loans "from acquaintances," she testified, and those loans were recalled a few weeks after she arrived. Asked why Bankman-Fried had not shared the company's shaky circumstances in her job offer, she said: "He hadn't known how to tell me."
