# Meta's suppression of Palestinian content is structural bias, not a bug — TechCrunch, 19 Oct 2023

TechCrunch's claim on 19 Oct 2023: Instagram's shadowbanning of Palestine-related content is "the latest in a lengthy history of incidents on Meta platforms that reflect an inherent bias against Palestinian users in its processes," not the technical error Meta invokes. Meta says it never intends to "suppress a particular community or point of view," and its newsroom post, updated 18 Oct, insists "there is no truth to the suggestion that we are deliberately suppressing voice." The assembled warrants make intent beside the point. Meta cannot navigate Arabic's over 25 dialects and has been criticized for under-diversifying language resources. Its black-and-white policies preclude moderating nuance, such as content discussing violence without condoning it. Partnerships with government agencies such as the Israeli Cyber Unit politically influence policy decisions. Enforcement tracks user reports: filmmaker Khitam Jabr lost 24 hours of posting on West Bank travel Reels she says drew anti-Palestine comments and reports. And during the 2021 Hamas–Israel outbreak, a report Meta commissioned from a third party concluded its actions had an "adverse human rights impact" on Palestinian users' freedom of expression and political participation.
