# Cue testified Apple never considered walking away from the Google deal

On 2023-09-26, in the open session of the US v. Google antitrust trial before Judge Amit Mehta in Washington, DC, DOJ lawyer Meagan Bellshaw asked Apple's Eddy Cue whether Apple would have walked away from the Google Information Services Agreement if the two sides could not agree on a revenue-share figure. Cue said he had never really considered that an option: "I always felt like it was in Google's best interest, and our best interest, to get a deal done." Bellshaw was questioning Cue about his 2016 renegotiation of the ISA with Google CEO Sundar Pichai, in which Apple sought a higher percentage of Google's revenue.
