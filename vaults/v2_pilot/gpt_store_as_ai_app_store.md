# Hypothesis: OpenAI's GPT store becomes the default "app store for AI" in 2024

In a TechCrunch piece dated 2023-12-19, the prediction is that OpenAI will push its GPT store hard through 2024 as the default "app store for AI" — the platform people get their AI tools from — following Apple's store model "all the way to the bank," and at the expense of Hugging Face and open-source models. Originally planned for a December launch, the store was delayed by the November C-suite upheaval that left Sam Altman more fully in charge, accelerating OpenAI's shift toward a "ship it" mindset.

Falsification: the store fails to ship, or becomes only a minor distribution channel for AI tools.
