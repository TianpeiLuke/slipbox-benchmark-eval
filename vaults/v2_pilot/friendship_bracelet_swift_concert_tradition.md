# Friendship Bracelet: The Fan-Tradition Object Running Through the Swift–Kelce Timeline

In the CBSSports.com timeline dated 2023-10-12, a friendship bracelet is a hand-made strand given as a gift, not sold as jewelry — Kelce called it "a fan tradition at her concerts" at Taylor Swift shows. Its meaning shifts across three entries. On June 29 Kelce made one for newcomers at an NFL tight ends camp, saying he was "taking a page out of Taylor Swift's book." On July 26, on the "New Heights" podcast, he said he made one "with his number on it" to give Swift herself; asked whether that meant his jersey number or phone number, he answered, "you know which one." On Sept 22 he wore a bracelet reading "Vigilante Sh--," the title of one of Swift's songs, at his nonprofit charity event.
