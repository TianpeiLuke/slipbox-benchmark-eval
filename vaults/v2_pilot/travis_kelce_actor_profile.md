# Travis Kelce: Chiefs No. 87, Two-Time Super Bowl Winner, New Heights Co-Host

Travis Kelce, the Kansas City Chiefs tight end who wears number 87, is the NFL-side party in Taylor Swift's crossover with football, as set out in The Age's 26 September 2023 timeline. Kelce helped bring his team to Super Bowl victory in both 2020 and 2023, and co-hosts the podcast *New Heights* with his brother — so he arrives in the story as a championship-winning player with his own national media platform.
