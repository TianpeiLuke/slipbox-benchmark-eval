# TuSimple Tells Regulators It Is Laying Off Most U.S. Staff and Selling American Assets

Autonomous trucking company TuSimple notified regulators on Monday 4 December 2023 that it is laying off most of its U.S. workforce and selling its American assets in order to relocate its operations to Asia, as reported in TechCrunch's Week in Review newsletter of 2023-12-09 ("Google fakes an AI demo, Grand Theft Auto VI goes viral and Spotify cuts jobs"). The departure follows internal controversies at the company and the loss of its truck-manufacturing partnership with Navistar. TechCrunch's roundup lists the exit among that week's major tech stories alongside Spotify's cuts of roughly 1,500 jobs.
