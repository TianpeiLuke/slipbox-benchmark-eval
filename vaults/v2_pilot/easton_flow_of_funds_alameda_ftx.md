# Notre Dame professor Easton traced billions between Alameda Research and FTX to customer deposits

In week three of Sam Bankman-Fried's fraud trial, University of Notre Dame professor Peter Easton, who helped the government prosecutions of Enron and WorldCom, testified about what he uncovered after tracing the flow of billions of dollars between Alameda Research and FTX (TechCrunch, Oct. 6, 2023). Easton testified that much of that funding came from customers, and that user deposits were used by both FTX and Alameda to purchase real estate, make investments, or direct funding toward political causes and charities. The reported coverage placed his testimony early in that week of proceedings.
