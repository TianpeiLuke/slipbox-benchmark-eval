# The Case for CeeDee Lamb Over Tyreek Hill for 2023 OPOY

As of December 5, 2023 (Sporting News), Tyreek Hill was the heavy favorite for NFL Offensive Player of the Year, on track to become the first player with 2,000-plus receiving yards in a single season — but the argument is that Hill may not reach that mark, and that CeeDee Lamb could take the award if he does not. The schedule drives it: of Miami's five remaining opponents after Week 13, four field pass defenses ranked in the league's top eight, which is a concrete reason to expect Hill's production to slow. On the other side of the ledger, Lamb's season average projects him to close at close to 1,600 yards, while his output over the last seven games alone projects him to closer to 1,800 yards. Lamb maintains that elevated pace and Hill fades down the stretch, and Lamb steals OPOY.
