# The counter-argument: name tricks indict Google, not the businesses

The premise that a business wins by optimizing its own name for Google search — as Thai Food Near Me, Barber Shop Near Me and dozens of "Near Me" businesses across the US do — drew a direct objection. In the October 2023 viral thread, after the official Google Maps account replied "Very relatable" with a crown emoji to a photo of the restaurant's brown-paper-covered windows, one person answered: "This is about how your products don't even work anymore." The force of the reply is its reversal of blame. SEO-driven names are read here not as clever strategy but as a symptom: if Google's rankings reliably surfaced the best nearby restaurant, a literal-search-term name would confer no advantage, and owners would not need one. The report is by The Verge, dated 2023-10-26.
