# Alameda's June 13, 2022 balance sheet listed FTX money owed as "ftx borrows"

During Sam Bankman-Fried's fraud trial, as reported by The Verge on 2023-10-31, an Alameda Research balance sheet dated June 13th, 2022 was shown in court that listed the money Alameda was borrowing from FTX under the label "ftx borrows." Bankman-Fried, asked about the document, seemed pretty hazy on it too.
