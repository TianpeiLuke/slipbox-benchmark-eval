# The Information Services Agreement (ISA): Google's default-search contract with Apple

The Information Services Agreement (ISA) is the contract that makes Google's search engine the default on Apple's products, in exchange for payments from Google to Apple of billions of dollars a year. The ISA has been in place since 2002; its current iteration was negotiated in 2016 by Apple executive Eddy Cue with Google CEO Sundar Pichai, over what share of the revenue Google earns from Apple users directed to its search engine Apple would receive. The agreement does not allow Apple to offer users a choice of search engines during device setup. The ISA is central to the US Department of Justice's antitrust case, US v. Google, according to The Verge's courtroom reporting of 2023-09-26.
