# US Economic Data Calendar After 1 October 2023: Jobs, Inflation, and the Fed Meeting

As reported by The Sydney Morning Herald on 1 October 2023, the latest monthly update on the US jobs market was due the following week, with a couple of important reports on inflation coming the week after that. The Federal Reserve's next meeting on interest rates ends on 1 November 2023. The Fed has insisted it will make upcoming rate decisions based on what incoming data say about the economy.
