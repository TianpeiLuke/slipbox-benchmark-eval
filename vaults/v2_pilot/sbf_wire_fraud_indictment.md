# Bankman-Fried on trial for seven counts of wire fraud and conspiracy, October 2023

As reported by The Verge on 2023-10-10, Sam Bankman-Fried, the founder of the failed cryptocurrency exchange FTX, was standing trial on seven counts of wire fraud and conspiracy. In the same passage, The Verge records the Securities and Exchange Commission's allegation that FTX was a fraud "from the start," with a "multi-billion-dollar deficiency caused by his own misappropriation of customer funds." The report places the trial in progress on the day Caroline Ellison took the stand.
