# Apple's wait-then-polish pattern as the model for its 2024 AI entry

TechCrunch's 2023-12-19 piece "8 predictions for AI in 2024" models Apple's likely AI entry on a repeated competitive pattern rather than on any announced product. According to the article, Apple has an established pattern of waiting, watching and learning from other companies' failures, then arriving with a refined and polished take that puts those earlier entrants to shame.

The prediction is that this pattern repeats in AI, for two reasons the article treats as the model's drivers. First, the timing rationale is defensive: if Apple waits too long, its competition may eat up the market. Second, and more importantly, the technology is described as ripe for Apple's particular kind of improvement — polish applied to rivals' rough prototypes — rather than as a frontier Apple must discover itself.
