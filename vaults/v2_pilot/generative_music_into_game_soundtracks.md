# Generative Music Models Reach Major Productions in 2024, Games First

A TechCrunch predictions article dated 2023-12-19 puts forward the hypothesis that generative music models will make it into a few major productions in 2024, games above all, where professional musicians can leverage the tools to create an unending soundtrack. The underlying logic is that audio, like video, will find niches in which the models' shortcomings aren't so visible. This sits inside the paper's broader prediction that agents, generated video and generated music graduate from "quaint" to "experimental" in 2024. The hypothesis is falsified if, by the end of 2024, no major shipped production credits generative music tooling.
