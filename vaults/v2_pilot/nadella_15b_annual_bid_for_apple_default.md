# Nadella Said Microsoft Would Pay $15 Billion a Year for Apple's Default Search Slot

During the U.S. antitrust trial against Google in October 2023, Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella said he was willing to spend $15 billion per year to get Bing into Apple's default search placement, per The Information's reporting, as covered by TechCrunch on October 31, 2023. Nadella and DuckDuckGo's CEO testified that week that their search engines would have been far more successful, even competitive with Google, had they made similar deals with Apple.
