# Google Weaponized Epic's Internal Messages at Trial, November 2023

In opening statements in Epic Games' antitrust suit over the Google Play Store, covered by The Verge on 2023-11-07, Google displayed Epic's own internal messages to the jury, including "How do we not look like the bad guys?" and "everything we're attempting is technically a violation of Google's policy, right?" Shown without surrounding context, those lines served Google's effort to recast Epic — which had opened by painting itself as the underdog up against a bully and a cheat — as a company that knew it was breaking the rules while it was doing so.
