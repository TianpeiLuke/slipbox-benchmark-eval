# Market Maker: The Defence's Definition of Alameda's Role on FTX

A market maker is a financial entity that acts as a trading partner for customers looking to buy and sell cryptocurrencies. Defense lawyer Mark Cohen used that definition during his opening statement on 2023-10-04 in Sam Bankman-Fried's FTX trial, reported by Fortune, arguing Alameda Research acted legally as an FTX customer, payment processor, and market maker — ordinary function, not self-dealing.
