# Judge Kaplan Forced a Concession on Whether Yedidia Warned Bankman-Fried About the $8 Billion

Adam Yedidia — Sam Bankman-Fried's college friend, Bahamas roommate, and FTX employee — testified that he had asked Bankman-Fried about the $8 billion hole on a padel tennis court in their luxury complex in June or July 2022. In his own testimony on 2023-10-31, The Verge reported, Bankman-Fried seemed to be trying to deny that conversation ever took place. Only after Judge Lewis Kaplan intervened to ask whether Yedidia had told him about that money, "in words or in substance," did Bankman-Fried admit he had been told.
