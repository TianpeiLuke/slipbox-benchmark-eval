# Wall Street's 29 September 2023 close: S&P 500 down 0.3 per cent, Dow down 0.5 per cent, Nasdaq up 0.1 per cent

Wall Street ended September 2023 — its worst month and quarter of the year — with further losses on Friday 29 September 2023. The S&P 500 slipped 0.3 per cent, or 11.65 points, to close at 4288.05, with the majority of stocks within the index sinking. The Dow Jones fell 0.5 per cent, 158.84 points, to 33,507.50. The Nasdaq composite edged 0.1 per cent higher, up 18.05 points, to 13,219.32. Reported in The Sydney Morning Herald on 1 October 2023, ahead of a lower Australian sharemarket open on futures pointing to a fall of 38 points, or 0.5 per cent.
