# Google Summit Notebooks Printed a Racially Insensitive "Cotton" Phrase, August 2023

Attendees of Google's K&I Black Summit, held in August 2023, were handed third-party notebooks whose inside pages were printed with the phrase "I was just cotton the moment, but I came back to take your notes," a play on "cotton" reported by TechCrunch's Dominic-Madori. TechCrunch described the wording as "highly insensitive" and the summit audience as mostly Black, so the merchandise was not well received. Afterward, Google pledged to "avoid similar situations as [it engages] with [merchandise] vendors going forward," according to TechCrunch's Week in Review of 2023-12-09.
