# Hypothesis: OpenAI's leadership fallout could redirect attention and funding to Meta's LLaMA and AI startups

A hypothesis in TechCrunch's ChatGPT explainer (published 2023-09-28, updated through OpenAI's November 2023 leadership crisis) is that the turmoil may shift advantage to OpenAI's rivals. CEO and co-founder Sam Altman's firing and swift return "raised concerns about its direction and opened the door for competitors," and two beneficiary groups are named: established rivals such as Meta and its LLaMA family of large language models, and independent AI startups, which the article suggests may more easily "get attention and funding as the industry watches OpenAI implode and put itself back together." The mechanism is reputational and financial rather than technical, unfolding while ChatGPT already had 100 million weekly active users and use inside more than 92% of Fortune 500 companies. The article calls the outcome unsettled: "The fallout is still settling."
