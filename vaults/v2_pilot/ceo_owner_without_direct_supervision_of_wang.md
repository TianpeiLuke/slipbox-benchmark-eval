# Bankman-Fried's testimony separated Alameda ownership from supervision of Wang

Testifying at his FTX trial on 2023-10-28, Sam Bankman-Fried drew a hard line between owning Alameda Research and managing it, according to The Verge's courtroom coverage. Although he acknowledged he was Alameda's CEO and also its majority owner, the report notes, "he wanted to be clear: he did not supervise Wang's direct work" — Wang being Gary Wang, the first person Bankman-Fried hired to program the computers at the trading firm he founded in 2017. The same testimony put Alameda's arbitrage returns at "50 percent to 100 percent annualized" and noted the firm had no risk team, something Bankman-Fried later called a "big mistake."
