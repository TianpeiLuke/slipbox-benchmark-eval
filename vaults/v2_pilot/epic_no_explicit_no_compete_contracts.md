# Epic's Attorney Conceded Google Wrote No Explicit Non-Compete Contracts

During opening statements in the Epic v. Google trial on November 7, 2023, Epic lead attorney Gary Bornstein admitted Google "was too clever" to draw up contracts that specifically forced developers not to compete with the Google Play Store. The concession narrows Epic's no-compete theory — that Google "pays actual and potential competitors not to compete" — to rest on conduct and alleged payments rather than written terms, an absence of documents Activision had already pointed to when it told The Verge in 2022 that it "never entered into an agreement that Activision would not open its own app store."
