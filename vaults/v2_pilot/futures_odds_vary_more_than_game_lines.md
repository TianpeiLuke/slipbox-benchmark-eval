# Futures Odds Diverge Across Sportsbooks Because of Liability, So Timing Matters

In a guide to line shopping published by Sporting News on 2023-11-06, a model of sportsbook pricing explains why futures are the exception to the usual uniformity of odds. Most game lines are similar at different sportsbooks, but the odds in futures markets can vary significantly. The mechanism is liability: sportsbooks sometimes have higher liabilities for specific teams and offer better odds on others to lay that off. Timing is everything. Getting it right lets a bettor take advantage of changes in different futures markets due to liability, and the only way to find these opportunities is shopping lines.
