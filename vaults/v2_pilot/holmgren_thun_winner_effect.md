# Holmgren's ROTY Case: 7-Foot Rim Defense, Outside Shooting and OKC's Pace

Sporting News (2023-10-04) cast Chet Holmgren of the Oklahoma City Thunder as a Rookie of the Year contender on the combined effect of his body and his system, not any one stat. His 7-foot frame and long wingspan give him "defensive prowess" that "will challenge opponents," while offensively he "capitalizes on mismatches" with scoring "from the paint to the 3-point line" and "underrated passing" that "syncs well with OKC's pace and style." That fit matters because the Thunder "anticipate a strong season, largely due to Holmgren," pairing his impact with the winning record that makes a contender visible.
