# Helena World Chronicle's D.C. class action alleges Google's anticompetitive siphoning of publisher content

The class action antitrust suit against Google and its parent Alphabet filed this week, as reported by TechCrunch on 2023-12-15, was brought by Helena World Chronicle, an Arkansas-based publisher of two weekly newspapers, in the U.S. District Court in D.C. The complaint accuses Google and Alphabet of anticompetitive behavior violating U.S. antitrust law, the Sherman Act, and others, on behalf of news publishers. Its core allegation is that Google "siphons off" news publishers' content, their readers and ad revenue through anticompetitive means — "starving the free press" and costing publishers "billions of dollars." The suit specifically cites new AI technologies such as Google's Search Generative Experience (SGE) and the Bard AI chatbot as worsening that harm.
