# Apple's ISA revenue-share percentage stayed out of the public record via closed sessions

During Eddy Cue's 2023-09-26 testimony in US v. Google, the specific revenue-share figures behind Apple's Information Services Agreement (ISA) — the deal dating from 2002 that he renegotiated with Google CEO Sundar Pichai in 2016 — were discussed only in closed court sessions. Cue said he wanted a higher percentage and Pichai wanted the existing terms; the resulting compromised number went unspoken in open court, and Google has paid Apple that undisclosed amount since.

Source: The Verge, 2023-09-26.
