# Alameda Research: Bankman-Fried's 2017 crypto arbitrage trading firm

Alameda Research is the cryptocurrency trading firm Sam Bankman-Fried started in 2017, during a crypto bull run. Testifying at his own fraud trial on October 28, 2023, Bankman-Fried told the jury he knew "basically nothing" about cryptocurrency at the time he founded it, but said he wanted to do arbitrage on it anyway. He described the firm's early arbitrage trading as wildly successful, citing annualized returns of 50 percent to 100 percent, before he went on to found the cryptocurrency exchange FTX. Bankman-Fried was the CEO and majority owner of Alameda Research.
