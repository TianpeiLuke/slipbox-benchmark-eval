# Fixing the $8 billion FTX software bug left Alameda owing about $8 billion anyway

Sam Bankman-Fried testified on October 31, 2023 at his fraud trial that correcting the FTX software bug — one that had overestimated the amount Alameda Research owed FTX by about $8 billion — did not remove the ledger gap: even after the fix, Alameda still owed FTX about $8 billion. Rather than escalate, Bankman-Fried directed Caroline Ellison to repay third-party loans and kept making investments.
