# "Search-optimized hellhole" as a 2023 phrase for traffic-shaped online life

A November 2023 Verge feature on the people accused of ruining the internet describes the professionals turning online life into what one tech writer recently called a "search-optimized hellhole" — a phrase capturing internet content reshaped to satisfy search algorithms and earn traffic rather than inform.
