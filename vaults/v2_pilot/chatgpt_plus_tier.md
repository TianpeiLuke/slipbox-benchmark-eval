# ChatGPT Plus

ChatGPT Plus is OpenAI's paid subscription tier for ChatGPT, the AI chatbot used by more than 92% of Fortune 500 companies. Its defining differentiator is access to GPT-4 and GPT-4 Turbo, described in a TechCrunch article published 2023-09-28. GPT-4 writes more naturally and fluently than previous models and remains largely exclusive to paying ChatGPT users. OpenAI paused Plus sign-ups in November 2023 citing a "surge of usage"; CEO Sam Altman announced subscriptions re-enabled on December 13, 2023, after the company "found more gpus".
