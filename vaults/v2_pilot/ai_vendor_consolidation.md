# 2024 AI industry consolidation hypothesis: most 2023 AI products will not survive

TechCrunch's 2023-12-19 predictions piece "8 predictions for AI in 2024" hypothesizes that 2023's AI products "will not all survive by a long shot," even while underlying capabilities "continue to grow and advance." The predicted driver is marketing meeting reality: companies that adopted machine learning systems to avoid falling behind face quarterly and yearly reviews of those claims and are likely to be "found wanting," prompting considerable customer withdrawal as benefits fail to justify costs and risks. The consequence is a round of consolidation in which the "wobblier riders of the wave fall and are consumed." Falsified if the 2023 product cohort persists largely intact through 2024.
