# Meta's Counter: Andy Stone Attributes Reduced Story Reach to a Bug, Not Bias

Against the accusation that Instagram shadowbanned Stories about Palestine in October 2023, Meta communications director Andy Stone attributed the reduced reach to a technical fault rather than moderation policy. In a Threads post, he wrote, "We identified a bug impacting all Stories that re-shared Reels and Feed posts" — meaning the limitation reached every account doing that, wherever located, irrespective of subject matter, and was corrected quickly. Stone declined to comment on the record for TechCrunch's story, dated 2023-10-19, and pointed to Meta's newsroom post, updated Oct. 18, which stated that Meta applies its policies "equally around the world" and that "there is no truth to the suggestion that we are deliberately suppressing voice." Meta had likewise blamed technical errors for removals of Palestine posts during the 2021 conflict.
