# The FTX Trial's Real Story: Fraud Plus a Destroyed Inner Circle

The Verge's argument on 2023-10-26, as the prosecution's case neared its end, is that the FTX trial matters on two tracks at once. The first is familiar: the prosecution made a strong case that Sam Bankman-Fried presided over the frauds at FTX and his trading firm Alameda Research, which purloined customers' funds, and he faces seven counts, two of wire fraud and five conspiracy charges. What gives the trial its weight, however, is "a simultaneous drama of ruined lives playing out in the courtroom" among the people closest to him. Adam Yedidia, his college roommate, testified with immunity after having texted "I love you Sam… I'm not going anywhere." Gary Wang, co-founder of FTX and Alameda, "sang like a canary"; Nishad Singh, a longtime family friend, pleaded guilty to six charges; Caroline Ellison, his ex-girlfriend, described the seven phony balance sheets she created. Wang, Singh, and Ellison are now felons. The Verge reads all of it as Greek tragedy, precipitated by one man's character flaw: the arrogance of Sam Bankman-Fried.
