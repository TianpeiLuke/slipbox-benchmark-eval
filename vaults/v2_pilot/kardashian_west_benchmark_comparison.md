# The Age's ranking of Swift–Kelce against Kardashian–West, and the hedge inside it

In "When pop culture and sport collide: A timeline of Taylor Swift's NFL takeover" (The Age, 26 September 2023), the final sentence ranks Travis Kelce and Swift's pairing on par with — and perhaps bigger than — Kim Kardashian and Kanye West. The warrant is structural: both couples fuse celebrity-music fandom to a mass sports or entertainment franchise, which is what licenses the comparison. The hedge in "perhaps" leaves the ordering unresolved, so the sentence asserts equivalence of kind while holding equivalence of scale open to evidence.
