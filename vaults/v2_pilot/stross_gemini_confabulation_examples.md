# Charlie Stross's Blog Post on Gemini Pro Confabulations (December 2023)

In a blog post published around 2023-12-07, science fiction author Charlie Stross documented many further examples of confabulation by Gemini Pro, the "lite" version of Google's Gemini model that began rolling out to Bard the day before. Among other mistruths Stross recorded, Gemini Pro asserted that Stross himself had contributed to the Linux kernel, which he never has. The observation was reported in TechCrunch's coverage of early Gemini impressions.
