# Epic's own mention of Project Liberty blunts Google's bad-faith angle

Google's attempt to cast Epic's Project Liberty as proof of bad faith runs into one complication: in the opening statements of Epic's 2020 monopoly suit against Google, reported by The Verge on 2023-11-07, Epic mentioned Project Liberty in its own opening. A fact the jury hears first from the side it is supposed to embarrass lands as disclosure, not exposure — so the same detail is a weaker sting when Google reaches for it later. Epic lead attorney Gary Bornstein spoke first, portraying Google as a bully that pays competitors not to compete.
