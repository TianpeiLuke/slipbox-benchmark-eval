# Pomerantz's homescreen warrant in Epic v. Google: two stores on every Samsung phone

In his opening statement for Google at the Epic v. Google trial, lead attorney Glenn Pomerantz advanced what The Verge's 2023-11-07 report called a commonsense warrant: Google cannot hold a monopoly on Android app stores because "every single Samsung phone comes with two app stores right on the homescreen." The Galaxy Store is preinstalled on every Samsung phone alongside Google Play. Pomerantz then answered Epic's install charts — the evidence that the vast majority of Android app installs come from Google Play, with only a tiny sliver from the Galaxy Store — by recharacterizing them as a record of user choice rather than of constraint: "When they show these charts that show all these downloads from Play and not from the Galaxy Store, that's what the Samsung phone users are choosing. They're touching Play. Nothing's keeping them from touching the Galaxy Store; it's just what works for them." The warrant's force lies in shifting the burden from market share to the absence of any barrier at the homescreen.
