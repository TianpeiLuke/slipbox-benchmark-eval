# About 200 Florida-based digital marketers drank and danced at the 2023 South Florida alligator party

At the South Florida rooftop alligator party The Verge covered on 2023-11-01, the author's harshest assessment of the roughly 200 digital marketers in attendance — taking shots and swaying to a dancehall reggae band — was that they dressed like they lived in Florida, which almost all of them did. She found almost everyone she met sympathetic, or at least nice enough not to deserve being maimed by the alligator, which measured five and a half feet rather than the advertised 10 feet.
