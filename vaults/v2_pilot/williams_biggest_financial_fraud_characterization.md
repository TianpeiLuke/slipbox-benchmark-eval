# Damian Williams: Bankman-Fried's alleged crimes "one of the biggest financial frauds in American history"

At a press conference, U.S. Attorney Damian Williams called Sam Bankman-Fried's alleged crimes "one of the biggest financial frauds in American history," according to TechCrunch's report of 2023-10-01 — the public characterization by the prosecutor bringing the FTX case, as Bankman-Fried's criminal trial on fraud and conspiracy charges began.
