# Publisher's Google antitrust suit alleges spoliation of chat messages, echoing Epic Games case

The class action antitrust suit that Arkansas publisher Helena World Chronicle filed against Google and Alphabet in the U.S. District Court in D.C. in December 2023 alleges evidence of improper spoliation by Google — specifically the destruction of chat messages. The complaint ties that allegation to a known precedent: the same issue of destroyed chat messages was raised in Epic Games' recent lawsuit against Google over app-store antitrust conduct, a case Epic won. The spoliation claim sits alongside the suit's other concerns, including allegedly changed AdSense rates, and appears among the counts supporting its requests for damages and injunctive relief against Google.
