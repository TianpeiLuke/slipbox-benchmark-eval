# Fed's data-dependence at risk if US economic reports get postponed

Stated as a hypothesis: postponements of scheduled US economic reports could undermine the Federal Reserve's insistence that it will make its upcoming interest-rate decisions based on what incoming data say about the economy.

Testable because of the reporting calendar itself, per The Sydney Morning Herald on 2023-10-01: the latest monthly update on the US jobs market is due the week after that date, with a couple of important inflation reports coming the following week, and the Fed's next meeting on rates ends on November 1.

Falsification condition: if the Fed's October–November guidance tracks the published data cleanly, the postponements have not impaired its stated data-dependent approach, and the hypothesis fails.
