# ChatGPT's Uses Go Well Beyond Conversation

Marking ChatGPT's first anniversary on 2023-11-30, TechCrunch catalogs what people use OpenAI's chatbot for beyond holding a conversation: completing and debugging code, composing music and essays, producing test answers, brainstorming business ideas, writing poetry and lyrics, and translating and summarizing text. The list runs all the way to the ornamental — ChatGPT can even emulate a Linux machine. All of that came from a product that started, a year earlier, as a basic free-to-use, web-based and chat-focused interface on top of one of OpenAI's existing models, GPT-3.5.
