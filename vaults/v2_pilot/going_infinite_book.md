# 'Going Infinite' — Michael Lewis's book on Sam Bankman-Fried

"Going Infinite" is a book by Michael Lewis — the financial chronicler of "Liar's Poker," "Moneyball" and "The Big Short" — recounting the rise and fall of the cryptocurrency entrepreneur Sam Bankman-Fried, founder of the FTX exchange. TechCrunch noted on 2023-10-02 that the book was set to debut on the first day of Bankman-Fried's trial at the Manhattan Federal Court, so it ran alongside the courtroom contest between his version and the government's version of what happened at FTX.
