# Pilbara Minerals and Qantas led ASX large-cap declines in the session reported 1 October 2023

In the ASX session reported by The Sydney Morning Herald on 1 October 2023, Pilbara Minerals fell 2.1 per cent and Qantas fell 1.5 per cent, and both were among the biggest large-cap decliners on the exchange that day. The report framed the session as one in which most sectors traded lower, with rate-sensitive healthcare and consumer companies among the weakest ahead of the Reserve Bank of Australia's meeting on Tuesday.
