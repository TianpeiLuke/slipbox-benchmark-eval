# Bankman-Fried's public claims of innocence after FTX's collapse

Sam Bankman-Fried maintained his innocence after FTX collapsed in late 2022. Appearing virtually from the Bahamas at The New York Times' DealBook Summit, the former FTX CEO said, "I didn't ever try to commit fraud on anyone; I was shocked by what happened this month." In a published DM exchange with a Vox reporter, he said he regretted filing for bankruptcy and thought that "regulators make everything worse." Reported by TechCrunch on 2023-10-01, ahead of his criminal trial on seven counts of fraud and conspiracy.
