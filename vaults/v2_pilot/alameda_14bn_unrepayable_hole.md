# Wang's courtroom figure for Alameda's roughly $14 billion hole

FTX co-founder Gary Wang testified in Manhattan federal court on Oct. 6, 2023 that he and Sam Bankman-Fried were aware in November 2022 that Alameda Research had no way to repay the roughly $14 billion hole in its books. The figure is Wang's account from the witness stand, given under a cooperation agreement with the government, not an audited measurement.
