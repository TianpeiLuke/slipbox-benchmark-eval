# Steps to Open a Funded US Sportsbook Account Before Betting NBA Rookie of the Year Futures

Precondition (per Sporting News, 2023-10-04): you are in a US jurisdiction where online sportsbooks are legal. The source warns that offshore sportsbooks advertise widely but are illegal and offer no protection against fraud.

1. Visit the online sportsbook, click its "sign up" or "register" button, and fill in the details form.
2. Accept the sportsbook's terms and conditions.
3. Claim the welcome bonus by following the referring site's link to the sportsbook; if a code is needed, it appears on that site. Read the promotion's requirements first, since restrictions may apply.
4. Make an initial deposit through the site's deposit options.

Postcondition: a funded account able to reach the basketball → NBA → futures tab and place a Rookie of the Year wager.
