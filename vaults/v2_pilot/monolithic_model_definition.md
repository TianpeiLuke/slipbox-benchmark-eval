# Monolithic LLM (vs. mixture of experts) — TechCrunch 2024 AI predictions

In "8 predictions for AI in 2024" (TechCrunch, 2023-12-19), a monolithic LLM means a single large general-purpose language model — as opposed to a mixture of experts, which is a collection of smaller, more specific and likely multimodal models. The article's predicted limit for 2024 is that a single monolithic model is unwieldy and expensive, whereas a mixture of experts may prove almost as effective while being much easier to update piecemeal. This sits alongside diminishing returns on parameter counts past roughly 500 billion parameters, where the compute could provably be deployed more effectively.
