# UEFA Champions League 2023/24 Group E final standings: Atletico Madrid top, Celtic last

At the close of the UEFA Champions League group stage on 12 December 2023, Group E finished with Atletico Madrid top on 14 points from four wins, two draws and no defeats, on a goal difference of +11. Lazio placed second on 10 points with a goal difference of 0, Feyenoord third on 6 points, and Celtic last on 4 points with a goal difference of -10.
