# FTX trading volume peaked at about $15 billion per day

TechCrunch's 60 Minutes coverage of Sam Bankman-Fried (published 2023-10-02) reports that trades on the FTX crypto exchange climbed to $15 billion/day at the peak of the platform's activity. The article cites that volume as the scale of trading that preceded FTX's multibillion-dollar collapse.
