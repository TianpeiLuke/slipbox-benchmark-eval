# Alameda Research's name was cover, not description — and "Wireless Mouse" was the real one

Testifying at his fraud trial on 2023-10-28, FTX founder Sam Bankman-Fried said Alameda Research took its name from Alameda County, California, where the firm's first office was set up, and that "Research" was chosen as deliberately blank cover. On the stand: "Effectively, we wanted to be under the radar at that point in time. I didn't want to call it Sam's Crypto Trading Firm or anything like that. We — there are a lot of competitors and people who we didn't particularly want to know what we were building out because they would race to do it." He added that the public name "was far better than the internal name that we had at that point, which was Wireless Mouse." Bankman-Fried started the crypto trading firm in 2017 and knew "basically nothing" about cryptocurrency then. The Verge's reporter countered that Bankman-Fried had earlier said on a podcast that the name made it easier to get a bank account — testimony the jury heard on day one of Gary Wang's case.
