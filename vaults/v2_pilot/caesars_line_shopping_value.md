# Caesars Sportsbook Spreads Half to Two Points Better Than Competitors

Sporting News' assessment dated 2023-11-06 reports that Caesars Sportsbook's odds and lines are consistently among the best in the United States. Its point spreads "can be a half-point to two points better than the competition's, and the moneyline odds may be more favorable." Sporting News concludes that Caesars Sportsbook is a must-see when shopping lines for a bet.
