# Real Madrid topped 2023/24 Champions League Group C on a perfect 18 points

In the 2023/24 UEFA Champions League group stage, Real Madrid won Group C with a perfect 18 points (6W 0D 0L, goal difference +9), completing six wins out of six through a 3-2 away win at Union Berlin on Dec. 12, 2023. Napoli finished second on 10 points (3W 1D 2L, +1) after a 2-0 win over Braga at the Diego Armando Maradona. Braga claimed the Europa League spot on 4 points (1W 1D 4L, -6); Union Berlin placed last on 2 points (0W 2D 4L, -4).
