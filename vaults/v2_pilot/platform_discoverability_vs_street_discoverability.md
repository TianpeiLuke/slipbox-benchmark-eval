# SEO findability trades away street-level and word-of-mouth discovery

The naming logic behind Thai Food Near Me, a New York-based Thai restaurant whose four owners included Teddy Jirapanan, is a trade-off rather than a free win. The business is optimized for the digital platforms diners use to find places nearby — literal search queries such as "Thai food near me" — and not for the person walking past on the street or getting a recommendation from a friend. Findability inside a search engine and index is maximized; discoverability to a passer-by and to word-of-mouth is what gets spent to buy it. The Verge reported this on 2023-10-26. SEO is defined there as making a business, website, or content more findable in search engine results.
