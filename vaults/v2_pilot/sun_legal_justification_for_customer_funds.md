# Can Sun testified SBF sought a "legal justification" for using consumer funds days before FTX's bankruptcy

Can Sun, FTX's former general counsel, testified in the third week of Sam Bankman-Fried's fraud and money-laundering trial, as reported by TechCrunch on 2023-10-06. Sun, who opted to cooperate with authorities as several of Bankman-Fried's former colleagues did, said Bankman-Fried asked him to create a theoretical "legal justification" for the use of billions in consumer funds. That request came just days before FTX filed for bankruptcy, and Sun's testimony was one of the prosecution's noteworthy moments that week.
