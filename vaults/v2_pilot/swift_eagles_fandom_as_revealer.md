# Swift's Eagles fandom makes her Chiefs cheering a signal — but only of affiliation

In The Age's 2023-09-26 timeline of Taylor Swift's NFL takeover, the evidential weight of Swift's Arrowhead appearance rests on a fandom conflict. Swift is known as an Eagles supporter: she is originally from Pennsylvania and references the team in "Gold Rush". So her visible backing of the Kansas City Chiefs — shouting "let's f—ing go" after Travis Kelce's touchdown, then chest-bumping other Chiefs fans, at the game following his 2023-09-21 invitation on The Pat McAfee Show — reads as abandoning her own civic team, which points to personal affiliation with Kelce. The warrant reaches fan behaviour only. It does not establish relationship status: a supporter switching teams signals affinity, not romance.
