# Shang: ChatGPT's target shifted from domain-specific chatbots to general-purpose systems via prompting

Ruoxi Shang, a third-year PhD student at the University of Washington studying human-AI interaction, attributes part of ChatGPT's enduring popularity in a TechCrunch article published 2023-11-30 to a change in what such systems were built to be. Chatbots had existed for decades, but the models behind ChatGPT were far more sophisticated than what users were accustomed to. Training on vast data moved the design target away from specialized, domain-specific chatbots toward general-purpose systems that people drive through instruction prompting, on the condition that users need learn no new interaction language. Shang notes that "AI models have enabled conversational agents to conduct conversations nearly indistinguishable from human dialogues."
