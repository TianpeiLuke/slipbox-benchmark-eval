# The two injunctions sought in Helena World Chronicle's December 2023 suit against Google

Beyond damages, the class action against Google and parent Alphabet filed this week in the U.S. District Court in D.C. by Arkansas publisher Helena World Chronicle asks the court for two injunctive remedies. The first would require Google to obtain consent from publishers before using their website data to train general-purpose artificial intelligence products — a obligation reaching not only Google's own AI offerings but those of its rivals. The second would require Google to let publishers who opt out of Search Generative Experience (SGE) continue appearing in Google's ordinary search results, addressing the complaint's allegation that publishers cannot block SGE because it shares GoogleBot, the same web crawler used by Google's general search service.
