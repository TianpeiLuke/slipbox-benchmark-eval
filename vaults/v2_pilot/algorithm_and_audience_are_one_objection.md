# Google Maps answered the joke — the algorithm's owner is also an audience for SEO names

Reading Thai Food Near Me — the New York Thai restaurant in Murray Hill, Manhattan, named after a literal Google search — as addressed only to a machine misses that the algorithm's owner publicly joined in. In March, before the restaurant opened, a photo of it went viral on Twitter, and the official Google Maps account, the very entity the meme-y restaurant name is designed to outsmart, couldn't resist: "Very relatable," the company replied, with a crown emoji. The name earned coverage on news sites and an influx of customers in its first crucial months. Google Maps' own amplification is part of what such names aim at, though the reception split — one reply to the company read, "This is about how your products don't even work anymore."
