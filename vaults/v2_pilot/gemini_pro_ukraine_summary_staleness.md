# Gemini Pro in Bard returned Ukraine war updates over a month out of date (2023-12-07)

On 2023-12-07, a TechCrunch author testing Gemini Pro inside Google's Bard chatbot asked for a summary of updates on the war in Ukraine. Gemini Pro did supply such a summary — notable because the model had elsewhere been declining to comment on controversial news topics, instead telling users to look it up themselves. However, the information Gemini Pro returned in that Ukraine summary was over a month out of date, despite the model having Google Search and Google News available to it.
