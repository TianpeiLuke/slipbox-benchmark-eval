# The Prosecution's "Man," Not "Boy," Framing of Sam Bankman-Fried

In his opening statement on 2023-10-04, FTX trial prosecutor Thane Rehn rejected the media's "boy" genius label for crypto founders. He called Sam Bankman-Fried a "man" who "stole billions of dollars from thousands of victims," per Fortune — refusing the sympathetic startup archetype and asserting an adult actor's culpability.
