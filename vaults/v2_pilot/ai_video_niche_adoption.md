# Hypothesis: AI-generated video finds 2024 niches where photorealism is not the test

TechCrunch's "8 predictions for AI in 2024" (published 2023-12-19) predicts that generated video will in 2024 find niches where its shortcomings "aren't quite so visible." The claim: in the hands of skilled creators, a lack of photorealism isn't a problem, and AI video will be used in fun and interesting ways — the stylized, imperfect output becomes an aesthetic asset rather than a defect once the audience stops measuring it against camera footage.

Falsified if AI video through 2024 is adopted only in contexts where it is judged against live-action realism standards, with no growth in stylized uses by skilled creators.
