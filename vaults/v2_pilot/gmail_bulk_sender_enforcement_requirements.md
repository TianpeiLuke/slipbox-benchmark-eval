# Google Sets Bulk-Sender Email Rules Starting Next Year, Announced Week of 7 October 2023

In the week of 7 October 2023, reported by TechCrunch's Week in Review, Google announced a series of significant changes to how it handles email from bulk senders, in an effort to cut down on spam and other unwanted emails. Starting next year, per Google's own description, bulk senders will need to authenticate their emails, offer an easy way to unsubscribe, and stay under a reported spam threshold. The requirements apply to bulk senders as a class, not to individual correspondents.
