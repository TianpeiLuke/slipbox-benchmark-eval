# Android sideloading as the Android–iOS boundary

In the Epic v. Google trial (reported by The Verge, 2023-11-07), the concept at issue is Android sideloading: the process by which users install apps from outside Google Play. Google lead attorney Glenn Pomerantz boasted that over a billion people have gone through the process Epic portrays as needlessly onerous. This openness is the boundary separating Android from iOS — Google's case rests on the simple fact that it allows alternate app channels at all, something Android rival iOS does not. Every single Samsung phone, Pomerantz noted, comes with two app stores on the homescreen, Play and the Galaxy Store. Epic has promised to "show that Google has closed off each and every other option" to the Play Store.
