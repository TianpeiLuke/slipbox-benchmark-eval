# Jirapraphanan's SEO-name logic: random names rank last, searched phrases surface

Teddy Jirapraphanan, one of four owners of Thai Food Near Me in Murray Hill, Manhattan, described the ranking logic behind naming a business after a Google query: "When you have a million restaurants close by, you will be in the bottom [of rankings] if it's a random name. But [when] we used Thai Food Near Me, people started knowing us" (The Verge, Oct 26, 2023). A distinctive but arbitrary name competes on visibility with every nearby rival in the category and lands at the bottom of local results; a name that literalizes the phrase customers type matches the search directly. Jirapraphanan is himself a constant searcher of "Thai food near me". The logic holds only where query volume exists for the phrase chosen.
