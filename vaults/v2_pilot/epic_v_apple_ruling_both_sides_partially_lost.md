# Epic's Antitrust Argument Against Apple Mostly Failed at Trial

Epic's antitrust argument against Apple did not succeed as its "Apple tax" campaign implied. In The Verge's explainer of Epic v. Google, published November 5th, 2023, the Apple suit is described as having gone to court in 2021, with a ruling that September "mostly decided in Apple's favor" — Epic failed to #FreeFortnite with its Llamacorn legal gambit, the August 13th, 2020 hotfix that let players buy V-Bucks directly through Epic's own payment processing and got Fortnite pulled from Apple's and Google's app stores for breaking the rules. The judge also picked a market definition "different entirely" from the one Epic pressed, and both parties ended up waiting for the Supreme Court to potentially weigh in.
