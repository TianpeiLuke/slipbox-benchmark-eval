# GPT store launch delayed past December 2023 by OpenAI's C-suite crisis

TechCrunch's predictions piece of 2023-12-19 reports that OpenAI's GPT store was originally planned for launch in December but was "understandably delayed due to the C-suite fracas" — the leadership shake-up of November 2023. The article describes the store as an "app store for AI" that OpenAI will push hard as the platform for AI tools.
