# 23andMe breach revised from 14,000 customers to 6.9 million people

23andMe's October 2023 breach turned out to be far larger than the genetic testing company first reported. On 1 December 2023, 23andMe said hackers had accessed the personal data of 0.1% of customers, or about 14,000 individuals, and did not initially say how many other users were impacted. The revised figure, reported in TechCrunch's Week in Review dated 2023-12-09: 6.9 million people had their names, birth years, relationship labels, the percentage of DNA they share with relatives, ancestry reports and self-reported locations exposed.
