# Kelce's August 2023 New York trip overlapped Swift's 1989 re-recording sessions

Travis Kelce took a week off Chiefs training to visit New York City in August 2023 and, per Vulture, this happened to be while Taylor Swift was re-recording her 1989 tracks in that city. The hypothesis, set out in a timeline published by The Age on 26 September 2023: the overlap is not coincidence but private time together, before either publicly acknowledged meeting. The article calls this a "suspicion" reportedly confirmed in September 2023, when it was reported the two had been "quietly hanging out" for months. Falsifiable: verified schedules of Kelce's training absence and Swift's studio bookings showing no shared dates in New York would leave only a coincidence.
