# Indictment alleges SBF spent stolen FTX customer deposits on real estate, investments and Alameda repayments

Sam Bankman-Fried is accused of using "billions of dollars in misappropriated FTX customer deposits" to help buy more than $200 million of real estate for himself, make billions of dollars of investments for his own interest, and repay Alameda's lenders, according to the indictment. The allegation was reported by The Verge on 2023-09-28, ahead of his trial on seven counts of wire fraud and conspiracy.
