# Blame for degraded Google results splits between the near-monopoly and the SEOs who game it

In a report on the search engine optimization industry published by The Verge on 2023-11-01, responsibility for bad search results is contested between two camps. In 2023, many people are unhappy with their ability to find information on the internet — which for almost everyone means the quality of Google Search results — calling the links that appear "absolutely unusable", "garbage", and "a nightmare" because "a lot of the content doesn't feel authentic." Some blame Google itself, asserting that an all-powerful, all-seeing, trillion-dollar corporation with a 90 percent market share for online search is corrupting access to the truth. Others blame the SEO practitioners, whose goal is to tell the algorithm whatever it needs to hear so a site ranks as high as possible and then shows visitors advertising. The disagreement is over agency: whether a dominant platform that rewards gaming sets the terms, or the gamers do.
