# AI Tool Customer Withdrawal Predicted for 2024 as Marketing Claims Face Review

In an article published 2023-12-19 on TechCrunch, the hypothesis is advanced that a considerable customer withdrawal from AI tools will occur during 2024. The reasoning is that marketing claims made for machine learning systems—often adopted by companies merely to avoid falling behind—will face their quarterly and yearly reviews, and will very likely be found wanting. The measurable benefits will fail to justify the costs and risks involved. On the far end of this spectrum, lawsuits and regulatory action against AI service providers that cannot back up their claims are anticipated as a consequence of the same dynamic.
