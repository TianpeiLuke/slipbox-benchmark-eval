# Steam Deck OLED's Size Complaint Survives the Refresh (Engadget, 2023-11-09)

Engadget's 89/100 review of the Steam Deck OLED (2023-11-09) answers its own praise with a doubled warning in the cons list: "Still ridiculously large" and "Seriously it's so big." The reviewer's "tiny hand gang" passage is the sharpest counter to the verdict: the OLED is "the same monstrous size as the original: 11.7 inches long, 4.5 inches tall and nearly 2 inches thick," and its thumbsticks are actually 1mm taller than the first version. With smaller-than-average hands, the device "looks and feels absolutely ridiculous" in the grip — unchanged since the original review.
