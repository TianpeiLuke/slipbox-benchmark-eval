# Nishad Singh, FTX's former head of engineering

Nishad Singh was FTX's former head of engineering. According to a CNBC article dated 2023-10-06, he was one of two former MIT friends of Sam Bankman-Fried who worked at the crypto exchange while living with the company's founder in the Bahamas. Gary Wang named Singh as a participant in the financial crimes he admitted committing at FTX, including wire and commodities fraud.
