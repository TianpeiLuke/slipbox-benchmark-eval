# Thompson's 2022 hypothesis: the generative-AI eruption may change work, thought and creativity

Derek Thompson's hypothesis, published in The Atlantic magazine's "Breakthroughs of the Year" for 2022, places ChatGPT inside "the generative-AI eruption" that "may change our mind about how we work, how we think and what human creativity is." Phrased that way, the claim is testable rather than rhetorical: measured task time on workplace assignments, judged quality of output, and the actual habits of creative practice can be compared against pre-ChatGPT baselines within a few years. The reference case is ChatGPT itself, which OpenAI released as a "low-key research preview" on top of its existing model GPT-3.5 and which reached one year old in the report dated 2023-11-30. If task time and quality metrics barely move, Thompson's "may" contracts to adoption and interface design, not to mind or creativity.
