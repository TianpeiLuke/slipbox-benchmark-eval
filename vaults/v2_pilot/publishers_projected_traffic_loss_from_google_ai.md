# Hypothesis: full rollout of Google's AI search will cut publisher traffic 20-40%

Publishers believe they will lose somewhere between 20-40% of their website traffic when Google's AI products fully roll out, according to The Wall Street Journal's report as relayed in a TechCrunch article dated 2023-12-15. The expectation is falsifiable: compare per-publisher Google referral volume before and after AI-search availability, controlling for overall search demand. Two related figures from the same reporting set the stakes — Google today drives nearly 40% of publishers' traffic, per data from Similarweb, and when online magazine The Atlantic modeled what would happen if Google integrated AI into search, it found that 75% of the time the AI would answer the user's query without requiring a click-through to its website.
