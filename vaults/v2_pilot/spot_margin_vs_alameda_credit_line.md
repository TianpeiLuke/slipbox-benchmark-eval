# FTX's Spot Margin Program Was Not Equivalent to Alameda's Credit Line

During cross-examination of FTX chief technology officer Gary Wang on 2023-10-10, at Sam Bankman-Fried's criminal fraud trial, defense attorney Christian Everdell raised FTX's spot margin program as a counter to Wang's claim that Alameda Research received a special credit line. The two arrangements look alike but are not equivalent. Wang had testified that Alameda got access to a special credit line and an option to take its balance into the negative without triggering liquidation — something he alleged other accounts at FTX didn't get. The spot margin program let users lend each other assets for margin trading, and in those cases it was possible to have a negative balance in a specific coin. It was not, however, possible for those accounts to avoid liquidation, as Wang testified Alameda could do, or to have an overall negative balance.
