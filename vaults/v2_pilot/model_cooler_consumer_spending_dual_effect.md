# Cooler US consumer spending cuts both ways: easing inflation, weakening growth

Economic data released Friday and reported on 1 October 2023 showed growth in spending by US consumers was cooler than expected in August, alongside inflation that was also a touch cooler than expected. That slowdown runs in two opposing directions. Fewer dollars pouring into purchases eases demand-pull pressure, giving companies less encouragement to try to raise prices further and supporting the Federal Reserve's attempt to push still-high inflation down to its target. But softer consumption may also dent what has been a big driver keeping the US economy out of a recession. Brian Jacobsen, chief economist at Annex Wealth Management, said spending growth had "come to a boil during a hot summer" and was cooling, citing higher energy prices, student-loan repayments and real disposable incomes declining since June.
