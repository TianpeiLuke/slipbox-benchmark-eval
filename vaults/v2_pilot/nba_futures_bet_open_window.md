# NBA Futures Markets Span the Whole Season, Including Rookie of the Year

NBA Rookie of the Year is one of the NBA futures markets, so wagering opens before the start of the NBA regular season and continues throughout the year, as with other NBA futures odds. Sporting News described (4 October 2023) these markets as settled on season-long outcomes, with odds posted at online sportsbooks and grouped under the NBA futures tab.
