# Third Point's Boroujerdi Testifies He Invested $60 Million in FTX Unaware of Alameda Ties

In week three of Sam Bankman-Fried's fraud trial, covered by TechCrunch on 2023-10-06, Robert Boroujerdi, managing director of the hedge fund Third Point, testified about working with Bankman-Fried and FTX — a partnership that resulted in a $60 million investment and in Third Point's lack of awareness of the special relationship between Alameda Research and FTX. Had he known of that relationship, Boroujerdi testified, Third Point would not have invested in FTX in the first place.
