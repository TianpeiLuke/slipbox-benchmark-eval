# New Heights — Kelce brothers' podcast as the venue for his Swift remarks

"New Heights" is the podcast Travis Kelce co-hosts with his brother, Philadelphia Eagles center Jason Kelce. In this timeline it is where Kelce's own remarks about Taylor Swift were voiced and then quoted: on the July 26 episode he said he attended Swift's concert but never met her, that "she doesn't meet anybody, or at least she didn't want to meet me, so I took it personal," and that he made a friendship bracelet bearing his number for her. When Jason asked whether it was his jersey number or his phone number, Kelce answered, "you know which one."
