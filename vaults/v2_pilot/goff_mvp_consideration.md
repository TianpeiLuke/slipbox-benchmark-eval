# Jared Goff's Case for NFL MVP Consideration, December 2023

Sporting News (December 5, 2023) argues Jared Goff belongs in the NFL MVP conversation on the league's own stated logic: the award "often ends up being someone, typically the quarterback, from one of the top-seeded teams," which is why Brock Purdy, Dak Prescott and Jalen Hurts led the betting boards after Week 13. Goff satisfies that test directly — his Lions are 9-3, poised to win the NFC South and still in contention for the No. 1 seed in the conference. The article adds a schedule edge: compared to the 49ers, Cowboys and Eagles, Goff and the Lions "may have the easiest schedule to finish the season," with five weeks left in the regular season.
