# FTX's Sponsorships and Political Giving Were Legitimacy Bought, Not Evidence of Soundness

The obvious counter-reading of Sam Bankman-Fried's 2021–22 visibility — that paying Tom Brady $55 million and Steph Curry $35 million, courting Anna Wintour's Met Costume Ball, meeting Mitch McConnell, and reportedly floating $5 billion to deter Donald Trump's re-run showed a healthy FTX — collapses in TechCrunch's 2023-10-02 account. Those outlays were purchases of legitimacy and "edge," made while the company was still raising roughly $2 billion, including $1 billion in 2021, from Sequoia, Temasek and Tiger Global rather than earning solvency. Exchange volume peaking at $15 billion/day measured speculation, not stability. The same activity that seemed to prove soundness preceded the multibillion-dollar collapse and the fraud-and-conspiracy trial at Manhattan Federal Court.
