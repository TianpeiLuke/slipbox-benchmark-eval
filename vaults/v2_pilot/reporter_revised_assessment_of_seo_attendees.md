# Florida Alligator Party Undercuts the SEO Villain Stereotype

The stereotype The Verge's reporter carried into South Florida was the SEO practitioner as "content goblin" — a hustler abandoning rules, morals, and good taste for traffic and cash, a nihilist in it for the thrills, as Cade Lee, over a decade in SEO, described it: "This is modern-day pirate shit" and "there's definitely an ego among all of them." She booked a plane ticket to a rooftop bar party hoping to watch one of these megalomaniacs maimed by the advertised 10-foot alligator released at the event.

Direct observation at that party contradicted the stereotype. She "found almost everyone I met to be sympathetic, or at least nice enough not to want to see them get maimed by a five-and-a-half-foot alligator" (the animal's actual size). Her harshest assessment of the 200 digital marketers taking shots and swaying to a dancehall reggae band was that they dressed like they lived in Florida — which almost all of them did.
