# OpenAI's product-company turn: Altman's post-November 2023 authority displaces board caution with appetite for markets

TechCrunch's 2023-12-19 predictions essay argues that OpenAI will run as a product company in 2024 because the leadership shake-up in November 2023 left Sam Altman more fully in charge, displacing the previous board. The consequence trickles down at every level of the company as a "ship it" mindset, exemplified by the GPT store — the "app store for AI" originally planned for launch in December but delayed due to the C-suite fracas — which OpenAI will push hard as the place to get AI toys and tools, never mind Hugging Face or any open source models. The mechanism is a swap of governing temperament: the caution and academic reserve that the previous board exerted gives way, in TechCrunch's phrasing, to an unseemly lust for markets and customers.
