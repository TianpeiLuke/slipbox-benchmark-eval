# Pichai's rebuttal to Expedia: Google's travel listings are among its most popular experiences

In the TechCrunch trial roundup dated 2023-11-13 — which spends part of its US v Google coverage on "why Expedia has a bone to pick with Google" — Sundar Pichai counters the travel company's complaint about Google's ad-rich vertical search results. His counter-argument: Google's travel listings are one of the most popular experiences the company has built, so those results serve users rather than merely taxing rivals. The roundup's own data leans his way: "cheap flights" ranks among the 20 search terms that earned Google the most revenue in the week of September 22, 2018, evidence of how much users turn to Google for verticals.
