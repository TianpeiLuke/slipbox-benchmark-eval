# Bankman-Fried Could Not Recall Who Decided to Spend $8 Billion of FTX Customer Funds

During his trial testimony on October 31, 2023, Sam Bankman-Fried, founder of the collapsed cryptocurrency exchange FTX, said he could not recall knowing anything about who was making decisions to spend $8 billion of customer funds, as prosecutor Danielle Sassoon questioned him in the wire fraud trial. Asked whether there were rules or requirements for how money borrowed from FTX would be returned, and whether there were rules for risk management, Bankman-Fried answered only, "I was concerned with overall risk management." He also said he wasn't trying to build blame for the spending, and had fired no one.
