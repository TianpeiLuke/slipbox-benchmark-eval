# Index: Eddy Cue testifies for Apple in US v. Google (The Verge, 2023-09-26)

Index to The Verge's 2023-09-26 report on Eddy Cue testifying in US v. Google, the DOJ antitrust trial over Google's search business, before Judge Amit Mehta in Washington, DC. Sub-topics covered:

- **The ISA**: the Information Services Agreement making Google default search on all Apple devices, in place since 2002 and renegotiated by Cue with Google CEO Sundar Pichai in 2016.
- **The 2016 revenue-share renegotiation**: Apple sought a larger share of Google's search revenue from Apple-directed users; specific numbers were reserved for closed court sessions.
- **The no-alternative defence**: "Certainly there wasn't a valid alternative to Google at the time" — Cue never considered switching providers or building Apple's own search.
- **Setup-choice arguments**: DOJ lawyer Meagan Bellshaw's iPhone setup screenshots (Appearance, location tracking, App Tracking Transparency) against Cue's "Setup is just critical stuff," plus the disputed ease of changing defaults.
- **Privacy versus payments**: Apple's privacy claims set against emails in which Cue railed at Google's privacy policies.
