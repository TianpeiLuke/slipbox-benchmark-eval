# Manual Line Shopping: Install Multiple Sportsbook Apps and Tap Between Them

Sporting News (2023-11-06) describes the manual route to line shopping as installing several sportsbook apps on one phone and comparing odds by hand. The procedure: download and install more than one sports betting app on your phone, then tap around among the sportsbooks to compare lines and odds for the bets you plan. In each app, pick a side, then use the drop-down menus and tabs to find the odds and line for that bet; moneylines, point spreads, totals and prop bets can all be shopped this way. Bet when you see an opportunity at better odds or an improved line compared to the competition. The trade-off: the manual task takes longer than an aggregated live odds feed, but you can check more sportsbooks, or the chosen few you feel consistently offer the best lines and odds.
