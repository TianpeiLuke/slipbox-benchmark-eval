# Bankman-Fried's Physical Demeanour Shifted Between Two Testimonies, 2023-10-10

In The Verge's courtroom coverage of 2023-10-10 — the fifth day of Sam Bankman-Fried's criminal fraud trial, in which he faces seven counts of wire fraud and conspiracy — the reporter recorded a change in the defendant's posture as the witnesses changed. Bankman-Fried "had been vibrating slightly" during the testimony of Gary Wang, FTX's chief technology officer. During Caroline Ellison's testimony later the same day, the reporter wrote, "his bouncing became more noticeable." By day's end, the defence's cross-examination of Wang "seemed like a sideshow." The observation is the journalist's own visual impression, not a measured or courtroom-documented fact.
