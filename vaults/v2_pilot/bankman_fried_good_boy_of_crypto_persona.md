# Bankman-Fried's pre-collapse persona as crypto's trustworthy face

Before his fall, Sam Bankman-Fried, founder of the cryptocurrency exchange FTX (founded 2019) and the trading firm Alameda Research, made himself out to be the "Good Boy of crypto" — the trustworthy face of a sometimes-shady industry, as reported by The Verge on 2023-09-28. He sat for many interviews both before and after FTX's collapse, and the quick rise of FTX as an industry force was at least in part due to his appetite for attention. At one point he told a colleague at FTX: "I honestly think it's negative EV for me to cut my hair. I think it's important for people to think I look crazy."
