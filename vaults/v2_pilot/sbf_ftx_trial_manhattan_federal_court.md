# Sam Bankman-Fried fraud trial opened at Manhattan Federal Court on 3 October 2023

Sam Bankman-Fried, the cryptocurrency entrepreneur accused of orchestrating "one of the biggest financial frauds in American history" through the multibillion-dollar collapse of FTX, the exchange he founded and led, stood trial from Tuesday 3 October 2023 at Manhattan Federal Court. Bankman-Fried had pleaded not guilty to some seven charges of fraud and conspiracy.
