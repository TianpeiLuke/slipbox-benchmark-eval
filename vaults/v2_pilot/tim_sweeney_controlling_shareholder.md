# Tim Sweeney's three roles at Epic and ownership of the Google and Apple suits

Tim Sweeney holds three stacked roles at Epic Games, the maker of Fortnite: CEO, co-founder, and — importantly — controlling shareholder, according to The Verge's profile published 2023-12-12. It is that last position, as owner of the company's controlling stake, that identifies him as the source of its litigation: the suits against Apple and Google were his idea, his fight from the very beginning, and not a board-driven initiative. He watched almost the entire Google trial in person, from a seat with a clear view of the jury, the judge, each witness, and Google's lawyers.
