# Google's User Choice Billing: a pilot letting Play developers bill themselves for a lower commission

User Choice Billing is a Google Play program, first introduced in November 2022 and still in pilot testing, that lets app developers use their own payment processing solution instead of Google's own first-party billing system. In exchange, Google reduces by 4% the standard 15% to 30% commission it charges on the sales it processes. It is open to all developers who sell apps in the 35 markets where it is available, including the U.S.; Spotify and Bumble were the initial testers, and Match took it up as part of its settlement agreement with Google. Epic Games declined to use it.
