TITLE: Bankman-Fried Opened His Testimony With Self-Description and Pre-Alameda Biography

BODY:
In his courtroom account that The Verge described on 2023-10-28 as "the story of FTX, from his point of view," Sam Bankman-Fried began by informing the jury he is "somewhat introverted, naturally," then launched into a prolonged tour of his pre-Alameda Research life that the reporter skipped. Only after that detour did he reach 2017. The same morning included explanations of "Amazon Web Services" and "database."
