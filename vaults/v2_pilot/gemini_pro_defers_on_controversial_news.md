# Gemini Pro in Bard Deflected a Topical News-Summary Request, 2023-12-07

On 2023-12-07, TechCrunch reported that Gemini Pro in Bard declined to comment on potentially controversial news topics when asked to summarize topical news, instead telling the user to "Google it themselves" — even though the model had Google Search and Google News at its disposal. TechCrunch's author noted that Gemini Pro "is loath to comment on potentially controversial news topics." When the author ran the same prompt personally, the repeat run produced a very similar refusal-plus-deflection response, so the outcome was not a one-off. By contrast, TechCrunch observed that ChatGPT answered the same kind of request with a bullet-list summary and citations to news articles.
