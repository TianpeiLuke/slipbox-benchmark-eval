# Google's Gemini multimodal claims outrun the model it actually shipped

Google's launch messaging for Gemini, described in TechCrunch's Week in Review on 2023-12-09, ran ahead of what was actually available. In a press briefing and blog posts, Google touted Gemini's coding capabilities and multimodal prowess, claiming that the model can understand images, audio and videos just as well as text. That claim is undercut by the product that shipped: Google did not release the full model, Gemini Ultra — only a "lite" version called Gemini Pro — and Gemini Pro is strictly text-in, text-out, and has proven mistake-prone. The gap between marketing and capability showed in the demo itself, where Google was caught faking a Gemini demo by tuning text prompts with still images off camera.
