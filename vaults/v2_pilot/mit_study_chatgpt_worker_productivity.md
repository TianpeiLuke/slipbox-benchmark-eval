# MIT study: ChatGPT cut task time 40 per cent, raised quality 18 per cent

A study by MIT researchers found that workers using ChatGPT completed tasks in 40 per cent less time than those who did not, while the quality of their output rose by 18 per cent. The tasks examined included drafting cover letters, writing delicate emails and producing cost-benefit analyses. The quality gains were assessed by third-party evaluators rather than by the participants themselves, who rated their own work more favourably still. The findings were reported by TechCrunch on 30 November 2023, a year after OpenAI released ChatGPT as a research preview.
