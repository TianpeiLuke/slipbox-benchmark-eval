# S&P 500 fell 4.9 per cent in September 2023, leaving 2023 up only 11.7 per cent

The S&P 500 dropped 4.9 per cent in September 2023, dragging what had been a big gain for the year down to 11.7 per cent, according to a Sydney Morning Herald report filed on 1 October 2023. Wall Street closed out its worst month and quarter of the year. Rising US Treasury yields were cited as a big reason: the 10-year returned to 4.58 per cent, near its highest level since 2007.
