# Why the FTX trial is bigger than Sam Bankman-Fried

The criminal trial of Sam Bankman-Fried, which began in late September 2023 as reported by The Verge on 2023-09-28, is likely to matter beyond whether the founder of FTX and Alameda Research is convicted, because the evidence entered in open court could be rough on the entire crypto industry. Bankman-Fried faces seven criminal counts: two counts of wire fraud and five counts of conspiracy charges. The reputational stakes, the article argues, lie in unpredictability rather than outcome — the industry must absorb whatever the government introduces. Yesha Yadav, a law professor at Vanderbilt University, framed it directly: "How much damage can this trial do to the already beaten-down reputation of the industry at this point?" Her answer: "This trial is going to be an excruciating moment for the industry because no one knows what kind of evidence might come out."
