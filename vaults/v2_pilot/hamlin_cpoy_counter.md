# The Availability Objection to Demar Hamlin as Comeback Player of the Year Favorite

A Sporting News article on NFL award longshots, published December 5, 2023, names Demar Hamlin the current favorite for Comeback Player of the Year while stating the objection to that position. Returning from a life-threatening injury like a cardiac arrest makes Hamlin "a great candidate," the piece grants, but it then concedes voters could look elsewhere, since Hamlin has seldom been active on game day and has rarely seen the field. The objection is that his on-field contribution is minimal beside an award meant to recognize a playing comeback. The article leaves the outcome resting on how vital voters deem playing to be.
