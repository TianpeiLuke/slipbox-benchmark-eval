# Bayern's 1-0 at Old Trafford on 12 December 2023 ended Manchester United's European season

Manchester United 0-1 Bayern Munich at Old Trafford on 12 December 2023, the final matchweek of the 2023/24 UEFA Champions League Group A: Kingsley Coman scored the only goal for Thomas Tuchel's side, in what the Sporting News described as "a limp display" from the hosts. The defeat left Manchester United on 4 points from six games — one win, one draw, four losses, goal difference -3 — bottom of Group A and out of Europe altogether, while Bayern Munich finished top with 16 points from five wins and no losses.
