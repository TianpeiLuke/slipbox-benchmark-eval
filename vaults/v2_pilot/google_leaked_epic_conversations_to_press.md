# Sweeney: Google leaked private Epic conversations to reporters, confirmed at trial

In a *The Verge* interview published 2023-12-12, Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeney said the evidence presented at the Epic v. Google antitrust trial confirmed practices he had suspected "since 2018 or so," when Epic first began confronting Google. One was Google leaking Epic–Google conversations to reporters in order to get negative stories written about Epic. Sweeney had been called a conspiracy theorist for believing it, and the four-week trial proved him right.
