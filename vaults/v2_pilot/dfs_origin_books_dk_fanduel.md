# DraftKings and FanDuel: DFS origins shape their sportsbooks

Sporting News on 2023-10-04 listed DraftKings Sportsbook and FanDuel Sportsbook among the best sportsbooks for NBA Rookie of the Year futures, tracing both to daily fantasy sports (DFS) operation before they became legal online sportsbooks. That lineage shows in DraftKings' variety of futures bets, intuitive app and competitive welcome bonus, and in FanDuel's one of the largest followings in the country, one of the best sports betting apps, and quality welcome bonus plus promotions for existing customers.
