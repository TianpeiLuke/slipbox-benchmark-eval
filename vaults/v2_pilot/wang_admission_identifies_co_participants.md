# Wang's courtroom admission named Bankman-Fried, Singh and Ellison in his FTX frauds

Gary Wang's account of his own FTX criminality reached past himself to named others. Asked by Assistant U.S. Attorney Nicolas Roos on Thursday 5 October 2023, "Did you commit financial crimes while working at FTX?", Wang answered "Yes," and said those crimes — wire and commodities fraud — were carried out with the help of Sam Bankman-Fried, FTX ex-engineering head Nishad Singh, and Caroline Ellison, who ran sister hedge fund Alameda Research. When Roos asked whether he saw any of those people in the courtroom, Wang stood, looked around, said "Yes," and identified "Sam Bankman-Fried." The argument's force is a sworn guilty plea, tempered by his six-page cooperation agreement and the up to 50 years in prison he still faces at sentencing.
