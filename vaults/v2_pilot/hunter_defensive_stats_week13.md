# Danielle Hunter Led the NFL in Tackles for Loss and Sat Third in Sacks Through Week 13 of 2023

Through Week 13 of the 2023 NFL season, Danielle Hunter led the league in tackles for loss and ranked third in sacks with 13.5, behind only Khalil Mack and T.J. Watt, according to Sporting News on December 5, 2023. Those figures anchored its case for Hunter as a Defensive Player of the Year longshot behind favorites Micah Parsons, Myles Garrett and Watt.
