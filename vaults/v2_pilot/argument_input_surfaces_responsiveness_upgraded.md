# Steam Deck OLED input feel: responsive but assessed briefly

In an Engadget review dated 2023-11-09, the Steam Deck OLED's controls are described as noticeably improved: the touchscreen "responds immediately to input," and the haptic feedback on the built-in trackpads "feels even more precise, populating in reactive pin-pricks under the textured plastic." The thumbsticks add improved texture that shouldn't gray with use, and sit 1mm taller than the original. The strength of this claim is the reviewer's own hands-on time, including switching between LCD and OLED units. Its limit is that the assessment stays brief and subjective — no measurements of latency, actuation force, or haptic response are offered.
