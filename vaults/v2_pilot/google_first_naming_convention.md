# Google-first (SEO-driven) business names

A Google-first or SEO-driven business name is a firm's legal or trading name chosen to match the literal phrase customers type into a search engine, instead of a distinctive brand term. The Verge's 2023-10-26 article "The restaurant nearest Google" names the pattern and its examples: the New York restaurant Thai Food Near Me, named after that exact query; Felix Silva's Coral Springs, Florida store Barber Shop Near Me, which he chose in 2019 from more than 20 alternatives; plus Affordable Dentist Near Me (a Texas chain of half a dozen), Antiques Near Me, seven Plumber Near Me businesses, Phone Repair Near Me in Cape Cod, Massachusetts, Psychic Near Me in Chicago, and more than 20 "Notary Near Me" iterations across the US. The logic, per owner Teddy Jirapraphanan: "When you have a million restaurants close by, you will be in the bottom [of rankings] if it's a random name." Optimized for digital discovery, the naming convention targets search results rather than passing foot traffic or word of mouth.
