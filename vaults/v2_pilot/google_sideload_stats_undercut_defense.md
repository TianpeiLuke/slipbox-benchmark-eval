# Google's Billion-User Sideloading Figure Counts a Setting, Not an Install

At the Epic v. Google trial's opening arguments, reported by The Verge on November 7, 2023, Google lead attorney Glenn Pomerantz boasted that "over a billion people have gone through the process Epic portrays as needlessly onerous to get apps outside the Play Store." The counterweight sits in the reporter's parenthetical qualification to that statistic: the billion-user figure measures people enabling a setting rather than users completing an install off Google's store. That distinction matters because Google deploys the number as proof that "Android users have a real choice" — flip a switch is a far weaker showing of a working competing channel than a billion sideloaded installs, and the gap is precisely what Epic attacks.
