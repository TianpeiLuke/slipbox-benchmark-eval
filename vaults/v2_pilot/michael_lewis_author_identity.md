# Michael Lewis, chronicler of financial adventure and misadventure

Michael Lewis is a writer described in a TechCrunch article of 2023-10-02 as the famed chronicler of financial adventure and misadventure. His earlier books include "Liar's Poker," "Moneyball" and "The Big Short." Here he is the author of "Going Infinite," a book on Sam Bankman-Fried and FTX, which he discussed in a 60 Minutes interview.
