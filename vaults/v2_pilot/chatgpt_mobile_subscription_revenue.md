# Data.ai: ChatGPT mobile apps generated nearly $30 million in subscription revenue within months of launch

Analytics firm Data.ai's data, cited by TechCrunch on November 30, 2023 (the one-year mark of ChatGPT's launch), showed that OpenAI's ChatGPT iOS and Android apps had generated nearly $30 million in subscription revenue. TechCrunch called the figure a hefty amount given that the apps had launched just a few months earlier.
