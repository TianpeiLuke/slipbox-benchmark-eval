# Sweeney: Google's trillion-dollar size was no guardrail

Tim Sweeney's reaction to the Epic v. Google verdict, published in The Verge on 12 December 2023, undercuts the very expectation he voices: that a firm of Google's scale necessarily runs respectable processes and leadership checks against misconduct. "It was really disconcerting to see the extent of bad faith efforts that were going on in a company of Google's size," he said of the assumption that "you'd think a trillion-dollar company" would restrain itself. What he listed instead was wrongdoing diffused through routine commercial channels, not policed by them: leaking Epic's conversations to reporters to prompt negative stories; paying other developers off so they would not launch their own stores; secretly paying carriers and OEMs not to carry competing stores. When Epic tried to bundle Fortnite with makers such as OnePlus and with "carriers of all sorts", they said Google had already done a secret deal with them.
