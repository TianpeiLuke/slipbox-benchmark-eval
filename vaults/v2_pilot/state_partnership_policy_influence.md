# Hypothesis: Meta's government agency partnerships bias moderation against Palestinian expression

A hypothesis drawn from a TechCrunch article published 2023-10-19: advocacy groups have raised concerns that Meta's partnerships with government agencies, such as the Israeli Cyber Unit, politically influence the platform's policy decisions. Extending that concern into a testable claim, the partnerships shift content-policy outcomes against Palestinian expression — the same user group reported for posting Palestine-related content regardless of whether it violates Meta's policies, and restricted from features such as posting or commenting for 24 hours.

The hypothesis is falsified if removal and restriction rates for comparable Palestine-related content show no association with escalation in those agency channels: more reports or referrals routed through partner agencies, but unchanged enforcement rates, would sever the claimed link.

Scope: Meta's platforms, notably Instagram, in the period following Hamas' attack on Israelis and Israel's retaliatory airstrikes and total blockade of Gaza.
