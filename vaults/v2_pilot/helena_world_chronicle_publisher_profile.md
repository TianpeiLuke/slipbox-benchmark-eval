# Helena World Chronicle, Arkansas Weekly Newspaper Publisher and Google Antitrust Plaintiff

Helena World Chronicle is a publisher based in Arkansas that owns and publishes two weekly newspapers in that state. It is the named plaintiff in the class action antitrust lawsuit filed this week — in the news reports of 2023-12-15 — in the U.S. District Court in D.C. against Google and parent company Alphabet, which it says is "starving the free press" and costing publishers "billions of dollars".
