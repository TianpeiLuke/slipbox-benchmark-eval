# How a character-reference meeting turned into Michael Lewis's "Going Infinite" (early 2021 onward)

Michael Lewis did not set out to write a book about Sam Bankman-Fried. "Going Infinite" began as a favor, in a sequence Lewis described in a 60 Minutes interview broadcast 2023-10-01:

1. In early 2021, a friend of Lewis's — both men lived in Berkeley — was considering backing FTX, the crypto exchange Bankman-Fried founded and led.
2. Before any financial commitment, that friend asked Lewis to meet the young billionaire in person to "evaluate his character."
3. Lewis met him and came away persuaded of his brilliance instead: "Eighteen months earlier, he had nothing. Now he had $22.5 billion. He was the richest person in the world under 30 [and] he was going to spend it to save humanity from extinction. My jaw was on the floor."
4. That same day Lewis decided to write about him — "another investment, of sorts."
5. The two went on to meet more than 100 times over the next two years.

The resulting book was set to debut on the first day of Bankman-Fried's trial in Manhattan Federal Court. Lewis did not identify the Berkeley friend, nor say whether that investment was ever made.
