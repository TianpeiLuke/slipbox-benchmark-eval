# Live Odds Feeds Are the Easiest Line-Shopping Method

Sporting News (2023-11-06) names a live odds feed as the easiest way to shop lines in US sports betting. Such a feed displays the differences in lines and odds for the same bet across many of the top sportsbooks in the country in one place — a "one-stop shop" where comparing prices takes just seconds rather than manually hopping between apps. The feed works for moneylines, point spreads, totals and prop bets. The trade-off: manual shopping across multiple downloaded sportsbook apps takes longer but can reach more books, or the few that consistently offer the best lines and odds.
