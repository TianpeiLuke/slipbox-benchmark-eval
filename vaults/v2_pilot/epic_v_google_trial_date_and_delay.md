# Epic v. Google reached trial 1,180 days after Epic first sued

Epic v. Google went to trial on 2023-11-06, "a mere 1,180 days after Epic originally sued," according to The Verge's 2023-11-05 explainer. The delay is the comparison: Epic's parallel antitrust suit against Apple — both filed after the 2020-08-13 Fortnite hotfix and the app stores' removal of the game — went to court in 2021, with a ruling that September mostly in Apple's favor. The Google case was "delayed again and again" until late 2023.
