# Google's Top-20 Revenue Search Terms, Week of September 22, 2018

In the US v Google antitrust trial, Judge Amit Mehta ruled to make public a list of the 20 search terms that earned Google the most revenue during the week of September 22, 2018. Ordered by revenue, the terms were: iPhone 8, iPhone 8 plus, Auto insurance, Car insurance, Cheap flights, Car insurance quotes, DirecTV, Online colleges, AT&T, Hulu, iPhone, Uber, Spectrum, Comcast, Xfinity, Insurance quotes, Free credit report, Cheap car insurance, AARP, LifeLock. Redacted were revenue per search term, query counts per term, and a separate list ordered by queries rather than revenue. TechCrunch dated the report November 13, 2023 and noted its own date slip, tying iPhone 8 revenue to the device's release on September 22, 2017.
