# Google calls Helena World Chronicle antitrust suit "meritless"

A Google spokesperson rejected the Helena World Chronicle class action filed this week in the U.S. District Court in D.C. (TechCrunch, 2023-12-15) as "meritless," denying the complaint's central allegations — that Google breaks the Sherman Act by "siphoning off" publishers' content, readers and ad revenue, and that AI products including Search Generative Experience and Bard worsen the harm.
