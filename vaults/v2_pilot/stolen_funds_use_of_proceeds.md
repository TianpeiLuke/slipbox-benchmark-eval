# Rehn's Opening: Three Alleged Destinations for FTX Customer Funds

In his roughly 30-minute opening statement on Oct. 4, 2023, prosecutor Thane Rehn told the jury that Sam Bankman-Fried allegedly stole FTX customer funds for three purposes: to facilitate his jet-setting lifestyle, to donate millions of dollars to political candidates, and to finance risky bets made through Alameda Research, the crypto hedge fund he also owned.
