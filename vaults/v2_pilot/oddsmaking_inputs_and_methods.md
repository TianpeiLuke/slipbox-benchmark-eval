# Sportsbook Opening Lines Come From Computer Models Run by Oddsmakers

According to Sporting News (2023-11-06), the best sportsbooks employ oddsmakers and dedicated oddsmaking services to produce their lines and odds. Those oddsmakers and oddsmaking services run computer models and algorithms that weigh analytics, statistics, injury and roster news, performance, and matchup data, among other inputs, to develop the lines and odds before betting opens. Not every operator does this work itself: other sports betting apps simply copy the lines that the oddsmakers and oddsmaking services set. The models' inputs are therefore the origin point for the prices that later move on public betting.
