# Cue's "Just Critical Stuff" Argument Against a Search Chooser at iPhone Setup

In his testimony in *US v. Google*, reported by The Verge on 2023-09-26, Eddy Cue — who negotiated Apple's current Google Information Services Agreement (ISA) with Sundar Pichai in 2016 — conceded that the ISA didn't allow Apple to offer users a choice of search engines during setup, and said he wouldn't want to do that anyway. His reasoning: "We try to get people up and running as fast as possible. Setup is just critical stuff." Showing people a bunch of search engines they've never heard of would be a bad user experience, and the source notes that Cue couldn't remember the names of some alternatives to Google. "We make Google be the default search engine," he said, "because we've always thought it was the best. We pick the best one and let users easily change it." The claim is scoped to first-boot setup, not to settings changes made later; DOJ lawyer Meagan Bellshaw countered that Apple already offers users a choice about lots of things, big and small, during setup.
