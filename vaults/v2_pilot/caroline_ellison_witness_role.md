# Caroline Ellison: Alameda Research head and prosecution's star witness in the FTX trial

Caroline Ellison ran Alameda Research, the sister hedge fund of the crypto exchange FTX, and had been a girlfriend of FTX founder Sam Bankman-Fried. Per a CNBC report dated 2023-10-06 covering Bankman-Fried's fraud trial, she and FTX co-founder Gary Wang were integral members of Bankman-Fried's personal and professional inner circles, and both pleaded guilty in December 2022. Ellison is considered the prosecution's star witness and was expected to take the stand under a cooperation arrangement similar to Wang's.
