# ChatGPT as priority number one and development platform inside OpenAI

In TechCrunch's 2023-11-30 account of ChatGPT's first anniversary, the term "ChatGPT" inside OpenAI had come to mean priority number one — not simply a one-off product but a development platform to build upon. That reframing showed up in the features layered onto the original chat interface on GPT-3.5: paid tiers including an enterprise plan, web searching, document analyzing, image creation via DALL-E 3, and the ability to "hear," "speak," "see" and take actions.
