# Sam Bankman-Fried's FTX trial began with jury selection on 3 October 2023

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-06 that the trial of Sam Bankman-Fried, former CEO and co-founder of the collapsed crypto exchange FTX, kicked off with jury selection on October 3. The case against him involves seven counts tied to fraud and money laundering at FTX and sister company Alameda. Courtroom access is limited, with no devices allowed.
