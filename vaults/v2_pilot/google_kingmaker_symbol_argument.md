# "Thai Food Near Me" as Google's Quiet Part, Spoken Out Loud

The central argument of The Verge's 2023-10-26 profile of the New York restaurant Thai Food Near Me is that the name is "a small but powerful symbol of Google's far-reaching impact on businesses over the past two decades and the lengths their owners will go to try to optimize their operations for the company's platforms." The restaurant is named after a literal Google search query and is optimized for the digital platforms diners use to find places nearby, not for the person walking past on the street or getting a recommendation from a friend.

The reasoning is circular by design, and that is its force: the name is "both notable and obvious," because anyone who has spent time searching online understands the reference immediately. The turn the article claims is that 25 years after Google Search first arrived, the name "says the quiet part out loud."
