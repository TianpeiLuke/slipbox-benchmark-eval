# FTX CEO John J. Ray III told Congress the exchange had "virtually no internal controls"

Asked at a House Financial Services Committee hearing whether FTX had significant risk management systems, John J. Ray III — the Enron turnaround veteran appointed CEO after FTX filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in the U.S. in mid-November 2022 — said "there were virtually no internal controls and no separateness whatsoever," and added that he did not "trust a single piece of paper" in the exchange's organization. Reported by TechCrunch on 2023-10-01.
