# Palestinian Journalist Leen Al Saadi's Instagram Restricted in 2022 After Podcast Trailer

In 2022, Leen Al Saadi, a Palestinian journalist based in Jordan, had her Instagram account restricted after she posted a trailer for the debut episode of the "Preserving Palestine" podcast, which covered a documentary about Palestinian street art under occupation. TechCrunch reported the episode on Oct. 19, 2023, among a series of Meta moderation incidents, as an example of Palestinian users' content being limited even when it does not support Hamas.
