# Defence's counter: Alameda's risk failure lay with CEO Caroline Ellison, not Bankman-Fried's design

Countering the prosecution's Alameda-centred account, Mark Cohen, one of Sam Bankman-Fried's lawyers, told the jury on Oct. 4, 2023 at the FTX founder's trial that Caroline Ellison, the former chief executive of Alameda Research, did not adequately protect her hedge fund from the inherent risk of the crypto markets. Alameda, he said, was a successful hedge fund, not a subterranean or shady front, and business practices between it and FTX were reasonable; Alameda acted legally as an FTX customer, payment processor and market maker. Locating the failure in Ellison's risk management, rather than in Bankman-Fried's design, Cohen framed working at a startup as "like building a plane as you're flying it," adding that businesses sometimes fail.
