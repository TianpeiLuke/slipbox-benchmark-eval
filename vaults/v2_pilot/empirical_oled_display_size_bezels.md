# Steam Deck OLED's 7.4-inch HDR Display Grows Visible Area via Smaller Bezels

Valve's Steam Deck OLED, reviewed by Engadget on 2023-11-09, replaces the original LCD with an OLED panel whose main upgrades are native support for HDR and faster frame rates. That OLED also has smaller bezels, making the display larger at 7.4 inches, compared with the original 7-inch screen. The OLED model tops out at 90Hz, while the original could only hit 60Hz, though the new display doesn't support variable refresh rates (VRR). The review notes the wider P3 color gamut, pure blacks, and a peak brightness of 1,000 nits versus the original's max of 400 nits — and observes that because of the black of any game, the screen's bezels meld with it, creating a more polished frame for retro aspect ratios. Physically, the device keeps the original's form factor at 11.7 inches long, 4.5 inches tall and nearly 2 inches thick.
