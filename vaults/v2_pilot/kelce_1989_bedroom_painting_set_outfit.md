# Fans Decode Kelce's Post-Game Outfit as Swift Reference at Arrowhead

After Taylor Swift's attendance at Arrowhead Stadium on Monday — the sighting reported on 26 September 2023 by The Age — extra observant fans noted that Travis Kelce's blue and white post-game outfit was supposedly labelled the "1989 bedroom painting set". That designation was read as perhaps an intentional reference to Swift's 2014 album, 1989, which she was set to re-release in October. The reading was spectators' inference, not anything Kelce or Swift stated.
