# Google's rebuttal to Megan Gray's Wired query-rewrite allegation

In October 2023, in response to the Wired opinion article by lawyer and privacy advocate Megan Gray — which alleged, on the example of a search for "children's clothing" being replaced with "NIKOLAI-brand kidswear", that Google manipulates people's search queries for maximum ad revenue — Google rejected the piece in very strong terms, calling it misleading and inaccurate while denying ever altering search terms. Wired removed the article for not meeting its standards.
