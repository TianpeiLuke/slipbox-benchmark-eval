# S&P 500 energy stocks stayed the market's best performers since summer 2023 despite a 2 per cent drop on 29 September 2023

Energy stocks in the S&P 500 fell 2 per cent as a group on Friday 29 September 2023, more than double the loss of any of the other 10 sectors in the index, after a barrel of US crude sank 92¢ to settle at $US90.79; ExxonMobil lost 1.6 per cent and Schlumberger 4.3 per cent. The Sydney Morning Herald reported on 1 October 2023 that the sector nevertheless remained the market's standout performers since the summer of 2023, with crude still up sharply from $US70 in June.
