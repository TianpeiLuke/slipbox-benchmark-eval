# FTX's listed assets rested on speculation, so the fall came hard

TechCrunch's 2023-10-02 piece on Sam Bankman-Fried draws one relation about FTX's crypto assets: they were underwritten by speculation and the promise of becoming worth more rather than by value already made, so once those valuations reversed the decline was abrupt and outsized. The numbers mark both poles. Michael Lewis, who met SBF in early 2021, says: "Eighteen months earlier, he had nothing. Now he had $22.5 billion." FTX raised some $2 billion, including $1 billion in 2021, from backers including Sequoia, Temasek and Tiger Global, and trades climbed to $15 billion/day. What followed is "the multibillion-dollar collapse of FTX", "one of the biggest financial frauds in American history", which many believed "brought down the whole crypto house".
