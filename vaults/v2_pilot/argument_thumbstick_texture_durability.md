# Valve's revised Steam Deck OLED thumbstick texture targets the original's graying problem

In its Steam Deck OLED review dated 2023-11-09, Engadget reports that "the thumbsticks on the new handheld have an improved texture that shouldn't turn gray after a few months' use." The claim is aimed squarely at a documented failure of the original Steam Deck's sticks, which grayed within months. But the sentence carries its own hedge — "shouldn't" — and Engadget offers no longevity data behind it. A materials improvement measured in months cannot be verified within a review cycle lasting days, so the fix remains asserted rather than proven at publication.
