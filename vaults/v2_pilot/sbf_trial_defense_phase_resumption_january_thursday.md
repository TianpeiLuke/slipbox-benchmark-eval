# FTX Trial Resumes Into Defense Phase With SBF Set to Testify, October 2023

The fraud and money-laundering trial of Sam Bankman-Fried, former CEO and co-founder of collapsed crypto exchange FTX, entered a new phase as proceedings resumed Thursday, having been confirmed during a teleconference Wednesday. Bankman-Fried will take the stand alongside a handful of other witnesses, with the onus shifting toward the defense after the prosecution brought its case to its conclusion. The trial had been on pause from October 20.
