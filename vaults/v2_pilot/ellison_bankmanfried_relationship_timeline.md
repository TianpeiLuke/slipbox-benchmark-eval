# Ellison put dates on three phases of her relationship with Bankman-Fried

On the first day of her testimony against former boss Sam Bankman-Fried, reported by The Verge on 2023-10-10, Caroline Ellison put specific dates on their personal relationship. She testified that the two slept together on and off from autumn 2018, while she was a trader at Alameda Research and Bankman-Fried was chief executive of FTX. After that, she described two discrete periods of dating: summer 2020 to summer 2021, which Ellison said the couple kept secret, and autumn 2021 to spring 2022, when the two lived together. Ellison had already pleaded guilty to criminal charges stemming from the relationship before taking the stand.
