# X's Post Volume: Yaccarino's 500 Million Contradicts Musk's 100–200 Million

A counter-argument sits inside TechCrunch's Week in Review of 2023-10-07 on X's publicly stated post volume. In September 2023, Elon Musk said X users were generating 100 million to 200 million posts every day, excluding retweets. Speaking at an event in the week of 7 October 2023, X CEO Linda Yaccarino offered a contradictory figure: she claimed X was seeing 500 million posts per day on the platform. TechCrunch runs the two numbers side by side under the headline "Musk comes clean about X's metrics — maybe" and declines to adjudicate, writing: "So who's right? Beats us." The discrepancy is recorded as unresolved rather than reconciled, and the qualification matters — Musk's range was explicitly stated to exclude retweets, so whether Yaccarino's 500 million shares that exclusion is not addressed. Neither figure is independently verified in the source.
