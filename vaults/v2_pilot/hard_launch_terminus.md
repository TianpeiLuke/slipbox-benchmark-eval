# Hard-Launch: The Deliberate Public Debut of a Couple

To "hard-launch" a relationship is to appear publicly as a couple for the first time, by design rather than by accident, thereby ending whatever speculation preceded it. In The Age's 26 September 2023 timeline of Taylor Swift's NFL takeover, the phrase asks whether Swift and Travis Kelce, on the day Kelce invited her to a game (announced 21 September 2023), were "finally about to hard-launch their friendship/potential romance to the world, laying all speculation to rest". The term distinguishes that intentional reveal from the ambiguous proximity before it: Kelce's New York trip during her 1989 re-recording, and September reports of "quietly hanging out" for months.
