# Why early Gemini Pro reports undercut Google's benchmark claim

Google's Gemini launch materials claimed the model meets or exceeds the performance of other leading gen AI models like OpenAI's GPT-4; posts from its first public week indicate Gemini Pro fell short of that in practice. The "lite" version, Gemini Pro, began rolling out to Bard on 2023-12-06, and within a day users on X (formerly Twitter) reported failures in unrelated categories: fact lookup (Gemini Pro named Brendan Gleeson Best Actor for 2023 rather than the actual winner Brendan Fraser, and told Science fiction author Charlie Stross he had contributed to the Linux kernel); word-form constraints (a seven-letter answer to a request for a 6-letter French word); news recency (a Ukraine war summary over a month out of date); and code generation (Filip Piekniewski's intersection of two polygons in Python, 2023-12-06; Edison Ade's Tic Tac Toe, 2023-12-06, which Bard finished on 3 tries against ChatGPT's first try). The warrant is independent reporting across task types, not one repeated complaint. Scope: anecdotal user reports rather than controlled evaluation, and Gemini Pro is not the most capable version of Gemini.
