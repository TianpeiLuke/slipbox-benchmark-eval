# Vocabulary definitions consumed the morning of Bankman-Fried's direct examination, 2023-10-28

On 2023-10-28, the first day of Sam Bankman-Fried's direct examination at his FTX trial, The Verge's reporter noted that "we spent a lot of the morning explaining vocabulary." Defining "Amazon Web Services" and "database" for the jury struck the reporter as "a bit too detail-oriented," so the full list went unreported. Bankman-Fried then offered his own definition of "market manipulation"; Judge Lewis Kaplan told the jury he was the final authority on that.
