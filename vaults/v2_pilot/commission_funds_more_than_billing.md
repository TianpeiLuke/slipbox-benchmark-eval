# Google argues its Play commission pays for more than billing

In Epic Games' antitrust trial against Google, which began November 6, 2023, Google contests the framing that the 15% to 30% commission it charges app developers on the sales it processes through Google Play's required first-party billing system is payment-processing markup. Its argument is that the commission instead funds other features and controls that keep Android users safe and guide them toward discovering new applications, plus the tools and services developers need to build and grow their apps. Google claims credit for reach beyond the mobile phone too: Android support across platforms, ranging from tablets to TVs to autos and more. It narrows the burden by noting that 99% of its developers qualify for a commission of 15% or less, based on their revenues.
