# FTX and Alameda Research filed Chapter 11 in mid-November 2022; John J. Ray III became CEO

In mid-November 2022, the crypto exchange FTX and the trading firm Alameda Research filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in the U.S. Sam Bankman-Fried resigned, and John J. Ray III — described as an Enron turnaround veteran — was appointed FTX's new CEO, according to TechCrunch's Oct 1, 2023 recap of the collapse.
