# FTX Founded as a Complement to Alameda Research

Sam Bankman-Fried co-founded crypto-trading firm Alameda Research in 2017, then co-founded FTX in 2019 as a complement to Alameda, to help bring in revenue and liquidity for the trading arm (TechCrunch, 2023-10-01).
