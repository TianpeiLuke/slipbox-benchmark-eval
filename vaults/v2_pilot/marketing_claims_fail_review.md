# 2024 ROI Reviews Will Find Hyped Machine-Learning Purchases Wanting

Hypothesis from TechCrunch's "8 predictions for AI in 2024" (published 2023-12-19): the hype built up in 2023 will be very hard for companies to follow through on. Machine-learning systems that enterprises adopted specifically in order to not fall behind carry vendor marketing claims, and those claims will face their quarterly and yearly reviews during 2024 — where, the authors expect, they will very likely be found wanting. The mechanism is reassessment, not a sudden drop in capability.

Falsified if enterprise reassessments broadly validate the vendors' 2023 claims and the purchased ML features pass their quarterly and yearly ROI reviews.
