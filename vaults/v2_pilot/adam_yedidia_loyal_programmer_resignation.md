# Adam Yedidia Testified Under Immunity, Having Texted "I Love You Sam" Before Resigning

Adam Yedidia, college roommate of FTX founder Sam Bankman-Fried, testified at Bankman-Fried's fraud trial with immunity because Yedidia was concerned he may have unknowingly worked on code that contributed to the fraud. The Verge's report of 2023-10-26 lists Yedidia first among the people who "loved Bankman-Fried most in the world" and are now caught up in the case. Before the break, Yedidia texted Bankman-Fried: "I love you Sam," followed by "I'm not going anywhere." Those messages were sent before Yedidia realized FTX had stolen customer funds — at which point, Yedidia resigned.
