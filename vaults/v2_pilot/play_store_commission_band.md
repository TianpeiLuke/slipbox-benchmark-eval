# Google Play charges app developers a 15% to 30% commission on processed sales

As of November 2023, Google required apps to use its own first-party billing system in Google Play and charged app developers a 15% to 30% commission on the sales it processed. TechCrunch recorded that rate on November 6, 2023, the day Epic Games' antitrust trial against Google opened.
