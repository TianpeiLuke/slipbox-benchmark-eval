# FTX bug overestimated Alameda's debt to FTX by about $8 billion

An FTX software bug overestimated the amount that Alameda Research owed to FTX by about $8 billion, according to testimony at Sam Bankman-Fried's wire-fraud trial reported by The Verge on 2023-10-31. FTX employee Adam Yedidia — Bankman-Fried's college friend, Bahamas roommate, and subordinate — fixed the bug in June 2022. Even after that correction, Alameda still owed FTX about $8 billion. Bankman-Fried said he learned of the fiat@ftx account, which tracked Alameda's debt to FTX, in June 2022 while senior staff were focused on fixing the bug, and did not learn what the account was for until October.
