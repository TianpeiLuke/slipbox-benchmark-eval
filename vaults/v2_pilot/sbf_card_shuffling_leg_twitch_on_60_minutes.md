# 60 Minutes' Behind-the-Scenes Shot of SBF Shuffling Cards as Lewis Took Notes

A 60 Minutes segment broadcast on 2023-10-02, described in TechCrunch's coverage of that report, showed Sam Bankman-Fried talking at his desk while shuffling playing cards, his leg twitching nervously, as Michael Lewis dashed off notes on a yellow legal pad with a flourish of his pen and a look of concentration. TechCrunch notes the reason for the card shuffling is never explained.
