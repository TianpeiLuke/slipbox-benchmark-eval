# Lithium at two-year lows on 1 October 2023 as Liontown bucks the slide

Lithium prices had dropped to their lowest level in two years as at 1 October 2023, reported The Sydney Morning Herald. Yet the ASX-listed lithium miner Liontown resisted that downward momentum in the sector's prices, according to the paper's "lowdown" on the local bourse that morning. The divergence stood out amid the same session's iron ore strength, which lifted iron ore heavyweights after that commodity advanced 1.4 per cent overnight.
