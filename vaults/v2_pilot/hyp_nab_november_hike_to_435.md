# NAB's forecast of an RBA hike to 4.35 per cent in November 2023

In a report covered by The Sydney Morning Herald on 2023-10-01, the National Australia Bank forecasts the Reserve Bank of Australia will lift the cash rate again in November 2023, to 4.35 per cent. The forecast sits against the market expectation cited in the same article that the RBA will keep rates on hold at its October meeting, with interest-rate sensitive sectors such as healthcare and consumer companies among the weakest on that view.

The proposition is falsifiable by the RBA's November 2023 decision: either the cash rate moves to 4.35 per cent, or it does not.
