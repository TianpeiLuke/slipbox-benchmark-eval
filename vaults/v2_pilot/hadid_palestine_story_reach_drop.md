# Bella Hadid's 2022 Instagram Story Alleging Palestine-Only Posting Restrictions and a Near-Million View Drop

In an Instagram Story in 2022, Bella Hadid, a supermodel, activist, and person of Palestinian descent, alleged that Instagram "disabled" her from posting content on her Story "pretty much only when it is Palestine based." She said she gets "immediately shadowbanned" when she posts about Palestine, and that her Story views drop by "almost 1 million." The TechCrunch report of 2023-10-19 cited her account as evidence that the belief Meta shadowbans Palestine-related content predates the October 2023 Gaza escalation.
