# FTX funding made Bankman-Fried's parents and brother politically undesirable

Sam Bankman-Fried's family lost the political access FTX money had once bought them, according to The Verge's account of his fraud trial on 2023-10-26. Joe Bankman and Barbara Fried — whose political action committee, Mind the Gap, was among the entities to receive FTX customer money as political donations, and who an FTX bankruptcy estate lawsuit alleges were given $10 million of that money — and Gabe Bankman-Fried, whose nonprofit Guarding Against Pandemics received millions from FTX, are described as having tarnished reputations. In the political arenas where they once operated, the article writes, they are suddenly undesirable.
