# Fed policy rate at highest since 2001 as 2024 cut expectations trimmed

As at the end of September 2023, the Federal Reserve's main interest rate stood at its highest level since 2001, and the central bank indicated the week before it may cut interest rates in 2024 by less than it had earlier expected. Wall Street had by then accepted "a new normal" of rates staying high for longer, as the Fed pushes still-high inflation down to its target.
