# FTX investor funds bought a Bahamas house for Joe Bankman and Barbara Fried

According to a financial expert called by the prosecution at Sam Bankman-Fried's trial, Joe Bankman — Bankman-Fried's father — and Barbara Fried lived together in a Bahamas house purchased for them using FTX investor funds. In a separate lawsuit filed by FTX's bankruptcy estate, Bankman and Fried are alleged to have been given $10 million of FTX customer funds. The Verge reported this on 2023-10-26, near the end of the prosecution's case, naming the pair among those closest to Bankman-Fried who were caught up in the proceedings.
