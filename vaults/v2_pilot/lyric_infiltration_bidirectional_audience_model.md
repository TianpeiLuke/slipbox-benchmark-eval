# Swift Lyric Titles Invading NFL Commentary, September 2023: Football Fans and Swifties Collide

On September 17, 2023, Travis Kelce scored a touchdown against the Jaguars and CBS announcer Ian Eagle called it "Kelce finds a blank space for the score," borrowing Swift's 2014 hit *Blank Space* to poke fun at Kelce's newfound Swiftie status. NFL Network's Rich Eisen added a slew of lyric references to a preshow — "shake it off," "anti-hero" and "all too well" — and Kelce answered the Instagram montage with "Well played Rich … Well played." Suddenly, football fans and Swifties were living through a surprising collision: Swift's lyrics were infiltrating NFL game commentary while her fans were furiously becoming football experts. All that needed to happen was for Swift herself to infiltrate a game, settling the rumours once and for all.
