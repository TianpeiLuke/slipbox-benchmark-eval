# A Placed Bet Locks In the Odds or Line Posted at Wagering

Sporting News (2023-11-06), in its guide to line shopping, states the rule in one sentence: "When you bet, you lock in the odds or line as currently posted." A ticket is priced at the moment it is accepted. Subsequent moves — a spread shifting because the public bet heavily on one side, or odds changing on fresh injury and roster news — apply to bets still to come, not to the wager already placed, whether those later changes would have helped or hurt.
