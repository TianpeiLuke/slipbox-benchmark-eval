# Prosecution's claim beyond diversion: concealed money flows and forged documents

In the opening statement reported by Fortune on 2023-10-04, prosecutor Thane Rehn pushed the government's case past the taking of FTX customer funds: Bankman-Fried allegedly directed employees to conceal the flow of money into FTX's coffers and forged financial documents distributed to lenders and investors. "The defendant lied to the world," Rehn alleged.
