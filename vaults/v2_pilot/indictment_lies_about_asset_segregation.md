# August 14, 2023 Superseding Indictment Calls SBF's Conflict-of-Interest Statements Lies

In a superseding indictment filed August 14, 2023, federal prosecutors alleged that Sam Bankman-Fried lied about consumer protection at the collapsed cryptocurrency exchange FTX. Specifically, the indictment states that Bankman-Fried's public claims that FTX was "avoiding or managing conflicts of interest" and that "as a general principle FTX segregates customer assets from its own assets across our platforms" were lies. The filing was made in the Southern District of New York case ahead of his fraud trial, as reported by The Verge on September 28, 2023.
