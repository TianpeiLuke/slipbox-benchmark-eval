# FTX raised roughly $2 billion, including $1 billion in 2021

Before FTX collapsed, the crypto exchange founded and led by Sam Bankman-Fried (SBF) had raised approximately $2 billion in total from investors, including $1 billion raised in 2021 alone. Backers named in TechCrunch's reporting on 2023-10-02 included Sequoia, Temasek and Tiger Global, among dozens of others.
