# Gatekeeper Commission Tax Model: Higher Consumer Prices and Unviable Product Categories

In a The Verge interview published 2023-12-12, after a federal jury found that Google holds an illegal monopoly over Android app distribution and in-app payment systems, Epic Games CEO and controlling shareholder Tim Sweeney described the market structure he was challenging. He called for change in an "industry which is being strangled by a few gatekeepers imposing insane amounts of control and extracting huge taxes." The model: concentration of distribution and billing power in a few hands acts as a tax levy. That tax has two linked effects, in Sweeney's account — it "raise[s] prices for consumers" and it makes "a lot of kinds of products just unviable," since categories whose margins cannot absorb the levy never get built.
