# SEO: the practice of making a business findable in search results

SEO (search engine optimization) is the practice of making a business, website, or content more findable in search engine results. *The Verge* gave that definition on 2023-10-26 to explain the name of Thai Food Near Me, a New York restaurant whose four owners, including Teddy Jirapraphanan, chose a literal Google search phrase as their brand, betting on the power of SEO to bring customers in. The restaurant is optimized for the digital platforms diners use to find places nearby, not for the person walking past on the street or getting a recommendation from a friend.
