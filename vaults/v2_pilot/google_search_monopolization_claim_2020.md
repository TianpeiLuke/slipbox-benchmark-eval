# Google Search Antitrust Trial: the 2020 Dominance Claim

The U.S. antitrust trial being heard in October 2023 is the 2020 claim over Google's dominance in the online search market, in which Google CEO Sundar Pichai testified on the Monday covered by TechCrunch's Oct. 31, 2023 report, defending the deals that make Google the default search engine. It is distinct from the separate claim filed in January 2023 by the Department of Justice with eight states, including New York, California and Colorado, which seeks to break Google's monopoly in digital advertising, not search.
