# Ellison's Courtroom Entrance and Identification of Bankman-Fried, October 10, 2023

On the first day of her testimony in Sam Bankman-Fried's criminal fraud trial, reported by The Verge on 2023-10-10, Caroline Ellison — former head of Alameda Research — walked into the courtroom hunched in on herself, wearing a dusty rose dress with a gray blazer over it. When the prosecution asked her to identify Bankman-Fried, she had trouble finding him and gazed around the courtroom for more than 20 seconds. The report attributes this to his being "incognito with his new haircut." After spotting him, she identified him as wearing a suit — which drew chuckles from the rest of the defense table, also all in suits.
