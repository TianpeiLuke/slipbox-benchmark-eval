# Wang's testimony: FTX platform code as the mechanism behind Alameda's access

Gary Wang's testimony at Sam Bankman-Fried's fraud trial in a Manhattan courtroom on Thursday, Oct. 5 and Friday, Oct. 6, 2023, put software at the center of the exposure: code baked into FTX's exchange platform gave sister hedge fund Alameda Research special access, including unlimited withdrawals on the exchange. Wang, 30, was technology chief for FTX, and those privileges, held while he ran the technology, are what he said allowed Alameda to withdraw and transfer funds derived from customers. Wang, who pleaded guilty in December and testified under a six-page cooperation agreement, faces up to 50 years in prison.
