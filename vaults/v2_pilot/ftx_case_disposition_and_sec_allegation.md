# FTX: the cryptocurrency exchange whose founder was convicted on seven counts

FTX is the failed cryptocurrency exchange founded by Sam Bankman-Fried, who was found guilty on seven counts including charges of wire fraud, as reported by The Verge on 2023-10-28 while Bankman-Fried was testifying at his own trial. The Securities and Exchange Commission alleged that FTX was a fraud "from the start," with a "multi-billion-dollar deficiency caused by his own misappropriation of customer funds." Bankman-Fried, who started his cryptocurrency trading firm in 2017 and testified he expected only a 20 percent chance of success with the exchange, characterized not creating a risk team as a "big mistake."
