# FTX's Collapse Traced to Alameda Research Drawing on Customer Funds

In The Verge's 2023-10-26 account of Sam Bankman-Fried's trial, FTX collapsed as the result of Bankman-Fried's trading firm Alameda Research purloining the exchange's customers' funds. Caroline Ellison, Bankman-Fried's ex-girlfriend, testified that Alameda took customer funds and that Bankman-Fried knew about it and even invested them against her advice; she also described the seven phony balance sheets she had created. Gary Wang, co-founder of FTX and Alameda Research, testified about the secret privileges he had created for Alameda in the FTX code. Customers who had money trapped on the FTX exchange when it collapsed lost access to funds that should have been available at any time they wanted it, even if the bankruptcy proceedings later make them whole.
