# NFL MVP betting favors quarterbacks from top-seeded teams

The NFL MVP award often ends up going to a player from one of the league's top-seeded teams, and typically to a quarterback on such a team. That positional and team-success pattern explains why Brock Purdy, Dak Prescott and Jalen Hurts sat atop the MVP betting boards after Week 13 of the 2023 season, per Sporting News on December 5, 2023: each quarters for a 49ers, Cowboys or Eagles club seeded near the top of its conference. Longshots listed there were C.J. Stroud and Jared Goff.
