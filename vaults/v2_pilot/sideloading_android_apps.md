# Sideloading on Android and Why It Shapes Epic v. Google

Sideloading is installing Android apps from outside Google Play, the store Google runs. Android permits it, and that single fact separates Epic Games' antitrust case against Google, which went to trial on November 6, 2023, from Epic's earlier case against Apple over the App Store: against Apple, Epic alleged there was no other way to load apps; against Google, it cannot, because Android allows sideloading. Epic instead contends Google makes the process cumbersome, citing Anti-Fragmentation Agreements (AFAs) that prevent Android device makers from modifying the OS to allow frictionless downloading outside Google Play.
