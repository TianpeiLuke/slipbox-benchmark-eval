# Meta's "bug" explanation cannot account for a multi-year pattern of restrictions on Palestinian content

Meta attributed the October 2023 Instagram shadowbanning complaints to a bug affecting all Stories that re-shared Reels and Feed posts. That single-incident technical account doesn't fit the pattern TechCrunch described on Oct. 19, 2023: the accusations were "the latest in a lengthy history of incidents" on Meta platforms reflecting an inherent bias against Palestinian users, documented by years of complaints from both inside and outside the company, and involving restrictions on content that never supported Hamas. Meta's own commissioned third-party report, issued during the 2021 Hamas–Israel violence, concluded its actions had an "adverse human rights impact" on Palestinian users' freedom of expression and political participation. Meta also blamed technical errors for removing posts about Palestine during that 2021 conflict.
