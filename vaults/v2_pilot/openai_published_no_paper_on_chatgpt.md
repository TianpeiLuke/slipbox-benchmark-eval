# OpenAI never published a research paper on ChatGPT itself

Stella Biderman, an AI researcher at Booz Allen Hamilton and the open research group EleutherAI, observes pointedly that OpenAI — despite having released dozens of research papers on its models — never published one on ChatGPT. She made that remark in a TechCrunch article dated November 30, 2023, marking ChatGPT's first anniversary, while stopping short of calling ChatGPT an AI breakthrough per se.
