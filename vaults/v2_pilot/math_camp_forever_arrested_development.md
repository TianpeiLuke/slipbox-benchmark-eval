# FTX Co-Conspirators Seemed Frozen in Bankman-Fried's "Math Camp Forever" World, Author Argues

Writing in The Verge on 2023-10-26, near the end of the prosecution's case in Sam Bankman-Fried's FTX trial — seven counts, two of wire fraud and five conspiracy charges — the author offers an argument about the witnesses: all the co-conspirators "seem oddly younger than their years, as though they got stuck in the 'math camp forever' environment Bankman-Fried created for them and simply failed to grow up." The claimed cause is Bankman-Fried's own milieu, and the effect is arrested development visible from the courtroom. Supporting evidence: Gary Wang, Nishad Singh and Caroline Ellison, the people closest to Bankman-Fried, are now felons whose lives are irrevocably changed.
