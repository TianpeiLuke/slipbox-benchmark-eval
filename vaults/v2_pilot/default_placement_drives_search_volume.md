# Default homepage placement causally raises search volume — Google's 2007 internal finding

Google documented internally, in 2007, that preselected placement on a user's browser homepage causally increases a search engine's query volume. Data from a 2007 Google product strategy meeting, presented at trial on October 28, 2023 by U.S. Justice Department lawyer Meagan Bellshaw to Google CEO Sundar Pichai, showed that when people changed their browser homepage to Google they performed 15% more Google searches, and when they switched away their Google searches dropped 27%. The meeting summary sent to Pichai and other Google leaders recorded that "Nitin argues that focusing on homepage market share is one of the most effective things we can do to make gains in search market share." The relation holds user inertia and switching effort constant: default placement itself supplies the volume. Reported by TechCrunch on October 31, 2023.
