# Kent Walker's defence of Google's search default status, 2023

Responding to the US trial on Google's blog, in a report published by The Age on 2023-10-22, Kent Walker, Google's president of global affairs, defended the company's position as the default search engine on three grounds. Users, browsers and device makers favoured Google's search product, he said, simply because it was the best in the business. Default status was not determinative, he argued: changing from Google Search to any other search provider was easy, and the company competes with many others on phones and PCs. "People don't use Google because they have to, they use it because they want to," he said. "This lawsuit simply ignores how intensely competitive and dynamic the technology industry is today."
