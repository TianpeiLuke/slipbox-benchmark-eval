# Wang testified FTX gave Alameda a $65 billion line of credit

On 6 October 2023, FTX co-founder Gary Wang testified in Sam Bankman-Fried's Manhattan fraud trial that Alameda Research was permitted to withdraw and transfer funds on FTX under a $65 billion line of credit.
