# Index: Anecdotal Gemini Pro failures in Bard reported 2023-12-07

TechCrunch's 2023-12-07 article "Early impressions of Google's Gemini aren't great" collects user reports from X and blog posts after Gemini Pro began rolling out to Bard on 2023-12-06. The failure reports cluster into five task classes:

- **Factual lookup** — 2023 Oscar winners answered wrongly by several users, differently each time
- **Confabulation** — Charlie Stross's blog post, including a false claim he contributed to the Linux kernel
- **Word-form and multilingual constraints** — French letter-count prompts (Benjamin Netter; the author's own run)
- **News summarization and recency** — refusals to comment; Ukraine war updates over a month out of date
- **Code generation** — polygon intersection, an HTML analog clock, Tic Tac Toe, a snake game

One further report, outside these classes, covers jailbreaking by Robust Intelligence.
