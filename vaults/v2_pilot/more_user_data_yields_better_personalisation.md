# Google's behavioural data as the personalisation engine across Search, mobile widgets and other services

In a search-engine comparison published in The Age on 2023-10-22, the author writes that the test "did remind me how much data Google collects — not just my searches but anything I say to a smart speaker, any search result or ad that I click." That accumulated behavioural data is the mechanism the article names for relevance: it "does also mean that Google (not just search but also mobile widgets and other services) personalises to me more effectively." The competing service in the same test, DuckDuckGo, collects less but "didn't perform as well and is harder to read." Personalisation in the article's account is therefore compounded across surfaces rather than confined to the search results page.
