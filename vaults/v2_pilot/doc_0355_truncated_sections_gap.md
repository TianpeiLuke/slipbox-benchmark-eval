# Verge's "Epic v. Google, explained" Is Flagged Outdated and Left Unfinished

The Verge's explainer "Epic v. Google, explained," published 2023-11-05 ahead of the trial that began November 6th, 2023, was revised on December 15th, 2023 with the banner: "It's basically over now. What happened? Read this, this, and this to get the whole picture." That banner replaces outcome reporting with three unlabeled links. The body below it still speaks in future tense and makes two promises it never keeps: the verdict and remedies, and the "juicy dirt on both companies" — teased with "More on that later!" and absent from the text.
