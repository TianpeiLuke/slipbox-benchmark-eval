# Google Knowledge Graph held 500 billion facts about 5 billion entities by 2020

Figures quoted in the Helena World Chronicle class action antitrust complaint against Google and parent Alphabet — filed in the U.S. District Court in D.C. and reported by TechCrunch on 2023-12-15 — put Google's Knowledge Graph, the database launched in May 2012 that supplies the "Knowledge Panel" beside search results, at 500 billion facts about 5 billion entities by 2020.
