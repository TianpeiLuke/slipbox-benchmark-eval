# Engadget's balance argument for the Steam Deck OLED: same footprint, better feel — for large hands only

Engadget's "In use" section (Steam Deck OLED review: It's just better, 2023-11-09) argues the Steam Deck OLED feels more balanced — less top-heavy, "slightly more manageable as a handheld" — despite an unchanged footprint: 11.7 inches long, 4.5 inches tall and nearly 2 inches thick, with thumbsticks actually 1mm taller than the first version. The supporting evidence is reduced mass, about 30 grams less than the original model or the equivalent of five quarters, plus internal redistribution: instead of collapsing the chassis around smaller hardware, Valve added a bigger battery and larger fan to its existing chassis. The warrant is the reviewer's subjective playtime, spending "a few cozy nights on my couch swapping between the LCD and OLED Steam Decks." The claim's scope excludes small hands — the reviewer has smaller-than-average hands and still calls the device "just massive."
