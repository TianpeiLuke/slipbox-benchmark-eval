# Equifax breach and marketing push as the explanation for LifeLock's search revenue rank

In a 2023-11-13 TechCrunch roundup of the US v. Google antitrust trial, "LifeLock" appears among the 20 search terms ordered by revenue for the week of September 22, 2018, a list Judge Amit Mehta ruled to make public. The explanation offered is twofold: the big Equifax data breach of 2017, described as a hot topic in September 2017, and LifeLock "making a big push to win business with people who wanted to buy identity theft protection." That attribution fails if "LifeLock" ranked comparably high in weeks preceding the breach, since the account depends on the breach creating the demand.
