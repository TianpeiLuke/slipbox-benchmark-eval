# Travis Kelce's 2016 "Kiss, Marry, Kill" Picks: Kiss Swift, Marry Perry, Kill Grande

In a 2016 segment with AfterBuzz TV, Travis Kelce — who helped bring the Kansas City Chiefs to Super Bowl victory in both 2020 and 2023 — played "Kiss, Marry, Kill". Given the options of Taylor Swift, Katy Perry and Ariana Grande, Kelce said he would kiss Swift, marry Perry and kill Grande. Reported by The Age on 2023-09-26, the clip resurfaced about six years later, setting tongues wagging as fans wondered how long the plan for the two to meet had been in the works.
