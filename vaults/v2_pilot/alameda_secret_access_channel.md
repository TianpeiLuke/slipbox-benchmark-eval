# The government's model of the Bankman-Fried scheme: Alameda as the conduit

In his opening statement on 2023-10-04, Fortune reported, prosecutor Thane Rehn identified Alameda Research, "a crypto hedge fund he also owned," as "the key to his alleged scheme." Using Caroline Ellison, his on-again off-again girlfriend and Alameda's CEO, as a front, Bankman-Fried had "secret access" to customer money — both cash and crypto.
