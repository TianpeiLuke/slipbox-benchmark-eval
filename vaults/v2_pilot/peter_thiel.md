# Peter Thiel: Facebook and PayPal Wealth, Patron of Singularity-Focused AI Research

Peter Thiel is a Silicon Valley tech investor whose wealth came from an early investment in Facebook and from his work with Elon Musk in the early days of PayPal, according to a profile of AI's founders published by The Age on 9 December 2023. The article says Thiel had developed a fascination with the singularity, a trope of science fiction describing the moment when intelligent technology can no longer be controlled, and that he acted as a funder worth courting: in 2010 Demis Hassabis, then a 34-year-old neuroscientist seeking money to build artificial general intelligence, attended a cocktail party at Thiel's San Francisco town house, having prepared for a year because he knew Thiel loved chess.
