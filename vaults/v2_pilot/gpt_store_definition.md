# GPT Store: OpenAI's Marketplace for User-Built, Monetizable Custom GPTs

The GPT store is an OpenAI marketplace where users can create and monetize their own custom versions of GPT. OpenAI announced it on November 6 at OpenAI DevDay, the company's first developer conference, as part of a slew of updates coming to GPT that also included GPT-4 Turbo (super-charged versions of GPT-4, OpenAI's latest language-writing model) and a multimodal API. TechCrunch reported the announcement in its continuously updated ChatGPT timeline, noting the DevDay news arrived before the leadership turmoil surrounding CEO and co-founder Sam Altman's firing and swift return.
