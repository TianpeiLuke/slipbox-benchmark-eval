# Kelce's moustache line implies contact with Swift the record has not granted

In The Age's 2023-09-26 timeline, Travis Kelce says of the moustache he grows every year in honour of Chiefs head coach Andy Reid: "We're not going to bring up Taylor Swift in this episode… But something tells me she's going to like it." The warrant for reading this as evidence of contact is the audience he names but never accounts for: in July, on New Heights, he reported the friendship bracelet with his number never reached her, and that "she doesn't meet anybody, or at least she didn't want to meet me." A confident prediction about a person he had not met therefore suggests prior contact, and a planned meeting. The status is suggestion, not proof.
