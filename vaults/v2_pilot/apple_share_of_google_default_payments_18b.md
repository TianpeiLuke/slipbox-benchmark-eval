# Apple took roughly $18 billion of Google's $26.3 billion in 2021 default payments

Of the $26.3 billion Google paid in 2021 to multiple browsers, phones and platforms — including Apple, Samsung and Mozilla — to be the default search engine, about $18 billion went directly to Apple, according to a New York Times report published earlier in October 2023. TechCrunch reported the figure on October 31, 2023, during the U.S. government's antitrust trial against Google; the Google–Apple default deal had been in place since 2003.
