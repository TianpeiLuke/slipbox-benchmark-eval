# OpenAI's December 2023 Axel Springer Deal Covered Both Model Training and Live ChatGPT Answers

In December 2023, OpenAI announced a content agreement with Axel Springer, the Berlin-based news publisher that owns Business Insider and Politico, which OpenAI framed as helping "provide people with new ways to access quality, real-time news content through our AI tools." The agreement had two distinct limbs. First, OpenAI would train its generative AI models on Axel Springer's journalism. Second, it would add recent Axel Springer-published articles to ChatGPT, so the reporting surfaces inside answers rather than only shaping the underlying model. That combination — a licensing deal that pays for a publisher's archive as training data while also giving that publisher's newest work privileged placement in a chatbot's responses — made the deal notable beyond the parties involved, and it was reported in TechCrunch's running ChatGPT timeline alongside other December 2023 OpenAI news, including the reopening of ChatGPT Plus subscriptions on December 13, 2023.
