# Contents index: TechCrunch's November 13, 2023 roundup of US v Google

This TechCrunch roundup of November 13, 2023 indexes five sections. First, why US v Google matters and the remedies at stake before Judge Amit Mehta: sharing APIs, bans on anticompetitive and exclusive deals with smartphone and computer manufacturers and wireless carriers, and surrendering data — the DOJ has said Google gets 16 times more data than Bing does everyday. Second, Google's concurrent antitrust matters: the Match Group settlement and the November 6 trial with Fortnite maker Epic Games over Google Play. Third, the declassified list of 20 search terms ordered by revenue for the week of September 22, 2018, and vertical search. Fourth, Jamie Rosenberg's November 8 testimony on MADA (11 pre-loaded Google apps) and RSA mechanics. Fifth, Expedia and Barry Diller on ad load.
