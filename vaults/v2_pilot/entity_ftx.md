# FTX: The Crypto Exchange at the Center of the Bankman-Fried Trial

FTX is the cryptocurrency exchange whose former chief executive, Sam Bankman-Fried, stood trial in October 2023 on allegations that he stole customer funds. In opening statements on the second day of the trial, prosecutor Thane Rehn told the jury that Bankman-Fried had repeatedly emphasized to FTX customers that "their money was safe and secure" but that "all of it, was built on lies". Defense lawyer Mark Cohen countered that "FTX was no Ponzi scheme" but a "very innovative, successful company", and that Bankman-Fried "didn't steal any money".
