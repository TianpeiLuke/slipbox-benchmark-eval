# Kaplan scolded Bankman-Fried for non-answers on two consecutive days at FTX trial

On two consecutive days at Sam Bankman-Fried's FTX trial, Judge Lewis Kaplan repeatedly scolded the defendant for not answering questions. Yesterday, during an evidentiary hearing, Kaplan scolded Bankman-Fried for not answering prosecutor Danielle Sassoon's questions on cross-examination. Today, 2023-10-28, Kaplan scolded Bankman-Fried for not answering his counsel Mark Cohen's questions on direct examination. Bankman-Fried also occasionally interrupted Cohen with "yes" and "yup." The repetition spans two distinct settings and two different examining lawyers, one adverse and one friendly.
