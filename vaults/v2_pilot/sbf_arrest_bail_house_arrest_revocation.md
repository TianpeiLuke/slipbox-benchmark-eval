# Sam Bankman-Fried's arrest, $250 million bail, and its revocation in August 2023

Sam Bankman-Fried, former CEO of the crypto exchange FTX, was arrested in December 2022 in the Bahamas, where FTX was based, then extradited to the U.S. to face criminal charges. He was released on a $250 million bail bond and remained under house arrest at his parents' home in Palo Alto. That release was revoked in August after he was accused of intimidating Caroline Ellison, Alameda Research's former CEO, by leaking her private diary, according to TechCrunch (2023-10-01).
