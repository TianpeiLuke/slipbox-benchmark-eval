# Index: "Sam Bankman-Fried didn't ask where the $8 billion went" (The Verge, Oct 31, 2023)

Navigation index for The Verge's October 31, 2023 courtroom report on the June 2022 thread of Sam Bankman-Fried's self-testimony at his fraud trial — seven counts of wire fraud and conspiracy. The decomposition entries attach to these parts of the article: the software bug overestimating Alameda's debt to FTX by about $8 billion, found by Adam Yedidia; Bankman-Fried's discovery of the fiat@ftx account in June 2022 but not its purpose until October; staff telling him "they were busy and I should stop asking questions because it was distracting"; his "no, no, and no" on directions, policies and measures protecting FTX customer money; Yedidia's padel tennis court exchange in June or July, and Judge Lewis Kaplan's intervention; and the Alameda balance sheet of June 13, 2022 listing the borrowed money as "ftx borrows."
