# NBA Rookie of the Year as a Futures Betting Market

The NBA Rookie of the Year award is the league's honour to its best first-year player, and it is offered to bettors as a futures market rather than a single-game wager, settling only once the season ends. Sporting News's 2023-10-04 guide notes it can be backed before the NBA regular season begins and throughout the year, with Victor Wembanyama the early favorite.
