# GPT-4 Turbo: OpenAI's "super-charged" GPT-4, announced at DevDay on Nov 6

GPT-4 Turbo is, in OpenAI's own description at its first developer conference, a "super-charged" version of GPT-4, the company's latest language-writing model. OpenAI named it on November 6 at OpenAI DevDay, in a batch of GPT updates that also included a multimodal API and a GPT store where users can create and monetize their own custom versions of GPT. According to TechCrunch's coverage of November 6, 2023 announcement, GPT-4 itself remains largely exclusive to paying ChatGPT users, and TechCrunch's December 2023 timeline records that the ChatGPT Plus subscription includes access to both GPT-4 and GPT-4 Turbo.
