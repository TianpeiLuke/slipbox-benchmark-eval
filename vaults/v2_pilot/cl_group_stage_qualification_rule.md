# Champions League Group Stage Advancement Model: Top Two Advance, Third Goes to Europa League

The UEFA Champions League group stage for the 2023/24 season ran on a fixed four-club, six-matchday format, and Sporting News's permutations coverage of Dec. 12, 2023 shows the reward structure at each tier. Finishing first or second earns one of the 16 places in the Round of 16 — Bayern Munich (16 points) and Copenhagen (8) in Group A, Arsenal (13) and PSV (9) in Group B. The third-placed club is transferred downward into the Europa League, as with Lens (8) in Group B, Braga (4) in Group C, and Benfica (4) in Group D. The fourth, bottom-placed club is eliminated from European competition entirely: Manchester United (4), Sevilla (2), Union Berlin (2) and RB Salzburg (4) all finished their groups pointless of any further European route.
