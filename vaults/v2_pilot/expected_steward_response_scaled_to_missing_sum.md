# A fund owner's reaction to missing billions would be proportional to the sum

In a hypothetical posted by The Verge on 2023-10-31, the reporter asked how she would react if, one fine June day, employees told her, "Hey, Liz, we have an accounting problem. We are missing several billion dollars." Her answer: a lot of shouting, plus having her assistant figure out which law enforcement agency to call immediately. The escalation scales with the amount. Misplacing $900 million is a five-alarm fire even for an institution the size of Citibank; misplacing several billion is kicking over a lantern in Chicago in 1871. So the absence of any such reaction is itself evidence about what the owner believed.
