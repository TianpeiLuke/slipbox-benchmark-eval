# Steam Deck OLED Wi-Fi hit 562 mbps, between the original Deck and a MacBook Pro

In an Engadget review published 2023-11-09, the Steam Deck OLED's new Wi-Fi 6E module and improved antennas reached a peak download speed of 562 mbps against an ordinary Wi-Fi 6 router. That result was roughly 10 percent faster than the original Steam Deck on the same network, and roughly 10 percent slower than a MacBook Pro tested on it. The comparison is with a Wi-Fi 6 router rather than a Wi-Fi 6E one, so the hardware's 6E capability was not being exercised by the test network.
