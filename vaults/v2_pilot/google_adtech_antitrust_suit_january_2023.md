# The DOJ's Separate Google Ad-Tech Lawsuit (January 2023)

Distinct from the online search monopoly case tried in 2023 is a separate antitrust suit the U.S. Department of Justice filed in January 2023 together with eight states, including New York, California and Colorado. That ad-tech action aims to "halt Google's anticompetitive scheme, unwind Google's monopolistic grip on the market and restore competition to digital advertising," according to TechCrunch's October 31, 2023 report on the search trial. The two cases therefore target different markets — search versus digital advertising — even though both stem from the government's broader claims about Google's platforms and partner deals blocking competition.
