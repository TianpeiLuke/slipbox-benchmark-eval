# fiat@ftx: the FTX account tracking Alameda Research's debt

"fiat@ftx" was the internal FTX account that tracked how much money Alameda Research owed to FTX. Sam Bankman-Fried testified that he learned of the account in June 2022, while his senior staff were focused on fixing a software bug that had overstated Alameda's debt to FTX by about $8 billion, but said he did not discover what the account was for until October.
