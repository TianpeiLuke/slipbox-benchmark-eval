# "Easter egg" as deniable planted reference in The Age's Swift–Kelce timeline

In The Age's account of Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce (published 2023-09-26), an "Easter egg" is a pop-cultural reference planted deliberately inside an artist's visible artefacts — an album title, a lyric, a garment — deniable enough to pass as coincidence, yet meant to be caught. The catching is the fans' labour: the paper credits "extra observant fans" and "amateur Swiftie sleuths on social media" with noticing that Kelce's blue and white post-game outfit was labelled the "1989 bedroom painting set", an apparent nod to Swift's 2014 album, which she will be re-releasing in October. The Age treats that decoding as evidence and attributes the habit to Swift, whose "penchant for Easter eggs must be rubbing off on the tight end player".
