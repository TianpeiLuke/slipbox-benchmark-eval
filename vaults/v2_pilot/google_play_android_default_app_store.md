# Google Play as the default Android app store

Google Play is Google's app store for the Android operating system, and it is the default channel through which the vast, vast majority of Android app installs occur. It pre-empts the alternatives: the Galaxy Store, which comes preinstalled on every Samsung phone, accounts for only a tiny sliver of Android installs. In The Verge's 2023-11-07 coverage of the Epic v. Google trial, Epic's opening argument treated that installed base itself — not a closed platform — as the measure of Google's power over Android distribution.
