# Meta Blocked Canadian News Access in August 2023 as Publisher Talks Stall

Meta began blocking news content for users in Canada in August 2023, in light of pressure to pay for the content under Canada's new arrangements with its government requiring large platforms to compensate Canadian media. As of TechCrunch's report of December 15, 2023 on the news publishers' antitrust suit against Google, Meta's own negotiations with Canadian publishers remained unresolved — a contrast to Google, which reached an agreement with the Canadian government the previous month.
