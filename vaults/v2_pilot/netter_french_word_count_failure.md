# Benjamin Netter's 2023-12-06 X Post: Gemini Pro in Bard Fails a Six-Letter French Word

On 2023-12-06 Benjamin Netter (@benjaminnetter) posted on X that Google's Gemini Pro, then newly running Bard, could not return a six-letter word in French when prompted for one, writing "FYI, Google Gemini is complete trash." TechCrunch reported the post on 2023-12-07 among early complaints about Gemini Pro's translation ability. Re-running Bard's prompt "Can you give me a 6-letters word in French?", TechCrunch got a seven-letter word.
