# GPT-4 Stayed Paywalled in ChatGPT but Free Through Bing Chat

As of TechCrunch's survey of ChatGPT on 2023-09-28, GPT-4 — OpenAI's latest language-writing model, described as able to write more naturally and fluently than previous models — remained largely exclusive to paying ChatGPT users. The notable exception was Microsoft: a reader could access GPT-4 for free through Microsoft's Bing Chat in the Microsoft Edge, Google Chrome, and Safari web browsers. That arrangement made Bing Chat, a consumer search product rather than an OpenAI subscription, one of the few no-cost routes to the model that ChatGPT itself held behind its paid tier. All other ChatGPT-side developments in the same passage came later — connecting ChatGPT to the internet for all users and integrating DALL-E 3 for text and image generation — and neither added free GPT-4 access inside ChatGPT.
