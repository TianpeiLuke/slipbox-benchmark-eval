# Russell Wilson as the Comeback Player of the Year longshot (Sporting News, Dec. 5, 2023)

A Sporting News awards-odds piece dated December 5, 2023 names Demar Hamlin the favorite for NFL Comeback Player of the Year and Russell Wilson the longshot. That framing makes Wilson the stronger candidate on the award's own terms. Hamlin's case rests on surviving a cardiac arrest, but the article concedes he "has seldom been active on game day and rarely seen the field," which Wilson's plainly is not: he returned to play well enough in 2023 that the article credits Broncos coach Sean Payton with having "resurrected Russell Wilson's career," after 2022 in Denver was a disaster. The article also states the award "will depend on how vital voters deem playing to be." If production matters, Wilson deserves the consideration.
