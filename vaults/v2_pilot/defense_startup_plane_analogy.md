# Cohen's Startup Analogy as Warrant for Bankman-Fried's Practices

In his opening statement at Sam Bankman-Fried's FTX trial (Fortune, 2023-10-04), defence lawyer Mark Cohen repeatedly argued that “working at a startup is like building a plane as you’re flying it” and that businesses sometimes fail. He extended the analogy: as that plane approached the “eye of the storm,” Bankman-Fried did not act like someone who was guilty. The warrant Cohen offers for the business practices between FTX and Alameda Research is startup risk-taking, not fraudulent intent.
