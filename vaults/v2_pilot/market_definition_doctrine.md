# Market Definition in Epic v. Google: Narrow Android Market vs. All Smartphones

In Epic v. Google, which went to trial on November 6th, 2023, "market definition" is the name The Verge gives to the question that decides the case: a monopoly on what? Epic claims Google holds illegal monopolies in "Android app distribution" and "Android in-app payment processing", so anyone who buys or develops for Android phones is stuck paying the "Google tax". Google answers that the real competition is Apple, because people can choose to buy an iPhone instead. The same conduct is lawful or unlawful depending on the boundary drawn: if the court holds the relevant market is phones and app stores, not specifically Android ones, Google is "on the road to victory"; if it accepts that Android apps are their own market, Epic is in better shape. The court could also pick a different market definition entirely, as the judge in Epic's Apple case did.
