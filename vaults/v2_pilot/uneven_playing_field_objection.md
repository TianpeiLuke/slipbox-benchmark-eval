# Google's "level playing field" defence ignores the advantages that make it better

In The Age on 2023-10-22, Kent Walker, Google's president of global affairs, argued in a blog response to the US antitrust trial that Google Search wins users, browsers and device makers on merit: default status is not determinative, switching is easy, and users reinstall Google when it is absent (most Firefox users moved off the Yahoo! default; "Google" was the number one search query on Bing in 2021). The article objects that these arguments ignore an uneven playing field — leaving unanswered whether user preference for Google is instead a product of its scale, longevity, ubiquity and accumulated user data, which no competitor could match.
