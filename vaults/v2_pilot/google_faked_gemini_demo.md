# Google caught staging a Gemini demo with off-camera text prompts (Dec 2023)

In TechCrunch's Week in Review dated 2023-12-09, Google was caught faking a demonstration of Gemini, the flagship AI model it unveiled that week: rather than letting the model process visual input live, Google tuned text prompts with still images off camera. The staging contrasted with what had actually shipped — only a "lite" version, Gemini Pro, which is strictly text-in, text-out — while Google's press briefing and blog posts touted Gemini's multimodal prowess, claiming the model understands images, audio and videos as well as text.
