# Google–Apple Search Deal: Payment for Default Placement, Restraint on Chrome

Under the Google–Apple search deal, in place since 2003, Google pays Apple for default search placement in Safari: of the $26.3 billion Google spent on defaults across browsers, phones and platforms in 2021, about $18 billion went to Apple, according to testimony in the U.S. antitrust trial reported by TechCrunch on October 31, 2023.

The consideration flowing back to Apple is restraint. Google agreed not to promote Chrome to Safari users, per Bloomberg, though Google had the means to do so through banners, pop-ups and similar prompts inside its other applications. That term's effect on Apple's own conduct: Apple never switched to a Google competitor as its search engine and never let iPhone users choose a browser when setting up their devices.
