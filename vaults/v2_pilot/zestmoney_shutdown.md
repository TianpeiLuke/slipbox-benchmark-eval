# ZestMoney, Goldman-backed Bengaluru BNPL lender, shuts down after failing to find a buyer

ZestMoney, a buy-now-pay-later startup based in Bengaluru, is shutting down. The company built its business underwriting small-ticket loans to first-time internet users in India and counted Goldman Sachs among its investors. It had employed roughly 150 people at its peak and raised more than $130 million over eight years of operating. According to TechCrunch's Week in Review dated 2023-12-09, the closure followed a failed search for a buyer.
