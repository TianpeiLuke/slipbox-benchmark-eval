# Dave Clark publicly attacked Flexport's founder and board on X, October 2023

On Monday 2 October 2023, in the week ending 7 October 2023, Dave Clark — the former Amazon executive ousted as CEO of Flexport just a year into the job — fired back at the company's founder and board in a lengthy post on the social media site X, calling recent reporting on the logistics company "deeply concerning." The TechCrunch "Turmoil at Flexport" item, published in its Week in Review digest of 7 October 2023, records that Clark's comments followed a CNBC report providing new information about his last days at Flexport, a freight forwarding and customs brokerage startup valued at $8 billion.
