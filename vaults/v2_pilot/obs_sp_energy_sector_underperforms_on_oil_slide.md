# S&P 500 Energy Sector Led Wall Street's Sector Losses on 29 September 2023

On Friday 29 September 2023, the last trading day of a September in which the S&P 500 dropped 4.9 per cent, energy producers sat on the losing end of Wall Street, hurt by the slide in oil's price. Energy stocks in the S&P 500 fell 2 per cent as a group, more than double the loss of any of the other 10 sectors that make up the index. Individually, ExxonMobil fell 1.6 per cent, and Schlumberger dropped 4.3 per cent.
