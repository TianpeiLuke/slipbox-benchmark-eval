# Epic's day-one chart put Google's Play margins at 70 percent on $12 billion

During Epic's opening argument on day one of the Epic v. Google trial, reported by The Verge on 2023-11-07, Epic lead attorney Gary Bornstein showed jurors charts of Google's app profit margins: 70 percent on $12 billion in revenue a year, per Epic's figure. Bornstein used those charts to frame Google's alleged payments to competitors as protection of that margin.
