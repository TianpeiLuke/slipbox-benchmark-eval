# Google Paid Rival-Supporting Developers Not to Launch Their Own App Stores, Sweeney Recounted

In a December 12, 2023 interview with The Verge, following a federal jury's finding that Google holds an illegal monopoly over Android app distribution and in-app payments, Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeney said the trial confirmed practices he had suspected since roughly 2018: that Google was "paying other developers off to convince them not to launch their own stores." Sweeney had attended nearly every day of the four-week trial in person.
