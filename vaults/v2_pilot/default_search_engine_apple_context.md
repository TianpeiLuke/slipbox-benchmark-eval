# Default search engine, as defined in US v. Google

In US v. Google, the antitrust trial over Google's search business, Eddy Cue testified on 2023-09-26 about what a "default search engine" means in practice: the engine preselected on a device, which a user must actively change rather than choose. Apple fixes that default to Google through the Information Services Agreement (ISA), in place since 2002, whose current iteration Cue negotiated with Google CEO Sundar Pichai in 2016. Cue acknowledged that the ISA doesn't allow Apple to offer users a choice of search engines during setup. Whether Google is "easily" changed is contested: DuckDuckGo's CEO testified it takes "too many steps."
