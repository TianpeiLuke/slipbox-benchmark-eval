# Alligator at South Florida SEO party measured 5.5 feet, not the advertised 10

In a South Florida rooftop bar party for SEO professionals and digital marketers, reported in The Verge's "The people who ruined the internet" on 2023-11-01, the alligator that had been promoted as 10 feet long measured only five and a half feet. The author, who had flown to the Sunshine State expecting the reptile, called the shortfall "Classic clickbait!" About 200 digital marketers attended.
