# "Agent" in TechCrunch's 2024 AI predictions: an AI system wired into the user's tools

In TechCrunch's predictions article "8 predictions for AI in 2024" (TechCrunch, published 2023-12-19), the term "agent" is defined by capability of access rather than by output quality. An AI qualifies as an agent, the article argues, only when it can reach things like a user's spreadsheets, ticket buying interfaces, and transportation apps — the step beyond merely summarizing text or making lists of things, which the article treats as the baseline any model can already do. It dates the first tentative attempts at this "agent" approach to 2023, and notes none of them caught on.
