# Epic's stake in v. Google: break up the Android app distribution and in-app payment monopolies

Epic Games' interest in Epic v. Google, which went to trial on November 6th, 2023 — 1,180 days after Epic first sued — is the break-up of what it calls Google's illegal monopolies in "Android app distribution" and "Android in-app payment processing." Epic charges up to ten times more per transaction than you pay with PayPal. The theory is straightforward: if those two monopolies are dismantled, developers buying or selling on Android are no longer stuck paying the "Google tax," the 30%-style in-app payment fee critics named — and therefore no longer pass that fee along to consumers in higher app prices. Both Epic and Google agreed the trial could decide the future of Google's app store.
