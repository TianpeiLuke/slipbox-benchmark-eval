# Bankman-Fried's "we" sometimes meant only himself

During his direct examination at the FTX trial, reported by The Verge on 2023-10-28, Sam Bankman-Fried on occasion used the first-person plural "we" while referring only to himself, the court correspondent notes. His lawyer Mark Cohen was the one asking the questions at that point in the testimony.
