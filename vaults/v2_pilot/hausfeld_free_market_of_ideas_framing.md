# Hausfeld frames the Google publishers suit as a fight for AI-era press competition

Housefeeds counsel Hausfeld, in the website announcement accompanying the class action filed the week of 2023-12-15 in the U.S. District Court in D.C., cast the case against Google and parent Alphabet as necessary to restore competition in the digital news and reference publishing markets and to establish guardrails for the free marketplace of ideas in the AI era. The suit, brought on behalf of Arkansas publisher Helena World Chronicle and a class of news publishers, invokes both the Sherman Act and the Clayton Act. Hausfeld's framing positions the relief sought — class-wide damages plus an injunction governing how Google uses publisher content in AI products — as a structural remedy for press sustainability, not merely a compensation claim.
