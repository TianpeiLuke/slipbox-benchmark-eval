# Steam Deck OLED as mid-cycle refresh, not successor (2023)

Valve's Steam Deck OLED, reviewed by Engadget on 2023-11-09, is a mid-cycle refresh rather than a Steam Deck 2 — the Switch OLED pattern, the same product line and form factor with better parts inside. The chassis is unchanged at 11.7 inches long, 4.5 inches tall and nearly 2 inches thick. What did change: a 7.4-inch HDR OLED panel at 90Hz replacing the 7-inch 60Hz LCD, peak brightness of 1,000 nits versus 400, battery life 30 to 50 percent longer per Valve, thumbsticks 1mm taller with improved texture, more precise trackpad haptics, better thermals, and a die-shrunk custom AMD APU — same CPU and GPU, only more efficient, cutting about 30 grams. Because the performance targets match the original, developers need not build games specifically for it.
