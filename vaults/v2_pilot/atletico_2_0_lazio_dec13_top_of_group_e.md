# Atlético Madrid's 13 December 2023 win over Lazio settled Group E's top spot

On 13 December 2023, Atlético Madrid beat Lazio 2-0 in the Spanish capital to claim first place in their UEFA Champions League Group E. Both clubs had already secured qualification for the Round of 16 before that fixture, so only the seeding order was at stake. The result falls a day after Sporting News's 12 December 2023 dateline on its Champions League permutations and scenarios article, which reviewed the group stage ahead of its final match night and listed Atlético Madrid alongside Real Sociedad as Spain's remaining representatives in the next round.
