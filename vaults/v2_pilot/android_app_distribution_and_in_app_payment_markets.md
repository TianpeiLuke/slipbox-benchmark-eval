# Epic's Two Named Monopoly Markets in Epic v. Google: Android App Distribution and Android In-App Payment Processing

In Epic v. Google, which went to trial on November 6th, 2023 — a mere 1,180 days after Epic originally sued — Epic Games names two markets in which it alleges Google holds illegal monopolies: "Android app distribution" and "Android in-app payment processing." Epic's theory is that if you buy or develop for Android phones, you are stuck paying the "Google tax," the in-app payment fee that attached to a Fortnite V-Bucks purchase until August 13th, 2020. The dispute over those boundaries is "market definition": the narrower the market, the stronger Epic's monopoly claim.
