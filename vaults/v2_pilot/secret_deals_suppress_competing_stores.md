# The Epic v. Google hypothesis: paid exclusion, not consumer preference, kept rival app stores off Android

Testable proposition from the Epic v. Google trial record, stated by Epic CEO Tim Sweeney in his interview with The Verge published 2023-12-12: rival Android app stores failed to reach devices because Google paid for that outcome — paying "other developers off to convince them not to launch their own stores" and "paying carriers and OEMs secretly not to carry competing stores." Sweeney says he suspected these practices since 2018, early enough that critics called him a conspiracy theorist, and says the testimony confirmed them; when Epic tried to bundle Fortnite with manufacturers like OnePlus and carriers of all sorts, they told him Google had already done a secret deal with them. Falsified if carriers, OEMs or developers declined competition for reasons documented independently of Google payments.
