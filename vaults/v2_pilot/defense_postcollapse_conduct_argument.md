# Cohen's Closing Argument: Bankman-Fried Gave Up Wealth Rather Than Steal, Fortune Says

Mark Cohen, defending Sam Bankman-Fried at FTX's criminal trial in New York, argued in his opening statement on 2023-10-04 that when the walls came closing in and his "plane approached the 'eye of the storm'," Bankman-Fried behaved as an innocent would, being willing to give up his personal wealth to make customers whole. Cohen concluded that the founder of two billion-dollar businesses "didn't steal any money."

Source: Fortune, 2023-10-04.
