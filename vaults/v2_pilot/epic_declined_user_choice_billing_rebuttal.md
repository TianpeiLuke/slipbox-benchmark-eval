# Google's User Choice Billing Counter to Epic's Payment-Processing Market Claim

Facing Epic Games' antitrust trial against Google that began November 6, 2023, Google answers Epic's claim of a foreclosed market for payment-processing solutions inside Android apps by pointing to its own alternative-billing program, User Choice Billing — and to Epic's refusal to use it. Introduced in November 2022 and still in pilot testing at trial time, User Choice Billing is open to all developers selling apps in the 35 markets where it is available, including the U.S., and cuts the standard commission — otherwise 15% to 30% under Google Play's first-party billing requirement — by 4% for companies that use their own payment processing. Spotify and Bumble were the initial testers, and Match, having settled with Google the week before the trial started, will also take advantage of the option. In Google's framing, developers who want to process payments themselves are invited to do so at a lower rate; Epic simply declined the offer.
