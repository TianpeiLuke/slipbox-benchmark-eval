# ChatGPT's research preview launch as a data-harvesting strategy

When OpenAI released ChatGPT "a year ago" — i.e. a year before TechCrunch's retrospective of 2023-11-30 — it did so as a "low-key research preview," reportedly spurred in part by an intense rivalry with the AI startup Anthropic. OpenAI leadership told the OpenAI rank-and-file at the time that the goal was to gather more data on how people use and interact with generative AI, in order to inform the development of OpenAI's future models. ChatGPT was not a new model at all in that framing: initially a basic free-to-use, web-based, chat-focused interface on top of one of OpenAI's existing models, GPT-3.5.
