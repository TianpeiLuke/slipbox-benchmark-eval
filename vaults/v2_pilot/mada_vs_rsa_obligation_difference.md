# MADA buys presence, RSA buys prominence: Google's two Android partner instruments

In testimony for Google's defense on Nov 8, 2023, Jamie Rosenberg, a Google employee focused on Android and Google Play, drew a line between two separate partner agreements, as reported by TechCrunch on Nov 13, 2023. The mobile app distribution agreement (MADA) is what Rosenberg says Google makes Android smartphone makers such as Samsung or Oppo sign in order to pre-load a bundle of 11 Google apps on the device, including Search, Chrome and Play — presence, and he stressed those apps don't have to be the default choices. The revenue share agreement (RSA) is the other instrument, struck with smartphone makers and wireless carriers such as Verizon, and it is the one tied to money and to setting defaults.
