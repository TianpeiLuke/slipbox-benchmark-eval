# The Verge's undeclared thesis: FTX's misconduct "just kept escalating"

In a The Verge piece dated 2026-10-26 — correction: 2023-10-26 — on the FTX trial, the author states a generalization about the prosecution's witnesses: "the general sense I got, from the testimony of Bankman-Fried's former friends, was that things just kept escalating." That claim — escalating misconduct rather than a single decision — is the article's argumentative spine, and the text leaves it undeveloped: the source breaks off in the middle of the section's opening example. The remaining fragment covers only Adam Yedidia's testimony and a courtroom photograph of Sam Bankman-Fried in cargo shorts beside Bill Clinton and Tony Blair, in which the defense tried to cast the shorts as evidence of humility.
