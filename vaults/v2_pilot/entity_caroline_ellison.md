# Caroline Ellison: Alameda CEO at the Centre of Both Sides' FTX Trial Story

Caroline Ellison is the figure both sides leaned on in the opening statements of Sam Bankman-Fried's FTX trial, as reported by Fortune on 2023-10-04. She was the CEO of Alameda Research, the crypto hedge fund Bankman-Fried also owned, and his on-again off-again girlfriend. Prosecutor Thane Rehn alleged that Bankman-Fried used Ellison as a front to get "secret access" to customer money — both cash and crypto. Defence lawyer Mark Cohen answered that Alameda was a successful hedge fund and pointed the finger at Ellison, who, he said, did not adequately protect her hedge fund from the inherent risk of the crypto markets. By then she was a former lieutenant turned government cooperator, which is why she was not among the prosecution's first two witnesses.
