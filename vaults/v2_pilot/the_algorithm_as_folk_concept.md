# The "almighty algorithm" is actually many algorithms

When search engine optimization practitioners boast that they have "vanquished the almighty algorithm," the singular entity they describe does not exist technically: the phrase denotes many algorithms, which popular imagination collapses into one unified force. That framing appears in The Verge's "The people who ruined the internet" (2023-11-01), where SEO is described as telling "the algorithm" whatever it needs to hear so a site ranks high in Google Search results and can then show visitors advertising.
