# DuckDuckGo's CEO contests Apple's claim that users can "easily" change search engines

DuckDuckGo's chief executive testified in US v. Google the week before 2023-09-26 that it takes "too many steps" to switch away from the default search engine, a direct rebuttal to the position Apple's Eddy Cue took on the stand on 2023-09-26 in the Washington, DC courtroom.

Cue, defending the Information Services Agreement (ISA) in place since 2002 and renegotiated with Google CEO Sundar Pichai in 2016, said Apple picks Google as default "because we've always thought it was the best," adding: "We pick the best one and let users easily change it."

That single word — "easily" — is a persistent point of contention in the trial. The ISA does not let Apple offer users a choice of search engines during setup, and Cue's own framing of setup as "just critical stuff" concedes the engine is fixed at the moment of first use, contesting the friction premise of Apple's defence.
