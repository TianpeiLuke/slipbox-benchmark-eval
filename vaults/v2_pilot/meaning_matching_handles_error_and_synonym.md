# Google's Meaning Matching: Looking Past the Literal Words of a Query

Meaning matching is Google Search's process of looking past the specific words in a query to infer what the searcher intends, handling misspellings, terms that never appear on a page but exist there as close synonyms, and phrasings that are unclear or incomplete. According to Google search liaison Danny Sullivan in a post on X, reported by The Age on 2023-10-22, this is a process separate from ads, used to match organic results to a query.
