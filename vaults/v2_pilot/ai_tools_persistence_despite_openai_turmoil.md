# Adoption and Investment as Grounds for AI Chatbots Outlasting OpenAI's Leadership Crisis

TechCrunch's ChatGPT coverage (published 2023-09-28, updated through December 2023) argues that AI chatbots are not going away anytime soon, resting the claim on two grounds: measured adoption and OpenAI's continued investment.

Adoption: ChatGPT reached 100 million weekly active users since its initial launch nearly a year ago, and is used by more than 92% of Fortune 500 companies.

Investment: OpenAI announced GPT-4 Turbo, a multimodal API and a GPT store at its first developer conference, OpenAI DevDay, on November 6; connected ChatGPT to the internet for all users; integrated DALL-E 3; and re-enabled ChatGPT Plus subscriptions on December 13, 2023.

The reasoning is that a user base that large and a product roadmap that active do not reverse because CEO and co-founder Sam Altman was fired and swiftly returned — a turmoil the article says may instead empower Meta's LLaMA models and rival AI startups.
