# Google Chat's history-off default deletes messages every 24 hours

History-off is the default setting on Google Chat that deletes messages every 24 hours, unless a user manually changed the setting. Because the deletion happens automatically rather than being tied to a retention policy a user opts into, the option distinguishes history-off chats from retention-based messaging inside the same product. Documented on 2023-10-31 by TechCrunch's report on the U.S. government's antitrust trial against Google, history-off sat at the centre of that deletion dispute: in February the Department of Justice accused Google of systematically destroying chats through it, despite Google being subject to a litigation hold.
