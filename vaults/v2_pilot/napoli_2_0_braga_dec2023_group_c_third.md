# Napoli 2-0 Braga, 12 December 2023: Group C's Consolation Europa League Place Settled

In the UEFA Champions League's final Group C matchday on 12 December 2023, Napoli beat Braga 2-0 at the Diego Armando Maradona, snuffing out Braga's slim hopes of second place. That result left Braga — fourth in the group with one win, one draw and four defeats, four points — with the group's consolation Europa League place, as Real Madrid finished top and Napoli qualified for the Round of 16.
