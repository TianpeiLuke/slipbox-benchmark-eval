# Why a rapid Epic v. Google verdict did not mean Epic had lost

A quick jury decision is easy to misread as proof the plaintiffs' case was weak or that jurors never engaged with it. Tim Sweeney countered that reading in *The Verge* interview published 2023-12-12, after Epic won its antitrust verdict against Google. He called the result "a great relief" rather than a sure thing, invoking trial lawyers' conventional wisdom — his words — that "when there's a rapid jury verdict, it's typically not good for the plaintiffs making a complicated case." Epic had asked the court and a nine-person jury to spend four weeks on "a major antitrust trial full of complicated facts and evidence," so Sweeney said "there was some trepidation going on." Speed, on his account, was the worrying signal, not the reassuring one.
