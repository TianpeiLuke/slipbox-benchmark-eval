# Mixture of experts: smaller specialized models replacing one monolithic LLM

In TechCrunch's "8 predictions for AI in 2024" (2023-12-19), a mixture of experts is defined as a collection of smaller, more specific models — and likely multimodal ones — assembled in place of a single monolithic large language model. The stated reasoning: a single monolithic model is unwieldy and expensive, whereas a mixture of experts may prove almost as effective while being much easier to update piecemeal. The concept appears in the prediction that diminishing returns on parameter counts will become clear during 2024.
