# Champions League Round of 16 — the competition's first knockout round

In the UEFA Champions League, the Round of 16 is the first knockout round of the competition, filled by the clubs that emerge from the group stage holding the qualifying positions in their section. For the 2023/24 edition, group-stage football closed on 12 Dec 2023, with "16 precious places in the first knockout round" claimed. The tables show two clubs per group marked qualified — Bayern Munich and Copenhagen in Group A, Arsenal and PSV in Group B, Real Madrid and Napoli in Group C, Real Sociedad and Inter Milan in Group D.
