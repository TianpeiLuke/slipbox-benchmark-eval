# The Kelce–Swift Timeline Argues by Accumulation, Not by Any Single Act

The CBSSports.com timeline dated 2023-10-12 supports its claim of a "rumored romance" between Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce by accumulation rather than by proof. Individually, each entry is ambiguous: Kelce's July 26 friendship bracelet with "my number on it" could be a jersey number, his September 21 invitation on the Pat McAfee show was spoken as a joke, and Jason Kelce's September 14 response "did not confirm them, but he also did not deny them." What makes the case, in the article's framing, is that the entries chain: Kelce's June 29 and September 22 Swift-bracelet gestures, Swift's attendance at five Chiefs games this season including the September 24 Week 3 game against the Bears and the article's present-day Sunday game against the Chargers, her cheering a three-yard touchdown in a 41-10 win, and being seen chatting with his mother, Donna Kelce. The inference holds only while the acts are read as coordinated.
