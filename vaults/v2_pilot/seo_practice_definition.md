# Search engine optimization as feeding the algorithm what it needs to hear

Search engine optimization (SEO) is the practice of telling a search algorithm whatever it needs to hear so that a site appears as high as possible in organic search results. In a 2023-11-01 Verge report on the digital marketers blamed for degrading Google Search, the work is compared to the Wizard of Oz projecting his voice to magnify his authority — distinct from simply buying the advertising space labeled "Sponsored" above the results. The practitioner leverages Google's supposed objectivity to lure people in and then, usually, shows them some kind of advertising: "Voilà: a business model!" Google holds a 90 percent market share for online search.
