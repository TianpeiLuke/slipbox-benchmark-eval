# Judge Rogers' Market Definition Gave Apple the Epic Case — and Haunts Google

In Epic's antitrust case against Apple, Judge Yvonne Gonzalez Rogers defined the relevant market for Fortnite as "digital mobile gaming transactions" rather than iPhone apps, and that definitional choice carried much of Apple's mostly favorable September 2021 victory. It is the precedent hanging over Epic v. Google, which went to trial on November 6th, 2023 — a mere 1,180 days after Epic originally sued. There, Epic's proposed markets are "Android app distribution" and "Android in-app payment processing"; Google counters that the real competition is the iPhone.
