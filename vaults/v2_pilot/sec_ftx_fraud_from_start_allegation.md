# SEC alleges FTX was a fraud "from the start," with a multi-billion-dollar shortfall from customer funds

The Securities and Exchange Commission alleges that FTX, the cryptocurrency exchange Sam Bankman-Fried founded in 2019, was a fraud "from the start," diverting customers' funds to his crypto trading firm Alameda Research, with a "multi-billion-dollar deficiency caused by his own misappropriation of customer funds," according to reporting by The Verge on 2023-09-28, shortly before Bankman-Fried's trial on seven counts of wire fraud and conspiracy began.
