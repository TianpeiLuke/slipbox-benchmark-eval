# Stroud's Value Impact Is the Case for His MVP Candidacy

The strongest argument for C.J. Stroud as a 2023 NFL MVP longshot is the change in team quality he produced, as argued in Sporting News' award-odds analysis of December 5, 2023. Stroud has taken one of the worst teams in the NFL — the Texans, who had 11 total wins across the last three seasons — and turned it into a playoff contender and potential division champion. That is the metric MVP voters reward, since the award typically goes to the quarterback of a top-seeded team. His counting stats back it: he leads the league in passing yards, is one of four quarterbacks to have started the whole season with a passer rating over 100, and his touchdown-to-interception ratio is the best in the NFL at 4 to 1.
