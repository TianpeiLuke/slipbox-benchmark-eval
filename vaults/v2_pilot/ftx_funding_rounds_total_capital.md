# FTX took in about $2 billion from 80+ investors within two years of founding; last public round was a $400M Series C

Sam Bankman-Fried co-founded FTX in 2019. Within two years, more than 80 investors provided about $2 billion in capital to the crypto exchange. Its last round of public funding was a $400 million Series C in January 2022, which boosted FTX's valuation to $32 billion (TechCrunch, 2023-10-01).
