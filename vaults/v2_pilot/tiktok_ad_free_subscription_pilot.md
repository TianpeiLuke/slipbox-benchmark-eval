# TikTok is testing an ad-free subscription tier at $4.99 per month

In TechCrunch's Week in Review dated 2023-10-07, TikTok reported it was testing an ad-free subscription tier for some users. For $4.99, subscribers get an ad-free experience on TikTok with no other strings attached. The pilot is limited: TikTok said it was running the plan in a single, English-speaking market outside the U.S. for now. No rollout beyond that one market has been announced, and TikTok offered no timeline, with TechCrunch cautioning that subscribers should not look for the option to arrive anytime soon.
