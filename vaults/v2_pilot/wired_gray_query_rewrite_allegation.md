# Wired removed Megan Gray's article alleging Google rewrites search queries for ad revenue

In October 2023, Wired published an opinion piece by lawyer and privacy advocate Megan Gray alleging that Google had accidentally revealed during the US antitrust trial that it manipulates people's search queries for maximum ad revenue. The example given was replacing a search for "children's clothing" with "NIKOLAI-brand kidswear". Google rejected the piece as misleading and inaccurate, denying it ever alters search terms. Wired removed the article for not meeting its standards.
