# Adversarial proximity: FTX co-defendants and housemates testify against Bankman-Fried, October 2023

In October 2023, FTX's trial in Manhattan turned on adversarial proximity. Gary Wang and Nishad Singh — Sam Bankman-Fried's former MIT classmates, co-workers, and housemates in the Bahamas — testified against their former classmate, confidant and boss, in a case alleging a crypto empire that defrauded thousands of customers out of billions of dollars.
