# Gary Wang: FTX's lesser-known co-founder and technology chief

Gary Wang, aged 30 when he testified against Sam Bankman-Fried in Manhattan federal court on October 5–6, 2023, is the lesser-known co-founder of FTX and served as its technology chief. The source identifies him as a former friend of Bankman-Fried's from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology, where the two met, and it also places Wang among those who worked at the crypto exchange while living with the founder in the Bahamas.
