# OEMs in Epic Games v. Google: The Android Gatekeepers

In Epic Games' antitrust case against Google, which went to trial on November 6, 2023, an "OEM" or original equipment manufacturer means a company that builds Android handsets and sells them. Google bundles the Play Store with other Google apps that Android OEMs have to agree to in order to use Android.
