# Alameda's privileges left the hedge fund owing FTX $8 billion of customer deposits, Wang testified

Testifying in Manhattan federal court on 6 October 2023, FTX co-founder and technology chief Gary Wang told jurors that the special privileges granted to Alameda Research ultimately resulted in the hedge fund owing FTX $8 billion worth of customer deposits. Wang gave his evidence under a cooperation agreement with the government, having pleaded guilty in December.
