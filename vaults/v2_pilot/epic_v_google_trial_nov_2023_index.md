# Epic v. Google trial notes index — November 6, 2023

These notes cover TechCrunch's November 6, 2023 report on Epic Games' antitrust trial against Google, which began November 6, 2023 over Google Play and its commission structure. Sections: legal theory and statutes (Sherman Act Sections 1 and 2, Cartwright Act, Unfair Competition Law, the Microsoft/Netscape precedent); the two relevant markets; commissions and User Choice Billing; OEM agreements, AFAs and Project Hug; and Google's defenses.
