# Kelce's Moustache Quip About Swift on New Heights, August 2023

In August 2023, on New Heights, the podcast he shares with his brother Jason, Travis Kelce discussed the moustache he grows every year in honour of Chiefs head coach Andy Reid. Asked whether he knew what Taylor Swift thought of it, Kelce stayed cheekily confident: "We're not going to bring up Taylor Swift in this episode … But something tells me she's going to like it." The remark carried a clear suggestion that Kelce had made contact with Swift, and was even going to meet her. Per Vulture, shortly afterwards he took a week off training to visit New York City, which coincided with Swift re-recording her 1989 tracks there.
