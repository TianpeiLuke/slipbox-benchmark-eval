# SBF's "Focused on Solutions" Rebuttal Fails Without a Corrective Act

Sam Bankman-Fried's answer to the inference that not investigating FTX's missing $8 billion showed consciousness of wrongdoing was that he was solving, not blaming. Testifying on October 31, 2023, in an exchange prosecutor Danielle Sassoon conducted in a tone so level The Verge's reporter said she "could have been filing her nails," Bankman-Fried said, "I wasn't trying to build out blame for it," and that he was "focused on solutions!" The objection is strained because no corrective act followed it: the reporter's follow-up, "Did he fire anyone? Nope!", and his "no, no, and no" answers to whether he had directed Alameda not to spend customer deposits, put policies preventing it, or set measures protecting that money.
