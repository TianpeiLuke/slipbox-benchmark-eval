# Travis Kelce made a friendship bracelet for NFL tight end camp newcomers, June 29, 2023

On June 29, 2023, Travis Kelce made a friendship bracelet to give to the newcomers at a camp for NFL tight ends, saying he was "taking a page out of Taylor Swift's book." CBSSports.com reported the episode on 2023-10-12 inside its timeline of the rumored Swift–Kelce romance, and flags the June 29 date as the candidate origin point of the whole sequence: "Is this where it all begin?" The friendship bracelet tradition comes from Swift's Eras Tour.
