# Epic's 90%-of-Android-downloads figure targets Google Play at trial

Heading into its antitrust trial against Google, which began November 6, 2023, Epic Games planned to put one usage figure in front of the jury: 90% of apps are still downloaded through Google Play. Epic would present that share as evidence that alternative Android app stores have not gained meaningful reach, and that Google makes it hard for them to succeed and reach consumers.
