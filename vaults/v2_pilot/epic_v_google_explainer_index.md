# Index: The Verge's "Epic v. Google, explained" (Sean Hollister, 2023-11-05)

Navigation for "Epic v. Google, explained," Sean Hollister's reader-facing explainer at The Verge, published 2023-11-05 ahead of the trial that opened November 6th — "a mere 1,180 days after Epic originally sued"; Hollister appended a December 15th, 2023 update marking it superseded. Notes grouped by the question each answers:

- **Background — the August 13th, 2020 trigger**: Epic's hotfix letting Fortnite V-Bucks be bought at a discount outside app store fees, Apple and Google pulling Fortnite, Epic's two lawsuits and the "Llamacorn" attack ad.
- **Competing legal claims**: Epic's monopoly and Play-payments-tying allegations versus Google's sideloaded-app-safety and free-app-store defenses.
- **Market definition**: "Android app distribution" and "Android in-app payment processing" versus phones and app stores generally.
- **Apple-case precedent**: the September 2021 ruling mostly in Apple's favor, with the Supreme Court possibly weighing in.
