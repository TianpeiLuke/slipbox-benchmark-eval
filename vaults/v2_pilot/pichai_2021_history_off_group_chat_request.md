# Pichai's 2021 Request to Turn Off Chat History, Testified as a Personnel Matter

During Sundar Pichai's testimony on October 30, 2023 in the U.S. government's antitrust trial against Google, DOJ lawyer Meagan Bellshaw pulled up a 2021 message exchange in which Pichai asked that history be turned off in a group chat. Pichai testified the request concerned a personnel matter, not the litigation hold. Google's history-off option deletes internal chat messages every 24 hours unless users change the setting.
