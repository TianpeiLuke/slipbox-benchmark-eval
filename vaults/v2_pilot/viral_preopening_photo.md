# Thai Food Near Me's sign photo went viral before opening, March 2023

In March 2023, a photo of the sign of Thai Food Near Me — a New York restaurant named after the literal Google search phrase, as reported by The Verge on October 26, 2023 — went viral on Twitter before the restaurant had even opened, brown butcher paper still covering the windows. Per the report, that visibility converted directly into attention and footfall: the restaurant's "goofy" name was enough to garner coverage on news sites, along with an influx of customers in the first crucial months of being open.
