# Crypto Blame Beyond FTX and Alameda Holders, Per TechCrunch Before the 2023 Trial

TechCrunch's 2023-10-02 article on Sam Bankman-Fried, published before his Manhattan Federal Court trial opened on the multibillion-dollar collapse of FTX, reports blame reaching past the holders who lost money in FTX and Alameda Research directly. The article describes an audience that is "indignant at what they feel was too-easy treatment for someone who many believe brought down the whole crypto house" — that is, people with no position in either firm attributing wider crypto losses to Bankman-Fried, whose collapse set off a ripple effect across the market.
