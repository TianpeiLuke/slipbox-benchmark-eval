# Featured Snippets as a traffic-diverting mechanism in the Helena World Chronicle suit

"Featured Snippets" are a Google search feature in which Google algorithmically extracts answers directly from webpages and surfaces them above ordinary results. In the class action antitrust suit that Arkansas publisher Helena World Chronicle filed in the U.S. District Court in D.C., reported by TechCrunch on 2023-12-15, these Snippets are cited alongside older question-and-answer tools like the Knowledge Graph (launched May 2012) as technologies shifting traffic away from publishers' websites rather than sending readers to them.
