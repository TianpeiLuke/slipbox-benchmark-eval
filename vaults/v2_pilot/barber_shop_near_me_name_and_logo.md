# Felix Silva named his Florida barbershop "Barber Shop Near Me" and drew a Google-pin logo (2019)

In 2019, Felix Silva settled on the name "Barber Shop Near Me" for his store in Coral Springs, Florida, after weighing more than 20 other options. He wanted a name that was neutral and memorable; another contender was "The Barber Shop". Silva then leaned fully into the Google joke in the branding: the shop's logo is a red location pin resembling Google's own, with a blue, white, and red barber pole pattern in the middle. The account comes from The Verge's reporting of 2023-10-26.
