# Why the Same Bet Is Priced Differently at Every Sportsbook

The Sporting News guide to line shopping (Nov 6, 2023) argues that price differences between sportsbooks are structural, not accidental, and that this is the reason line shopping "always makes sense."

Each sportsbook sets its own market: some employ oddsmakers and oddsmaking services using computer models and algorithms, while other betting apps simply copy those lines. Some books therefore open at different prices "right from the jump." Then each book responds to its own money. Since a sportsbook's goal is to even out betting on both sides so it can earn the profit built into the odds without worrying which side wins, it moves a line or changes odds when the public bets heavily on one side.

The decisive point is independence: one sportsbook may be forced to move a line because of betting, while another is not. That produces a divergence on the identical bet, and the divergence is the opportunity — a chance to lay less, win more, and improve your chances.
