# Bing's chatbot-augmented, graphic redesign observed in The Age's October 2023 search test

In a search-engine comparison published by The Age on 22 October 2023, the writer observed that Microsoft's Bing had overhauled its layout over the past year to be very graphic and augmented by generative AI explanations from its chatbot. The same test reported that the resulting ability to ask follow-up questions was appealing in idea but, in practice, was not really there yet.
