# NFL Award Longshots: High Play, Long Odds, Little Credit

In a Sporting News betting piece dated 2023-12-05, an NFL award longshot is defined as a player or coach whose betting odds are very long despite performing at a high level, overlooked because other players have commanded the spotlight and the voters' narrative. The article's reasoning: short odds reflect being the best at a position all season, so equally long odds reflect the opposite perception, not necessarily the opposite play. The stated example: Vikings defensive end Danielle Hunter leads the league in tackles for loss and ranks third in sacks at 13.5, yet trails Micah Parsons, Myles Garrett and T.J. Watt in name recognition. With five weeks left in the regular season, a longshot can still become a contender, and a bet placed later pays less than one placed at that moment.
