# Rosenberg's Nov 8 Defense of Google's Android Pre-install and RSA Payments

In the US v Google antitrust trial, Jamie Rosenberg, a Google employee who focuses on Android and Google Play, testified on November 8, 2023 in Google's defense that the deals putting Google Search and Chrome on Android amount to fair competition rather than exclusion. Rosenberg described a mobile app distribution agreement (MADA) that requires Android smartphone makers such as Samsung or Oppo to pre-load a bundle of 11 Google apps, including Search, Chrome and Play, and stressed that those apps need not be the default choices, according to Bloomberg's account of the testimony. Rosenberg also cited revenue share agreements (RSAs) with smartphone makers and wireless carriers such as Verizon — payments that, in Google's telling, reward partners for building and selling more Android devices, in a rivalry with Apple he called "as intense as it gets."
