# Google paid $26.3 billion for default search placement in 2021, court told

Testifying on 28 October 2023 in the U.S. government's antitrust trial against Google, Google's search head Prabhakar Raghavan stated that Google paid $26.3 billion in 2021 to multiple browsers, phone makers and platforms for making Google the default search engine. Recipients included the companies Apple, Samsung and Mozilla. Of that $26.3 billion total, roughly $18 billion went directly to Apple, under a Google–Apple search deal in place since 2003.
