# The Steam Deck OLED's 62 percent Hades battery gain rests on a worn baseline

The headline 62 percent battery improvement Engadget reports for the Steam Deck OLED overstates the new machine's gain, because the comparison unit is not a neutral baseline. The reviewer tested against her own original LCD Steam Deck — the model she first reviewed at launch and calls her most-played device, the one whose thumbsticks turned gray and whose short battery life she listed as a downside. On *Hades* at 800p with a 60 fps cap and similar brightness, the OLED lasted four hours and 23 minutes against the original's two hours and 42 minutes. Engadget concedes the caveat directly: batteries degrade with use. The resulting figure also exceeds Valve's own claim of 30 to 50 percent, so some of that margin belongs to the aged cell rather than to the OLED.
