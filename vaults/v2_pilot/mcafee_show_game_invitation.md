# Kelce Publicly Invites Swift to a Chiefs Game on The Pat McAfee Show, September 21, 2023

On September 21, 2023, on The Pat McAfee Show, Kansas City Chiefs tight end Travis Kelce announced he had invited Taylor Swift to watch one of his games, reporting what he told her: "I've seen you rock the stage in Arrowhead, you might have to come see me rock a stage at Arrowhead and we can see which one's a little more lit." He added: "We'll see what happens in the near future." The Age's account of the remark treats it as the moment the rumour passed from media report to Kelce's own statement, raising the question of whether the two would appear publicly at a game together.
