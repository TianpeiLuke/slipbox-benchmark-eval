# Default placement, not search quality, sets rivals' scale — the testable counterfactual from Google antitrust testimony

A testable proposition emerging from testimony in the U.S. government's Google antitrust trial (TechCrunch, 2023-10-31): a search engine of comparable product quality given equal access to paid default placement would reach competitive scale in online search. The evidence base is rival executives' own accounts. Earlier in October 2023, the CEOs of Microsoft and DuckDuckGo testified that their search engines would have been far more successful, even competitive with Google, had they been able to make deals with Apple like Google's — a deal in place since 2003. Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella said he was willing to spend $15 billion per year to get Bing into Apple's default search. The proposition is falsified if a rival granted default placement of that kind failed to grow share, and it is the direct negation of Google's defense that it is default because it is the best.
