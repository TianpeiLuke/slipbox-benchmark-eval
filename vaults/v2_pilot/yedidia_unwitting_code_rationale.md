# Yedidia's Explained Reason for His Government Deal, 2023-10-04

Asked by lead prosecutor Danielle Sassoon why he made a deal with the government, Adam Yedidia testified on 2023-10-04: "I was concerned that I had unwittingly written code that contributed to a crime." That quoted concern is the reason Yedidia gave for his immunity agreement, in the FTX proceedings reported by Fortune.
