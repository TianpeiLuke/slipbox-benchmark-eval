# Altman's Firing and Swift Return Raised Concerns About OpenAI's Direction

TechCrunch reports that Sam Altman, CEO and co-founder of OpenAI, was fired and then returned swiftly, and that this episode "raised concerns about its direction and opened the door for competitors." The article, dated 2023-09-28 and later updated, describes the period as "leadership chaos" and situates it after OpenAI's first developer conference, DevDay, on November 6. TechCrunch adds that the fallout might empower rivals such as Meta and its LLaMA large language models, or help other AI startups attract attention and funding as the industry watched OpenAI "implode and put itself back together."
