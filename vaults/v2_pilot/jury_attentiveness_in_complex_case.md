# Epic's Sweeney and Courtroom Reporters Both Saw a Watchful Jury in the Google Trial

In his Verge interview dated 2023-12-12, Epic CEO Tim Sweeney said the nine-person jury in the four-week Google antitrust trial "was really following the case carefully." The jurors "weren't snoozing off as you might expect with the complexity of these documents," he said; instead "everybody was paying attention, they're looking at the question asker and the witness and taking notes and reading documents." A bracketed editor's note added that "every journalist in the courtroom agreed the jury was attentive," and that most were also surprised by the rapid verdict.
