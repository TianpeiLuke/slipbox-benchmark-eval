# Bankman-Fried's Direct-Testimony Clarity May Not Survive Sassoon's Cross

Writing from The Verge's coverage of Sam Bankman-Fried's direct testimony on 2023-10-28, the reporter notes that Bankman-Fried was clearer and much easier to understand than he had been at the evidentiary hearing the previous day, with "a minimum of word salad today." The hypothesis offered is that this fluency stems from one of two causes the reporter cannot separate: he was either "more relaxed, or he'd just been more rehearsed." The prediction attached to it is that the clarity will not hold, since the reporter states he "will certainly be watching to see if he suddenly becomes much less coherent when Sassoon gets him for the cross" — referring to prosecutor Danielle Sassoon, whose questions Bankman-Fried had failed to answer at that hearing.
