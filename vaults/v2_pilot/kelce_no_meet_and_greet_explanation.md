# Kelce Blamed Swift's Show-Day Voice Protocol for Their Failed Contact at the Kansas City Eras Tour

Speaking on New Heights, the podcast he shares with his brother, in July 2023, Travis Kelce described how a friendship bracelet bearing his number never reached Taylor Swift at her Eras Tour concert in Kansas City. "I was disappointed that she doesn't talk before or after her shows because she has to save her voice for the 44 songs she sings," he said. "She doesn't meet anybody, or at least she didn't want to meet me, so I took it personal [sic]." Kelce's account frames the failed contact as a consequence of Swift's concert-day protocol rather than a personal rejection, though he allowed for the alternative reading.
