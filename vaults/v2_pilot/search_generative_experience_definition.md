# Google's Search Generative Experience (SGE)

Search Generative Experience (SGE) is Google's AI-powered search product, cited in a class action antitrust suit filed in the U.S. District Court in D.C. in the week of December 15, 2023. The suit's plaintiff, Arkansas publisher Helena World Chronicle, describes SGE as offering web searchers a way to seek information in a conversational mode, with answers delivered inside the results page rather than through a click to the source. Google "ultimately keeps users in Google's 'walled garden' as it 'plagiarizes' their content," publishers cannot block SGE, because it runs on the same web crawler as Google's general search service, GoogleBot.
