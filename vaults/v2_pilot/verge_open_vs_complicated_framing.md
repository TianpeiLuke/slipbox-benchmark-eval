# Simplicity vs. complexity as the deciding factor in Epic v. Google

In an article published by The Verge on 2023-11-07, the reporter covering the Epic v. Google opening statements argues the trial "may come down to simple v. complicated": the jury may reward whichever side it understands most easily. Her own shift grounds that argument. Walking into the courtroom on Monday morning, she thought it "impossible" Google could lose, reasoning that if Epic couldn't prove Apple's walled iOS garden was a monopoly, the comparatively open Android was harder still to convict. After both sides addressed the jury, she was "not quite as sure," because the presentations were asymmetric. Epic lead attorney Gary Bornstein painted "a black-and-white picture of good and evil with itself as the clear underdog," calling Google a bully and a cheat that "bribes" or "blocks" rivals. Google lead attorney Glenn Pomerantz spent his 45 minutes explaining that Android's dominance is merely the natural outcome of competing with the iPhone. The reporter judges Epic's narrative "compelling" and concludes she isn't sure Google's opening countered it.
