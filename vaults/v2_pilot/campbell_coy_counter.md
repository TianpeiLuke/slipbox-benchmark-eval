# Campbell's COTY Favorite Status May Reward Two Seasons, Not 2023 Alone

An objection to Dan Campbell being the favorite for NFL Coach of the Year, at +200 via DraftKings as of Sporting News's Dec. 5, 2023 awards-odds rundown, is that his price may be measuring the wrong thing. Campbell has done a tremendous job with the Lions, but Sporting News questions whether he leads the board because of how well he has done his job this season or over the last two. In 2023 the team benefited from groundwork laid last season, so the incremental gain attributable to Campbell this year is smaller than the headline record suggests. Were the award for best coach over two seasons, he would deserve it; for this season alone, not so much.
