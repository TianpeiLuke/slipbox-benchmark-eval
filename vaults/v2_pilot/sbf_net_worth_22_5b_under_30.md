# Lewis's 60 Minutes account of SBF's $22.5 billion at age under 30

On a 60 Minutes interview broadcast 2023-10-01, described in TechCrunch on 2023-10-02, author Michael Lewis recounted meeting Sam Bankman-Fried in early 2021: “Eighteen months earlier, he had nothing. Now he had $22.5 billion. He was the richest person in the world under 30 [and] he was going to spend it to save humanity from extinction.” Lewis said his "jaw was on the floor," and he went on to meet SBF more than 100 times over the next two years while reporting "Going Infinite".
