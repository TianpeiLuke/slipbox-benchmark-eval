# Sam Bankman-Fried: FTX founder and criminal fraud defendant

Sam Bankman-Fried is defined here as the founder of the failed cryptocurrency exchange FTX and the defendant in a criminal fraud trial, as reported by The Verge on 2023-10-10. He was on trial on seven counts of wire fraud and conspiracy. The Securities and Exchange Commission alleges that FTX was a fraud "from the start," with a "multi-billion-dollar deficiency caused by his own misappropriation of customer funds." Those characterizations come from the government's allegations, not from findings the article reports as settled.
