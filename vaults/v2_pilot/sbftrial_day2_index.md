# Trial Index — United States v. Sam Bankman-Fried, Day 2 (2023-10-04)

This index covers day two of the United States v. Sam Bankman-Fried trial on 2023-10-04, reported by Fortune. It orders the day's four movements: jury selection that Wednesday morning; the government's opening by prosecutor Thane Rehn, portraying Bankman-Fried as a lying "man" who stole billions; the defense opening by Mark Cohen countering with a good-faith "boy" startup founder; and the prosecution's first two witnesses, beginning with FTX customer Marc-Antoine Julliard, a London-based cocoa trader who said he never recovered almost $100,000. The article closes before the day's adjournment.
