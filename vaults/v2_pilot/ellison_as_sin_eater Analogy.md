# Ellison's named-but-unpaid role at Alameda is the Verge author's worst-ex-boyfriend test case

In its October 10, 2023 report on Sam Bankman-Fried's criminal fraud trial, The Verge's author bases her verdict that Caroline Ellison is the paradigmatically terrible ex-boyfriend case on a single swapped pair: Ellison was made the named head of Alameda Research, the hedge fund at the center of the FTX collapse, while being refused equity — the author's formulation is that no ex of hers "made me the CEO of their sin-eater hedge fund while refusing to give me equity." The claim's force comes from a reversal of ownership and accountability, the person whose name carried the fund's sins holding none of its upside. The author confines the argument to her personal standing as a spurned partner, not the legal record: Ellison had already pleaded guilty to fraud, conspiracy to commit fraud, and money laundering before testifying that Bankman-Fried "directed me to commit these crimes."
