# Steam Deck OLED's 1,000-nit HDR Panel Doubles the Original LCD's Brightness

The Steam Deck OLED's HDR display reaches a peak brightness of 1,000 nits, against the original Steam Deck LCD's maximum of 400 nits, per Engadget's review dated 2023-11-09. Valve's panel also adds a wider P3 color gamut and pure blacks. In practice, the reviewer wrote, the screen's bezels meld with the black of any game, framing retro aspect ratios and leaving room for higher-contrast colors.
