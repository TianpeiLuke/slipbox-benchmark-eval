# TechCrunch's Equity episode, week of 2023-10-07: SBF trial, VC deals and the Fearless Fund bar

TechCrunch's Week in Review of 2023-10-07 lists what its Equity podcast episode covered that week: the trial of Sam Bankman-Fried, the disgraced entrepreneur accused of orchestrating FTX's collapse; investment deals from the VR firm Rainforest and from At One Ventures, Section 32 and Greylock, discussed amid declining venture funding; and the barring of Fearless Fund — a firm founded to invest in women of color — from awarding grants to Black women founders.
