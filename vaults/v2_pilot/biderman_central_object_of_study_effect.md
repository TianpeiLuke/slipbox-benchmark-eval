# ChatGPT Redirected the AI Field by Lowering the Skill Barrier

Stella Biderman, an AI researcher at Booz Allen Hamilton and the open research group EleutherAI, told TechCrunch on 2023-11-30 that ChatGPT's influence on the AI field came from lowering a barrier to use, not from a jump in capability. She does not consider it an AI breakthrough per se, but a "user experience breakthrough" that took generative AI mainstream — and its primary effect, she says, was "encouraging people training AIs to try to mimic it, or encouraging people studying AIs to use it as their central object of study." The mechanism she gives: "Previously you needed to have some skill, albeit not be an expert, to consistently get usable stuff out of [text-generating models]." Once that requirement disappeared, ChatGPT "brought a very large amount of attention to and discussion about the technology."
