# The Sam Bankman-Fried Fraud Trial Before Judge Lewis Kaplan

The Sam Bankman-Fried fraud trial is the six-week federal criminal proceeding over the collapse of the bankrupt cryptocurrency exchange FTX, held in a Manhattan courtroom before U.S. District Judge Lewis Kaplan and begun in early October 2023. The trial is scheduled to resume on Tuesday 10 October 2023 with key testimony expected from Caroline Ellison, the prosecution's star witness. Bankman-Fried faces seven federal charges.
