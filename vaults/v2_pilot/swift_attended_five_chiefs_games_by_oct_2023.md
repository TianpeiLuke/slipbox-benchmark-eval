# Five Chiefs Games as the Swift-Kelce Speculation Marker

In a Taylor Swift–Travis Kelce timeline published by CBSSports.com on 2023-10-12, the article attributed intensified dating speculation to Swift attending five Kansas City Chiefs games so far in the 2023 regular season, including the Sunday game against the Los Angeles Chargers. The article also placed her first attendance at a Chiefs game in the month before its publication.
