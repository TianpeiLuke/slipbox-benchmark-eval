# DOJ at trial: Google gets 16 times more data than Bing every day

In its "why this case matters" framing of the US v Google antitrust trial, TechCrunch reported on Nov 13, 2023 that the DOJ said Google gets 16 times more data than Bing does everyday. The government's theory: Google's platforms and partner deals lock competitors out of the data needed to improve search products.
