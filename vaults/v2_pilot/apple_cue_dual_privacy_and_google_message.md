# The two-message model of Eddy Cue's 2023-09-26 testimony in US v. Google

The Verge's 2023-09-26 report on US v. Google framed Eddy Cue's testimony as running on two simultaneous claims: that Apple believes in protecting its users' privacy, and that Apple believes in Google. Which search engine Apple believes in matters because the Information Services Agreement — in place since 2002, renegotiated by Cue with Google CEO Sundar Pichai in 2016 — makes Google the default search engine on all Apple devices and pays Apple billions of dollars a year. The Verge put the tension at the center of the session: whether those two statements can be simultaneously true became the question of the day in the Washington, DC, courtroom before Judge Amit Mehta.
