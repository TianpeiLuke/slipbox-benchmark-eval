# Sam Bankman-Fried's appearance and demeanor in court, 2023-10-04

Fortune's account of day two of Sam Bankman-Fried's trial in New York on 2023-10-04 records what it observed in the courtroom: his cheekbones were more prominent after spending about seven weeks in a Brooklyn prison, and he spent most of his day there staring into a laptop while seated between his two attorneys.
