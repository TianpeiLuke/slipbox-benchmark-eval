# Copenhagen 1-0 Galatasaray, Parken, 12 December 2023: Group A's decider went the hosts' way

In UEFA Champions League Group A on 12 December 2023, FC Copenhagen beat Galatasaray 1-0 at Parken, Lukas Lerager scoring the only goal. Going into that final matchweek Galatasaray knew that a win over Copenhagen would take them through; the defeat ended that route. Copenhagen instead finished second in the group on 8 points, behind Bayern Munich on 16, with Galatasaray on 5.
