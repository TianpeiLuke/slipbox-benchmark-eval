# Anna Wintour reportedly asked SBF to sponsor the Met Costume Ball

In a TechCrunch report dated 2023-10-02 drawing on Michael Lewis's 60 Minutes interview, Lewis recounts that Anna Wintour, the Vogue editor, initiated contact with Sam Bankman-Fried (SBF) — reportedly over a Zoom video call — to ask him to sponsor the Met Costume Ball. The ask is notable for its inversion of norms: SBF, then FTX's founder, was known in that period for curl-mussed hair, t-shirts and cargo shorts, not formal-fashion patronage, and the request came while FTX marketing deals were running, including payments to Tom Brady ($55 million) and Steph Curry ($35 million) reported in the same article.
