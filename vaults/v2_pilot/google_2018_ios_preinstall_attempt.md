# Pichai: Google asked Cook in 2018 to preinstall Google on every iOS device

Testifying in the U.S. antitrust trial against Google on Monday 30 October 2023, Google CEO Sundar Pichai admitted that in 2018 Google asked Apple CEO Tim Cook to preinstall Google on every iOS device, according to TechCrunch's report of 31 October 2023. Pichai pitched the move as tying Google and Apple's services so closely together that they would become inseparable, and as mutually profitable: Google would gain more searches and the associated data, while Apple would collect more revenue under the two companies' existing search agreement. Cook declined. Apple does not preload third-party software onto its devices and was not going to make an exception for Google.
