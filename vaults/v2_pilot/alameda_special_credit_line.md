# FTX's "Special Credit Line" for Alameda Research (Gary Wang's October 2023 Testimony)

In testimony described by The Verge on 2023-10-10 during Sam Bankman-Fried's criminal fraud trial, Gary Wang — chief technology officer of FTX and co-owner of both FTX and Alameda Research — said Alameda had been given access to a special credit line, defined as the Alameda-only arrangement permitting the firm to take its balance into the negative without triggering liquidation. Wang alleged that other accounts at FTX received neither element of that arrangement. Defense attorney Christian Everdell tried to undercut the claim by pointing to FTX's spot margin program, which let users lend each other assets for margin trading and could leave a balance negative in a specific coin. But per Wang, spot margin accounts could not avoid liquidation or hold an overall negative balance — so the credit line's boundary is the combination of both privileges, which Wang said belonged to Alameda alone.
