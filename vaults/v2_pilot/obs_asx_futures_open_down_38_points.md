# ASX futures pointed to a 38-point opening fall on 1 October 2023

Futures for the Australian sharemarket on 1 October 2023 pointed to a fall of 38 points, or 0.5 per cent, at the open, signalling a retreat after Wall Street closed its worst month and quarter of the year with further losses on Friday. The S&P 500 slipped 0.3 per cent, the Dow Jones fell 0.5 per cent, and the Nasdaq composite edged 0.1 per cent higher.
