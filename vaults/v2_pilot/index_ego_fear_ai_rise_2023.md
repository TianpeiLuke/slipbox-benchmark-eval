# Episodes in The Age's account of AI's rise — index

Four scenes anchor The Age's 9 December 2023 account of AI's rise.

- **2015, Napa Valley:** Elon Musk and Larry Page argue over whether AI will elevate the world or destroy it — or at least inflict grave damage. They stop speaking soon after that party.
- **A few weeks later, Rosewood hotel, Menlo Park, California:** Musk dines with Sam Altman, then running a tech incubator, and several researchers in a private room — the dinner that led to OpenAI later in the year.
- **2010, Peter Thiel's San Francisco town house:** Demis Hassabis, a 34-year-old neuroscientist, finds a chessboard overlooking the Palace of Fine Arts and a swan pond; the birth of DeepMind follows.
- **November 2023, OpenAI's boardroom:** rebel board members try to force out Altman over five chaotic days, then back down.
