# Second Bankman-Fried trial scheduled for March 2024 with additional charges

As of The Verge's reporting on 2023-09-28, a second trial of Sam Bankman-Fried, founder of the failed cryptocurrency exchange FTX, was scheduled for March 2024, carrying additional charges beyond those in the first proceeding. That first trial covered seven counts of criminal charges: two counts of wire fraud and five counts of conspiracy.
