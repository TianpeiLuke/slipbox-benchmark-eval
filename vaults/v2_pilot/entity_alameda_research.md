# Alameda Research: The Hedge Fund at the Centre of the FTX Trial

Alameda Research, the crypto hedge fund also owned by Sam Bankman-Fried, was named in both 2023-10-04 opening statements as central to the case. Prosecutor Thane Rehn called it the key to the alleged scheme, through which Bankman-Fried had "secret access" to customer money. Defence lawyer Mark Cohen countered that Alameda was not "subterranean or shady" but "a successful hedge fund", acting legally as an FTX customer, payment processor, and market maker.
