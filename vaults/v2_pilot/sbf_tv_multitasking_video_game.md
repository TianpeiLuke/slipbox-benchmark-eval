# Lewis on SBF's eyes moving during his first television appearance

Michael Lewis, author of "Going Infinite", recounted in a 60 Minutes interview, reported by TechCrunch on 2023-10-02, that he was in the room when Sam Bankman-Fried made his first television appearance: "If you watch the clip, you'll see his eyes going back and forth. It's because he's trying to win his video game at the same time he's on the air."
