# OpenAI's five-day boardroom crisis, November 2023

In November 2023, rebel members of OpenAI's board tried to force out Sam Altman because, they believed, they could no longer trust him to build AI that would benefit humanity. Over five chaotic days, OpenAI looked as if it were going to fall apart. The board then backed down, pressured by giant investors and by employees who threatened to follow Altman out the door. The drama gave the world its first glimpse of the bitter feuds among those who will determine the future of AI, according to The Age's report of 2023-12-09. Altman had earlier described the infighting as "disagreement, mistrust, egos" among "the closest people".
