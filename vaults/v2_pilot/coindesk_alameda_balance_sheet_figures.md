# CoinDesk's publication of Alameda Research's balance sheet triggered FTX liquidity concerns

In early November 2022, concerns surrounding FTX's liquidity grew after CoinDesk published a copy of Alameda Research's balance sheet. That balance sheet showed the crypto trading firm held $14.6 billion in assets and $8 billion in liabilities as of June 30, 2022. Alameda was co-founded by Sam Bankman-Fried in 2017.

Reported by TechCrunch on 2023-10-01.
