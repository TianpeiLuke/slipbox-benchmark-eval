# Loss of Control on Cross-Examination: Naftalis's Warrant for Calling SBF's Testimony a "Hail Mary"

Former federal prosecutor Josh Naftalis's warrant for calling Sam Bankman-Fried's decision to testify a "Hail Mary" is the transfer of control that occurs the moment cross-examination begins. Speaking to TechCrunch on 2023-10-06, as Bankman-Fried's FTX trial resumed with the defense onus after the prosecution rested and ahead of a verdict and potential sentencing on seven counts tied to fraud and money laundering, Naftalis said: "Once it goes to cross[-examination], he doesn't get to say, 'I'm done.' He can't just walk out if he doesn't like how it's going." On direct examination a defendant sets the sequence, the emphasis and the stopping point; that agency ends at cross, where he cannot stop, qualify or abandon answers the way he can while telling his own story.
