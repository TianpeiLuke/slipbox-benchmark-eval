# Ad load in Google Search varies with the request, per The Age (2023)

In its test reported on 2023-10-22, The Age observed that some Google Search queries returned more ads than others and that the ads shown varied widely with the request. Google attributes that variation to ad keyword matching in its ad systems and denies steering users toward the most monetisable subjects.
