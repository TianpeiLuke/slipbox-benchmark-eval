# Bankman-Fried Answered "No" to All Three Control Questions on FTX Customer Deposits

During his October 2023 trial testimony, Sam Bankman-Fried was asked three straightforward control questions about Alameda Research and FTX customer money, and answered no, no, and no: whether he had directed his employees at Alameda not to spend FTX customer deposits, whether he had put in any policies to prevent Alameda employees from spending FTX customer money, and whether he had put in place any measures at Alameda to protect the FTX customer money. The Verge reporter Liz Goodwin noted the contrast: "Oh, but he was testifying to Congress about keeping customer funds safe." The examination was conducted by prosecutor Danielle Sassoon in the FTX fraud trial, dated 2023-10-31.
