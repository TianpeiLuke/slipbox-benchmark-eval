# Champions League group tie-breakers run goal difference, then goals scored (Group D, 2023/24)

UEFA Champions League 2023/24 group standings are ordered by points, then goal difference, then goals scored, so clubs level on one criterion are split by the next in that sequence. Group D, settled on matchday six, Dec. 12, 2023, demonstrates both steps. Real Sociedad and Inter Milan both finished on 12 points from six games (3 wins, 3 draws, 0 losses) after a 0-0 draw at San Siro, leaving goal difference to decide first place: Real Sociedad +5 ahead of Inter Milan +3. Benfica and RB Salzburg both ended on 4 points and a goal difference of -4 (1 win, 1 draw, 4 losses), so goals scored ranked Benfica above RB Salzburg, with Arthur Cabral's stoppage-time strike in a 3-1 away win in Austria decisive.
