# Imagine with Meta AI — Meta's stand-alone web image generator

Imagine with Meta AI is Meta's stand-alone generative-AI experience on the web, rolled out in the week of 8 December 2023 and reported by TechCrunch on 2023-12-09. Users create images by describing them in natural language, and the tool makes high-resolution images from text prompts. It is powered by Meta's existing Emu image-generation model. TechCrunch places it alongside similar products: OpenAI's DALL-E, Midjourney and Stable Diffusion. The launch was framed as Meta not being outdone by Google's Gemini release that same week, a chatbot-and-model race that also included X (formerly Twitter) and OpenAI.
