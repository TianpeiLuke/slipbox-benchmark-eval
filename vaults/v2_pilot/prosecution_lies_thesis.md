# Rehn's Core Allegation: SBF's Success "Was Built on Lies"

In his roughly 30-minute government opening statement on Oct. 4, 2023, in Sam Bankman-Fried's FTX trial, prosecutor Thane Rehn's central claim was that Bankman-Fried's apparent success — "all of that, all of it, was built on lies" — because "behind the curtains, he was not what he appeared to be." The warrant is the divergence between Bankman-Fried repeatedly telling FTX customers their money was "safe and secure" and his alleged "secret access" to that customer money via Alameda Research.
