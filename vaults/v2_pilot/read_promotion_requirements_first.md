# Read the Terms Before Accepting Any Sportsbook Promotion

Sporting News (Oct. 4, 2023), in its NBA Rookie of the Year odds guide, urges bettors to read the requirements for any available promotions and offers before accepting them, so they know the restrictions before betting. The argument's scope is any available promotion at an online sportsbook, not merely the ROTY market. The reasoning: sportsbook bonuses arrive attached to conditions, so a welcome bonus or promo code claim accepted without reading those terms can turn out to be one you cannot actually cash out — the odds page's own instruction to qualify for a bonus by following its link is exactly the step where restrictions bite.
