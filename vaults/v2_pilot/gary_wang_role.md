# Gary Wang — CTO of FTX, co-owner of FTX and Alameda

Gary Wang is the chief technology officer of FTX, the failed cryptocurrency exchange, and co-owner of both FTX and Alameda Research. Wang testified against FTX founder Sam Bankman-Fried, who stood trial on seven counts of wire fraud and conspiracy, saying Alameda received a special credit line and an option to take its balance into the negative without triggering liquidation — something he alleged other FTX accounts didn't get. On 2023-10-10, defense attorney Christian Everdell cross-examined Wang the same day as Caroline Ellison.
