# Epic's OEM evidence: the cancelled OnePlus deal and the Google–Samsung agreement

Epic Games' antitrust case against Google, tried from November 6, 2023, leads with an agreement theory, because Android allows sideloading and Epic cannot claim, as it did against Apple, that no other way exists to load apps. Two episodes carry that theory. Epic had an agreement with the device maker OnePlus to make Epic games available on OnePlus devices through the Epic Games app, and OnePlus cancelled the deal over concerns that the arrangement would have it bypassing the Google Play Store. The trial will also examine a deal between Google and Samsung that Epic says was designed to prevent the Galaxy Store from becoming a competitive threat and to require Google Play Billing on apps distributed by Samsung. Epic places both inside the "Anti-Fragmentation Agreements" (AFAs) it cites, which prevent Android OEMs from modifying Android to allow frictionless downloading of apps outside Google Play.
