# Epic's Google case rests on the Microsoft browser-monopoly precedent

Epic Games' antitrust trial against Google, which began on November 6, 2023 and concerns Google Play's app distribution and its 15% to 30% commissions, is analogically dependent on the Microsoft antitrust rulings, as reported by TechCrunch. In those earlier cases, courts found that Microsoft had a monopoly over the operating system and was abusing it by making it hard for users to download alternative browsers, like Netscape, on Windows devices. The analogy carries weight because Android, unlike Apple's iOS, permits sideloading — so Epic's theory of the case runs on a dominant platform owner's agreements constraining rivals rather than on the absence of any alternative.
