# Bankman-Fried's own definition of "risk-neutral": a world-destroying coin flip

In her testimony on 2023-10-10, Alameda Research's former head Caroline Ellison described a coin-flip thought experiment Sam Bankman-Fried used: if tails destroyed the world and heads made it twice as good, he would still flip. He called that stance being "risk-neutral" — a term The Verge read as a fancy way of saying he was a gambling addict. Ellison, who pleaded guilty to fraud, conspiracy to commit fraud, and money laundering, said Bankman-Fried "directed me to commit these crimes," and put the figure at "We ultimately took about $14 billion, some of which we were not able to pay back."
