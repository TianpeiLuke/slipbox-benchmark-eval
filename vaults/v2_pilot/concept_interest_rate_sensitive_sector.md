# Interest-rate-sensitive sectors: equities priced off the policy rate

An interest-rate-sensitive sector is an equity grouping whose share prices move more than the market when the policy rate changes, because its cash flows are long-dated or its customers depend on credit. Rates rising compress valuations in both cases, since higher yields on government debt make riskier assets less attractive at any price.

In The Sydney Morning Herald's reporting of 1 October 2023, healthcare and consumer companies were named among the weakest sectors on the ASX as markets expected the RBA to keep rates on hold, with National Australia Bank forecasting another hike to 4.35 per cent in November. The 10-year US Treasury yield sat at 4.58 per cent, near its highest since 2007.
