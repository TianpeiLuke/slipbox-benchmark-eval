# Steam Deck OLED Weighs 30 Grams Less Despite Bigger Battery and Fan

In its Steam Deck OLED review (Engadget, 2023-11-09), The Steam Deck OLED weighs about 30 grams less than the original Steam Deck model, or the equivalent of five quarters. That reduction comes despite Valve adding a bigger battery and a larger fan to the existing chassis around a die-shrunk version of the custom AMD APU.
