# Danielle Hunter's Case for Defensive Player of the Year, December 2023

As of Sporting News' awards-odds coverage dated 2023-12-05, with five weeks left in the NFL regular season, Danielle Hunter was the longshot candidate for Defensive Player of the Year behind the betting favorites Micah Parsons, Myles Garrett and T.J. Watt.

The argument is twofold. On production, Hunter led the league in tackles for a loss and ranked third in sacks with 13.5, trailing only Khalil Mack and Watt. On impact, his play was instrumental in the Minnesota Vikings turning things around after a 1-4 start.

The claimed obstacle is perception rather than performance: Hunter lacks the name recognition of Parsons, Garrett and Watt, even though he has played as well, if not better, in 2023. The conclusion offered is narrower than a trophy prediction — sustained production earns him the right to be in the conversation.
