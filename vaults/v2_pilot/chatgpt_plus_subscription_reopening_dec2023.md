# OpenAI Re-opens ChatGPT Plus Sign-ups, 13 December 2023

On 13 December 2023 OpenAI re-enabled sign-ups for ChatGPT Plus, its paid subscription tier, according to a TechCrunch timeline of ChatGPT product updates first published 28 September 2023 and updated through December 2023. Sign-ups had been paused in November 2023, when OpenAI attributed the pause to a "surge of usage." CEO Sam Altman announced the reversal on X: "we have re-enabled chatgpt plus subscriptions! 🎄 thanks for your patience while we found more gpus." The Plus subscription includes access to GPT-4 and GPT-4 Turbo.
