# U.K. Judicial Office Guidance Permitting Judges to Use ChatGPT

Guidance issued by the U.K. Judicial Office permits judges in the United Kingdom to use ChatGPT, along with other AI tools, to write legal rulings and perform court duties. TechCrunch records the item in the December 2023 section of its running ChatGPT timeline, in an article dated 2023-09-28 and updated afterward. The guidance also lays out ways to use AI responsibly in the courts; the two measures TechCrunch names are being aware of potential bias and upholding privacy. TechCrunch's account is abbreviated: it does not reproduce the full set of conditions the Judicial Office attached to the permission, so the boundaries of what a U.K. judge may automate in a ruling cannot be settled from this source alone.
