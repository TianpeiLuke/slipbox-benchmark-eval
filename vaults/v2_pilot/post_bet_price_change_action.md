# Once a Bet Is Locked, a Moving Line Can Only Be Answered With New Action

Sporting News (November 6, 2023) states that when you bet, you lock in the odds or line as currently posted. A wager already placed cannot be repriced if the market later moves in your favour, so the available procedure is to respond with fresh action rather than to revisit the original ticket. Because lines shift when the public bets heavily on one side, and because each sportsbook's book differs, a subsequent move usually appears as improved odds or a more favorable line somewhere else. The step is to place additional action there, at that new price.
