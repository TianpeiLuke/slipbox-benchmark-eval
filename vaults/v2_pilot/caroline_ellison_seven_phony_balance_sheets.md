# Caroline Ellison's testimony on phony balance sheets and Alameda's use of customer funds

Caroline Ellison, Sam Bankman-Fried's ex-girlfriend, testified at his trial that she created seven phony balance sheets. Writing in The Verge on 2023-10-26, the account of her testimony records that she was clear on two points: Alameda Research took customer funds, and Bankman-Fried knew about it. He even invested those customer funds against her advice. Ellison, alongside Gary Wang and Nishad Singh, is now a felon, with their lives irrevocably changed by possible imprisonment, house arrest, restitution requirements, barred job categories, and the publicity.
