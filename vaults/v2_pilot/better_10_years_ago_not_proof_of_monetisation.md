# Web Growth, Not Monetisation, Is The Simpler Explanation For "Google Search Was A Lot Better 10 Years Ago"

Running through the discussion of Google's search dominance in *The Age* article of 2023-10-22 is the recurring claim that "Google Search was a lot better 10 years ago". The counter-argument: conceding that perception, the article calls it a stretch to attribute any decline to Google's monetisation practices — the suspicion that it uses its ubiquity to squeeze people for money even at the expense of product quality. A simpler explanation is available in the sheer growth of the web Google must index.
