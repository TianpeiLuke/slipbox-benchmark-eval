# Jane Street: Sam Bankman-Fried's Pre-FTX Trading Employer

Jane Street, the high-frequency trading firm, is the finance employer Sam Bankman-Fried worked for before founding the crypto exchange FTX and its hedge fund Alameda Research. The identification comes from testimony by his MIT friend in the Southern District of New York trial, reported by *Fortune* on 2023-10-04; Bankman-Fried left Jane Street before recruiting friends into Alameda and FTX.
