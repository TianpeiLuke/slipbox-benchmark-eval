# The emergency meeting that ended with Alameda staying open

Gary Wang testified at Sam Bankman-Fried's fraud trial on 2023-10-06 that, following early-November 2022 media reports detailing Alameda Research's business practices and its ties to FTX, Bankman-Fried convened an emergency meeting with Wang and FTX engineering head Nishad Singh to discuss shutting Alameda down. The participants ended that meeting with the decision not to proceed, Wang said, because he and Bankman-Fried knew Alameda had no way to repay the roughly $14 billion hole in its books.
