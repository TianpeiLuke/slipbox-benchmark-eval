# Ellison testified FTX Ventures' $2 billion came from money allocated to Alameda

During week two of Sam Bankman-Fried's FTX fraud trial, Caroline Ellison, former CEO of the trading firm Alameda Research, testified in detail that FTX Ventures — the $2 billion venture fund — was funded with money that had previously been allocated to Alameda by third-party lenders. TechCrunch reported Ellison's account of the fund's funding source in trial coverage dated 2023-10-06.
