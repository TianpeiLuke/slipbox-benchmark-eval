# Pichai changed Google Chat's history-off default in February 2023 after the DOJ litigation hold

Sundar Pichai testified on 30 October 2023 in the U.S. antitrust trial against Google that he personally acted on the history-off default in Google Chat in February 2023, in order to comply with the Department of Justice's litigation hold, according to CNBC's account, as reported by TechCrunch on 31 October 2023. Google's history-off option deleted internal chat messages every 24 hours unless a user changed the setting; the DOJ had accused Google in February of systematically destroying chats this way.
