# FTX as Greek Tragedy: One Flaw, a Ruined Chorus

The central argument of the Verge's 2023-10-26 trial coverage is that the FTX case "is a Greek tragedy, precipitated by one man's character flaw: the arrogance of Sam Bankman-Fried." The chorus destroyed by that flaw is the cast closest to him: college roommates Adam Yedidia and Gary Wang, the co-founder of FTX and Alameda Research; longtime family friend Nishad Singh; ex-girlfriend Caroline Ellison; parents Joe Bankman and Barbara Fried; and brother Gabe Bankman-Fried. Bankman-Fried himself faces seven counts, two of wire fraud and five conspiracy charges, while Wang, Singh, and Ellison ended up felons. Most of the chorus was never accused of running the fraud — Yedidia testified under immunity, having once texted Bankman-Fried "I love you Sam. I'm not going anywhere" — so their ruin comes from proximity to the flawed protagonist rather than equal culpability.
