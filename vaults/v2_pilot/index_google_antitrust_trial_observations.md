# Index — Five revelations from the Google antitrust trial, 30–31 October 2023

A breakdown published by TechCrunch on 30–31 October 2023, anchored on Google CEO Sundar Pichai's Monday testimony in the U.S. government's antitrust trial over Google's 2020-era search dominance, organizes the week's disclosures into five points.

1. Default-payment totals: $26.3 billion paid in 2021 across browsers, phones and platforms including Apple, Samsung and Mozilla, roughly $18 billion of it to Apple, per search head Prabhakar Raghavan's 28 October testimony.
2. The Chrome-promotion ban: Google agreed not to promote Chrome to Safari users.
3. The iPhone preinstall bid: Pichai's 2018 attempt to get Tim Cook to preinstall Google on every iOS device.
4. Deleted chat logs: history-off default vs. the DOJ's litigation hold.
5. Google's early letter on defaults: a 2007 email citing 15% and 27% swings.
