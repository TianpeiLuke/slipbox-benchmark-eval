# Thai Food Near Me: A Manhattan Restaurant Named After a Google Search

Thai Food Near Me is a small Thai restaurant on a busy street in the Murray Hill neighborhood of Manhattan, co-owned by four people, one of them Teddy Jirapraphanan. Its name is a literal Google search string. "Everywhere I go, I'm craving Thai food," Jirapraphanan says. "I have to search 'Thai food near me' all the time." The name seemed silly and unserious to the owners at first; some were embarrassed to tell friends about it, for fear of it sounding too ridiculous. Jirapraphanan is exactly the kind of customer Thai Food Near Me hopes to scoop up. (The Verge, 2023-10-26)
