# Rate-hold bets and higher-for-longer pricing weigh on ASX healthcare and consumer stocks

On 1 October 2023, *The Sydney Morning Herald* reported that most ASX sectors traded lower, but interest-rate-sensitive sectors — healthcare and consumer companies — were among the weakest, as the market priced in expectations that the Reserve Bank of Australia would keep rates on hold at its October meeting, with the RBA due to meet on Tuesday. Those expectations sat on top of higher-for-longer pricing: the National Australia Bank forecast another rate hike, to 4.35 per cent, in November. The consumer softness showed up in individual counters, with Treasury Wine Estates down 1.9 per cent and Woolworths down 1.2 per cent, taking the consumer staples sector 1.1 per cent lower.
