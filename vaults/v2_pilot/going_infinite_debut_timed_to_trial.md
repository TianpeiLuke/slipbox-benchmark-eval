# Sources behind the TechCrunch SBF/FTX report: Michael Lewis's "Going Infinite" and his 60 Minutes interview

The TechCrunch article dated 2023-10-02 about Sam Bankman-Fried (SBF) draws on two pieces of Michael Lewis material. The first is Lewis's book "Going Infinite," on SBF's rise and fall, which the article says debuts on the first day of SBF's trial in Manhattan Federal Court. The second is Lewis's interview broadcast on 60 Minutes "yesterday," that is, 2023-10-01. Both surfaced in the week before the FTX fraud trial began. Lewis's earlier books include "Liar's Poker," "Moneyball" and "The Big Short."
