# Sherman Act: the U.S. antitrust statute behind Epic's claims against Google

The Sherman Act is the U.S. antitrust statute Epic Games planned to invoke against Google at the trial that began November 6, 2023, taking Section 1 and Section 2 claims alongside California's Cartwright Act and Unfair Competition Law. Section 1 concerns restraints of competition; Section 2 concerns monopolization. Epic's case rested on arguing that Google restrains competition within two separate markets: the distribution of apps to Android users, and the market for payment processing solutions for content inside Android apps.
