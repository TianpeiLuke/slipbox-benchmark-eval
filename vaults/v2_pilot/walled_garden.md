# "Walled garden" in The Verge's Epic v. Google coverage: a closed platform controlling distribution and payment

In its December 12, 2023 account of Epic's jury verdict against Google, The Verge sets "walled garden" in quotation marks as a descriptor for Apple — Epic's "rival," which the piece notes "almost entirely won a similar case two years ago." In that usage, the term denotes a closed mobile platform whose owner controls both app distribution and in-app payment: the same two powers the nine-person federal jury found Google held an illegal monopoly over, in "Android app distribution and in-app payment systems," and had illegally tied together through "its Google Play billing system to its app store." Apple's verdict, by contrast, went Epic's way only in part.
