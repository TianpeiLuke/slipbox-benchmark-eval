# Contents map: Verge's Tim Sweeney post-verdict interview (12 Dec 2023)

Navigation for The Verge's 12 December 2023 interview with Tim Sweeney, conducted the night after the Epic v. Google verdict. Sections in order:

- Verdict facts: a nine-person federal jury found Google holds an illegal monopoly over Android app distribution and in-app payment systems, and illegally tied Google Play billing to its app store — unlike the Apple case Epic "almost entirely" lost two years earlier.
- Sweeney's role and courtroom presence: Epic CEO, co-founder and controlling shareholder; attended every day of the four-week trial save one.
- Historic significance: first major tech antitrust verdict since US v. Microsoft in the 1990s.
- Courtroom surprises: jury attention, rapid verdict, relief.
- Google's alleged conduct: leaks to reporters, payments to developers, carriers and OEMs.

The published text is truncated at "a trillion-dollar compa" — before Project Hug.
