# ChatGPT users reported "lazier" GPT-4 behaviour in November 2023; OpenAI confirmed the issue on 8 December

Starting in November 2023, ChatGPT users reported that the chatbot felt "lazier" than usual, citing simpler answers and refusals to complete requested tasks, according to TechCrunch's running ChatGPT timeline. Some attributed it to the "winter break hypothesis," the argument that AI performs worse in December because it "learned" to do less work over the holidays; others suggested the chatbot was simulating seasonal depression.

OpenAI confirmed it was aware of the issue but said it was not sure why it was happening. In a post on 8 December, the company's @ChatGPTapp account stated that GPT-4 had not been updated since 11 November and that the behaviour "certainly isn't intentional," adding that model behaviour "can be unpredictable" and that OpenAI was looking into fixing it.
