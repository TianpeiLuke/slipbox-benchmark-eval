# Epic's hypothesis in Epic v. Google: sideloading that Google makes cumbersome is not effective competition

Epic Games' antitrust trial against Google, which began November 6, 2023, rests on a testable proposition. Because Android permits sideloading, Epic cannot claim, as it did against Apple, that there is no other way to load apps. So Epic hypothesizes that Google makes sideloading and alternative app stores cumbersome enough that their formal availability is not effective competition — a claim Epic will support with the figure that 90% of apps are still downloaded through Google Play.

The hypothesis is falsified if consumers install alternative stores such as Samsung's Galaxy Store or Amazon's Appstore at scale absent Google's agreements with device makers and developers, including the Anti-Fragmentation Agreements (AFAs) that bar OEMs from enabling frictionless downloading outside Google Play.
