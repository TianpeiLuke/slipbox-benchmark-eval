# AI governance as a fight over who owns a trillion-dollar future

The public argument over whether artificial intelligence should be controlled or set free is also a contest for ownership. According to a December 9, 2023 report in The Age, the debate "has pitted some of the world's richest men against one another": Elon Musk, Larry Page, Mark Zuckerberg of Meta, tech investor Peter Thiel, Satya Nadella of Microsoft, and Sam Altman of OpenAI. Each "have fought for a piece of the business – which one day could be worth trillions of dollars – and the power to shape it." The report names the paradox at the centre of that competition: the people who say they are most worried about AI are among the most determined to create it and enjoy its riches, justifying their ambition by claiming they alone can keep AI from endangering Earth.
