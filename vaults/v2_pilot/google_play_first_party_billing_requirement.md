# Google Play's first-party billing requirement

As of the Epic Games v. Google trial that began on November 6, 2023, Google required apps selling digital content on Google Play to use its own first-party billing system, Google Play Billing. That rule is separate from the commission rate Google charges on those sales, which the source states as 15% to 30%, and from exceptions to it, such as the User Choice Billing pilot.
