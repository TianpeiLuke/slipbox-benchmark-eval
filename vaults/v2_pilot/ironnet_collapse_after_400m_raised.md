# IronNet, Keith Alexander's cybersecurity venture, shuts down after $400 million raise

IronNet, a Virginia-based cybersecurity startup founded in 2014 by retired four-star general and former NSA director Keith Alexander, has shut down and laid off its remaining staff, as reported in TechCrunch's Week in Review dated 2023-10-07. The company had raised more than $400 million in funding. Despite that capital, IronNet failed to gain traction after going public in August 2021. Its stock price continued to steeply decline in the wake of an initial spike following the listing.
