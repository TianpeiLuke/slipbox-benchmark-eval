# Sept 17, 2023: Ian Eagle's "blank space" call starts NFL Swift-lyric references

On Sept 17, 2023, Travis Kelce caught a touchdown pass from Patrick Mahomes, and CBS Sports' Ian Eagle called it: "Kelce finds a blank space for the score," per a CBSSports.com timeline article dated 2023-10-12. The article's author treats that broadcast call as the first of "many, many, Swift lyrics being referenced during NFL games," labeling the day "The references begin" and tagging it with the lyric "I watched it begin again."
