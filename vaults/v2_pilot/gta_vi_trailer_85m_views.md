# First Grand Theft Auto VI trailer hit 85 million YouTube views in 22 hours, breaking MrBeast's 24-hour record

The first trailer for Grand Theft Auto VI, published by Rockstar Games, accumulated 85 million views within 22 hours of release. That pace surpassed the prior record for most YouTube views in 24 hours, which had been held by a MrBeast video, as reported in TechCrunch's Week in Review newsletter covering the week of 2023-12-09. The franchise's previous entry, Grand Theft Auto V, remains the second-best-selling video game of all time behind Minecraft.
