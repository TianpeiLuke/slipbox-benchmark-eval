# A French word-length test suggests Gemini's multilingual weakness

On December 6, 2023, Benjamin Netter posted on X that Gemini Pro could not produce a six-letter word in French, writing "FYI, Google Gemini is complete trash." On December 7, 2023, TechCrunch's Devin Coldewey replicated the task by running the prompt "Can you give me a 6-letters word in French?" through Bard, which was then serving the "lite" model Gemini Pro, and reports that the model answered with a seven-letter word — a second failure of the same kind. Coldewey offers this as corroboration of outside reports of weak multilingual performance in Gemini, and frames it accordingly: two anecdotal prompt failures, one by a user and one by the author, not a measured multilingual benchmark. Translation "doesn't appear to be Gemini Pro's strong suit."
