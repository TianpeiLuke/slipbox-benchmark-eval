# Sports Betting Lines Move With Public Action and New Information

According to Sporting News (2023-11-06), betting lines and odds are not static once posted by sportsbooks. They are subject to change based on the betting public's reaction and any additional information that comes to light ahead of the game — sportsbooks will move the line or change the odds if the public bets heavily on one side. Because the betting is rarely the same at every sportsbook, one may be forced to move a line while another is not.
