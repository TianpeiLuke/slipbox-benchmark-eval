# US v Google's ripple effect on parallel Big Tech antitrust cases

A ruling in US v Google may have a ripple effect on other Big Tech cases, since enforcers need one landmark judgment to demonstrate antitrust law remains relevant. The parallel matters named in a TechCrunch roundup dated 2023-11-13: the FTC sued Amazon in September for using anticompetitive and unfair strategies to illegally maintain its monopoly power; the DOJ has been investigating Apple for years over the company's policy for third-party apps on its devices and whether it unfairly favors its own products; and there's an ongoing case between the FTC and Facebook, wherein the agency calls on Facebook to sell Instagram and WhatsApp. The proposition is falsified if those cases proceed and settle unaffected by the Google judgment. Judge Amit Mehta hears the Google case.
