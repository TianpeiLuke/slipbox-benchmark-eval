# Caroline Ellison's "$14 billion" figure on the stand, 2023-10-10

Testifying at Sam Bankman-Fried's criminal fraud trial on 2023-10-10, Caroline Ellison — head of Alameda Research during the implosion of Alameda and FTX, already pleaded guilty to fraud, conspiracy to commit fraud, and money laundering — said Bankman-Fried "directed me to commit these crimes" and stated: "We ultimately took about $14 billion, some of which we were not able to pay back." The remark, quoted at the end of The Verge's fifth paragraph, was repeated in the seventh as she described the losses.
