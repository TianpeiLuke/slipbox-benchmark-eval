# Inter and Real Sociedad Level on 12 Points After San Siro Goalless Draw

Inter Milan and Real Sociedad played out a 0-0 draw at San Siro on 12 December 2023, the final matchday of the 2023/24 UEFA Champions League group stage. The pair finished Group D inseparable on 12 points each from six games, both with a record of 3 wins, 3 draws and 0 losses. Real Sociedad took first place on goal difference, +5 against Inter's +3.
