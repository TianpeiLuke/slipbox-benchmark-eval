# Three Sustained "Objection, Narrative!" Against Bankman-Fried on Direct Testimony

During Bankman-Fried's own direct testimony on the morning of October 28, 2023 — the first day the FTX founder took the stand in his own defense — reporter Lauren Feiner of The Verge tallied three objections of "Objection, narrative!" to Bankman-Fried's answers that Judge Lewis Kaplan sustained. Her observation of that morning's testimony: "the man really loves the sound of his own voice." Cross-examination by prosecutor Danielle Sassoon had not yet begun.
