# Hypothesis: resumed US student-loan repayments divert household dollars from consumption

Hypothesis, as reported by The Sydney Morning Herald on 2023-10-01: the resumption of US student-loan repayments may funnel dollars away from the consumer spending that has helped keep the US economy afloat, so post-September retail and personal-consumption data should decelerate in a way attributable to debt repayment. It would be falsified if those releases show no such deceleration. Supporting logic in the source: spending growth cooled in August alongside cooler inflation, and Brian Jacobsen of Annex Wealth Management cited student loan debt repayments among factors slowing a hot summer.
