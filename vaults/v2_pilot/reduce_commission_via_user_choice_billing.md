# Opting into Google Play's User Choice Billing to cut commission by 4%

A Google Play developer can lower the platform's standard 15% to 30% commission by 4% through User Choice Billing, the alternative-billing option Google first introduced in November 2022 and which remained in pilot testing as of November 6, 2023.

Steps, as implied by the program's terms:

1. Confirm eligibility: the option is open to all developers selling apps in the 35 markets where User Choice Billing is available, including the U.S.
2. Stand up your own payment processing solution for in-app sales rather than relying solely on Google's mandatory first-party billing system.
3. Opt into User Choice Billing; the 4% reduction applies to companies that use their own payment processing.

Spotify and Bumble were the initial testers, and Match took advantage of the option as part of its settlement with Google; Epic Games declined to use it. Google's own framing is that its commissions are not tied solely to billing, and 99% of its developers already qualify for a commission of 15% or less based on revenue.
