# Procedure: Betting NBA Rookie of the Year Futures (Sporting News, 2023-10-04)

Sporting News (2023-10-04) gives these steps for an NBA Rookie of the Year futures bet, once you hold a funded account at a legal online sportsbook:

1. Open the basketball section of the sportsbook website.
2. Select the NBA.
3. Enter the futures tab, where Rookie of the Year odds and NBA MVP odds are posted.
4. Choose your Rookie of the Year selection — Victor Wembanyama was the early favorite, with Scoot Henderson and Chet Holmgren also cited.
5. Enter the stake amount and finalize the bet slip.

Postcondition: a recorded futures ticket on that player, settling at season's end. Offshore sportsbooks are illegal and offer no fraud protection.
