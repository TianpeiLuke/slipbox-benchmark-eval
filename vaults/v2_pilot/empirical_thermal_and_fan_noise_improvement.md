# Steam Deck OLED kept the reviewer's palms cooler during back-to-back play, with no extra fan noise

Comparing the Steam Deck OLED against the original LCD Steam Deck in back-to-back play sessions for an Engadget review dated 2023-11-09, the reviewer found the OLED's grip left their palms noticeably cooler. Valve had fitted the mid-cycle refresh with a larger fan alongside its die-shrunk custom AMD APU, and that bigger fan produced no additional audible noise.
