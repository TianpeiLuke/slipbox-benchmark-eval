# Google's rebuttal to Epic: open Android and you hurt its users and its competitiveness

Opposing the remedy Epic Games sought at the trial that began November 6th, 2023 — breaking up Google's control of Android app distribution and in-app payment processing so developers stop paying the "Google tax" — Google argued, as reported by The Verge on November 5, 2023, that a win for Epic would rebound against Android itself. In Google's telling, it could make Android phones less safe by dismantling basic protections against sideloaded apps. It would also damage Android's ability to compete with the iPhone, because it (arguably) can't run a competitive app store by giving it away for free. The safety and competitiveness predictions are Google's own claims about a hypothetical outcome, not established findings, and The Verge flags them as such with "according to Google, anyhow."
