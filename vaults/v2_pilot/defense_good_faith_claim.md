# Cohen's defence opening: "Sam didn't defraud anyone"

In his opening statement on 4 October 2023, defence lawyer Mark Cohen staked Sam Bankman-Fried's case on a single claim: "Sam didn't defraud anyone" — the FTX former CEO a nerdy startup founder who acted in "good faith," not the prosecution's "cartoon of a villain."
