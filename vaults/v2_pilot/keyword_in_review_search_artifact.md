# Silva's vegan-keyword search and his light bulb moment about Google presence

Felix Silva, owner of Barber Shop Near Me in Coral Springs, Florida, frequently searches Google for keywords like "vegan" to find restaurants. One time, Google returned results for a restaurant that wasn't vegan but had a customer review containing the keyword, highlighted.

"It was a light bulb moment… like, 'Okay, this is the path I need to take. I really need to dial in and focus on the online presence, specifically Google, because that is going to be the foundation of the business," Silva said, as reported by The Verge on 2023-10-26.

The anecdote is his own demonstration that a business's Google presence, not its actual offering, decides what turns up in search.
