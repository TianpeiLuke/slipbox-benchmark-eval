# Cue's rebuttal to the DOJ: Apple does not measure the Google ISA as a share of its profits

On September 26, 2023, in the US v. Google antitrust trial before Judge Amit Mehta in Washington, DC, Justice Department lawyer Meagan Bellshaw pressed Apple's senior vice president of Internet Services, Eddy Cue, on the Information Services Agreement (ISA) — the default-search deal with Google, in place since 2002, that pays Apple billions of dollars a year and which Cue renegotiated with CEO Sundar Pichai in 2016. Against her point that the ISA accounts for a significant portion of Apple's profits, Cue replied that Apple does not look at the money that way: the profits figure omits the work Apple did to make its platform attractive enough for the deal to perform. He had already argued the deal was about more than economics, and that "Certainly there wasn't a valid alternative to Google at the time."
