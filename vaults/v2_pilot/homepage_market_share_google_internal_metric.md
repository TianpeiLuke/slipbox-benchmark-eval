# Homepage Market Share as Google's Search Growth Lever (2007)

"Homepage market share," as used in a 2007 Google product strategy meeting, means the share of users' browser homepages occupied by Google. Meeting notes sent to CEO Sundar Pichai and other Google leaders framed it as the lever for search growth: "Nitin argues that focusing on homepage market share is one of the most effective things we can do to make gains in search market share." Data presented at that meeting showed that when people changed their browser homepage to Google they performed 15% more Google searches, and when they switched away their Google searches fell 27%. DOJ lawyer Meagan Bellshaw put the 2007 email in front of Pichai during the antitrust trial on October 30, 2023.
