# Kelce Told Listeners the Friendship Bracelet He Meant for Swift Never Reached Her

On the July 2023 episode of *New Heights*, the podcast Travis Kelce shares with his brother, Kelce said he had intended to give Taylor Swift a friendship bracelet with his phone number on it during her Eras Tour concert in Kansas City. The bracelet never made it to her wrist, and Kelce likewise never got her digits. This is the first documented contact attempt in the timeline The Age published on 26 September 2023 under the section heading "Cruel Summer."
