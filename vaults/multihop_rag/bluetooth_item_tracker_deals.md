# Bluetooth Item Tracker Deals

Four Bluetooth trackers were discounted in The Verge's under-$25 Black Friday roundup. The Apple AirTag was $24, down from $29 — 17 percent off, with a four-pack at $80; it helps you find lost items with ultra wideband technology, and while you get the best compatibility with an iPhone, Apple released an Android app that can detect an AirTag's location and notify you if one seems to be following you.

The Tile Pro was $25, down from $35 — 29 percent off, at Amazon and Best Buy. It is the company's loudest Bluetooth tracker and has the widest range at 400 feet, and it has a user-replaceable battery, unlike other Tiles.

The 2022 Tile Slim was $20, down from $35 — 43 percent off at Amazon, $25 at Best Buy. It is as thin as two credit cards, so you can stash it in a wallet, passport holder, or luggage, and it features a 250-foot range plus compatibility with both Android and iOS devices.

Chipolo's Card Spot was down to $20.94, $14 off, at Best Buy — one of its best prices to date. It is another slim Bluetooth tracker you can fit into your wallet, with IPX5 water resistance, and it can tap into Apple's extensive Find My network.
