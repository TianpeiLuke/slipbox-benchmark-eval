# November 10 Reporting On Punishment Day

On Friday, Nov. 10, 2023, reporters tracked Michigan's travel to Penn State hour by hour as the college football world waited for the Big Ten's decision, which ESPN's Heather Dinich reported was expected to be announced early Friday afternoon while the team left for State College at 1 p.m. CT ahead of Saturday's game. Dinich reported that sources indicated a Big Ten response was coming early that afternoon and that Michigan would leave at 1 and fly midafternoon out of Detroit after clearing security checks, per usual routine.

The decision came right down to the wire. Adam Rittenberg reported that Michigan's administration had not heard from the Big Ten as of that hour, that the team would begin preparations to leave for Penn State around 1 p.m. ET, and that going down to the wire with any discipline was, as several sources noted, not really fair to players. Austin Meek reported Harbaugh boarding the bus, and observers on the ground in Ann Arbor had him heading to the airport with his team as Michigan awaited word. Harbaugh's attorney Tom Mars told ESPN he fully expected Harbaugh to fly with the team: "I'm confident he's going to be on that plane one way or another."

Dinich also mapped the possible outcomes and a limit on the conference's own authority. She reported the punishment could be anything ranging from "indefinite suspension to public reprimand or lofty fine," and cautioned against assumptions while noting that a suspension was certainly on the table. She added that anything more than two games or a fine of $10,000 would require special permission from an executive board composed of league representatives.
