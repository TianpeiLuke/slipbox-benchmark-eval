# Nicolas Jackson's Goal Disallowed For Offside

Chelsea saw a third goal of the first half disallowed at Tottenham on Nov. 6, 2023 when Nicolas Jackson put the ball in the net on 37 minutes but Raheem Sterling was offside in the buildup, just past the midfield stripe.

Sporting News judged there was very little argument to be had, and the game played on. While it was a decision that featured little, if any, controversy, it added to the mayhem of the half and contributed to the game's overall narrative.
