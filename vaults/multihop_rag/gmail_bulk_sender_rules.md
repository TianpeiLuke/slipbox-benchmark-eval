# Gmail's New Rules For Bulk Senders

Google announced a series of significant changes to how Gmail handles email from bulk senders, and said that starting the following year those senders must authenticate their emails, offer an easy way to unsubscribe, and stay under a reported spam threshold. The announcement came in the week covered by TechCrunch's October 7, 2023 Week in Review, and its stated purpose is to cut down on spam and other unwanted email.

The requirements a bulk sender must satisfy, as announced, are:

1. Authenticate its outgoing email.
2. Offer recipients an easy way to unsubscribe.
3. Keep its reported spam rate under a threshold.

Two scope conditions matter. First, the rules are addressed to bulk senders, not to all Gmail correspondents. Second, they were announced in October 2023 as taking effect "next year," so they were not yet in force at the time of the announcement.
