# Queen Elizabeth's Reluctance to Accept Camilla

It took a while for Queen Elizabeth and Camilla to grow close, and according to historian Robert Lacey the Queen was reluctant to accept Charles's request to be more accommodating to Camilla — going further, in the record, than The Crown shows: unlike in the drama, she is said to have once referred to Camilla as "that wicked woman" to Charles's face.

As The Crown season six begins, Charles is frustrated at the public for demonising Camilla long after his split from Diana. He asks the Queen whether she had received the invitation to Camilla's 50th birthday; she says she has, but cannot attend as she is in Derbyshire. When Charles points out that plans "can always be changed", she replies: "Why would we want to change it?" She does not attend, but later tells Prince Philip that she does not want Camilla to be considered "wicked, because she's not", or to be unkind to her.

The relationship changed over time. The Queen grew to become closer to Camilla, and despite an announcement in 2005 that Camilla would become the Princess Consort rather than Queen Consort, it was announced that Camilla would be queen seven months before Elizabeth's death.
