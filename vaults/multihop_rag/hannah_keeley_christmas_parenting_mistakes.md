# Hannah Keeley's Five Christmas Parenting Mistakes

"The biggest mistakes parents make at Christmastime" is a set of five holiday blunders identified by parenting expert Hannah Keeley, the Virginia-based expert who set them out in an email to Fox News Digital published on 9 December 2023.

The organising premise is that pressures are high for parents and caregivers around the holidays, which means there is room for error. Keeley identified the top five holiday blunders that parents often make, and presented them as things parents should try to avoid rather than as a diagnosis of any particular family.
