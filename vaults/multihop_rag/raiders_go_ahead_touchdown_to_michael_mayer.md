# Raiders' Go-Ahead Touchdown To Michael Mayer

At 10:45 p.m. on Nov. 12, 2023, Aidan O'Connell faked a sweep to Josh Jacobs and threw a touchdown pass to a leaping Michael Mayer in the end zone — the first touchdown of the game, with 14:10 left — putting the Raiders up 16-9 over the Jets.

New York answered with explosive plays but only three points. Starting at the 25 at 10:47 p.m., the Jets traded big gains at 10:48 p.m. as Breece Hall caught a screen pass and burst down the left sideline for 35 yards to the Las Vegas 40, then Garrett Wilson made a wide-open grab for 13 to the Raiders' 28. The drive stalled at 10:51 p.m.: a trick-play throw from Garrett Wilson to Lazard missed, a low Zach Wilson throw to Cook was missed as well, and after Wilson escaped pressure for a yard the call was to Greg Zuerlein for the field goal, making it Raiders 16, Jets 12.

Las Vegas then set about running clock. The Raiders started at the 25 with 11:58 to play at 10:54 p.m.; at 10:56 p.m. O'Connell's deep shot to Davante Adams was covered double, Jacobs raced up the middle for nine yards to set up third-and-1, and picked up that yard on the next carry. At 10:58 p.m., facing third-and-8, O'Connell hit Austin Hooper just shy of the marker, leaving a fourth-and-1 decision from the Las Vegas 44, on which C.J. Mosley was injured and had to come off. At 11 p.m. came a gutsy call by Pierce: the Raiders faked a sneak and handed off to Carter, who raced around the left end and past the defense for 15 yards and a first down to the Jets 41 — 15 yards on fourth-and-inches. At 11:01 p.m. Zamir White took his first carry of the day and appeared to lose the football after gaining five, but the Raiders recovered; Jacobs then took the next handoff and, though seemingly bottled up, was pushed ahead by the line for seven yards and a first down to the Jets 29, with Las Vegas grinding away the clock and 6:14 left to play.
