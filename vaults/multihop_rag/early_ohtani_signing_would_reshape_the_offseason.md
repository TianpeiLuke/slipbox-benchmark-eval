# An Early Ohtani Signing Would Reshape The Offseason

Reporting from Alden Gonzalez of ESPN on Nov. 11, 2023 raised the possibility that Shohei Ohtani could sign before the Winter Meetings, and Sporting News argued that pretty much everyone in baseball would love that to happen — because an early resolution benefits both the signing team and every other club.

For the signing team, the gains are commercial and competitive: it will kick the Ohtani hype machine into gear as soon as possible, with merchandise to sell, and the front office will use the signing to help lure other potential free agents, maybe even for a "play with Ohtani" discount. For the other teams, the warrant is opportunity cost — knowing Ohtani is off the board relatively soon into the offseason would allow them to shift to other priorities, like trading for Juan Soto or deciding which free-agent pitchers to pursue with the money that might otherwise have been earmarked for Ohtani.
