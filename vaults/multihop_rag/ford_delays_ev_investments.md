# Ford Delays EV Investments

Ford is delaying about $12 billion in planned investments on EVs, including construction of a second battery plant with joint venture partner SK On, due to softening demand for higher-priced premium electric vehicles, TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-30.

The mechanism TechCrunch identifies is a price ceiling rather than an absent market: while EV sales have grown, consumers aren't willing to pay a premium for an EV over a gas or hybrid vehicle, and that price pressure has squeezed profits — in the case of Ford's EV business, causing losses to grow.
