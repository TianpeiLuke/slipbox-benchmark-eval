# Third Quarter Sequence Of Packers-Chiefs Week 13

Green Bay led Kansas City 21-12 at the end of the third quarter of the Week 13 "Sunday Night Football" game, having traded touchdowns with the Chiefs through the period, per Sporting News' live log of Dec. 3, 2023.

The half opened with Kansas City moving the ball. At 9:47 p.m. Mahomes hit Kelce with a floated effort as the Chiefs opened the second half with a flourish, and at 9:54 p.m. a delicate touch pass between the Magic Man and Kelce produced yet another first down on third-and-long, taking Kelce up to 81 yards on four grabs with Kansas City threatening to breach the red zone. At 9:57 p.m. the Chiefs finally got into the end zone courtesy of an Isiah Pacheco scamper through the midway, but Pacheco was unable to find the target again on the two-point conversion, keeping the Packers in control at 14-12.

Green Bay answered immediately. At 10:03 p.m. Love hit Dillon out in front, who made his defender miss before racing beyond the marker for a first down, the first-year starter continuing to make the right play in the cold. At 10:10 p.m. Love placed a throw perfectly between triple coverage to a streaking Romeo Doubs to put Green Bay on the doorstep of the end zone once more, and at 10:11 p.m. he lined one into the path of Watson, who leapt high to rip it from the sky for the touchdown that made it 21-12. At 10:23 p.m. Pacheco, described as as hard-nosed as they come, carried an entire pile on his back to take the ball to the goal line, a showing by the second-year ball-carrier that had him over the century mark on the day.
