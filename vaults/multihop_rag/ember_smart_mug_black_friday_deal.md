# Ember Smart Mug Black Friday Deal

Ember Smart Mugs came down to record-low prices for Black Friday 2023: a 10-ounce mug for as low as $90, or a 14-ounce cup for as low as $110.

Engadget says these make great gifts for anyone otherwise hard to shop for, provided they enjoy a hot beverage every now and then. Used with their companion app, the mugs can keep drinks at an optimal temperature for up to 80 minutes, or even longer if the cup is kept on its charging coaster.
