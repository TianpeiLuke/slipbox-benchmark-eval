# Streamers Do Not Represent Typical Player Behavior

Against the assumption that Twitch streamers stand in for players generally, Polygon's 2023-10-19 piece holds that the playing habits of a Twitch streamer do not represent the average player by any means — while conceding that content creators certainly shape public opinion about games.

The premise under attack is that streamer sentiment reflects independently formed consumer opinion. "I don't at all think that streamers are embodying some typical player behaviors, like 'Brandon,'" the anonymous AAA developer says, posing a name for an imaginary everyday consumer.

His follow-on point is that the resemblance runs the other way: "There's definitely a model of consumers nowadays who probably parrot those sentiments. They did not arrive at those conclusions themselves."
