# How Wonder Fixes 2D Mario's Difficulty Pacing

The claim is that Nintendo has addressed a long-standing gripe about the pacing of previous 2D Marios — the removal of the rush to get to the "good stuff."

The grounds are the structural problem in earlier games. Because they are meant for both experienced gamers and children who might be new to the franchise, they often locked more challenging levels behind the story's completion, which meant the beginning of the game could be a slog as you rushed through easier levels to get to the more challenging bits.

The fix, and the warrant connecting it to the claim, is that Wonder adds difficulty ratings and hidden paths that lead to 5-star levels long before you finish the game, so the harder content is available from early on. And the reviewer adds that the harder content is well tuned: 4- and particularly 5-star levels are balanced just right, so completing one feels like a rewarding challenge without crossing over into punishing.
