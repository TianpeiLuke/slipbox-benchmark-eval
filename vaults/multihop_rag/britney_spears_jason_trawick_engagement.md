# Britney Spears' Engagement To Jason Trawick

Britney Spears and Jason Trawick, a Hollywood agent who worked at WME, began dating in 2009 and became engaged in December 2011, but called off the engagement in January 2013 and announced they were going their separate ways.

Spears said in a statement sent by her representative to ABC News: "Jason and I have decided to call off our engagement. I'll always adore him and we will remain great friends." Trawick issued his own statement at the time: "As this chapter ends for us a new one begins. I love and cherish her and her boys and we will be close forever."
