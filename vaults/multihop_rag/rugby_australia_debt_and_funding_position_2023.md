# Rugby Australia's Debt And Funding Position In 2023

Rugby Australia was forced to take out a $40m loan from Ares to keep the game afloat, and as of Oct. 10, 2023 had around another $15m left to spend from it while looking to borrow up to another $60m.

The purpose of the additional borrowing is to be in the strongest possible position to capitalise on two major money-making events — the 2025 British and Irish Lions series and a home men's World Cup — and succeed. RA is also in the early stages of looking to renew its next broadcast deal, hoping to at least double its current arrangement with Nine Entertainment/Stan Sport, which pays $29m per year until the end of 2025.

The states have their own claims on that money. The QRU expressed its desire for Rugby Australia to reinstate the annual $1.7 million in funding to each of the Super Rugby franchises that was withdrawn following the Covid pandemic to help keep the game afloat, and expressed satisfaction that RA was willing to discuss reinstating it. Meanwhile, The Roar understands the ACT Brumbies sent RA confirmation of solvency for the next 12 months.
