# John Hynes Contract And Predators Permission

John Hynes, who was let go by the Predators after the previous season, was technically in the final season of his contract with Nashville when Minnesota hired him — which meant the Predators had to grant permission to their divisional rival to speak to their former head coach, and that Hynes would be paid by both Minnesota and Nashville that season, per Yardbarker on Nov. 27, 2023.

Now behind the bench of his third NHL franchise, he faced an uphill climb to get Minnesota back in the playoff picture. Hynes would suit up behind an NHL bench for a ninth consecutive season, having been fired by the Devils and hired by Nashville midway through the 2019-20 campaign.
