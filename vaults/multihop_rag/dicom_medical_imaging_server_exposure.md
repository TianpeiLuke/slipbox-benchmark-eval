# DICOM Medical Imaging Server Exposure

Thousands of exposed servers are spilling the medical records and personal health information of millions of patients due to security weaknesses in a decades-old industry standard designed for storing and sharing medical images, TechCrunch reported on 9 December 2023.

The standard, known as Digital Imaging and Communications in Medicine (DICOM), is the internationally recognized format for medical imaging. As discovered by Aplite, a Germany-based cybersecurity consultancy, security shortcomings in DICOM mean many medical facilities have unintentionally made private data accessible to the open web.
