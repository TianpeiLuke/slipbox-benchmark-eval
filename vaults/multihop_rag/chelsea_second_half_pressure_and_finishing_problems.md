# Chelsea's Second-Half Pressure And Finishing Problems At Tottenham

Chelsea quickly made their one-man advantage count upon the restart, but their recurring end-product issues reared their head, as Palmer rifled wide in the 47th minute in a fast start from the Blues. Poor finishing looked to be their Achilles heel: Jackson flicked over the bar from James' cutback pass following a short free-kick.

Tottenham seemingly responded three minutes later as substitute Rodrigo Bentancur flicked a free-kick behind for Eric Dier to volley into the top-left corner, but Dier was adjudged offside as Bentancur nodded the ball into his path.

Chelsea eventually clung on for a fourth league win of the season after Bentancur narrowly put an 86th-minute header wide, as Spurs valiantly tried to chase an unsuccessful equaliser in which Son saw a 93rd-minute shot denied by Sanchez.
