# LinkedIn's New AI Tools

LinkedIn unveiled a string of new AI features spanning its job hunting, marketing and sales products in the week covered by TechCrunch's October 7, 2023 Week in Review, as reported by Ingrid.

Three of the announced features are named: a big update to LinkedIn's Recruiter talent sourcing platform, with AI assistance built into it throughout; an AI-powered LinkedIn Learning coach; and a new AI-powered tool for marketing campaigns.
