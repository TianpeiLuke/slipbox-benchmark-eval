# AirPods Pro Second Generation Lightning Deal At Walmart

The second-gen AirPods Pro with a Lightning port were $169 in Walmart's Black Friday 2023 sale — their lowest price to date — Mashable reported on 25 November 2023.

For comparison, the roundup notes that Amazon "has never put them on sale for less than $189", and frames the Lightning model as the pick "if you're not ready to make the switch over to USB-C just yet".

These wireless earbuds from autumn 2022 "sound wonderful and have almost uncanny active noise cancelation", said Mashable senior editor Stan Schroeder, and they last for up to six hours on a single charge.
