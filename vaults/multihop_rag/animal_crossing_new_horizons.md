# Animal Crossing: New Horizons

Animal Crossing games have long been loved for their chill farming sim vibes and adorable villagers, and Polygon says Animal Crossing: New Horizons perfected the genre — a pick it rates great for newcomers to consoles and veterans of the genre alike.

The game sticks you on an island where you design a cute house, harvest materials, catch bugs and fish, and court animal villagers. Time passes as it does in real life, so the game paces itself perfectly for daily engagement, with tasks to accomplish and fees to pay Tom Nook, the raccoon who lends you all of your supplies and lodging. It is also social: you can visit other friends' islands, so friends who also have the game on Switch can give you a tour and lend a hand by gifting bells or other items.
