# Jacksfilms' Criticism of SSSniperwolf's Reaction Videos

Jack Douglass has made content on YouTube since 2006, primarily focusing on comedy videos, and in the past has called out "reaction" content that he says fails to meet the bar for fair use — even making parody videos reacting to reaction content. Polygon reported this on 2023-10-25.

Within the last year he set his sights on Alia Shelesh's content, citing a lack of credit or links to original creators and their work, and a lack of transformative commentary. Shelesh has been a content creator for roughly a decade, making YouTube and TikTok videos, and her recent work is largely reaction content posted to both platforms; she has been aware of Douglass' commentary, even making jokes about him in her own reaction content.

In addition to a video callout, Douglass revived an old joke channel called JJJacksfilms, poking fun at Shelesh's content and celebrating times she deleted clips. He also regularly made content on Twitch reacting to and even grading her reaction content, complete with a bingo game for viewers to play. The JJJacksfilms streams were designed to apply pressure to Shelesh, encouraging her to credit original creators and provide substantial commentary.
