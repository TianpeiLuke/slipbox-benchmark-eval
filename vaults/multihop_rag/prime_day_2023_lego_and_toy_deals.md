# Prime Day 2023 Lego And Toy Deals

WIRED's toy picks include a kids' commemorative Lego theme set, an Optimus Prime action figure, a Transformers playset, a card-slotting kids' speaker, Lego artificial flowers, Squishmallows, a 1,060-piece Lego Ninjago set, a 4,049-piece Avengers: Age of Ultron set, and the Lego City Stuntz Ultimate Stunt Riders Challenge.

The commemorative set is the kids' version rather than the enormous 6,000-piece adult set, and such sets tend to increase in value if kept sealed for a few years — though it was $34 less when the sale first started. On the action figure: one of the most gratifying parts of parenthood is realizing your children are fascinated by the same toys and characters you loved, and it never fails to blow the writer's mind that their 6-year-old knows who Optimus Prime is; this figure transforms from a truck into a robot to save Earth from the evil Decepticons and would make a great holiday present. Adrienne's 6-year-old son has the playset, paid full price for in a moment of weakness: incredibly sturdy, lasting several years of being stomped on and thrown into battle with other playsets, with the Grimlock T-Rex motorcycle obviously the coolest. The durable, portable kids' speaker handles bedtime stories, music, and other content via slotted cards, with a kid-friendly radio and sleep mode, appearing in WIRED's Best Kids Speakers guide as suitable for ages 3 to 12. Lego's artificial flowers — roses, poppies, daisies and snapdragons — solve the problem of a beloved vase whose flowers don't last. The pancake-stack Squishmallow, complete with a butter-and-syrup flower garnish, leads a Prime Day sale that also includes a narwhal, hedgehog, mushroom, and possum. Editor Adrienne So says her kids were obsessed with Lego Ninjago for a hazy few years — the show was originally commissioned as a limited run then extended for its popularity — and the biggish 1,060-piece set is reasonably priced for a relatively simple build with a ton of minifigs. The Avengers set is from the 2015 film Age of Ultron, with three light-up arc reactors and compatibility with the separately sold Iron Man figure. And the City Stuntz Ultimate Stunt Riders Challenge, the most interactive pick, comes with a 360-degree loop, a ring of fire, an "alien tower" vertical climb, a ramp, two toy motorcycles, and four Lego City minifigures, with three stunt challenges configurable in a variety of ways — at the lowest price WIRED has tracked so far.
