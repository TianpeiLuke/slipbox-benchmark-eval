# NBA Christmas Day Games Tradition

The NBA's Christmas Day games are an annual holiday fixture older than the NFL's Super Bowl, dating back to 1947, when the league featured three games as a festive treat for basketball fans, as Essentially Sports set out on Dec. 25, 2023.

Since then, NBA Christmas Day has only grown in stature and significance. Both it and the NFL's Thanksgiving tradition hold a special place in the hearts of fans, creating an annual spectacle that goes beyond the typical sporting event. The discriminating measure of its standing is audience: the previous season, Christmas Day games averaged 4.27 viewers across all platforms, and these games attract about 200% more viewers than any other regular season game.
