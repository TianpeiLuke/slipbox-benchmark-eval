# Warriors 2021 Draft Kuminga And Moody

In June of 2021, Jonathan Kuminga and Moses Moody sat on a stage on the Chase Center court, with then-GM Bob Myers to their left, as the Warriors introduced their newest first-round draft picks — and by Yardbarker's Nov. 29, 2023 assessment, the A-grade the picks drew has not stuck.

Myers said at the time: "We're excited. I talk about the character first because that's what's going to make that A-grade stick or not. It's gonna be on what they do from here on out, not what they've done so far." The Warriors did receive glowing grades from draft analysts following their selections of Kuminga, out of the G-League Ignite program, at No. 7, and Arkansas' Moody at No. 14. More than two years later, both players were struggling to make the sort of impact the Warriors had hoped for when they flaunted their two-timeline plan: Kuminga, though he had flashes and was only 21, was almost as raw as he had been as an 18-year-old, playing nearly 20 minutes per game for a thin Warriors team while his shooting percentages — 43.8% overall, 19.4% on threes — dipped to career lows. Moody had been the more stable of the two, a reliable catch-and-shoot threat at 38.2% from three and an earnest defender, but did not do much outside the limits of his job description.
