# Badges And Level Variety In Super Mario Bros. Wonder

Badges are Wonder's customization mechanic: they are earned by completing specific levels, and some may even be required to find secrets or reveal alternate exits. Some allow you to jump farther or float, while others make extra coins appear, and there are protective badges too — like the one that automatically saves you if you fall into lava or poison. It is a mechanic that feels inspired by recent roguelikes such as Hades, and it does a great job of adding customizability and replayability.

The level design is similarly varied. Wonder features six main worlds, each with a distinctive theme, but within those you will still run into reimagined haunted houses, pirate ships, underwater stages and more, along with a number of familiar baddies and some new foes across an incredible range of environments.

Even without the aid of a Wonder Flower, the sheer variety of level types is impressive. Alongside classic stages capped off by grabbing a flag, there are races, badge challenges, puzzle levels and more. KO Arenas let you pick your power-up of choice — Fire Flower, Elephant Suit, Bubble Mushroom or the Drill Hat — before taking down waves of enemies, while Break Time levels provide quick 30-second bursts of excitement. You will never be bored, even if you want a change from standard platform jumping.
