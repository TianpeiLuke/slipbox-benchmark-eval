# Kishida's Pitch to Global Investors 2023

Japanese prime minister Fumio Kishida was preparing, in the week of 1 October 2023, to make his strongest ever push for a bet on Asia's biggest advanced economy, as BlackRock founder Larry Fink and other heads of global funds gathered in Tokyo for a series of events to attract investment. "I would urge you to evaluate what we are doing in my country, look at the underlying strength of our economy and our plans for the future and then invest in Japan," Kishida said in a recent speech at the Economic Club of New York.

The pitch followed a month of conferences on the same question. Throughout September, in Tokyo, New York, Hong Kong and London, the world's biggest investment banks hosted a series of conferences for the world's biggest investors, all gravitating around the theme of whether Japan is really back. The events were run by JPMorgan, Goldman Sachs, Bank of America, Mizuho, BNP Paribas and Jefferies, and according to both attendees and hosts the answer is now clearer than it has been in a very long time: Japan is back, but global investors may take a little more persuasion that it is truly back for the duration.
