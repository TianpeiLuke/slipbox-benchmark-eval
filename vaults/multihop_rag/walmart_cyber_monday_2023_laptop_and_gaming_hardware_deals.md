# Walmart Cyber Monday 2023 Laptop And Gaming Hardware Deals

WIRED's Walmart Cyber Monday roundup of 27 November 2023 picked out a Nintendo Switch OLED bundle, two mainstream Windows laptops, a PlayStation DualSense controller and the LG DualUp monitor as the computing and gaming hardware worth buying in the sale.

The Nintendo Switch OLED (8/10, WIRED Recommends) typically sells for $349, but the discounted bundle adds a digital download of Super Smash Bros. Ultimate and a three-month Nintendo Switch Online membership; those extras usually cost $60 and $10 respectively, so WIRED framed it as roughly $70 of bonuses thrown in for free. On laptops, WIRED said it likes the 14-inch version of one model, which carries most of the same components — an AMD Ryzen 7 (7730U) processor, 16 gigabytes of RAM and 512 gigabytes of storage with a 16-inch touchscreen — calling that plenty of power for just about everything and the large screen nice for editing photos and browsing the web, while warning it is not a good choice for video editors or gamers. The second laptop is the newer, faster version of one of WIRED's top picks in its Best Cheap Laptops guide, with a newer 13th-generation Intel i5 chip, more RAM (16 gigabytes, which WIRED calls a must-have with Windows 11) and a nicer IPS display for the same price as the guide's pick; again it is not a gaming rig, but fast enough for everyday tasks like web browsing and editing documents.

The DualSense controller deal covers several different colour options at the same price, and WIRED's favourite part of the controller is the haptic feedback, which delivers directional and immersive sensations — almost like surround sound, but in a controller — rather than random rumbles. The LG DualUp monitor is singled out because, while nearly any monitor can be rotated vertically for a work-from-home setup, the DualUp is built to be vertical with a wider 16:18 aspect ratio, so less content is cut off and it is less narrow on the eyes; it also has great colour and ships with an arm mount that makes it easy to set up.
