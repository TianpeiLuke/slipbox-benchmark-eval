# Sonos Black Friday 2023 Sale

Sonos kicked off its Black Friday sale for 2023, bringing a range of discounts on smart speakers and home theater gear. The deals include the flagship Sonos Arc soundbar down to $719, a $180 discount; the mid-range Sonos Beam (Gen 2) at $399, a $100 drop from $499; and the more basic Sonos Ray at $223, a $56 drop — all three of which are recommendations in Engadget's guide to the best soundbars. In other home theater deals, the Sonos Sub (Gen 3) subwoofer was $160 off at $639, while the more compact Sonos Sub Mini was $86 off at $343.

Elsewhere, the Sonos Roam portable speaker was down to $134 from its usual $179, while the Sonos Roam SL — effectively the same device without built-in microphones — was down to $119 from its usual $159; the Sonos Era 100 smart home speaker was $50 off at $199, with the Roam and Era 100 both highlighted in Engadget's guide to the best smart speakers. For adding Sonos functionality to existing audio gear, the Sonos Amp was $599, a $100 discount, and the Sonos Port was $399, a $50 discount. Engadget likes Sonos speakers for delivering relatively clean and balanced sound and a straightforward app that connects to numerous music services, though their main appeal is their ability to link up with the company's other devices in one connected audio system; deals on any Sonos gear are fairly uncommon, and Sonos says this sale will run through November 27.
