# GitLab Q3 Earnings Preview, December 2023

GitLab Inc. (GTLB) was due to post its Q3 results after the market closed on the Monday following Seeking Alpha's December 3, 2023 week-ahead preview, going in with a split verdict: Seeking Alpha's Quant Rating system suggested a Hold on valuation concerns, while Wall Street analysts advocated a Buy.

As of the December 3, 2023 preview, the stock had surged 10% for the year — the specific basis for the Quant system's valuation concern. Consensus estimates ahead of the print were EPS of -$0.01 and revenue of $141.04M. The accompanying Earnings Insight noted that GitLab had beaten both EPS and revenue expectations in eight straight quarters, so the estimate figures should be read against an unbroken recent record of upside surprises.
