# StarNews Mobile's $3M Pre-Series A Round

Janngo Capital, an Africa-focused venture capital firm, led StarNews Mobile's $3 million pre-Series A funding round announced on 30 October 2023, and the other notable investors included the soccer players Aurélien Tchouaméni of Real Madrid, Jules Koundé of Barcelona and Mike Maignan of AC Milan, all affiliated with the athlete representation agency Excellence Sport Nation. Kamgaing highlighted the shared African heritage of these players, underscoring their connection to StarNews' mission and their genuine interest in engaging with the African audience and investing in startups across the continent. They join the ranks of investors like Blaise Matuidi, a former French international who has previously invested in the Sudanese fintech company Bloom and currently heads Origins, a VC firm run in collaboration with other soccer players who serve as limited partners.

The round is aimed at creator payouts and market expansion. Kamgaing revealed that the platform distributed $1 million to content creators in the prior year while maintaining an average monthly payout of around $70,000 to those creators, and the fresh injection of capital serves as a significant uplift for Africa's creator economy — an underserved sector in African tech, despite startups like the e-commerce-focused Selar holding their own. The funding will equip the Los Angeles-based company, which then had 40 employees, to better support its creators and gradually enhance payouts in the coming years, a crucial step in bolstering the region's creative industry. In a press statement Kamgaing outlined expansion plans to strengthen the startup's dominant presence in Francophone Africa while quickly expanding across strategic markets like Nigeria and Ghana, and on the call he said StarNews is looking to be in 11 markets by next year.

Fatoumata Bâ, founder and executive chair of Janngo Capital, affirmed the firm's support for StarNews, accentuating the platform's unique positioning, strategic partnerships, technology and content. That support is rooted in the belief that StarNews is well-positioned to capitalize on the immense growth potential of Africa's creative industry, which she said is poised for a fourfold expansion by 2050 and the possibility to generate up to $20 billion in GDP.

Fernand Tchouameni, spokesperson and investment advisor for Aurelien Tchouameni, framed the investment in diaspora terms: "I am very excited and proud to back StarNews, the first media platform that supports African creators and tells the world the real African story. Being of Cameroonian descent, I can also see StarNews becoming a bridge to connect with the African diaspora, and I personally look forward to being on it."
