# England And Scotland Lineup Changes For The Nations League Finale

England made two changes for the Dec. 5, 2023 Nations League finale at Hampden: Beth Mead returned to the starting lineup and Esme Morgan replaced Jess Carter.

The changes were made to the side that had come from two goals down to earn Friday's late 3-2 win against the Netherlands that kept hopes of progression alive. Mead started for the first time since suffering an anterior cruciate ligament injury just over a year earlier, having proved her worth coming off the bench at half-time against the Dutch to insert some directness into England's play. Pedro Martínez Losa swapped four players from the Scotland side that earned a 1-1 draw with Belgium on the Friday, with Lee Alexander notably replacing the former England international Sandy MacIver in goal.
