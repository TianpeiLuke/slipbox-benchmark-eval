# Denise George's Firing As US Virgin Islands Attorney General

Denise George, the 16th attorney general of the US Virgin Islands, was summarily dismissed on New Year's Eve after spending more than three years trying to seek justice in the Epstein matter. The dismissal came four weeks after the Epstein estate deal was struck and four days after George filed the suit against JPMorgan; the official news arrived from the Office of the Governor while she was cooking callaloo, Caribbean greens, at her father's house, when a security officer arrived at the door and handed her a letter.

The pressure preceding it was persistent. One after another, texts, calls and emails rolled in on the subject of the Epstein files with the message: wrap them up — ASAP. People in St. Thomas wanted the Epstein scandal, and the uncomfortable questions it was raising, to go away, and fast. George says she needed time.
