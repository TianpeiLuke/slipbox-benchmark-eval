# Walmart Cyber Monday 2023 Portable Bluetooth Speaker Deals

WIRED's Walmart Cyber Monday roundup of 27 November 2023 named two discounted portable Bluetooth speakers: Bose's Soundlink Revolve+ and the newest JBL Flip.

Bose's Soundlink Revolve+ offers excellent sound in a neat and water-resistant design with up to 17 hours of battery on hand. WIRED anchors the deal to the speaker's usual price: it normally sits well above $300, so this is the best time to grab this powerful portable.

JBL's Flip speakers offer excellent bang for your buck, including great sound that WIRED calls more detailed, powerful and poised than you would expect from a portable and waterproof design, and the newest model adds a separate tweeter for better detail. WIRED is explicit that this is only a modest sale, but argues that because you will almost always pay around $100 or more, it is still a great time to grab it.
