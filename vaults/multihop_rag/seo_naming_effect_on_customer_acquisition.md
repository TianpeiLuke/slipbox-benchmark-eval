# The SEO Naming Effect On Customer Acquisition

The Verge argued that Thai Food Near Me's name really did help the restaurant — but not through the mechanism its owners intended. The initial SEO "hack" was likely technically ineffective, but it was memorable and funny, and the joke led to many, many, many articles and forum posts about the restaurant's name, which tipped the SEO scales in its favor; all of that content, including The Verge's own story, will help the restaurant's prominence on Google platforms. One SEO trick, the reporter wrote, has eaten another.

The grounds for saying the direct hack failed are a test and a concession. A Google Maps search for "Thai food near me" from the reporter's home in Brooklyn yielded plenty of options literally near her but not Jirapraphanan's business a few miles away. Robert Sampson, a co-owner who handles much of the back-end work including SEO, followed the online discussion about the utility of the name and conceded part of it: "The people who said that [the name is] not such a good idea… I think they're responding more from a national brand campaign. It is true that if you're in California, and you type 'Thai food near me,' you're going to have a little bit of a hard time finding us. But for local search, I think the name works really well." Danny Sullivan's position was that a "near me" name is not a silver bullet for gaming the search algorithm.

The warrant connecting name to durable ranking is the feedback loop that follows a first visit. As with Thai Food Near Me, the most powerful thing an SEO-driven name might be able to do is get customers in the door; from there it is up to the business to give them a good experience, whether that is a great plate of pad see ew or the perfect haircut, and then the cycle continues — happy customers leave good reviews, and good reviews help the business's Google Maps profile rank higher. Felix Silva also uploads high-quality photos and videos to his page and shares updates, another SEO move, since some experts say active profiles can improve a business's rankings.
