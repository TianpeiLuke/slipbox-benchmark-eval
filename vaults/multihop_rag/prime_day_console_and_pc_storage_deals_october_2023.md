# Prime Day Console And PC Storage Deals, October 2023

Storage deals in The Verge's October 10th, 2023 Prime Day gaming roundup covered removable cards and internal drives. A 1TB SanDisk Extreme microSD card was $79.99 at Amazon, marked down from $89.99, a $13 discount — enough to store a whole lot of games on a Steam Deck or Nintendo Switch. The 512GB Samsung Evo Select microSD card was $30, down from $40, a 25 percent discount, offering extra storage for a Nintendo Switch, Steam Deck, or Asus ROG Ally.

On internal storage, the Western Digital WD_Black SN850X SSD was $70, down from $180, a 61 percent discount for the 1TB model with heatsink; it is one of the fastest M.2 drives around, with sequential read speeds of up to 7,300 MB/s and write speeds of up to 6,600 MB/s, and performs best in a PC with a PCIe 4.0 motherboard or in a PS5 with a requisite heatsink. The Western Digital WD_Black C50 Storage Expansion Card for Xbox Series X / S consoles was selling for a new low price of $124.99 at Amazon, $25 off.
