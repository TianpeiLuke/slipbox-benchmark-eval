# Owen Teale's Barry The Bear Route Into Acting

Owen Teale's acting career began with a car full of booze and a bear costume: suspended from his sixth-form college for organising a riotous Christmas party and in need of a job, he took work as a fairground mascot and was pointed towards drama school by co-workers, The Sydney Morning Herald reported on 20 October 2023.

"They held an investigation into the party and I was the first to be called in," says the 62-year-old Welsh actor, best known as gimlet-eyed master-at-arms Ser Alliser Thorne in Game of Thrones. "They'd found the empty bottles and people were falling over drunk. I said, 'yeah, I did it. It was me. I borrowed my mother's car, loaded it up with booze and smuggled it in'." Teale had no intention of joining his father at the limestone quarry that fed the steel works at Port Talbot; he wanted to escape South Wales and find a bigger world. A kindly teacher called Mr Davies suggested he might find work at Barry Island Pleasure Park, a fairground near Cardiff — "At the time, it was a rust bucket of a big wheel and one big dipper," Teale recalls. "But I went along and auditioned. I was hoping to be a magician's assistant or something like that, but they said 'you'd be great as the bear. Here's the costume'."

The role of Barry the Bear was deceptively hard: "His remit was to wander around the park greeting visitors, but you could only do an hour at a time because you sweated so much. I wore pyjamas under the costume to absorb the sweat, but it would fill the rubber hands. After a while, I'd get bored, sit on the big wheel and go round and round." The sight of a disconsolate, sweaty bear riding the ferris wheel caught management's attention and Teale was sacked — at which point a couple of co-workers, students from the Guildford School of Acting, suggested he audition at their alma mater. He was accepted, awarded a study grant, and Barry the Bear got a ticket out of Wales. He is due to play Scrooge in a Melbourne season of A Christmas Carol.
