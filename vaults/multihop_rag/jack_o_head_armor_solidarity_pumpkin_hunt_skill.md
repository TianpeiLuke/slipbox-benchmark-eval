# Jack-o'-Head Armor's Solidarity (Pumpkin Hunt) Skill

Once equipped, the Jack-o'-Head armor in Monster Hunter Now has the skill Solidarity (Pumpkin Hunt): a party-based skill where having multiple players with the skill active increases attack power, an effect increased by five during the Halloween event. Polygon described it on 2023-10-25.

That makes it a rather situational skill, since you not only need to be hunting with other players but to be doing so during the Halloween event, which ends after Oct. 31, to see the most benefit.

Polygon offers two reasons to craft it anyway: it is entirely possible the skill will appear in future armor pieces to increase its potency, such as during the next year's Halloween event, making it a sound investment for those looking to play with other hunters; and for solo players it might simply be a piece to add to a collection, especially if the ability to transmog — change the appearance of armor — comes to the game one day.
