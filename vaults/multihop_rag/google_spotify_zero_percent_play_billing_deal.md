# Google's Zero Percent Play Billing Deal With Spotify

The second piece of trial evidence Tim Sweeney called astonishing was the Spotify deal: Google gave Spotify a 0 percent fee and let Spotify process its own payments, keeping 100 percent — an arrangement Google did for Spotify and for nobody else.

Sweeney framed Spotify as the one company with negotiating power comparable to Fortnite's, and faulted it for using that power to cut a special deal with Google instead of fighting for the good of all developers. His summary of the consequence: "If you're a smaller developer than Spotify, you get screwed." He added that when Spotify does use Google's own payment service, it pays 4 percent rather than the 30 percent Google forces other developers to pay — and said that is what the rate should be, calling four percent "a perfectly reasonable rate for an unbundled payment system."
