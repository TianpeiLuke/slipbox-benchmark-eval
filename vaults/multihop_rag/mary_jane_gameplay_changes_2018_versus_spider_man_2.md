# Mary Jane's Gameplay Changes Between 2018 And Spider-Man 2

Essentially Sports catalogued on 1 November 2023 what changed mechanically for Mary Jane between Insomniac's 2018 Spider-Man and Marvel's Spider-Man 2, finding both similarities and drastic differences.

On the similar side, the first game had a mission where Mary gets to use a taser to shock the guards; there is no taser in Spider-Man 2, but she has a stun gun now. Both games also have moments of hiding and sneaking past enemies: in the original there were missions like the one in Grand Central Station where MJ had to stay hidden from a number of Mr. Negative's demons to defuse a bomb, and in Spider-Man 2 she is seen hiding from the hunters during the 'Wake Up' quest.

The difference lies in her abilities. In the 'Wake Up' mission she is seen sneaking past the hunters but is also capable of performing takedowns on them, which was not the case in the previous title — her missions have become better with the developers actually spending time giving her a chance to fight. She is pretty skilled with the stun gun during missions like 'Good Men', thanks to her training with Sable, and she now has the option to run away and hide when spotted by enemies. The playability factor around her has increased a lot compared with being just an observer for the most part in the first game, which the article calls the biggest and most visible difference.
