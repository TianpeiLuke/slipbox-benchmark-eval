# Why The Franchise Tag May Not Suit Chase Young

The 49ers could try to slap Chase Young with the franchise tag if they're worried about paying him and Nick Bosa big bucks long-term — the tag was worth just under $20 million last season, so on its face it may look like San Francisco's best avenue toward keeping its Super Bowl window ajar.

The counter-consideration is cap mechanics: one-year deals are often complicated to fit into smaller amounts of salary cap space, so the 49ers would likely prefer to get a long-term agreement in place with Young to keep him.

The Sporting News' conclusion is that franchising him may not be worth the hassle — even though he is easily the team's best free agent in 2024.
