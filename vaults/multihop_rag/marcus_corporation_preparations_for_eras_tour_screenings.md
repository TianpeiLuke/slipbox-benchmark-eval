# Marcus Corporation's Preparations For Eras Tour Screenings

Greg Marcus, chief executive and chair of the Marcus Corporation, headquartered in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, has been in the movie business for years but never expected to be urging moviegoers to take out their phones during a film — let alone crafting friendship bracelets in preparation for an opening weekend. In a promotion for his theater chain, he strings beads together while humming "Shake It Off."

"This is different," says Marcus. "Take your phone out. Take selfies. Dance, sing, get up, have a good time. We want to create an atmosphere." In his theaters, like many other chains, there will be friendship bracelet stations, and sound systems have been modified for more of a concert feel. While Marcus grants it will be strange to see an AMC logo before a film playing in his theaters, he doesn't particularly mind: "I'm just happy it's there."
