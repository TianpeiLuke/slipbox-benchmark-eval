# Deft's Multimodal Search Capabilities

Deft's search accepts both natural-language constraints and images. Users can search for things like "wooden lamps with three legs under $400," or upload an image and modify their search to "lamps under $400 that look like this." Users can also paste the image directly into the search bar — though it is hard to know you can do this if someone does not tell you it works, and the company is working on highlighting the image search feature better.

What distinguishes its indexing is the range of signals it reads. The company said it does not just look at the listing and description for search results; it also looks at materials, reviews, photos and more. This way, when someone searches for a "pet-friendly" couch, Deft can return results with stain and scratch-resistant material.

The catalog is deliberately bounded. Deft is currently focusing on furniture and home décor search but aims to expand to different categories along the way. It has built its own scraping tech to add products to its search results, and shows results from Target, Pottery Barn and other places on the web. It has not added searches from Amazon yet because it feels there are a lot of "junk" listings on the site and it will take some work to clean up that data.
