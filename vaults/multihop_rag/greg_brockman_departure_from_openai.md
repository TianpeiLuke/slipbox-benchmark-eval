# Greg Brockman Departure From OpenAI

OpenAI's longtime president and co-founder Greg Brockman was first removed as chairman of the board and then resigned from the company outright, citing "today's news" — the firing of Sam Altman on Friday, November 17, 2023.

By Brockman's own account, he got a text from OpenAI chief scientist and co-founder Ilya Sutskever shortly after noon on the Friday asking for a quick call. After Brockman sent a Google Meet link, he was told that he was being removed from the board as chairman "but was vital to the company and would retain his role" as president, and that Altman had been fired. Brockman then announced his resignation from OpenAI, sending a memo internally and publishing the text on X: "After learning today's news, this is the message I sent to the OpenAI team." He also posted jointly with Altman that "Sam and I are shocked and saddened by what the board did today," thanking the people they had worked with at OpenAI, their customers and their investors, and saying they were "still trying to figure out exactly" what had happened.

Brockman's account also places events the day before, on November 16: according to his post on X, Sutskever texted Altman on the Thursday evening about scheduling a Friday noon call, and Brockman alleges that Mira Murati, OpenAI's CTO and now interim CEO, was informed on Thursday night that Altman would be fired. Three senior OpenAI researchers resigned after Brockman, including director of research Jakub Pachocki and head of preparedness Aleksander Madry.
