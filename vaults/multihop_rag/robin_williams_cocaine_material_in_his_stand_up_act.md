# Robin Williams' Cocaine Material In His Stand-Up Act

Robin Williams wove cocaine into his stand-up jokes, and audiences laughed despite the light this shed on how deep his cocaine use ran, according to the Vice documentary account reported by FOX News Entertainment on 19 October 2023.

Howie Mandel recalled the pattern directly: "I can't tell you how many times Robin came onstage at The Comedy Store and a big part of his routine was cocaine and the drugs that he had done."

Mandel's assessment separates the comedy from what it described: "And even though everybody was laughing, and it was really funny, he was really doing a lot of cocaine. He found the funny side of it, but ultimately it was real and really, it's not funny, but that's who he is."
