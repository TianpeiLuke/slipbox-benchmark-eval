# Amazon Prime Air Pharmacy Drone Delivery

At the top of Amazon's 2023 robotics announcements was a pair of news items revolving around the Prime Air service: starting this year, customers in College Station, Texas, will be able to get medications from Amazon Pharmacy delivered via drone, and next year the service will launch in a third U.S. city as well as yet-to-be-named spots in the U.K. and Germany.

The service had its share of ups and downs over the years, including layoffs in 2020 and company-wide job cuts earlier this year, and Amazon is approaching the project with baby steps — it is currently limited to one city in Texas and another in California. Aside from difficulties scaling, there is also a whole bunch of regulation to contend with, and Amazon has worked with local and national governing bodies to ensure the same day delivery service complies; TechCrunch frames this as a brave new world with stumbles likely on the way to a potential future where delivery drones from companies like Amazon and Alphabet's Wing are a common sight overhead. Two things work in Amazon's favour on the pharmacy front: it doesn't trade in narcotics, meaning opioids won't be flying over anyone's heads, and the company is going to start rolling out the new MK30 drone, which it claims is significantly quieter than the last model.
