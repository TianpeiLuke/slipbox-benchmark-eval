# BetMGM Sportsbook: Deep MLB Markets And The Only NBA Live-Streaming App

BetMGM provides MLB bettors with an expansive portfolio and is known for comprehensive odds markets generally, but its distinguishing NBA feature is that its sports betting app is the only one that offers live streaming of NBA games, so bettors can watch the action as they bet.

For baseball, traditional bet types are readily available: moneylines, where one bets on the outright winner; run lines, focused on the margin of victory; and totals, predicting combined team scores. For those who want a deeper drop into the game there is a deep lineup of player and team prop bets, from forecasting a player's strikeouts to predicting a team's cumulative runs. For long-term visionaries there is an impressive array of futures bets, such as which team will clinch its division or which player will be honored as the league's Most Valuable Player. The online sportsbook and its intuitive betting app cater to a vast range of markets for all the premier sports, with NBA betting a significant highlight.

NBA promotions are abundant. Past promotions have included "bet $10, get $200," under which bettors receive 20-1 odds and their bet is deemed victorious as long as a single three-point field goal is scored in the game. For a new account, BetMGM Sportsbook usually has a bonus of up to $1,500, and if the first bet loses the value can still be recovered in bonus bets.
