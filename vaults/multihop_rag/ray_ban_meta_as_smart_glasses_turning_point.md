# Ray-Ban Meta As A Smart Glasses Turning Point

The Verge's reviewer argued that Meta has not reinvented smart glasses but has nailed the execution, and that the cultural timing is ripe: we are more desensitized than ever to surveillance, and a lot of us are trying to look less at phone screens. Her warrant runs by analogy — if smartwatches can field notifications, then maybe smart glasses can replace the camera and take calls in scenarios where phones are not ideal.

She kept the claim bounded. She has no idea whether these will succeed, and at heart remains a smart glasses skeptic; to her the device appeals most to gadget nerds and content creators rather than the average Joe. But she calls them the most practical smart glasses she has worn, and argues that even if they flop they have set a new bar for what smart glasses can and should be able to do — because, having tried a ton of them, she had never before had a pair that looks good, is priced reasonably, has multiple use cases, and delivers what it says it will.
