# The Report Of Ohtani's 'Affinity' For Boston

On Nov. 27, 2023, ESPN's Jeff Passan reported in passing that Shohei Ohtani "has an affinity for Boston" — an aside inside a piece about 10 teams to watch the rest of the offseason, placed in the section on the Red Sox and their need to sign Yoshinobu Yamamoto.

Passan's line was: "Boston's bats are good, or good enough at least, to avoid having to spend like mad in a down year for free agent hitters -- unless, of course, it's for Ohtani, who has an affinity for Boston. If the Red Sox don't hit that jackpot, they're backfilled nicely with a system that includes outfielder Roman Anthony, shortstop Marcelo Mayer and catcher Kyle Teel, among the best trios of offensive prospects in baseball."

Sporting News characterised it as not exactly breaking news, since rumors had been swirling, but as an aside from an in-the-know reporter that just might give one fan base a little too much hope and excitement — adding that it is cool when the best player in the world is a free agent and has an "affinity" for your town. The item was published after Thanksgiving, with the writers pleased Ohtani had not decided to sign while the turkey was still warm.
