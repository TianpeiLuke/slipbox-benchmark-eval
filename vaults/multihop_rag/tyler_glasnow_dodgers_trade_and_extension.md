# Tyler Glasnow's Trade To The Dodgers And Five-Year Extension

The Dodgers acquired right-handed starter Tyler Glasnow and outfielder Manuel Margot from the Rays on Thursday, Dec. 14, 2023 for RHP Ryan Pepiot and OF Jonny Deluca, and by Friday, Dec. 15 Glasnow had agreed to a five-year, $135 million extension in Los Angeles.

The Athletic's Ken Rosenthal first reported on Wednesday, Dec. 13 that the Rays and Dodgers were working on a trade sending Glasnow to L.A. with Manny Margot also going to the Dodgers and Pepiot and Deluca going to Tampa Bay; Rosenthal clarified the deal was not complete and changes were possible, but the base of the deal was Glasnow to the Dodgers. ESPN's Jeff Passan reported the trade coming to fruition the next day, noting Glasnow was expected to sign an extension as a result of the trade: the 30-year-old was in the final year of a two-year deal he signed with the Rays in 2023 and was set to be a free agent in 2025. Passan then reported the extension figures on Dec. 15 — a five-year, $135 million deal beginning in 2024 that could reach up to $145 million if the Dodgers picked up a $30 million option at the end of the contract, and, unlike Ohtani's deal, containing no deferred money.
