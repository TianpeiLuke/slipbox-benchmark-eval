# JLab Go Air Pop Budget Earbuds

The JLab Go Air Pop was down to $16.44 for Prime Big Deal Days, from $25 — about $6 less than usual.

Engadget highlights this set in its budget wireless earbuds guide: it gets you a compact shape, IPX4-rated water resistance, eight or so hours of battery life and a full set of functional touch controls for dirt cheap.

Its boomy sound is not exactly great, but it is serviceable, which is a victory when you are talking about $16 wireless earbuds. And while it lacks a companion app, JLab bakes three EQ presets into the earbuds themselves.
