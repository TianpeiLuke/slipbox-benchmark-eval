# Steam Deck OLED Thermals

Playing the Steam Decks back-to-back highlighted another significant quality-of-life improvement: heat management. The reviewer's palms have gotten toasty playing the OLED model, but they haven't broken a sweat like they often do with the original.

The new fan may be larger, but it's not any louder, and it clearly works a treat.
