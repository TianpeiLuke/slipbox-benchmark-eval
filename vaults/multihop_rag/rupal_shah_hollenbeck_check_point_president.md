# Rupal Shah Hollenbeck, Check Point President

Rupal Shah Hollenbeck has led Check Point for almost two years as of November 2023, and is one of the few C-suite level women in tech: nearly 17% of tech companies currently have a woman serving as CEO, according to AnitaB.org, which supports women in computing and tech.

She is a seasoned executive. After Intel, where she spent more than two decades, she spent time at Oracle, and she sits on the board of Neythri Futures Fund, which invests in female founders tapping into a growing base of South Asian female leaders. Although she has been in tech for nearly 30 years, Seeking Alpha reports that it has not gotten easier to be a woman in a male-dominated industry.
