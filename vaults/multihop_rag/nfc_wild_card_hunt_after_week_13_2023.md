# NFC Teams Outside The Playoff Field After Week 13 2023

The Los Angeles Rams (6-6) sat eighth in the NFC after Week 13 of the 2023 season, first among the teams outside the seven-team playoff field, per Sporting News on Dec. 3, 2023.

The order below the cut ran: 8. Los Angeles Rams (6-6); 9. Seattle Seahawks (6-6); 10. Tampa Bay Buccaneers (5-7); 11. New Orleans Saints (5-7); 12. New York Giants (4-8); 13. Chicago Bears (4-8); 14. Washington Commanders (4-9).

Sporting News judged the Rams and Seahawks the best wild-card bets among that group, while the Saints and Buccaneers would still have their sights on making the playoffs by winning the weak NFC South over the Falcons.
