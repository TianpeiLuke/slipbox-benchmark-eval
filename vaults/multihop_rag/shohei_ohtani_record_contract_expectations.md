# Ohtani Had Already Received Several $500 Million-Plus Offers

Jon Heyman of the New York Post reported on Sunday, Dec. 3, 2023 that Shohei Ohtani had already received several deals topping $500 million, which could mean he wound up being the first $600 million player in MLB history, per Sporting News' winter meetings tracker.

Heyman added that there were believed to be five teams in the running for Ohtani, and Sporting News noted it was no surprise the bidding was heating up with the star expected to sign soon.

It had been expected since before he hit free agency that Ohtani would set the record for the largest fully guaranteed contract in North American sports history, with Jeff Passan reporting the deal could reach as much as $600 million.
