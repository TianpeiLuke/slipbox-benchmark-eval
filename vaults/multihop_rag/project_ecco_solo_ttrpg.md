# Project ECCO

Project ECCO is a solo TTRPG of time travel and cosmic horror, picked by a Polygon contributor for whom solo games have become the primary method of gaming because getting a consistent group together is near-impossible.

The contributor's stated selection problem is balance: they need a game whose mix of narrative and mechanics is not overwhelmingly crunchy but also not so open-ended that it produces choice paralysis, and Project ECCO found that balance. It is easy to get into and relatively quick to play, at three to 10 hours per game, with hundreds of narrative prompts, unlockable time travel "devices" such as coins, dice, and tarot cards, and a layout the contributor calls absolutely stunning. Played in the pages of a yearly planner, it hits every beat of a good time-travel story — time loops and timeline divergences, a shady and controlling Time Travel Agency, and an unknowable cosmic Entity consuming spacetime — and as you play you literally burn through the days of your planner, creating an artifact marked and changed by the telling of your agent's story. For an example of gameplay, the contributor points to the Project ECCO miniseason of My First Dungeon with game designer Elliot Davis.
