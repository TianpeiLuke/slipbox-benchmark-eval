# Chicago Fire-Inter Miami Second Half: Four Goals In 23 Minutes

A goalless first half at Soldier Field on Oct. 4, 2023 gave way to five second-half goals, with Chicago beating Inter Miami 4-1. Before the restart, Gerardo Martino received a strange yellow card after coming straight out to the center circle to discuss things with the referee, apparently chastised for how late Inter Miami returned from the locker room; Chicago head coach Frank Klopas told the Apple TV+ broadcast he wanted his team to be "more calm on the ball," acknowledging they had chances but had not taken them. In the 47th minute Robert Taylor gave a loud grunt and held his shoulder after an innocuous touch off his chest, but appeared to be fine.

Chicago struck first in the 50th minute: DeAndre Yedlin, having pushed up the field on the attack, completely lost his runner at the back post and dogged it back in transition, and Shaqiri made him pay. Three minutes later Inter Miami levelled — in the 52nd minute Jonathan Dean, prone on the ground with his arms outstretched as he turned his back to the shot, handled a Benjamin Cremaschi effort and was lucky to receive only a yellow card, and Josef Martinez slammed the penalty home with Chris Brady going the wrong way. Klopas made the first substitution in the 55th minute, withdrawing Brian Gutierrez — pulled with a sore knee, per the broadcast — for Maren Haile-Selassie.

Inter Miami then spurned two clear chances and were punished. In the 58th minute Josef Martinez squared to Facundo Farias on the break, but the young Argentine missed wide left with the goal gaping; in the 59th Martinez again unlocked the defense but Benja Cremaschi, with only the goalkeeper to beat, put a golden opportunity wide across the face of goal. In the 61st minute Taylor's cheeky backheel found Farias, whose shot was blocked, with Chicago under siege. Then in the 62nd minute a sensational vertical pass from Fabian Herbers released Haile-Selassie — Serhiy Kryvtsov completely failing to see the runner — and the substitute finished coolly for 2-1. In the 66th another defensive calamity let Herbers deliver an inch-perfect lateral cross around both defenders for Haile-Selassie to slide in and finish, Drake Callender getting a slight touch but unable to keep it out. Martino responded in the 68th with Jean Mota and Nick Stefanelli on for Dixon Arroyo and Robert Taylor. In the 73rd Farias gave the ball away in midfield, Gaston Gimenez fed Shaqiri, and Shaqiri split two defenders, cut inside onto his left foot and buried it inside the near post past a helpless Callender for 4-1.

Late on, tempers frayed. In the 83rd minute referee Joe Dickerson had to step between Sergio Busquets and Miguel Navarro as the two jawed at each other, Navarro's parting words unrepeatable, and Klopas withdrew Fabian Herbers for an extra defender in Carlos Teran. In the 90th minute Farias was booked for a hip check on Navarro, possibly retaliation for Navarro's jawing at Busquets, and three minutes of added time were shown.
