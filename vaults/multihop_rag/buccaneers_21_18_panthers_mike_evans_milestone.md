# Buccaneers 21-18 Panthers And Mike Evans' 1,000-Yard Milestone

Tampa Bay's Mike Evans scored on a 75-yard reception in a 21-18 win over Carolina in Week 13 of the 2023 season and joined Jerry Rice as the only players in NFL history to string together 10 consecutive seasons with 1,000-plus receiving yards.

Evans finished with seven receptions for 162 yards, increasing his season totals to 61 catches for 1,012 yards and 10 touchdowns; Rice holds the league record of 11 straight seasons with more than 1,000 yards receiving. The Bucs won for only the second time in the past eight games. Carolina scored late to pull within a field goal, but No. 1 overall draft pick Bryce Young threw an interception to end any chance of coming from behind in the Panthers' debut under interim coach Chris Tabor.
