# Prime Day 2023 Wi-Fi Router And Mesh Deals

WIRED's networking picks are a Netgear Nighthawk router, the Wi-Fi 6 router at the top of its Best Wi-Fi Routers guide, Eero's Pro 6E mesh system, and an older Eero router on sale for $45.

The Netgear Nighthawk series is well regarded and supplies some of the picks in WIRED's Best Routers guide; this model isn't one of the fancy ones but is a solid choice, with Wi-Fi 6 support, coverage up to 3,500 square feet, and a USB input for connecting a storage drive as shared disk space. The top-of-guide Wi-Fi 6 router is ideal for the average home seeking more reliable Wi-Fi on a budget: a slick black finish with four antennas, reliable performance, four gigabit Ethernet LAN ports, a single gigabit WAN port, and a USB 3.0 port on the back; it is frequently discounted but hasn't been this low since Amazon's last Prime Day event. For Wi-Fi dead zones, a mesh router may be needed, and WIRED's upgrade pick, Eero's Pro 6E (7/10, WIRED Recommends), makes the process as simple and hands-off as it can be, with each unit covering 2,000 square feet; the brand's subscription is pricey at $10 a month after the cheaper option was eliminated, and while you don't have to subscribe for the system to work, it adds nice features like parental controls.
