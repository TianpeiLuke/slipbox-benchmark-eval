# Manchester United's Pressing Statistics, October 2023

Manchester United ranked top of the Premier League for possessions won in the final third and for high turnovers in late October 2023, but only second (behind Everton) for winning the ball in the middle third and tenth for PPDA, Sky Sports found when checking Erik ten Hag's claims on 28 October 2023.

Ten Hag was indeed right that United rank very highly in their pressing stats in the final two-thirds of the pitch: they are, as he says, top of the league in possessions won in the final third and high turnovers. He is slightly wrong about the middle third, where United are second behind Everton. There is still work to be done, though: their passes allowed per defensive action (PPDA) tally ranks 10th — so, average — in the league, which shows that while Ten Hag's side do win the ball high up the pitch, opponents do tend to keep the ball as well as play it around United's pressing forwards.

The other pressing concern, which Ten Hag referenced in Friday's press conference, is finishing chances after winning the ball high. United have the fourth-highest amount of shots after winning the ball in their final third, but the glaring statistic is no goals from 104 high turnovers. As expected, Manchester City have the highest amount of build-up attacks in the Premier League this season, so if United do win the ball high up in Sunday's derby, they need to start taking those chances.
