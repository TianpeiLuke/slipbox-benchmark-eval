# Reece James' Hamstring Injury And Return Timeline

As of 20 October 2023, the best read on Reece James' status was that he would be back in Chelsea's squad for Saturday's home derby against Arsenal, most likely as a substitute, though not at 100 per cent.

The 23-year-old was not called up by England for the internationals over the preceding window, and the idea was that he would work to be ready for the Arsenal game. Yet there had been stories of an injury setback, even that he might need surgery to get to the root of his hamstring issues. Pochettino bustled his way through those questions on the Friday, saying of the hamstring: "It is much better. He is progressing well, still not at his best, 100%. But maybe to help the team at some point …. maybe, he is close to be there." Malo Gusto was available again after his own suspension.

The Guardian noted it had not been straightforward and seemed to remain that way, and drew two takeaways from Pochettino on James with a common thread: first, that James could be like a coiled spring when he finally returned to action, and second, that Pochettino was in no mood to criticise him for his indiscipline — on the contrary, he was happier to talk up James's passion.
