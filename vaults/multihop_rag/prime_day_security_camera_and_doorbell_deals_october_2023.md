# Prime Day Security Camera And Doorbell Deals, October 2023

During Amazon's October 2023 fall Prime Day event, The Verge listed several security camera discounts. Google's Nest Cam (indoor, wired) — the most stylish, if least flexible, indoor camera — was on sale for $69.99, down from $100, a 30 percent discount, with the option of 24/7 recording plus smart alerts for people, pets, and more. The latest Ring Indoor Cam with a physical privacy shutter was just $29.99 at Amazon, half its regular price of $59.99; it is a compact wired camera that records in 1080p and requires a subscription for viewing motion-activated recordings.

Among the outdoor models, the Ring Floodlight Cam Wired Pro was $50 off at Amazon, selling for $199.99, with radar-powered motion detection for more accurate alerts and floodlights giving off 2,000 lumens. Google's Nest Cam with floodlight was $80 off at Amazon, also selling for $199.99, and is one of the only floodlight cameras that offers 24/7 recording, with on-device AI processing that is quick to report a person, animal, or vehicle. Arlo's budget line was a bargain at $130 for three cameras — $44 each, down from $350, a 63 percent discount: the Arlo Essential Spotlight Cameras work over Wi-Fi so they do not need a hub, though they can connect to an Arlo base station for local storage and Apple Home support, and while only 1080p they have great video quality, a built-in siren and spotlight, and excellent smart alert features, which require a monthly subscription.
