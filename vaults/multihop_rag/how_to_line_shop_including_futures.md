# How To Line Shop, Including Futures Markets

The easiest way to line-shop is to find a live odds feed that shows the differences between lines and odds for the same bets at many of the top sportsbooks in the country; it is a one-stop shop and takes just seconds. First, look at such a feed for current opportunities. Second, if you want to go further, perform the task manually — it takes longer but lets you check more sportsbooks, or the chosen few you feel consistently offer the best lines and odds. Third, to do that, download and install more than one of the best sports betting apps on your phone. Fourth, tap around among the sportsbooks to compare lines and odds for the bets you plan.

Futures deserve their own pass, and this is where the payoff is largest. People tend to bet futures at their favorite online sportsbook, but shopping for better odds on any futures bet is worth the time: most game lines are similar across sportsbooks, whereas the odds in futures markets can vary significantly.

The precondition for those differences is a book's own exposure, and timing governs whether you capture them. Sportsbooks sometimes carry higher liabilities for specific teams and offer better odds on others to lay that off; get the timing right and you can easily take advantage of changes in different futures markets driven by liability. The limit is that the only way to find these opportunities is to shop lines.
