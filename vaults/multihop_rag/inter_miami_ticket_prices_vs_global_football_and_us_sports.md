# How Inter Miami's 2024 Prices Compare To Europe, The NFL, The NBA And MLS

After its 2023-to-2024 increase, Inter Miami boasted some of the most expensive seats in world football, rivaling other major US sports as well. The cheapest 2024 Inter Miami season ticket was $867, which works out to $51 per match across the 17 home games of the upcoming MLS season, while a seat in the West Reserved section — upper deck near the middle of the pitch — was priced at $140 per match.

Against European clubs the comparison is stark. According to Telemundo this made Inter Miami one of the most expensive tickets in the entire world: the cheapest Barcelona season ticket, for example, could be had for $377 for the season, and even with prices muted because the next few seasons would be played outside the under-construction Camp Nou, they were still just 25% cheaper than tickets to Barcelona's famed home ground. One of Europe's most expensive tickets is at Tottenham, thanks partly to the new Tottenham Hotspur Stadium, and even there the cheapest ticket was around $974 with the most expensive package $2,498 for the year. Inter Miami's most expensive 2024 premium packages, running supporters over $45,000, dwarf any of the most luxurious Premier League tickets: the most expensive EPL season ticket is at Fulham, where Craven Cottage luxury booths are valued at $3,620, while Barcelona's most expensive season ticket is $1,826 for the full year.

Against US sports and MLS peers the gap is also large. The NFL, the most popular sport in the United States by a massive margin, had an average ticket price of $112 in 2022, while the average NBA ticket price was $94 that year. Orlando City SC, one of Inter Miami's chief in-state rivals, had its cheapest 2024 season ticket at just $324 — over half the cost of any Inter Miami season ticket — with its most expensive premium ticket at $2,898, while nearby rival Atlanta United's cheapest 2024 season ticket was $605, or just $525 for founding members.
