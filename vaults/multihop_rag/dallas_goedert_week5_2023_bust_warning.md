# Dallas Goedert As A Week 5 2023 Bust Candidate At The Rams

CBS Sports flagged Dallas Goedert as a "Bust Candidate" for Week 5 of the 2023 season at the Los Angeles Rams: the claim is that Goedert is a distant third in targets on the Eagles for a reason — he's not explosive in the least and the Philadelphia offense is much more concerned with its explosive components — and the warrant is that the Rams' recent tight end concessions came against secondary tight ends they barely covered, so they do not predict anything for Goedert.

The usage grounds are blunt. For three weeks in a row, with seven or fewer targets, Goedert has failed to reach even 50 receiving yards, and his target volume dipped from seven in Weeks 2 and 3 to four in Week 4, the same number as D'Andre Swift — and it shouldn't be lost on fantasy managers that since Swift became a staple in the Eagles offense, Goedert has taken a backseat. Coach Nick Sirianni again said "we need to get him the football," citing that plays designed for him wound up going to other Eagles because of how defenses played Goedert. The column's film review disputes that explanation: on his nine third-down routes in Week 4 there was nothing teams were doing to take him away, he still looked sluggish in his movements and didn't run many deeper routes, though on a couple he did get open and Jalen Hurts had better opportunities elsewhere. The mixed evidence from that game is that two targets came in the red zone and a third was a deep contested catch for 17 yards, while the fourth was dropped on a short throw. On the season Goedert has three red-zone targets and zero end-zone targets, and while there is always a shot he scores from short range, the Eagles like to run the ball near the goal line.

The matchup evidence is presented as a trap. The Rams got stung for two touchdowns to tight ends at Indianapolis the previous week, one on a 35-yard play where Mo Alie-Cox got matched up with defensive-end-turned-outside-linebacker Mike Hoecht, the other a five-yard toss to Andrew Ogletree, who ran past Hoecht off the snap for an easy score — but those players aren't considered primary tight ends like Goedert, so it's unlikely the Rams would cover Goedert the way they covered them, which is to say barely. Before Week 4, the Seahawks, Bengals and 49ers tight ends, including George Kittle, combined for eight non-PPR and 19 PPR fantasy points over three games against the Rams, whose pass stats against tight ends were much better before the Colts laid some numbers on them. The breakdown lists Philadelphia at the Rams, the Eagles favoured by 4.5, an over/under of 50.5, an opponent ranked 23rd versus tight ends, 9.3 projected points, a 12th tight end ranking, and year-to-date totals of 13 receptions on 19 targets for 88 yards, no touchdowns and 5.5 fantasy points per game. The verdict prefers Jake Ferguson and Zach Ertz in all formats, plus Kyle Pitts and Cole Kmet in non-PPR.
