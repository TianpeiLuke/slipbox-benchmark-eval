# Scott Fitterer Panthers Hot Seat

Yardbarker's Nov. 30, 2023 NFC hot-seat roundup named general manager Scott Fitterer as the Carolina Panthers' hottest seat, while conceding that owner David Tepper should have the hottest seat — but he was not going to fire himself.

The grounds are that Fitterer was partly responsible for constructing the least impressive roster in football. The warrant is displacement: when Tepper runs out of people on the sidelines to fire, he will take out his frustration on the GM instead. The argument is therefore about who is available to be blamed, not only about who is most culpable.
