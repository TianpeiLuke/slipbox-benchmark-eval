# Hamas October 7 2023 Surprise Attack on Israel

On 7 October 2023, Hamas carried out what Business Today described as the biggest coordinated attack in Israel, capturing some territory in southern Israel and taking control of some military bases and soldiers.

The reaction the report documents was directed at Israel's famed intelligence agency, Mossad, which is known for its intelligence-gathering capability. For some observers, the belief that Mossad was among the best in the spying world was shattered that day. The report is explicit that it was not the fact of an attack that surprised people, but the scale at which Hamas operated.
