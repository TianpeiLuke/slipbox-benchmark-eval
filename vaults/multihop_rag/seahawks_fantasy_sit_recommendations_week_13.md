# Seahawks Fantasy Sit Recommendations For Week 13

Sporting News advised on Nov. 30, 2023 that most Seattle skill players should be benched for the Week 13 Thursday night game at Dallas — sitting Geno Smith, Jaxon Smith-Njigba, tight ends Noah Fant and Will Dissly, the Seahawks D/ST and kicker Jason Myers — while still starting DK Metcalf and Tyler Lockett as volume-based WR3s.

The case against Smith is that he has dropped from the top 12 last season to a cumulative QB20 in 2023, with two great starts, three so-so ones and the rest forgettable; the Cowboys have allowed only two strong fantasy performances by quarterbacks, from Brock Purdy and Jalen Hurts, and the piece does not expect Smith to make it three. The two starts are hedged. Metcalf can flash big plays and stay involved until he disappears when Smith cannot get him the ball, and Smith might be judicious about throwing his way with second-year corner sensation DaRon Bland in coverage — yet you simply cannot sit him against anyone because of his big-game ability, while expecting no more than the WR3 production his overall scoring has delivered in 2023. Lockett sits two notches above Metcalf at WR32 to Metcalf's WR34 in half-point PPR total points this season, a big dropoff from their WR13 and WR18 finishes last season, and he has a decent matchup against Stephon Gilmore, but he too remains a volume-based WR3.

The remaining sits each rest on a specific matchup or usage ground. Smith-Njigba is only interesting for the desperate in deeper leagues, since his matchup against Jourdan Lewis is on par with the other two wideouts and he could mirror some of what Curtis Samuel gave the Commanders the previous week in Dallas — but forget 100 yards, with around 50 the ceiling and a big boost needed from garbage time. On the tight ends, Noah Gray is a better fantasy player than Noah Fant this season and Will Dissly is barely better than Will Mallory. The Seahawks D/ST is getting better defensively against the pass, but not enough to trust here because they are getting gashed on the ground too. And Myers, one of the most reliable fantasy kickers, is in a bad spot because the Cowboys do not give up many field-goal attempts.
