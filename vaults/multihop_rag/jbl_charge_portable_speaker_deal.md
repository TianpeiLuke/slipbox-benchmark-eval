# JBL Charge Portable Speaker Black Friday Deal

Walmart's $89 Black Friday price on the older version of Mashable's favourite portable Bluetooth speaker is "an even better deal than advertised", because the speaker usually retails for $179.95 on the JBL website, the 25 November 2023 roundup said.

The speaker is decently loud for its compact size and IPX7-rated for waterproofing.

It is also capable of charging other devices via USB — hence its name — making it ideal for travelling.
