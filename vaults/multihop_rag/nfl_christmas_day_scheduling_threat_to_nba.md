# NFL Christmas Day Scheduling Threat To NBA

Essentially Sports argued on Dec. 25, 2023 that the NFL's recent scheduling decisions could threaten the NBA's stronghold on Christmas, even though the enduring success and importance of Christmas Day games suggest the 77-year-old NBA tradition will continue for the foreseeable future.

The grounds are audience trend and star power. Traditionally the NFL has avoided Christmas Day games that fall outside its broadcasting window, but the previous year it averaged 22.1 million viewers on Christmas Day games, and 2023 could be even bigger: the NFL lined up a mouth-watering slate for Christmas, with superstars like Patrick Mahomes, Brock Purdy and Jalen Hurts all set to take the field, and by some estimations the NFL's total viewership during the festive period could outperform the NBA's by almost 75 million viewers — a huge dilemma for a league that has traditionally maintained a firm grip on Christmas. The scope condition that limits the threat is the NFL's own stated policy: its top executives have said they will not schedule games on Christmas if the holiday falls outside their regular broadcasting window, so it remains to be seen whether the NFL pursues Christmas Day games in future.
