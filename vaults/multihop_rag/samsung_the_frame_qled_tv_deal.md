# Samsung The Frame QLED TV Deal

The 55-inch Samsung The Frame smart TV was 35 percent off and down to a new record low of $978 at Amazon for Black Friday 2023, with Samsung itself listing it for a couple of bucks more and other size options also discounted.

The Frame has been super popular thanks to its design, which mimics framed artwork when not in use: it has a matte, anti-reflective display and an included slim wall mount to get the true "artwork" effect when hung on a wall. As an actual TV, it supports 4K content and Quantum Dot technology, and it runs on Samsung's Tizen TV operating system.
