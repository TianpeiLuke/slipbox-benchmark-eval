# Trible, No-Code App Building And The Creator Economy

TechCrunch's Equity podcast featured a throwback conversation from TechCrunch Disrupt 2023, when Alex sat down with Serhii Bohoslovskyi, the founder of Trible, a no-code app builder that helps people construct online courses.

The pair caught up on the state of the creator economy, the use of no-code tooling today and how it is received by nontechnical creators, and the security of startups with roots in Ukraine.
