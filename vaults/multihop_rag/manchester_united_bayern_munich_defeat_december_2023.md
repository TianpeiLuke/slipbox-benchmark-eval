# Manchester United's 1-0 Defeat To Bayern Munich, December 2023

Manchester United lost 1-0 at home to Bayern Munich on the final Champions League group matchday, reported by The Independent on 12 December 2023, with Kingsley Coman scoring on 71 minutes from a Harry Kane assist to condemn United to a seventh home defeat of the season.

The goal came at near walking pace. Coman weaved his way through the centre, the ball was eventually worked to Kane, and the English forward clipped an artful ball back through for Coman, who finished emphatically. Bayern were already through and perhaps looking to make a point after their own 5-1 defeat to Eintracht Frankfurt, but did not even need to step it up to win; they were quite low-intensity themselves, and Coman's winner came from the forward almost walking through the middle of their team. Kane did not score but did set it up. Bayern still had more of the ball and more of the attacks, which was another tension for Ten Hag to overcome: United were playing in quite a controlled way when they were going to eventually have to go for it, yet Bayern were getting through that with relative ease, so the team almost looked like they were in two minds. Bruno Fernandes blazed United's best opportunity over the bar after good work from Aaron Wan Bissaka.

The atmosphere matched the performance. There was no energy or intensity; the Stretford End eventually implored United to "attack attack attack" but the complete absence of any such impulses left some of the stadium booing the side off at the end, and it was another utterly forgettable display in a drab game before a muted crowd. News came through shortly before the hour that Lukas Lerager had scored for Copenhagen, though it did not really change the atmosphere. In context this was far from United's worst performance of the season — nowhere near as bad as Bournemouth or Crystal Palace or Newcastle, or the other defeat to Newcastle, and not as self-destructive as they had been in Denmark and Turkey and at home to Galatasaray — and they were vaguely competent on the night. But they still lost, 24 years after Ole Gunnar Solskjaer's stunning intervention against the same opponent.
