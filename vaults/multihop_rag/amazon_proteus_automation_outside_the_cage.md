# Amazon Proteus And Automation Outside The Cage

Asked about projects like Proteus and the goal of moving automation outside the cage, Amazon Robotics chief technologist Tye Brady confirmed the direction and attached a safety number to it: "We're moving outside the cage. What we can see with those investments is in 2022, as compared to manual buildings, we've reduced the recordable injury rate by 15%."

The comparison is specific — 2022 results, measured against manual buildings — and is offered as the payoff of investing in robots that operate alongside people rather than behind safety enclosures.
