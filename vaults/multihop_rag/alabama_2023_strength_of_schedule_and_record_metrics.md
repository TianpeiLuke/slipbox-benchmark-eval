# Alabama's 2023 Resume Metrics Compared With Texas

According to ESPN, the Crimson Tide ranked sixth in the Football Power Index and No. 7 in strength of record, with Texas at No. 7 in FPI and No. 6 in strength of record, per Sporting News on Dec. 1, 2023.

Alabama had three victories against ranked teams that season, but none of those were top-10 victories; Georgia would be the signature victory on the schedule. Sporting News judged the two teams' totals comparable, but argued that again the matchup against Georgia would carry much more weight on conference championship weekend — it was the chance for an ultimate signature victory.
