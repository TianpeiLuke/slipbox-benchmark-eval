# Lions 34, Packers 20: Detroit's Supporting Cast Wins At Lambeau

The Lions cruised to a 34-20 win over their hated NFC North rivals at Lambeau Field on Thursday Night Football, Sporting News reported on 2023-09-28. The night's emblem was Amon-Ra St. Brown, who had stretched beyond an exasperated Packers defense to trot into the end zone, then, shrouded in a sea of green with few options around him, chose between celebrating with his sapphire-clad teammates and taking a leap of faith into the Lambeau Field abyss — he hurled himself into the green lagoon, then smiled. His impersonation of the Lambeau Leap was one of many high points in a memorable night for Detroit, fueled by a stout defensive showing, a gritty run game and a bit of fairy dust from the likes of St. Brown, Sam LaPorta and Josh Reynolds.

The game had appeared destined to be a showcase of quarterbacking excellence, with Jared Goff and Jordan Love having proved themselves formidable options under center since taking the reins of their respective offenses. But it was the supporting cast that proved so pivotal for Dan Campbell's side: David Montgomery was a nuisance, slipping through crevices to puncture the heart of the Packers' defense, and when it wasn't him it was the receiving corps of unheralded youngsters who had all come up trumps in recent weeks. For Love and company, the affair was another indication of just how much work there is to be done before they compete for the divisional crown — there were certainly some flits of delight, throws that highlighted how much talent Love possesses in his golden arm, but there were low points too, misshapen efforts that fell harmlessly to the turf or harmfully into the opposition's hands. The line score by quarter: Lions 14, 13, 0, 7 for 34; Packers 3, 0, 8, 9 for 20.
