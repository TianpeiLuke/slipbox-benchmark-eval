# The Thaler "Creativity Machine" Copyright Ruling

Stephen Thaler, who owns a computer system he calls the "Creativity Machine," had a copyright application for an AI-generated "painting" rejected by the US Copyright Office on the grounds that the Creativity Machine had created it — and in a ruling in August 2023, a US District Court in Washington, D.C. sided with the Copyright Office.

"Copyright has never stretched so far… as to protect works generated by new forms of technology operating absent any guiding human hand, as plaintiff urges here," Judge Beryl A. Howell commented. "Human authorship is a bedrock requirement of copyright." MBW's framing on 2023-11-30 was that US legal precedent is, at least so far, on the NMPA's side.
