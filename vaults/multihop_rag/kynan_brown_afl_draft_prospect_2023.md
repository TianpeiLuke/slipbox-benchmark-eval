# Kynan Brown AFL Draft Prospect 2023

Kynan Brown, profiled in Kevin Sheehan's top 40 for the 2023 AFL Draft as reported by The Roar on Nov. 15, 2023, is a left-footed inside midfielder who has quick hands, sharp vision and creates play through the middle of the ground with his creativity. State: Victoria Metro. State League Club: Oakleigh Chargers. Community Club: Blackburn. Date of Birth: 13/01/05. Height: 181cm.

He had a remarkably consistent year which included starring for Victoria Metro in the National Championships, averaging 22.8 disposals at 80 percent efficiency as well as 4.5 clearances and 4.8 tackles to win their best and fairest award. He had similar numbers with the Oakleigh Chargers in the Coates Talent League, including kicking five goals and having 25 disposals in a best on ground performance in the Wildcard Round win against the Northern Knights. He tested very well at the state Draft Combine in Victoria, with 6:12 on the 2km time trial and 3.04 seconds for the 20m sprint rounding off a productive season. The eligibility condition that sets him apart: he can be selected under the father-son rule as the son of former Melbourne utility Nathan D Brown, who played 146 games for the Demons. Weapon: Footy IQ and ball-winning ability. AFL player comparison: Errol Gulden (Sydney Swans). Cal Twomey believes the father-son prospect has shown enough to be on an AFL list in 2024, but is more likely to be a later selection in the draft; Brown averaged 23 disposals and 4 tackles for Vic Metro across the under-18 national carnival.
