# Martin Scorsese's Internet-Slang TikTok Quiz

Martin Scorsese put his knowledge of internet slang to the test in a TikTok challenge posted by his daughter, Francesca Scorsese, on Thursday 5 October 2023. The father-daughter duo appeared on camera together while Francesca explained the format to the 80-year-old director: "I'm going to ask you to guess what specific slang terms mean."

Scorsese restated the rules in his own words, in what The Independent calls classic style: "You're going to tell me the slang word and I have to give you what I think it means."
