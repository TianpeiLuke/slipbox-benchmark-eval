# Curtis Samuel's Week 10 Injury Status

Curtis Samuel (toe) was officially ACTIVE for the Commanders' Week 10 game against the Seahawks at 4:25 p.m. ET, with ESPN's Jeremy Fowler reporting he was expected to play. He had been limited in practice by the toe injury that had plagued him since he left Week 8's game against the Eagles, but barring a setback he would return to action that weekend.

The fantasy advice was to wait. Samuel had been working his way into flex territory with a couple of good performances before the injury, but Sporting News would not consider him worthy of a start that week unless an owner was in a bind, because it was unclear whether he would regain the same workload or play in limited action as he worked back. Jamison Crowder had emerged in his absence and Jahan Dotson's production had increased drastically as well, making the wait-and-see approach the preferred one heading into Week 10.
