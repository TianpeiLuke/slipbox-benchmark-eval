# OpenAI's Updated Preparedness Framework

OpenAI's "Preparedness Framework," described in a new document and blog post covered on 18 December 2023, is the company's internal process for identifying, analyzing, and deciding what to do about "catastrophic" risks inherent to the models it is developing. The update is part of an expansion of OpenAI's internal safety processes to fend off the threat of harmful AI: a new "safety advisory group" will sit above the technical teams and make recommendations to leadership, and the board has been granted veto power — though whether it will actually use that power is another question entirely.

TechCrunch's framing is that policies like these normally do not warrant coverage, since in practice they amount to closed-door meetings with obscure functions and responsibility flows that outsiders will seldom be privy to. What makes this one worth examining is the recent leadership fracas at OpenAI and the evolving AI risk discussion, given that OpenAI is the world's leading AI development company.

The report notes that the framework appears to have been retooled after November's shake-up, which removed the board's two most "decelerationist" members: Ilya Sutskever, who remained at the company in a somewhat changed role, and Helen Toner, who left entirely.
