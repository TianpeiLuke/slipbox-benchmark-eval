# Pebble's Moderation-First Market Hypothesis

Pebble's founding hypothesis was that consumers were hungry for a Twitter alternative that prioritized trust, safety and moderation from the start. The company staffed for it: Pebble's founding team included co-founder Sarah Oh, Twitter's former human rights advisor.

What would falsify the proposition is a moderation-first Twitter alternative that consumers decline to adopt — which is the shape of the outcome TechCrunch is reporting on 2023-10-24, in a piece about Pebble shutting down.
