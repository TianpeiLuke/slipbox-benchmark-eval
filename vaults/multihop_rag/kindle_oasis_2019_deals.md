# Kindle Oasis 2019 Deals

The Kindle Oasis is the most expensive model in the lineup, starting at $249.99, and comes in two storage configurations, 8GB or 32GB, with or without ads on the lock screen like the other models. The 2019 version looks very similar to the previous model, retaining a waterproof 7-inch 300ppi E Ink display and Audible audiobook support via Bluetooth, but unlike previous models it can adjust the color temperature to a yellow-toned display, making it easier to read at night.

As of The Verge's October 16th, 2023 roundup, there was no standalone discount: the 8GB Oasis had in the past dropped as low as $174.99, which remains the lowest price to date for the standalone configuration, but the ad-supported Oasis was only retailing at Amazon starting at $249.99. Bundles were discounted. The Kindle Oasis Essentials Bundle (2019), which packages the most recent Oasis with a power adapter and a choice of fabric or leather cover, was $280 at Amazon for the 8GB model, down from $310, a 10 percent discount — specifically, the 8GB ad-supported configuration with a power adapter was $279.97 with a fabric cover, $30 off, or $289.97 with a leather cover, also $30 off.
