# Raiders vs Giants UNDER 37.5, Week 9

Sporting News took the UNDER 37.5 in Raiders vs. Giants in Week 9 of 2023, predicting Raiders 17, Giants 13. It admitted the number sounds ludicrous as a best bet, but argued this game took the cake in a Week 9 schedule loaded with underwhelming matchups.

The grounds are the state of both offenses. The Raiders had just fired their head coach and general manager and demoted starting quarterback Jimmy Garoppolo after appearing lost on Monday night, while the Giants had just got Daniel Jones back after scoring a combined 33 points across three miserable performances in a row — and Darren Waller, their best pass-catcher, was hurt.

The defensive form seals it: both the Giants and Raiders had been pretty good defensively of late, so the column expected Danny Dimes and Aidan O'Connell to have a tough time finding any rhythm in the passing game. Not a fun one to watch, it concluded, but a fun result for UNDER bettors. Other Week 9 totals the column liked were Colts at Panthers OVER 44, Commanders at Patriots OVER 41, and Saints OVER 25.5 vs. Bears.
