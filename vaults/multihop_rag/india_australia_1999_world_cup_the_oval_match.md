# India vs Australia At The 1999 World Cup

Australian dominance continued at the 1999 World Cup, where they defeated India by 77 runs. Australia posted a target of 283 runs in 50 overs courtesy of a brilliant 83 off 99 balls by Mark Waugh.

India had an early batting collapse but were stabilised by a sensible 100 off 138 balls by Ajay Jadeja, with Robin Singh also scoring 75 runs off 94 balls; India were nonetheless bowled out within 205 runs in 48.2 overs. Glenn McGrath picked up three wickets for Australia, with Damien Fleming and Steve Waugh picking up two each.
