# Pebble's Electric Travel Trailer

California-based EV startup Pebble unveiled a prototype of its flagship all-electric travel trailer, TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-22.

The $100,000 EV is designed to support a digital nomad looking to get lost in the wilderness, and it can live off-grid for seven days.
