# India's 2019 World Cup Semi-Final Against New Zealand

India lost the 2019 semi-final to New Zealand by 18 runs at Manchester. The cricket World Cup had returned to England for the fifth time, and India were playing their sixth semi-final at the same ground where they played their first World Cup semi-final in 1983, though against a different team.

Indian bowlers raised hopes by restricting New Zealand to 239 for 8 in 50 overs, with Bhuvneshwar Kumar taking three wickets; Ross Taylor's 74 runs in 90 balls and skipper Kane Williamson's 67 in 95 balls were the top scores for New Zealand. Kiwi Man-of-the-Match bowler Matt Henry then sent openers KL Rahul and Rohit Sharma back to the pavilion soon, followed by the dismissal of Dinesh Karthik in the 10th over of India's innings. With the Indian top order failing, Ravindra Jadeja with 77 runs in 59 balls and wicketkeeper MS Dhoni with 50 runs in 72 balls tried to steady the Indian ship, but once they fell it was all over for India.
