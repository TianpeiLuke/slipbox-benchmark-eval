# Elizabeth Warren's Criticism Of Apple Blocking Beeper

U.S. Senator Elizabeth Warren (D-Mass.) rejected Apple's security rationale for cutting off Beeper Mini, attacking it as pretextual while the Beeper team scrambled for a fix. In a post on X, formerly Twitter, she wrote: "Green bubble texts are less secure. So why would Apple block a new app allowing Android users to chat with iPhone users on iMessage? Big Tech executives are protecting profits by squashing competitors. Chatting between different platforms should be easy and secure."

Her argument turns Apple's own premise against it: if the alternative to iMessage — green bubble texts — is less secure, then blocking an app that extends iMessage to Android cannot be explained by user security, leaving competitive self-interest as the explanation.
