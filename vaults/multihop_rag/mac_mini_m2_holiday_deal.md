# Mac Mini M2 Holiday Deal

The base model of Apple's diminutive desktop computer, the Mac mini, was on sale for $100 off at Amazon, B&H Photo and Best Buy for both Black Friday and Cyber Monday 2023; the wider Black Friday roundup lists the 256GB model at $500 at Best Buy and the 512GB model at $690 at Amazon with a clippable coupon, while the Apple roundups put a 512GB SSD version at $699 at B&H Photo.

It earned an 86 in Engadget's review because it packs a lot of processing power, using the same M2 chip as the two current-model MacBook Airs while being much cheaper than either laptop. Though Engadget expects an upgrade to the new M3 chip at some point over the next year, the current model is still a great option for a speedy desktop that runs macOS, and it has a headphone jack, a Gigabit Ethernet port, two USB-A connectors, two Thunderbolt 4 USB-Cs and an HDMI 2.0 port.
