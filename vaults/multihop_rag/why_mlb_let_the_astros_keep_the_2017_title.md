# Manfred's Rebuttal To Stripping The Astros' 2017 Title

Many thought taking away the World Series win, essentially nullifying 2017, was the best way to move forward; MLB thought otherwise, and Commissioner Robert Manfred's counter-argument attacked the workability of that remedy rather than the wrongdoing itself, per Essentially Sports on 2023-10-22. In his words: "Once you go down that road of changing what happens on the field, I just don't know how you decide where you stop." The premise he denied was that a clean line could be drawn around which on-field results to revise — and so the Commissioner's Trophy was still legitimate in the eyes of, well, the Commissioner.

A second strand of his objection was practical timing: by the time the controversy was uncovered, the Astros were well into establishing a streak of successful years, and Manfred called the process of stripping the Fall Classic title "difficult and impractical." Already unpopular among the masses, he made things worse for himself when he called the trophy a "piece of metal." He apologized later, but the team kept the championship victory.
