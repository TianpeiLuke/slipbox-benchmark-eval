# DocuSign Q3 Earnings Preview, December 2023

Online signature company DocuSign (DOCU) was scheduled to release its Q3 results on Thursday of the week following Seeking Alpha's December 3, 2023 preview, after the closing bell, with Wall Street analysts cautious at a Hold rating and Seeking Alpha's Quant Rating system more optimistic at a Buy — the reverse of the usual split.

Company guidance framed the print. After surpassing expectations in Q2, DocuSign guided Q3 revenue to between $687M and $691M and raised full-year sales guidance to $2.73B-$2.74B from a prior $2.71B-$2.73B. Consensus estimates ahead of the report stood at EPS of $0.63 and revenue of $690.12M — the revenue consensus sitting inside the company's own guided range.

Seeking Alpha author The Asian Investor supplied the bear case: DocuSign's net dollar retention rate was decreasing and top-line growth was slowing. That analyst argued a drop below 100% could potentially trigger a negative stock catalyst, on the grounds that the shares had historically been volatile after earnings. The 100% threshold is the specific trigger identified — the concern is retention falling under that level, not the direction of the trend alone.
