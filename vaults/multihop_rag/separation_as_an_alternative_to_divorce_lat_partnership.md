# Separation As An Alternative To Divorce: The "LAT" Partnership

New York City-based relationship expert Susan Winter argues that a long separation like Will Smith and Jada Pinkett Smith's is not a failed marriage but a recognised alternative form of one — a "living apart together" partnership — and that it can be the better choice. Speaking to The Independent in October 2023, she drew a sharp line between separation and divorce: divorce, particularly for Hollywood couples, means lengthy legal processes, from dividing financial assets to creating child custody agreements, whereas a separation carries none of that machinery. Although the terms of the Smiths' separation are not public, Winter believes they are showcasing a non-conventional relationship unfamiliar to the public, one whose visible feature is living in separate homes.

Her grounds are that the arrangement's organising purpose is continuity of the partnership rather than its dissolution: "It's an LAT partnership, meaning 'living apart together.' While everybody is at a different point as to why they are doing this, the centre point is the same: the preservation of the partnership is paramount." She attaches a scope condition to the benefit and a limit to what success looks like: "Separations can also be beneficial if both individuals are working on themselves and have the mutual goal of reuniting, or making their relationship better. However, that doesn't necessarily mean that their improved relationship will result in them living together again." The argument arises because the Smiths' seven years apart raise the general question of when couples should give up and when a separation should become a divorce — a question, the article notes, that has no simple answer.
