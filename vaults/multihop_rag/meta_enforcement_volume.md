# Meta Enforcement Volume

Meta reported removing or marking as disturbing more than 795,000 pieces of content in Hebrew and Arabic in the three days following October 7, for violating the policies described in its Israel-Hamas war response. The figure appears in the company blog post covered by TechCrunch on 13 October 2023.

Meta also gave a rate comparison for one specific policy: "As compared to the two months prior, in the three days following October 7, we have removed seven times as many pieces of content on a daily basis for violating our Dangerous Organizations and Individuals policy in Hebrew and Arabic alone." Both the 795,000 total and the sevenfold daily increase are scoped to Hebrew and Arabic content, not to the platform as a whole.
