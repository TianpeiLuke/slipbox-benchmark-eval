# GPT Model Releases From GPT-3.5 To GPT-4

ChatGPT first launched to the public as OpenAI quietly released GPT-3.5: the model broke cover with ChatGPT, a fine-tuned version of GPT-3.5 that is essentially a general-purpose chatbot able to engage with a range of topics including programming, TV scripts and scientific concepts.

Writers everywhere rolled their eyes at the new technology, much as artists did with OpenAI's DALL-E model, but the chat-style iteration seemingly broadened its appeal and audience. GPT-4 followed on March 14: a powerful image- and text-understanding AI model, released to paying ChatGPT Plus users and through a public API, with developers able to sign up on a waitlist to access the API.
