# Gravity Falls: Northwest Mansion Mystery

"Northwest Mansion Mystery" is season 2, episode 10 of Gravity Falls and Polygon's pick for an especially spooky episode to get in the Halloween spirit. It was the Oct. 8 entry in the 2023-10-10 Halloween Countdown, listed on Hulu and Disney Plus.

Polygon frames the series first: Alex Hirsch's paranormal comedy about 12-year-old twins Dipper and Mabel, who solve supernatural mysteries in their great-uncle's home in rural Oregon, is the perfect entry point for young horror-loving audiences — a pastiche of The X-Files, Twin Peaks, and Adventure Time all rolled into one, with the freewheeling, funloving spirit of a summer vacation. The more time that has passed since it concluded, the more miraculous it feels in hindsight.

In the episode, a ghost has taken up residence in the mansion estate of the wealthiest family in Gravity Falls on the eve of their annual party, and Dipper has been enlisted to exorcise it. Upon arriving, Dipper and popular girl Pacifica Northwest uncover a dark family secret buried for generations, one that threatens not only to tarnish the family's reputation but to engulf the entire town. Critic Toussaint Egan notes it is kind of wild how scary the episode gets for a series ostensibly aimed at young children — taxidermy animals bleeding from their mouths, flaming skeletal lumberjacks, and hapless partygoers petrified into screaming wooden statues — and calls it a blast and a solid stand-alone introduction to the show's spooky and "spoopy" delights.
