# Savannah Montano And Jake Paul's 2020 FaceTime Callout

Savannah Montano was quite a trending name back in 2020, mostly because of her relationship with Bellator MMA fighter Dillon Danis, Essentially Sports recounted on 2023-10-07. Montano is a successful Instagram model with around 1.6 million followers on her social media, and the couple turned a lot of heads during their relationship, sharing NSFW steamy pictures and videos for their fans.

Despite that controversial relationship, Montano also gained attention after Jake Paul used her to call out Dillon Danis for a fight. After knocking out ex-NBA star Nate Robinson, 'The Problem Child' was on the hunt for his next opponent, and at the time the name that popped up the most was the 2-0 Dillon Danis. So when Paul shared a screenshot of a FaceTime with Danis's ex-girlfriend, fans started drawing connections. Paul somehow found the model's number and sent her a FaceTime request, and many began to believe Montano was having an affair and cheating on her then-boyfriend Danis. Paul posted two stories: in the first he wrote, "Aye [Dillon Danis], come get your girl back," and in the second he took another hit at the fighter by writing, "Can't wait to see you soon." The article poses the resulting question — was Paul actually involved in an affair with Savannah Montano?
