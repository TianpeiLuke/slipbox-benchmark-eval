# Why IMPA Saw No Pre-Attack Funding Spike

Asked where IMPA was before October 7, given the general debate about why the intelligence agencies gave no warning, Ostrovitch-Levi rejects the premise that a financing signal was there to be seen: "We had no possibility of seeing a spike in fundraising towards October 7."

Her grounds are that the money flow is continuous rather than event-driven, and mostly offshore: "Hamas collects lots of money all the time, and the security agencies deter money transfers. There was no sudden increase in the raising of billions, and you must also remember that most of the money is raised globally, not in Israel. The hundreds of millions of dollars that Hamas receives have been accumulating for years."
