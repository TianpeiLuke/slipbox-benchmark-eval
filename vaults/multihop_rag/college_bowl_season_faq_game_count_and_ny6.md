# College Bowl Season: Game Count And The New Year's Six

The college bowl game season lasts three weeks and consists of 43 games, including the NCAA football national championship.

Within that slate the New Year's Six is a series of high-profile bowl games: the Rose Bowl Game presented by Capital One (Pasadena, California), the Allstate Sugar Bowl (New Orleans), the PlayStation Fiesta Bowl (Glendale, Arizona), the Chick-fil-A Peach Bowl (Atlanta), the Goodyear Cotton Bowl Classic (Arlington, Texas) and the Capital One Orange Bowl (Miami Gardens, Florida).
