# Convoy's Digital Freight Shutdown

The digital freight broker Convoy told employees in the week before TechCrunch's 2023-10-22 report that it was shutting down, due to what executives described as a "massive freight recession." TechCrunch's takeaway: it turns out that disrupting the freight business is hard.

The abrupt closure wiped out investors, and comes about 18 months since the Seattle-based company raised $260 million in fresh funding that pushed its valuation to $3.8 billion.
