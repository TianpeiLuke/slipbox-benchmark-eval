# Day Two Of The Bankman-Fried Trial: Jury Selection And The Courtroom Audience

On the second day of Sam Bankman-Fried's fraud trial, the jury was selected on Wednesday morning, after which lawyers for the government and then for Bankman-Fried delivered opening statements that told two very different stories of the former FTX CEO's sudden rise and almost instantaneous fall. The central question framed by the proceedings was who Bankman-Fried, the former CEO of crypto exchange FTX, really is: a liar and a fraud, or someone who acted in good faith and, like many a startup founder, flew too close to the sun. That answer, the account notes, ultimately lies with the jury.

The day featured pointed allegations, a friend from MIT, and an audience replete with big names, including Bankman-Fried's professorial parents and Damian Williams, the U.S. attorney for the Southern District of New York. Bankman-Fried himself spent most of the day in court staring into a laptop while seated between his two attorneys. The prosecution's account of his alleged crimes was described as featuring a study in contrasts.
