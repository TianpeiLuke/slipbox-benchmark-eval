# Apple Gift Card And Amazon Credit Deal

To get $15 in Amazon store credit at no extra cost during the 2023 holiday sales, the steps are: purchase an Apple gift card totaling $100 or more from the retailer, then use the code APPLET5USA at checkout to see the offer. The precondition is the $100 minimum purchase from Amazon.

The deal applies to the digital version of the card. As of the writing of both roundups, you could also get the credit with a physical Apple gift card by using the code APPLET5USAP — but with a caveat that determines whether the procedure pays off: you may not want to clip the coupon on those cards' product page, as Amazon's listing says that will only give you a $10 credit. As a reminder, an Apple gift card can be used for App Store credit, subscription services like Apple Music or iCloud, and purchases made at a physical Apple store.
