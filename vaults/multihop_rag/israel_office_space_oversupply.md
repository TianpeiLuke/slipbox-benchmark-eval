# Israel's Office Space Oversupply

Even before the war, the industry was talking about there being too large a supply of office space in Israel, and according to the Central Bureau of Statistics construction was begun on 3.56 million square meters of office space between 2020 and 2022. Avison Young Israel co-CEO Guy Amosi's grounds for concern are drawn from a live search: a company asked his firm to find 15,000 square meters and to map out options from Rishon Lezion to Kfar Saba, not looking in Tel Aviv where prices are high and where contracts were closed two years ago for NIS 150 per square meter; they found a huge number of projects in the area between Rishon Lezion and Kfar Saba, with millions of square meters being built, and Amosi says he really does not know who will occupy all those areas. He fears many areas in the central region could remain empty for a long time before they are occupied, and is particularly bothered by offices built without an anchor tenant to begin with, noting that even in Petah Tikva half a million square meters of new office space is being built.

The argument is qualified in two directions. Location still counts: Amosi sees an advantage in offices in a good location such as Park Naimi in the central region at Messubim Junction near Roads 4 and 1, and he points out that Midtown was written off when it was built next to Azrieli yet those are now the most sought-after towers, so everything is a question of how long it takes to occupy, and in the end everything will be occupied. He is also currently doing four or five deals for companies that must rent space, not large-scale, from firms ending contracts or growing and wanting more space. The causes he lists for the slowdown stack up: there was Covid and then a crisis in high-tech that led to a chain reaction of reduced spending, companies began to check every investment, interest rates rose and inflation is rampant all over the world, which he insists is not a local bubble, and above all this comes the war; but in general real estate works in waves and the wave will come again.
