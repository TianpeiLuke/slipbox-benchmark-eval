# Reaction to the Lewis Portrayal of SBF

Michael Lewis's 60 Minutes interview drew criticism that it treated [Sam Bankman-Fried](sam_bankman_fried.md) too favourably. The objection is not to any particular fact but to the framing: that a sympathetic "tragic fall" account is being extended to a defendant on fraud charges.

Shopify's Tobi Lutke asked how Bankman-Fried was "still getting super positive media coverage when the media has nothing good to say about the founders that have actually created things." Ryan Selkis called the interview "infuriating," arguing that Lewis and others "were so far up his ass they couldn't tell truth from fiction." Dan Held disputed a specific Lewis claim — that FTX had "a great real business" that would still be profitable absent a run on customer deposits — on the grounds that FTX was not a bank.

TechCrunch's own assessment was that it is unclear whether the interview delivers a "kicking or a coat of polish," given Lewis's treatment of Bankman-Fried's self-proclaimed and unproven idealism.
