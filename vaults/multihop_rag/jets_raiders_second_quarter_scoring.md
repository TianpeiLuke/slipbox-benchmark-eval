# Jets-Raiders Second Quarter Scoring

The second quarter of the Nov. 12, 2023 Jets-Raiders game produced two more field goals and no touchdowns, ending with the Jets ahead 9-6 at the half after Daniel Carlson drilled a 54-yarder at 9:45 p.m.

New York's points came at 9:12 p.m., but only after penalties erased a touchdown. At 9:02 p.m. Breece Hall appeared to convert an 11-yard first down before Laken Tomlinson was flagged for holding, leaving second-and-19. At 9:06 p.m. Garrett Wilson made a leaping seven-yard grab and Zach Wilson made a stellar run along the right sideline, initially ruled a touchdown but confirmed on review to have stepped out at the 3, making it a 20-yard run and first-and-goal. At 9:08 p.m. Hall ran three yards up the middle for a touchdown, but Uzomah was flagged for holding a second time while trying to block Maxx Crosby, pushing it back to first-and-goal from the 13. At 9:09 p.m. Hall gained a yard and was then stuffed by Crosby, and after a Jets timeout, at 9:12 p.m. Wilson's checkdown to Michael Carter was tipped by Adam Butler, so New York settled for a field goal and a 9-3 lead. Between those plays, at 9:05 p.m., Raiders corner Amik Robertson had to be helped off the field, appearing to be in the fencing response while on the ground; at 9:15 p.m. the team ruled him out with a concussion.

The Raiders' half ended with a long kick set up by a Jets penalty. Las Vegas went three-and-out from its own 25 at 9:15-9:16 p.m., an out-of-bounds AJ Cole punt giving the Jets the ball at their own 19; at 9:20 p.m. Dalvin Cook ran for 10 and then nine yards to the Jets' 38 before Crosby brought him down, but at 9:22 p.m., needing only a yard, Cook and Hall were both bottled up at the line and New York punted into the end zone, giving Las Vegas the ball at its own 20 with 7:18 left. At 9:27-9:29 p.m. Jacobs carried five straight times for 20 yards and O'Connell sneaked for three on third-and-1. At 9:34 p.m. a 13-yard Jakobi Meyers catch and an 18-yard strike to Adams went for naught as the Jets' pass rush finally got home, sacking O'Connell, pressuring him into throwing out of bounds and sacking him again, pushing the Raiders from the New York 26 back to the Jets' 46. New York took over at its own 14 with 1:52 left at 9:36 p.m.; at 9:38 p.m. Zach Wilson scrambled 20 yards down the left sideline to the Jets' 41, becoming the team's leading rusher with two carries for 40 yards; and at 9:39 p.m., after an eight-yard Conklin catch, a one-yard scramble loss and an incompletion to Allen Lazard down the left sideline, the Jets punted into the end zone. At 9:42 p.m. the Raiders moved efficiently — a six-yard Jacobs run, a nine-yard quick pass to Adams out of bounds at 32 seconds, and an eight-yard dump-off to Jacobs stopping the clock at 26 seconds — and at 9:43 p.m. Bryce Huff's roughing the passer penalty after a five-yard pass to Mayer moved Las Vegas to the Jets 37 with 21 seconds left, setting up Carlson's 54-yarder at 9:45 p.m.
