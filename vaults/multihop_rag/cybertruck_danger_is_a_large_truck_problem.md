# The Cybertruck's Danger Is A Large-Truck Problem

The Verge argued that if the Cybertruck is particularly deadly for pedestrians, cyclists, and other vulnerable road users, it's because it's a large truck in America in the year 2023 — not because of anything unique to it.

The grounds are an asymmetry in the available evidence: there is lots of data showing that America's favorite type of vehicle is also one of the most deadly, and very little data about the Cybertruck in particular, because it has only been out for a couple of weeks and in extremely limited quantities. The warrant is the inference from class to instance, which is why the piece calls for more data and testing before anyone claims to know the specific dangers posed by this sharply angled, stainless steel contraption. That data is not coming soon: neither the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration nor the Insurance Institute for Highway Safety — two independent bodies that test new vehicles — have any plans to conduct crash safety tests with the Cybertruck.
