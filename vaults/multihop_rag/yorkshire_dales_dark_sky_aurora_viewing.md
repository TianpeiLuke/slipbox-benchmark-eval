# The Yorkshire Dales: England's Largest Dark Sky Reserve

The Yorkshire Dales are an oft-viable UK destination for the aurora, since a northerly, light-pollution-free location is the gold standard for seeing the Northern Lights here. The aurora was seen in the Dales as recently as April 2023. The park is one of England's Dark Sky Reserves and the largest in the UK, and will host a joint Dark Skies Festival with the North York Moors National Park in February 2024.

It has four Dark Sky Discovery sites — designated nationwide for "good sightlines of the sky" and a lack of light pollution — at Buckden National Park Car Park, Tan Hill Inn, and both Hawes and Malham National Park Visitor Centres. According to the park's website, on clear nights you can see as many as 2,000 stars, the Milky Way and even the International Space Station. The Perseid meteor shower occurs in August, though the best time to see the lights is still between September and March; wrap up warm and follow aurora updates online or on specially dedicated apps.
