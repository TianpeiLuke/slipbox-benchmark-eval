# Castlevania: Nocturne (Netflix)

Castlevania: Nocturne, created by Clive Bradley and starring Edward Bluemel, Thuso Mbedu, and Pixie Davies, is Polygon's Netflix pick in its 2023-10-25 roundup of the best new TV series on every streaming service, and the outlet calls it a terrific installment that meets and exceeds the original Castlevania at its best.

The predecessor set a high bar. Based on Konami's acclaimed action-adventure game series and produced by Texas-based Powerhouse Animation Studios, the original 32-episode adult animated series was an unmistakable success for the streamer when it premiered in 2017, chronicling Trevor Belmont, the last surviving descendant of a long line of monster hunters, and Sypha, a powerful magic wielder from a reclusive tribe of magicians, as they joined forces with Alucard, half-human son of Dracula, to slay his vengeful father and save humanity from extinction.

Nocturne picks up three centuries later. Trevor and Sypha may be dead, but their legacy lives on in new protagonist Richter Belmont. Set amid the French Revolution, the series follows Richter as he is enlisted by a young sorceress named Annette to fight against a conspiracy of aristocrats allied with a powerful enemy known as the Vampire Messiah; haunted by the death of his mother at the hands of a mysterious vampire named Olrox, Richter must call on every ounce of his courage to face this new darkness and assume his birthright as a Belmont. Critic Toussaint Egan praises its brilliantly realized character designs, exhilarating action sequences, beautiful score, and nuanced take on real-life revolutions, calling it both an excellent continuation and a fantastic entry point for new audiences.
