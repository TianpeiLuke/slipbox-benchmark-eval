# Roblox's Hiring And Recruiting Layoffs

Asked at the Code Conference about the layoffs Roblox recently made in its recruiting division, CEO David Baszucki said the company is actually continuing to hire and has never done layoffs. His explanation was about the rate of headcount growth rather than a contraction: in Q1 of 2023 Roblox was growing headcount at 50 percent a year, which is very rapid and requires a very, very large recruiting team, and the company has committed and shared publicly that over the next year its bookings will grow faster than its headcount — so headcount growth is probably not going to be 50 percent next year.

Asked whether the cuts said anything bigger about the state of the business, Baszucki answered, "No, absolutely not," adding that Roblox has done an amazing job of continuing to grow steadily through all of this over the last two years. The interviewer noted that Roblox had not done layoffs during the pandemic.
