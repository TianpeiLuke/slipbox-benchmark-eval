# Samsung Smart Monitor M8

The Samsung Smart Monitor M8 was down to just $500, which is $200 off its starting price — though it regularly sells for $600 — and the lowest price Engadget had seen thus far.

This 32-inch display has built-in apps and lets you access streaming services just as you would on a smart TV. It can also act as a smart home hub, showing your smart camera feeds and more.

The discounted model is the updated 2023 version of the monitor that launched at CES 2022.
