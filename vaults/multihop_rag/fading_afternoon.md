# Fading Afternoon

Fading Afternoon, developed by yeo for Windows PC, is ranked 44 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31, and critic Joshua Rivera frames it around time.

His premise is that video games demand an account for your time: some track the minutes and hours you have spent on screen, others impose strict limits on how long you play or how much you can do, but all of them constantly ask how you will spend your time. Seiji Maruyama does not know how much time he has left — fresh out of prison, he is not a young man anymore, but he does not want the streets to know, so he returns to his life as a yakuza heavy hoping to make his mark again.

The game ends when Seiji's time runs out. Based on the choices you make, the consequences of which are initially obfuscated, that could be five minutes after the game begins or five hours. He has a bad cough and a pack of cigarettes, each a metaphor for the ticking clock inside him. You could go to work, brawling on the streets, or simply pass the time — listen to a jazz band, play video poker, buy a home, fall in love. You have the time, until you do not.
