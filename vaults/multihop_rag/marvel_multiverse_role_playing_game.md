# Marvel Multiverse Role-Playing Game

The Marvel Multiverse Role-playing Game is the lightweight superhero RPG a Polygon contributor had been looking for: they had tried quite a few without finding one that hit the mark, were initially dubious about this one, and say it blew them out of the water.

The game is utterly devoted to giving players the feeling of playing a hero in the Marvel universe, with tons of cool powers and power categories based on key Marvel heroes like Spider-Man and the Hulk, in a system the contributor calls easy, streamlined, and carrying the four-color comic book flavor. The feature singled out as probably the coolest is that, while there is an exhaustive list of Marvel heroes and villains at the back of the book, you can create your own hero with their own power set and use that "bestiary" of heroic and villainous figures as allies and enemies in your own adventures — making it a must-have for anyone who has wanted to make their own X-Gene Mutant, Spirit of Vengeance, or Spidersona.
