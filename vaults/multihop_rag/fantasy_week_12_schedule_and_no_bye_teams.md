# Fantasy Week 12 Schedule And No-Bye Teams

No running backs were on bye in Week 12 of the 2023 NFL season — Sporting News called it a Thanksgiving miracle on Nov. 21, 2023 — and the week also carried NFL action on four different days: Thursday, Friday, Sunday and Monday.

The week arrived with three regular-season weeks remaining until the fantasy football playoffs, so that every decision — from the tough starts to the difficult sits, the savvy waiver adds to the costly drops — could ultimately decide whether a team was a contender or a pretender. Trade deadlines had passed in most leagues, so the majority of fantasy owners had to soldier on toward the home stretch with the main core they had built to that point.

The offsetting complications Sporting News listed for the bye-free week were plenty of short turnarounds, some potential weather concerns and the dreaded injury bug; the column positioned itself as having been ahead of the curve with sleepers and breakouts all season and therefore more prepared to weather any storm than the average fantasy Joe.
