# Ginger Snaps (Werewolf Body Horror)

Ginger Snaps, directed by future Orphan Black co-creator John Fawcett, is Polygon's answer to a sizable hole in the monster movie canon — there just are not enough good werewolf movies. It was the Oct. 2 pick in the 2023-10-10 Halloween Countdown, listed on the Criterion Channel, Shudder, Peacock, Vudu, Tubi, Freevee, and Plex.

The film is a delightfully gruesome story about two sisters: one of them gets her period for the first time and is turned into a werewolf shortly after, and things get messy fast.

Critic Pete Volk says the film does more than fill in the werewolf canon — it is a grisly body horror story placed onto an effective puberty allegory with a compelling central sister relationship, and is recommended to anyone who loves Jennifer's Body, or who has ever watched something like Sam Raimi's Spider-Man and thought it was great and surprisingly gooey but wanted stories like that about teen girls. The movie has amassed a dedicated cult following in the years since its release.
