# Liverpool's Early-Season Form In 2023-24

By 20 October 2023 Liverpool had surpassed pre-season expectations of a new-look team: three points off the top, three goals scored in every Premier League home game, a 100% record in all competitions at Anfield, cruising through the Europa League group stage, and individuals shining.

The Guardian judged it not unreasonable of Jürgen Klopp, however, to expect much more as the season developed. Klopp framed the demand as intrinsic to his job: "You saw so many teams here growing in the direction we wanted and all became better step by step or the manager had to go," he said. "We will try together to get better step by step and show our real face more often than not."
