# Colts 31-28 Titans In Overtime, Week 13 2023

Gardner Minshew threw a four-yard touchdown pass to Michael Pittman with 2:31 left in overtime as the Indianapolis Colts (7-5) beat the Tennessee Titans (4-8) 31-28 for their fourth straight victory in Week 13 of the 2023 season.

The Colts had to score a touchdown because Nick Folk had made a 46-yard field goal with 4:19 left in overtime to put Tennessee ahead. Tennessee lost their first true home game of the season and had yet to win consecutive games; the Titans blew a 17-7 lead, and Folk missed his first extra point of the season with 5:26 left in regulation, a kick that would have put Tennessee up 26-25.
