# Twitch as an Alternative Sports Viewing Platform

Twitch has become a platform where sports streamers and fans have flocked, Polygon reported on 2023-10-20 — an alternative to the live events and cable TV through which fans have historically consumed sports, as media consumption has shifted and many, especially in younger generations, have sought out sports content on social platforms.

The influx has a specific origin. During the COVID-19 shutdowns in 2020, athletes and sports leagues around the world joined the platform to stream video games while live sports were temporarily on hiatus, and years later some are still active: WNBA player Aerial Powers regularly streams herself playing games like NBA 2K and Apex Legends, while Argentinian former soccer player Sergio Agüero uses his channel to livestream Kings League and Queens League games. Organizations like the NBA, the NFL, and the UFC have their own Twitch channels where they promote and share content — the UFC, for instance, runs watch-along streams hosted by former mixed martial artist, boxer, and kickboxer Jens Pulver.

Twitch has built structure around this. In recent years it introduced a TwitchSports channel as well as a stand-alone sports category to help users find live sporting events and sports-related content; the category page had 2.7 million followers as of publication, and offers creators commentating and analyzing various sports, reaction and watch-along streams, some live sports feeds, and streams of creators playing sports video games, spanning motorsports and soccer to tennis, volleyball, and even pickleball.

The scale and audience are documented: EnterpriseAppsToday reported that in 2023 Twitch's "Sports" category registered around 8 million hours of watch time, and YPulse survey research from April found that 66% of Gen Zers are tuning into platforms like Twitch to follow their favorite sports. "In contrast to the more spectator-oriented sports watching of earlier generations, Gen Z has evolved the experience into an interactive digital experience through social and streaming platforms," says Anastasia Pelot, YPulse's content marketing manager.
