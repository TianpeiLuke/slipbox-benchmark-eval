# The Merovingian's Henchmen In The Matrix Reloaded

The Merovingian's henchmen in The Matrix Reloaded are, on Polygon's reading, vampires — a seemingly throwaway line leading to a blink-and-you'll-miss-it scene with wild implications for everything you are looking at.

The setup is in the first film, where The Oracle tells Neo that things that go bump in the night are in fact real, the result of a program misbehaving, an error in the virtual world of The Matrix. In Reloaded we meet some of those misbehaving programs as the Merovingian's henchmen, and his wife Persephone notes that they are very hard to kill before killing one of them with silver bullets. Polygon's point is scale: while the twin ghosts get significantly more screen time in Reloaded, the world of the films gets much more interesting once you realize they are just two members of a whole assortment of ghoulies, and that a superhuman kung fu god is not the only cool thing a character can be in these movies.
