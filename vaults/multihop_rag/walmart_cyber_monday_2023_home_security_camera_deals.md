# Walmart Cyber Monday 2023 Home Security Camera Deals

WIRED's Walmart Cyber Monday roundup of 27 November 2023 covered three discounted security cameras: the EufyCam 3 system, a second, versatile Eufy outdoor camera, and Google's wired Nest Cam.

The EufyCam 3 (8/10, WIRED Recommends) records crisp footage at up to 4K with 16 GB of local storage on a connected home base, expandable to 16 TB; integrated solar panels top up the battery, and onboard AI can detect people, pets and vehicles and even learn to recognise faces. WIRED calls it a pricey system and attaches an explicit caveat: it stopped recommending Eufy cameras after a couple of security incidents, and only started testing them again recently after the company took steps to improve. The second camera, which WIRED says will appear in its Best Outdoor Security Cameras guide in the next update, offers up to 3K resolution, a second telephoto lens for zoom, 360-degree pan and 70-degree tilt, and comes with a solar panel and 8 GB of onboard storage; a limited frame rate and flaky AI detract slightly, but WIRED calls it a decent backyard option, and repeats the same Eufy security-incident caveat.

Google's Nest Cam (Wired) is described as the smartest indoor security camera you can get: it blends in easily and accurately detects people, animals and vehicles, and it boasts HDR and a high frame rate to make the most of its 1080p footage. The stated limitation is history retention — you get only three hours unless you subscribe to Nest Aware, which WIRED calls pricey starting from $6 per month, though that subscription also includes familiar face recognition.
