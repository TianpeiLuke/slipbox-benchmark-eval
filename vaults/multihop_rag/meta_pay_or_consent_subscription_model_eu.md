# Meta's EU Pay-Or-Consent Ad-Free Subscription

Meta's pay-or-consent model, rolled out in November 2023, is a binary offer to EU users of its social networks Facebook and Instagram: agree to being tracked and profiled by the behavioral ads business in order to continue or get free access to its products, or else pay a monthly subscription of at least €9.99 per month for an ad-free version of its mainstream social networks.

The defining feature is that the two branches trade off against each other in different currencies. As TechCrunch characterizes it, Meta's updated offer to EU users is either hand over your privacy or hand over your hard earned cash. What is presented to the user as a "choice" — TechCrunch puts the word in scare quotes — is a choice between being profiled and paying.
