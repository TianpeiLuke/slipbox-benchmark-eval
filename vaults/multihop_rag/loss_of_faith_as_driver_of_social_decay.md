# Lost Guiding Light: How Faith's Decline Is Said To Produce Social Decay

The model many Americans and faith leaders hold, as reported by Fox News Lifestyle, runs from a society losing its guiding light to a set of downstream breakdowns: corresponding social decay, and breakdowns of family, education, national heritage and basic science, are treated as the inevitable outfall of that loss.

The relation is stated as a graded one rather than a switch: there are numerous signs that individual welfare and social cohesion suffer as God becomes a part-time or non-existent guide in our lives and our nation. The article's summary of the present state within that model is that faith in God today clearly faces challenges, with society at large likely suffering.
