# NBA MVP Winners Since 2010

The Sporting News' list of NBA MVPs since 2010, published Oct. 4, 2023, runs from Derrick Rose in 2010-11 to Joel Embiid in 2022-23.

In order: 2022-23 Joel Embiid (Philadelphia 76ers); 2021-22 Nikola Jokić (Denver Nuggets); 2020-21 Nikola Jokić (Denver Nuggets); 2019-20 Giannis Antetokounmpo (Milwaukee Bucks); 2018-19 Giannis Antetokounmpo (Milwaukee Bucks); 2017-18 James Harden (Houston Rockets); 2016-17 Russell Westbrook (Oklahoma City Thunder); 2015-16 Steph Curry (Golden State Warriors); 2014-15 Steph Curry (Golden State Warriors); 2013-14 Kevin Durant (Oklahoma City Thunder); 2012-13 LeBron James (Miami Heat); 2011-12 LeBron James (Miami Heat); and 2010-11 Derrick Rose (Chicago Bulls).

The list stops at the 2022-23 season, since it was published before the 2023-24 campaign was played.
