# US Funding Of UNRWA Across Presidential Administrations

The U.S. has historically been the single-largest funder of UNRWA, a U.N. agency that relies on governments to support its work, giving more than $500 million to Palestinians from April 2021 through March 2022, including more than $417 million that went to UNRWA. U.S. support to UNRWA has fluctuated throughout different presidential administrations.

Total U.S. aid to the West Bank and Gaza peaked at $1 billion in 2009, after Israel sealed off the territory, and reached $1 billion in annual contributions again in 2013, when former Secretary of State John Kerry helped restart peace talks between Israel and Hamas. In 2018 the Trump Administration cut almost all of the money the U.S. typically gives to UNRWA, amounting to roughly 30% of the organization's total budget. The Biden administration then restored funding to UNRWA and other organizations helping Palestinians in 2021.
