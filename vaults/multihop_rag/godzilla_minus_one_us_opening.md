# "Godzilla Minus One" Takes Third With $11 Million

"Godzilla Minus One" took third place on the North American charts with $11 million from 2,308 locations on the first weekend of December 2023 — the biggest opening for a foreign film in the U.S. that year.

The well-reviewed Japanese blockbuster, distributed by Toho International, cost only $15 million to produce and had already earned $23 million in Japan. Toho's 33rd Godzilla film is set in the aftermath of World War II, stars Ryunosuke Kamiki, and was directed by Takashi Yamazaki. Koji Ueda, President of Toho Global, said in a statement: "This year, we made a concentrated effort to answer the demand of the marketplace and make Godzilla globally accessible across many different platforms."
