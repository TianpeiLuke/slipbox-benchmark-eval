# Ten Hag's Rebuttal Of Carragher's "Going Backwards" Claim

Erik ten Hag disagreed with Jamie Carragher's claim that Manchester United are going backwards this season, attacking the premise directly by saying the statistics show his team has actually improved, Sky Sports reported on 28 October 2023.

Carragher's argument, made in his column in The Daily Telegraph, was that United's summer signings have led to the club becoming "stale" and that Ten Hag should now be questioned about whether he can get the best out of his squad. Ten Hag hit back at those claims in his pre-match press conference before the Manchester City derby. "In possession we have to do better, but there are reasons and I don't go into them, but everyone is seeing why. But there are also facts so I disagree [with Carragher], we are much better," he said. The premise he denies is the factual one about performance level, and the evidence he offers is pressing data: "In high ball regains, we are a top of the Premier League. In the middle [of the pitch] ball regains, we are top. The pressing is very good. So that's not the truth, what he [Carragher] is telling."

Ten Hag conceded ground on consistency without conceding the direction of travel. In a fortnight in which United had picked up three straight wins against Brentford, Sheffield United and FC Copenhagen, albeit via far from convincing performances, Christian Eriksen said United still need to find their "balance and routines" this season. Asked about those comments, Ten Hag said: "We are in the right direction. We have a way to go. I see positives but I see negatives. What Christian is referring to is we are not consistently in possession on the level where we can be. Like Arsenal away, the first 30 minutes in Bayern Munich away. We should do this on a consistent basis. We don't take the benefit in the attacking transition moments, we should be because the ability in the team can take benefit from it. We pay attention to it, we need to grow. Then games will become easier." United hosted Manchester City in a Super Sunday derby that weekend, live on Sky Sports with a 3.30pm kick-off, billed as the latest acid test of how far the side had improved from last season.
