# DraftKings Week 14 DFS Receiver, Tight End, Flex And Defense Picks

Sporting News filled out its Week 14 DraftKings DFS lineup with receivers DJ Moore ($6,500), Drake London ($4,600) and Jaxon Smith-Njigba ($4,100), tight end Brevin Jordan ($3,100), flex receiver Jameson Williams ($3,500) and the Texans defense ($4,000), publishing on Dec. 7, 2023.

Moore (Bears vs. Lions) was picked for his chemistry with Fields, a connection shining more each week with 210 yards over the past two games plus a touchdown; Sporting News was especially fond of the 22 targets with a leaky secondary again on tap, and noted Moore cooked the Lions for almost 23 fantasy points in Week 12 and was well-rested off the bye. London (Falcons vs. Buccaneers) followed the rule that when the Buccaneers appear on the schedule you look for which WR1 will have a big day: it was not the greatest feeling seeing the Falcons, but London hauled in six of seven targets for 54 yards and almost added a score in Week 7 against Tampa, so history was on their side — and because they splurged for McCaffrey and Kamara they had to sacrifice salary elsewhere, making London a high-upside option even in an offense that does not throw efficiently. Smith-Njigba (Seahawks @ 49ers) was coming off seven of 11 targets for 62 yards against the Cowboys, a step up from a disappointing outing against San Francisco, with Tyler Lockett's involvement lessening as the rookie stepped up; if Seattle trailed, he should see action with a negative game script.

Jordan (Texans @ Jets) had been a great streaming option and fill-in for those who lost Dalton Schultz (hamstring), hauling in three of four targets for 64 yards against the Broncos; if Schultz was out again Jordan was a serviceable option with touchdown upside against a reeling Jets team, and if Schultz played, Sporting News would pivot to the Jets' Tyler Conklin, $100 less with plenty of opportunity in an even better matchup. Williams (Lions @ Bears) was the last-dollars swing: he typically has around three chances a game to make a big play, something he delivered on in two of the past three weeks including against the Bears two weeks earlier, making him the perfect boom-or-bust target in a game that could feature a lot of points. The Texans defense (@ Jets) was chosen to exploit the Jets' offense, one of the few that could be trusted to score fewer than 10 points every week: with Zach Wilson reportedly reluctant to play, that left Tim Boyle or Trevor Siemian to lead the Jets, and as the Texans tried to make a playoff push this one could get ugly.
