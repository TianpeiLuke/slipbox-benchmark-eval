# Jabra Elite 3 Deal

Another pick from Engadget's budget wireless earbuds guide, the Jabra Elite 3, came down to $40 at Best Buy for Black Friday 2023, matching the best price Engadget has tracked.

This pair's mildly bass-boosted sound is great for the money, and its snug, IP55-rated design is well-suited for workouts. It's a relatively basic set, though, lacking ANC, multi-device pairing, wireless charging and wear detection — omissions that are easier to accept for $40. Engadget gave the Elite 3 a score of 88 in its review from September 2021.
