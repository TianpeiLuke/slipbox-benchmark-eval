# Cocoon (Geometric Interactive)

Cocoon, developed by Geometric Interactive for Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, Windows PC, Xbox One, and Xbox Series X, is ranked 6 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31, and critic AD calls it one of the most memorable puzzle games she has ever played.

On the surface the premise is relatively simple — you play as a buglike creature that picks up and places glowing orbs to solve puzzles — but that is where the brilliant twist hides: each orb functions like a world unto itself, which you can explore or from which you can extract a new power for your bug. As you collect orbs you thread together puzzles that have you weaving in and out of realms in a truly mind-bending experience.

Geometric Interactive was founded by developers who previously worked on Limbo and Inside, and the game's brain teasers unfold in a dark sci-fi world, going light on a story that combines biological and mechanical philosophies alike while you hear the hum of engines and the moist squishy sounds of unknown creatures' moving flesh. Cocoon has one-off puzzles that are downright brilliant, but what makes it truly great is the sum of its parts: the pacing of each section feeds smoothly from one challenge to the next, and like the best puzzle games it presents instances where an earlier brain teaser serves as an unsaid tutorial teaching you a step for a later, more complicated puzzle.
