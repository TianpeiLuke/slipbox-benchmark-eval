# Claiming FanDuel's Bet $5 On Any Moneyline, Get $150 Offer

FanDuel's promo code link for the Eagles vs. Seahawks Monday Night Football game let new customers bet $5 on any moneyline and get $150 in bonus bets if their team wins, per Sporting News on Dec. 18, 2023.

The steps are: use the exclusive FanDuel promo code link to sign up for a new account, then bet at least $5 on any moneyline. On top of your winnings, you get $150 in bonus bets if your team wins.

The distinguishing condition — and the one that shapes which side to take — is that the moneyline bet must win to claim the $150 in bonus bets. Sporting News therefore suggested that a bettor wagering on that night's MNF game would probably want to go with the favored Eagles, and pointed readers on to odds and more picks for the matchup.
