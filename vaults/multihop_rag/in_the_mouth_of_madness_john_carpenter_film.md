# In The Mouth Of Madness: Carpenter's Apocalypse Trilogy Closer

In the Mouth of Madness is among the wildest movies John Carpenter has ever made — and Polygon notes that is saying something. Starring Sam Neill, Julie Carmen and Jurgen Prochnow, and watchable on YouTube, it follows insurance investigator John Trent (Neill), who is hired to find a missing famous horror novelist; things become increasingly unhinged as the plots of the author's books and the various monsters seem to invade the real world.

Neill, a staple of this list, is absolutely fantastic responding to the horrors of hell, slowly becoming exactly as off-kilter as they are: by the third act the door to hell is halfway open and Trent is ready to dive headfirst into the void, which the reviewer says is honestly how every movie's third act should go. Its place in Carpenter's work is specific — this is the third film in his apocalypse trilogy, which also includes two other stone-cold classics, The Thing and Prince of Darkness, which are not on the list but which the reviewer recommends anyway.
