# Ryley Sanders AFL Draft Prospect 2023

Ryley Sanders, profiled in Kevin Sheehan's top 40 for the 2023 AFL Draft as reported by The Roar on Nov. 15, 2023, is a strong-bodied midfielder who is powerful and courageous in his attack on the ball and in the pressure he exerts on the opposition. State: Tasmania. State League Club: Sandringham Dragons. Community Club: North Launceston. Date of Birth: 21/01/05. Height: 185cm.

Strong two-way running is another feature of his game, on display in dominating for the Allies at the National Championships, where he averaged 35 disposals at 80 percent efficiency, 14.5 contested possessions, five clearances and four tackles to win the Larke Medal as the best and fairest player. He was also named captain of the All Australian team after leading the Allies to their first title. He caught the eye with a strong performance for the Australia under-18 team in its representative match against Port Adelaide's SANFL side and backed it up in the second match against Carlton's VFL side, where he collected 24 disposals to be one of Australia's best in victory. He finished the year as a back-to-back Coates Talent League premiership player with the Sandringham Dragons, averaging 31 disposals across nine matches. Weapon: Consistent high-level performances. AFL player comparison: Andrew Brayshaw (Fremantle).
