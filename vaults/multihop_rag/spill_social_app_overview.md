# Spill Social App Overview

Spill is a social media app founded by Alphonzo Terrell as a competitor to the platform now known as X; it hails itself as a safer place for Black Americans and the LGBTQ community and has amassed around 200,000 users, according to Terrell.

The app, still in beta mode, had raised a total of $5 million in pre-seed funding as of November 2023, including a recent $2 million extension round led by Collide Capital. Its origin is tied directly to the Twitter layoffs: Terrell's journey with Spill started the previous year at AfroTech, which he arrived at just days after being laid off from what was then Twitter along with about 3,000 others amid Elon Musk's acquisition. He hopped on a call with former Twitter colleague DeVaris Brown about a business idea and spent three days in an Airbnb creating the pitch deck for what became Spill, then went around the conference taking last-minute coffee meetings to see who would give his company a chance.

A year later, at midnight on the Friday of AfroTech, Terrell held up a glass of Dom Pérignon and toasted his one-year anniversary of being laid off at Twitter — at an AfroTech after-party in Austin surrounded by thousands of people who now know his name and his app. Spill is the cool kid in town.
