# Prime Day 2023 Home Security Camera And Sensor Deals

WIRED's home security picks are a SimpliSafe system at 50 percent off, Petcube's Bites 2 and Bites 2 Lite (discounted to $80 from about $100), a budget video doorbell with a $3-per-month or $30-per-year subscription, a LoRa water leak detector kit, and a battery-powered 1080p doorbell with an indoor hub.

SimpliSafe (9/10, WIRED Recommends) makes home security easy to set up and expand with multiple motion, door, and window sensors plus panic buttons and key fob controllers; the brand has frequent sales — you've probably heard podcast ads with discount codes — but WIRED doesn't typically see it reach 50 percent off, and several smaller bundles are discounted between 40 and 50 percent with different accessory variations. WIRED didn't like the indoor SimpliCam, though it has since been updated and the new one is untested. Petcube makes some of WIRED's favorite pet cameras, and the Bites 2 holds more treats (1.5 pounds) and larger pieces (up to 1 inch in diameter) than some others tried, looking good with an extensive four-microphone array that sounds great both ways; the Bites 2 Lite loses the laser and uses plastic rather than aluminum but is significantly cheaper. The budget video doorbell, even with its subscription, is one of the cheapest options around and made the honorable mentions section of WIRED's Best Video Doorbells guide: 1080p video with a limited field of view and notifications that aren't the fastest, but reliable performance — and if you don't want a subscription, consider the bundle with Sync Module 2 at $47, also half-price. On leaks: water damage is one of the most frightening and potentially expensive disasters a homeowner can face, but learning of a leak swiftly keeps damage to a minimum; this kit comes with four sensors and a hub, using the relatively long-range LoRa standard to alert you the minute any sensor detects water, and is WIRED's pick for larger properties in its Best Water Leak Detectors guide. The chunky doorbell offers 1080p video with a wide 162-degree field of view, takes regular AA batteries or can be wired, and comes with an indoor hub that records video locally to a microSD card while doubling as a Wi-Fi repeater and chime, with wide smart home compatibility including Apple's HomeKit Secure Video — what earned it a place in the Best Video Doorbells guide.
