# Aric Almirola Joe Gibbs Racing Xfinity Move

Unbeknownst to most at the Washington Commanders game the previous Sunday, many in NASCAR became aware of part of Joe Gibbs Racing's 2024 plans in the Xfinity Series and the apparent hiring of a Cup veteran, CBSSports.com reported on Dec. 7, 2023: while serving as a guest of the team, Joe Gibbs was seen introducing Aric Almirola to Commanders owner Josh Harris, with lip readers online ascertaining that Gibbs appeared to tell Harris that Almirola was now driving for them.

According to Bob Pockrass, the expectation was that Almirola would drive part-time for Joe Gibbs Racing — where he began his NASCAR career in the 2000s — in the Xfinity Series, joining a driver lineup that would also include Chandler Smith and Sheldon Creed. That would align with expectations for Almirola's racing future, as he had entertained continuing to race part-time when it was announced he would not return to Stewart-Haas Racing in 2024.
