# Rebecca Timlin-Scalera and the Founding of Cancer Couch

Rebecca Timlin-Scalera founded Cancer Couch after being surprised to learn how few funds actually go into breast cancer research, her husband Tom Scalera told Fox News Digital.

"I think most people we talk to are completely shocked because of all of the money that's raised by 'pink awareness' and other activities, which are great — not much of that money goes into research," Scalera said. "When Rebecca found out, she immediately was motivated. She said, 'I'm going to take all my skills and all my ability and create this foundation.'"

Timlin-Scalera died of metastatic breast cancer in 2019. While her mission was personal, her husband said she was focused on the future: "Rebecca was hoping that she could accelerate treatments and that she would be a beneficiary of it. But she was always very, very focused on helping others and maybe helping our children." She was not a Mombie herself, but she loved the spirit of the effort, her husband said. "The thing about metastatic breast cancer is it seems to really take so many powerful and amazing young women," Scalera said. "Rebecca was certainly a rock star. Everything was possible with her. There were no limitations on what she could accomplish in life. She was the perfect kind of person to convert her challenge with metastatic breast cancer into a foundation that is really focused on making a change for others."
