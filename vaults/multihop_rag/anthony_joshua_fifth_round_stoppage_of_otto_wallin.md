# Anthony Joshua's Fifth-Round Stoppage Of Otto Wallin

Anthony Joshua stopped Otto Wallin in the main event in Riyadh when Wallin's corner called for the stoppage at the end of the fifth round, as reported by The Roar on 23 December 2023.

Joshua, a strong favourite, needed more than just a victory to convince sceptics his best days weren't behind him, and the British boxer earned some style points against the 33-year-old Wallin, who is from Sweden. Joshua, moving to 27-3-0, hadn't looked the same since holding the WBA, IBF and WBO titles, but the 34-year-old delivered a throwback performance, jabbing and moving forward with confidence while backing down Wallin, who fell to 26-2-0. A strong right-left combination in the fifth round eventually led to the stoppage.

Joshua declined the throwback framing himself: "Just another fight. I respect Otto. Not so much a throwback, just another day in the office. I'm just a gifted fighter that has a special gift and I use it to the best of my ability. We just want to do whatever it takes to be victorious. All I want to be is just be victorious for as long as I can. I'm searching for greatness."
