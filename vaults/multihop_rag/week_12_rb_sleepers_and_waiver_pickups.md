# Week 12 RB Sleepers And Waiver Pickups

Sporting News' Week 12 sleeper and waiver running backs, named Nov. 21, 2023, were Rhamondre Stevenson, Kyren Williams, Alexander Mattison and AJ Dillon — each argued for on a combination of workload and a soft run defense.

Sleeping on Stevenson at the Giants is done at your own peril: he has accumulated 231 yards from scrimmage and a touchdown over New England's past two games, the Patriots are coming off a bye, and Washington had just carved the Giants up on the ground the previous week, surrendering a whopping 174 yards and a touchdown. Kyren Williams at the Cardinals should go right back into lineups the moment Los Angeles activates him from IR, because Arizona had just made Devin Singletary look like Barry Sanders, so Williams' return from an ankle injury should be triumphant. Mattison against the Bears is worth rolling out as well, against another below-average run defense.

Dillon's case is about a suddenly empty backfield. Things look very promising for the short-term workload of Green Bay's AJ Dillon at the Lions, since veteran Aaron Jones injured his knee and rookie Emanuel Wilson hurt his shoulder in Week 11, leaving Dillon as the Packers' main man in Motown. Power backs have enjoyed the most success against the Lions, as evidenced the previous week by Chicago's Roschon Johnson averaging 5.0 yards per carry and D'Onta Foreman finding paydirt.
