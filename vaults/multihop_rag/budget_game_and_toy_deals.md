# Budget Game And Toy Deals

Three games and toys made the under-$25 list. Mass Effect Legendary Edition was $12, down from $60 — 80 percent off at Amazon as a Steam code. It is a compilation of the first three Mass Effect games, repackaged and optimized for 4K with reduced load times, improved performance, and various gameplay tweaks.

A six-pack of single-shot Nerf MicroShot blasters was going for just $19.99, $13 off, at Amazon — The Verge's pick for a family Nerf war. Each derringer-style blaster loads and fires one dart, requiring you to pick your shots carefully before having to reload.

Love Letter was on sale at Amazon for $6.29, around $9 off. The fairly simple card game for two to six players is easy to get into and acts as a great intro to more elaborate tabletop games.
