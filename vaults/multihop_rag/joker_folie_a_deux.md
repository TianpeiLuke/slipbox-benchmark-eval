# Joker: Folie à Deux

Joker: Folie à Deux is the sequel to Todd Phillips' 2019 drama Joker, dated Oct. 4 in Polygon's 2023-10-24 DC slate rundown, and reportedly a musical thriller.

Polygon describes the original Joker as an acerbic drama effectively amounting to a DC-themed riff on Martin Scorsese's King of Comedy, and argues it did not necessarily call for a sequel, ending in a wide-open place that invites viewers to write their own next chapter. The reason a sequel exists anyway is commercial: nothing breeds sequels like success, and the first Joker was a billion-dollar hit for DC during a period when it was struggling to find that kind of payday from any of its properties.

The cast has Joaquin Phoenix reprising his role as Arthur Fleck/the Joker, Lady Gaga taking on the role of Harley Quinn, and Zazie Beetz returning as Arthur's neighbor and entirely unwitting love interest, Sophie. Polygon's inference is that DC may have handed Phillips a blank check and free rein, since that seems like the best way to explain the musical-thriller premise.
