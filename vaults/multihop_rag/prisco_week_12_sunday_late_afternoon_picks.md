# Prisco's Week 12 Sunday Late Afternoon Picks

Pete Prisco's Week 12 late-afternoon picks, published by CBS Sports on 2023-11-23, cover four games. For Los Angeles Rams at Arizona Cardinals, listed as a pick'em at Sunday 4:05 p.m. ET on Fox and fubo, he noted the Rams were coming off a comeback victory over the Seahawks while the Cardinals lost a tough one to the Texans; Kyler Murray had played well in his two starts, which he expected to continue, and the Cardinals would slow down Matt Stafford and gang to win it. Pick: Cardinals 27, Rams 23. For the Browns-Broncos game at 4:05 p.m. ET on Fox and fubo, he called it suddenly a big game for both teams — the Broncos had won four straight while the Browns were 7-3 and would be in the playoffs right then. It would be the first road start for Browns rookie quarterback Dorian Thompson-Robinson, which wouldn't be easy, but the Cleveland defense is dominant and would shut down the Denver offense in a low-scoring game. Pick: Browns 20, Broncos 12.

For the Chiefs-Raiders game at 4:25 p.m. ET on CBS and Paramount+, he observed that the Chiefs were coming off a tough Monday night loss and had to travel on a short week, and that the Raiders had looked so much better the past three games, but this was a tough test against a wounded Super Bowl champ — the Chiefs get back on track. Pick: Chiefs 27, Raiders 17. For the Eagles-Bills game at 4:25 p.m. ET on CBS and Paramount+, the Eagles were playing on a short week after a road victory over the Chiefs on Monday night while the Bills were coming off an impressive victory over the Jets; Josh Allen looked good in that game and would again here, keeping it close, but the Eagles find a way late. Pick: Eagles 31, Bills 30.
