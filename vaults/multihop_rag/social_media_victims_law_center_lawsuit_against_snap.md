# The Social Media Victims Law Center's Lawsuit Against Snap

In April 2023, the relatives of more than 65 victims, represented by the Social Media Victims Law Center, filed lawsuits against Snap, the parent company of Snapchat — an app known for its disappearing messages features, and the platform used by the vast majority of the suit's victims.

The suits are one expression of a wider response by bereaved parents. In their grief, victims' parents are motivated to end this crisis to prevent another family's suffering while also giving meaning to their loss, and many have launched awareness campaigns, founded educational programs and advocated for legislative change.

What is new is the venue: some parents are now taking to the civil courts, targeting the tech giants whose platforms facilitated their children's purchases of the pills that killed them.
