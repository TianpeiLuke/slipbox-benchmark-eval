# Week 13 Fantasy WR Sleepers And Waiver Adds

Sporting News named Curtis Samuel (vs. Dolphins), Jayden Reed (vs. Chiefs), Lynn Bowden Jr. (vs. Lions), Jonathan Mingo (@ Buccaneers), Cedric Tillman (@ Rams) and DeVante Parker (vs. Chargers) as Week 13's crop of sleepers and waiver-wire adds on Nov. 29, 2023, each backed by a usage or matchup argument.

Samuel saw a heavy dosage of targets in Week 12, and with Washington passing at a high clip another high-usage day could be in store. Reed had scored in three straight games and, while his ceiling is higher in standard formats, he still had flex appeal in a potentially negative game script. Bowden — alongside A.T. Perry and Keith Kirkwood — was one of the only healthy Saints receivers, and a favorable matchup against Detroit gave each of them some flex value in deep leagues.

Two more rested on opponent weakness. Mingo drew a plus matchup against a reeling Buccaneers secondary, with a potential negative game script boosting his fantasy ceiling. Tillman, a talented rookie getting more run as the season progressed, was set up for a high-usage day against the Rams with Amari Cooper (ribs) banged up — although P.J. Walker had struggled under center.
