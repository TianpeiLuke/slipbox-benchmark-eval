# The Stepfather's Role After a Father's Death

The Reddit poster gave the family background behind the aisle decision: she lost the father of her children — Calla and Hawthorn — when they were six and eight, and remarried four years later, and it was made clear to the children from the outset that her second husband "was not going to be filling the role of a dad in their lives".

The relationship was introduced gradually. "We were friends for several months, dated some, stronger feelings developed and I introduced him to the kids to see how they would get along," she wrote. "We halted for a year while my kids and I did some therapy because their reaction to my husband was strongly negative, because they didn't want to replace their dad. But once therapy was ongoing, they were doing better. From there, things move faster but the kids were on board for things to move on." Her partner "was fine with that, as long as he was respected and they could work toward being a caring family".

The bond with the late father remained the stronger one. While she had a close relationship with Calla, it was "extremely tough" for her child to lose her father, and while Calla has a good relationship with her stepfather, "it comes nowhere close to the bond she had with her dad". The mother also said she continued to get close to her daughter after she turned 12, and recalled a point in Calla's childhood when she asked her mother to walk her down the aisle.
