# Expert Disagreement Over What The Oscars Slap Meant Given The Separation

Dating coach Jacob Lucas attacks the sincerity premise behind Will Smith's 2022 Oscars outburst, arguing that the newly revealed separation makes the reaction implausible as a husband's defence. "That's a weird reaction to have somebody who you've been broken up with for six years, you know?" he asked. "So, were they really broken up or is Jada saying that as an attention-seeking thing?" His challenge cuts both ways: either the couple were not genuinely separated, or Jada Pinkett Smith's account of the separation is itself being questioned.

Relationship expert Susan Winter takes the opposite reading of the same incident, holding that it demonstrates rather than undermines the strength of the commitment. "I feel as though Will has a tremendous love and commitment to Jada, as well as to the marriage and the family unit," she said. "The pride that he takes in defending his wife is extraordinary, almost to the detriment of his image, at times." Both experts spoke to The Independent in October 2023, after the separation was disclosed.
