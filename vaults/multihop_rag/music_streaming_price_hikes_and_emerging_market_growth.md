# Music Streaming Price Hikes and Emerging Market Growth

Robert Kyncl told the Code Conference in September 2023 that the opportunity ahead of the music business is twofold: "One, the continued growth in emerging markets, and also the GDPs of those countries will be rising at the same time. So there's lots of growth there. And then there is the price elasticity optimization in mature markets."

Music Business Worldwide read that last point as a reference to the price hikes seen among all the major music streaming services over the past year or so, and to the growing belief within the music industry that streaming price hikes will be sustained — evidenced recently, at the time of writing in September 2023, by Deezer's second price hike within a year.
