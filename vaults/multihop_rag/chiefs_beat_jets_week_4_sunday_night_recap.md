# Chiefs Beat Jets 23-20 On Week 4 Sunday Night Football

The Kansas City Chiefs beat the New York Jets 23-20 in the "Sunday Night Football" game played at MetLife Stadium on Sunday, Oct. 1, 2023. After leading by as much as 17 points, Kansas City watched New York tie the game at 20 heading into the fourth quarter; the Chiefs then took the lead on a field goal early in the final frame, and a crucial turnover by Jets quarterback Zach Wilson eventually decided the game, with Kansas City never giving the ball back.

The quarter-by-quarter line was Chiefs 17-3-0-3 for 23 and Jets 0-12-8-0 for 20. Chiefs quarterback Patrick Mahomes did not have his best game — 200 passing yards, one touchdown and two interceptions — but made clutch plays in the game's biggest moments. Wilson had one of his best games as a pro with 245 passing yards and two touchdowns. Despite the commotion surrounding Taylor Swift's attendance, Travis Kelce had a relatively quiet night with six receptions for 60 yards; instead the Chiefs offense was powered by running back Isiah Pacheco, who rushed for 115 yards and a touchdown.

Kickoff was 8:20 p.m. ET (7:20 p.m. CT), the same start time set for all "Sunday Night Football" games in the 2023 NFL season — 8:20 p.m. local time in New Jersey and 7:20 p.m. local time in Kansas City, which sits in the Central time zone. Entering the game, Kansas City was looking for its third straight win after a season-opening loss to the Lions, while the Jets were hoping to avoid a third straight defeat after a Week 1 win over the Bills.
