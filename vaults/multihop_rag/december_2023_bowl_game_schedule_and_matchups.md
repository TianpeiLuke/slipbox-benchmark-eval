# December 2023 Bowl Game Schedule And Matchups

Sporting News' bowl odds listing gave eight upcoming matchups with venues, kickoff times and broadcasters, four on Wednesday, Dec. 27 and four listed for Thursday, Dec. 27.

The Wednesday slate was: the Military Bowl Presented by GoBowling.com, Virginia Tech vs. Tulane, at Navy-Marine Corps Memorial Stadium in Annapolis, MD, 2 p.m. ET on ESPN; Duke's Mayo Bowl, UNC vs. West Virginia, at Bank of America Stadium in Charlotte, NC, 5:30 p.m. ET on ESPN; the DIRECTV Holiday Bowl, Louisville vs. USC, at Petco Park in San Diego, CA, 8 p.m. ET on FOX; and the TaxAct Texas Bowl, Texas A&M vs. Oklahoma State, at NRG Stadium in Houston, TX, 9 p.m. ET on ESPN. The Thursday slate was: the Wasabi Fenway Bowl, SMU vs. Boston College, at Fenway Park in Boston, MA, 11 a.m. ET on ESPN; the Bad Boy Mowers Pinstripe Bowl, Rutgers vs. Miami, at Yankee Stadium in the Bronx, NY, 2:15 p.m. ET on ESPN; the Pop-Tarts Bowl, North Carolina State vs. Kansas State, at Camping World Stadium in Orlando, FL, 5:45 p.m. ET on ESPN; and the Valero Alamo Bowl, Arizona vs. Oklahoma, at the Alamodome in San Antonio, TX, 9:15 p.m. ET on ESPN.
