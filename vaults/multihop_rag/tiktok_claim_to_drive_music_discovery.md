# TikTok's Claim To Be The Driving Force Behind Music Discovery

TikTok's argument, made by Global Head of Music Ole Obermann on the back of the Luminate findings, is that "TikTok is the driving force behind music discovery in the industry, and that TikTok users are active, engaged and highly valuable drivers of music industry revenues."

The stated motive for commissioning the research was to move beyond the anecdotal: "TikTok is already famous for being the launchpad for creating viral hits and breaking new artists, but we wanted to understand how TikTok and its users impact the music industry more broadly," Obermann said in a statement, adding that "Luminate's findings prove what we had long known." MBW places the study in a pattern: it is part of an ongoing effort by TikTok to highlight its growing role in the development of global music culture, and last year TikTok released a report asserting that 13 out of the 14 songs to reach number one on the US Billboard Hot 100 in 2022 were driven by viral trends on TikTok.
