# How Eileen And Jeff Shiffrin Met Through Skiing

Mikaela Shiffrin is the second child of Eileen and Jeff Shiffrin, who met in 1985 at St. Elizabeth's Medical Center in Brighton, Massachusetts, as reported by Essentially Sports on 3 November 2023.

Eileen had skiing in her blood and grew up with a passion for the sport: when she was a child her parents would take her and her siblings up Mt. Greylock during the winter, where they used sickles to cut the meadow grass and prepare a slope for their children to ski on, and that is where her deep love for the sport took root. She had to pause that passion when she started studying at a nursing college; her mother became a nurse, and that journey led her to the love of her life. Fate brought her together with Jeff, an anesthesiologist who at the time was completing his cardiothoracic rotation. Jeff himself was an avid skier when he was young and would often visit Vermont on weekends with his family as a child.

Eileen described him as "brilliant, jovial, and curious, with a solution to every problem". Unable to resist her charm, Jeff asked Eileen out on a date, and he even had to convince her to give their love another chance — that's when the epic love story began. The couple took their first vacation together to the snowy wonderland of Aspen, where the snowy trails and scenic winter reignited Eileen's old passion for gliding down the soft powdery snow; she stated, "If it wasn't for Jeff, I wouldn't have kept skiing", and said Jeff reawakened the love of the sport that had remained in her heart over the years.
