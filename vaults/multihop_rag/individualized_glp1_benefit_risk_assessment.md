# Individualized Benefit-Risk Assessment For GLP-1 Drugs

The decision about whether to take a GLP-1 drug in spite of the gastrointestinal risks should depend on each patient's individual situation, the researchers said -- there is no single answer that holds for everyone taking the class.

Etminan gave the two ends of the range that make the assessment individual. "This decision should be individually assessed," he said. "There are patients where the benefit of the drugs might outweigh the risks (very obese individuals), whereas in other situations (healthy individuals who just want to lose a few pounds), the risks might outweigh the benefits."
