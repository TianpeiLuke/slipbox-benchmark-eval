# Meta's Response To The Norwegian DPA Referral

Meta's answer to Norway's referral of its consentless tracking ads to the EDPB attacked the premise that emergency action was needed at all, arguing the referral was surprising given a commitment the company says it has already made.

Meta spokesperson Matt Pollard wrote in an emailed statement: "We are surprised by the NDPA's [Norwegian data protection authority's] actions, given that Meta has already committed to moving to the legal basis of consent for advertising in the EU/EEA. We remain in active discussions with the relevant data protection authorities on this topic via our lead regulator in the EU, the Irish Data Protection Commission, and will have more to share in due course."

The rebuttal leaves the timing question open, which is the point on which it is weakest. Asked when Meta will be moving to a lawful basis for tracking and profiling users in the region, Pollard declined to specify a timeframe: "We have not announced a date. We are still working through with policymakers what our transition to Consent will look like, and will have more to share in due course."
