# The Oklahoma-Texas Red River Rivalry 2023 Betting Line

No. 12 Oklahoma faced No. 3 Texas in Dallas with the Longhorns favoured by 5.5 points, according to CBS Sports' Week 6 lines published 1 October 2023.

The stakes are described on two levels. Always among college football's top spectacles, that year's Red River Rivalry could serve as a Big 12 Championship Game preview: after one month the Sooners and Longhorns were the only remaining undefeated Big 12 teams and the only ranked schools in the conference — a dream start for two programs that would love nothing more than to run the league one more time before heading to the SEC in 2024.

The playoff implications are described as even more pressing than the conference ones. Neither team would be eliminated from that conversation on Saturday, but the road becomes increasingly more difficult for the loser: a two-loss team has never cracked the playoff, so whoever comes up short in Dallas will likely have to win out to stand a chance.
