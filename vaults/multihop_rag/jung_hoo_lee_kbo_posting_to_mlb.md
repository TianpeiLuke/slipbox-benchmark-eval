# Jung Hoo Lee Posted From The KBO To MLB

Star Korean outfielder Jung Hoo Lee was set for a move to MLB: his KBO team, the Kiwoom Heroes, announced the 25-year-old would be posted on Monday, so his 30-day negotiating window with MLB teams began Tuesday at 8 a.m. ET, per Jeeho Yoo of Yonhap News in Sporting News' Dec. 4, 2023 tracker entry.

Lee was hampered by injury in 2023 but slashed .349/.421/.575 in 2022 with 36 doubles, 10 triples and a career-best 23 home runs. He had a .340 career average in seven KBO seasons and had mostly played center field in recent years.
