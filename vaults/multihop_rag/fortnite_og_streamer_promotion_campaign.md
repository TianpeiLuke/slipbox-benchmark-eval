# Fortnite OG Streamer Promotion Campaign

Epic's re-launch of Chapter 1 comes with more fanfare than your average Fortnite season, TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-30. Ninja, aka Tyler Blevins, one of the first streaming superstars and still among the most popular, promoted the new content by showcasing a giant replica of one of the game's iconic Durr Burgers in his backyard, and other classic Fortnite streaming stars got the same treatment.

Ninja posted on October 27, 2023: "Woke up to find the OG Fortnite is coming back November 3rd AND THERES A BURGER IN MY BACKYARD … lets talk about it." timthetatman posted the same day: "THERE IS NO WAY FORTNITE SENT ME THIS @FortniteGame."
