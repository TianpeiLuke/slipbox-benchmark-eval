# Notre Dame's Second Quarter Against USC, Oct. 14 2023

Notre Dame outscored USC 17-3 in the second quarter of their Oct. 14, 2023 game, turning a 7-3 lead into a 24-6 halftime advantage on a Spencer Shrader field goal and two Audric Estime touchdown runs, both set up by Caleb Williams interceptions.

The quarter began at 8:13 p.m. with a big Notre Dame play: Chris Tyree caught a short pass and took it 16 yards behind blockers for a first down to the Notre Dame 42. On the next play Jordan Faison took a reverse that fooled the USC defense and gained 16 yards to the USC 26. On third-and-7 Hartman threw short to Mitchell Evans, who was stopped for a five-yard gain, and Notre Dame settled for Shrader's 36-yard field goal at 8:16 p.m.: Notre Dame 10, USC 3. USC started at its own 25 and moved the ball on the ground — a Washington jet sweep for 10 to the USC 35 and a six-yard Jones run up the middle, giving the Trojans 47 rushing yards in the half — then Williams converted third-and-4 with a pass to Mario Williams at the line of scrimmage. After off-setting penalties reset the downs, Williams and Rice could not connect deep, but Lloyd bounced outside for seven yards into Notre Dame territory. DJ Brown then made a solo tackle on Lloyd three yards behind the line; USC punted but Notre Dame was flagged for running into the kicker, giving USC fourth-and-1, which the Trojans went for. Williams kept on an option for eight yards to the Notre Dame 38, and after an incompletion he appeared to score a 38-yard rushing touchdown that would have tied the game at 10 — but left guard Emmanuel Pregnon was called for offensive holding, wiping out the play and creating second-and-20.

Branch then gained 17 yards to set up third-and-3 at the Notre Dame 31, Jones was stopped a yard short on third down, and USC again faced fourth-and-1 with the offense staying on the field. This time JD Bertrand brought Jones down for a loss of four as soon as he took the handoff, giving Notre Dame the ball at its own 33. Notre Dame went three-and-out — Estime gained four, Hartman missed Faison deep, and Roland-Wallace swatted the ball out of Evans' hands over the middle — and Branch returned the punt 12 yards to the USC 25 before a holding penalty brought the ball back to the USC 6. Williams found Rice on a crossing route but Rice dropped it, then hit Branch in the slot for 10 yards and a first down to the 16. At 8:42 p.m. Williams threw his second interception, aiming for Singer but hitting Watts in double coverage after the ball was tipped at the line — Watts' second pick of the game, and notable because Williams had thrown one interception all season and now had two, both to Watts. Estime cashed it in immediately at 8:43 p.m., plowing ahead two yards for the touchdown: Notre Dame 17, USC 3.

USC's next drive also ended in a turnover. Williams connected with Mario Williams for 10 yards and a first down after missing Washington deep, then had to throw the ball away when Bertrand chased him in the backfield, and at 8:50 p.m. he again threw into double coverage and was picked off, this time by Benjamin Morrison, in what the Sporting News called a half to forget for him. Hartman responded with a perfect 24-yard deep pass to Rico Flores along the sideline to the USC 26, scrambled 12 yards to the USC 4, and after Estime runs of five and eight set up first-and-goal at the 1, Estime powered in from a yard out at 8:54 p.m. with 1:30 left in the half: Notre Dame 24, USC 3. USC started at its own 25 after a touchback and made a late push — Williams hit Washington for nine and Mario Williams for 20, then scrambled three yards as USC used a timeout with 39 seconds left facing second-and-7 from the Notre Dame 43. A four-yard run brought up third-and-3 and forced USC's last timeout with 30 seconds left; Boubacar Traore then sacked Williams for a loss of six, and when USC tried to snap with several Notre Dame defenders in the backfield, Marcus Freeman called timeout in time to prevent the penalty. The Trojans made the most of the defensive miscue and forced timeout with a 48-yard field goal at 9:03 p.m., closing a disastrous half at Notre Dame 24, USC 6.
