# Unculturable Canine Bacterium and the Doxycycline Suggestion

Needle and his colleagues have not been able to grow the new bacteria in the lab, so the standard route to choosing an antibiotic was closed to them. Normally, to determine what antibiotics might work best against a particular type of bacteria, labs grow the bugs in a petri dish and then try to kill them with various medications.

With that culture-based test unavailable, the team fell back on the organism itself: its structure offers some clues about which medications might be the best choice to fight it, Needle said. On that basis he suggested that the antibiotic doxycycline may be effective.

The scope of the claim matters. It is a suggestion inferred from structure, not a susceptibility result, because the confirming experiment could not be run — as reported by Yahoo News on November 21, 2023.
