# Prime Day Video Game Title Deals, October 2023

The Verge's October 10th, 2023 Prime Day gaming roundup listed discounts across current and older titles. God of War Ragnarök for PlayStation 5 was $62, down from $70, an 11 percent discount; while its third-person action gameplay feels similar to its predecessor, its story is far more ambitious, with Kratos wishing to bury his past while trying to convince his young son Atreus not to follow a tempting path toward discovering his godhood and defying prophecy. Demon's Souls (2020), a remake of the 2009 FromSoftware game rebuilt from the ground up by Bluepoint Games, was $30, down from $70, a 57 percent discount on the physical version at Amazon. Stray was $24, down from $40, a 40 percent discount on PS5 — a puzzle-based stealth game where you have to see solutions as a cat would and occasionally run for your life from the all-consuming zurk species.

Nintendo Switch titles featured heavily. Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope, an excellent turn-based strategy game pairing classic Mario characters with the chaotic Rabbids, was $30, down from $60, a 50 percent discount. Mario Golf: Super Rush was $39.99, $20 off, in either physical cartridge or digital form at Amazon, and Super Mario Maker 2 was also $39.99, $20 off, for physical or digital. Metroid Dread — the fifth mainline entry in Nintendo's franchise and a 2D side-scroller that is a direct sequel to the 2002 Game Boy Advance title Metroid Fusion — was $40, down from $60, a 33 percent discount on the digital version. Cuphead on Switch was $28, down from $40, a 30 percent discount; inspired by 1930s animated cartoons, it is a platformer and bullet-hell game with a high skill ceiling and little room for error, and the Switch cartridge version includes the Delicious Last Course DLC expansion adding Ms. Chalice as a playable character.

Elsewhere on the list: the comedic first-person shooter High On Life for PC was $36.02 on Steam, $39 off, featuring celebrity cameos as talking alien guns; Goat Simulator 3 for PS5 was $9.99 at Amazon, $20 off; Crisis Core: Final Fantasy VII Reunion on Xbox Series X and PlayStation 4, with a free PS5 upgrade, was $24.99 at Amazon, $25 off; and Streets of Rage 4 for PlayStation 4 was $19.29 at Amazon, $15 off, described as a whole lot of four-player co-op arcade brawling for not a lot of money. A $100 digital PlayStation Store gift card was effectively $100 for $110 of value, a 9 percent discount: for Prime Big Deal Days, buyers got a $10 Amazon digital credit with the purchase of a $100 PlayStation gift card by redeeming the on-page coupon or using code PSN100 at checkout; the cards are normally sold in $10, $25, $50, and $100 values.
