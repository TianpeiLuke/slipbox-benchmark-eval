# NBA Christmas Vs NFL Thanksgiving Tradition Comparison

Essentially Sports concluded on Dec. 25, 2023 that although the viewership figures paint a pretty one-sided picture, the choice between the NBA's Christmas Day games and the NFL's Thanksgiving Day games is much more nuanced and comes down to personal preference.

The grounds set the two leagues as genuine rivals rather than a clear hierarchy. The United States is home to several entertaining sports and leagues, but football and basketball have resonated more deeply with the majority of the population; the NBA and NFL share a fierce rivalry as the two preeminent professional sports leagues in the country, and while the NFL enjoys greater popularity within the United States, the NBA far outperforms its counterpart in global popularity and impact. Both leagues have crafted their own festive traditions, and these special events have become cherished customs: Thanksgiving Day games are as intertwined with Thanksgiving as turkeys, while Christmas Day games have become as synonymous with Christmas as eggnog and presents. The warrant is that each tradition holds its own significance, adding to the diverse fabric of American sports culture — whether one prefers the slam dunks and three-pointers of the NBA or the hard-hitting action on the football field, both offer a festive and entertaining way to celebrate holidays with family and friends.
