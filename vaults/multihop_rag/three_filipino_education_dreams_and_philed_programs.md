# Three Filipino Education Dreams And The PhilEd Programs

The proposal maps three Filipino education dreams onto three PhilEd programs. The first dream is to enroll in a chosen course in the school of one's choice, which will be funded by the School Vouchers Program, transferred from DepEd to PhilEd. The second dream, especially for Filipino parents, is to save enough money to fund the college education of their children, hence the prevalence of pre-need Educational Plans; because most pre-need companies went into bankruptcy, causing financial ruin and emotional distress to plan holders, there has been a massive market failure and government must intervene, but the intervention should not involve the government offering educational plans, rather insuring them. Just as the Philippine Deposit Insurance Corp. insures bank deposits, PhilEd would insure educational plans, and just as PDIC is funded from premiums paid by banks, PhilEd would fund this program from premiums paid by educational plan companies; PhilEd would be ideally suited to this because it could swap failed educational plans with school vouchers from its repository.

The third dream is based on the realization of Filipino school graduates that their learning must continue after they leave their alma maters, because in this modern age the fount of personal knowledge must continuously be replenished. The parallel drawn is with healthcare: Health Maintenance Organizations continuously monitor and minister to health, so Lifelong Learning Organizations are needed to continuously monitor and minister to learning; HMOs exist in the Philippines while LLOs do not. In partnership with international development agencies and the private sector, PhilEd would create this industry, with the international development agencies providing technical assistance grants to create the developmental and regulatory framework and the private sector organizing the LLOs, while PhilEd acts as the "bastonero" or project manager, managing the project as well as mobilizing government support and assistance.
