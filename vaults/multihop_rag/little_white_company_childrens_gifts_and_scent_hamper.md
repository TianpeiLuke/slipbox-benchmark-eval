# The Little White Company's Children's Gifts And The Scent Stories Hamper

The Little White Company is the brand's children's line, offering gifts for the youngest members of the family and for friends with newborns. The Independent's guide picks the Jingles Reindeer Comforter, made from recycled fibres with a squidgy red nose and felt antlers for an all-over soft and soothing feel. The framing around the section is that not everything on the day will go to plan — there may be squabbles, and you may not have got round to making your own Christmas pudding — but Christmas will be wonderful just the way it is, and it is kids who make it extra special.

For adults, the guide picks the Scent Stories Hamper as an affordable gift, stocking filler or present for surprise visitors: candles, hand wash, diffusers and more wrapped in a hand-woven basket, with scents of myrrh, fir tree and Nordic wood said to have the recipient breathing in the magic of Christmas through a swirl of seasonal aromas.
