# Thanksgiving Observance Outside the United States

Thanksgiving is not observed only in America: while it is one of the most important cultural holidays in the United States, Canada also has its own Thanksgiving Day, held on the second Monday in October, and Liberia celebrates Thanksgiving on the first Thursday of November.

The distinguishing feature between these observances is therefore the date rather than the existence of the holiday — the American fourth-Thursday-in-November timing is one national convention among several.
