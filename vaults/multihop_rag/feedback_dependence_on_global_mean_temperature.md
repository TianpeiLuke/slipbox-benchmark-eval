# Feedback Dependence on Global Mean Temperature

In addition to the pattern effect, radiative feedbacks depend on global mean temperature itself: warming Earth by 1 K from the Last Glacial Maximum emphasizes different feedbacks — for example the sea ice albedo feedback — than warming by 1 K from a Miocene hothouse world, or warming from 4 to 5 K in a high-emission scenario a century or two from today, where the water vapor feedback matters (Bloch-Johnson et al., 2021).

The consequence of that relation is methodological: the pattern effect and the feedback temperature dependence together add uncertainties to estimates of climate sensitivity based on the paleorecord, but quantifying their effects would make these records more relevant to constraining climate sensitivity and expected future warming.
