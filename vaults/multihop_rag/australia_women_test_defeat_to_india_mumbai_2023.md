# Australia Women's Test Defeat To India In Mumbai, December 2023

Australia stumbled to their first Test defeat to India, by eight wickets in Mumbai on Sunday, after being outplayed by the hosts in foreign spinning conditions, as reported by The Roar on 24 December 2023.

The scorecard detail given is that Australia were all out for 209 in their first innings and looked far more confident in their second effort of 261 despite the wicket showing more signs of wear; the hosts then easily chased down 75 in the fourth innings.

The report places the loss against Australia's recent record. It comes after one of the more difficult periods for the side in the past six years, following the retirements of Meg Lanning and Rachael Haynes. Earlier in 2023 Australia won their fourth straight global ICC event in the 50-over World Cup, then retained the Ashes via a drawn multi-format series in England in July — a tour that included losing four of six white-ball matches, with Australia's win in the Test enough to leave them level on points with England and hold onto the trophy. The current tour of India is not a multi-format series, and the defeat in the one-off Test marks Australia's first loss in a four-day game since January 2014.
