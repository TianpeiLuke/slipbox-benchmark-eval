# Echo Frames Notification Control And The VIP Filter

The VIP Filter is the Echo Frames' notification-control feature: a list you curate so that only select apps and contacts will notify you.

Its defining behaviour is deliberately minimal. The glasses play a sound whenever you get a notification, and then you pull your phone out to check; they won't read your texts. The Verge's reviewer appreciated that restraint, citing the annoyance of Siri taking ten years to read out the URL of a funny link a friend sent. The trade-off implied by the design is that, because you only hear a ping, you have to be ruthless when setting up your VIP list.
