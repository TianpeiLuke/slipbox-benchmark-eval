# Rocket League

Rocket League mashes racing games and soccer games together into something bizarre and unforgettable; it appears in Polygon's 2023-10-20 list of the 35 best couch co-op games for Nintendo Switch.

Polygon's framing is that racing games and soccer games are fun on their own.

The game asks what others do not dare to: what if you played soccer, and what if you did that with rocket-powered vehicles?
