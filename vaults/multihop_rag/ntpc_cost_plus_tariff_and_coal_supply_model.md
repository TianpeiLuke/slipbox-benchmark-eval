# NTPC's Cost-Plus Tariff And Assured Coal Supply Model

NTPC earns the majority of its revenue from the sale of power generated from thermal-based generating stations, and more than 90 per cent of that revenue is tied to long-term power purchase agreements under a cost-plus based tariff regime. The regime ensures pass-through of costs such as plant capacity charge, supplementary capacity charge covering emission control system cost, interest on loan, interest on working capital, and energy cost for fuel, and it allows a Return on Equity of 15.5 per cent over and above those costs.

The pass-through is not unconditional. Complete recovery of capacity charges is allowed when the plant shows technical availability, the plant availability factor, over and above the normative level, typically 85 per cent, meaning the plant should be technically available to generate power with sufficient coal in place; and pass-through is allowed subject to normative parameters and provisions defined in the PPA. The fuel leg is secured by long-term fuel supply agreements with Coal India Limited and Singareni Collieries Company Limited for supply of coal for 20 years, for a total Annual Contracted Quantity of 183 MTPA, and by captive coal mining with a capacity of 71 MMT.
