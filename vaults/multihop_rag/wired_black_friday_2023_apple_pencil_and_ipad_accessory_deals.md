# WIRED Black Friday 2023 Apple Pencil And iPad Accessory Deals

WIRED's Apple Black Friday guide of 25 November 2023 listed five discounted iPad accessories: a compact USB-C hub, both generations of the Apple Pencil, a tablet stand and Hyper's multiport adapter dongle.

The hub has been used by WIRED with iPads but is also great for MacBooks: plug it into your laptop's USB-C port and you get another USB-C port for passthrough charging, a USB-A port, an HDMI output and a headphone jack, in a fairly compact and thin body that adds little bulk. WIRED notes it dipped to $40 back in August but still calls the current price a good deal. The first-generation Apple Pencil has been around a while but still functions well, with pressure sensitivity (lines thicken as you press harder), tilt sensitivity for shading, and handwriting support in search fields and apps like Notes; it works with the iPad (6th generation and later), iPad Air (3rd generation), iPad Mini (5th generation), iPad Pro 12.9-inch (1st and 2nd generation), iPad Pro 10.5-inch and iPad Pro 9.7-inch, and WIRED notes it dips to this price often but still rates the deal.

The second-generation Apple Pencil is the most feature-packed stylus Apple offers, compatible with the iPad Air (4th generation and later), iPad Mini (6th generation), iPad Pro 11-inch (1st generation and later) and iPad Pro 12.9-inch (3rd generation and later); it adds wireless pairing and wireless charging when magnetically attached to the top edge of supported iPads to the first model's pressure sensitivity, tilt sensitivity and handwriting support, plus a double-tap gesture for switching tools quickly. It too hits this low often, but WIRED calls it a solid discount if you do not want to pay full price. The tablet stand often goes on sale during the holidays but remains a good deal; it appears in several WIRED guides including Best iPad Accessories and Best Work-from-Home Gear, and is portable, lightweight and easy to use. Hyper's adapter dongle has a 60-watt USB-C port, USB-A, a MicroSD slot, an SD card slot, 3.5-mm audio and 4K 60-Hz HDMI in a sleek, compact space-gray metal enclosure with rounded edges designed to blend in with Apple devices; writer Jaina Grey has carried one for years, frequently losing and finding it, and says it really helps turn an iPad into a portable workstation.
