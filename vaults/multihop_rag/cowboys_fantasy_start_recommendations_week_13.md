# Cowboys Fantasy Start Recommendations For Week 13

Sporting News advised on Nov. 30, 2023 starting nearly the whole Dallas side for the Week 13 Thursday night game against Seattle — Dak Prescott, CeeDee Lamb, Jake Ferguson, the Cowboys D/ST and kicker Brandon Aubrey — with Brandin Cooks the one Cowboy to fade.

The case for Prescott is his form and his ranking: after a sizzling seven-game stretch he has ascended behind only Josh Allen and Jalen Hurts among fantasy quarterbacks this season, and although the Seahawks have not given up many big games to QBs, you need to keep rolling with Dak on his heater, especially at home — though one cannot be surprised if it is more of a 250-and-2 kind of night. Lamb can eat again in the slot against Jamal Adams, avoiding the Seahawks' better cornerbacks on the outside, but a massive game should not be expected because the running game, defense and special teams will have plenty of success in another big victory.

Ferguson is the start over Cooks on matchup grounds. Ferguson has slumped over the past two weeks, catching a combined four-of-eight targets for 67 yards and no touchdowns against the Commanders and Panthers, but should be more active working the middle of the field since the Seahawks' tight end defense is a bit inflated by the numbers — the game is set up to be more of a Ferguson game than a Cooks game. Cooks is faded because of rookie Devon Witherspoon, quickly emerging as the Seahawks' best corner, because the nature of the zone defense is to limit big plays, and because between Lamb, Pollard, the tight ends and the younger receivers Cooks will not be needed much. The Cowboys D/ST is once again the top-scoring fantasy defense thanks to six massive outings including the past two games, and will get it done against Geno. Aubrey has been elite as a rookie with his field-goal production and should be good for a couple of field goals to complement the Cowboys' touchdowns.
