# Patriots-Broncos Start Time And Broadcast Details

The Patriots-Broncos Sunday night game of Dec. 24, 2023 kicked off at 8:15 p.m. ET (6:15 p.m. MT) at Empower Field at Mile High and aired on NFL Network in the United States.

Viewers could also watch on Fubo, which offers a free trial, or stream on NFL+. Those in Canada could watch on TSN and CTV2 in English and RDS in French, or stream it on DAZN, which carries every NFL game all season. Rich Eisen handled play-by-play and Kurt Warner served as the color analyst, with James Palmer and Sherree Burruss on the sidelines.
