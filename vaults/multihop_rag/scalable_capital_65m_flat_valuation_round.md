# Scalable Capital's $65M Flat-Valuation Round

Scalable Capital, a Munich startup that aims to make investing in financial markets accessible to a wider range of consumers, has raised €60 million in equity — $65 million at 7 December 2023 rates — at a flat valuation of $1.4 billion. Balderton led the round, with participation from HV Capital out of its new growth fund and other unnamed existing backers, and the company will use the funds to build out its business in the six countries where it is already active — Germany, Austria, Italy, France, Spain and the Netherlands — and to expand into more.

The valuation was the same as the last time Scalable Capital raised money: $180 million in 2021, in a round led by Tencent with BlackRock, HV and Tengelmann participating. That is despite the startup now being "four or five times bigger" than it was two years ago, according to co-CEO Erik Podzuweit, who co-founded the company with Florian Prucker.

"Yes, we are four or five times bigger than we were at the last round," Podzuweit said in an interview. "So like for like, obviously it's a very attractive deal for the investor. But in the current market environment, I think it's really cool that we managed to get to pull this off." He confirmed the company still has "the majority of the money from the last funding round" in the bank, but that the extra injection means it can be a little more aggressive: "We can do a bit more marketing, we can hire the top people but we also have a much bigger equity cushion." The company has now raised more than $345 million. TechCrunch updated the piece to correct the 2021 amount to $180 million rather than $140 million, and to specify the customer numbers and total raised after the company provided them post-publication.
