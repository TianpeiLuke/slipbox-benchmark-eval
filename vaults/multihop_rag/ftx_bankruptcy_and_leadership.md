# FTX's Bankruptcy And Leadership

The man who represented FTX before Congress after its failure told lawmakers the exchange had effectively no internal governance at all, and the prosecutor bringing the case called Bankman-Fried's alleged crimes one of the biggest financial frauds in American history. TechCrunch reported both on 2023-10-01.

Ray represented the company during a House Financial Services Committee hearing regarding FTX. Asked whether the firm had significant risk management systems, Ray said at the time that "there were virtually no internal controls and no separateness whatsoever," and added that he did not "trust a single piece of paper" in the exchange's organization. Separately, in a press conference, U.S. Attorney Damian Williams called Bankman-Fried's alleged crimes "one of the biggest financial frauds in American history."
