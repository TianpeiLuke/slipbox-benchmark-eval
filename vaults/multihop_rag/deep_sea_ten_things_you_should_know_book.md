# Deep Sea: Ten Things You Should Know (Book)

"Deep Sea: 10 Things You Should Know" answers the top 10 questions Jon Copley knows people have about the deep sea and also tackles some of the myths and popular misconceptions he sometimes hears.

Copley described the book in a video interview with Live Science, in which he discussed the latest discoveries and where deep sea research is heading in a warming world. Interviewer Sascha Pare noted that four years earlier, when she was a student in his deep sea ecology lectures, he had just published his first book, "Ask an Ocean Explorer" (Hodder & Stoughton, 2019), which had 25 chapters each answering a question people commonly ask him as a deep sea biologist. What distinguishes the new book is its scope: the shorter format is an opportunity to focus and update the information, since there have been quite a few discoveries in lots of different aspects of deep sea biology since the earlier book was written.
