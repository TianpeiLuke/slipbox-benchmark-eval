# Jusant (Climbing Game)

Jusant, developed by Don't Nod for PlayStation 5, Windows PC, and Xbox Series X, is ranked 9 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31, and critic Ari Notis calls it the first and only game he has played that gets rock climbing.

The framing comes from bouldering: boulderers, rock climbers who do not use safety gear but also do not ascend very high, call the routes they climb "problems," the idea being that since you are never that far from flat ground, completing your route is more a game of mind over matter — you do not finish a problem, you solve it, like a puzzle.

Jusant is not technically about bouldering, since a climbing game where you are never more than 12 feet off the ground would have precisely zero stakes, but Notis calls it an apt conduit for the spirit of bouldering: every route you traverse is a problem to solve, navigated by alternating triggers to dictate which hand goes on which handhold, with environmental hazards and a pesky stamina wheel making things progressively trickier. You are always clear on your heading — up — and figuring out how to get there is another matter.

Notis contrasts this with how climbing usually appears, functional at best, whether it is Link refusing to acknowledge handholds are a thing or Nathan Drake snapping to ledges with a level of magnetism only thought possible at CERN. Jusant sees the inherent grace in the sport and portrays it on screen not as a means of getting somewhere but as the reason to go there in the first place.
