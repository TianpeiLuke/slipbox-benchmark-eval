# Romero's Unchecked Kick-Out On Colwill

Cristian Romero kicked Levi Colwill in the calf in the 22nd minute of Tottenham vs. Chelsea on Nov. 6, 2023 — behind the play, while lying on the ground — and no action was taken at all.

Sporting News' framing is that just four minutes after Destiny Udogie's escape, another Tottenham defender again got away with only a booking despite a VAR review for a possible red card; Romero, often criticised for his reckless nature, had a kick-out at Colwill that went unpunished. Social media clips circulated at the time noting that Romero intentionally kicked Colwill but VAR had not checked it.
