# Betting On Kentucky And Nearby Sports Teams

The teams likely to draw Kentucky bettors fall into two groups — the state's college programs and the professional franchises just outside its borders, since no "Big Four" professional team plays within state lines — as set out by CBS Sports on 13 October 2023.

Among the colleges, the Kentucky Wildcats are one of the most iconic college basketball programs in the nation and should be an extremely popular betting option for Bluegrass State residents: eight-time national champions, led by legendary coach John Calipari since 2010, with four Final Four appearances during his tenure and a national title in 2012, and an all-time record of 2,021-637 that is the best in college basketball history; though best known for basketball, Kentucky has also found more success on the gridiron in recent years in the SEC, including a dozen bowl appearances since 2006. The Louisville Cardinals lack the Wildcats' historic edge but won the more recent national title, cutting down the nets in 2013, with 10 Final Fours and the 12th-best record in college basketball history at 1,726-874; Louisville has also become a bigger name in college football, well known for 2013 Heisman winner Lamar Jackson, having transitioned to the ACC in 2014 and finished in the AP Top 25 seven times since 2004. The Western Kentucky Hilltoppers don't have the widespread following of UK or Louisville but have a nice fanbase in the southern portion of the state and the overall athletic success to demand attention: a longtime Division I-AA, now FCS, power that fully transitioned to FBS in 2009 with nine winning seasons since, and a men's basketball program with 23 NCAA Tournament appearances and an all-time mark of 1,622-720, 14th in history, just a couple of spots behind Louisville.

Among the professional teams, many Kentucky residents look just north of the border to the Cincinnati Bengals: a franchise long known for its futility in the early days that became more of a contender under Marvin Lewis with seven playoff appearances between 2005-15, then got another jolt from stars such as Joe Burrow and Ja'Marr Chase, who led Cincinnati to a Super Bowl appearance in 2021 and an AFC title game appearance in 2022; the Indianapolis Colts and Tennessee Titans are also NFL franchises within short drives of parts of Kentucky. In baseball the state's allegiances are more split, with the St. Louis Cardinals drawing a big following particularly in the western portion of the state, but the Cincinnati Reds are largely considered the most popular team for Kentucky residents — winners of modern-era titles in 1975, 1976 and 1990 who haven't advanced past the NLCS since, and who have largely struggled in recent years, though the 2023 team pushed for a playoff spot all season, just in time to draw the attention of new Kentucky sports bettors.
