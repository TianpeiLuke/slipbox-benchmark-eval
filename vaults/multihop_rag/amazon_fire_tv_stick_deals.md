# Amazon Fire TV Stick Deals

Amazon's most affordable Fire TV Stick was down to $16 for Black Friday 2023, only $1 more than its record-low price, with decent discounts across most of the Fire TV lineup — including a $20 discount on the new Fire TV Stick 4K Max.

This cheapest model supports HD video as well as most major streaming services, and comes with a pared-down version of the Alexa voice remote. For the most powerful dongle Amazon makes, with support for 4K HDR content and Wi-Fi 6, you'll have to spend $40 for the new Fire TV 4K Max.
