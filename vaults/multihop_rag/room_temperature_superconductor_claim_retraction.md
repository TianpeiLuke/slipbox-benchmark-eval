# Another Room-Temperature Superconductor Claim Retracted

A new, supposedly room-temperature superconducting material is not what the scientific community hoped it would be, Tim wrote in TechCrunch's November 11, 2023 roundup.

The Nature-published paper detailing the material is facing retraction.

The conclusion drawn from that retraction is about the field's prospects, not just this paper: with the paper facing retraction, the odds of researchers discovering a room-temperature superconductor are looking even longer.
