# The Toaster Oven As Kitchen Workhorse

Senior photo editor Amelia Holowaty Krales loves a toaster oven: it is compact, works fast, and is perfect for reheating pizza, making nachos, and yes, even toast.

She uses hers more than her regular oven for sure — and probably more than any other item in her kitchen.

Hers is a pretty basic model, but these days many come with other features, like air frying and convection oven capabilities.
