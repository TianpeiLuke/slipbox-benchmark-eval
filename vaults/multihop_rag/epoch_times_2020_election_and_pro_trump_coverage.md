# The Epoch Times, The 2020 Election And Pro-Trump Coverage

In the runup to the 2020 election, The Epoch Times directed millions of dollars in advertising toward supporting President Donald Trump's campaign and published dozens of articles parroting his lies about the election, which produced huge growth in both its audience and its coffers.

That strategy drew criticism from fact-checking groups and got the outlet banned from advertising on Facebook, but it ultimately paid off, putting the once-fringe newspaper on a path that perhaps only its leader — who claims to have supernatural powers — could have foreseen. As its marketing strategy shifted, so did its content: by 2020 it had become a megaphone for the United States' most extreme right-wing stories. There was plenty to write about, since an election marred by disinformation, the bubbling culture wars and, most helpfully for a media company missioned with ending the Chinese Communist Party, a pandemic originating in China all provided endless opportunities for takes aligned with hawkish conservatives and conspiracy theorists.

The Epoch Times was also an early and aggressive promoter of election misinformation, according to the Election Integrity Partnership, a coalition of researchers that documented misinformation around the 2020 vote. That group cited the news organization as a "repeat spreader" of false and misleading voter fraud stories, as well as a major promoter of debunked conspiracy theories about Dominion voting machines and of the "Stop the Steal" movement aimed at overturning the election results. Months after the election, The Epoch Times still refused to acknowledge the results.
