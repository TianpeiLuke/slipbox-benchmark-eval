# Amazon Prime Holiday 2023 Delivery Cutoffs

Amazon Prime subscribers in an eligible US ZIP code can get Same-Day Delivery on certain items through December 24th, provided they meet the "minimum threshold" of eligible items in the cart, which can vary by location — in LA, for example, the threshold is over $25.

Prime members can get One-Day Delivery for eligible products through December 23rd with no minimum purchase amount, or Two-Day Delivery through December 22nd. Prime members in select US cities can also take advantage of two-hour grocery delivery through December 24th. The Verge cautions that shipping times and prices may vary depending on the product type, size, and whether it's shipping directly from Amazon or a third-party seller, so shoppers should check the expected arrival date at checkout and choose accordingly.
