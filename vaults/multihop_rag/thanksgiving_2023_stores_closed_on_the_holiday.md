# Thanksgiving 2023: Stores Closed on the Holiday

Several major US retailers were closed on Thanksgiving Day, 23 November 2023, in observance of the federal holiday, reopening the following day for Black Friday: Costco, Walmart, Trader Joe's, Aldi and — for the first time — most Walgreens stores.

All Costco stores in the US were closed on Thanksgiving Day, as noted by the retailer's official site; Costco warehouses reopened on the Friday with hours depending on store location, and the retailer had already started some of its online sales for Black Friday, with some in-store sales running from 24 November through 27 November. Walmart was closed on the holiday before reopening the next day for Black Friday; its usual hours are 6am to 11pm every day, but Black Friday opening times are based on store locations. A further retailer covered in the same round-up, not named in this passage, was likewise closed on Thanksgiving Day and reopened the next day, with day-after hours varying by store location.

Trader Joe's stores were closed on 23 November in observance of the federal holiday and reopened on Black Friday, with hours of operation depending on each store. According to Aldi's official website, all its stores were closed on Thanksgiving Day; Aldi reopened on Black Friday, its hours usually running from 8.30am to 9pm, though specific opening and closing hours vary by location. Most Walgreens stores were closed on 23 November 2023, according to a press release the brand shared on 16 November — the first time Walgreens had closed its doors on the holiday — although some 24-hour stores were still open during certain hours on Thanksgiving Day, depending on store location; Walgreens stores reopened on 24 November, with opening and closing times depending on location.
