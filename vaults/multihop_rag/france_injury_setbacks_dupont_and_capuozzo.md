# France's Injury Setbacks: Dupont And Capuozzo's HIA

Damaging setbacks had arrived with regularity for France by 6 October 2023, most notably Antoine Dupont's fractured cheekbone, though the prop Cyril Baille had returned from injury in the thrashing of Namibia along with the estimable Jonathan Danty in midfield, and both started against Italy.

On the night itself Ange Capuozzo failed his head injury assessment — another unwelcome blow — while Galthié's thoughts turned to managing his resources for the quarter-final: Melvyn Jaminet replaced Thomas Ramos, and Yoram Moefana, freshly introduced in midfield for Gaël Fickou, jogged in for the seventh try on 63 minutes.
