# Agility Robotics' Digit Warehouse Robot

Digit is Agility Robotics' warehouse robot, which co-founder Jonathan Hurst makes a point of describing as human-centric, not humanoid — a distinction meant to emphasize what it does over what it's trying to be.

Its features bear that out. Digit has a head containing cameras, other sensors and animated eyes, and a torso that essentially works as its engine. It has two arms and two legs, but its legs are more bird-like than human, with an inverted-knees appearance resembling so-called digitigrade animals such as birds, cats and dogs that walk on their toes rather than on flat feet.
