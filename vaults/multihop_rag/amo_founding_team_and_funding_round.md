# Amo's Founding Team And Funding Round

Many key members of the Zenly team are now working at Amo. In fact there are 10 co-founders: in addition to Antoine Martin, Corentin Kerisit, Michael Goldenstein, Claire Pluvinage, Charly Delaroche, Julien Martin, Quentin Perez, Nicolas Fallourd, Alexis Druon and Jean-Baptiste Dalido are all Amo co-founders and all former Zenly people.

The second reason Amo's launch is highly anticipated is the money behind it. The startup closed a $20 million (€18 million) funding round in February or March 2023 at a valuation around €100 million, with New Wave leading the round and Coatue and DST Global Partners also participating. There are also 80 angel investors on Amo's cap table.

TechCrunch flags why that is remarkable on three counts at once: it is a highly unusual funding round, as it happened in 2023 during a VC funding downturn, Amo is a mobile consumer startup with no source of revenue for now, and the startup did not have any product out there at the time.
