# Team News And Lineups For Manchester United vs Chelsea, December 2023

United lined up 4-2-3-1 and Chelsea 4-3-3 at Old Trafford on Dec. 6, 2023, with both sides missing a long list of players, per Sporting News.

For Manchester United, midfielder Casemiro and defender Lisandro Martinez remained out, while Christian Eriksen might not return in 2023. Mason Mount missed United's defeat at Newcastle United on the Saturday and Jonny Evans had not played for almost a month because of a thigh issue; Amad Diallo and Tyrell Malacia had long-term injuries. The Man United lineup (4-2-3-1, right to left) was: Onana (GK) — Dalot, Maguire, Lindelof, Shaw — Amrabat, McTominay — Antony, Bruno Fernandes, Garnacho — Hojlund, with 12 substitutes to be confirmed.

For Chelsea, Ben Chilwell remained unavailable and Malo Gusto was missing with a knee injury, but fellow defender Reece James was available again after suspension, as was Marc Cucurella. Romeo Lavia and Christopher Nkunku were likely to still be sidelined and Conor Gallagher was banned after receiving two yellow cards against Brighton; Carney Chukwuemeka, Trevoh Chalobah and Wesley Fofana were out. The Chelsea lineup (4-3-3, right to left) was: Sanchez (GK) — Disasi, Silva, Colwill, Cucurella — Sterling, Fernandez, Caicedo — Mudryk, Palmer, Jackson, with 12 substitutes to be confirmed.
