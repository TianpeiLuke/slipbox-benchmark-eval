# The 49ers' 2024 Free Agent Class

Only 16 49ers were set to be free agents in the 2024 offseason — Chase Young plus 15 others — and just two of those others, Tashaun Gipson and Clelin Ferrell, were full-time starters, with Young eventually figuring to take over as starter for Ferrell.

The full list of 49ers free agents for the 2024 offseason was Brandon Allen (QB), Oren Burks (LB), Sam Darnold (QB), Ross Dwelley (TE), Jon Feliciano (C), Clelin Ferrell (EDGE), Demetrius Flanagan-Fowles (LB), Tashaun Gipson (S), Kevin Givens (DT), Jauan Jennings (WR), Javon Kinlaw (DT), Ray-Ray McCloud (WR), Terrance Mitchell (CB), Matt Pryor (OT), Charlie Woerner (TE) and Chase Young (EDGE).

The Sporting News draws the inference that Young's quest to earn a deal with the 49ers could be aided by this thin field: while the team will have some key role players like Sam Darnold, Jauan Jennings and Oren Burks set to get new deals, the free-agent class is short on high-end talent, which will make it easier to focus on keeping Young if they so desire — so fans should view his acquisition as more of an audition to remain with the 49ers than a pure rental.
