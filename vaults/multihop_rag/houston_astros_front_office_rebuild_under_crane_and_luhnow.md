# How Jim Crane And Jeff Luhnow Rebuilt A Losing Astros Front Office

A decade before this 2023-10-22 retrospective, the Houston Astros were struggling to stay afloat, Essentially Sports recounts. Parting ways with the National League after 50 years of play, the team was looking forward to an American League move, hoping their fortunes would be reversed after regularly ranking near the bottom of divisional standings; the Stros applied every method they could think of to win, and they weren't all above board. Jim Crane obtained club ownership on Nov. 17, 2011, and that same year brought in Jeff Luhnow, a former McKinsey & Company management consultant, as General Manager. In 2014 Crane brought in A.J. Hinch as manager after the firing of Bo Porter — and there began the story of the rise and decline of one of baseball's biggest controversies.

Luhnow had always been more about the numbers than people. Wanting to take out the humanity factor from baseball, he was almost an outcast in the sport when Crane hired him, but his radical approach had worked in St. Louis, giving them two championships in six years — why not the Bayou City next? The Astros had not seen a World Series title yet and hadn't even made the playoffs since 2005. Apparently desperate for any measures, they resorted to cheating, and the article notes that perhaps the biggest surprise was that Luhnow didn't consider his methods to be dishonest.
