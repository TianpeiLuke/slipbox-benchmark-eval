# AfroFuture Rising Star Challenge Competition

The Rising Star Challenge is AfroFuture's annual flagship competition for unsigned and emerging artists in Africa, described by co-founder Abdul Karim Abdullah as the brand's way of supporting live music not only in Ghana but throughout Africa. Eligibility is limited to unsigned and emerging artists.

Entry and selection run in fixed stages. To enter, artists upload an original song to Audiomack and create an Instagram Reel that includes an introduction about the artist, their approach to music and the music-making process, and what they want their potential audience to know about their style of music. As a lead-up to the festival, 10 musicians are then chosen from a shortlist of 25 unsigned and emerging artists. From those, five finalists are chosen to perform on AfroFuture's Rising Star Stage alongside the festival's headliners. For the grand finale, one artist is selected for the ultimate prize — an exclusive recording agreement with Sony Music Africa for a single release. Winners additionally receive global distribution for their new single, marketing support such as a music video and more, free access to AfroFuture's recording studio, and mentoring and training from industry executives and leading musicians and producers.

In 2022 the format was extended with the Rising Star DJ Challenge, a call for DJs from across the diaspora to share their talents and win a place on the AfroFuture stage. Created in partnership with TuneCore to amplify the next generation of African superstars and find the next breakout DJ from the continent, it followed a different sequence: the top ten entries were selected by a panel of industry experts composed of TuneCore executives and AfroFuture DJs; once announced, fans across the world voted on social media to narrow the group down to three DJs; the top three finalists then battled it out at the AfroFuture Music Museum in Accra, Ghana on 27 December; and the contest winner performed at AfroFuture 2022 and walked away with a cash prize.
