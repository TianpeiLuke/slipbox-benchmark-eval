# The Rio Postponement And The Death Of An Eras Tour Fan

A heat wave in Brazil forced Taylor Swift to postpone the second night of her Eras Tour shows in Rio de Janeiro in late 2023. The decision came after one of her fans, 23-year-old Ana Clara Benevides, died from a cardiac arrest during Swift's first performance in the country.

Swift told Time that performing for her fans regardless of circumstance is "part of my identity as a human." She said: "I know I'm going on that stage whether I'm sick, injured, heartbroken, uncomfortable, or stressed. That's part of my identity as a human being now. If someone buys a ticket to my show, I'm going to play it unless we have some sort of force majeure."
