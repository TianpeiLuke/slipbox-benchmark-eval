# Kenwright As Producer: More Than 500 Productions Across Six Decades

Bill Kenwright's defining professional identity was as a producer: in a multiple award-winning career spanning six decades he produced more than 500 West End, Broadway, UK touring and international theatre productions, films and music albums, and was awarded a CBE for his services to film and theatre in the 2001 New Years Honours List.

The route into producing was oblique — he began putting on plays in order to give himself roles, and discovered he had an aptitude for pulling the strings behind the scenes. His biggest successes came from staging new productions of two musicals, Willy Russell's Blood Brothers and Andrew Lloyd-Webber and Tim Rice's Joseph and the Amazing Technicolor Dreamcoat, and making them long-running touring hits. He also staged productions of The Shawshank Redemption, The Exorcist, Cabaret, Evita, Saturday Night Fever and The Sound of Music.

At the time of the report his current shows included Sir Ian McKellen starring in Frank and Percy in London, with Twelve Angry Men, Calendar Girls The Musical, Heathers The Musical and Blood Brothers all on tour. Dame Judi Dench, Woody Harrelson, Billie Piper, Rob Lowe and Felicity Kendal have also starred in his productions.
