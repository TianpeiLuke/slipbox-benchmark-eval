# Supply Signals Pointing To A MacBook Pro M3 Refresh

There is a very good chance Apple will have at least one MacBook Pro refresh to show off at the event: both Kuo and Gurman have indicated that will be the case.

The supply-side evidence is specific. Gurman pointed out that the 13-inch, 14-inch and 16-inch MacBook Pro all currently have extended shipping times on Apple's web store, which is often an indicator that the company is winding down the supply of existing products.

Gurman also reported that Apple has been testing M3 Max and M3 Pro MacBook Pro models, which could now be ready for prime time.
