# Agility Robotics' Prediction Of Ubiquitous Humanoids

Agility Robotics co-founder and CEO Damion Shelton proposed that the warehouse robot is "just the first use case" of a new generation of robots he hopes will be embraced rather than feared as they prepare to enter businesses and homes.

His prediction sets a testable horizon: "So in 10, 20 years, you're going to see these robots everywhere. Forever more, human-centric robots like that are going to be part of human life. So that's pretty exciting." Whether such robots become ubiquitous within that 10-to-20-year window — and whether they are embraced rather than feared — is what the claim stands or falls on.
