# The 2023 Luminate Study Of TikTok And Music

TikTok's 2023 report was produced by Luminate researchers who studied data from the US, UK, Germany, Brazil and Indonesia, as well as global data, for a 12-month period from July 1, 2022 to June 30, 2023, and found that "compared to average consumers, TikTok users are significantly more likely to be music streamers."

The study drew on three input streams: consumption data from Luminate Connect, audience insight from the Luminate Music 360 Study, and TikTok engagement volumes provided by the platform — across five markets, the US, UK, Brazil, Indonesia and Germany, as well as globally.
