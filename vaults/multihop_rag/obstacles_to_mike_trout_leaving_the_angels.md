# Obstacles To Mike Trout Leaving The Angels

Essentially Sports raised the obvious objection to any Dodgers-Trout scenario on 18 November 2023: the counter attacks the premise that Trout would switch to another Los Angeles team at all, since he has been pretty persistent about sticking with the Los Angeles Angels from the very start and does not want to leave, even though the team has been transparent about not stopping him should he choose to play elsewhere.

The article then supplies the pressure that works against that preference. It seems the Angels will have a hard time affording a player like Trout in the foreseeable future, as they have to win a good number of games to stay in the playoffs and keep a steady income of revenue throughout the seasons — and considering their 73-game win record that season, that seems like a pipe dream right now.

The remaining scope condition is age and runway: Trout is 32, which means he has already passed the MLB average retirement age of 30, but he still has a lot left to give to the diamond for approximately six more years. The piece closes by putting the choice — Ohtani, Trout, both or neither — to its readers.
