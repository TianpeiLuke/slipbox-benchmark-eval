# Premier League

## Definition

The Premier League is the top tier of the English football league system, comprising 20 clubs, sitting above the Championship, League One, and League Two.

## Context

The corpus places the Premier League at the summit of a four-tier domestic pyramid: 20 Premier League clubs sit above the 24-club Championship, League One, and League Two, and this tier structure also determines entry rounds into the Carabao Cup (League Cup), where the 12 Premier League clubs not in European competition join at the second round and the remaining eight join at the third round.
Premier League clubs are also bound by league-wide financial rules: Everton were docked 10 points by an independent commission for breaching those rules with a £19.5m overspend in 2021-22, described as the biggest sporting sanction in Premier League history.
Match officiating in the league is overseen by the Professional Game Match Officials Limited (PGMOL), which has repeatedly apologised for VAR errors in Premier League fixtures, including a missed penalty in Manchester United's opening-weekend match against Wolves in the 2023/24 season.
Historical success in the competition is also tracked league-wide: Manchester United and Chelsea are named among only seven clubs to have won the "modern" Premier League, with Chelsea having won it twice since United's last title in 2012/13.

## Key Characteristics

- **Top of a four-tier pyramid** — 20 clubs, above the Championship (24), League One (24), and League Two (24).
- **Subject to league-wide financial rules** — breaches can trigger independent-commission points deductions, as seen with Everton's 10-point penalty for a 2021-22 overspend.
- **Officiated under PGMOL** — the body responsible for referees and VAR decisions across Premier League matches, including a public apology track record for errors.
- **A title tracked historically** — described in the corpus as having been won by only seven clubs in its "modern" era, used as a shorthand for a club's stature (e.g., Manchester United vs Chelsea).
- **Tracked match-by-match across a season** — referenced in the corpus via a club's progress through the campaign (e.g., a club's "11th Premier League match of the season").
