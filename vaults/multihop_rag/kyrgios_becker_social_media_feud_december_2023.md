# The Kyrgios-Becker Social Media Feud Of December 2023

Nick Kyrgios escalated his feud with Boris Becker on Wednesday 19 December 2023 by sledging the German great's criminal past, bringing up Becker's bankruptcy-related charges, as reported by The Roar on that date.

The dispute ran on X and had a specific origin. Kyrgios sparked it by claiming that current players are superior to those of the 1970s and 1980s, using Becker — a six-time Grand Slam title winner — as his example when arguing that older tennis players were poor pundits on modern tennis because the game has evolved so much. He told The Athletic: "The game was so slow back then. I've watched Boris Becker and I'm not saying they weren't good in their time, but to say that they would be just as good now, it's absurd." Kyrgios also reposted other quotes from Becker's interview and shared another post claiming he was the one bringing in more money for his fellow players.

The escalation drew on Becker's conviction. Becker was found guilty of four charges under the Insolvency Act, having been accused of hiding assets in relation to his bankruptcy, and he spent eight months in prison the previous year.
