# Investing Countercyclically In A Cooled Market

TechCrunch argues that Scalable Capital's focus on ramping up at a time when the market looks like it has cooled is very much in line with the ethos of investing itself, where people often put down their money at lower points if they believe that it will represent a great deal in the longer run.

The argument is offered with its own caveat: only time will tell whether that firm belief in growth over the longer term will play out as hoped.
