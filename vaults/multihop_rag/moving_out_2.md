# Moving Out 2

Moving Out 2 turns the experience of packing up and moving — a pain in the neck in real life — into a goofy, whimsical group experience. It appears in Polygon's 2023-10-20 list of the 35 best couch co-op games for Nintendo Switch.

Players take the role of a member of the F.A.R.T. moving crew.

The job is attempting to move furniture, and other items, around some highly inconvenient spaces.
