# Tottenham's Home Defeat To West Ham, December 2023

Spurs fell to their fourth defeat in five games with a 2-1 home reverse against West Ham on 7 December 2023, and, as The Roar reported that day, they only had themselves to blame.

Ange Postecoglou's men were sitting pretty at 1-0 up thanks to an early Cristian Romero header, then missed a host of chances to extend the lead against a Hammers side who couldn't get out of their own end. Having spurned their own opportunities, Tottenham presented two gift-wrapped chances to their opponents. Their defence was nowhere to be seen as Mohammed Kudus was able to shoot, his effort deflecting off two Spurs players and into the path of Jarrod Bowen to level the scores. Then Destiny Udogie's blind backpass — short of the mark, intended for Guglielmo Vicario — allowed James Ward-Prowse an easy shot on goal; he hit the post, and the rebound fell perfectly for the midfielder to finish, silencing the stadium.
