# HIV Pre-Exposure Prophylaxis and Other Prevention Tools

Pre-exposure prophylaxis (PrEP) is a class of powerful medicines that prevent HIV-negative people from contracting the virus if exposed, suppressing the amount of HIV in a person's blood until it is undetectable.

PrEP drugs are available as daily pills. There is also an injectable drug called cabotegravir (brand name Apretude) that is given bimonthly. Some African countries have additionally licensed a vaginal ring for HIV prevention; it is less effective than PrEP pills but works for a full month. Beyond medicines, condom use and voluntary male circumcision also cut transmission.
