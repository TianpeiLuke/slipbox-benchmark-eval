# How BetRivers Sportsbook's Up-To-$500 Second Chance Offer Works

BetRivers Sportsbook is another site that has launched in many states and could be among those coming to Vermont, Sporting News wrote on 2023-09-26. Like the other sites described in the article, BetRivers has a "second chance" welcome offer that gives new sign-ups a free bet refund worth up to $500 should they lose their first wager.

The steps: sign up at BetRivers Sportsbook using the bonus code SPORTSRIV; deposit at least $10 into your account; make a first bet, and if you lose, get the bet amount back as a Bonus Bet worth up to $500 — with the explicit caveat that this amount varies from state to state and could be different in Vermont. You'll see the Bonus Bet in your account within 24 hours, and then you'll have 30 days to use it. The Bonus Bet has to be used on wagers with odds of -200 or longer, and if you win with it you'll receive the winnings but not the bet amount back. On the product, the BetRivers site has all the most popular sports and leagues covered plus a lot of other international markets, with plenty of props and futures as well as a robust live betting platform, ongoing promos like matched bonus bets, parlay insurance and "bet & get" bonuses, and a well-liked loyalty program, iRush Rewards.
