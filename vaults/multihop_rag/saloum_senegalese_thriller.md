# Saloum (Senegalese Thriller)

Saloum is a criminally underseen Senegalese thriller that Polygon calls one of the best movies of 2022 — the Oct. 10 pick in its 2023-10-10 Halloween Countdown, listed on Shudder and AMC Plus.

The film follows three extremely cool mercenaries whose post-mission flight home gets interrupted; the trio find themselves in a mysterious village where something is off, and the tension slowly ramps up into a chaotic finale.

Critic Pete Volk calls the trio of lead actors absolutely magnetic — Yann Gael of 1899, Roger Sallah, and the late Mentor Ba bring to life a trio of loyal friends who also happen to be extremely deadly professionals — and, with fantastic costume design, a stirring score, and compelling images from award-winning music video director Jean Luc Herbulot, calls the film 84 minutes of genre-bending excellence. Saloum first premiered at the 2021 Toronto International Film Festival and went on to win awards at Fantastic Fest and the Vancouver International Film Festival, and yet far too few people have seen it.
