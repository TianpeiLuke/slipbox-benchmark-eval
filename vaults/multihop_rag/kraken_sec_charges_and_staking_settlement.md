# Kraken's SEC Charges And Staking Settlement

Binance's resolution came less than a day after the SEC charged Kraken, the third largest crypto exchange by trading volume, with allegedly operating as an "unregistered securities exchange, broker, dealer and clearing agency."

That was not Kraken's first SEC matter. Separately, in February, Kraken agreed to end crypto staking services for U.S. clients and settled a past suit with the SEC after agreeing to pay $30 million in charges for "disgorgement, prejudgment interest and civil penalties."
