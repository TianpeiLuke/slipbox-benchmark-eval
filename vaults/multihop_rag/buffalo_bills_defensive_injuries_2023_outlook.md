# Buffalo Bills Defensive Injuries And The 2023 Outlook

The New York Times argued on 16 October 2023 that this can still be Buffalo's season despite defensive injuries, because the rest of the AFC is the key variable and no other team has taken the conference over.

The grounds are comparative rather than absolute. The Bills are diminished and have imperfections, but Buffalo had already defeated the Dolphins, and the other AFC teams aren't exactly peaking: Kansas City and Cincinnati appear easier to defend that season than in the recent past, and it remains to be seen how well the Dolphins hold up. The warrant is that a contender only needs to be better than the available competition, not to match its own earlier form.

An executive agreed on both halves of the point: "Matt Milano and Tre'Davious White, those are huge injuries for that football team. The Bills are not as dominant as they felt going into it with so many injuries on defense. But the division they are in, they will still be able to slug it out and get to where they need to go. Nobody has really emerged in the AFC to unseat these teams at the top."
