# Totals Bet Outcomes: Push And Overtime

An over/under wager has three potential outcomes: if the combined score exceeds the set total, the "over" bet wins; if the score is below the set total, the "under" bet wins; and if the combined score of the game matches the total exactly, the bet is typically considered a "push" and the wager is refunded.

A push therefore occurs when the combined score of a game matches the set total established by the bookmaker, meaning bettors neither win nor lose and their original wager is returned. To avoid this outcome, most sportsbooks employ half-points in their totals: by setting a total at 200.5 instead of 200, sports betting sites ensure that a game's combined score cannot possibly match the set total exactly.

Overtime is a separate boundary case. In most sports and with the majority of sportsbooks, overtime does count toward a totals wager; Sporting News advises always checking the specific rules of the sportsbook you are betting with to confirm, but says overtime is generally included in totals wagers.
