# The Fourth Quarter Of Bengals 24, Bills 18

Cincinnati closed out a 24-18 win on Nov. 5, 2023 by stretching the lead to 14 and then running out the clock after Buffalo's late touchdown. Early in the quarter the Bills tried trickery on first down with a lateral and flea flicker that Allen fired away empty down the right side; he then scrambled for nine before Mike Hilton stopped him and sneaked for a yard to reach the Cincinnati 22. At 10:46 p.m. came the crucial turnover: Allen hit Dalton Kincaid up the middle, but as the tight end was flipped into the air he lost his grasp and the Bengals recovered at their own 13.

Cincinnati answered with a long scoring drive. Irv Smith dropped a first-down pass before Tee Higgins made a leaping nine-yard grab past the marker; on third-and-1 Zac Taylor called play action rather than a run and Burrow found Tanner Hudson for 17 to the Cincinnati 39 — Hudson, who had four total catches on the season entering the game, had four in this game alone. Burrow then connected on the big shot to Ja'Marr Chase for 32 yards to the Bills' 29, Mixon ran for four, Burrow threw one away and Cincinnati used its final timeout with 10:19 left; on third down Burrow found Higgins on a slant for 18 to the Buffalo 7. After a Mixon toss play lost three and Burrow found Smith for eight to the 2, Burrow missed Mixon on a swing pass, and Cincinnati took the field goal at 11 p.m. for a two-touchdown lead, 24-10, with 8:08 to play.

Buffalo needed the whole possession to answer. Allen went back to Kincaid for nine to start the drive, ran right past a Sam Hubbard tackle for seven and a first down at the Bills' 41, hit Khalil Shakir for nine, then threw an incompletion to avoid a Hubbard sack before Latavius Murray's first carry gained two and a first down at the Bengals' 48. Allen scrambled right for 11, and a low pass to Trent Sherfield ruled incomplete was upheld on challenge, leaving second-and-10 trailing by 14 with 5:33 left and one timeout. Sherfield then caught nine and Murray powered ahead for two and a first down at the Bengals' 26; Allen connected with Shakir for three and Murray for six to set up third-and-1 at the 17 with under four minutes left. At 11:13 p.m. Allen fired the deep strike Bills fans had been waiting for, beating rookie corner DJ Turner to hit Diggs for a 17-yard touchdown, and Buffalo converted the two-point try — again to Diggs — for 24-18.

The Bengals then killed the game. Starting at the 25, Cincinnati passed on the obvious run and let Burrow air out a 32-yard deep pass to Boyd at the Bills' 43; Mixon ran for two, Buffalo used its final timeout with 2:37 left, and Mixon's four-yard run brought up third-and-4 at the Bills' 37 and the two-minute warning — a first down would win it, since Buffalo could not stop the clock. At 11:21 p.m. Mixon got five yards outside right and was pushed out of bounds, and the Bengals knelt out the 24-18 win.
