# Lions-Raiders First Half: Detroit Leads 16-7 As Gibbs And St. Brown Move The Ball

Detroit led Las Vegas 16-7 at halftime of the Oct. 30, 2023 "Monday Night Football" game, scoring on three field goals and a Sam LaPorta touchdown. The Lions started with the ball (8:15 p.m.) and leaned on their rookies: Goff handed to Jahmyr Gibbs on the first two snaps and hit LaPorta on third down to convert (8:18 p.m.), Gibbs picked up another first down on a catch-and-run with David Montgomery out (8:19 p.m.), and Gibbs had six touches early (8:21 p.m.) before Craig Reynolds spelled him with a bruising 11-yard run (8:22 p.m.). The drive stalled and Riley Patterson made a 44-yarder at 8:26 p.m. for a 3-0 lead.

The first quarter then turned on turnovers. Josh Jacobs took a pass 12 yards for a Raiders first down after a flag was picked up (8:31 p.m.), but Garoppolo came up a yard short fighting for a first down and Las Vegas punted (8:33 p.m.). Josh Reynolds lost the ball after a catch and the Raiders scooped it up deep in Lions territory (8:36 p.m.), only for Kerby Joseph to intercept Garoppolo just in front of the end zone two plays later (8:38 p.m.). The Raiders forced Detroit's first punt of the night with a three-and-out (8:43 p.m.), then went for it on 4th & 2 near midfield (8:46 p.m.) and Garoppolo's pass went to no man's land after Hunter Renfrow fell down (8:47 p.m.). Goff hit Amon-Ra St. Brown on back-to-back plays for 20 yards (8:49 p.m.) and found Kalif Raymond wide open on 3rd & 11 for a red zone first down (8:51 p.m.); the quarter ended 3-0. A holding call on Taylor Decker pushed Detroit back (8:55 p.m.), Raymond came up about eight yards short on fourth down (8:56 p.m.), and after failing to draw the Raiders offsides, Patterson made another field goal at 8:58 p.m. for 6-0.

Las Vegas' one good drive came in the second quarter, and Detroit answered twice. The Raiders gained four yards and punted (9:03 p.m.); Goff hit St. Brown for 44 yards (9:06 p.m.); Maxx Crosby dropped Jameson Williams for a six-yard loss (9:08 p.m.); St. Brown answered with a 24-yard catch (9:09 p.m.); and Patterson kicked a third field goal at 9:11 p.m. for 9-0. The Raiders then strung together back-to-back first downs helped by a 15-yard low-block penalty on Aidan Hutchinson (9:15 p.m.), Austin Hooper's second catch of the drive (9:17 p.m.) and Jacobs' running into the red zone (9:19 p.m.) — Davante Adams had no catches while Jacobs had 12 touches (9:20 p.m.) — and Jacobs took it in himself at 9:21 p.m. for the game's first touchdown, cutting it to 9-7. Crosby wrapped up Gibbs for his third explosive play on a ball-carrier (9:25 p.m.), Goff hit Jameson Williams for 22 yards after Williams went catchless on six targets in Baltimore (9:26 p.m.), a horsecollar penalty on a Gibbs run was offset by holding (9:30 p.m.), St. Brown picked up another first down to reach 94 first-half yards after entering questionable (9:33 p.m.), LaPorta's first down put Detroit at the Raiders' 18 with Dan Campbell using timeouts at 31 seconds (9:35 p.m.), and LaPorta scored his fourth touchdown of the season at 9:36 p.m. for 16-7. The Raiders ran one play and went to the half, due to start the second half with the ball (9:39 p.m.).
