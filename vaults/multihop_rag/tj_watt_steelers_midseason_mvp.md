# T.J. Watt Steelers Midseason MVP

Yardbarker's Nov. 9, 2023 AFC midseason team MVP list named linebacker T.J. Watt the Pittsburgh Steelers' MVP, on the argument that he was the player who seemed to make the most of the plays Pittsburgh needed.

The grounds start with the team's oddity: the Steelers were the first team in NFL history to be outgained by their opponents in each of their first eight games and still have a winning record, at 5-3. It had not been pretty to watch, but Pittsburgh always made plays when needed. Watt's own line supports the attribution: the 2021 NFL Defensive Player of the Year was tied for the second-most sacks in the league with 9.5, and also had two forced fumbles, three fumble recoveries and one interception. The entry was written by Aaron Becker.
