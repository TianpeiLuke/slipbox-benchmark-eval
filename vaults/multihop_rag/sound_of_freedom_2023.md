# Sound Of Freedom

Sound of Freedom became available to rent on Amazon, Apple, and Vudu in early November 2023. The crime thriller runs 2 hours 11 minutes, is directed by Alejandro Gómez Monteverde, and stars Jim Caviezel, Mira Sorvino, and Bill Camp.

Polygon calls it one of the most surprising and controversial box-office hits of the year, and qualifies its premise: the film purports to be a true story about stopping child trafficking, but the truth is much more complicated than that.
