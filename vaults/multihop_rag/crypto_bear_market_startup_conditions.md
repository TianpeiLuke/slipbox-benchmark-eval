# Building Web3 Startups In Bear-Market Conditions

The claim advanced in TechCrunch's 2023-10-18 coverage of Alliance DAO's demo day is that the winners of the next crypto cycle are likely being built now, even though the eight companies presenting are not building in the most winsome conditions. While media attention remains largely fixed on crypto's best-known players, a host of startups in the web3 industry are trying to make a name for themselves despite bear-market conditions.

The grounds are the state of the cycle as read by an insider: "The crypto industry is likely near the bottom of the cycle in terms of venture funding and developer activity," Qiao Wang told TechCrunch. The warrant joining that to the conclusion is historical precedent — despite a chilly market there were a number of novel startup ideas among Alliance startups, and if history is an indication, cycle bottoms are when the eventual winners get built.
