# Canelo And McGregor's Pay-Per-View Histories

Essentially Sports set out both fighters' pay-per-view records on 2023-10-18 as evidence of their drawing power. On Canelo's side: his fight with Floyd Mayweather collected 2.2 million pay-per-view buys; his 2017 bout against Julio César Chávez Jr. collected 1 million PPV buys; his first battle with Gennady Golovkin gathered 1.3 million PPV sales; and his most recent battle at the time, against Jermell Charlo, collected 700K PPV sales. These were listed as just a few examples, with the observation that over the years Canelo has fought in increasingly profitable bouts.

On Conor McGregor's side, he has created his own history in MMA as well as boxing: his fight against Mayweather generated 4.3 million pay-per-view buys; his second fight against Dustin Poirier collected 1.6 million buys; the infamous fight against Khabib Nurmagomedov saw 2.4 million PPV sales; and his second fight against Nate Diaz collected 1.65 million PPV buys. The article's conclusion from those numbers is that a Canelo versus McGregor clash would break all their previous records by a mile.
