# Sportsbooks Recommended For Line Shopping

Sporting News names four big brands whose competitive lines in dozens of states make them the books to shop for the best prices. Caesars' odds and lines are consistently among the best in the country, making it a must-see when shopping lines: its point spreads can be a half-point to two points better than the competition's, and the moneyline odds may be more favorable.

The other three each offer a different edge. BetMGM's lines and odds are consistently competitive, and sports betting promos and odds boosts abound, so a better price on many bets can be found there. DraftKings has more open betting markets than most online sportsbooks, with lines and odds that are always competitive. FanDuel is committed to posting competitive odds and runs so many generous promos and odds boosts that it is always worth a look.
