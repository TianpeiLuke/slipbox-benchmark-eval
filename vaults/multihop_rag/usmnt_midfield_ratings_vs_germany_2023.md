# USMNT Midfield Ratings Against Germany, October 2023

In the United States' 3-1 defeat to Germany on Oct. 14, 2023 the Sporting News rated the three central midfielders Yunus Musah 6, Weston McKennie 4 and Gio Reyna 7.

Musah did well in possession but struggled defensively — he dazzled at times on the ball but was beaten on the first Germany goal by whiffing with his slide. His nutmeg of Florian Wirtz was a highlight moment of the match and he was active on both ends of the pitch, yet a few bad giveaways or defensive moments betrayed his usual stoutness in the middle; overall the mistakes didn't detract from his performance as much as Sergino Dest's, but they were still concerning.

McKennie drew the harshest verdict. Other than a pair of critical blocks right before halftime he was a mess through the first 45 minutes, constantly unsure of where he should be as Germany built forward with incisive pace, and one of many U.S. players caught unaware on the first-half Ilkay Gundogan goal. He didn't put a first-half pass wrong, going 18-for-18 before the break and 28-for-30 overall, but his positioning was all wrong out of possession: McKennie was a sieve as the U.S. midfield was invisible defensively, leaving their defenders completely unshielded, particularly at the top of the penalty area. It was only a matter of time before the floodgates opened after halftime, and he was eventually withdrawn in what felt like one of Gregg Berhalter's only tactical substitutions of the match.

Reyna, in his first 45 minutes of international football under Berhalter since the World Cup fallout, was his usual self — drawing the attention of German defenders and proving visionary with his passing, combining with Folarin Balogun on a number of occasions on the counter in a link-up U.S. fans have been begging for. He was only fit for 45 minutes as he returns from a leg injury, but it's no coincidence that Germany truly secured midfield dominance when he came off, and the Sporting News called it a promising performance for a young playmaker who should head back to Borussia Dortmund after the international break with plenty of confidence.
