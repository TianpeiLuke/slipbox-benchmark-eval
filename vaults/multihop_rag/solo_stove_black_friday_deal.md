# Solo Stove Black Friday Deal

Solo Stove's Black Friday 2023 deals knocked up to $245 off fire pit bundles, up to $100 off fire pits by themselves, and even more.

A standout is the Ranger Backyard Bundle 2, which was $145 off and down to $320: it includes the company's most compact fire pit along with its accompanying shield, stand, lid and shelter — everything needed for an easy setup right out of the box — and the 2.0 version of the Ranger includes a removable base plate and ash pan, both of which make the fire pit much easier to clean.
