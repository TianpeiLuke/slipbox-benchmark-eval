# Diablo 4 Barbarian Double Swing Endgame Build

This Barbarian endgame build, based on AaronActionRPG's Double Swing guide at IcyVeins and published by Polygon on 2023-10-19, is a general endgame build capable of finding success in all activities. It assumes you are over level 50, have access to all 58 skill points, and are making progress on your Paragon Boards; readers wanting detail on item affixes or the reasoning behind the Legendary powers are pointed to the original IcyVeins guide. The build uses Double Swing as its core skill, plus Steel Grasp, Ground Stomp, Iron Maelstrom, Charge, and Lunging Strike.

Because all 58 points are needed, the order of selection does not matter as long as the prerequisite points exist in earlier Skill Tree tiers. The allocation is: Lunging Strike / Enhanced Lunging Strike / Combat Lunging Strike; Double Swing (rank 1-5) / Enhanced Double Swing / Furious Double Swing; Endless Fury; Ground Stomp (rank 1-5) / Enhanced Ground Stomp / Strategic Ground Stomp; Charge (rank 1-5) / Enhanced Charge / Power Charge; Aggressive Resistance (rank 1-3); Prolific Fury (rank 1-3); Steel Grasp / Enhanced Steel Grasp / Fighter's Steel Grasp; Thick Skin; Defensive Stance (rank 1-3); Counteroffensive (rank 1-3); Pit Fighter (rank 1-3); No Mercy (rank 1-3); Iron Maelstrom / Prime Iron Maelstrom / Supreme Iron Maelstrom; Duelist (rank 1-3); Wallop; Concussion (rank 1-3); and Walking Arsenal.

For Technique, take One-Handed Mace. AaronActionRPG recommends five Vampiric Powers — Anticipation, Prey on the Weak, Ravenous, Domination, and Metamorphosis — and to insert those powers you need these Pacts available on your armor: 8 Ferocity, 3 Divinity, and 2 Eternity.

The build also needs powerful Legendary Powers and Unique items. AaronActionRPG's recommendations by slot are: Helm — Weapon Master's Aspect; Chest — Aspect of Numbing Wrath (dungeon drop); Gloves — Aspect of Anemia (dungeon drop); Pants — Aspect of Might (dungeon drop); Boots — Exploiter's Aspect; Two-handed Bludgeoning — Aspect of Retribution (dungeon drop); Two-handed Slashing — Accelerating Aspect; Dual Wield 1 — Edgemaster's (dungeon drop); Dual Wield 2 — Aspect of Berserk Ripping (dungeon drop); Amulet — Earthquake Aspect; Ring 1 — Aspect of Berserk Fury; Ring 2 — Aspect of Limitless Rage. Stat priorities are left to AaronActionRPG's original guide.

For the Paragon Board system, which Polygon calls very complex, Glyphs must be carefully paired with specific boards: Starter Board / Bloodfeeder Glyph; Decimator / Ambidextrous Glyph; Carnage / Might Glyph; Flawless Technique / Ire Glyph; Weapon Master / Territorial Glyph; Bone Breaker / Exploit Glyph; Warbringer / Wrath Glyph. Glyphs should be leveled to 15 in this specific order: Exploit, Territorial, Ambidextrous, Might, Wrath, Bloodfeeder, Ire. Players can path their own way through the boards following these basic guidelines, but for the best results should follow AaronActionRPG's path exactly.
