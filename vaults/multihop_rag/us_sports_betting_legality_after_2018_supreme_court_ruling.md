# US Sports Betting Legality Since The 2018 Supreme Court Ruling

NBA betting is permitted in the US but its legality varies across the nation, because the significant turning point came in 2018 when the US Supreme Court overturned a longstanding federal law and allowed individual states the autonomy to determine their own sports betting regulations.

That groundbreaking decision sparked a wave of change: since the landmark ruling, over 30 states have embraced sports betting, including wagers on the NBA. For those residing in or visiting betting-friendly states, the experience promises fairness and security, because state-regulated online betting platforms are in place, ensuring bettors receive fair odds and adhering to strict standards for a safe and transparent betting environment.

The boundary case is offshore books. Bettors should ensure they are using only legal online sportsbooks for any type of betting: offshore sportsbooks advertise widely on the internet, but they are illegal and offer no protection against fraud and other criminal activities.
