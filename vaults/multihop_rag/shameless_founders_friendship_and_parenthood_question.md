# The Parenthood Conversation Shameless's Founders Had To Have As Business Partners

Years ago, when discussing when — or even if — they each wanted to have kids, Michelle Andrews and Zara McDonald knew it was a conversation they needed to have not just as friends but as business partners, because Shameless depends on both of them appearing together. "I hate that we had to do this, but it was like, how do we have kids?" says McDonald. "How do we do this and have a family if the whole business is relying on us showing up together at the same time?"

The context is a friendship fully entwined with the business. On the Sunday Life photo shoot in Sydney, the two friends' support for each other was as obvious as it was genuine: they discussed outfit choices together, and every comment from either woman about not being able to make a particular item of clothing work was enthusiastically refuted by the other. Since launching Shameless their lives have been entwined, and since meeting they have been through break-ups, engagements and now weddings. Andrews tied the knot with her long-term partner Mitchell Rees last December, with McDonald as a bridesmaid; McDonald is now planning her own wedding, to partner Oliver Keogh, to take place next year.
