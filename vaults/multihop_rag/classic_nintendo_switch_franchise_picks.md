# Classic Nintendo Franchise Picks For The Switch

Polygon's advice for a first-time Switch owner is to start with the modern-day Zelda, Mario, and Animal Crossing games, on the grounds that Nintendo is home to some of the most iconic gaming franchises.

The selection is explicitly a narrowing: it is hard to pick just a handful from this wealth of options, to say nothing of other iconic Nintendo series, so Polygon says it has narrowed the field to some of the platform's absolute best.
