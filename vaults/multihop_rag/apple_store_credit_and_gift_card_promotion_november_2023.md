# Apple Store Credit And Gift Card Promotion, November 2023

Apple's November 2023 holiday promotion is not a discount at all: instead of cutting prices, Apple gives buyers of select items a gift card of Apple Store credit to spend later, running from the time of WIRED's 25 November 2023 guide through 27 November, with up to $200 in credit available.

The defining feature is the substitution of future store credit for an immediate price cut. WIRED's framing is that Apple notoriously does not run sales on its own storefront; what it usually does instead is throw in a gift card for Apple Store credit when you purchase select items. The amount of credit scales with the product bought: buying an iPhone 14 or iPhone SE earns up to $75 in store credit, while buying a MacBook Air earns up to $200, with the full list of qualifying items published by Apple.

A separate and distinct mechanism runs at another retailer: Amazon has a sale on Apple gift cards, where each card has its own unique corresponding coupon code. WIRED's example is a $100 Apple Gift Card bundled with a free $15 Amazon credit using the code APPLET5USAP, with many other cards to choose from. This second offer is a discount on the gift card itself rather than Apple's own purchase-triggered store credit, so the two should not be confused.
