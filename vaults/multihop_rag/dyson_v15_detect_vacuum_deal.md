# Dyson V15 Detect Vacuum Deal

Dyson's Black Friday 2023 deals include a $200 discount on the Dyson V15 Detect Absolute, bringing the cordless vacuum down to $550. Engadget says it's hard to tell if this is a record-low price, but considering the standard V15 Detect is going for between $650 and $750 across the internet, it considers this a good deal.

In addition to its strong cleaning power, the V15 Detect has a laser-powered optic cleaner head that illuminates the floor ahead as you clean so you can see dust and grime more clearly. It also has a piezo sensor, which sizes and counts dust particles as you clean and shows that information on its LCD display.
