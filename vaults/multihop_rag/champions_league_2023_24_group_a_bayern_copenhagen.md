# Champions League 2023/24 Group A: Bayern Munich And Copenhagen Qualify

Bayern Munich and Copenhagen qualified from Group A of the 2023/24 Champions League, with Galatasaray third and Manchester United bottom, per Sporting News on Dec. 12, 2023.

The final table read: Bayern Munich (Q) 6 games, 5 wins, 0 draws, 0 losses, +6 goal difference, 16 points; Copenhagen (Q) 6, 2, 2, 2, 0, 8; Galatasaray 6, 1, 2, 3, -3, 5; Manchester United 6, 1, 1, 4, -3, 4. Bayern Munich coasted into the knockout rounds, but Copenhagen, Galatasaray and Manchester United all entered the final matchweek with a chance of joining the Bundesliga giants. Galatasaray knew that if they beat Copenhagen at Parken they would go through, but Lukas Lerager scored the only goal in a 1-0 win for the hosts.

The results were: Sept. 20, Galatasaray 2-2 FC Copenhagen; Sept. 20, Bayern Munich 4-3 Manchester United; Oct. 3, Manchester United 2-3 Galatasaray; Oct. 3, FC Copenhagen 1-2 Bayern Munich; Oct. 24, Galatasaray 1-3 Bayern Munich; Oct. 24, Manchester United 1-0 FC Copenhagen; Nov. 8, Bayern Munich 2-1 Galatasaray; Nov. 8, FC Copenhagen 4-3 Manchester United; Nov. 29, Galatasaray 3-3 Manchester United; Nov. 29, Bayern Munich 0-0 FC Copenhagen; Dec. 12, FC Copenhagen 1-0 Galatasaray; Dec. 12, Manchester United 0-1 Bayern Munich.
