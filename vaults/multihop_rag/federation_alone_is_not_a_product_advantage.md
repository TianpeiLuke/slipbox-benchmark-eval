# Federation Alone Is Not A Product Advantage

The Verge conceded the strongest objection to platforms like Mastodon rather than resisting it: the most consistent argument against their long-term viability is that most people don't give a hoot about the underlying protocols and infrastructure of their apps and just want things to be easy, reliable, and useful — and that is "absolutely, unequivocally true."

The concession is turned against a bad argument on the fediverse's own side. Making the "It's federated!" argument is like making the "It's better for privacy!" argument: it makes you feel good, and at best it's a useful tiebreaker, but it doesn't actually matter. The point of the fediverse is not that it's federated. All that matters is the product.
