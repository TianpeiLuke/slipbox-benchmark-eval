# BG3 Warlock Class Features: Hit Points And Proficiencies

The Baldur's Gate 3 Warlock uses a d8 Hit Die per warlock level, starting with 8 + your Constitution modifier hit points at 1st level and gaining 5 + your Constitution modifier per warlock level after 1st. Its signature feature is the Warlock Spell Slot, used to cast Warlock spells and regained by taking a short rest.

The class's proficiencies are narrow. For weapons a Warlock is proficient with Simple Weapons only, and for armor with Light Armor only. Saving throw proficiencies are Wisdom and Charisma. At creation a Warlock chooses two skills from Arcana, Deception, History, Intimidation, Investigation, Nature, and Religion — a list that skews toward the academic and the socially manipulative rather than the physical.
