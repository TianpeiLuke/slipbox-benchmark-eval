# Mastodon's Undercounted Users And Twitter's Traffic

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-13, in reporting by Sarah, that Mastodon actually has more than 407,000 more monthly users than it thought, and that Twitter appears to have a lot more traffic than Musk said.

Both figures are revisions to previously believed numbers rather than growth events: Mastodon's own count was too low, and Twitter's traffic is higher than the figure Musk gave. The report attaches a qualification to the Twitter side — the peak now is about the same as it was a decade ago, and it is unclear what the social platform can do to encourage more growth. So the higher-than-claimed traffic figure is not read as evidence of expansion.
