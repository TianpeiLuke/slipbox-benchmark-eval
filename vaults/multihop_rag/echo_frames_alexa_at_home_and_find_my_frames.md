# Echo Frames: Alexa At Home And Find My Frames

Testing Alexa commands on the Echo Frames at home, The Verge's reviewer was pleasantly surprised that issuing them didn't trip up any of their Echo speakers.

The frames also support a feature that lets you ask any Echo speaker you own to locate them. The reviewer called that nice but doubted most people will use it frequently: as a lifelong glasses wearer, theirs are always either in a case or on the nightstand, and they have a specific drawer for sunglasses. The feature also carries a precondition — it relies on the glasses having a decent charge.
