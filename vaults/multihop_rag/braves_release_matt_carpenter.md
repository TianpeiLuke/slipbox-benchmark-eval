# Braves Cut Matt Carpenter After Trying To Trade Him

Atlanta cut Matt Carpenter on Monday, Dec. 18, 2023, having reportedly hoped to trade him after acquiring him from the Padres earlier that offseason, per FanSided's Robert Murray.

In the original deal the Braves acquired Carpenter and took on $4 million while also trading for Ray Kerr, but the team was looking to deal his contract away to someone else, according to The Athletic's Ken Rosenthal.
