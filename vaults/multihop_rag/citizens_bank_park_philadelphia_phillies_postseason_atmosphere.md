# Citizens Bank Park: Philadelphia Phillies Postseason Atmosphere

Citizens Bank Park ranked No. 1 among the eight remaining 2023 playoff teams' home-field environments in The New York Times' 8 October 2023 ranking. It opened in 2004, has a capacity of 42,792 and drew an average attendance of 38,157 in 2023.

Two of the three no-hitters in postseason history have taken place there. Those in Philly would like to forget the Astros' combined no-hitter in Game 4 of the previous season's World Series, so the ranking concentrates instead on Roy Halladay's 2010 no-hitter in Game 1 of the NLDS against the Reds: in his first postseason start, Halladay allowed only one baserunner, when Jay Bruce walked with two outs in the fifth inning.

The reasoning is about the fans. Philadelphia fans have thrown snowballs at Santa Claus and batteries at J.D. Drew; their Phanatic mascot is iconic, but part of his charm is the way he taunts and antagonises opponents, making it a real love-it-or-hate-it place to play. The Phillies and their fans are capable of producing unforgettable moments, like the Wednesday night when Bryson Stott hit a grand slam and the ballpark provided the only commentary necessary. "I yelled at the dugout and couldn't really hear myself," Stott said, "so I knew the crowd was loud. Any time we get to play here, you know it's going to be loud from the very first pitch. I wouldn't want to play anywhere else. It's a phenomenal time every time we take the field here in the postseason." Bryce Harper added: "I've always said it, we've got the best fans in baseball. It's an amazing place to play."
