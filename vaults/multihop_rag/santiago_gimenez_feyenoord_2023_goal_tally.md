# Santiago Gimenez's 39-Goal 2023 At Feyenoord

Santiago Gimenez scored 39 goals in the 2023 calendar year for Feyenoord and Mexico, placing him sixth on TalkSport's list of the year's top scorers. One of the most exciting strikers in world football, the 22-year-old has taken European football by storm.

Gimenez joined Dutch side Feyenoord in the summer of 2022 and fired Arne Slot's men to the Eredivisie championship with 15 goals. With 20 goals in the current season along with two strikes for Mexico, it is clear to see why Arsenal, Tottenham and Chelsea have all been linked with a move; he seems destined for a big move in the near future.
