# Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade

The Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade is the annual parade held in New York City on Thanksgiving Day, and is the world's largest Thanksgiving parade.

Its defining features are giant balloons of cartoon characters that float above the city sky, along with marching bands and dancers. Photographs of the event show the Tom Turkey float in place to start the parade, the Ronald McDonald, Charlie Brown, Olaf and Sinclair DINO balloons being inflated or flying as the parade passes down 6th Avenue, marching bands, flag wavers, clowns on roller blades and other performers, with an NYPD officer standing by.
