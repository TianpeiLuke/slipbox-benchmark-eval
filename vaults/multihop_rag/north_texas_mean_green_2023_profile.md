# North Texas Mean Green 2023 Profile

North Texas' 2023 team was carried by junior quarterback Chandler Jones and undermined by the worst defense in the country. Until two of his passes were picked off in a 38-20 defeat to UTSA the previous Saturday, Jones had thrown 262 passes through seven games with 19 touchdowns and no interceptions. He started his career at Louisiana-Monroe, snatched the job from Stone Earle early in the season and had not disappointed first-year coach Eric Morris.

The defense was another story. Since allowing 681 total yards to Cal in the opener, its past three opponents had rung up 1,460 total yards and 15 touchdowns, and its yield of 479 yards per game was the worst in the country — a line Sporting News, writing on Nov. 7, 2023, imagined SMU super sophomore quarterback Preston Stone licking his chops over.
