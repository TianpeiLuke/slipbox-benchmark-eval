# Russell Wilson Week 13: Start Him Because Volume Should Finally Meet His Efficiency

CBS Sports' Week 13 verdict on Russell Wilson is Start Him, on the argument that his elite efficiency has been held back only by attempt volume, and that the Texans matchup should supply the volume. The efficiency grounds: there is no denying he is now among the most efficient quarterbacks in the league — top five among qualifying quarterbacks in completion rate at 68.3%, first in touchdown rate at 6.3%, fourth in off-target rate at 7.8%, and he has clearly worked out his issues with his offensive line because he has landed the fourth-longest time-to-throw average at 3.14 seconds. He has done this without firing downfield a ton, ranking 32nd in passing ADOT at 6.7 yards. The stated reason it hasn't translated for Fantasy is that Wilson is averaging 29 attempts per game, and even lower over his past six games at 26.

The warrant for the change is matchup-driven: expect Wilson to throw more in what could become a shootout against the Texans, because quarterbacks against Houston are averaging 34.8 pass attempts per game and the Texans pass rush shouldn't be a problem. The conclusion is to count on Wilson for a 20-plus point game and thus start him over Kyler Murray, Baker Mayfield, Sam Howell and Jordan Love. Scope: Wilson is a Denver quarterback, #3, age 35 with 12 years of experience; the weekly breakdown lists the matchup at Houston with Houston favored by 3.5 and an over/under of 47, the opponent 10th versus quarterbacks, projected points of 17.8 and a QB rank of 10th; year-to-date he has 2,199 passing yards, 266 rushing yards, 21 touchdowns, 4 interceptions and 20.4 Fantasy points per game, with projections powered by SportsLine.
