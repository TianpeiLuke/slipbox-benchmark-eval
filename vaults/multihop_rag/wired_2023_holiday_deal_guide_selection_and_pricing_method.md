# WIRED's 2023 Holiday Deal Guide Selection And Pricing Method

WIRED assembles its 2023 Black Friday and Cyber Monday deal guides by testing products year-round, hand-picking the deals from devices it has already tested, and judging each discount against the actual street prices those products carried at retailers over the past few months rather than against list price. The same method statement appears in both the Walmart Cyber Monday roundup published 27 November 2023 and the Apple Black Friday roundup published 25 November 2023.

The steps are: test products throughout the year; handpick deals from that tested pool; compute the advertised discount from recent real street prices at retailers; cross out any product that is sold out or no longer discounted as of publishing; and update the guide periodically as the sale runs. The Apple guide shows the update cycle in practice — it was published on 26 November with prices updated throughout for Cyber Monday sales and with the Otterbox sale, the HyperDrive 6-in-1 Dongle and the 16-inch MacBook Pro added, then updated again on 27 November with a new deal on the 14-inch MacBook Pro (M3 Pro, 2023) and with pricing and live deals refreshed as Cyber Monday came to an end.

A stated precondition on the whole exercise is commercial: WIRED discloses that if you buy something using links in its stories, it may earn a commission, which it says helps support its journalism. Because the crossing-out and price refreshes happen only as of publishing and at each periodic update, the prices in a guide are not guaranteed to hold at any later moment a reader arrives.
