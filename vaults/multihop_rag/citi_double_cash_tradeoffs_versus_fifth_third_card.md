# Tradeoffs Of The Higher-Rate Cash Back Alternative Versus The Fifth Third Card

Against the case for switching to the higher-earning flat-rate alternative, the review argues that the extra earnings may not cover what you give up: that card doesn't earn a welcome bonus, and its benefits are comparatively slim — for example, it doesn't waive foreign transaction fees and it doesn't offer cell phone insurance.

The conclusion is conditional on the arithmetic working out for the individual cardholder: if the extra 0.33% in earnings doesn't offset these shortcomings, the Fifth Third 1.67% Card is likely the better option.
