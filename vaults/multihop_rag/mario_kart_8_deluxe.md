# Mario Kart 8 Deluxe

Polygon says no multiplayer Nintendo Switch list would be complete without mentioning the most recent edition of the 20-year Mario Kart series; it appears in the outlet's 2023-10-20 list of the 35 best couch co-op games for Nintendo Switch.

Though the game came out in 2017, it still reigns supreme as far as kart racing goes.

It also still gets new courses, such as the recent addition of Mario Kart 7's Rainbow Road.
