# ProtonVPN Black Friday Deal

Engadget's favorite VPN service, ProtonVPN, held a rare sale for Black Friday 2023 that brings a monthly subscription down to only $4 for a total of 30 months — $120 for two and a half years of access.

ProtonVPN passed Engadget's tests with high marks, but what made it stand out among other VPN security services was its independently audited no-logs policy and the fact that the company has proven it doesn't comply with law enforcement requests to reveal data. For those wanting to jump in head-first with Proton services, the company discounted Proton Unlimited — which includes access to VPN, Mail, Calendar, Drive and Pass — to just under $9 per month for the first year.
