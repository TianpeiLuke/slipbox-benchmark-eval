# Ole Miss' Stalled Drives And Jaxson Dart's Injury

Jaxson Dart went down at 9:37 p.m. against Georgia on Nov. 11, 2023 after being hit by Zion Logue and Daylen Everette, entering the injury tent and being replaced by Spencer Sanders with the score Ole Miss 14, Georgia 38.

The night had been rough for the Rebels' offense throughout. On the very first play of the game, at 7:14 p.m., Ole Miss's offensive line was called for a false start. At 7:19 p.m. Dart completed a 33-yard pass to Caden Prieskorn to reach Georgia's red zone at the five-yard line, but at 7:40 p.m., going for it on fourth-and-three, Dart threw the ball away and Ole Miss turned it over on downs at the UGA 40. At 9:06 p.m. CJ Allen sacked Dart, and at 9:07 p.m. punter Fraser Masin faked and ran it himself for 24 yards to the 50, only for a personal foul to push the Rebels back to their own 35 for first-and-25.
