# Anker Soundcore Liberty 4 NC Earbuds Deal Prices

The Anker Soundcore Liberty 4 NC was $80 for October Prime Day 2023 and fell to $70 for Black Friday, and is the runner-up in Engadget's budget wireless earbuds guide behind the Space A40.

Engadget's 10 October 2023 headphone list quoted it as "$80 $100 Save $20", saying the Liberty 4 NC carries most of the same benefits as the Space A40 but uses a "stem" design reminiscent of Apple's AirPods, puts a little more emphasis on the bass by default — changeable with EQ tools in Anker's app — and supports wear detection; $80 was a $20 discount matching its lowest price to date.

By 24 November 2023 Engadget reported it down to $70 at Amazon, B&H and Best Buy, beating its previous low by $10 and falling $30 below Anker's list price. That piece repeats that it offers a highly similar feature set to the Space A40 in a larger stem design, with more bass-forward sound by default "which some may find fatiguing", slightly more extensive touch controls, and technical support for wear detection: "If you prefer this kind of bulkier but easier-to-remove shape, this is an excellent value."
