# Trump's Detroit Speech Attacks On Electric Vehicles

Appearing before a crowd in suburban Detroit the night before The Verge's September 28, 2023 piece, former President and current Republican frontrunner Donald Trump tried out his new attack lines against electric vehicles.

EVs are too expensive, he argued, according to The Detroit News; they don't have enough range and will spur American job losses. EV batteries were another target: "They get rid of them," Trump said, according to Discourse Blog, "and lots of bad things happen, and when they're digging it out of the ground to make those batteries, it's going to be very bad for the environment." Those trying to protect the environment by promoting EVs, he said, are "environmental lunatics."
