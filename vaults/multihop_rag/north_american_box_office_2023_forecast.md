# The North American Box Office Forecast For 2023

Paul Dergarabedian believes the two concert films — Taylor Swift's and Beyoncé's — should help lift the North American box office to more than $9 billion in 2023.

The proposition is anchored by two comparison points that would show whether it holds: the $7.4 billion taken in 2022, which the forecast would have to exceed, and the $11.4 billion of 2019, which it would edge closer to without reaching.
