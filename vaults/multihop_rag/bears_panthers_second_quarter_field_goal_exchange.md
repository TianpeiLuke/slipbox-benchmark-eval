# Bears-Panthers Second-Quarter Field Goal Exchange

The second quarter of the Nov. 9, 2023 Bears-Panthers game produced three field goals and no touchdowns, ending with the Panthers ahead 10-9 at halftime.

Carolina struck first: at 8:55 p.m. Young came under pressure on third-and-11 and threw it into the ground, and former Bears kicker Eddy Pineiro drilled the field goal to make it Panthers 10, Bears 3. Chicago answered on a drive that began at 9:03 p.m. when Foreman pushed forward for a key first down and rookie Tyler Scott took off for 16 yards to take the Bears over midfield, survived a holding call that set them back to second-and-20 at 9:05 p.m., and ended in a punt at 9:07 p.m. After Justin Jones' sack forced a Carolina three-and-out at 9:11 p.m., the Bears started with very strong field position; Bagent hit Roschon Johnson for a first down at 9:14 p.m., Tyler Scott caught a 15-yard pass at 9:17 p.m. to put Chicago in field goal range, and at 9:19 p.m. Bagent held onto a bad snap but threw incomplete, bringing out Cairo Santos for a field goal that made it 10-6, Panthers.

The half closed with a second Bears field goal. At 9:25 p.m. the Panthers faced third-and-3 out of the two-minute warning, hoping for points before halftime and due to start the second half with the ball; at 9:28 p.m. an illegal contact penalty, the second of the night on the Bears, gave Carolina a fresh set of downs; and at 9:29 p.m. Tyrique Stevenson came very close to an interception on an errant Young pass, forcing a punt with just over a minute left. A 15-yard unnecessary roughness penalty on Deion Jones helped the Bears inch closer at 9:34 p.m., a perfectly executed play got the ball to Cole Kmet for a first down and out of bounds at 9:38 p.m., and at 9:39 p.m. Bagent narrowly avoided a sack and ran for positive yardage to move Santos' attempt closer with three seconds left. Santos converted at 9:40 p.m., and Sporting News gave the Bears credit for working into field goal range in a game that might be lucky to see an offensive touchdown. The game was carried on Prime Video.
