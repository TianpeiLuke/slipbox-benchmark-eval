# Notre Dame's First Quarter Against USC, Oct. 14 2023

Notre Dame led USC 7-3 after the first quarter of their Oct. 14, 2023 game, scoring first on a four-yard Sam Hartman touchdown pass to Gi'Bran Payne set up by Xavier Watts' interception, with USC answering through a Denis Lynch field goal.

Coverage picked up at 7:29 p.m. with the stadium dark as fans welcomed the Fighting Irish to the field. USC started with the ball at its own 25; MarShawn Lloyd ran up the middle for a yard on the first offensive play, and Caleb Williams scrambled for no gain on the next. At 7:37 p.m. Williams found Brendan Rice for an 11-yard gain and a first down, then over-shot Lake McRee and was picked off by Watts, who returned the ball all the way to the USC 12. Two Audric Estime runs picked up eight yards to set up third-and-2, and at 7:39 p.m. Hartman dropped back and threw to a leaping Payne, who back-pedalled into the end zone for the four-yard touchdown: Notre Dame 7, USC 0.

USC's response drive began with Zachariah Branch returning the kickoff to the USC 19. Austin Jones gained five on first down, and a defensive holding call on Notre Dame's Benjamin Morris on a second-down pass gave USC a first down at the 34. A Williams rush for four was followed by a snap infraction creating second-and-11, then a three-yard Jones run; Williams connected with Tahj Washington but the receiver was stopped three yards short of the marker, bringing out the punting unit, and Notre Dame fair caught at its own 14. Estime opened Notre Dame's next drive with an 11-yard run past the marker and caught a pass for four to set up second-and-6, but at 7:53 p.m. the Fighting Irish punted after Estime gained only a yard and Hartman's pass to Rico Flores Jr. fell incomplete.

USC began its next drive at its own 33 with 6:38 left in the quarter. Williams was flushed from the pocket and scrambled 11 yards, with a Notre Dame defensive holding penalty adding to the gain and moving USC to the Notre Dame 46. Williams then hit Dorian Singer up the middle to the Notre Dame 35 after a one-yard Jones run, and Jones added three on the ground before Williams dumped a pass to Branch for seven yards and another first down. Singer made a 13-yard catch but was flagged for offensive pass interference for pushing off Morrison, backing USC to the Notre Dame 39 for first-and-25; Michael Jackson then caught a pass for 12 yards and a personal foul on Morrison moved USC to the Notre Dame 13 with an automatic first down. Three short plays stalled the offense at the Notre Dame 8, and on fourth-and-5 Lynch drilled a 25-yard field goal at 8:04 p.m.: Notre Dame 7, USC 3. Devyn Ford returned the ensuing kickoff 28 yards so Notre Dame began at its own 36, and the quarter closed with Jeremiyah Love losing a yard before Hartman completed a seven-yard pass to Jordan Faison, leaving the Irish to open the second quarter on third-and-4.
