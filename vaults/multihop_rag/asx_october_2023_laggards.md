# ASX Laggards On 10 October 2023

Healthcare companies (down 0.1 per cent) fell despite CSL (down 0.4 per cent) delivering broadly in line with expectations, with Pro Medicus (down 0.2 per cent) and Ramsay Health Care (down 0.5 per cent) also falling.

Bendigo and Adelaide Bank (down 1.3 per cent) and Westpac (down 0.2 per cent) were among the biggest large-cap decliners, while Bank of Queensland shed 6.9 per cent after missing consensus expectations. Lithium miner Liontown (down 3.3 per cent), TPG Telecom (down 1.3 per cent) and gold miner Northern Star (down 1 per cent) were also among the biggest large-cap decliners.
