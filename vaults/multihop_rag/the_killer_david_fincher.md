# The Killer (David Fincher)

The Killer is David Fincher's Netflix film starring Michael Fassbender, Tilda Swinton, and Sophie Charlotte, which Polygon says is, like the assassin at its center, difficult to pin down. It ranked 17 on the outlet's best movies of 2023 list of 2023-10-07.

The difficulty is a set of tensions: the film is astonishingly gorgeous and well made but at the same time stubbornly unflashy; bleak and violent but at the same time hilarious and irreverent; fascinated with exploring how media shapes identity but completely resistant to assigning any actual meaning. It is the kind of movie that can throw in a five-minute fight scene better than almost any fight in a mainstream movie that year, then never go back to hand-to-hand combat again — in other words, a David Fincher movie through and through.

Polygon's Austen Goslin ties this to the director's method: Fincher is famous for his precision on set and his dedication to finely honing every aspect of his films until they are exactly what he needs, and yet his movies never feel stodgy, tight, or lifeless. The Killer breezes by every assassin movie and thriller trope, executing each one flawlessly while making a snide comment about it, feeling so effortless and smooth that it makes you wonder why all movies cannot be this good — Fincher's gift being films whose quality and handmade perfection are so precise they can be mistaken for the work of factory machines, until you look a little closer.
