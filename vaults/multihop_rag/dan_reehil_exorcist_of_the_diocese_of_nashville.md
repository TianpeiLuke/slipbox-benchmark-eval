# Fr. Dan Reehil, Exorcist of the Diocese of Nashville

Fr. Dan Reehil is a priest of the Diocese of Nashville who, in 2018, received exorcism training at Ateneo Pontificio Regina Apostolorum in Rome and was installed as the exorcist of the Diocese of Nashville that same year.

Speaking to Fox News Digital in October 2023, he warned that Ouija boards and other attempts to converse with the dead are spiritually dangerous practices that should not be viewed as innocent fun.

The premise behind that warning is a claim about what exists. "We too often forget that there is much more to this world than meets the eye," Reehil told Fox News Digital. "Angels, demons, spirits and souls do exist and they can have an impact on our life."
