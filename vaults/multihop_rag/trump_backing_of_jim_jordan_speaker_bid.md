# Trump's Backing Of Jim Jordan's Speaker Bid

To win over GOP colleagues, Jim Jordan relied on backing from Donald Trump — the party's front-runner in the 2024 election to challenge President Joe Biden — and on groups pressuring rank-and-file lawmakers for the vote. It was not enough, and in fact backfired on some. "Jim Jordan will be a great speaker," Trump had said on Tuesday, 2023-10-17, outside a courthouse in Manhattan where he is facing business fraud charges. "I think he's going to have the votes soon, if not today, over the next day or two."

The alliance behind that endorsement is long-standing. Jordan has been a top Trump ally, particularly during the Jan. 6 Capitol attack by the former president's backers who were trying to overturn the 2020 election he lost to Biden. Days later, Trump awarded Jordan a Medal of Freedom.
