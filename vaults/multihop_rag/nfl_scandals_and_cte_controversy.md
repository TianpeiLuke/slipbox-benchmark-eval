# The NFL's Own Scandals, From Kaepernick To The CTE Controversy

The NFL has had its fair share of, as Essentially Sports put it on 2023-10-25, "bumps" down the road. The list given runs from Colin Kaepernick taking a knee during the national anthem to protest against racism, to domestic violence by players, to the Deflategate and Bountygate scandals.

There have been bigger crises as well: the Union of Concerned Scientists accused the NFL of trying to intimidate scientists studying the link between CTE and pro football. The article's closing judgment is nonetheless forward-looking — the NFL is already way past other leagues in terms of revenue, and now with the pivotal shift in its strategy, "America's favorite pastime" is on its way to world domination.
