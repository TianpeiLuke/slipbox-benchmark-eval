# Pregame Notes From Bills At Bengals

Before the highly anticipated Sunday Night Football matchup on Nov. 5, 2023 at Paycor Stadium, Damar Hamlin's return to Cincinnati was the emotional thread: he was on the field for a workout in the hours before kickoff and, as expected, was officially listed as inactive for the game, with Lindsey Thiry reporting he was not expected to be active but was taking in his return to the stadium.

Other pregame news concerned Buffalo's secondary and Cincinnati's ceremony. The Athletic's Joe Buscaglia reported that Rasul Douglas would play but that Dane Jackson was getting more of the first-team snaps in warmups. Before the game, former punter Kevin Huber and former long-snapper Clark Harris were recognized on the jumbotron as Cincinnati legends — Huber having played the most games as a Bengal at 216 and Harris ranking fourth at 202 — and the two served as the rulers of the jungle for the night.

The mood and the picks rounded out the buildup. Joe Burrow arrived at the locker room wearing diamond headphones, Bengals fans striped the stadium, and on the FNIA panel Jason Garrett was the lone analyst to pick the Bills, keeping it from being a clean sweep for Cincinnati.
