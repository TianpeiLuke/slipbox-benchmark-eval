# Patrick Kane's Fit With The Buffalo Sabres

The Sabres have a leg up on everyone in the Kane sweepstakes because the winger is a Buffalo native who grew up in the southern part of the city. He had been connected to Buffalo since the previous season, when rumors of an inevitable breakup between Kane and the Blackhawks began to surface, and this time the Sabres had plenty of cap space to sign him.

The on-ice argument is about a specific weakness. Bringing in Kane would greatly help Buffalo's struggling offense and power play: entering the final week of November 2023 the Sabres averaged 2.81 goals per game, the eighth-lowest mark in the league, and their power play was clicking at 16.4 percent, 23rd in the NHL. Kane has collected about 32 percent of his career points on the man advantage, where he can be an excellent set-up man or a one-time option on the dot.

The counter-consideration is the team's own trajectory. Buffalo had not been as good as expected to start 2023-24 after being viewed as a team that would take the final step out of its rebuild given how close it came to the postseason the previous year; sitting under .500 and sixth in the Atlantic, the Sabres were no longer atop the list of Stanley Cup contenders if that is what Kane has his eyes set on.
