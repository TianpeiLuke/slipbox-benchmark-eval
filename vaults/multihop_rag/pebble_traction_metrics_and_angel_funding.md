# Pebble's Traction Metrics And Angel Funding

Despite its challenges, Pebble initially had decent retention: by week four it was seeing retention rates of 30%, and its invite list was working, as 60% of people who received a Pebble invite converted to become users.

Some 10,000 users arrived at that list from early press, like TechCrunch's coverage of its first outside funding — a $1.1 million angel round that included investors like former Google VP Bradley Horowitz, Android co-founder Rich Miner and the former CEO of Wikipedia, Katherine Maher. Data from Similarweb indicates the Pebble website topped 99,300 visits last month, indicating repeat usage.
