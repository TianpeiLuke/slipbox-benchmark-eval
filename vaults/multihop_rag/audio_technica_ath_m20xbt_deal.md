# Audio-Technica ATH-M20xBT Deal

The Audio-Technica ATH-M20xBT was a modest $10 off for Black Friday 2023, bringing the wireless headphones down to $69. The pair fell to $55 during Amazon's Prime Day sale in July, but this is about as low as it has been outside of that.

The M20 is the budget pick in Engadget's wireless headphones guide thanks to its comfortable fit, 60-hour battery life, multi-device connectivity and relatively detailed audio quality. It lacks ANC and doesn't have the sturdiest design, but it should do the job if you just need a no-frills wireless headphone for cheap.
