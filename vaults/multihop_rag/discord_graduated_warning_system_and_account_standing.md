# Discord's Graduated Warning System And Account Standing

Discord is introducing a new warning system for people who violate its policies: outside of incidents involving the most "extreme" violations, the company is moving away from permanent bans, doing so under the belief that most users will choose to be better online citizens given the chance and proper guidance.

In practice the system notifies a person when they have violated one or more of Discord's policies and details the restrictions on their account. It also links out to the company's Terms of Service, Community Guidelines and appeal mechanism. A new "Account Standing" tab within the settings menu lets users see all their current and past policy violations.

Discord frames the design as a model for the industry. "We think we've built the most nuanced and proportionate reporting system," Badalich told Engadget, adding that the company hopes other platforms will look to what Discord has created for inspiration in their own enforcement efforts. "We believe people, especially teens, have the capacity to grow."
