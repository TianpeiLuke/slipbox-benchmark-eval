# Britney Spears And Charlie Ebersol

Britney Spears and Charlie Ebersol — known as the co-founder of Tempus Ex Machina and The Company — started dating in 2014 and broke up officially in June 2015, after eight months together. Their relationship was fairly low profile.

Spears had previously explained what drew her to Ebersol, telling People at the time: "There was something about him that made me feel really peaceful and at ease. And he was really hot, too. Adorably hot."
