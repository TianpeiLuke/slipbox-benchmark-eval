# Kalmus's Critique of Biden Climate Policy

Kalmus attacks the premise that President Joe Biden's record makes him a climate champion, arguing that the Inflation Reduction Act, Biden's signature climate bill, does too little to shut out the fossil fuel industry, and that rejoining the Paris accord is no achievement because "That Paris Agreement will take us to about 3 degrees Celsius of global heating. I don't think we'll have a civilization at 3 degrees Celsius."

His frustration extends beyond the administration: not his suburban neighbours in North Carolina, who continue to drive emissions-belching SUVs to big-box stores, and perhaps not even some in the science community, like those who kicked him out of the American Geophysical Union's annual fall meeting last year after he unfurled a banner and protested on the major science conference's biggest stage. The article supplies two checks on his claim. Rhodium Group, a nonpartisan think tank, estimates greenhouse gas emissions will drop 32%-42% below 2005 levels by 2030, well short of Biden's own benchmark for progress, with the inflation bill responsible for about a quarter of that projected decrease. And a 2022 United Nations report estimated global temperatures would rise between 2.1 and 2.9 degrees Celsius by 2100 if countries held to their climate commitments, while many countries remain off that pace.
