# Messiah of Evil (1973 Cosmic Horror)

Messiah of Evil, by Willard Huyck and Gloria Katz, is an undersung classic of '70s cosmic horror and a surreal, nightmarish experience that Polygon says more than deserves its status as a cult horror classic. It was the Oct. 1 pick in Polygon's 2023-10-10 Halloween Countdown, available on Prime Video, Shudder, Pluto TV, and Plex.

The film centers on Arletty, a young woman who journeys to the remote coastal town of Point Dume, California, to track down her missing father, a reclusive painter plagued by disturbing visions of an impending apocalypse. After crossing paths with an eccentric aristocrat and his groupie companions, the group bears witness to the realization of the painter's prophecy as the townspeople are transformed into flesh-eating ghouls and the messiah of evil returns to the mortal realm.

Polygon's Toussaint Egan calls it at times convoluted and bizarre but genuinely entertaining, filled with memorable scares and chilling set-pieces — from a cross-eyed albino man gleefully devouring a field mouse whole to an unsuspecting woman watching a Western as the theater around her slowly fills with ravenous undead zombies. He places it somewhere between the ineffable gothic horror of H.P. Lovecraft and the anti-consumerist allegories of George A. Romero, notes the horrors hold up over 50 years after release, and recommends it as an excellent start to a month of horrors thanks to its combination of inadvertent silliness and legitimate terror.
