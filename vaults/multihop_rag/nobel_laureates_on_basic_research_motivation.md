# The 2023 Laureates On Basic Research As The Motivation

Moungi Bawendi argued that curiosity, not application, is what drives the work: he said he was not thinking about the possible applications of his research when he started on quantum dots. "The motivation really is the basic science. A basic understanding, the curiosity of how does the world work? And that's what drives scientists and academic scientists to do what they do," he said.

Louis Brus made a compatible but qualified case. He was glad to see recognition for the area of chemistry he practices, and said the practical applications of quantum dots, like creating the colors in flatscreen TVs, were something he had hoped for when he started the work decades ago. His warrant for treating unpredictability as intrinsic: "Basic research is extremely hard to predict exactly how it's going to work out. It's more for the knowledge base than it is for the actual materials. But in this case, it's both."
