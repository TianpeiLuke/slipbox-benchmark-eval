# Dylan Carlson Cardinals Trade Candidacy

Dylan Carlson was ranked No. 17 on Yardbarker's Nov. 13, 2023 list of the top 25 MLB offseason trade candidates: it was a bit of a surprise that he had not changed hands over the summer, when various reports suggested the Cards were making him available as they looked for ways to add a controllable starter, and the same logic applied that winter.

Carlson had been surpassed by Lars Nootbaar as the starting center fielder. St. Louis needed multiple starting pitchers and would likely bring in at least one via trade. Carlson probably had less value than he did before the deadline: not only did he finish with a mediocre .219/.318/.333 batting line, he underwent season-ending surgery on his left ankle in September. There would nevertheless be teams willing to take a shot on a former top prospect who had looked like an average or better center fielder 12 months earlier. Carlson was projected for a modest $1.8M salary and had three years of arbitration control.
