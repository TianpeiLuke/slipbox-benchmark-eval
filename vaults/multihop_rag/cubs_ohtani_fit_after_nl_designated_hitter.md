# The Cubs' Ohtani Fit After The NL Designated Hitter

Jesse Rogers of ESPN reported on Nov. 16, 2023 that "Timing could be right for a Cubs-Ohtani union," because the obstacle that blocked the fit the first time around has since been removed.

The Cubs were one of the seven finalists for Ohtani in his first free agency, but the lack of the designated hitter in the National League at the time made it hard for him to choose Chicago: his primary goal was showing he could both hit and pitch at a high level for an MLB team, and trying to play the outfield instead of just DHing would have been too much even for Ohtani.

Now the NL has the DH, and the Cubs are itching to make a sport-shaking signing — another one, having already poached Craig Counsell from the rival Brewers to be their new manager. Sporting News noted it would be shocking if they did not make a corresponding player-acquisition move, with Ohtani at the top of the list.
