# Favorites And Underdogs In Moneyline Odds

The favorite is the participant — team or individual — expected to win the event or matchup, and the underdog is the participant deemed less likely to win compared to the opponent. The distinction is a judgment about relative chances, and it is what the sign on a moneyline price encodes.

In moneyline odds, especially in the American odds format, the favorite is usually represented with a minus (-) sign: if Team A is a favorite listed at -150, a bettor would need to wager $150 to win $100. Underdogs, again particularly in the American format, are usually represented with a plus (+) sign: if Team B is an underdog at +130, a bettor would win $130 for every $100 staked, should Team B win.
