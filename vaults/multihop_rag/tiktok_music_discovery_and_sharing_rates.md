# TikTok Users' Music Discovery And Sharing Rates

The Luminate study found that the TikTok audience can be an effective marketing tool for artists, because TikTok users in particular are considerably more likely to discover and share music than overall social media and short video platform users.

In the UK, TikTok users were 108% more likely to discover and share music than other social media users; in Germany they were 100% more likely; and US TikTok users were 71% more likely. "In doing so, TikTok users help both emerging and established artists to reach new audiences," the Luminate report stated.
