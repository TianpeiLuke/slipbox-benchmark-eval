# Travis Kelce's "Vigilante S***" Friendship Bracelet

Fans spotted Travis Kelce wearing a beaded friendship bracelet reading "Vigilante S***" — a reference to Taylor Swift's song from her 2022 album Midnights — in a photo taken at the Kelce Car Jam charity event on Friday 22 September 2023, days before Swift attended his Kansas City Chiefs game. A fan shared the photo in a TikTok video posted on 25 September, after Swift had been seen enthusiastically cheering him on from the box seats at Arrowhead Stadium, fuelling speculation that she and the athlete were dating.

The now-viral photo suggested Kelce may have hinted at the dating rumours before Swift attended his game. Besides the Swiftie-themed jewellery, Kelce wore a white striped shirt with silver chain necklaces, black sunglasses and black pants to the event. In the comments, fans both praised and questioned him for the bracelet, and many pointed out how fans of the "Bad Blood" singer famously trade and wear friendship bracelets at her Eras Tour concerts. "OF ALL 230 SONGS IN THE DISCOGRAPHY I'M SICK," one fan commented; another wrote, "It's unbelievable how invested I am in this"; a third wrote, "I'm gonna need to go back and see all photos of him from the last three months and dissect if he's wearing friendship bracelets."

The provenance of the bracelet is uncertain. In other photos from the same charity event, shared to Instagram by Jordan's KC photography, Kelce was not wearing the friendship bracelet, leading many people to believe he had received it from a fan that day.
