# Broncos 21, Vikings 20 On Sunday Night Football

Denver completed a major comeback in the Mile High City on Sunday Night Football on Nov. 19, 2023, beating Minnesota 21-20 by quarters of Vikings 0-10-7-3 and Broncos 3-6-0-12. Both teams entered the game on red-hot win streaks, but only one could keep the momentum rolling, and for the majority of the contest it looked like it would be Joshua Dobbs and the Vikings.

Trailing by eight going into the final frame, Denver's offense finally woke up in the game's biggest moment. The Broncos had not found the end zone all night, but Russell Wilson still led an impressive game-winning drive in the final three minutes. Courtland Sutton was as clutch as can be, coming up with a key catch on fourth down to keep the drive alive, and when Denver reached the red zone — where it had stalled several times during the night — Wilson went right back to Sutton, who hauled in a monster touchdown reception to give the Broncos a one-point lead with a minute remaining.

Dobbs had run out of magic. Denver forced a turnover on downs on Minnesota's final drive, extending its win streak to four games while handing the Vikings their first loss in five weeks.
