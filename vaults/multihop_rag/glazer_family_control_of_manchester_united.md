# Glazer Family Control Of Manchester United

The Glazer family are the American owners of Manchester United, six siblings who hold overall control of the club and are hugely unpopular with supporters; their regime began with a now-outlawed leveraged buyout in 2005 that saddled the club with debts which at one stage soared as high as £1bn, as described by talkSPORT in October 2023.

The defining features of Glazer control are its structure and its persistence. Brothers Joel and Avram are the two most hands-on of the six siblings, and it was they who instructed the New York-based Raine banking group to handle the bidding process after the family announced it was open to offers. Their grip also runs through the boardroom: half of United's 12-strong board are members of the Glazer family, and in the event of a split vote Joel and Avram, as co-chairmen, have the deciding say — which is why any proposal put to a board vote could be expected to be ratified. The announcement that the family would sell was seen by the majority of United fans as the beginning of the end of a regime they regarded as divisive and destructive.

The boundary case is partial sale rather than exit. Because the Glazers will remain in situ under Sir Jim Ratcliffe's minority arrangement, the outcome has not gone down well with supporters — talkSPORT reported on 16 October 2023 that the news had not been too warmly welcomed by United supporters, who would still have to put up with the family in control of the club — though it is seen as a first step towards Ratcliffe eventually removing the Americans and taking overall control.
