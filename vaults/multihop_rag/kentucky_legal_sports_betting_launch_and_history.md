# Kentucky's Legal Sports Betting Launch And History

Kentucky sports betting has thrived since retail and online sportsbooks launched in September 2023, with the governor announcing that roughly one month after launch upwards of $250 million had been wagered and roughly 500,000 mobile accounts created for online sportsbooks, as reported by CBS Sports on 13 October 2023.

The legislative and launch dates are given precisely. On 31 March 2023 Kentucky became the 34th state to legalize sports betting and the 25th with online/mobile access; retail locations opened on 7 September and Kentucky online sports betting became legal on 28 September. The state has always been the thoroughbred horse betting capital of the U.S., and as of the fall of 2023 it added sports betting to its wagering menu.

Two features distinguish the market. There are no restrictions on which sports can be wagered on or what kinds of prop bets can be placed, unlike restrictions on residents in other states, so anyone using Kentucky sports betting sites can bet on everything from football to college basketball, and Kentuckians can legally wager on all major professional, collegiate and international sports. There is also a wide variety of sportsbook apps to choose from, which the report calls one factor making the market such a success. With excitement building among customers, the report judges it feasible that Kentucky generates over $1 billion in sports betting handle by the end of 2023.
