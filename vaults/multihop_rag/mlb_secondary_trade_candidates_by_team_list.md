# MLB Secondary Trade Candidates By Team

Alongside its top 25 ranked names, Yardbarker's Nov. 13, 2023 offseason trade candidates piece listed a further set of secondary candidates organized by club, one to four players per team.

The list ran: A's — Seth Brown; Braves — Vaughn Grissom; Brewers — Adrian Houser, Devin Williams, Brandon Woodruff; Cardinals — Alec Burleson, Tommy Edman, Nolan Gorman, Tyler O'Neill; Giants — J.D. Davis, Mike Yastrzemski; Guardians — Josh Naylor, Cal Quantrill; Mariners — Ty France; Marlins — Josh Bell; Mets — Jeff McNeil; Nationals — Hunter Harvey, Lane Thomas; Orioles — Anthony Santander; Padres — Scott Barlow; Phillies — Nick Castellanos; Rays — Manuel Margot, Harold Ramírez; Red Sox — Kenley Jansen, Chris Martin, Nick Pivetta; Rockies — Brendan Rodgers; Royals — Salvador Perez; Tigers — Spencer Turnbull; Twins — Kyle Farmer.
