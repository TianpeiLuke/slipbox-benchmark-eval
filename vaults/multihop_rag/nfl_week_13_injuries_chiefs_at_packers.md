# NFL Week 13 Injuries Chiefs At Packers

In the Week 13 final injury reports published by CBSSports.com on Dec. 1, 2023, with the Chiefs listed at -6 at the Packers, Kansas City was relatively healthy while Green Bay would be without running back Aaron Jones.

McKinnon was a limited participant on both Thursday and Friday, so his prospects of playing on Sunday appeared to be trending in a positive direction; Bolton was still on IR and working his way back from a wrist injury. Green Bay would be without Jones as the running back continued to deal with a knee injury. Reed returned to practice Friday after missing both Wednesday and Thursday due to a chest injury, while Alexander — who had missed six of the Packers' 11 games that year — was limited throughout the week.
