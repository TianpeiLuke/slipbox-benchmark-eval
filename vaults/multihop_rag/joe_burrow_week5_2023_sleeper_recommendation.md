# Joe Burrow As A Week 5 2023 Sneaky Sleeper At Arizona

CBS Sports listed Joe Burrow as a "Sneaky Sleeper" for Week 5 of the 2023 season at Arizona: the claim is that if there is a matchup in which Burrow could piece together a decent stat line, this is it, and the warrant is that the Cardinals' passive pass rush removes the pressure that had been exposing an injured Burrow.

The grounds start with Burrow's condition. The weekly breakdown lists Cincinnati at Arizona, the Bengals favoured by 3, an over/under of 44.5, an opponent ranked 25th versus quarterbacks, 20.8 projected points, a 19th quarterback ranking, and year-to-date totals of 728 passing yards, three rushing yards, two touchdowns, two interceptions and 8.9 fantasy points per game. The previous week Burrow looked like a quarterback playing on one leg — not getting much into his throws, not challenging downfield, getting the ball out as fast as he could and sensitive to pass-rush pressure, and unable to get much going even in garbage time. His air-yards profile is bleak: 1 for 4 on throws of 10-plus air yards and 0 for 2 on throws of 15-plus in that game, and 11 for 38 and 2 for 22 respectively on the season. He has thrown just two touchdowns all year, both against Baltimore in Week 2, both from inside the five-yard line and both to Tee Higgins, and his red-zone line is unfathomable at 7 of 15 with at least nine of those throws going into the end zone. Opponents had begun to sniff out his issues: the Browns and Titans generated pressure rates between roughly 38% and 42% with blitz rates of at least 31% in their wins over Cincinnati, while the Rams and Ravens barely pressured or blitzed and either lost or nearly lost.

That is what makes Arizona the exception. The Cardinals are dead last in blitz rate this season at 14% and third-worst in pass-rush pressure rate at 28%; they had nine sacks after Weeks 1 and 2 but only three since, against the Cowboys and 49ers, and appear to prefer using just four rushers, which should help Burrow. They also rank eighth-worst in completion rate allowed at 69.8% — a figure heavily boosted by Brock Purdy's 95% completion rate the previous week — and are bottom-five in both yards per catch (11.7) and missed tackles on pass plays (49). The recommendation is explicitly bounded: Burrow is in no way a must-start, and there is plenty of good reason to go with Trevor Lawrence, Russell Wilson, C.J. Stroud and even Joshua Dobbs over him, but the mild appeal should at least keep him in lineups over Dak Prescott and Daniel Jones. The column also notes Denver and Chicago as similarly forgiving matchups.
