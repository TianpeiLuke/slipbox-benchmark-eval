# Battery Life and Charging

## Definition

Battery life and charging is the pair of consumer-electronics specs — how long a device runs on a charge, and how fast and via what connector it refills — that reviewers and shoppers use to compare phones, earbuds, handhelds, smart glasses, and the power banks and cables that feed them.

## Context

Across the corpus, battery life and charging speed function as headline review criteria and buying-guide sort keys rather than a single defined term: Engadget's Steam Deck OLED review leads with "its battery life is better" as one of the device's core upgrades, and The Verge lists "improved battery life and audio" as a top pro for Amazon's third-gen Echo Frames. The charging half of the pair is increasingly organized around USB-C: Engadget notes that Apple's iPhone 15 line and the second-generation AirPods Pro both moved to USB-C charging, and Polygon's portable-charger guide treats USB-C Power Delivery wattage as the key spec for matching a power bank to a phone, tablet, Steam Deck, or laptop. Capacity for power banks is measured in mAh, per Polygon's guide, while device battery life is reported in hours (the Echo Frames reviewer logged about five hours of media playback and calls before hitting 30 percent) or in minutes of measured playtime (Engadget's Steam Deck OLED testing). Gogoro's battery-swapping business shows the same pair of concerns scaled up to electric vehicles: swapping a depleted battery for a charged one at a station is explicitly framed as a way to cut "charging time" relative to plugging in.

## Key Characteristics

- **Duration is measured empirically, not just claimed** — Engadget timed the Steam Deck OLED at four hours 23 minutes playing Hades versus two hours 42 minutes on the original LCD model under identical settings, a 62 percent gain that beat Valve's own 30-to-50-percent claim.
- **Charging speed is a distinct spec from capacity** — Valve advertises the Steam Deck OLED going from 20 to 80 percent in 45 minutes, a speed claim separate from its 50Whr battery capacity; Polygon likewise separates a power bank's mAh capacity from its wattage-based recharge speed.
- **USB-C is displacing proprietary connectors as the charging standard** — Apple's iPhone 15 and USB-C AirPods Pro, and the iPad's shift off Lightning to USB-C on its 10th-generation model, are all cited by Engadget as part of a single connector transition.
- **Power Delivery (PD) wattage governs compatibility between chargers and devices** — Polygon's guide notes that only power banks supporting the PD 3.0 spec can recharge a Steam Deck at its fastest rate, and that a laptop needs PD 3.1 and a compatible cable to reach 140 W charging.
- **Battery swapping is an alternative to plug-in charging at the vehicle scale** — Gogoro's network lets riders exchange a depleted battery for a charged one at a station instead of waiting to charge in place, which TechCrunch frames as a way to reduce charging time for EV adopters.
