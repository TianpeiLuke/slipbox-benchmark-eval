# Prop Bet Examples By Sport

Every major sport with odds at the best sports betting apps will have prop bets, and the shape of those props follows each sport's statistics. The NBA offers a wide range, varying from player to team bets and leaning heavily on the statistical side of the game: how many points a player will score, the number of assists or three-pointers a player will have, which player will score the first basket. Team props are also available, such as whether a team will lead at the end of a certain quarter, or an over/under on the total number of points a team will score.

The NFL is the king of sports betting, and NFL betting sites have no qualms about showcasing every possible bet type. Bettors find many prop opportunities, especially for the Super Bowl; during the regular season one of the most popular options is the "anytime touchdown scorer" bet, where you wager on a player to score a touchdown at any point in the game, and other props focus on one team or both teams.

Baseball, hockey and soccer follow the same logic on their own stat lines. MLB props focus primarily on individual players and statistical achievements such as home runs, RBI and strikeouts — an over/under for a starting pitcher's strikeouts, or the number of bases a hitter will reach. NHL sites carry the props you would expect from a major North American league, such as player assists or a certain player scoring a goal, plus shots on goal, team goals in a game, and which team will score first. In Major League Soccer, where demand has increased as the sport's popularity grows nationwide, props often focus on individual accomplishments such as scoring a goal or collecting an assist, and also include how many cards the two teams will combine to draw or a team's total number of corner kicks.
