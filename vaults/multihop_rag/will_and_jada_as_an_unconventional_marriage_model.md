# Will And Jada As A New, Unconventional Marriage Model

Relationship expert Susan Winter argues that the Smiths' seven-year separation is not a stalled marriage waiting to be repaired but a fluid arrangement outside the traditional marriage design — an evolving model that could come to serve other couples. Her starting point is a limit on what outsiders can know: the public will never fully learn what "heavy-duty work" Will Smith and Jada Pinkett Smith are doing in their relationship, and, she says, it is not necessarily anyone's business.

The grounds are that a couple who have already built something different have no reason to revert to the conventional form. "They're in the rough with a machete, carving a new path for themselves," Winter said. "Why would they say: 'Hey, let's go back to the house and just relax?' They're really exploring something vastly different. It doesn't end up that you go back into that little box of 'this is a marriage, we're living together'. It just doesn't do that." The Independent frames the same point as a question: if the Smiths have spent seven years apart as a married couple, why would they decide to return to a shared household?
