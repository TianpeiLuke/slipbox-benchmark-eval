# IPVM's Rebuttal Of Hikvision's Human Rights Record

Experts on the surveillance tools used in the repression of Uyghurs reject Hikvision's compliance defence on the ground that the company's own history shows it has not followed through on previous commitments to preserve human rights — attacking the premise that its assurances can be taken at face value.

The specific rebuttal comes from Conor Healy, director of government research at the surveillance research publication Internet Protocol Video Market (IPVM), in a statement to the Guardian.

"Hikvision's critical role in surveilling and oppressing Muslims in Xinjiang and the company's failure to take accountability shows that the company is not serious about ethics or protecting human rights," Healy said.
