# Schumer's AI Insight Forum On Creative Copyright

On Wednesday November 29, 2023, US Senate Majority Leader Chuck Schumer held an Artificial Intelligence (AI) Insight Forum to discuss the importance and role of creative copyright and intellectual property in the development of AI — a sign that senior political figures in Washington are becoming increasingly interested in the impact of AI on the law.

Dennis Kooker, Sony Music Entertainment's President of Global Digital Business & US Sales, delivered a speech at the Forum outlining the major music company's position on AI and copyright law.
