# ChatGPT As A User Experience Breakthrough, Not An AI One

Stella Biderman, an AI researcher at Booz Allen Hamilton and the open research group EleutherAI, told TechCrunch that she does not see ChatGPT as an AI breakthrough per se, but as a bonafide "user experience breakthrough" that took generative AI mainstream. TechCrunch adds a supporting observation: OpenAI, which has released dozens of research papers on its models, tellingly never released one on ChatGPT.

The ground for calling it a UX rather than a technical advance is a change in the skill required of users: "Previously you needed to have some skill, albeit not be an expert, to consistently get usable stuff out of [text-generating models]. Now that that's changed … [ChatGPT has] brought a very large amount of attention to and discussion about the technology."

The warrant, in Biderman's framing, is where the effect landed — on the research and development community's attention rather than on capability: "The primary impact [ChatGPT] has had [is] encouraging people training AIs to try to mimic it, or encouraging people studying AIs to use it as their central object of study."
