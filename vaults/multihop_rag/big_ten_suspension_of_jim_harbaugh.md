# The Big Ten Suspends Jim Harbaugh For Three Games

The Big Ten officially announced that Michigan coach Jim Harbaugh would be suspended for the remaining three games of the 2023 regular season, prohibited from coaching on the sideline for the rest of that stretch, including the Wolverines' rivalry game with Ohio State. The announcement was reported by Chris Vannini on Nov. 10, 2023.

The stated basis was a conference rule violation rather than an NCAA finding. In its statement the Big Ten said Michigan had been found in violation of the Big Ten Sportsmanship Policy for conducting an "impermissible, in-person scouting operation over multiple years."
