# BG3 Paladin: Melee And Magic Through Oaths

Polygon names Paladin the best class for melee and magic in Baldur's Gate 3. Paladins start out mixing Divine magic into their melee abilities and are the most magic-focused of the melee classes.

Paladins get all armors and shields along with a good mix of weapons. Their subclass is an Oath, and the Oath determines what kind of magic they receive: Oath of the Ancients adds healing magic, Oath of Devotion gets defense-focused magic, and Oath of Vengeance adds offensive magic. Paladins also get a handful of other spells for dealing out even more holy justice. Oath of the Ancients is the best subclass for a Paladin, with the reasoning and the skill and feat picks left to Polygon's separate Paladin build guide.
