# Believe And Alejandra Olea, Managing Director, Americas

Believe is a music company whose steady growth in Latin music in the past few years has been led by Alejandra Olea, its Managing Director, Americas; the worldwide success of the act discussed in the feature is described as just part of that growth story.

The profile appears in MBW's World Leaders, a regular series in which the publication turns the spotlight toward some of the most influential industry figures overseeing key international markets, and the series is supported by PPL. The interview with Olea was published on 7 December 2023.
