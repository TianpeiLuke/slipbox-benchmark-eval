# OpenAI's Nonprofit-To-For-Profit Structure

OpenAI started out as a nonprofit when it launched with financial backing from Tesla CEO Elon Musk and others, with stated aims to "advance digital intelligence in the way that is most likely to benefit humanity as a whole, unconstrained by a need to generate financial return." That changed in 2018, when it incorporated a for-profit business, OpenAI LP, and shifted nearly all its staff into that business, not long after releasing its first generation of the GPT large language model for mimicking human writing.

Around the same time, Musk — who had co-chaired its board with Altman — resigned from the board, in a move OpenAI said would eliminate a "potential future conflict for Elon" due to Tesla's work on building self-driving systems. The resulting arrangement is a split one: OpenAI's board has preserved its nonprofit governance structure, while the startup it oversees has increasingly sought to capitalize on its technology by tailoring its popular chatbot to business customers.
