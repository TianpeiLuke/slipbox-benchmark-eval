# Prediction: AI Agents And Generative Media Mature

TechCrunch predicts that some niche applications of AI models will grow beyond "eh" status in 2024, specifically agent-based models and generative multimedia.

For agents, the reasoning is that if AI is going to help you do more than summarize or make lists of things, it will need access to things like your spreadsheets, ticket buying interfaces and transportation apps. 2023 saw a few tentative attempts at this "agent" approach, but none really caught on, and the prediction is deliberately bounded: no agent is expected to really take off in 2024 either, but agent-based models will show their stuff a little more convincingly than they did the year before, and a few clutch use cases will show up for famously tedious processes such as submitting insurance claims.

For generative media, the prediction is that video and audio will find niches where their shortcomings are not quite so visible. In the hands of skilled creators, a lack of photorealism is not a problem, and AI video will be used in fun and interesting ways; likewise, generative music models will likely make it into a few major productions such as games, again where professional musicians can leverage the tools to create an unending soundtrack.
