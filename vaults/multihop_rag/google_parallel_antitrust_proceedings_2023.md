# Google's Parallel Antitrust Proceedings In Late 2023

As of mid-November 2023, Google was fighting antitrust on more than one front at once. The Epic Games case was not its only antitrust case in trial: Google had settled a separate antitrust lawsuit with dating site Match Group the week before November 13, and on November 6 it went to trial with Fortnite maker Epic Games, which hoped to prove that Google engages in anticompetitive behavior with regard to its Android app store, Google Play, and its commission structure. Separately, Google was involved in an antitrust lawsuit with the Department of Justice over its alleged search monopoly.

The Epic case had shed co-plaintiffs on its way to trial. Epic's lawsuit originally involved Match and several U.S. attorneys general, but Google reached a settlement with the attorneys general in October 2023 and with Match the week before November 6, so Epic fought the antitrust battle on its own. The lawsuit, held in the U.S. District Court in California's Northern District, would include testimony from a number of Google and Android execs, including CEO Sundar Pichai, and Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeney, as well as from Apple, Netflix and other Android developers. Epic, meanwhile, had lost its antitrust battle with Apple and was asking the Supreme Court to weigh in.
