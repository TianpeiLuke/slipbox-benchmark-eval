# Super Mario Bros. Wonder's Art And Overworld Map

This might be the best Mario and friends have ever looked, and that includes recent 3D titles like Odyssey: there is just so much detail packed into every level and character, from the determined face Mario makes when he runs around to the little flames that sprout from your feet every time you jump.

Wonder's map is also surprisingly engaging. In the beginning the reviewer was not sure the ability to freely roam around added all that much, but as he progressed he found a number of secret nooks and crannies that would not have fit if the game were locked to a handful of paths. And with Wonder Flowers giving Nintendo the freedom to mix and match mechanics and enemies, there is just so much to see.
