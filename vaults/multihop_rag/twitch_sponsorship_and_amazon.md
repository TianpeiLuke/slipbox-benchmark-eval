# Twitch Sponsorship And Amazon

Twitch describes its creator revenue as three stacked pillars — direct viewer support, advertising, and sponsorships — and places itself as leader on the first, heavily invested in the second, and behind on the third. On direct support, "Other services have tried it but from what I can tell them, what streamers have told me, is that Twitch is really the leader in that way." On advertising, "We've spent a lot of time on advertising, because there's no social media, content distribution service in the world that doesn't rely on advertising as a component. So ads have been a big focus." On the third, a Twitch executive told TechCrunch on 2023-10-26, "which is where we're behind, is sponsorships."

The sponsorship pillar is being built to segment by creator profile rather than to offer one deal: Twitch is "focused a lot on growing sponsorship opportunities for streamers and ensuring that we have three — based on what size you are, where you're located, how experienced you are, what type of content you're creating, where you're located in the world — three options to support you as a creator." The gap it is meant to close is the one creators report: they can't subsist off of just subs and ad revenue, and have to have branded partnerships that are difficult to land for midsize and smaller creators.

Amazon is the mechanism Twitch is using to close that gap. Twitch frames itself as having "this unique opportunity, being part of the third largest and fastest growing advertising business in the world, AKA Amazon," and is working so that advertisers who come in through Amazon advertising "understand the magic of Twitch, that they can easily set up influencer deals programmatically. And ultimately, you don't have the measurement that they expect."

The predicted effect of the Amazon channel is a widening of the advertiser pool and of the deal types it can support. Because of Amazon's diversity of advertisers, where before Twitch was more focused on endemic advertisers such as game publishers, it is now able to expand that reach to a much broader set of advertisers, and to serve the needs of both the affiliate marketing end of sponsorships, which is more of a performance model, and the top of the funnel, where advertisers are looking for more brand awareness.
