# Embracer Group's Restructuring And Studio Closures

Engadget called the Embracer Group the clearest example of rampant, surprise layoffs in 2023: in June 2023 Embracer announced that a six-year, $2 billion funding deal had fallen through and that it was going to restructure — meaning layoffs and studio closures.

Since that announcement, Embracer has shut down Volition, Free Radical Design and Campfire Cabal, divested Goose Byte, and fired developers at Saber Interactive; more than 900 people lost their jobs during these moves. Meanwhile, Embracer's share price rose by 11 points in November. The company had spent the preceding years acquiring prominent midsize studios — including Gearbox Software (Borderlands), Crystal Dynamics (Tomb Raider), Eidos-Montreal (Deus Ex) and Square Enix Montreal (Deus Ex Go) — and over the past decade grew its portfolio to cover more than 100 game studios, among them Volition (Saints Row), Coffee Stain (Goat Simulator), Free Radical Design (TimeSplitters) and Zen Studios (Pinball FX). The holding company also secured the rights to The Lord of the Rings in 2022, promising to turn it into "one of the biggest gaming franchises in the world."
