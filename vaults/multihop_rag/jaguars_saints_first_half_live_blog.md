# Jaguars-Saints First Half: Two Etienne Touchdowns And A Fake Punt Build A 17-6 Lead

Jacksonville led New Orleans 17-6 at halftime of the Oct. 19, 2023 "Thursday Night Football" game, scoring two Travis Etienne Jr. touchdowns and adding a field goal set up by a fake punt. The Jaguars received the opening kickoff at 8:15 p.m. and drove straight down the field: a 21-yard completion to Evan Engram and a 15-yard Etienne Jr. dump-off reached Saints territory (8:17 p.m.), Lawrence found Christian Kirk for 10 to reach the New Orleans 16 (8:21 p.m.), Jamal Agnew took a reverse to the two (8:22 p.m.), and Etienne Jr. barreled in from two yards out at 8:23 p.m. — 5:16 into the game — against a Saints defense that had not allowed an opening-drive touchdown since the previous season.

New Orleans wasted its early chances. The Saints avoided a three-and-out on a five-yard Carr-to-Kamara third-down completion (8:28 p.m.) and Carr rushed 10 yards for a first down across midfield (8:29 p.m.), but a Jacksonville blitz forced a throwaway on third-and-seven and rookie Blake Grupe pushed a 51-yard attempt right at 8:33 p.m. — his third miss in five kicks. On Jacksonville's first play after the miss, Paulson Adebo punched the ball out of Kirk's hands and the Saints recovered at their own 41 (8:37 p.m.), but the Jaguars forced a three-and-out (8:40 p.m.). Moments later Tevaughn Campbell ran into his own returner Agnew on a punt, forcing a muff that New Orleans recovered deep in Jacksonville territory with 3:36 left in the first quarter. A seven-yard Kamara reception gave first-and-goal at the six (8:44 p.m.), but the Saints could not convert and Grupe made a 23-yard chip shot at 8:47 p.m. for a 7-3 score with 1:26 left in the quarter, which ended after Lawrence hit Kirk for nine on third-and-three (8:52 p.m.).

Jacksonville stretched the lead early in the second quarter. Kirk took a handoff and tossed it back to Lawrence, who found Agnew for nine (8:55 p.m.); Lawrence escaped pressure for a 26-yard run, the second-longest of his career, showing no sign of knee trouble (8:57 p.m.); and Etienne Jr. took a pitch 17 yards down the sideline at 8:58 p.m. for his second touchdown of the game and his sixth in three games, making it 14-3. New Orleans repeatedly went for it and failed: an 18-yard Carr-to-Moreau completion reached the Saints' 48 (9:03 p.m.), Darious Williams batted away a third-down pass to Michael Thomas and Allen kept the offense out on fourth-and-three (9:05 p.m.), and Carr's fourth-down attempt to Chris Olave was tipped away, giving Jacksonville the ball at its own 44. The Saints defense forced a three-and-out (9:10 p.m.), Kamara ran 21 yards on the next drive's first play (9:12 p.m.), but Tre Herndon broke up a third-down pass to Olave and New Orleans punted with 7:17 left (9:15 p.m.). Another Saints three-and-out stop plus a 15-yard penalty on Antonio Johnson for running into returner Rashid Shaheed gave New Orleans the ball at the Jacksonville 46 (9:20 p.m.). Allen again went for it on fourth-and-three (9:23 p.m.) and Kamara's 17-yard catch-and-run converted (9:24 p.m.), but Carr scrambled for only three on third-and-eight before the two-minute warning (9:26 p.m.), and Grupe made a 35-yard field goal at 9:29 p.m. for 14-6.

The half ended with Jaguars trickery and a reversed interception. Completions to Kirk and Engram both ended out of bounds to stop the clock, leaving Jacksonville all three timeouts inside two minutes (9:32 p.m.). The Saints forced another punt (9:35 p.m.), but Pederson called a fake punt at 9:36 p.m. on which punter Logan Cooke hit Tim Jones for 17 yards. At 9:38 p.m. Marcus Lattimore appeared to intercept Lawrence inside the 10 with 34 seconds left, diving in front of intended receiver Agnew; on review at 9:41 p.m. officials ruled the ball hit the ground, making it an incomplete pass on fourth down, and Brandon McManus converted the field goal at 9:42 p.m. with 32 seconds left for a 17-6 lead. Carr fumbled on the final play, recovered by one of his own linemen, and New Orleans left the field to boos (9:44 p.m.).
