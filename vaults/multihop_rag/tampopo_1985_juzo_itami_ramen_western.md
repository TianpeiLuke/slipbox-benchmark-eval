# Tampopo (1985): Juzo Itami's "Ramen Western"

Tampopo is Juzo Itami's 1985 "ramen western," running 1h 54m, starring Tsutomu Yamazaki, Nobuko Miyamoto and Kōji Yakusho. The plot is simple: a pair of truck drivers stop at a run-down ramen shop, befriend the widowed owner, and help her turn the restaurant's fortunes around.

What sets it apart, in Polygon's account, is that the hilarious romp also happens to be one of the most gorgeous depictions of food ever put on screen — a lovely story of community, passion and human nature all filtered through the appreciation of good food, making it a cinematic feast. Tampopo is available to stream on Max and Criterion Channel, or for digital rental or purchase at Amazon, Apple and Google Play.
