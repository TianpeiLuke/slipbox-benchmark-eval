# Turo's Buy-Now-Pay-Later Option

Peer-to-peer car-sharing marketplace Turo has introduced a buy-now-pay-later option, TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-30. Customers can now reserve a car without immediately paying for it until seven days before a trip, and Turo says it built the BNPL option in-house.
