# Alabama's CFP Situation Ahead Of The 2023 SEC Championship Game

No. 8 Alabama (11-1) had to beat No. 1 Georgia in the SEC championship at 4 p.m. on Saturday on CBS to keep its College Football Playoff hopes alive, with the question being whether the Crimson Tide would endure a three-year drought in the CFP, per Sporting News on Dec. 1, 2023.

By kickoff the Crimson Tide would know what had happened with No. 7 Texas. Alabama did get help with No. 3 Washington beating No. 5 Oregon in the Pac-12 championship game on Friday. In what had become an annual tradition, Alabama coach Nick Saban might have to do some politicking to get the Crimson Tide into the CFP conversation even with a victory against Georgia.

The Bulldogs were 5.5-point favorites against Alabama in the SEC championship game at Mercedes-Benz Stadium in Atlanta, so all of this might be a moot point — yet Alabama did have a path back to the CFP if they knocked off the top-ranked team in the country.
