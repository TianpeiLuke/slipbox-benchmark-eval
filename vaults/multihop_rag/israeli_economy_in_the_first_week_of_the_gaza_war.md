# Israeli Economy in the First Week of the Gaza War

As the first week of fighting in the war in Israel's south drew to a close, the main index on the Tel Aviv Stock Exchange, the Tel Aviv 35, had fallen 6.4% over the week, with financial markets trying to gauge the damage the Israeli economy would sustain. Globes reported on 12 October 2023 that this was clearly a very significant event, but that the fog of war made it hard to estimate its full consequences.

The assessment came from Prof. Leo Leiderman, chief economic adviser at Bank Hapoalim and a professor of economics at Tel Aviv University, who set out for Globes his proposals for rehabilitating the economy and repairing the damage the war would cause. Asked what had been going through his mind in those first days, Leiderman said: "What most preoccupies me is not the economy but concern for the families of the soldiers, the wounded, and the captives, and solidarity with them. I have four relatives from Kibbutz Nir Oz who are still missing, and we have no information about them. I find it hard to understand how a war like this landed on us in a country as powerful as Israel. But we have no choice but to win."
