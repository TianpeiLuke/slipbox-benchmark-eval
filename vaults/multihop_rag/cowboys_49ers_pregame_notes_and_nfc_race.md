# Cowboys-49ers Pregame: Gregory Inactive, Jones' Test, And The NFC Top Seed

The Sporting News pregame log for the Oct. 8, 2023 Cowboys-49ers "Sunday Night Football" game recorded that new 49ers acquisition Randy Gregory was officially inactive against his former team (7:11 p.m.), and that Jerry Jones had said that week the game would be a test of how the Cowboys matched up with the NFL's best — added pressure on a Dallas team the 49ers had knocked out of the playoffs in back-to-back years (8:10 p.m.).

The blog also framed the NFC stakes at 7:01 p.m.: with the Eagles expected to win in Los Angeles, the 49ers needed a win to keep pace atop the conference, and having played the last two NFC championship games on the road, the No. 1 seed was undoubtedly in the back of their minds.

Other pregame notes covered atmosphere and matchups. At 6:45 p.m. the blog reported a continued run of good prime-time weather, with high-70s and sunny conditions in Santa Clara leaving "no excuses" for the two offenses. At 7:36 p.m. it flagged that CeeDee Lamb was coming off back-to-back games with only four catches and might be due for a big performance. At 7:56 p.m. it noted that Frank Gore and Vernon Davis — described as greats from the 49ers' last golden era under Jim Harbaugh — were in the building.
