# IPL 2024 Auction: Final Round Results

The last round of the IPL 2024 auction was flagged at 8:35 PM IST as a short and crisp round, and it was mostly recalls of players who had gone unsold earlier. Karun Nair was recalled at 8:35 PM IST and went unsold again. At 8:38 PM IST Manish Pandey returned to KKR at a base price of Rs 50 L. At 8:40 PM IST Rilee Rossouw was recalled, drawing a bidding war between DC and PBKS that crossed 5 Cr, with PBKS finally winning for Rs 9 Cr. At 8:44 PM IST Steve Smith went unsold again.

At 8:45 PM IST Lockie Ferguson went to RCB at a base price of Rs 2 Cr, and Mujeeb Ur Rahman was recalled and became a KKR player for Rs 2 Cr. At 8:47 PM IST LSG completed their roster with Arshad Khan for Rs 20 L. At 8:50 PM IST Mohammad Nabi found a new home at Mumbai Indians for Rs 1.5 Cr, and at 8:51 PM IST the English bowler Gus Atkinson went to KKR for Rs 1 Cr. At 8:54 PM IST Abid Mushtaq went to RR for 20 L, and Swastik Chikara went to DC, completing Rishabh Pant and company's squad. At 8:59 PM IST Avinash Rao Aravely went to CSK for 20 L, and at 9:00 PM IST Sourabh Chauhan went to RCB for Rs 20 L and the South African Nandre Burger went to RR for Rs 50 L.
