# Paul Blackburn Athletics Trade Candidacy

Paul Blackburn was ranked No. 18 on Yardbarker's Nov. 13, 2023 list of the top 25 MLB offseason trade candidates and described as arguably the most desirable realistic trade candidate on the Oakland roster.

The A's had torn things to the studs and were unlikely to compete within the two remaining years of Blackburn's arbitration window. The right-hander, who would turn 30 the following month, worked to a 4.43 ERA with a decent 22.4% strikeout rate over 103 2/3 innings that past season and was projected for a $3.2M salary. A Blackburn trade would not be an offseason-defining move, but he had a good chance to be flipped to a contender seeking affordable back-end starting pitching.
