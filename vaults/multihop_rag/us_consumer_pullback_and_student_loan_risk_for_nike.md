# US Consumer Pullback and Student Loan Risk for Nike

Amid decades-high inflation rates, consumers have been pulling back on apparel and footwear, and with the resumption of student loan payments looming, some analysts expect those sectors to take an even greater hit.

Survey evidence quantifies the intent: Jefferies conducted a survey on US consumer spending and found 54% of respondents plan to spend less on apparel and accessories, while 46% plan to spend less on footwear — which, CNBC noted, does not bode well for Nike.

The timing means the effect was not yet measurable in these results. It is still too early to gauge the impact of student loan payments on Nike: its first quarter ended in late August, and payments were not set to resume until October 2023.
