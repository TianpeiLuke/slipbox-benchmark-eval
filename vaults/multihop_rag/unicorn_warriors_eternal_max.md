# Unicorn: Warriors Eternal (Max)

Unicorn: Warriors Eternal, created by Genndy Tartakovsky and Darrick Bachman and starring Hazel Doupe, Demari Hunte, Tom Milligan, and Paul Tylak, is called Tartakovsky's latest masterpiece in Polygon's 2023-10-25 roundup of the best new TV series on every streaming service.

Polygon introduces the creator as a name that needs no introduction among animation fans: the creator of such well-known and beloved series as Dexter's Laboratory, Samurai Jack, and Primal, Tartakovsky is known for imaginative worlds populated with eccentric and memorable characters.

The show is a fantasy action series over two decades in the making, following a group of immortal heroes who are reincarnated across time and space to do battle against an ancient evil. Critic Toussaint Egan writes that it combines several of Tartakovsky's most passionate interests — the science fiction of Osamu Tezuka's Astro Boy, the steampunk-adjacent Victorian settings of Hayao Miyazaki's work, and the idiosyncratic character designs of Popeye creator E. C. Segar — to create an experience that feels unlike anything else on television in 2023, animated or otherwise.
