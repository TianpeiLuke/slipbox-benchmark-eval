# Bumble's CEO Transition To Lidiane Jones

Dating app Bumble announced in the week to November 11, 2023 that it is replacing founder CEO Whitney Wolfe Herd with Slack CEO Lidiane Jones.

Jones only started as CEO at Slack in 2022, stepping in for another founder CEO, Stewart Butterfield — so the move repeats, one company over, the succession pattern she was hired into.

Ron and Sarah write that while Bumble now has a clear line of succession, the move leaves Slack in a bit of a pickle.
