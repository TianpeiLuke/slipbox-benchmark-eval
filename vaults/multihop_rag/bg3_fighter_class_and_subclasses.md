# BG3 Fighter: The Beginner Melee Class

Polygon names Fighter the best melee class for beginners in Baldur's Gate 3, because it is the easiest class to wrap your head around: there are no spells and no Ki Points to manage — you see a baddie and you stab said baddie.

Fighters start as a very straightforward melee class. With high strength they get all armors and shields, they choose a method of combat to focus on and receive buffs to it, and they gain both a way to heal themselves and a way to get a second attack once per short rest. At level 3 a Fighter chooses a subclass from Battle Master, Eldritch Knight, and Champion. Battlemasters get a set of Superiority Dice that let them perform Maneuvers to control the flow of battle and the battlefield; Eldritch Knights add magic into the Fighter's arsenal; Champions keep it simple with a better chance to deal a Critical Hit, for double damage. Champion is the best subclass for a Fighter, with the reasoning and the skill and feat picks left to Polygon's separate Fighter build guide.
