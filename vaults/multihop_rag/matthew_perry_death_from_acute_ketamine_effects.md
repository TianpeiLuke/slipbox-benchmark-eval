# Matthew Perry's Death From Acute Effects Of Ketamine

Matthew Perry's cause of death was listed as "acute effects of ketamine" on a Friday, and in the days afterward -- reported December 19, 2023 -- experts began speaking out about misconceptions surrounding the drug.

The autopsy named contributing factors beyond ketamine. Other conditions that contributed to Perry's death, according to the autopsy report, included "coronary artery disease [and] buprenorphrine effects." Also, "prescription medications and loose pills" were found at the residence, the report said.

Dr. Bankole Johnson, CEO and founder of Casa Privée in Miami, a concierge medical facility that offers ketamine infusion therapy to help patients manage various conditions, told Fox News Digital that in his opinion, Perry's death likely was linked to recreational ketamine use -- though he stated that he did not treat or examine the actor.
