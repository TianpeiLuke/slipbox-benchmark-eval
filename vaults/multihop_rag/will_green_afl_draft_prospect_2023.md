# Will Green AFL Draft Prospect 2023

Will Green, profiled in Kevin Sheehan's top 40 for the 2023 AFL Draft as reported by The Roar on Nov. 15, 2023, is a developing tall who impressed throughout the year with his ruck craft, marking and mobility when playing for the Northern Knights, Victoria Metro and the Australia under-18 team. State: Victoria Metro. State League Club: Northern Knights. Community Club: Northcote. Date of Birth: 08/09/05. Height: 204cm.

He holds his ground very well both under the ball in stoppages and in marking contests, and provides great opportunities for his teammates at ground level. He averaged 12 disposals and 24 hitouts when representing Victoria Metro at the National Championships to earn All Australian honours. Both his running vertical jump of 87cm and 2km time of 6:34 at the national Draft Combine were impressive indicators of his natural leap and endurance. Weapon: Mobility and marking. AFL player comparison: Brodie Grundy (Sydney Swans).
