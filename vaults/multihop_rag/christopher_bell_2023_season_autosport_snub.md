# Christopher Bell 2023 Season Autosport Snub

Essentially Sports argued on Dec. 21, 2023 that Christopher Bell must have felt hard done by after an impressive season that went under the radar and left him off Autosport's Top 50 drivers of 2023.

The grounds are qualifying excellence and playoff progression. The #20 Toyota Camry driver progressed to the championship race for a second successive season, though a cruel twist of fate waited in the season finale at Phoenix Raceway, where he was forced to retire early from the biggest race of his career with a broken brake rotor. With an average starting position of 9.6, the 29-year-old was the sole driver to have an average starting position below 10; a win in the Round of 8 race at Homestead-Miami propelled him to a consecutive Championship 4 appearance; and he dominated qualifying throughout the season, especially in the playoffs, recording poles in the first three playoff races. The Joe Gibbs Racing driver finished the season with two wins, 19 top-tens and ten top-fives, eventually finishing fourth in the final NASCAR Cup Series standings as another of the prominent performers of the calendar year.
