# Gogoro's Company Profile And Open Platform Model

Gogoro, founded in 2011 by former HTC executives Luke and Matt Taylor, considers itself the Android of all EVs: it offers its proprietary technology to other automobile manufacturers alongside selling its own branded scooters with swappable batteries. Alongside its hometown market in Taiwan, the company already operates in China, Indonesia, Singapore, Israel and the Philippines.

The open-platform side of the model is concrete in Taiwan. About 11 vehicle manufacturers there are building vehicles of different sizes and configurations based on Gogoro's standard battery size, including the likes of Suzuki, Yamaha and Aeon Motor. A company executive indicated that some of these may come to India along with Gogoro's swappable battery technology over time.
