# The Connor Stalions Central Michigan Sideline Investigation

Central Michigan said it is investigating whether suspended Michigan staffer Connor Stalions was on the CMU sideline during the Chippewas' 1 September game at Michigan State, as reported by The New York Times on 3 November 2023.

The evidence trail is dated: screenshots of a person who looks similar to Stalions began circulating online on the Monday night, and The Athletic obtained more photos of the person on the sideline on the Tuesday. Central Michigan athletic director Amy Folan said: "We became aware of these photos late yesterday and we are in the process of determining the facts surrounding them."

Stalions' place in the wider case is set out. Suspended with pay by Michigan on 20 October, he is at the centre of the NCAA's investigation into the alleged scouting and sign-stealing scheme, and sources told The Athletic the previous month that he bought tickets to games in at least seven Big Ten stadiums before those teams played the Wolverines over the past three seasons, including the 2023 season. Purchasing the tickets is not a violation of NCAA rules, but using them to scout and record other teams would violate the rules prohibiting in-person, on-campus scouting and the audio or video recording of signals.
