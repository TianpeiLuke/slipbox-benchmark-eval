# Daily Habits For Ageing Well After Fifty

The Guardian's 10 October 2023 summary sets out five simple guidelines that anyone over 50 would do well to follow: engage with something new every day, take a daily walk of at least 20 minutes, socialise regularly, take a daily multivitamin for seniors, and check the protein content of your meals.

The stated precondition is age — the guidance is framed for anyone over 50, and is offered as the same advice one would give an ageing parent.

The article's closing reframe is that applying these steps to yourself rather than only to your parents is a matter of perspective: "Perhaps we should think of it as self-parenting."
