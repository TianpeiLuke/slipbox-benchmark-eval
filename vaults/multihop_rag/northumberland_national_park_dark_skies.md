# Northumberland: The Darkest Skies In The UK, But Storm-Dependent

Northumberland's National Park is another International Dark Sky Park and reportedly boasts the darkest skies in the UK, making it an excellent stargazing spot as well as an aurora site. Although it sits at a more southerly latitude than many other light-spotting locations, the aurora has been seen over Hadrian's Wall and Bamburgh in the past, and the county remains one of the most reliable locations to see the display in England.

The limiting condition is solar activity: lights are only usually visible here after particularly strong geomagnetic storms, so it may be an idea to keep track of these if planning a visit. As elsewhere, areas near the coast — themselves in an Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty — can be better for seeing the aurora due to the presence of fewer clouds.
