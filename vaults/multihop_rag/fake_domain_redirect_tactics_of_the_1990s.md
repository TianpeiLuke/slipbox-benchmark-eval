# Fake Domain Redirects In The 1990s

One of Daron Babin's signature moves back in the '90s involved fake domain names: "I could make it look like it was somebody else, but it actually redirected to me!" What he and his competitors did was legal but well beyond what the dominant search engine allowed.

The consequences fell on users rather than on him. He never faced any consequences, but in the end internet users at large felt the effects: "It muddied up Yahoo, ultimately," he said, "but while it worked, we banked."

Babin also offered a forward-looking note the same evening: "We're entering a very weird time, technologically, with AI, from an optimization standpoint." Anyone who thought the internet was already saturated with SEO-oriented content should buckle up.
