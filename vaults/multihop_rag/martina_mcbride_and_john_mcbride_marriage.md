# Martina McBride And John McBride's Marriage

Country star Martina McBride and her husband John McBride, a sound engineer, have been married for 35 years — more than three decades together — and share three daughters: Delaney, Emma and Ava. The "Valentine" singer marked the milestone anniversary on Instagram earlier in 2023: "We made it to 35 years! Thirty. Five. Years. Wow. This guy makes me laugh every day and he's a great dad to our 3 girls. He is a friend to many. But he's my best friend. Love you John."

The couple are also business partners: they jointly own Blackbird Studios in Nashville, which they opened in 2002 and where they have since welcomed country artists including Tim McGraw and Faith Hill, Taylor Swift, Dolly Parton, Sheryl Crow and Blake Shelton.
