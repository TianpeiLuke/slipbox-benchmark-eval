# Prime Day 2023 Mac Desk And Hub Accessory Deals

WIRED's Mac accessory picks are a Mobile Pixels portable monitor, a lap laptop stand, the Twelve South StayGo Mini hub, a triple-display docking station at its lowest tracked price, Satechi's 108-watt wall charger, a four-port 100-watt desk charger, and a tablet stand.

The portable monitor attaches to a laptop screen using magnets — you'll have to be comfortable sticking and leaving adhesive magnets on the back of your laptop — connects via USB-C, and slides out to reveal a 13-inch display, adding some bulk and thickness but removable when not in use. The laptop stand suits people who prefer working from bed or the couch: its height isn't adjustable, but the base can be raised to the best angle for typing, watching a movie on a tablet, or sketching, with extra space for a coffee mug and a small drawer for knickknacks. The hub, used with iPads but also great for MacBooks, plugs into a USB-C port to add another USB-C port for passthrough charging, a USB-A port, an HDMI output, and a headphone jack, staying fairly compact and thin. The docking station is the runner-up to the Twelve South StayGo Mini in WIRED's Best MacBook Accessories guide, supporting up to three displays at once via DisplayPort or HDMI each, with six USB 3.0 ports — two front, four back — a gigabit Ethernet port, and up to 60 watts of output to charge the laptop, so plugging in a MacBook gives an instant multi-monitor setup. Satechi's 108-watt wall charger is recommended for MacBook Pro owners, with three USB-C ports each able to charge at 100 watts when only one device is connected, making it perfect for the 16-inch MacBook Pro; charging three gadgets simultaneously splits power at 45, 30 and 30 watts, or 58, 30 and 20, or 65, 20 and 20, depending on the devices, and while it's on the larger side it isn't as big as Apple's and its plug folds up. The four-port charger has enough power to juice two MacBook Pros simultaneously and suits a desk full of devices, with a little stand to prop it up, a short cord to the outlet, and four USB-C ports outputting 100 watts each; it dipped to $90 in July but this is still a great deal. The tablet stand, featured in several WIRED guides including Best iPad Accessories and Best Work-from-Home Gear, is lightweight, portable, and easy to use, and this deal has appeared during previous shopping holidays.
