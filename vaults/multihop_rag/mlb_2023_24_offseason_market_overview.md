# The 2023-24 MLB Offseason Market After The Winter Meetings

As of Sporting News' Dec. 23, 2023 free agency tracker, the 2023-24 MLB offseason was a market whose two biggest names were already settled but whose bulk was still unresolved: Shohei Ohtani and Juan Soto had already found new homes, while much of the offseason had yet to play out.

The defining features of the remaining market were a marquee international free agent, a set of unsigned veterans, and a trade market waiting on them. Japanese pitcher Yoshinobu Yamamoto was the focus of many teams as he prepared to sign a massive deal, while Blake Snell, Cody Bellinger and Jordan Montgomery remained on the market. The trade market was also expected to be active once more dominoes fell, with Dylan Cease, Corbin Burnes and Tyler Glasnow among the starters who could be moved that winter. The boundary condition on the calendar was that the Winter Meetings were over, but because of the timeline of the major decisions by Ohtani and Yamamoto, there could be a flurry of activity leading up to the end of the year.
