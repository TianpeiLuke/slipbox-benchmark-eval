# Catherine Called Birdy (2022): Lena Dunham's Coming-Of-Age Comedy

Catherine Called Birdy is Lena Dunham's 2022 adaptation of the beloved children's novel, running 1h 48m, starring Bella Ramsey, Andrew Scott and Billie Piper. Polygon calls it an outstanding coming-of-age story and the rare book-to-movie adaptation done right: a warm story about the difficulties of teenage girlhood and the expectations that come with it in any era, but especially medieval times, anchored by performances from Ramsey (Game of Thrones) and Scott (Fleabag).

Ramsey plays Birdy, a young girl whose father (Scott) is trying to arrange a marriage for her to save the family's finances; strong-willed and given to playfulness and mischief, Birdy is intent on disrupting his plans. The film's distinguishing achievement, per the reviewer, is its layered portrayal of the pair — neither is pure hero or pure villain, and Dunham complicates the book's version of them to much success. The reviewer put it on one Saturday afternoon expecting enjoyable background fare and was instead completely enthralled for all 108 minutes, calling it one of the most delightful movies of the year. It is available to stream on Prime Video.
