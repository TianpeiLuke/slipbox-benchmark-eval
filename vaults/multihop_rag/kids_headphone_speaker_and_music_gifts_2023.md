# Kids' Headphone, Speaker And Music Gifts 2023

The Verge's kids guide lists five audio gifts: BuddyPhones Cosmos Plus at $79.90+ (Amazon, Walmart, BuddyPhones), Skullcandy Jib True 2 at $28.17+ (Amazon, Best Buy, Walmart), the Amazmic Kids Karaoke Microphone at $15.29 (Amazon), the fifth-gen Echo Dot Kids at $27.99 (Amazon, Best Buy, Target), and A Very Spidey Christmas on vinyl at $19.99+ (Merchbar, Discogs Marketplace).

Headphones built for kids are, the guide argues, low key some of the best gadgets parents can own — handy during homework and helpful in keeping screen time less hectic, especially in the car. The noise-canceling BuddyPhones Cosmos Plus offer wireless and wired connectivity, an included case, shareable audio, and most importantly a volume limiter to protect young ears. With the 3.5mm headphone jack quickly going extinct, Skullcandy's Jib True 2 saves the budget for parents anxious about giving children a pricey pair of earbuds: they sound just fine and have built-in Tile tracking so you can find them if misplaced. The Amazmic microphone is an evolution of the terrible echo mics '90s kids may remember — instead of only hearing their own voice, a child gets the backing track of whatever song you play via Bluetooth. The animal-themed Echo Dot Kids has all the features of the regular fifth-gen model plus age-appropriate games, and comes with a one-year subscription to Amazon Kids Plus, which houses a large assortment of audiobooks, and a two-year accident protection plan. A Very Spidey Christmas is a real five-song holiday EP on vinyl featuring Shameik Moore, Chris Pine, and Jake Johnson, including the Xmas classic "Spidey-Bells" — following a brief gag in Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse about Spider-Man having a Christmas album.
