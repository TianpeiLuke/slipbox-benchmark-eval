# Riddick Bowe vs Elijah Tillery, 1991: The Ring Brawl

Elijah Tillery was disqualified against future world champion Riddick "Big Daddy" Bowe in a bad-tempered 1991 heavyweight bout, after being dragged out of the ring over the top rope by Bowe's manager, talkSPORT recalled on 15 November 2023.

After the bell ended round one, Bowe landed a late jab in response to Tillery blowing him a kiss. Tillery began kicking Bowe, and the pair brawled until Riddick's fiery manager, Rock Newman, leaned in from outside and hauled Tillery out of the ring via a crude WWE neckbreaker.

Amid the chaos, the referee decided that Tillery's kicks were worthy of a DQ and called the insanity off.
