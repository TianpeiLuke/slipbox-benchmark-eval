# Jawan

Jawan, described by Polygon as the biggest Indian movie of the year, landed on Netflix in early November 2023. It is an action thriller running 2 hours 50 minutes, directed by Atlee, with a cast including Shah Rukh Khan, Nayanthara, and Vijay Sethupathi.

Polygon notes that Atlee also directed Mersal, and that between this film and Pathaan megastar Shah Rukh Khan is truly back. Its one-line characterisation of the film is that it is basically "Robin Hood meets Charlie's Angels."
