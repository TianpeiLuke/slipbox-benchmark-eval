# Beats Studio Pro Deal Listings

The Beats Studio Pro appear in three 2023 roundups: on sale at $271.15 in blue at Amazon and $299.99 in other colors from Best Buy and Verizon against a normal $349.99 in The Verge's December list, and in both WIRED Prime Day roundups at the lowest price WIRED had tracked.

The Verge notes that after six years Apple-owned Beats replaced the Beats Studio 3 with the Studio Pro earlier in 2023: the over-ears offer much better sound quality thanks to features like support for lossless audio over USB-C, cancel noise much better, and work with iOS and Android features like Fast Pair and Google's Find My Device platform. WIRED's general roundup, reviewing them at 7/10, wishes the battery life were longer and the controls and EQ options more robust, but says the price makes them more worthwhile and that they do have excellent noise canceling. WIRED's Apple roundup (also 7/10) credits excellent calling and noise canceling, good sound quality, fantastic wireless range, a stable fit and compact design, while noting missing key features including sensors for auto-pause and multi-band EQ — concluding that with improved sound quality and ANC plus extras for both Android and Apple devices they remain a solid option, especially on sale.
