# Dylan Cease White Sox Trade Candidacy

Dylan Cease was ranked No. 6 on Yardbarker's Nov. 13, 2023 list of the top 25 MLB offseason trade candidates after a disappointing follow-up to his Cy Young runner-up campaign: he allowed a 4.58 ERA over 177 innings that past season, nearly two and a half runs higher than his sparkling 2.20 mark of the prior year, with his true talent probably lying somewhere in the middle.

Cease still missed plenty of bats — a 27.3% strikeout rate and 13.6% swinging strike percentage — while issuing a few more walks than ideal, and his fastball averaged 95.6 mph, down a tick from the preceding season. The right-hander had shown the ability to pitch at an ace-caliber level, was two seasons from free agency and was projected for an $8.8M arbitration salary, making him more affordable than the other high-end starting pitchers near the top of the list. That extra year of control probably gave him more trade value than any of Burnes, Bieber or Glasnow, and it also made him less likely to be traded that offseason.

First-year GM Chris Getz had suggested no one on the roster was truly untouchable, an understandable approach for a team that had just lost 101 games, though neither owner Jerry Reinsdorf nor Getz had called this a rebuild. Any slim hopes the White Sox may have had of competing in 2024 would be dashed by trading Cease, the only above-average starting pitcher on the roster.
