# Lyseggen's "Netscape Moment" Rebuttal On Meltwater And AI

Jørn Lyseggen rejects the premise that generative AI displaces Meltwater, arguing instead that the AI shift is an opportunity for challengers and that Meltwater is already positioned as one.

"I consider OpenAI's ChatGPT the 'Netscape moment' in ushering in this new era," he said — an interesting comparison, since Netscape certainly changed how the world looks for information although it is far from being part of what we use today. "AI is changing the game for players to challenge the old guard. I think Meltwater's tech stock is already the most modern and AI-centric in its category. We will continue to invest in AI and that's something we are very excited about. We are pushing very hard." Meltwater today says it analyzes around 1 billion documents daily for clients in communications, marketing and PR.

The premise Lyseggen is attacking is the framing that Meltwater's focus feels like a throwback to a problem that has now essentially been fixed and may well be made more efficient by would-be competitors; unsurprisingly, he thinks there is more opportunity for his company regardless.
