# Vermont's Online Sports Betting Law: Up To Six Sites, Launching January 2024

Vermont's new online sports betting law authorizes up to six sites to launch in the state, with the launch of legal online sports betting scheduled for January 2024, Sporting News explained on 2023-09-26. Five operators applied for licenses. As of that date, FanDuel, DraftKings and Fanatics were already scheduled to launch on Jan. 11, 2024, while BetMGM and ESPN Bet were also among the applicants, making them possible candidates to launch in Vermont in the new year. Because the law allows for as many as six online sportsbooks, and only some were known, the article also surveyed other sites thriving in other states that could potentially come to Vermont eventually.

The competitive consequence is the defining feature of the market: the best sports betting sites in the country are focused on Vermont, and once they launch they'll all be fighting for market share with exclusive Vermont sportsbook bonuses and promotions, including deposit match bonuses, bonus bets, first bet offers and more. Typically all you have to do is sign up for a new account with a Vermont online sportsbook to become eligible to receive the welcome bonuses, although in some cases you'll need to enter promo codes when registering to receive the offers; after that, you can continue to claim other ongoing Vermont sportsbook promotional offers.
