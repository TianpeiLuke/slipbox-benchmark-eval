# Tyreek Hill Dolphins Midseason MVP

Yardbarker's Nov. 9, 2023 AFC midseason team MVP list named wide receiver Tyreek Hill the Miami Dolphins' MVP, calling him arguably the best wide receiver in the NFL and saying he had somehow reached a new level of dominance in his age-29 season.

The grounds are league-leading production: the seven-time Pro Bowler led the NFL in receiving yards with 1,076 and in touchdown catches with eight, and he was on pace to break Calvin Johnson's season record for receiving yards of 1,964. The forward-looking claim carries a condition — as long as the Dolphins maintained their success on offense down the stretch, Hill should earn Offensive Player of the Year honors.
