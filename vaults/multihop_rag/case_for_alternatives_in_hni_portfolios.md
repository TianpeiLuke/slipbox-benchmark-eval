# The Case For Alternatives In HNI Portfolios

Alternative Investment Funds, Portfolio Management Services and Market Linked Debentures genuinely deserve a place in the portfolio of every high-net-worth investor, because their distinct value propositions, unique features, distinctive risk-return profiles and low correlation with traditional asset classes let HNIs improve portfolio performance, generate alpha and grow wealth faster. The grounds are that the growing number of HNIs, together with their desire to diversify portfolios and access opportunities outside traditional avenues such as stocks, mutual funds and fixed deposits, prompted innovation of existing investment products and led to the emergence of a new asset class known as "Alternatives".

The warrant is an asymmetry of access rather than a difference of principle: although the same principles of financial and investment planning apply to all investors, HNIs have an advantage in that they can choose from a broader range of conventional and unconventional investment opportunities, so investing in unconventional avenues is available to them in a way it is not to others. The recommendation carries explicit conditions. Before investing, investors should conduct thorough research to understand how these investment options work; it is important to consider the past track record and the management team before making investment decisions; and consultation with investment advisors knowledgeable in this area can undoubtedly be beneficial.
