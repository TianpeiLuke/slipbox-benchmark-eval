# Champions League 2023/24 Group G: Manchester City And RB Leipzig Qualify

Manchester City and RB Leipzig confirmed their knockout places from Group G of the 2023/24 Champions League after four games played, with Young Boys third and Red Star Belgrade bottom, per Sporting News on Dec. 12, 2023.

The final table read: 1. Man City (Q) 6 games, 6 wins, 0 draws, 0 losses, +11 goal difference, 18 points; 2. RB Leipzig (Q) 6, 4, 0, 2, +3, 12; 3. Young Boys 6, 1, 1, 4, -6, 4; 4. Red Star Belgrade 6, 0, 1, 5, -8, 1. City's 3-2 comeback win over the Bundesliga team on November 28 clinched top spot in the group for Pep Guardiola, and Young Boys dropped into the Europa League.

The results were: Sept. 19, Young Boys 1-3 RB Leipzig; Sept. 19, Man City 3-1 Red Star Belgrade; Oct. 4, RB Leipzig 1-3 Man City; Oct. 4, Red Star Belgrade 2-2 Young Boys; Oct. 25, RB Leipzig 3-1 Red Star Belgrade; Oct. 25, Young Boys 1-3 Man City; Nov. 7, Man City 3-0 Young Boys; Nov. 7, Red Star Belgrade 1-2 RB Leipzig; Nov. 28, Man City 3-2 RB Leipzig; Nov. 28, Young Boys 2-0 Red Star Belgrade; Dec. 13, RB Leipzig 2-1 Young Boys; Dec. 13, Red Star Belgrade 2-3 Man City.
