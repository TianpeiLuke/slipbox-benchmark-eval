# CDC's 2021-2022 Flu Burden Estimate And Vaccination Recommendation

During the 2021-2022 influenza season, the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention estimates that there were nine million illnesses, four million medical visits, 10,000 hospitalizations and 5,000 deaths related to the flu.

On the strength of that burden, the CDC recommends that everyone 6 months and older get a flu vaccine every season, with rare exceptions.
