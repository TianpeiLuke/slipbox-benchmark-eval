# Newegg Price Match Guarantee

Newegg offers price matching on select items only: eligible products are identified by the label "Price Match Guarantee" that appears in the price box on the right side of the product page. The match applies whether you find the lower price on Newegg itself or at what Newegg judges to be a major retailer.

The procedure has two deadlines. You need to notify Newegg of the lower price within 14 days of purchase; you are then issued a credit you can use on the Newegg website, but you have only 90 days to do so.

The exclusion, per The Verge's October 25th, 2023 summary: Newegg's price-match guarantee is not valid during major holiday sales events, including Black Friday, Cyber Monday, and Green Monday.
