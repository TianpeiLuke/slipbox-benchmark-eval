# eBay Best Price Guarantee

eBay offers a generous price-matching policy outside the holiday peak: if you find a lower price from a qualifying competitor on an eBay Deal item that you bought within 48 hours — or on another product carrying the "Best Price Guarantee" symbol — you get "110 percent" of the difference. That means eBay gives you an eBay coupon for the difference between what you paid and what the competitor is selling it for, plus an additional 10 percent of that price difference.

The preconditions are that the products must be new, unopened, and identical to the competitor's item. You have to contact eBay Customer Service, and if they verify your claims are legitimate you receive the coupon and can use it toward your next eBay purchase.

Two limits bound the policy, per The Verge's October 25th, 2023 summary: as a general rule eBay does not match the prices of purchases made during the weeks of Thanksgiving and Cyber Monday, and the coupons expire within 30 days of being issued.
