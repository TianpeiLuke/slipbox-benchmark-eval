# Cooperating Witnesses and Guilty Pleas

[FTX](ftx.md) co-founder and former CTO Gary Wang and [Alameda Research](alameda_research.md)'s former CEO Caroline Ellison both pleaded guilty in December 2022 to federal criminal charges relating to the FTX collapse. Both also face civil penalties from the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission and the Commodity Futures Trading Commission alongside the criminal charges.

Both agreed to cooperate with prosecutors and became major witnesses at trial, given their close ties to [Sam Bankman-Fried](sam_bankman_fried.md), FTX and Alameda.
