# FTX's Cooperating Witnesses

FTX co-founder and former CTO Gary Wang and Alameda Research's former CEO Caroline Ellison both pleaded guilty in December 2022 to federal criminal charges in relation to the FTX collapse, and both planned to cooperate with prosecutors. TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-01 that they would be major witnesses in the trial, given their close ties to Bankman-Fried, FTX and Alameda.

The criminal charges were not their only exposure. Alongside them, Wang and Ellison face civil penalties from the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) and the Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC).
