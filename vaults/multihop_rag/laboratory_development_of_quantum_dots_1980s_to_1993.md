# Laboratory Development Of Quantum Dots, 1980s To 1993

In the 1980s, Alexei Ekimov, 78, and Louis Brus, 80, honed the theory and developed early laboratory techniques for creating particles that emit varying colors by adjusting sizes.

In 1993, Moungi Bawendi, 62, developed new chemical methods for producing the particles quickly and uniformly — which soon enabled a variety of scalable commercial applications, including in electronics displays.
