# Diablo 4 Season 2 Experience and Leveling Changes

One of the most notable changes in Diablo 4's second season update is an increase in experience gains: Blizzard claims players will be able to reach the level cap of 100 about 40% faster than in Season of the Malignant, which had already increased experience gains compared with Diablo 4's launch. Polygon reported this on 2023-10-04.

The process should also be much smoother, as monsters will now match player levels between 55 and 75.

Game director Joe Shely told the press panel that last season players set a goal for themselves — get to level 100 — but many found they were never able to hit that milestone because XP gains were so slow. The change should allow more players to reach the level cap each season, letting them play with a truly maxed-out build.
