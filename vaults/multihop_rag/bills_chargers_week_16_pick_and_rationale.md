# Why Sporting News Picked The Bills In Week 16

Sporting News' Dec. 21, 2023 preview picked the Bills, arguing that Buffalo would keep rolling because the Chargers' new coach could not right all his team's wrongs overnight.

The grounds are the state of each team: for Los Angeles, losing by less than 38 points would be a great place to start, an ironic benchmark set by the previous week's rout. For Buffalo, the expectation was that they would try to replicate their massive success running the ball the previous week against Dallas, and that the Los Angeles defense would begin sagging heavily in the second half. The warrant joining grounds to pick is the judgment that the Bills' ground-game formula travels: they can beat the Chargers through the air but would probably run a copy of their Week 15 game plan against L.A. and every team that came their way afterwards. The stated pick is the Bills.
