# Blink Video Doorbell And Outdoor 4 Deal

The Blink Video Doorbell bundled with the latest Blink Outdoor security camera was available on Amazon for $89.99 — $90 off, down from $180, a 50 percent discount — as reported on October 28th, 2023, alongside continuing half-price offers on other Blink security cameras and video doorbells, including the Blink Mini cameras. The Verge pitched the bundle as a way to keep track of all the ghosties and ghoulies about to visit your front door on Halloween.

Both are 1080p cameras with tech that lets you clearly see trick-or-treaters after the sun goes down, plus support for motion detection and two-way audio. The battery-powered Blink Outdoor 4 also covers a wider field of view and supports person detection, which the prior model did not.

As budget picks they lack some premium features, including smart alerts, and you have to pay extra for features like cloud-based storage — but otherwise, The Verge wrote, they are a pair of great security devices that will protect your home just fine for years to come. The Blink Video Doorbell is described as the best budget-friendly buzzer, offering motion-activated recording and alerts, night vision, two-way audio, and up to two years of battery life.
