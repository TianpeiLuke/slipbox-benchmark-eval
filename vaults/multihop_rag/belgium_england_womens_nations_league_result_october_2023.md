# Belgium 3-2 England: The Women's Nations League Result, October 2023

Tessa Wullaert's brace fired Belgium to a dramatic 3-2 home win over England in League A1 of the 2023-24 UEFA Women's Nations League, reported by Sport Grill on 31 October 2023.

Wullaert equalised for Belgium in first-half injury time before taking advantage of Georgia Stanway's handball to fire in an 85th-minute winning penalty.

The result put Belgium second in the group, one point ahead of England and two points behind group leaders the Netherlands, with those two rivals set to meet on 1 December in a blockbuster showdown.
