# The Attention Economy And Limbic Capitalism

The possibilities of climate action exist within a media ecosystem that has monetized our attention and that profits from our hate and division: algorithms that reap advertising profits from maximizing time-on-site have figured out that what keeps us clicking is anger.

The mechanism is not only economic but neurochemical. The system is addictive, with notifications delivering hits of dopamine as part of what historian and addiction expert David Courtwright calls "limbic capitalism." Society has more or less sleepwalked into this outrage-industrial complex without having a real analytic framework for understanding it.

The framing on offer misdescribes the structure. The tech platforms and some research groups or think tanks offer up "misinformation" or "disinformation" as the framework, which presents the problem as if bad content were poisoning the well, rather than the structure itself being rotten. As Evgeny Morozov has quipped, "Post-truth is to digital capitalism what pollution is to fossil capitalism — a by-product of operations."
