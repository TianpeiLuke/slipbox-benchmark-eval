# Packers Edge Chiefs 27-19 On Week 13 Sunday Night Football

The Green Bay Packers edged past the Kansas City Chiefs 27-19 in the Week 13 "Sunday Night Football" game at Lambeau Field on Sunday, Dec. 3, 2023, beneficiaries of another Jordan Love masterpiece — an increasingly common sight in the 2023 season, per Sporting News. The quarter-by-quarter line was Chiefs 3-3-6-7 for 19 and Packers 7-7-7-6 for 27.

It was the game's final moments that drew the most intrigue, mostly because of their incredulous nature; Sporting News called it a sorry end to an enthralling contest, with Love and Patrick Mahomes duelling like gladiators, each painting pictures atop the verdant green canvas. The night was beautiful, chaotic, messy and eye-catching, and above all else it was cold. Although there were a number of ballads to be written about the calls that could have been, the Packers would not care: the win put them back in the postseason race, all on the back of their lithe young hurler. Sporting News tracked live updates from the clash under the lights at Lambeau Field.
