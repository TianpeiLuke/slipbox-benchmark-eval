# Hikvision Video Surveillance Company Profile

Hikvision is one of the world's largest makers of video surveillance equipment, based in Hangzhou, China, and it is among the vendors behind the surveillance cameras deployed in the occupied Palestinian territories.

What distinguishes it from other camera vendors is its record: the company has been accused of aiding what the US has categorized as a genocide. It is already infamous among international human rights groups, has been blacklisted by the US, and has been identified by the UK as a security threat, in both cases for being complicit in China's repression of the Uyghur ethnic minority.

The company's headquarters are in Hangzhou, in eastern China's Zhejiang province.
