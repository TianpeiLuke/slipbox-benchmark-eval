# Spire Motorsports 2024 Personnel Hires

Spire Motorsports announced a number of personnel moves the week of Dec. 4, 2023, CBSSports.com reported on Dec. 7: it hired Stephen Doran as crew chief for Smith's No. 71 Chevrolet and Luke Lambert as crew chief of the No. 77 for Carson Hocevar.

Doran joined Spire from Stewart-Haas Racing, where he was an engineer on the No. 4 team, while Lambert moved to Spire after being crew chief of the No. 42 at Legacy Motor Club in 2023. In addition, Spire hired Doug Duchardt, whose career in racing has included stints at General Motors and Chip Ganassi Racing, as its new team president.
