# Jack Harlow Announced As The Lions' Thanksgiving Halftime Performer

The Detroit Lions announced on Oct. 30, 2023 that Jack Harlow would perform at their Thanksgiving Halftime Show, powered by Verizon, the following month. The announcement was noted in the Sporting News "Monday Night Football" live blog at 7:40 p.m., which relayed the Lions' own post confirming Harlow as the performer.

Harlow was present at the Raiders-Lions game that night. At 7:20 p.m. the blog reported that he was in the house supporting the Lions, with the NFL noting he was "repping the Honolulu blue" and pointing viewers to his upcoming Thanksgiving halftime performance.
