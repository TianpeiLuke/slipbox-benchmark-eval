# Jordan Croft AFL Draft Prospect 2023

Jordan Croft, profiled in Kevin Sheehan's top 40 for the 2023 AFL Draft as reported by The Roar on Nov. 15, 2023, is a tall forward who is lightly built but extremely athletic. State: Victoria Metro. State League Club: Calder Cannons. Community Club: Maribyrnong Park. Date of Birth: 12/05/05. Height: 200cm.

He uses his mobility and height to create opportunities as a marking forward, with his speed and precise kicking key features of his game. He only fully focused on a potential AFL career the previous year after previously playing volleyball. He starred with the Calder Cannons and Victoria Metro that year, kicking 33 goals in the talent pathway, and his 2km time of 6:05 at the national Draft Combine was a strong indicator of his elite endurance but also his competitiveness for a player of his size. The eligibility condition attached to him: he can join the Western Bulldogs under the father-son rule as the son of former Bulldogs utility Matthew Croft, who played 186 games for the club, and he nominated as a father-son prospect for the Bulldogs. Weapon: Aerial ability. AFL player comparison: Max King (St Kilda).
