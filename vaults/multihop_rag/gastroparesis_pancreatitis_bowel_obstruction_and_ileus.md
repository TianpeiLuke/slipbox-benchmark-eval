# Gastroparesis, Pancreatitis, Bowel Obstruction And Ileus

Four gastrointestinal conditions frame the reported risks of these weight loss and diabetes drugs, and each has a distinct definition. Stomach paralysis, officially known as gastroparesis, prevents the nerves and muscles in the stomach from moving food into the small intestine, which keeps digestion from occurring, as described on Cleveland Clinic's website.

In addition to stomach paralysis, the drugs were linked to a greater risk of pancreatitis -- inflammation of the pancreas -- and bowel obstruction, which prevents food from passing through the small or large intestine, the release stated.

A fourth condition appears on the regulatory side rather than in the study: the U.S. Food and Drug Administration lists "ileus" as a potential side effect of Ozempic. Ileus is the inability of the intestine, or bowel, to contract normally and move waste out of the body, according to Mayo Clinic.
