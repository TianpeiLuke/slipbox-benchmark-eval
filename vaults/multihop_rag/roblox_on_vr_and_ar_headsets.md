# Roblox On VR And AR Headsets

Roblox recently put its app on the Meta Quest headset and got 1 million installs in five days, even though it was not officially in the store yet and was only available in a beta users had to go find rather than download from the store. Asked at the Code Conference where VR is going for Roblox, David Baszucki said he is a big believer, long term, that the most immersive form of 3D experience will be VR-type experience, noting there is some great sci-fi around the far-off future of VR. He said he had not tried Apple's Vision Pro.

His framing of the company's position is device-agnostic. Because Roblox is a platform where a young creator shows up, builds something, pushes a button, and has it run on any device, auto-translated into any language, possibly building a business on top of it, there is a huge benefit to being very, very good on all devices and tracking the growth of devices — so Roblox can contribute to the growth of Meta Quest, which he is excited about. If someday there are 500,000 or 500 million VR headsets, he said, Roblox will be a huge part of it, but the company is not in the position of predicting the growth of any hardware device.

On AR he was more speculative but saw a specific fit. He raised open questions — whether the future is mixed reality, lighter-weight overlaid stuff, or the vision of some of the companies that have been started — and said that someday, as he wrote in a blog post 10 years ago, we will have full overlay, whether via contact lenses, lightweight and all of that. AR is very interesting for immersive 3D communication because alongside communication where a world is simulated and people sit together in it, there is communication where grandma and grandpa are sitting at the kitchen table: AR supports a vision where some people are on VR devices and some on AR devices, so you can either put grandma and grandpa in the chair or go to the favorite family destination.
