# Broadcast And Kickoff Details For Chiefs-Packers Week 13

The Chiefs and Packers clashed at Lambeau Field in Green Bay on Sunday, Dec. 3, with kickoff slated for 8:20 p.m. ET, and the game was broadcast on NBC in the United States and TSN 1/4 in Canada, per Sporting News.

Per usual, that week's edition of "Sunday Night Football" was broadcast on NBC, with Cris Collinsworth returning to the fore as color analyst, joining lead broadcaster Mike Tirico and sideline reporter Melissa Stark. Cord-cutters could find the action on Peacock or Fubo, which offered a free trial. Viewers in Canada could watch on DAZN, which carries every NFL game, or on TSN 1/4.
