# Mastercard's CFO Doubts UPI's Commercial Sustainability

Asked about Mastercard's prospects in emerging markets such as India, Mastercard's CFO Sachin Mehra praised UPI for helping with digitization but voiced reservations about its commercial sustainability, saying it is an "incredibly painful experience" for ecosystem participants. TechCrunch reported the remarks on 2023-10-13.

The premise Mehra attacks is not that UPI works. He concedes the digitization case outright — UPI has helped with digitization — and targets the inference drawn from it: that a payments rail which succeeds at digitizing an emerging market is therefore commercially sustainable. His counter is that the experience is "incredibly painful" for ecosystem participants, that is, for the intermediaries who have to make money on the rail, so success at the digitization objective does not carry over into a viable commercial model for the participants.

Two scope conditions matter for reading the claim. The speaker is the CFO of Mastercard, a competitor in the payments market he is being asked about, and the question was specifically about Mastercard's own prospects in emerging markets such as India — so the remark is a competitor's assessment, offered in that framing, rather than a neutral evaluation.
