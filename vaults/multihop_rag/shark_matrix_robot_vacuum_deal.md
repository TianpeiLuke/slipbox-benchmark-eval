# Shark Matrix Robot Vacuum Black Friday Deal

Mashable's 25 November 2023 Walmart roundup called this model of Shark's 2023 Matrix vacuum "the most packed standalone robot vacuum you'll find for less than $200 this Black Friday".

It maps your home and vacuums and mops the rooms you want it to, offering a more thorough cleaning with Matrix mode.

During dry vacuuming it cleans in a crosshatch pattern to grab any debris missed on the first pass, and during mopping it scrubs the floor from multiple angles to eat away at stubborn stains.
