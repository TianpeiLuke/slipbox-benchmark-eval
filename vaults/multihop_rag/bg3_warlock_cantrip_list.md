# BG3 Warlock Cantrip List

A Baldur's Gate 3 Warlock starts with two cantrips chosen from the Warlock cantrip list, which contains eight options.

Blade Ward makes you take only half the damage from Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing attacks. Bone Chill prevents the target from healing until your next turn, and an undead target additionally receives Disadvantage on Attack Rolls. Eldritch Blast conjures one beam of crackling energy. Friends grants Advantage on Charisma Checks against a non-hostile creature. Mage Hand creates a spectral hand that can manipulate and interact with objects. Minor Illusion creates an illusion that compels nearby creatures to investigate. Poison Spray projects a puff of noxious gas. True Strike grants Advantage on your next Attack Roll.
