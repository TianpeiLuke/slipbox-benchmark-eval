# Creed III (Michael B. Jordan)

Creed III is Michael B. Jordan's directorial debut in the Rocky franchise, in which he also stars alongside Tessa Thompson and Jonathan Majors. Polygon calls it a knockout sports drama and ranked it 42 on its best movies of 2023 list of 2023-10-07; it was available on Prime Video, MGM Plus, or for digital rental/purchase.

Polygon traces the arc: in 2015's Creed, Jordan established his bona fides as a true movie star when he assumed the role of Adonis Creed, the lost son of Apollo Creed and heir apparent to his father's title as heavyweight boxing champion of the world. In Creed III he has taken on the mantle of the franchise and made it his own as both star and director, enriching Adonis' story with a perspective and style that feels at once fresh and familiar.

Toussaint Egan's verdict names electrifying fight sequences and powerful lead performances, combining anime-inspired choreography with a powerful narrative about strained relationships and redemption.
