# Argentina Host Paraguay At The Monumental In 2026 World Cup Qualifying

Argentina continued their start to CONMEBOL 2026 World Cup qualifying with a home date against Paraguay, the first of two qualifiers for the world champions in that international break. The match was played at Estadio Monumental, kicking off on Thursday, October 12 at 8 p.m. local time in Buenos Aires.

Sporting News translated that kickoff across major territories: Thursday, Oct. 12 at 7 p.m. ET and 4 p.m. PT in the USA and Canada; Friday, Oct. 13 at 12 a.m. BST in the UK; Friday, Oct. 13 at 9 a.m. AEST in Australia; Friday, Oct. 13 at 4:30 a.m. IST in India; Friday, Oct. 13 at 7 a.m. HKT in Hong Kong; Friday, Oct. 13 at 7 a.m. MYT in Malaysia; Friday, Oct. 13 at 7 a.m. SGT in Singapore; and Friday, Oct. 13 at 11 a.m. NZST in New Zealand.
