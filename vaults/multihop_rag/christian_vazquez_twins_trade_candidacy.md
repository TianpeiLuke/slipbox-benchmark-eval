# Christian Vazquez Twins Trade Candidacy

Christian Vázquez was ranked No. 23 on Yardbarker's Nov. 13, 2023 list of the top 25 MLB offseason trade candidates as another route Minnesota could consider to clear money: the Twins had signed the veteran catcher to a three-year, $30M free agent deal the previous offseason, and he had a tough first season in Minneapolis, limping to a .223/.280/.318 line over 355 plate appearances, albeit with his typical brand of excellent defense.

Ryan Jeffers easily surpassed him as the No. 1 option on the depth chart, and a $10M per year backup catcher is a luxury for a team trying to trim salary. Minnesota likely would not be able to offload the entire deal, but the club could try to shed around half the money or take on a smaller contract in return. Vázquez's year was not much worse than a season that got Omar Narváez two years and $15M the previous winter, while Tucker Barnhart secured a two-year, $6.5M guarantee despite a much lesser offensive track record.
