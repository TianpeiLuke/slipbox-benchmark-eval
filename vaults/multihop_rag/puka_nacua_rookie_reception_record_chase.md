# Puka Nacua Rookie Reception Record Chase

CBSSports.com's Dec. 19, 2023 Week 16 wide receiver preview reported that Puka Nacua needed 18 more catches to set the rookie record for most catches in a season.

The same run of notes recorded that Deebo Samuel was on pace to become just the fourth player since 1992 to average more than 11 yards per target with at least 60 targets in multiple seasons, the other three being Tyreek Hill, Tyler Lockett and DeSean Jackson.
