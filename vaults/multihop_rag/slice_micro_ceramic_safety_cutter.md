# Slice Micro Ceramic Safety Cutter

Reporter Emilia David admits TikTok made her buy this small safety cutter, but it has been indispensable to someone who may or may not have an online shopping addiction: the Slice Micro Ceramic Blade safety cutter's tiny blade cuts through paper packaging tape cleanly, opens plastic wrapping, and keeps her from going insane opening blister packaging.

Its advantage over the alternative is that it does not damage whatever is inside, which unfortunately happens very often with her regular metal box cutter.

The downside is that it is so small you may lose track of it when not in use, though it does have a built-in magnet and a handy hole for a keyring. And while it does not fully slice through a cardboard box, it will still leave a scratch — which could ultimately damage the ceramic blade if not used properly. She has had hers a few months, so she is not worried about it dulling yet, but it is unclear whether the blade is replaceable.
