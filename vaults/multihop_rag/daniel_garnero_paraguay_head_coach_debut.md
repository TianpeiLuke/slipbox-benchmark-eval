# Daniel Garnero's Debut As Paraguay Head Coach

Daniel Garnero made his debut as Paraguay's new permanent head coach in the Oct. 12, 2023 qualifier against Argentina, having spent the last two years in charge of Paraguayan giants Libertad and taken over from Guillermo Barros Schelotto, who was let go after the September qualifiers just three days shy of his two-year hire anniversary.

The 54-year-old Garnero is an Argentine who made one appearance for the national team as a player and spent most of his career with Argentine club Independiente; he has managed at club level in Paraguay since 2015 with Sol de America, Guarani and Olimpia before Libertad. The Sporting News called it quite the test for his debut — taking on Lionel Messi and the defending World Cup champions, his home nation. Garnero set out his approach: "Vamos a jugar contra una selección que es la mejor del mundo. No solamente por logros, sino por idea colectiva y rendimientos individuales. Mi intención siempre es jugar con la pelota. Debemos ser inteligentes y agresivos en las recuperaciones" — his intention is always to play with the ball, and the team must be smart and aggressive in recoveries, which the blog called a bold claim.

Garnero rang the changes after Paraguay's defeat to Venezuela, making five alterations to that starting lineup. Ivan Ramirez came in at right-back for Robert Rojas for just his fourth international cap, the entire midfield three was swapped out with Mattias Espinoza, Alvaro Campuzano and Richard Sanchez entering, and Adam Bareiro took over at striker from Gabriel Avalos, earning his debut international cap. Forward Miguel Almiron is Paraguay's most prominent goalscoring threat and was in good form with three goals in his past five matches for Newcastle United. Paraguay had questions at goalkeeper, as mainstay Antony Silva had been phased out leaving three inexperienced internationals on the roster; New York Red Bulls starter Carlos Coronel, who earned his first two caps in the September matches, remained in net. Messi's Inter Miami teammate Diego Gomez was named to the Paraguay roster but was forced to withdraw due to injury.
