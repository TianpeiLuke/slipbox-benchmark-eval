# US Home Prices Peaked In June 2022 And Returned To A Record High By July 2023

National U.S. home prices did not peak until June 2022 — even as mortgage rates soared and purchase applications plummeted amid the Federal Reserve's first interest rate hikes — and although prices fell more than 5% from that peak by January 2023, they returned to a record high in July 2023. This is the observation that has so far vindicated the majority of real estate veterans, who despite a widely bearish industry outlook avoided arguing that home prices would drop the way they did during the 2008 crash that kicked off the Global Financial Crisis.

The backdrop, as of the reporting date of 2023-10-06, is that housing market pessimists had been sounding the alarm for years about a pending crash in U.S. residential real estate. Even before the Fed began hiking interest rates to fight inflation in 2022 — pushing mortgage rates to a 23-year high in October 2023 — years of surging home prices had left some experts warning that the housing market was a massive bubble ready to pop.
