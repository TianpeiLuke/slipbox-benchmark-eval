# Matt Gaetz's Twitch Debut Failure

Rep. Matt Gaetz (R-FL) famously struggled with his own Twitch debut. As recounted by TechCrunch on 2023-10-02, he launched his channel the previous year with a 30-minute diatribe about the January 6 Capitol riot, featuring former Trump speechwriter Darren Beattie, and the stream peaked at just six live viewers.

He left his chat room open when he finally went offline, which allowed Twitch users to bombard his channel with ASCII dicks. Between walls of lewd — but incredibly creative — text art, users bashed Gaetz and encouraged other viewers to read about the allegations of sex trafficking against him; the Justice Department ultimately concluded its investigation without charging Gaetz. The account @theserfstv posted on September 30, 2022: "Looks like Matt Gaetz foray into streaming on Twitch is working out."
