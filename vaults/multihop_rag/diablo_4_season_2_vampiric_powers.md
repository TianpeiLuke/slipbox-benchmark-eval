# Diablo 4 Season 2 Vampiric Powers

Season 2 of Diablo 4 adds Vampiric Powers, which allow players to slot five additional abilities into their character to gain new effects.

There are 22 total Vampiric Powers.

Each is class agnostic, meaning no power is unique to any one class. Polygon states this identically in both its Sorcerer build guide of 2023-10-18 and its Barbarian build guide of 2023-10-19.
