# Brandon Pfaadt's Start In World Series Game 3

Brandon Pfaadt started Game 3 of the 2023 World Series for Arizona and finished with four strikeouts and three earned runs on four hits and two walks over 5.1 innings, his night ending at 9:35 p.m. in the top of the sixth when Torey Lovullo went to right-hander Miguel Castro.

Pfaadt began sharply. At 8:08 p.m., in the top of the first, he breezed through the top of the Rangers' order: Marcus Semien popped out to right, Corey Seager grounded out to first base on a first-pitch sinker and Adolis Garcia flew out to center, taking only 10 pitches. After the Rangers' three-run third, he bounced back the way Arizona needed at 8:58 p.m., sending Evan Carter, Mitch Garver and Jonah Heim down in order and striking out Garver for his third strikeout of the day, leaving the fourth inning at Rangers 3, Diamondbacks 0. At 9:19 p.m. he survived a lead-off walk to Nathaniel Lowe unscathed in the fifth, striking out Josh Jung before Leody Tavares lined out to third and Semien grounded out to third; the fifth inning also ended Rangers 3, Diamondbacks 0.

His exit came in the sixth: Corey Seager flew out to center to start the inning, but after Pfaadt issued a free pass to Adolis Garcia, Lovullo brought in Castro with Evan Carter coming to the plate.
