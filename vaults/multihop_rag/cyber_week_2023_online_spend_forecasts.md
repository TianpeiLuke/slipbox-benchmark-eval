# Cyber Week 2023 Online Spend Forecasts

Adobe Analytics predicted $37.2 billion in online spend for the full five days of Cyber Week 2023, up just 5.4% year on year and accounting for 16.8% of all holiday spend, with Black Friday alone seeing $9.6 billion in sales, up roughly the same at 5.7% versus the previous year's published figure of $9.13 billion. Salesforce did not provide forecasts.

Adobe and Salesforce were actively tracking sales for Thanksgiving, Black Friday and the whole of "Cyber Weekend," which not only kicks off holiday shopping but has a particularly strong showing in online sales due to people traveling to be with family. As a bellwether for the rest of the holiday period — traditionally the biggest period for retail sales in the year — the flat sales speak to another tough year for online retailers.
