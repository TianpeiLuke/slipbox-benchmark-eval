# Satechi Foldable Wireless Charging Stand Deal Listings

Both of WIRED's October 10, 2023 Prime Day roundups list Satechi's fold-up wireless charging stand, whose unusual design lets it charge a phone and a wireless earbuds case — if the case supports Qi wireless charging — plus a third device through a USB-C port, in something that fits in your bag.

It has a 10,000-mAh capacity with LEDs to show how much juice is left. The one gripe in both roundups is speed: it offers up to 10 watts of wireless charging power for phones, 7.5 watts for iPhones, 5 watts for earbuds, and 10 watts from the USB-C port.
