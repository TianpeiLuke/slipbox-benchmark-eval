# Concert Tour

## Definition

In the corpus, a concert tour is the live, multi-city, multi-date run of shows an artist performs — such as Taylor Swift's "ongoing 'Eras Tour'" and Beyoncé's "Renaissance" tour — that generates its own ticket revenue and can later be packaged, using footage from those shows, into a companion concert film for theatrical release.

## Context

In the corpus the term names both a live-event undertaking and, increasingly, the commercial engine behind an associated concert film: Taylor Swift's "ongoing 'Eras Tour'" is described as a 151-date stadium tour projected to gross about $1.4 billion, while Beyoncé's "Renaissance" tour is described as a "39-city, 56-show" run that "kicked off in Stockholm, Sweden in May and ended in Kansas City, Missouri in the fall" and made over $500 million while drawing over 2.7 million concertgoers.
Both artists' tours are directly tied to concert films — Swift's "Taylor Swift: The Eras Tour" and Beyoncé's "Renaissance: A Film by Beyoncé" — which theatrical distributors built around footage and momentum from the live tour itself, so the tour functions in the corpus as the originating live event that a concert film later packages for wider audiences.
The corpus also frames touring as increasingly inaccessible: "concert tickets are more expensive than ever," with "inflation, Ticketmaster's monopolistic practices, and increased demand after COVID-related touring delays" cited as causes, and tours are described as never having been accessible to everyone because "geography, economics, health and safety concerns, and physical or mental ability" make stadium and arena concerts infeasible for large parts of a fanbase.

## Key Characteristics

- **Multi-city, multi-date structure** — Swift's Eras Tour spans 151 dates and Beyoncé's Renaissance tour spans 39 cities and 56 shows, both crossing international markets.
- **Repeatable show format** — each Eras Tour concert "spans over 180 minutes, with more than 40 songs from at least nine albums and 16 costume changes," performed against a fixed setlist that Swift memorized by "singing the entire set list out loud" during preparation.
- **Major revenue driver** — the Eras Tour is projected to gross roughly $1.4 billion and the Renaissance tour made over $500 million, figures separate from and larger than the box-office receipts of either artist's related concert film.
- **Rising ticket-access barriers** — average North American Eras Tour resale prices reportedly rose to $3,801, a "2,321% increase" over Swift's 2018 Reputation Stadium Tour average of $157, driven by inflation, resale-market dynamics, and pandemic-era touring delays.
- **Physical toll on performers** — Swift described committing to play a show "whether I'm sick, injured, heartbroken, uncomfortable, or stressed" absent "force majeure," and the tour's Rio de Janeiro leg was disrupted by a heat wave following a fan's death during a performance.

## Background (external)

Outside this corpus, a concert tour is generally defined as a series of concerts by an artist or group of artists in different cities, countries, or locations, often named to distinguish one tour from another and to link a particular tour with a specific album or product.
