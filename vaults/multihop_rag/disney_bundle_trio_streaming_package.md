# Disney Bundle Trio Streaming Package

The Disney Bundle Trio is Polygon's recommendation for anyone who wants to slim down their streaming subscriptions without losing out on great content.

The problem it addresses is the streaming service carousel — juggling options such as Paramount Plus, Netflix, Max, and maybe more. The bundle's value case is that Disney Plus already has an impressive catalog, including plenty of original content and a bucketload of Marvel and Star Wars, and the Trio deal adds Hulu and ESPN on top, providing hours of sitcoms and sports.
