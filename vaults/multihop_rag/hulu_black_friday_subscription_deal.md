# Hulu Black Friday Subscription Deal

Hulu's Black Friday 2023 deal gives one year of the ad-supported tier for only $12, which comes out to just $1 per month — 85 percent off the standard monthly price.

Engadget calls it a great deal for anyone who's been wanting to see what all the hype is about for shows like The Bear and Only Murders in the Building. The deal is available to new subscribers, and to returning subscribers who have not been paying for Hulu for at least one month.
