# Meta's Subscription Age Restriction And Teens

The subscription is only being offered to people aged 18 and up, which raises questions over how it will comply with requirements in the DSA and DMA not to process children's data for ad targeting, TechCrunch noted on 2023-10-30.

Meta's stated position on that gap: "We're continuing to explore how to provide teens with a useful and responsible ad experience given this evolving regulatory landscape."
