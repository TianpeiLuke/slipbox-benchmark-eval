# Global Warming Of 1.2 Degrees And Its Impacts

The world, on average, is about 1.2 degrees Celsius hotter than it was before the industrial revolution — the scientific fact underpinning the COP28 negotiations.

The Verge notes that this might not sound like much, but it is enough to drive more devastating hurricane and wildfire seasons, and to suck drought-vulnerable places dry while displacing other communities faced with rising tides.
