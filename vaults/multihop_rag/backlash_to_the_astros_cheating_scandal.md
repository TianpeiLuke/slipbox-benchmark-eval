# The Backlash: Inflatable Trash Cans, "Take The Trash Out" And LeBron James

Cheating is never taken in a positive light, Essentially Sports observed on 2023-10-22: players once accused of using PEDs face the consequences long after they have retired, and only six years on from 2017 the league was still angry. Back then, social media was a landmine the Astros probably stayed away from. It was the trash cans that did them in — the irony was too brilliant, or hilarious viewed another way, to ignore.

Boos and jabs at away games were the least of it. When the Astros went to play against the Los Angeles Dodgers, the team they had defeated in the 2017 World Series, fans retaliated by throwing inflatable trash cans onto the field; others brought tin trash can caps to bang on when the Astros played against their teams, and the phrase "Take the trash out" took a life of its own. It wasn't just baseball fans who were furious — other sports also considered it a tragedy, because fair play is taken seriously and no one likes being cheated out of an award; even NBA star LeBron James spoke out in criticism against Houston. 2017 was still fresh in most fans' minds: the Astros were then in their seventh straight postseason and might make a repeat trip to the Fall Classic, but the stain of their past mistakes will likely always haunt them.
