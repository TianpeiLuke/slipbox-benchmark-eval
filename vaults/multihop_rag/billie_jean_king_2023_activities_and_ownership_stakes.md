# Billie Jean King's 2023 Activities And Ownership Stakes

Billie Jean King, who turns 80 in November 2023, is still globetrotting in support of more investment and equity in women's sports: in the three months before 2023-10-13 she attended the Women's World Cup in Australia, kicked off the player draft for the new women's professional hockey league in Toronto, and is opening an office in London for a tennis business venture involving the international Billie Jean King Cup.

She is part of ownership groups involved with the Los Angeles Dodgers, the NWSL's Angel City FC and the PWHL hockey league that starts in January 2024. She recently launched the production company "Pressure is a Privilege," a phrase associated with the 39-time Grand Slam winner, and is an executive producer and host of "Groundbreakers," a documentary about female athletes that airs on PBS on Nov. 21. There is also an effort by members of Congress to award King the Congressional Gold Medal, one of the highest U.S. civilian honors, given to individuals whose achievements have a lasting impact in their field.
