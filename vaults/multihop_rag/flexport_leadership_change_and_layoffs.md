# Flexport Leadership Change And Layoffs

Logistics company Flexport is laying off 20% of workers, or about 600 people, topping off a spate of staff upheavals at the company, TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-22.

The following week TechCrunch supplied the leadership context: Flexport founder Ryan Petersen had just taken back the CEO title after his hand-picked successor was pushed out. Petersen's big message has been getting the company's financial house back in order, and he has criticized former CEO Dave Clark of overspending, specifically around hiring and expanding too quickly. Petersen has spent the past month cutting costs, including laying off about 20% of its workers, or about 600 people.
