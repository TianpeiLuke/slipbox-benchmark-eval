# Scope and Update Policy of Polygon's Best New Streaming Series Roundup

Polygon maintains a continuously updated post naming the most recent series on each of the major streaming platforms that its staff liked, plus some other options for readers whose tastes differ from the primary pick. The roundup covers Netflix, Hulu, Prime Video, Max, Disney Plus, Paramount Plus, Apple TV, and Peacock.

The rationale given is that the streaming landscape is vast and hard to keep track of — TV shows pop up and get canceled seemingly overnight, and even a reader actively looking for a new show to sink hours into can find the choice overwhelming.

The post is explicitly a living document rather than a fixed ranking: Polygon says it will keep the post updated. As of the 2023-10-25 version, the latest update had added Castlevania: Nocturne, Gen V, Hijack, and The Continental.
