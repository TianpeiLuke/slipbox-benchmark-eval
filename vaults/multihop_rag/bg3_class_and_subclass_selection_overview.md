# Choosing A Class And Subclass In Baldur's Gate 3

Class selection in Baldur's Gate 3 is one of the most consequential decisions a player makes, and the game forces it at the very start with minimal context to work from. A character's class mostly determines how that character will fight, but with 12 classes and 46 subclasses spread across them, the choice is not straightforward.

Familiar shorthand distinctions — caster versus puncher, melee versus ranged — do not map cleanly onto Baldur's Gate 3 or onto D&D more generally, because the classes overlap heavily, especially as characters level up. That overlap is why no class can simply be declared best or even better than another. The workable selection rule is to focus on what you want to do, or how you want to do it, and choose from there. Polygon's own ranking of the classes orders them subjectively by difficulty to play, placing caster classes toward the harder end on the grounds that spells and spell slots are more to manage than punching things.
