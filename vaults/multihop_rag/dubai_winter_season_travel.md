# Dubai As A Year-Round Destination, Season By Season

Dubai is synonymous with all things luxury paired with a rich history and heritage, and has plenty to offer the traveller looking to immerse themselves in culture while also enjoying the finer things in life. The defining claim of The Independent's guide is that it is a year-round destination whose activities and experiences vary depending on the time of your visit.

The seasonal division runs as follows. Over the winter months Dubai enjoys balmy temperatures and pleasant weather throughout the season, making it the ideal time to get outdoors and explore its more active, adventurous side. In early summer, as temperatures climb, it is the perfect spot for a blend of beach breaks and sightseeing. In the hotter months of June and July, its world-famous waterparks come into their own, while air-conditioned cultural activities and evening tourism — sight-seeing after sunset — are other season-perfect options.

November carries a specific draw: Dubai Design Week takes place around the start of the month, the region's largest creative festival, shining a spotlight on pioneering designers and all things creative via exhibitions, installations, talks, workshops and markets.
