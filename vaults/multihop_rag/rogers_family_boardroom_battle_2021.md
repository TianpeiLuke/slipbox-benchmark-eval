# The Rogers Family Boardroom Battle Of 2021

The bad blood inside the Rogers family spilled into full view in October 2021, when Edward Rogers publicly battled his sisters and his mother for control of the board and won in court. The result was that five Rogers Communications directors were fired and replaced by five allies of Edward Rogers. Soon after, then-CEO Joe Natale was sacked and replaced by Tony Staffieri, the former chief financial officer; Melinda Rogers-Hixon and Martha Rogers had opposed the CEO change.

This was not an isolated episode. The children of late founder Ted Rogers have frequently fought one another — sometimes driving down Rogers' stock price as investors grew worried about the company's governance.
