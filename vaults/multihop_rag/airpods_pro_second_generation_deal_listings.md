# AirPods Pro Second Generation Deal Listings

The second-generation AirPods Pro appear in three 2023 roundups as the best wireless earbuds for iPhone owners, though by The Verge's December listing the deepest Black Friday discounts had disappeared.

The Verge explains why they're the pick: compared with the base AirPods, the Pros offer better sound quality and active noise cancellation plus seamless iOS integration and a MagSafe-compatible charging case. In 2022 Apple launched a version with improved sound quality and better noise cancellation, onboard volume controls, and a new MagSafe charging case that can beep if misplaced thanks to deeper Find My integration, with an extra swappable tip for smaller ears; a year later Apple released the updated model with a USB-C charging case, adding a more robust IP54 rating for water and sweat resistance and, when paired with Apple's Vision Pro headset, support for lossless audio. During Black Friday The Verge saw steep discounts of up to $60 off on both versions, but those deals have since disappeared: the non-USB-C AirPods Pro can be bought for $189.99 in-store at Micro Center, the next best being $229 ($20 off) for the 2022 buds at B&H Photo, while the USB-C version is only $3 off at $237 from Amazon, Walmart, and B&H Photo — though Best Buy's Plus and Total members can save $12.50. WIRED's general Prime Day roundup calls them the best earbuds if you have an iPhone, with the newer USB-C model (8/10, WIRED Recommends) having redesigned audio infrastructure and some of the best noise canceling and microphones it has heard. WIRED's Apple roundup notes the latest version (9/10, WIRED Recommends) was announced only the month before at Apple's hardware event and was already on sale: identical outside to the old pair but internally changed, with slightly improved sound, new Adaptive Audio features, and a new Conversation Awareness mode that turns audio down and lowers background sound so you can hear someone talking to you — the Lightning-port version being out of stock, "but who wants those anyway?"
