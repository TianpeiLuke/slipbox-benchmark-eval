# Crypt of the NecroDancer

Crypt of the NecroDancer is, in Polygon's description, the perfect combination of dungeon crawler and rhythm game, with some sweet tunes to enliven the whole experience. It appears in the outlet's 2023-10-20 list of the 35 best couch co-op games for Nintendo Switch.

Dancing through the game's levels is even more fun with a friend.

The catch Polygon names is that having someone else there does not necessarily make it easier — you both still have to stay on beat.
