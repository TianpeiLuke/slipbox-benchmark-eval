# Foreman's Touchdown And The Bears' Third-Quarter Lead

D'Onta Foreman scored the only offensive touchdown of the Bears-Panthers game at 10:13 p.m. on Nov. 9, 2023, running it in to give Chicago its first lead of the night at 16-10, and the third quarter ended with that score intact.

The scoring drive began with strong starting field position: at 10:10 p.m. Foreman ran 11 yards for a first down that already put the Bears in field goal range, at 10:11 p.m. Chicago was inching toward the goal line at third-and-1 from the five, and at 10:12 p.m. Donte Jackson broke up a well-designed play in the end zone after the Bears picked up the first down, before Foreman finished it. Carolina had punted into that position after Young gained a few yards back but was sacked by Yannick Ngakoue on third down at 10:05 p.m.

Chicago then played conservatively with the lead, coming out with three Foreman runs in four plays at 10:26 p.m. after turnovers had doomed them the week before, and at 10:28 p.m. handing off on third-and-19 after an offensive pass interference call, giving the Panthers solid field position. A less than impressive punt by Trenton Gill at 10:29 p.m. set Carolina up well inside Bears territory, but back-to-back penalties made it first-and-30 at 10:31 p.m., and at 10:32 p.m. the Panthers ran it with Chuba Hubbard on first-and-30, showing little interest in letting Young air it out even while trailing in a lost season. Carolina's earlier possession had shown brief life — a rare first down at 10:18 p.m. on an 11-yard throw to Marshall and another to Adam Thielen at 10:19 p.m. that took them to about midfield — before Young threw behind the line of scrimmage on third-and-15 at 10:21 p.m. and the Panthers punted, giving the Bears poor field position. The game was carried on Prime Video.
