# Why Upgrade A Home Theater

Polygon argues that upgrading home theater hardware and related accessories is worth it because it summons the most compelling parts of the silver screen into your own living room, whatever it is you want to watch.

The grounds are two. First, the pull of staying in keeps growing: the call of the couch gets stronger every year as new gadgets, movies, TV shows, and streaming services launch, so you may find yourself considering how to curate a space for watching coveted new and old releases without leaving the house. Second, the alternative carries costs the home setup avoids — tickets and reserved seating — though the piece concedes you will still have to spend money on snacks, just not nearly as much. The warrant joining them is that a high-quality home cinema setup delivers enough of the theatrical experience that the remaining reasons to go out shrink to the snacks.
