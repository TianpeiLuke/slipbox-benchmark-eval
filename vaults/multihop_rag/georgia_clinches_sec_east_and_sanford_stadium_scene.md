# Georgia Clinches The SEC East And The Sanford Stadium Scene

Georgia clinched the SEC East title at 6:45 p.m. on Nov. 11, 2023 — before its game against Ole Miss — when Missouri defeated Tennessee, giving the Bulldogs the division for the third straight year and the sixth time in seven seasons.

The scene in Athens matched the occasion. At 6:35 p.m. the celebrities had arrived, with Bubba Watson, AJ Minter, Kelee Ringo and Stetson Bennett among those on Georgia's sidelines. At 5:15 p.m. the live blog noted that the weather was anything but ideal for a top-10 matchup — in the 40s with a chance of rainfall throughout the evening — but asked whether that was better for the Dawgs, since the last time ESPN's College GameDay was in town for a crucial SEC matchup Georgia beat Tennessee on a rainy afternoon, and Kirby Smart and his crew seem only to get better in rough weather.
