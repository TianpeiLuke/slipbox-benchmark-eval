# The Reaction To Francesca Scorsese's Slang Video

Francesca Scorsese's TikTok of her father guessing popular slang terms had been viewed one million times by the time The Independent reported on it on 10 October 2023, days after its Thursday posting. Commenters were shocked both to see the legendary director on their screens and that he managed to guess many of the terms correctly.

"Can't believe we live in a world where I get to watch Martin Scorsese become familiar with the term sneaky link. Blessings," read one comment. "I cannot believe I exist in a timeline where I can watch Martin Scorsese film TikToks," said another. A third wrote: "I can't believe I'm watching one of the greatest filmmakers of our lifetime be quizzed by his daughter about teen slang lol," while someone else said: "Gonna describe sneaky links as personal picadillos from now on." It was not Scorsese's first appearance in one of his daughter's TikToks: in April 2021 Francesca went viral asking him to identify "feminine products" such as an eyelash curler, bobby pins, a hair donut and nipple pasties.
