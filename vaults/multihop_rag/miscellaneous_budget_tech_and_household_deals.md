# Miscellaneous Budget Tech And Household Deals

Five odds and ends rounded out the under-$25 list. Govee's 100ft Smart LED Strip Lights were $15, down from $25 — 40 percent off at Amazon; the basic strip comes with 64 preset scenes so you can create different vibes based on emotions, holidays, nature and more, offers various music modes, and connects to your phone via Bluetooth or the Govee Home app.

The Logitech C270 HD webcam was $19, down from $40 — 53 percent off at Amazon, $22 at Target. It shoots in 720p at 30 frames per second, features a 55-degree field of view and a mono microphone: nothing fancy, but it gets the job done.

The LifeStraw personal water filter was $10, down from $20 — 50 percent off at Amazon. Its basic filter removes unwanted viruses, bacteria, and microplastics as you drink, giving you a safe way to hydrate no matter where you are.

Two more: the COSRX Snail Mucin Essence, viral all over skincare TikTok, was discounted to a mere $13.99 — a lil' icky, yes, but its hydrating powers are well known, and a skincare aficionado in your life will thank you. And Hasbro's Jenga: Super Mario Edition was selling for $14.99, $7 off, at Amazon; the twist on the classic block-building game has you scaling the tower as Mario, Luigi, Peach, and Toad to defeat Bowser.
