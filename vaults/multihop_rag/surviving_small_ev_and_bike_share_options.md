# Small EV And Bike-Share Options That Survived 2023

Although 2023 was a bummer year for fans of small EVs, all is not lost: city bike-sharing programs are booming and increasingly electric. D.C.'s Capitol Bikeshare notched a record 428,000 rides in the month of May, and that same month New York's Citi Bike recorded 867,838 rides in a single week, with the program continuing to expand.

Beyond e-bikes, Arcimoto lives on as the rare three-wheeled EV holdout in the U.S. The Fiat 500e is coming to North America in 2024 in limited quantities, several years after its debut in the EU. A startup called Telo Trucks is attempting to prove that Americans will buy small trucks again, and is developing a pickup with the footprint of a two-door Mini Cooper. In addition, while GM said it would kill the Chevy Bolt and the larger Bolt EUV, the automaker now says it will keep the latter Bolt around.
