# The All Blacks' Final Moments In The 2023 World Cup Final

New Zealand's last chance in the 2023 Rugby World Cup final died in the 80th minute when Ardie Savea forced a ball out wide just as the men in black saw space and looked to open up the Springboks — a killer blow, in The Roar's phrase.

The wider failing was handling. The All Blacks had their chances, but their usual safe handling — a feature throughout the World Cup after their first-up loss to France — evaded them against the Springboks' blitz defence in the wet conditions, and all night their timing was off.

The end came at a set piece. Wayne Barnes, who was booed relentlessly, called a scrum reset as the seconds ticked by; moments later New Zealand hearts sank as the Springboks won the scrum and then hung onto the ball.
