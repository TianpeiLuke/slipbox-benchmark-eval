# The Guardians' Manager Search After Terry Francona's Resignation

Terry Francona announced his resignation following an 11-year tenure with Cleveland that included a 921-757 record (.549), six playoff trips and the 2016 AL pennant — a record that made him the winningest manager in franchise history, Essentially Sports reported on 2023-10-30. He had managed the Guardians until the conclusion of the 2023 season, but owing to health concerns he decided to put his wellbeing first.

Since Cleveland usually has one of the lowest payrolls in baseball, the team's lineup won't be receiving a lot of outside reinforcement. On the managerial hire itself, the Guardians are clearly not keen on getting someone from within MLB and are open to outside hires as well, as long as the new candidate can follow Terry's principles. One applicant slated for an interview was Craig Albernaz, the catching and bullpen coach for the San Francisco Giants, according to Paul Hoynes of Cleveland.com; despite lacking Major League managerial experience, the 40-year-old has led two championship-winning Tampa Bay Rays teams. Another likely candidate could be Carlos Mendoza, bench coach for the New York Yankees, who also lacks MLB managerial experience but at 43 is familiar with the Yankees system, suiting him well for Cleveland.
