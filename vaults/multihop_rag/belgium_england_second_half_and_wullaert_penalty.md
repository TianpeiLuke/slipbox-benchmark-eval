# Belgium-England: The Second Half And Wullaert's Penalty

Belgium won the game on 31 October 2023 through Tessa Wullaert's 85th-minute penalty, awarded by referee Esther Staubli after Georgia Stanway handballed Yana Daniels' 83rd-minute effort; Wullaert fired it into the bottom-left corner despite Mary Earps diving the correct way, Sport Grill reported.

England had the better of the half without converting. On the restart Fran Kirby quickly drew a foul from Kassandra Missipo, but Alessia Russo fumbled control from Chloe Kelly's free-kick and her shot was blocked; Nicky Evrard then saved Kirby's shot from Lucy Bronze's pullback pass in the 52nd minute as England continued to test the Red Flames' defence. Just past the hour mark England were given an almighty scare as Wullaert pounced on a through ball and side-stepped both Jess Carter and Millie Bright, only for Earps to block her shot with a stunning save.

Belgium were forced to defend for much of the last half-hour as Russo and Stanway saw shots denied, while substitute Rachel Daly nodded wide in the 80th minute from Keira Walsh's deep corner towards the left post. Sarina Wiegman's Lionesses were eventually made to pay for their wasted chances.
