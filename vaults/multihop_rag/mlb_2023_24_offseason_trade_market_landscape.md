# MLB 2023-24 Offseason Trade Market Landscape

As of Yardbarker's Nov. 13, 2023 rundown of the top 25 MLB offseason trade candidates, trade season had not yet arrived: the offseason had gotten rolling, but the Tigers' acquisition of Mark Canha from Milwaukee was the biggest swap of the first couple of weeks, and the GM Meetings simply served to set the stage for future transactions.

Notable trades were surely coming, but unlike at the summer deadline, projecting which players would change hands was difficult at that stage of the offseason. Aside from the A's, no team was clearly in rebuild mode; clubs like the Rockies, Royals, White Sox, Nationals and Angels faced uphill paths to contention, yet none had indicated it was prepared to punt on the 2024 season entirely. The Padres, Twins, Rays, Brewers and Guardians were all aiming to contend but had questions about their payroll — standard operating procedure for Tampa Bay, Milwaukee and Cleveland, while San Diego and, to a lesser extent, Minnesota had shown a willingness to spend in recent years but were scaling back, in both cases likely tied to their uncertain local television rights contracts.

That informed a trade landscape headlined by a few stars deep into their arbitration windows. Without many clear rebuilders, it was possible the winter would see more swaps of big leaguers at positions of need between teams that expected to compete.
