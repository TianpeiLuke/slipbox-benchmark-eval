# Canelo-Charlo: Early Prelim Results And Main Card Start Times

At 7:42 p.m. ET on the night of Canelo-Charlo, Sporting News reported that the early preliminary fights were all wrapped up and the pay-per-view portion was moments away, in its live coverage of 2023-09-30. Early on there had been knockouts from Frank Sanchez and Oleksandr Gvozdyk, while Terrell Gausha won a majority decision; readers were pointed to the early results further down the page.

The main card start times were listed as 8 p.m. ET, 5 p.m. PT, 1 a.m. BST, 10 a.m. BST, 1 a.m. BST and 10 a.m. AEST.
