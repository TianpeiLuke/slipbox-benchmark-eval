# Astarion, Baldur's Gate 3 Vampire Companion

Astarion is a haughty elf in Baldur's Gate 3 whom you can either play as or recruit to your party very early on — and he is a vampire, with dealing with his vampirism forming a huge part of the game, should that be something you want to do.

Polygon's framing is that if Baldur's Gate 3 was your introduction to the Forgotten Realms of Dungeons & Dragons, you might be shocked to find out about a lot of stuff tucked away in the world's most famous tabletop role-playing game — and the great thing about Baldur's Gate 3 is how it centers all that weird stuff.
