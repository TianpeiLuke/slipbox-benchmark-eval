# Anker 511 Nano 3 Charger

Anker's 511 (Nano 3) charging brick was down to $20, from $23 — described as a mild $4 discount, but still a welcome price for a worthwhile smartwatch accessory.

The reason Engadget recommends it: new Apple Watches do not come with power adapters, so if you are looking for one that can deliver a quick charge, this is the pick.

At Black Friday The Verge listed the Anker 511 Charger (30W) at $16, down from $23 — 30 percent off — noting that Anker's 30W 511 Charger juices phones quickly and is small enough to carry around in a purse.
