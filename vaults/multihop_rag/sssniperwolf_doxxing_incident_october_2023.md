# The SSSniperwolf Doxxing Incident, October 2023

On Oct. 13, Alia Shelesh posted an Instagram story with the text, "Should I go visit @Jacksfilms? He lives 5 mins away from my shoot," then added more posts throughout the night, including a photo of the outside of Jack Douglass' house captioned "Let's talk like adults." Polygon reported this on 2023-10-25.

Douglass was livestreaming on Twitch when Shelesh posted those images. He disconnected and posted a tweet the same day: "Sssniperwolf just doxxed me on her IG. Creepy, gross, violating," alongside a screenshot of her story. "What you do is disgusting. You steal content AND stalk youtubers. @YouTube demonetize this dangerous 'creator' or just get her off your platform. She posted an IG story right outside our home and deleted it."

As Douglass' fans waited for YouTube to respond, they scoured Shelesh's content looking for potential additional rule violations. Shelesh posted updates making light of the situation, including a since-deleted photo of her and her sister captioned "We show up to ur house wyd??" and a post over Douglass' tweet captioned: "This creep has been harassing me for months then plays victim saying I threatened him when I just wanted to talk to him. I have no ill intentions. It's so sad when people have to constantly create drama to pay their bills."

The incident inspired a series of videos and posts from influencers and commentary channels, with public opinion largely in favor of Douglass, and also inspired a fair few shitposts and memes — some aimed at YouTube, encouraging the platform to respond.
