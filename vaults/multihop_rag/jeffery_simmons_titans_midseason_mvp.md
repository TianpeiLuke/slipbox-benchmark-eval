# Jeffery Simmons Titans Midseason MVP

Yardbarker's Nov. 9, 2023 AFC midseason team MVP list named defensive tackle Jeffery Simmons the Tennessee Titans' MVP: there had not been too many bright spots for the Titans, but Simmons continued to be a wrecking ball on the defensive line.

The grounds are his team-relative production and the counterfactual attached to it: he led the team in tackles for loss with six and ranked second in sacks with 4.5, and if not for Simmons, Tennessee's defense would rank a heck of a lot lower than 20th in yards allowed per game at 337.8. The entry was written by Michael Gallagher.
