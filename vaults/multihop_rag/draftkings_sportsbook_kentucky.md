# DraftKings Sportsbook In Kentucky

DraftKings is a sportsbook with roots in daily fantasy sports whose defining feature among Kentucky books is a rewards program aimed especially at daily fantasy players, as described by CBS Sports on 13 October 2023.

The rewards mechanism is the discriminating detail: rewards points are earned for most wagers and can be redeemed for free bets, merchandise, or daily fantasy entries in any sport DraftKings offers. Beyond that it offers a variety of betting markets — thousands of ways to bet on sports, including pick'em and props pools — with a wagering menu that includes live and in-game bets, cross-sports parlays and moneylines, and it is known for frequent and generous profit boosts that customers can use on specific bet types, including same game parlays. At the time of writing, new customers could bet $5 and receive $200 in free bets with the current promotion.

The report frames the appeal as giving bettors more ways to "have skin in the game" and get closer to the games they love.
