# Max Kepler And Jorge Polanco Twins Trade Candidacy

Max Kepler and Jorge Polanco were ranked No. 13 and No. 14 on Yardbarker's Nov. 13, 2023 list of the top 25 MLB offseason trade candidates because Minnesota was cutting payroll: president of baseball operations Derek Falvey had confirmed the team was paring back, The Athletic's Dan Hayes reported the organization could end in the $125M-140M range, and at that moment the Twins had around $120M in projected spending.

Trading either Kepler or Polanco would clear room. Kepler was making $10M and would be a free agent after the following season; Polanco was playing on a $10.5M deal whose contract carried a $12.5M team option for 2025 with a $750K buyout. They were each good players: Kepler was a plus defender in right field who hit .260/.332/.484 with 24 homers that year, while Polanco was limited to 80 games but ran a .255/.335/.454 showing, ranked among the sport's better offensive second basemen and saw some third base action for Minnesota late in the year.

The Twins had Matt Wallner and Trevor Larnach as left-handed hitting corner outfield options and were deeper on the infield, where Willi Castro and Kyle Farmer — another trade candidate — were possibilities at second and third base behind starters Edouard Julien and Royce Lewis. Top prospect Brooks Lee, the No. 8 overall pick in 2022, was not far from MLB readiness. The Twins could look to move Kepler or Polanco for rotation depth or to bring in a righty bat to balance the lineup.
