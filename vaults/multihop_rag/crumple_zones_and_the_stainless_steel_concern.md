# Crumple Zones And The Stainless Steel Concern

A crumple zone is the area of a vehicle designed to crush or crumple upon impact — often located at the front — which absorbs some of the impact of a crash and so protects the driver and other occupants.

The mechanism is energy management: by absorbing and dissipating energy, crumple zones help to prevent or reduce injuries to a vehicle's occupants during a collision. The boundary case that has drawn scrutiny is stiffness: a stiffer vehicle, perhaps one made from stainless steel, could complicate that process. The Verge notes that the crash test videos Tesla featured during its delivery event have been the focus of the most questions, with many centred on the truck's crumple zones, or lack thereof.
