# Suzie Taylor Converts Her Winnings Into A Creator Career

Suzie Taylor, one of the two contestants who won $185,000 for living in a barren room with a stranger for 100 days, is using the exposure to jumpstart her career as a content creator. With 78 million YouTube viewers and counting having watched her get pushed to her psychological limits for cash, she is reinvesting her winnings into becoming a creator herself.

In tandem with the release of MrBeast's video about her, Taylor posted a video called "I Spent $185,000 From MrBeast," paying homage to MrBeast's old-school guerilla philanthropy videos as she drives around Los Angeles doing good deeds for strangers: buying a child a hoverboard at Target, giving money to homeless people, and paying for everyone's ice cream at a very crowded dessert shop. She is quite literally using the money she won from MrBeast to jumpstart her online career, emulating the same playbook that made MrBeast who he is.

The subscriber numbers show the effect. According to the Wayback Machine she had 300 subscribers in April; on Sunday 17 December, the day after she and MrBeast posted their videos, she had around 12,000; and by Wednesday 20 December she had broken 100,000. If Taylor can keep her subscribers engaged beyond her 15 minutes of fame, she will not just have won $185,000 from MrBeast — she will have made a down payment on a whole new career.
