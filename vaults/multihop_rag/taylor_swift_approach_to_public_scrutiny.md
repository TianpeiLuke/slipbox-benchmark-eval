# Taylor Swift's Stated Approach To Public Scrutiny

Taylor Swift told Time in December 2023 that she has nothing to hide in her relationship with Travis Kelce, and defined what she means by a public relationship: "When you say a relationship is public, that means I'm going to see him do what he loves, we're showing up for each other, other people are there and we don't care. The opposite of that is you have to go to an extreme amount of effort to make sure no one knows that you're seeing someone. And we're just proud of each other." Although her love life makes headlines with every Kansas City Chiefs game she attends, the "Blank Space" singer said she is focused on being there for her partner rather than on concealing the relationship.

On scrutiny more broadly, the "Anti-Hero" singer said she tries not to let the paparazzi get to her even though so many of her outings with friends become headlines: "Over the years, I've learned I don't have the time or bandwidth to get pressed about things that don't matter. Yes, if I go out to dinner, there's going to be a whole chaotic situation outside the restaurant. But I still want to go to dinner with my friends." She described the change in her perspective over time: "Life is short. Have adventures. Me locking myself away in my house for a lot of years — I'll never get that time back. I'm more trusting now than I was six years ago."
