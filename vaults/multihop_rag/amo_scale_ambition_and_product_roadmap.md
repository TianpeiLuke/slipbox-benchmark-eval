# Amo's Scale Ambition And Product Roadmap

Amo's near-term goal is a hit, with monetization deferred: maybe Amo will end up fostering a creator economy with exclusive content that can really make your profile stand out, and maybe the company will add some premium features over time, but for now Amo wants to find a hit.

Antoine Martin set the bar in daily active users, revising up from his own Zenly experience: "We're prioritizing scale because my goal is to create an indestructible company. And it's Zenly's founder who is saying that! I used to think that 18 million [daily active users] would be enough to make a company indestructible. But I was wrong. I think you need 100 million [daily active users]." On shipping early, he said: "We're deliberately shipping something eight or nine months after the launch of the company because we swore to ourselves that it wouldn't take us a year to get started, that we'd learn more by building in public."

TechCrunch's assessment is that ID is an opinionated take on social apps and whether it will work is open, but given the past experience of the team and Amo's deep pockets, if there is one team that has a shot at trying something radically new in the space, it is Amo. And while ID is Amo's first idea, the company most likely has other ideas in the consumer social space — Amo did not name its app "Amo," after all — so it will be interesting to follow the launch of this new app, but also Amo's story as a company.
