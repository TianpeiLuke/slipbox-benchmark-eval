# Mary Jane's Mission Pacing And The Verdict On Spider-Man 2

Essentially Sports argued on 1 November 2023 that the best thing about Mary Jane's quests in Marvel's Spider-Man 2 is that they never try to overpower the ongoing story: the claim is that restraint improves them, and the warrant is that keeping her sequences within limits lets each character shine without her missions overlapping Peter's or Miles' stories.

The grounds are about proportion. The game's narrative remains intact no matter what, and there never occurs a feeling of the developers trying too hard to make her missions stretch beyond their boundaries; although they are designed well this time, the article commends Insomniac's team for keeping a limit on not making MJ's gameplay moments seem forced or going beyond a certain point. At times it may feel like her playable sequences should have been extended, but the article treats the decision not to as correct — sometimes less is more and fine. Despite Spider-Man 2 being more of Peter's story in its entirety, MJ's role has been given equal significance, with visible growth in her arc especially considering her profession as a journalist; she is Peter's love interest, but that's not everything she's there for, and players can easily see it happening throughout the game's duration.

The verdict concedes a limit on the claim before stating it. It's obvious to expect that a lot of fans who didn't like MJ's missions in the first installment won't like her this time either, but considering how she was before and how she is in Spider-Man 2, the overall change is quite praiseworthy. Her missions now have actual meaning attached to them rather than acting like fillers; players who want a few interesting moments aside from the main quests featuring Miles and Peter can get a sense of finishing well-designed stealth quests, and Mary's gameplay missions end up increasing the overall quality of Spider-Man 2 — so it is great to see Insomniac improving on this aspect instead of removing it completely.
