# Line Shopping In Sports Betting

Line shopping is the practice of checking the lines and odds for a prospective bet at multiple online sports betting sites, and it is the key that unlocks the best odds and lines at top betting sites. The process is that simple, and the result is quite often better odds and improved lines.

Its scope is any market, not just one. Shopping around means checking the posted lines and odds for the same bets at different sportsbooks, and it can be done for moneylines, point spreads, totals bets, prop bets and more; finding the best line or better odds on any bet is like finding money.

In practice a bettor shops using multiple sportsbook apps on a phone or via live odds feeds, picking a side and then using drop-down menus and tabs to find the odds and lines for that bet — and finally betting when an opportunity appears at better odds or an improved line compared with the competition.
