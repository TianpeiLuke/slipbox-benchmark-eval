# Hades

In Hades you play as Zagreus, prince of the underworld, fighting your way out of hell and onto the surface in what Polygon calls a truly excellent roguelite.

It has a fantastic, full cast of gods who give you various powers to mix and match during each run, and those deities are just one set among many lovable, complicated characters you meet as Zagreus' story unfolds. Polygon's central observation about its design is that each run where you die does not feel like a loss, because the story is simply that propulsive. With so many weapon and buff combinations to try, you will be eager to hop into your next playthrough, which is what makes the Switch's portability crucial for this game. The verdict: Hades is for the Greek mythology nerds, but it is also for everyone.
