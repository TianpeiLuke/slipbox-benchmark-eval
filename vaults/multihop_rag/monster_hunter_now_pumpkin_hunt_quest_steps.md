# Monster Hunter Now Pumpkin Hunt Quest Steps

The Pumpkin Hunt quest steps are found in the Events part of the Special quests screen in Monster Hunter Now, and all steps expire at the end of the Halloween event at the end of Oct. 31, as Polygon detailed on 2023-10-25.

Step 1 of 3 comprises: obtain Lollipop Candy from gathering points (10), for 1 Pumpkin Ticket and 100 HR Points; slay large monsters (15), for 1 Pumpkin Ticket and 100 HR Points; and slay monsters (30), for 1 Pumpkin Ticket and 100 HR Points.

Step 2 of 3 comprises: obtain Lollipop Candy from gathering points (20), for 5 Pumpkin Tickets and 150 HR Points; slay large monsters (30), for 5 Pumpkin Tickets and 150 HR Points; and slay monsters (50), for 5 Pumpkin Tickets and 150 HR Points.

Step 3 of 3 comprises: obtain Lollipop Candy from gathering points (50), for 1,000 Zenny and 200 HR Points; slay large monsters (60), for 1,000 Zenny and 200 HR Points; and slay monsters (80), for 1,000 Zenny and 200 HR Points. Polygon credits FranklyNinja on Reddit for helping fill in the gaps, and notes that as with the story items acquired from the wild, Lollipop Candy is obtained by interacting with any gathering point.
