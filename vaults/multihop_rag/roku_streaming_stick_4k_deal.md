# Roku Streaming Stick 4K Deal

Roku's Black Friday 2023 sale knocked the price of its Streaming Stick 4K down to $30 at Amazon and its own online store — a 40 percent discount.

The Streaming Stick 4K is one of the best streaming devices available right now thanks to its support for all the major HDR formats, Dolby Vision and AirPlay 2, among other features. Roku's TV interface remains super user-friendly, and this dongle also supports private listening with headphones paired to a smartphone via Roku's companion app.
