# Chants of Sennaar

Chants of Sennaar, developed by Rundisc for Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 4, Windows PC, and Xbox One, is ranked 20 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31. In it you climb a kind of Tower of Babel, and your job is to decipher the distinct language each group of people speaks, eventually aiming to translate fluidly from one language to another.

You do so by matching pictographic symbols — the characters for each language — to images of the nouns, actions, and concepts in a large dictionary-style book.

Critic N. Clark says it sounds complicated but is wonderfully fun because the mechanics are so simple: you explore the isometric world, rendered in a gorgeous cel-shaded style, witnessing interactions between cultures and attempting to puzzle out the meaning of their written words, with the game parceling out words to assign meaning to in little packets so as not to overwhelm you. By the end you will have translated numerous languages and scaled your way to the top of the tower — and perhaps even changed the tower itself.
