# The Second Half Of Canada 2-3 Jamaica

Jamaica turned a 3-1 aggregate deficit into a 4-4 tie and away-goals progression during a wild second half at BMO Field on Nov. 21, 2023. They started the half with constant pressure and came ridiculously close to an equaliser at 48 minutes, when Bailey's shot hit the underside of the crossbar, bounced on the line and was cleared, with Jamaica appealing in vain that the ball had crossed the line. By 52 minutes Bailey looked dangerous and Canada were riding a storm; by 57 minutes the match had settled, Jamaica seeming deflated by the missed chance.

Then came three minutes that changed the tie. At 62 minutes Lowe took the ball off a Canadian defender and fed Nicholson in the box, and the big striker smashed it home for 1-1 on the night and 3-2 on aggregate. At 66 minutes a counter-attack sprung Gray clear down the left and his ball was prodded in by Nicholson for 1-2 and 3-3 aggregate, putting extra time on the table. But at 69 minutes Canada struck back: Davies floated in a deft cross and Kone redirected it over Blake for 2-2 on the night and a 4-3 aggregate lead. Canada made a triple change at 74 minutes, bringing on Junior Hoilett, Jonathan Osorio and Mark Kaye, with Cyle Larin and Tajon Buchanan among those replaced.

The decisive moment came at 78 minutes, when Eustaquio handled in the box and De Cordova-Reid converted the penalty for 2-3 on the night and 4-4 on aggregate, putting Jamaica through on away goals. At 84 minutes Gray had a brain-fade, collecting two yellow cards in a minute — the second for booting the ball away for no reason — and was dismissed. Canada dominated with the man advantage: at 88 minutes David got a free header in the six-yard box but ballooned it over the bar. Nine minutes of added time were announced at 90 minutes, and at 95 minutes Canada were doing all the attacking against a well-organised Jamaican defence, with Hoilett blasting a shot well over from outside the box. Full time finished Canada 2-3 Jamaica, 4-4 on aggregate — Jamaica's first-ever win on Canadian soil sending them to the Nations League semifinal and Copa America 2024, an extraordinary result given Canada had been cruising with a 3-1 aggregate lead until the 62nd minute.
