# Bournemouth's Comeback Win At Nottingham Forest With A Solanke Hat-Trick

Dominic Solanke scored a hat-trick to earn Bournemouth a 3-2 win at Nottingham Forest on 23 December 2023, ruining Nuno Espirito Santo's first game in charge at the City Ground, as reported by The Roar that day.

Forest scored through Anthony Elanga and Chris Wood and had defender Willy Boly controversially sent off in the first half. They were level until Solanke headed home in the fifth minute of stoppage time.
