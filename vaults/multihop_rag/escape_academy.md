# Escape Academy

Escape Academy delivers an escape room experience in video game form, and Polygon says it absolutely scratches that itch. It appears in the outlet's 2023-10-20 list of the 35 best couch co-op games for Nintendo Switch.

Up to two couch co-op players can attend this school for puzzle enthusiasts, finding solutions and cracking codes to avoid legitimately perilous fates — like a tower flooding with water as you climb.

Hints, and red herrings, are everywhere.
