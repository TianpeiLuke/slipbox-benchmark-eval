# Past Lives (Celine Song)

Past Lives is Celine Song's radiant romantic drama starring Greta Lee, Teo Yoo, and John Magaro, ranked 2 on Polygon's best movies of 2023 list of 2023-10-07 and available for digital rental/purchase. It is about a love that might have been, but also about a person who might have been — or two people, or three — and about the complicated relationships people have with themselves, especially the various versions of themselves that exist in the past, the present, the future, and on the paths not taken.

It unfolds in three time frames. In Seoul, around the turn of the millennium, Na Young and Hae Sung are 12-year-old classmates and best friends who might be starting to develop deeper feelings for each other, but Na Young's family is planning to emigrate to Canada. Twelve years later, Na Young has changed her name to Nora Moon, played by Greta Lee, and is a young writer living in New York; she falls into an intense Skype affair with Hae Sung, played by Teo Yoo, but plans to visit each other never come together. In another 12 years, Nora is still in New York with her husband Arthur, played by John Magaro, and Hae Sung comes to visit, threatening to destabilize all their lives.

Critic OW calls it a film of almost impossible delicacy: Song and her brilliant trio of actors hold so much within its patient frame — unspoken feelings, unrealized possibilities, layers of cultural subtext — that you find yourself holding your breath while watching, even before its heart-stopping final scene, and the 35mm cinematography by director of photography Shabier Kirchner of Small Axe absolutely glows. The film has a lot to say about the immigrant experience and about the uncertain spaces between childhood and adulthood, and a philosophical, maybe even spiritual dimension, but all of this emerges quite naturally from a simple, beautifully observed, achingly romantic story about the sweet pain of the one that got away.
