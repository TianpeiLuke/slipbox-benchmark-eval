# Polygon's Selection Process for the Top 50 Games of 2023

Polygon assembled its top 50 games of 2023 by having the staff vote, champion, and debate over the course of several weeks before ultimately throwing up its hands and marveling at the resulting list of mammoths, curiosities, puzzle boxes, and black holes.

The eligibility rule was broad: any video games released in 2023, that received substantial updates in 2023, or that achieved renewed cultural relevance in 2023 were eligible for the list.

A cutoff date bounded consideration. The previous year the cutoff had been Nov. 30 — which, Polygon notes, is why a certain Firaxis Games title sits fairly high on that list — and this year the cutoff was the same. The stated consequence is deferral rather than exclusion: should the staff be thoroughly enamored with Warhammer 40,000: Rogue Trader or Avatar: Frontiers of Pandora, Polygon said it would make sure they were considered for the next year's top 50. The article carrying this description was published 2023-10-31.
