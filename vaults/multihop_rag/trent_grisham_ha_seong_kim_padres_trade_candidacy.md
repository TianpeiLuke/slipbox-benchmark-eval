# Trent Grisham And Ha-Seong Kim Padres Trade Candidacy

Trent Grisham and Ha-Seong Kim were ranked No. 8 and No. 9 on Yardbarker's Nov. 13, 2023 list of the top 25 MLB offseason trade candidates as the Padres' alternative payroll lever: if San Diego held Juan Soto, dealing Grisham and/or Kim could be another means of trimming payroll, because while neither would make anywhere near Soto money in 2024, they were each on mid-level salaries of note.

Grisham was projected for a $4.9M salary in his second-to-last season of arbitration; Kim was slated for an $8M salary and would be due a $2M buyout on a 2025 mutual option at season's end, becoming a free agent the following winter. Grisham had the extra year of control but was the worse player: he drew plenty of walks but had hit below the Mendoza line in two straight seasons, coming off a .198/.315/.352 line over 555 plate appearances, with the appeal on the other side of the ball. He was an above-average defensive center fielder, which kept him a low-end regular even if he was best suited for the bottom of a lineup, and San Diego could kick Fernando Tatis Jr. over to center field if they dealt him, freeing right field for a bigger offensive threat.

It was a similar story with Kim: dealing him would free Jake Cronenworth to move back to second base, opening first base for a better hitter, though it would be tough to replace Kim's overall production since he was a plus defender at multiple infield spots. He hit .260/.351/.398 with 17 home runs and stole 38 bases in 152 games, so trading Kim would not be as damaging to the lineup as a Soto deal but would be tougher than parting with Grisham.
