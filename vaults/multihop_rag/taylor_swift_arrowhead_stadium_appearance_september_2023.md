# Taylor Swift's Arrowhead Stadium Appearance, September 2023

Two days after Travis Kelce's 22 September 2023 charity event, the "All Too Well" singer was spotted cheering for the Kansas City Chiefs tight end from the box seats at Arrowhead Stadium in Kansas City, Missouri, sitting next to Kelce's mother Donna and appearing to yell "let's f***ing go" in response to Kelce's third-quarter touchdown reception.

The Chiefs beat the Chicago Bears 41-10. After the game, Kelce was seen leaving the stadium in a blue and white splatter-paint denim suit, and eagle-eyed fans were quick to believe the outfit paid homage to Swift's forthcoming album 1989 (Taylor's Version), due for re-release on 27 October. According to photos and videos posted online, the two escaped the crowd at Arrowhead in Kelce's convertible, and the football star reportedly rented out Prime Social Rooftop in Kansas City for a post-game celebration with his family, his teammates and Swift.

A witness described the party to Entertainment Tonight: "Travis bought out the restaurant for his family and team. Taylor arrived, wearing a denim dress, and was seen snacking, having some cocktails and dancing alongside Travis. The two were very affectionate with one another but kept things fun and lighthearted. Travis' teammates also showed up to the after-party, as well as his mom, Donna Kelce. The party lasted until 2 am."
