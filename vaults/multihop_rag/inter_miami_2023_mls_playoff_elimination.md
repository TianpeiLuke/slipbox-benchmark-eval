# Inter Miami Eliminated From The 2023 MLS Playoff Race With Two Games Left

Inter Miami were officially eliminated from the MLS playoff race on Oct. 7, 2023 with two matches remaining, when Supporters' Shield winners FC Cincinnati dealt the deathblow with a 1-0 victory at DRV PNK Stadium; that defeat, coupled with CF Montreal's 4-0 victory over Portland, was enough to see them eliminated from contention. Inter Miami were in simply too big a hole in the race, one which even Lionel Messi could not drag them out of: Messi joined the club with it bottom of the Eastern Conference, the gap was too big to overcome, and while his injury certainly played a role, there was just not enough room for error.

The elimination scenario had been set out before kickoff. If Inter Miami did not win, two of three possible outcomes would trigger elimination: CF Montreal beating the Portland Timbers; NYCFC winning versus D.C. United; and Chicago Fire picking up points against Charlotte FC. All were conceivable, so Inter Miami wanted to keep matters in their own hands a little longer — they would eventually need other results to climb the table, but survival that night was solely up to them.

Three days earlier, the 4-1 defeat at the Chicago Fire had already left their hopes on life support. That result boosted Chicago's postseason hopes significantly, moving them into a playoff position in the Eastern Conference standings, while Inter Miami needed to win out in their final three games and get outside help. The club was winless in four games with Messi out injured, no longer controlled its own destiny, and had seen its chances of a postseason berth nearly evaporate.
