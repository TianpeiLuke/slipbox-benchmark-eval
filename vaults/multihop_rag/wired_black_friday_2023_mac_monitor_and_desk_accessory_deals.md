# WIRED Black Friday 2023 Mac Monitor And Desk Accessory Deals

WIRED's Apple Black Friday guide of 25 November 2023 listed four discounted desk items: an adjustable laptop stand, an affordable full-size monitor, a microfiber desk mat and a portable second screen.

The laptop stand flexes up to 22 inches high, so you can adjust it depending on whether you are working with an external monitor or solely off a MacBook, and it is super compact and portable. WIRED's stated complaint is that although the stand's angle adjusts up to 45 degrees, it is too wobbly to type on if you are using the laptop's own keyboard.

The monitor is described as both affordable and well built; WIRED reviews editor Julian Chokkattu has used it for years and it has served him well, and while its price often dips under $100, this sale price is a few dollars lower than WIRED has tracked. The microfiber mat keeps a desk looking nice and clean and doubles as a giant mousepad; it only comes in black, but WIRED calls it a great minimalist and affordable option. The portable monitor is Chokkattu's favourite of its kind and a great second screen that takes up no desk space, but it requires sticking adhesive magnets onto your laptop, is best suited to 13- to 14-inch laptops, and is best for apps like Slack rather than anything needing crisp colours; it drops to this price often but WIRED still calls it a good deal.
