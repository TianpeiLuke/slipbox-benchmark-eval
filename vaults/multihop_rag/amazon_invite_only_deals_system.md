# The Amazon Invite-Only Deals System

Invite-only deals are a system Amazon introduced during Prime Day in summer 2023 to make it easier for Prime members to access deals expected to sell out quickly, and the company brought it back for Prime Big Deal Days.

Its scope is limited but broad within that limit: the feature is only available on select products, though it has spread across a variety of categories and price points. The mechanics are simple — if a product is part of the system, a "Request Invite" button appears on the right-hand side, and clicking it gives you a chance to buy at the sale price. The important caveat is that there's no guarantee you'll receive the invite. WIRED points readers to its story on How to Shop Like a Pro During Amazon Prime Day for more on how the program works.
