# Cutting Wear Mass May Raise Toxicity

Emissions Analytics' Nick Molden challenges the assumption that reducing tire wear mass is straightforwardly progress: "The reason I'm worried about that is I'm sure you can reduce that wear mass, but if you do that by making the compound more toxic, you could actually make the environmental impact worse."

The premise under attack is that mass of debris is the right measure of environmental harm; the analogy offered is that such a trade could be akin to increasing a car's fuel efficiency by switching to leaded fuel.

Molden's proposed remedy is knowledge rather than a specific target. He believes what is needed is better understanding, and to that end he recently created the Tyre Emissions Research Consortium, a LinkedIn group with about 1,000 members, with the intent of breaking down the silos individual researchers were working in.
