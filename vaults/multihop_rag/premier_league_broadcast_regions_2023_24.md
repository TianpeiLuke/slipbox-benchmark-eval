# Where To Watch A Premier League Match Across Major Regions

Sporting News listed how to watch the December 2023 Premier League match across selected areas of the world's major regions, with the United States on Peacock, Canada on Fubo, the UK on Amazon Prime Video, Australia on Optus Sport, India on Star Sports 3, and Asia on Jio TV and Hotstar VIP.

The region-by-region breakdown as published was: USA — streaming on Peacock; Canada — streaming on Fubo Canada; UK — streaming on Amazon Prime Video; Australia — Optus Sport; India — Star Sports 3 on TV; Asia — Jio TV and Hotstar VIP.

The accompanying notes said the match would be available in the UK on Amazon Prime TV; that the game was available for streaming in the USA via Peacock; that every Premier League game that season was live streaming exclusively via Fubo in Canada, with a new free five-day trial for new subscribers; and that fans in Australia could stream every match live and on demand on Optus Sport.
