# Citigroup's Elimination Of Overdraft Fees

Citibank eliminated overdraft fees in 2022 after government officials urged an end to the practice, and CEO Jane Fraser told the December 2023 Senate hearing that doing so neither made the bank unprofitable nor upended its business model.

Menendez commended Fraser for the move and then tested it in the exchange. Asked "Is it fair to say that Citi is still a profitable bank?" Fraser replied, "Yes, Senator." Asked whether removing overdraft fees had upended the business model, she answered, "No, Senator. We work hard to protect our customers and make sure that they don't fall into overdraft." Menendez used the line of questioning to press the other three CEOs: "I suggest you call Mr. Fraser after this hearing to figure out how you can still be able to eliminate the fees in their entirety and still run a profitable bank," he urged Scharff, Moynihan, and Dimon. Fortune also notes that big banks like Citi face competitive pressure from fintech startups and smaller banks, such as Ally Bank, that offer bank accounts without overdraft fees.
