# Vail: North America's Premium Ski Destination

Vail is North America's premium ski destination and the third-largest mountain ski resort in the US. Its iconic peaks, celebrated après and glamorous villages foster an exclusive atmosphere for travellers to share piste stories over Swiss-style fondue, likely in the company of some famous faces; celebrated après and scenic gondola rides dot Colorado's pistes.

With Iglu Ski, sophisticated skiers can spend seven nights at the ski-in, ski-out Arrabelle in Lionshead Village from £1,888.50pp. On-site spa facilities, a rooftop pool and a lavish plaza of boutiques complement the old-world opulence, and the Eagle Bahn gondola is right outside. Departs 2 December 2023. Return flights from London Heathrow to Eagle County Regional Airport start from £868 with United in January.
