# Premier League Standings And Other Results November 2023

Manchester City led the Premier League table on 28 points, with Liverpool going into second on 27, above Arsenal on goal difference, and Tottenham fourth on 26 after successive defeats, The Roar reported on Nov. 12, 2023.

Aston Villa moved to fifth, three points off the top, with a 3-1 win over Fulham at Villa Park, courtesy of an own goal from Antonee Robinson before strikes from John McGinn and Ollie Watkins. Sheffield United clawed their way off the bottom of the table with a 1-1 draw at Brighton, while Tomas Soucek grabbed a late winner as West Ham came from behind at the London Stadium to beat Nottingham Forest 3-2.
