# Paraguay's Outlook And Lineup Under New Coach Daniel Garnero

Paraguay went to Buenos Aires on Oct. 12, 2023 seeing themselves as real contenders for a 2026 World Cup place: they had not been close to qualifying for the last World Cup and had not reached a FIFA tournament since 2010, but with the 2026 field — and by extension qualifying across all federations — expanding this cycle, Los Guaranies would consider themselves genuine candidates, making points a premium in every match.

New head coach Daniel Garnero, making his debut, rang the changes after a defeat to Venezuela last time out, making five alterations to that starting lineup: the entire midfield was swapped out, and Adam Bareiro took over at striker, earning his debut international cap in place of Gabriel Avalos. The starting lineup (5-4-1) was C. Coronel (GK) — I. Ramirez, G. Gomez, Balbuena, Alonso, Espinoza — R. Sanchez, Campuzano, Almiron, Bareiro — Sosa.

Forward Miguel Almiron was Paraguay's most prominent goalscoring threat and was in good form with three goals in his past five matches for Newcastle United. The team had questions at goalkeeper: mainstay Antony Silva had been phased out, leaving three inexperienced internationals on the roster, and New York Red Bulls starter Carlos Coronel, who earned his first two caps in the September matches, remained in net. Lionel Messi's Inter Miami teammate Diego Gomez was named to the Paraguay roster but was forced to withdraw with injury.
