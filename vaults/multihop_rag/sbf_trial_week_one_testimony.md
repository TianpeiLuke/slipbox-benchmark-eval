# Week One Testimony in the SBF Trial

Two witnesses testified in the opening week of the FTX trial.

Gary Wang, [FTX](ftx.md)'s co-founder and CTO, testified on the extent to which [Alameda Research](alameda_research.md) was used for whatever purposes he and [Sam Bankman-Fried](sam_bankman_fried.md) saw fit, and that Alameda pulled funding directly from FTX customers. His testimony followed a guilty plea entered in December 2022.

Matt Huang, co-founder and managing partner at the crypto investment firm Paradigm, testified about a relationship with FTX stretching back to 2019. Paradigm invested $278 million in FTX across 2021 and 2022 and is party to a class-action lawsuit accusing it and others of defrauding customers by promoting FTX. Huang testified that he was left uninformed about FTX's use of customer money to keep Alameda afloat, and that knowing would have halted his involvement.
