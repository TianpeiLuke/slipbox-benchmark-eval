# Why Canada Belongs On A Skier's Bucket List

Canada may be further afield than the Alps or the Dolomites, but The Independent argues a trip there makes it onto the bucket lists of ski lovers everywhere for good reason: from the world-famous Whistler Blackcomb and its attractive resort towns to the steep verticals of Revelstoke, there is enough choice — for every level of skier — to keep you coming back for years.

The defining feature of the Canadian offer is breadth across skier types. Families, couples and groups alike can enjoy varied, all-round resorts like charming Tremblant, beginner-friendly family resorts such as Big White, and expert areas like Fernie, with some of North America's steepest terrain, best runs and most challenging off-piste. Several of the resorts can be combined into one single trip, offering exceptional variety and thousands of acres in areas of outstanding natural beauty such as Banff National Park. Add reliable snow cover and some of the longest ski seasons in the world, and there is a compelling case for making your next ski trip a Canadian one.
