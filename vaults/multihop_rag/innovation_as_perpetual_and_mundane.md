# Innovation As Perpetual And Mundane

For successful products and businesses, innovation is perpetual: there is a never-ending search to find the next new thing that enhances the user experience, extends product reach, expands revenue, or all three simultaneously. Product design makes innovation less daunting and increases chances of success because it is a multidisciplinary process with structures and frameworks to catalyze innovation, and technologists have a role in that process extending beyond simply validating the technology or concept, which is their customary responsibility.

The discriminating feature of innovation is novelty, not grandeur. The word innovation has a simple meaning — to introduce something new or a novel method of doing something — yet we frequently inflate it to mean something magical, grandiose and world-changing, when it can just as easily be mundane and straightforward.

The boundary of the concept is set by where the "new" applies: it can be localized to a team, product, process or business unit, and the "novel" can be well-known and established practices that have simply not been incorporated into your workflow or product. Sometimes innovation comes from closing small gaps and is not always a grand eureka moment.
