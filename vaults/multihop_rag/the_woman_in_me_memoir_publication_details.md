# The Woman In Me: Publication Details Of Britney Spears' Memoir

*The Woman in Me* is Britney Spears' memoir, published by Simon & Schuster on 25 October 2023 at $49.99 and reportedly written for a $US15 million ($23.3 million) advance; its defining subject is the conservatorship under which her father and others held almost complete control of her life for 13 years.

The Sydney Morning Herald's 1 October 2023 books preview describes the book as a candid account of a life in which the "huge-selling American singer has negotiated the slings and arrows of outrageous fortune in her 41 years", with the controversial conservatorship at its heart — an arrangement that nonetheless "still failed to stop it going off the rails many times" — and predicts it is sure to be a bestseller and exactly what her fans want.

The Guardian, reviewing the memoir on 24 October 2023, observes that it "feels fitting" the book is released a week before Halloween: however much was already known about the 13 years Spears lived under the conservatorship, her recounting of its imposition, life under it and her inability to escape it "is nothing short of a horror story", appalling "like something out of a Victorian novel, not the very real experiences of a thirtysomething in the last decade".
