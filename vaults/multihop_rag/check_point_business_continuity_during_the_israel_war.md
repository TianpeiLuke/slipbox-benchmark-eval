# Check Point Business Continuity During the Israel War

Like many companies in Israel, Tel Aviv-based Check Point has been impacted by the war: President Rupal Shah Hollenbeck said in November 2023 that some of its employees have been killed and others have been called to serve.

She stressed, however, that operations are normal and business is continuing as usual. Check Point has levels of redundancy around the world and research and development happening in several locations. In addition, the company's supply chain is 100% outside of Israel, and its tech support centres and data centres are found around the world. "We have the right level of redundancies and business continuity plans in place around the world," Hollenbeck said. "So we're okay."
