# Week 13 Panthers-Buccaneers: Carolina's Playcalling Uncertainty Makes Its Skill Players Risky

CBS Sports argues that Carolina's skill players are risky Fantasy propositions in Week 13 because nobody knows how the Panthers offense will operate, while Tampa Bay's injury-thinned defense is an offsetting reason for optimism. The grounds are drawn from the split in Carolina's own season: when Thomas Brown took over the playcalling for three games earlier this year, the Panthers averaged a 64% pass rate, Chuba Hubbard dominated touches — two games with 17-plus touches, none with more than 10 PPR points — and Adam Thielen had a large target share of 26.5% with a tiny ADOT of 4.33. In the two games since, the Panthers pass rate dipped below 60%, Hubbard shared more regularly and Thielen's ADOT nearly doubled. Hubbard had a good game last week; Thielen has not seen monster numbers since before the Week 7 bye.

The warrant is that the uncertainty of how the Panthers will operate moving forward, with Brown basically calling the offense without any oversight, keeps these players risky — though it helps a lot that the Buccaneers are dealing with injuries that hurt their run defense, with Lavonte David and Devin White missing the game, and that keep their pass defense from improving, with Jamel Dean also out. The verdicts: STARTS — Rachaad White, Mike Evans, Adam Thielen and Baker Mayfield (low-end QB1); SITS — Chris Godwin (PPR flex), Cade Otton (PPR bye-week replacement), Bryce Young and the Panthers DST; SLEEPERS — Chuba Hubbard (low-end RB2/high-end flex) and the Buccaneers DST.
