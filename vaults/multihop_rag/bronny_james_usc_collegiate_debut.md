# Bronny James's Collegiate Debut For USC

Bronny James, the oldest son of LeBron James, was set to play his first college game on Sunday against Long Beach State after suffering cardiac arrest while practicing that summer, per Sporting News on Dec. 9, 2023.

He was expected to be on a minutes restriction, but he would be moving into the role that had been envisioned for him as an impactful presence off the bench, and he might immediately provide a boost there since USC could definitely use another body to improve depth.

James, a guard, was listed at 6-foot-4 and 210 pounds, and had had a great prep career; ESPN viewed him as a top-20 recruit. He excelled in the McDonald's All-American game, made big plays to aid Team USA's win in the most recent Hoop Summit, and was expected to contribute immediately. Sporting News argued it really did not matter whether he was starting, but that whether he was on the court to close out games would be intriguing to see.
