# Theory: An AGI And Mission Rift Between Altman And The Board

The proposition is that Altman's zeal for AGI, even absent a secret project or agreement, led to a major rift between him and the board. Altman is a proud techno-optimist and often speaks fondly of the possibilities of AGI, or artificial general intelligence, a theoretical software system that achieves human-like intellect and versatility. The board's statement pointedly includes that "OpenAI was deliberately structured to advance our mission: to ensure that artificial general intelligence benefits all humanity" and that new leadership was necessary.

The supporting observation is a directional one: it has been obvious to all that Altman took the company in a much more corporate direction from its origins, changing its legal status and aggressively pursuing enterprise and consumer applications, which does not sound a lot like the "mission" the board wants to advance.

What counts against it is timing. This shift did not happen today, and it certainly does not seem like a plausible reason for abruptly firing the CEO and a few others on a beautiful fall Friday afternoon — a gradual drift does not explain an abrupt dismissal.
