# Warde Manuel's Rebuttal Of The Harbaugh Suspension

Michigan athletic director Warde Manuel attacked the Big Ten's suspension of Jim Harbaugh on the ground that the conference skipped the due process of a complete investigation. In a statement he did not mince words: while the program had been disappointed to hear about the allegations, he said, it also felt the conference acted before any complete investigation in suspending Harbaugh on Friday.

Manuel described the suspension as "completely unethical" and "an assault on the rights of everyone (especially in the Big Ten) to be judged by a fair and complete investigation," adding in the statement reported by Pete Thamel on Nov. 11, 2023 that he found it "completely unethical, insulting to a well-established process within the NCAA, and an assault on the rights of everyone (especially in the Big Ten) to be judged by a fair and complete investigation."

He also turned the precedent back on the conference's other members: "All of the Head Coaches in the Big Ten (some who have been accused of actively participating in the trading of signals of opponents) and my Big Ten AD colleagues can rejoice today that someone was 'held accountable,' but they should be worried about the new standard of judgment (without complete investigation) that has been unleashed in this conference."
