# England's Olympic Qualification Heartbreak

Scoring six goals against Scotland on Dec. 5, 2023 was not enough to close the goal difference gap on the Netherlands, because the Dutch scored a fourth against Belgium deep in added time to secure top spot in the Nations League group ahead of England.

No one could question the effort, the heart or the drive of Sarina Wiegman's Lionesses in the game, but qualification hinged on a back and forth of goals in the final round of fixtures — England's against Scotland and the Netherlands' in their win over Belgium — a showdown that ended in an anxious wait and then complete heartbreak. An added-time save by Mary Earps at full stretch from a header by substitute Martha Thomas kept England in it. With the Netherlands having scored in the 91st minute, Bronze's powering header put England back in for two minutes, until the Dutch scored in the 95th to crush the Lionesses while they stood in a huddle waiting for news of the other game's result, the pain sweeping across their faces as it came through.
