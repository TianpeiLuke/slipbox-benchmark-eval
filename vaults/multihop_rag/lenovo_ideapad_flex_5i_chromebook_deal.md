# Lenovo IdeaPad Flex 5i Chromebook Deal

Lenovo's IdeaPad Flex 5i Chromebook Plus was on sale for $379 at Best Buy for Black Friday 2023, $120 off its list price. This is the most recent version of the top pick in Engadget's Chromebook buying guide.

Its 14-inch, 1,920 x 1,200 IPS touchscreen is good for the money, it's plenty fast for the kind of web browsing and light work you'd do with Chrome OS, and both its keyboard and trackpad are comfortable. This model comes with a Core i3-1315U processor, 8GB of RAM, 128GB of eMMC storage, one USB-A port, two USB-C 3.2 ports and a microSD card slot, plus a decent 1080p webcam. The chassis isn't especially light at 3.6 pounds and Engadget found the battery to last roughly seven hours in testing, which is just OK — but for less than $400 this is a strong value for anyone who just wants an affordable notebook for the basics.
