# Daniel Craig Against Leaving An Inheritance

Daniel Craig argues that large inheritances are "distasteful" and that his children should not expect a pile of money when he is gone: "My philosophy is to get rid of it or give it away before you go. I don't want to leave great sums to the next generation," he told Saga Magazine in 2020, as reported by FOX News Entertainment on 10 October 2023.

The James Bond star shares one daughter with wife Rachel Weisz, has an older daughter from his previous marriage to Fiona Loudon, and is also step-dad to Weisz's son from a previous relationship.

A year after the Saga interview he repeated the sentiment in Candis magazine, calling inheritance "distasteful" and pondering the overall nature of wealth. His warrant is a maxim plus an example: "Isn't there an old adage that if you die a rich person, you've failed? I think Andrew Carnegie gave away what in today's money would be about 11 billion dollars, which shows how rich he was because I'll bet he kept some of it, too."
