# Cruise, GM And Honda's Japan Robotaxi Joint Venture

Cruise, General Motors and Honda are launching a robotaxi service in Japan under a new joint venture, TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-22. The service will launch with Origin vehicles in Tokyo in 2026.
