# The Batman Part II (Matt Reeves)

The Batman Part II is dated Oct. 3 in Polygon's 2023-10-24 DC slate rundown, again with Robert Pattinson as the Caped Crusader, greenlit immediately after the first film's success.

Polygon frames the sequel against low expectations for its predecessor: the long, contentious history behind 2022's The Batman did not leave much room for hope, since it was one of those movies that got bounced from team to team for years, changing shape at every bounce, and looked destined to be driven more by market calculation than by a specific vision. Director and co-writer Matt Reeves nonetheless found an effective take on the Dark Knight that resonated with audiences and landed solidly at the box office, and DC immediately greenlit a sequel.

No plot details were clear at the time, though Pattinson and Reeves had been blue-skying about a lot of things they would like to do — the Court of Owls storyline, maybe, or Mr. Freeze, or Calendar Man.
