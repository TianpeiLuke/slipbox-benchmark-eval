# Betting The Masters: Markets And The Augusta National Factors

The Masters is one of the most prestigious golf tournaments globally and gets significant interest from sports bettors, with sportsbooks providing a wide array of odds ranging from standard outright winner bets to unique prop bets. Popular options include Top 10, 20 or 30 Finishes, betting on whether a golfer will finish within the specified range; Head-to-Head Matchups, picking one golfer to outperform another in the tournament; and Group Matchups, betting on which golfer in a predefined group will perform best. The best sports betting sites also offer odds boosts and free contests leading up to the Masters.

For successful Masters betting it is crucial to consider statistics pertinent to the tournament, with driving distance, accuracy and greens in regulation playing significant roles. Because Augusta National prioritizes accuracy and precise approach shots, bettors may want to focus on golfers with strong iron play and a history of performing well there.
