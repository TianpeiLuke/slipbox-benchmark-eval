# Simply Homes' Operating Metrics And Markets

Founded in 2020, Simply Homes spent its first couple of years developing its platform and associated models before buying its first home in January 2023. By the end of December 2023 the startup was expected to have 108 units, or homes, in its portfolio, and since its first-quarter launch it has seen revenue grow by more than 50% quarter over quarter.

Over 80% of Simply Homes' tenant base are single parents who would need to work an estimated 150 hours a week to afford market-rate rent on a home, CEO Brian Bagdasarian notes. Using HUD's Housing Choice Voucher program through Simply Homes, these families are paying no more than 30% of their income for rent, he claims.

Simply Homes currently operates in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania and Cleveland, Ohio, with a goal of expanding into Baltimore, Maryland and parts of the Midwest, including additional markets throughout Ohio and in St. Louis, Missouri, among other cities. The company looks for stable markets that are not susceptible to wild fluctuations in the housing industry.
