# India vs Australia In The 2015 World Cup Semi-Final At Sydney

Australia beat India by 95 runs in the second semi-final of the 2015 World Cup in Sydney on 26 March, which Zee Business describes as Australia's revenge for the 2011 quarter-final. Australia outplayed India in almost all departments of the game: Man-of-the-Match Steve Smith scored 105 runs in 93 balls and Aaron Finch 81 runs in 116 balls, taking Australia to 328 runs for 7 wickets in 50 overs, reported elsewhere in the same publication as a target of 329 with seven wickets lost. Umesh Yadav picked up four wickets for India.

In reply India needed a few big innings but, apart from skipper MS Dhoni with 65 runs in 65 balls, Shikhar Dhawan with 45 runs in 34 balls and Ajinkya Rahane with 44 runs in 68 balls, the batting order collapsed miserably; India crumbled within 233 runs in 46.5 overs, with Dhoni the highest scorer for the Indian side. James Faulkner picked up three wickets for Australia, with Mitchell Johnson and Mitchell Starc taking two wickets each. Australia went all the way to lift their fifth ODI World Cup trophy, defeating New Zealand in the final.
