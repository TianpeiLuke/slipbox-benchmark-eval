# Tesla DOJ Subpoenas, 2023

Tesla has the attention of the U.S. Department of Justice — again, TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-30. This time it has received requests for information, including subpoenas from the DOJ, on three subjects: perks, the advertised range of its EVs, and personnel decisions.
