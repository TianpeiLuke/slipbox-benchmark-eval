# Premier League Broadcast And Streaming Rights In India

In India, the Premier League's rights are owned by the Star Sports Network, so matches are broadcast on various Star Sports channels, while live streaming runs through Disney+ Hotstar VIP. The distinguishing feature of the arrangement is that television and streaming are split across two different platforms of the same group, with an additional streaming route for a separate audience.

On the broadcast side, fans get English commentary on Star Sports Select, and regional fans also get the opportunity to watch the match in their favourite language: regional commentary in Bangla and Malayalam is made available for key weekend matches on Star Sports 3 and Star Sports 1 Bangla.

On the streaming side, matches are live-streamed on Disney+ Hotstar VIP, and fans have to subscribe to Hotstar's VIP plan to stream Premier League matches live. Indian Premier League fans can also stream the match live on Jio TV.
