# Denver's Fourth-Quarter Comeback And Ryland's Game Winner

Denver erased a huge deficit with two Russell Wilson touchdown passes and two successful two-point conversions in the closing minutes, only for Chad Ryland's 56-yard field goal at 11:20 p.m. to win it 26-23 for New England.

The live blog recorded the sequence in order: at 10:44 p.m. the Broncos edged deep into Patriots territory as Wilson hit Jerry Jeudy for 13 yards to reach the New England 3-yard line; at 10:45 p.m. Wilson found Lucas Krull for a 3-yard touchdown and the two-pointer that followed was good, making it a competitive football game again; at 11:04 p.m. Wilson connected with Brandon Johnson for a 21-yard touchdown and the two-point attempt succeeded, tying the game after the Broncos had overcome a huge deficit; at 11:16 p.m. the game was in a strong position to go to overtime with New England at its own 26 with less than a minute to play in regulation; and at 11:20 p.m. Ryland's 56-yard field goal was good and the Patriots won it.
