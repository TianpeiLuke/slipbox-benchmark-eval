# Sennheiser Open-Back Headphone Deals

The Sennheiser HD 560S, another open-back pair of wired headphones Engadget highlights in its gaming headset guide, came down to $150 at Amazon, B&H, Sennheiser's online store and other retailers for Black Friday 2023 — not an incredible deal, as the pair often sold for the same rate earlier in the year, but about $30 less than the street price seen since the summer.

Regardless, the HD 560S offers a pleasingly balanced, spacious and detailed sound profile and a frame that's comfortable to wear for extended periods; it can sometimes sound a little piercing in the treble, it's not as sturdily built as the DT 900 Pro X and it will leak and let in lots of noise, but for audio-centric headphones under $200 it's one of the better options in the price range. In other Sennheiser deals, the Drop X Sennheiser HD 6XX was on sale for $169 at Drop, a $50 discount only $10 more than the lowest price tracked; Engadget hasn't formally reviewed that pair, but its audio expert Billy Steele gave positive impressions in a hands-on back in 2016, and the pair it's based on — the Sennheiser HD 650 — has long been a favorite among audiophiles, making it worth a look for an open-back pair with more bass warmth.
