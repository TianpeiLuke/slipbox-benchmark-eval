# Relief Pitcher Signings At The 2023 Winter Meetings

Three veteran relievers found 2024 homes during the 2023 winter meetings — Craig Kimbrel with the Orioles, Kirby Yates with the Rangers and Chris Devenski with the Rays — per the reporting Sporting News carried in its tracker.

On Wednesday, Dec. 6, Ken Rosenthal of The Athletic reported the Orioles were bolstering their bullpen by signing Craig Kimbrel to a one-year, $13 million deal; Joel Sherman of the New York Post reported he had a $1 million buyout for 2025 and a $13 million club option. The 35-year-old right-hander amassed 23 saves in 69 innings in 2023 with a 3.26 ERA, and was sitting on 417 career saves, ranking eighth all-time, with a chance to vault all the way up to fourth — Francisco Rodriguez, fourth, at 437 and Kenley Jansen at 420 saves being the only other active reliever ahead of him. Rosenthal had reported earlier that Wednesday morning that Baltimore was "close" to a deal with the free agent and former Phillies closer; Baltimore would be without All-Star closer Felix Bautista for the entire 2024 season after he received Tommy John surgery in October, and even though stud reliever Yennier Cano had emerged as the top candidate to take the job, Kimbrel could bolster the back end of the O's bullpen.

On Tuesday, Dec. 5, Rosenthal reported that Kirby Yates, who would be 37 when the season began, was headed to the Rangers on a one-year deal; the right-hander, an All-Star closer for the Padres in 2019, posted a 3.28 ERA in 61 appearances for the Braves in 2023. Jeff Passan of ESPN reported that Chris Devenski, 33, agreed to a new one-year deal with the Rays that included a club option for 2025; Devenski began 2023 with the Angels but was released in late August and signed with Tampa Bay, posting a 5.08 ERA in 29 games for the Angels and dropping three full runs off that number in nine games with the Rays.
