# ChatGPT Adoption Milestones And Usage Statistics

Roughly a year after its release, ChatGPT had 100 million weekly active users, and OpenAI CEO Sam Altman also revealed that over two million developers use the platform, including more than 92% of Fortune 500 companies.

Two other markers frame that scale. OpenAI hit the one-year anniversary of ChatGPT: what began as a "low-key research preview" evolved into a powerhouse that changed the AI industry forever, and Altman looked back on the night before its launch in a post on X on November 30, 2023 — "a year ago tonight we were probably just sitting around the office putting the finishing touches on chatgpt before the next morning's launch. what a year it's been…" Meanwhile neither Apple nor Google chose an AI app as its app of the year for 2023, despite the success of ChatGPT's mobile app, which became the fastest-growing consumer application in history before that record was broken by Meta's Threads.

Adoption among the general public is far thinner than the headline numbers suggest. Ongoing polling by Pew Research shows that although ChatGPT is gaining mindshare, only about 18% of Americans have ever actually used it — suggesting the language model is not quite as popular or threatening as some would have you think.
