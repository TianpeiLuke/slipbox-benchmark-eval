# Game Preservation And The Ship Of Theseus Problem

Elizabeth Harper argues that live online games erase their own past: "With MMOs, it's easier than ever for gaming experiences to be lost completely to time. New versions overwrite old versions, making original games inaccessible."

Her ground is World of Warcraft's own record, and the warrant is an identity paradox: "World of Warcraft will be 19 years old in November, and practically every feature and zone the game launched with has been remade in the years since…. It's a ship of Theseus." Continuous replacement of a game's parts leaves a title that carries the same name while no longer being the thing players remember.
