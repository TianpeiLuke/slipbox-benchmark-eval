# Yuki Matsui's Five-Year Deal With The Padres

Japanese reliever Yuki Matsui signed a five-year, $28 million contract with the Padres on Saturday, Dec. 23, 2023, ESPN's Jeff Passan reported, with opt-out clauses in Years 4 and 5 that give San Diego an out if Matsui deals with elbow trouble.

Matsui was one of NPB's best arms out of the bullpen, starring for the Tohoku Rakuten Golden Eagles for nearly a decade; he tallied 236 saves over the course of his career and posted a 2.40 ERA in 659.2 innings of work. In 2023 he recorded a 1.57 ERA in 57.1 innings with 39 saves for the Golden Eagles. The signing had been foreshadowed on Tuesday, Dec. 18, when Sankei Sports' Yuki Yamada reported the Padres were "close to signing" the Rakuten reliever, one of the top Japanese pitchers on the market.
