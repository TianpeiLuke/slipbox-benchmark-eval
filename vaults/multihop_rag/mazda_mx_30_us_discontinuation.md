# The Mazda MX-30's US Discontinuation

Mazda said in July 2023 that it planned to discontinue the MX-30 EV in the U.S. at the end of 2023, although the automaker will continue selling the EV in Japan and the EU.

The MX-30 is small by Cybertruck standards — Tesla's vehicle is 25% longer and wider — but the only truly diminutive things about the MX-30 were its 100-mile range and its availability: stateside, Mazda sold it exclusively in California, the state with the most EV chargers by a factor of five.
