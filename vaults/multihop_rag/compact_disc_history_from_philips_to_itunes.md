# The Compact Disc, From Philips To iTunes

The compact disc is a portable medium that can record, store and play back audio, video and other data in digital form, first introduced in 1982 according to the tech company Philips' website, FOX News Lifestyle reported on 11 November 2023.

Its defining features are its origin and function: Philips co-developed the technology along with Sony, and the format handles digital audio, video and other data on a portable carrier.

Its boundary condition is displacement by download. In 2001 Apple introduced iTunes, a platform that sold digital songs online for just 99 cents each, according to the Smithsonian's National Museum of American History, and iTunes made purchasing music so easy and affordable that it has virtually replaced the CD as of 2023.
