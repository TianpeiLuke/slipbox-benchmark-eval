# The INEOS Plan For Manchester United's Football Operations

Sir Jim Ratcliffe is demanding an executive role and overall control of Manchester United's day-to-day football matters, and plans to appoint his own sporting director, with former Tottenham and Southampton transfer guru Paul Mitchell one of the main names in the frame, talkSPORT reported on 18 October 2023.

The plan reaches across the club's football leadership. Ratcliffe's arrival raises doubts over the future of current sporting director John Murtough and chief executive Richard Arnold, although manager Erik ten Hag is believed to be on a solid footing despite United's poor start to the season. Sir Dave Brailsford — the mastermind behind Team GB's cycling success at the London 2012 Olympics and director of sport for Ratcliffe's company INEOS — is expected to have a prominent role in any reshuffle. Ratcliffe also has ambitious plans to upgrade Old Trafford and turn it into a 90,000-seat stadium. He wants the deal ratified in time for the January transfer window, though because of FFP concerns there is no guarantee United will be able to spend big. Despite turning 71 the day before the board meeting, his plans appear to centre on long-term stability and success rather than a short-term turnaround in United's fortunes.

The ownership rationale is that Ratcliffe would take charge of footballing matters, something the Glazers have been accused of having little to no interest in. He is already a top-tier European owner through OGC Nice — and also owns Lausanne in Switzerland — where there has been some success but nothing compared to Nice's French riviera neighbours, the club to which Ratcliffe appears set to look for inspiration.
