# Totals Bet Worked Examples: Win And Loss

Settling a totals bet means adding the two teams' scores and comparing the sum against the posted line. Sporting News works this through twice for the NBA — once where the bet wins and once where it loses.

In the winning case, the NBA over/under odds for a game between the Los Angeles Lakers and the Boston Celtics are 220.5 points. A bettor who believes the game will be high-scoring and that the teams' combined score will exceed this mark places an "over" bet. The game unfolds with the Lakers scoring 115 points and the Celtics 110, a combined score of 225. Since 225 exceeds the set total of 220.5, the bettor who wagered the "over" wins.

In the losing case, a top sports betting app sets a total of 200.5 points for an NBA game between the New York Knicks and the Chicago Bulls. A bettor who believes the game will be defensive and low-scoring places an "under" bet, predicting a combined score of 200 points or less. The game turns out offensively charged: the Knicks score 105 and the Bulls 100, a combined 205. Since 205 exceeds the set total of 200.5, the bettor who wagered the "under" loses.
