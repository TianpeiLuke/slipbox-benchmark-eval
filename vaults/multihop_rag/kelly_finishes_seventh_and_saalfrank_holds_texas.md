# Kelly Finishes The Seventh And Saalfrank Holds Texas

Merrill Kelly closed out the bottom of the seventh in World Series Game 2 on 29 October 2023 by getting Jonah Heim to watch two straight strikes after falling behind 3-1, leaving the Rangers just six outs to score three runs with the Diamondbacks ahead 4-1.

Andrew Saalfrank then took over for the bottom of the eighth as Kelly got a well-earned rest, with Emmanuel Rivera staying in the game at third base. Nathaniel Lowe promptly grounded out to second, Josh Jung grounded out to third, and with two quick outs recorded, Leody Taveras came up to try to salvage something for Texas: he fell behind 0-2, took two pitches and grounded out to short. The Rangers challenged the play, but the call was confirmed, sending the game to the ninth with Arizona ahead 7-1.
