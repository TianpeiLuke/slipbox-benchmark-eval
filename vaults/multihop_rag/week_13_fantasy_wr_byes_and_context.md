# Week 13 Fantasy Wide Receiver Byes And Context

Six byes and some inopportune injuries thinned out Sporting News' Week 13 fantasy wide receiver PPR rankings, published Nov. 29, 2023, leaving fewer studs and sleepers than usual while owners still had to fill the same number of starting spots — so those in 12-team leagues might have to dig deep for decent WR3s and flexes. In the final weeks of the fantasy regular season the goal is rolling out a strong receiver corps of wideouts set up for high-usage days, and having them in plus matchups makes a winning week more likely.

The Ravens, Bills, Vikings, Raiders, Giants and Bears were idle in Week 13, which meant Davante Adams, Stefon Diggs, Justin Jefferson, DJ Moore, Zay Flowers, Jakobi Meyers and Jordan Addison were among the most notable wideouts omitted from the rankings. Deep-league owners were also without Odell Beckham Jr., K.J. Osborn, Gabe Davis, Jalin Hyatt and Khalil Shakir.

The byes cut both ways for matchups. Owners would not get to pick on the poor receiver defenses of the Giants and Vikings, though it was a relief to see the Ravens' shut-down secondary on the sidelines.
