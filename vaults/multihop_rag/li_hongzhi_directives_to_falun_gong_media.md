# Li Hongzhi's 2009 Directives To Falun Gong Media

In 2009, Li Hongzhi came to speak to his followers — volunteers who worked at The Epoch Times' offices in Manhattan — and gave the group two simple instructions: they needed to reach people outside of the Falun Gong religious community, and they needed to make money, a lot of it.

"Ensure that the paper gains a foothold in ordinary society and turns profitable," Li said.

Li also told them that Falun Gong's media ought to put a "constructive" spin on the news in order to advance the group's aims, and that it was not wrong to cover a politician favourably if that politician shared Falun Gong's conservative values and had goals aligned with their own. He framed the rationale in salvational terms: "If someone comes along now who can help to halt the downward spiral that the world is in, then he is truly someone extraordinary!" Li said. "He would in effect be helping us! Wouldn't he be helping us to save people?"
