# Steve Borthwick's Three Changes To England's XV For The Semi-Final

Steve Borthwick made three changes to England's starting XV for the 2023 Rugby World Cup semi-final against South Africa, explaining that "the start is crucial, the end is vital." Marcus Smith had hardly put a foot wrong at fullback against Fiji, but the world champions were an entirely different proposition at the back, so the high-ball security and hulking frame of Freddie Steward returned to the No. 15 jersey. At inside centre, Manu Tuilagi started hoping to repeat his heroics of four years earlier, when his early try in a dominant semi-final performance took England past the All Blacks.

The other two changes came in the tight five. Joe Marler started ahead of vice-captain Ellis Genge at loosehead prop, and Ollie Chessum was replaced in the second row by George Martin, who was winning only his seventh England cap but got the starting nod alongside Maro Itoje — leaving Genge, Chessum and the rest of the replacements hoping to make an impact sizeable enough to overcome the Springboks' infamous 'bomb squad'.

England's starting XV was Freddie Steward, Jonny May, Joe Marchant, Manu Tuilagi, Elliot Daly, Owen Farrell, Alex Mitchell; Joe Marler, Jamie George, Dan Cole, Maro Itoje, George Martin, Courtney Lawes, Tom Curry, Ben Earl. The replacements were Theo Dan, Ellis Genge, Kyle Sinckler, Ollie Chessum, Billy Vunipola, Danny Care, George Ford and Ollie Lawrence.
