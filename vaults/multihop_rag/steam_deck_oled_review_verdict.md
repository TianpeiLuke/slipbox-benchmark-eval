# Steam Deck OLED Review Verdict

Engadget's verdict, scoring the Valve Steam Deck OLED 89, is that this isn't the Steam Deck 2 some are waiting for — it's just a welcome improvement on an already good device, at $549 at Steam. The pros listed are improved battery life, bright and smooth visuals, better thermals, feeling less top-heavy, and a long charging cable; the cons are that it is still ridiculously large — "Seriously it's so big."

The grounds are the specific gains: the Steam Deck OLED is the facetuned, photoshopped, spit-shined version of Valve's handheld, and it features tangible, clever improvements, with the updated display as the highlight while things like faster charging, improved antennas and smoother haptics are welcome bonuses. The larger warrant is strategic: more importantly, this is Valve renewing its commitment to portable PC gaming, and that's a relief to see, since the Steam Deck is the granddaddy of handheld PCs and Valve has the resources to continue innovating in this space. For now, the OLED model is a half-step toward the Steam Deck 2, which may be the final version of the device — unless Valve finally learns how to count to three.
