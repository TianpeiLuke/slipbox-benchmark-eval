# The Travis Kelce Relationship And Eras Tour Film Publicity

Taylor Swift's apparent relationship with Kansas City Chiefs tight end Travis Kelce has only further brightened the spotlight on the concert movie.

The film needed little paid promotion as a result. According to ad tracking firm iSpot, TV ads for the film ran only a few dozen times as of Oct. 6, 2023, including several spots during NFL broadcasts — whereas a Marvel movie, by comparison, might run several thousand TV commercials.
