# Victrola Revolution Go Record Player

The Victrola Revolution Go record player, one of the more unique suggestions from Polygon's 2023 Holiday Gift Guide, was on sale for around $125 in a variety of striking colors as of 2023-12-09, down from a normal price of $199.99.

Polygon justifies the pick by noting it recommends a lot of vinyl in its weekend posts, so a solid record player fits. The portable unit is equipped with a convenient carrying strap, a built-in speaker, and an on-board battery that can keep it running for up to 12 hours on a single charge.
