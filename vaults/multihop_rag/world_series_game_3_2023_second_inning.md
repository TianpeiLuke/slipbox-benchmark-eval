# The Second Inning Of World Series Game 3, 2023

The second inning of Game 3 of the 2023 World Series ended scoreless at Rangers 0, Diamondbacks 0, with both halves undone by defensive plays — a Rangers double play in the top and Adolis Garcia throwing out Christian Walker at home in the bottom.

In the top of the second at 8:22 p.m., Rangers star rookie Evan Carter led off with a hard-hit single to right-center, but Mitch Garver topped a grounder to Brandon Pfaadt for a 1-4-3 double play and Jonah Heim flew out to center to end the inning; Pfaadt had needed only 18 pitches to get through his first six batters.

The bottom half was the more dramatic. At 8:25 p.m. Walker smoked a first-pitch fastball off the wall in right-center for a lead-off double. At 8:27 p.m. Tommy Pham singled to right field and Walker tried to score from second, but Garcia — who has one of the best outfield arms in baseball — hosed him at home plate to save a run, with Pham advancing to second on the throw; the Sporting News noted Walker had blown through the stop sign. At 8:30 p.m. Lourdes Gurriel Jr. popped out to second, and with two outs Alek Thomas ripped a line drive back at Max Scherzer that ricocheted perfectly to third baseman Josh Jung, who made a laser throw to first to get Thomas in time — closing what the blog called a bizarre inning.
