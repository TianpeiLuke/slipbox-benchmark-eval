# Michigan State's 2024 Schedule Under A Division-Free, Expanded Big Ten

Michigan State's schedule changes materially in Jonathan Smith's debut season because the Big Ten expands and does away with divisions, CBS Sports reported on 2023-11-25. For roughly the last decade the Spartans had found themselves playing in a stacked Big Ten East division, with Michigan, Ohio State and Penn State all annual opponents; that arrangement ends.

Smith will also get the chance to face some familiar opponents from his time at Oregon State, given that four Pac-12 schools are joining the conference. As far as the 2024 season is concerned, Michigan State still faces Ohio State and Michigan — the Wolverines being a protected rival — but avoids Penn State. Oregon is the only newcomer it will face that fall, with the Spartans set to travel to Eugene, Oregon, in early October.
