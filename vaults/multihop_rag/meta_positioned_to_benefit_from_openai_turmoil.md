# Meta Positioned To Benefit From The OpenAI Turmoil

The claim is that Meta is perhaps best positioned to capitalize on the mess OpenAI has created for itself. On the surface Microsoft looks like the biggest winner amidst the turmoil, as it was already apparently looking to reduce its reliance on OpenAI even though it remains one of its major shareholders. But Facebook's parent Meta could also stand to benefit, as businesses pursue multi-modal strategies or models with a more "open" ethos embedded.

The grounds are Meta's scale and history rather than its openness alone. Meta is not the only company flaunting its "open" approach to AI development — notably, the likes of Hugging Face, Mistral AI and 01.AI, which have all raised sizeable sums at lofty valuations with similar goals in mind. But as a $900 billion juggernaut with a long history of courting developers through open source endeavors, Meta has the distribution and developer relationships the smaller open players lack.

The warrant is a low bar on what "open" has to mean to win the moment: Meta's decision to pursue "openness" over "closedness" seems well vindicated right now, and regardless of whether Llama is or is not really open source, it is likely "open enough" for most people.
