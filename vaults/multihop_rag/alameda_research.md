# Alameda Research

Alameda Research is the crypto-trading firm Sam Bankman-Fried co-founded in 2017, before FTX existed. FTX came two years later, in 2019, and Bankman-Fried co-founded it as a complement to Alameda — to help bring in revenue and liquidity for the trading arm. The founding order is the discriminating fact: the exchange was created to serve the trading firm, not the other way round.

The feature that later set Alameda's balance sheet apart was how much of it consisted of the affiliated exchange's own token. A report showed that Alameda's largest asset was $3.66 billion of "unlocked FTT," alongside $2.16 billion of "FTT collateral." FTT was the token behind FTX. So the trading firm's single biggest asset was the token issued by the exchange founded to support it.
