# Alameda Research

Alameda Research is a crypto trading firm and the sister company of [FTX](ftx.md), both associated with [Sam Bankman-Fried](sam_bankman_fried.md). Testimony in the FTX trial established that Alameda drew funding directly from FTX customer deposits.

The relationship between the two firms was not disclosed to several investors, and its concealment is central to the fraud charges: witnesses testified that knowledge of the relationship would have prevented their investments.
