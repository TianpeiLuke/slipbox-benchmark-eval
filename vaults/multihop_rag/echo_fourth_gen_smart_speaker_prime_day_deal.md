# Echo Fourth-Gen Smart Speaker Prime Day Deal

The fourth-generation Amazon Echo smart speaker was $55 at Amazon during the October 2023 fall Prime Day event, down from $100 — a 45 percent discount. The Verge called it the best Amazon Alexa smart speaker thanks to its excellent sound, nice design, and built-in smart home hub, and a great way to get started with the smart home.

Beyond being a smart speaker, it is also a Matter controller, a Thread border router, and a Zigbee bridge, so it can connect all your gadgets to your smart home.
