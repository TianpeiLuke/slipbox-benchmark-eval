# The Third-Gen Echo Frames Are Only An Incremental Improvement

The Verge's verdict on the third-gen Echo Frames is that Amazon isn't straying far from its existing formula and the changes are "a half-step in the right direction" rather than enough to turn the Echo Frames into a must-have gadget.

The grounds are the specific gains: the new frames are lighter, the battery lasts longer, the bass is more bassy, and they look a lot more stylish than previous iterations, and the reviewer singles out improving the range of available styles as a much-needed change. The warrant for the negative verdict is that each gain falls short of the comparison that matters: beefing up the audio is good, but it wasn't enough of a change to make these stand above the competition or serve as a compelling alternative to wireless earbuds — and, most crucially, the reviewer wasn't convinced to use Alexa more than they otherwise would have. The summary judgment: Amazon is getting there with form, but is not quite there with function.
