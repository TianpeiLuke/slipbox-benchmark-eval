# Britney Spears And Sam Asghari: Marriage And Divorce

Sam Asghari filed for divorce from Britney Spears in August 2023 after only 14 months of marriage, listing the date of separation as 28 July 2023 and citing "irreconcilable differences." The two had met in 2016, when Asghari was cast as Spears' love interest in the music video for "Slumber Party," from her 2016 album Glory; they made the relationship Instagram official in 2017 and supported each other through milestones including the 29th annual GLAAD Media Awards, where Spears accepted the Vanguard Award for her work promoting equality and acceptance. In April 2022 Spears announced she was pregnant with her third child and their first together, but the following month said in an Instagram statement that she had suffered a miscarriage; the couple wrote: "We have lost our miracle baby."

They married that June at her California home in Los Angeles, a lavish ceremony one month after the miscarriage announcement, with a guest list that included Selena Gomez, Paris Hilton, Drew Barrymore, Madonna, and Donatella Versace, who designed the wedding dress. Spears' close family — her mother Lynne, father Jamie, sister Jamie-Lynn, and her two sons — were not present. In August 2023, months after Asghari had shut down speculation about a divorce, TMZ claimed he had confronted Spears about rumours she had cheated on him; the fitness instructor filed the same day. His statement read: "After six years of love and commitment to each other my wife and I have decided to end our journey together. We will hold onto the love and respect we have for each other and I wish her the best always. S*** happens. Asking for privacy seems ridiculous so I will just ask for everyone including the media to be kind and thoughtful." Days before the memoir's release he told TMZ, on 13 October, that he had obtained an early copy and was not worried about being painted negatively: "I already read it and I'm very proud of her. She put a lot of work into it, and it was very hard. I'm definitely excited, and I'm extremely proud of her. It was a very tough one [to write]." Fans had previously praised him for his continuing support of Spears through her conservatorship and custody battle. Spears' two children, Sean, 18, and Jayden, 17, are shared with her second ex-husband, Kevin Federline.
