# Vampire Survivors

Vampire Survivors is a deceptively simple roguelike bullet-hell in which players min-max various buffs to avoid waves of mob attacks, and Polygon's writer says they have donated hours and hours of their life to it. It appears in the outlet's 2023-10-20 list of the 35 best couch co-op games for Nintendo Switch.

Multiplayer changes everything: having more reinforcements to fight waves of attacks means each player can try out unique combinations of weapons and buffs.

The constraint to plan around is that you and your teammates must stay on the same screen, because there is no split screen.
