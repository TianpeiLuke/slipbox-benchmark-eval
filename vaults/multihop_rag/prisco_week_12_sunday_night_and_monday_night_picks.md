# Prisco's Week 12 Sunday Night And Monday Night Picks

Pete Prisco's Week 12 prime-time picks, published by CBS Sports on 2023-11-23, cover two games. For the Sunday 8:20 p.m. ET game on NBC and fubo, he argued the Chargers are having major issues right now and face one of the best teams in the league; the Ravens offense will miss tight end Mark Andrews, but the Chargers defense is perfect for any team throwing the football, so the Ravens will win it with a big game from Lamar Jackson. Pick: Ravens 30, Chargers 23.

For the Monday 8:15 p.m. ET game on ABC/ESPN and fubo, he noted both teams were coming off tough losses, with the Bears blowing a huge lead late — though it was more damning for the Vikings, a team that had been surging. His warrant: Minnesota's defense will be the difference as they get the best of Justin Fields. Pick: Vikings 24, Bears 14.
