# The Paris AI Research Talent Pipeline

France's AI momentum is not a surprise, because a talent pipeline has been feeding it for years. Tech giants have historically set up AI research labs in Paris, as there is a large talent pool of PhD students in math, computer science and engineering. They usually graduate from Polytechnique, ENS or Inria, sometimes move to the U.S. for postdoctoral research, and then move back to France to join a research lab — so the loop runs school, US postdoc, return to a Paris lab.

The corporate labs anchoring that loop are specific. Facebook (now Meta) created its Paris research lab back in 2015 with Yann LeCun at the helm of the AI initiative; this research group is called FAIR, for Facebook Artificial Intelligence Research. Google followed suit with its own research center dedicated to AI in Paris.

The quality signal comes from a practitioner who had recently had a private meeting with some of the engineers and scientists working on AI projects: "The FAIR team is extremely impressive. Just look at what they've done with Llama," an entrepreneur told TechCrunch.
