# Arizona's Defensive Performance Versus Utah

Arizona's defence held a cloud over the Utes' heads in the second half, with junior safety Dalton Johnson leading the Wildcats by logging 11 tackles as well as two tackles for loss in the routing. "Dalton is a fantastic tackler," Fisch said. "When he's around the ball, he gets them down."

With the rain starting to clear up to start the second half, the Wildcats got off to a slow start but picked up much of their thunderous momentum after junior safety Gunner Maldonado broke up a pass on fourth and three. Since that fourth-down stop, the UA logged an interception and another turnover on downs.

Although the defence was surging, the Wildcats had not scored since the first drive of the second quarter, and their offense seemed to have lost its touch.
