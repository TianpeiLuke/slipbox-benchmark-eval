# All Blacks Selection Changes For The 2023 Semi-Final

Ian Foster recalled Sam Whitelock to the starting side as the only change to the pack for the All Blacks' 2023 World Cup semi-final, and brought Mark Telea back on the wing ahead of Leicester Fainga'anuku.

Whitelock — whose breakdown penalty win after 37 phases denied Ireland a match-winning penalty in the quarter-final — was recalled to partner Scott Barrett in the second row, with Brodie Retallick moving to the bench. Telea, described as a tryscoring machine, had been axed for the quarter-final after missing a team curfew.

Foster explained both calls: "That's the team we think best for this week. Mark has done his time. He made a mistake and accepted what was happening but you don't linger in that space. He's been our form winger. We have got a faith in him and think he is in good place to play this game. We really enjoyed Leicester Fainga'anuku's game last week and he should be proud of that and it's a tough selection. It's a chance for us to get Mark on the park."
