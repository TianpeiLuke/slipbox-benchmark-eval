# The Stalled Gogoro–Hero MotoCorp Partnership

In April 2021 Gogoro partnered with Indian two-wheeler giant Hero MotoCorp to roll out its battery-swapping network in the country, but as of December 2023 that deal has not yielded any results.

Asked how Gogoro will use the Hero MotoCorp partnership, and why it did not choose the local automobile player for its debut and instead introduced its own range of smartscooters, CEO Horace Luke gave a sketchy response, saying the top management of Hero MotoCorp did wish him success before the launch. "[Hero MotoCorp's] brand and company as a whole is very B2C focused . . . As we launch with a B2B focus first, we keep them abreast of all the stuff that's happening," he said. "We're an open platform. One day, they'll be ready [to] launch a vehicle using our system. But it's really a chicken and egg [situation]. I need to prove that there is a platform that is ready before they actually can go in, come in and do that."

Luke described the same sequencing about partners generally: "Everybody's waiting for me to bring the network," he said without divulging specific details. "Once the network goes live, [the partners will] bring in their capacity and capability."
