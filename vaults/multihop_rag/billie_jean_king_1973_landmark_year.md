# Billie Jean King's Landmark Year Of 1973

In the summer of 1973 a 29-year-old Billie Jean King established the WTA, won the Wimbledon triple crown in singles, doubles and mixed doubles, achieved equal pay at the U.S. Open, and beat self-proclaimed chauvinist Bobby Riggs in the "Battle of the Sexes" match. Her 2023 schedule is described as reminiscent of that year.

King's own account compresses the sequence: "We started the WTA four days before Wimbledon. I won all three titles at Wimbledon, which for me was a big deal. Then equal prize money came into being, it started in 1972 with us saying we're not coming back (to the U.S. Open in 1973). Then King-Riggs. That's all in 3 months. I can appreciate it since being away from it so long. How the heck did we do that?"
