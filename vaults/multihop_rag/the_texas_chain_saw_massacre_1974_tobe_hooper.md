# The Texas Chain Saw Massacre: A $140,000 Budget And A $30m Return

The Texas Chain Saw Massacre is Tobe Hooper's 1974 masterpiece, starring Marilyn Burns, Gunnar Hansen and Allen Danziger, and available on Peacock, Shudder, AMC Plus, Tubi and Freevee. Another shoestring production gone huge, it made over $30 million at the box office on a budget of around $140,000.

The movie follows a group of friends who find themselves hunted by a family of cannibals in the middle of Texas, and is a chilling, violent fever dream that permanently lodges itself in the minds of those who watch it. Eight films have followed, including a Netflix version in 2022, but the original stands out as an unhinged encapsulation of pure chaos and terror — and at a tight 83 minutes it is well worth the small time investment to catch up on one of the most influential horror movies ever made.
