# Amari Cooper Week 13: Start Him, Because Joe Flacco Should Restore His Downfield Role

CBS Sports' Week 13 verdict on Amari Cooper is Start Him, resting on a quarterback change rather than on the matchup. The preconditions: it appears Cooper will play through a rib injury he suffered last week, and it also appears he will catch passes from new Browns quarterback Joe Flacco. The article calls this a great thing for Cooper — over his past two weeks his ADOT sat at 7.57 yards, roughly half of what it was in his first nine games at 15.42 yards, because the Browns clearly wanted Cooper to run shorter routes and make things easier on rookie quarterback Dorian Thompson-Robinson. That is expected to be out the window now: Flacco wasn't shy about chucking the rock deep with the Jets, attempting passes of 15-plus Air Yards on 20 percent of his throws, and he had no problem focusing on one primary receiver, with Garrett Wilson drawing 8, 14 and 11 targets in Flacco's starts.

The warrant is stated as a hope rather than a certainty: that both Flacco and Cooper are experienced and capable enough to develop rhythm to salvage the Browns passing game, and the article concedes it is the only thing to buy into here, because the Rams have allowed the 12th-fewest Fantasy points to wide receivers on the season and have done well against deep throws against wide receivers, allowing a 40% completion rate with two touchdowns, both top six on the season. The conclusion is that Cooper's volume should hopefully push him to a decent Fantasy stat line, and the author would give him a whirl over Terry McLaurin, Marquise Brown and Diontae Johnson. Scope: Cooper is a Cleveland wide receiver, #2, age 29 with 9 years of experience; the weekly breakdown lists the matchup at the L.A. Rams with the Rams favored by 3.5 and an over/under of 40.5, the opponent 3rd versus wide receivers, projected points of 11.4 and a WR rank of 23rd; year-to-date he has 47 receptions on 86 targets for 765 receiving yards, 2 touchdowns and 12.3 Fantasy points per game, with projections powered by SportsLine.
