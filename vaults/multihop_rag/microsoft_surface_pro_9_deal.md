# Microsoft Surface Pro 9 Deal

A configuration of Microsoft's Surface Pro 9 with an Intel Core i5-1235U processor, 16GB of RAM, a 256GB SSD and Microsoft's Surface Pro Keyboard was on sale for $1,000 at Best Buy for Black Friday 2023 — $540 off Microsoft's list price. Without the keyboard, a variant with a faster Core i7-1255U chip was down to a new low of $1,097 at Amazon, Microsoft, B&H and other retailers, roughly $200 off the config's usual street price, while another Core i7 model with 512GB of storage was on sale for $300 more.

The Surface Pro 9 tops Engadget's guide to the best 2-in-1 tablets. While its 12th-gen chip is about to be two generations old, this is still the device to get for the functionality of a laptop in the slim, touch-enabled design of a tablet; Engadget notes we're past the point of the year when Microsoft would normally announce a refresh, but the current model should still be useful for anyone coming from an older Surface Pro in desperate need of an upgrade.
