# Dead After Dinner

Dead After Dinner, by Jenn Martin, combines the relentless procedural revelation of For the Queen with Knives Out-style murder mystery, and Jason Morningstar — owner, creative director, and lead designer at Bully Pulpit Games — calls it endlessly hilarious in Polygon's 2023 roundup.

The premise is fixed and the outcome is not: everyone is part of a miserable family stuffed with resentment, somebody is getting killed, there will be uncomfortable questions, and a murderer will definitely be revealed — or will they. Morningstar describes it as a truly pick-up-and-play murder mystery that will delight you and your scenery-chewing friends.
