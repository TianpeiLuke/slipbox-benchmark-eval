# Beats Fit Pro Deal Listings

The Beats Fit Pro appear in both The Verge's headphone roundup and WIRED's Prime Day Apple roundup, where they score 9/10, WIRED Recommends; WIRED notes they dipped to $125 back in August but calls the current price still a good deal.

The Verge describes them as the latest fitness earbuds from Apple, offering a sportier design and better sound than the first-gen AirPods Pro, with wing tips that keep them in place when you're active while remaining comfortable over long listening sessions; their functionality with Android phones is slightly limited, but iPhone users get all the conveniences of Apple's H1 chip and deeper OS integration and connectivity. Their only real downside, in The Verge's account, is that the included charging case is overly large and lacks wireless charging — more forgivable at lower price points. WIRED credits a comfortable fit, great sound, seamless integration with both iOS and Android, active noise canceling and decent mics: equipped with Apple's proprietary H1 headphone chip they work like AirPods with an iPhone, and work great on Android too, complete with an app for a fit test and control customization.
