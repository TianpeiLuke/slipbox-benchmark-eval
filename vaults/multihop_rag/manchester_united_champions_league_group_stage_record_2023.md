# Manchester United's 2023-24 Champions League Group-Stage Record

Manchester United finished bottom of their 2023-24 Champions League group with four points, in fourth place, eliminated from European competition altogether rather than dropping into the Europa League, The Independent reported on 12 December 2023.

The decisive results came from outside the Bayern fixture. It was not Bayern who eliminated them: it was Galatasaray, who won at Old Trafford and scored six goals over 180 minutes against United, and FC Copenhagen, who qualified instead of them — Rasmus Hojlund's former club will be in the last 16 while his current one will not, making Copenhagen the anomalies in the draw, the outsiders with the spot that had seemed reserved for United. The second place United aimed for went instead to Copenhagen, a club with a fraction of United's budget, and that is where the damage was done. On the final night the Danish side's victory over Galatasaray ensured any United win would be irrelevant, leaving only a slim hope beforehand.

The campaign produced records of the wrong kind. When Coman scored, United became the first Premier League side to concede 15 goals in a Champions League group stage. United won a lone game in the competition, and even that required a penalty save to deny FC Copenhagen a draw. Two months earlier Raphael Varane had said United could win the Champions League; the quadruple Champions League winner may need a rethink. There was no hint of a repeat of their most famous comeback against Bayern, nor even the sense of heroic failure that accompanied their 2010 quarter-final exit to the same club — just crushing failure. When Maguire hobbled off holding his groin and Evans came on, United had a man who played in their 2007-08 Champions League-winning campaign dragooned into service 16 years later.
