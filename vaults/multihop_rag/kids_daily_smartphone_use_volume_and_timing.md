# How Much And When Kids Use Their Phones

Kids spent a median of 4.5 hours per day on their phones, with the maximum amount of time reaching 16 hours, according to the study.

Checking behavior was frequent and highly variable. Most teens check their phones frequently, ranging from two to 498 times per day, and adolescents were more likely to check their phones over 100 times a day, the study found.

Use spilled well outside of free time. A whopping 97% of kids used their phones during school hours, while nearly 60% used them overnight -- between the hours of midnight and 5 a.m. -- on school nights.

Social media such as Instagram and Snapchat took up most screen time, at 32% during the school day, followed by YouTube and gaming, the study found. Overnight phone use was primarily spent engaging with the same media, although YouTube appeared to be the longest-running app because videos were often left playing during the night.

The overnight numbers are skewed by a minority: teens spent an average of 20 minutes per night on their phones, while some spent up to five hours. Sixty-seven percent of participants picked up their phones on school nights, but the median number of pickups per night was only once.
