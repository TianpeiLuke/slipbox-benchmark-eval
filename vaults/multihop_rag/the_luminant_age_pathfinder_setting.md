# The Luminant Age

The Luminant Age is a Pathfinder 2nd edition-compatible setting whose contributor-turned-fan describes becoming an immediate enthusiast after being invited to contribute.

What caught the writer's eye first were the Four Humanities ancestries, named Blood, Clay, Silk, and Horn. Many Thursday mornings of 2023 became a whirlwind of weird as the playtest party encountered teleporting flickrats, healed via esseweaving, and were hunted down by cultists in stryx bonemasques. The city they play in, Ourichor, is built on giant oil-rig-like platforms in the open ocean; there is no sun and there are no stars, but three moons that you are allowed to see. The predominant religion, the Angelites, memorialize their dead by preserving their eyeballs, which can be worn as mourning jewelry. The writer calls the setting as enchantingly addictive as the fictional ichor that propelled Ourichor into the renaissance era the project is named after, and notes the current scope: only two exquisitely illustrated supplements on creatures and equipment are available, with those who love the unusual advised to watch for more in years to come, such as the upcoming Paragons of Luminant Age, which introduces NPCs and explores their motives through the four moons.
