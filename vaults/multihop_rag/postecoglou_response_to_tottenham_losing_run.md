# Postecoglou's Response To Tottenham's Losing Run

Ange Postecoglou's response to the 7 December 2023 defeat was less damning than his captain's but still described the showing as poor: the claim is that Tottenham keep dominating games without taking advantage, and the warrant is that playing nice football can disguise how a team is going, so only winning counts as evidence of progress.

"It's another game where we've dominated a game of football but we haven't taken advantage," Postecoglou said. "I thought we were poor in both areas with our finishing and also both goals we conceded were terrible."

He then refused the consolations available to him and named the only remedy he would accept: "There's no point in feeling sorry for ourselves, looking for a cuddle anywhere. There's only one way to change our circumstances and that is to come here on Sunday and put in a performance, not just play good football but go out there and show some conviction about ourselves as a team." He tied that to a standard he says he set on arrival: "Sometimes we can disguise how we're going by playing some nice stuff but like I said from day one, that's not what I'm about. I want to win and that's why I came to this football club and that's the message. We've still got a long way to go, I've said that from the start, we're still right at the beginning what we need to create and days like today just give me further evidence and fuel of how much we need to do."
