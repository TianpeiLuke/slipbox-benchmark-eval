# FanDuel Sportsbook: MLB And NBA Markets, Props And Same-Game Parlays

FanDuel is a top destination for MLB bettors looking to bet daily and a name that resonates strongly within the NBA betting community, with popularity and success akin to its counterpart DraftKings. For baseball it offers the staple bets — run lines, where you bet on the margin of victory, and totals, predicting the combined score of both teams — plus dozens of player props allowing bettors to focus on individual performances such as hits, home runs or strikeouts, and an array of game props and futures bets for longer-term outcomes like division champions or MVP candidates.

For basketball, alongside the standard NBA odds, FanDuel stands out with an expansive selection of NBA player and game props, and a unique feature allowing users to create same-game parlays that combine NBA moneylines, totals and props into a personalized bet.

Newcomers to FanDuel's online sportsbook can typically claim $150 in bonus bets, and the book may offer three months of NBA League Pass to sweeten the deal. Claiming the promotion is straightforward and needs no special promo code: prospective bettors must sign up, deposit and place a $5 bet to be eligible.
