# Lucid Q3 2023 Deliveries

Lucid missed Wall Street delivery estimates in the third quarter by about 500 vehicles, TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-22. The automaker delivered 1,457 of its luxury Air sedans, reporting flat growth year-over-year.

The results sent shares down as investors worried about softening demand for Lucid's only EV.
