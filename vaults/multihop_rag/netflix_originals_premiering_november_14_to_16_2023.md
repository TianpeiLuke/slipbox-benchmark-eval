# Netflix Originals Premiering November 14 To 16, 2023

Ten Netflix originals appear in this block of Polygon's November 2023 listing, each with Netflix's own synopsis.

Criminal Code, a Netflix Series: to crack the code of an investigation into a larger-than-life robbery, federal agents need to get creative. Dubai Bling: Season 2: new relationships, new love and plenty of new drama as Dubai's favorite frenemies ball out and fall out in epic fashion. How to Become a Mob Boss, a Netflix Documentary: narrator Peter Dinklage guides a darkly satirical how-to guide exploring the rise and fall of history's most notorious mob bosses, from Al Capone to Pablo Escobar, and their tactics for success. The Netflix Cup: Swing to Survive, a Netflix Live Event: Netflix's first-ever live sports event, in which Formula 1 drivers from Drive to Survive pair with PGA TOUR professionals from Full Swing to compete in a match play tournament at Wynn Golf Club at Wynn Las Vegas, streaming live from 3 p.m. PT / 6 p.m. ET on Tuesday, November 14. Suburræterna, a Netflix Series: while chaos rules over Rome, established alliances are at risk as tensions rise with emerging clans, and the world of "Suburra" takes a new turn. Feedback, a Netflix Series: a former rock star with a faulty memory and a family in shambles, all due to his addiction problem, sets off on a frantic search for his missing son. Matt Rife: Natural Selection, a Netflix Comedy: a new stand-up special from the comedian. Best. Christmas. Ever!, a Netflix Film: after a twist of fate brings their families together for Christmas, Charlotte sets out to prove her old friend Jackie's life is too good to be true. The Crown: Season 6 Part 1: Queen Elizabeth II reflects on her legacy and lineage as Diana dazzles the public in the final weeks of her life and the monarchy faces a reckoning. In Love and Deep Water, a Netflix Film: romance, mystery and mayhem unfold aboard a luxury liner heading for the Aegean Sea when a butler and a passenger try to solve a baffling murder.
