# Taylor Swift's Attendance At Kansas City Chiefs Games

By early December 2023 Taylor Swift had attended five of Travis Kelce's games, including one on 3 December, often seated in the Kelce family suite — and since her first NFL appearance in September her occasional attendance had drawn pushback from NFL fans who believed her presence was distracting from the game itself. Swift told Time she has little sense of how much she is on screen: "There's a camera, like, a half-mile away, and you don't know where it is, and you have no idea when the camera is putting you in the broadcast, so I don't know if I'm being shown 17 times or once." She said she does not pay much attention to the cameras and fans around her because she is watching her boyfriend play.

Her answer to the criticism was direct: "I'm just there to support Travis. I have no awareness of if I'm being shown too much and pissing off a few dads, Brads, and Chads." Asked about the many Swifties who had started watching NFL games since she began dating Kelce, she joked: "Football is awesome, it turns out. I've been missing out my whole life." Her effect on the league was visible in October, when a Chiefs-Jets game she attended with famous friends became the most-watched Sunday NFL show since Super Bowl LVII in February 2023.
