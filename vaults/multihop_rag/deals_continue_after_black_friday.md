# Black Friday Is Not Your Last Chance To Save

Polygon argues that Black Friday likely will not be your last opportunity to save, even though there will be amazing deals during Black Friday and Cyber Monday on everything from tabletop and board games to toys and video games.

The grounds are the writer's own track record: as someone who has been covering deals for The Verge and Polygon for years, he says he is shocked by how often he sees these low prices — often spoken of as if they are a flash in the pan — extending through much of December. The warrant is that the scarcity framing around the weekend is a marketing artefact rather than a description of when prices actually fall. The argument carries its own qualifier, which is what saves it from complacency: it is more likely that price cuts on products you want will go unnoticed unless you are tracking deals yourself.
