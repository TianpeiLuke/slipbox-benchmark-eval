# Amazon Prime Free Games And DLC

Amazon offers freebies for Prime members round the calendar year, not just during the holiday months, and Polygon reports that Amazon's suite of giveaways has improved over time.

The catalog is not fixed: offerings expire over time and new ones are added. As a sample of what was up for grabs as of the article's November 2023 publication, Polygon named Rage 2, Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic, and Doom 3, and said it collects the latest free games and DLC on an ongoing page.
