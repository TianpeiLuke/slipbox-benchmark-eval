# BG3 Cleric: The Healer And Its Seven Domains

Polygon names Cleric the best healer class in Baldur's Gate 3. Clerics are the other side of the Paladin's mix of magic and melee: less frontline, with only Light and Medium armors, but with more spells and more powerful ones.

Clerics get an extra boost from Channel Divinity, which grants an additional Divine magic ability such as Turning Undead to drive away zombies, or Guided Strike, which gives a +10 to Attack Rolls. A Cleric's subclass is called a Domain and is chosen at character creation from a list of seven: Life, Light, Trickery, Knowledge, Nature, Tempest, and War. Each Domain has different spells, actions, and even proficiencies attached. Broadly, Life focuses on healing; Light emphasizes Radiant and Fire magic both in and out of combat; Trickery is good for sneaking; Knowledge grants extra Skills out of combat plus brain-based spells; Nature is flavoured a little more like a Druid; Tempest is more combat- and Thunder damage-focused; and War is all about combat. Light Domain is the best subclass for a Cleric, with the reasoning and the skill and feat picks left to Polygon's separate Cleric build guide.
