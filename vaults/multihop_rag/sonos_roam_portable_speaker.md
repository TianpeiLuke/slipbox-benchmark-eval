# Sonos Roam Portable Speaker

The Sonos Roam was $134, down from $180 — 26 percent off at Best Buy and Sonos. It is a truly portable Sonos speaker with a rugged design built to withstand the elements, featuring wireless charging and support for AirPlay 2, Alexa, and Google Assistant, and it is senior commerce editor Brandon Widder's pick.

For the longest time his go-to portable speaker for camping and backpacking was the Ultimate Ears Roll 2: small and efficient, but it did not mesh well with the rest of his audio setup, especially on sweltering summer days when he barely made it beyond his own backyard.

A couple of years ago he splurged on the Sonos Roam. The rugged, pint-sized device is on the pricier side compared with other Bluetooth speakers, but it produces solid sound for the size, offers wireless charging, and can automatically jump between his home Wi-Fi network and Bluetooth — a convenience he has come to appreciate when strapping the speaker to his bike and heading out the door.

One limit: the Roam only supports voice commands when connected to Wi-Fi, so while he cannot fire off his usual quips at Alexa in the backcountry, he certainly can still do it poolside with a drink in hand.
