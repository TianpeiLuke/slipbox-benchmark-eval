# Harper's Island (Slasher Series)

Harper's Island is a 2009 slasher miniseries that Polygon calls a perfect low-rent masterpiece and the show that makes slasher TV look fun and easy. It was the Oct. 12 pick in the 2023-10-10 Halloween Countdown, available to purchase on Amazon, Apple, and Vudu.

Critic Zosha Millman's premise is that in theory slasher TV should be easier to pull off, since an episodic structure lends itself well to a slowly dwindling cast with a killer on the loose and a different death each episode — and yet show after show flubs it because they cannot find interesting enough characters or dilemmas in what should be an endlessly engaging premise. The rules are different on Harper's Island.

The small Pacific Northwest island was home to a serial killing in which Abby, played by Elaine Cassidy, lost her mom almost a decade earlier; she returns at the start of the series only under the most extreme of circumstances, her best friend Henry's wedding, played by Christopher Gorham, and the island's deadly legacy is about to get a whole lot worse. Being from 2009, the show feels like a time capsule for both bootcut jeans and miniseries; at a tight 13 episodes it knows what it is about — killing people — and gets right to it, brutally. Millman's admiration is for how long it sustains a levelheaded approach to a purely absurd And Then There Were None scenario, right down to how long nobody knows they are being picked off, with almost no death like the last, which seems purely impractical from a serial killing perspective but makes for great TV; the episode titles are the onomatopoeias for how people die.
