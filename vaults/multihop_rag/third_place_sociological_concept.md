# The Third Place

The third place is a sociological term that refers to a physical space that isn't work or home, where people can socialize without the obligations of productivity. The membership test is simple: if you can hang out there, it's a third place.

The category has boundary cases in both directions. During the pandemic, virtual communities served as ad hoc third places in lieu of coffee shops, bookstores and gyms, and Twitch, which saw exponential growth during the pandemic, was one of them. In the other direction, a place can be plentiful and still fail the role: last year's San Diego-based TwitchCon, for all of its faults, took place in a walkable city abundant with nearby places to hang out, allowing attendees to bond, network and interact outside of the convention center, while there's no shortage of third places in Las Vegas — but for many TwitchCon attendees, nightclubs and casinos aren't accessible alternatives to San Diego's more casual venues.
