# Drake London Home Road Fantasy Split

CBSSports.com's Dec. 19, 2023 Week 16 wide receiver preview reported that Drake London was averaging 15.88 PPR fantasy points per game at home that season and just 7.37 on the road.

The adjacent note in the same list recorded that after missing the previous week's game, Tyreek Hill needed to average almost 153 yards per game in his final three to reach his stated goal of 2,000 receiving yards that season.
