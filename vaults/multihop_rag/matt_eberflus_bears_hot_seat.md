# Matt Eberflus Bears Hot Seat

Yardbarker's Nov. 30, 2023 NFC hot-seat roundup named head coach Matt Eberflus as the Chicago Bears' hottest seat, on the record he had compiled: the Bears had only one division win during Eberflus' tenure, and his seven wins were the fewest by a Bears head coach through his first 29 games.

The qualifier is that in his second season as head coach the defense had improved, but positive results were hard to come by behind a lackluster offense ranked 19th in yards (323.2) and 21st in points per game (20.2). The warrant is that partial improvement on one side of the ball does not offset a historically poor win total for the franchise.
