# WIRED's Prime Day 2023 Apple Roundup Scope

WIRED's Apple-specific Prime Day roundup exists because discounts on Apple products are few and far between: with Amazon's second big sale event of the year, officially called Prime Big Deal Days, it found a variety of Apple deals covering iPads, Apple Watches, iPhone cases, and MagSafe accessories.

Its scope is not limited to one store — the deals aren't just at Amazon, and WIRED links to other retailers like Best Buy when available — and the list is maintained as the event runs: an October 11 update added new deals on the Apple Magic Keyboard, Apple Studio Display, ESR HaloLock 3-in-1 Travel Wireless Charging Set, Monoprice Extra Wide Microfiber Desk Mat, and Apple AirTags.
