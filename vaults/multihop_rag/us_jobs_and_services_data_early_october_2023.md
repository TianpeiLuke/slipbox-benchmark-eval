# US Jobs And Services Data In Early October 2023

Wednesday's ADP report suggested private employers added 89,000 jobs last month, a much sharper slowdown in hiring than the 140,000 economists expected. The report does not have a perfect track record in predicting what the more comprehensive jobs report from the US government will say, which was due to arrive on the Friday. Mike Loewengart, head of model portfolio construction at Morgan Stanley Global Investment Office, said that "if Friday's report also shows the labour market is cooling, stock investors may worry a little less about indefinitely higher interest rates".

A second report on the economy said growth in US services industries slowed in September by a touch more than economists expected. The figures were reported by The Age on 4 October 2023 alongside a note that oil prices slumped overnight.
