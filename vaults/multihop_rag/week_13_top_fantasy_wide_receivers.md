# Week 13's Top Fantasy Wide Receivers

Sporting News argued on Nov. 29, 2023 that Tyreek Hill (@ Commanders) belonged in the top spot for Week 13 PPR leagues, on the grounds that he did his thing despite a tough Week 12 matchup and now faced a putrid Washington stop unit. CeeDee Lamb (vs. Seahawks) was not far behind, with Dallas' WR1 aiming to maintain his efficient play against Seattle.

Usage was the warrant for the next tier. Amon-Ra St. Brown (@ Saints) had been a target hog, seeing at least nine looks in each of his past six games, with no reason to expect his workload to decrease in a bounce-back game for the Lions; Keenan Allen (@ Patriots) looked to continue his dominant ways as Justin Herbert's go-to target with routine double-digit target potential. Mike Evans (vs. Panthers) sat in the top five having totalled four touchdowns over the past three weeks. A.J. Brown (vs. 49ers) faced a sound San Francisco coverage unit in Sunday's NFC Championship rematch, but his ability to dominate in the red zone still made him a top-10 PPR play.

Two arguments came with caveats. Ja'Marr Chase (@ Jaguars) averaged over 20 yards per reception in Week 12, and although the drop-off from Joe Burrow to Jake Browning is massive, Chase still had WR1 upside given his usage. Matchup carried the rest: Michael Pittman Jr. (@ Texans) faced a Houston pass defense that had been shredded for weeks, so another 100-yard receiving day could be in store; Jaylen Waddle (@ Commanders) was looking to post a second consecutive 100-plus-yard day against a struggling Commanders coverage unit; and Tank Dell (vs. Broncos) continued to rise, rounding out the top 10 as a trusted option of C.J. Stroud's.
