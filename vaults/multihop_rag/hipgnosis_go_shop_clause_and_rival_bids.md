# Hipgnosis Go-Shop Clause and Rival Bids

The 'go-shop' clause in the HSF/HSC proposal is the mechanism connecting the $440 million offer to the date of Hipgnosis Songs Fund's continuation vote. While HSF shareholders consider HSC's offer for the 29 catalogs, other non-Hipgnosis companies are also invited to bid for the same portfolio; the clause allows the HSF board to consider those approaches for a limited period, until 11.59pm on 23 October 2023. On 28 September, HSF informed investors that "credible third parties are already engaged in this go-shop process" following its 14 September announcement.

Two conditions constrain how far a rival can get. HSM, on behalf of HSC, holds a 'matching right': if HSC decides to match a rival's higher bid, HSF has to sell the catalogs to HSC rather than to the rival. Separately, any 'superior' offer would have to exceed the aggregate cash net proceeds that would ultimately reach HSF's coffers if HSC's $440 million offer were executed. Within those limits it remains a theoretical possibility that a non-Hipgnosis company swoops for the 29 catalogs and gazumps HSC.

The consequence for the timetable is the reason the continuation vote date was only "likely". HSF said on 28 September that if a superior proposal is received during the go-shop period — whether or not HSC uses its matching right to beat it — it may be necessary to adjourn the HSF AGM, and therefore the 2023 continuation vote, into November.
