# SuperDraft Player Prop Parlay Mechanics

On SuperDraft, users win 3X the amount wagered if a two-legged player prop parlay hits, and 10x their entry if all four of the day's featured props hit — so a $20 entry fee would yield a $200 payout. The more legs added to a player prop parlay, the larger the potential payout becomes, but as the long odds indicate, completing a clean sweep is challenging.

The practical step is that the four featured picks need not be played together. Instead of parlaying all four, a user could make separate two-legged parlays to increase win probability, or attempt to hit it big by combining all four picks for the 10x potential payout.
