# Giving Experiences Instead Of Material Gifts

Another way to save money at the holidays is to skip material gifts and grant your time instead — in many cases your family would rather spend quality time with you.

Credit Karma's Courtney Alev recommended planning low-cost activities such as a hike with your family or attending a free event in your city. Other options she listed include a holiday movie marathon with your friends, a free museum with your parents, or a volunteering day with your family.
