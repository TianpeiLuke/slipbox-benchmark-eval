# X's Content Moderation Retrenchment Under Musk

TechCrunch's argument is that X under Musk has very evidently been flying the ship in the opposite direction to the responsible governance the DSA intends to encourage — which is why it attracted so many warnings from EU lawmakers even long before the formal probe of 18 December 2023.

The one piece of countervailing evidence is acknowledged: it is notable that X recently started offering a research program for outsiders to get data on systemic risks arising on the platform in the EU. But the Commission obviously has doubts that X has gone far enough on the transparency front to meet the DSA's bar.

The warrant connecting the retrenchment to regulatory exposure is that EU regulators are set to assess Musk and company's claims that replacing a formal content moderation function with crowdsourced opinions is a valid approach to tackling diverse information risks, and consequently to look into how much Musk's gutting of content moderation teams and processes has cost X's users in terms of harm exposure.
