# USC's Remaining Schedule Gives It A Path Despite The Gonzaga Loss

Sporting News argued on Dec. 9, 2023 that there was no question USC had enough talent in place to make a run at a title despite what went down against Gonzaga, and that the shape of its schedule offered a great opportunity on paper to get off to a strong start.

The grounds are the specific fixtures. Following the Long Beach State game the Trojans would visit Auburn and Alabama State as they ventured into the south to prepare for conference play. USC opened Pac-12 play at Oregon and Oregon State late that month before hosting Cal and Stanford, and would only be an underdog against the Ducks.

The counterweight is the back half: the Trojans play Arizona and UCLA twice, so their schedule is difficult. The warrant that carries the claim is conditional on one variable — if James is special as a difference maker, the Trojans are talented enough to factor into the Pac-12 race.
