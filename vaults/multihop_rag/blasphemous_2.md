# Blasphemous 2

Blasphemous 2, developed by The Game Kitchen for Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, Windows PC, Xbox One, and Xbox Series X, is ranked 47 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31. Polygon's claim is that long gone are the days when the Metroidvania genre languished untouched for years on end, and that despite an influx of entries in recent years few have exhibited as much mastery as this one.

Building off the strong roots of the first game, Blasphemous 2 continues to use Spanish Catholicism as a narrative and aesthetic touchpoint, telling a twisted religious tale that is about as far from proselytizing as you can get.

Critic Russ Frushtick notes that after the success of the first game the developers focused on refining the combat, adding multiple weapons and bizarre, hidden customization options that let players take command of how their character rips this world to shreds. The last 2D Metroidvania he played with this much polish and charm was Hollow Knight; Blasphemous 2 might not reach those same heights, but it comes damn close.
