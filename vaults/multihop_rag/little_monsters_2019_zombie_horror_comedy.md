# Little Monsters (2019): The Kindergarten Zombie Comedy

Little Monsters is Abe Forsythe's 2019 horror comedy, running 1h 34m, starring Lupita Nyong'o, Alexander England and Josh Gad. England (Alien: Covenant) plays Dave, a foul-mouthed and down-on-his-luck rock musician living with his sister and nephew after a rough breakup. Trying to get on the good side of Miss Caroline (Nyong'o), his nephew's kindergarten teacher, Dave agrees to chaperone the class's field trip to a petting zoo.

The comic complication is that the petting zoo sits right next to a US Army base experiencing a zombie outbreak. As the class is cornered by the undead horde, Dave has to help Miss Caroline get everyone out alive — and whether he wins her heart or at least grows as a person is left unspoiled by Polygon, which does note that Josh Gad gets attacked by zombies in the process. Little Monsters is available to stream on Hulu.
