# StarNews Mobile As An African Mobile Video Network

StarNews Mobile is an African mobile video network that offers African content creators a platform for monetizing their work through a subscription model, and which had secured $3 million in pre-Series A funding as of 30 October 2023. As of that date the service was available in six African countries — Cameroon, where it started, plus Nigeria, Ghana, Congo, Benin and Ivory Coast.

What distinguishes it from the platforms creators would otherwise use is the route to the audience and the route to payment. The five-year-old mobile video network prioritizes offering monetization avenues for African content creators through distribution partnerships with major telecommunications operators like MTN and Orange, because content creators often face challenges in generating revenue due to limitations on existing streaming platforms. StarNews therefore connects those creators to mobile users who often lack access to relevant content, both because mobile data plans are expensive and because there is a shortage of services tailored to local markets — a win-win for both parties.

The concept came out of an adjacent business rather than from streaming. Founder and CEO Guy Kamgaing, a Los Angeles-based Cameroonian entrepreneur, launched the startup in 2017, drawing on his extensive 10-year experience in content monetization, particularly within the VAS (Value-Added Services) business, during which he actively participated in text- and SMS-based campaigns and monetization endeavors that provided the foundation for StarNews.

Kamgaing described the founding insight as joining an unmonetized supply of content to a distribution relationship he already had: "I realized that while I could monetize jokes and horoscopes via telcos for several years, no one was monetizing content on the traditional platforms, especially in Africa. There are thousands of creators online that don't make money on YouTube or other platforms. So it was just putting two and two together, saying, 'Well, this content is here that no one knows about and is very difficult to get the audience on YouTube and search, but I can help creators make money because I have a relationship with the telcos.'"
