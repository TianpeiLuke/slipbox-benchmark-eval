# The Teenage Engineering EP-133 K.O. II Sampler

The EP-133 K.O. II — full name the EP-133 K.O. II 64MB Sampler Composer — is Teenage Engineering's sampler, and its defining surprise was that it both looked cool and, at $299, was reasonably priced.

Engadget set that against a theme running through Teenage Engineering's recent products: "you need more money." The Field range represents the Swedish company's most exclusive music making gear, so when its website teased a new product with a colorful countdown, the wallets of Teenies everywhere braced for impact. When the timer hit all zeros, the big reveal turned out to be the EP-133 sampler instead.
