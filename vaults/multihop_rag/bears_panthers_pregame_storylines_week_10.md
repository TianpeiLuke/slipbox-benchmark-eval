# Bears-Panthers Pregame Storylines

In the hours before the Nov. 9, 2023 "Thursday Night Football" game, Sporting News' live blog flagged the quarterback matchup, turnovers, a key Panthers absence and the weather as the storylines to watch in a meeting of teams with a combined three wins.

At 6:15 p.m. it was confirmed that Tyson Bagent would start for the Bears, making it an undrafted rookie facing a rookie No. 1 pick — and despite that, Chicago was favored. At 6:35 p.m. the blog noted that Bryce Young had thrown an interception in only one of his last five games before crashing back to Earth with three picks against the Colts the previous week, and that more than ever it felt like the game would be won by the quarterback who could avoid turnovers, with Bagent having thrown five picks in his last two starts. At 6:50 p.m. standout edge rusher Brian Burns was ruled out with a concussion, a major blow to the Panthers' defense; the team listed Burns, CJ Henderson and DJ Chark among its inactives.

Nearer kickoff the notes turned to conditions and presentation. At 7:30 p.m. the Bears were confirmed to be donning orange, including an alternate orange helmet. At 7:46 p.m. it was 52 degrees and clear in Chicago — not as cold as it could be in the Windy City at that time of year, but still a rare colder-weather game for Bryce Young. At 8:00 p.m. the blog resolved to keep spirits high despite the matchup, on the principle that all football is good football, and at 8:09 p.m. joked that perhaps the most interesting storyline of the night would be whether Al Michaels could keep a brave face through what might not be the most appealing game.
