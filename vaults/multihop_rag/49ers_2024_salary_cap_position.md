# The 49ers' 2024 Salary Cap Position

As of Oct. 31, 2023, the 49ers did not project to have much cap space in 2024 — OverTheCap listed San Francisco with just $1,479,772 in available space, the seventh-least in the NFL.

The team was nonetheless equipped to increase that space with relative ease. It had at least one player on the roster, Randy Gregory, with an eight-digit salary but no guaranteed money left on his deal, meaning the 49ers could release him without taking a dead-cap hit — the financial term for a player no longer on the roster still being owed guaranteed money — which would open up $14 million more in cap space. Beyond that, the team had 10 other players on the active roster set to have cap hits of at least $14.1 million who could restructure or extend their contracts.

Those 10, with their 2024 cap hit, base salary and dead-cap hit, were: Trent Williams $31.7 million / $20.1 million / $29.9 million; Arik Armstead $28.6 million / $17.4 million / $25.9 million; Deebo Samuel $28.6 million / $21 million / $26.5 million; Fred Warner $24.5 million / $15.3 million / $25.6 million; George Kittle $22 million / $13.4 million / $18.9 million; Charvarius Ward $17.9 million / $12.6 million / $17.2 million; Javon Hargrave $15.5 million / $8.9 million / $33.4 million; Nick Bosa $14.6 million / $1.1 million / $76.5 million; Brandon Aiyuk $14.1 million / $14.1 million / $14.1 million; and Christian McCaffrey $14.1 million / $11.8 million / $8.6 million.
