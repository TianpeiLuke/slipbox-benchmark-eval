# Masks Of The Masks

Masks of the Masks, by Hazel Amber Goswick, is a tabletop RPG presented as a fully illustrated comic book — an homage to Bronze Age superhero stories that, like Watchmen, critically explores and examines the violence inherent in its medium. The contributor recommending it says superhero comics remain their life's great passion, and that of everything they played in 2023 this is the game they want to shout about from the rooftops.

As an object it is described as an incredible piece of art, an absolute joy to behold as you flip through it, with love, consideration, and attention to detail dripping off every move and every panel. In play the contributor calls it a revelation: a brilliant blend of Belonging Outside Belonging and Powered by the Apocalypse that builds on luminaries of both systems to create something that sings. They also single out some of the most wicked advancement mechanics they have run into in years, which add to the play experience while heightening the ever-looming sense of dread and tragedy essential to comics of that period.
