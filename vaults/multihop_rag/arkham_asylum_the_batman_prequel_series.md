# Arkham Asylum (The Batman Prequel Series)

Arkham Asylum is the second of Matt Reeves' two HBO Max spinoffs of The Batman, intended as a prequel to the movie; its actual title had not been revealed as of Polygon's 2023-10-24 report.

The project changed shape during development. It was previously planned as a series about the workings of the Gotham police department, but mutated in the development stage, and Boardwalk Empire creator Terence Winter, originally in charge of the series, departed the project.

In its current form Reeves has described the series as an origin story for characters seen in Arkham in The Batman, with more of a horror movie or "haunted house" feel. The Staircase's Antonio Campos is now the showrunner.
