# Gamer Reactions At EGX To The Microsoft-Activision Deal

BBC Newsbeat spoke to fans at the gaming convention EGX in the week of 13 October 2023 to get their take on the Microsoft-Activision deal, and the reactions it gathered were positive.

Oliver, 16, said he thought the deal might be good news for Call of Duty: "It will just expand the market for gaming, because it's a company with more money to fund the games," he said.

Sophie, who was named Ubisoft's content creator of the year, told the BBC she "just wants the games to win." "We want everyone to be able to play video games that they want to play, we want them to be accessible for everyone," she said. "Obviously, we're going to have our opinions, but if the end result is good games, that's fine with us."
