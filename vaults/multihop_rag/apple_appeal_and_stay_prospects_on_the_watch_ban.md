# Apple's Appeal And Stay Prospects On The Watch Ban

Apple has already indicated it plans to appeal the ITC ruling, but appealing is a lengthy process that can take around 18 months, and while the company will also probably ask for a stay on the import ban, Smith Brittingham said a stay is not likely to be granted.

The reason is the standard involved: to win a stay Apple would have to prove it would be irreparably harmed — in other words, make a convincing case that it would go out of business if it can't sell the Apple Watch. The Verge noted that a hail mary veto is unlikely, but that doesn't mean Apple is going to simply accept an import ban on a $17 billion segment of its business. Apple spokesperson Nikki Rothberg told The Verge in a statement that the company was "pursuing a range of legal and technical options to ensure that Apple Watch is available to customers," which the outlet read as meaning the watch is going back on sale one way or another — the only question being which path Apple takes.
