# UMG's USCO Filing Rejecting Fair Use For AI Training

Universal Music Group's response to the US Copyright Office rejected the notion that using copyrighted material to train large language models is "fair use" — a position MBW called unsurprising and the starkest possible contrast to Google's assertion that prohibiting or limiting the use of copyrighted materials for AI training would undermine the purpose of copyright and foreclose the opportunities of the technology.

The filing's own example puts the claim bluntly: "The wholesale appropriation of UMG's enormous catalog of copyright-protected sound recordings and musical compositions to build multibillion commercial enterprises [in AI] is anything but fair use."
