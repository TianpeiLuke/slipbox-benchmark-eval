# Overdraft Fees Are A Choice That Disproportionately Harms Black And Brown Communities

Sen. Menendez argued at the December 2023 Senate hearing that charging overdraft fees is a choice rather than a necessity, and a choice that disproportionately harms Black and brown communities — so eliminating the fees would "change the course of events for a significant part of [your] consumer base."

The grounds Fortune cites for the disparity claim are research findings: a 2021 study found that Hispanic Americans paid $3.1 billion in overdraft fees a year and Black Americans were hit with $1.4 billion in total, and another survey found they paid double the amount of fees that White people did. The warrant joining the grounds to the claim is the framing Menendez pressed on the banks: because the fees are discretionary rather than structurally required, the unequal burden they impose is an outcome the banks choose and could therefore reverse.
