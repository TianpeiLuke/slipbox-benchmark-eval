# Max Kepler And Jorge Polanco On The Twins' Trade Market

MLB Network's Jon Morosi reported on Sunday, Dec. 17, 2023 that Max Kepler and Jorge Polanco could both be on the trade market, though their market could hinge on the status of other infield free agents.

The Twins were coming off an AL Central title but could be looking to deal away two key players from the team. Kepler appeared in 130 games for Minnesota and posted an impressive .260/.332/.484 slash line with 24 homers. Polanco had been moved to an infield utility role with Edouard Julien and Royce Lewis emerging as regulars, but was still solid in 80 games, logging a .255/.335/.454 slash with 14 homers.
