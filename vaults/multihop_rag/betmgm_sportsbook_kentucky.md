# BetMGM Sportsbook In Kentucky

BetMGM is the mobile sportsbook of a company long known for its resorts and casinos, distinguished among Kentucky books by billing itself as "the king of parlays" and by a signup offer aimed at larger players, as described by CBS Sports on 13 October 2023.

The parlay claim rests on extensive options for that bet type, and the app features boosted bets, in-game betting, same game parlays and more across all major and professional sports. The signup offer is a $1,500 risk-free offer, which credits a customer with bonus bets in the event their first real-money wager loses; by contrast the boosts and risk-free offers on the site cater to smaller players, since the limits for those tokens are generally less than at the other sites.

Its rewards program is the other differentiator: credits can be redeemed for bonus bets or MGM Resort credit, and with a friendly interface the report calls this sportsbook one of the best in the business for a reason.
