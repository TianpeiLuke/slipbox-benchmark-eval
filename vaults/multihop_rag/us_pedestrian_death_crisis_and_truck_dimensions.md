# The US Pedestrian Death Crisis And Truck Dimensions

Trucks are historically a nightmare for pedestrian safety — the Cybertruck is a truck, and the same goes for every Ford, GMC, Hummer, and Ram truck on the road today.

The Verge identifies the underlying issues as weight, height, and dimensions, and says these are what have contributed to the current crisis in pedestrian safety, in which more people are dying on the road than at any time in the last 40 years.
