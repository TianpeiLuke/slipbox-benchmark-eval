# Jones Defends Leaving Cooper And Hooper Out Of The World Cup Squad

Against criticism of his squad selection, Eddie Jones said on Tuesday, Oct. 16, 2023 that he had no regrets about leaving experienced players such as Quade Cooper and Michael Hooper out of the side for the 2023 World Cup.

He conceded the critics' right to their view while rejecting its authority over his decision: "Everyone's got their own judgment and they're allowed to have that judgment."

His own reasoning rests on a longer horizon than the tournament just played: "My choice was that I decided to go with youth. And I stand by that because I think we've got a lot of good young players who are really going to benefit from that experience at the World Cup, and going forward to the 2027 World Cup."
