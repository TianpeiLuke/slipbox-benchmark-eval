# Bankman-Fried's Post-Bankruptcy Media Tour

After FTX declared bankruptcy, Bankman-Fried went on an extensive media tour, doing interviews with The New York Times' Andrew Ross Sorkin and Good Morning America — and he kept giving interviews even under house arrest, including with The New Yorker.

The Verge's reading of his motive: Bankman-Fried seemed to think that if he just explained things, everyone would understand that what he did was a mistake, not a crime.
