# GPT Model Lineage And The ChatGPT Launch

GPT (Generative Pre-trained Transformer) is the large language model that ChatGPT runs on, and OpenAI had been developing it since 2016 — unveiling GPT-1 in 2018 and iterating it to GPT-3 by June 2020. With the November 30, 2022 release of GPT-3.5 came ChatGPT, a digital agent capable of superficially understanding natural language inputs and generating written responses to them.

The first iteration's limits were plain: it was rather slow to answer and couldn't speak to questions about anything that happened after September 2021, not to mention its issues answering queries with misinformation during bouts of "hallucinations." Even so, that kludgy first iteration demonstrated capabilities far beyond what other state-of-the-art digital assistants like Siri and Alexa could provide — the discriminating feature that set it apart from what came before.
