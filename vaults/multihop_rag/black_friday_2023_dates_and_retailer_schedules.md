# Black Friday 2023 Dates And Retailer Schedules

Black Friday 2023 lands on November 24, with Cyber Monday 2023 following a few days later on November 27 — but the early deals have already begun, and Engadget says the entire month of November might as well be renamed Holiday Shopping Month, as that's when companies really start to get serious with their discounts.

Walmart Black Friday deals begin early this year on November 8: Walmart+ members get a few hours of early access if they shop online on November 8, with access for everyone else starting later that day, and Walmart will match its online Black Friday deals in stores starting November 10. Amazon will likely have a Black Friday sale in 2023 — while the online retailer hasn't made its deals known yet like Walmart has, it will almost certainly have deals across all the types of products it sells, and Engadget anticipates record-low prices on Amazon devices in particular, like Echo speakers, Fire TVs, Kindles and more, since in years past Amazon Black Friday deals have brought some of the best prices of the year on headphones, tablets, smartwatches, robot vacuums and more. Most retailers have Black Friday sales, so Target, Best Buy and others are expected to join Walmart and Amazon in discounting inventory, and in consumer electronics most companies are expected to run some sort of promotion — meaning big-ticket items from brands like Apple, Samsung, Google, Sony, LG, Sonos and others should be available much cheaper.
