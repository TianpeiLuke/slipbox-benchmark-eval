# PPDA: Passes Allowed Per Defensive Action

PPDA is the number of opposition passes allowed outside of the pressing team's own defensive third, divided by the number of defensive actions by the pressing team outside of their own defensive third, as defined by Sky Sports on 28 October 2023.

The discriminating feature of the metric is the direction in which it reads: a lower figure indicates a higher level of pressing, while a higher figure indicates a lower level of pressing. Both the numerator and the denominator are restricted to the same zone of the pitch — everything outside the pressing team's own defensive third — so the measure describes pressing in the attacking two-thirds rather than defending near one's own goal.
