# WeWork's Chapter 11 Bankruptcy And Collapse

As predicted, flexible office-space firm WeWork filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection, listing over $18.6 billion of debt in a remarkable collapse for the once high-flying startup co-founded by Adam Neumann and bankrolled by SoftBank, BlackRock and Goldman Sachs, TechCrunch reported on November 11, 2023.

The filing had been visible in the market a week earlier. On November 3, 2023, TechCrunch wrote it would not be surprised if WeWork ended up filing for bankruptcy, noting the company had been grappling with significant challenges.

The equity collapse behind that expectation: WeWork's stock hit a new low, dropping over 47% after hours to just $1.21, which brought the company's market cap down to a mere $121 million — a drastic fall from the $47 billion valuation it achieved after its SoftBank-led Series H round in early 2019.
