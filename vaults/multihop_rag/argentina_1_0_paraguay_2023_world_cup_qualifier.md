# Argentina 1-0 Paraguay, CONMEBOL World Cup Qualifying, October 2023

Argentina beat Paraguay 1-0 at the Estadio Monumental in Buenos Aires on Oct. 12, 2023, with Nicolas Otamendi's fourth-minute goal the only score, taking Argentina to nine points from a possible nine to start World Cup qualifying.

Argentina completely dominated from start to finish, both with and without Lionel Messi, though it was a bit more nervy than they would have liked at the end. Otamendi's early goal seemed to foreshadow a coming onslaught, yet that was all Argentina would get, and while it was enough for victory they could have had many, many more. Messi came off the bench shortly after halftime but even he was powerless to change the narrative, hitting the post twice including once directly from a corner. Lautaro Martinez, Rodrigo de Paul and Nico Gonzalez were all very active in the final third and had multiple chances, but none found the all-important second goal to kill the match off. At the other end Emiliano Martinez had another clean sheet as Paraguay failed to produce more than a single quality attacking chance.

The match details were kickoff at 8 p.m. local (7 p.m. ET / 4 p.m. PT), location Estadio Monumental in Buenos Aires, and referee Raphael Claus of Brazil. The Sporting News' verdict at fulltime was that Argentina were most certainly deserved victors but made it harder than it had to be by failing to finish a whole host of great chances: the entire team was impressive until it came time to shoot.
