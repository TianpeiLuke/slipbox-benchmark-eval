# Prime Day 2023 Headphone Deals In WIRED's Roundup

WIRED's Prime Big Deal Days audio picks include Sony's WF-1000XM5, the Jabra Elite 7 Active as an invite-only deal, Anker's Space A40 and Liberty 4 NC at $80 ($20 off), an office noise-cancelling favorite, and its favorite workout headphones for running and biking.

While its predecessor was one of the best pairs of wireless earbuds around, the WF-1000XM5 (7/10, WIRED Recommends) is still no slouch: balanced sound, comfortable to wear, active noise cancellation, and up to eight hours on a single charge with ANC on, stretching to around 12 hours without. The Jabra Elite 7 Active top WIRED's list of favorite workout earbuds: released in 2021 but still the smallest, with the teeniest case, and the most comfortable tried, with a rubber tip that kept them securely in place while running even under a beanie rubbing against them, customizable ambient noise via the Sound+ app, IP57 sweat resistance, 8 hours of battery life and up to 30 hours in the case. Anker's Space A40 earbuds (8/10, WIRED Recommends) are packed with features for the price — noise canceling, wireless charging, 10 hours of battery life — and sound good while being lightweight and comfortable, which is why reviewers say they nearly give Apple's AirPods a run for their money; Anker's Liberty 4 NC (8/10, WIRED Recommends) are another feature-packed pair with great noise canceling. The office pick uses advanced signal processing and four built-in microphones to limit surrounding sound, making it great for focusing in a busy environment and for Zoom calls, and while these often fluctuate in price WIRED thinks this is a great deal. The running and biking pick suits any sport where you must stay aware of traffic but still want to listen to podcasts; they fit under a helmet and have a decent 10-hour battery life.
