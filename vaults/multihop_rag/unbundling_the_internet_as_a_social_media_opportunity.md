# Unbundling The Internet As A Social Media Opportunity

The Verge argued that the social media landscape now faces the same opportunity Craigslist's challengers had: a rare chance to unbundle the internet, to pull apart an existing system and rebuild it, piece by piece, in vastly better ways.

The claim's condition is explicit — "if we do this correctly," meaning if the next phase of how we congregate and communicate online is built for humans and not advertisers. The grounds and the warrant are one and the same prediction about what success would look like: there won't be a new titanic company to rival Meta or a platform with eye-poppingly huge numbers like Facebook. What we get instead, the argument runs, is something much bigger — an entirely new infrastructure for our online lives that no company or platform controls.
