# Fintech Startups On The 2023 Ranking Lists

Several fintech startups recently made LinkedIn's top startups of 2023 list: Ramp placed No. 1, Synctera No. 11, Esusu No. 13, Sardine No. 16, and Tapcheck No. 48.

CB Insights also released its Fintech 100 list, made up of the "most promising 100 fintech startups of 2023."
