# The Swans' Matchday Experience Model For Local Attendance

The Sydney Swans' attendance strategy, described on 26 October 2023 by executive GM of marketing Loretta Johns, has two linked components: target local communities, and make match days compelling for fans on and off the field.

The off-field half is delivered through food, entertainment and promotion. "Our team has put a lot of work into sourcing some wonderful food offerings, entertainment and activities for kids, while we've had a local focus in promoting our games," Johns said. At North Sydney Oval that means quince paste and hand-picked grapes, cheddar cheese and biscuits in a little box with beautiful wrapping — "they've really thought about what they're doing and made it an experience."

The local-community half works through partnership as a promotional channel, not just a supplier relationship. At Henson Park, the Swans' other home ground in Sydney's gentrified inner west, "they've gone to microbreweries in the local area and invited them in, but also promoted through their clientele to come to the football" — the local business both provides the offering and supplies an audience.
