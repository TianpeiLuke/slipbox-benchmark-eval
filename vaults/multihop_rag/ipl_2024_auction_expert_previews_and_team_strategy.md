# IPL 2024 Auction: Expert Previews And Team Strategy

Pundits at the IPL 2024 auction argued that franchises should shop to their squad's specific gaps rather than chase headline names, with the ground conditions and existing leadership setting each team's priorities. AB de Villiers made the case for Royal Challengers Bangalore at 12:50 PM IST: "RCB will focus on the bowlers because it is difficult to defend at the Chinnaswamy Stadium. I am very excited with the Cameron Green but I am sure the major focus will be on the bowlers." The warrant is the venue itself — a ground where defending totals is hard makes bowling the binding constraint.

Zaheer Khan made a parallel case for Lucknow Super Giants at 12:51 PM IST: "LSG will be looking at the smart buys. That should be their thought process. They got to be smart." LSG coach Justin Langer, at 12:53 PM IST, grounded that restraint in the squad already assembled: "Everyone around the cricket world is watching today. Everyone is excited to see who the big earners are. KL Rahul is a calm captain and he is comfortable with the squad he has."

IPL Chairman Arun Dhumal spoke at 1:10 PM IST, crediting the fans for the league's standing: "Dubai resonates the the spirit of cricket. Welcome to the fans who are at the Coca-Cola arena for the auction. It's because of the fans that IPL is now the second most popular league in the world."
