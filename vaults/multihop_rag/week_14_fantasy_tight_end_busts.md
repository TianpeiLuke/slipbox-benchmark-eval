# Week 14's Biggest Potential Tight End Busts

Sporting News named Dallas Goedert, Evan Engram and Pat Freiermuth as the biggest potential tight end busts of Week 14 in the 2023 season.

Goedert (at Cowboys) was not a "must-start" if he returned from his forearm injury that week — though he was also not a "must-sit": he had three catches and 50 yards against Dallas in Week 9 before exiting early with the forearm injury, so he could clearly produce against that defense, but it was smart to check other options before trusting him. The same went for Engram (at Browns), generally more reliable in PPR leagues but facing a Cleveland defense that allowed the fewest fantasy points per game to tight ends. Both Engram and Freiermuth (vs. Patriots) were in tough spots against top-five tight end defenses.
