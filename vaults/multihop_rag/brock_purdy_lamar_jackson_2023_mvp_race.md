# Brock Purdy Leads Lamar Jackson In The 2023 MVP Odds

According to oddschecker, as cited by Yardbarker on 2023-12-24, 49ers quarterback Brock Purdy was listed as the heavy favorite to be named league MVP at -230, while Baltimore signal-caller Lamar Jackson held the second-shortest odds at +525.

Purdy, the final pick of the 2022 NFL Draft, had seen his career unfold as if it were a fairy tale that just keeps getting more remarkable: the 23-year-old ranked first in the NFL in QBR at 76 and in touchdown passes with 29, while ranking third in passing yards with 3,795 — all especially impressive considering he was coming off offseason elbow surgery. Jackson might not have been posting the ridiculous numbers of his 2019 MVP campaign, but the adjustment in his play style in his first season under offensive coordinator Todd Monken had led to sustainable, winning football. Nevertheless, Baltimore needed an upset win for Jackson to capture another MVP Award; he had thrown for 3,105 yards and 17 touchdowns while adding 741 yards on the ground with five scores that season.
