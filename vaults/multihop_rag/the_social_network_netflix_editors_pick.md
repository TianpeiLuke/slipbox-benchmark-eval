# The Social Network As Netflix Editor's Pick

Polygon makes The Social Network its editor's pick for November 2023 on Netflix, and deliberately declines the obvious argument for it.

The obvious case — that with each passing year David Fincher's Mark Zuckerberg biopic grows increasingly more relevant as Facebook's, or rather Meta's, grip on the internet becomes stronger — is one Polygon explicitly sets aside as having been done to death. Its grounds are instead performance and season. It praises Jesse Eisenberg and Andrew Garfield for their electric performances and zinging chemistry, recommending that readers come for the Facebook and Mark Zuckerberg origin story and stay for the tale of doomed friendship. And it argues the film is a great November movie specifically because of the autumnal shots of Harvard and the Winklevoss twins rowing — "impeccable vibes to fuel the dark academia aesthetic TikTok videos." The piece also passes on a popular fan theory that the bestselling romantic comedy Red, White, and Royal Blue started off as fanfic of The Social Network.
