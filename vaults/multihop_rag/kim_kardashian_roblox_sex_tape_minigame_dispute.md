# Kim Kardashian's Dispute With Roblox Over A Sex Tape Minigame

Sarah Michelle Gellar was not the first celebrity to publicly call out Roblox's parental controls. In the season one premiere of The Kardashians on Hulu, Kim Kardashian confronted the platform after her son Saint, eight, discovered what claimed to be unseen footage of her 2007 sex tape with her ex-boyfriend Ray J. Saint was playing Roblox on a family member's iPad when an icon of Kardashian's crying face appeared on screen; when he showed it to his mother, she pulled the iPad away after reading that the minigame had been registered under the username "KimsNewSexTape." In the episode Kardashian said she planned on "suing" Roblox for using her name and likeness: "I have all the time, all the money, and all of the resources to burn them all to the f***ing ground. This is supposed to be unreleased footage from my old tape, this is the last thing I want as a mom, for my past to be brought up 20 years later."

According to Polygon, the minigame's developer was reportedly banned from the platform when the episode aired in April 2022. A Roblox spokesperson confirmed to the outlet that the game existed but said the "referenced video was never available on our platform - we have strict moderation and policies to protect our community, including zero tolerance for sexual content of any kind which violates our Community Rules. The text reference to the tape that got around our filters was quickly taken down and fortunately visible only to an extremely small number of people on the platform. We also swiftly took down the associated experience and banned the community developer involved with the incident."
