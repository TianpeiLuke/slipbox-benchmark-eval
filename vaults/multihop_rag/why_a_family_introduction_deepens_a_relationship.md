# Why Introducing A Partner To Your Family Can Deepen The Relationship

Relationship experts argue that letting a partner see you with your family — including the awkward parts — deepens the relationship rather than endangering it, because the act is a vulnerable one. "I think introducing a significant other to family is a really vulnerable act, and that vulnerability is going to be what allows for greater intimacy," Saba Lurie told The Independent. "When your significant other sees you with your family, they may see another side of you that they may not have had access to before."

The obvious objection — that a partner may discover your relationships with your parents and extended family are complex — is answered by framing that discovery as evidence of self-knowledge, provided you have explained the family dynamics in advance. "I think that we tend to be our best relational self when we've really done the work around understanding all of our past relationships," Rachel Zar explained. "You can tell your partner: 'I don't have a close relationship with my brother, and here's why. Here are the intentional choices that I've made around it.' Or you can say: 'Here's my chosen family. These are the people who are important to me.' Your significant other will see that you've been introspective and have takeaways around how you've navigated your family's origin." The conclusion carries a condition and a limit: as long as the couple have discussed their relationship expectations both before and during the holidays, the introduction may deepen the relationship and be a step in the right direction — or it may simply be an opportunity to bring two important people in your life together. And the timing remains the couple's own call: "I don't think there's ever going to be a perfect time for folks," Lurie said. "I think it'll depend on the relationship between the two partners and also on the family dynamics, and what feels more comfortable for all parties. I don't think there's going to be a right or wrong time for the introduction."
