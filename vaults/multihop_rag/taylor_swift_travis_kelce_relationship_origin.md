# How Taylor Swift And Travis Kelce's Relationship Began

Taylor Swift, 33, revealed in her December 2023 Time interview — her first time publicly speaking about Travis Kelce — that the relationship began in July 2023, after he confessed on his podcast that he had tried and failed to give her a friendship bracelet with his phone number on it during one of her Eras Tour shows. "This all started when Travis very adorably put me on blast on his podcast, which I thought was metal as hell," the "Bad Blood" singer explained. "We started hanging out right after that. So we actually had a significant amount of time that no one knew, which I'm grateful for, because we got to get to know each other."

She also corrected the widely held assumption about her first appearance at Arrowhead Stadium in September: by then the two were already a couple. "By the time I went to that first game, we were a couple. I think some people think that they saw our first date at that game? We would never be psychotic enough to hard launch a first date," she said. The "All Too Well" singer had been romantically linked to the tight end since that summer, when he first confessed to the attempt to pass her his number.
