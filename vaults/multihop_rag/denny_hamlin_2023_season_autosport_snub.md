# Denny Hamlin 2023 Season Autosport Snub

Essentially Sports argued on Dec. 21, 2023 that Denny Hamlin's omission from Autosport's Top 50 drivers of 2023 seemed unjustified, given how much he led and how consistently he performed.

The grounds are his season's numbers and their context. Finding a fan of Hamlin in the NASCAR fandom is a tough task — continuously surrounded by controversy, Joe Gibbs Racing's #11 Toyota Camry driver has been subject to a lot of criticism — but his ability as a race car driver has never been in doubt. As the season entered the decisive playoff phase, it seemed 2023 would be the year his championship drought finally ended: the 43-year-old dominated the first round of the playoffs, was the driver to watch out for, won his first playoff race at Bristol and cruised to the next round. Hamlin later revealed that a shoulder injury prevented him from being at his best as the playoffs progressed, and having to depend much on his weaker arm he stayed in contention for the championship four race until the final race in Martinsville. He secured three wins, 14 top-fives and 19 top-10 finishes in 2023 and led a whopping 998 laps, finishing fifth in the final standings as one of the most consistent and best performers of the season. The piece concedes the counterfactual: a championship trophy would surely have put him in the upper echelons of Autosport's Top 50.
