# WIRED Black Friday 2023 Multi-Device Wireless Charging Stand Deals

WIRED's Apple Black Friday guide of 25 November 2023 covered nine discounted wireless chargers and stands, spanning an outlet-equipped charging orb, foldable travel Duos, WIRED's top and runner-up picks from its Best Apple 3-in-1 Wireless Chargers guide, a bare MagSafe puck, a linen-wrapped dual pad and an ESR travel kit.

The orb charges an iPhone when you simply stick the phone to it, and on the back it carries two USB-A ports, two USB-C ports and three AC outlets; WIRED calls it compact and tidy, says its own unit has been going strong for more than two years, and notes the price fluctuates very often and has hit as low as $60 in the past, though it still rates the current deal. The travel Duo suits people who travel with an iPhone and an Apple Watch, replacing two separate chargers with one compact, foldable unit that also charges AirPods with a wireless charging case and other Qi-certified devices. WIRED's best 2-in-1 stand from the Best Apple 3-in-1 Wireless Chargers guide has a MagSafe spot for charging an iPhone at top speed (up to 15 watts) plus a recessed spot below for an AirPods case, and holds the iPhone in portrait or landscape orientation, which is great for StandBy; the discount is not huge, but WIRED has never seen it cheaper. A second, similar 2-in-1 stand — WIRED's favourite for people who need to charge only an iPhone and AirPods — offers the same MagSafe 15-watt spot, an AirPods spot below and portrait or landscape orientation for StandBy mode, again at a small discount that is nonetheless the lowest WIRED has seen.

WIRED's top charger in that guide has a T-shaped design suspending two devices midair while staying compact: it charges an iPhone at the fastest 15-watt speed Apple allows, fast-charges the Apple Watch Series 7/8/9/Ultra, and powers AirPods Pro or AirPods with a wireless charging case from its base, all from a single outlet; it dipped as low as $100 back in August but WIRED still calls this a great deal. The runner-up pick in the same guide charges all your Apple devices at top speed and includes a 6.6-foot cable and a 36-watt wall charger. Among single-device options, one of WIRED's favourite MagSafe chargers delivers the maximum 15-watt speed, sits securely on a desk and can charge an Apple Watch too, though it ships without a power adapter and only includes a USB-C-to-USB-C cable, while the Belgian linen-wrapped pad is the stylish choice to match interior decor, coming in muted tones including camel, charcoal, forest and natural, charging two devices at once with LED charging indicators and rubber feet, with the caveat that you must be precise when placing the phone. Finally, the ESR travel kit props an iPhone in portrait or landscape and can display an Apple Watch in Nightstand mode; its main body folds open with a MagSafe pad for the iPhone and a slot behind for AirPods, but it does not support fast charging and is capped at 7.5 watts for the iPhone and 5 watts for the Apple Watch — it dipped lower to $40 back in July, though WIRED still calls it a good, affordable charger and a solid deal.
