# Messi's Fitness And Inter Miami's Offseason, October 2023

Lionel Messi's club Inter Miami were eliminated from MLS playoff contention by a 1-0 loss to FC Cincinnati the weekend before Argentina's Oct. 12, 2023 qualifier against Paraguay — and that elimination changed the calculus around his minutes for Argentina.

Messi had missed the previous four matches, including Argentina's fixture against Bolivia, but returned for the final 30 minutes against Cincinnati and was powerless to turn the tide after Inter Miami missed so many gilt-edged chances in the first half. Had Inter Miami still been in the playoff hunt with a postseason run on the horizon, Argentina might have played it a bit safer with Messi in this international window; with his long offseason break confirmed, he could be more aggressive with his minutes, knowing that after Inter Miami's final two meaningless regular-season matches he would only have international breaks to keep him sharp until February. Reports on the day said Messi would not start but was likely to play much or all of the second half, with Lautaro Martinez and Julian Alvarez both starting a national team match together for the first time — something World Cup hero Mario Kempes was a big fan of. With Messi starting on the bench, reports also indicated Nicolas Otamendi would wear the captain's armband until Messi entered.

A rumour that Messi could head to Barcelona on loan to cover the gap left by Inter Miami's failure to qualify never made much sense and was squashed on numerous fronts. Inter Miami coach Gerardo Martino made light of it in his post-match press conference after the Cincinnati defeat, saying he would only go to Spain "for fun" — "Is he going for fun? Maybe he's going to visit Barcelona. Other than that, I don't know anything else about it" — while Fabrizio Romano shot it down from a journalistic standpoint. There is not enough of a break for Messi to reasonably integrate with Barcelona, and at 36 years old he is sure to welcome the extended time off.
