# Right-Wing Online Audience Growth

There is clearly an audience for right-wing ideology online, TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-02, as conservative influencers continue to build massive platforms, largely by provoking outrage. The observation is offered as the counterpoint to Republican candidates' weak youth engagement: the audience exists, but the politicians are not the ones capturing it.

On Twitch, debate streams between creators across the political spectrum are wildly popular, and between May 2021 and May 2022 the site's Politics tag tripled in viewership. The platform itself is a haven for some figures on the far-right who have since been kicked off of YouTube.
