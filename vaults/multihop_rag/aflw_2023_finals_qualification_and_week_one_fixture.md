# AFLW 2023 Finals Qualification And Week One Fixture

The 2023 AFLW finals eight was not decided until the final game of the regular season, when Sydney downed Fremantle by 22 points to leapfrog St Kilda into the final spot, The Age reported on 3 November 2023.

The Saints had kept themselves in the race with a 20-point win over Carlton earlier in the day, 7.4 (46) to 3.8 (26), and with only 0.4 of a percentage point separating Sydney in ninth and St Kilda in eighth, the Swans' win assured them of retaking eighth spot. Had the Saints qualified, it would have been their maiden finals appearance. Saints goalkicker Jesse Wardlaw had a heavy collision with Carlton's Keeley Sherar late in the final quarter of their clash.

The resulting week-one finals fixture was: on Saturday November 11, the First Qualifying Final, Adelaide v Brisbane Lions, 2.35pm local (3.05pm AEDT) at Norwood Oval, and the First Elimination Final, Gold Coast v Sydney, 6.15pm local (7.15pm AEDT) at Heritage Bank Stadium; on Sunday November 12, the Second Elimination Final, Geelong v Essendon, 1.05pm AEDT at GMHBA Stadium, and the Second Qualifying Final, Melbourne v North Melbourne, 3.05pm AEDT at Ikon Park.
