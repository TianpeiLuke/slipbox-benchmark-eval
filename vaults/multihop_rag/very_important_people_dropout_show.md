# Very Important People: A Reboot Of CollegeHumor's Hello My Name Is

Very Important People is a Dropout show revealed in the fifth-anniversary announcement video and, in Sam Reich's telling, a reboot of something the company did 12 or 13 years ago on CollegeHumor called Hello My Name Is. Brennan Lee Mulligan recently shot an episode of it, and calls the original "one of my favorite CollegeHumor things of all time when I was a fan at home watching CollegeHumor".

The format is the defining thing: on the original they would put Josh Ruben, specifically, in really ambitious makeup, and sit him down for a Charlie Rose-style interview in that character — a character he would have only a minute to create. In the reboot Vic Michaelis is hosting, and the cast is a rotating cast of characters from Dropout's improv world.

Reich describes his response to the current slate with a term of art: "Every once in a while I get what I call the 'quality chills'? I got quality chills while I was watching the Dungeons and Drag Queens shoot; this creepy-crawly sensation on your back like, This is really good. Because this is really good. A lot of quality chills."
