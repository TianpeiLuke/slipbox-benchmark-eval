# Vermont Sportsbook Promo Codes: What They Cover And Who Can Use Them

Sporting News' promo-code FAQ of 2023-09-26 sets the boundaries of these offers. Can you get more than one bonus from a Vermont online sportsbook? No — you'll almost certainly be limited to one bonus from each Vermont online sportsbook, and sometimes the limit will be one per household. However, you can claim that one bonus from every Vermont online sportsbook, and sign up at as many sportsbooks as you like. What sports can these bonuses be used on? Pretty much any sport should qualify: if betting on the sport is available in Vermont, you can use your bonus on it.

Why use the promo codes on The Sporting News page? The stated reason is that those codes get you the best bonuses available — when you plug in the code during registration, the sportsbook knows the publisher sent you and gives you the bonus it negotiated for you. Are these promo codes for regular customers too? No: the promo codes on the page are for new customers only, and once you've got an account with a sportsbook you can take advantage of other bonus offers and ongoing promos instead.
