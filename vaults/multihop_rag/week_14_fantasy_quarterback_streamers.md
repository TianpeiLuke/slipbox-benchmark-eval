# Week 14 Fantasy Quarterback Streamers And Sleepers

Sporting News called Week 14 of the 2023 season a challenging one from a streaming perspective, naming Gardner Minshew, Jameis Winston, Baker Mayfield and Joe Flacco as the best options.

Minshew (at the Bengals) was executing Shane Steichen's scheme at an efficient clip and now faced a Cincinnati stop unit ranking below league average in dropback EPA. Assuming Winston (vs. Panthers) got the starting nod in place of the banged-up Derek Carr (head, shoulder, back), his gunslinger play style could lead to a respectable fantasy output against the reeling Panthers. Mayfield (at the Falcons) had not been asked to do much with his arm in Week 13, but a potential negative game script in Atlanta could force him to air the ball out at a higher clip, boosting his fantasy prospects. Although the Browns did not get the win in Flacco's first start with his new team, the veteran did everything he could to keep Cleveland within striking distance, racking up 254 yards and two touchdowns; another 250-plus yard, multi-touchdown effort could again be in store if he continued to start in place of Dorian Thompson-Robinson (concussion).
