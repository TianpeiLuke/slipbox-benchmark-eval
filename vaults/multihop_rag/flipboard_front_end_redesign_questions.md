# Flipboard's Front-End Redesign Questions

After integrating its back end with the fediverse, Flipboard may reconsider what its front end should look like for this new age of social media.

CEO Mike McCue framed the problem as a set of open questions rather than a plan: "The front end was built at a time pre-federation. What are the implications of federation in the front end? How do we think about curation and all the things, all the capabilities and tools that we've created over the years? How does that work in a world that's federated, and from a user experience point of view? That is a great question."
