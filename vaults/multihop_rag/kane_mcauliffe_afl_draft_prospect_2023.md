# Kane McAuliffe AFL Draft Prospect 2023

Kane McAuliffe, profiled in Kevin Sheehan's top 40 for the 2023 AFL Draft as reported by The Roar on Nov. 15, 2023, is a powerful midfielder who wins his own ball and is a penetrating left-foot kick. State: South Australia. State League Club: North Adelaide. Community Club: Central Augusta. Date of Birth: 01/03/05. Height: 187cm.

He is a strong and aggressive tackler as well as an accomplished extractor of the ball from stoppages, using his clean hands and vision to set up the play. He progressed from dominating under-18 games at club level to playing seven matches at reserves level, averaging 18.7 disposals. He featured among South Australia's best in the National Championship matches against the Allies and Victoria Metro, and averaged 18 disposals, 3.8 clearances and 8.5 tackles across the tournament. He showcased his power and speed at the national Draft Combine, with his running vertical jump of 95cm ranking third and 2.93 seconds for the 20m sprint ranking fifth. Weapon: Power and left-foot kick. Player comparison: Jack Crisp (Collingwood).
