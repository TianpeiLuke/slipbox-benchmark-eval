# Erling Haaland's 50-Goal 2023 At Manchester City

Erling Haaland scored 50 goals in the 2023 calendar year for Manchester City and Norway, placing him fourth on TalkSport's list of the year's top scorers. No player in Premier League history has made more of an impact following their arrival than Haaland, who broke the goal record for a single season with 36 strikes the previous term.

He has already been firing in many top-flight goals this term, as well as being the joint-leading scorer from the Champions League group stages, and he is the leading goalscorer in the Premier League this season. Just six of his 50 goals in 2023 came for his national side Norway, with Haaland helping the Citizens to a historic treble the previous campaign.

With two games left for City in 2023, Haaland had time to become the overall top scorer, though it remained to be seen whether Pep Guardiola would play him against Everton and Sheffield United. Haaland had missed City's last five games with a foot injury, but it was believed he could be in contention to return to action.
