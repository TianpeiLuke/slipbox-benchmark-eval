# India's Twin Renewable And Thermal Capacity Push In 2023

As reported on 11 November 2023, the Indian Power Ministry has scheduled tenders of around 50 GW annually of renewable energy for the next five years, in order to achieve the target of 500 GW of non-fossil fuel capacity. Alongside the government's thrust on ramping up renewable energy capacity, building incremental thermal capacity has also been a priority, because energy security has become an important issue with a consistent increase in demand in recent years and power demand is now at all-time high or peak levels.

The observation is used to position a specific company: PSU thermal major NTPC appears to be well-placed to capture both themes, energy transition and energy security.
