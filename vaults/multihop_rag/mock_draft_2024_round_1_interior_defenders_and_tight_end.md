# 2024 Mock Draft: Brock Bowers Plus Two Interior Defensive Linemen In Round 1

CBS Sports' 2024 NFL Mock Draft of 2023-11-17 projects one tight end and two interior defensive linemen in the first round. At Pick 8, Tennessee takes Brock Bowers, TE, Georgia, a junior at 6'4" and 240 lbs, ranked 4th prospect and 1st at the position; the mock calls him the lone remaining blue-chip talent at that point and reasons that if Will Levis is the future, the Titans need to surround him with the skill talent that may give him a chance to succeed.

At Pick 13, Denver takes Jer'Zhan Newton, DL, Illinois, a junior at 6'2" and 295 lbs, 27th prospect and 2nd at the position, infusing some youth into a unit that was not long ago a strength for the Broncos, with Newton described as a high-motor player who will help establish the culture in Denver. At Pick 31, Kansas City takes Kris Jenkins, DL, Michigan, a senior at 6'3" and 305 lbs, 43rd prospect and 5th at the position, adding depth to its interior defensive line.
