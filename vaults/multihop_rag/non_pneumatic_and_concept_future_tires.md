# Non-Pneumatic And Concept Future Tires

A non-pneumatic tire replaces air pressure with modern composites to provide the right mix of stiffness and flex with significantly more durability — the near-future example being the Michelin Uptis, which Michelin says could reduce tire replacements by 20 percent. Variations of this theme have been in development for decades, but models are in testing now for consumer and industrial use.

The idea is one of several attempts to change the equation altogether, rather than only finding sustainable replacements for tire manufacturing compounds or researching what today's compounds do to our health.

A more radical and more distant vision is Goodyear's 2020 ReCharge concept, which proposed that a biodegradable tread could be recharged simply by inserting refillable capsules with seasonal compounds. As a concept it is pretty far out there, and Goodyear's Helsel confesses as much, calling them "purely conceptual designs" that "might never be produced as such" — but he says the non-pneumatic nature of those concepts, and the advanced sensors embedded within, are indicative of ongoing research into future tires.
