# Deloitte's Guidance On Structuring Enterprise AI Adoption

Jim Rowan, a principal at Deloitte who works with clients on how to build generative AI across companies in an organized fashion, says the way through the tension facing CIOs is to set up structure and organization around how generative AI gets implemented over time. The precondition he is addressing is the tension between a CIO's desire to please internal customers — especially when some of that pressure could be coming from the CEO — and a CIO's natural tendency to move cautiously, even with something as potentially transformative as generative AI.

His first step is deliberately not a technology step. "A lot of the way we're working with companies is thinking about what is the infrastructure that they need to be successful. By infrastructure, I don't necessarily mean technology, but who are the people, what are the processes and the governance…and giving them the capabilities to set that up," Rowan said.

A big part of that work is talking about use cases and how to use the technology to address a given problem.
