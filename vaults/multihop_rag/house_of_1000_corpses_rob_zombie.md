# House of 1000 Corpses (Rob Zombie)

House of 1000 Corpses may be the true answer to the question "What is the best The Texas Chain Saw Massacre sequel?" — Polygon's Oct. 29 pick in the 2023-10-10 Halloween Countdown, listed on AMC Plus, Pluto TV, and Tubi.

The movie follows a group of teens putting together a book on roadside attractions who stumble upon a serial killer-themed rest stop run by a weirdo. After that strange encounter the kids set off on the road, only for their car to break down a few miles later; their helpful hitchhiker companion Baby knows just the place they can get help — the Firefly mansion, where her family lives. From there things devolve into a host of horrific stage plays, cursed experiments, and gruesome murders.

The family at its center, whom Rob Zombie went on to make two more movies about, is repulsive and absolutely horrible but still zany enough to be truly unsettling in a way few horror movies can be. Critic AG calls it a grisly, grotesque film that captures all the grime of the '70s movies that inspired it, but says what really makes it worth watching is the way it balances silliness and terror, doling each out in proportional quantities until you cannot figure out whether to laugh or scream, and end up doing both.
