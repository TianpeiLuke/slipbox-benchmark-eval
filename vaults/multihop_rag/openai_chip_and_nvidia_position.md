# OpenAI Chip Rumors And Nvidia's Position

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-13 that rumors were swirling that OpenAI may be considering developing its own AI chips, and set that against Nvidia's position: Nvidia's ongoing stock market rally is likely driven by the current boom in AI, so an in-house OpenAI chip would make things interesting.

Two further figures anchor the week. ChatGPT's mobile app hit a record $4.6 million in revenue in the previous month, but growth is slowing. And the newsletter argues there is no need to shed tears for Nvidia quite yet, pointing to Brian's piece breaking down how Nvidia became a major player in robotics — that is, Nvidia's exposure is not confined to supplying the AI boom.

The surrounding context that week was AI coverage generally. TechCrunch's most-read stories consistently continue to be about AI, the AI hype cycle continues apace, and that week brought a batch of stories about what the newsletter calls the seedy underbelly of AI, including how humans are part of the problem, continuing to trick AI systems into generating boobs and 9/11 memes. TechCrunch spoke with investors to figure out whether the future of AI has hope beyond daft memes, and reports that it does. The OpenAI chip item is explicitly a rumor as of that date, not a confirmed plan.
