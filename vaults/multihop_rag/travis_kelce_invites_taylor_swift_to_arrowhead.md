# Travis Kelce Invites Taylor Swift To Arrowhead

On 21 September 2023 Travis Kelce announced on The Pat McAfee Show that he had invited Taylor Swift to watch one of his games, The Age reported on 26 September 2023.

He described the invitation in his own words: "I told her, 'I've seen you rock the stage in Arrowhead, you might have to come see me rock a stage at Arrowhead and we can see which one's a little more lit.' We'll see what happens in the near future."

The timeline then moves to the fated sighting of Swift at Arrowhead Stadium on the Monday, cheering on No. 87 with gusto.
