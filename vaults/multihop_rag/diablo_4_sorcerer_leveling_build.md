# Diablo 4 Sorcerer Leveling Build

This Sorcerer leveling build, based on Lexyu's Sorcerer leveling guide at IcyVeins and published by Polygon on 2023-10-18, is purely for leveling — that is, allocating your 58 skill points between levels 1 and 50 — and harnesses Lightning to speed through the leveling process. There are a few options for Core skills, but this is Lexyu's Chain Lightning variation, which also uses Frost Bolt, Teleport, Frost Nova, Lightning Spear, and Ice Armor. Readers wanting more detail on item affixes or other great Legendary powers are pointed to the original IcyVeins guide.

The skills must be purchased in a specific order, because some require a number of allocated skill points in the tree to unlock, so new skills must be unlocked before existing ones are enhanced. The order is: Frost Bolt / Enhanced Frost Bolt; Chain Lightning; Glinting Frost Bolt; Enhanced Chain Lightning / Greater Chain Lightning; Teleport; Frost Nova / Enhanced Frost Nova / Mystical Frost Nova; Chain Lightning (rank 2-5); Fireball (rank 1 only, for the Enchantment slot); Lightning Spear; Glass Cannon (rank 1-3); Devastation; Elemental Dominance (rank 1-3); Coursing Currents (rank 1-3); Elemental Attunement; Ice Armor / Enhanced Ice Armor / Shimmering Ice Armor; Enhanced Teleport / Shimmering Teleport; Enhanced Lightning Spear; Vyr's Mastery; Invoked Lightning Spear / Lightning Spear (rank 2-5); Frost Bolt (rank 2-5); Conduction (rank 1-3); Electrocution (rank 1-3); Align the Elements; Protection (rank 1-3); Icy Veil (rank 1-3); and Frost Nova (rank 2-3).

The Enchantment slot is key to a ton of Sorcerer damage. Two slots are gained while leveling: Fireball goes in the level 15 slot, and Chain Lightning in the level 30 slot.

At the start of a new character, complete three dungeons first to collect their Aspects, then place them on a piece of gear, ideally jewelry when possible — bearing in mind these Legendaries should be replaced with new versions using the imprint system every few levels. They are: Recharging Aspect from Zenith in Fractured Peaks, which grants X Mana each time Chain Lightning bounces off you; Aspect of Control from Sunken Library in Kehjistan, which deals X% more damage to Immobilized, Stunned, or Frozen enemies; and Edgemaster's Aspect from the Oldstones dungeon in Scosglen, where skills deal X% increased damage based on Primary Resource when cast, with the maximum benefit at full Primary Resource.
