# The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom

Tears of the Kingdom, developed by Nintendo EPD for Nintendo Switch, is Polygon's No. 1 video game of 2023 in its list of 2023-10-31.

Polygon frames the achievement against the odds: the premise is familiar — Princess Zelda is missing, Ganondorf has returned, and an age-old hero in a green tunic has to step in — and the game did not seem poised to make anything new of it, being a sequel to one of the most popular Zelda games ever made, originally conceived as DLC, and built on the same map. Yet it is something else entirely: not just that Breath of the Wild was a rough draft for Tears of the Kingdom, but that the entire Zelda series was a collection of stepping stones leading in winding, influential pathways to this wacky, wonderful, and thoroughly new world of Hyrule.

Critic M. Myers singles out Ultrahand — slathering pieces of wood in what amounts to magical Gorilla Glue and watching the physics engine roar to life in response. Going in expecting not to build much and just follow the story, Myers left feeling like a genius engineer, building all manner of bizarre contraptions, mostly long bridges, to sail through skies and trundle over mountaintops. Myers also recalls first diving deep into the darkness of the Depths and the amazement of discovering a whole other world underneath the known one, filled with skeleton horses and gloom-splattered Bokoblins, and the moment of realizing where Princess Zelda really was — and, many hours later, learning where she really was.

Myers never wanted to stop playing, and though 2023 in video games spoiled everyone with hearty meals and sweet desserts, never stopped thinking about it — picking the Switch back up to seek out another Lightroot or solve another shrine's puzzle until there were none left, then just wandering, collecting ingredients, talking to Great Fairies, imagining the next adventure. The game, Myers writes, feels like someone holding your hands very close as they lean in to whisper, with eyes twinkling, "Can I tell you something?"
