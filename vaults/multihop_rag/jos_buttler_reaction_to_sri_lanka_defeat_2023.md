# Jos Buttler's Reaction To The Sri Lanka Defeat

Speaking after England's defeat to Sri Lanka at the 2023 World Cup, captain Jos Buttler admitted his team had been "short of our best by a very long way" and said there was "no clear answer" as to how to turn their fortunes around.

His full comment placed the weight of the campaign on the captaincy: "It's incredibly tough, an incredibly disappointing tournament. As captain, you feel that a lot."

He also expressed personal and collective disappointment while ruling out a single fix: "We've been short of our best by a very long way. I'm disappointed for myself and the boys that we've not given a good account of ourselves… if there was one golden nugget that we're not doing then we'd pick that up."
