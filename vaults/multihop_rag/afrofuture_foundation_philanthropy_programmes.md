# AfroFuture Foundation Philanthropy Programmes

The AfroFuture Foundation is a programme established to give back to the local Ghanaian community in a long-term and meaningful way, connecting with local groups and international charities to educate and feed students and families in Accra. Its goals are strategically aligned with the United Nations' 17 sustainable development goals.

Its education strand began in 2017, when AfroFuture collaborated with local schools within Madina Zongo, Greater Accra, and the Kumasi Ashanti Region to provide $5,000 in school supplies — notebooks, pads, pencils, pens, erasers, textbooks — and bookbags, an initial commitment to the fourth sustainable development goal of quality education. The following year the organisation partnered with the Water Aid Foundation and raised 10,500 Ghana Cedis (over $2,000) to provide clean water to families in need in the Northern Region of Ghana. Also in 2018, AfroFuture partnered with Twitter Blackbirds (an employee resource group) and AirBnB on two initiatives, AfroFuture Reads and AfroFuture Feeds, raising $10,500 to renovate and remodel the Genesis School of Orphans in Jamestown, Greater Accra Region — a school that had recently been demolished and its land sold, leaving students studying under tents for the remainder of their last term.

The AfroFuture Feeds strand aims to bring awareness to and eradicate hunger in Ghana, working through Ghana's "Zero Hunger" sustainable development goal by providing meals via food trucks and direct donations to Ghanaian communities during volunteer opportunities involving both AfroFuture staff and participants. It has raised over $5,000 to feed 500 families within five impoverished neighbourhoods in Ghana. In 2021, AfroFuture Feeds welcomed 72 volunteers to help hand out 500 packaged meals and water; in 2022 it welcomed over 250 volunteers and delivered groceries to families across Accra and surrounding communities.
