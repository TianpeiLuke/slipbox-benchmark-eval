# Egypt's Role In The Israel-Hamas War And The Leverage It Creates

The war between Israel and Hamas has turned the spotlight on Egypt's role as a regional powerbroker, potentially winning it Western support as it tries to escape a grinding economic crisis. The structure linking the two is that Egypt's two assets in this conflict — long-held ties to Israel and a border with Gaza — make its stance decisive for outcomes the West wants.

Specifically, Egypt's position is shaping up as key to the fate of any refugees and to a sustained flow of aid for the besieged territory's 2 million residents, after Israel cut off crucial supplies in response to Hamas's deadly Oct. 7 assault. That dependence is what converts Egypt's geography and relationships into potential leverage over Western backing for its economy, as of the reporting date of 2023-10-22.
