# TRI's Eldercare Robotics Focus

For the Toyota Research Institute, much of the "what's next" focuses on supporting aging populations. As TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-01, the organization invests a good portion of its resources in building out technology designed to help older people live more independently.

That aim is at the root of the research TechCrunch shared from the institute the week before, and the article attributes the accompanying remarks to noted senior research scientist Benjamin Burchfiel.
