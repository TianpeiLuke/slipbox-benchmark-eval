# Walmart's Black Friday 2023 Store Hours

For 2023 Walmart launched its Black Friday deals online first, with the last round of rollbacks going live on Wednesday 22 November at 3 p.m. ET, while in-person shoppers had to wait until Friday 24 November at 6 a.m., when stores officially opened, Mashable reported on 25 November 2023.

Store hours can differ depending on the location, and shoppers had until 11 p.m. to finish their Black Friday shopping.

Mashable frames this as the end of an era: "The days of lining up outside big-box stores on Black Friday eve have officially ended, and we're not sorry. Not only is this kinder on Walmart employees, but it puts the kibosh on the kinds of doorbuster melees that often took place in the past. (They were a national embarrassment.)" There is no need to brave the crowds, it adds, since the best Walmart Black Friday sales can still be found online.
