# The First Quarter Of Bengals 24, Bills 18

Cincinnati led Buffalo 14-7 after a high-scoring opening quarter on Nov. 5, 2023, having won the toss and taken the ball first. Trayveon Williams returned the opening kickoff to the 24; Burrow started with a checkdown to Joe Mixon for eight and another short pass Mixon took 15 yards to the Bengals' 47. Williams took the first two carries by a Bengal for seven and one, and Tanner Hudson — notable because much had been made of the tight end position after Irv Smith Jr.'s red-zone fumble the week before — caught a six-yard pass for a first down. Burrow dodged a sack, rolled left and flipped 18 yards to Tee Higgins at the Bills' 21, Mixon ran three up the middle and Burrow found Smith for 11 to the 7 and first-and-goal; at 8:30 p.m. Smith made a leaping grab over Jordan Poyer for a seven-yard touchdown, his first of the year, for 7-0.

Buffalo answered immediately. Ty Johnson returned the kickoff to the Bills' 15, Allen found Dalton Kincaid for five out of an RPO, then hit Stefon Diggs for nine on third-and-5 and again for a 34-yard strike, Diggs carving down the left sideline to the Bengals' 37. Allen scrambled and flipped a pass to Khalil Shakir that went 22 yards to the Bengals' 15, then connected with Kincaid for 13 to the Cincinnati 2, and at 8:39 p.m. rushed in for a two-yard touchdown — drawing a taunting flag as he ran through the right side — to tie it 7-7 with 6:03 left in the quarter.

Cincinnati retook the lead on a long drive. Williams returned to the Cincinnati 31; Burrow's throw to Ja'Marr Chase was broken up but he was roughed on the play for 15 yards; Andrei Iosivas, involved after back-to-back weeks with a touchdown, caught nine on a rollout and Mixon ran three for the first down at the Bills' 42. Facing their longest third down of the day at third-and-10 after a Mixon no-gain and a missed connection with Smith, the Bengals stayed perfect on third downs as Tyler Boyd caught 15 in front of the sideline to the Bills' 27, and Hudson's second catch went 13 to the 14. Burrow escaped the pocket and got the ball to Higgins, who dove for the pylon but was ruled out at the 2; Cincinnati then twice tried Chase, who was tackled at the 2 on a screen and could not hold a leaping grab in the end zone. At 8:52 p.m. Mixon bulldozed up the middle for a two-yard touchdown and a 14-7 lead. Buffalo took the ball at the 25 after a fair catch at the 2 with 35 seconds left, and James Cook's first rush of the game gained two to end the quarter.
