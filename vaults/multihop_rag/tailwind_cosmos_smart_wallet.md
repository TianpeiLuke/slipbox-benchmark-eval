# Tailwind, Cosmos-Based Smart Wallet

Tailwind is a seed-stage crypto wallet focused on the Cosmos ecosystem, founded by Bao Mai and presented in Alliance DAO's All11 cohort on 2023-10-18. Its distinguishing design goal is to help users focus on decentralized applications by abstracting away from chains and allowing users to pick a single gas token for transactions.

Bao Mai previously was a founding engineer at JunoSwap, an automated market maker. Tailwind was raising a seed round at the time of the demo day.
