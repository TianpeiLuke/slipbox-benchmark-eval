# The Fediverse And The ActivityPub Protocol

The "fediverse" is a collection of decentralized social networking applications, like Mastodon, that communicate with one another over the ActivityPub protocol. Its defining feature is that the applications are separate and decentralized but interoperate through a shared protocol, so power is not centralized with a Big Tech CEO in charge.

Mozilla — the mission-driven tech company behind the Firefox browser, Pocket reader and other apps — is now investing its energy into it, on the belief that consumers are hungry for a new way of social networking where trust and safety are paramount. Mozilla senior director of content Carolyn O'Hara, outlining the company's fediverse strategy to TechCrunch, put the ambition this way: the idea is to rethink social networking from the ground up.
