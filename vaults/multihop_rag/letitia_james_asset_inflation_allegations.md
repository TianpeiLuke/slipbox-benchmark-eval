# Letitia James Asset Inflation Allegations

Letitia James, a Democrat, sued Trump and the Trump Organization a year before this September 2023 ruling, alleging a pattern of duplicity that she dubbed "the art of the steal" — a twist on the title of Trump's 1987 business memoir "The Art of the Deal."

The lawsuit accused Trump and his company of routinely inflating the value of assets like skyscrapers, golf courses and his Mar-a-Lago estate in Florida, padding his bottom line by billions.
