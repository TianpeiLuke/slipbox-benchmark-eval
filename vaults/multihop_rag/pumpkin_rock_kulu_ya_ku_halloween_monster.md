# Pumpkin Rock Kulu-Ya-Ku Halloween Monster

A Halloween-themed Kulu-Ya-Ku holding a pumpkin rock will "appear in larger numbers than usual" in Monster Hunter Now from Saturday, Oct. 28, at 9 a.m. local time until the end of Oct. 31, as Polygon reported on 2023-10-25.

By its very nature Kulu-Ya-Ku appears in all three habitats — Forest, Desert, and Swamp — meaning they can appear anywhere on the map. Beyond the novelty of battling a Halloween-themed monster, taking down these special pumpkin rock Kulu-Ya-Ku rewards players with Pumpkin Tickets.

Niantic has confirmed that any pumpkin rock Kulu-Ya-Ku tagged with paintballs during the event will continue to drop Pumpkin Tickets after the event ends, giving players extra time if needed.
