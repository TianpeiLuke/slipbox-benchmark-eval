# Luigi's Mansion 3

Luigi's Mansion 3 puts players in the role of Mario's taller counterpart rather than the plumber in the red hat. It appears in Polygon's 2023-10-20 list of the 35 best couch co-op games for Nintendo Switch.

Local co-op becomes available with player two as Gooigi, a spooky goo Luigi, after finishing the intro walkthrough and getting E. Gadd's briefcase.

Together the players platform their way through the haunted hotel to save Mario, Peach, and numerous Toads.
