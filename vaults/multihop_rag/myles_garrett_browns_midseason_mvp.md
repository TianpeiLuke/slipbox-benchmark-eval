# Myles Garrett Browns Midseason MVP

Yardbarker's Nov. 9, 2023 AFC midseason team MVP list named defensive end Myles Garrett the Cleveland Browns' MVP and argued he could be leading the race for Defensive Player of the Year.

The grounds run across three kinds of contribution: the No. 1 overall pick in 2017 was tied for the second-most sacks in the league with 9.5 and had the most forced fumbles with four; he also blocked a field-goal attempt in a Week 7 win over the Colts; and he was the ringleader of a defense that allowed the fewest yards per game at 234.8. The warrant is that individual disruption plus the unit's league-best result together make the case.
