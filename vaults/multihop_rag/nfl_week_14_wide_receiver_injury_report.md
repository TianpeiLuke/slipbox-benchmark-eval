# Week 14 Wide Receiver Injury Report

In Week 13 of the 2023 season Demario Douglas (concussion) and Rashid Shaheed (thigh) missed game action, and Tank Dell (leg), Amari Cooper (concussion), Christian Watson (hamstring) and Marquise Brown (heel) exited early from their respective games, per Sporting News on Dec. 7, 2023.

Two updates followed. Christian Kirk suffered a groin injury on Monday night and figured to be more doubtful than questionable for that week's game against Cleveland; if he was out, Zay Jones and Tim Jones would see more targets, but with Trevor Lawrence (ankle) also hurt it was tough to count on either. Douglas (concussion) was ruled out for Week 14.

Sporting News said it would, as usual, update the wide receiver PPR rankings throughout the week for the latest player movement and analysis.
