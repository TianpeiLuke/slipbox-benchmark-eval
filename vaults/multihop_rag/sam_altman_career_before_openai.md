# Sam Altman's Career Before OpenAI

Sam Altman, 38 as of November 2023, is not trained as an AI engineer, yet has been seen as a Silicon Valley wunderkind since his early 20s. The defining credential of that earlier phase is that he was recruited in 2014 to take the lead of the startup incubator YCombinator.

The reputation attached to him at the time is captured in YCombinator co-founder Paul Graham's 2014 announcement that Altman would become its president: "Sam is one of the smartest people I know, and understands startups better than perhaps anyone I know, including myself." Graham said then that Altman was "one of those rare people who manage to be both fearsomely effective and yet fundamentally benevolent."
