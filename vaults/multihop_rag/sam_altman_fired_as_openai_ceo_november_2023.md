# Sam Altman Fired As OpenAI CEO In November 2023

Sam Altman was fired as CEO of OpenAI late on Friday, November 17, 2023, by the board of directors of OpenAI, Inc. — the 501(c)(3) nonprofit that acts as the governing body for OpenAI, the startup behind ChatGPT, GPT-4 and DALL-E 3. He both left the company's board of directors and stepped down as CEO, with chief technology officer Mira Murati stepping in as interim CEO.

OpenAI announced the shake-up in a post on its official blog; the company's management team was made aware shortly after. The blog post said Altman's departure followed a "deliberative review process by the board" which concluded that Altman "wasn't consistently candid in his communications" with other board members, "hindering its ability to exercise its responsibilities," and that the board "no longer has confidence in [Altman's] ability to continue leading OpenAI." In a joint statement the board wrote that "OpenAI was deliberately structured to advance our mission: to ensure that artificial general intelligence benefits all humanity," said it remained "fully committed to serving this mission," thanked Altman for "his many contributions to the founding and growth of OpenAI," and said that "we believe new leadership is necessary as we move forward" and that Mira, "as the leader of the company's research, product, and safety functions," was "exceptionally qualified" as interim CEO. OpenAI said it would begin a formal search for a permanent CEO immediately.

Altman responded on X: "i loved my time at openai. it was transformative for me personally, and hopefully the world a little bit. most of all i loved working with such talented people. will have more to say about what's next later." The removal came unexpectedly. OpenAI had just hosted its first developer conference, OpenAI DevDay, the week before with Altman emceeing; Altman had spoken at the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation conference and at an Oakland, California event on the Thursday. According to The Verge and The New York Times, citing multiple internal sources, OpenAI employees learned of Altman's firing when it was announced publicly. The same change also saw co-founder Greg Brockman step down as chairman of the board while remaining OpenAI's president, reporting to Murati.
