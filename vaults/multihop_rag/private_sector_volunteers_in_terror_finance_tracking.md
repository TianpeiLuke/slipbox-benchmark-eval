# Private Sector Volunteers In Terror Finance Tracking

The network blocking terror financing funds includes many entities, including private citizens: IMPA has been approached by many experts from the private sector — people with intelligence experience because they have worked in these organizations, or with high-tech experience — who "have mobilized like crazy."

"The world's financial intelligence authorities are now at the heart of this struggle, and we're working together to thwart the financing channels that we've located," Ostrovitch-Levi said. "Israeli entrepreneurs in Silicon Valley have contacted us; they write code, search for information, and give us lots of information. We aggregate and examine everything."
