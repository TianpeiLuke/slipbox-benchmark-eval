# What Makes A Five-Star Ski Holiday

A five-star ski holiday, as The Independent defines it, is one where the skiing is only part of the product: picture indulging in delectable regional dishes from personal chefs, Michelin-star dining and exhaustive pillow menus, or upgrading your après pints to champagne flutes as you peruse high-end Alpine boutiques. From the Swiss slopes of Zermatt to the pistes of Vail in Colorado, it's not just the snow that sparkles.

The distinguishing features are service and access rather than terrain. Bespoke adventure sports, including heliskiing, meet private ski instructors and pre-warmed boots in these prestigious resorts, while chalet board accommodation ensures there's post-slope cake and canapes waiting, with a side of steamy hot tub sessions. The framing case is envy of the privately chauffeured other side of the mountain — if the unbecoming lug of skis back from the slopes has you envious, it is time to join in with your own glamorous winter getaway. The guide then rounds up the world's most exclusive ski resorts and the lavish gondola-hugging chalets to book for unrivalled luxury off-piste.
