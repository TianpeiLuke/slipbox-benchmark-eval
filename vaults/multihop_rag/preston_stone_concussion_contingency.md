# Preston Stone's Concussion Contingency

Preston Stone's development hit a possible speed bump the week before the North Texas game when he left early in the fourth quarter at Rice after getting hit hard, zipping to the locker room and returning minus his helmet. As of Nov. 7, 2023 his availability for Friday depended on concussion protocol.

If protocol kept him out, redshirt freshman Kevin Jennings was expected to start for SMU; Jennings had thrown 42 career passes with 33 completions, four touchdowns and no picks. Sporting News set a conditional betting plan on that contingency: should Stone not play, it would call an audible and play the Under on the total, advising readers to monitor the situation closely and prepare to take action within an hour of kickoff.
