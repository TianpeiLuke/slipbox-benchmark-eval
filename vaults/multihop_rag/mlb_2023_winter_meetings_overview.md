# What The MLB Winter Meetings Are And Why The 2023 Edition Mattered

MLB's winter meetings are the window in which many of the top executives, players and agents all come together at the same spot, which makes them always a prime time for moves to happen, per Sporting News' 2023 tracker published Dec. 3, 2023.

The discriminating feature is the concentration of decision-makers and the chain reactions it produces. The previous year's winter meetings wound up being explosive: Aaron Judge, Justin Verlander, Trea Turner and Xander Bogaerts all came away with new contracts. The 2023 edition could wind up being similarly busy — there was a growing sense Shohei Ohtani could sign during the winter meetings, and a move of that magnitude could kick off the next wave of moves; there was also a number of top players on the trade market, including star Padres outfielder Juan Soto.

The 2023 meetings ran starting Sunday, Dec. 3 through Wednesday, Dec. 6, and were held in Nashville, with MLB's offseason expected to really kick into high gear over that span. Sporting News tracked all the latest news and rumors, following any signings and trades from Nashville.
