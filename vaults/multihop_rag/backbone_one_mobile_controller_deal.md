# Backbone One Mobile Controller Deal

The Backbone One mobile game controller came down to $70 at Amazon, Target, Backbone's online store and other retailers for Black Friday 2023 — a $30 discount, or 30 percent off its $100 price, and an all-time low for the USB-C version. Both the USB-C and Lightning models were on sale; the latter is compatible with Apple's iPhone 15 series as well as numerous Android phones.

Engadget gave the Backbone a positive review last year and recommends it in its guide to the best gaming handhelds: it's more cramped than a traditional gamepad, naturally, but it has all the requisite buttons to play modern games, it's extremely easy to set up and it works well for remote streaming from a console or PC. If nothing else, it's a much better value than the PlayStation Portal.
