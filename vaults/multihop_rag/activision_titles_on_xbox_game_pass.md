# Activision Titles On Xbox Game Pass

In a post on X on Monday, Activision Blizzard said it expects to start adding its titles to Game Pass starting next year, meaning it is quite possible that the new Call of Duty, among other games, will be playable on Xbox from day one.

That would reverse a long-standing arrangement: prior to the deal, Sony was usually the company benefiting from exclusives with Call of Duty — often early access to the game, testing and bonus features — but the BBC reports that may well now be a thing of the past. Microsoft has a habit of making its big blockbuster games available to play on Game Pass, for a monthly fee, on the first day they release, and the BBC leaves it to gamers to decide whether buying the game or paying to access it is the better deal.

The precedent that makes the concern concrete is Starfield: Bethesda, which is owned by Microsoft, launched the game in September 2023 to critical acclaim, and it was available exclusively on PC and Xbox and playable on Game Pass from day one. The BBC notes that this pattern concerned the UK regulator so much that it made sure Activision Blizzard games could not be streamed exclusively on Xbox for 15 years.
