# Daye's Vaginal Microbiome Screening Service

Daye launched a vaginal microbiome screening service a year before the STI test, as part of an ambitious push into gynaecological health, and tells TechCrunch it has had "thousands" of customers for that service.

The microbiome service is a little more complicated to engage with than the STI test, because samples need to be sent back along with an ice pack — and, hopefully, with minimal postal delays — so that the lab can detect live pathogens. The STI test is simpler: the PCR test looks for genetic material, dead or alive, and this type of test does not distinguish, so the user just needs to pop their sample in the extraction solution provided before posting it off for analysis. Daye also gave TechCrunch a breakdown of the main customers for its vaginal microbiome screening service a year-plus on from launch, saying there are three main groups.
