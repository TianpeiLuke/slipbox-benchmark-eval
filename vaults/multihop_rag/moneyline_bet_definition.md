# The Moneyline Bet

A moneyline bet is a wager whose sole focus is identifying the winner of a game or event. The victory margin and the total points scored have no bearing on it: it does not matter whether the chosen team clinches a win 2-1 or by a blowout of 50-0, because the payout remains unchanged. In this way the moneyline distills sports to their essence — cheering for a victor — and its primary draw is that simplicity, which lets even first-time bettors quickly get a handle on the basics.

Every moneyline bet has a favorite and an underdog as determined by the sportsbook, "favorites" and "underdogs" referring to the perceived stronger and weaker participants respectively. Moneylines hinge on the outright winner and are presented with either a positive (+) or a negative (-) sign: a positive moneyline indicates the underdog and represents the amount of profit on a $100 bet, while a negative moneyline indicates the favorite and shows how much you need to bet to earn a $100 profit.
