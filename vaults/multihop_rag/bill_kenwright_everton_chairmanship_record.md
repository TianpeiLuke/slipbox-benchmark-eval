# Kenwright's 19 Seasons As Everton Chairman

Bill Kenwright spent 19 seasons as Everton chairman, having been on the board since 1989 and taking over as chairman at Goodison Park in 2004. Over that tenure he oversaw 12 finishes inside the Premier League top eight, including fourth place in 2005, while the Merseyside club were also FA Cup finalists in 2009.

Everton said he had led the club through "a period of unprecedented change in English football". These figures are as reported by the BBC on 24 October 2023, at the time of his death.
