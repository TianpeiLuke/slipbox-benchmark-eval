# Metaverse Interoperability And 3D File Formats

Challenged by The Verge's Jay Peters that Roblox is its own walled garden — given other shared virtual experience apps like Fortnite or Meta's Horizon Worlds — and asked whether interoperability between them is possible, David Baszucki distinguished two types. One concerns valuable items, such as a pair of shoes from Nike, and whether an NFT supports the interchange; that may not be a 3D graphical technical spec so much as a cloud ownership spec. The other is the graphical interchange itself, and there he argued the genre is evolving so quickly toward networking hundreds of people in realistic 3D — are my shoes ultimately made of leather, do they actually bend and flex — that any 3D interchange format will be almost like a five-year-old file format by the time it exists. Roblox does bring in every type of industry file format it can and is trying to open that up as fast as it can.

Asked whether there will be energy five years out toward an interchangeable file format, Baszucki said there already is, but that it collides with what Roblox is under the covers: really trying to be a 3D world simulator, where the wheels fall off the car, the car falls on the ground, and your clothing is made of cloth. Existing file formats may not support that kind of physically rich description, so he imagines that for a long time, when Roblox brings in avatar files or similar assets, it will be using AI to upsample them into a physical manifestation.
