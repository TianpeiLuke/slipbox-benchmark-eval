# ICC World Cup 2023: Leading Wicket Takers

As listed on 18 November 2023, the leading wicket takers of the ICC Men's ODI World Cup 2023 were: 1. Mohammed Shami (IND), 7 matches, 23 wickets; 2. A Zampa (AUS), 11 matches, 23 wickets; 3. D Madushanka (SL), 9 matches, 21 wickets; 4. JJ Bumrah (IND), 11 matches, 20 wickets; 5. G Coetzee (SA), 8 matches, 20 wickets; 6. Shaheen Shah Afridi (PAK), 9 matches, 18 wickets; 7. M Jansen (SA), 9 matches, 17 wickets; 8. RA Jadeja (IND), 11 matches, 16 wickets; 9. Josh Hazlewood, 11 matches, 16 wickets; 10. MJ Santner (NZ), 10 matches, 16 wickets; 11. BFW de Leede (NED), 9 matches, 16 wickets.

The accompanying commentary gives the bowling detail, and for the top two names cites figures that differ slightly from the table. Shami announced his return to the World Cup by picking a fifer in the very first match he played for India, and is described as having picked up 24 wickets in just six matches while conceding just 257 runs, including three five-wicket hauls and a seven-wicket haul against New Zealand in the semi-final on Wednesday, 15 November at the Wankhede Stadium in Mumbai. Adam Zampa, the highest wicket-taker for the Aussies, picked up 23 wickets including three four-wicket hauls while conceding 515 runs. Dilshan Madushanka finished high on the list despite Sri Lanka's dreadful campaign, picking up 21 wickets and conceding 525 runs. Jasprit Bumrah took 20 wickets conceding 373 runs and had the best economy rate in the whole tournament at 4.06. The 23-year-old Gerald Coetzee picked up 20 wickets for the Proteas in eight matches conceding 396 runs. Shaheen Shah Afridi was the best bowler for Pakistan with 18 wickets conceding 481 runs in nine matches. Marco Jansen, standing 6 feet 7 inches tall and making full use of his height, took 17 wickets in nine matches conceding 450 runs. Ravindra Jadeja bowled beautiful spells especially in the middle overs, taking 16 wickets and conceding just 398 at an economy rate of 4.25 in 10 matches. The Australian pacer Hazlewood was phenomenal with the new ball, creating movement batters struggled with, and picked up 16 wickets in 11 matches. New Zealand's bowling mostly depended on Mitchell Santner, effective on the turning tracks of India, who picked up 16 wickets and conceded 449 runs in 10 matches.
