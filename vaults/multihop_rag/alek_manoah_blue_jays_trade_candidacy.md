# Alek Manoah Blue Jays Trade Candidacy

Alek Manoah was ranked No. 19 on Yardbarker's Nov. 13, 2023 list of the top 25 MLB offseason trade candidates after what the piece called perhaps the most disappointing 2023 performance of any player: within one season he went from Cy Young finalist to essentially unplayable, pitching to a 5.87 ERA over 19 big league outings while his walks more than doubled and his strikeouts fell from 22.9% to a modest 19% clip.

He lost his spot in the Toronto rotation and was eventually demoted to the minors; Manoah never pitched in Triple-A and received an injection in his throwing shoulder at the end of the season. As for how the Jays would proceed after that disastrous year, general manager Ross Atkins had said the previous week that the team would give him "a strong leg up" on the No. 5 spot in the rotation. The Jays were not broadcasting a desire to trade him, although they would not be doing themselves any favors by declaring they were ready to move on.

Dealing Manoah to bring in a lower upside but higher floor pitcher would not be a surprise with the Jays firmly in win-now mode. The former first-round pick was controllable for four more seasons and would not reach arbitration until the following winter.
