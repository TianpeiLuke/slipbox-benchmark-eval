# Pochettino And Arteta's Friendship From PSG

Mauricio Pochettino calls Mikel Arteta "family" because the two joined Paris Saint-Germain at the same time in early 2001 — Pochettino then 28, Arteta 18 — and hit it off immediately, with Pochettino wowed by Arteta's charisma and his reading of the game. "Already, he was a coach," Pochettino said.

The relationship explains Pochettino's tolerance of what some perceive as Arteta's histrionic touchline behaviour, about which he has no qualms. "It's how you are and about your character," Pochettino said. "He feels football. I am 51 and still sometimes I feel that decisions are no good or unfair. You are competitive. Like with Mikel or myself or other coaches that played football, in our minds, we still believe we can play. Still, we have the animal instinct. We cannot be thinkers."

The boundary Pochettino draws is not suppression but direction: for him it must be about channeling the emotion in the right way rather than stifling it. The Guardian added on 20 October 2023 that, more broadly, Pochettino was in a better place after the wins at Fulham and Burnley, which followed the Carabao Cup victory over Brighton on the day that Reece James was charged.
