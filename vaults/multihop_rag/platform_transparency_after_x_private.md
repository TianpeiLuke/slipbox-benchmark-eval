# Platform Transparency After X Went Private

An EU investigation would, indirectly, be a key way for the public to gain significantly more transparency around how X is being used — and abused. That is TechCrunch's claim about what regulatory scrutiny of the platform would deliver beyond any compliance finding.

The grounds are a change in X's corporate status. Since Musk took Twitter private, the company no longer produces quarterly earnings reports, and it is not held as closely to account as a result.

The warrant joining grounds to claim is the substitution of one disclosure channel for another. Public-company reporting is what ordinarily forces periodic, public disclosure about a platform's operations and scale; taking the company private removed that channel and the accountability pressure attached to it. A regulatory investigation compels disclosure through a different route, so it would restore to public view information about the platform's use and abuse that the market no longer extracts.
