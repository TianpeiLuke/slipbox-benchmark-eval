# Kevin Nguyen's Non-Writing iPhone Apps

Beyond his five writing apps, Kevin Nguyen's homescreen holds Wallet, Google Authenticator, Photos, Camera, Google Maps, Settings, Clock, Chase, Arc, Slack, Hello Weather, Pins, and Letterboxd.

Four come with explanations. Arc's iOS app is fairly incomplete, but it syncs with your sidebar, so he opens a bunch of tabs and reads those links on his phone when he gets on the subway. Hello Weather has been his salvation since the death of Dark Sky. Pins is how he uses Pinboard to save longform stories, meticulously tagged with the kind of notes you might expect from a features editor.

Letterboxd he calls quietly the best social app and a great way to triangulate what is out, streaming, or in theaters: if two or three friends have all logged an older film recently, it probably means it just hit Criterion or there is a revival at IFC.
