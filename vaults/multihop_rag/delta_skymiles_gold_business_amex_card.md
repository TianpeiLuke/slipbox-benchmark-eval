# The Delta SkyMiles Gold Business American Express Card

The Delta SkyMiles Gold Business American Express Card is an airline-type business credit card aimed at small business owners who fly with Delta regularly: it carries a welcome bonus of up to 75,000 miles and perks such as free checked bags and priority boarding, with a $0 intro annual fee for the first year and $99 thereafter.

For that $99 a year, cardholders receive priority boarding, the first checked bag benefit (including for guests), discounts on in-flight purchases, and baggage insurance — reducing travel costs and upgrading the travel experience. The boundary of the product is equally clear in Fortune's 2023-12-12 assessment. The card does not provide access to Delta Sky Club or Delta's airport lounges, which is listed as a con alongside the $99 annual fee; the pros are the free checked bag on Delta flights, the 75,000-mile welcome bonus, and no foreign transaction fees. The rewards structure is called decent but "nothing to write home about," offering 2x miles on just a handful of categories such as advertising, shipping, and dining. The card is therefore best for business owners who plan to fly with Delta frequently, since many of its perks can be used specifically on Delta flights, and it is a good card to hold long-term for the perks and discounts even if your spending is better put on another business card.
