# Will Smith's Slap Of Chris Rock At The 2022 Oscars

In March 2022, at the 94th annual Academy Awards, Will Smith walked onto the stage and slapped the comedian Chris Rock, then yelled at him to "keep my wife's name out your f***ing mouth." The altercation followed Rock's joke — "Jada, I love you. GI Jane 2, can't wait to see you" — referring to Jada Pinkett Smith's shaved head, which is a result of her alopecia. It quickly became the most talked-about moment of the evening, with the pair initially appearing to laugh off the tense moment. Later that night, while accepting the award for Best Actor, Smith became teary and said he was a "fierce defender of his family."

The incident was reassessed in October 2023 after Pinkett Smith revealed the couple had been separated since 2016 and so were separated at the time of the ceremony. UK-based dating coach Jacob Lucas, quoted by The Independent, tied part of the surrounding controversy to that timing and questioned Smith's behaviour and word choice at the awards given the separation.
