# Hipgnosis Investment Advisory Agreement Amendments

The amendments Hipgnosis Songs Fund confirmed on 28 September 2023 to its Investment Advisory Agreement (IAA) with Hipgnosis Song Management are a conditional, self-reversing termination mechanism tied to the fund's discount to NAV. They were set out in a circular to shareholders alongside a number of other new measures.

The first amendment gives HSM a 12-month notice period as HSF's investment advisor should the IAA be terminated at any point following the upcoming 2023 continuation vote. The second sets a trigger: if HSF's share price stands at an average discount to operative NAV of 10% or more across the month of January 2025 — with that NAV determined at the time of publication of HSF's interim report for the period to 30 September 2024 — the HSF board says it intends to serve notice to terminate the IAA with HSM. The board also gave itself the freedom to un-terminate the IAA during the entire notice period that would follow; for example, if notice were served at the end of January 2025, the board would have until the end of January 2026 to withdraw its termination decision.

One factor potentially making a future termination of HSM less likely is what would follow it: MBW understands that in such a scenario HSM would have a first-refusal option to acquire HSF's copyright portfolio, and could potentially turn to Blackstone — the backers of Hipgnosis Songs Capital — to find the money to pull off such an acquisition.
