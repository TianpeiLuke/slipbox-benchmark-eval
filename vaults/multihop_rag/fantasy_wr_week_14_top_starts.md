# Week 14 PPR Wide Receivers To Start

Tyreek Hill (vs. Titans) stood as the unquestioned WR1 for Week 14 in Sporting News' PPR rankings of Dec. 7, 2023, after the league's leading receiver torched the Commanders for five catches, 157 yards and two touchdowns.

The framing is that fantasy owners were gearing up for the last week of the fantasy regular season in most leagues, with the rankings loaded top to bottom because just two teams were on bye, and rolling out a strong receiving corps in a potential must-win week was essential.

CeeDee Lamb (vs. Eagles) and the Cowboys looked to avenge their road loss to the Eagles in Week 9, with Dallas' WR1 routinely carrying double-digit reception potential. Amon-Ra St. Brown (@ Bears) was relatively quiet the previous week but was likely to rebound against a Bears defense he totalled nine catches for 95 yards against in Week 11. A.J. Brown (@ Cowboys) reached the century mark for the first time in four games in Week 13, and another high-usage day looked to be in store for a potential shootout. Keenan Allen (vs. Broncos) caught just five of nine targets the previous week, but as Los Angeles' only reliable receiving option a get-right game felt likely. Justin Jefferson (@ Raiders) was set to suit up for the first time since Week 5 and was a must-start whenever active. Stefon Diggs (@ Chiefs) had not totalled 100-plus receiving yards in any of his past six games, but had every chance to get back on track against a Chiefs coverage unit that showed some leaks in Sunday's loss to the Packers.
