# AFLW Crowds Short Of Season-Extension Targets

Average AFLW attendance in 2023 was a little over 2,500 a match — tracking approximately on par with previous seasons, with a finals uptick likely to come — but crowds were short of the targets that could accelerate the extension of the regular season, which grows from 10 games that year to 12 games by 2025.

The league enjoyed strong attendances in round one, partly attributed to the so-called Matildas effect, but some clubs struggled to attract fans in large numbers across the season: crowds of less than 1,500 were recorded in both traditional markets such as Melbourne and Perth and in areas targeted for growth such as Blacktown and the Gold Coast.

Against that backdrop, Nicole Livingstone, general manager of women's football at the AFL, said the Swans' off-field offering had tapped into something "uniquely Sydney" and helped make matches more attractive.
