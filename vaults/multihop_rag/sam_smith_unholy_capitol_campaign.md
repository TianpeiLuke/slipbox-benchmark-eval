# The Sam Smith "Unholy" Campaign At Capitol

CMG, along with its colleagues at Capitol UK and the artist's team, was behind the Grammy-winning global smash that is Sam Smith's "Unholy", an unapologetically ribald banger from a singer who refuses to be pigeonholed as a sweet balladeer. Michelle Jubelirer says Sam drove the record from its creation through the decision for it to be the first single; she felt it was a huge leap forward, bold and brash and so distinctive from the first listen, and one of the strongest songs and records Sam had ever created.

Her own role was to marshal everyone in the company to come up with creative and strategic ideas to lead the global effort behind it, and to make sure the team, led by Arjun Pulijal, worked closely with Jo Charrington and her team at Capitol UK and with Sam's managers Jack Street and Sam Evitt. Digital marketing was key to the effort: the song is in the Top 3 of all-time creations and shares for a pre-release campaign, and the global teams worked together to connect that pre-release momentum to the biggest looks at the DSPs, in the media and at radio. Jubelirer calls it extremely gratifying that "Unholy" broke records at the streaming services and says the Grammy won with Kim Petras was well-deserved; she adds that Sam was the first new artist to break big at the company after it launched, that they are considered family, and that she wants Sam to be with the label forever and as much a part of its legacy as the great superstars who came before.
