# Jedd Fisch On What The Utah Win Signified

Asked whether the win against Utah — who had beaten the Wildcats in the last six meetings — signified anything further about the program, Jedd Fisch said the win signified that they are having a great year, declining to make a larger claim about the program's trajectory.

His grounds were the team's disposition rather than the result alone. "[It signifies] that we're not gonna back down from anybody, and we feel really good about trying to continue to improve, get more physical, get tougher," Fisch said.
