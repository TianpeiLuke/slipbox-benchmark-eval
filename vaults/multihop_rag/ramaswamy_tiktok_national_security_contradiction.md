# Ramaswamy's TikTok National Security Contradiction

The objection TechCrunch raises on 2023-10-02 attacks the premise that Ramaswamy's TikTok presence is a coherent outreach strategy: his presence on the platform conflicts with his own stated stances on social media and on young voters. His strategy is puzzling because he appears to be trying to replicate the success of previous candidates who became social media stars over the course of their campaigns, while holding positions that undercut the very audience and platform he is courting.

Two of his own positions supply the contradiction. Like many of his Republican colleagues, Ramaswamy has accused TikTok of being a threat to national security, and during a town hall days before he launched his account he described the platform as "digital fentanyl" from China. He has also proposed barring anyone under 25 from voting, unless they serve in the military or pass a civics exam. His communications director, Tricia McLaughlin, did not immediately respond to TechCrunch's request for comment.
