# Carabao Cup 2023/24 First Round Format And Results

The opening round of the 2023/24 Carabao Cup saw all 72 clubs from the Championship, League One and League Two take part, drawn into 36 single-elimination knockout matches based on geography — a draw involving northern clubs and a separate draw for southern-based clubs — with matches level after 90 minutes going straight to penalties, per Sporting News on Dec. 19, 2023.

The results on Tuesday, Aug. 8 were: Huddersfield 2-3 Middlesbrough; Swansea 3-0 Northampton Town; Mansfield Town 2-0 Grimsby Town; Newport County 3-1 Charlton; Peterborough 1-1 Swindon Town, Peterborough advancing 4-1 on penalties; Plymouth Argyle 2-0 Leyton Orient; Bolton Wanderers 1-0 Barrow; Gillingham 3-1 Southampton; Forest Green 1-3 Portsmouth; Rotherham 1-1 Morecambe, Rotherham advancing 4-2 on penalties; Cheltenham 0-2 Birmingham City; Accrington Stanley 1-1 Bradford City, Bradford advancing 4-1 on penalties; Stevenage 1-1 Watford, Stevenage advancing 4-3 on penalties; Hull City 1-2 Doncaster; Millwall 0-4 Reading; Sheffield Wednesday 1-1 Stockport, Sheffield Wed. advancing 4-1 on penalties; Sunderland 1-1 Crewe Alexandra, Crewe advancing 5-3 on penalties; Notts County 0-2 Lincoln City; Preston North End 2-2 Salford City, Salford City advancing 4-2 on penalties; Harrogate Town 1-0 Carlisle; Barnsley 2-2 Tranmere Rovers, Tranmere advancing 7-6 on penalties; Exeter City 2-1 Crawley Town; Derby County 0-2 Blackpool; Blackburn Rovers 4-3 Walsall; Port Vale 3-2 Fleetwood; Sutton United 2-2 Cambridge United, Sutton advancing 6-5 on penalties; MK Dons 0-2 Wycombe; Stoke City 2-1 West Brom; Wrexham 0-0 Wigan Athletic, Wrexham advancing 4-2 on penalties.

On Wednesday, Aug. 9: Leeds United 2-1 Shrewsbury; Bristol City 5-1 Oxford United; AFC Wimbledon 2-1 Coventry City; Cardiff City 2-2 Colchester, Cardiff advancing 3-0 on penalties; Ipswich Town 2-0 Bristol Rovers; Burton 0-2 Leicester City. On Wednesday, Aug. 16: QPR 0-1 Norwich City.
