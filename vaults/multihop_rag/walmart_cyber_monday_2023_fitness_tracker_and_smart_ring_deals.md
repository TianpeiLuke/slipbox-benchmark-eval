# Walmart Cyber Monday 2023 Fitness Tracker And Smart Ring Deals

WIRED's Walmart Cyber Monday roundup of 27 November 2023 picked three discounted wearables: the Ultrahuman Ring Air smart ring, the screenless Whoop 4 band and Fitbit's Versa 4 smartwatch.

The Ultrahuman Ring Air (7/10, WIRED Recommends) is a fitness and sleep-tracking ring that does not require a subscription. Made from titanium, it comes in a few different colours and tracks your movements through the day and night to give you a huge data dump in the companion app. WIRED's stated caveats are that the fitness tracking is flaky and the black version scuffs easily, though the four-day battery life is good.

The Whoop 4 is WIRED's pick for the most unobtrusive wearable: there is no screen, so it offloads data to a companion app that tries to guide you toward a healthier lifestyle and boost your fitness levels. Its subscription is pricey at $30 per month, but a year of it is included with this deal. The Versa 4, which WIRED calls the best Fitbit smartwatch available, lived up in testing to its claim of six-day battery life provided you do not leave the display on the entire time, and it pairs a vibrant, snappy 1.5-inch AMOLED touchscreen with plenty of Google apps such as Maps to round out a rich feature list.
