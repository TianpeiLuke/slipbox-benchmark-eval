# Don't Go Breaking My Heart (2011): Johnnie To's Romantic Comedy

Don't Go Breaking My Heart is a 2011 Hong Kong romantic comedy directed by Johnnie To and Wai Ka-fai, running 1h 55m, starring Louis Koo, Daniel Wu and Gao Yuanyuan. Polygon presents it as evidence of To's range — one of our great modern directors, equally adept in hard-boiled triad crime dramas and light-hearted romantic comedies — and calls the film one of the many high marks of the Hong Kong director's legendary career.

The structure is a love triangle. Fresh off the end of a long-term relationship, Chi-yan (Gao Yuanyuan) is an analyst for an investment bank caught between two men: Sean (Koo), a CEO who works across the street and yearns for her through the tall corporate glass windows that separate them, and Kevin (Wu), an alcoholic former architect who helps Chi-yan move on and is inspired by her to start creating again. Polygon calls the result a sincere, funny and truly charming romantic time. It is available to stream on Netflix.
