# Tyreek Hill's Receiving-Yards Pace, 2023

Six catches for 163 yards and a touchdown against Carolina left the Dolphins' Tyreek Hill with 812 receiving yards through the first six games of 2023, second in NFL history to Hall of Famer Don Hutson, who had 819 through six with Green Bay in 1942, The New York Times reported on 16 October 2023.

The full top ten for receiving yards through six NFL games reads: Don Hutson (1942) 819; Tyreek Hill (2023) 812; Wes Welker (2011) 785; Elroy Hirsch (1951) 766; Santana Moss (2005) 743; DeAndre Hopkins (2015) 726; Raymond Berry (1960) 725; Jerry Rice (1986) 723; Bob Boyd (1954) 718; and Bobby Mitchell (1962) 714.
