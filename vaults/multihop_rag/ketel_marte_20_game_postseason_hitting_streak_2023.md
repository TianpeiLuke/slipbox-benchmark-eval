# Ketel Marte's 20-Game Postseason Hitting Streak

Ketel Marte extended his postseason hitting streak to 20 games at 8:15 p.m. on Oct. 31, 2023, singling up the middle to lead off the bottom of the first inning of Game 4 of the World Series against the Rangers.

He kept producing through the night. At 9:17 p.m. the Sporting News noted that at least Marte showed up for the Diamondbacks, as he went 2-for-2 after a double off Andrew Heaney. At 9:51 p.m. he was retired for the first time that night, on a ground ball that moved Geraldo Perdomo over to second.

His last plate appearance of the game came late: at 11:14 p.m. Marte swung at a pitch well out of the zone and struck out.
