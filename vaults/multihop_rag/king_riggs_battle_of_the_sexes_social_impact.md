# King-Riggs Battle Of The Sexes And Its Social Impact

Billie Jean King has said the King-Riggs match was about social change — women standing up for themselves in all areas — but she argues it was really about men, too, because men started to shift a little as a result.

Her evidence is a single illustrative case with a long tail: Obama was 12 years old when he saw the King-Riggs match, and he said it affected him a lot. The warrant she draws from that is generational rather than immediate: "Guys are much better thinking about their daughters than they used to be. All these things add up."
