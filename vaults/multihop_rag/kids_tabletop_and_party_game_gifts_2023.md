# Kids' Tabletop And Party Game Gifts 2023

The Verge's 2023 kids gift guide lists four tabletop and party games: Poetry for Neanderthals at $19.99 (Amazon, Walmart, Target), We're Not Really Strangers Kids Edition at $10+ (Amazon, We're Not Really Strangers, Toys R Us), Shadows in the Forest at $31.99 (Amazon, Legacy Toys), and Hurry Up Chicken Butt at $19.99 (Target, Exploding Kittens).

In Poetry for Neanderthals, up to six players are tasked with getting their team to guess a word or phrase using only single-syllable words — and if you fail, be prepared to get whacked with an inflatable club. The kids edition of We're Not Really Strangers is pitched as an enjoyable exercise for having deeper conversations with children, with thoughtful prompts likely to elicit unexpected answers from both adult and child, plus rainbow-colored pencils as a playful touch. Shadows in the Forest is a cute tabletop game you play in the dark, à la flashlight tag: an asymmetrical game for two to seven players in which one player uses the lantern to search for the others' Shadowling characters, encouraging the group to work together. Hurry Up Chicken Butt is the guide's example of the best kind of children's game — one that gets them learning or doing something positive without realizing it — a fast-paced, easy-to-learn game of hot potato with parents that works in a bit of exercise and gets everyone laughing together.
