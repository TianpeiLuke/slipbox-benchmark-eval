# Encounters Of The Spooky Kind: Sammo Hung's Jiangshi Martial Arts Comedy

Encounters of the Spooky Kind is Sammo Hung's jiangshi — Chinese hopping vampire — martial arts comedy, starring Sammo Hung, Chung Fat and Dick Wei, and available to watch on the Criterion Channel, where it was finally added earlier in the fall of 2023 after years of being unavailable digitally. Polygon's reviewer describes it as an absolute blast.

Its defining feature is that it is frequently a one-man show: Sammo directed the movie, co-wrote it, choreographed the action, and stars as Bold Cheung, a pedicab driver and skilled martial artist who is also kind of a dolt, and who is dared to spend the night in a haunted house with a hopping vampire — a dare he accepts, because he is "Bold" Cheung, after all. What follows is a Looney Tunes-style slapstick action horror movie with legitimate scares (the vampire makeup is terrific: gray with a gross texture, like a wet papier-mâché mask), dazzling rhythmic martial arts choreography, and perfectly placed dashes of comedy, including an extended Duck Soup homage.

Best known for his collaborations with childhood friends Jackie Chan and Yuen Biao, Sammo's excellence as a director, choreographer and performer is on full display; the reviewer argues he does not get proper credit globally because of the genres and nation he has primarily worked in, and that what separates him from his counterparts is his skill with the camera rather than the choreography alone. Yuen Biao co-stars as the silent vampire, selling the undead creature's fight sequences with stiff limbs and startling hops. The boundary case for recommendation is Evil Dead fans: Sam Raimi basically directly ripped one of Spooky Kind's fight sequences for Evil Dead II.
