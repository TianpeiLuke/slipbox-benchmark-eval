# Samsung Galaxy Buds 2 Deal Listings

Samsung's Galaxy Buds 2 appear in The Verge's December roundup at a best price of $89.99 ($60 off) at Best Buy — about $10 more from Amazon, Walmart, and Verizon — and in WIRED's October list as another of its picks for Android users (9/10, WIRED Recommends), with that deal limited to the olive green model.

The Verge calls them a good-sounding pair with active noise cancellation, wireless charging, and a subtle design, and says the price isn't bad for Samsung's entry-level buds, which hit above their weight class on noise cancellation and audio quality. WIRED describes them as a slightly more affordable version of the pricier Samsung set, similar in price to the standard AirPods, getting five hours of battery life with active noise canceling on and an impressive 7.5 hours with it off, coming with a charging case and delivering solid bass via a dual-driver array.
