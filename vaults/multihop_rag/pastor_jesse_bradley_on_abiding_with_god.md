# Pastor Jesse Bradley: The Motto Matters Less Than What Is Written On Hearts

Pastor Jesse Bradley of Grace Community Church in Auburn, Washington, near Seattle, told Fox News Digital this week that "In God We Trust" remains just as essential today as the United States faces cultural upheaval with numerous signs that faith is waning. His central claim is that relationship, not inscription, is what counts: "It's good to have trust written on our currency, but what really matters is that it is written on our hearts."

His grounds are a claim about where a nation's character resides. "The soul of a nation is the core, the essence and the deepest part of a country," he said. "Closeness with God is reflected and expressed in our relationships, the laws of the land, and morality. Real trust is essential in a vibrant relationship with the living God, not merely lip service." He ranks that relationship above the competing goods on offer: "Abiding with God goes beyond and is more valuable than money, technology and entertainment." And he rejects partial observance: "We can't compartmentalize God into one day, hour or place. This relationship with our Maker is 24-7, as God is the source of our strength, love, security and hope."

His diagnosis of the present is that the country has substituted counterfeits for the real thing — "We have forsaken living water and created idols that can't even hold water" — and that "We have wandered away from God in countless ways, including idolatry, materialism, pride and hatred." From this he draws the conclusion: "I believe we are at a crossroads spiritually," and "America needs to come to her senses and realize the goodness of God." He closes on mercy rather than judgement: "God knows us completely and loves us perfectly," and "May God have mercy on us in spite of our rebellion."
