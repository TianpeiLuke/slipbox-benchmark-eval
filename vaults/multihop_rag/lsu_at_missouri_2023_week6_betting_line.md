# LSU At Missouri: The 2023 Week 6 Betting Line

No. 23 LSU was favoured by 6.5 points at No. 21 Missouri, according to CBS Sports' Week 6 lines published 1 October 2023.

The two teams' trajectories are set in contrast. LSU had already gone from a trendy College Football Playoff pick to a two-loss team, while Missouri was looking to improve on an impressive start that already included a Week 3 win against defending Big 12 champion and longtime former conference-mate Kansas State.

The matchup's pivot is LSU's defense against Missouri's quarterback. LSU was looking to right the ship after a pair of shaky defensive performances: the Tigers allowed 426 yards in a 34-31 scare against Arkansas in Week 4 before giving up a whopping 637 yards in a 55-49 loss at Ole Miss on the Saturday. Missouri quarterback Brady Cook ranked fourth in passing within the SEC with 1,468 yards and would bring another test for that unit; through five weeks Cook had thrown 11 touchdowns and was interception-free.
