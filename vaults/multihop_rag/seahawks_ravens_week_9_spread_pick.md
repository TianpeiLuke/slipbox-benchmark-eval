# Seahawks +6 At Ravens, Week 9

Sporting News took the Seahawks at +6 in Baltimore in Week 9 of 2023, predicting Ravens 26, Seahawks 23. The reasoning starts from the line itself: Baltimore's defense had been incredible of late and the Ravens seemed to be flying under the radar at 6-2, but the sportsbooks had clearly taken note of that run by giving the NFC West-leading Seahawks a full touchdown on the road — which looked like a great opportunity to back the underdog, considering Seattle had won two straight and gone 2-1 on the road, including a big win over the high-flying Lions in Detroit.

The supporting claims concern personnel trending upward. Geno Smith had not been the same Pro Bowl quarterback as in 2022 but had still shown moments of brilliance for Pete Carroll's squad, and with DK Metcalf and Kenneth Walker III a week closer to full health — and backup running back Zack Charbonnet looking on the verge of a breakout — the column liked Seattle to at least make a game of it. The Seahawks' defense had quietly been pretty good, and the offense was capable of fireworks when everything clicked. Bettors were told they could take an extra 1.5 points as insurance if they wanted, but the pick was Seattle to cover the +6.

Other Week 9 spreads the column liked were Patriots (-3) vs. Commanders and Raiders -1.5 vs. Giants.
