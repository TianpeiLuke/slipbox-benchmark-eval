# Flipboard's Path To The Fediverse

Flipboard announced its intention to participate in the fediverse earlier in 2023, and began by integrating its app with Mastodon via an API and setting up its own Mastodon server, flipboard.social, ahead of full ActivityPub integration. That first step allowed Flipboard to get a feel for the world of decentralized social media and learn how its users would respond, and it also gave the company a way to stay connected to social media after Twitter/X increased its API fees for third-party developers, making it unsustainable for many developers to continue working with the company.

"As we said in the earlier part of this year, we will be embracing ActivityPub into Flipboard and effectively reworking our whole backend to that," Flipboard CEO Mike McCue explained to TechCrunch. He noted that the company had first integrated with Mastodon at the API level, so users could log into their Mastodon accounts, see those posts and interact with others in the fediverse from Flipboard — "but you had to have an account on all those platforms."
