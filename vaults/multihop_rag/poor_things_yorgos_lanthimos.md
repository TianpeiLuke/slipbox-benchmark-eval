# Poor Things (Yorgos Lanthimos)

Poor Things is Yorgos Lanthimos' deeply weird, neo-Frankensteinian fable about a reanimated woman's quest for independence, identity, and the meaning of life, starring Emma Stone, Mark Ruffalo, and Willem Dafoe. Polygon ranked it 15 on its best movies of 2023 list of 2023-10-07, when it was in theaters, and joked that along with everything else it is also goth Barbie.

Polygon calls it the unlikeliest but most effective Oscar bait — a reunion of The Favourite star Emma Stone, its Greek surrealist director, and its screenwriter Tony McNamara — tipped to win a few real Oscars despite making The Favourite look like a normal movie.

Adapted from a cult novel by the eccentric Scottish writer, illustrator, and typesetter Alasdair Gray, the film reworks Frankenstein to explore themes of feminism, sex, and social rot. Lanthimos doubles down on the baroque stylings of The Favourite to create an astonishingly visually dense film of outlandish costumes, lavish sets, and fantastical painted backgrounds, often shot through woozy fish-eye lenses, and it is also, surprisingly, one of the year's most hilarious movies, in no small part thanks to Mark Ruffalo's game campery and McNamara's cleverly twisted syntax. But critic OW insists the movie is Stone's: her performance is fearless, frank, funny, and intensely physical, and Bella is no less than a deconstructed human being, whose slow reassembly makes you fall in love with an insatiable, ferocious force of nature whose appetite for life and love for humanity never dim despite their many disappointments.
