# The Boy and the Heron (Hayao Miyazaki)

The Boy and the Heron is Hayao Miyazaki's film starring Soma Santoki, Masaki Suda, and Ko Shibasaki, ranked 7 on Polygon's best movies of 2023 list of 2023-10-07 and playing in theaters at the time.

Polygon's frame is texture, which it calls essential to Miyazaki's work: sometimes the most visceral memories of his movies are the ways liquids glob or glide while wood flecks flint and flake in tiny explosions, and famously even his still shots contain some motion, often the slightest of movements that make the world feel more full and more real.

The story follows 11-year-old Mahito, who is bored and miserable after moving to the country following his mother's death in a World War II bombing. His grief has a touch familiar to those who have lived with it — bland and isolating — but it is not long before an errant heron proves the veil between Mahito and his more vivid dream world is much thinner than he knew.

Critic ZM writes that no matter which side of the fantastic the film is on, it makes the texture of the world feel enthralling: little details such as a bandage loosening in water or a granny's gait weave together into a world that feels at once like anything Studio Ghibli has made and wholly its own. It meditates on its themes without lingering, never letting its story falter even as its hero languishes in country tedium, and shows that Miyazaki is always game to top himself, finding new ways to add layers to every part of his work.
