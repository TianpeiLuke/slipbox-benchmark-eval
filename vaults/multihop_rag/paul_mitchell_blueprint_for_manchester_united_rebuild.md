# Paul Mitchell's Blueprint For A Manchester United Rebuild

Paul Mitchell argues that Manchester United must define an identity and a five-year blueprint and then hold to it, because consistency of decision-making over that period is what produces sustainable success — a blueprint he laid out while still at Monaco, reported by talkSPORT on 16 October 2023.

His grounds begin with context and end with comparison. "I think context is important to measure what Manchester United's current situation is, but I think they do need to get to the point of really putting down what Manchester United needs to be in the modern game," he explained. "We are a long time from the Sir Alex Ferguson era. Erik ten Hag is a top coach from Ajax but they need a top blueprint not only for now but for the next five years, and work towards that, and sometimes on that journey there are hard moments when you don't get the right results, even though you're doing the right things." The warrant is that results in the short run are a noisy signal: "You have to know that, over that period, the consistency of the decision-making will yield a good end result. I think we've seen that both Manchester City and Liverpool, that when you adopt that long-term strategy you can get real consistency and sustainable success."

The content of the blueprint is an identity statement spanning the whole club: "putting down their identity of what they want to be today, but most crucially what they want to be in five years' time, and where they want to be, and what they want to look like; from everything from young players, to the style of play, to the whole culture peace in Manchester United." There is a counter-consideration attached — almost certain to consult Ralf Rangnick before any approach from Ratcliffe, Mitchell may be warned off a club where long-term thinking has not existed — but should Ratcliffe get his man it could signal exactly the change in fortunes he and every supporter has been hoping for.
