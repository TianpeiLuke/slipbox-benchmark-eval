# The Sampling Workflow On The K.O. II

Grabbing sounds on the K.O. II is straightforward: to sample from a PC, phone or other sound-making device, you just need to connect it with a 3.5mm cable; for everything else you use the built-in mic, which Engadget's reviewer found surprisingly good, recording a few short vocal phrases and other found sounds that came out well — assuming you're in a quiet environment.

If you don't have samples yet, the K.O. II ships with a bunch pre-installed, and they're pretty great: a good mix of drums, bass pads and lead sounds, certainly enough to get you going straight away, though you'll definitely want to add your own to make projects unique. The presets use about half of the 64MB of memory, but they can be backed up and deleted to free the slots for your own material. Maximum sample length is 20 seconds — the same as on the OP-1 Field.
