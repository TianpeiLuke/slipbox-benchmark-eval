# Spider-Man: Across The Spider-Verse Debuts On Netflix

Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse became available to stream on Netflix in the first weekend of November 2023, the biggest debut of that week by Polygon's reckoning. It is a superhero action film running 2 hours 20 minutes, directed by Joaquim Dos Santos, Kemp Powers, and Justin K. Thompson, with a cast including Shameik Moore, Hailee Steinfeld, and Oscar Isaac.

The highly anticipated follow-up to 2018's Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse sees Miles Morales facing off not only against a dimension-hopping nemesis in the form of the Spot, but against a whole multiverse of Spider-Mans, Spider-People, and even a Spider-Dinosaur as he attempts to save the day once again. Polygon's review, quoted in the roundup, concedes that not every theme and plot and moment lands, particularly with the other part of the story still most of a year away, but argues that the theme of the Spider-Verse movies is shaping up to be a story about people trying to be bigger and bolder themselves, reaching beyond what they are told they are capable of. On that reading it is no wonder that every part of Across the Spider-Verse is an attempt to outdo the first movie: the idea of growing, of surpassing and ignoring everyone else's limits, is the heart of this series' heroes and their individual journeys, and the movies themselves look designed to follow suit.
