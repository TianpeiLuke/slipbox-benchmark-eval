# Kieran Crowley Criticises The Italian Rugby Federation

Kieran Crowley pushed back on 6 October 2023 against the idea that Italy's World Cup should be judged on its two heavy defeats, arguing instead that the players deserve better treatment off the field from the Italian Rugby Federation — and that this, not the results, is the real problem.

His tenure ended in profound disappointment, though the crushing defeats by the All Blacks and now the hosts were offset by victories against Namibia and Uruguay. Crowley had wanted to continue: "I've said before, I'd like to take this team forward. I think the next World Cup is our cycle … we'll get to the next tournament, and a lot of these players will have 60, 70 caps. But that wasn't my decision."

The premise he attacks is that the last two games are a fair measure of the group. "What needs to happen is that these players need to be treated with a bit of respect off the field … They [the Italian Rugby Federation] need to get some of that stuff sorted. Quite honestly, I don't know if I'd want to be involved unless that gets sorted. I respect these guys, these players and staff. They've gone way beyond … and yet people will judge us on these last two games. It's disappointing."
