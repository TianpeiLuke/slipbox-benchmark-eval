# EU Formal Request for Information to X

Late on Thursday October 12, 2023, the European Commission sent X a formal request for information under the [Digital Services Act](digital_services_act.md), following its public warning two days earlier and X CEO Linda Yaccarino's high-level response.

The Commission said the request followed "indications received by the Commission services of the alleged spreading of illegal content and disinformation, in particular the spreading of terrorist and violent content and hate speech." It examines X's compliance across notices on illegal content, complaint handling, risk assessment, and measures to mitigate identified risks.

X had until October 18 to supply information about the activation and functioning of its crisis response protocol, and until October 31 for the remaining requests. EU officials confirmed to TechCrunch that **no investigation had been opened at that point**; the request could prefigure formal proceedings under Article 66 but is not itself that step. Were proceedings opened, it would be the first DSA investigation since the VLOP compliance deadline took effect that summer.

At Musk's projected revenue of roughly $3 billion for the year, a 6% penalty would reach about $180 million.
