# The Strategic Role Of Technologists In Product Innovation

Technologists play a strategic role in product innovation and should bring a metaphysical perspective in addition to being punctilious. Their job is to communicate the essence of a technology and think strategically about the application of technologies to problem spaces, and they are most constructive when they translate the technicals of "how to make X do Y" into "these are the types of products and services realizable with technology X."

The author concedes the difficulty: for most technical leads and software developers this is a mode inversion from their traditional tactical and direct interaction with technology, and the context switch from daily build-and-operate is challenging — but it is paramount to developing successful and innovative products, because technologists are uniquely positioned to generate strategic insights translated from dense technical minutiae that drive innovative business cases and product experiences.

The claim is offered without conditions on product stage or technology maturity: regardless of whether a product is growing or in a stable stage, and whether it employs established or emerging technologies, integrating technologists into the product strategy and design process enriches the final outcome. There is a technology perspective extending beyond the operations and mechanics of the code "factory floor" which provokes innovation — sometimes leading to small, impactful moments of innovation, and sometimes to a brilliant revolution.
