# Crosby, Malkin And Letang's 18th Season Together

Sidney Crosby, Kris Letang and Evgeni Malkin took the ice for their 18th season together on Oct. 10, 2023, set to surpass Yankees legends Derek Jeter, Mariano Rivera and Jorge Posada as the longest-tenured trio of teammates in North American sports history.

The Sporting News framed it at 7:02 p.m. before puck drop: if it feels like the three have been in Pittsburgh forever, that's because they had.

The record spans four leagues — Pens Inside Scoop noted the trio were set to surpass the New York Yankees' Jeter, Rivera and Posada as the longest-tenured trio of teammates in NHL, MLB, NBA and NFL history — with the group billed as the Big Three.
