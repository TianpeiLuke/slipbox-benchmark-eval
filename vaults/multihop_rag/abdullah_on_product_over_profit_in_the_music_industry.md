# Abdullah on Product Over Profit in the Music Industry

Asked what one thing he would change about the music industry, AfroFuture co-founder Abdul Karim Abdullah said he would have it focus on the product rather than the profit. His ground for that claim is an effect he attributes to the current emphasis: "Profit as a metric has lowered the caliber of the content we consume." The warrant joining the two is his judgement that this has "diluted the purity of music" — that is, using profit as the governing metric changes what gets made, not merely how it is sold.

Asked separately what advice he would give an aspiring entrepreneur in the music industry, Abdullah said: "remain true to your purpose and to always be authentic. With discipline and a solid foundation around you, you can reach your desired level of success – even when you meet adversity or when doors continue to close – you must never give up."
