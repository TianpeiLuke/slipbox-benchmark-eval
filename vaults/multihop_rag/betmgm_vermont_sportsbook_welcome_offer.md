# How BetMGM Sportsbook's Up-To-$1,500 First Bet Offer Would Work In Vermont

BetMGM's welcome offer is a "First Bet Offer" that pays back up to $1,500 in Bonus Bets if you lose your first bet after registering, Sporting News explained on 2023-09-26. Known as the "King of Sportsbooks," BetMGM Sportsbook is another favorite around the US and had applied for an online sports betting license, so it should be one of the new sites in Vermont; when that happens, Vermont bettors should be able to take advantage of that offer.

The steps: start by entering the bonus code SPORTSPICK when you create your BetMGM Sportsbook account; deposit at least $10; make a first bet, and if you lose, get back the equivalent amount in Bonus Bets up to $1,500 within 24 hours of the bet being settled. The payout structure depends on stake size — for losing bets up to $50 you receive the Bonus Bet as a single bet, while if you bet more than $50 and lose you'll get your refund, up to $1,500, in five Bonus Bets divided equally at 20% each. You'll have seven days to use your Bonus Bet(s), and if you use a Bonus Bet and win your wager, you receive the winnings but not the wager amount back. On what you can win: as with similar offers, you can win whatever you get from placing winning wagers with your Bonus Bets, and the longer the odds on those bets, the more you can potentially win. On the product, BetMGM offers lots of ways to bet on many different sports and leagues with a wide range of standard pregame bets — moneyline, spread, totals — plus lots of props, futures and live betting opportunities, and is generous with bonuses and promos including free pools, ongoing refer-a-friend bonuses and the BetMGM Rewards program.
