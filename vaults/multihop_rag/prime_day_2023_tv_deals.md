# Prime Day 2023 TV Deals

WIRED's TV picks are Samsung's The Frame, a quantum-dot OLED, a 65-inch Amazon Fire TV, the Hisense U8K, the Hisense U7, a very large TCL, and its mid-tier favorite for most people.

Samsung's The Frame is for anyone who cares about the aesthetics of their space and doesn't want to stare at a TV screen all the time: it looks like a piece of art when not in use, blending into a well-curated room. The quantum dot-enabled OLED is the brightest organic LED display WIRED has tested, with shockingly bright colors, great contrast, and small bezels aiding a super immersive picture — one of the best TVs for brighter rooms. Amazon's Fire TVs are a solid and affordable way to get a quality screen for very little money, and this 65-inch model has Dolby Vision support for good colors plus voice control with Alexa. The Hisense U8K is among the best-value TVs WIRED has ever seen, with a mini-LED display for ultra-bright colors and great contrast, and super easy setup and use thanks to the onboard Google interface (8/10, WIRED Recommends). Hisense is a purveyor of quality mid-tier TVs and the U7 is no exception: for a larger screen to enjoy sports, its 144-Hz native refresh rate makes games — real and virtual — look smooth as silk, and it supports the latest high dynamic range codecs for the brightest, most vibrant colors. The TCL is a solid option for a super large screen without a lot of cash: support for the latest HDR codecs like Dolby Vision for great colors and a built-in Fire TV interface with essentially any app you want; not the brightest or prettiest TV ever, but big with bold color. WIRED's favorite TV to recommend for most people is the mid-tier model (7/10, WIRED Recommends), with quantum dot technology and support for every HDR format for stunning colors, plus a game mode whose software can upscale 1080p to 120 frames per second — noting that, unlike older models, this one comes with Google TV instead of Roku, and that this is the lowest price tracked so far.
