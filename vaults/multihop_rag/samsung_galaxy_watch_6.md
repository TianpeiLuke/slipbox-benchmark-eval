# Samsung Galaxy Watch 6

The new Samsung Galaxy Watch 6 went down to $270 for October Prime Day — reported two days later as $269 and still on sale — which is the lowest price Engadget had seen since it came out a few months earlier.

Engadget's verdict: you cannot get a better smartwatch than this one if you have an Android phone, with bonus points if you have a Samsung phone.

It is smaller and lighter than previous versions. Samsung brought back the spinning bezel on the Classic version, which makes the software's UI easier to navigate, and it has improved performance and updated health and fitness tracking tools.
