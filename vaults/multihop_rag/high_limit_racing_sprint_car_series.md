# High Limit Racing: Kyle Larson's Sprint Car Series

High Limits Racing is a Sprint Car Series co-owned by NASCAR Cup Series driver Kyle Larson and four-time World of Outlaws Series champion Brad Sweet, who is Larson's brother-in-law. What distinguishes it is that Larson runs it while remaining a full-blown title contender in the Cup Series with Hendrick Motorsports — the venture by the two motorsports athletes has been a success so far, and one of their races recently pulled an extraordinary crowd, which included a rather popular figure in the world of motorsports, Dale Earnhardt Jr.

The 2021 Cup Series champion is in the prime of his career and has a lot of years left ahead of him in NASCAR, but when he decides to hang up his boots, he will hope that the High Limits Racing Sprint Cup Series achieves a high level of national popularity so that he will not have to take himself out of the racing business.
