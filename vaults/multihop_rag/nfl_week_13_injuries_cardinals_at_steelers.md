# NFL Week 13 Injuries Cardinals At Steelers

In the Week 13 final injury reports published by CBSSports.com on Dec. 1, 2023, with the Steelers listed at -5.5 against the Cardinals, Arizona carried two questionable players and Pittsburgh only one game designation.

Despite missing the entire week of practice, the Cardinals were holding out hope for Brown to play by listing him as questionable. McBride was also questionable after being limited on Thursday and Friday, but told reporters that he was "good to go." Pittsburgh listed Adams as its lone player with a game designation to wrap up the week; safety Minkah Fitzpatrick (hamstring) practiced fully throughout the week, carried no designation, and told reporters Friday that he would play that weekend.
