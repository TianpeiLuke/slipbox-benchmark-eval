# Scalable Capital's Brokerage Platform And Scale

Scalable Capital started as a digital wealth management platform and now describes itself as a full-service brokerage. As of December 2023 it has 1.2 million savings plans on the platform, which the company says works out to over 600,000 customers, and close to €17 billion under management, with products covering ETFs, stocks, funds, bonds, cryptocurrencies, derivatives and loans. It gives users access to investing in 8,000 stocks, 2,500 ETFs and 3,500 funds among other products, and ETFs are the most popular today.

What distinguishes its positioning, as with others like it, is that it leaves the door open for smaller and more incremental buy-ins from its customers. Co-CEO Erik Podzuweit also points out that the startup's average user age is 35 — a person perhaps with more disposable income than some of the younger consumers that other neobrokers have courted.
