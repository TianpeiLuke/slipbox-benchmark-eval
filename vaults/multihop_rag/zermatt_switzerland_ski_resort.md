# Zermatt: 360km Of High-Altitude Slopes Beneath The Matterhorn

Zermatt has 360km of high-altitude slopes and is an oasis for advanced skiers to cruise down linked blues and tackle advanced reds at the foot of the Matterhorn. Rustic charm oozes from the Swiss resort's chalet lodgings, and the car-free town is where you'll find glitzy brands — Moncler to Omega — if you fancy a taste of the finer things in life.

Ski Solutions offers a seven-night stay at Ulysse 8, a five-star chalet in Zermatt, for £1,565pp on chalet board, with a ski instructor available from Monday to Friday at no extra cost. Six cosy bedrooms sleep 12 keen skiers in the chalet opposite the charming Winkelmatten chapel, a short walk from the Matterhorn Express gondola, with wellness rooms that guarantee relaxation after a day on the slopes. Departing 13 January 2024. Return flights from London Luton to Zurich Airport start from £107 with easyJet in January.
