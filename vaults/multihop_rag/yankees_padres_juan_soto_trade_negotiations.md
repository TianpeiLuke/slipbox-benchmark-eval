# How The Yankees-Padres Juan Soto Negotiations Progressed

Talks between the Yankees and Padres for Juan Soto went from stalled on Sunday, Dec. 3, 2023 to "likely" by Wednesday, Dec. 6, per the reporting Sporting News carried in its winter meetings tracker.

On Sunday, Dec. 3, Jon Heyman of the New York Post reported the Yankees were pushing hard to acquire Soto from the Padres, but that the teams had not spoken since San Diego requested a six-player package in return for Soto and Trent Grisham — an ask that included pitchers Michael King and Drew Thorpe, whom the Yankees were not inclined to move. Jeff Passan reported a trade of Soto appeared to be "edging closer to becoming a reality," and that while several teams were out on him because of his large salary and pending free-agent status, there still could be a large market for the star outfielder.

By Wednesday, Dec. 6, YES's Jack Curry reported the Yankees had "intensified efforts" to trade for Soto and that a deal "is likely," expecting New York to deal four or five players; WFAN's Brandon Tierney reported YES was told by the team to prepare for a 2 p.m. ET emergency "Hot Stove Show" and that there was a chance the Yankees completed the deal by lunch. New York had landed Alex Verdugo the previous night in a deal with the Red Sox, and although there was some speculation Verdugo could be flipped to the Padres, MLB Network's Jon Morosi reported that New York was "expected" to keep the outfielder rather than include him in a deal with San Diego.
