# Zane Duursma AFL Draft Prospect 2023

Zane Duursma, profiled in Kevin Sheehan's top 40 for the 2023 AFL Draft as reported by The Roar on Nov. 15, 2023, is a mercurial forward or midfielder who reads the play exceptionally well, is strong overhead and deadly accurate around the goals. State: Victoria Country. State League Club: Gippsland Power. Community Club: Foster. Date of Birth: 28/08/05. Height: 189cm.

He was an outstanding player for Victoria Country at the National Championships to earn All Australian honours, including a match-winning performance against Victoria Metro in the final game where he amassed 22 disposals, 10 marks and four goals. He was also dominant for the Gippsland Power, winning their best and fairest and earning selection in the Coates Talent League Team of the Year, and finished the year with 42 goals across 20 matches in all competitions. His natural leap was highlighted at the national Draft Combine with his running vertical jump of 97cm ranked second overall. He hails from a talented football family, with his brother Xavier at Essendon and sister Yasmin playing for Port Adelaide. Weapon: Aerial skill and goal sense. AFL player comparison: Isaac Heeney (Sydney Swans).
