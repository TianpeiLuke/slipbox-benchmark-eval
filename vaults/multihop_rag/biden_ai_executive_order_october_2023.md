# The Biden AI Executive Order Of October 2023

The Biden Administration unveiled its next steps in addressing and regulating artificial intelligence development on Monday: an expansive executive order (EO) that seeks to establish further protections for the public as well as improve best practices for federal agencies and their contractors.

The order is framed as a whole-of-government instrument. "The President several months ago directed his team to pull every lever," a senior administration official told reporters on a recent press call. "That's what this order does, bringing the power of the federal government to bear in a wide range of areas to manage AI's risk and harness its benefits ... It stands up for consumers and workers, promotes innovation and competition, advances American leadership around the world and like all executive orders, this one has the force of law."

Its provisions are phased rather than immediate. The actions will be introduced over the next year, with smaller safety and security changes happening in around 90 days and more involved reporting and data transparency schemes requiring nine to 12 months to fully deploy. To keep that schedule, the administration is also creating an "AI council," chaired by White House Deputy Chief of Staff Bruce Reed, who will meet with federal agency heads to ensure the actions are being executed on time.
