# India vs Australia: ODI World Cup Head-To-Head Record

As of 18 November 2023, India had faced Australia a total of 13 times in ODI World Cups, with the Aussies dominating on eight wins; India had, however, won the last two of those meetings, most recently in the league fixture of the 2023 World Cup in Chennai. The 2023 final was the fourth time India had qualified for an ODI World Cup final, whereas for Australia it was their eighth.

The article frames the fixture as a callback: two decades earlier these two goliaths of the cricketing world faced each other in the final of the ICC Men's ODI World Cup in Johannesburg, South Africa, and plenty of wounds are still to be healed for India because the defeat in the 2003 final against Australia still feels fresh. Writing before the result, it says it is only time to tell whether the Aussies will repeat history or whether India will write history by winning their third ODI World Cup.
