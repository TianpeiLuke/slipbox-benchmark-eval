# 2024 Mock Draft: Six Edge Rushers Projected In Round 1

CBS Sports' 2024 NFL Mock Draft of 2023-11-17 projects six edge rushers in the first round. At Pick 5, Chicago takes Dallas Turner, Alabama, a junior at 6'4" and 252 lbs, ranked 14th prospect and 3rd at the position: Chicago traded for and signed Montez Sweat to a lucrative contract extension but Yannick Ngakoue has given them nothing, so Turner allows them to have a potentially dynamic duo. At Pick 9, Atlanta takes Jared Verse, Florida State, a junior at 6'4" and 260 lbs, 12th prospect and 2nd at the position, because Atlanta likes heavy-handed pass rushers who can set the edge and Verse falls into that category — the Falcons have added some older veterans at the position but it is not a viable long-term solution. At Pick 10, Washington takes Laiatu Latu, UCLA, a senior at 6'5" and 265 lbs, 11th prospect and 1st at the position: after moving on from Chase Young and Montez Sweat, Washington needs pass rush, and Latu may end up being the best out of this draft class.

At Pick 19, New Orleans takes JT Tuimoloau, Ohio State, a junior at 6'4" and 270 lbs, 15th prospect and 4th at the position, adding a powerful rusher — Cam Jordan is 34 years old with two years remaining on his contract, and the Saints have a quality rotation of rushers until Tuimoloau eventually takes over. At Pick 20, from Houston, Arizona takes Chop Robinson, Penn State, a junior at 6'3" and 254 lbs, 29th prospect and 6th at the position, adding an edge rusher with higher upside to Jonathan Gannon's defense. At Pick 24, from Cleveland, Houston takes Bralen Trice, Washington, a junior at 6'4" and 274 lbs, 26th prospect and 5th at the position: DeMeco Ryans is not going to settle for one or two pass rushers, he wants an Army, and Trice paired with Jonathan Greenard and Will Anderson Jr. is a solid foundation.
