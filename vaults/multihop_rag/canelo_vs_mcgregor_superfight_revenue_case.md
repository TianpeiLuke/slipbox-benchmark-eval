# The Revenue Case For A Canelo-McGregor Superfight

Essentially Sports argues on 2023-10-18 that a potential clash between Saul "Canelo" Alvarez and "Notorious" Conor McGregor would be a money magnet, funnelling income from a variety of channels and leaving either party significantly wealthier than before. The claim rests on both the spectacle — the excitement and allure of two mammoths of combat sports getting in the ring to pummel each other to the brink of their endurance is undeniable — and on the enumerated revenue streams: pay-per-view sales, live gate revenue, merchandise sales from either party, and various promotional events around the fight.

The warrant offered against the obvious objection — would people actually want to watch it — is the demand already demonstrated: the attention the prospect gathered in the small span of their X (Twitter) feud should be evidence enough. To that the article adds that both fighters command a massive fan base, counted in millions across the globe.
