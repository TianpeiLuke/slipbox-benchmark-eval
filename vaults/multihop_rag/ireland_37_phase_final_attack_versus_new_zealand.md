# Ireland's 37-Phase Final Attack Against New Zealand

Ireland's last attack of the 2023 Rugby World Cup quarter-final ran to 37 phases and five minutes before ending in a turnover, with the 28-24 scoreline staring back at them from the giant screen. You could feel the desperation around the Stade de France, awash with green thanks to the Irish fans doing their part, as Ireland went through phase after phase after phase after phase hunting the try that could overturn the deficit.

The move started well inside their own half, with 77 minutes on the clock. It took in 37 gruelling phases of Bundee Aki grinding out extra yards with each punishing carry, Tadhg Beirne left at a literal standstill through tiredness, and Johnny Sexton — for the final time ahead of retirement — running the famous wraparound upon which he has built maybe the greatest Ireland career of all time.

But it ended, almost inevitably, with a turnover deep in the New Zealand 22, the ball being kicked to touch and the men in green sinking to the floor bereft.
