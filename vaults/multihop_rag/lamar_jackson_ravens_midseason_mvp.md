# Lamar Jackson Ravens Midseason MVP

Yardbarker's Nov. 9, 2023 AFC midseason team MVP list named quarterback Lamar Jackson the Baltimore Ravens' MVP, concluding that he was the reason, once again, that Baltimore was a Super Bowl contender.

The grounds are his production and its timing: Jackson had inserted himself into the league MVP conversation during his team's four-game win streak, and the 2019 NFL MVP had thrown for 1,954 yards and nine touchdowns while posting 440 yards and five touchdowns rushing. The warrant is that dual-threat production concentrated in a winning run is what converts individual numbers into team contention.
