# Canelo Alvarez's Undisputed Super Middleweight Titles And $180 Million Net Worth

Canelo Alvarez reached his career high by becoming the first boxer to be the undisputed champion at super middleweight, holding the WBA Super, WBC and Ring Magazine super middleweight titles since 2020 and the IBF and WBO titles since 2021, Essentially Sports reported on 2023-10-18. Alongside the belts, his pockets became deeper and deeper, to the point that Canelo boasted a $180 million net worth in 2023. The most significant addition to his bank balance came when he signed a lucrative $365 million deal with DAZN and Golden Boy Promotions.

The article's accompanying image credit places Saul "Canelo" Alvarez of Mexico at the weigh-in ceremony prior to his boxing fight against Jermell Charlo of the USA, at the T-Mobile Arena esplanade in Las Vegas, Nevada, on September 29, 2023.
