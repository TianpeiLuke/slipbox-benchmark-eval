# Julian Nagelsmann's Career And His Bayern Munich Dismissal

At just 36 years old when he took the Germany job, Julian Nagelsmann was a rising star who had spent three successful seasons at Hoffenheim followed by two years at RB Leipzig, which catapulted him to the managerial post at Bayern Munich in 2021.

He steered the Bavarian giants to the Bundesliga title in his first season, but was sacked by the club in March of 2023 amid a furious league title fight. The dismissal came as a shock to much of the European football landscape, as the club had performed exceptionally in the Champions League and was still very much in the title race, sitting just a point behind Dortmund.

Now, with a chance for redemption at the helm of the national team, Nagelsmann would see his time begin with a friendly against the United States.
