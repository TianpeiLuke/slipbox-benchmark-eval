# Diablo 4 Barbarian Leveling Build

This Barbarian leveling build, based on Lexyu's Barbarian leveling guide at IcyVeins and published by Polygon on 2023-10-19, is purely for leveling — allocating your 58 skill points between levels 1 and 50. As with most Barbarian leveling guides it uses Hammer of the Ancients as the primary skill, since it deals massive damage while remaining very cheap to cast, plus Frenzy, Death Blow, Rallying Cry, Leap, and Wrath of the Berserker. Readers wanting more on item affixes or other Legendary powers are pointed to the original IcyVeins guide.

Skills must be purchased in a specific order, because some require a number of allocated points in the tree to unlock, so new skills must be unlocked before existing ones are enhanced. The order is: Frenzy / Enhanced Frenzy; Hammer of the Ancients / Enhanced Hammer of the Ancients / Furious Hammer of the Ancients; Combat Frenzy; Rallying Cry / Enhanced Rallying Cry / Tactical Rallying Cry; Hammer of the Ancients (ranks 2-3); Leap / Enhanced Leap / Power Leap; Hammer of the Ancients (ranks 4-5); Death Blow / Enhanced Death Blow / Warrior's Death Blow / Death Blow (rank 2-5); Wrath of the Berserker / Prime Wrath of the Berserker / Supreme Wrath of the Berserker; Pit Fighter (ranks 1-3); Tempered Fury; Furious Impulse (ranks 1-3); Walking Arsenal; Hamstring; No Mercy; Expose Vulnerability (ranks 1-3); No Mercy (ranks 2-3); Swiftness (ranks 1-3); Thick Skin (ranks 1-3); Counteroffensive (ranks 1-3); Defensive Stance (ranks 1-3); Slaying Strike (ranks 1-3); Aggressive Resistance; and Prolific Fury.

Once the Weapon Expertise system unlocks, place the two-handed sword in your Technique slot, which causes all of your Hammer of the Ancients damage to make enemies Bleed.

At the start of a new character, complete three dungeons first to collect their Aspects, then place them on a piece of gear, ideally jewelry when possible — replacing these Legendaries with new versions using the imprint system every few levels. They are: Aspect of Ancestral Force from the Sunken Ruins dungeon in Scosglen, which makes Hammer of the Ancients quake outwards, dealing X% of its damage to enemies; Aspect of the Expectant from the Underroot dungeon in Scosglen, where attacking enemies with a Basic Skill increases the damage of your next Core Skill cast by X% up to X%; and Edgemaster's Aspect from the Oldstones dungeon in Scosglen, where skills deal X% increased damage based on Primary Resource when cast, with the maximum benefit at full Primary Resource.
