# Eagles-Seahawks Week 15 Monday Night Context

Going into the Week 15 2023 Monday night game at Lumen Field, Seattle was 6-7 and on the outside looking in on the playoff picture, while Philadelphia arrived as losers of two straight, both in blowout fashion.

A win on Monday night against the suddenly reeling Eagles could be a big boost to the Seahawks' chances going forward — a matchup that was actually a pretty good one after looking beneath the surface. For Philadelphia, the sky was suddenly falling, as the Cowboys had taken over the NFC East division lead, complicating matters as the regular season wound down. The sense of urgency for both sides meant two teams with plenty to lose, setting up a fun Monday night under the lights.
