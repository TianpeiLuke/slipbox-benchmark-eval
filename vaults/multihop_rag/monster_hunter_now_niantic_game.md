# Monster Hunter Now (Niantic)

Monster Hunter Now, developed by Niantic for Android and iOS, is ranked 29 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31. Polygon's assessment is that while Niantic's post-Pokémon Go track record has not been the most consistent, this game shows the company at its best, with a refined combat system, simple matchmaking, great monster variety, and extensive character upgrades.

Critic Matt Leone qualifies the praise on monetization: those upgrades are part of a setup that gets a little heavy-handed, but if you are patient there is plenty of game here for free.

The distinguishing feature is how the real-world layer is used. Unlike in some of Niantic's other games, the real-world elements do not feel tacked on: the design blends almost perfectly with the company's map tech, allowing players to feel like they are tracking monsters as they get outside and walk around — a big step forward in fusing Niantic's real-world tech approach with combat and exploration mechanics that can stand up on their own.
