# LG Sound Bar C (SC9)

Finding the right soundbar to pair with a fancy TV can be a challenge — you first have to decide what features you want, then land on a design you like — so Polygon's shortcut is that if you opted for the LG C3 OLED, you might as well get the soundbar LG made to be its perfect companion in both respects.

The SC9 soundbar and wireless subwoofer bolster the C3's phenomenal picture quality with amazing audio, including Dolby Atmos support. Purchased through LG's website, the package comes with a mounting bracket to fasten the soundbar to your wall, which suits a wall-mounted television.
