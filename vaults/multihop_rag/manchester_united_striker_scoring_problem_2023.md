# Manchester United's Striker Scoring Problem, 2023

Manchester City forward Erling Haaland had scored more Premier League goals in the 2023-24 season (9) than Manchester United's five forwards had managed in all competitions combined (8), and United's top scorer was actually defensive midfielder Casemiro with four strikes, Sky Sports reported on 28 October 2023 ahead of the derby.

Erik ten Hag declined to place the blame on the forwards alone. Asked if they were at fault for the frontline problem, he replied: "Fault? It's co-operation. It has to click. We have shown in some games, we have so many in overload positions to the opponents goal and we don't net or don't even hit the target. We should be doing this better."

Marcus Rashford's start to the season had been sobering, with one goal so far that term, but Ten Hag said he had full faith the England forward, who scored 30 goals last season, can find his scoring boots again. "If you give him the trust and I play him every game, most games also he finishes the game. I have strong belief he will return to scoring a lot of goals," said Ten Hag. "It also has to do with the co-operation, with the movement around and the distribution to him. We have to work on that and that's my focus point."
