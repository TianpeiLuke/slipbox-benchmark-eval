# Google's Secret OEM And Carrier Deals And Its Deleted Chats

Asked what surprised him in the courtroom, Tim Sweeney said the trial confirmed practices he had suspected since around 2018 — when some people called him a conspiracy theorist for saying so — including that Google leaked Epic's conversations to reporters to get negative stories written, paid other developers off to convince them not to launch their own stores, and went around paying carriers and OEMs secretly not to carry competing stores.

Sweeney gave a concrete instance from Epic's own experience: when Epic tried to bundle Fortnite with other smartphone manufacturers such as OnePlus and with carriers of all sorts, those partners told Epic they couldn't do a deal because Google had done a secret deal with them. He called it disconcerting to see the extent of bad-faith efforts inside a company of Google's size, saying you would expect a trillion-dollar company to have developed respectable processes and leadership structures providing checks and balances against wrongdoing. Instead, he said, Google employees were "rampantly destroying all their chats on these topics": long conversation threads would start to get into a spicy antitrust issue, somebody would point out that history was on, and the chat would go silent — history turned off so the documents would be destroyed. Sweeney said it was great to see all of that called out in detail at trial.
