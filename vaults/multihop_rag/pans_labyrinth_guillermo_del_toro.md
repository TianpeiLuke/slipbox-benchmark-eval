# Pan's Labyrinth (Guillermo del Toro)

Pan's Labyrinth is not just a great horror movie but one of the Great Movies, and among the best examples of a fairy tale for adults. It was the Oct. 22 pick in Polygon's 2023-10-10 Halloween Countdown, available to purchase on Amazon, Apple, and Vudu.

Set during the aftermath of the Spanish Civil War, the film follows Ofelia, played by Ivana Baquero, after her pregnant mother takes her to live with her new stepfather Captain Vidal, played by Sergi López — a fascist Falangista hunting down the republican rebels hiding out in the Spanish forest. In this terrifying time Ofelia encounters a faun, a mythical creature played by Doug Jones who claims she is the reincarnated Princess Moanna, daughter of the king of the underworld, and tells her that if she completes three tasks she can return to the king and queen and live forever as princess of the underworld.

Critic Joshua Rivera writes that, like many folk tales before they are sterilized by pop culture, it is a fairy tale full of menace, danger, and horror — but also hope: both a refuge from a turbulent time and an attempt to understand it, a parable that becomes timeless in its specificity with new meaning to discover on every revisit. Widely considered writer-director Guillermo del Toro's magnum opus, it shows him operating at the peak of his powers with lavish production design, evocative colors, and naturalistic performances juxtaposed with quiet, dangerous magic. What makes it a great film, Rivera says, is astonishingly simple: some movies endeavor to make sure you understand them, while the best ones help you understand yourself — Pan's Labyrinth wants to know what kind of person you are by the time the credits roll.
