# Inter Miami-FC Cincinnati First Half: Three Times Off The Woodwork, Still 0-0

Inter Miami completely dominated the confirmed 2023 regular season champions in the first half at DRV PNK Stadium on Oct. 7, 2023 but went in level at 0-0, having hit the woodwork three times inside the opening 30 minutes. Needing victory to stay in the MLS playoff picture, they were so unlucky not to cash in on multiple occasions; FC Cincinnati had one really good look that they missed but were otherwise on their heels the entire 45 minutes, with head coach Pat Noonan telling the broadcast his side showed "a lack of courage on the ball" that half, "as bad as its been all year." Lionel Messi was available off the bench and might yet have a job to do.

The half opened with an Inter Miami free-kick from about 35 yards, taken by Noah Allen with Messi on the bench; the training-ground delivery meant to create a lateral cross for DeAndre Yedlin was overcooked and wasted over the end line (3rd minute). Inter Miami held the attacking possession early, but Matt Miazga held firm before being fouled by Josef Martinez in the air (8th). In the ninth minute Tomas Aviles missed with the goal gaping, whipping a quality finish at a bouncing ball near his hip from a corner but crashing it off the crossbar; seconds later FC Cincinnati countered with venom and Sergio Santos slid to meet a delivery from the right that flashed wide of the near post. Callender then made a diving, full-stretch save in the 12th after Brandon Vazquez settled a ball from deep and found Santos, whose shot was affected by a Tomas Aviles tackle.

Inter Miami kept coming. In the 14th minute Facundo Farias took a dangerous free-kick just outside the area — Martinez having been bundled over from behind by Junior Moreno — and the floated delivery found a teammate's head but the shot was weak and easily caught by Celentano. Josef Martinez's hold-up play created a chance for a charging Benja Cremaschi in the 20th, but his driven effort went wide. In the 22nd Martinez ripped a shot from a tight angle about 15 yards out that beat Celentano but clattered the far post, and in the 24th Cremaschi, fed through on a one-two with Martinez, bent the ball with the outside of his right foot around Miazga and onto the near post — a third woodwork strike in under half an hour. Rain began falling hard in South Florida as Sergio Busquets blasted another set-piece into the wall in the 26th, with fans chanting "Messi, Messi." Obinna Nwobodo was booked for a very late tackle on Farias in the 28th and gave the referee sarcastic applause. In the 30th Farias forced Celentano into a full-stretch save with a right-footed blast, the Supporters' Shield winners barely surviving the siege. By the 38th, with no clear-cut chances since that save, the broadcast showed Messi looking very antsy on the bench as some Inter Miami players — not him — were called off the touchline to warm up. A goalmouth scrap in the 43rd left Celentano scrambling wildly but nobody could get on the end of it.
