# HIV Key Population Treatment Gaps

Several key populations sit far below the UNAIDS treatment targets: an estimated 44% of transgender people with HIV are on ART, HIV-positive men who have sex with men have 78% ART coverage, just 65% of HIV-positive sex workers take ART, and 69% of HIV-positive people who inject drugs do.

Compared with the general adult population, these groups have far higher HIV prevalence, ranging from fourfold greater among sex workers to 14-fold greater among transgender people. Those numbers could be an undercount, as many countries do not track these populations. Punitive laws, police harassment, harsh stigma and social taboos keep many people out of HIV care, while high rates of incarceration and sexual violence also raise their risk of acquiring HIV. Lifting discriminatory policies and weaving HIV care into trusted, community-based programs will be key to reaching these demographics. Stigma remains a live public issue: representatives of HIV organizations marched along Whitehall in London, England on March 18, 2023, calling for an end to the stigma that still surrounds HIV.
