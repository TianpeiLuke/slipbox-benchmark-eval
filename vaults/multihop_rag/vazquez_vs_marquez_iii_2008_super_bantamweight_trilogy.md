# Vazquez SD 12 Marquez III: The Best Of The Most Exciting Modern Trilogy

Israel Vazquez beat Rafael Marquez by split decision over 12 rounds on March 1, 2008 at the Home Depot Centre in Carson, California, retaining the WBC and Ring Magazine super bantamweight titles in the third and best fight of their series. It won Fight of the Year for 2008.

Sporting News argues the two Mexicans turned in the most consistently exciting trilogy in modern boxing history, criminally overlooked; there was an ill-advised part four, won by Marquez, but this was and always will be a trilogy. Where Ali-Frazier II, Gatti-Ward II and Barrera-Morales II did not live up to acts I and III of those fistic plays, Vazquez and Marquez produced three back-to-back classics that will stand the test of time. The only reason the first encounter, won by Marquez, was not awarded Fight of the Year 2007 was that it was beaten by the rematch, won by Vazquez, in the same year.

As in fights one and three, the ebb and flow was incredible, with both men trading hellacious combinations and Vazquez hitting the canvas in round four. Marquez's superior speed and timing was carrying the day, but Vazquez slowly battled back and was aided further when his rival was deducted a point for a low blow in round 10. The 12th and final round took the fight into the stratosphere: desperate for a big finish, Vazquez nailed his countryman in the closing seconds and only the ropes held Marquez up. Ruled a knockdown, that crucial moment gave Vazquez the split decision and his championships.
