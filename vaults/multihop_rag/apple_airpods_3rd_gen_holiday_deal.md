# Apple AirPods 3rd Gen Holiday Deal

The third-generation AirPods with the Lightning charging case dropped to $140 across the 2023 holiday sales — at Amazon and Target on Cyber Monday, at Amazon, Target and Best Buy over Black Friday, and at Amazon and Best Buy in the wider Black Friday roundup. They carry a list price of $169 but often sell for closer to $150, so this is $10 off the usual; other accounts put it as matching the lowest price seen, with the earbuds typically retailing between $10 and $20 higher over the past few months.

These non-Pro AirPods came out in 2021 and got a review score of 88 from Engadget. They pair quickly with iPhones and are a good complement to Siri's helpfulness, with the usual AirPods conveniences like hands-free Siri, Find My device tracking and quick pairing with other Apple gadgets; they don't have ANC — you'll need the AirPods Pro for that — but they do have Adaptive EQ which monitors and adapts sound in real time, the fit is comfortable, and they're water-resistant so they can handle workouts or the weather. The reservations: you can get better-sounding and more featured earbuds for much less, and because this is an open design there isn't much in the way of deep bass or noise cancellation, though they sound noticeably richer than their predecessor and their haptic touch controls are more convenient; not everyone will find the wider earpieces comfortable, but they may suit an iPhone owner who hates the feeling of traditional in-ear headphones.
