# Prior SEC And CFTC Actions Against Binance

The DOJ charges against Binance came over five months after the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission accused the exchange and Zhao of lying to regulators about its operations, filing 13 charges against the defendants in the federal case.

The SEC's theory concerned the relationship between Binance and its U.S. arm: Zhao and Binance were allegedly "intimately involved" in directing the trading entity's business operations and providing crypto-related services to the Binance.US platform, which claims it is an independent exchange in the SEC filing.

In late March 2023 the U.S. CFTC also filed a suit against Binance, Zhao and its Chief Compliance Officer Samuel Lim for allegedly breaking trading and derivatives rules.
