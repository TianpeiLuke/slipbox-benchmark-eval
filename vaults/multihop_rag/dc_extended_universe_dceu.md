# The DC Extended Universe (DCEU)

The DC Extended Universe was the original name for DC Comics' movie and TV line, launched with Zack Snyder's Man of Steel in 2013. Polygon characterizes it as having gone through some major growing pains since that launch.

Its defining feature is strategic instability. Looking for ways to compete with Marvel Comics' massively popular Marvel Cinematic Universe, DC repeatedly switched tracks and tactics on the DCEU — sometimes steering toward a single interconnected universe, then backtracking into a more director-driven plan.

The boundary case that illustrates the second mode is Todd Phillips' Joker, a film that could be made without fitting into any specific previous continuity. Polygon set out this account on 2023-10-24.
