# The Prop Bet

A prop bet is a "side bet" with a sportsbook on a specific event or statistical accomplishment within a game, and these rarely have anything to do with the outcome of the game itself. Examples include betting on LeBron James scoring over 20 points in a single game, or the Dallas Cowboys gaining over 300 receiving yards in a game.

Props are easy to understand and add a twist to most sporting events, because you can bet on in-game events not tied to the outcome. They are popular throughout the year and are posted for every major sport, but the pinnacle for prop betting is the Super Bowl — the most popular day of the year for prop bets, which range from the typical to the exotic. Sporting News notes that you do not have to wait for the Super Bowl to get involved.
