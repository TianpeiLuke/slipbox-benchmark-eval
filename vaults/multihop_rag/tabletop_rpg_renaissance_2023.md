# The Tabletop RPG Renaissance Of 2023

After more than a decade of talk about the "board gaming renaissance," Polygon's 2023-12-13 roundup declares that tabletop role-play is finally having its moment in the spotlight.

The reporting separates the trigger from the cause. Dungeons & Dragons' OGL fiasco has clearly lit a fire under many players eager to make a change, but a sizeable cohort of writers and designers have been toiling away at excellent games for years, and their tireless efforts have yielded a bumper crop of excellent, some might say genre-defining, TTRPGs. As with board games, a few modern classics have cropped up: Polygon points to The Quiet Year and The One Ring, both making their second appearance on this list. The list's method is stated too: as before, Polygon asked nearly two dozen writers, designers, presenters, actors, and personalities from around the world of tabletop gaming to share the TTRPGs that made an impact on them and their players that year.
