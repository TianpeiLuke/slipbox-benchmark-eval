# Turk's Assessment Of Bedard's Release Speed

Tim Turk said that on a shot release basis only he would pick Connor Bedard over Auston Matthews: the claim is that Bedard can get the puck off the quickest with the most deception and optimal speed, power and accuracy from a starting point to a finishing point, and the warrant is that Bedard is uniquely selective — he does not need the preparation other shooters take, as reported by The New York Times on 17 November 2023.

The grounds are behavioural. "What makes him unique is that he can be selective with it," Turk said. Looking back on that first blind introduction he laughs: "You know, most, when you're in a non-pressure, non-stressful situation, you'll play around with the puck a lot and then you'll take your shot because I'm telling them to take their time. To me, when he takes a shot, it looks like he just bends down, picks it up with his hand and places it where he wants to." Turk attributes the underlying belief to Bedard himself: "He believes that 'Hey, f— it, the puck is only two and a half inches wide, I can put it wherever I want.'"

The comparison is carefully bounded. Turk would take Bedard's shot against anyone's today, and says: "On a shot release basis only, if I had to bet on who could get the puck off the quickest, with the most deception, with optimal speed, power and accuracy based on a starting point to a finishing point, I'm picking Connor Bedard over Auston Matthews. And I'm not taking anything away from Auston. It's just a little bit different because one's a righty, one's a lefty and one's got a little bit of a higher-angle pull-in change."
