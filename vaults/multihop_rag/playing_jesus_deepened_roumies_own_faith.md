# Playing Jesus Deepened Roumie's Own Faith

Asked how playing the role of Jesus in "The Chosen" had changed his life and changed him, Jonathan Roumie said in December 2023 that it has allowed him to go deeper into his own relationship with Him.

"It's caused me to examine what exactly is my relationship with Him — and how can I go further in that relationship, how can I get close to Him, how can I grow deeper in communion with Him," he said. "So it's challenged me on that level."

He framed the challenge and the growth as the same thing: "But as it challenges me, it also allows me to go further in that relationship — and to deepen my prayer life."
