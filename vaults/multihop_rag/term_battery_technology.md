# Battery Technology

## Definition

Battery technology is the set of chemistries, materials, and control software used to store and deliver electrical energy, spanning the dominant lithium-ion cell chemistry, emerging alternatives such as aqueous zinc-iodine cells, software that modifies how existing cells charge and age, and system-level approaches such as swappable battery networks for electric vehicles.

## Context

In the corpus, battery technology surfaces across three distinct threads: a software startup that retrofits charging behavior onto existing lithium-ion cells, academic materials research seeking a safer lithium-ion alternative, and an EV company built entirely around a swappable-battery network rather than fixed onboard cells. Breathe Battery Technologies is covered as a startup whose software can be slipped into just about any lithium-ion battery in use today, giving it either faster charging speeds or greater longevity. Separately, researchers at Nanyang Technological University worked on aqueous zinc-iodine batteries as a substitute for lithium-ion cells, addressing lithium-ion's own drawbacks — chief among them the fire and explosion risk from thermal runaway. Gogoro takes a third approach: rather than improving the cell itself, it sells scooters and licenses a "standard battery size" so that swappable batteries can be exchanged at network stations instead of charged in place, and it is expanding this battery-swapping model from Taiwan into India. The sector is also volatile as a business matter — ONE, described as an "EV battery startup," cut about a quarter of its workforce in November 2023, citing market conditions.

## Key Characteristics

- **Lithium-ion is the incumbent chemistry** — it dominates personal electronics and EVs, with 2020 consumption estimated at 185 GWh and projected to reach 2,035 GWh by 2030, driven mainly by EV demand.
- **Lithium-ion's core weakness is thermal runaway** — overheated lithium-ion batteries carry an inherent risk of burning or exploding, and have been responsible for a number of past fires.
- **Software can retrofit existing cells** — Breathe Battery Technologies' software does not change cell chemistry or hardware; it changes charging behavior on lithium-ion batteries already deployed, trading off faster charging against greater longevity rather than delivering both at once.
- **Zinc-iodine is a safety- and cost-motivated alternative** — a negative iodine electrode paired with a positive zinc electrode gives lower cost and better environmental and safety profiles than lithium, but at the price of lower energy output and vulnerability to zinc dendrite growth and anode corrosion.
- **Swappable-battery networks decouple the vehicle from the cell** — Gogoro's model relies on a standardized battery size shared across roughly 11 partner vehicle manufacturers in Taiwan, letting riders exchange a depleted battery for a charged one at a station instead of waiting to charge it.
- **The battery-technology sector carries real business risk** — EV battery startup ONE cut roughly 25% of its workforce in November 2023 citing market conditions, illustrating that advances in the technology do not insulate the companies pursuing them from downturns.
