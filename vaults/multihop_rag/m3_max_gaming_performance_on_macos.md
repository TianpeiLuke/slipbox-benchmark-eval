# M3 Max Gaming Performance On macOS

Gaming results on the M3 Max MacBook Pro are impressive: playing the Soulslike title Lies of P — "basically Pinocchio with more swords" — the gameplay was smooth and detailed, something that would have seemed impossible not all that long ago.

Lies of P is one of a small but increasing number of day-and-date releases for macOS; a couple of years ago it would have been inconceivable for the operating system to get the game the same day as PS5, Xbox Series X/S and Windows. Sony's PS5 DualShock controller connects seamlessly and makes for a fun portable gaming experience.

The scope condition on this result is the library, not the hardware: the macOS gaming library still has a long way to go.
