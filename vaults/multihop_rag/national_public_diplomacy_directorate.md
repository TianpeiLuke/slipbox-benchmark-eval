# The National Public Diplomacy Directorate

The National Public Diplomacy Directorate, headed by Moshe Aviv, is the body through which Israel's spokespeople and volunteers have been working around the clock in the weeks before 3 December 2023. Its defining operating rule, in Eylon Levy's account, is constant availability: "we try to be available 24/7". He gives the shift pattern as evidence, having been interviewed at six in the morning one Friday and finishing with a CNN interview at 2am the following morning, and having done four BBC interviews on one Saturday; and it is not just him, with Mark Regev doing interviews at all hours of the night and Tal Heinrich in the US being interviewed non-stop in prime time, as part of what Levy calls an amazing team of professionals.

Its second defining feature is inter-agency collaboration. The work is done with Israel Police, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the IDF Spokesperson, the Israel Prisons Service, the Shin Bet, the National Security Council, and the Ministry of Diaspora Affairs, which is responsible for producing items for digital channels. Levy says it is amazing to see every day, when all these bodies assess the situation together, how they put their egos aside, and that if any channel wants an official spokesperson for the Israeli government then they will be there, because they cannot abandon the battlefield.
