# Sudden Fear (1952)

David Miller's Sudden Fear is a crime-and-seduction drama that Polygon's Tasha Robinson says is worth watching for young Jack Palance and his startling cheekbones alone — though she names Joan Crawford the real star of the show. It streams free with ads on Tubi, free with a library card on Kanopy, and is rentable on Amazon, Vudu, and Apple.

Crawford plays Myra Hudson, a successful, wealthy playwright who winds up on both ends of a revenge scheme. She boots handsome leading actor Lester Blaine, played by Palance, off her latest play because she does not think he is convincing as a romantic lead; when he then "just happens" to run into her, seduce her, and get her to fall for him, sharp viewers will think they know where the story is going — but that will only get them one twist down what Robinson calls a magnificently curvy road. She classes it as another "everyone's a potential double-crosser" story, directed with surprising style and a few winks at a noir-savvy audience.
