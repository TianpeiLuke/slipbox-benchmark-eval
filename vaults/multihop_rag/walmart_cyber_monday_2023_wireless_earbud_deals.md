# Walmart Cyber Monday 2023 Wireless Earbud Deals

WIRED's Walmart Cyber Monday roundup of 27 November 2023 recommended five discounted wireless earbuds: Samsung's Galaxy Buds2 Pro, Bose's QuietComfort Ultra, JBL's Endurance Peak 3, Sony's WF-1000XM5 and the Technics EAH-AZ80.

Samsung's Galaxy Buds2 Pro (9/10, WIRED Recommends) are among WIRED's favourite wireless earbuds, particularly for Android users; they work best with Samsung phones but pair easily with any Android phone, and they offer solid noise cancelling, up to 7.5 hours of battery life and a wireless charging case. Bose's QuietComfort Ultra improve on the brand's top earbuds with a minor boost in noise cancelling and new spatial audio skills designed to expand the sound of everything you play; they were released late in 2023 and this is the first solid discount WIRED has seen. JBL's Endurance Peak 3 are described as that rare pair you can comfortably wear at the gym, on the trail or even in a hot tub thanks to hearty water resistance and a sport-ready fit; they are light on extras such as noise cancelling, but at this price WIRED calls them a great investment as go-to workout companions.

Sony's WF-1000XM5 (7/10, WIRED Review) bring together multiple generations of great sound, top features and excellent noise cancelling in Sony's most compact presentation yet; they are not cheap, so WIRED says the discount is key for anyone wanting to be an early adopter of Sony's latest flagship buds. The Technics EAH-AZ80 (9/10, WIRED Recommends) are among WIRED's favourite top-shelf earbuds for their fantastic sound, features and noise cancelling and their ability to connect to up to three devices at once; WIRED notes this is one of the few sales it has seen all year, which makes their very high asking price a lot more palatable.
