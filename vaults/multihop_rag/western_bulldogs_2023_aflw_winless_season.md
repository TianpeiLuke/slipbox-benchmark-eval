# The Western Bulldogs' 2023 AFLW Season

The Western Bulldogs endured a dismal 2023 AFLW season, suffering their biggest-ever defeat — a 57-point loss to Sydney, 14.12 (96) to 6.3 (39), at Whitten Oval — and finishing with a single win, The Age reported on 20 October and 3 November 2023.

That Sydney defeat was the Bulldogs' eighth loss in a row, and they had just two rounds left to clinch a win, next playing 17th-placed West Coast and then top-four side North Melbourne. Richelle Cranston, for whom the Dogs are a third club after starting at Melbourne and having a stint with Geelong before heading to the Bulldogs the previous year, lined up in that match. For the Swans, the victory had them challenging for a top-eight spot alongside the likes of Gold Coast, Geelong, Collingwood, St Kilda and Carlton.

In the final round against the Kangaroos, Britney Gutknecht kicked the Dogs' only goal early in the last term to avoid a record for the lowest AFLW score in the history of the competition. The Dogs have plenty to work on for next season as they look to regain a competitive edge, which was lost after a finals berth the previous year; along with being in a rebuild, they were badly injury-hit that season and used 32 players, more than any other club.
