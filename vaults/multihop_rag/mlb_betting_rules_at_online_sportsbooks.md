# General MLB Betting Rules At Online Sportsbooks

Every sportsbook has terms and conditions for all bets, MLB included, and while those rules may be specific to each book, some general rules should apply everywhere. For example, MLB First 5 Innings bets are usually considered actionable after five innings of play, or 4.5 innings if the home team has the lead. Another common rule is weather-related: if a game is suspended due to rain or other conditions, delays may lead to bets being refunded depending on the circumstances and the specific rules of your sportsbook.

Other rules found at online sportsbooks for MLB betting are: wagers include extra innings unless specified otherwise; the game must start on the scheduled day (local stadium time) for bets to have action; the game must go 9 innings, or 8½ if the home team is winning, for the run line to have action; on "team to score first" wagers, once a run is scored the prop will be graded regardless of how long the game lasts; wagers on postseason baseball series are based on the team to win the series, regardless of the number of games involved; and for player props, the player must be in the starting lineup for wagers to have action.

It is important to read through the rules for baseball betting before placing your MLB bets, and they can be found in the terms and conditions section of your sportsbook's website.
