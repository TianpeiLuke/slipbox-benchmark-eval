# October 2023 Prime Day Gaming Deals Overview

Amazon's fall Prime Day event was underway on October 10th, 2023, and The Verge published a roundup of the best gaming deals it could scrounge up during Prime Big Deal Days, spanning PlayStation 5, PS4, Xbox Series X / S, Nintendo Switch, and PC, and extending to hardware deals on things like controllers, streaming peripherals, and the consoles themselves. The outlet also pointed readers to its full roundup of recommended deals across all categories.

The writer's framing of the value on offer: Amazon Prime Day is one of the best times of the year to save on a wide variety of video games, with titles released in the last few months getting decent discounts — which still beats paying full price — and many older games from the last few years falling to their best bargain-bin prices, which is great for stocking up on those "Man, I've been meaning to get around to that one" games.
