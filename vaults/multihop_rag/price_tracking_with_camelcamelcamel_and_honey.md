# Price Tracking With CamelCamelCamel And Honey

Because there is a limit to the products any deals desk writes about or even sees, Polygon's advice is to track deals yourself rather than leave it to chance that someone reports a price cut on the product you want — and it names two tools for doing so.

If you only shop on Amazon, CamelCamelCamel remains one of the best tools for tracking price changes and price history. You do not need an account to see pricing information available through the app, but making a profile lets you set up watchlists and get email price alerts for your favorite products. You can populate a watchlist manually, or import an existing Amazon wishlist into your CamelCamelCamel profile, and Polygon advises grabbing its Camelizer browser extension too. Honey and its accompanying extension also track pricing history changes on specific products, but across a number of retailers rather than Amazon alone, and as an added bonus Honey will automatically search for promo codes when you check out at a supported retailer.
