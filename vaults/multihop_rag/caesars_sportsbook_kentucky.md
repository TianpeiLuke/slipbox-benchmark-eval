# Caesars Sportsbook In Kentucky

Caesars Sportsbook is the app of a well-known brick-and-mortar brand with glitzy properties from Atlantic City to Las Vegas, distinguished among Kentucky books chiefly by a rewards program whose credits can be redeemed for use at Caesars properties, as described by CBS Sports on 13 October 2023.

Its betting features are breadth plus casino: the app offers hundreds of ways to wager, includes casino games alongside sports, and provides odds boosts, parlays, same game parlays and Quick Picks. New customers can get up to $1,000 in bonus bets if they lose their first bet, and the sportsbook offers an extensive betting menu while specialising in specific boosts related to your home state teams.

The report positions it against BetMGM as the closest comparison, noting that similar to MGM, the rewards program is what Caesars is most known for.
