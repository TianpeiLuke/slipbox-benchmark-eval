# Cowboys-49ers Second Half: Three Prescott Interceptions And A 42-10 Final

San Francisco turned a 21-7 halftime lead into a 42-10 win over Dallas in the second half of the Oct. 8, 2023 "Sunday Night Football" game, aided by three Dak Prescott interceptions. Dallas started the half with the ball and Prescott hit CeeDee Lamb for a 29-yard gain into 49ers territory (10:13 p.m.), but San Francisco's defense tightened and limited the Cowboys to a Brandon Aubrey 50-yard field goal at 10:17 p.m., cutting the lead to 21-10.

The 49ers answered immediately. Purdy hit Brandon Aiyuk for 23 yards (10:21 p.m.); an offensive holding call on Spencer Burford wiped out a 40-yard Aiyuk gain (10:23 p.m.); and Deebo Samuel went 43 yards on the very next play to put San Francisco in the red zone (10:25 p.m.). At 10:27 p.m. George Kittle caught his third touchdown of the night for a 28-10 lead. At 10:32 p.m. Prescott, heaving downfield under pressure, was intercepted by Tashaun Gipson. Purdy reached 242 yards with three touchdowns after a 19-yard catch by Jauan Jennings (10:38 p.m.), and the third quarter ended 28-10 with San Francisco a yard from a 25-point lead.

The fourth quarter was a formality. Purdy hit Kyle Juszczyk at 10:47 p.m. for the 49ers' fifth touchdown and his own fourth, making it 35-10. At 10:52 p.m. Fred Warner intercepted a deflected Prescott pass — completing a night with a sack, a forced fumble and an interception — and Jordan Mason ran in another touchdown at 10:53 p.m. for 42-10, with Leighton Vander Esch down injured. At 11:00 p.m. Oren Burks recorded the 49ers' third interception of the night. Tony Pollard broke a 26-yard run at 10:59 p.m. as Dallas finally moved the ball too late. Sam Darnold entered for San Francisco at 11:04 p.m. with 12 minutes still on the clock, Mitch Wishnowsky punted well at 11:07 p.m. with the 49ers trying to finish injury-free, Cooper Rush came in for Dallas at 11:09 p.m., and a Kevin Givens sack ended Rush's first drive and set up a Bryan Anger punt at 11:14 p.m. with 49ers backups in nearly all around. Officials broke up a brief skirmish as Dallas' frustrations mounted (11:24 p.m.), and the game ended at 11:26 p.m. with San Francisco unbeaten through five games.
