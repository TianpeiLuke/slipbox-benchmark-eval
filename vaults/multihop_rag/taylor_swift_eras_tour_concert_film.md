# Taylor Swift: The Eras Tour Concert Film

"Taylor Swift: The Eras Tour," compiled from several Swift shows at Southern California's SoFi Stadium and directed by Sam Wrench, debuted on Friday, 2023-10-13, with advance ticket sales worldwide already past $100 million and an opening expected to launch at $100 million or possibly more. Movie theaters readied for an onslaught like they had never seen before: Swifties would descend, and dancing would be encouraged. By 2023-11-07 the almost three-hour film was the highest-grossing concert film of all time.

The film exists because the live tour was out of reach for most fans. Swift's camp was motivated to get it out even as her stadium tour continues internationally; the tour, projected by Pollstar to gross some $1.4 billion, crashed Ticketmaster's site, saw sky-high resale mark-ups and left many fans priced out. In July, Pitchfork reported that the average cost of a ticket for the North American leg was $3,801 — a 2,321% increase from her 2018 Reputation Stadium Tour, where the average resale price for North American shows was $157. The movie would be a way for millions more to experience the Eras Tour, offering up-close, intimate access to a show they never would have been able to attend otherwise, with adult tickets sold at $19.89 — a reference to her birth year and 2014 album, a re-recording of which was due out Oct. 27 — higher than the average movie ticket but several thousand less than many tickets to see Swift live, and with no Ticketmaster queue to wait in.

The release broke several theatrical norms. It arrived uncommonly fast, just a little over two months after the SoFi shows; speed was one reason Swift's father, Scott Swift, is said to have sought out a direct deal with AMC. Swift produced the film herself and, with 274 million followers on Instagram, didn't need a studio to promote it. After a premiere in Los Angeles on Wednesday, 2023-10-11, there were no advance screenings until the movie began playing at 6 p.m. local time Friday — where most wide-release movies open with Thursday showings and Friday daytime screenings.
