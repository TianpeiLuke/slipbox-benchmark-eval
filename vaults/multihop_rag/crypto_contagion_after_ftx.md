# Crypto Contagion After FTX

The collapse of [FTX](ftx.md) was the first of several failures across the crypto industry. BlockFi filed for Chapter 11 in November 2022, and Genesis Global Trading filed in January 2023.

Ayelet Noff, CEO and founder of the public relations firm Sliced Brand, which represents cryptocurrency businesses, told TechCrunch that [Sam Bankman-Fried](sam_bankman_fried.md) "pushed back crypto adoption by at least a year or two," describing a recurring pattern in which each such event sets the whole market back.
