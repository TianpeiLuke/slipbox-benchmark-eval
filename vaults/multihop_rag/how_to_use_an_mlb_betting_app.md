# How To Use An MLB Betting App

MLB betting apps make it easy to wager on teams and players while keeping track of your bankroll and profits, and Sporting News sets out four steps for using one.

First, download the app: visit the sportsbook's website via the provided links, claim the best new-customer bonus and promos if you have not signed up previously, and download the sportsbook's betting app for your phone or tablet. Second, place your bets: you can bet on MLB games either pre-game or live, finding all the traditional pre-game bets like game lines and run totals as well as live betting options, which can become more granular — such as the result of the next at-bat. Third, keep tabs on odds: the app helps you track shifting odds and facilitates live betting, offering real-time updates on all odds so you can react to game flow as it happens.

Fourth, bet from within legal states. As long as you are in a state with legal online sports betting you can use the app to make bets, track your account, and add or withdraw funds. The precondition is location: you can only place bets when you are within the state lines, though you can access your account and monitor wagers from anywhere you have internet access.
