# Country Music Couples And Their Advice On Lasting Marriages

FOX News's November 2023 roundup argues that country music offers an unusual concentration of durable celebrity marriages, and that the couples themselves are the best source of advice on how such marriages survive. Its grounds are the pattern across the genre: country music couples have serenaded their way into each other's hearts for years, and much like the resilient love story of Tim McGraw and Faith Hill or Garth Brooks and Trisha Yearwood, there is a sweet melody in the lives of several country stars who have weathered the storms of fame while creating strong bonds in their lasting romances.

The warrant tying the examples to the conclusion is endurance through the specific pressures of celebrity: beyond the stage lights, these loving country music couples — or musicians and their long-lasting partners — have proven they have kept in tune with one another through the highs and lows of their relationships. The article then collects each couple's own stated tips.
