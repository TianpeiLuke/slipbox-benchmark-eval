# X Post-Volume Discrepancy

X CEO Linda Yaccarino's post-volume figure contradicts Elon Musk's: speaking at an event in the week covered by TechCrunch's October 7, 2023 Week in Review, she claimed X was seeing 500 million posts per day on the platform, whereas in September Musk had said X users were creating 100 million to 200 million posts every day, excluding retweets.

The contradiction attacks the reliability of the platform's own content-volume claims rather than any single argument built on them: two of X's most senior figures, weeks apart, gave figures that differ by a factor of roughly two to five. TechCrunch does not adjudicate between them, closing the item with "So who's right? Beats us." The item also does not state whether Yaccarino's 500 million figure excludes retweets, as Musk's explicitly did — so part of the gap may be definitional and part may not.
