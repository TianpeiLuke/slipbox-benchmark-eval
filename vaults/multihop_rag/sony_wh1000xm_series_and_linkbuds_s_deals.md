# Sony WH-1000XM Series And LinkBuds S Deals

The Verge lists five Sony sets: the WH-1000XM5 at around $328 ($70 off) from B&H Photo, Best Buy, and Target; the older WH-1000XM4; the LinkBuds S at around $148 (Amazon, Target) against a $199.99 list price; the WH-CH720N at around $98 (Amazon, B&H Photo, Best Buy) against a $149 retail price; and the WF-C700N at $89.99 (Amazon, Best Buy).

The WH-1000XM5 remain The Verge's favorite pair of noise-canceling headphones: not a huge departure from the previous model, but improving on everything that made the XM4s awesome with better active noise cancellation, improved voice call quality, and boosted overall sound, so that despite a disappointing lack of new features they stay a top recommendation for a premium pair. Their predecessor, the WH-1000XM4, are still terrific — excellent noise cancellation, good sound, simultaneous pairing to two devices, plush ear pads for long wear, folding much more compactly than the newer model for frequent travelers, up to 30 hours on a single charge, and USB-C charging. The LinkBuds S don't look as peculiar as the regular LinkBuds and are some of the comfiest earbuds around thanks to a lightweight build of just 4.8 grams per bud, with active noise cancellation and support for Sony's LDAC Bluetooth codec for higher-quality audio streaming. The budget WH-CH720N, worth a look under $150, have adjustable noise cancellation, are exceptionally light for a comfortable fit, support Bluetooth, and offer up to 35 hours of continuous playback on a charge, though they lack LDAC; the listing shows $98 against $150, 35 percent off, in blue, white, and black, at $98 at Amazon and $100 at Best Buy. The WF-C700N are exceptionally comfortable noise-canceling earbuds with decent sound and useful software features including Fast Pair support on Android and location-based sound settings; their noise cancellation doesn't truly drown out the world, and voice call quality isn't particularly impressive, but they do fine as an affordable way to tune out some noise.
