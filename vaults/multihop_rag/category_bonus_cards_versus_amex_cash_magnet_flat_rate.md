# Category Bonus Cards Versus The Cash Magnet's Flat Rate

If you tend to spend more money in select categories, such as groceries or online retail purchases, the Blue Cash Everyday Card from American Express may be a better fit than the flat-rate Cash Magnet.

The rotating-category option carries a cost as well as an upside. It takes a little more work to take advantage of rotating spending categories, but if you tend to spend money in the different categories — such as restaurants, Amazon purchases or gas — you could earn more cash back rewards with the Discover it Cash Back card. Plus, Discover will match all of the cash back you earned during your first year as a cardmember.
