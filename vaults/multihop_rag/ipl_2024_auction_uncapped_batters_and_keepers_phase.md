# IPL 2024 Auction: The Uncapped Batters And Wicketkeepers Phase

After the capped players were done at 04:00 PM IST the auction broke for an hour, returning at 05:07 PM IST for the second phase, which opened with the uncapped players. At 05:17 PM IST Subham Dubey joined Rajasthan Royals for 5.8 crore after an intense bidding war between DC and RR.

The uncapped batters produced the day's most striking small-name prices. At 05:20 PM IST Manan Vohra, Sourav Chauhan and Priyansh Arya went unsold, and Sameer Rizvi — described as a right-handed Suresh Raina — came up to a bidding war between CSK and GT. At 05:24 PM IST it was still between CSK and GT with Rizvi already past the 6 Crore mark, the blog noting he had been outstanding in the UP T20 League; at 05:26 PM IST Delhi joined the race and the price reached Rs 8 Cr before CSK finally took him at 8.4 Cr. At 05:28 PM IST KKR picked Angkrish Raghuvanshi for a base price of Rs 20 L, and at 05:30 PM IST Sarfaraz Khan came up and went unsold. At 5:34 PM IST Arshin Kulkarni went to LSG for Rs 20 L, and Shahrukh Khan drew a bidding war between GT and PBKS that crossed 6 Cr; at 5:41 PM IST Punjab Kings pulled out at 6.2 Cr and Shahrukh Khan found a new home at Gujarat Titans for 7.4 Cr. At 5:45 PM IST Ramandeep Singh went to KKR at a base price of Rs 20 L.

The uncapped wicketkeepers' set began at 6:00 PM IST after the break, when England's Tom Kohler-Cadmore went to RR for Rs 40 L. At 6:05 PM IST Delhi Capitals picked up a backup wicketkeeper, Ricky Bhui, for Rs 20 L, and at 6:07 PM IST Kumar Kushagra drew a bidding war between CSK and GT, with DC joining and winning at Rs 7.2 Cr after the price breached Rs 7 Cr.
