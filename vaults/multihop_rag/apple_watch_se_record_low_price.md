# Apple Watch SE At A Record-Low Price

Apple's latest entry-level wearable, the Apple Watch SE, hit a new record-low price at Walmart for Black Friday 2023, working out to 28% savings, Mashable reported on 25 November 2023.

It is lighter and 20 percent faster than the original Apple Watch SE from 2020.

It features most of the same health tools as the new flagship Apple Watch Series 9 — including heart rate notifications, Emergency SOS, Cycle Tracking, and Fall and Crash Detection — and offers a comparable 18-hour battery life and water resistance.
