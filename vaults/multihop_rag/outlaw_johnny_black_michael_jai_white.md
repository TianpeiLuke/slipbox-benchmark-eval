# Outlaw Johnny Black

Outlaw Johnny Black premiered on VOD in early November 2023, available to rent on Amazon, Apple, and Vudu. The satirical Western runs 2 hours 10 minutes, is directed by Michael Jai White, and stars White alongside Anika Noni Rose and Erica Ash.

Polygon frames it as Michael Jai White's long-awaited follow-up to Black Dynamite: a "West-ploitation" movie about an outlaw who pretends to be a preacher and settles in a new troubled town. The outlet notes that the star and director spoke to it at length about the movie and the long road it took to get made.
