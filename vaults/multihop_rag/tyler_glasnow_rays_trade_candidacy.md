# Tyler Glasnow Rays Trade Candidacy

Tyler Glasnow was ranked No. 5 on Yardbarker's Nov. 13, 2023 list of the top 25 MLB offseason trade candidates because of his salary: the Rays signed him to an extension in August 2022 while he was completing his rehab from Tommy John surgery, and the big right-hander was set to make $25M the following season, the largest single-year salary in franchise history.

That naturally led to questions about whether the Rays were prepared to meet that figure, particularly in an offseason where they had a loaded arbitration class. If they made him available — and he had already surfaced in rumors — Glasnow would be a very appealing target: he was quite good that year, working to a 3.53 ERA while striking out more than a third of opponents over 21 starts, and although an oblique strain cost him the first two months of the season, he avoided the injured list thereafter.

He was a year away from free agency, which made another extension with Tampa Bay appear unlikely. Yet dealing him would represent a tough blow to the Rays' chances of competing in 2024: they were already down Shane McClanahan for the entire season and would be without Jeffrey Springs and Drew Rasmussen for at least a good portion of the year. While they would likely bring back some kind of MLB help as part of a Glasnow trade return, the rotation depth could be a problem.
