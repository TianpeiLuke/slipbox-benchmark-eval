# Apple AirPods Second-Gen, Third-Gen And AirPods Max Deals

The Verge's roundup lists three Apple options: second-gen AirPods with a wired charging case at $99 (Amazon, Walmart) against a $129 retail price, third-gen AirPods from $164, and the AirPods Max at $479.99.

The second-gen AirPods' sound quality isn't quite up to par with newer models, but they remain the cheapest Apple earbuds on the market and continue to pair seamlessly with a range of Apple products thanks to their built-in H1 chip; the MagSafe-charging-case version is a lot harder to find new unless you shop used or refurbished, though Adorama still has new pairs at $119.99, $30 off. The third-gen AirPods are the latest iteration of the base model, blending a bit of the AirPods Pro look with the hard plastic build of the second-gen model, and adding IPX4 water resistance and improved sound quality over it, even if they still lack in the low end: the Lightning-only charging case version is on sale at Amazon for $164, $5 off, while the MagSafe version is $169.95 at Staples and Costco for members — just $6 more and a better deal overall. For the best sound quality in noise-canceling headphones, The Verge recommends the AirPods Max, which pair seamlessly with other Apple devices and sound superb, especially with Apple's spatial audio feature for a more immersive experience with compatible content; they don't support lossless audio, but they have the best transparency mode of all the headphones on the list and incredible build quality thanks to a luxe design using aluminum, steel, and fabric over plastic. With an MSRP of $549 they are expensive but often on sale for far less — currently $479.99, $70 off, at Amazon in select colors as well as at Best Buy.
