# Shameless Media: A Self-Funded Podcast Company Built On Pop Culture

Shameless Media is the Melbourne-founded company built by Michelle Andrews and Zara McDonald out of their passion for smart conversations about pop culture, launched in 2019 and home not just to the Shameless podcast but also to Everybody Has a Secret, Shameless Book Club and Love etc. As of this 7 October 2023 profile it has 1.1 million followers across social media, plus 1 million unique listeners and 2.5 million podcast downloads a month, and 11 staff.

Its defining financial feature is independence: for the first 10 months Shameless was a weekend project, researched and recorded around the pair's other jobs, and in those early days McDonald and Andrews were self-funded, buying their own podcasting equipment and spending just $70 to print advertising flyers. Even now they have never taken on any outside investment, and the company has been profitable from day one.

The origin story runs through their former employer. Nine years ago Andrews and McDonald were both 20 and working entry-level jobs in Melbourne at women's media company Mamamia, covering late shifts, often on weekends, and regularly sharing an Uber home; a friendship grew out of their shared love of pop culture. "We'd be watching the same TV shows and listening to the same podcasts," says Andrews, now 29. Their back-seat chats led to the idea for Shameless — "a pop culture podcast for smart people who love dumb stuff". The story of how they left Mamamia after having the idea rejected at the final hour has been told many times, but these days they rarely think of it. "It ended up being amazing for us that they didn't want the show," says Andrews. "We feel so far removed from that time," adds McDonald, also 29. They are now well known as the "Shameless girls".
