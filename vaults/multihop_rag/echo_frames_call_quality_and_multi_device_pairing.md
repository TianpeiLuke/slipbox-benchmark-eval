# Echo Frames Call Quality And Multi-Device Pairing

Call quality on the third-gen Echo Frames was good enough: The Verge's reviewer called a friend during an hour-long walk and she was able to hear them despite beeping trucks and wind, though in her words they sounded "echo-y" and she could hear a lot of the ambient noise — at no point, however, was she unable to hear them.

The reviewer also liked that the glasses can be paired to multiple devices and switch between the two, something the Ray-Ban Meta Smart Glasses weren't really capable of. The scope condition on that advantage: it is only useful if you're getting clear glasses, since you shouldn't be wearing sunglasses indoors while on your laptop.
