# Netflix Originals Premiering November 21 To 24, 2023

Twelve Netflix originals appear in this block of Polygon's November 2023 listing, each with Netflix's own synopsis.

Leo, a Netflix Family title: Adam Sandler is a lizard named Leo in a coming-of-age musical comedy about the last year of elementary school as seen through the eyes of a class pet. Crime Diaries: The Celebrity Stylist, a Netflix Film: when an up-and-coming stylist is found stabbed to death in his home, a young detective is given 20 days to solve the case, inspired by true events. High on the Hog: How African American Cuisine Transformed America: Season 2, a Netflix Documentary: host Stephen Satterfield travels across the United States to uncover how African-American cuisine has fueled social justice movements, transformed communities and awakened cultural creativity in America. Squid Game: The Challenge, a Netflix Series with new episodes released weekly through December 6: 456 real players enter the competition show in pursuit of a life-changing reward of USD $4.56 million, competing through games inspired by the original show plus surprising new additions, with their strategies, alliances, and character put to the test as competitors are eliminated around them. My Daemon, a Netflix Anime: to save his mother, a kind-hearted boy and his tiny daemon friend set out across post-apocalyptic Japan as dark forces close in. My Little Pony: Make Your Mark: Chapter 6: Opaline has captured nearly all the power of Equestria and time is running out, so the ponies must team up with the dragons to save magic and restore peace. A Nearly Normal Family, a Netflix Series: the world of a seemingly perfect family shatters when a shocking murder proves they are willing to make desperate moves to protect each other. DOI BOY, a Netflix Film: a refugee builds a new identity as a sex worker in Thailand and gets caught up in a client's risky scheme that might lead to a better life. I Don't Expect Anyone to Believe Me, a Netflix Film: a writer's career and entire life go off script when he falls prey to a dangerous web of criminals right before moving to Barcelona. Last Call for Istanbul, a Netflix Film: a chance meeting at the airport leads two married people to an unforgettable night full of excitement, desire, and temptation in New York City. My Demon, a Netflix Series: chaebol heiress Do Do Hee is an adversary to many and Jung Koo Won is a powerful entity superior to humans, but when he loses his powers he is forced to collaborate with her to regain them, and sparks of romance begin to fly. Wedding Games, a Netflix Film: when Alex and Eva choose to exchange vows at the very spot they first crossed paths, a series of hilarious missteps derails their journey to the altar.
