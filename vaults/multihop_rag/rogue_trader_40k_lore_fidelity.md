# Rogue Trader And 40K Lore Fidelity

Polygon argues that in the grand scheme of things, Rogue Trader is one of the most complete and detailed explorations of the 40K universe you can find.

The grounds are lineage and execution. The game is an homage to the Warhammer 40K RPGs from Fantasy Flight Games, including Rogue Trader and Dark Heresy, sourcebooks the reviewer spent years as a teenager and young adult poring over, learning the tiny details of life in the Imperium. The claim is that developer Owlcat Games has paid that same attention to every detail of the Koronus Expanse, and the evidence offered is what the game lets you visit: ancient facilities staffed by tech-priests of the Machine God, the hostile xenos city of Commorragh, and massive cities built to honor the God-Emperor of Mankind.
