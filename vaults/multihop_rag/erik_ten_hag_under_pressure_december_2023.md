# Erik Ten Hag Under Pressure In December 2023

Erik ten Hag faced a demanding week in early December 2023 amid reports indicating disunity within his team, and by mid-December the pressure around the Dutch boss continued to get more palpable as Manchester United's season continued to slip away.

On Dec. 6, 2023, Sporting News reported that the United manager had faced a demanding week amid reports indicating disunity within his team, and that his players did exceptionally well to block out the noise and focus on footballing matters. Two hours before kickoff against Chelsea the live blog noted it had been a relatively busy start to the week off the pitch for Ten Hag, and that it was time to see what happened on it. Sky Sports Premier League (@SkySportsPL) posted on December 5, 2023 that Erik ten Hag claimed there was not a good culture among staff and players before he joined Man United.

By Dec. 13, 2023, Sporting News framed the situation as one in which, as Manchester United's season continued to slip away, the pressure around Ten Hag continued to get more palpable.
