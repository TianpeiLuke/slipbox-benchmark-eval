# Erik Ten Hag Under Pressure In December 2023

Erik ten Hag faced a demanding week in early December 2023 amid reports indicating disunity within his team, and by mid-December the pressure around the Dutch boss continued to get more palpable as Manchester United's season continued to slip away.

On Dec. 6, 2023, Sporting News reported that the United manager had faced a demanding week amid reports indicating disunity within his team, and that his players did exceptionally well to block out the noise and focus on footballing matters.
