# Knock at the Cabin (M. Night Shyamalan)

Knock at the Cabin, starring Dave Bautista, Jonathan Groff, Ben Aldridge, and Rupert Grint, marks M. Night Shyamalan's return to one of the most understated yet prevailing themes of his work: religious terror and despair in the face of the unexplainable. Polygon ranked it 19 on its best movies of 2023 list of 2023-10-07 and listed it on Prime Video or for digital rental/purchase.

The film follows a family — Jonathan Groff, Ben Aldridge, and Kristen Cui — who travel to a secluded cabin in rural Pennsylvania for a vacation and find themselves menaced by a group of four heavily armed intruders who refuse to let them leave. The family is presented with a warning and an ultimatum: every single person on the planet is about to die, and the only way to prevent this catastrophe is for one of them to choose to sacrifice themselves for the sake of humanity. As the family struggles to escape their captors, the undeniable weight of their situation sets in — could the kidnappers be telling the truth, and if so, would any of them be willing to submit to the unthinkable cruelty of their fate?

Polygon's Toussaint Egan situates the film in a fascinating chapter in the career of one of the most idiosyncratic directors working today, coming off the success of 2021's Old, and distinguishes its kind of horror: this is not a movie whose horrors rely on brutality and gore — though there is certainly that and then some — but one whose apocalyptic premise provokes both outright terror and disquieting introspective dread, challenging the audience to look inward for the answers.
