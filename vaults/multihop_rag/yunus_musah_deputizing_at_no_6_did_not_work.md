# Playing Yunus Musah At No. 6 Against Germany Did Not Work

Deploying Yunus Musah in the defensive midfield role against Germany on Oct. 14, 2023 did not work, and the attempt to transform him into some semblance of Tyler Adams was almost certain to go wrong. The evidence: whenever Germany quelled an American surge in the first half and regained possession, they plowed with ease through the center of the park.

The warrant is a mismatch between the job and the player's habits. Musah is elite at maintaining possession when operating as a box-to-box midfielder, and lately his club play with Pulisic at AC Milan had put him in more of an attacking role; even granting that he might have the skill set to succeed at the No. 6, he did not appear comfortable transitioning to it on short notice. By contrast, the decision about how to replace Gio Reyna in the lineup was simple — it was always the plan for Reyna to play roughly half the game, and given the sometimes ridiculous controversy over his reserve role at the World Cup the obvious choice was to start him — whereas how to replace Adams in the formation was complex, too much so to be resolved in one afternoon.

The related claim is that removing Reyna compounded the problem: the collapse from a 1-1 halftime tie not only followed his departure but was probably connected to it, since the U.S. threatened enough with Reyna on the field to make the opposition defend more often than it preferred, while his replacement Luca de la Torre dulled the attack and did nothing to dissuade Germany's rampage toward the American net. The overall verdict is that the USMNT conjured a more compelling attack because of Reyna's presence, but the ability to control the game went missing.
