# The Lithium Chloride Hydrogel Soaking Experiment

The researchers made tubes of hydrogel from polyacrylamide, a polymer made of long, threadlike molecules, cut the tubes into thin disks, soaked them in water containing different amounts of lithium chloride — a salt — and weighed the disks each day to see how much of the salty water they had absorbed.

The step that mattered was duration. Such tests had been done before, but for relatively short times, Díaz-Marín says: "Instead of soaking for two days, we did this for up to two months." The extra time made a big difference.
