# Champions League 2023/24 Group C: Real Madrid And Napoli Qualify

Real Madrid completed six wins out of six in Group C of the 2023/24 Champions League and Napoli qualified alongside them, with Braga third and Union Berlin bottom, per Sporting News on Dec. 12, 2023.

The final table read: Real Madrid (Q) 6 games, 6 wins, 0 draws, 0 losses, +9 goal difference, 18 points; Napoli (Q) 6, 3, 1, 2, +1, 10; Braga 6, 1, 1, 4, -6, 4; Union Berlin 6, 0, 2, 4, -4, 2. Real Madrid, the 14-time European champions, completed their perfect record with a topsy-turvy 3-2 win away to Union Berlin, a result that ruled the Bundesliga side out of the Europa League — a spot Braga claimed as consolation after Napoli snuffed out their slim hopes of second place with a 2-0 win at the Diego Armando Maradona.

The results were: Sept. 20, Real Madrid 1-0 Union Berlin; Sept. 20, Braga 1-2 Napoli; Oct. 3, Union Berlin 2-3 Braga; Oct. 3, Napoli 2-3 Real Madrid; Oct. 24, Braga 1-2 Real Madrid; Oct. 24, Union Berlin 0-1 Napoli; Nov. 8, Napoli 1-1 Union Berlin; Nov. 8, Real Madrid 3-0 Braga; Nov. 29, Real Madrid 4-2 Napoli; Nov. 29, Braga 1-1 Union Berlin; Dec. 12, Napoli 2-0 Braga; Dec. 12, Union Berlin 2-3 Real Madrid.
