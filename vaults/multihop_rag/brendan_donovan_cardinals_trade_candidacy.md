# Brendan Donovan Cardinals Trade Candidacy

Brendan Donovan was ranked No. 21 on Yardbarker's Nov. 13, 2023 list of the top 25 MLB offseason trade candidates and framed as a longer shot possibility should the Cards move a controllable hitter for starting pitching: he was more valuable than Dylan Carlson and less likely to be on the move, but he would net a clearer rotation upgrade if St. Louis gave him up — likely turning the middle infield over to a combination of Tommy Edman, Nolan Gorman and Masyn Winn in that scenario.

The 26-year-old hit .284/.365/.422 with 11 homers through 371 plate appearances that past season. A flexor tendon injury pushed him to designated hitter and eventually required surgery that ended his year a couple of months early, and he was expected back for spring training. If healthy, Donovan brought excellent contact skills and a solid plate approach, and he was a multi-positional defender who could play any of the corners in addition to second base. He had four years of remaining control and would not get to arbitration until the following offseason.
