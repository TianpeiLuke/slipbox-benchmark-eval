# NASCAR Next Gen Muffler Noise Reduction Test

CBSSports.com reported on Dec. 7, 2023 that the topic of mufflers — while anathema to the purest of gearheads — has occasionally popped up in the Next Gen era, particularly with NASCAR now using mufflers in urban settings at stadiums such as the Los Angeles Coliseum and on street courses such as Chicago, where noise ordinances are more of a consideration.

Kyle Larson told reporters he did not notice a difference in cockpit cooling with the mufflers tested on Wednesday, but spoke positively of the idea overall. "I definitely think our racecars are way too loud, and probably are still too loud with the mufflers," Larson said. "I think the cars can be quieter just to help the fan experience."
