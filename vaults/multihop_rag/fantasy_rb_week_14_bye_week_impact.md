# Week 14 Bye Week Impact On Fantasy Running Backs

Only the Cardinals and Commanders were on bye in Week 14 of the 2023 season, taking lead backs Brian Robinson Jr. and James Conner off the table, plus Antonio Gibson for those in deep leagues, per Sporting News on Dec. 7, 2023.

This followed a six-team "byepocalypse" in Week 13, so the PPR edition judged that the Week 14 byes did not hurt much beyond Robinson and Conner.

What stung for fantasy was the loss of the matchups rather than the players. Both Washington and Arizona had been defensive turnstiles, ranking among the 10 most generous teams to running backs that season, so removing two below-average run defenses from the schedule hurt. Many of the stud running backs had tough matchups in Week 14 — though Sporting News added that it was only right that many of the elite backs must beat the best defenses to lift fantasy squads to the big dance.
