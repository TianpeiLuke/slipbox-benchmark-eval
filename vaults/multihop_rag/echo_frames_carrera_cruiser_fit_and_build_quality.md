# Echo Frames Carrera Cruiser Fit And Build Quality

The Verge's reviewer found the Carrera Cruiser didn't fit their face well — with a low nose bridge, the glasses slipped down every five seconds and had to be pushed back up — and disliked the build quality: while they look high fashion, they feel plasticky in the hand and not befitting of their nearly $400 price tag.

Because the Cruiser only comes with sunglass lenses, the reviewer wore them during the commute and on outdoor walks, and reports they are "a vibe": they were described as channeling Yeezy, a Kardashian reject, a card-carrying member of the Jersey Shore mafia, and a time traveler from the '80s, and definitely turned heads on the street. The reviewer had fun wearing them and thinks most folks could pull the look off so long as they put a little swagger in their step, but personally wouldn't pick this style for day-to-day sunnies. The frames are made of a lightweight acetate weighing 46.3g.
