# The Cancelled China Tour And The NYCFC Noche d'Or Replacement

Inter Miami's planned two-match tour of China in early November 2023 was cancelled for "commercial and bureaucratic" reasons, and was replaced by a hastily arranged friendly against NYCFC at DRV PNK Stadium on November 10 dubbed the "Noche d'Or" or "Night of Gold" in honor of Lionel Messi's most recent Ballon d'Or.

The tour existed because of the club's elimination from 2023 MLS playoff contention, which left Messi facing nearly a full month off from competitive fixtures before taking the field for Argentina against Uruguay and Brazil in CONMEBOL World Cup qualifying. Likely with the sole intent of keeping Messi fit in that time off, and the added bonus of raking in significant cash, Inter Miami scheduled the China trip after their regular season concluded, expecting to visit two Chinese Super League sides — Qingdao Hainiu on November 5 and Chengdu Rongcheng three days later.

It was reported on October 30 that the planned tour was off, with Gaston Edu of TyC Sports reporting the visit had been cancelled due to commercial and bureaucratic reasons.
