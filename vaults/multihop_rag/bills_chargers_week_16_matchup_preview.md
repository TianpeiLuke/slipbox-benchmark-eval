# Bills vs. Chargers Week 16 Matchup Preview

The Buffalo Bills (8-6) were 12-point favorites with the OVER/UNDER set at 44 for their Week 16 Saturday game at SoFi Stadium against the Los Angeles Chargers (5-9), previewed by Sporting News on Dec. 21, 2023.

Buffalo would be the first foes faced by Chargers interim head coach Giff Smith, and the Bills entered quarterback Josh Allen's home state as favorites. The game featured one team heating up and looking like a unit no one would want to face in the playoffs, while the other was sifting through the rubble of a disastrous 2023 season and building for 2024 and beyond.
