# Shovel Knight: Treasure Trove

Shovel Knight is a pixel art Metroidvania with the feel of a great NES game, thanks to its retro aesthetic and excellent platforming and battling. It appears in Polygon's 2023-10-20 list of the 35 best couch co-op games for Nintendo Switch.

The definitive edition, which includes the original Shovel of Hope campaign, is available on Nintendo Switch.

That edition supports local co-op for two players.
