# UK Dementia Burden and Projections

About a million people are living with dementia in the UK, and this is expected to rise to about 1.7 million by 2040; last year dementia took the lives of 66,000 people in England and Wales, and it is now the leading cause of death in Britain, with Alzheimer's accounting for two-thirds of cases.

The prospect of the new drugs has raised hopes that, after years of effort, scientists may be closing in on ways to directly tackle the UK's dementia crisis — a crisis whose projected growth the article describes as carrying potentially grim consequences.
