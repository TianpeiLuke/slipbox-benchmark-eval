# Inter Miami Supporters Protested The 2024 Price Hike By Sitting In Silence

Inter Miami's five supporters' groups, known as La Familia, registered their anger at the 2024 season ticket price hike by staying quiet before kickoff against FC Cincinnati on Oct. 7, 2023. Fifteen minutes before kickoff, Michelle Kaufman of the Miami Herald reported that the groups had threatened to stage a protest over the hike but had called it off — at least until the next game — because the club offered to meet with them the following week. Five minutes before kickoff, with no active protest under way, the supporters' section made a collective effort not to be its usual cheerful self, sitting quietly with no drums, chanting or flag-waving instead of making noise; Sporting News read this as a very clear sign of frustration that would significantly hamper Inter Miami's home field advantage in such a massive game.

The night's framing sharpened the contrast. It was Fan Appreciation Night at DRV PNK Stadium, where Inter Miami bucket hats were given to the first fans through the gates. Sporting News commented that the best way to appreciate the fans would be to not price loyal supporters out of season tickets, and that by jacking 2024 membership rates to among the most expensive not just in MLS but in the world, the club had chosen maximum profit at the expense of its fanbase, making high turnover among regular in-person support likely — many who could afford the high-demand tickets would not be the fans who had supported the club since its inception in 2020.

A billing error compounded the alarm. Mike Ryan, a Miami local who regularly appears on the radio program The Dan LeBatard Show, posted his shock at receiving a 2024 renewal of $30,600 for his two seats, which in 2023 had cost just $6,802. Hours later he posted that Inter Miami representatives had told him the pricing was incorrect because of a computer glitch that sent supporters quotes for additional seats they did not actually have. Even so, his follow-up indicated the error quote was double the actual cost, which still left his seats rising from around $6,800 to $15,300 for two — an increase of 125% year-on-year.
