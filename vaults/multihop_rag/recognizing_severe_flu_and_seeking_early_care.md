# Recognizing A Severe Flu And Seeking Early Care

When flu symptoms are severe or start to change dramatically, the rule both Miller and Sylvester give is to seek medical attention immediately rather than spend time deliberating about whether it is warranted.

Miller thinks most people do not understand how severe the flu can be, so they do not recognize the signs that they need immediate medical attention. The window can be very short: "In my case, it was very quick -- it was within 24 to 48 hours that it went from just coming on, to me being in the hot seat."

Her instruction is to treat the flu as a serious illness and to act on the body's signals. "Don't dismiss the flu as just the flu. It's so much more than that. And it can be life-altering," she urged. She continued, "If you're sick and the symptoms are severe, don't waste time wondering if you should seek medical attention. Listen to your body -- it's better to go in proactively, rather than regret it later."

Sylvester agreed, stressing the need for people to be their own patient advocates. "It's important to seek care early, especially if symptoms start to change dramatically," he said.
