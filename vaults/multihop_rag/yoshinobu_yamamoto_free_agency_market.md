# Yoshinobu Yamamoto's Free Agency Market At The Winter Meetings

The Yankees were reportedly going to meet with Yoshinobu Yamamoto, the top pitcher on the market, in New York on Monday, and MLB Network's Jon Morosi reported New York was considered one of the top teams to sign him along with the Red Sox, Giants, Dodgers and Cubs, per Sporting News' tracker entry of Wednesday, Dec. 6, 2023.

That would be the second time Yamamoto had met with a New York team: per Will Sammon of The Athletic, he reportedly already met with Mets owner Steve Cohen in Japan the previous week. Sporting News framed it as the Yankees possibly being on the verge of adding Soto but perhaps not being done.

Earlier, on Sunday, Dec. 3, Susan Slusser of the San Francisco Chronicle reported that teams believed the Giants "might have an edge" in the pursuit of the Japanese ace. The Giants had missed out on big names in the past like Aaron Judge and Carlos Correa, but were looking to turn that around in 2023 with heavy involvement in the markets for Ohtani, among others.
