# Capitol's International-First, Direct-To-Fan Campaign Model

Capitol Music Group's changed approach to artist campaigns is to engage directly with international partners earlier than ever before, rather than unfairly relying on its ex-US affiliates within UMG to do the heavy lifting early on, since those affiliates have their own local repertoire to work on. The stated reason for the early emphasis on international is to start the process of building revenue streams as early as possible, specifically brand building and direct-to-fan monetization.

The model's range is illustrated by Ice Spice: she still has not released her debut album, yet is recognised around the world through commercials, syncs in video games, TV and film, a big soundtrack presence, and innovative products on her own store, with Michelle Jubelirer pointing to the Ice Spice American Flag and Chia Pet as examples. The case shows the mechanism working before the usual album milestone, which is the point of moving the international and commerce work earlier in a campaign.
