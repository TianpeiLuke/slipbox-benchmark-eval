# The Wonder Flower Transformation Mechanic

The Wonder Flowers are the biggest twist in the game: in addition to the one Bowser stole, there is one hidden in almost every stage, and touching them transports you to an alternate dimension where the rules of the Marioverse have been completely rewritten.

The defining feature is unpredictability rather than a fixed effect. In one level Super Stars rain down from the sky; in another you might be transformed into a giant slime. You may also run into Wonder Flowers that convert the game from a side scroller into a top-down 2D maze, or suddenly find yourself the target in a shooting gallery. Almost every flower is different and the possibilities are seemingly endless.

The scale of the design work behind that is documented: in a recent volume of Nintendo's Ask the Developer, the reviewer learned the team had a wall of sticky notes with over 2,000 ideas — and after playing Wonder, he believes it. Wonder Flowers feel like they add an extra half a level to every stage; they are a delicious dessert on top of an already bountiful meal.
