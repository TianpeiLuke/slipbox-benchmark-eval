# 62% Of US TikTok Users Pay For Music Streaming

According to an analysis commissioned by TikTok and carried out by US market monitor Luminate, 62% of US TikTok users pay for a music streaming service, compared to 43% of all consumers.

Music Business Worldwide's 2023-11-21 report puts that figure in context. TikTok counted 150 million users in the United States as of February 2023, which means that, read through the lens of the Luminate analysis, approximately 93 million TikTok users in the US appear to be paying for a subscription to one of the various streaming services available in the United States, including Spotify, Apple Music and others. For comparison, MBW reported in July that as of February, in the United States, Spotify counted 44.4 million paying subscribers, Spotify rival Apple Music had 32.6 million subscribers, and Amazon Music had 29.3 million subscribers. The framing MBW gives is that TikTok is in the process of reinventing itself as both a partner to music companies and a competitor to streaming and distribution services, and the analysis sheds light on why the ByteDance-owned short video platform has focused so intensely on expanding its services in the music world.
