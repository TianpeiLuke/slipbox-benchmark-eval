# Chris Stapleton And Morgane Stapleton's Marriage

Chris and Morgane Stapleton met while working at neighbouring music publishing companies on Music Row, married in October 2007, and had been together 16 years as of the November 2023 article; they share five children, three of them boys under six. Morgane has been the country star's muse for nearly two decades and is also a working collaborator: she sings background on 10 of his songs on the album "Higher," is a producer on the project, and spends every performance on stage with him, tambourine in hand.

Asked at the 2023 Rock & Roll Hall of Fame induction ceremony for the key to a successful marriage, Stapleton, 45, told Fox News Digital: "The key to a successful marriage, I'd say communication. We're always working on that… love and kindness and patience. I think you have those things in play, hopefully, you can overcome whatever obstacles." Stapleton, whose big break in the country scene came eight years earlier with the album "Traveller," has addressed other challenges as well, recently revealing that he and his wife had started going to therapy together.
