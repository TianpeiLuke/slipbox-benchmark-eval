# Walmart Price Match Policy

Walmart does not price-match the prices of its competitors. Its online store does offer price-matching on items purchased from Walmart stores, but only if the item is in stock at Walmart.com.

The boundaries of that narrow policy are geographic, temporal, and structural. It does not apply to shoppers who live in Alaska, Hawaii, or Puerto Rico; it does not apply to special event prices, like those seen during Black Friday; Walmart does not retroactively price-match items bought from Walmart.com that have dropped in price after the time of purchase; and it does not price-match items purchased from Walmart Marketplace retailers or third-party sellers. The Verge published this summary on October 25th, 2023.
