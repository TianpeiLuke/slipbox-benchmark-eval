# Rogers Communications' Rebuttal On Information Access

Rogers Communications denied the sisters' premise that they were improperly cut off from information, saying the company took "reasonable steps" to protect company information from a competitor. Spokesperson Sarah Schmidt added that Melinda Rogers-Hixon and Martha Rogers could have resolved the matter by choosing a different lawyer, "and they chose not to do so" — putting the cause of their exclusion on their own choice rather than on any vendetta.

The company also disputed that the courtroom was the right venue and that the dispute was harming the business. "This matter should be resolved privately," Schmidt said by email. "We've demonstrated that we will not be distracted by these actions — we have significant momentum in the market, our merger is tracking ahead of plan, and we remain squarely focused on doing what's right for our customers and stakeholders."
