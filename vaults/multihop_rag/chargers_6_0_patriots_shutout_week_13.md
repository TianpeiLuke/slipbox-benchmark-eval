# Chargers 6-0 Patriots, Week 13 2023

The Los Angeles Chargers (5-7) shut out the New England Patriots 6-0 in Week 13 of the 2023 season on the strength of two 38-yard Cameron Dicker field goals in the second quarter — their first shutout in six years.

Justin Herbert threw for 212 yards and set up the pair of Dicker field goals that were all Los Angeles needed. Keenan Allen came back from a thigh injury to catch five passes for 58 yards. New England made a quarterback switch, benching Mac Jones in favor of Bailey Zappe, but the Patriots (2-10) still lost their fifth straight game; for the first time in franchise history they had been shut out at home twice in a season. Zappe was sacked five times, two by Khalil Mack, giving Mack a career-high 15 for the season and 99 in his career. Zappe went 13 for 25 for 141 yards and did avoid an interception for the first time in three games, the previous two in relief of Jones.
