# Creator Economy Sustainability And Creator Venture Capital

Writing in TechCrunch's 2023-10-13 startups roundup, the newsletter's author argues that creators getting together to work out how to make the job sustainable makes sense, but that creators raising venture capital does not.

The grounds are the two events reported that week. Amanda reported that VidCon is still kicking, and that for the first time the conference hosted an industry leadership summit where creator economy experts and creators could hash out their grievances with the state of the business and share ideas to make the job of a creator more sustainable. Lauren reported that a former TikTok employee is building a social app for content creators to network and "spill the tea," so creators can help each other out making better, more engaging content. Both are creators pooling knowledge rather than capital.

The warrant joining grounds to claim is an economics argument about returns: it is, in the author's words, "so eye-wateringly hard to make money as a creator" that he would love to see the pitch that convinces a VC to cut a check to a creator with a realistic expectation of a venture-scale return. Venture capital requires venture-scale outcomes, and individual creator economics are held not to reach that scale — which is why the peer-support route is endorsed and the VC route is not. The claim is scoped to creators raising venture capital as individuals; it is stated as the author's own view in a newsletter, not as a survey finding.
