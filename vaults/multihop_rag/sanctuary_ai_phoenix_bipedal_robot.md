# Sanctuary AI's Phoenix Bipedal Robot

Phoenix is Sanctuary AI's newest and first bipedal robot. Its demonstrated capabilities are concrete and commercial: it can stock shelves, unload delivery vehicles and operate a checkout.

Those are early steps toward what CEO Geordie Rose sees as a much longer-term goal of getting robots to perceive the physical world and be able to reason about it in a way that resembles intelligence. Like other humanoids, Phoenix is meant to look endearing, because how it interacts with real people is a big part of its function.
