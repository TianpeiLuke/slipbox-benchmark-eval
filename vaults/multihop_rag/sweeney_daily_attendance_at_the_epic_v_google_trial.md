# Sweeney's Daily Attendance At The Epic V. Google Trial

Tim Sweeney attended the Epic v. Google trial every day save one, and told The Verge he did so because Epic was asking a lot of the court system and the jury — spending four weeks on a major antitrust trial full of complicated facts and evidence — and it "wouldn't be right to start something like this and not show up."

He added a precedent for the practice: Phil Schiller sat throughout the entire Epic v. Apple trial, as did Sweeney, so in his view showing up is "just necessary to show respect for the legal process." Asked what happened on the single day he was absent, Sweeney declined to share, saying only that there were "non-Epic issues" he had to deal with. The interviewer noted that he had himself been present every day of the trial.
