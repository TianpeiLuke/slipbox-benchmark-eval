# The Big Ten's Head-Coach Responsibility Doctrine

The Big Ten justified suspending Jim Harbaugh without proving he knew of the sign-stealing scheme by arguing that a head coach bears responsibility for preventing such a scheme from taking place. The conference said it was "proven" that an illegal sign-stealing scheme existed, but conceded it could not prove that Harbaugh knew about it — so the punishment rested on his position rather than on his personal conduct.

The warrant joining that claim to the penalty is a claim about what a head coach represents. As reported by Stewart Mandel on Nov. 10, 2023, the Big Ten also asserted that it was not actually sanctioning Harbaugh, for whom it had no proof of wrongdoing, but that he "embodies the university for purposes of its football program." On that reading the sanction attaches to the institution and lands on the coach only because he stands for it.
