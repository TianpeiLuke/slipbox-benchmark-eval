# Adil Rashid's T20I Milestones And Series Form

Adil Rashid reached his 100th T20I wicket in the first match of the December 2023 series against West Indies — on his 100th T20I appearance — and finished the series with eight wickets at 15 and an economy rate of 6.26.

In the first T20I in Barbados he had Kyle Mayers caught by Brook in the deep for 35 at 79-3, his 99th T20I wicket, off what was in truth a bit of a drag down but delivered from the front of the hand so it skidded on; England needed that badly. He then bowled Hetmyer's dismissal for 1 at 101-4, caught by Duckett on the midwicket sponge after Hetmyer danced out of his crease and got a good piece of the length ball — the 100th wicket, and a crucial one in the context of the game as England looked to ramp up the pressure. In the deciding fifth T20I he finished with figures of 4-0-21-2, and the live blog noted he had had a great series with those eight wickets at 15 and an absurd economy rate of 6.26, but was going to end on the losing side. The Guardian's report added that amid the wider theme of transformation Rashid's excellence remained constant: the 35-year-old, who had risen to the top of the T20 bowling rankings for the first time that Wednesday, took two wickets and conceded just 21 in the decider, while Topley was even better, also claiming a pair of victims but conceding only 17 — England took plenty of positives from the series, but the result was not one.
