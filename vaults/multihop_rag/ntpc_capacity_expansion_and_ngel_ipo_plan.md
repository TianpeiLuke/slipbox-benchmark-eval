# NTPC's Capacity Expansion And The NGEL IPO Plan

NTPC plans to have 130 GW of installed power generation capacity comprising 60 GW of renewable energy, up from a current renewable level of 3.3 GW. That is a long-term target; in the near term NTPC plans to add 15-16 GW of renewable energy by FY26, with around 7.7 GW of RE capacity currently under construction and 11 GW at different stages. Because all-time high peak power demand makes the case for thermal-based capacity as energy security, NTPC also plans to add 10-11 GW of thermal-based capacity by FY26, a target raised from 7 GW earlier. Together this implies capacity growth during FY23-26 of around 10-11 per cent CAGR from current levels. Management guided to capex of around ₹28,373 crore for FY24, with FY25 and FY26 on similar lines and capex shooting up thereafter on account of growth in renewable-related spending.

On monetisation, an earlier call had highlighted that NTPC was attempting to monetise its stake in its renewable energy arm NTPC Green Energy Ltd by selling a stake to a strategic partner; during the Q1FY24 earnings call management said the company is now looking to unlock value by way of an IPO rather than onboarding a strategic investor, with the timeline not yet certain because it depends on market-based factors and on how quickly substantial RE capacity is on the ground. Financing capacity supports the plan: the company had a debt-to-equity ratio of around 1.24 times as of H1FY24 and, on account of its stable business model, can access debt at a cheaper rate than private power generation companies, so the reasonable D/E gives NTPC scope for further debt financing to fund its capex requirements.
