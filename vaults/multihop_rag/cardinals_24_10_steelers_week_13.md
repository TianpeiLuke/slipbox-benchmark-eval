# Cardinals 24-10 Steelers, Week 13 2023

James Conner ran for 105 yards and a pair of touchdowns against his old team as the Arizona Cardinals beat the Pittsburgh Steelers 24-10 in Week 13 of the 2023 season — the Cardinals' first win at Pittsburgh since 1969.

Arizona took control late in the first half with a 99-yard touchdown drive that ended with a five-yard strike from Kyler Murray to Trey McBride, and led the rest of the way while enduring a pair of lengthy delays due to severe weather. The Steelers lost starting quarterback Kenny Pickett to an ankle injury late in the first half and scored their only touchdown in garbage time.
