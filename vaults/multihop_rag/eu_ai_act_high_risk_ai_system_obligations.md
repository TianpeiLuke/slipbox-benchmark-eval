# EU AI Act High-Risk AI System Obligations

The AI Act package imposes obligations on AI systems classified as "high risk" — those having "significant potential harm to health, safety, fundamental rights, environment, democracy and the rule of law."

The parliament wrote: "MEPs successfully managed to include a mandatory fundamental rights impact assessment, among other requirements, applicable also to the insurance and banking sectors. AI systems used to influence the outcome of elections and voter behaviour, are also classified as high-risk. Citizens will have a right to launch complaints about AI systems and receive explanations about decisions based on high-risk AI systems that impact their rights."

The package also promotes regulatory sandboxes and real-world testing, to be established by national authorities, to support startups and SMEs to develop and train AIs before placement on the market.
