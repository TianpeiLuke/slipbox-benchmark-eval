# Emily Ratajkowski's M Le Monde Oversized Jeans Shoot

Emily Ratajkowski's photo shoot for M Le Monde magazine sparked backlash among plus-size models and activists, who claimed it promotes unhealthy narratives about body image.

The supermodel, 32, shared several images from the shoot in late November 2023. For the fashion editorial, photographed by Oliver Hadlee Pearch, Ratajkowski posed in a variety of looks featuring denim jeans and flowing tops — but it was the second image that caught the attention of her Instagram followers. In that photo, Ratajkowski is seen wearing a pair of low-rise, wide-leg denim jeans double her size, showing off her toned figure as the oversized jeans fall at her feet; as she holds up the waistband with one arm, she stands in one leg of the pair to highlight the noticeable size gap.

The shoot — styled by Charlotte Collet and creative directed by Jean-Baptiste Talbourdet-Napoleone — instantly sparked backlash after Ratajkowski shared the images to Instagram. In her comments section, thousands of users criticised both Ratajkowski and the team behind the photo shoot for the "out of touch" image and claimed the shoot was "fatphobic".
