# Anecdotal Evidence Undercuts Google's Gemini Pro Claims

Against Google's promises for Gemini Pro, TechCrunch's rejoinder on 7 December 2023 was blunt: "the anecdotal evidence suggests otherwise." The counter-argument attacks the evidential basis of the launch claims rather than the model's architecture, setting user experience after release against what Google said the model would do.

The grounds are the reception of the rollout itself. A "lite" version of Gemini, Gemini Pro, began rolling out to Bard the day before the article, and it did not take long before users began voicing their frustrations with it on X, formerly Twitter.
