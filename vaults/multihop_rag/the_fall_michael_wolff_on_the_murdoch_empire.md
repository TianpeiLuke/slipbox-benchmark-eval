# Michael Wolff On Fox News And Rupert Murdoch

Michael Wolff's latest book, published by The Bridge Street Press at $34.99 and out at the time of writing, turns his blowtorch on Fox News and the recently retired Rupert Murdoch — "two players who had massive roles in getting Trump into power" — The Sydney Morning Herald listed on 1 October 2023.

Its defining characteristic, per the reviewer, is the author's method: "One thing you can be sure of in a Michael Wolff book is plenty of controversial detail and quotes that are not always fully substantiated and sourced."

Its place in his output is also noted: the American writer has tackled the Donald Trump White House in three books and has already written a couple about Murdoch, to whom he now returns. "Next year? Surely not," the reviewer adds.
