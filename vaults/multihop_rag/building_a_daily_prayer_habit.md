# Building a Daily Prayer Habit

Jonathan Roumie's account of building a habit of prayer starts from the claim that it "is easier than people think", and the steps he describes are: connect with Jesus through prayer in order to disconnect from the chaos of the real world, the divisiveness and the anxiety that exists; tap into that habit consistently; and rely on it daily, especially through busy seasons.

The stated goal of the practice sets its direction: people can find "peace and a deeper relationship with Christ, and I think that's really the goal of the prayer challenge," he said. The expected outcome of consistency follows from that: "As you consistently tap into that habit, you find your relationship with Jesus growing deeper and deeper, just automatically. And as a result, you then experience much more peace, tranquility and serenity in your life and the ability to really muscle through some of the most difficult obstacles and challenges that life can throw at you."

The obstacle the procedure has to work against is distraction, and it is by Roumie's own account substantial. Asked how anyone keeps a focus on prayer, on God and on a connection with God in the midst of trying times when it is very easy to get distracted in today's world, Roumie answered: "Yes, and I think that for me, I'm very easily distracted — and technology is one of the things that contributes to that distraction." The daily reliance is what he uses against it: "This is something that I've been so grateful for — and especially as we go into the hubbub and the busyness of the Christmas season, it's something that I'm relying on daily, to be able to just cut through all that noise."
