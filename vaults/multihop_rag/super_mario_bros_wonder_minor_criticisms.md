# Minor Criticisms Of Super Mario Bros. Wonder

The reviewer's reservations are explicitly minor and do not disturb the overall verdict. Mummy enemies draw a complaint — pulling their wraps feels a bit tedious — and he fears the Elephant Suit might be a bit overpowered in comparison to the Drill Hat.

The other two are complaints about scarcity rather than quality: the 5-star levels and rhythm stages are so good he wishes there were more of them, and he is slightly disappointed the adorable penguins from the movie did not make an appearance somewhere in Wonder's ice levels.
