# Week 14 PPR Wide Receiver Sleepers And Waiver Pickups

Sporting News named Elijah Moore (vs. Jaguars), Noah Brown (@ Jets), Odell Beckham Jr. (vs. Rams), Jonathan Mingo (@ Saints) and Xavier Gipson (vs. Texans) as its preferred waiver-wire pickups in PPR formats for Week 14, publishing on Dec. 7, 2023.

Each case turns on a vacated target share. Moore had just seen 12 targets from Joe Flacco and was in line for another high-usage day with Amari Cooper (concussion) potentially sidelined. Brown should also see a steady target share after Tank Dell's season-ending leg injury. Beckham Jr. was likely to see increased usage with Mark Andrews (leg, ankle) done for the regular season.

Mingo had seen six-plus targets in his past four games, as his rapport with Bryce Young strengthened by the week. Gipson led the Jets in receiving that past week with five receptions for 77 yards, and with New York routinely playing from behind, similar stat lines could be in the works.
