# Humanoid Prototype Reality Check: Figure And Optimus

Against the scale of Figure AI's market claim stands the state of its actual hardware: at the moment, Brett Adcock's firm doesn't have a prototype that's ready for market. Founded just over a year before 2023-11-05 and having raised tens of millions of dollars, it had recently revealed only a 38-second video of Figure walking through its test facility in Sunnyvale, California.

The same gap applies to the highest-profile competitor. Tesla CEO Elon Musk is also trying to build a humanoid, called Optimus, through the electric car-maker's robotics division, but a hyped-up live demonstration in 2022 of the robot's awkwardly halting steps didn't impress experts in the robotics field. Seemingly farther along is Tesla's Austin, Texas-based neighbor Apptronik, which unveiled its Apollo humanoid in an August 2023 video demonstration.
