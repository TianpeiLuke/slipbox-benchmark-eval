# The Wilma Mankiller Barbie In The "Inspiring Women" Series

Mattel announced in November 2023 that it was releasing a Barbie doll of Wilma Mankiller, the first female Principal Chief of the Cherokee Nation, as part of the toy company's "Inspiring Women" series. Mankiller is described as having inspired countless Native American children as a powerful but humble leader who expanded early education and rural healthcare. On 6 December the Cherokee Nation hosted an event to celebrate the doll.

The doll's design, as described by Mattel, portrays Mankiller's dark hair and has her wearing "a richly pigmented turquoise dress with ribbon striping that represents the four directions: north, south, east and west." It also comes with a basket, the look inspired by an iconic photograph of Mankiller holding a woven basket.
