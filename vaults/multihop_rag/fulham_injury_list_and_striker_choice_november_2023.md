# Fulham's Injury List And Striker Choice, November 2023

Fulham's injury situation had not improved ahead of their November 2023 home match against Manchester United: Adama Traore was suffering from a thigh problem, Issa Diop was nursing a foot issue, Kenny Tete was out due to an unspecified injury, and Tosin Adarabioyo was suffering from a groin injury. However, Tete, Adarabioyo and Traore were on their way to being fully fit and could at least make the squad for the weekend's high-stakes match against Man United.

The coach's biggest concern revolved around his No. 9 position. With his first goal in the EFL Cup match, Rodrigo Muniz proved that he could be a good pick for the role on a regular basis, and the coach hinted that he could choose Muniz over Raul Jimenez, who was yet to score since joining Fulham that summer. Antonee Robinson, Tim Team and Bernd Leno would be joining the team again to help them hold the fort at the back.
