# Golf Ball Rollback Application To Men's Majors

If the rules pass, the R&A, which organizes The Open, and the USGA, the organizer of the U.S. Open, will have the limited-flight balls in those two majors — but the other men's majors are for Augusta National and the PGA of America to decide, Essentially Sports reported on Dec. 3, 2023.

Those two bodies had reacted differently. Seth Waugh, the PGA of America CEO, denounced the proposals in a memo to the R&A, taking along nine other PGAs across the globe. Fred Ridley, the chairman of Augusta National, did not exactly bang the drum for the Model Local Rule, but the former amateur golfer did stand behind the governing bodies, saying he would much prefer to see the issue finally addressed. Notably, Augusta infamously "Tiger-proofed" its course to make it tougher for golfers — read Tiger Woods.
