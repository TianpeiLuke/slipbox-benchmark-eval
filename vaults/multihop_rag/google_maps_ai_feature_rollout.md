# Google Maps' AI Feature Rollout

Google is rolling out a variety of updates for Maps, incorporating AI technology to enhance user experience, TechCrunch reported on November 3, 2023.

The feature is currently available in France, Germany, Japan, the U.K. and the U.S.

Google has plans for further expansion beyond those five markets.
