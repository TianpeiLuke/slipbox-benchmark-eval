# Travis Kelce's "Breeder" Remarks On The New Heights Podcast

Travis Kelce, 34, made remarks about starting a family during a February 2023 episode of New Heights, the podcast he hosts with his brother Jason, that resurfaced in October 2023 amid his romance with Taylor Swift — after a source claimed to Yahoo Entertainment that the couple "have talked about kids" within the next year. In the episode, recorded while the Philadelphia Eagles and Kansas City Chiefs were preparing to meet in the Super Bowl, Jason asked their mother Donna which of her sons she was "rooting for." "You've said in the past to both of us that you root for me because I've given you grandkids," said Jason, who shares three children — Wyatt, four, Elliotte, two, and Bennett, seven months — with wife Kylie Kelce. "I've always known that you root for Travis. So who do you actually root for?"

Pressed again, "if in a battle to the death," Donna deflected by asking whom Jason would save if she and her husband Ed Kelce were stranded in the middle of the ocean, and said she could not pick which son to root against; Jason joked, "Remember if you choose me, Wyatt and Elliotte grow up without a father." Travis then chimed in about wanting his own family: "I gotta start breeding. I've gotta start breeding, to all the breeders out there." Jason replied "Please don't," and Donna advised: "Wait until you find the right person. Find the right person." Travis doubled down: "I'm gonna find a breeder, and I'm gonna get kids so that mom can love me again."
