# Warhammer 40,000: Rogue Trader — Premise

Warhammer 40,000: Rogue Trader is a game about the perils and pleasures of sitting atop the hierarchy of one of the most over-the-top and brutal sci-fi settings around — the universe whose tagline is "In the grim darkness of the far future, there is only war."

Polygon's framing is that even in a merciless, brutal dystopia that grinds its people into dust, some get to enjoy being at the top of the food chain. Rogue Traders in 40K are freelance explorers scouting the far frontiers of the Imperium of Man: a risky career, but one that comes with luxurious rewards — agency, freedom, power, and a giant flagship. As the player, the reviewer explores a system of the Imperium with a loyal crew of companions, making impactful choices and determining the fate of those around them.
