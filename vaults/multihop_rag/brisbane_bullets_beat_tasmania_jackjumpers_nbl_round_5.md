# Brisbane Survive Tasmania's Fightback To Win 90-87

Nathan Sobey peeled off a game-high 28 points as Brisbane survived a late Tasmania fightback to continue their NBL resurgence with a tense 90-87 win, their first victory after seven straight losses in Sunday fixtures in a run dating back to the previous season.

More importantly, the result levelled Brisbane's season record at 4-4 after five rounds. It did not come easily: fourth-quarter specialists Tasmania trimmed a seven-point deficit back to a single point when Majok Deng drove to the rack with 34 seconds remaining, the two sides traded blows in the dying stages, and the JackJumpers almost forced overtime when they pinched Sam McDaniel's inbounds pass on the final play — Jack McVeigh getting a hand in to force the turnover and Tasmania finding Jordon Crawford, whose decent look at a game-leveller rimmed out. The JackJumpers, who trailed by seven at three-quarter time, kept alive their record of winning every fourth term that season, but it wasn't enough.

Brisbane captain Sobey led from the front while defensive specialist Mitch Norton kept Crawford (16 points) relatively quiet until the final period. Tyrrell Harrison and Josh Bannan (10 points each) were important for the Bullets, and Chris Smith (11) and Isaac White (10) gave solid contributions off the bench. The gut-wrenching loss ended a horror round five for the JackJumpers, who lost at home to Melbourne United before going down to the Bullets, leaving them 4-4 after a strong 4-2 start; McVeigh (18 points) and Deng (17) gave strong offensive contributions for Tasmania, who lost Marcus Lee for the final few minutes after the starting centre fouled out.
