# Reliance Retail Ventures Store Expansion

Reliance Retail has grown from a modest 1,691 stores in FY14 to 18,040 stores and over 66 million square feet of retail space by FY23, making it the largest player in India's organised retail segment, operating brick-and-mortar, e-commerce, cash and carry, B2C and B2B formats across the retail supply chain.

Business Line frames the runway in terms of share: with a low 3 per cent share of the market, there is scope for huge expansion from a combination of market share gains and a high-growth market where the consumption theme is picking up steam, driven by growth in per capita income. For perspective, in the US, organised retail giant Walmart today has a little over 10 per cent share of the retail industry, though how things will pan out in the Indian retail industry remains open.

Recent growth has been solid: FY21-23 revenue and EBITDA CAGR of 38 and 50 per cent respectively, fuelled by store additions, acquisitions totalling $1.2 billion, and a phygital model with digital-brand acquisitions such as Netmeds and Clovia. Besides apparel, grocery and electronics, the company has gained a foothold through acquisitions in newer segments such as pharmacy (Netmeds), fashion (Zivame, Clovia) and D2C (Urban Ladder), which the article expects to drive growth in the medium term. With dominance in the digital and retail ecosystem and scope to enhance synergies across them, Reliance is strategically positioned to garner a good share of the consumer spending that will happen over the next decades through its consumer-facing businesses.
