# FAPE And PEAC: History And Programs

FAPE, the Fund for Assistance to Private Education, was part of the Special Fund for Education set aside by the American government in 1963 from the surplus funds authorized by the War Damage Act of 1962, and was organized under Executive Order 156 issued by President Ferdinand Marcos on 5 November 1968. From initial seed money of $6,154,000, or P215 million based on the 1968 peso-to-dollar exchange rate, the FAPE fund had in 55 years barely grown to P277 million; in contrast, the trustees managing the fund expanded their services, basically as a service provider to the DepEd, and as a consequence it was spun off as a separate organization, the Private Education Assistance Committee, PEAC.

Over the years PEAC has evolved into co-implementing externally funded programs, namely the DepEd's Education Service Contracting, Teachers' Salary Subsidy, the SHS Voucher Program, In-Service Training, and Bayanihan for Basic Education, as well as implementing internally funded programs of assistance for private education in the areas of training, school quality assurance, school improvement programs, and grants programs. In sum, PEAC has been an effective instrument to operationalize "complementarity" between private and public schools as provided in the Philippine Constitution, and an incorruptible partner of the education agencies in efficiently co-implementing national subsidy programs for students and teachers in private schools.
