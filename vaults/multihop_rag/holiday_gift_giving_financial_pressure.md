# The Financial Pressure Of Holiday Gift Giving

Many people feel that the only way they can show love is to spend a lot of money on gifts, a belief reinforced by family expectations and by social media and TV ads showing lavish gifts — and Fortune's 2023-12-23 reporting shows that pressure turning into guilt and embarrassment for borrowers who cannot meet it.

Plauché, who is both a mom and a new grad with a salary to match, says she can barely manage her daily expenses, yet still feels a responsibility to give her family gifts because that is how they have traditionally shown each other love: "I don't want to give my family this message that they're not loved and it definitely adds a lot of pressure." Johnson feels guilty because the holidays are when she feels she can show her family how grateful she is for them, and she has always prided herself on being a great gift giver who not only finds something nice but knows how to spot unique and special gifts. "I think I'm a little embarrassed to tell them that this year I might not be able to be a good gift giver," she said. The article's advice starts from this recognition: acknowledging that you are feeling pressure is a good first step towards making a plan to set expectations and be realistic about what you can spend.
