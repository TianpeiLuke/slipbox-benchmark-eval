# Curtis Samuel Week 16 Waiver Target

CBSSports.com's Dec. 19, 2023 Week 16 wide receiver preview listed Curtis Samuel, WR, Washington, No. 4, among its Week 16 waiver adds, arguing he remained badly under-rostered: he was the top waiver wire add at wide receiver the previous week as well, but still sat at just 57% rostered.

The grounds are recent scoring: he had three straight games with double-digit fantasy points and had topped 19 in two of those. The matchup was not great, but it was easier for him than for Terry McLaurin and Jahan Dotson. His weekly breakdown at age 27, seven years' experience: at NYJ, NYJ -3, O/U 37, opponent vs. WR 14th, WR rank 30th, rostered 58%; year-to-date 56 receptions, 77 targets, 549 receiving yards, 5 TD, 11 fantasy points per game.
