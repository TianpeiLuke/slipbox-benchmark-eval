# Induced AI's Plain-English Workflow Automation

Sam Altman is among the backers of Induced AI, an AI startup founded by two teenagers that aims to help businesses automate workflows in "previously unexplored" ways.

Manish reported the story in TechCrunch's Week in Review of October 7, 2023. Induced AI was founded that same year, and its product lets businesses type their back-office tasks into it in plain English, converting those instructions into pseudo-code in real time.
