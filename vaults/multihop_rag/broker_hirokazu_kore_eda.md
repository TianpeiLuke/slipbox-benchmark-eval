# Broker (Hirokazu Kore-eda)

Broker is Hirokazu Kore-eda's film starring Song Kang-ho, Gang Dong-won, Bae Doona, and Lee Ji-eun, ranked 43 on Polygon's best movies of 2023 list of 2023-10-07 and available on Hulu or for digital rental/purchase.

Polygon frames it as a return to form: the director of Shoplifters comes back with another story about people committing crimes just to survive — this time two men who sell abandoned babies on the adoption black market.

Critic PV singles out the performances: Song Kang-ho and Bae Doona are electrifying, Song as another shifty, funny schemer who is in way over his head and Bae as a hard-headed cop pursuing them, but it is Lee Ji-eun, as a young mother who becomes entangled in the scheme, who steals the show, with a fierce intelligence and hardened worldview concealed by a veneer of youthful innocence. Plus one very adorable baby, which never hurts.
