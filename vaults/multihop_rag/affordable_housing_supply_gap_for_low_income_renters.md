# The Affordable Housing Supply Gap For Low-Income Renters

The United States has long had an affordable housing crisis, but it has been exacerbated as of late by a surge in mortgage interest rates and low inventory, and the problem is especially acute for lower-income families.

The argument, as put to TechCrunch in December 2023, is that the market is not producing housing for them from either direction. Most iBuyers are focused on buying, renovating and either selling or renting homes in middle to upper class neighborhoods. And most home builders are "out of touch and building homes that no one who needs affordable housing could ever afford," Simply Homes CEO Brian Bagdasarian told TechCrunch in an interview.
