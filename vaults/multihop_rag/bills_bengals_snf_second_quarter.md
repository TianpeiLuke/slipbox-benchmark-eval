# The Second Quarter Of Bengals 24, Bills 18

Cincinnati took a 21-7 lead into halftime on Nov. 5, 2023 after a quarter in which Buffalo's only significant contribution was an interception. Josh Allen's deep shot to Deonte Harty fell incomplete, offensive holding was declined, and the Bills punted first. Trenton Irwin returned the kick to the Bengals' 22 and Cincinnati picked up where it left off: Burrow hit Higgins on a curl for 12, Chase was tackled for a two-yard loss before Mixon followed Orlando Brown up the middle for 12 and a first down at the Bengals' 44, and Mixon and Williams pushed to third-and-3 at the Bills' 49. A brutal false start by Alex Cappa backed Cincinnati up, and though Burrow found Drew Sample for six the Bengals remained two yards short; Burrow clearly wanted to go for it on fourth-and-2 but Cincinnati punted, Brad Robbins booting it for a fair catch at the Bills' 12.

Allen then made his first mistake. After an overthrown deep shot to Harty, Cam Taylor-Britt leapt in front of Gabe Davis for his third interception of the season and Allen's ninth of the year, giving the Bengals excellent field position at the Bills' 32. Cincinnati squandered it: Hudson caught nine to start the drive, but a quarterback sneak was stopped short, a delay of game backed them up, a Burrow scramble for a first down came back on offensive holding to make it third-and-15 from the Bills' 37, and after the Bengals accepted five yards on a Bills offsides rather than an eight-yard Irwin grab, Leonard Floyd sacked Burrow at the Bills' 37. Offensive holding was declined and Cincinnati lined up for a field goal that was good from 55 — but a false start on Brown wiped it out, and the Bengals punted instead, leaving Buffalo at its own 9.

Buffalo went three-and-out and Cincinnati made it pay. Cook rushed for six then two to set up third-and-2, and BJ Hill sacked Allen at the Bills' 8 to force the punt, which Irwin returned nine yards to the Bills' 45 — back-to-back Cincinnati drives starting in Buffalo territory. After back-to-back Burrow incompletions to Hudson and Chase, Boyd picked up nine over the middle to set up fourth-and-1 at the Bills' 36; the Bengals went for it and Chase slipped past his defender for 11 yards to the Bills' 25, taking the game to the two-minute warning. Mixon gained three to the 21 and Buffalo used its second timeout; Burrow danced out of pocket traffic and threw a perfect deep ball to Boyd, who lost the handle at the 2-yard line, setting up third-and-7. At 9:44 p.m. Sample lined up in the backfield, darted out, caught a short pass and raced to the end zone for 21-7, leaving Buffalo 1:37 and one timeout.

Buffalo's two-minute drive died on a penalty. Allen found Shakir for 22 to the 47 and Kincaid for 11 into Cincinnati territory at the 42, then two more short completions to Kincaid, on the second of which Taylor-Britt was injured, forcing a Bengals timeout. Allen then aired the ball deep down the left side and officials ruled intentional grounding after discussion — NBC's Terry McAulay explained that Allen threw it well past Davis — a huge penalty that pushed Buffalo out of field-goal range back to the Bengals' 48, and the clock ran out with Cincinnati leading 21-7.
