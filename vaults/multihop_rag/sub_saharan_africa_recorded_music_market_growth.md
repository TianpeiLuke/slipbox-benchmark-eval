# Sub-Saharan Africa Recorded Music Market Growth

Sub-Saharan Africa (SSA) was the world's fastest-growing recorded music region in 2022, recording 34.7% year-on-year growth according to IFPI figures, and by the time of this October 2023 report the growth opportunity for subscription music streaming in the region was becoming significantly clearer.

Africa-focused music streaming service Mdundo illustrates the trend: it recently reported a 239% year-on-year jump in revenue from paid subscriptions and a 23.2% year-on-year increase in monthly active users. Geographically, Mdundo's focus is on five key SSA markets — Nigeria, Kenya, Tanzania, Ghana and South Africa.

The connectivity base underpinning that opportunity is also expanding. According to a 2022 report from GSMA, 615 million people in sub-Saharan Africa are expected to sign up for mobile services by 2025, on top of the roughly 500 million already subscribed as of 2020.
