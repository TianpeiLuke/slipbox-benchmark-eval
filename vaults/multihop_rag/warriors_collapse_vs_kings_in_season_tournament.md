# Warriors Collapse Vs Kings In-Season Tournament

The Warriors fell to 8-10 following Tuesday's disastrous collapse to the Kings in Sacramento, Yardbarker reported on Nov. 29, 2023: leading by five with 58 seconds to go and a spot in the next round of the In-Season Tournament on the line, Golden State gave the game away in a sequence of errors.

Andrew Wiggins fouled De'Aaron Fox, Stephen Curry passed the ball straight to Malik Monk on his own side of the court, Draymond Green threw the ball out of bounds, and Monk scored five points in the final 37 seconds to send the Warriors back to San Francisco forced to ask some tough questions. On the floor for those minutes were Curry, Klay Thompson, Green, Wiggins and Kevon Looney — four of the five drafted by the Warriors, with Steph, Klay and Draymond the bedrock of four championship teams and perhaps the NBA's greatest dynasty this century.
