# Thomas Hand's Grief And Irish Background

Thomas Hand — Dublin-born, raised Roman Catholic, and not Jewish — was first told in the days after the 7 October attack that his daughter Emily was dead, and said in one interview that he welcomed her death because it was better than her being held captive and tortured by Hamas; he later found out she had been taken hostage, FOX News Lifestyle reported on 17 November 2023.

He moved to Israel in 1992, a nation he called a multicultural "paradise" up until 7 October, and his suffering over the past 40 days has made international headlines. Life has been almost unbearable for him since that day; he was described as "fragile" by one person working with family members of the Israeli hostages. Hand spoke to Fox News Digital beside Michael Levy, whose brother Or was also taken hostage by terrorists, and they were accompanied by Israeli mental health expert Dr Ofrit Shapira-Berman.

A lean man to begin with, Hand said he has lost 8 kilos — about 18 pounds — since his daughter was taken hostage on 7 October. "I'm Irish, so I have a couple of beers before I go to bed and that sets me off because I don't want to think," he said. "I wouldn't be able to sleep. I'm eating very little. My stomach is shrunk… I had a hot dog today. I always wanted an American hot dog, and I was full after that." He has also lost interest in news amid his own pain and the ongoing pain of the entire nation: "At first it was a morbid curiosity to maybe see Emily amongst it all. And then it was just too much. I couldn't watch it anymore. I don't watch the news. I don't watch these videos." Further pain is still to come, he said, if and when the hostages return and find out loved ones have been killed and entire families massacred.
