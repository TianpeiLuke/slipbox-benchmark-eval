# Call Of Duty's PlayStation Availability After The Microsoft Deal

The big question fans kept asking after Microsoft's Activision deal — whether Call of Duty will still be on PlayStation — has a reassuring answer for Sony owners: as the BBC reported on 13 October 2023, it will be 2038 before it is even possible that the game could become an Xbox exclusive.

The stakes behind the question are set by the franchise's size. Call of Duty is one of the biggest-selling video game franchises of all time, ranking behind just Mario, Tetris and Pokemon.

The date is the operative scope condition: the answer is not that Call of Duty will never leave PlayStation, but that exclusivity is off the table until 2038.
