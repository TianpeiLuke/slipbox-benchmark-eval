# Musk And Tesla's Safety Rating Record Claims

Elon Musk is "highly confident" that the Cybertruck will be safer than other trucks on the road, for occupants and pedestrians alike — a counter to the safety-nightmare framing that rests on Tesla's existing record.

The supporting grounds The Verge sets out: Tesla has historically achieved high safety ratings thanks to its underlying architecture, which makes the car more rigid and better protects passengers; the location of the battery in the floor of the vehicle also gives the Model Y and other Tesla vehicles a lower center of gravity, which improves road stability and decreases the chances of a rollover; and all four of Tesla's vehicles — the Model S, X, 3, and Y — have earned five-star ratings from Euro NCAP.
