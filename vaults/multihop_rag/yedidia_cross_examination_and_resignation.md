# Yedidia's Cross-Examination And Resignation

Yedidia's testimony ended on a fiery note that was later struck from the record: asked why he had lost faith in FTX and resigned, he said, "FTX defrauded all its customers."

On cross-examination, defense attorney Christian Everdell asked about the long hours employees worked and Yedidia's concern about Wang being near burnout, which had led Yedidia to institute a rule not to wake Wang at night for bug fixes because he needed sleep. Everdell also drilled Yedidia on his high level of compensation in less than two years at FTX: his base salary was between $175,000 and $200,000, but he received multiple bonuses of more than $12 million in cash and company equity. Yedidia said he is now teaching math — geometry and algebra — at a high school; he invested most of the millions he earned as bonuses back into FTX, and his equity stake is now worthless. As FTX was failing, Yedidia said, he was by Bankman-Fried's side, and he highlighted a Signal exchange in November 2022 in which he wrote, "I love you Sam. I'm not going anywhere," explaining that he wrote it because so many people had left. Asked what changed, Yedidia said he learned that FTX customer deposits had been used to pay loans to creditors, and that Alameda's actions seemed "flagrantly wrong."
