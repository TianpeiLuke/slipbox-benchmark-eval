# Planet Terror (Robert Rodriguez)

Planet Terror is Robert Rodriguez's zombie-outbreak action horror film, released alongside Quentin Tarantino's Death Proof as the theatrical double feature Grindhouse. It stars Rose McGowan, Freddy Rodriguez, and Michael Biehn, and Polygon calls the shot of McGowan aiming the grenade launcher of a leg-mounted assault rifle from the back of a motorcycle one of the images most emblematic of the bombastic action and humor of Rodriguez's oeuvre.

The story follows a go-go dancer named Cherry Darling, played by McGowan, and her estranged lover "El Wray," played by Freddy Rodriguez, who find themselves among a handful of survivors after a deadly biochemical agent sparks a zombie outbreak. Banding together, the survivors must find a way to escape the town while fighting against the rogue military commander, played by Bruce Willis, who unleashed the virus in the first place.

The film's distinguishing quality is deliberate excess rather than craft in the conventional sense. Polygon's Toussaint Egan writes that the story and dialogue are intentionally tongue-in-cheek and the furthest thing from the film's saving graces; inspired by the exploitation films of the '70s, the movie gleefully relishes its over-the-top throwback aesthetic, with gory action, comically implausible plotlines, and unabashed sexuality. It is recommended as a peak "turn your brain off and enjoy" sort of movie.
