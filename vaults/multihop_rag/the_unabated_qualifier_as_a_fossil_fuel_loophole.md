# The "Unabated" Qualifier As A Fossil Fuel Loophole

The Verge argued that COP28's language about phasing down "unabated" coal carves out a huge loophole for fossil fuels — and that the same is true of the word wherever it is applied, to coal, oil, or gas.

The grounds are what the qualifier permits: it means polluters can continue burning fossil fuels as long as they pair that burning with technologies that capture greenhouse gas emissions. The warrant is that those technologies do not yet do the work the qualifier implies — they are emerging, typically do not capture 100 percent of the emissions, and remain unproven at scale — so an "unabated" commitment allows continued combustion rather than requiring its end.
