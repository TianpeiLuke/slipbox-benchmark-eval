# Sam Howell As A Week 5 2023 Sneaky Sleeper Against Chicago

CBS Sports listed Sam Howell as a "Sneaky Sleeper" for Week 5 of the 2023 season against Chicago: the claim is that Howell is a sleeper with 20-point potential rather than a confident start, and the warrant is that the matchup is one of the easiest available for a quarterback while Washington's own play-calling caps his volume.

The grounds for the upside are the Bears' weaknesses. Chicago ranks in the bottom-seven in completion rate allowed (70.1%), yards per catch allowed (12.2), yards after catch per reception allowed (6.26, second-most) and missed tackles (52), and ranks poorly in pass-rush pressure rate and blitz rate with just two sacks all season. That matters for Howell because his numbers when not pressured — a 73.8% completion rate, 7.2 yards per attempt and a 9.7% off-target rate — are all about league average rather than impressive, so a clean pocket is a plus given this week's opponent. He is also coming off the most efficient and busiest game of his young career: after a horrid four-interception loss in Week 3, he completed nearly 71% of his throws for 290 yards with a touchdown and a very clutch 40 rushing yards at Philadelphia, throwing 41 passes and running six times, with overtime helping his cause. The breakdown lists Washington at home versus Chicago, the Commanders favoured by 6, an over/under of 44.5, an opponent ranked 31st versus quarterbacks, 21.3 projected points, a 14th quarterback ranking, and year-to-date totals of 961 passing yards, 82 rushing yards, five touchdowns, five interceptions and 16.1 fantasy points per game.

The ceiling is limited by volume and by Howell's own scoring history. The Commanders like limiting his pass attempts in general — he has thrown over 31 times only in high-scoring shootouts, in Weeks 2 and 4 — and while a shootout is possible on Thursday it is not a certainty; Howell could just as easily put up nominal stats while Washington wins. He has one game in five as a pro with multiple passing touchdowns and two others with one rushing and one passing score. Through four weeks the Bears face an average of 31.8 pass attempts per game, and even in a game Chicago led for much of the previous week the Broncos tried just 28 throws — though three of the four quarterbacks Chicago faced found three touchdowns on their limited attempts. The recommendation is ranked accordingly: the author would rather start Jordan Love, Jared Goff or Justin Fields, but taking Howell over Dak Prescott and Joe Burrow isn't crazy given their matchups and situations.
