# Prime Day 2023 iPhone Wireless Charger Deals

WIRED's charging picks include its favorite Apple 3-in-1 wireless charger, an orb-shaped charger with extra ports, Apple's dual USB-C port power adapter, the MagSafe Duo, iOttie's Velox Pro car mount, and Nomad's MagSafe wireless charger.

The 3-in-1 charger has a compact T-shaped design that won't take much space on a nightstand or desk, with a charging tree suspending two devices midair: it charges an iPhone at the fastest 15-watt speed Apple allows, fast-charges the Apple Watch Series 7/8/9/Ultra, and uses the base for AirPods Pro or AirPods with a wireless charging case, all from a single outlet; normally around $150, it was discounted to $100 in August, so Prime Day may not be the last chance to get it cheaper. The orb charger starts charging as soon as you stick an iPhone to it, and its back holds two USB-C ports, two USB-A ports, and three AC outlets — both cute and tidy, with a price that fluctuates often. Apple's dual USB-C port power adapter is essential if you tend to forget chargers on the go, recharging an iPhone and MacBook simultaneously and, at 35 watts, powerful enough for an iPad Pro or iPhone 15 Pro Max. The MagSafe Duo is an excellent travel companion for iPhone and Apple Watch owners, combining both chargers in a compact, foldable form factor that also charges AirPods with a wireless charging case and other Qi-certified devices. iOttie's Velox Pro Magnetic Wireless Cooling Charger suits dash or windshield mounting with an iPhone 12 or later: its suction cup attaches to a dashboard pad or windshield and proved secure in testing, and a telescopic arm with a ball joint delivers a wide range of movement, though charging maxes out at 7.5 watts — the USB-C cable can be removed once the phone is full — and a built-in fan helps keep temperature down when it's hot. Nomad's charger is one of WIRED's favorite MagSafe wireless chargers, delivering the maximum 15-watt speed but not meant to be held up while using the phone; it has no power adapter but includes a USB-C-to-USB-C cable, sits securely on a desk, and can charge an Apple Watch too.
