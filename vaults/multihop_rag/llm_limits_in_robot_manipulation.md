# LLM Limits In Robot Manipulation

The argument, made in TechCrunch's 2023-10-01 piece, is that large language models supply high-level cognitive intelligence to robots but leave the manipulation problem unsolved, so that is the missing piece worth filling in. There has been big progress with the advent of LLMs in imparting this high level of cognitive intelligence into robots: if you have a robot that picks up a thing, instead of having to specify an object you can tell it to pick up the can of Coke, or tell it to pick up the shiny object, or do the same thing in French.

The grounds are the tasks where those gains stop: if you want a robot to plug in a USB device or pick up a tissue, those models just don't work. The warrant is a division of labour between cognition and fine manipulation — the models are really useful, but they don't solve that part of the problem. The speaker adds a reason for optimism about the remaining piece: "the thing we're really excited about now is that we actually have a system and that the fundamentals are correct."
