# Joshua Vs Wilder 2024 Superfight Prospect

As of CBSSports.com's Dec. 23, 2023 preview, the prospect of Anthony Joshua and Deontay Wilder fighting in March 2024, in the first of a rumored two-fight deal, was described as incredibly exciting.

The 34-year-old Joshua, who lost his trio of titles to Oleksandr Usyk in 2021, had been linked to talks of a fight against the 38-year-old Wilder dating back to when both first became titleholders in 2015. That long build raised the knee-jerk question most boxing fans were thinking: after such a long wait toward this seemingly inevitable fight, why not just pair them together now?
