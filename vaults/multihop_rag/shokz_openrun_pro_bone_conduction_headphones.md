# Shokz OpenRun Pro Bone Conduction Headphones Deal Prices

The Shokz OpenRun Pro was $125 for both Prime Big Deal Days in October 2023 and Black Friday in November — $25 more than the lowest price Engadget has tracked, but $55 below its usual $180 street price.

These are bone conduction headphones: they hug or go around the side of the head and deliver sound by passing vibrations through the skull, which Engadget notes "is not as uncomfortable as it sounds". The main benefit is that this leaves the ear canals completely open, so a playlist or podcast can be enjoyed without being shut out from the outside world. The usual downside is that the sound is not as full as with a traditional pair, and they are harder to use in noisier areas — though Engadget Editor Jon Turi found this pair delivers more low-end depth than most of its peers. Battery life is up to 10 hours per charge and the fit is described as fairly comfy.

The price appears identically across three Engadget roundups: the Prime Day headphone list of 10 October 2023, the still-shoppable Prime Day list of 12 October 2023, and the Black Friday headphone list of 24 November 2023, which quotes "$125 $180 Save $55" and says they are worth a look "if you want a pair of headphones that don't totally shut you out from the outside world".
