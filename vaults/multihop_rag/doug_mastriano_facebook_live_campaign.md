# Doug Mastriano's Facebook Live Campaign

There have been Republican outliers who managed to build significant followings, but as of TechCrunch's 2023-10-02 report none had managed to turn their social engagement into votes the way Trump's Twitter account did in 2016. Pennsylvania Sen. Doug Mastriano is the example: he shunned traditional media during the race, instead relying on Facebook Live to build a far-right grassroots network that secured him the Republican nomination in the state's most recent gubernatorial race.

He still lost to Josh Shapiro, whose campaign focused on reaching young voters through collaborations with Gen Z for Change, visiting college campuses and posting updates on BeReal — a contrast between an online base that wins a primary and an outreach strategy that wins a general election.
