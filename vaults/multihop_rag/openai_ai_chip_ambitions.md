# OpenAI's AI Chip Ambitions

The company has suggested that it might be entering the AI chip market at some point in the future, in an effort to shore up the speed and performance of its API services.

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman had previously pointed to industry-wide GPU shortages for the service's spotty performance, and producing its own processors might mitigate those supply issues while potentially lowering the current four-cent-per-query cost of operating the chatbot to something more manageable.
