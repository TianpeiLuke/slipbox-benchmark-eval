# Marcus Semien's Big Hits In World Series Games 3 And 4

Marcus Semien drove in runs in both Game 3 and Game 4 of the 2023 World Series, with his Game 4 output — a two-run triple and a three-run home run — accounting for five runs batted in on Oct. 31, 2023.

In Game 3 on Oct. 30, at 8:41 p.m. in the top of the third, Semien came up clutch with a single to left-center. The Sporting News noted the star second baseman had been struggling in the postseason but delivered here, driving in Nathaniel Lowe to give the Rangers a 1-0 lead.

In Game 4 the following night he did far more damage. At 8:39 p.m. he hit a two-run triple just inside the foul line that took an eternity for Lourdes Gurriel Jr. to reach, scoring two to make it 3-0 Rangers. At 9:09 p.m. he blasted a three-run home run to put the Rangers up by double digits at 10-0, in what the blog called ugly, ugly, ugly for Arizona as a bullpen that had been so great in the postseason was getting bludgeoned. Later, at 10:34 p.m., Semien struck out to end an inning for the Rangers.
