# Early Fediverse Messiness As A Product Opportunity

The Verge argued that the rough edges of today's fediverse are not a problem with the protocols but an opportunity for better products.

The grounds are a candid list of what early users should expect while things stay messy: it might feel like you're seeing the same posts too many times, and like you see some posts that obviously weren't meant to be seen in the app or feed you're using; some particularly thirsty influencers are going to go hard on cross-posting tools that threaten to clutter up all your feeds everywhere and become totally unavoidable. The warrant converting those complaints into an opportunity is that each is a product problem someone can solve, and the incentive exists to solve it: there's plenty of money to be made in the fediverse, and plenty of space for new products to take off. The scope condition is that we're still in the very early days, and it's going to be messy for a while.
