# Gellar And Prinze's Strict Household Rules

Freddie Prinze Jr. said he and Sarah Michelle Gellar "are strict as hell" with their two children, and that Gellar has "100 rules" the kids know they cannot break, in a 2021 Us Weekly interview reported by FOX News Entertainment on 10 October 2023.

The "Buffy the Vampire Slayer" star and her husband have been married for more than 20 years and share two kids, aged 13 and 11 at the time of the 2021 interview. "Look, I don't believe in whooping kids. I've never whooped mine. My mom whooped me. They were, like, preventative whooping, so I know how to be strict," the "I Know What You Did Last Summer" star told the outlet. Of the rules he added: "They know they can't break [those rules]. Friendships come and go. I tell my kids all the time that family is forever. So, we have rules, and they've got to follow [them]."

Gellar told Us Weekly in 2020 that as parents their "expectations are a little higher than most": "I think [our children] know what the rules are. We have [family dinner] as many nights as we can. We have no phones at the table. We sit, we all have dinner together."
