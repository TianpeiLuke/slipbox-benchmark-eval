# Communicating Financial Limits With Family

If you are experiencing a lot of financial pressure this holiday season, Credit Karma's Courtney Alev recommends that you try to communicate your feelings to your loved ones rather than absorbing the strain silently.

Her reason is that unspoken strain curdles: "One of the hardest things is resentment can build up if you feel like you need to buy gifts for your family and you can barely make your student loan payments, so bring things up." The procedure has a deliberately low disclosure bar — sharing your financial struggles can be hard, and if you want to talk but do not want to share every detail of your finances, that is okay; what you ultimately want is for your loved ones to understand how you are feeling. Plauché, one of the borrowers profiled, only recently started sharing her money worries with her family: "I have been open with my family, just that I'm not going to be able to give gifts that year."
