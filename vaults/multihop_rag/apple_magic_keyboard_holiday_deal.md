# Apple Magic Keyboard Holiday Deal

Apple's Magic Keyboard is Engadget's pick for one of the best iPad accessories you can buy, and while it's a pretty pricey add-on, at $215 at Amazon for Black Friday 2023 it's a little easier to justify.

It magnetically attaches to the latest iPad Pros and keeps them "floating" above the keyboard, which also has a trackpad. Engadget found it comfortable for typing and likes that it acts as a cover when closed.
