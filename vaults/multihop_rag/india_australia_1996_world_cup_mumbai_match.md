# India vs Australia At The 1996 World Cup

Australia beat India by 16 runs in a match described as one of the most exciting played between the two sides. Australia batted first and posted a target of 259 runs in 50 overs, courtesy of a brilliant 126 runs off 135 balls by Mark Waugh, with Venkatesh Prasad and Venkatapathy Raju picking up two wickets each for India.

India batted well courtesy of Sachin Tendulkar, who made a brilliant 90 runs off 84 balls, along with Sanjay Manjrekar, who scored 62 runs off 91 balls, but India were bowled out within 242 runs in 48 overs as Damien Fleming took five wickets for Australia.
