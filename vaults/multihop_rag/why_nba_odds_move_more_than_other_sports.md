# Why Basketball Odds Fluctuate More Than Other Sports

Basketball odds are known to fluctuate more than those of other sports, and the factors driving those shifts range from having only a few impact players per team to the sheer frequency of games.

That structure creates an exploitable relationship for the bettor. By following a single team you can track its momentum swings, which players tend to get on hot or cold streaks, and how well the team plays at home versus on the road, then use that information to your advantage for both pre-game and live wagers. Similar categories of moneylines, spreads, totals and NBA prop bets should appear across both formats.

The NBA is also special because of the number of bets available for every game, ranging from pre-game to live betting and parlays: adding up the standard bets, game props, player props, in-game betting and more, there is a staggering volume of bets for every NBA game at online sportsbooks.
