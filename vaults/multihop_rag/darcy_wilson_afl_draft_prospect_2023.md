# Darcy Wilson AFL Draft Prospect 2023

Darcy Wilson, profiled in Kevin Sheehan's top 40 for the 2023 AFL Draft as reported by The Roar on Nov. 15, 2023, is an elusive and creative forward or wingman who covers the ground exceptionally well and has innate ability around goals. State: Victoria Country. State League Club: Murray Bushrangers. Community Club: Wangaratta Rovers. Date of Birth: 18/08/05. Height: 186cm.

He is still developing physically but had an outstanding 2023 season, averaging 25.4 disposals and kicking 16 goals for the Murray Bushrangers to win the team's best and fairest and earn selection in the Coates Talent League Team of the Year. He also featured among Victoria Country's best at the National Championships and produced a strong performance representing Australia as part of the AFL Academy in the clash against Port Adelaide's SANFL team, racking up 19 disposals and taking five marks. His running capacity was confirmed when he finished second in the 2km time trial at the national Draft Combine with a time of 5:52, and he also demonstrated his power by producing the best running vertical jump with a 98cm effort. Weapon: Elite running and ball-winning ability. AFL player comparison: Sam Walsh (Carlton).
