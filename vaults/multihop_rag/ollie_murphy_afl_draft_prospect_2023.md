# Ollie Murphy AFL Draft Prospect 2023

Ollie Murphy, profiled in Kevin Sheehan's top 40 for the 2023 AFL Draft as reported by The Roar on Nov. 15, 2023, is a lightly built tall defender who sets up the play with his neat left-foot kicking. State: Victoria Metro. State League Club: Sandringham Dragons. Community Club: East Brighton Vampires. Date of Birth: 16/09/05. Height: 200cm.

Although new to playing in defence that season, he adapted remarkably well, showing real composure with the ball in hand and strength overhead, and he is a capable decision maker on when to mark or spoil in one-on-one contests. He was outstanding for Victoria Metro in the National Championships and won his team's MVP award along with All Australian honours after averaging 11.2 disposals and 4.5 marks per match, capping off a great year by playing in the Sandringham Dragons premiership team in the Coates Talent League. Weapon: Height and intercept marking. AFL player comparison: Jake Lever (Melbourne).
