# Crunchyroll Anime Streaming Service

Polygon calls Crunchyroll the best streaming service for anime lovers or newcomers, with classics like JoJo's Bizarre Adventure appearing alongside modern behemoths such as Jujutsu Kaisen, Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba, and Spy x Family.

Beyond the back catalog, subscribers are privy to premieres shortly after their debut in Japan, such as the highly anticipated Attack on Titan finale. As a gift, Polygon lists a Crunchyroll Premium gift card at $80 for a year, at prices taken at time of publishing.
