# Boycott On The Selection Of Bairstow And Woakes

The brunt of Geoff Boycott's criticism of England's team composition at the 2023 World Cup was directed at two players — Jonny Bairstow, who had averaged just 25.2 in the tournament, and all-rounder Chris Woakes, whose nightmare campaign continued against Sri Lanka with figures of 0/30 from six overs and a fourth-ball duck.

On Bairstow, Boycott's grounds are fitness and form: "Bairstow has struggled since his horrific injury [breaking his leg 12 months ago]. I have said before I believe Jonny came back too soon and was short of fitness and match practice. In the recent five Ashes Tests his keeping at Lord's was poor with missed chances and he made only three significant batting scores out of nine innings. That shortage of runs has carried over to his patchy one day performances."

On Woakes the argument is about conditions rather than ability, and the blame falls on the selectors: "Chris Woakes is an excellent bowler of swing and seam on English type pitches. His record abroad is poor as he does not have the pace or guile for flat batting surfaces. It is not the lad's fault. He was selected but how could people picking the squad go against all the statistics."
