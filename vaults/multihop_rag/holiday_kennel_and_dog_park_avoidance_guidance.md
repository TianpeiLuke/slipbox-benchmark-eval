# Holiday Kennel and Dog Park Avoidance Guidance

Experts recommend that dog owners keep their pets out of kennels and other areas, such as dog parks, where infection might be more likely because of crowded conditions and close contact. The advice is given even though a record number of Americans are expected to travel during the holiday season.

The reason for the step is density of animals. "When you congregate a bunch of animals together, there's a larger likelihood of getting an infectious disease from other dogs," said Dr. Kurt Williams, director of the Oregon Veterinary Diagnostic Lab at the Carlson College of Veterinary Medicine at Oregon State University.

The avoidance rule is not absolute. Still, Williams said, there are some facilities where you are less likely to have a problem because of how they are designed, so facility design is the stated exception to a blanket ban.

Alongside avoidance, Williams gives two standing steps. "I've been telling people to work closely with their vet," he said. "And to make sure their dog has all the vaccines available, in particular those for respiratory diseases we are familiar with." The vaccination step is scoped to the familiar respiratory diseases, not to the unidentified illness itself.
