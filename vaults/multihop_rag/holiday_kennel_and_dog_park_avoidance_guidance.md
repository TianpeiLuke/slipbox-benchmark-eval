# Keep Dogs Out Of Kennels And Dog Parks Over The Holidays

Experts recommend that dog owners keep their pets out of kennels and other areas, such as dog parks, where infection might be more likely because of crowded conditions and close contact. The advice is issued for the holiday season, when a record number of Americans are expected to travel — precisely the period when owners are most likely to board a dog.

The reasoning behind the step is density of animals. "When you congregate a bunch of animals together, there's a larger likelihood of getting an infectious disease from other dogs," said Dr. Kurt Williams, director of the Oregon Veterinary Diagnostic Lab at the Carlson College of Veterinary Medicine at Oregon State University.

The avoidance rule is not absolute. Williams said there are some facilities where you are less likely to have a problem because of how they are designed, so facility design modifies the risk rather than crowding alone determining it.

Two further steps accompany the avoidance. "I've been telling people to work closely with their vet," Williams said. "And to make sure their dog has all the vaccines available, in particular those for respiratory diseases we are familiar with." The vaccination step is explicitly scoped to respiratory diseases that are already familiar — it is not offered as protection against the unfamiliar illness itself.
