# BG3 Rogue: The Stealth Class

Polygon names Rogue the best class for stealth in Baldur's Gate 3. Rogues are Dexterity-based, sneaky fighters who only get Light Armor, so they have to focus on staying unseen and quick.

Rogue weapons tend to be simple, and favour the Finesse type — meaning you can add your Dexterity modifier instead of your Strength. All Rogues can use Dash for double movement, Disengage to avoid provoking Opportunity Attacks, and Hide, all as Bonus Actions. They also get Sneak Attack combat actions that deal extra damage whenever they have Advantage. At level 3 a Rogue chooses from the Thief, Arcane Trickster, and Assassin subclasses: Thief Rogues get an extra bonus action, Arcane Tricksters mix in magic, and Assassins get advantages over surprised or unprepared enemies. Thief is the best subclass for a Rogue, with the reasoning and the skill and feat picks left to Polygon's separate Rogue build guide.
