# Michigan's 2023 Team Culture And Expectations

Sporting News argued that the 2023 Wolverines were the most mature player-led Michigan football team on the field since 1997 — the year the program beat Ohio State 20-14 in a top-five showdown and split the national championship with Nebraska — and that the win over Ohio State showed a team that no longer merely hopes to win The Game but expects to. The grounds were the season's two parallel stories: an off-field one in which the team kept overcoming obstacles, self-inflicted and otherwise, and an on-field one described as a withering march through the Big Ten toward a third straight College Football Playoff berth.

The obstacle was Jim Harbaugh's three-game suspension from the Big Ten over the illegal in-person scouting and sign-stealing operation, which dominated headlines more than the Wolverines dominated opponents in the second half of the season. Staffer Connor Stalions and assistant coach Chris Partridge were gone, and Harbaugh was suspended; he addressed the team on Friday, and McCarthy listened closely. "The one thing he said that stuck out, the whole mantra, 'The Team, The Team, The Team,'" McCarthy said. "He said we are that team."

The warrant is the pattern of who blinks in high-magnitude games. Ohio State had not lost three in a row to Michigan since 1995-97, and since 1997 the Buckeyes had found a way in these games, with 2006 and 2016 the best examples; on a stage somewhere in between those classics, the Wolverines pointed the pressure the other way. Marvin Harrison Jr. had five catches for 118 yards and a touchdown, but Michigan then ran seven minutes off the clock and added a field goal — a further point of criticism for Ryan Day and defensive coordinator Jim Knowles.

The players' own framing matched. Just as the year before, McCarthy and Corum took turns repeating the famous Kobe Bryant mantra in the postgame press conference: "The job is not finished," McCarthy said. "We've been here before." Corum added, "We've been going through things all year, so we know how to stick together and play for each other and always have our brother's back." The previous two years had ended with Big Ten championship game victories followed by losses to Georgia and TCU in the CFP semifinals, and the story would now continue at least into the Big Ten championship game against No. 17 Iowa. The NCAA investigation would continue too, but Sporting News concluded that the Wolverines would play out the rest of the season on their terms — and that Harbaugh returned Monday.
