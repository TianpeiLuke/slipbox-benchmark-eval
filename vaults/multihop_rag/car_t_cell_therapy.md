# CAR-T Cell Therapy

CAR-T therapy is another route of targeting cancer cells, defined by a sequence of steps performed on the patient's own cells: T-cells are isolated and collected from blood drawn from the patient; chimeric antigen receptors, which can affect cancer cells, are induced in these T-cells by infecting them with a nullified virus engineered to generate CARs; such cells are cultured to multiply and then reinfused into the patient's bloodstream. The targeting is marker-specific, so that leukemia and lymphoma cancers, which have CD-19 markers, are targeted by the CAR-T cells, which then destroy the cancer cells.

The segment has also witnessed significant M&A, with Celgene acquiring Juno Therapeutics for $9 billion and Gilead acquiring Kite Pharma for $12 billion. The boundary case these approaches speak to is that novel routes like this may address the shortcomings in hematological, that is blood, cancers, which have not seen as much success as solid cancers.
