# Britney Spears' Unconfirmed Romances With Wade Robson And Colin Farrell

Two of the romances attributed to Britney Spears in the early 2000s were never confirmed by either party. Shortly after her breakup from Justin Timberlake, Spears was rumoured to be dating Wade Robson, a dancer and choreographer who appeared in the music video for her 2009 song "(You Drive Me) Crazy"; neither Spears nor Robson confirmed the rumours.

In 2003, Spears and Colin Farrell made headlines when they were spotted holding hands at the premiere of The Recruit, sparking speculation that they were dating. Farrell explicitly denied it in an interview with Entertainment Tonight at the time, saying the two were just friends: "She's just a mate. Seriously, we just met a week ago, [we're] having a laugh." The quote was reported by People in 2003.
