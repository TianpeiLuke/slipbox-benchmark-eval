# Echo Frames Lens, Frame Options And Pricing Tiers

With the third-gen Echo Frames you can choose between clear, prescription, blue light filtering, and sunglass lenses, in multiple shapes including round, cat eye, square, rectangle, and "modern" rectangle — starting at $269.99 for the most basic clear lenses, $299.99 for blue light filtering lenses, and $329.99 for sunglasses.

For a flashier style, Amazon is also continuing its partnership with Carrera, and that extra pizzazz comes with a heftier price tag: the Cruiser (the review unit) and the Sprinter model both cost $389.99, the Sprinter being a more boxy, Wayfarer-esque frame available with sunglass or blue light filtering lenses. The Verge argued the expanded range matters because style is one of the most underrated criteria for smart glasses — you're just not going to wear something that makes you look like a dweeb — and because everyone's face and vision is different, so a successful pair accounts for that by offering a wide range of styles, colors, and fits.
