# Week 14 PPR Wide Receivers To Downgrade

Sporting News argued on Dec. 7, 2023 that it was tough to trust Calvin Ridley (@ Browns), Drake London (vs. Buccaneers), Jordan Addison (@ Raiders) and Jayden Reed (@ Giants) in Week 14 PPR lineups.

The premise it attacks is that recent production or nominal role guarantees a start. Although Ridley had produced of late, a brutal matchup against a Browns coverage unit that entered Week 13 allowing the second-fewest fantasy points per game to wide receivers raised a red flag, compounded by the loss of Trevor Lawrence (ankle). London's week-to-week output was way too unpredictable, and with the fantasy season on the line for some managers there was no reason to roll him out.

Addison was likely to take a step back with Jefferson back in the fold, while the Packers' receiving situation was too dispersed to put faith into Reed in PPR formats — though Sporting News allowed that he might get a boost with Christian Watson (hamstring) banged up.
