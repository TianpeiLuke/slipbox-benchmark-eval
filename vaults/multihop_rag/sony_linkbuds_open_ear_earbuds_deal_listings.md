# Sony LinkBuds Open-Ear Earbuds Deal Listings

Sony's standard LinkBuds appear in The Verge's December roundup at around $138 from Amazon, Walmart, Best Buy, and Sony directly, against a normal $179.99, and in WIRED's Prime Day roundup (8/10, WIRED Recommends).

The Verge calls them a clear departure from conventional earbuds, using an "open-style" donut-shaped design instead of typical silicone ear tips, which allows better spatial awareness and, for some, a more comfortable fit; they provide crisp sound quality and excellent voice call performance, though as a result of that design they aren't very practical in louder environments. Possibly their coolest feature is the controls, which let you tap the side of your head rather than the earbud itself — "certainly very Sony." WIRED likes them for the same open-ear design that lets you hear what's going on around you, calling them a great option if you do a lot of city walking or use earbuds somewhere it's important to be aware of your surroundings, "like while feeding lions at the zoo."
