# NFL Week 11 Power Rankings, 25-32

At the bottom of Sporting News' Week 11 rankings on Nov. 13, 2023, the Green Bay Packers were 25th at 3-6 (previous week: 25), showing improved work from Jordan Love and his young weapons spreading the ball well in the passing game, but with run-defense woes and key holes in coverage that had all but doomed their playoff hopes. The Los Angeles Rams were 26th at 3-6 (26), hoping to find answers during a bye as Sean McVay's good early-season coaching job went by the wayside with key offensive injuries, needing Matthew Stafford to save face or the season would look a lot like 2022. The Tennessee Titans were 27th at 3-6 (27), with Will Levis going through rookie lumps after a dazzling debut as brutal offensive line woes exacerbated by injuries got to him and the whole offense — though the Titans still needed to keep starting him, since they could not overcome those systemic problems or those in overall pass defense.

The Chicago Bears were 28th at 3-7 (29): they do not care how they win, and should have been pleased their running game and defense bailed out a limited passing game with Tyson Bagent to secure another Week 10 win, hoping to finish strong with Justin Fields soon. The Arizona Cardinals jumped to 29th at 2-8 (32) after an immediate offensive spark with Kyler Murray and James Conner back, looking invigorated for Jonathan Gannon on defense and special teams and likely to finish strong and play a lot of spoiler. The New England Patriots were 30th at 2-8 (28), having tried to win by hiding Mac Jones and their quarterbacks in general and nearly pulling it off with a dominant running game and short-handed defense making plays, but without results in Germany ahead of a tough bye for Bill Belichick.

The New York Giants were 31st at 2-8 (30) and could flush away the season without Daniel Jones and Tyrod Taylor, with early and relentless injuries to the offense and a defense now falling apart too — the question being whether Brian Daboll would get a massive mulligan after his rookie playoff leadership. The Carolina Panthers were last at 1-8 (31), finding better defense and special teams to stay in the game in Chicago, but with Bryce Young still unable to unlock a limited passing game and an offensive line and running game that had not helped nearly enough.
