# Ja'Marr Chase's Week 10 Injury Status

Ja'Marr Chase (back) was officially ACTIVE for the Bengals' Week 10 home game against the Texans, after taking his status down to kickoff. ESPN's Jeremy Fowler reported late Saturday that Cincinnati was "hopeful" Chase would play but would not know for sure until after a pre-game workout, and The Athletic's Dianna Russini had reported that the star receiver would work out on Sunday morning with his status determined afterward. Chase had scared the fantasy community on Wednesday by being listed as a DNP, but back-to-back limited practices brought much more optimism, and with Tee Higgins (hamstring) already ruled out Cincinnati was hoping he could play for the 1 p.m. ET kickoff.

The wider team picture was rough. Despite a win over the Bills the previous Sunday, the Bengals were a beaten-down bunch, and their defense would also be without prominent edge rusher Sam Hubbard, complicating matters for a team hoping to stack wins while competing for an AFC North crown. On the bright side, Joe Burrow appeared finally healthy and should have no problems targeting Tyler Boyd, Trenton Irwin and Andrei Iosivas.

The fantasy conclusion followed the two branches: if Chase suited up, owners should enjoy an even larger workload than he already had; if he sat, Boyd was a must-start and one of Cincinnati's secondary receivers was worth a try in deep leagues.
