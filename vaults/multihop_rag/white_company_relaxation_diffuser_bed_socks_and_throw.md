# The White Company's Relaxation Picks: Diffuser, Bed Socks And Throw

The relaxation strand of The White Company's Christmas guide is premised on not rushing through the season — finding time for a long bath, a Sunday lie-in, or a glass of wine as the log fire crackles. The Motion Sensor Plug In Electronic Diffuser is described as a gadget that delivers the ideal amount of relaxing scent at the touch of a button: add a favourite scent oil and the motion-activated sensor helps control when it is released, providing a tranquil atmosphere in whatever room it is used.

For putting your feet up, the guide picks Cashmere Bed Socks, available in a range of colours and made from pure GCS-certified yarns to take the chill off — and, since everyone loves cosy socks at Christmas, recommended for gifting too. For more warmth it suggests a Faux Fur Throw made from soft and silky recycled fibres, with a matching Soft Fur Hot Water Bottle, on the grounds that together they give so much cuddle comfort on chilly winter evenings that you won't mind how long spring takes to come around.
