# Youth Depression And Suicide Screening Rates Since COVID

In the years since the COVID pandemic began, children and teens are experiencing depression and anxiety more than ever before, according to Schalley.

She anchored the claim in her own hospital's screening data: "Here at Children's, our patients ages 11 and older are screening at risk of depression and/or suicide at nearly 30%," she said.
