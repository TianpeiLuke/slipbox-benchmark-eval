# VTuber Graduations and Agency Branch Shutdowns

"Seeing agencies like Hololive and Nijisanji shut down entire branches or graduate talents without any real kind of information is heartbreaking for just about everyone involved in the process," the anonymous Nijisanji source told Polygon for its 2023-10-19 piece.

The record bears the pattern out. In 2020 Hololive shuttered its Chinese division, graduating more than a handful of streamers. More recently Nijisanji announced the abrupt graduation of one of its more prominent talents, Mysta Rias, who revealed that his choice to leave was due to burnout. And in August, Hololive's parent company Cover Corporation announced that two talents would be graduating from its Holostars division barely a year after debuting, with no statements from the graduating talents provided outside the official announcement.

In some cases VTubers are not given a choice and can be graduated prematurely, though the agency itself never specifies.
