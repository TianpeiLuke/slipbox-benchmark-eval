# Bears 12, Vikings 10 On Monday Night Football

Chicago beat Minnesota 12-10 on Monday Night Football on Nov. 27, 2023, by quarters of Bears 0-3-3-6 and Vikings 0-3-0-7, ending what Sporting News called the honeymoon period for Joshua Dobbs in Minnesota. The folk-hero quarterback threw four interceptions, leading a sluggish offense that did not find its footing until the fourth quarter in a game Minnesota would not have needed many points to win.

The Bears' defense swarmed Dobbs all night, sacking him only twice but generating a great deal of pressure as the defensive backs made plays. Despite the barrage of turnovers, Chicago largely failed to capitalize, scoring only three points off the four Dobbs interceptions.

Chicago's own first turnover — a deflating fumble by Justin Fields when the Bears had a chance to extend their lead to two possessions — was quickly turned into a Vikings touchdown. That score looked like it might be the difference, particularly after Fields fumbled away the ball again on the next possession, but the Bears' defense put the ball back in his hands in the final minutes and Fields delivered. Facing 3rd & 10 outside field goal range, he hit an open D.J. Moore for a 36-yard gain; from there Chicago only had to kneel and let Cairo Santos, who drilled the 30-yard attempt with 10 seconds remaining, seal it.
