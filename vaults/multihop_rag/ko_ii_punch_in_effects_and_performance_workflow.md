# K.O. II Punch-In Effects And Performance Workflow

On top of the master effects, the K.O. II offers 12 "punch in" effects applied by holding down the FX key and then pressing any of the 12 black pads, each marked with its effect name (Level, Pitch and so on).

The punch-in effects express themselves differently based on how much pressure you apply, making the experience very expressive. The same keys correspond to modulation tools when used with the fader: FX+7 adds the "Level" punch-in effect, which is rhythmic gating, while Fader+7 assigns gain/level to the fader until you choose another modulator such as Attack or Low Pass Filter. Engadget's reviewer also found — by accident, though it is in the manual — that you can solo groups by pressing the FX button and the corresponding group, and can press multiple buttons to solo multiple groups or sounds at the same time; with a group or groups soloed you can then apply punch-in effects to create a lot of variations in real time. The reviewer read all of this as intent: the performative nature that is a burden for songs is a feature here, and with so many touches like this they began to assume Teenage Engineering envisioned the K.O. II as a playful performative device rather than a linear song-making machine.
