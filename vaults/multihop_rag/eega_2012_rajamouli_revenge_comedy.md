# Eega (2012): S.S. Rajamouli's Slapstick Revenge Comedy

Eega is a 2012 film directed by S.S. Rajamouli, running 2h 14m, starring Sudeepa, Nani and Samantha, which Polygon calls one of the very best movies on Netflix. It is a whirlwind slapstick comedy revenge thriller from the director of RRR and the Baahubali movies, and its logline is the test of whether it is for you: a man is murdered by a romantic rival and reincarnated as a fly, then teams up with the woman he loves to exact revenge on his killer.

Polygon describes it as joyously fun and absolutely bonkers — complimentary — with exciting action sequences, groundbreaking visual effects and plenty of laugh-out-loud jokes. Its key formal constraint is that the fly is completely silent, which pushes Rajamouli to employ classic tricks from silent cinema for laughs and gasps alike, much to the film's benefit. Eega is available to stream on Netflix.
