# The White Company's Nightwear And Pre-Lit Christmas Tree

This part of the guide is organised around Christmas rituals — traditions handed down through generations such as opening the sloe gin after the last day of work or a ceremonial placing of the star on top of the tree — and suggests starting a new one, such as a designated "morning in bed" or an "afternoon pyjama party," so that Christmas is done your way.

For those lazy days the guide picks the Jersey Piped Printed Classic Pyjama Set, whose soft jersey-blend fabric invites curling up in bed or on the sofa, with both the collared pyjama shirt and the trousers printed with tiny stars for a subtle sparkle. For the ritual where the tree takes centre stage, it picks the Pre Lit Grand Spruce Christmas Tree: 7.5ft tall, with high-quality faux foliage in an impressively natural-looking shade of green, built-in lights offering various settings for different lighting effects, and quick, easy assembly and takedown that leaves more time for the festive rituals themselves.
