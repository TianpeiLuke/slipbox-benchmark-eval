# The Good Fight (Paramount Plus)

The Good Fight, created by Robert and Michelle King and starring Christine Baranski, Audra McDonald, and a rotating cast of favorite TV actors, is Polygon's Paramount Plus pick in its 2023-10-25 roundup of the best new TV series on every streaming service, and critic Joshua Rivera calls it the Star Trek of legal dramas — always different, full of ideas, and gloriously messy.

Rivera frames it against a broken promise: one of the initial promises of streaming television was that it would bring shows that were not possible on more traditional broadcast or cable networks, but in practice this has not amounted to much more than bloated episode length and the occasional one-off experiment, like Arrested Development season 4 or Netflix's choose-your-own heist show Kaleidoscope. The Good Fight was different.

The show opened its first season in 2017 with a very clear mission statement: Diane Lockhart, played by Christine Baranski and carried over from series predecessor The Good Wife, prepares to leave her cushy job as a law firm partner to become a fashionable expat in Italy after the election of Donald Trump — until she loses her nest egg to a Ponzi scheme and is forced back to work, not at her old majority-white firm but at Chicago's largest Black law firm.

With that setup the show endeavored to tell the story of Trump-era America as it was written and, while it had its blind spots, often did a better job than most cable news networks, telling unflinching stories about race in America, right-wing internet trolls, and the outrage economy that drives culture wars. It was also one of the most inventive shows on television — any given episode could have animated musical segments explaining geopolitics, stories about a secret court that Mandy Patinkin runs like a game show, or a cutaway gag ruminating on whether Jeffrey Epstein had his penis frozen for preservation.
