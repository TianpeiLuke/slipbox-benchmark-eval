# The Early Gigs Reich And Mulligan Came Up Through

Sam Reich and Brennan Lee Mulligan traded stories about the jobs they did before Dropout. Reich recalls watching himself performing at the New York Renaissance faire, and is emphatic about the standard there: "those are a bunch of incredibly fierce and talented performers... Incredibly talented, comedic improvisers, so good at thinking on their feet. Those long days, on your feet all day."

Mulligan's example of what those days led to: "Just hoping to get a callback — hoping to get a callback for the Orbit gum activation in Times Square, where me and 20 other improvisers will be paid $100 to receive 10 hours of abuse from real strangers on the street." Reich countered with "a month of background work on Third Watch, which was like the worst Law & Order," to which Mulligan replied: "I was a camera PA on Criminal Intent, which was the actual worst Law & Order! And it wasn't even during the Vincent D'Onofrio episodes." Reich: "Oh my god. Didn't even have the pleasure of watching Vincent work."
