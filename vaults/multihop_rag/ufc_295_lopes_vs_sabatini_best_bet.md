# UFC 295 Best Bet: Pat Sabatini's Wrestling Over Diego Lopes

CBS Sports' best bet for Diego Lopes versus Pat Sabatini at UFC 295 is the Pat Sabatini moneyline at -115, chosen despite the writer calling it a sticky fight. The grounds: according to the odds, both men are most likely to win by submission, which makes sense given that both have 12 submission victories on their resume. Where it gets tricky is that neither man has a submission loss, so both have been dominant in applying their grappling.

The discriminating factors are stylistic. Lopes is a bit more dangerous on his feet, but Sabatini has the better wrestling and can probably impose his will to force the fight to the ground; the open question is whether either man can trap the other in a submission once Sabatini puts the fight on the floor. The odds suggest the fight won't go the distance at -190, but the writer is not so sure this doesn't become a three-round grind on the ground — hence taking the slight favorite to score the win on the strength of his wrestling rather than betting a finish.
