# USC's Supporting Cast Around Bronny James

USC's supporting roster in 2023-24, as Sporting News described it on Dec. 9, 2023, was defined by a versatile small forward and a tall, deep frontcourt that would let Bronny James play at the guard spots.

Small forward Kobe Johnson, younger brother of Atlanta Hawks forward Jalen Johnson, was a great glue guy who could score, rebound and facilitate. The frontcourt was anchored by 6-foot-6 Washington State transfer DJ Rodman, 6-foot-11 senior Joshua Morgan, 6-foot-11 freshman Arrinten Page and 7-foot-1 sophomore x-factor Vincent Iwuchukwu, so James would have plenty of bodyguards and would presumably get minutes at the guard spots.

The boundary case in the group is Iwuchukwu, who had had his own health scare the previous season. He was one of the nation's top recruits and came off Andy Enfield's bench; his potential as a game-changer was well known, but he was striving for consistency. Sporting News argued that if he and James developed chemistry among the USC reserves, the team was going to be tough to handle over the course of 40 minutes on a nightly basis.
