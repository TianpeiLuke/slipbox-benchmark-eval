# The Springboks' Selection Philosophy: Playing For South Africa

Jacques Nienaber argued before the 2023 World Cup final that Springbok selection is not about individuals at all but about the country — "We explain to the whole squad why we are going a certain route. It is not about individual selection… It is for South Africa" — speaking shortly after announcing two changes to his starting side and five in total, having gone in with a forwards-heavy 7-1 bench split.

The warrant is that shared explanation removes ego from the equation and lets players accept decisions they might otherwise contest: "As selectors we know there will be pressure on us if we don't deliver a result but at the back of our minds we know this selection is for South Africa. The players take it like that. It is not about person or ego, not that they have egos, but you can't think of that. You have to think about South Africa and the Springboks."

The Roar's reading is that the Springboks are seemingly playing for more than themselves — they are playing for their country — and calls it a powerful force, one that saw them progress through the knockout stages to reach the final after consecutive one-point victories. Pressure, on this account, is relative: the South African laughs at the notion of what pressure is compared to the inequality and challenges the Rainbow Nation continues to experience.
