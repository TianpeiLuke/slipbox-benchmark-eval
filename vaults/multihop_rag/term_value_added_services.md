# Value-Added Services (VAS)

## Definition

Value-Added Services (VAS) is named in the corpus as the business area Guy Kamgaing spent ten years in before founding StarNews Mobile, described as "content monetization, particularly within the VAS (Value-Added Services) business."

The corpus's only illustration of that work is text/SMS-based campaigns monetizing content such as jokes and horoscopes in partnership with telcos.

The corpus does not itself define VAS as a general category; a broader definition, drawn from an external source, is given in Background (external) below.

## Context

The term surfaces in the corpus as the professional background that led StarNews Mobile founder and CEO Guy Kamgaing to launch the platform: he spent ten years in "content monetization, particularly within the VAS (Value-Added Services) business," running text/SMS-based campaigns before founding StarNews in 2017. That VAS experience is presented as the direct precursor to StarNews's model — Kamgaing describes having monetized "jokes and horoscopes via telcos for several years" before realizing the same telco relationships and monetization mechanics could be applied to creator content instead. The corpus does not itself define VAS beyond this SMS/text-campaign example, so the general definition below is drawn from external reference.

## Key Characteristics

- **Text/SMS-based campaigns** — the corpus's sole description of Kamgaing's VAS work is running text/SMS-based campaigns to monetize content, by his own account "jokes and horoscopes via telcos."
- **Ten-year background, later reused** — Kamgaing's VAS-era monetization techniques are described as the experience he drew on when he "realized" the same telco relationship could be applied to creator content, founding StarNews in 2017.
- **Depends on a telco relationship** — Kamgaing attributes his ability to monetize content to "a relationship with the telcos," the same kind of relationship StarNews later built with MTN and Orange.

## Background (external)

The corpus names VAS but does not define the category itself. Per Wikipedia, Value-Added Service is a telecommunications term for "all services beyond standard voice calls and fax transmissions" — non-core offerings that enhance a carrier's basic service and raise average revenue per user, delivered either directly by the network operator or through third-party content providers (Wikipedia, *Value-added service*, accessed 2026-09-02). Typical examples include ringtones and ringback tones, SMS-based premium content, mobile TV, location-based services, mobile advertising, and mobile money — the same SMS/text-campaign category Kamgaing describes working in (Wikipedia, *Value-added service*, accessed 2026-09-02).
