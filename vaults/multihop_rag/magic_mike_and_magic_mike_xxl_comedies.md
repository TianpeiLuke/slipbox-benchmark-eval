# Magic Mike (2012) And Magic Mike XXL (2015)

Polygon treats Magic Mike and Magic Mike XXL as a paired recommendation: Steven Soderbergh's Magic Mike (2012, 1h 50m) and Gregory Jacobs' Magic Mike XXL (2015, 1h 55m), both starring Channing Tatum, Matt Bomer and Joe Manganiello. The reviewer's summary of what they have in common is that they are two joyous celebrations of bodies in motion contained within working-class stories of trying to Make It Work in the face of a gruelling world, with Tatum, Manganiello and the rest of the ensemble soaring through unforgettable set-pieces.

The two are distinguished by what each does with the material. Soderbergh's Magic Mike is described as an excellent subversive rom-com that inverts many standard gender tropes of the genre. The follow-up is even more rapturously joyous, celebrating pleasure in its many forms — "like Hellraiser without the pain," in the reviewer's phrase — and doubles as both a road trip movie and a "the crew gets back together for one last job" movie, with Manganiello going all out in a minimart dancing to "I Want It That Way." Both are available to stream free with ads on Tubi, or for digital rental or purchase at Amazon, Apple and Google Play.
