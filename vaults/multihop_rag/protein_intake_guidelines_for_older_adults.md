# Protein Intake Guidelines For Older Adults

Dr Eamon Laird's guideline, reported by The Guardian on 10 October 2023, is that older adults should be taking at least 1 to 1.5g of protein per kilogram of body weight, drawn from dairy, fish or non-processed meat.

The stated purpose is that these foods "can help maintain muscle strength and function, reducing the risk of frailty as we age", and both Susan Saunders and Laird agree that the important thing is that older people consume plenty of protein.

The obstacle the article flags is compliance rather than the rule itself: while most of us can probably persuade our parents to take a multivitamin, suggesting they change their diet is more complicated — appetites fade as we age, chewing becomes more difficult, and those living alone do not always feel inclined to cook.
