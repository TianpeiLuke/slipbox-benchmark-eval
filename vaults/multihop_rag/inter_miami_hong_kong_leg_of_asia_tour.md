# Inter Miami's Hong Kong Leg Of The Asia Tour

Inter Miami will play a collection of top players from the Hong Kong First Division on February 4, 2024 at Hong Kong Stadium, which seats approximately 40,000 fans, as part of a tour of Asia in early February announced by the club in early December. The club will hold an open training session the day prior, with tickets going on sale December 15 on the ticket exchange site Klook.

While the match was the only one in Asia confirmed at the time, Inter Miami's reference to the trip as a "tour of Asia" suggested more matches could be announced in due time. Sporting News noted it was notable that Inter Miami would indeed travel to Asia after their attempts to play matches in China back in November fell through.

"We're very excited to be visiting Hong Kong and indeed Asia for the very first time," said Jorge Mas, Inter Miami CF Managing Owner. "From the very beginning we set out to be a global club at Inter Miami. This is a wonderful opportunity to do just that; we hope to enthuse and inspire new Inter Miami fans in Hong Kong and across Asia who share our passion for fútbol." Co-owner David Beckham added: "Hong Kong is a beautiful city with a great sports scene. Throughout my career, I have spent a lot of time in Asia — I am so pleased to have the opportunity to bring Inter Miami to play our first international tour match in this fantastic city."
