# Legal Sign Stealing Versus What The Astros Did

Sign stealing in baseball is legal so long as it is done without extra help — that is the boundary the Astros crossed, per Essentially Sports on 2023-10-22. Powers of observation are as strong a skill as pitching or hitting and they are perfectly legal in baseball: a batter can sometimes figure out from a pitcher's unconscious movements, or 'tells', what kind of ball is coming their way, and at any given moment non-verbal signs are flying around between a dugout and the field as a way for players to communicate with a manager or coach. As long as it is done without extra help, this sign stealing is in fact allowed. What Houston did was not.

The rules and countermeasures moved in response. Before the details of the scandal came out, MLB had handed out warnings here and there against sign stealing. After the fiasco, new restrictions in 2020 prevented the video replay from including the catcher's signs. In 2022, PitchCom devices debuted, allowing a pitcher to communicate with the catcher without signs, with a few other players on the field also able to wear one. The article leaves the effectiveness open: but will these tactics help?
