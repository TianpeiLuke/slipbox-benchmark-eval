# Beats Fit Pro Black Friday Deal

The Beats Fit Pro was back down to $159 for Black Friday 2023, which isn't an all-time low but is still roughly $20 below its average street price.

This is the "best for workouts" pick in Engadget's wireless earbuds buying guide, as it packs many of the iOS-friendly conveniences of Apple's AirPods in a sportier and more stable design; it also uses physical control buttons instead of touch panels, and its punchy sound plays well with most popular music. The limitations: it can't connect to multiple devices at once, it doesn't support wireless charging, and its ANC, while fine, isn't as capable as the AirPods Pro's. The Fit Pro earned a score of 87 in Engadget's review from 2021.
