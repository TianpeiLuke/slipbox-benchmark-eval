# Epoch Times Conservative Donor Funding

From 2020, The Epoch Times gained a new revenue stream: where Falun Gong practitioners had been a reliable source of small donations in previous years, the group began receiving gifts and grants from big-money conservative donors, reporting $8.4 million in revenue from contributions and grants across 2020 and 2021.

The shift came alongside a shift in allies and content. The Epoch Times' subscription page began hosting glowing testimonials from Steve Bannon, Glenn Beck and the far-right Arizona congressman Paul Gosar, and the organization became a reliable source for misinformation around Covid, its treatments and the vaccines.

The Epoch Times is not required to list its donors, but tax documents filed by scores of donors for that period, accessed through ProPublica's nonprofit explorer, show some of those funds came from conservative donors and foundations. In 2021 it received $55,750 from the National Christian Charitable Foundation, which connects anonymous donors with Christian causes, and $31,000 from Donors Trust, a fund for conservative and libertarian donors. Smaller donations came from individuals — real estate agents, investors and surgeons among them — and from small family foundations, most of which support right-wing causes including evangelical Christian groups, anti-vaccine groups and far-right media organizations. Most of the donors contacted by NBC News did not respond to interview requests, and two declined.
