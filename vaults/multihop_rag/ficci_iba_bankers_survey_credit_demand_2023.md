# FICCI-IBA Bankers Survey Credit Demand 2023

The seventeenth round of the FICCI-IBA survey was carried out for the period January to June 2023, with a total of 24 banks participating — public sector, private sector and foreign banks together representing about 79 per cent of the banking industry as classified by asset size.

Its backdrop was strong: the Indian economy posted an impressive 7.2 per cent growth in the financial year ended March 2023 and 7.8 per cent in the first quarter of the then-current fiscal year, while credit growth continued to rise, signalling robust demand conditions as well as banks' improved appetite towards retail borrowers. The health of the banking sector has witnessed an encouraging turnaround, marked by healthier bank balance sheets and a gross NPA ratio at a decade low.

The findings show long-term credit demand growing in sectors such as infrastructure, textiles, chemicals, food processing and metals, with iron and steel also seeing accelerated long-term loan disbursements in the past six months. Infrastructure is witnessing an increase in credit flow, with 67 per cent of respondents indicating an increase in long-term loans, against 57 per cent in the previous round. The outlook for non-food industry credit growth over the next six months is optimistic, with 42 per cent of participating banks expecting growth above 12 per cent, compared with 36 per cent in the previous round.
