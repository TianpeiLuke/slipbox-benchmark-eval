# Bills vs. Chargers Week 16 Player Props

Sporting News flagged two player props worth considering for the Bills-Chargers Week 16 game: Dalton Kincaid UNDER receptions and Gerald Everett OVER receiving yards.

Kincaid was a limited participant in practice on Wednesday due to a shoulder injury, but it had been reported he was likely to suit up on Saturday at less than 100%; the suggestion centred completely on the inference that Kincaid suits up but takes part in fewer packages and plays than usual, which with Buffalo likely to cruise was not a far-fetched chain of events to target. For Everett, the reasoning was that whether or not Easton Stick continued to play decently, the receiving-yards line should be attainable for the Chargers tight end: Everett had tallied 41, 39, 44 and 43 receiving yards in his last four outings, and the Bills were likely to score a lot, necessitating that Los Angeles pass a lot — so even without such a big spread it was a small ask to suggest Everett could net half of what he had averaged in the past month.
