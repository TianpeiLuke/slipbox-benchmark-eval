# Lourdes Gurriel Jr. Re-Signs With The Diamondbacks

Lourdes Gurriel Jr. re-signed with the Arizona Diamondbacks on a three-year, $42 million contract with a club option for a fourth year and an opt-out after his second season, as reported on Sunday, Dec. 17, 2023 by The Athletic's Ken Rosenthal, with the terms from ESPN's Jeff Passan.

The Diamondbacks had acquired Gurriel the previous offseason in a trade involving Daulton Varsho and Gabriel Moreno, and after he helped Arizona reach the World Series he was ready to come back.
