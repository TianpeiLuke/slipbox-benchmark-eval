# The Costs Of Relinquishing Independence After Acquisition

Against the short-term benefit of being bought by a larger company, Engadget argued that there are real downsides to relinquishing independence.

The grounds are the ways corporate ownership constrains a studio: having a corporate overseer can result in rigid production timelines that hinder a studio's ability to pivot, and despite all promises otherwise, developers may be forced to adhere to a specific tone, vibe or game-development structure. The warrant is a claim about distance and accountability — owned studios are held accountable by people outside the actual development of a game, and the bigger the company, the further away its bosses are from the creative process. The extreme negative outcomes for an acquired indie studio are layoffs and closures, and Engadget notes a lot of these were seen in 2023.
