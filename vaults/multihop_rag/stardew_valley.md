# Stardew Valley

Solo developer and composer Eric Barone created both a game and a phenomenon with Stardew Valley, which is inspired by the Harvest Moon series.

You inherit a farm from your grandfather and move in, eager to leave the corporate life behind; from there you farm, mine, fish, and make friends with the locals in Pelican Town. Polygon's description of its pull is that it is so easy to think "just one more day" and then lose hours, days, and even months to building out your farm, wooing a townsperson, and completing community bundles. The multiplayer is also excellent, since you can share a farm with up to three other players.
