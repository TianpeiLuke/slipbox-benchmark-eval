# Fortnite's Map As A Source Of Player Nostalgia

In Fortnite, the map is everything — that is the claim TechCrunch makes on 2023-10-30 in trying to read Epic's motive. The grounds are how players relate to it: a bad map can ruin a chapter, and players often wax nostalgic about in-game locales, usually alliterative hotspots with names like Tilted Towers or Loot Lake.

The argument stops short of a confident explanation, and says so: without a clear glimpse into what Fortnite's player base looks like these days, it's tough to know why Epic would decide to go retro and bring the game back to an older save state.
