# India FY24 Capex-Led Growth and Fiscal Position

In a challenging global environment, Indian real GDP growth — estimated at 6.2 per cent year-on-year in real terms in FY24 — is resilient, with high frequency indicators suggesting strong construction and infrastructure activity and soaring urban demand. Manufacturing production has been unusually strong in Q2FY24, especially in infrastructure-oriented structures such as cement and steel, and power supply grew vastly in Q2 after a tepid Q1, helped by a dry August 2023 and buzzing industrial activity.

Public investment is the engine. Both the Union and the states front-ended capex in H1FY24, with capital expenditure up 48 per cent year-on-year for the Union in that period, and reports suggesting capex of major states was also up 45 per cent year-on-year in 5MFY24. Carrying this capex forward into H2FY24 would be important to sustain the multiplier.

On the fiscal position, the deficit for 5MFY24 stood at 36% of the FY24 budget estimate, and gross direct tax revenue experienced a substantial increase of 18 per cent year-on-year up to 9 October, allaying concerns of fiscal slippage, the note said.
