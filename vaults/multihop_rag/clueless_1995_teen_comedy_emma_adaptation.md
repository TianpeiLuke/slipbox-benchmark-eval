# Clueless (1995): The Teen-Comedy Emma Adaptation

Clueless is Amy Heckerling's 1995 teen comedy, running 1h 37m, starring Alicia Silverstone, Brittany Murphy and Paul Rudd, and it is defined by what it adapts: it takes the general framework of Jane Austen's Emma — a rich, bored young woman who cannot stop getting involved in everyone's business — and transports the story from Regency-era England to 1990s Beverly Hills, where Emma becomes Cher, a chic, stylish and popular high school student who thinks she knows what is best for everyone.

Polygon places it against the other Emmas to make its case: Gwyneth Paltrow's pastel-swathed 1990s version makes Emma haughty yet lovable, Joanna Sotomura in the 2013 webseries Emma Approved turns the socialite into an ambitious if misguided lifestyle guru, and Anya Taylor-Joy's more recent rendition gives her a piercing mean streak worthy of the original — but the most memorable Emma, the reviewer argues, isn't an Emma at all, but a Cher. The Austen beats still play out: Cher takes an unpopular new student under her wing and tries to set her up with a match that will catapult her to social fame, all the matches end in catastrophic failure, and Cher goes too far and learns something about herself along the way — all rendered in bright, bold 1990s fashion and slang with iconic quotable lines. Clueless is available to stream on Paramount Plus or free with ads on Pluto TV.
