# Amazon Fire HD 8 And Fire Max 11 Tablets

Amazon's Fire HD 8 was down to $60 for the Prime Big Deal Days event — $5 more than the tablet's all-time low, but still $40 off its list price, applying to the 32GB model; the 64GB version was on sale for $75. Either way, the Fire HD 8 is likely the cheapest competent tablet for most people: it is slower than the Fire HD 10 and its display is limited to 1,280 x 800 resolution, but it is lightweight, can last more than 10 hours on a charge, and is still quick enough for basic streaming and reading. It is not the best value at its standard going rate, but for $60 it is hard to do better.

The Fire Max 11 is Amazon's largest and most performant tablet, with an 11-inch 2,000 x 1,200 resolution display, an aluminum frame, a respectable octa-core processor and 4GB of RAM. It was on sale for $150, matching its all-time low.

Its limits are the same as the rest of the line: the same software limitations and lock-screen ads as any other Fire slate, which makes it a tough sell at its usual $230. If you already use a bunch of Amazon services and do not want to shell out for an entry-level iPad, it is a big upgrade over the Fire HD 8 — just do not expect to get any real work done with it.
