# Hayes' Delayed Start And Her Chelsea Commitment

Emma Hayes will remain exclusively with Chelsea through the end of their WSL campaign and the Champions League season, and will not work with the U.S. in international windows, as reported by The New York Times on 15 November 2023.

She stated the position in her press conference on the Friday: "I'm here until the end. I haven't died, I haven't gone anywhere. I'm here, doing this job. My full focus and attention is on what I do for Chelsea."

The scheduling squeeze is spelled out: Hayes could be tied up with Chelsea as late as 25 May if the London club makes the Champions League final, and notably an international window that would theoretically be her first in charge begins just two days later, on 27 May.
