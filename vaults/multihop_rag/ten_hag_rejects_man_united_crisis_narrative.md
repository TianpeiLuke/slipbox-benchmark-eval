# Ten Hag Rejects The Crisis Framing Of Manchester United's Season

Asked about a potential crisis at Manchester United after the Chelsea win, Erik ten Hag rejected the premise outright: "Crisis? Not for us. We keep calm. We know where we have to build. We are going in the right direction. This team is capable to be successful," per Sporting News on Dec. 6, 2023.

The premise he attacks is that United's results amount to a crisis rather than a build. The immediate evidence he could point to is that United were successful that night thanks to McTominay; Ten Hag explained how his midfielder had managed to become more involved in an attacking sense that season: "He has a very good smell when to arrive and a very good finish."

He did not claim the performance was flawless, which qualifies the rebuttal rather than abandoning it: "It is not a frustration, in the end, we won but we could have made our lives much easier. We started very good, proactive, brave, we created a lot of chances in that moment, we could have gone up with two or three goals."
