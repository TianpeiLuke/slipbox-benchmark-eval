# Meta Quest 2 Black Friday Deal

The Meta Quest 2 VR headset was $50 off and down to $249 at several retailers for Black Friday 2023, matching the best price Engadget has seen; at GameStop and Target the device came with a $50 gift card.

Despite the launch of the impressive Quest 3, Engadget still considers the Quest 2 to be one of the best VR headsets available right now — precisely because of its more affordable price. It's still the best way to jump into VR without spending a ton of money, and it has the perks of being completely cordless and comfortable to wear for long sessions; the hardware includes fast-switching LCDs with a smooth 90Hz refresh rate, and it comes with Meta's motion controllers.
