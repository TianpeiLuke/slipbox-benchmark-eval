# Michigan State Hires Jonathan Smith Away From Oregon State

Michigan State hired Jonathan Smith as its next football coach, the school announcing the move on Saturday, as reported by CBS Sports on 2023-11-25. Smith, 44, had spent the past six seasons as coach at Oregon State, where he guided the Beavers to 18 wins over the past two seasons, including a 10-3 mark and a No. 17 finish in the AP Top 25 during a 2022 campaign that earned him Pac-12 Coach of the Year honors. He was among the initial pool of candidates linked to the Michigan State opening after the Spartans fired fourth-year coach Mel Tucker with cause midseason amid sexual harassment allegations, and he later emerged as the leading candidate to replace Tucker during the final week of the regular season — a week in which Oregon State suffered a 31-7 loss at rival Oregon.

Michigan State athletic director Alan Haller framed the hire around Smith's record: "Jonathan has a proven track record of success, building the Oregon State program from the ground up by implementing a plan resulting in sustained historic success for the Beavers. He's been a part of championship staffs, coached in the College Football Playoff, and understands what's required to be successful at the highest level, learning from some of the game's most successful coaches." Haller added that on the field Smith's teams are tough and physical yet innovative, that Oregon State that year had controlled the line of scrimmage on both sides of the ball while ranking among the nation's leaders in both rushing offense and rushing defense, and that Smith has shown the ability both to recruit talented student-athletes who fit his system and to develop and maximize players once they're in the program. Smith, a former quarterback for the Beavers from 1998-2001, exited Oregon State with a 34-35 overall mark as coach, including a 1-1 record in bowl games, as he joined the Big Ten coaching ranks. In a statement posted to social media he thanked the players, coaches and colleagues of the last six years, said he would cherish the memories and friendships, and expressed his deepest gratitude to the people of OSU and Corvallis, noting that when he first arrived as a freshman college student he had no idea the opportunities and relationships the community would provide for him and his family.
