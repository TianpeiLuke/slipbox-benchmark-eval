# India's 1983 World Cup Semi-Final Against England

India beat England by six wickets with 5.2 overs to spare in the semi-final at Manchester on 22 June 1983, storming into a World Cup final for the first time. India had lost in the first round of the 1975 and 1979 World Cups and were underdogs in the third World Cup, which was being held in England for the third consecutive time, but in that tournament India wronged many of their critics by reaching the semi-finals and playing the mighty hosts.

With the ball, skipper Kapil Dev struck three wickets while Roger Binny and Man-of-the-Match Mohinder Amarnath took two each as India restricted England to 213 all out in 60 overs. In reply Mohinder Amarnath played a useful innings of 46 and Yashpal Sharma scored a fine 61, while Sandeep Patil played a fiery innings of 51 not out in just 32 balls.
