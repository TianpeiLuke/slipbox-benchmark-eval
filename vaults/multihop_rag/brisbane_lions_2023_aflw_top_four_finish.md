# Brisbane Lions' 2023 AFLW Top-Four Finish

The Brisbane Lions finished the 2023 AFLW home-and-away season fourth, clinching a double chance by toppling defending premiers Melbourne by 25 points, 8.5 (53) to 4.4 (28), at Brighton Homes Arena and denying the Demons the minor premiership, The Age reported on 3 November 2023.

A year after Melbourne walked away with the 2022 premiership, the Lions avenged their upset grand final loss. They had been at risk of slipping from the top four after a 21-point loss to St Kilda the previous week, but the win secured fourth place and avoided a sudden-death elimination final, marking coach Craig Starcevich's 75th game in style; Melbourne ceded the minor premiership to Adelaide and dropped to second. Powerhouse duo Ally Anderson (22 disposals) and Jade Ellenger (22) went to work in the midfield while Dakota Davidson (two goals) led the charge forward, and captain Bre Koenen was superb in defence on Melbourne's leader Kate Hore, collecting a game-high 12 intercept possessions and limiting the prolific goalkicker to one goal. Brisbane got the game on their terms early with five repeat entries inside 50 in the opening 10 minutes; Davidson and Ellie Hampson drew first blood before Casey Sherriff kicked Melbourne's opener, then Tayla Harris, Tyla Hanks and Hore slotted a goal each in the second term to put the Demons ahead, before Melbourne conceded four unanswered goals from Taylor Smith, Sophie Conway (two) and Ruby Svarc while remaining goalless in the third. Olivia Purcell (14 contested possessions) and Hanks (11) showed their mettle, but the Demons were outclassed and sorely missing Paxy Paxman (hamstring); Melbourne could be without Maeve Chaplin in their finals campaign after the defender sustained a concussion injury in the third term.

Earlier in the season the Lions had ended Adelaide's unbeaten run with a three-point victory at Brighton Homes Arena in a top-four thriller, 5.12 (42) to 6.3 (39), in a spectacle worthy of being a final. Adelaide started fast, the Lions had the better of the second quarter with three goals to one, the Crows took back control in the third with three goals to none, and the fourth was a heated arm wrestle with scores tied four minutes from time; Bella Dawes kicked the only goal of the last term, but a series of behinds in the final minutes won the game, including one from Lily Postlethwaite that broke the stalemate. Had the Lions lost, they were at risk of falling out of the top four with second-season side Essendon chasing their spot. The two clubs have had intense battles in the competition's history, including two grand finals. Last season's best and fairest Ally Anderson said her side loved playing at its Springfield training ground, which it has made a fortress, and works well in hot conditions: "It was really hot. It was a grind… But yeah, it was a good finish. Every week that we play here, every time that we play here, we get better and better. So, it was just a matter of time before we finally got the first win and then it hopefully [will] open the floodgates a bit."
