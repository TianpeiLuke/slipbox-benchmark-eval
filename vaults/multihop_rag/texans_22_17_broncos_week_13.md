# Texans 22-17 Broncos, Week 13 2023

The Houston Texans held on to beat the Denver Broncos 22-17 in Week 13 of the 2023 season, sealed when Jimmie Ward intercepted Russell Wilson in the end zone with nine seconds left.

Nico Collins had a career-high 191 receiving yards and a fourth-quarter touchdown. Ward leapt in front of intended receiver Lucas Krull to secure Houston's fourth victory in five games and snap a five-game winning streak for Denver (6-6). Wilson was intercepted a season-high three times, all of them after halftime, with Derek Stingley Jr. taking the first two picks for the Texans. Rookie CJ Stroud threw for 274 yards for Houston (7-5).
