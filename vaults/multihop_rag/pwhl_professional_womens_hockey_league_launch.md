# The PWHL: A Professional Women's Hockey League Launch

The PWHL is the new women's professional hockey league Billie Jean King is part of an ownership group for, with teams in Boston, New York, Minnesota, Toronto, Ottawa and Montreal. Its defining purpose, in King's account, is to be a league where the very best players will play — the thing earlier attempts had not achieved. It took five years to build, plus all those years of the other leagues, everyone trying, and it began with a request: U.S. Olympic gold medalist Kendall Coyne asked King, "can you help us?"

What distinguishes it in practice is how the players are treated. King went to Toronto and gave an opening speech about trailblazers: "It was amazing because the families were crying, the players were crying, they said 'we've never been treated like this, it's amazing, we feel like pros for the first time.'" There were a lot of little kids there, and King's framing is generational — "Kids are going to have an amazing opportunity that the generations before them never had." All three of Canada's networks carried it: "It's a religion up there."
