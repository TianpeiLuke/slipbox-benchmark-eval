# Why Investors Can Continue To Accumulate NTPC

Business Line maintained a positive stance on NTPC on 11 November 2023 and advised investors to continue accumulating the stock, on account of growth plans in place along with strong revenue and earnings visibility due to the assured return model, plus a decent dividend yield of around 3 per cent. The grounds are the track record since the previous call and the reasons behind the re-rating. Since the accumulate rating on NTPC in bl.portfolio dated 13 August 2023, the stock has gained around 48 per cent, majorly on account of growth in earnings as well as a re-rating in the stock from a trailing P/E of around 10 times then to 12 times now.

The earnings evidence is that during FY23 the company's revenue increased by around 32 per cent, and while there was flattish growth in PAT during that period on account of an increase in fuel cost, PAT grew nearly 30 per cent year-on-year in H1FY24. NTPC is now trading at a one-year forward P/E of around 11 times, which is around 35 per cent higher than its historical five-year average P/E of around 8.2 times. The warrant that turns an already re-rated stock into a continued buy is that the re-rating happened mainly due to company-specific and industry-level structural positives, which justify the current premium to the historical average, and due to the company's aggressive capacity addition plans; the premium is therefore treated as earned rather than as stretched valuation.
