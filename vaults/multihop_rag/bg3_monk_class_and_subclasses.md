# BG3 Monk: The Speed Class

Polygon names Monk the best class for speed in Baldur's Gate 3. Monks are a melee class kind of like Fighters, but with a lot more pizzazz: their best Ability is Dexterity, so their combat is more martial arts than swordplay.

Monks do not get armor and have only a limited set of weapons available, but they make up for it with Unarmed Strikes that deal extra damage. They take a little more finesse to play than Fighters. Monk subclasses unlock at level 3 and comprise Way of the Four Elements, Way of the Open Hand, and Way of Shadow. Way of the Four Elements mixes spells into your Monk abilities, using renamed spells from the caster classes; Way of the Open Hand expands your attacks to include things like knocking down enemies or pushing them with extra melee attacks; Way of Shadow makes your Monk sneaky and stealthy, effectively a ninja subclass. Polygon's recommendation is stated as "Way of the Open Palm is the best subclass for a Monk," with reasoning and skill and feat picks left to its separate Monk build guide.
