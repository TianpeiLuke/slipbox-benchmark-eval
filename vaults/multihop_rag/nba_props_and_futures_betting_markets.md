# NBA Props And Futures: What They Cover And When To Bet Them

NBA player and team props are growing in popularity, and sportsbooks are providing more NBA props than ever, because it is much easier to focus on a single player and how he will perform than on the entire team. Player props might cover how many points a player scores or how many rebounds he pulls down, typically provided in an over/under format. Team props range from which team will score the first bucket to the total points scored in a quarter to which team will lead at halftime, with the selection often robust, especially when high-profile teams play.

Futures bets are bets on events that will happen in the future, and Rookie of the Year is only one of them: legal online sportsbooks carry a wide selection of NBA futures. They can usually be bet throughout the year, including well before the season starts and through the regular season, and the further away from the event, the more likely you are to find heavier odds in your favor.

Alongside futures, bettors can wager on many NBA markets during the regular season and postseason. Game lines — moneylines, point spreads and totals — are the most popular, but team and player props, parlays and more are also available. The NBA is the second-most popular odds market to bet on in the US, behind NFL betting odds, which means every online betting site always has a great selection of NBA odds.
