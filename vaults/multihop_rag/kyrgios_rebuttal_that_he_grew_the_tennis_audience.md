# Kyrgios' Rebuttal That He Grew The Tennis Audience

Nick Kyrgios rejected the premise that he owes his living to earlier generations by attacking its factual claim about who brings audiences and money into tennis: his counter is that he himself has been one of the few players to bring millions more fans to the sport and to make more money for everyone, and that he built his career off the court without the help of others.

Writing on X on 19 December 2023, Kyrgios replied: "Uhhh what? I've been arguably one of the most watched players outside the big three the last decade? Fans around the world and have brought so many more people to the sport. I've created my own stuff off the court," adding, "Try to not hide any more assets x". He also wrote: "Bro what. I've been one of the FEW PLAYERS that have brought million more fans to the sport and have made more money for everyone. NETFLIX…. Don't remember seeing Boris on there. Guy is ridiculous. I've made my career off the court without the help of the others."

Kyrgios then narrowed what he was and was not disputing, conceding the historical record while denying the inference drawn from it: "Not once have I taken 'slams' away from Becker, or any of these legends. Not once have I said I've impacted the game MORE. I've simply said that I've built my own brand and done it my way & brought fans. Apparently a slam finalist has no credibility. Night, done with this convo."
