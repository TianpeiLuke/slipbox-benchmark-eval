# Buffalo's Against-The-Spread Trends In 2023

Buffalo was 6-7-1 against the spread in 2023 going into Week 16, a streaky record that had swung from a 3-1 start to a six-game stretch without a single cover.

To say the team was streaky in this regard would be an understatement: the Bills went 3-1 ATS in their first four games, but that ride on the gravy train was interrupted by a brutal six-game stretch between Weeks 5 and 10 in which they failed to cover once. They appeared to have moved past their midseason ATS doldrums, riding a 3-0-1 streak into Week 16.
