# YouTube's "Dream Track" Voice Cloning Experiment

On November 16, 2023 YouTube unveiled "Dream Track," an experiment enabling creators to clone the vocals, via AI tech and with official consent, of well-known stars.

Charlie Puth's voice is on there. John Legend's voice is on there. Demi Lovato's voice is on there. In fact the voices of a number of well-known recording artists signed to Warner Music Group and Universal Music Group — or labels within each major — are on there. Something conspicuous is not: any endorsement whatsoever from the world's second-biggest recording company, Sony Music Entertainment, or its artists.
