# Psagot's Asset Growth Without Profit

Psagot's business has been growing but it is not managing to translate this into profits, because most of the incoming money went into money market funds — a product with negligible management fees and low profits.

The inflow figures are substantial: its mutual funds took in NIS 1.4 billion in October and NIS 7 billion in the year to the end of October, giving it NIS 43 billion under management. Yet Psagot posted a loss of NIS 27 million for the first half of 2023, largely attributed to its non-bank credit business, an activity begun before Zim acquired the company. About six weeks before the report, Psagot sold control of its main holding in that area, AppliCheck, to its founder Meny Guy, and now holds 20% of that company.
