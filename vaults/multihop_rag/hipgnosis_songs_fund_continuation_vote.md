# Hipgnosis Songs Fund Continuation Vote

A 'continuation vote' at Hipgnosis Songs Fund (HSF), the UK-listed music rights fund, is a vote of the firm's shareholders on whether to keep the entity going as a closed-ended investment entity — that is, as a publicly-traded bundle of music rights. It takes place at HSF's Annual General Meeting, and the 2023 vote looked likely to be held on Thursday 26 October, according to an announcement from the firm on 28 September 2023.

The 2023 vote was only "likely" rather than definite because it might still get bumped into November, and its outcome was unknown at the time of reporting, with a looming $440 million transaction that could potentially influence it. The vote itself was not a new event: it had been scheduled ever since HSF went public in 2018, five years after the float.

The changes HSF announced on 28 September invited the question of whether they amounted to concessions designed to keep investors happy and confident in long-term prospects — and voting to keep going. One such measure directly extends the mechanism: if shareholders vote at the next AGM to remain a publicly-traded fund, another continuation vote will be put to HSF shareholders at an Extraordinary General Meeting in January 2026, roughly two years later.
