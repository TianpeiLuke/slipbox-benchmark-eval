# Interceptions By Bullard And Daijahn Anthony

Both quarterbacks were intercepted in the closing minute of the first half of Georgia's Nov. 11, 2023 win over Ole Miss, which ended the second quarter at Ole Miss 14, Georgia 28.

At 8:34 p.m. Jaxson Dart missed his target and Javon Bullard grabbed his second pick in two games, giving the Dawgs the ball at the Rebels' 45-yard line with only a minute remaining in the second quarter. At 8:39 p.m. the tables turned: Carson Beck threw his own miscue, which deflected off receivers' hands for an interception by Daijahn Anthony — a pick going into the locker room.
