# Week 14 Fantasy Tight End Sleepers And Streamers

Sporting News' leading Week 14 2023 tight end sleeper was Taysom Hill (vs. Panthers), whose matchup against the league's second-worst run defense gave him a high ceiling regardless of format.

Hill can be tough to trust in full-point PPR leagues, but he had been more involved as a receiver that year, so he would get a few catches to go with the rushing stats, and with Derek Carr (concussion) banged up he might throw more that week too. Gerald Everett (vs. Broncos) and Isaiah Likely (vs. Rams) had matchups against bottom-six tight end defenses, while Cole Kmet (vs. Lions), Jake Ferguson (vs. Eagles) and Kyle Pitts (vs. Buccaneers) also had above-average matchups giving them higher floors and higher ceilings than usual. For those in deeper leagues, Tyler Conklin (vs. Texans) and Cade Otton (at Falcons) both faced defenses that entered the previous week in the top five for fantasy points per game allowed to tight ends; Brevin Jordan (at Jets) and Tanner Hudson (vs. Colts) also had highly favorable matchups and could work as deep-league PPR plays, though Jordan's outlook was entirely dependent on whether Dalton Schultz (hamstring) was healthy.
