# microSD Card Recommendations And Deals

The Samsung Pro Plus microSD card is Engadget's top pick in its best microSD card guide, because it was the most consistent of the ones tested, with some of the best overall speeds and all the important ratings a good card should have: U3, V30 and A2. It was on sale for $20 during October Prime Day and after, and it comes with a full-sized adapter in case you need to use it with a camera or connect it to your computer, making it a great pick for a tablet, smartphone or Nintendo Switch.

The Pro Plus bundle with a reader was also discounted: $23, down from $38, a $15 saving — though it often goes for $25. Engadget notes that this card had the fastest sequential write speeds and random performance of any card it tested, and the included USB reader helps it reach higher speeds on compatible devices.

Two other cards were called out. SanDisk's officially licensed microSDXC card is a good pick for extra Switch room, particularly when on sale, and a 128GB model was within a dollar of its all-time low at just $18, down from $31 — a $13 saving. And at Black Friday, the Samsung Evo Select microSD card in 512GB was $25, down from $35 — 29 percent off — offering extra storage for a Nintendo Switch, Steam Deck, or Asus ROG Ally at a good price point.
