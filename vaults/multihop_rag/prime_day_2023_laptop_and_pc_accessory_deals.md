# Prime Day 2023 Laptop And PC Accessory Deals

WIRED's Prime Big Deal Days computing picks span the 2023 MacBook Air, last year's 13-inch MacBook Pro, the Razer Blade 14, the Das Keyboard MacTigr, an AMD 16-core CPU, Plugable's USB-C Triple Display Docking Station, and the SanDisk Extreme Pro flash drive.

The 2023 MacBook Air (8/10, WIRED Recommends) is one of WIRED's favorite laptops of the year, with an excellent six-speaker sound system, a 1080p front-facing webcam, and a 10-core variant of the M2 processor from the previous year's model — blazingly fast for most normal work and able to handle some light video editing and other heavier tasks. The 13-inch MacBook Pro (7/10, WIRED Review) is last year's model with the M2 chip and Touch Bar, sharing the new MacBook Air's processor and offering no major hardware upgrades except a fan, which lets the processor run warmer and eke out more power over longer periods — useful for pro-level tasks like video editing if you can't pay the premium for Apple's bigger Pro models. The Razer Blade 14 earned an 8/10 WIRED Recommends award, with a gorgeous 16:10 display, plenty of USB ports, a full-size HDMI output, and an Nvidia RTX 3080 Ti (upgradable to the 40-series); it's the laptop WIRED reviewer Eric Ravenscraft used through Starfield's launch weekend. The Das Keyboard MacTigr, which may require clicking "See More Buying Options," pairs well with Apple's laptops and PCs thanks to a dedicated Mac layout, Cherry MX Red switches, a two-port USB-C hub, and a high-quality all-metal build, and it rarely goes on sale. AMD's 16-core behemoth, a top pick from WIRED's build-your-own-PC guide, is a killer CPU for high-end 4K or 144-Hz gaming but gets so hot it should never go into a PC without a liquid cooler like the Asus ROG Ryujin II. Plugable's dock supports up to three displays at once, via HDMI or DisplayPort each, plus six USB 3.0 ports — two front, four back — and gigabit Ethernet, with up to 60 watts of output to charge a laptop, so returning to a workstation with a MacBook takes one plug for an instant multi-monitor setup. The SanDisk Extreme Pro tops WIRED's Best USB Flash Drives guide, balancing speed, reliability, and price, with a sleek aluminum case, a keyring loop, a slider for the USB-A plug, two years of reliable regular use behind it, and a lifetime warranty.
