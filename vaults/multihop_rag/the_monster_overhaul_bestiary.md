# The Monster Overhaul

The Monster Overhaul, by Skerples, reimagines not just the monsters but the very notion of what a bestiary can and should be.

The book is divided into 20 categories, each containing 10 critters that hew to a theme, and the categories themselves are unusual: there is "Dragons," of course, but also "Summer" and "A Wizard Did It," with "Summer" monsters including the Froghemoth and Pyromancers. Some entries may sound similar to classic D&D monsters while others are entirely new. Tables abound to help build and flesh out encounters — "Summer" has a set of generic swamp hexes, other entries have lairs and dungeons, and there is an entire flowchart table for populating a megadungeon. Polygon's contributor argues that every page is designed to make the reader think about monsters, about how to make them feel new, recontextualise them, or simply subvert player expectations, and that like all great RPG supplements it not only offers answers but teaches the reader how to continue answering such questions long after the published tables and suggestions are exhausted — a monstrous achievement that should be on every GM's shelf.
