# St Kilda's 2023 AFLW Finals Near Miss

St Kilda came within 0.4 of a percentage point of a maiden AFLW finals appearance in 2023: Fremantle ended their four-game streak with a comfortable 24-point win in October, and although the Saints won their do-or-die final-round match, Sydney's victory over the Dockers sealed the last spot, The Age reported on 20 October and 3 November 2023.

The Fremantle defeat was comprehensive. The Dockers dominated from the start to win 6.9 (45) to 3.3 (21) at Fremantle Oval, flying out of the gates from the first siren and producing their highest-scoring quarter of the season with 26 points in the opening term — conversely the most the Saints had conceded in any quarter that season. Aine Tighe set the tone with a brilliant goal less than 30 seconds into the first quarter, followed by two majors from Gabby O'Sullivan and another by Angelique Stannett; Ella Friend slotted a much-needed goal for the visitors to make it 4.2 (26) to 1.3 (9) at quarter-time. With less than a minute till the long break, captain Hayley Miller (two goals) kicked the only major of the second quarter for a 26-point half-time lead, and despite second-half goals from Tyanna Smith and J'Noemi Anderson the Saints struggled to eat into the deficit. Dana East was everywhere for Fremantle, leading the game in disposals (19) and contested possessions (11), alongside Orlagh Lally (19 disposals, five clearances), while Jaimee Lambert was the shining light for the otherwise subdued Saints with a game-high seven clearances and 13 contested possessions. Having been on the verge of making their first finals appearance, St Kilda (4-4) could now miss the top eight even if they beat the fourth-placed Brisbane Lions and the ninth-placed Carlton; Fremantle (4-4) faced reigning premiers Melbourne next before finishing against 11th-placed Sydney.

In the do-or-die match that followed, Georgia Patrikios showed her class with 25 disposals and eight contested possessions in a powerhouse display, while Hannah Priest was a brick wall in defence with nine intercept possessions. Abbie McKay worked overtime to counter Patrikios in the midfield, racking up 28 disposals and 12 clearances alongside Mimi Hill (30 disposals), and defender Keeley Sherar (17) toiled against a potent Saints attack, but her side was outclassed across the park. It was not enough: Sydney prevailed in a fierce tug-of-war with the Dockers to seal their first AFLW finals appearance, coming from four points down at half-time in a low-scoring game, with a third-term goal from Rebecca Privitelli sparking a 5.10 (40) to 2.6 (18) win on the Sunday at Fremantle Oval.
