# Dream Scenario (Kristoffer Borgli)

Dream Scenario is Kristoffer Borgli's black comedy about viral fame, starring Nicolas Cage, Julianne Nicholson, and Michael Cera, ranked 23 on Polygon's best movies of 2023 list of 2023-10-07 and playing in theaters.

There is nothing special about Paul Matthews, played by Cage: he is a frumpy college professor in a small, cold town with a wife and daughter and students who take his biology class because it is required — and he has started to appear in the dreams of everyone in the world.

Critic JR writes that the film gives audiences plenty to ponder, with sudden turns both hilarious and deeply uncomfortable, all held together by a tremendous performance from Cage in his best role since 2021's stunner Pig. In Cage's hands, Paul Matthews' plight, while farcical, stays grounded in humanity and all the petty, cringeworthy foibles that come with it.
