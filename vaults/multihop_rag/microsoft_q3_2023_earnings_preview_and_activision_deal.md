# Microsoft Q3 2023 Earnings Preview and Activision Deal

Microsoft was set to report its Q4 performance after the closing bell on Tuesday 24 October 2023, joining Alphabet that day. Seeking Alpha's Quant Rating system turned bullish just a few days before the earnings, upgrading its view to Strong Buy from Hold, while Wall Street analysts maintained their optimism with a Strong Buy rating.

The deal context is stated with a caveat about timing: after more than a year of rigorous examination by antitrust investigators across the world, Microsoft had just completed its $69B acquisition of Activision Blizzard, the largest deal in video game history — but its benefits would not be reflected in the company's financial report.

Consensus EPS estimates were $2.65 and consensus revenue estimates $54.54B. On the earnings insight, Microsoft had outperformed EPS forecasts in 7 of the last 8 quarters, and revenue in 6 of those quarters. Also reporting that day were General Electric, Verizon Communications, Visa, Coca-Cola, 3M, General Motors, Raytheon Technologies, Teladoc Health, Dow, Snap, Texas Instruments, Halliburton, Corning, Novartis, Waste Management, Spotify Technology, Kimberly-Clark and more.
