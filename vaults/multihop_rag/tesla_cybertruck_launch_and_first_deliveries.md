# Tesla Cybertruck Launch And First Deliveries

The first Cybertruck deliveries occurred on November 30, 2023 at the Tesla Gigafactory in Austin, where about 10 high-profile customers — including Reddit co-founder Alexis Ohanian — took possession of the truck, ending a four-year wait since the vehicle's debut.

The boxy vehicle is Tesla's first new model since 2020, when it started delivering the Model Y, yet the Cybertruck's initial debut predates that moment: CEO Elon Musk showed off an early version of the pickup at a memorable 2019 event, when it accidentally smashed two windows while attempting to demonstrate the Cybertruck's durability.

Much remained unsettled at the point of delivery. It was still unclear whether the Cybertruck would kick off the vehicle's journey toward success or failure, and TechCrunch listed open questions it expected Tesla to address: how the Cybertruck compares to other Teslas, how it compares to electric pickups from competitors including Ford or GMC, and when Tesla will release the cheaper, single-motor Cybertruck variant it promised back in 2019.
