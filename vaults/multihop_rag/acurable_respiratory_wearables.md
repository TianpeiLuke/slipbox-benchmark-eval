# Acurable's Patient-Friendly Respiratory Wearables

Acurable is a medical device company that makes patient-friendly wearable devices for diagnosing and managing respiratory conditions at home; its Esther Rodriguez-Villegas was featured on Found during the week covered by TechCrunch's October 7, 2023 Week in Review.

Rodriguez-Villegas came to the company as a career-long academic and, by her own account in the segment, never intended to become a founder. What changed her mind was learning how difficult the medical devices available at the time made it to detect and treat diseases such as sleep apnea and epilepsy — the gap that Acurable's home-use wearables are built to close.
