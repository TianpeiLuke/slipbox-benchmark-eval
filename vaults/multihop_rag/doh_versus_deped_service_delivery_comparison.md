# DoH Versus DepEd As Service Delivery Systems

In a paper presented at the 5th Paderanga-Varela Memorial Lecture entitled "Alternate Service Delivery Systems: Health and Education", the authors looked at the Department of Health and the Department of Education as service delivery systems, delivering healthcare and education respectively, and concluded that the service delivery system of the DoH is far superior to that of the DepEd.

Two reasons are given for the conclusion. The first is that the DoH has devolved its hospitals to the local government units while the DepEd has not. The second is that the DoH has spun off its health financing into the Philippine Health Insurance Corp., PhilHealth, while the DepEd has not. From this comparison the article argues for the creation of the Philippine Education Development Fund, PhilEd.
