# Cincinnati Bengals: Joe Burrow's Durability And The Line

The New York Times argued on 16 October 2023 that Joe Burrow is looking healthier over the past two weeks, but worried he will wear down against the defenses on Cincinnati's remaining schedule.

The grounds are the calendar. The bye week comes at a great time for Burrow to get even healthier, but on the other side of it are San Francisco, Buffalo, Houston, Baltimore, Pittsburgh and Jacksonville, and Cincy still has a second game with the Steelers plus games against Kansas City and Cleveland. The warrant is that a quarterback recovering from injury faces cumulative rather than one-off risk across a run of strong defenses.

An executive located the underlying problem in protection rather than health: "You cannot always expect Joe Burrow to create magic with a bottom-half offensive line. The line is my concern all the way."
