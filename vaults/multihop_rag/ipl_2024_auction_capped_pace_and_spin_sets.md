# IPL 2024 Auction: The Capped Pace And Spin Sets

The capped pace set at the IPL 2024 auction produced several sales while the spinners' set that followed produced none at all. Pace results were: Alzarri Joseph to RCB for 11.50 Cr; Shivam Mavi to LSG for 6.4 Cr; Umesh Yadav to GT for Rs 5.8 Cr; Dilshan Madushanka to MI for Rs 4.6 Cr; Jaydev Unadkat to SRH for 1.6 Cr; Chetan Sakariya to KKR for Rs 50 L; with Lockie Ferguson and Josh Hazlewood unsold.

The sequence ran: at 3:15 PM IST Chetan Sakariya came up with KKR the only bidder at Rs 50 L, and Lockie Ferguson went unsold. At 3:17 PM IST Alzarri Joseph drew a bidding war between DC and CSK; with CSK pulling out, LSG joined at 4 Cr, RCB joined to breach the 10 Cr mark at 3:18 PM IST, and RCB took him for 11.50 Cr at 3:21 PM IST. The same minute, Umesh Yadav drew a bidding war between SRH and GT that breached 4 Cr, Delhi joined at 5.2 Cr, and Gujarat Titans finally landed him for Rs 5.8 Cr. At 3:28 PM IST Shivam Mavi drew a battle between RCB and LSG that breached 6 Cr, with LSG winning at Rs 6.4 Cr. At 3:48 PM IST Jaydev Unadkat drew a bidding war between DC and SRH, which SRH won at 1.6 Cr, and Josh Hazlewood remained unsold. At 3:52 PM IST Dilshan Madushanka drew a bidding war between LSG and MI.

The spinners' set came up at 3:55 PM IST. Adil Rashid was up next at 3:56 PM IST and remained unsold, as did Akeal Hosein, Mujeeb Rahman, Mohammad Waqar Salamkheil, Tabraiz Shamsi and Ish Sodhi.
