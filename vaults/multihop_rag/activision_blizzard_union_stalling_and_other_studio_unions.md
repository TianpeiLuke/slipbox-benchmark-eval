# Activision Blizzard's Union Stalling And Other Studio Unions

Executives at Microsoft's newest acquisition, Activision Blizzard, spent the past few years stalling internal unionization efforts — though QA employees at Raven Software, a subsidiary of Activision, successfully voted to unionize in May 2022, and Microsoft has vowed to respect organization attempts now that Activision-Blizzard-King is under its control.

Engadget also lists the other companies with unions established in the past two years: Avalanche Studios, Anemone Hug, CD Projekt RED, Experis Game Solutions, Keywords Studios, Sega of America, Tender Claws and Workinman Interactive.
