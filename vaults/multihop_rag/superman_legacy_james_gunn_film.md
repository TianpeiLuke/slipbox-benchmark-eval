# Superman: Legacy (James Gunn)

Superman: Legacy, dated July 11 in Polygon's 2023-10-24 DC rundown, is the first of the new Gunn/Safran projects to actually get a release date, and it seems expressly designed to break the DCEU mold.

The clearest signal of that break is casting and authorship: Henry Cavill is moving on from the Superman role, and James Gunn plans to script the film himself.

Gunn made several pointed references to the film being a break from past visions of Superman, calling it "the true foundation of [their] creative vision for the DC Universe" and saying he "can't wait to introduce [their] version of Superman." The advance description at the time said only that it will feature the character "balancing his Kryptonian heritage with his human upbringing."
