# The Myth That We Know Less About the Deep Sea Than Mars

The biggest, most pervasive myth about the deep sea, Jon Copley says, is the idea that we know almost nothing about it — the very popular claim that we know more about the moon or Mars than the deep ocean.

His rebuttal concedes exactly one premise and denies the rest: that claim is only really true for one very specific aspect of knowledge, having a detailed map of the terrain of a solid surface, because the moon and Mars are not covered in seawater, which blocks radar and means sonar must be used in the deep ocean. Apart from that, he says, we know far more about the deep ocean than about those other places. The deep sea has nonetheless become, in his phrase, a "realm of myth and darkness."
