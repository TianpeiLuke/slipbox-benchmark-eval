# Prime Day Robot Vacuum Deals, October 2023

Robot vacuums saw decent sales during Amazon's October 2023 fall Prime Day event, including on top-of-the-line models that are discounted less often — with the reviewer's standing advice being never to buy a bot that is not on sale, because they are always on sale. Ecovacs' newest flagship robot vacuum and mop, the Deebot X2 Omni, launched the week before and was already $200 off, down from $1,499.99 to $1,199.99 at Amazon; a complete redesign of the Deebot X1 Omni, it has a new square shape — which the reviewer, who had started testing it, said gives it the advantage of getting into corners better than most rounder bots — and a charging dock that empties the bin, washes and dries the mops and drains, and refills the robot's water tank, with the party trick of lifting its mops up 15mm to avoid getting carpets wet.

Further down the range, Yeedi had a sale on its brand-new Yeedi Cube, the first budget robot vacuum mop with a self-emptying, self-washing, self-filling dock, at $559.99 at Amazon, $140 off. The Roomba Combo j7 Plus, the first Roomba vacuum to also mop, was listed at $399 at Walmart and down to $799 at Amazon from $1,100; it has an auto-empty charging dock and can avoid a long list of common household objects including socks, charging cables, and pet poop. The Roomba j6 Plus, which avoids fewer objects than the j7 but still dodges poop, was called a steal at $399.99. The Roborock Q5 Plus was $399.99, down from $700, a 43 percent discount — it can empty itself and features mapping and virtual keep-out zones with lidar navigation, and is one of the few Roborocks available without a mop, making it a good choice for homes with lots of carpet.

At the budget end, the basic bump-and-roll Roomba 692 was down from $299.99 to $165, and the Shark Ion AV753 robot vacuum — one of the reviewer's favorite budget bots — was just $129.99, $100 off.
