# The SuperDraft Lineup For Lions-Packers Week 4 And How Its Multiplier Scoring Works

Sporting News also listed its favorite weekly DFS plays on SuperDraft for the Week 4 Lions-Packers game on 2023-09-26, a format whose scoring works differently from salary-cap contests. SuperDraft's DFS scoring involves point multipliers as opposed to traditional salary-capped or tiered contests: each player is assigned a scoring multiplier based on their value as determined by SuperDraft, users can roster any player with no restrictions, and a winning lineup will likely feature a strong mix of stud fantasy contributors with 1x-1.6x boosts as well as matchup-based sleepers with larger multipliers. The most notable scoring rules for SuperDraft NFL contests are half-point PPR, four-point passing touchdowns, and two-point bonuses for 100 rushing yards, 100 receiving yards and 300 passing yards.
