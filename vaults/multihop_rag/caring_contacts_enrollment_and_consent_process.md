# How Patients Are Enrolled And Consented Into Caring Contacts

Every patient who receives a mental health assessment in the emergency department at Children's Nebraska is offered the chance to participate in Caring Contacts, and about 98% of families agree to join the program, said Schalley.

The precondition for being offered the program is how the patient arrives or screens. "These are individuals who arrive due to suicide ideation or suicide gesture or who screen positive for suicide risk through the universal screening tool that all emergency department patients ages 11 and older receive," Schalley told Fox News Digital.

Enrollment is then completed by the guardian, and it authorizes a fixed span of contact: parents or legal guardians sign a release form, which allows social workers at the hospital to keep in contact with the patient over the next year.
