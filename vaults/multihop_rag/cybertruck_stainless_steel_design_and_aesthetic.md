# Cybertruck's Stainless Steel Design And Aesthetic

The Cybertruck's name, neon logo and exterior evoke a sort of cyberpunk, post-apocalyptic aesthetic — and the reason, per TechCrunch, is simply that Musk wants it to. The vehicle was also referred to as Cybrtrk in an early trademark.

The material choice drives the shape and the problems. The electric pickup is made of steel, a tough material that resists rust but is hard to shape; the material's rigidity influenced the EV's starkly geometric design, and it also led to launch delays and panel gaps on Cybertruck test vehicles.

Musk has described the tank-like vehicle as "an armored personnel carrier from the future — what Bladerunner would have driven." TechCrunch's rejoinder: while Bladerunner is a stunning film, it depicts a horrific dystopia.
