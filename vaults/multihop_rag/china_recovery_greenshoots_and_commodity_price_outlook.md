# China Recovery Greenshoots and Commodity Price Outlook

A developing story as of October 2023 is the greenshoots of recovery in China, backed by domestic consumption during Golden Week and tourism related to the Asian Games. For now these seem to be buttressed on stimulus and base effects, as China is now fully open, and the long-term trajectory remains to be seen.

The near-term consequence is for commodities: in any case, for now this has provided a floor for commodity prices, especially base metals. Fickle commodity prices will also be victims of fresh geopolitical tensions in West Asia, which has pushed up Brent crude. Food prices could also play truant with climate change affecting cropping area worldwide, with sugar and rice prices booming, the note said.
