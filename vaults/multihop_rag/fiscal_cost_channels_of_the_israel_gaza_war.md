# Fiscal Cost Channels of the Israel-Gaza War

Leo Leiderman set out the war's cost to the Israeli budget as a set of direct and indirect channels: "As with any war, the current war has and will have direct and indirect impacts."

On the spending side, the war will substantially raise defence spending in the coming years for procurement of equipment, ammunition and advanced systems in accordance with the new needs. A second direct expense is payment for the reserve duty days of those mobilised. A third channel runs through financing costs: given current interest rate levels around the world, the cost of servicing public debt as a proportion of GDP will rise.

On the revenue side, he expects a decline in state revenues from taxation. The combined result of these channels is a rise in the fiscal deficit.
