# InZone H9 And Seagate Xbox Expansion Card Deals

Two gaming accessories were discounted during the anti-Prime Big Deal Day sales: the Sony InZone H9 headset at $245, down from $300 — 18% off — and the 1TB Seagate Storage Expansion Card at $130, down from $220 — 41% off.

The H9 is at the top end of Sony's InZone gaming headsets, featuring active noise cancellation, compatibility with both PCs and the PlayStation 5, and the ability to connect simultaneously to two devices, one over 2.4GHz wireless and one over Bluetooth; it was $245 at Amazon and $250 at Best Buy. The Seagate Storage Expansion Card is proprietary SSD expansion for the Xbox Series X/S consoles; the plug-and-play drives are designed to be as fast as the Xbox internal SSD and are sold in 512GB, 1TB and 2TB configurations, with the 1TB at $130 at Best Buy.
