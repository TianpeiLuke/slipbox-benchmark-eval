# Limitations Of The UBC GLP-1 Claims Analysis

The study's own authors qualify how far its findings can be pushed: the claims analysis could not establish subjects' full medical histories, could not separate the risk of individual GLP-1 drugs, and did not identify which groups are most susceptible.

"We did not have access to medical charts to ascertain all subjects' medical histories," said Etminan. "Also, we could not look at risk with individual GLP-1 drugs, but this is probably a class effect of these drugs." That concession undercuts any inference from the results to a particular product, while leaving the class-level signal intact.

The researchers also did not determine whether certain groups were at higher risk of this adverse side effect, but Etminan said he suspects that those with preexisting GI conditions might be more susceptible -- a suspicion, not a finding.
