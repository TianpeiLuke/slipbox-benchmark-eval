# Carabao Cup 2023/24 Second Round Results

In the second round of the 2023/24 Carabao Cup the 12 Premier League clubs not involved in European competition joined the 36 teams advancing from the opening round, per Sporting News on Dec. 19, 2023.

The round's talking points were that Fulham overcame Tottenham Hotspur in a penalty shootout, Chelsea came from behind to beat AFC Wimbledon, Nottingham Forest were knocked out by an injury-time winner against Burnley, and Wrexham were knocked out by fellow League Two side Bradford City. The field comprised 12 Premier League clubs, 14 Championship clubs, 10 League One clubs and 12 League Two clubs.
