# Travis Kelce's College-Era Tweets Resurface

Travis Kelce said he had been trying to delete his old tweets long before they went viral, confessing on the 22 November 2023 episode of his New Heights podcast that funny posts of his from 2010 and 2011 had resurfaced amid his rumoured relationship with Taylor Swift. The 34-year-old football star spoke candidly about his previous posts on X — formerly known as Twitter — in the episode with his brother Jason Kelce.

When Jason quipped that everyone is "a big fan of college Travis on Twitter", the Kansas City Chiefs star acknowledged that his posts weren't really noticed at the time. "What's hilarious is that nobody followed me back then," he said. "But I will say I have been trying to get all those tweets deleted. Like since I got into the league, I'm like: 'Gosh, I just want all those things to get deleted.'" He poked fun at how the tweets are now mocked, adding: "And sure enough, it came surface-level and everybody f***ing dove in, in 2011. What a f***ing year that was."

The brothers discussed the viral responses, including how Jimmy Fallon and his band performed a song titled "The Ballad of Travis Kelce" on The Tonight Show, in which the late-night host recited Kelce's old tweets. They highlighted the now fan-favourite post: "I just gave a squirle a piece of bread and it straight smashed all of it!!!! I had no idea they ate bread like that!! Haha #crazy." Kelce explained the tweets had no deeper meaning: "I was just using Twitter like a diary. I'm just out here saying nonsense - 'I just gave a squirrel a piece of bread' - and spelled squirrel like a jacka**." Jason, of the Philadelphia Eagles, mocked the spelling and claimed it was the reason the post went viral at all, joking: "I'm only spelling squirrel like that from now on, I don't know why it's spelled the other way." Earlier in November 2023, fans took to X to share screenshots of Kelce's old tweets, including a 2010 post reading: "#Chipotle will always be a great choice!!!!"
