# FTX

FTX was a crypto exchange that at its height was the third-largest in the world, backed by an unusually broad investor base and carrying enormous daily trading volume. Within two years, more than 80 investors provided about $2 billion in capital to FTX, helping Bankman-Fried propel his vision into a reality.

Its funding peaked in January 2022, when the company raised $400 million in a Series C round that boosted its valuation to $32 billion — its last round of public funding. Trades on FTX climbed up to $15 billion a day, and TechCrunch's 2023-10-02 piece notes that as that activity grew, SBF also got tied up in the corridors of political power. By the time his trial began, TechCrunch's 2023-10-01 account framed the whole story as one question: how did the once third-largest crypto exchange get here?
