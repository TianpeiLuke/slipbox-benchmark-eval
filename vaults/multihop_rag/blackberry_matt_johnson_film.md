# BlackBerry (Matt Johnson)

BlackBerry is Matt Johnson's indie take on tech breakthroughs and backstabbing, starring Jay Baruchel, Glenn Howerton, and Johnson himself, ranked 38 on Polygon's best movies of 2023 list of 2023-10-07 and available on AMC Plus or for digital rental/purchase. Polygon calls it more Social Network than the recent streak of corporation-flavored inventopics such as Air, Tetris, and The Beanie Bubble, finding darkly comedic laughs in capitalist meltdowns.

The story turns on two performances the critic says are destined to fly under the radar: wannabe businessman Jim Balsillie, played by Glenn Howerton, initially brushes off lowly inventor Mike Lazaridis, played by Jay Baruchel, until he sees a gold mine — a reimagined mobile prototype that does not actually work but could definitely sell.

Johnson's screenplay, co-written with his Dirties and Operative Avalanche cohort Matthew Miller, delivers all the bravado one expects from this type of rise-and-fall saga. Critic Matt Patches writes that Howerton rewires his Always Sunny Dennis persona into a true warhead, barking at Doom-playing brainiacs until they get results, while Baruchel's arc as a tech genius sinking in corporate quicksand is almost a mini Breaking Bad. Johnson's touch is delivering a searing indictment of Canada's own Silicon Valley behavior with puncturing goofs and plenty of chewed-up scenery — a fun movie in which the keyboard clacks and the cast absolutely roars.
