# Zack Moss Colts Midseason MVP

Yardbarker's Nov. 9, 2023 AFC midseason team MVP list named running back Zack Moss the Indianapolis Colts' MVP because he stepped up in a big way with Jonathan Taylor missing the first four weeks of the season.

The grounds are what he produced in that window and where it left him: after not playing in Week 1, Moss rushed for 445 yards and scored four touchdowns over his next four games, helping Indy win three of four and stay in the thick of the AFC South, and he was second in the NFL in rushing, 37 yards behind San Francisco's Christian McCaffrey. The warrant is that stepping into an absent starter's role and producing at a league-leading rate is what makes the fill-in the team's most valuable player.
