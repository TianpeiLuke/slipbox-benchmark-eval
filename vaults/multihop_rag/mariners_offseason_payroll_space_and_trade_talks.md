# Mariners' Cleared Payroll Space And Winter Meetings Trade Talks

MLB Network's Jon Morosi reported on Tuesday, Dec. 5, 2023 that following the trade the Mariners made with the Braves, Seattle had cleared payroll space and now had room to make a splash for top free agent slugger Cody Bellinger, per Sporting News' winter meetings tracker.

Seattle had Julio Rodriguez in center, but with Teoscar Hernandez still on the market and Jarred Kelenic now dealt, there was room for Bellinger in the outfield.

Earlier, on Sunday, Dec. 3, Morosi reported the Mariners and Rays — frequent trade partners in recent years — could be talking shop again at the winter meetings, discussing Rays utility infielder Isaac Paredes and potentially star left fielder Randy Arozarena in talks to start the meetings.
