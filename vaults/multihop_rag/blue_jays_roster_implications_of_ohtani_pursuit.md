# Why Signing Ohtani Could Have Cost Toronto Bichette And Guerrero

The argument advanced in a Nov. 30, 2023 report by the New York Post's Jon Heyman is that signing Ohtani would not simply add to the Blue Jays lineup but could force them to subtract from it, with rivals believing Bo Bichette and Vladimir Guerrero Jr. could hit the trade block if Toronto landed the star.

The grounds are two facts about Toronto's roster and payroll: both infielders are free agents after the 2025 season, and signing Ohtani would add substantial payroll. The warrant joining those grounds to the conclusion is a cost calculation — given the added payroll of signing Ohtani, it is possible Toronto would view keeping the other two phenoms as too pricey. The argument concedes the tension in its own conclusion: trading the two infielders might seem counterproductive to signing Ohtani in the first place, since those two would be the biggest supporting bats in the lineup around him. The scope condition is that the Blue Jays were at the time one of the teams believed to still be among the strongest in the mix for Ohtani, so the whole chain was conditional on a signing that had not happened.
