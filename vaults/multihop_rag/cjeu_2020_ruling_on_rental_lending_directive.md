# The CJEU's 2020 Ruling On The Rental/Lending Directive

In 2020 the European Court of Justice ruled that the "reciprocity" principle no longer applies to EU member states, even if it exists in copyright treaties and national laws, because the European Union's Rental/Lending Directive of 2006 does not allow EU member states to withhold payments to nationals of non-EU countries.

The ruling left the remedy with the legislature rather than the courts: the CJEU made it clear that the EU legislature has the power to change this by rewriting the directive governing copyright payments. "It is for the EU legislature alone, which has exclusive external competence in the matter, to decide on such a limitation," the court stated following the ruling.
