# Sportsbook Welcome Offer Roundup For The ACC-SEC Challenge

Sporting News' Nov. 29, 2023 roundup of college basketball promo codes presented six sportsbook welcome offers — from Caesars Sportsbook, BetMGM, Bet365, FanDuel, DraftKings and BetRivers — that together let a new user claim $5,000-plus in bonuses, pegged to that night's college basketball slate including the top-20 ACC-SEC Challenge matchup between Tennessee and North Carolina.

The exclusive promo codes were described as unlocking thousands of dollars in bonus bets for all of that week's CBK games, with the caveat that offers may vary among states. The listed set was: Caesars Sportsbook, get $1,000 First Bet on Caesars, promo code NEWS1000; BetMGM, $1,500 First Bet plus a $50 bonus bet, code TSN50; Bet365, First Bet Safety Net up to $1,000 or Bet $5 Get $150 in bonus bets, code TSNCO; FanDuel, bet $5 on any moneyline and get $150 in bonus bets if your team wins, claimed by link rather than a code; DraftKings, up to $1,200 in welcome offers, also claimed by link; and BetRivers, a 2nd chance bet up to $500, code SPORTSRIV.

The framing claim is that these top-rated sportsbooks — BetMGM, Bet365, FanDuel, Caesars Sportsbook, DraftKings and BetRivers — make it worth a new user's while when signing up, to the tune of $5K-plus in bonus bets.
