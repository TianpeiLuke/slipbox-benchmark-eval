# Music Streaming And Vinyl Subscription Gifts

Beyond the familiar Spotify gift cards (Amazon, Best Buy, or Target), The Verge highlights several other music gifts for a giftee who is a musician or who just loves to unwind with music.

Apple Music is listed at $25 as a great gift for the casual listener, offering more than 100 million ad-free songs with spatial audio and support for Dolby Atmos; there's no designated Apple Music gift card, so you buy a regular Apple gift card — which the guide counts as an upside, since the recipient can also put it toward the latest AirPods. For true audiophiles, a Tidal gift card (Walmart, PayPal, or Best Buy) allows streaming in the highest audio quality possible: the ad-free service starts at $10.99 per month, with $9 extra per month adding support for lossless FLAC audio, Dolby Atmos Music, and Sony's 360 Reality Audio formats. A Fender Play subscription suits someone who has always wanted to learn guitar, with a continuously updated catalog of hundreds of instructor-led video lessons; it typically starts at $9.99 a month, but an annual subscription can currently be gifted for $74.999, half off. A Vinyl Me, Please subscription sends records monthly in a chosen genre — hip-hop, rock, or jazz — with booklets about each record and access to exclusive discounts, with three-month subscriptions starting at $128 and including four records, one a limited-time bonus. Lastly, a Ticketmaster gift card lets a loved one see their favourite musicians live, whether that's Olivia Rodrigo, Bad Bunny, or The Rolling Stones.
