# Trevor Lawrence Cleared To Start After Week 7 Pregame Workout

Trevor Lawrence was confirmed to start for the Jaguars against the Saints on Thursday, Oct. 19, 2023, ending speculation about his status. At 6:36 p.m. the Sporting News live blog reported, citing Cameron Wolfe, that Jaguars head coach Doug Pederson had told him Lawrence would start that night.

The decision hinged on a pregame workout. At 6:26 p.m. the blog noted that Pederson had said earlier on Thursday he expected Lawrence to start pending that workout, and that Lawrence was then going through the regimen — doing dropbacks and throwing, moving well, with Pederson watching from a distance without much worry. Lawrence's 20-minute pregame workout, per Wolfe, showed good lateral movement making cuts and throwing on the run, after which he dapped up trainers and quarterback Mike McCoy with a smile heading back into the locker room.
