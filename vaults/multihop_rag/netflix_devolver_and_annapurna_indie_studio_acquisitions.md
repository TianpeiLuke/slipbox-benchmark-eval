# Netflix, Devolver And Annapurna's Indie Studio Acquisitions

On a smaller scale than Microsoft or Sony, companies like Netflix and Devolver Digital have also dabbled in acquisitions recently: Devolver started buying studios in 2020 and now owns Croteam, Dodge Roll, Doinksoft, Firefly Studios, Nerial and System Era Softworks; Annapurna Interactive bought South African studio 24 Bit Games in November; and Netflix, which launched its Games division in 2021, has already purchased four studios, including Oxenfree developer Night School and Alphabear company Spry Fox.

Night School co-founder Sean Krankel told Engadget in June that the move to Netflix was a boon for the studio, providing financial security, a dedicated working space and plenty of marketing support for its projects. "A small subset of teams are good to go for the next 10 years, but others have these peaks and valleys, and we were somewhere in between," Krankel said. "We weren't in danger of anything going sideways. But we were at a spot where we're like, it would be cool to tether to somebody who has a similar vision, and somebody that we could work with that would like, de-risk us."
