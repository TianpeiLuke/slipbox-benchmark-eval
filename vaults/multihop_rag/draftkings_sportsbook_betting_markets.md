# DraftKings Sportsbook: MLB And NBA Markets And Promotions

DraftKings is a premier sportsbook platform with an extensive array of Major League Baseball betting options for novice and seasoned bettors, and one of the most recognizable names in online sports betting for NBA wagering. Its MLB odds include intriguing player and game prop bets, such as predicting the First Inning Winner or First Team to Score, which allow bettors to engage with games beyond simple outcomes; for the bigger picture it provides futures bets, letting fans bet on potential division victors or predict the next World Series champion.

On the NBA side, DraftKings is noted for frequent promos to enhance NBA odds, a diverse range of prop bets tailored to the most prominent NBA players, and a seamless live NBA betting experience through its app.

Its new-customer offer requires no special code, with a total promotional value of up to $1,200. In the past DraftKings has run special promotions tailored to NBA enthusiasts — one allowed customers to earn $200 in bonus bets simply by placing a $5 bet on any NBA Finals game — which Sporting News reads as a commitment to delivering value to its betting community.
