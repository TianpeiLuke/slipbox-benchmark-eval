# Roblox's Dating Prediction

David Baszucki predicted at Roblox's developer conference, and repeated at the Code Conference in the interview published October 12th, 2023, that someday someone will build a dating app on Roblox: it will be very safe, it will be for 17-and-up people, and it will be an interesting way for people to connect. The proposition rests on a claimed market gap — there is probably a third of the population that will not go on Bumble or Tinder or Hinge or whatever, just because of awkwardness — and on a mechanism: in immersive 3D avatar-type communication, where one person can be Shrek and another Donald Duck, there is a breakdown of the friction and of the fear of a video call.

The prediction was hedged with a precondition. Baszucki said the company was very careful on that slide, pairing it with the 17-plus, ID-validated users in 17-plus experiences, so parents do not have a heart attack — given Roblox's history of safety and civility and its focus on that.

What would falsify it is straightforward from the way he framed it: if avatar-mediated interaction does not in fact reduce the awkwardness that keeps that third of the population off existing dating apps, or if no such app is built for the ID-validated 17-plus tier, the prediction fails.
