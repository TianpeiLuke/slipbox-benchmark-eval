# Scalable Capital's Decision Not To Seek A Banking License

Scalable Capital is holding steady and not pursuing a banking license itself, co-CEO Erik Podzuweit said in December 2023 — a choice he defends on grounds of speed and cost.

"It's leaner, obviously," he said, likening the choice to how some e-commerce companies might invest in building out their own logistics while others work with third parties; in lieu of a license, Scalable's banking partners include ING.

The position is explicitly provisional rather than permanent: "Maybe I wouldn't rule [getting a license] out forever. But right now we're focusing on building products and launching new markets and we are faster doing it our way."
