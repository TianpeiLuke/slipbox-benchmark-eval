# Diana and Dodi's Paris Car Crash, 1997

In both The Crown and in real life, Diana and Dodi Fayed — along with their chauffeur, Henri Paul — were killed on 31 August 1997 when their Mercedes-Benz was pursued by paparazzi and crashed in the Pont de l'Alma tunnel in Paris. Diana, a mother of two, was 36 years old.

The fatal car crash was recreated in the very first scene of season six. The incident itself is not shown on-screen: a car carrying Diana and Dodi is seen speeding past a man walking his dog, before entering the tunnel, and as the vehicle's horn blares he calls emergency services on his cell phone.
