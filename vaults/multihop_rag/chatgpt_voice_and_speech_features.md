# ChatGPT Voice And Speech Features

OpenAI added a voice to ChatGPT — announcing a new voice for verbal conversations along with image-based smarts — and then rolled ChatGPT Voice out to all free users, even while the company's leadership was in flux.

The feature was first announced in September and granted to paid users on a rolling basis before the free rollout. The underlying text-to-speech model can create a voice from text prompts and a few seconds of speech samples; OpenAI worked with voice actors to create the five voice options, and users can try it by going to the settings in the mobile ChatGPT apps and tapping the "headphones" icon.

One consequence noted at the time: now that ChatGPT Voice is available to all free users, it can be used to replace Siri on an iPhone 15 Pro and Pro Max by configuring the new Action Button, letting the user ask ChatGPT questions and listen to its responses — like a much smarter version of Siri.
