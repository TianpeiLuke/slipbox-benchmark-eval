# Minecraft (Switch Local Multiplayer)

Minecraft is a no-brainer for anyone looking to spend time with friends in a sandbox world, according to Polygon's 2023-10-20 list of the 35 best couch co-op games for Nintendo Switch, which calls it a bestseller.

Players mine, build, dig, fish, and plant crops.

They also protect themselves from the monsters that come out at night — and, most of all, survive.
