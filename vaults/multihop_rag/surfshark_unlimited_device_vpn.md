# Surfshark, Best For Multiple Devices

Surfshark was named the best VPN for multiple devices: it is one of few VPN services that offer use on an unlimited number of devices without bandwidth restrictions, according to the company. Specifications: unlimited simultaneous connections and devices; Windows, Mac, Android, iOS and Linux; Chrome, Firefox and Edge browser extensions; dedicated IP addresses with an additional purchase; 1 free month and a 30-day money-back guarantee; $2.49/month at Surfshark.

That convenience comes without a significant increase in price — subscriptions cost about $2.49 to $12.95 for a monthly subscription — and the company recently conducted its first independent audit. Since the initial review it added QR-code-enabled login across devices and expanded support for alternative ID profiles, a feature some of its competitors already had.

Testing was mixed but passing. The reviewers ran into some trouble connecting to Surfshark's WireGuard protocol and tested on IKEv2 instead; the VPN speed was a bit slow and struggled to connect for the geoblock test at first, but ultimately passed. What sets it apart from other VPNs with unlimited connection options is access to a larger number of servers and availability on more types of devices. As noted elsewhere in the guide, connecting to a router can already provide nearly unlimited access to devices in a single household.
