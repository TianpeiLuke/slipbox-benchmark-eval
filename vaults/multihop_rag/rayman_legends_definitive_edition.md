# Rayman Legends Definitive Edition

The Definitive Edition of Rayman Legends has all of the platforming and hijinks the series is known for. It appears in Polygon's 2023-10-20 list of the 35 best couch co-op games for Nintendo Switch.

Players work through delightful levels and time trials, and collect to their heart's content.

Having multiple players is particularly helpful for respawning, as the game ramps up in difficulty in its later levels.
