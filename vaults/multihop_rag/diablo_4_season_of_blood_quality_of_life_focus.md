# Season of Blood's Real Draw Is Quality of Life, Not Vampire Powers

Polygon's claim, published 2023-10-04, is that although Diablo 4's Season of Blood brings some cool new vampire powers into the fold, the real star of the second season is its sweeping quality-of-life changes.

The grounds are what those changes do for ordinary play: players will now have an easier time leveling up, gaining quality loot, and just navigating the open world of Sanctuary.

The warrant is that these improvements touch the activities every player performs constantly, which is why they outweigh a headline feature. The reporting basis is a roundtable interview game director Joe Shely and associate game director Joe Piepiora gave to Polygon and other outlets ahead of Blizzard's Oct. 4 livestream, in which they gave additional context on Nightmare Dungeon changes, Renown, and more.
