# Red Sox Acquire Tyler O'Neill From The Cardinals

The Red Sox acquired outfielder Tyler O'Neill from the Cardinals on Friday, Dec. 8, 2023 in a deal that sent pitching prospects Nick Robertson and Victor Santos to St. Louis, the New York Post's Jon Heyman reported.

The trade came days after Boston traded Alex Verdugo to the Yankees. O'Neill enjoyed a breakthrough 2021 season with the Cardinals, batting .286 with 34 home runs and a .912 OPS, but he had struggled to recapture that level of production over the following two seasons and was set to be a free agent after 2024.
