# Portfolio Management Services In India

Portfolio Management Services are a managed-account alternative available alongside AIFs to Indian HNIs, governed by guidelines that date back to 1993 but that have grown in popularity with HNIs only recently. The growing financialisation of savings, the formalisation of the economy and the growing prosperity of tier 2 and 3 cities have raised PMS AUM, excluding EPFO and PF money, to ₹5.28 lakh crore as of July 2023. Because the minimum investment is ₹50 lakh, this option too is available only to HNIs.

The discriminating features are drawn against mutual funds. Both PMS and mutual funds provide expert guidance in navigating volatile markets and seizing wealth-creating opportunities, but because mutual funds cater to retail investors their guidelines are relatively stringent and compliance is rigorous, whereas PMS investment guidelines are somewhat more flexible, allowing portfolio managers to outperform mutual funds. A second appeal to HNIs is ownership: in PMS the beneficial ownership of securities belongs to the investors, as opposed to mutual funds where ownership belongs to the fund. PMS comes in two variants, discretionary and non-discretionary; in the former the portfolio managers make all decisions, in the latter the investor does.
