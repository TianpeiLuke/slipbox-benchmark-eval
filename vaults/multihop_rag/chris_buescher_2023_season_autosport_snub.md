# Chris Buescher 2023 Season Autosport Snub

Essentially Sports argued on Dec. 21, 2023 that Chris Buescher was a major exclusion from Autosport's Top 50 drivers of 2023, on the strength of his best-ever season in NASCAR's premier competition.

The grounds are his results and their context. RFK Racing found a fresh breath of motivation that year: since Brad Keselowski arrived in 2022 the team had continued evolving on and off the track, and while Keselowski had instilled his experience and winning mentality, he failed to produce a race win in 2023 — instead it was Buescher who outshone the Cup Series veteran. Buescher ended his season with three wins, nine top-fives and 17 top-tens, with two of his three wins coming in succession at Richmond and Michigan, and he continued his dream run in the Cup Series playoffs to finish seventh in the final standings. The limit on the case is stated too: the #17 Ford Mustang driver could not secure a win in the Round of 8 and did not have enough points to make a case for the championship four race at Phoenix.
