# 2024 Mock Draft: Four Quarterbacks Off The Board In Round 1

CBS Sports' 2024 NFL Mock Draft, published 2023-11-17, projects four quarterbacks going in the first round. At Pick 1, from Carolina, Chicago takes Caleb Williams, QB, USC, a junior listed at 6'1" and 215 lbs, ranked 2nd prospect and 1st at the position; the reasoning is that Justin Fields is expected to return to the field that week, but is it too little, too late — Chicago will have a chance to take a quarterback No. 1 overall and it may be too much to pass up a second consecutive year. At Pick 2, the N.Y. Giants take Drake Maye, QB, North Carolina, a sophomore at 6'4" and 230 lbs, ranked 5th prospect and 2nd at the position; there was doubt about Daniel Jones' viability as a long-term starter when he signed his extension, and following his ACL injury there is no less concern, so New York will have to eat a grandiose amount of dead salary cap — a small price to pay if Maye ends up being the quarterback many expect he could be.

At Pick 11, Tampa Bay takes J.J. McCarthy, QB, Michigan, a junior at 6'3" and 202 lbs, ranked 21st prospect and 4th at the position, replacing Baker Mayfield; the note observes that it was a Michigan quarterback that led the Buccaneers to the promised land last time, so perhaps lightning strikes twice. At Pick 21, Minnesota takes Jayden Daniels, QB, LSU, a senior at 6'4" and 210 lbs, ranked 76th prospect and 8th at the position; the Joshua Dobbs story has been fun, but the reality is that Kirk Cousins will be a free agent after the season and the organization could opt for a change, with Daniels described as one of the most productive quarterbacks in football.
