# Meta Quest 2 Black Friday And Cyber Monday Bundle Price

The Meta Quest 2 was $249, down from $299.99 — a $50.99 saving — in both Amazon's Cyber Monday sale and Walmart's Black Friday doorbusters, with different credit sweeteners attached at each retailer.

Mashable's Amazon Cyber Monday roundup of 27 November 2023 listed it as "Meta Quest 2 — $249 $299.99 (save $50.99) + free $50 Amazon credit with code META50", flagged as a hot deal.

Mashable's Walmart doorbuster roundup of 25 November 2023 listed the same headline price for the 128GB model — "$249 $299.99 (save $50.99)" — but with a free $50 Meta Quest Store credit rather than an Amazon credit.
