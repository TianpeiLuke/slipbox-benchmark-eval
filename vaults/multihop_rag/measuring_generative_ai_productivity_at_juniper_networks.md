# Measuring Generative AI Productivity At Juniper Networks

Sharon Mandell, CIO at Juniper Networks, says that measuring increased productivity from generative AI remains a challenge even as her company participates in an initial pilot with Microsoft around Copilot for Office 365. Anecdotally she has heard a range of feedback, from people who love it to those who are less impressed, and Microsoft has begun to provide dashboards that at least show the level of adoption and usage.

The obstacle she identifies is the absence of a productivity baseline. "The hard thing about this is you don't have data on people's level of productivity. So no matter what, you're using somewhat anecdotal information until you get really good at understanding these dashboards from Microsoft showing you how people are using it," she said.
