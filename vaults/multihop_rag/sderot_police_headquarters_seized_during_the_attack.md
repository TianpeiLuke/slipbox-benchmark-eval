# Sderot Police Headquarters Seized During the Attack

Younis Tirawi, who reports on Palestinian affairs, said that Hamas militants had taken over the Sderot police headquarters and armoured vehicles in the town on 7 October 2023.

An analyst posting as OSINTtechnical wrote the same day that some of the images coming out of Sderot over the previous few hours were reaching degrees of horror "that I don't really think I can use words to properly describe. Truly disturbing acts of terror directed at civilians."
