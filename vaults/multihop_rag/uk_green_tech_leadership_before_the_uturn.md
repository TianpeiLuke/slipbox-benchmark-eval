# UK Green Tech Leadership Before The U-Turn

Not that long ago, the UK seemed a rather brighter beacon in the industrial transition toward reversing the global climate breakdown, TechCrunch's 2023-10-13 commentary recalls. The countrywide goals were laid out, COP26 at least offered a forum and a spotlight, and London has made strides in establishing itself as a hub for green tech startups.

On the narrow but viable path toward net zero, leaders were at least taking the right steps. Then came the nadir of the last few weeks.
