# Bharti Airtel: The EV/EBITDA Case That PE Would Have Missed

At the start of FY22, in April 2021, Bharti Airtel's trailing PE was negative or invalid due to losses in FY21, so the stock would have been avoided by anyone going on PE alone. On a trailing EV/EBITDA basis, however, it was trading at 10 times, and when that was considered against its estimated next two years' EBITDA CAGR of over 20 per cent, the valuation, combined with qualitative factors, made it an interesting buy. Since then net profits have improved substantially as operating leverage played out.

bl.portfolio recommended a buy on the stock on this basis in April 2021, and as of the article's publication on 14 October 2023 the stock had returned 80 per cent since. The stated lesson attached to the observation is that the stock would have been easily missed if PE was the metric used to spot the performers.
