# Binance's CEO Succession: Richard Teng

Richard Teng, Binance's former global head of regional markets, will be the exchange's new CEO, Zhao shared in a post on X on the afternoon of Tuesday, November 21, 2023. Teng previously was the CEO of the Financial Services Regulatory Authority at Abu Dhabi Global Market, among other executive roles.

In response to stepping down, Zhao said "it is the right thing to do," adding, "I made mistakes, and I must take responsibility."

Zhao will remain a shareholder and said he will be "available to the team to consult as needed."
