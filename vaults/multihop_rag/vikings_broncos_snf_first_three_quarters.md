# The First Three Quarters Of Vikings-Broncos On Sunday Night

Through three quarters on Nov. 19, 2023 Minnesota led Denver 17-9, having scored the game's only two touchdowns while Will Lutz kicked all of the Broncos' points. The Vikings received to start the game, and on their opening drive Joshua Dobbs was popped by SS Kareem Jackson on 3rd & 1 while LB Baron Browning pried the ball loose; Denver recovered at Minnesota's 30. The Vikings' defense held after the turnover — LB Danielle Hunter's timely sack forced 3rd & 15 — and Lutz buried a 31-yarder for a 3-0 lead. Dobbs was evaluated in the medical tent after a big hit but did not miss a snap. Minnesota gained 28 yards in six plays before a big Broncos stop on 3rd & 8, and Denver went three-and-out when Wilson threw the ball away under heavy pressure, with Brandon Powell returning the punt 12 yards. The Vikings' run game then marched down the field, spell back Ty Chandler collecting 29 yards on the drive and starter Alexander Mattison 27, reaching Denver's 4-yard line as the first quarter ended 3-0 Broncos.

The second quarter opened with the game's first touchdown. At 8:56 p.m. Dobbs scrambled right, looked to have nowhere to go, shed a tackle on the sideline, regained his composure and found TE Josh Oliver wide open in the end zone for a 7-3 lead. After another Broncos three-and-out, Dobbs found TE T.J. Hockenson for 29 yards on 3rd & 2 to reach the red zone, but was sacked for an 11-yard loss on 3rd & goal and Greg Joseph drilled a 34-yarder for 10-3 with 8:09 left in the half. Wilson and Sutton then connected on a 33-yard pitch and catch for Denver's first big play and first red-zone trip, but Wilson's five-yard completion to Jerry Jeudy on 3rd & 7 came up short and head coach Sean Payton chose to kick from Minnesota's 12; Lutz made it 31 yards for 10-6. Dobbs and center Garrett Bradbury had several bad exchanges under center, with Dobbs fumbling his second snap of the game and recovering both. Dobbs hit Jordan Addison for 17 and Hockenson for 10 to reach midfield at the two-minute warning, but Mattison was stuffed on a three-yard run and Dobbs missed his back on a swing route on third down, forcing a punt. Wilson then completed four passes for 46 yards to reach Minnesota's 34, and Lutz closed the half with a 52-yard field goal, responsible for all of Denver's points in a 10-9 game.

The third quarter belonged to Minnesota. Denver received to start the half but a holding penalty derailed the drive and it came up way short on 3rd & 20, with Powell returning the punt 19 yards to the Vikings' 31. Minnesota picked up two first downs in five plays for 43 yards, Mattison gaining 23 on the ground and Dobbs and Addison connecting for 18; Broncos CB Patrick Surtain II was then flagged for a blatant defensive pass interference on an interception on 3rd & 13, an automatic first down into the red zone, and at 10:06 p.m. Dobbs scrambled 10 yards into the end zone for 17-9. Denver went three-and-out again when Wilson could not link up with Marvin Mims Jr. on 3rd & 3. Then, at 10:21 p.m., Mattison coughed up the football on an inside handoff and Denver recovered at its own 34. The turnover jolted the Broncos' offense to life: Javonte Williams rushed 18 yards on the first play and Wilson found Jerry Jeudy for 19, leaving Denver with 1st & 10 at Minnesota's 21 as the fourth quarter began, with the Vikings up 17-9.
