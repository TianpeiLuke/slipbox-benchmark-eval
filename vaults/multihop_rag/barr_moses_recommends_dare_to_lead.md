# Barr Moses Recommends "Dare To Lead"

Barr Moses recommends "Dare to Lead: Brave Work. Tough Conversations. Whole Hearts," by Brené Brown, saying that reading a Brené Brown book is like tackling the Sunday crossword puzzle — equal parts challenging and rejuvenating — and that Dare to Lead is no different. Moses is the CEO and co-founder of Monte Carlo, a data observability startup, and co-wrote a technical book on the topic, O'Reilly's "Data Quality Fundamentals: A Practitioner's Guide to Building Trustworthy Data Pipelines," sharing advice on achieving reliable data at scale.

Her grounds are the book's research base and its practical output. Dare to Lead pulls on Brown's decades of research, interviews and experience as a professor at the University of Houston speaking with CEOs, founders and other executives to understand what great leadership looks like and how to achieve it, and she distils four key skill sets great leaders possess while highlighting ways to empower employees to be courageous in the face of adversity and change.

Moses also draws a link to the present AI moment: published in 2018 before the Gen AI wave hit, Dare to Lead addresses what human leaders have to bring to the table that AI and ML currently do not — empathy and connection. As a bonus, the book provides helpful resources to assess leadership strengths and growth opportunities that can make for a fun and informative team building activity.
