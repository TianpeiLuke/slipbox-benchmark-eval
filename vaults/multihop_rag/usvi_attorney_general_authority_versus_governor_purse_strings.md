# USVI Attorney General Authority Versus The Governor's Purse Strings

The structural tension in the US Virgin Islands arrangement is that legal authority and financial authority sit in different hands. Officially, Denise George, not the governor, was in charge of making the legal calls.

But Governor Bryan was still her boss and held the purse strings. It was a delicate balance, George said.
