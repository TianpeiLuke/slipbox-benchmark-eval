# Candace Cameron Bure's Strict But Loving Boundaries

Candace Cameron Bure described her parenting style to L.A. Parent as strict but loving — "We set firm boundaries, but there is always love and grace" — and said her goal is not to make her kids happy but to guide them into responsible adults, FOX News Entertainment reported on 10 October 2023.

The "Full House" star is a mom of three with husband Valeri Bure — Natasha, Lev and Maksim, all now in their early 20s — and spoke to the outlet when they were teenagers. "I hope my kids would say that I'm a loving mom," she said. "They would also describe me as strict." She continued: "I'm the mom that needs to meet the parents before my kids can go to a new friend's house. I want to know who they're with, where they're going. Even now that they're driving, I want them to text me when they arrive and when they're heading home."

Bure explained that they "work hard to keep our communication open and talk through any situation" and are "always willing to listen", but added: "I don't always say yes. My goal as a parent is not to make my kids happy but to guide them to be respectful, curious, responsible, resilient, kind, giving and thoughtful adults."
