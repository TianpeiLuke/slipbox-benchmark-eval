# MacBook Pro M2 And M1 Generation Deals

The 14- and 16-inch MacBook Pros from early 2023 with M2 Pro / Max processors are still more than capable and may even be a better buy than the new M3 models if you can find them at a steep discount, The Verge wrote on October 26th, 2023. The base 14-inch MacBook Pro with an M2 Pro chip, a 512GB SSD, and 16GB of RAM was selling for $1,629 at Amazon, $370 off, a new low price that might not last very long, while the 14-inch model with 1TB of storage, a 12-core M2 Pro CPU, and 19 GPU cores was down to $1,999 at B&H Photo, a steep $500 off.

The 16-inch M2 Pro model fared worse. The best and only deal available on the creator-friendly laptop was at Amazon, where the base M2 Pro model with 512GB storage was $2,299.99, $200 off — not great, the writer said, hoping for better deals before the laptop is no longer available new.

Two-generation-old 2021 MacBook Pros could occasionally be had at steep enough discounts to still be worth considering, though base models were unlikely to still be around; a souped-up configuration was the opportunity. The 16-inch MacBook Pro from 2021 with 16GB of RAM and an expanded 1TB of storage was available at B&H Photo for $1,799, $900 off, and a tricked-out M1 Max model (10-core CPU / 32-core GPU) with 64GB of RAM and 2TB of storage was $2,499 at B&H Photo, $1,800 off — not exactly affordable, the writer conceded, but that laptop once sold for $4,299 and still performs well in 2023.
