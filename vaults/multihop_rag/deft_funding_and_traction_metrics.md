# Deft's Funding And Traction Metrics

Deft raised $1.8 million in a seed round from Hustle Fund, Frontier VC, Vitalize, Long Ecommerce Ventures, 43 Ventures and angel investors.

As of November 3, 2023 the startup said it is on track to hit 50,000 monthly active users by the end of the year. The co-founders did not reveal the exact search volume the product is catering to, but said Deft is seeing "tens of thousands" of searches every week, and that people using Deft for the second time do more searches than on their first visit.

The team also noted that it has a conversion rate — a measure of people buying the product versus seeing it — five times higher than the standard e-commerce rate, which is around 2-3% according to various estimates.
