# Mac Mini M2 Deals

As of October 26th, 2023, the base M2 Mac Mini was selling for a new all-time low of $479 at Amazon and B&H Photo, $120 off, while the M2 Pro version was at a respectable $1,149 at B&H Photo, $150 off. The Verge called the M2 Mac Mini a great value for a desktop computer, with the M2 Pro version functioning like a baby Mac Studio for much less.

The latest Mac Mini comes in a base configuration with Apple's M2 processor or a more powerful configuration with the M2 Pro, and is an excellent upgrade to one of the most affordable yet performant desktop computers you can get, as long as gaming is not your priority. The M2 model starts with 8GB of RAM and a 256GB SSD for $599, while the M2 Pro model features a superior processor plus gigabit ethernet, 16GB of RAM, and 512GB of storage for $1,299. The M2 Pro model also has an expanded port selection, going from two USB-C ports to four — almost like getting an M2 Pro-powered MacBook Pro 14 in desktop form. One caveat applies to the whole line: buying any Mac Mini means providing your own mouse, keyboard, and monitor.
