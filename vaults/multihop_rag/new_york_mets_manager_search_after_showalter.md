# The Mets' Manager Search After Buck Showalter

In his first season as manager Buck Showalter guided the Mets to a 101-61 record, earning the 2022 NL Manager of the Year Award; but despite having baseball's largest budget, the Mets ended 2023 with a disastrous 75-87, Essentially Sports reported on 2023-10-30. Under new president of baseball operations David Stearns, the organization is taking a fresh approach — the man who described 2023 as "the roller coaster of disappointment and hope" presiding over the Mets views his limited options.

Two names are floated. Gabe Kapler, who made his exit three days before the San Francisco Giants season's conclusion, could be a suited candidate. Another likely possibility would be Craig Counsell, who is nearing the end of his contract with the Milwaukee Brewers; Counsell is described as the guy to watch out for that summer because of his excellent relationship with Stearns, who is a former Brewer himself.
