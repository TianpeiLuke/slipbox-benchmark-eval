# Bayern Munich's 2-2 Comeback Draw At RB Leipzig

Bayern Munich struck twice in the second half to come from two goals down and snatch a 2-2 draw at RB Leipzig, but the champions dropped to third in the Bundesliga with their second draw of the season. Goals from Leroy Sané and Harry Kane cancelled out Leipzig's first-half lead; the result left Bayern on 14 points, two behind the leaders, Bayer Leverkusen, while Leipzig sat fifth, a further point behind.

Leipzig struck twice in six minutes, with Loïs Openda putting them in front in the 20th minute when he completed a quick break, then scoring again through Castello Lukeba, who stabbed in after the goalkeeper Sven Ulreich misjudged a corner. The Bavarians' comeback was completed in the 70th minute, with Kane clearing the ball in his own half and Musiala finding Sané for the equaliser.

"Two very different halves from our perspective," the Bayern coach, Thomas Tuchel, said. "In the first we started well but only for the first two minutes and missed that huge chance. We were too slow, made too many mistakes and were not courageous enough. After the break we showed a different mentality. It was a bit wild, with a lot of risk but it is a positive result, not to lose after being 2-0 down. We showed character." Bayern were due to travel to Copenhagen in the Champions League the following week, while Leipzig were to host Manchester City.
