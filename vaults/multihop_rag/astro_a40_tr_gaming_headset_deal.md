# Astro A40 TR Gaming Headset Deal

Engadget's favorite dedicated gaming headset for most people, the Astro A40 TR, came down to $100 at Amazon for Black Friday 2023 — a $30 discount — and the deal bundles the headset with Astro's MixAmp M80, an optional amp that connects to Xbox controllers and provides various audio controls.

Even without the accessory, Engadget likes the open-back A40 for providing a wider soundstage than most gaming headsets. It's not as open or detailed as the Beyerdynamic DT 900 Pro X or Sennheiser HD 560S and it misses more detail in the treble range, but it's immersive, with a slight bass boost that gives in-game effects a little extra boom. The headset as a whole looks "gamer-y," but it's comfortable, and while its built-in mic isn't the clearest tested, it's at least serviceable for basic chat.
