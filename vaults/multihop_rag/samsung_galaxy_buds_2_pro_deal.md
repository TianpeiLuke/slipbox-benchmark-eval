# Samsung Galaxy Buds 2 Pro Deal Prices

The Samsung Galaxy Buds 2 Pro were $160 for Black Friday 2023 with a free $10 Amazon gift card, and $169.99 — $60 off — in early December, positioned throughout as the pick for Samsung and Android users rather than an outright top choice.

The Verge's 7 December 2023 roundup said Samsung's most high-end pair, launched the previous year, is a good option for an Android user or Samsung devotee who wants premium earbuds, with top-notch sound quality even better than their predecessor; Samsung Galaxy phone owners can also take advantage of higher bit rate audio, while everybody gets good active noise cancellation and relatively robust IPX7 sweat and water resistance. It listed them at $169.99 ($60 off) at Amazon, B&H Photo and Best Buy. Wired's 19 October 2023 deals post called them one of its top earbud picks for Samsung owners (9/10, WIRED Recommends), producing excellent sound quality with a super comfortable fit and IPX7 water resistance, but noted they lack the multi-device connectivity Wired likes on the Pixel Buds A-Series — "if you mainly use your earbuds with a Samsung phone, these are a great fit for you".

Engadget's 24 November 2023 Black Friday list put them at $160 with a free $10 Amazon gift card, "a good way from their all-time low — they very briefly fell to $105 last year — but it's still $30 or so less than their average selling price in recent months". Engadget says they are not a top pick in any of its guides but are worth a look for Galaxy phone users, since they quickly pair with and swap between other Samsung devices including TVs and support a higher-quality audio codec on Samsung handsets; more generally they are one of the better-sounding pairs Engadget has tested, with impressive clarity and well-controlled bass, comfortable and waterproof. The drawbacks: no simultaneous connection to two devices, and poor five-hour battery life. Engadget gave them a review score of 86 the previous year.
