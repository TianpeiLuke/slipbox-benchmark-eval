# Bird's E-Bike Relaunch And Delisting

Bird is doing e-bikes again, TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-22 — but instead of the VanMoof knockoff of previous years, Bird has partnered with TradeHubb, an e-bike supplier, and Spring, a strategic retail growth company, to launch the bike.

The move reverses a recent strategy: Bird last year ditched its retail bike offering to focus on shared rides in an attempt to reach profitability. The struggling company was also recently delisted from the stock market. TechCrunch's writer says she tried reaching out to learn more but got no response, and asks readers with a tip to get in touch.
