# Dale Earnhardt Jr's Visit To High Limit's Commonwealth Clash

Dale Earnhardt Jr attended High Limit Racing's Commonwealth Clash at the Lernerville Speedway on a Tuesday, having requested the visit himself and also urged Kyle Larson to take part in the race, which Larson obliged.

Earnhardt Jr and Brad Sweet used to have a working relationship, as Sweet was a development driver for JR Motorsports in the NASCAR Nationwide Series. The 49-year-old was quite happy with his experience after the event, and before the action started he conducted an interview with Larson and Sweet, where he asked about the origins of the competition.
