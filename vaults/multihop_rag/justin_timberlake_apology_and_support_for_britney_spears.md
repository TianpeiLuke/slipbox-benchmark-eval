# Justin Timberlake's Apology And Support For Britney Spears

Justin Timberlake publicly apologised to Britney Spears on Instagram in 2021 after the documentary Framing Britney, which addressed the public scrutiny Spears faced during the 2000s, prompted criticism of how he had behaved following their breakup. Neither had ever acknowledged the rumours about why they split. His apology read: "I've seen the messages, tags, comments, and concerns and I want to respond. I am deeply sorry for the times in my life where my actions contributed to the problem, where I spoke out of turn, or did not speak up for what was right. I understand that I fell short in these moments and in many others and benefited from a system that condones misogyny and racism."

In June 2021 he also backed Spears after she testified publicly in a bid to end the conservatorship controlled by her father, Jamie Spears. At the Los Angeles court hearing, Spears alleged she had been forced to take lithium against her will and claimed the team managing her conservatorship had prevented her from having another baby by refusing to remove her IUD. Timberlake wrote on X, formerly known as Twitter: "After what we saw today, we should all be supporting Britney at this time. Regardless of our past, good and bad, and no matter how long ago it was. What's happening to her is just not right. No woman should ever be restricted from making decisions about her own body."
