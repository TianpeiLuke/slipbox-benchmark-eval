# GameStop Meme Stock Rally, December 2023

GameStop stock rose more than 20% over the five days to Seeking Alpha's December 3, 2023 week-ahead, a move the article placed within renewed volatility across the one-time meme stocks rather than treating it as company-specific news.

The attribution is explicitly sourced: BTIG technical strategist Jonathan Krinsky ascribed the upward trend to renewed buying interest in that group of stocks. The window is narrow and specific — the past five days as of the December 3, 2023 publication — and the explanation offered is a flow-and-sentiment one from a technical strategist, not a fundamental or earnings-driven account of the move.
