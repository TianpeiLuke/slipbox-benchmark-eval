# Elizabeth Debicki on Recreating the Paparazzi Pursuit

Australian actor Elizabeth Debicki, who took over the role of Diana from Emma Corrin in season five, said the scene was "difficult to recreate" — especially with her character being hounded by the paparazzi.

"It was heavy and very manic, and incredibly invasive. And it had a kind of pressure to it," Debicki said in an interview with Netflix. "At times it's almost like an animalistic response to being pursued, by that many actors playing the press, because there's nowhere you can go and you only have to be in a situation like that for about a minute, before you realise this is completely unbearable."
