# The Hallow Prayer App: Contents and Features

Hallow is a prayer app whose defining feature, as Jonathan Roumie describes it, is instant portability: it holds thousands of prayers, and a user can go to their phone and — if they find their mind wandering or their anxiety building — click on the app and start praying or meditating on one of the challenges or one of the prayers, and be instantly transported into a sacred space no matter where they are. No matter whether you live in a major city or somewhere that is just really busy, you can pop on your headphones, connect to the app, start praying with a meditation, and be instantly transported.

The app is not a replacement for Scripture but offers robust access to the Bible. Roumie narrates most of the New Testament in the app, so users have access to the Bible through many works and passages of Scripture, and through other people as well; if a user wants something very specific, it is there.

Beyond prayer and Scripture, the app carries resources for mental health, and it carries music. For Roumie as a musician, music is transportive and nourishing for the spirit — being able to listen to music that gets you in the mindset for prayer and worship. There are, he says, so many options that people have that it is kind of mind-bending.
