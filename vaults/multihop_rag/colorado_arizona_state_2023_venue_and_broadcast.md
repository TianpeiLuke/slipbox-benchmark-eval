# Colorado vs. Arizona State 2023: Venue And Broadcast Details

The Week 6 2023 Colorado-Arizona State game kicked off at 6:30 p.m. ET on Saturday, Oct. 7 at Mountain America Stadium in Tempe, Arizona, on the Pac-12 Network, with streaming through Pac-12 Network or Fubo.

The listing's fields are date (Saturday, Oct. 7), time (6:30 p.m. ET), location (Mountain America Stadium in Tempe, Arizona), TV channel (Pac-12 Network) and live stream (Pac-12 Network, Fubo). The Sporting News added that it would be a sold-out Mountain America Stadium in Tempe.

The access boundary matters: viewing was available only on Pac-12 Network for those who have the cable extension, and streaming the game was available through Fubo, which offers a free trial. The note covers scheduling and distribution only, not the outcome.
