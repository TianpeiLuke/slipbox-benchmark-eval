# Tech Layoffs In April 2023

April 2023's largest announced tech cut was Meta's: on April 18 the company said it expected to lay off 10,000 jobs in the coming months, on top of the 11,000 jobs cut in November.

The month opened with Bloomberg reporting on April 3 that Apple was laying off a small number of roles on its corporate retail teams. Redfin announced on April 13 that it had laid off 201 employees, about 4% of its workforce — the third time the Seattle-based real estate company had reduced its workforce since June. Lyft announced on April 21 that employees would learn whether they had a job via an email to be sent out on April 27; as promised, the April 27 layoffs affected 26% of the workforce, about 1,072 people. On April 25 Anthemis Group announced it would lay off 16 people, 28% of employees, and Rapid — previously known as RapidAPI — laid off 50% of its staff, believed to have impacted 115 people. On April 26 Amazon announced it was shutting down its Halo Health division effective July 31, among other divisions; those layoffs are part of the 9,000 employees announced in March, and including the 18,000-person layoffs announced in January this brings the total to 27,000 job cuts, or 8% of Amazon's corporate workforce that year. On April 27 Dropbox announced it would lay off 500 employees, 16% of staff, and Clubhouse announced it had laid off more than 50% of staff; a Clubhouse spokesperson declined to comment on the number of people impacted or the number remaining, though founder Paul Davison had told TechCrunch the previous October that Clubhouse had close to 100 employees.
