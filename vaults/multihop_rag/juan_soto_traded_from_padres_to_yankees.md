# Juan Soto Traded From The Padres To The Yankees

The Padres reportedly sent Juan Soto to the Yankees in exchange for four pitchers and catcher Kyle Higashioka on Wednesday, Dec. 6, 2023, according to the New York Post's Joel Sherman, as carried in Sporting News' winter meetings tracker.

Soto, the biggest star on the trade market, immediately added a potent left-handed bat to a lineup sorely in need of some thump from the left side, and provided much-needed support to 2022 AL MVP Aaron Judge.

Earlier that day MLB Network's Jon Morosi reported the trade was "close to being finalized," with right-handers Michael King and Drew Thorpe expected to highlight the return to San Diego and the package to include at least two more players, neither of which would be Alex Verdugo. Sherman also reported that the Yankees would not just be getting one outfielder from the Padres: Trent Grisham would also likely be on the move to New York. Grisham enjoyed a pair of breakout seasons to start his career in San Diego and had twice earned Gold Gloves, in 2020 and 2022, but his offensive production had dwindled, with him batting under .200 in back-to-back seasons.
