# Week 12's Top Fantasy Running Backs

Sporting News' Week 12 standard-league running back rankings, published Nov. 21, 2023, put Christian McCaffrey at Seattle first as always — the most matchup-proof running back in the NFL — with Austin Ekeler, Saquon Barkley, the Detroit pair of David Montgomery and Jahmyr Gibbs, and Raheem Mostert making up the rest of the top group.

Ekeler, facing the Ravens, ranks right behind McCaffrey and will look to bounce back after an uncharacteristically rough showing in Green Bay the previous week. Barkley, facing the Patriots, looked as good as he had all season against the Commanders with 140 total yards and two touchdowns; since the Patriots have surrendered 116 scrimmage yards per game and nine total touchdowns to running backs this season, Sporting News likes Barkley to pick up where he left off — and to drive up his free-agency value.

Montgomery and Gibbs against the Packers earn a rare exception to the rule that running mates seldom both crack the top five: entering Week 11, Green Bay had surrendered nearly 140 all-purpose yards per game to running backs, and between Detroit's offensive line, the Lions' high-flying offensive attack and the ruthless 1-2 punch the pair has been this season, the feast could be on very early for Dan Campbell's team. Mostert at the Jets should continue to eat for Miami with rookie De'Von Achane hurt at the knee almost immediately after returning from IR; Gang Green has not been nearly as good against the run as against the pass, with Jets opponents averaging 139 rushing yards per game over the past two weeks. With backup Salvon Ahmed also on IR with a foot injury, Sporting News suggests stashing Jeff Wilson Jr. as a handcuff.
