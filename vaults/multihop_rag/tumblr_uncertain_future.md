# Tumblr's Uncertain Future

Tumblr, one of the most interesting social networks on the internet, appears to be in trouble: it is not dying, but it is not not dying either.

Whatever happens next, this state of affairs is a bummer for a lot of reasons — including that Tumblr promised to work with ActivityPub, which would have been a big win for the fediverse, and that it is still maybe the web's best and most versatile posting tool.

Matt Mullenweg, the CEO of Automattic, which owns Tumblr, spent a bunch of time this week answering people's questions about the future of Tumblr, and it paints a sad but interesting picture of what it really takes to build a better social network. As the writer puts it, it is all making him root for Mastodon even harder.
