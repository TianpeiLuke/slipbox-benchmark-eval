# Creature Commandos Animated Series

Creature Commandos is a seven-episode adult animated series with James Gunn listed as writer, part of DC's slate as reported by Polygon on 2023-10-24. It will also include Suicide Squad head Amanda Waller, who this time "creates a black ops team out of monstrous prisoners."

The team will include Frankenstein's monster — here called Eric Frankenstein and voiced by David Harbour — a character with a long, complicated DC history of his own.

The series is set to star Frank Grillo of Prison Break as Rick Flag Sr., Maria Bakalova of Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3 as Princess Ilana Rostovic, Indira Varma of Game of Thrones as the Bride of Frankenstein, and Alan Tudyk of Harley Quinn as Doctor Phosphorus.
