# Apple AirPods Max Holiday Deal

The over-ear AirPods Max, normally far too expensive for Engadget to recommend, came down to $450 at Amazon and Best Buy over the 2023 holiday sales — described in the Apple roundups as a new low price and $30 cheaper than Prime Day in October, and in the headphone roundup as $20 more than the lowest price seen but about $30 below the pair's usual street price. They were $519 at B&H Photo, and Apple itself sells the headphones for $550.

The Apple deal roundups praise them as well-designed and comfortable, implementing all of the helpful Siri integrations, with great noise cancellation and Spatial Audio; while they're a little heavy, the fit is still comfortable and the on-device controls, though unique, are simple and "a joy to use." The headphone roundup is more qualified: the AirPods Max are still overpriced for most even with this discount, but remain undeniably premium, with top-tier ANC, a superbly balanced sound profile, a high-quality aluminum design, reliable controls and all the usual AirPods features — though they're hefty and their included "case" is bafflingly flimsy. Engadget gave the AirPods Max a review score of 84 in late 2020.
