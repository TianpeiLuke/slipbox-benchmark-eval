# Heat Index and Humidex Mapping Method

Rather than using satellite data to estimate land surface temperatures as previous urban heat studies had done, Chakraborty and colleagues assessed heat stress using the U.S. National Weather Service heat index and the Canadian meteorological service's humidity index (humidex), combining air temperature and humidity to better describe how the heat feels.

Using models that combine these variables was a more accurate way to catalogue the heat stress of US cities over the period 2014 to 2018.
