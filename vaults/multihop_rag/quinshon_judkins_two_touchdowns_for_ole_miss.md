# Quinshon Judkins' Two Touchdowns For Ole Miss

Quinshon Judkins scored both of Ole Miss's touchdowns against Georgia on Nov. 11, 2023, running in a 15-yarder at 7:23 p.m. and a four-yarder at 7:59 p.m.

The first came on the Rebels' opening drive: at 7:22 p.m. Judkins got into the end zone but holding was called on Ole Miss, giving them the ball at Georgia's 15; a minute later he tried again and it was good, putting the Rebels on the board with a 15-yard touchdown run. The first quarter ended Ole Miss 7, Georgia 14.

The second tied the game early in the second quarter at 7:59 p.m., when Judkins ran it in for four yards; there was a flag on the play, but it was on the Georgia defense for having 12 players on the field.
