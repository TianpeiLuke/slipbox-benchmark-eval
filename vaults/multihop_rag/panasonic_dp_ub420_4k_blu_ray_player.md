# Panasonic DP-UB420 4K Blu-ray Player

Streaming has become the de facto way to watch movies, but Polygon argues that sometimes you want the reliability and quality of a physical disc — and recommends the Panasonic DP-UB420 as a dedicated 4K Blu-ray player that will not break the bank.

Its coverage is the selling point: the model handles everything from CDs and DVDs to 3D Blu-ray and 4K Blu-ray Discs, so it should cover an entire physical media collection. One of the DP-UB420-K's most compelling features is its HDR Optimizer, which once set up tweaks your content to look its best on your TV, whether that is a high-end OLED screen or a run-of-the-mill LCD panel.
