# Polite Society (Nida Manzoor)

Polite Society is Nida Manzoor's genre-hopping film starring Priya Kansara, Ritu Arya, and Nimra Bucha, ranked 32 on Polygon's best movies of 2023 list of 2023-10-07 and available on Prime Video or for digital rental/purchase.

Ria, played by Priya Kansara, dreams of being a stuntwoman and loves her sister deeply. So when her sister drops out of art school and gets engaged to some jabroni she has known for a month, Ria plans a wedding heist to rescue her from what is surely a fate worse than death.

Polygon's Zosha Millman writes that the film vaults between genres and tones and makes it all look easy, melding wedding-prep comedy with Ria's action thrill ride, and that although Ria's story is singular, everyone can relate to the enthusiasm and care she brings to her life — even if the rest of us are still trying to nail our flying reverse spinning kick.
