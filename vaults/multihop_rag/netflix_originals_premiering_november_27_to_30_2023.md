# Netflix Originals Premiering November 27 To 30, 2023

Twelve Netflix originals close out Polygon's November 2023 listing, each with Netflix's own synopsis.

Go Dog Go: Season 4, a Netflix Family title: Tag and her best friend Scooch love lending a paw to their friends around Pawston, whether delivering cookie boxes or hosting the Dogcathalon. Comedy Royale, a Netflix Series: hand-picked by Korean comedy icons, five teams of rising comics battle for a chance to host a Netflix show. Love Like a K-Drama, a Netflix Series: four actresses from Japan go to South Korea to audition and act alongside Korean actors in a series of love stories. Onmyoji, a Netflix Anime: in the gilded enclave of the imperial court, a gifted mystic befriends a brilliant musician and together they solve cases rising from the demonic realm. Verified Stand-Up, a Netflix Comedy: 10 comics, 10 sets, one show, filmed at Webster Hall in New York City across two episodes, featuring Asif Ali, Dulcé Sloan, Gianmarco Soresi, Isiah Kelly, Leslie Liao, Nimesh Patel, Robby Hoffman, Rosebud Baker, Sabrina Wu, and Vanessa Gonzalez. American Symphony, a Netflix Documentary, is listed without a synopsis. Bad Surgeon: Love Under the Knife, a Netflix Documentary: a shocking docuseries about one of the biggest frauds in modern medical history, exposing a thoracic surgeon famous for groundbreaking achievements in regenerative medicine. The Bad Guys: A Very Bad Holiday, a Netflix Family title: Mr. Wolf and his crew of animal outlaws realize they will need to restore the city's Christmas spirit to keep their annual holiday heist afloat. Family Switch, a Netflix Film: Jess and Bill Walker are trying to keep their family connected as their children grow older and more distant, until a chance encounter with an astrological reader causes a full body switch on the morning of the most important day of each of their lives — starring Jennifer Garner, Ed Helms, Emma Myers and Brady Noon, directed by McG and based on the book "Bedtime For Mommy" by Amy Krouse-Rosenthal. Hard Days, a Netflix Film: already running from a mess of problems, a desperate cop thinks he has gotten away with a hit-and-run, but there was a witness and they have got his number. Obliterated, a Netflix Series: an elite special forces team must thwart a deadly threat in Las Vegas in a high-octane action-comedy from the creators of "Cobra Kai." Virgin River: Season 5 Part 2: Mel adjusts to a different pace of life, Jack works to grow his business, and the town faces new threats as secrets begin to surface.
