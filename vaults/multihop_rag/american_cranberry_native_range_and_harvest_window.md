# The American Cranberry's Native Range And Harvest Window

The American cranberry is one of the few fruits native to the northern United States, growing for millennia amid the sandy soils and short summers of southeastern Massachusetts and Cape Cod, FOX News Lifestyle reported on 23 November 2023.

Its discriminating constraints are seasonal and geographic: it has only a brief autumn harvest, from late September to early November, and a short shelf life.

The boundary condition on availability follows from that: fresh cranberries were known only in a few areas of the nation that possess the right combination of sandy soil, long summer days and long winter dormancy.
