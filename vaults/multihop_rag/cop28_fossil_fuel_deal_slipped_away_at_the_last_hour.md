# The COP28 Fossil Fuel Deal That Slipped Away At The Eleventh Hour

What would have been a historic deal to tackle a planetary crisis slipped out of reach at the eleventh hour at COP28 — even though climate-vulnerable countries and environmental advocates scored some key wins with clean energy after heated negotiations wrapped up in the United Arab Emirates, a top oil- and gas-producing country.

This was the closest yet that countries have come to striking a global deal to phase out the use of coal, oil, and gas. But the summit was arguably still a home game for fossil fuel interests, who threw their weight around the United Nations climate conference — the 28th Conference of the Parties, or COP28 — where tens of thousands of delegates and activists from nearly every nation on Earth gathered over two weeks to wrangle over the future of fossil fuels. The Verge's December 13th piece surveyed the biggest decisions made in Dubai now that the dust had settled, decisions that could determine how the world is powered in future.
