# Wolf Pack, The Palestinian Biometric Database

In 2021, the Washington Post revealed the existence of a vast database called Wolf Pack, which contained images of, and all the information available on, all 3 million Palestinians in the West Bank.

That revelation is offered as the reason Palestinians' fears about camera surveillance are not unfounded, whether or not any given camera is running facial recognition.

As Matthew Mahmoudi, an Amnesty International researcher on AI and human rights, put it, Palestinians "don't need to see" that the cameras are employing facial recognition to "know that they are being watched at every turn."
