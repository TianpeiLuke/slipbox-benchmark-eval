# SBF Arrest and Pretrial Detention

[Sam Bankman-Fried](sam_bankman_fried.md) was arrested in December 2022 in the Bahamas, where [FTX](ftx.md) was based, and was extradited to the United States to face criminal charges. He was released on a $250 million bail bond and remained under house arrest at his parents' home in Palo Alto.

That bail was revoked in August 2023 after he was accused of intimidating [Alameda Research](alameda_research.md)'s former CEO Caroline Ellison by leaking her private diary.

In January 2023 he pleaded not guilty to all counts, which include wire fraud, conspiracy to commit money laundering and conspiracy to misuse customer funds. He faces up to 115 years in jail if convicted on all charges.
