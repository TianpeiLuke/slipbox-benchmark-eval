# Solar Cycle Maximum Forecast Revision

The major news about the sun in 2023 was that the solar maximum is arriving faster and will be more explosive than initially forecast: the peak would likely arrive by 2024 and be more active than the original prediction, and in October space weather scientists released an updated forecast for the current solar cycle that is more in line with what experts told Live Science.

When the current solar cycle began in 2019, forecasts from space weather scientists suggested that the solar maximum would arrive sometime from 2025 onward and be relatively weak compared with past solar cycles. Numerous warning signs early in the year — rising sunspot numbers, powerful X-class solar flares and changes in Earth's upper atmosphere — hinted that the solar cycle was not progressing as expected, and in June Live Science published an exclusive, in-depth feature on the upcoming solar maximum that laid out the revised picture. During the solar maximum the sun is much more chaotic than during the solar minimum. When the maximum does arrive, Earth can expect to be bombarded with more solar storms, resulting in frequent auroral displays, radio blackouts, potential satellite problems and disruptions to the migration patterns of some animals.
