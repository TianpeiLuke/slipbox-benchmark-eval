# Gemini Pro's Translation And Multilingual Weakness

Translation did not appear to be Gemini Pro's strong suit in early December 2023 tests: it struggled to give a six-letter word in French. Benjamin Netter posted an example on X on 6 December 2023 with the verdict "FYI, Google Gemini is complete trash."

When the TechCrunch reporter ran the same prompt through Bard — "Can you give me a 6-letters word in French?" — Gemini Pro responded with a seven-letter word instead of a five-letter one, which he took as giving some credence to the reports about Gemini's poor multilingual performance.
