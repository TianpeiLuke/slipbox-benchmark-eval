# Spiritfarer

Spiritfarer is a melancholy game in which you play as Stella the ferrymaster, who guides spirits into the afterlife, meeting wonderful characters and helping them find closure. It appears in Polygon's 2023-10-20 list of the 35 best couch co-op games for Nintendo Switch.

The game combines exploration on the seas with management sim elements, as you build housing, kitchens, and more, and learn to farm, cook, and craft.

In co-op, player two is Stella's cat Daffodil, who can do most management sim tasks — she just cannot talk to characters, and thus cannot trigger quests.
