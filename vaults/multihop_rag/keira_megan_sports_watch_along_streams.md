# Keira Megan's Sports Watch-Along Streams

U.K.-based Keira Megan, who goes by keirameganxx on Twitch and was previously part of the Sports Accelerator Program, has been on the platform for about a year and a half with a focus on soccer and motorsports like Formula 1. Polygon reported this on 2023-10-20, noting that while Formula 1 has always been primarily popular in Europe, it has seen growth among new fans in the United States in recent years thanks in part to docuseries like Netflix's Drive to Survive.

She does not stream actual races or games because of broadcast rights; instead she hosts watch-along streams for fans who want to hear her commentary.

Megan says that although she does not have as many followers on Twitch as on her YouTube channel, there are elements of Twitch she prefers, including the ability to be herself and engage with fans more closely. "People want to know what you want to say," she says. "They come into my Twitch channel and ask me about the race. They asked me three days later about a [soccer] match. They want to talk about it. I think Twitch is good for that. I think it's good for one-on-one. I find that Twitch is where I'm the most engaged with my audiences, and they feel closest to me."
