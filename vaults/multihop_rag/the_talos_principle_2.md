# The Talos Principle 2

The Talos Principle 2, developed by Croteam for PlayStation 5, Windows PC, and Xbox Series X, is ranked 45 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31.

Like its predecessor, it tackles grand science fiction ideas, particularly about what it means to be human — a theme thoroughly explored in this installment, set in a post-human society of AI-powered robots carrying on human culture and civilization — and it is again replete with philosophical commentary and references to famous artists and thinkers. Critic Nicole Clark's favorite is a riff on Werner Herzog's famous quote about birds: "The enormity of their flat brain. The enormity of their stupidity is just overwhelming."

But, Clark writes, if you are like her you are playing because you are an absolute freak for light refraction, gravity, and geospatial puzzles: there are so many puzzles in this game, including a puzzle metagame spread across its gorgeous map, and each is excellent, teaching you new concepts before refracting them and forcing you to think differently.
