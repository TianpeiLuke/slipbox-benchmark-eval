# Home Depot And Lowe's Price Match Policies

The Home Depot will match the price of identical, in-stock products from other retailers, except for membership-based wholesalers. To claim it, bring the ad or listing with you in-store to the register. Two limits apply: the policy does not cover special events like Black Friday, and The Home Depot will only honor requests submitted by the original buyer — no friends or family allowed.

Lowe's will match the price of identical, in-stock products when you present an ad, product listing, or photo from a qualifying online or local retailer, including Amazon, Best Buy, Target, and Walmart. To request a match, head to the store, call 1-877-465-6937, or chat online. The product must have been purchased directly from the retailer and not a third-party seller; the policy does not apply to special sales like Black Friday and other holiday promotions; and Lowe's does not match prices from one Lowe's store to another. The Verge published these summaries on October 25th, 2023.
