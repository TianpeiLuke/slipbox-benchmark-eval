# Marshall's Frisco Bowl Profile: Pennington Starting, Ali Carrying The Offense

The toughest blow for Marshall in the Frisco Bowl was being without second-year starting quarterback Cam Fancher, who had entered the transfer portal; instead Marshall started freshman Cole Pennington, the son of former Marshall star QB Chad Pennington, per Sporting News on Dec. 18, 2023.

Pennington had thrown six interceptions and zero touchdowns on only 79 passing attempts that season. Marshall's offense would continue to run through star running back Rasheen Ali: the senior speedster had carried the offense even when Fancher was under center, totaling 1,256 yards and 15 touchdowns on the season. The offense had struggled despite Ali's efforts, ranking 93rd in scoring and 89th in yards per game.

On the season Marshall was 4-8 against the spread and 6-6 on the over/under board, and the Herd won only three of their eight conference games.
