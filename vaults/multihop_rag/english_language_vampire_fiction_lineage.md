# The Lineage Of English-Language Vampire Fiction

Vampire fiction has a long and distinguished pedigree in English, dating from the moment Robert Southey introduced the British Isles to the concept of the coffin-sleeping undead at the turn of the 19th century.

Polygon's organising claim about that lineage is that it tracks its readership: from that starting point onward, the authors of vampiric stories have mirrored the changing face of the society that reads them.
