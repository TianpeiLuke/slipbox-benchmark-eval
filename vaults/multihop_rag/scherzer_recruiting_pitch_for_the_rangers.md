# Scherzer's Recruiting Pitch For The Rangers

Rangers pitcher Max Scherzer made a public recruiting pitch for Shohei Ohtani on Nov. 21, 2023 as a guest on Foul Territory, summed up in one line: "Money's great, but winning's better."

Sporting News noted this was not necessarily a rumor but an example of how much players would love for Ohtani to join their team; the clip of Scherzer sharing his pitch for Ohtani to join the Rangers was posted by Foul Territory on November 20, 2023.
