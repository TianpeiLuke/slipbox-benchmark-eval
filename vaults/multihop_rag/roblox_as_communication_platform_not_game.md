# Roblox As A Communication Platform, Not A Game

Roblox CEO David Baszucki argued onstage at the Code Conference, in an interview with Verge deputy editor Alex Heath published October 12th, 2023, that Roblox should be understood as a communication and connection platform rather than a games company, because "this type of technology is bigger than gaming." Asked why he was not content to go after the entire gaming market — bigger than the music and movie industries combined — he said the company's publicly shared goal is to reach a billion daily active people, and that its vision goes almost to the future of communication.

The grounds are a lineage and a set of examples. Baszucki placed Roblox in the evolution of the mail system, the telegraph system, the phone system, and, as seen in covid, the video system, arguing that inevitably there is a generation beyond that: immersive 3D communication. His examples span playing together, trying to graduate from high school together during covid, a simulation of the Roblox office inside Roblox where staff come together for serendipitous events, and going to a concert. Gaming, on this account, is a part of it — side by side with concerts and working together — which is why the company has evolved to what he called a utility vision of the platform. Roblox is determined to be a platform even more than a product, something users can develop games and experiences within.

He resisted the metaverse label without conceding a change of direction. Asked by content creator Neil Shankar about a brand marketing push, apparently by the sports division, to distance Roblox from metaverse connotations — Shankar's own introduction to metaverse concepts came from Matthew Ball's essays, in which Roblox was largely the center — Baszucki said he would not call it distancing but an evolution of terminology: the company has always used "human co-experience" or "bringing people together," the metaverse context was coined with the book Snow Crash long ago and has ebbed through various flows, and Roblox has never really used the term much and will probably always think of itself as a communication and connection platform.
