# Melbourne CBD Confidence And The New Fair Work Ombudsman On Wage Underpayment

"This is as strong a level of confidence in the market that we have seen since before COVID," said The Leasing Agency group managing director Terence Yeh, as the return of international students and a growing number of apartments in inner-city Melbourne provided a shot in the arm for CBD hospitality businesses.

The same wrap reported that corporate leaders caught underpaying staff should expect zero sympathy from new Fair Work Ombudsman Anna Booth, who believes chief executives and board directors are directly responsible for any failures to pay correct wages.
