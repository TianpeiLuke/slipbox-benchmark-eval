# Kyler Murray Cardinals Hot Seat

Yardbarker's Nov. 30, 2023 NFC hot-seat roundup named quarterback Kyler Murray as the Arizona Cardinals' hottest seat: the inconsistent Murray had five more games that season to prove to a new front office and coaching staff that he was the long-term answer at quarterback.

The grounds for the pressure are the alternative available to Arizona: if he did not show them what they wanted to see, the Cardinals had the draft capital — including two first-round picks — to land a franchise quarterback at the top of the QB-rich 2024 NFL Draft. The warrant is that a team with both a new decision-making regime and the ammunition to replace a quarterback has little reason to keep an inconsistent incumbent.
