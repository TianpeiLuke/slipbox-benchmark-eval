# Surfshark VPN Black Friday Deal

Surfshark's Black Friday 2023 deal knocks up to 86 percent off two-year plans, with a few extra months of service free depending on the plan selected.

Engadget considers Surfshark one of the best VPN services available right now, particularly for a solid VPN at an affordable price, and believes the mid-tier plan, Surfshark One, will be best for most people: a two-year plan came down to $2.69 per month, with an additional four months of service thrown in for free.
