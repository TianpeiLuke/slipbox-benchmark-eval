# Atumtek Bluetooth Selfie Tripod

News writer Jess Weatherbed's pick solves the group-photo problem: on holiday with a partner or family you generally get stuck with three options — a cramped selfie, leaving someone out to take the picture, or asking a total stranger to take it for you. She would rather not ruin a vacation working out which nearby individuals are least likely to dip the minute she hands over her phone, and finds it depressing that her mum is in so few family photos because she was always on the other side of the camera.

A decent Bluetooth-enabled tripod resolves these issues, and she has had good experiences with Atumtek's 60-inch Self-Stick Tripod. It features a discreet, detachable Bluetooth shutter remote you can pair with your smartphone, sparing you from having to set a timer and run like hell: get into position and use the remote to snap as many shots as you need to catch a flattering angle.

It also extends up to 60 inches to squeeze everyone into a group selfie if you are using it as a selfie stick. She suggests it could be a brilliant gift for any "Instagram boyfriends" who spend hours of their vacations as their partner's dedicated paparazzi.
