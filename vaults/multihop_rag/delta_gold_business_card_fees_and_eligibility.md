# Delta Gold Business Card Fees And Eligibility

The Delta SkyMiles Gold Business American Express Card charges a $99 annual fee with a $0 intro annual fee for the first year, no foreign transaction fee, and a purchase APR of 20.99%–29.99% variable; to qualify, cardholders should aim for a "good" FICO credit score of 690 or better.

Fortune's verdict on whether the fee is worth it is conditional: the $99 annual fee may be worth it for business owners who frequently travel with Delta, because perks like a free checked bag could easily cover it. Credit score is not the only gate — card issuers look at other factors like income, too. The article carries an explicit currency caveat: card details are accurate as of the publish date of 2023-12-12 but are subject to change at any time at the discretion of the issuer, and readers should contact the card issuer to verify rates, fees, and benefits before applying.
