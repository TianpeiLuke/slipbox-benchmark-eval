# Nicolas Jackson's First Premier League Hat-Trick At Tottenham

Nicolas Jackson scored the first Premier League hat-trick of his career at Tottenham, striking a 75th-minute winner and then twice in injury time as Chelsea came from behind to win at manager Mauricio Pochettino's former home ground, where the Argentinean had managed Spurs between 2014 and 2019. The Senegal forward's treble was also his first hat-trick as a Chelsea player and wrapped up a clinical victory.

Jackson had pushed for his third goal of the campaign earlier, with his 58th-minute shot punched away by Vicario, who then blocked Palmer's rebound shot. Chelsea continued to press and eventually got their reward as Sterling broke forward up the right flank at the start of the 75th minute and found Jackson unmarked to rifle his pass into the left corner, putting the Blues ahead.

Jackson rounded off Tottenham's nightmare evening a minute later as he turned Gallagher's pass into the net, then completed his hat-trick two minutes after that. The 22-year-old first turned in Connor Gallagher's 94th-minute pass, followed by a clinical strike from Palmer's pass in the 97th minute, which secured his first Premier League hat-trick.
