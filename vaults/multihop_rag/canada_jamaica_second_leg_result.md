# Jamaica 3-2 Canada: The Reggae Boyz Advance On Away Goals

Jamaica's first-ever victory on Canadian soil saw the Reggae Boyz fight back to reach the CONCACAF Nations League semifinal and qualify for Copa America 2024, a 3-2 win in the second leg at BMO Field in Toronto sending them through on away goals after a 4-4 aggregate tie. The match kicked off at 7:30 p.m. local (7:30 p.m. ET / 4:30 p.m. PT) on Nov. 21, 2023 with Cesar Ramos of Mexico refereeing. Canada scored through Alphonso Davies (25) and Ismael Kone (69); Jamaica through Shamar Nicholson (63 and 66) and Bobby De Cordova-Reid (78).

It looked like a comfortable night for Canada when Davies opened the scoring on 25 minutes after a lung-bursting run, his shot saved onto the crossbar before the ball spun in on the goal line, giving the hosts a 3-1 aggregate lead; but for Jamaican stopper Andre Blake, who made two great saves from Cyle Larin before that opener, there could easily have been a couple more. The home side could not get a second, and Jamaica grew in confidence, particularly after half-time.

In three second-half minutes the tie was blown apart by two Nicholson goals — a Canadian mistake handing him the first on a plate, then Demarai Gray's individual brilliance setting up a tap-in for the second. At 2-1 on the night and 3-3 on aggregate the match looked destined for extra time and penalties, but Jamaica could barely contemplate glory before Kone's header made it 2-2 and restored Canada's single-goal aggregate advantage. Canada appeared to have weathered the storm until first-leg hero Stephen Eustaquio's handball in the box let De Cordova-Reid score the winning penalty with 12 minutes of regulation to play. Jamaica held on through nine minutes of stoppage time despite Gray being sent off for a second yellow card for dissent on 84 minutes.

The consequence for Canada is a further hurdle: they will have to defeat Trinidad and Tobago in a one-off playoff in March to reach the Copa America 2024 tournament to be hosted in the U.S.
