# Florida State's Exclusion And Jordan Travis' Injury

The debate over Florida State's exclusion from the 2023 College Football Playoff would not have arisen had star quarterback Jordan Travis not broken his leg two weeks earlier, The New York Times reported on 3 December 2023.

The Seminoles team that beat Louisville 16-6 in the ACC Championship Game was relying on a third-string quarterback, and the win was far from impressive. Knowing it was going to unjustly break someone's heart, the committee decided in that room on Saturday night to break Florida State's, concluding the Seminoles weren't good enough.

Travis posted his own reaction on 3 December 2023: "devastated. heartbroken. In so much disbelief rn, I wish my leg broke earlier in the season so y'all could see this team is much more than the quarterback. I thought results matter. 13-0 and this roster matches up across any team in those top 4 rankings. I am so sorry. Go Noles!"
