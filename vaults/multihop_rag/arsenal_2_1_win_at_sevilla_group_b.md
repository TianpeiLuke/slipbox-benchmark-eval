# Arsenal's 2-1 Win At Sevilla In Group B

Arsenal beat Sevilla 2-1 at the Sánchez-Pizjuán on 24 October 2023 to go back to the top of Group B, surviving a last kick of the game on 97 minutes when Lucas Ocampos, with the ball sitting up and the hosts 2-1 down, sliced his shot past David Raya's left hand and into the stand.

Mikel Arteta said his side were forced to play many games in one — controlling when it was called for, holding on when they had to, and slipping in the knife when the opportunity arose. Ocampos' miss left Sevilla facing the prospect of another Europa League adventure and left Arsenal's future in the continent's premier competition in their own hands.

As Ocampos sat on the turf the stadium fell quiet, except for the 3,000 Arsenal fans high in the corner who had watched what their coach described as another step in the team's development and "a brutal experience". Arteta called it a victory of merit and a demonstration of the emotional management he had demanded, with Declan Rice and William Saliba among those who impressed — though none shone brighter than Gabriel Jesus.
