# Archie Roberts AFL Draft Prospect 2023

Archie Roberts, profiled in Kevin Sheehan's top 40 for the 2023 AFL Draft as reported by The Roar on Nov. 15, 2023, is a left-footed half-back who is a penetrating kick, a smart interceptor, and uses his anticipation and clean hands to have an impact. State: Victoria Metro. State League Club: Sandringham Dragons. Community Club: Hampton Rovers. Date of Birth: 18/11/05. Height: 184cm.

He showed good rebounding speed and fine decision-making ability to set up play from defence for Victoria Metro during the National Championships, where he averaged 20.8 disposals and 3.8 marks to earn All Australian honours, before becoming a back-to-back premiership player for the Sandringham Dragons in the Coates Talent League. His impressive time of 6:08 for the 2km time trial at the national Draft Combine gave a glimpse of his elite endurance and competitiveness. Weapon: Clean hands and ball use. AFL player comparison: Daniel Rioli (Richmond).
