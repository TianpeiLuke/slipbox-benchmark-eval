# X's Response To The EU

X's answer to the EU's warning came from CEO Linda Yaccarino, in a letter released in response to the warning earlier that week, saying a leadership group had been convened to consider X's response and that "tens of thousands" of pieces of content had been removed, along with "thousands" of posts and "hundreds" of accounts linked to terrorist groups, violence or extremism. She also said the company formerly known as Twitter is responding to law enforcement requests, but claimed it had not received any requests from Europol at the time.

The company gave the press nothing comparable. TechCrunch contacted X for a response to the EU's warnings about its DSA compliance and, at press time on 10 October 2023, the company had not responded — beyond firing back its usual automated reply, which reads: "Busy now, please check back later."
