# Keeping Cancer Fundraising Enjoyable

Tom Scalera's claim is that making the event fun was the key to fundraising for metastatic breast cancer: "Putting the fun in it was always the key."

The ground is the difficulty of the subject matter itself. "We've been very connected to the Mombies since Rebecca's diagnosis," Scalera said of his wife. "It's such a heavy topic and it's hard to create an event that people want to be a part of and enjoy where, at the same time, they are helping to fight a deadly disease that impacts so many women. But putting the fun in it was always the key."

The warrant is that this was the founder's own intent for the foundation, which makes the Mombies its ideal expression: the Mombies are focused on fun, and "it's all that Rebecca ever wanted for this foundation," Scalera said. "The Mombies are the perfect personification of that."
