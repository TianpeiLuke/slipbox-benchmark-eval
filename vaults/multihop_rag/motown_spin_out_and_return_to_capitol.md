# Motown's Spin-Out And Return To Capitol

Motown was spun out of Capitol Music Group to become a standalone frontline label in early 2021, and was spun back into CMG at the top of 2023. The interviewer notes that the return came with an inevitable downsizing process.

Michelle Jubelirer's assessment as of December 2023 is that Motown is once again fully unified within CMG's culture after being separate from the company for a couple of years, and that CMG has strengthened its longtime partnership with Quality Control, with whom it continues to share tremendous success and plans for a big future.
