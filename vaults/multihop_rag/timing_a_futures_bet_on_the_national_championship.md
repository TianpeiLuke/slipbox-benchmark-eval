# When To Place A Futures Bet On The National Championship

Sporting News argues that choosing when to bet on the NCAA football national championship comes down to risk versus reward, with no timing that gives both the best odds and the best information.

The grounds are two opposing effects of waiting. Making your bets when the lines are first released gives you the best odds and potential winnings, but with precious little information — you might back a team that is clearly not going to win by Week 4. The longer you wait, the clearer the picture becomes thanks to data and team performance, but the odds tighten up because sportsbooks are using that same information to make adjustments. The warrant joining them is that sportsbooks price on the same information the bettor gains, so information and price move together: you will have a more educated choice, but the odds will be less favorable and your payout smaller.
