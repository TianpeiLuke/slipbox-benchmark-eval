# The Final Overs Of The T20 Series Clinch

England took two late wickets and pushed the decider into its final over, but West Indies got home with nine needed from six balls after Shai Hope had kept the requirement below a run a ball.

Reece Topley had Rovman Powell caught for 8 at short third man by Rashid, a smart low catch to his left, leaving West Indies 113-5 and needing 20 from 21 balls, with the live blog noting England had not given this up. In the 18th over Buttler gambled by continuing with the relatively expensive Rehan Ahmed and it did not come off: Hope skimmed a back cut between short third and backward point for four to move the requirement back below a run a ball, making it very hard to see how England could win. Sam Curran then conceded only one run from the first four balls of a superb penultimate over — with a short break while England called for a dry towel as the ball got very wet — leaving 10 needed from eight balls; Andre Russell then slugged a low full toss straight down the throat of Livingstone at long-on for 3, and England, behind the game throughout the innings, were again dreaming of an improbable victory with West Indies needing 10 from 7. Jason Holder was almost run out first ball of the 19th over, taking a very dodgy single to mid-off and finishing well short when Curran just failed to gather Rashid's throw, leaving nine to win from six balls with Chris Woakes to bowl; Holder then inside-edged Woakes wide of leg stump for three, which would have been four but for an outstanding stop from Rehan Ahmed, putting West Indies 127-6. The Guardian's report judged the outcome had seemed inevitable from the start: with such a meagre target West Indies needed little more than to keep their heads, and though both Topley and Curran conceded only two with their final overs to push the contest into its final over, that never looked in doubt.
