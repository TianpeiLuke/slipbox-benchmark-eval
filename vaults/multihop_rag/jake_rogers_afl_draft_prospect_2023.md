# Jake Rogers AFL Draft Prospect 2023

Jake Rogers, profiled in Kevin Sheehan's top 40 for the 2023 AFL Draft as reported by The Roar on Nov. 15, 2023, is a tenacious livewire through the midfield — a Gold Coast Suns Academy member who is a one-touch player with great vision and creativity. State: Queensland. State League Club: Gold Coast Suns Academy. Community Club: Broadbeach. Date of Birth: 02/03/05. Height: 170cm.

He showed all those traits at the National Championships by starring for the Allies, where he consistently set up his team through the middle of the ground. Quick and with elite agility, he was always in the contest, averaging 22.5 disposals at 77 percent efficiency, 5.5 clearances and 5.2 tackles to be a driving force for the title-winning team. He also impressed for the Australia under-18 team in its second match against Carlton's VFL side at Marvel Stadium, gathering 26 disposals and six clearances to claim the medal as his team's best player. Weapon: One-touch hands and creativity. AFL player comparison: Touk Miller (Gold Coast Suns).
