# Aaron Glenn Lions Hot Seat

Yardbarker's Nov. 30, 2023 NFC hot-seat roundup named defensive coordinator Aaron Glenn as the Detroit Lions' hottest seat, on the claim that his unit's poor scoring defense had become a pattern rather than a blip.

The grounds are that the Lions finished near the bottom of the NFL in points against — 31st and 28th — the past two seasons and were on pace to do so again for the third time in as many years under Glenn, at 24th and 23.5 points allowed. Furthermore, the unit had regressed in 2023, with opponents averaging 29 points per game since Week 7, a five-game stretch. The warrant is that three straight years of bottom-tier scoring defense, worsening rather than improving, is what puts a coordinator's job at risk.
