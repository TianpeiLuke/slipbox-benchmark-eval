# Apple Watch SE

The Apple Watch SE was on sale for $199 during October Prime Day.

Engadget calls it the best smartwatch to get if you are new to the wearable world, or if you just want a basic, relatively affordable watch that has all of the basic features you would need.

On top of comprehensive health and fitness tracking, iPhone notifications and dozens of fun watch faces, you get features like crash and fall detection, noise monitoring and Emergency SOS.
