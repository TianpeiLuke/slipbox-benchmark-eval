# USC vs. Notre Dame 2023: Broadcast And Venue Details

The Week 7 2023 USC-Notre Dame game was played at Notre Dame Stadium in South Bend, Ind. on Saturday, Oct. 14 at 7:30 p.m. ET, televised on NBC and streamed on Peacock or Fubo.

The defining features of the listing are its four fields: date (Saturday, Oct. 14), time (7:30 p.m. ET), location (Notre Dame Stadium, South Bend, Ind.) and TV channel (NBC), with live stream options listed separately.
