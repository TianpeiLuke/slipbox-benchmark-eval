# IPL 2024 Auction: The Accelerated Round

The accelerated round of the IPL 2024 auction was announced at 7:10 PM IST as the last round of the auction, and it moved quickly through recalled and remaining names. At 7:16 PM IST Finn Allen came up in the accelerated round and went unsold, while KKR picked up Sherfane Rutherford for Rs 1.5 Cr. At 7:20 PM IST Ashton Turner went to LSG for Rs 1 Cr and Rassie van der Dussen went unsold. At 7:21 PM IST Tom Curran came up with RCB the sole bidder at Rs 1.5 Cr, and at 7:23 PM IST Odean Smith went unsold.

At 7:25 PM IST David Willey, the former RCB pacer, found a new home in Lucknow for Rs 2 Cr, and Spencer Johnson came up to a bidding war between KKR and GT, with Delhi Capitals jumping in to make it a three-way race; at 7:30 PM IST it was GT versus DC with the bid crossing the 10 Cr mark, and Gujarat Titans paid 10 Cr for the pacer. At 7:38 PM IST CSK bagged Mustafizur Rahman for Rs 2 Cr, and RCB and DC locked in a bidding war for Jhye Richardson that Delhi Capitals won for Rs 5 Cr. At 7:41 PM IST KKR and RCB were locked in for Nuwan Thushara with MI joining, and at 7:44 PM IST MI won the race at Rs 4.80 Cr.

At 7:47 PM IST Mumbai Indians picked Naman Dhir for Rs 20 L, and at 7:51 PM IST MI also picked Anshul Kamboj for Rs 20 L. At 7:53 PM IST Sumit Kumar drew a contest between DC and KKR, which DC won at Rs 1 Cr, and at 7:55 PM IST Ashutosh Sharma went to PBKS for Rs 20 L. At 8:00 PM IST Robin Minz drew a bidding war between MI and CSK with SRH joining at Rs 3 Cr, but Gujarat Titans won the race at Rs 3.6 Cr. At 8:10 PM IST Punjab Kings picked Prince Choudhary for Rs 20 L, and at 8:11 PM IST Jathavedh Subramaniam went to SRH for Rs 20 L.
