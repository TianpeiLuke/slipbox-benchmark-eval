# Jean-Claude Blanc and the United Chief Executive Challenge

The question The Guardian posed on 25 December 2023 is whether any chief executive can make Manchester United a best-in-class elite club again — and the candidate in frame is Jean-Claude Blanc, billed as the "Lionel Messi of business" by Nice's chief executive, Fabrice Bocquet.

Blanc's credentials are unusually broad for the role. He has an MBA from Harvard, and at 60 years old has been Juventus CEO and president, CEO at Paris Saint-Germain and at French Tennis, and the executive overseeing the Tour de France.

The boundary on those credentials is the club itself. The report argues that once Blanc, or whoever becomes Arnold's successor, is in place, it will dawn rapidly that United are a one-off hydra-headed beast of a club which is treacherously difficult to control. And there is the competitive gap: across town Manchester City have accrued six championships since United's last in 2013, and boast a commercial operation that leaves Ratcliffe's new concern light years behind.
