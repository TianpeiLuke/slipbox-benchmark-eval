# Amazon Rainforest Tipping Point

"The Amazon is probably past its tipping point," Kalmus said. "And I can't believe that it happened during my lifetime."

The article qualifies the claim: estimates of when the Amazon could "tip" and transition to savannah vary, and Timothy Lenton, a University of Exeter professor who studies climate tipping points and the Amazon's resilience, said most estimates suggest the Amazon could cross a threshold as soon as 2 degrees C of warming.
