# Other CONMEBOL Results On Argentina-Paraguay Night

The other CONMEBOL World Cup qualifier played on Oct. 12, 2023 ended Colombia 2-2 Uruguay, with Darwin Nunez depositing a late penalty for Uruguay to share the points.

The scoring ran through James Rodriguez. An hour and 15 minutes before the Argentina-Paraguay kickoff he had scored a golazo to put Colombia in front 1-0 before halftime — his first-ever goal against Uruguay, giving him a goal against six of the nine CONMEBOL opponents, with Argentina, Brazil and Paraguay the three he had yet to score against. Uruguay briefly levelled early in the second half before Colombia restored its advantage minutes later, and Nunez's late penalty produced the draw.

The Sporting News read the result through Paraguay's interests: it is far too early to be watching outside results too intently, but the draw was a great result for Paraguay, who will want every CONMEBOL nation outside the top two to drop as many points as possible — the messier it gets around them, the better their chance of securing a qualifying spot in the expanded World Cup field.
