# The US CLIVAR Pattern Effect Workshop, 2022

To foster work on the pattern effect across research communities, 140 scientists from around the world and from different disciplines gathered for a US CLIVAR (U.S. Climate Variability and Predictability Program) workshop in May 2022 in Boulder, Colorado.

The Eos article presents the current consensus that emerged during that workshop and raises questions that its authors say require urgent attention from scientists.
