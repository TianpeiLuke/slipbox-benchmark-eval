# Travis Kelce's First Contact Attempt And The Eras Tour Bracelet

The first link between Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce was established in 2016 when Kelce played "Kiss, Marry, Kill" on AfterBuzz TV and said he would kiss Swift, and the first real contact attempt came in July 2023, when he tried and failed to give her a friendship bracelet with his number on it at her Eras Tour concert in Kansas City, The Age reported on 26 September 2023.

In the 2016 game Kelce — who helped bring his team to Super Bowl victory in both 2020 and 2023 — was given the options of Swift, Katy Perry and Ariana Grande, and said he would ultimately kill Grande, marry Perry and kiss Swift. That was about six years earlier, but it more recently set tongues wagging as fans wondered how long the plan for them to meet had been in the works.

Seven years on, things started to heat up: in July, speaking on the podcast he shares with his brother, New Heights, Kelce said he intended to give Swift a friendship bracelet with his number on it during her Eras Tour concert in Kansas City. The bracelet never made it to her wrist, nor his digits to her phone. "I was disappointed that she doesn't talk before or after her shows because she has to save her voice for the 44 songs she sings," he said. "She doesn't meet anybody, or at least she didn't want to meet me, so I took it personal[sic]." At that point any attraction seemed one-sided, but Kelce, exhibiting the tenacity typical of a professional sportsman, remained determined to get her attention — and the Kelce brothers could not stop talking about Swift on the podcast, her name popping up everywhere, including during conversations about facial hair.
