# Live In-Play Golf Betting

Live betting offers the opportunity to bet on golf tournaments as they unfold, with the main options covering the overall tournament victor, winners of individual rounds, and direct head-to-head matchups. Using a sportsbook app streamlines the process and gives immediate access to fluctuating, real-time odds.

The most lucrative live bets in golf often revolve around backing players who have had a slow start but have the talent to stage a comeback.

The live odds set by sportsbooks for golf events are determined by continually evaluating player performance alongside considerations such as course dynamics, prevailing weather, and player stamina.
