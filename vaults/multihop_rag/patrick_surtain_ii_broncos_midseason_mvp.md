# Patrick Surtain II Broncos Midseason MVP

Yardbarker's Nov. 9, 2023 AFC midseason team MVP list named cornerback Patrick Surtain II the Denver Broncos' MVP, predicting it should be no surprise if he earned a second straight Pro Bowl nod.

The grounds are his eight-game production: the 23-year-old budding star had one interception and eight passes defended, tied for 11th in the NFL. The warrant is that top-tier coverage volume from a young corner at that rate is Pro Bowl form.
