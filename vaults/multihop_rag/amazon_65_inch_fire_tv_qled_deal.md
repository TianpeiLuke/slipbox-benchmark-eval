# Amazon's 65-Inch Fire TV QLED Deal

Amazon's 65-inch Fire TV hit its lowest ever price in November 2023, Mashable reported in its Cyber Monday roundup on 27 November 2023 — "and we're not mad about it".

The set carries a 66 percent five-star review rating.

Its selling points, per the roundup, are stunning 4K QLED resolution, adaptive brightness that adjusts to the lighting of the room, the ability to project famous art or personal pictures on it when not streaming anything, and "that quintessential Alexa voice control".
