# UFC 295 Best Bet: Tom Aspinall's Moneyline In A Fight Likely To End Fast

CBS Sports' best bet for Sergei Pavlovich versus Tom Aspinall at UFC 295 is the Tom Aspinall moneyline at -120, chosen because his more diverse game makes it slightly more appealing than the exotic time-of-finish lines. The grounds on how quickly both men work: you have to go all the way back to June 2017 to find a Pavlovich fight that went past the opening round; only three of his 19 career fights have hit the five-minute mark, and he has five wins that came in less than one minute. Not to be outdone, Aspinall has six fights that ended in less than one minute and three that went past the opening round.

That produces what the article calls a wild situation, in which this interim heavyweight championship fight has +500 odds for the fight ending in the first minute — the "favorite" among the time-of-finish lines. The fight going past 1.5 rounds is +165, which the writer finds somewhat appealing since this is a five-round fight and it would make sense for both men not to dump their entire tank chasing a quick finish. Still, the pick lands on Aspinall's moneyline on the strength of his more diverse game.
