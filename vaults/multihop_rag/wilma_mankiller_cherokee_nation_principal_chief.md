# Wilma Mankiller, First Female Principal Chief Of The Cherokee Nation

Wilma Mankiller was the Cherokee Nation's first female Principal Chief, leading the tribe for a decade until 1995. A social worker by background, she focused on improving social conditions through consensus and on restoring pride in Native heritage. She died in 2010. Her surname, a military title, drew snide remarks, which she met with humour and a straight-faced reply: "Mankiller is actually a well-earned nickname."

Her likeness appears on a US quarter issued in 2021, and she is the second Native American woman honoured with a Barbie doll — the aviator Bessie Coleman, who was of Black and Cherokee ancestry, was depicted earlier in 2023. Other dolls in the "Inspiring Women" series include Maya Angelou, Ida B. Wells, Jane Goodall and Madam C.J. Walker.
