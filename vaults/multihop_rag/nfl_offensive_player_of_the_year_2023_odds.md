# 2023 NFL Offensive Player Of The Year: Hill The Favorite, Lamb The Longshot

Tyreek Hill was the current favorite for 2023 NFL Offensive Player of the Year and CeeDee Lamb the longshot, and Sporting News argued on Dec. 5, 2023 that Lamb could steal the award if Hill faded down the stretch.

There was a good reason why Hill was such a heavy favorite: the Dolphins wide receiver was on track to become the first player with 2,000-plus receiving yards in a single season. But Sporting News saw a solid chance he would not hit that mark, because of Miami's five remaining opponents, four had pass defenses ranked in the league's top eight. If he slowed down, someone like Lamb could catch up.

The Lamb case rests on his trajectory. Based on his current season average, Lamb was on track for close to 1,600 yards, but based on his production over the last seven games he could finish with closer to 1,800. He would probably need Hill to fade down the stretch to have a shot — but if Hill did and Lamb continued to produce, Lamb could steal OPOY from Hill.
