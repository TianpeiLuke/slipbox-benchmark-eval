# Kevin Durant Sensitivity And Burner Account Criticism

Against the case that Durant is simply disrespected, Essentially Sports set out on Dec. 1, 2023 the counter-case that his own conduct and post-Warriors record explain much of the criticism — attacking the premise that the disregard is unearned.

His vulnerability to criticism and thin-skinned nature has on several occasions got the better of him: NBA legend Charles Barkley called Durant "sensitive" in an interview with 60 Minutes, saying "He's very sensitive. Great player. He's part of that generation who thinks he can't be criticized." That susceptibility became glaringly evident when he used a burner account in 2017 to defend himself on Twitter, and was exposed after inadvertently failing to switch accounts and posting a tweet through his verified account. Fans also criticize him for a lack of leadership and a lack of titles since leaving the Warriors: since his 2019 departure Durant has not secured an NBA title, and despite forming a formidable trio with Kyrie Irving and James Harden in Brooklyn, their best result was a second-round exit in the 2021 playoffs at the hands of the Milwaukee Bucks. Fans and media also strongly disapprove of his inclination to switch teams repeatedly even when he receives substantial support from the organizations — after the Brooklyn experiment did not work he requested a trade, and at the previous year's trade deadline the Nets traded him to the Suns, where despite teammates like Devin Booker and Chris Paul he again fell short of a title. His inability to clinch an NBA title without Curry and the Warriors has heightened the narrative questioning his status as an icon of the game.
