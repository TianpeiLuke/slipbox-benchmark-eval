# England And South Africa Meet Again, Four Years After The 2019 Final

England and South Africa met in the semi-finals of the 2023 Rugby World Cup four years after contesting the 2019 final, an all-or-nothing match to decide which nation got a shot at World Cup glory. The 2023 stage was the last four rather than the decider, but Sporting News judged that would not make an ounce of difference to either camp as the long-time rivals prepared for a battle that could redefine the meaning of a "physical encounter."

The 2019 final was a game of many firsts. It capped off a sensational tournament played outside the sport's traditional strongholds for the first time; it was the first final in which South Africa scored a try; and by full-time the 32-12 score in the Springboks' favour sealed a record-equalling third title for the Rainbow Nation.
