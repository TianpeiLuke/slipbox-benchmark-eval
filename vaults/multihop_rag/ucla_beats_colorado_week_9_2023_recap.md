# UCLA Beats Colorado 28-16 At The Rose Bowl In Week 9

UCLA topped Colorado 28-16 at the Rose Bowl on Saturday, Oct. 28, 2023, in Deion Sanders and Chip Kelly's only Pac-12 matchup. The win improved the Bruins to 6-2, clinching bowl eligibility and keeping them alive in the Pac-12 race with a 3-2 league mark; Colorado fell to 4-4, its quest for bowl eligibility continuing with two of its final four opponents, Oregon State and Utah, ranked.

In his third start of the season, Bruins quarterback Ethan Garbers passed for 269 yards and two touchdowns. He and Collin Schlee combined for two interceptions in a UCLA first-half turnover-fest that included four turnovers in all, among them two fumbles by Carson Steele; the Bruins fixed the problem and committed no second-half turnovers. Colorado could not protect quarterback Shedeur Sanders, who threw for 217 yards and one touchdown while being sacked seven times, tying a season high. The Buffaloes' running game vanished after halftime — Sanders handed off only four times in the final two quarters, the first coming halfway through the fourth, and Colorado scored its only touchdown on that drive, finishing with 38 rushing yards, its lowest total of the season. Safety Shilo Sanders missed much of the game after being ejected late in the first half for targeting, while Travis Hunter shone in his second game back from injury, recording both of the game's interceptions. The quarter-by-quarter line was Colorado 6-0-3-7 for 16 and UCLA 0-7-7-14 for 28.

Kickoff was 7:30 p.m. ET (4:30 p.m. PT) at Rose Bowl Stadium in California. Pregame, the live blog noted UCLA's homecoming uniforms were a blast from the past (6:07 p.m.), that Colorado had not won in Pasadena since 2002 and was seeking its first win there since 2020 (7:00 p.m.), and that Deion Sanders offered some pregame motivation (7:20 p.m.).
