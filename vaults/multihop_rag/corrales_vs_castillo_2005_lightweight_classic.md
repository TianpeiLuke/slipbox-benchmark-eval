# Corrales TKO 10 Castillo: The 2005 Lightweight Classic

Diego Corrales stopped Jose Luis Castillo in the tenth round on May 7, 2005 at the Mandalay Bay Events Centre in Las Vegas, with the WBC, WBO and Ring Magazine lightweight titles at stake, in what Sporting News calls one of the greatest fights in history.

Both men arrived rebuilt after losses to Floyd Mayweather. Corrales had reestablished his image after being outclassed by Mayweather in 2001: he fell short against the excellent Joel Casamayor, then avenged that defeat to pick up the vacant WBO super featherweight crown, and on moving up to lightweight came from behind to stop WBO champion Acelino Frietas. Castillo also had history with Mayweather — many fans believe the Mexican star deserved the decision in April 2002 — but after Mayweather won the rematch decisively, Castillo rebuilt with excellent wins over Juan Lazcano, Casamayor and Julio Diaz, the Lazcano triumph giving him the vacant WBC title.

Despite functional height and reach advantages, Corrales elected to meet Castillo on the inside and the action was incredible. In the 10th, Castillo decked his man with a sharp left hook and then put him over again with another left; after both knockdowns "Chico" spat out his mouthpiece, drawing a point deduction. Battered and woozy with his left eye almost closed, Corrales needed a miracle and found it: when the bout resumed he nailed his man with a hard right and an even harder left, Castillo wilted, and he was stopped seconds later by a savage, head-snapping assault. There's been nothing like it since.
