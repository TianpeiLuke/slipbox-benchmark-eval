# Kids' Maker And DIY Kit Gifts 2023

The Verge's kids guide includes two maker gifts: the Kit Adam mechanical keyboard at $99.99 (KBDcraft) and the Pinecil portable soldering iron at $25.99+ (Amazon, Pine64).

Building a mechanical keyboard is a hobby often enjoyed by nerdy adults, the guide notes — but the Kit Adam by KBDcraft lets you build one with Lego-style bricks: a hot-swappable 60 percent board whose case is constructed of those bricks, making it simple and fun to build together and infinitely customizable with your own pieces. The Pinecil comes with a safety warning: it's just for big kids, like teenagers or early teens if they're responsible enough. It's described as the perfect introductory tool into the world of electronics tinkering — affordable, quick to heat up, able to dial in the exact temperature you need, and even runnable off a USB-C PD power bank.
