# The ICC ODI Men's Cricket World Cup

The ODI Men's Cricket World Cup is the ICC's flagship championship — the Sporting News calls it the heart and soul of ICC Championships — and the 2023 edition was held in India from October 5 to November 19.

Its defining features are its organiser and its cycle: it is organised every four years by the International Cricket Council (ICC), and it is one of the most viewed sporting tournaments in the world.

The article that introduces it frames its purpose as a retrospective, taking a look at the history of the competition and some of the landmark moments that have helped make the World Cup what it is today, published on Oct. 1, 2023, ahead of the men's ODI showpiece.
