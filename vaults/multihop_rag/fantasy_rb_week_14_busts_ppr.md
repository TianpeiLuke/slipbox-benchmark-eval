# Week 14 PPR Running Backs To Downgrade

Sporting News pushed back on three running backs whose Week 14 PPR usage did not survive the matchup test, publishing on Dec. 7, 2023.

James Cook (@ Chiefs) barely cracked the top 25 thanks to his passing-down usage, but did not thrill Sporting News at Arrowhead against the pissed-off defending champs: Kansas City's front seven had been tough against speedsters and more vulnerable to power backs, and the 5-11, 190-pound Cook fell 100 percent into the former category. Temper your expectations.

The same reasoning applied to Zach Charbonnet (@ 49ers): it was great that the Week 13 injury he sustained on Thursday night was just a bruise, but not great that he now faced a 49ers defense that had already stifled him on Thanksgiving. Managers were probably starting him if Kenneth Walker III remained out with his oblique injury, but should consider other options given depth, since the floor and ceiling remained low against that uber-confident Niners defense. Tyler Allgeier (vs. Buccaneers) continued to see his role diminish for the NFC South-leading Falcons, with coach Arthur Smith finally realizing that Bijan Robinson gives Atlanta the best chance at continued success; Tampa Bay had a pretty good run defense, so Allgeier could not be trusted outside of a desperate flex play with his nine average touches over the past two weeks.
