# Jada Pinkett Smith's 2023 Disclosure Of A Seven-Year Separation

Jada Pinkett Smith revealed in October 2023 that she and her husband Will Smith had been separated for the previous seven years — that is, since 2016 — while remaining married. The disclosure came in an interview with People given while she was promoting her memoir Worthy; The Independent's timeline piece dates the publication to 11 October, while its other reports on the same interview date it 10 October. Pinkett Smith, 52, said of her 26-year marriage to the 55-year-old actor that the couple are "still figuring it out": "We've been doing some really heavy-duty work together. We just got deep love for each other and we are going to figure out what that looks like for us."

She anchored the timeline to the 2022 Academy Awards, when Will Smith slapped Chris Rock after the comedian made a joke about her appearance: at that moment, she said, the pair had already been separated for six years. The revelation was treated as major news — "sent shockwaves around the world," in The Independent's phrasing — because both Smiths had previously doubled down on their stance against divorce, so a lengthy separation raised a new question: when should a couple simply get a divorce?
