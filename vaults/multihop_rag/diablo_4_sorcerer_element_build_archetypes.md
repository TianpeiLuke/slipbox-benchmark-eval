# Diablo 4 Sorcerer Element Build Archetypes

Sorcerers are Diablo 4's pure caster class and use elemental magic to differentiate themselves from the spells of the Druid and Necromancer. The class has six Key Passives but only three elements — Lightning, Fire, and Ice — and those elements are what a build is based around, as Polygon set out on 2023-10-18.

Each element maps to a role and to a choice between two Key Passives. Lightning builds use Lightning skills to deal with large groups of enemies at once, ending up with either the Overflowing Energy Key Passive or the Vyr's Mastery Key Passive depending on whether you are in melee range. Ice builds specialize in slowing enemies down to make their attacks easier to survive, taking either the Avalanche Key Passive or the Shatter Key Passive depending on how much the build relies on crowd control. Fire builds are all about big explosions and burning enemies, typically using the Combustion Key Passive for burning enemies or the Esu's Ferocity Key Passive for chunky critical strikes.

The range over which this holds includes a survivability constraint. When making your own Sorcerer build you will likely stick to one element for your main damage sources, but Polygon says that is not the most important thing to remember: Sorcerers die very, very easily in Diablo 4 and have the weakest defensive stats by far, even though they can deal a massive amount of damage when built correctly. To avoid ending up dead more often than not, invest heavily in defensive skills while making your way toward one of the six Key Passives.
