# Warriors Aging Core Curry Thompson Green

If the Warriors seemed old in November 2023, it was because they were, Yardbarker reported on Nov. 29: Curry, 35, could still play at an MVP level, but Green, 33, and Thompson, 33, were not the players they had been during Golden State's run to five straight Finals.

Green, when he was not suspended, could still uncork vintage defensive performances, but his offensive game had not aged well as the league trended toward more shooting. The typically affable Thompson, having suffered two major leg injuries since 2019, was bristling at questions about the starting lineup. The Warriors' starting five — a dominant unit over the last decade — was being outscored by an alarming 10.6 points every 100 possessions that season.
