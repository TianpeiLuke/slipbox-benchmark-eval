# MLBTR Trade Candidate Ranking Methodology

Before presenting its top 25 MLB offseason trade candidates on Nov. 13, 2023, Yardbarker set out its method: the list is neither a strict ranking of players' trade value nor solely a ranking by likelihood of being moved, but an attempt to balance both of those things — an inherently subjective exercise.

The one external input the method relies on is stated explicitly: all projected salaries for arbitration-eligible players are courtesy of MLBTR contributor Matt Swartz. That is the source of every arbitration projection quoted for the players on the list, so the salary figures attached to individual candidates should be read as Swartz projections rather than as agreed contract terms.
