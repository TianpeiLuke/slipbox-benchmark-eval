# Overcooked! All You Can Eat

Overcooked! All You Can Eat is a definitive remaster that bundles Overcooked and Overcooked 2, both excellent games, into one delicious package. It appears in Polygon's 2023-10-20 list of the 35 best couch co-op games for Nintendo Switch.

Players prepare orders for hungry customers while facing ridiculous odds.

Those odds include cooking in a hot air balloon with cooktops that sometimes catch on fire, or while crossing a busy street.
