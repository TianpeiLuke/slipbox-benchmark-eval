# Eloy Jimenez White Sox Trade Candidacy

Eloy Jiménez was ranked No. 12 on Yardbarker's Nov. 13, 2023 list of the top 25 MLB offseason trade candidates, and the case rested on his no longer looking like a core piece for the White Sox: various injuries had kept him off the field throughout his big league tenure, he had only two seasons of 100+ games, and he had not reached 500 plate appearances since his 2019 rookie year.

Jiménez was still an excellent hitter when healthy as recently as 2022, when he ran a .295/.358/.500 line over 84 games. His production fell that year, as he posted a .272/.317/.441 slash with 18 homers over 489 trips to the dish — not especially imposing for a player best suited at DH. Yet he had shown greater offensive upside that was not matched by many in that winter's free agent class, so if the Sox were prepared to move on, another team could look to buy low.

He would make a $13M salary the following year, the final guaranteed season of his contract. He was guaranteed a $3M buyout on a $16.5M club option for 2025, and the deal contained an $18.5M team option for the 2026 campaign.
