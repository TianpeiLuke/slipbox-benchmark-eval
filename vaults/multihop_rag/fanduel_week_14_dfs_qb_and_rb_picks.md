# FanDuel Week 14 DFS Quarterback And Running Back Picks

Sporting News built its Week 14 FanDuel DFS lineup around quarterback Gardner Minshew of the Colts ($6,900) and running backs Joe Mixon ($7,200) and Breece Hall ($6,400), publishing on Dec. 7, 2023 as the 2023 NFL season wound down and season-long leagues came to an end.

Minshew (Colts @ Bengals) was picked because Minshew Mania was eyeing a postseason spot with the Colts at 7-5 heading into the final few weeks, and now got a Bengals team limping to the finish thanks to Joe Burrow's season-ending thumb injury; the Colts were a motivated bunch in a tight AFC South, evidence continued to mount that they had knocked the Shane Steichen hire out of the park, and Minshew was a capable fantasy starter as shown by his 312 yards and two touchdowns the previous week — worth another DFS flier in a week offering very few worthwhile value options.

Mixon (Bengals vs. Colts) had been forced to step up without Burrow under center, doubling as a runner and receiver, with Jake Browning relying heavily on the veteran as evidenced by Mixon's two touchdowns on Monday night against the Jaguars; Burrow's injury had made Mixon arguably the Bengals' top fantasy option, and the matchup was good against an Indianapolis team that had had issues defending running backs, notably getting torched by Derrick Henry and Tyjae Spears one week earlier. Hall (Jets vs. Texans) was a worthy dart throw because his role made him one: he continued to see plenty of carries and a big role in the passing game, and while the offense could not score, the volume was there against a Texans team that had struggled to defend running backs out of the backfield. Sporting News added a contrarian argument — that because it is borderline insane to include a Jets player in any DFS lineup, they might be among the few rostering him, which could help their cause.
