# No. 17 Arizona 42-18 No. 22 Utah, November 2023

No. 17 Arizona blew out No. 22 Utah 42-18 at a sold-out Arizona Stadium on Saturday 18 November 2023, extending their winning streak to five games and marking their first win over the Utes since 2015. The game was many things for the Wildcats: Senior Day, Military Appreciation Day, and their last home game of the season. With the UA needing a win to keep its own destiny alive for a Pac-12 Championship appearance, it handled the pressure once again against a ranked team, getting halfway through en route to winning out and doing it in statement fashion.

It was also the first time the UA took down four ranked teams in a season since 1989, with Arizona the only team in the country to do so that season.

"Great win for the program," Arizona head coach Jedd Fisch said following the win. "Great win for our team, great win for our seniors, great win for Tucson, appreciate Tucson coming out in full force, selling out the stadium. ... I think you might as well put the season tickets on sale for next year on Monday and see if we can get six sell outs."
