# The White Company's Christmas Decorations And Winter Scents

The decorations part of The White Company's Christmas range covers wreaths, garlands and Christmas lights intended to give a house a seasonal lift for welcoming friends. The Independent's guide singles out a pinecone wreath made from a mixture of faux greenery, pinecones and berries, which it says will add rustic charm to a front door or can be used as a decorative element on an interior wall or table.

Scent is the second half of the same idea — setting a tone of warmth and friendship. The Winter Collection is built on spicy, warming notes of cinnamon, clove and orange, available as candles, diffusers or oils, with plenty of affordable gift options within it. For an alternative seasonal scent, the Nordic Woods Collection is described as transporting you to a wintery forest with fresh, woody notes of birchwood, the rich warmth of amber, and the more soothing scent of eucalyptus.
