# Kickoff Times And Broadcasters For The England-South Africa Semi-Final

England and South Africa contested the 2023 Rugby World Cup's second semi-final at the Stade de France in Saint-Denis, kicking off at 9 p.m. local time on Saturday, October 21, 2023. Around the world that was Saturday, Oct. 21 at 3:00 p.m. ET in the USA and Canada and 8:00 p.m. BST in the UK, and Sunday, Oct. 22 at 6:00 a.m. AEDT in Australia, 8:00 a.m. NZDT in New Zealand, 12:30 a.m. IST in India, and 3:00 a.m. in Singapore (SGT), Malaysia (MYT) and Hong Kong (HKT).

TV networks worldwide were showing all 48 matches at the tournament live, including many on free-to-air television. The listings gave: USA — no TV channel, streaming on Peacock; Canada — TSN, streaming on TSN+; UK — ITV, streaming on ITV X; Australia — Channel 9, streaming on Stan Sport and 9Now; New Zealand — Sky GO on both; India — no TV channel, streaming on Fan Code; and Hong Kong, Malaysia and Singapore — BeIN Sports for both TV and streaming.

For scale, video content from the Rugby World Cup and organisers World Rugby drew more than 2.1 billion views on social platforms alone in 2019, almost six times the previous figure.
