# The Third Quarter Of Bengals 24, Bills 18

Buffalo scored the only points of the third quarter on Nov. 5, 2023, cutting Cincinnati's lead to 21-10 while both offenses stalled. Khalil Shakir brought the opening kickoff out to the 26 and Sean McDermott's stated aim of avoiding third-and-longs showed in a five-yard opening pass to Diggs. An ineligible-receiver-downfield flag brought up second-and-10, but Allen went back to Diggs, then twice to Kincaid — nine yards to the Bills' 41 and 11 more to Cincinnati's 48. Allen rifled a 15-yard pass to Diggs over Nick Scott's head to the Bengals' 33, dumped off to James Cook for eight, and Cook ran for seven and a first down into the red zone for the first time since Buffalo's opening drive, at the Cincinnati 17. Pressure then limited Allen on consecutive plays — an incompletion to Cooks to avoid a sack and a quick throw to Cook stopped after a yard — and on third-and-9 he could not connect with Gabe Davis in the end zone, so Tyler Bass drilled a 34-yard field goal at 10:13 p.m. for Bengals 21, Bills 10.

Cincinnati's response drive covered ground but ended empty. Williams returned the kickoff to the 16, though a penalty pushed the spot back to the Cincinnati 8. Mixon rushed for three and caught a pass for three, and a healthy Burrow showed his value on third-and-4 by scrambling for seven and the first down. Higgins caught a short pass and made several players miss up to the Cincinnati 43, but Burrow's deep shot to Chase was just short with Dane Jackson breaking it up, and after a Mixon dump-off lost a yard the Bengals faced third-and-11; Burrow looked Chase's way again and came up empty, leaving Chase with three catches for nine yards on seven targets, and Brad Robbins' punt pinned Buffalo at its own 9.

Buffalo could not escape. Allen threw quickly to Cooks for four, missed Diggs to the left, and after a timeout with the play clock at three seconds was flushed from the pocket and brought down by Trey Hendrickson for a yard, forcing a punt that Trenton Irwin fair-caught at the Bengals' 42 with 5:06 left in the quarter. Cincinnati moved again — Mixon's five-yard checkdown, an 11-yard Higgins catch to the Bills' 42, then, after Jordan Poyer blew up a pitch to Mixon for a four-yard loss, another nine-yard Higgins reception — but Burrow could not connect with Boyd on third-and-5, and after a timeout with 2:21 left the Bengals went for it on fourth-and-5 only for Drew Sample to be brought down three yards shy. Buffalo took over at its own 35, and Allen powered for a 10-yard run and hit Kincaid for 11 to reach the Cincinnati 44; back-to-back Cook plays of three rushing and six receiving set up third-and-1 at the Bengals' 35, and Allen sneaked left for three and the first down as the quarter ended.
