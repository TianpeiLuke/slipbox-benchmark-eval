# ExpressVPN For Streaming, Travel And Gaming

ExpressVPN was picked as the best VPN service for travel, gaming and streaming, chosen on the criteria of access to the most locations with high speed connections and no lag. It was one of the fastest VPNs tried, and now comes with an in-house password manager, ExpressVPN Keys, included with the subscription to manage and autofill logins across sites — making it easier to toggle between streaming and gaming accounts while browsing securely.

Its speed result came with a caveat. An internet speed test measured faster upload and download speed than using no VPN at all, practically unheard of compared with the other VPNs tested, but being this fast is likely a fluke due to the service circumventing traffic shaping by the ISP, or another disparity, because even top VPNs will in some way slow down speeds.

On reach and security: with 2,000 servers in 160 cities, according to the company, it had one of the broadest global reaches; it passed the geoblock, streaming and gaming tests; and it does regular security audits. Network Lock is its kill switch feature, which keeps your data safe even if you lose connection to the VPN. Subscription costs range from $8.32 to $12.95 per month depending on the term of the plan, and include the password manager.

Its main limitation is device count: users can connect up to five devices at once, which is on the lower side compared with other services — though it works on a bunch of devices from smart TVs to game consoles, unlike some other services that lack support beyond the usual suspects like smartphones and laptops.
