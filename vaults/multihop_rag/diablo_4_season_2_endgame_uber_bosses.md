# Diablo 4 Season 2 Endgame Bosses and Uber Uniques

Diablo 4's season 2 adds five new endgame bosses where players will spend their time at max level and earn some of the game's most powerful items: Grigoire The Galvanic Saint, the Echo of Varshan, The Beast in the Ice, the Dark Master, and the Echo of Duriel. Polygon reported this on 2023-10-04.

Each boss has its own laundry list of busy work to complete in order to unlock the encounter — such as completing Nightmare Dungeons above level 30 or completing Grim Favors — but each also comes with Unique rewards. Because the bosses each offer their own pool of Unique items, farming for a specific piece becomes easier: instead of running dungeons and hoping the desired item drops, players can gather all the materials, kill The Beast in the Ice, and repeat if they do not get what they wanted.

The Echo of Duriel is the most interesting of the five, since Duriel has a chance to drop the ultimate, super-rare uber Uniques that were previously almost impossible to acquire; to compensate, players must kill the four other endgame bosses each time they want to face Duriel.

Blizzard's designers did not build these bosses to be as tough as the ultimate Uber Lilith fight, but they should hopefully prove a challenge to most players while giving a targeted way to farm must-have items. The Echo of Duriel, who is level 100, will be the most difficult of the new bosses, and is the only way for players to farm Uber Uniques in Diablo 4. For players with more in-game gold than time, Blizzard confirmed in the interview that players will be able to trade with one another to get the items needed to summon Duriel and the other bosses.
