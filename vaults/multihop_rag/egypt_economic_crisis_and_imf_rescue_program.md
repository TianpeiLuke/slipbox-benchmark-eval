# Egypt's Economic Crisis And Its IMF Rescue Program

Egypt, which reached a deal with the IMF in December 2022, was as of October 2023 already in talks on boosting that rescue program to over $5 billion from $3 billion, people familiar with the discussions told Bloomberg that month. This is happening as the most populous Arab nation wrestles with its worst economic outlook in decades and President Abdel-Fattah El-Sisi prepares for elections in less than two months' time — a combination that opens up opportunities for Cairo, though a host of domestic and regional considerations are seen ruling out any refugee deal.

According to Riccardo Fabiani, project director for North Africa with Crisis Group, it is possible the IMF's key shareholders in the US and Europe may pressure the Washington-based lender to soften its requirements and move ahead with the program despite Cairo's slow pace of reforms.
