# The Cancellation Of The Sono Sion Solar EV

Sono abandoned car-making in 2023: instead of starting production of the Sion as planned, the German automaker pivoted to selling to third-party automakers and laid off 300 workers. No longer an automaker, Sono now focuses on embedding its solar tech into other vehicles.

Sono had once led the charge to bring solar panels to electric cars with the five-seater Sono Sion hatchback, which it priced at $25,000 — the name is unrelated to Toyota's Scion badge — and intended to put into production in 2023.
