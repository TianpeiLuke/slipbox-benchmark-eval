# The Proposal To Expand Patrick McHenry's Speaker Pro Tempore Powers

The proposal, floated by a bipartisan group of lawmakers during the October 2023 speakership vacancy, was to give the interim speaker pro tempore, Rep. Patrick McHenry, R-N.C., more power to reopen the immobilized House and temporarily conduct routine business — in one formulation, more powers for the next several months to at least bring the House back into session and conduct crucial business. Its legal basis is that the House had never ousted its speaker before Kevin McCarthy, and McHenry could tap the temporary powers that were created after the Sept. 11, 2001, terror attacks to ensure continuity of government.

Two boundary conditions define the idea's limits. It was swiftly rejected by Jim Jordan's own ultra-conservative allies — a "betrayal," said Rep. Jim Banks, R-Ind. And McHenry himself has brushed off attempts to take the job more permanently after he was appointed to the role following the unprecedented ouster of McCarthy more than two weeks earlier.
