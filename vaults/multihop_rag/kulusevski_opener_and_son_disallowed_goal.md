# Kulusevski's Opener And Son's Disallowed Goal

Dejan Kulusevski opened the scoring for Spurs against Chelsea on Nov. 6, 2023, benefitting from a large deflection off Chelsea defender Levi Colwill that left Roberto Sanchez with no chance to make a save, and the match got out of hand quickly with both sides flying forward with electric attacking intent.

The flood of VAR interventions then began on a relatively innocuous decision: Heung-Min Son seemingly put Tottenham 2-0 up in the 14th minute, but on review the play appeared extremely tight. Ultimately Son did look as if he was just an inch offside, and the goal was ruled out — a call circulated at the time alongside the official VAR image.
