# Dismissed Theories: A Murati Coup And A Personal Matter

TechCrunch pushes back on two explanations circulating for Altman's removal. The first is that interim CEO Mira Murati engineered the ouster: probably not — she seems cool, and anyway what CTO wants to be CEO?

The second is that the trigger was personal or legal rather than professional. When someone is kicked out in a hurry, it is not uncommon that there was some kind of unprofessional behavior in the workplace; some CEOs get a pass on things like having kids with direct reports, but not all. Altman also has three siblings, and his younger sister Annie has publicly accused him of abuse — allegations TechCrunch says it has no way of evaluating, and which involve private matters.

The premise both theories rest on is that the board's action had a personal or legal cause. TechCrunch's read on the board's language in dismissing Altman is that it was not a legal or personal problem that provoked the action, but a professional or business one.
