# Taylor Swift's First Chiefs Game Appearance On 24 September 2023

Taylor Swift took Travis Kelce up on his offer and attended the Chiefs' Week 3 game against the Chicago Bears on 24 September 2023, according to CBS Sports' timeline published 12 October 2023.

Swift was dressed on theme and watched intently, cheering and even chest bumping someone during big plays; she was especially thrilled to see Kelce get in the end zone on a three-yard catch that put the Chiefs up 41-0, and Kansas City went on to win 41-10 to improve to 2-1. She sat next to Donna Kelce in the box, the two seen talking and laughing as the game went along.

The postgame press conference seemed more about Swift and Kelce than about the actual game, with the media wanting any insight into the relationship and how Swift ended up at Arrowhead. "He told me at the last minute," Patrick Mahomes said when asked if he knew Swift was coming. "There's some things with Trav where he kind of just says it and you don't know if it's true or not. He says it so calmly. ... Friday he was like, 'Yeah, I think she's coming to the game this weekend,' and then moved about his business." Head coach Andy Reid, asked about his star tight end's supposed relationship, quipped, "I set them up."

Afterwards the two were photographed together for the first time, walking side by side, Kelce fittingly wearing an outfit with "1989" in the title and Swift wearing a Chiefs zip-up; they were then spotted leaving in a convertible with the tight end driving, and Kelce reportedly rented out a restaurant for a private party with Swift and his teammates. A source told Entertainment Tonight that "Taylor Swift likes that Travis Kelce 'pursued her' and the two are having a 'great time getting to know each other,'" adding that "'Taylor wants a guy that's into his career, does his own thing, but is also supportive and understands her,'" and "'Travis fits those qualities.'"
