# Yankees Land Alex Verdugo From The Red Sox

The Red Sox agreed to send starting outfielder Alex Verdugo to the Yankees for right-handers Richard Fitts, Greg Weissert and Nicholas Judice on Tuesday, Dec. 5, 2023, per Jeff Passan of ESPN as carried in Sporting News' winter meetings tracker — only the seventh time in the last 50 years that the two clubs had agreed to a deal.

Verdugo had been the Red Sox's starting outfielder for the last four seasons, having come over as the centerpiece in the trade that sent Mookie Betts to the Dodgers. He hit .281 with 43 home runs, 206 RBI and 16 stolen bases over 2,071 plate appearances with Boston, and led the American League in outfield assists in 2020 and 2023.

In return, Boston got Weissert and two pitching prospects headlined by Fitts — the Yankees' No. 4-ranked pitching prospect and No. 12-ranked prospect overall.
