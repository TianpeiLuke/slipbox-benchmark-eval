# India vs Australia At The 1987 World Cup

The 1987 tournament was the first time the World Cup was hosted outside of England, and the first India-Australia meeting there was one of the closest matches played between the two sides: Australia won by just one run. Batting first, Australia posted a target of 271 runs courtesy of a 110-run knock off 141 balls by Geoff Marsh. India fought well through two well-made 70s by Krishnamachari Srikkanth and Navjot Singh Sidhu, but were bowled out in 49.5 overs with 269 runs on the board; Craig McDermott took four wickets for Australia.

The two sides met a second time in the same World Cup and India won that one by 56 runs. Batting first, India set a target of 290 runs courtesy of four half-centuries, by Sunil Gavaskar, Navjot Singh Sidhu, Dilip Vengsarkar and Mohammad Azharuddin. Australia were bowled out within 233 runs, with David Boon scoring 62 off 59 balls, while Mohammad Azharuddin and Maninder Singh picked up three wickets for India.
