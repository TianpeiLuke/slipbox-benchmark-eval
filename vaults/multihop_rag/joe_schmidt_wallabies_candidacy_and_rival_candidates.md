# Joe Schmidt's Wallabies Candidacy And The Rival Candidates

The Roar reported on 11 December 2023 that Joe Schmidt — the 2018 World Rugby coach of the year, former Ireland coach and All Blacks assistant — is interested in the vacant Wallabies coaching job, with Dan McKellar, Stephen Larkham, Andy Friend and Ian Foster also remaining interested in the role.

The link the report draws is personal. Peter Horne's close relationship with Schmidt could be one of the keys to landing him: after leaving his Irish post following the 2019 World Cup, ending six years in the job, Schmidt worked alongside Horne at World Rugby, and it is believed the duo have a strong working relationship.
