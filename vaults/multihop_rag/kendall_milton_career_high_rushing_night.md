# Kendall Milton's Career-High Rushing Night

Kendall Milton set a career high in single-game rushing yards against Ole Miss on Nov. 11, 2023, reaching 127 yards entering the final quarter with a 51-yard run that helped wrap up the third quarter at 9:48 p.m.

He scored twice along the way. At 8:22 p.m. Milton ran in a score that stood after review — his fifth straight game with a touchdown — giving the Dawgs a two-possession lead at Ole Miss 14, Georgia 21 with only a few minutes until halftime. At 9:17 p.m. he took it home again with a 33-yard touchdown that extended Georgia's lead to 21 points, with nine minutes left in the third quarter and the live blog asking whether Ole Miss could find momentum and rally.
