# India's Record Of World Cup Semi-Final Appearances

India first entered the semi-finals of the World Cup at the 1983 Prudential Cup in England, where they emerged as the tournament winners. They moved into the last-four stage on the next two occasions when the World Cup was held in India, in 1987 and 1996, and were in the semi-finals again in 2003 in South Africa. Eight years later they were in the last four at the 2011 World Cup as hosts, and repeated the feat in 2015, 2019 and 2023.

The summary statistic given on 11 November 2023 is that in their last six World Cups, India have been in the semi-finals on five occasions.
