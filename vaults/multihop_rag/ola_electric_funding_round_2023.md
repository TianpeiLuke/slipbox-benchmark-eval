# Ola Electric Funding Round 2023

Ola Electric raised $384.4 million in a funding round, which included about $240 million in debt, TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-30. Singapore's sovereign wealth fund Temasek led the funding round and Indian government-backed lender State Bank of India bankrolled the debt.

The new round values the Bengaluru-headquartered electric vehicle startup at about $5.4 billion.
