# Sterling's Equaliser Disallowed For Handball

Raheem Sterling's 21st-minute equaliser for Chelsea at Tottenham on Nov. 6, 2023 was nullified on review, making Chelsea the next side to see a goal chalked off in a half full of VAR interventions.

The decision came because Sterling's initial effort, blocked by Pedro Porro, deflected straight back onto the arm of the Chelsea forward. The call was debated at the time, with clips circulating asking whether it was the correct decision to rule out Sterling's goal.
