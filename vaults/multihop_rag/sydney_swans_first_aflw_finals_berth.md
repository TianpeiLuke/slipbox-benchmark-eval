# Sydney Swans' First AFLW Finals Berth

Sydney, winless in their debut season the previous year, secured eighth spot and their first AFLW finals berth in 2023 by storming home with three unanswered goals against Fremantle, The Age reported on 3 November 2023.

"Any club that's able to go from winless one year to a finals berth, it's incredible," Sydney co-captain Chloe Molloy told Fox Footy after the match. "You can see the emotion on the girls' faces. Coming into the season, the odds were against us. Just to get into the eight, we've managed to do that. We're stoked. It's pretty special for the club." Missing star ruck Ally Morphett to a season-ending wrist injury, Laura Gardiner stood tallest in the midfield — the 20-year-old off-season recruit from Geelong had the ball on a string with a game-high 33 disposals and 18 contested possessions — while Molloy (two goals), Montana Ham, Rebecca Privitelli and Brooke Lochland hit the scoreboard.

The Swans' rise had been signalled earlier in the season by a record-breaking win over the Western Bulldogs, propelled by a Chloe Molloy masterclass: after a five-goal second term that included a four-minute burst of three majors, Sydney had already brought up their largest score in an AFLW game by half-time, and while the Bulldogs held their own in the last quarter the damage was done. The total was also the biggest the Dogs had conceded in their history, topping the 83 Melbourne kicked against them in round three. Molloy, the Swans' star off-season recruit and a former Collingwood ace, was a class above, kicking 3.4 from her 28 possessions and playing a crucial role in setting up numerous other Sydney goals. "She was itching to get in there [in the midfield]," Swans coach Scott Gowans said. "We kept her forward deliberately. It was important Chloe played a good block of time forward so we could have a look at it." That weekend the Blues lost to Greater Western Sydney by two points, 8.1 (49) to 7.5 (47), denying Carlton the chance to leapfrog into the top eight.
