# Ratcliffe's Bid For A Manchester United Stake

As of 28 October 2023, Sir Jim Ratcliffe's bid to buy 25% of Manchester United for an inflated £1.3bn in return for control of football policy was expected to go through imminently, marking a new phase in the protracted sale of the club.

Erik ten Hag had overseen his squad through those recent weeks and, despite any implications the deal might have for the futures of his players, did not think the matter was a distraction. "Of course, they are committed to the club but especially they are committed to this team," Ten Hag said. "They want to be successful and are therefore aware you need [to focus on] the team, your teammates. That is how the players are thinking and they are not thinking about strategic reviews or structures or whatever."
