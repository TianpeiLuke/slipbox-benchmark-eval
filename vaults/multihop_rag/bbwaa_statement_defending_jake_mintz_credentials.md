# The BBWAA's Statement: Mintz Earned His Credential

The Baseball Writers Association of America responded to the incident by defending Jake Mintz, a BBWAA member, rejecting the claim that he had no business holding a credential. "For the record, Jake is a BBWAA member in good standing and has earned the right to be credentialed through his hard work and quality baseball coverage," the association wrote.

The statement then attacked the manner of the criticism and where it was aired: "To assert otherwise, in vulgar terms, is both unprofessional and unacceptable. The BBWAA is deeply troubled that the league's own network would permit the disparaging of one of our members in this fashion. Scrutinizing our work is part of the territory but comments such as these should have no place on MLBN."
