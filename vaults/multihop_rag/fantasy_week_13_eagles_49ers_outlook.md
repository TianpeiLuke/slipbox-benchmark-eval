# Week 13 Eagles-49ers: San Francisco's Defense Since Chase Young Makes Philadelphia's Starters Underwhelm

CBS Sports argues that the 49ers defense has been transformed since acquiring Chase Young, and that this should suppress — without eliminating — Eagles Fantasy production in Week 13. The grounds: in three games since getting Young, the Niners have allowed 30 points total, yielded just 3.3 yards per rush to running backs and 5.9 yards per attempt to quarterbacks, pressured quarterbacks at a 43.2% rate without blitzing much, accumulated 15 sacks and picked off four interceptions. The warrant is symmetry — as many problems as the Eagles present with their offense, the Niners figure to be just as menacing with their collective defense.

Two further conditions push the same direction. The Eagles' defense played over 90 snaps last Sunday and could be without a couple of starters in the front seven, and the Eagles' offense could be forced into an uncomfortable game script if the Niners build a lead. Even concepts like utilizing Jalen Hurts' mobility on downfield throws and targets to D'Andre Swift are things the Niners have dealt with successfully this season. The conclusion is deliberately bounded: not that you would sit any of your usual Eagles starters in Fantasy, but that some might underwhelm given the circumstances. A scheduling postscript notes the Eagles play at Dallas the following week. The verdicts: STARTS — Jalen Hurts, Christian McCaffrey, A.J. Brown, DeVonta Smith, Brock Purdy, Brandon Aiyuk, Deebo Samuel, George Kittle, D'Andre Swift and the 49ers DST; SITS — the Eagles DST.
