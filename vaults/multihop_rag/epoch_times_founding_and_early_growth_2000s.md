# The Epoch Times' Founding And Early Growth In The 2000s

The Epoch Times was born out of the Falun Gong leafleting campaign in the United States: it was started in Georgia in 2000 by John Tang, a Falun Gong practitioner who remains its CEO, and in essence it was a Chinese-language public relations newsletter.

The group's long-term goals were ambitious from the outset — to expose the Chinese Communist Party, and to save the world in a supernatural war against communism.

Through the early aughts, The Epoch Times grew from an online effort into a weekly physical newspaper, with a home base in New York and a TV production company, New Tang Dynasty Television. It raised money from followers and was staffed by unpaid volunteers. Its pages mixed aggregated articles on international issues from Voice of America with Thanksgiving Day explainers, dispatches from Falun Gong parades, and exposés on atrocities alleged to have been committed by the Chinese Communist Party.
