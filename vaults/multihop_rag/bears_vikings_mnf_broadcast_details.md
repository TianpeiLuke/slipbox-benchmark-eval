# Bears-Vikings Monday Night Broadcast Details

Chicago Bears vs. Minnesota Vikings kicked off at 8:15 p.m. ET (7:15 p.m. local time) on Monday, Nov. 27, 2023 from U.S. Bank Stadium in Minneapolis, Minn., televised on ESPN, which has long held the broadcast rights to Monday Night Football. Joe Buck led the broadcast as play-by-play announcer with Troy Aikman as color analyst and Lisa Salters providing updates from the sidelines.

Streaming was available via ESPN+ or Fubo, which offers a free trial, with viewers in Canada able to stream every NFL game via DAZN.

The series history favored the home side: the Bears had not beaten the Vikings since 2020, with Minnesota 6-1 against Chicago over the previous four seasons, and the Vikings were 8-3 at home in the fixture since 2012.
