# Epstein-Related Settlements With The US Virgin Islands

Epstein's estate was the first to settle with the Virgin Islands: a year before the November 2023 account, it agreed to pay $105 million plus half the proceeds from the sale of Little St. James, AKA "Little St. Jeff," the private island where authorities allege some of Epstein's crimes took place. Then in January 2023 Wall Street billionaire Leon Black settled for $62.5 million, and in September 2023 JPMorgan Chase settled for $75 million. None of the parties admitted to wrongdoing, and all of them declined to comment for the story.

George's justification for the terms was that the civil racketeering case was complex and warranted a resolution that at least in part met Epstein's myriad wrongdoings in the USVI. In the end the estate returned $80 million in tax incentives, agreed to pay half a million to remediate environmental damage to one of Epstein's islands, committed half the proceeds from the future sale of Little St. James, and established a victims' compensation fund. The estate settled first; the other battles soon followed.
