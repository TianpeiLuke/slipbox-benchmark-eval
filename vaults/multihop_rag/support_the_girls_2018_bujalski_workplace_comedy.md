# Support The Girls (2018): Andrew Bujalski's Workplace Comedy

Support the Girls is a 2018 day-in-the-life comedy from writer-director Andrew Bujalski (Computer Chess), running 1h 33m, starring Regina Hall, Haley Lu Richardson and James Le Gros. Hall plays the manager of a Hooters-like sports bar dealing with training new hires, rude customers crossing lines, and an idiotic boss, all while trying to take care of her girls through various major and minor crises.

Polygon's case rests on Hall's performance: one of the great performers of our time, giving a tremendously layered turn in one of the richest roles she has had, as Lisa — a protective force in her girls' lives who can put on a brave face in front of them and in support of them even when circumstances seem on the verge of a total spiral. Haley Lu Richardson as the peppy Maci and Shayna "Junglepussy" McHayle as the no-nonsense Danyelle stand out among many good supporting turns. The reviewer calls it funny, heartwarming and undeniably tangible in its ground-level depiction of a hectic workplace — a movie about looking out for each other in a trying world.
