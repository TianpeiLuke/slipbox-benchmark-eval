# UFC 295 Best Bet: Under 1.5 Rounds In Frevola vs Saint-Denis

CBS Sports' best bet for Matt Frevola versus Benoit Saint-Denis at UFC 295 is Under 1.5 rounds at -143, on the grounds that both men's recent fights end fast. After four of his first five fights in the UFC went to decision, Frevola has not seen the second round in his four most recent bouts, and three of Saint-Denis' five UFC bouts have checked in at under 1.5 rounds. The warrant: the matchup is a good mix of fighters looking to find a quick finish.

The article offers a higher-risk alternative with its own supporting evidence: Saint-Denis is +155 to win by KO, TKO or DQ, because Frevola has two knockout losses on his ledger — one in an even one minute and the other in just seven seconds — so Saint-Denis can finish fights quickly and Frevola has gotten drubbed out of fights fast in the past.
