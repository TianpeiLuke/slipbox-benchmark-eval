# NASCAR $7.7 Billion Media Rights Deal

The NASCAR media rights deal reported by Essentially Sports on Dec. 1, 2023 is a seven-year agreement worth $7.7 billion, finalized just days earlier with Fox Sports, NBC, Amazon and Warner Bros. Discovery, securing an average annual revenue of $1.1 billion — almost a 40% increase over the sport's existing media rights deal.

There is no denying that the new deal stunned the NASCAR community; with many still trying to digest it, it gave rise to divided opinions, and while the figure sounds lucrative, there seemed to be more to it than meets the eye. It is public knowledge that NASCAR officials invested plenty of time in negotiating the deal, and securing $1.1 billion a year is something, especially when the sport is supposedly on the wane — it looked like the sanctioning body made it count when it mattered most. The boundary case that complicates the headline number is the audience trend: with the Cup Series already facing a 5% drop in viewership that year, Sports Business Journal journalist Adam Stern, appearing on the Dirty Mo Live podcast, discussed how the new deal could affect the ratings.
