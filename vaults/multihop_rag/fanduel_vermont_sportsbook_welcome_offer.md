# How To Claim FanDuel Sportsbook Vermont's Prelive And Welcome Offers

FanDuel's Vermont offer combines a prelive sign-up bonus with its standard welcome offer, for a total of $300 in Bonus Bets, Sporting News explained on 2023-09-26. Vermont already knows FanDuel thanks to its daily fantasy sports site; FanDuel Sportsbook is one of the most popular across the US and had already applied for a license to launch in Vermont. Its welcome offer invites new sign-ups simply to make an initial $5 wager and get back $200 in Bonus Bets, while for Vermont bettors FanDuel was running an Early Sign Up Special giving an extra $100 in Bonus Bets for signing up early between then and Jan. 10, 2024.

The steps, in order: use the links on the page to create your FanDuel Sportsbook account; register before Jan. 10, 2024 at 11:59 p.m. ET and receive $100 in Bonus Bets available to use on the Jan. 11 launch day; once the site goes live, make a first deposit of at least $10 into your FanDuel account and place a qualifying bet of at least $5; whether your bet wins or loses, you receive $200 in Bonus Bets. The conditions attached: you can use your Bonus Bets in whatever increments you like, but you have to use them within seven days or they will expire, and winning wagers with Bonus Bets award the winnings only and not the bet amounts. On the product itself, you can bet on around two dozen different sports at FanDuel, with lots of props, futures, alternate lines and popular premade same-game parlays, plus ongoing promos like profit boosts, free pools, pick-em contests and special "no sweat" bets. On what you can win: whatever you win with your Bonus Bets is yours to keep, which should mean a nice profit given that the prelive offer gave $100 in Bonus Bets for free and the welcome offer requires only a $5 bet up front to receive $200. The article also warns that FanDuel sometimes adds perks to the offer, as it did at the start of the NBA season when it threw in a free three-month subscription to NBA League Pass.
