# Fitness And Mindfulness Subscription Gifts

For health and wellness enthusiasts, The Verge lists services offering a wealth of streamable fitness classes for working out at home, alongside gifts for self-care through meditation or even massages.

Peloton's monthly subscription is listed at $13 — $12.99 a month for thousands of streamable fitness classes spanning strength training to stretching, available to everybody without owning a Peloton device. A Headspace gift card gives members access to hundreds of expert-taught meditations designed to help them relax, sleep better, and improve their mental health, at $12.99 monthly or $69.99 annually. Fitbit Premium subscriptions start at $9.99 a month and are available to all, even without a Fitbit — though owners get more in-depth metrics — and include thousands of guided and customizable workouts covering everything from martial arts and dance to meditation, plus guided programs on topics like nutrition and the option of one-on-one work with a professional health coach for an extra $54.99 per month. For those who prefer in-person classes, a ClassPass gift card lets a giftee try thousands of local gyms and fitness studios as well as nearby salons and spas. And for someone too busy to prepare healthy meals, a gift card to Fresh N Lean, Blue Apron, HelloFresh, or any meal prep service with a healthy selection of meal kits will be very welcome.
