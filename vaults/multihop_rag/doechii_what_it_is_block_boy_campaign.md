# Doechii's "What It Is (Block Boy)" Campaign

Tampa-born rapper and singer Doechii, signed to CMG via a joint venture partnership with Top Dawg and TDE at the start of Michelle Jubelirer's reign as Chair and CEO, was among Capitol's priorities over the past 12 months. Her "What It Is (Block Boy)" is closing in on becoming the artist's first Platinum record in the States and, earlier in the week of the 7 December 2023 interview, was named TikTok's fourth biggest global track of 2023. A debut Doechii album is anticipated in 2024, and other new-act priorities at CMG next year include Young Miko and Rylo Rodriguez.

Jubelirer cites the track as a case in point for the company's methods: it is platinum in the U.S. and has surpassed 1.1 billion worldwide streams so far, and the team instigated innovative digital strategies through TikTok in Nigeria, then Kenya, which in turn prompted a reaction in the Philippines and then in China with Douyin. Within a few weeks there were 1.5 million daily creates using the artist's music across those platforms.
