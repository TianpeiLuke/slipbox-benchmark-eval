# Analyst Assessment Of Altman's Departure Impact

Gartner analyst Arun Chandrasekaran argued that Altman's exit "is indeed shocking as he has been the face of" generative AI technology — the ground for the shock being his public role rather than any operational gap.

Chandrasekaran qualified the consequence for the company itself: OpenAI still has a "deep bench of technical leaders," but its next executives will have to steer it through the challenges of scaling the business and meeting the expectations of regulators and society.
