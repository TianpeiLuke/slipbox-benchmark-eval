# Democratic Dominance In Campaign Social Media

Democrats are still dominating the Republican party in digital strategy, TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-02, even as Ramaswamy rakes in TikTok engagement in ways he did not intend. Ocasio-Cortez's Instagram Stories garnered her nationwide popularity when she campaigned in 2018, reaching further than her local constituency, and in the years since, Democratic campaigns have increasingly prioritized social media strategy, establishing politician-influencers who wield content for votes.

The quantitative anchor is a 2022 midterm report by the Alliance for Securing Democracy, which found that in the Senate races 47% of Democratic candidates had TikTok accounts compared to 12% of Republican candidates. Of the major party House candidates, 30% of Democrats and 10% of Republicans were on TikTok; as a platform, TikTok was most prevalent in gubernatorial races.
