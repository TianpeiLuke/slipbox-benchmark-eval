# Xbox Series X Holiday Price Drop, 2023

As of 2023-12-09, Walmart and Amazon were offering the Xbox Series X as a standalone purchase for $349.99 — the same price as the earlier Diablo 4 Xbox Series X bundle, and a $150 discount off the standard price of the Microsoft console. Polygon's stated position is that nobody should pay more than $350 for an Xbox Series X during the holidays.

The trade-off is that this deal lacks a pre-packaged game, but Polygon notes plenty of sales were floating around to add excellent titles to an Xbox library without paying full price. Amazon was offering modest discounts on $50 Xbox Gift Cards at $45 each, with no stated limit on quantity, and Target had discounted three-month Game Pass Ultimate subscriptions to $44.99 from $49.99, giving access to a rotating library of titles for the Series X including Hi-Fi Rush, Cocoon, Venba, and Starfield.
