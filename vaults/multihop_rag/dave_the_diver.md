# Dave the Diver

Dave the Diver, developed by Mintrocket for Nintendo Switch and Windows PC, is ranked 12 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31. Polygon says you could correctly describe it as a fishing game and a restaurant management simulator, but that would undersell it considerably.

Diving into the mysterious Blue Hole, Dave spends the first two quarters of his day swimming deeper into the colorful abyss, discovering both sea life and a story that is equally absurd and earnest. When not picking up sea urchins or spearfishing sharks, Dave assists the rest of the cast — his sushi business partners, a community of seafolk, an anime-obsessed weapons expert, and a pair of dolphins. At night he slings sushi and pours drinks at the restaurant, frantically running back and forth between clearing dishes, delivering sushi, and refilling the freshly ground wasabi. In between, Dave is harvesting rice and vegetables on a farm, curating a hatchery, racing seahorses with mermaids, and taking down a suspicious group masquerading as environmental activists — and somehow there is even a well-done rhythm video game, starring one of those anime idols the arms dealer loves, that makes perfect sense.

Critic N. Carpenter says it really should not work and that she cannot imagine another game where all these disparate ideas coalesce so seamlessly, but the game would feel less complete without any one of them: it makes for such a compelling loop and such consistent advancement of the story that she kept finding herself in a "one more day" mindset.
