# Altman's Argument On AI's Promise And Existential Risk

Sam Altman predicted that AI will prove to be "the greatest leap forward of any of the big technological revolutions we've had so far."

He paired that claim with its counterweight rather than leaving it alone: he also acknowledged the need for guardrails, calling attention to the existential dangers future AI could pose.
