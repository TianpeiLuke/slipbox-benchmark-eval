# AfroFuture Audience Demographics

About 45% of AfroFuture's festival audience attends from North America, 13% from Europe and Asia, and about 12% come from African countries other than Ghana and South Africa, according to co-founder and CEO Abdul Karim Abdullah in October 2023.

He describes AfroFuture as a global platform that invites people from all over the world, and an event for those who enjoy the intersection of music, food, fashion and art. The brand resonates with the global Black diaspora, and has received significant support from the United States, the United Kingdom, the Caribbean and neighbouring African countries. Attendees are largely upwardly mobile, tech-forward Gen Z and millennial consumers who are committed to the culture and represent over 40 countries across the globe.
