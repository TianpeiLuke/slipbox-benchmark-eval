# Diamondbacks Land Eduardo Rodriguez On A Four-Year Deal

Left-hander Eduardo Rodriguez reportedly agreed to a four-year contract with Arizona worth around $20 million per year on Wednesday, Dec. 6, 2023, according to ESPN's Jeff Passan as carried in Sporting News' winter meetings tracker; the deal was still pending a physical per Passan's report.

Sporting News framed it as the reigning NL champions landing one of the free agent market's top pitchers.

A season earlier, Rodriguez posted a career-low 3.30 ERA across 152.2 innings with the Tigers and pitched to a 3.66 FIP. He decided to opt out of his deal with Detroit to test the free-agent market.
