# Palo Alto Networks Acquires Dig Security For $400 Million

Palo Alto Networks is acquiring Israeli tech company Dig Security for an estimated $400 million, TechCrunch reported on November 3, 2023.

The deal comes at a time when the tech industry in Israel is disrupted due to political events.

This acquisition will bolster Palo Alto's cloud security-focused Prisma business.
