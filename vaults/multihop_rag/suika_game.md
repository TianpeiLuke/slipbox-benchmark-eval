# Suika Game

Suika Game, developed by Aladdin X for Nintendo Switch, is ranked 43 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31. Critic JL describes it as an engrossing 2048-meets-Tetris-with-physics fruit drop game that has become her go-to whenever she needs to kill some time, and credits VTubers with introducing her to it.

She specifies that she is talking about the official Nintendo Switch version, not the horrific ad-plagued knockoffs that have flooded the App Store.

What makes it special, in addition to its cutesy gameplay, is its quality as a social game: the same way people sit around and talk about their NYT Connections, they sit around and talk about their fruitless attempts at getting double watermelons or breaking the 3,000 point threshold. It is also a great game to watch — nothing is funnier than seeing somebody's Suika run go downhill in 30 seconds flat, which JL notes is a reason the game has taken the streaming world by storm.
