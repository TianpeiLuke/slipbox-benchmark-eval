# Liverpool-Arsenal Draw At Anfield, December 2023

Liverpool and Arsenal played out a thrilling 1-1 draw at Anfield on Saturday 23 December 2023, a result that ensured the London side would top the English Premier League on Christmas Day, as reported by The Roar that day.

Arsenal led through defender Gabriel's fourth-minute header from Martin Odegaard's free kick — described as the Gunners' fastest ever goal at Anfield in Premier League history, giving them a three-and-a-half-minute lead — before Mohamed Salah beat David Raya at his near post to level in the 29th minute. Liverpool came closest in the second half and hit the woodwork twice, through Trent Alexander-Arnold and substitute Harvey Elliott.

The standings consequence is stated precisely: Mikel Arteta's team go into Christmas a point clear of Liverpool and third-placed Aston Villa.
