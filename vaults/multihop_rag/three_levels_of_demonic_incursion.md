# Three Levels of Demonic Incursion

"Demonologists distinguish three levels of demonic incursion," explained Fr. Dan Reehil: temptation, obsession (which is also called "oppression") and possession. The three form an ascending structure, distinguished by how far the demonic influence reaches into the person.

Temptation is the universal level — something experienced by everyone. Obsession is the middle level: it is "when demons torment people physically, spiritually, mentally or emotionally, or some combination of these." Possession, arguably the most well-known of the three levels, is when a demon or demons "take physical possession of a person's body and make use of its faculties," said Reehil.

The model carries a limiting condition at its top level: possession is not continuous. "Possession is usually intermittent, almost never an uninterrupted possessing of the person's body," Reehil added.
