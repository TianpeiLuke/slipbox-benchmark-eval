# Mario Party on Switch

The Mario Party series reigns supreme as a family-friendly, chill party time, in Polygon's assessment; it appears in the outlet's 2023-10-20 list of the 35 best couch co-op games for Nintendo Switch.

In this board game-style title, players pick their favorite Nintendo character and then roll the dice to land on a spot on the board.

Players compete in minigames, including a baseball-bat-swinging rhythm game, a rowboat race, snow catching, and many more.
