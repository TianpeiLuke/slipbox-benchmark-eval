# GOP Attacks On EVs As "Faux Populism"

Sydney Ghazarian, an organizer with the climate-focused Labor Network for Sustainability, argued on the Citations Needed podcast that Trump and the GOP's attacks on EVs smack of "faux populism," since Republicans largely stand opposed to unionization.

The record she points to undercuts the pro-worker premise of the attacks: when he was president, Trump appointed anti-union judges and anti-union members to the National Labor Relations Board; his Supreme Court judges issued rulings that were devastating to public sector unions; and his promises to prevent auto factories from shutting down largely failed. Ghazarian also attacked the industry's framing of the strike itself: "What this industry narrative about UAW's demands costing too much alongside the EV transition seems to neglect is the fact that auto companies are getting billions of dollars from taxpayer-funded EV subsidies to make it work. It's their responsibility to use public funding in ways that serve the public and planetary good, you know, and central to that is not leaving workers and communities behind in the transition to a green economy."
