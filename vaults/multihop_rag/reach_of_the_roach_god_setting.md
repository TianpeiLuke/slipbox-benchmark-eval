# Reach Of The Roach God

Reach of the Roach God is the hardcover high-watermark of A Thousand Thousand Islands, a series of zines presenting a system-agnostic setting inspired by the folklore of Southeast Asia — included as a bonus entry in Polygon's 2023 roundup.

In it, Odoyoq, the roach god, senses new ways it can insinuate itself into the lives of non-roachkind. The three schemes are presented both as traditional adventures and as collections of facts — about the setting, about the people living there and their motivations, about the desires of the god and the machinations of its agents — and events quickly throw a small village, a monastery, and a necropolis into chaos. Two gazetteers and a sourcebook on the roaches follow, giving the GM ample material to expand the campaign. All of it is wrapped in Munkao's artwork, whose vistas and cultural portraits the contributor calls gorgeous, while his work on feelers and chitin and filth is revolting in the best possible way. The buying advice comes with a scope condition: this is likely the final installment in the ATTI series and unlikely to be reprinted, so get the book while you can.
