# Samsung Galaxy S23 Series

The entire Samsung Galaxy S23 series was on sale for Prime Day, including the S23, S23+ and S23 Ultra.

Engadget calls these some of the best Android phones you can buy right now, and recommends springing for the S23 Ultra if you want all of the latest features from Samsung.

The Ultra runs on a Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 chip and has a big, bold 120Hz display, built-in S Pen and an excellent main camera.
