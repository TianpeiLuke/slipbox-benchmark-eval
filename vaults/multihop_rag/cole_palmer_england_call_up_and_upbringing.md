# Cole Palmer's England Call-Up And Upbringing

Cole Palmer said an England call-up had not formed part of his thinking when he joined Chelsea, even though his excellence at Stamford Bridge created the opportunity. "I wasn't even thinking of an England call-up when I first went to Chelsea," he said.

Nor was it on his agenda as a boy, when his mother banned him from playing in the garden. "I was always breaking her pipes," he recalled. He has come a long way since then, particularly in 2023. "Yeah, it's been crazy," he said. "The changes have happened so fast. From winning the Euros to signing for Chelsea then getting signed up here."
