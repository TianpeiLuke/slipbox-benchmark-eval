# Siya Kolisi's Reaction After The 2023 World Cup Final

Springboks captain Siya Kolisi said after the 2023 Rugby World Cup final that he could not explain the win — "There are no ways I can explain it" — and gave credit to the beaten All Blacks.

He singled out New Zealand's resilience with a man down: "I want to give credit to the All Blacks. They took us to the end, they took us to a dark place. It shows what kind of team they are, to fight with a man down from early in the game. They put us under so much pressure."

He closed by crediting his own side and expressing relief: "Credit to my boys too for the fight. I am just grateful we could pull it off."
