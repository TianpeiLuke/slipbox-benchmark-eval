# Pregame And First Pitch For World Series Game 4, 2023

Game 4 of the 2023 World Series between the Rangers and Diamondbacks was played on Tuesday, Oct. 31 at Chase Field in Phoenix, with first pitch set for 8:03 p.m. ET (5:03 p.m. local time).

The pregame updates began at 4:45 p.m. with a lineup change: Emmanuel Rivera was replacing Evan Longoria in the Diamondbacks' lineup for Game 4. At 6:45 p.m. the blog noted that the Diamondbacks were earning Chris Russo's trust after the two spent some time together before the game.

At 7:35 p.m. the Sporting News framed the stakes for Texas: for all of the Rangers' losses, a win that night with Nathan Eovaldi and Jordan Montgomery in line to start the next two games would put Texas in a terrific position. At 7:55 p.m. the blog reported that Jon Rahm and Michael Phelps were among the stars in attendance at Chase Field.
