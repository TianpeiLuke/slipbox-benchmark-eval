# Pebble's Missing Native Mobile App

Among the other things that may have hurt Pebble's traction was its lack of a native mobile app. As T2, the startup prioritized building for the web to be agile and more flexible, but lost out on the opportunities for discovery driven by the app stores.

Co-founder and CTO Michael Greer's assessment, given to TechCrunch on 2023-10-24: "that might have been a mistake."
