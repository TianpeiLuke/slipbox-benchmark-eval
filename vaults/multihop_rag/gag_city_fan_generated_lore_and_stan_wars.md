# Gag City's Fan-Generated Lore And Stan Wars

X (formerly Twitter) users began crafting elaborate narratives about Gag City's inhabitants and government in early December 2023. One posted an image of Barbz storming the Pink House, which another user described as the fandom's own January 6th; another posted an image of pink-clad citizens protesting in the streets of Gag City, calling for Minaj to release the album's track list. Though some may believe Gag City is a utopia, one account posted an image of a matronly Minaj handing out CDs of her album to impoverished children "on the outskirts of Gag City," implying that the pink society also has a class divide problem.

The arrivals genre carried the joke. On 7 December 2023 posts announced that "Lois Griffin, Marge Simpson, and Linda Belcher have arrived in Gag City," that "Starbucks lemon loaf has arrived in Gag City," and asked "not the homophobic dog arriving in Gag City?!," while another user offered the "fun fact: katy perry is actually the first artist that ever recorded a music video in gag city (2010)." One post showed "nicki minaj address[ing] the citizens of gag city prior to the inauguration tonight."

Gag City is also riddled with stan wars, as fans of rival pop stars posted images of their faves vying for Minaj's seat at the head of her city's government. In a nod to Greek mythology, one account posted an image of a Trojan horse decorated in Beyoncé's "Renaissance" disco motif, announcing on 8 December: "BREAKING: The Barbz have discovered a mysterious, metallic horse at the front gates of Gag City. An inscription reads 'For their return home, the Hive dedicate this offering to Onika.'"
