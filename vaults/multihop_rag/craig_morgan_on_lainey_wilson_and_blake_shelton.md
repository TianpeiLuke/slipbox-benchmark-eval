# Craig Morgan On Lainey Wilson And Blake Shelton

Craig Morgan told Fox News Digital, in an interview published on 16 October 2023, that he knew Lainey Wilson would be a superstar long before her career took off, and joked that his close friend Blake Shelton is "a complete idiot all the time".

Of Wilson he said: "I've known since the very beginning. We actually wrote together. I knew Lainey before anyone knew who Lainey Wilson was, because we were on the same label we wrote together." He continued: "And man, I knew way back then that she was going to be a superstar. She's hardworking, she's a God-fearing, America loving, just a beautiful soul. On top of all that, she has a talent that can't be touched right now."

Of Shelton, the joke came with affection: "I meant there's never a time that I'm with Blake that isn't fun. He's been one of my best friends in this business for a long time. So, Blake actually had to do his vocals out in Los Angeles because of his schedule. But you know what? He's a such a sport and a champ but an idiot as well."
