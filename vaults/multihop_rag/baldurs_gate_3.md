# Baldur's Gate 3

Baldur's Gate 3, developed by Larian Studios for PlayStation 5 and Windows PC, is ranked 2 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31, and Polygon says it hit at the perfect moment.

The timing argument has two parts. Despite the handful of truly great computer role-playing games released since the early 2000s — Larian itself made waves with its Divinity: Original Sin series in 2014 and 2017 — the genre had largely remained a modern niche; but Dungeons & Dragons has undergone something of a renaissance thanks to actual-play series like Critical Role and Dimension 20, and Larian had two years of player feedback with which to build its new masterpiece. That masterpiece launched in August and garnered the kind of attention that catapults a game from niche interests to widespread acclaim: old CRPG fans finally came home, the Mass Effect generation discovered a new kind of RPG, and D&D fans found an exceptional digital version of their beloved tabletop game to play alone or with their party in co-op.

Critic RG writes that like a good DM, the game teases out story beats that feel personal to you, cleverly luring you into experiences that seem like they would not make sense in anyone else's campaign; every act delivers quiet, memorable character moments and anxiety-inducing battles, all feeling organic, letting the game replicate as closely as a video game can D&D's best feature — the intoxicating sense that anything could happen at any moment. The best games that year told their stories in interesting ways, but Baldur's Gate 3 takes experimental storytelling to another level, with every choice and every new direction a simple success, fail, or crit away; one roll of the dice after another can take you all the way from a crashed Nautiloid to the city of Baldur's Gate itself, and it is the spinning whir of its d20 that defined video game storytelling in 2023.
