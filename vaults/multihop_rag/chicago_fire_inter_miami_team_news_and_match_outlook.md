# Chicago Fire-Inter Miami Lineups, Absentees And Pre-Match Outlook

For the Oct. 4, 2023 meeting at Soldier Field, Sporting News listed Chicago Fire (4-2-3-1, right to left) as 34. Brady (GK) — 24. Dean, 16. Omsberg, 5. Czichos, 6. Navarro — 26. Doumbia, 30. G. Gimenez — 17. B. Gutierrez (Haile-Selassie, 55'), 10. Shaqiri, 21. Herbers (Teran, 85') — 19. Koutsias (Przybylko, 69'), and Inter Miami (4-3-3, right to left) as 1. Callender (GK) — 2. Yedlin, 27. Kryvtsov, 31. Miller, 32. Allen — 3. Arroyo (J. Mota, 67'), 5. Busquets, 30. Cremaschi — 16. Taylor (Stefanelli, 67'), 17. Martinez, 11. Farias. The same report also carried a Chicago lineup listing of Brady (GK) — Dean, Omsberg, Teran, Navarro — Doumbia, G. Gimenez — B. Gutierrez, Shaqiri, J. Torres — Kamara with a nine-man bench of Richey (GK), Teran, Haile-Selassie, Casas Jr, Aceves, Burks, Souquet, Przybylko and Kamara, and an Inter Miami listing of Callender (GK) — Yedlin, Kryvtsov, Miller, Allen — Arroyo, Busquets, Cremaschi — Farias, Martinez, Taylor with a bench of Dos Santos (GK), McVey, Sailor, Ulloa, J. Mota, D. Ruiz, Sunderland and Stefanelli.

Frank Klopas rotated his two young attackers, Brian Gutierrez and Jairo Torres, along with famed Swiss international Xherdan Shaqiri in the attack, and kept his striker rotation rolling with Kei Kamara and Georgios Koutsias alternating up front and MLS veteran Kacper Przybylko coming off the bench. Injuries to defenders Mauricio Pineda and Miguel Navarro, plus striker Victor Bezerra, kept them out of the match, while Chris Mueller was done for the year with a hip problem.

Inter Miami were definitely without both Jordi Alba and Leonardo Campana, out with muscle injuries. Midfielder Gregore had returned to training after months out with a foot injury and could be involved in coming matches as he ramped up his fitness. Tomas Aviles was suspended for yellow card accumulation, while Ian Fray and Corentin Jean were out for the season with ACL injuries. The outlook: Inter Miami had been mired in a slump without Lionel Messi, and with the Argentine likely to miss out again, the Herons were heavy underdogs on the road at Chicago; goals were expected because both sides had conceded in bunches for most of the year.
