# Eintracht Frankfurt 5-1 Bayern Munich

Eintracht Frankfurt pulverised Bayern Munich 5-1 on Dec. 9, 2023, snapping the champions' unbeaten Bundesliga run that season with a sensational three-goal first-half performance.

In an explosive first half Eintracht completely overran Bayern and exposed their error-prone backline, scoring three times in 24 minutes. Omar Marmoush opened the account in the 12th minute, drilling in the rebound after Fares Chaibi had hit the crossbar following a miskick from Bayern's Noussair Mazraoui; after more slapdash defending Éric Dina Ebimbe doubled the lead in the 31st minute and Hugo Larsson made it 3-0 five minutes later, benefiting from a Joshua Kimmich error. Kimmich made amends just before the break with a powerful shot from 20 yards, but it did not stop the home side: five minutes after the restart Eintracht made it 4-1 with another textbook quick break, Ebimbe beating Manuel Neuer for his second of the afternoon, and Ansgar Knauff slotted in to make it 5-1 on the hour — Frankfurt becoming the first team in 48 years to score five goals in one hour against Bayern in the Bundesliga since their own 6-0 win in 1975. "There is no need for an explanation," said Bayern midfielder Thomas Müller. "We have to congratulate Frankfurt who deserved the win. They took the fight out of us in that first half hour. When you lose 5-1 then a reaction has to follow. We won't lose our heads now. We don't have enough time but we will bounce back." Bayern, who had a game in hand after the previous week's game against Union Berlin was postponed due to snow, remained second on 32 points, with leaders Bayer Leverkusen on 35 travelling to third-placed VfB Stuttgart, who had 30 points, on the Sunday.
