# Tony Pollard's Fantasy Outlook In 2023

Sporting News grew steadily more confident in Tony Pollard through late November 2023, moving him back toward the top of the running back rankings on Nov. 21 and calling him a clear start for the Week 13 Thursday night game on Nov. 30.

The Nov. 21 case was relief at a return to form: Pollard looked solid against Carolina the previous week, averaging 5.1 yards per carry and finally getting into the end zone for the first time since Week 1, which put the Cowboys back just outside the top five for a juicy matchup against a Washington defense that had just been lit up by Saquon Barkley and the Giants.

The Nov. 30 case adds the game script and the opponent. Pollard and the Cowboys' defense are a reason Dak Prescott may not need to go nuts passing; the Seahawks have now joined the bottom five teams in giving up fantasy points to running backs; and Pollard has come on over the past two weeks against the Panthers and Commanders after sliding into RB2 range, projecting to something like 80 yards and a score. Rico Dowdle, by contrast, is a stretch to get mop-up production and is a sit.
