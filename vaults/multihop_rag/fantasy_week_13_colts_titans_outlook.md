# Week 13 Colts-Titans: Expect Plenty Of Rushing From Both Sides

CBS Sports argues that the Week 13 Colts-Titans game should be run-heavy on both sides, making Zack Moss and Derrick Henry the plays and the passing games secondary. On the Indianapolis side, losing Jonathan Taylor stings for the Colts, but Zack Moss was unreal in his efficiency between Weeks 2 through 5 when Taylor was either out or barely played — 5.0 yards per carry is given as a generic example — and he has only been a little bit worse, 4.4 yards per carry, since Taylor took a larger chunk of the workload starting in Week 6. On the Tennessee side, Derrick Henry was back in a familiar spot handling plenty of carries against an inferior opponent, scoring twice and tallying 76 rush yards, and the pattern offered as the warrant is that anytime he is in a close game or a Titans win he tends to do well.

The matchup evidence supports volume for both backfields: the Colts rank in the bottom five in Fantasy points allowed per game this season while the Titans are closer to league average, but both are in the top six in running-back rush attempts per game against — at least 24.0 each — over their past four games. The verdicts: STARTS — Michael Pittman, Derrick Henry and Zack Moss; SITS — DeAndre Hopkins (PPR flex at best), Josh Downs (PPR flex at best), Will Levis and the Titans DST; SLEEPERS — the Colts DST and Gardner Minshew.
