# Jamie Carragher's "Sad End" Verdict On Ronaldo's Saudi Move

Liverpool legend Jamie Carragher described the five-time Ballon d'Or winner's switch to the Gulf nation as a "sad end" to his career. The claim this attacks is the premise that Cristiano Ronaldo remained an elite goalscorer after leaving European football — Carragher's verdict treats the Saudi move as the closing, diminished chapter of a career rather than a continuation of it.

The counter-evidence offered against Carragher's reading is Ronaldo's own output: he continues to prove his doubters wrong.
