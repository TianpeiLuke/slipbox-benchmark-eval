# Netflix Originals Premiering November 7 To 10, 2023

Eleven Netflix originals appear in this block of Polygon's November 2023 listing, each carrying Netflix's own synopsis.

The Improv: 60 and Still Standing, a Netflix Comedy: The Improv turns 60 with original performances from today's most celebrated artists and biggest concert acts, along with exclusive and rarely seen moments from one of the largest comedy archives — a one-night event honoring artists who defined comedy and culture for decades, all while standing in front of a simple brick wall. The Billionaire, the Butler and the Boyfriend, a Netflix Documentary: how a conflict between the world's wealthiest woman and her daughter spiralled into national scandal. The Claus Family 3, a Netflix Film: when the delivery of presents goes wrong and Grandpa Noël gets in trouble, siblings Jules and Noor must work together to save Christmas. Cyberbunker: The Criminal Underworld, a Netflix Documentary: how a group of hackers powered the darkest corners of the internet from a Cold War-era bunker in a quiet German tourist town. Escaping Twin Flames, a Netflix Documentary: a three-part series from the Emmy-nominated team behind Seduced: Inside the NXIVM Cult, pulling back the veil on Twin Flames Universe, a controversial online community led by Jeff and Shaleia that sells online classes guaranteeing harmonious union with your destined partner, revealing through exclusive access to former members stories of coercion and exploitation — from encouraging stalking behavior to manipulating gender identities — and documenting family members' efforts to rescue their loved ones. Robbie Williams, a Netflix Documentary: after 25 years of his record-breaking solo career, Robbie looks back on his younger self and reflects on a lifetime spent in the spotlight. Akuma Kun, a Netflix Anime: a boy raised by a demon works with his half-human partner Mephisto III as paranormal investigators solving murders and mysteries. Temple of Film: 100 Years of the Egyptian Theatre, a Netflix Documentary: Guillermo del Toro, Rian Johnson and other film luminaries look back at LA's historic Egyptian Theatre as it returns to its former movie palace glory. At the Moment, a Netflix Series: an anthology set during the pandemic following 10 unique love stories of passion and heartache. The Killer, a Netflix Film: after a fateful near miss, an assassin battles his employers — and himself — on an international hunt for retribution he insists is not personal.
