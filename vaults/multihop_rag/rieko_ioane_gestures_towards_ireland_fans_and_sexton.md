# All Blacks Defend Rieko Ioane's Gestures Toward Ireland Fans And Sexton

The All Blacks downplayed Rieko Ioane's gestures towards Ireland fans and retiring great Johnny Sexton ahead of their 2023 World Cup semi-final in Paris, with the three-time world champions saying they had learned to "respect Argentina" before Saturday's match (6am AEDT).

The incident followed New Zealand's 28-24 nail-biting quarter-final win over Ireland: Ioane copped a spray from Sexton after the lethal back cupped his ear at Irish supporters at the Stade de France after the final whistle and put his finger to his lips in a 'shush gesture'.

Ian Foster, who made two changes to his starting side and three in total, defended his centre's actions while twisting the knife into Sexton's finish: "It happens on most sporting parks when tensions are high and a lot at stake. Unfortunately recently it's also included players saying a few things to referees. Is it right for the game? I don't know. You don't hear a lot of players complaining about it." Captain Sam Cane added: "It's players' different personalities and emotions running high in those moments. There's always people who have different tactics but it's always just left out there."
