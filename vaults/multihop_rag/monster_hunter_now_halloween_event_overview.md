# Monster Hunter Now Halloween Event Overview

Halloween in Monster Hunter Now is the first themed event to come to Capcom and Niantic's monster-hunting AR game, as Polygon reported on 2023-10-25.

The main draw is acquiring the Jack-o'-Head Halloween armor, which is sourced from collecting Pumpkin Tickets, a currency specific to the event.

The weekend also introduces a pumpkin rock Kulu-Ya-Ku and the chance to add a Halloween medal to your collection.
