# Atlassian's Acquisition Of Loom In 2023

Atlassian announced it was acquiring the former tech unicorn Loom, a US-based pioneer in enterprise video chat and screen recording. The Age reported the deal as its quote of the day on 12 October 2023.

The quote attached to the announcement, attributed in the report to Loom co-founder and billionaire Mike Cannon-Brookes, was: "With the continued growth of distributed work, Loom helps bridge the collaboration gaps for dispersed teams in deeply human ways."
