# Laura Jean McKay's Short Story Collection

Laura Jean McKay's short story collection, published by Scribe at $29.99 on 3 October, gathers work written over 20 years and again features plenty of animal life, in stories ranging in length from one page to 20, The Sydney Morning Herald listed on 1 October 2023.

Its context is her earlier success: McKay won the richest writing prize in the country during the pandemic for her timely novel The Animals in that Country, about a virulent virus that allowed infected people to understand what animals were saying, and was quickly called prescient — "whether that label sticks in her return to the short story remains to be seen".

The reviewer's recommendation is direct: "If you haven't read her, now's the time to start."
