# Inter Miami's 2024 Season Ticket Prices Rose Up To 113% After Messi's Arrival

Inter Miami's 2024 season ticket prices rose enormously over 2023, with the cheapest package jumping from $485 to $884 — an increase of 78.8% — and a lower-level midfield club seat, which includes upscale food and beverage options, going from $3,600 to $7,650, an increase of 113% that more than doubled year-over-year. A discount was available for fans who renewed by October 7. Lionel Messi's arrival had brought a craze to glimpse one of the sport's most talented and legendary players, and with the skyrocketing attention came a leap in prices that the club appeared to be passing on to supporters.

The result was that in 2024 Inter Miami would sport some of the world's most expensive tickets, rivaling Champions League giants such as Barcelona and Arsenal and other massive European clubs while dwarfing other MLS ticket packages. Fox Sports reported the club had an extensive waiting list for 2024 season tickets, but the massive price increase could push out many of the team's most dedicated supporters who had been with the team since its inception in 2020, causing massive turnover in the fanbase. Sporting News also noted that for most clubs, season-ticket packages include only home league matches, and while members usually get first access to knockout-competition tickets, those come at additional cost.

The full 2024 pricing structure, courtesy of Inter Miami's season ticketing release via the Miami Herald on September 28, 2023, ran as follows, with sections 2 through B considered "premium" seating and C through L "standard": 2 Mid Loge $45,900; 3 North Loge $42,840; 4 South Loge $42,840; 5 Midfield Club $7,650; 6 East Field Seats $13,005; A Northwest Prime $5,440; B Northwest Midfield $4,420; C West Sideline $2,720; D West Reserved $2,380; E East Midfield $2,635; F East Upper Midfield $2,125; G East Sideline $1,955; H East Reserved $1,360; I North Goaline $1,054; J South Goaline $1,020; K South Stand $867; L North Stand Reserved $884.
