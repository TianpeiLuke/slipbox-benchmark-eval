# BetMGM Player Props For The Scooter's Coffee Frisco Bowl

BetMGM Sportsbook offered player props on five players for the Scooter's Coffee Frisco Bowl between the Roadrunners and the Thundering Herd, per Sporting News on Dec. 18, 2023.

Frank Harris's props were 237.5 passing yards, OVER (-115) / UNDER (-115); passing TDs OVER 1.5 (-125) / UNDER 1.5 (-105); and anytime touchdown scorer (-110). Joshua Cephus's props were 80.5 receiving yards, OVER (-115) / UNDER (-115), and anytime touchdown scorer (-190). Rasheen Ali's props were 80.5 rushing yards, OVER (-115) / UNDER (-115), and anytime touchdown scorer (-175). Kevorian Barnes's props were 64.5 rushing yards, OVER (-115) / UNDER (-115), and anytime touchdown scorer (-135). Robert Henry had an anytime touchdown scorer prop at (-130).
