# WIRED Black Friday 2023 MacBook Air And Mac Mini Deals

WIRED's Apple Black Friday guide of 25 November 2023 covered four discounted consumer Macs: the 13-inch M2 MacBook Air, the 15-inch M2 MacBook Air, the M1 MacBook Air and the Mac Mini.

The M2 MacBook Air (7/10, WIRED Recommends) has a modern design with a MagSafe connector so the laptop will not fly off if you trip on the cable, and the M2 chip delivers plenty of power for everyday tasks, solid battery life and a 1080p webcam. WIRED is careful about the price history: it has dipped lower, to $899, in the past and it hits this price often, but WIRED still calls it a solid deal, while noting Apple is rumoured to launch M3-powered 13-inch and 15-inch MacBook Airs next year. The 15-inch MacBook Air (8/10, WIRED Recommends) is the first large-screen option in the Air lineup and packs the same features as the 13-inch version including the redesigned chassis and 1080p webcam, with the M2 delivering excellent performance and about 10 hours of battery life; WIRED recommends opting for 16 gigabytes of RAM if it fits your budget, and notes it is also on sale for $1,449 ($250 off).

The M1-powered MacBook Air (9/10, WIRED Recommends) came out in 2020 but remains one of the most powerful laptops you can buy at this price and WIRED's favourite budget MacBook, offering great performance, battery life that easily lasts a full workday, an improved keyboard and a lightweight chassis good for travel — with the caveat that it frequently goes on sale for this price. The Mac Mini (9/10, WIRED Recommends) is a powerful, compact desktop that only needs a monitor, keyboard and mouse; it does not pack as many ports as the M2 Pro version, but still offers two Thunderbolt 4 USB-C ports, two USB-A, HDMI, a 10-gigabit Ethernet connection and a 3.5-mm headphone jack, and WIRED notes it has dipped this low a few times before but is still a great deal.
