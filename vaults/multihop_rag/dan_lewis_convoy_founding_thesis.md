# Dan Lewis's Convoy Founding Thesis

Convoy co-founder and CEO Dan Lewis's argument for starting the company was that trucking combined a very large money flow with unusually little startup competition — "I saw a massive opportunity and few people going after it," Lewis told TechCrunch. The former Amazon and Google executive, who has a background in strategy and management consulting, said that when he was struck by the urge to start a company, he researched the money-attracting industries of the world and then, using AngelList, saw how many companies were trying to disrupt those industries.

The grounds are the counts that search returned: thousands of companies working on industries ranging from telecommunications and fashion to video games and food, while billions of dollars were going into trucking each year but fewer than 30 startups showed an interest in the field. The warrant is that a large industry with few entrants is where opportunity concentrates. Asked later whether his method of deciding on a direction for a startup is still a good method, Lewis said, in short, yes. TechCrunch revisited the May 2022 interview on 2023-10-22, after Convoy's collapse.
