# The Private Sector's Role In Philippine Sustainable Development

Coming off the pandemic and given the current global economic climate, it is opportune for the government to proactively drive actionable policies and programs that focus on building the resiliency of the economy through sustainable development, with greater emphasis on addressing climate change. That is the article's conclusion about timing, and the accompanying claim is that navigating external and domestic economic headwinds will not be an easy feat for the administration.

The warrant for optimism is the private sector's role as an important catalyst for sustainable development: with the private sector sharing industry knowledge and resources and potentially even leading certain socio-economic programs and projects of the National Government, the hope is that AmBisyon Natin 2040, of long and healthy lives for Filipinos that are strongly rooted, comfortable and secure, will be achieved.
