# Nobody Saves the World

Nobody Saves the World has players take on characters named "Nobody" as they work together to attempt to cure the world of a poisonous blight and figure out why they have no memory of their past lives. It appears in Polygon's 2023-10-20 list of the 35 best couch co-op games for Nintendo Switch.

The twist is in progression: instead of a classic perk tree, characters unlock various forms — like a turtle, ghost, knight, or ninja — each with its own set of moves.

What is more, the forms can synergize with one another, making for some incredibly absurd skill combos.
