# Liverpool 3-0 Brentford Salah 200th English Goal

Liverpool beat Brentford 3-0 with Mohamed Salah continuing his remarkable Anfield scoring record with two more goals to bring up his 200th in English football, The Roar reported on Nov. 12, 2023, leaving them second, just a point behind City, with Tottenham fourth and two points adrift.

The Reds broke the deadlock after two earlier efforts were disallowed by VAR. Only Erling Haaland, on 13, had scored more in the Premier League that season than the Egypt international, who took his tally to 10 by scoring for the sixth successive home game to write another entry in the club's history books. Diogo Jota's late strike then put a quick end to questions over Liverpool's mini-stumble after the draw at Luton and the Europa League defeat in Toulouse.
