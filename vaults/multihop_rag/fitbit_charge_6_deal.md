# Fitbit Charge 6 Deal

Fitbit's newest fitness tracker, the Charge 6, was $60 off and down to a record low of $100 for Black Friday 2023.

It's the follow-up to one of Engadget's favorite fitness trackers, the Fitbit Charge 5, and it brings back the physical side button that previous generations had while integrating even more with Google services. It has an AMOLED touchscreen that displays activity stats and smart notifications, a built-in GPS for mapping outdoor workouts, a week-long battery life and the ability to display turn-by-turn directions on its screen from Google Maps.
