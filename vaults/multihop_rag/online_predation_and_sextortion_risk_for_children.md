# Online Predation And Sextortion Risk For Children

Kelman argues that children are structurally unable to protect themselves online, because their brains are not fully developed -- "so they aren't able to sniff out who is safe on the other end of the chat or the game."

Kelman stressed that kids "can't unsee what they have already seen," and that many adult apps may put kids "in harm's way."

The harm she names is coercive and self-perpetuating: "Kids are coerced into sending photos of themselves, often finding themselves victims of sextortion after they share the photos," the expert warned. "This leads to shame, despair and fear of telling their parents."
