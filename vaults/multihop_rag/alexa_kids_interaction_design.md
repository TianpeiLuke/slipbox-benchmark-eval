# The Two-Way Interaction Design Of Explore With Alexa

The interaction model behind "Explore with Alexa" runs in both directions: unlike more traditional conversations with Alexa, it is not just kids asking Alexa a question and receiving a response — Alexa is now asking kids questions too. As Venkataswamy put it, "One of the things we think is really cool about this paradigm is kids aren't just asking Alexa questions and getting the answers — Alexa is now asking kids questions," giving as an example a trivia question like "What's the fastest animal on Earth?"

Kids reach the experience through three paths. The first is an explicit trigger: uttering a particular phrase that kicks off "Explore with Alexa," such as "Alexa, let's explore animals" or "Alexa, tell me an animal fact." The second, described as the more interesting way to use the feature, is organic conversation — a kid asking something like "What does a lion's roar sound like?" or "How fast can a cheetah run?" also drops them into the more conversational Q&A experience. The third path is Alexa-initiated: over the few months following the launch, Alexa will also prompt kids on some occasions, asking if they want to hear something interesting about animals.

The warrant for making Alexa the questioner is pedagogical rather than technical: as any teacher will tell you, by having kids try to think of the answer first, the answer will stick in their minds better when they hear the response. Underneath, the AI's job is content transformation at scale — "What our AI is doing is taking trusted content and then figuring out what's fun, looking at what's fun, turning them into a trivia question — so it is doing useful things for us at scale that we wouldn't have been able to do without this tooling…but we feel really good about the safety guardrails that are in place right now in terms of content," says Venkataswamy.

The model holds over a deliberately narrow range as of this description. "Right now it's, it's narrow. Alexa is asking kids trivia questions," Venkataswamy continues. "But we want to continue expanding on that and making it more interactive." Two-way interaction, in other words, currently means trivia prompts and nothing wider, with expansion stated as an intent rather than a shipped capability.
