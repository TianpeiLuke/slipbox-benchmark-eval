# Silica As A Tire Filler

Silica is an increasingly important tire filler that both displaces less renewable materials and makes tires more efficient. "Conventional tires rely on carbon black to strengthen the rubber matrix as the tire is cured," says Dale Harrigle, Bridgestone's chief engineer for replacement tires. "In a tire that uses silica in the tread compound, a portion of the carbon black is removed and replaced with silica, which allows for increased wet performance and reduced rolling resistance."

Its sourcing is changing too. Silica traditionally comes from sand, but Goodyear, for example, is now using silica sourced from a rice processing byproduct called husk ash.

The combined result: less harmful rubber, more grip, better wear, and better range or fuel economy.
