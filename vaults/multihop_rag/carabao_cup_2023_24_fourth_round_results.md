# Carabao Cup 2023/24 Fourth Round Results

The fourth round of the 2023/24 Carabao Cup trimmed the competition down to 16 teams for the next phase, and saw defending champions Manchester United eliminated 3-0 at home by Newcastle United, per Sporting News on Dec. 19, 2023.

EFL League Two side Mansfield Town were the lowest-ranked team left in the competition and were drawn against League One team Port Vale.

The results were: Tuesday, Oct. 31, Mansfield Town (L2) 0-1 Port Vale (L1); Wednesday, Nov. 1, Ipswich Town (Ch) 1-3 Fulham (PL); Wednesday, Nov. 1, Man United (PL) 0-3 Newcastle United (PL); Wednesday, Nov. 1, Bournemouth (PL) 1-2 Liverpool (PL); Wednesday, Nov. 1, Chelsea (PL) 2-0 Blackburn Rovers (Ch); Wednesday, Nov. 1, West Ham (PL) 3-0 Arsenal (PL); Wednesday, Nov. 1, Everton (PL) 3-0 Burnley (PL); Tuesday, Oct. 31, Exeter City (L1) 2-3 Middlesbrough (Ch).
