# India's 2003 World Cup Semi-Final Against Kenya

India beat underdogs Kenya by 91 runs in the last-four stage match in Durban on 20 March 2003. Man-of-the-Match India skipper Sourav Ganguly scored an unbeaten 111 runs in 114 balls and Man-of-the-Tournament Sachin Tendulkar an 111-ball 83, as India comfortably scored 270 for four wickets in 50 overs.

In reply Kenya were no match for Indian dominance: barring skipper Steve Tikolo, who top-scored with 56 runs, no Kenyan batsman could score more than 29, and Zaheer Khan took 3 wickets in 9.2 overs for just 14 runs. Though India breezed into the semi-finals easily, the tournament is also remembered for Kenya's best run in the World Cup, as a team from a country with limited cricket facilities and no proper grassroots structure reached as far as the semi-finals.
