# Killing for Country By David Marr

David Marr's October 2023 book is an investigation of the Native Police and his own family's connections to it through his great-great-grandfather, published by Black Inc. at $32.99 on 3 October, The Sydney Morning Herald listed on 1 October 2023.

The book's defining premise is a genealogical shock the reviewer poses to the reader: how would you feel if, while doing a bit of genealogical research, you discovered that you were descended from professional killers of Aboriginal people? For Marr, who has "been writing about the politics of race all my life. I know what side I'm on", it prompted this confronting investigation.

Marr describes the book as "an act of atonement, of penance by storytelling". The reviewer calls it a timely, vital story.
