# 8BitDo Ultimate Bluetooth Hall Effect Controller

8BitDo makes a pricier version of the Ultimate controller with Nintendo-style face buttons, called the Ultimate Bluetooth. The two are virtually identical and it can work over 2.4 GHz with PC too, but its biggest benefit is its Gulikit Hall effect analog sticks, which are resistant to developing drift over time by design.

By comparison, the potentiometer-based sticks used in the Ultimate 2.4G — and all of the other controllers Polygon mentions up to that point in the roundup — bear no such guarantee. Polygon hedges the practical significance: there is a possibility you may never experience the drift issue firsthand, but the anti-drifting sticks alone might be a reason for some to consider buying the Ultimate Bluetooth. The pad is similar in many ways to Nintendo's Switch Pro controller, with motion control support, a rechargeable battery, and a Nintendo-style face button layout, though it lacks some of the same features: it does not support NFC, so you cannot tap an amiibo on it, and its haptics are not up to par with Nintendo's. It is, however, one of the few third-party Switch controllers that can wake your console from sleep — if it is paired, press the home button, then shake the controller to awaken the Switch.
