# Kostas Tsimikas' Collarbone Break In The Klopp Collision

Liverpool defender Kostas Tsimikas broke his collarbone when he collided with manager Jurgen Klopp on the sideline during the match reported by The Roar on 23 December 2023.

The sequence given is that Tsimikas was challenged by Arsenal forward Bukayo Saka and stumbled into Klopp, who fell on top of him.

Klopp's own assessment, quoted in the report, is that Tsimikas' collarbone was "definitely broken", adding that he will be "out for a long time."
