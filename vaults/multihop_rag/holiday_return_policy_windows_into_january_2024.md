# Holiday Return Policy Windows Into January 2024

Most Black Friday 2023 return policies extended through January 2024, but each retailer set its own purchase window and deadline.

Best Buy said certain products bought from October 27 until December 30 may be returned before January 13. Target will accept most unopened items in new condition within 90 days of purchase. Walmart had an extended holiday return policy in effect allowing items purchased between October 1 and December 31 to be returned on or before January 31, 2024. Amazon's extended return policy applied to most items purchased between November 1 and December 31, returnable before January 31, 2024 — with one carve-out: Apple-brand products purchased in that time frame can only be returned on or before January 15, 2024.
