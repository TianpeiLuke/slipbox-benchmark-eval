# Jared Goff And Hendon Hooker's Trade Value

Jared Goff was enjoying the second-best start to a season of his career from a statistical standpoint, behind only his 2018 Super Bowl season with the Rams, which raised the question of whether another team might come after his injured backup, former University of Tennessee quarterback Hendon Hooker, The New York Times reported on 16 October 2023.

The context is that the Lions were 5-1 and Goff was heading toward a likely contract extension. Randy Mueller raised the thought the previous week, recalling examples from his career as a GM when offering a pick one round higher than a prospect's actual draft round proved alluring. Detroit used a third-round choice for Hooker, who is recovering from ACL surgery and would have been valued higher if healthy — and the question is whether that could be true again at some point in the future.
