# Shayda's Festival Run And Australian Oscar Selection

Noora Niasari's debut feature Shayda has been storming the global festival circuit since it premiered at the Sundance film festival in January 2023, where it won an audience award, and has been selected to represent Australia in the international film category at the Oscars, The Guardian reported on 1 October 2023.

Released in Australia on 5 October 2023, the film had by then already claimed the top prize at CinefestOz and opened the Melbourne international film festival.

The film draws on Niasari's personal experience, which informed the debut feature.
