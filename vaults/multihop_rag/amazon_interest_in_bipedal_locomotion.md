# Amazon's Interest In Bipedal Locomotion

Put to Tye Brady that one of the big appeals of bipedal robots is their ability to operate in brownfield settings — and that Amazon doesn't really have that problem — he redirected the rationale to mobility itself: "Our interest in systems like Agility is in the bipedal nature. The walking nature of that."

He then made the criterion form-agnostic: "Whether it's two legs, four legs, or it's rolling on wheels. If it performs that mobility function, we have interest, because we know that we need to move goods."
