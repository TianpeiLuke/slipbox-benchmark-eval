# France's Eight-Try Sequence Against Italy

France scored eight tries against Italy on 6 October 2023, leading 31-0 at half-time after any hint of home nerves was calmed by Damian Penaud's opening try, which stemmed from the first attack — Uini Atonio and Thibaud Flament both busting through tackles to create a huge overlap for the wing.

Thomas Ramos belted over a penalty and the pressure continued unabated, Penaud fluffing a grubber kick with another big overlap created. The hosts quickly came again and Penaud got his kick right this time, picking out Louis Bielle-Biarrey with a chip the wing converted impeccably, before Ramos flopped over after another fluent exchange with Gaël Fickou. At 24-0 Italy temporarily steadied a listing ship: after Ange Capuozzo was removed for a head injury assessment, Simone Ferrari powered over, but the television match official spotted a high hit by Ferrari on Maxime Lucu and the try was chalked off. Almost immediately Penaud went over at the other end to secure a bonus point after fielding Matthieu Jalibert's cross-kick, making it 31-0 at the break.

Things got worse for Italy when Jalibert darted through a gap seven minutes after the break, and when France hooker Peato Mauvaka crashed across the line, Italian heads were beginning to drop alarmingly. Italy mustered a consolation score via Manuel Zuliani, converted by fly-half Tommaso Allan, before Yoram Moefana completed a bright cameo with his second.
