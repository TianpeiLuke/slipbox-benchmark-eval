# The College Football Week 6 2023 Ranked Matchup Slate

Four AP Top 25 matchups were on deck in Week 6 of the 2023 college football season, headlined by the annual Red River Rivalry between No. 3 Texas and No. 12 Oklahoma at the Cotton Bowl Stadium in Dallas, as previewed by CBS Sports on 1 October 2023.

The four games are described in turn. The Red River Rivalry would be the first time the two rivals had matched up undefeated since 2011, both sitting at 5-0. Saturday would also mark the first time top-ranked Georgia had squared off against a ranked opponent that season, as the Bulldogs welcomed No. 20 Kentucky in a matchup of 5-0 squads — one of two ranked SEC matchups. The other saw No. 21 Missouri, looking to stay undefeated, host No. 23 LSU, which had already lost two games after opening the year as a playoff contender. The fourth and final AP Top 25 matchup was in ACC territory, where No. 25 Louisville, 5-0 in its first season under coach Jeff Brohm, hosted No. 10 Notre Dame; the Fighting Irish were looking to secure a second ranked victory in as many weeks and remain in the playoff hunt after a comeback win at Duke in Week 5.

The framing is the season stage: an eventful first month was in the rearview mirror, and October should provide even more separation between pretenders and contenders in the ongoing College Football Playoff race. Betting lines for those battles and other key Week 6 matchups were released on the Sunday.
