# The Penguins' Third-Period Weakness Carried Into 2023-24

The Sporting News argues that Pittsburgh's third-period struggles from last season have seemingly carried over to the start of the 2023-24 campaign, on the evidence of the Oct. 10, 2023 opening-night loss.

The grounds are a prior-season pattern plus a fresh instance of it. Last year Pittsburgh finished tied for the most losses after taking a lead into the third period, and the crew followed that trend on Tuesday night by letting a 2-1 lead to start the final frame slip away.

The warrant is continuity between the two seasons' collapses — the same failure mode, blowing a third-period lead, recurring immediately at the start of a new year rather than being an isolated result.
