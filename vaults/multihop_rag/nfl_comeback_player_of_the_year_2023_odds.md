# 2023 NFL Comeback Player Of The Year: Hamlin Favored, Wilson The Longshot

Demar Hamlin was the current favorite for 2023 NFL Comeback Player of the Year and Russell Wilson the longshot, and Sporting News argued on Dec. 5, 2023 that whether Hamlin holds up depends on how vital voters deem playing to be.

The case for Hamlin is that coming back from a life-threatening injury like a cardiac arrest makes him a great candidate to win CPOY. The qualification is that it would be understandable if voters were to look elsewhere, since Hamlin had seldom been active on game day and rarely seen the field.

The case for Wilson rests on the contrast between his two Denver seasons. Wilson was supposed to be the next great franchise quarterback for the Broncos when they acquired him prior to the previous season, but rather than lead the team back to prominence, Wilson was terrible and so was Denver. That season Wilson had played well even when the Broncos had not: he was not producing mind-blowing stat lines, but he was keeping his team competitive, playing efficiently and not making many mistakes. Until his three-interception day the week before, he had the best touchdown-to-interception ratio in the league, and even with those picks his was still second-best.
