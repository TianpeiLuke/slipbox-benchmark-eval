# Uber's Q3 2023 Bookings By Segment

In the third quarter of 2023, Uber saw total bookings rise from $29.1 billion to $35.3 billion, a gain of around 21%. In business segment terms, Uber generated $17.9 billion in ride-hailing bookings (up 31% year over year) and $16.1 billion worth of delivery bookings (up 18%). Those key business groups generated $5.1 billion and $2.9 billion in revenue, respectively, during the September quarter.

There is nuance to the revenue figures, and the rule changes in question do affect startups that operate in related categories. On the ride-hailing front, Uber told its investors that its Q3 2023 revenue was "negatively impacted by business model changes in some countries that classified certain sales and marketing costs as contra revenue by $161 million." That was not the only legal change that reshaped Uber's results: under its delivery business results, the company added that its revenue was "negatively impacted by business model changes that classified certain sales and marketing costs as contra revenue by $360 million."

The combined impact of those two items was 8 percentage points of growth.
