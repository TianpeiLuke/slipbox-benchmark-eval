# NASCAR 2024 Spotter Changes Elliott And Blaney

Two of the biggest stars in NASCAR would have new eyes in the sky in 2024, CBSSports.com reported on Dec. 7, 2023, as the spotters for both Chase Elliott and Ryan Blaney announced they would be stepping down: Eddie D'Hondt announced Saturday he would not return as Elliott's spotter next season, followed Monday by Josh Williams announcing he would step down as Blaney's spotter.

According to Bob Pockrass of Fox Sports, former NASCAR Xfinity Series driver Tim Fedewa, the longtime spotter for Kevin Harvick, would take over as Blaney's spotter the following season. A possible landing spot for Williams existed at Spire Motorsports, as Williams had previously spotted for Zane Smith in the Craftsman Truck Series.
