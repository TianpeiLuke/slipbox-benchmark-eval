# Prime Day 2023 Apparel And Footwear Deals

Three clothing picks close out this part of WIRED's roundup: an Orolay coat, a pair of trail-and-road running shoes, and recycled workout clothes.

The coat is very similar to one WIRED recommended in its affordable cold-weather gear guide; Orolay coats consistently go viral because they're budget-friendly and warm, and while WIRED hasn't tested this exact jacket the reviews are positive and the price is right, with other coats from the brand also on sale. Adrienne is currently running in the shoes, which have a wide toe box and are designed to switch easily between trails and roads, making them incredibly versatile. On the clothing: last Prime Day WIRED was shocked that Amazon even carried one of its favorite recycled clothing brands, and is shocked again — this is the price seen in July for some of the best recycled workout clothes.
