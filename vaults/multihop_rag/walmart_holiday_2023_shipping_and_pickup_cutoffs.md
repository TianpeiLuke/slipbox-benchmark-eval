# Walmart Holiday 2023 Shipping And Pickup Cutoffs

Walmart offers two-day shipping on items sold directly from Walmart, free on orders over $35, but orders must be placed by 2PM local time on December 21st to arrive in time for Christmas; next-day shipping is also available in some areas.

Walmart Plus subscribers get free next-day and two-day shipping with no order minimum, though a package's arrival date may vary depending on whether Walmart or a third-party seller is shipping the item. The retailer offers in-store pickup through December 24th, depending on availability, as well as same-day delivery on December 24th, with same-day pickup available on orders made by 12PM local time. A package can also be delivered in less than two hours on Christmas Eve if ordered by 12PM local time with a $10 Express Delivery fee — and Walmart Plus members get one Express Delivery free during the month of December.
