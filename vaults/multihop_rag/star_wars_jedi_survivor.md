# Star Wars Jedi: Survivor

Star Wars Jedi: Survivor, developed by Respawn Entertainment for PlayStation 5, Windows PC, and Xbox Series X, is ranked 32 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31. Polygon's verdict is that it is not an Empire Strikes Back level of sequel, but it comes extraordinarily close to being one of the best follow-ups in the entire Star Wars franchise.

The game improves upon Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order in every conceivable way, with a more entertaining, action-oriented start and a ton of aerial movements and lightsaber stances that make you feel even more like a seasoned Jedi Knight. Quality-of-life improvements like fast travel make it less frustrating to play, while new locales packed with hidden collectibles and upgrades make exploration more rewarding.

Critic CA argues that what makes it truly special is not this crude matter but its luminous heart: the memorable characters you meet on your galactic journey, whether the people you help or the friendships you forge and reconcile with; the classic, crowded cantina where you actually want to check in with the barkeep; the tactile, reverent way you craft your lightsaber; and the kinetic set-pieces that recall the serials Star Wars was originally inspired by.
