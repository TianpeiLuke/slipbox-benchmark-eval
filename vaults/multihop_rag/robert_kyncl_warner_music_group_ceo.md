# Robert Kyncl, Warner Music Group CEO

Robert Kyncl is the CEO of Warner Music Group, and the defining feature of his profile in the music business is that he came to it from 'Big Tech' — specifically from YouTube, where he was Chief Business Officer. That background is why, in the months before September 2023, he had been keeping a close eye on AI in the music industry.

His current role explains a second position associated with him: he has been pushing for price hikes at music streaming services. Both themes — AI in music, and streaming pricing — came up during Kyncl's Q&A session at the Code Conference in Laguna Niguel, California, on Tuesday 26 September 2023, as reported by Music Business Worldwide two days later.
