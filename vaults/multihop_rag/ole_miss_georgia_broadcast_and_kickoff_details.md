# Ole Miss-Georgia Broadcast And Kickoff Details

Ole Miss vs. Georgia kicked off at 7 p.m. ET on Saturday, Nov. 11, 2023 — the final game of the season in Sanford Stadium, played between the hedges — and was televised on ESPN.

The call was handled by the trio of Chris Fowler, Kirk Herbstreit and Holly Rowe. Streaming was available through the ESPN app or Fubo, which offers a free trial.
