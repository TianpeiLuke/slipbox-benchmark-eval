# Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle

Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle is a silly collaboration between Nintendo and Ubisoft that became one of the sleeper hits of its release year. It appears in Polygon's 2023-10-20 list of the 35 best couch co-op games for Nintendo Switch.

The squad tactics game pairs Mario and friends with their own doppelganger Rabbids against a veritable sea of evil Rabbids.

Polygon flags a scope limit on the co-op: local play is limited to five specific challenges, so the outlet would not recommend buying the game specifically for co-op alone — but those levels are especially fun, since working together changes the tactical dynamics.
