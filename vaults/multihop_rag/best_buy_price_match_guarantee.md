# Best Buy Price Match Guarantee

Best Buy's Price Match Guarantee applies when a product meets four preconditions: it is new, it is identical to the competitor's product, it is immediately available at a qualifying competitor's store or on its site, and it is not shown on Best Buy's exclusion list — which includes "items for sale the Friday before Thanksgiving Day through the Monday after Thanksgiving."

To get the difference, contact Best Buy via its on-site chat and be able to show the ad or website with the lower price point; alternatively, call 1-888-237-8289 or visit any Best Buy or Pacific Sales store.

Best Buy will also, upon request, match an in-store or online price that was lowered during the return and exchange period, which typically lasts up to 15 days.
