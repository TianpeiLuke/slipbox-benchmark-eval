# The Wonderful Story of Henry Sugar

The Wonderful Story of Henry Sugar is Wes Anderson's Netflix short starring Benedict Cumberbatch, Ralph Fiennes, and Ben Kingsley, ranked 35 on Polygon's best movies of 2023 list of 2023-10-07.

Cumberbatch plays a rapscallion who seeks to learn how to see without his eyes in order to cheat at gambling, but Polygon says the premise is mostly immaterial: Henry Sugar and the three other Roald Dahl stories Anderson adapted for Netflix are opportunities for the director to continue to push his aesthetic forward.

Polygon's Pete Volk describes that aesthetic: in his recent work Anderson has made bare the artifice essential to filmmaking, and these shorts take that even further — actors recite Dahl's words verbatim, often speaking directly to the camera, and the sets are treated like those on a theater stage, collapsing or building in real time to aid scene transitions. Even in short form, Anderson never ceases to amaze.
