# Greg Brockman's Account Of Altman's Removal

Greg Brockman, OpenAI co-founder and the board's chairman, rejected the board's framing of an orderly leadership transition in which he would step down as chairman but remain as president: later on X, formerly Twitter, he posted a message he had sent to OpenAI employees in which he wrote, "based on today's news, i quit." "Sam and I are shocked and saddened by what the board did today," Brockman wrote — contesting the premise that the decision reflected a considered board judgment they were party to.

His account of the process supplies the grounds. In an X post on Friday night, Brockman said Altman was asked to join a video meeting at noon that Friday with the company's board members, minus Brockman, during which OpenAI co-founder and Chief Scientist Ilya Sutskever informed Altman he was being fired. Brockman added that he was informed of his own removal from the board in a separate call with Sutskever a short time later.
