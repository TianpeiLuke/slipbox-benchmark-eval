# Napoleon (Ridley Scott)

Ridley Scott's Napoleon, starring Joaquin Phoenix, Vanessa Kirby, and Tahar Rahim, does not try to encompass the entire life of one of modern history's most important and captivating figures; instead it seeks to establish three things about Napoleon Bonaparte — that he was one of the greatest battle commanders, tacticians, and military men ever to live; that he was a petty and weird little pervert; and that those first two things are deeply and irrevocably intertwined. Polygon ranked it 26 on its best movies of 2023 list of 2023-10-07, when it was in theaters.

The consequence of that framing, critic AG writes, is that Napoleon is undeniably hilarious. It has gigantic battles with beautiful photography and framing and a more jaw-dropping sense of scale than almost any movie made in the last decade.

But between those massive and awe-inspiring battles, the film finds time to gawk at France's first emperor parading around his palace, screaming at the English about boats, and pouting about being cucked — the story of Bonaparte by way of Barry Lyndon.
