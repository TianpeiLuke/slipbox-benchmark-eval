# Echo Frames Open-Ear Audio And Bass Performance

Treated as what they mostly are — a pair of open-ear headphones rather than smart glasses — the Echo Frames are alright for the category but nothing mind-blowing, and bass is the weak point: it is purportedly three times better on the new model, but still won't bring the thump of even standard AirPods.

The reviewer's test case was Stray Kids: Felix's TikTok-breaking rumbly baritone is absolutely lost on these, and in God's Menu the famous "cookin' like a chef I'm a 5-star Michelin" line is reduced to zero-star Michelin — on a loud train it was inaudible even with the volume at maximum. The review notes this isn't a problem limited to the Echo Frames but a common problem with any open-ear audio headphones, while adding that the same song on the Meta smart glasses sounded a bit richer. The frames were a lot better for podcasts or less bass-heavy songs, but you still can't get away from the feeling of listening to something from a few feet away instead of right next to your ear.
