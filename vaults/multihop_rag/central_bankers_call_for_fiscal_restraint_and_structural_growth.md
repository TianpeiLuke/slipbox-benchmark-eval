# Central Bankers' Call For Fiscal Restraint And Structural Growth

Central bankers warn that any failure to scale back fiscal spending risks coming at the cost of yet higher interest rates — the price of continued fiscal expansion is paid through monetary policy. They also want elected officials to put in place policies that help deliver sustainable growth.

Agustin Carstens, the former governor of the Bank of Mexico who is now general manager of the BIS, states the conclusion: "A change in mindset needs to happen. Growth needs to depend less on fiscal and monetary policy, it should depend more on structural policies."
