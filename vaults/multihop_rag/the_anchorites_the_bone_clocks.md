# The Anchorites In The Bone Clocks

The Bone Clocks is a book about a psychic girl who gets into a bit of a pickle, only to learn about vampiric ghosts called Anchorites, who achieve immortality by murdering others.

Polygon sets that up through the author's habits: David Mitchell loves to write books that start as one thing then end up as another, and he writes them knowing readers know he is famous for this, so he often turns those books into other things still — a real trip. The reviewer insists the reveal is not a spoiler, just something you should know about the vampires.
