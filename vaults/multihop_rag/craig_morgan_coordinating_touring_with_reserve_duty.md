# Craig Morgan On Coordinating Touring With Reserve Duty

Craig Morgan says he balances his music career and membership of the U.S. Army Reserve with a lot of "coordination", because the military side, unlike the music side, requires him to manage his own schedule, Fox News Digital reported on 16 October 2023.

Of his music career he says: "So, all I really have to do is show up and do the one important thing and that's sing and entertain."

The contrast is with the Reserve: "The military, on the other hand, requires a lot more of my personal thought process. I don't have someone making my schedule for me. So there's a lot of coordination going on between the military side and my civilian side."
