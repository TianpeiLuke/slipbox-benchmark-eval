# FTX Fraud Charges And The SEC's Allegation

Sam Bankman-Fried, the founder of the failed cryptocurrency exchange FTX, faced seven counts of wire fraud and conspiracy — specifically, two counts of wire fraud and five conspiracy charges — and the Securities and Exchange Commission alleged that FTX was a fraud "from the start," with a "multi-billion-dollar deficiency caused by his own misappropriation of customer funds." The Verge repeated this framing at the head of each of its trial dispatches through October 2023, and in its October 28th, 2023 write-up reported that Bankman-Fried had been found guilty on seven counts including charges of wire fraud.

The boundary of the concept is the mix of charge types: the indictment paired substantive wire fraud counts with conspiracy counts rather than resting on a single offence, and the SEC's civil allegation about the exchange's origins ran alongside the criminal case rather than being part of it. The Verge's reporter, writing as the prosecution's case neared its end on October 26th, 2023, added an impression of the people the charges swept in: all the co-conspirators seemed oddly younger than their years, as though they had got stuck in the "math camp forever" environment Bankman-Fried created for them and simply failed to grow up.
