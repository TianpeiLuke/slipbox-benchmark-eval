# Yoshinobu Yamamoto's 12-Year, $325 Million Deal With The Dodgers

The Dodgers reportedly signed Japanese ace Yoshinobu Yamamoto to a 12-year, $325 million contract on Tuesday, Dec. 19, 2023, the largest MLB deal ever for a pitcher, according to ESPN's Jeff Passan and YES Network's Jack Curry.

The agreement ended the Yamamoto sweepstakes and surpassed Gerrit Cole's deal with the Yankees by $1 million. It extended what the tracker called Los Angeles' incredible offseason, as Yamamoto joined Shohei Ohtani on the defending NL West champions.
