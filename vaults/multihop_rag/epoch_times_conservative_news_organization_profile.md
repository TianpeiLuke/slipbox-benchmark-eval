# The Epoch Times As A Conservative News Organization

The Epoch Times is, as of October 2023, one of the United States' most successful and influential conservative news organizations — a nonprofit newspaper powered by Falun Gong, the religious group persecuted in China that launched it more than two decades ago as a free propaganda newsletter to oppose the Chinese Communist Party.

Its discriminating features are its funding model and its scale claims. The organization is funded through aggressive online and real-world marketing campaigns and by big-money conservative donors, and it boasts of being the country's fourth-largest newspaper by subscriber count. That claim comes with a boundary condition the article states explicitly: unlike most major newspapers, The Epoch Times is not audited by the two major independent collectors of circulation data, so the ranking rests on its own count.

Financially, the nonprofit has amassed a fortune, growing its revenue by a staggering 685 percent in two years to $122 million in 2021, according to the group's most recent tax records. As a nonprofit it is exempt from most federal taxes, and its stated mission, according to its tax filings, is independent journalism "outside of political interests and the pursuit of profit, for the public benefit and to be truly responsible to society."
