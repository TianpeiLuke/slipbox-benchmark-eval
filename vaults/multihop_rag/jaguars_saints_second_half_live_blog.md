# Jaguars-Saints Second Half: A Tied Game Broken By Christian Kirk's 44-Yard Score

New Orleans erased a 17-6 halftime deficit to tie the Oct. 19, 2023 "Thursday Night Football" game at 24, only for Christian Kirk's 44-yard touchdown to give Jacksonville a 31-24 win. The Saints received to start the half at 9:58 p.m., in desperate need of offense. They went for it on fourth down after coming up half a yard short on third — their third-down record was one-for-nine at that point (10:00 p.m.) — and Alvin Kamara turned the half-yard need into a 17-yard gain (10:01 p.m.). Taysom Hill, barely used to that point, kept a read option for six yards down to the Jacksonville 23 (10:02 p.m.), but a second-down penalty pushed New Orleans back 10 yards and Blake Grupe settled for a 42-yard field goal at 10:06 p.m., his third make in four attempts, cutting the lead to 17-9.

Jacksonville then extended it through a Saints turnover. At 10:12 p.m. the Jaguars called a draw on third-and-six from their own 29 and Travis Etienne Jr. came up less than a yard short; Pederson punted rather than go for it. At 10:17 p.m. Carr forced a third-down throw that was deflected into the air and into the arms of Jaguars linebacker Foye Oluokun, who sprinted the length of the field and dove for the pylon — a pick-six for a 24-9 lead. Carr then completed six straight passes to move swiftly into Jacksonville territory (10:17 p.m.). On the Saints' best, up-tempo drive (10:25 p.m.), Dennis Allen again went for it on fourth down and Kamara moved the chains to the Jaguars' nine (10:26 p.m.); Hill picked up gains of one and five (10:31 p.m.); a late hit on Foster Moreau by Rayshawn Jenkins moved New Orleans to the two (10:32 p.m.); and Kamara was denied twice from two yards out as the third quarter ended 24-9, the Saints still without a touchdown but facing third-and-one.

The fourth quarter belonged to New Orleans until the very end. After Kamara was chased down short by Evan Lloyd (10:37 p.m.), Hill punched it in from a yard out behind a lead block from defensive lineman Khalen Saunders at 10:38 p.m.; Grupe's extra point doinked off the upright and in for 24-16 with 14:21 left. The Saints defense forced a Jaguars punt (10:42 p.m.) and Carr's offense went three-and-out (10:47 p.m.) after Olave appeared to pull up on the first deep ball of the night. A Paulson Adebo pass interference call gave Jacksonville an automatic first down (10:50 p.m.), but on fourth-and-inches Etienne Jr. was stopped on a read option for a turnover on downs at 10:54 p.m., setting the Saints up at their own 47 with 7:29 left. Carr hit Michael Thomas for 17 and Hill for 19 (10:56 p.m.), then found Thomas at the back of the end zone for the touchdown and lofted the two-point conversion to a wide-open Kamara to tie it 24-24 at 10:57 p.m. Jacksonville punted after three straight stops (11:03 p.m.), and the Saints punted after three plays as Carr appeared to reach for his groin scrambling on third down, staying down before walking to the sideline (11:06 p.m.). At 11:12 p.m. Kirk took a short pass from Lawrence and weaved through the defense for a 44-yard touchdown and a 31-24 lead with 3:08 to go. New Orleans reached the Jaguars' 41 by the two-minute warning on completions to Hill (18) and Kamara (nine and seven), then Kamara's 18-yard run gave a first down at the 18 (11:17 p.m.) and an Olave catch plus a Kamara run reached the six (11:19 p.m.). On third-and-goal Carr found Moreau wide open in the back left corner but the ball went off his fingertips (11:21 p.m.), and on fourth down Buster Brown covered Olave for an incompletion (11:23 p.m.). Three Lawrence kneels ended it at 11:25 p.m.
