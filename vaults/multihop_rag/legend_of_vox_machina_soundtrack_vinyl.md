# The Legend Of Vox Machina Soundtrack Vinyl

Fans of The Legend of Vox Machina series on Prime Video could pick up a two-disc Limited Edition vinyl LP of the soundtrack for $37.56 on Amazon as of 2023-12-09 — its lowest price to date.

The soundtrack is packaged in a full-color gatefold sleeve and contains exclusive tracks not featured in the series.
