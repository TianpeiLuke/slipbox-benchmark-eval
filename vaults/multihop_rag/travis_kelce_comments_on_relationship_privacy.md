# Travis Kelce's Comments On Relationship Privacy

Travis Kelce broke his silence on Taylor Swift's Chiefs game attendance on his podcast on 27 September 2023, while saying he would not speak much more about the relationship, according to CBS Sports' timeline published 12 October 2023.

On the game itself, Kelce said: "To see the slow-motion chest bumps, to see the high-fives with Mom, to see how Chiefs Kingdom was all excited that she was there -- that s--- was absolutely hysterical. It's definitely a game I'll remember, that's for damn sure."

He then set the limit, noting that he wants "to respect both of our lives," and drew a distinction between his own exposure and Swift's: "Yeah, my personal life -- that's not so personal. I did this to myself Jason, I know this. What's real is that you know it is my personal life. ... She's not in the media as much as I am doing this show every single week."
