# The Neon Demon (Nicolas Winding Refn)

The Neon Demon is Drive director Nicolas Winding Refn's 2016 horror movie about the cutthroat modeling industry. It was the Oct. 15 pick in Polygon's 2023-10-10 Halloween Countdown, listed on Prime Video, Hoopla, and Freevee.

Elle Fanning plays Jesse, a young model who has just moved to Los Angeles and is quickly taken under the wing of makeup artist Ruby, played by Jena Malone. As Jesse becomes more successful, she meets various members of the industry willing to part the seas for her even as she makes enemies caught up in a wake she unknowingly creates; even when people are being nice to her, no one lets Jesse pass without making it clear they are jealous of her beauty and curious about what she can give them, or what they can take from her.

Critic AG notes that, as in Drive, Refn's version of LA is soaked in dread and seediness in every corner — every person reads like a threat, and every new competitor or friend seems to be looking for a new way to drain the life out of the people around them, until they finally do. What really sets the film apart, as with all Refn projects, is how it looks: the griminess of the world turns brightly lit photo shoots into shadow-filled nightmares, with people who stalk the sets like jungle predators. Though it did not get the same acclaim as other so-called elevated horror movies of the time, in hindsight it fits perfectly among the ranks of The Witch, It Follows, and Under the Skin, dripping with cynicism, cruelty, and a creeping atmosphere.
