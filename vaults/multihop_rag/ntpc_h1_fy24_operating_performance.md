# NTPC's H1 FY24 Operating Performance

During H1FY24 NTPC reported a plant availability factor in its coal-based generating stations of 93.32 per cent, against 92.05 per cent in H1FY23, while the plant load factor, that is capacity utilisation, was 76.62 per cent against 77.27 per cent a year earlier and a national average of 68.75 per cent. Generation rose around 4 per cent year-on-year to 212 billion units. Coal supply during H1FY24 was 113 MMT including 4 MMT of imported coal, against consumption in the corresponding previous period of 112 MMT including 10 MMT of imported coal.

Revenue growth was flattish in H1FY24 at close to ₹88,058.44 crore, which management attributed to shutdowns in certain plants due to repair work and grid restrictions, expected to improve in upcoming quarters. EBITDA grew around 18 per cent year-on-year to ₹25,091 crore, and EBITDA margins expanded from 25 per cent to 29 per cent year-on-year, mainly on account of lower fuel cost due to a reduced share of imported coal. The margin comparison has a scope condition: imported coal prices rose steeply in FY23 and the pass-through of that increase might not have been allowed completely, which is what caused the decline in margins in FY23.
