# Skinamarink (Kyle Edward Ball)

Skinamarink is Kyle Edward Ball's feature debut and, in Polygon's judgment, hands down the most peculiar and divisive horror film to come out in 2023 — and also one of the best. It ranked 30 on the outlet's best movies of 2023 list of 2023-10-07 and was available on Hulu, AMC Plus, Shudder, or for digital rental/purchase; the cast is Lucas Paul, Ross Paul, and Dali Rose Tetreault.

The premise is that a pair of siblings awake in the middle of the night to find their home transformed into an inescapable nightmare of yawning hallways, dimly lit corridors, and looming walls devoid of any windows or doors. As they search aimlessly for safety and familiarity, the children find they are not alone in the house, haunted by an unknowable entity that seems to feed on their fear as it twists their lives into ever more frightening shapes — or maybe not, since the film's narrative is notoriously sparse and open to interpretation. The effect is nonetheless one of the most unsettling scenarios seen that year.

Polygon's Halloween Countdown write-up of 2023-10-10 calls it one of the most polarizing horror releases of 2023 and describes its method: the film eschews the formal conventions of traditional cinematography and plot, consisting instead of a series of canted-angle shots of dark hallways and yawning darkness that force the audience to ruminate on the horrors that lie therein. The critic adds that he vibed with it heavily and, unsurprisingly, also enjoyed Ball's 2020 short film Heck.
