# The Scope Of The EU's DSA Investigation Into X

Per the Commission, the EU's investigation of X covers four areas. The first is compliance with DSA obligations related to countering the dissemination of illegal content in the EU, notably in relation to the risk assessment and mitigation measures adopted by X, as well as the functioning of the notice and action mechanism for illegal content in the EU mandated by the DSA, including in light of X's content moderation resources.

The second is the effectiveness of measures taken by X to combat information manipulation, with a focus on the effectiveness of its "Community Notes" system of crowdsourcing fact-checking to aggregated views of users, who may vote to label disputed tweets with additional context. The EU said the probe will also look at the effectiveness of related policies mitigating risks to civic discourse and electoral processes.

The third is transparency requirements, with a focus on "suspected shortcomings" by X in giving researchers access to publicly accessible data as mandated by Article 40 of the DSA, and suspected shortcomings in its ads repository, the ads transparency library the regulation also mandates. The fourth is suspected deceptive design elements in X's user interface, notably in relation to checkmarks linked to certain subscription products, the so-called Blue checks.
