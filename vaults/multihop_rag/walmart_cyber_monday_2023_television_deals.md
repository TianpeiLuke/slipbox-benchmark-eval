# Walmart Cyber Monday 2023 Television Deals

WIRED's Walmart Cyber Monday roundup of 27 November 2023 recommended a spread of discounted TVs spanning Samsung's 8K QN900C, LG's C3 OLED and a large LG OLED, Samsung's Frame, and two Sony sets — the XR A90J and the X90L.

WIRED notes it is not often that a newly released TV goes on sale, and it had called the QN900C (8/10, WIRED Recommends) "among the prettiest you can buy," gushing over its "brilliantly clear and detailed video," rich colour shading, deep blacks and butter-smooth motion processing; it displays up to 8K resolution, so WIRED says the only issue besides price may be finding enough 8K content. WIRED associate reviews editor Parker Hall describes the C3's picture quality as "Absolutely loaded with detail," with vivid, high-contrast images across the 4K display, and WIRED cites stunningly fast response times of 9.2 milliseconds plus support for every current-generation console feature by way of four HDMI sockets as justifying crowning the C3 its Best TV for Gamers. Restating that pick, WIRED says the set earns the gaming crown for its lightning-fast 9.2-millisecond response time and four HDMI 2.1 ports for connecting a PC, Xbox, PlayStation and Switch, and that outside gaming it works fantastically well, with perfect black levels and a great centre mount that helps place it on existing TV stands.

On the larger and design-led sets, WIRED observes that extra-large OLED TVs typically carry extra-large price tags, and calls this LG model one of the rare deals that lets you have top-tier image quality and a massive display at a reasonable cost, with perfect black levels and gorgeous HDR making it one of the best TVs for larger rooms, especially for darker movies and shows. WIRED has always liked Samsung's Frame models because they hide in plain sight — when not in use the TV transforms into one of a myriad of famous art pieces and virtually disappears into the decor — and says this is an especially large one, perfect for a fancy living room where you do not want the TV to be the centre of attention, with the 55-inch model also on sale. Of Sony, WIRED says the XR A90J faces stiff competition from LG and Samsung at full retail price, but at this shockingly discounted price you are arguably getting the best deal in premium-yet-affordable TVs, at least for the next few days while it is on sale, and it features Sony's 4K upscaling to make HD shows and films appear as if in 4K plus built-in Google TV; the X90L shows that Sony's LED sets still pack a punch with excellent picture processing, good brightness and great local dimming for rich contrast, and like a lot of TVs this time of year the 65-inch set is at the lowest price WIRED has seen all year.
