# M3GAN (2023 AI Horror Film)

M3GAN, directed by Gerard Johnstone and starring Allison Williams, Violet McGraw, and Amie Donald, is a refreshingly goofy — and fairly creepy — spin on the "AI is going to murder us all" horror trope. Polygon ranked it 34 on its best movies of 2023 list published 2023-10-07, and listed it as available on Prime Video or for digital rental/purchase.

Polygon frames the film against its moment: 2023 was such a big year for big conversations about AI, including AI art, AI writing, and AI style theft, that a fresh wave of AI-kills-us-all horror movies was no surprise.

The premise is that when a tech whiz played by Allison Williams has to take responsibility for her dead sister's kid, she distances herself by building a robot to handle parenting for her, and it goes badly. Scripted by Akela Cooper with the same shamelessly messy, giddy verve she brought to Malignant, M3GAN is described as a good time for knowledgeable horror fans, packed with referential humor but still channeling real anxiety about the place computers have in people's lives and how far that will eventually go.
