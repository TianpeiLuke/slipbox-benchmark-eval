# Graham Arnold Socceroos Coaching Appearance Record

Graham Arnold would prowl the technical area for a 59th time on the Thursday, taking him clear of Frank Farina as the Socceroos' record-holder for matches coached, in a match against Bangladesh as 2026 World Cup qualifying started, The Roar reported on Nov. 13, 2023.

On the Tuesday, Football Australia CEO James Johnson, who had held firm on Arnold 20 months earlier, congratulated the coach on the impending record-breaking match. In a remarkable few weeks the previous year Arnold had gone from on the nose to a man revived; since then he had been linked to club jobs in Europe but opted to stick with the national team. Arnold, in his second stint as national team manager, also represented the Socceroos 56 times as a player and has been involved in national team coaching ranks since 2000, first as an assistant to Farina and then Guus Hiddink, before a brief period as caretaker coach in 2006. "It's certainly a proud moment and an achievement that I'll reflect on when the time is right," Arnold said. "I look at some of the names on that list – Farina, Arok, Thompson, Rasic – guys I played under or assisted in the early part of my coaching career, and it gives you some perspective on what a journey it's been so far."
