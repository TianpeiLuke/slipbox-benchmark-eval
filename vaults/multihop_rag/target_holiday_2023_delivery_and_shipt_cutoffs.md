# Target Holiday 2023 Delivery And Shipt Cutoffs

Target offers both same-day delivery and in-store pickup for orders placed by 6PM local time on Christmas Eve, and packages within an hour on Christmas Eve via Shipt for orders placed before 4PM local time.

The Verge advises choosing your desired location as "My store" in the upper-left corner of Target's website, which prompts it to indicate automatically whether the item you're buying is available for pickup nearby. An annual Shipt subscription provides unlimited deliveries on orders above $35 for $99 a year, currently going for half off; alternatively you can pay for same-day shipping with Shipt on a per-order basis via Target's website, with fees varying by location and order contents. Target RedCard credit card holders receive free two-day shipping on many items from the retailer's website, and many — but not all — orders over $35 are eligible for free two-day shipping even without a RedCard, determined by the item's location and your ZIP code. Target stores will be open on Christmas Eve, though it's wise to get pickup and same-day shipping orders in by December 23rd to be safe. As with Walmart and Amazon, items shipped or sold by third-party retailers can mean longer lead times; product listings usually spell out the options for pickup, same-day delivery, and standard shipping, with estimated availability times, before you add them to your cart.
