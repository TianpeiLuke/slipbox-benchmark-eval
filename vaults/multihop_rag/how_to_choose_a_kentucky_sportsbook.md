# How To Choose A Kentucky Sportsbook

CBS Sports set out four criteria to work through when choosing the best Kentucky sportsbook, published 13 October 2023: bonuses, rewards, odds and markets.

First, bonuses. Sportsbooks often offer generous promotions and bonuses, especially for new customers, so you should review the best sports betting promo codes and offers. Second, rewards. Some sportsbooks offer cash back or other rewards based on your betting activity — for example, Fanatics offers up to 5% FanCash on every bet, which you can use for more bets or team gear from Fanatics. Third, odds. Every sportsbook sets its own line on each market, so it is important to find the best odds in order to maximise your return when you win; many bettors use multiple sportsbooks to "shop" for lines, and there are sites that allow you to compare odds from multiple sportsbooks. Fourth, markets. Each sportsbook will allow you to bet on different games and events — "markets" — in different ways, and in addition to common bets like spread, total and moneyline, you'll want an app with same game parlays, props, futures and other fun bet types.

The procedure assumes an operating precondition stated by the third criterion: because pricing varies between books, the method works best when the bettor is willing to hold accounts at more than one sportsbook rather than choosing a single app once.
