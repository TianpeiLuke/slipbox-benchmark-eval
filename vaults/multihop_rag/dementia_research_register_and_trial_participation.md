# Joining The Dementia Research Register For Trial Participation

Health coach Susan Saunders' advice, reported by The Guardian on 10 October 2023, is a two-step procedure for anyone with a parent in the early stages of dementia: investigate all the available research trials, and start by joining Dementia Research, a nationwide register that matches volunteers to research programmes.

The precondition is stated explicitly — the advice is for a parent in the early stages of dementia, not later ones — and Saunders speaks from experience, having found herself "parenting" her mother, who had dementia, while her own daughters were babies.

The scope of the advice as given is limited to finding and entering trials through that register; the source does not extend it to any other treatment step.
