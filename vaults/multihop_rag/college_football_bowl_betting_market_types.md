# College Bowl Betting Market Types

The common college football betting lines offered on bowl games and the College Football Playoff are moneylines, point spreads, totals, props and parlays.

College bowl odds are posted at the best college football betting sites, and alongside game odds sportsbooks offer a wider variety of prop bets to match the hype surrounding bowl odds, the College Football Playoff and National Championship games; because of football's popularity at top online betting sites, most bowl games feature expanded selections of betting markets. The moneyline is the simplest bet available on a bowl game — your only responsibility is to choose which team you believe will win, with favorites indicated by negative odds and the underdog carrying positive odds. In point spread betting you wager on the game with a spread factored in: the favorite must win by the point spread or more, while the underdog needs to either lose by fewer points than the spread or win outright. The totals bet, commonly known as the over/under, requires you to determine whether the combined score of both teams will end up over or under a predicted total, with no concern for which team wins or loses. Props can be team or individual player bets — such as which team is winning at halftime, or the total number of receptions a player collects — and are best understood as side bets made with the sportsbook about events or statistical milestones that happen in the game. Parlays are multi-leg bets on a single bet slip in which all parts must be successful for the bet to win; they offer larger payouts, but the chances of winning compared with an individual bet are greatly reduced.
