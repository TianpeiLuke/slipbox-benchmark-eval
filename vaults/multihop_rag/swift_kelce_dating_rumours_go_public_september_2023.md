# The Swift-Kelce Dating Rumours Going Public In September 2023

The Swift-Kelce dating rumours moved into public commentary in mid-September 2023, according to CBS Sports' timeline published 12 October 2023.

On 14 September, Jason Kelce — who is very close with his brother — was asked whether rumours of the two hanging out were true; he did not confirm them, but he also did not deny them. On 17 September the rumours began to pick up, and a lot of people, commentators included, started making Swift references around Kelce. When the tight end caught a touchdown pass from Patrick Mahomes, CBS Sports' Ian Eagle said, "Kelce finds a blank space for the score." The timeline notes this was only the start of many, many Swift lyrics being referenced during NFL games.
