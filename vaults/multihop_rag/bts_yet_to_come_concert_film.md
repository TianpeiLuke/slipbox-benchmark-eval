# BTS Yet To Come Concert Film

In February, K-pop septet BTS released a filmed version of their "Yet to Come" concert — a one-night-only performance for 50,000 fans in Busan, South Korea — and the concert film made more than $50 million worldwide.

The figure is notable because the show had already been given away once: it was previously streamed via the Korean platform Weverse, allowing a reported 49 million fans to watch at home. Polygon reports the theatrical release still cleared $50 million after that.
