# Hipgnosis $440 Million Catalog Sale to Hipgnosis Songs Capital

As of late September 2023, Hipgnosis Songs Fund's shareholders were considering a proposal to sell 29 of the company's catalogs to another Hipgnosis company — the Blackstone-backed Hipgnosis Songs Capital (HSC) — for USD $440 million. The transaction was being managed for both HSF and HSC by two separate teams within their mutual investment advisor, Hipgnosis Song Management (HSM), which is run and part-owned by Merck Mercuriadis.

HSF's stated motivation ties the deal to its share price problem. When it announced its intention to explore the sale two weeks before this report, HSF confirmed that one of its motivating factors was that the transaction could potentially act "as a catalyst for a re-rating of [HSF's] share price" — in other words, lifting the share price closer to the company's operative NAV.

Two benchmarks were given for the price. HSF confirmed earlier in September that HSC's $440 million offer for the 29 catalogs equates to a 26% increase on the amount HSF originally paid to acquire them. The $440 million also reflects a +51% premium on the value of those 29 catalogs implied by HSF's public market cap over the 30 days ending 13 September.
