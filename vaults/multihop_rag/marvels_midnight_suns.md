# Marvel's Midnight Suns

Marvel's Midnight Suns, developed by Firaxis Games for Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, Windows PC, Xbox One, and Xbox Series X, is ranked 22 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31. An editorial note explains its eligibility: the game was released in 2022 but just barely missed the cutoff for the 2022 list.

Polygon's critic anticipates the objection — another licensed Marvel game — and answers it: she played Marvel's Avengers too, and this is not that. It might seem like it at first, because Midnight Suns makes the grave error of introducing Iron Man and Doctor Strange as its tutorial characters, who she judges may be the most irritating characters in the entire video game, a call she says she is allowed to make having beaten the game. Players must press on and give it time to win them over.

What it becomes in its mid-game and endgame is the romance and humor of Fire Emblem: Three Houses combined with the high-stakes tactical battles of XCOM 2. It is a card-based strategy game in which each hero has a customizable deck; Maddy Myers started off favoring Captain Marvel, Magik, and Blade simply because their moves and hilarious dialogue kept her entertained, but soon realized every single character has something exciting or unexpected to bring to the battlefront. Over 100 hours later she has leveled up every character and played all the main story missions and an unknowable number of optional ones without getting sick of the combat or the kooky cast that grows all the time, including the Deadpool DLC — and once hooked, players may stick around to chuckle at Wolverine attending Blade's book club, which is an actual storyline in the game.
