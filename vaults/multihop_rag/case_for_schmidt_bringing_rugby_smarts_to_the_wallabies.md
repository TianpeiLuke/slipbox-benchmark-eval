# The Case For Schmidt Bringing Rugby Smarts To The Wallabies

Several sources told The Roar in December 2023 that Joe Schmidt is exactly the type of person the Wallabies need: the claim is that the highly detailed Schmidt would bring rugby smarts to the Australian program, and the warrant is that the Wallabies' detail and nous were clearly absent on the field over the previous year, which is precisely what his coaching supplies.

The evidence offered for that capability is what his Ireland teams demonstrably did: the manipulation and ability to play both sides of the ruck was clearly visible during his Irish tenure.

The argument also carries a political ground about acceptance within Australian rugby: should Rugby Australia move on him, Schmidt's backing would be largely supported by the Super Rugby franchises.
