# FoodSaver Vacuum Sealer

The FoodSaver vacuum sealer machine was $147, down from $220 — 33 percent off at Amazon. It keeps food fresh by squeezing all the air out of the package and sealing it for long- or short-term storage, and it is news writer Emma Roth's pick.

She never knew how much she needed a vacuum sealer until she got one: she is the type of person who shops at wholesale clubs despite only needing food for two people, so when she buys meat she gets a lot of it at once, some of which inevitably gets stored in her fridge or freezer.

While she cannot speak to the quality of other vacuum sealers, the FoodSaver she has is awesome — it not only keeps raw meat and other food fresher for longer in the fridge, but also saves space in the freezer, since each package of meat becomes much flatter once the air is sucked out. With the machine she can load up whatever she wants in a FoodSaver bag and insert the open end, and it vacuums up all the air and then closes the bag with its heat sealing feature in one fell swoop.
