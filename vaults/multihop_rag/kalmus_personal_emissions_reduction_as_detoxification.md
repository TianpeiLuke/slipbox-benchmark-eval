# Kalmus's Personal Emissions Reduction as Detoxification

About a decade ago Kalmus ditched a promising academic career in astrophysics to study the physics of climate change instead, and made personal changes that cut his emissions by about 90% according to his own math: he stopped flying, became a vegetarian and gave up gasoline-powered cars, driving a Tesla instead.

He describes the motivation as visceral rather than political. It was not a political statement, but a detoxification: "Burning fossil fuels started to feel more and more disgusting," he said.
