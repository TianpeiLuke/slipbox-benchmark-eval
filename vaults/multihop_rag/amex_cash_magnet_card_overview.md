# American Express Cash Magnet Card: Overview

The American Express Cash Magnet Card is a basic rewards credit card with a $0 annual fee that offers a flat cash back rate on all purchases with no limit on how much you can earn in rewards. Its appeal is the absence of bonus-category management: you don't have to worry about the hassle of keeping track of and activating bonus spending categories, which makes it ideal for those looking for a basic rewards card without annual fees.

Its stated pros are no annual fee, a flat cash back rate, and an attainable new-cardmember bonus. Its cons are that it charges foreign transaction fees, has limited redemption options, and offers few card benefits. Beyond rewards, it may be worthwhile for someone who wants to take advantage of a low APR for a major purchase or balance transfer.
