# Beeper's Product Lineup, Pricing And Availability

Beeper Mini launched to the public on the Google Play Store at $1.99 per month with a seven-day free trial — competitively priced against the $15 per month charged by the recently acquired competitor Texts.com, bought by Automattic.

The startup says it is able to keep costs down by targeting a wider market that includes Android, iPhone, Mac, Windows and Linux. The product lineup is in transition: the original Beeper app had 50,000 sign-ups in the first week and now has around 100,000 testers in a closed beta; that app, which spans mobile and desktop, supports RCS via Beeper's open source bridge, and Beeper Mini will be able to send and receive SMS and RCS soon. The earlier Beeper will be rebranded as "Beeper Cloud" as the iMessage-only Beeper Mini launches. Over time Beeper Mini will add the other networks back into the platform, as well as SMS and RCS, and Beeper Cloud will be sunset — at which point Beeper Mini will become known as just Beeper once again.

TechCrunch issued two corrections on the article: on 12/5/23 at 2:55 PM ET, that the price of Beeper Mini was listed incorrectly in one part of the article and is $1.99/mo, not $2.99/mo; and on 12/6/23 at 2:33 PM ET, adding "[sent it]" to clarify that the Beeper app does not look like an iPhone app.
