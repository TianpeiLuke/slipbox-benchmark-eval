# CD Unfamiliarity As A Generational Marker

The mothers who filmed three young Swifties failing to open a CD case treated the moment as a measure of how far technology had moved, and online viewers responded overwhelmingly with "I feel old", FOX News Lifestyle reported on 11 November 2023.

"The girls' struggle with the CD was a wake-up call to how times have changed since we 'cut the cord,'" Schmidt commented, and the mothers could not help but laugh as they realised how different things now are thanks to technology. "Watching the girls grapple with the CD case felt like stepping back in time," Jody, one of the moms, told Fox News Digital. "That moment of pure innocence when the girls first attempted to open the CD case was priceless," said Tia, another mom. "I knew then that we had to flip the camera on to capture the rest of their experience with the CD." The hilarious moment brought laughter not only to the three moms but to the scores of viewers who saw the video on TikTok, and the Gen Zers captured the attention of viewers online whose overwhelming response was "I feel old".

It was not the first time a young person went viral for trying to open a CD: in May, Fox News Digital reported on a mom who showed her 10-year-old daughter's struggle as she attempted to pop a "Now That's What I Call Music! 21" CD (2006).
