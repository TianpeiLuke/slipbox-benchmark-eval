# Kristen Bell's Household Screen-Time Rules

Kristen Bell's screen-time rule is that her daughters are not allowed phones or screens at all during the week, with TV permitted on weekends on fairly liberal terms, FOX News Entertainment reported on 10 October 2023.

The rule as she states it: "They're not allowed to play with our phones or have any screens really. They are allowed to watch TV on the weekends, and we're pretty liberal with it, which I've been considering paying more attention to." The weekday ban has a predictable weekend consequence: "But since they're not allowed at all during the week, they will wake up at 6:30 on a Saturday morning, and they will watch TV until noon. I will also sleep until 9:45, which is really nice, and then get stuff done."

The limit Bell herself flags is the handover at the end: "But I will say, I notice how hard it is to pull them away from the TV at noon." She also admits the weekend rules get a little lax.
