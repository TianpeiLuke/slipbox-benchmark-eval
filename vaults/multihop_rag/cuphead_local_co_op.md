# Cuphead (Local Co-Op)

Cuphead is a modern-day, punishingly difficult platformer with the beautiful look of vintage animation, included in Polygon's 2023-10-20 list of the 35 best couch co-op games for Nintendo Switch.

Its art direction and soundtrack are wholly unique, making the game a joyride from start to finish despite the challenge.

For couch co-op, Mugman — Cuphead's sibling — can join on a local journey.
