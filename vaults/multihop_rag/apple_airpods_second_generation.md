# Apple AirPods, Second Generation

Apple's second-gen AirPods were back down to $89, from $129 — a $40 saving, and $10 off their usual street price.

Engadget cannot broadly recommend the base AirPods to most people these days: their one-size-fits-all design will not work for everyone, and there are better-sounding options for the money.

The conditional recommendation is narrow — if you are all-in on Apple devices and just want the usual AirPods perks in an unsealed design, this deal makes the pair a little more palatable.
