# Lubin vs Ramos: A Controversial Decision In The Canelo-Charlo Co-Main Event

The co-main event on the Canelo-Charlo card was Jesus Ramos (20-0, 16 KOs) against Erickson Lubin (25-2, 18 KOs) in a WBC/WBA super welterweight title eliminator, which came up at 10:24 p.m. ET according to Sporting News' live coverage of 2023-09-30. The bout was headlined in that coverage as "Erickson Lubin takes controversial decision over Jesus Ramos in snoozer," with scores of 115-113, 116-112 and 117-111.

The live entries tracked a fight short on action. Through seven rounds, at 11:04 p.m. ET, Ramos had been in control in what was a relatively tame affair, a workman-like effort as he continued to apply pressure while Lubin seemed content to fight off his back foot. By 11:16 p.m. ET, with two rounds left, the crowd was fed up with the lack of action and fans were more interested in watching the celebrities walk in. At 11:29 p.m. ET the verdict landed as a surprise, and the coverage's own wording is inconsistent on who the judges named: "Well, that was surprising. It felt like Ramos was in control but all three judges found a way to give Ramos the decision. Although it wasn't a great fight, it certainly didn't appear to be a close fight and it definitely wasn't one that Lubin appeared to be winning. Alas, Lubin won a fight that few will remember outside of the controversial decision."
