# Caroline Ellison Testimony

In the second week of the FTX trial, former [Alameda Research](alameda_research.md) CEO Caroline Ellison testified that she took $14 billion from customers to repay debts to lenders, using those funds as a line of credit under the instruction of [Sam Bankman-Fried](sam_bankman_fried.md).

She also testified that FTX Ventures, a $2 billion venture fund, was funded with money that had previously been allocated to Alameda from third-party lenders.

Although the case carries no bribery charges, Ellison further testified to paying Chinese officials $150 million to restore access to $1 billion in frozen trading accounts.
