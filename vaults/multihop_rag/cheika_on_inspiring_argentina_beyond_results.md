# Cheika: Inspiring Argentina Is Not About Results

Michael Cheika argued before Argentina's 2023 World Cup semi-final that inspiring a country is not a matter of results at all but of ambition and conduct: "We want to inspire not just by our results but by our ambition, by our ability to get back up and overcome obstacles."

Asked whether inspiring the country was easy, he said "It's not easy", and then set out the standard he was applying: "To inspire is not about results, it's not just one player who played well; no, it's about our behaviour in general, our ability to be ready to come back from difficult times and seizing our opportunities."

The reason the stakes are so high for his players is scarcity of the occasion itself: "There are not a lot of opportunities in life – us playing in a semi-final is even rarer and we want to have no regrets." Cheika had maintained that his Los Pumas side was too tight earlier in the tournament but had begun to free themselves up since qualifying for the knockout stages, and rewarding their fans by doing so.
