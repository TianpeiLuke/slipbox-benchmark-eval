# Capitol's 2023 Frontline Roster Highlights

Beyond its headline campaigns, Michelle Jubelirer's team secured Toosii's biggest record to date with the 3X platinum "Favorite Song", plus one of the US's biggest-selling hip-hop debuts of the year with Offset's "Set It Off". Long-term CMG signings including Troye Sivan, recently Grammy nominated for his "Rush" video, Niall Horan, Fletcher and Maggie Rogers have each celebrated some of the biggest successes of their careers to date.

Discussing Capitol's long-term investment in these acts, Jubelirer says all are amazing songwriters and performers who are successful globally and have built extremely passionate fan bases, and that the focus continues to be on building a rich world for their superfans. Her stated measure of success is broader than the charts: success is not just about momentary chart position, and merch, product drops and experiential moments are key to building and maintaining the engagement of superfans, so while streaming is crucial, for many artists these areas are equally so.
