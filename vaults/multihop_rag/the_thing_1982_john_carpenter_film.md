# The Thing: A Paranoid Thriller Premise Wrapped In Alien Gore

The Thing is John Carpenter's postmodern creature feature, starring Kurt Russell, Wilford Brimley and Keith David, and available on Peacock. It takes the idea of alien monsters and makes them simultaneously more recognisable and more gross and unworldly than in any other movie in history; the second adaptation of the novella Who Goes There?, it remains thrilling, terrifying and absolutely disgusting more than 40 years after its release.

The setup: a group of researchers work at an Antarctic base when a dog from a local Norwegian camp rushes in, with Norwegian men hot on its heels trying to kill it by any means necessary. Once the American crew takes the dog in and shelters it, they discover it is an alien that can transform into any living creature, mimicking it perfectly — which makes every one of them a suspect. That is one of the great paranoid thriller premises of all time, and it just so happens to also be filled with gross and fantastic alien gore. There's nothing quite like The Thing.
