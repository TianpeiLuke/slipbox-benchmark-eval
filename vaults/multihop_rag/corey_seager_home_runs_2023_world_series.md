# Corey Seager's Home Runs In The 2023 World Series

Corey Seager hit home runs in both Game 3 and Game 4 of the 2023 World Series, and by Game 4 all three of his hits in the series had been home runs — a fact the Sporting News highlighted alongside the observation that his World Series MVP case might be hurt by his hitting under .200 in the series.

In Game 3 on Oct. 30, at 8:43 p.m. in the top of the third, Seager ripped a two-run home run, smoking an inside changeup 421 feet over the right field wall for what the blog called two-out magic, giving Texas a 3-0 lead.

In Game 4 on Oct. 31, again at 8:43 p.m., Seager broke the game open: the 2020 World Series MVP took Kyle Nelson deep for a two-run home run to give the Rangers a commanding 5-0 lead, with the blog noting he had three hits in the series and all of them were home runs. Later that night, at 10:01 p.m., Ryne Nelson struck Seager out, and the blog observed that his World Series MVP case might be hurt by his sub-.200 series average.
