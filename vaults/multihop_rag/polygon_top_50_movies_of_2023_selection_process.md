# Polygon's Selection Process for the Best Movies of 2023

Polygon builds its best-movies-of-the-year list by having each of the staff's film fans create an individual ballot of their top movies every year, with an option to rank the entries numerically or simply weight them; those ballots are then used to generate a collective list, weighted by strength of opinion so that a much-seen mainstream movie does not gain too much advantage over a terrific but underseen indie. The result is then published, with the top 10 picks on each staffer's list listed in the comments.

The eligibility rule is that any movie released in the US during that calendar year qualifies. A stated limitation follows from the publication timing: because the list is published in early December, some December releases are underrepresented.

Polygon frames the exercise as inherently difficult, likening a collaborative best-movies list to forming a committee to definitively rank apples and oranges — how to account for personal tastes, or for access when a given movie is only available in certain markets at certain times, or for the fact that the entries are so qualitatively different they have little in common. The weighted-ballot procedure exists to accommodate that spread: the staff describes its collective tastes as eclectic, with some hungriest for action, horror, epic fantasy, or challenging science fiction, some preferring complicated drama or dark neo-noir, and others drawn to musicals, comedy, animated adventures, and other lighter fare. The best-of-the-year list is meant to reflect that range.
