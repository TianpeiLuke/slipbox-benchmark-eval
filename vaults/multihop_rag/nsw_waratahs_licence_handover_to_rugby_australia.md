# The NSW Waratahs' Licence Handover To Rugby Australia

The New South Wales Rugby Union was in the latter stages of handing over its licence to Rugby Australia, The Roar revealed in the week before Oct. 10, 2023 — a move by which the NSW Waratahs, who were on the brink of insolvency, handed over their commercial and high-performance property.

The practical effect is that all male and female Waratahs players, including coaches and high-performance staff, as well as all commercial and operations staff, will run out of RA's office. NSW Waratahs chief executive Paul Doorn informed stakeholders of the decision the previous week.

RA is cognisant that not every Super Rugby franchise will follow suit immediately, but the governing body is hopeful that now one of the two biggest states has agreed to hand over the keys, others will follow swiftly, with the Melbourne Rebels expected to be next. RA chief executive Phil Waugh says building trust back between the governing body and the states is essential to their plans.
