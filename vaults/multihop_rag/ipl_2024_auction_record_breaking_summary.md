# IPL 2024 Auction: A Record-Breaking Day

The IPL 2024 auction, held on 19 December in Dubai, was a record-breaking day in IPL history: for the first time ever, two players breached the Rs 20 Cr mark, with Mitchell Starc and Pat Cummins — two Australian pacers — leading the money charts. Cummins went to Sunrisers Hyderabad for Rs 20.50 Cr, but the record only lasted 30 minutes as Kolkata Knight Riders broke the bank for Mitchell Starc, paying a whopping Rs 24.75 Cr.

Uncapped Indians such as Shubham Dubey and Sameer Rizvi also stole the show, bagging big contracts for their performances in regional T20 leagues. A total of 72 players, including 30 overseas players, were sold for Rs 230.45 Cr combined; the live blog wrapped at 9:05 PM IST with that same total.

Going in, at 1:00 PM IST the 17th IPL auction was moments from starting, with stars like Mitchell Starc, Pat Cummins, Travis Head and Rachin Ravindra in the spotlight as franchises looked to fill their gaping holes for the upcoming edition of the cash-rich league.
