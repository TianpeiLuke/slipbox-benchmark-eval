# Amex Cash Magnet Redemption Process

With the American Express Cash Magnet Card, cash back rewards are redeemed for statement credits to your account. There is no minimum required to redeem, and rewards convert at $1 of statement credit for every reward dollar redeemed — 50.50 reward dollars, for example, redeems for a $50.50 statement credit.

Statement credits are applied to the account within three days. The one restriction to observe afterwards is that statement credits cannot be used toward the minimum amount due: unless the statement credit reduces your balance to $0, you're still required to make the minimum payment.
