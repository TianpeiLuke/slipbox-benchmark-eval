# I Know Places: A Love Song About Hiding From The Cameras

I Know Places continues 1989's theme of thwarted love, and was fuelled by Taylor Swift's increasing anxiety over holding down relationships in the spotlight. Its defining premise is a hypothetical conversation: rather than a real romance, it answers what she would say to a prospective partner worried about the attention she attracts.

She explained the thinking to the Grammy Museum: "I was like, 'No-one's gonna sign up for this. There are just too many cameras pointed at me, there are too many ridiculous elaborations on my life'. But I decided to write a love song [about] what would I say if I met someone really awesome and they were like, 'Hey, I'm worried about all this attention you get'. So I wrote this song about, 'Hey, I know all these places we could hide. We can outrun them'."
