# Thanksgiving 2023 Retail Closure Policy

Because Thanksgiving Day is a federal holiday in the US, some stores shut down completely on the holiday — and for the two largest chains this is now a standing policy rather than a one-off: both Target and Walmart announced permanent closures on the holiday in 2020, with the policy remaining in place ever since.

The origin of that policy was the pandemic. The outbreak had initially pushed these chains to close their doors on Thanksgiving in order to limit crowding and spread out the shopping season.

The closure is not universal, and it is short. Not all grocery stores remain closed on Thanksgiving Day — there are some retailers that shoppers could visit on the morning of the holiday, in preparation for the annual feast — and once Thanksgiving Day comes to a close, many stores reopen on the Friday, operating during their earlier or usual business hours in preparation for some of the biggest sales of the year.
