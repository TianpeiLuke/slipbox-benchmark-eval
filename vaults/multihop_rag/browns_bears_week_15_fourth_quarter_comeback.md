# Browns' Fourth Quarter Comeback Against The Bears

Chicago led 17-7 heading into the fourth quarter before Cleveland scored on successive drives to overturn the game, per Sporting News on Dec. 17, 2023.

The Browns opened the fourth quarter with a field goal, then scored a game-tying touchdown with 3:08 left in the contest. Cleveland forced a three-and-out, then drove down the field in eight plays to kick a go-ahead field goal with 32 seconds left, burning all of Chicago's timeouts in the process. The Bears got a bit of life when Tyler Scott caught a short pass from Fields and raced up the right sideline 30 yards to move up to Cleveland's 45, but after back-to-back incompletions they were forced to try for the Hail Mary rather than kick the long field goal.

Bears coach Matt Eberflus said afterward: "At the end of the game, I loved the way our guys battled at the end. And what a nice play by T Scott to put us in position and, again, we were just too far back. We ended up throwing the Hail Mary at the end and we had a chance there too, that's just about focus and concentration at the very end and making a really good play."
