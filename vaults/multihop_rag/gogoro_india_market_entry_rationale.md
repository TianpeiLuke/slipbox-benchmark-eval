# Gogoro's Rationale For Entering India

Gogoro co-founder and CEO Horace Luke wants to "go big" in India even as the Taiwanese company faces challenges in its home country, and his case rests on two claims about what India offers.

The first is market size: India is the world's biggest two-wheeler market, where 15 million to 20 million new two-wheelers hit the road every year. The second is leverage beyond India itself — Luke sees the world's most populous country as a launch pad that will accelerate Gogoro's global expansion into other markets, a tactic TechCrunch judged reasonable considering India is already on a spree to become a competitive manufacturing hub for all major international brands and products, from smartphones to satellites.

Speaking at the company's launch in New Delhi in December 2023, Luke said the company has picked India as a strategic market from which it can reach a number of new users and enter new markets, starting with neighboring Nepal.
