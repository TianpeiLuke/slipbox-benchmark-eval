# BetMGM's Vermont Offer Is The Most Generous "Second Chance" — If You Lose First

Sporting News argues on 2023-09-26 that getting $1,500 back in Bonus Bets makes the BetMGM Sportsbook offer one of the most generous versions of the "second chance" offers that let you lose a first wager but get a second try at it. The advantages: it enables you to lose a first bet but still get a second chance at winning; you can receive back up to $1,500 in Bonus Bets, and if it is more than $50 the bets get split up into smaller amounts you can use separately; and you receive the Bonus Bet(s) within 24 hours, a quick turnaround.

The conditions that limit it are the mirror image of the structure. You have to lose your first wager to receive any welcome bonus at all — the bonus is contingent on a loss, unlike offers that pay out either way. You have to use your Bonus Bet(s) within seven days. And winning with the Bonus Bet(s) earns you the winnings, but not the bet amounts back.
