# Amex Cash Magnet Cardholder Benefits

Compared to the benefits of other credit cards, the American Express Cash Magnet Card is more limited, but it offers four perks. American Express Experiences gives access to exclusive ticket pre-sales and cardmember-only events, including major Broadway shows, concerts, and sport competitions. Car rental loss and damage insurance applies when you use the card to pay for your entire car rental cost and decline the rental company's collision damage waiver: you automatically receive secondary coverage against damage or theft of the rental vehicle.

The Global Assist hotline is available when you are traveling 100 miles or more from home, providing emergency assistance including medical referrals, emergency cash wires and missing luggage services — though you are responsible for paying the charges of any third-party service provided. Plan It applies on purchases of $100 or more, allowing you to split purchases into equal monthly payments at a fixed fee, similar to how buy now, pay later apps work.
