# Atalanta 3-2 AC Milan

Atalanta snatched a 3-2 victory at home to Milan on Dec. 9, 2023 thanks to a back-heel flick in added time from Luis Muriel, after a double from Ademola Lookman.

Muriel struck in the 95th minute, two minutes after Milan defender Davide Calabria had been dismissed for a second booking. Olivier Giroud and Christian Pulisic scored the Milan equalisers. Milan remained third on 29 points and Atalanta were seventh on 23.
