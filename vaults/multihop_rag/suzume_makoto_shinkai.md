# Suzume (Makoto Shinkai)

Suzume is Makoto Shinkai's film starring Nanoka Hara and Hokuto Matsumura, and Polygon argues Shinkai's career seems to have been building up to it: everything that did not quite work in Your Name and Weathering with You manages to coalesce here. It ranked 14 on the best movies of 2023 list of 2023-10-07 and streams on Crunchyroll.

The film has all the hallmarks of Shinkai's recent, most popular works — a young couple brought together by strange fantastical elements, a looming disaster threatening to upend the world, and clear blue skies.

But Polygon's Petrana Radulovic stresses the interior story: while Suzume is about two young people banding together to save the world from disaster, it is equally about the protagonist overcoming her own trauma. As she travels cross-country with an animated chair, who is actually a handsome and mysterious young man, she starts to connect with other people instead of closing herself off. Her quest to stop a giant earthquake-creating worm comes from having lost her mother in the 2011 Tōhoku earthquake and tsunami, and slowly but surely she rediscovers her will to live and realizes her life is not disposable. Unlike Shinkai's other works, the romance is subtle — the barest hint of it — which makes it resonate even more, in a beautifully haunting story rooted in a real-world tragedy.
