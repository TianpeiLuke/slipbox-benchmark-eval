# Bank Of America's Warning Of More Housing "Turbulence"

A Bank of America team led by U.S. economist Jeseo Park warned in the week of 2023-10-06 that there is more "turbulence" coming for the housing market because of high mortgage rates: "With rates likely staying higher for longer, we are cautious of potential turbulence ahead," the group wrote. The grounds are the persistence of high rates rather than a repeat of a past crash — the team said they were getting an eerie feeling of "déjà vu," but it was not 2008 that came to mind, it was the 1980s.

The forecast attached to this warning is qualified. Bank of America had previously forecast 0% home price growth and falling home sales for the full year 2023, but it did not offer a new prediction in its latest note. The warrant joining the rate outlook to the housing outlook runs the other way as well: eventually, as inflation fades and leads the Fed to cut interest rates, housing affordability will improve. "At that stage, we should see a more stable and healthy housing market," Park and her team wrote. "Until then, hang tight, it may be a bumpy ride."
