# UTSA's 2023 Offensive Profile Going Into The Frisco Bowl

UTSA hit its stride over the second half of the 2023 season, scoring at least 34 points in every win since Oct. 7 and proving an offensive juggernaut, before losing 29-16 to Tulane in its last outing, per Sporting News on Dec. 18, 2023.

Everything started with star quarterback Frank Harris. The dual-threat QB completed 64.9% of his passes that season for 2,506 yards and 18 touchdowns — impressive, though he had totaled 4,059 yards and 32 touchdowns on a 69.6% completion rate the previous year, so while UTSA's offense was good, Sporting News had seen a better version of the unit in recent seasons. Still, Harris' experience and talent often shone through. UTSA's leading receiver Joshua Cephus did the heavy lifting for the unit, catching 82 passes for 1,049 yards and nine touchdowns, and the offense also had four ball carriers with at least 323 rushing yards, so there were plenty of threats for head coach Jeff Traylor to unleash.

By team metrics, UTSA averaged 31.7 points and 424.6 total yards per game, ranking 37th and 39th in the nation respectively. Roadrunners opponents ranked 69th in third down conversion rate, but the defense allowed an 87.5% score rate in the red zone, which ranked 99th. Against the betting number, UTSA was 5-6-1 ATS and 4-7-1 to the OVER.
