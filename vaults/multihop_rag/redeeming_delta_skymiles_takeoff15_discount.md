# Redeeming Delta SkyMiles With The Takeoff15 Discount

To capture the Takeoff15 discount, cardholders book flights through the Fly Delta app or their Delta SkyMiles account using their card, which yields a 15% discount on the miles price — but only on Delta and Delta Connection flights.

The scope limit matters because Delta has more than 20 airline partners and cardholders can book through airlines such as Air France and Virgin Atlantic; the 15% discount does not extend to those partner bookings. Cardholders can use their miles and their cash to buy flights. Fortune presents the discount as a reason to hold the card long-term, since a heavy Delta user could save thousands of miles over the course of a year. The best bargains on award flights are found through Delta SkyMiles Award Deals: recent deals from Seattle (SEA) had included Chicago (ORD) for 8,000 miles or Auckland, New Zealand (AKL) for 64,000 one way in economy plus taxes and fees, and because flash sales come and go it is a good idea to keep an eye on the site. Other redemption options include booking travel through Delta Vacations, using miles for upgrades, or even buying champagne at the Delta Sky Club — though the best value will most likely be on award tickets.
