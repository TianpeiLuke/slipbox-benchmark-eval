# Walmart's Black Friday 2023 Deal Rollout

Walmart launched its first round of Black Friday deals on 8 November 2023 and dished out another round the day before Thanksgiving, with some of the retailer's most popular rollbacks already sold out by the time Mashable updated its roundup on 25 November 2023 at 4:00 a.m. EST.

"Leave items in your shopping cart too long and you'll risk losing out on your haul," the roundup warns. The update note says the story had been refreshed with the latest deals from the Walmart early Cyber Monday sale.

Although the official Black Friday programming had kept the writers busy all month, "the big day has finally arrived — actual Black Friday": Mashable compiled a list of over 180 of the best deals from Walmart's Black Friday sale and highlighted favourites from different departments, promising to keep updating the list throughout Cyber Monday.
