# Roger Penske Calming Blaney At The Phoenix Finale

Team owner Roger Penske, often referred to as "The Captain," stepped in during the Phoenix championship race to remind Ryan Blaney that he was not racing against Ross Chastain but against the Hendrick Motorsports duo, as reported by Essentially Sports on 6 November 2023.

The situation was that Blaney's competitive spirit almost got the best of him: he was racing for the championship and eager to take the lead from Chastain in the closing few laps, while also aware that he needed only to maintain his position to win the title. In the post-race conference the Team Penske driver expressed his frustration: "There is no secret that I can snap on the radio. That's been my whole life. That's been my whole career. It's just kind of something I do." He described the intense battle with Chastain, who blocked him multiple times, making it a tough fight, and the final push came when Blaney made contact with Chastain — a move made out of frustration, as he admitted.

Penske's calming influence helped Blaney regain composure, ensuring he didn't take any unnecessary risks; the result was a championship for Blaney and another testament to the guidance of "The Captain" in high-pressure situations. The report's closing generalisation is that in NASCAR it's not just about speed and strategy but about maintaining composure under intense pressure, with the wisdom of experienced leaders like Penske playing a crucial role in their teams' success.
