# Saudi Co-Leadership As Egypt's Way Out Of The Dilemma

One way out of Egypt's dilemma may be for it to offer Saudi Arabia a role co-leading the Palestinian issue for the Arab world in return for financial support, according to strategists. The proposition rests on Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman being willing: aware of domestic anger at Israel, he might welcome such an initiative to strengthen his regional profile — though he was absent from the Summit for Peace held in Cairo on Saturday, 2023-10-21, which is the one contrary indication the account records.

The fluid situation may offer Egypt other opportunities to take a mediating role that could be rewarded, Crisis Group's Riccardo Fabiani said. For now, Egypt will try to play a constructive part "in the hope that its contribution will be acknowledged by its international and regional partners and potentially rewarded economically," he said.
