# Amazon Kindle Kids Edition Deal Listings

The Kindle Kids appears in The Verge's 2023 kids gift guide at $119.99 (Amazon, Best Buy) and in WIRED's Prime Day roundup at a price matching what it saw in July for the best Kindle for kids.

The Verge describes the latest Kindle Kids (2022) as offering young readers a sharp 6.2-inch display with adjustable front lighting, USB-C charging, and a battery lasting weeks on a single charge — with the best part being that Amazon's kid-ified ebook reader is ad-free and comes with a two-year warranty against accidental damage plus access to a ton of age-appropriate content. WIRED adds that this model is waterproof with adjustable warm lighting for reading at night, that a Warrior Cats special edition is available for $120, and that the standard Kindle Kids is also on sale and a bit cheaper at $80 ($40 off) but lacks the adjustable warm lighting and waterproofing. Whichever you get, Amazon throws in a protective case, a one-year subscription to Amazon Kids+, and a two-year no-questions-asked replacement guarantee.
