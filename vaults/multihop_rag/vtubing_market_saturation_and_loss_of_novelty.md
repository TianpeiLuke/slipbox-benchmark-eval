# VTubing's Market Saturation and Loss of Novelty

A streamer with agency Nijisanji, who is not approved to speak publicly, told Polygon for its 2023-10-19 piece: "I do feel that VTubing has become less novel as the market has become more saturated."

The claim links declining novelty to the number of entrants — saturation of the market rather than any failure of the format itself.

The same source extends the complaint to the agency relationship: "There are obviously some ups and downs when it comes to working with an agency as well, which requires a lot of patience if I'm honest. And there are some decisions that are made that sometimes don't make a lot of sense."
