# Xbox Wireless Controller Deal

Official Xbox Series X/S Wireless Controllers were as low as $40 at Microsoft and Best Buy for Black Friday 2023, with the discount applying to various colors; typically the gamepad retails between $50 and $60 depending on the colorway.

The Series X/S controller doesn't have the DualSense's advanced haptics and requires AA batteries for power by default — Engadget recommends grabbing a rechargeable set — but some will always find its shape and asymmetrical joysticks more comfortable, and it's also simpler to set up and use on PC.
