# The USMNT's October 2023 Friendlies Against Germany And Ghana

Gregg Berhalter and the U.S. men's national team scheduled friendlies against Germany and Ghana in October 2023 as they continued preparations for the 2024 Copa America and the 2026 FIFA World Cup. The U.S. first took on Germany in Hartford, Connecticut on October 14.

That match doubled as the debut for Germany's new head coach. The 15th-ranked Germans were under new management, so the USMNT served as the first match for Julian Nagelsmann, the 36-year-old who had been out of a job since departing Bayern Munich back in March. Most European countries were busy with Euro 2024 qualifying at that point, but Germany, as tournament hosts, qualified automatically, which opened their schedule for the friendly.

On October 17 the U.S. traveled to GEODIS Park in Nashville, Tennessee to face Ghana, two nations with a deep history of contentious matchups including three consecutive World Cup tournaments in 2006, 2010 and 2014.
