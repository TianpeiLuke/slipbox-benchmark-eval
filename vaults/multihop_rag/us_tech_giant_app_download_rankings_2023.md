# US Tech Giants' 2023 App Download Rankings

Meta and Google both lost ground in Apple's 2023 U.S. iPhone download rankings. Meta, which last year held the No. 3, No. 4 and No. 9 spots with WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook respectively, showed up in the top 10 this year with a slightly different mix: Facebook is gone from the charts, while Meta's new app Instagram Threads was the No. 3 app by U.S. downloads, with Instagram and WhatsApp following at No. 6 and No. 9, both slipping from their ranking last year.

Google had dominated last year's list with the No. 2 app YouTube, just behind TikTok, as well as No. 5 Google Maps, No. 6 Google Search and No. 7 Gmail. In 2023, however, Google's only two apps in the top 10 by downloads were No. 7 Google Search and No. 8 YouTube, both also slipping in ranking from last year.

The only other top app in the list not from Meta, Google or a Chinese firm was Max, formerly HBO Max, which showed up as the No. 3 app by downloads in the U.S.
