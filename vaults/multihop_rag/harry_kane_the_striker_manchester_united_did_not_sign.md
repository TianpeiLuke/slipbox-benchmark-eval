# Harry Kane, The Striker Manchester United Did Not Sign

Harry Kane, the England captain and the forward Manchester United neglected to bid for in the summer, unlocked United's defence with a lovely flick to set up Bayern Munich's winner on 12 December 2023 — a reminder, The Independent wrote, of what could have been for United.

The comparison the reports draw is with the striker United did buy. Rasmus Hojlund has at least struck in the Champions League, but in domestic competitions he has been outscored 18-0 by Kane. United, cast out of continental competition altogether, became just the fourth English team to prop up a Champions League group when it concluded, and there was something predictable about the manner of their demise.

The Bayern game also showed one reason why Kane is at Germany's biggest club rather than England's: his side are through with ease and among the favourites for the competition, even if they do look lacking in a few areas.
