# Deft's Revenue Model And Pricing Tiers

Deft does not show ads on its platform — the discriminating feature against the incumbents it criticizes. It relies on affiliate commissions to generate revenue, but wants subscriptions to be its primary revenue driver.

Deft is currently testing two paid tiers: a $10 a month plan, which will offer better search customization to filter out some brands, and a $20 per month plan, which will enable a personal shopping service that will continue to look for suitable products even after you stop searching manually.

Its roadmap moves outward from its own site: Deft aims to release a browser extension that helps users search for products by the end of 2023 or early 2024, and in the next three years wants to build and release search APIs that other e-commerce sites could use.
