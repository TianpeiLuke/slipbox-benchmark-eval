# OpenAI Board Negotiations Over Altman Reinstatement

Over the weekend that followed Sam Altman's firing, OpenAI's board held on-and-off talks about bringing him back as CEO; the talks repeatedly stalled, and at one point it was confirmed that Altman would not return and that new leadership would take hold.

The sequence ran roughly as follows. Investors — reportedly furious at the turn of events — exerted pressure on OpenAI's board to reinstate Altman, going so far as to recruit Microsoft, with Satya Nadella said to be sympathetic. On November 18 The Verge reported that the board had agreed in principle to resign and to allow Altman and Brockman to return, but it waffled, missing a deadline by which many OpenAI staffers were set to leave the company; Altman was said to be ambivalent about coming back and to be asking for "significant" governance changes. Bloomberg, citing sources close to the matter, reported that the board was in talks with Altman about returning as CEO as soon as that week, with discussions involving Quora CEO Adam D'Angelo, one current board member, and possibly other directors. On November 19 The Information reported that Altman was expected to meet at OpenAI's San Francisco headquarters as executives pushed to have him reinstated, with Brockman invited to join though it was unclear whether he would.

Negotiations then hit a snag: Bloomberg reported that Brad Lightcap and Mira Murati, among others, were pushing the board to reinstate Altman, but the directors resisted, and as of midday Sunday the board had not resigned out of concern over who could replace them and was vetting candidates — one possible new addition being Salesforce co-CEO Bret Taylor. Later, according to The Information citing an internal memo sent by Sutskever, Altman would not be returning as CEO, and OpenAI appointed Emmett Shear, the co-founder of video streaming site Twitch, as interim CEO, replacing Murati. A number of investors and OpenAI employees had tried to bring Altman back after the sudden firing, but following a weekend of negotiations it was confirmed that he would not return; what that meant for ChatGPT's future and for the OpenAI DevDay announcements remained to be seen.
