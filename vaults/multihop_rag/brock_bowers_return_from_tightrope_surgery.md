# Brock Bowers' Return From TightRope Surgery

Brock Bowers returned against Ole Miss on Nov. 11, 2023 and scored at 9:50 p.m. on an 8-yard pass from Carson Beck — his first touchdown since Oct. 7 — with the Dawgs showing no signs of slowing down, having scored 31 unanswered points against the Rebels.

His availability had been uncertain into the evening: at 5:30 p.m. the live blog asked whether Bowers would return for his final home game, describing the tight end as a game-time decision who could come back after a high ankle sprain sustained a month earlier, with a report noting No. 19 was out for warmups.
