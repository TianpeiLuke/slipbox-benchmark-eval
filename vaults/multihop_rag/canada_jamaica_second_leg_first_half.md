# The First Half Of Canada 2-3 Jamaica

Canada led 1-0 on the night and 3-1 on aggregate at halftime in Toronto on Nov. 21, 2023, in what had been relatively smooth sailing but could have been more emphatic but for the heroics of Andre Blake. The match began on a cold, wet, windy night at BMO Field, with Canada leading 2-1 from the first leg; the home side started confidently on the ball, and Jamaica — who had never beaten Canada on the road — faced a tough task.

Blake kept the tie alive almost single-handedly. At 7 minutes Daniel Johnson's deep free kick was cleared for the game's first corner, which was poorly delivered. At 9 minutes Tajon Buchanan played an enticing ball across the six-yard box and Lowe connected with a slide at the back post, but Blake got his body in the way for an impressive block. At 12 minutes Buchanan lofted a ball over the top for Larin, who muscled through only for Blake to save, with the assistant belatedly flagging offside on the initial run. At 15 minutes came perhaps the pick of the saves: David poked the ball to Larin in the six-yard box and Blake was again in the right position to block the low shot, making it look only a matter of time before Canada scored.

The goal arrived at 25 minutes. Davies sprayed the ball wide to David, who played an inch-perfect pass back to Davies in the six-yard box; Blake blocked the goal-bound effort but it looped up, hit the crossbar and spun in, with Larin making sure not to touch the ball in case he was offside — 1-0 on the night, 3-1 on aggregate.

Jamaica threatened sporadically thereafter without equalising. At 29 minutes Nicholson was through on goal after a lofted pass but spooned his shot well wide, and the offside flag went up anyway. At 31 minutes Gray played the ball across the six-yard box and Bailey stabbed it straight into Milan Borjan's arms — Jamaica's best chance so far. Canada looked dangerous on the break: at 35 minutes Davies drilled a low ball through the penalty box that deflected off a defender to safety, and at 37 minutes Eustaquio's free kick to the back post just eluded Miller's head, making a second goal feel imminent. At 43 minutes Davies threatened down the left again but was flagged offside, and at 45 minutes Jamaica came oh so close as Gray cut in from the left and delivered a delicious ball through the six-yard box that Nicholson could not quite reach. Two minutes were added, and the half ended with the assessment that Jamaica would need a miracle — though one goal could make the home side nervous.
