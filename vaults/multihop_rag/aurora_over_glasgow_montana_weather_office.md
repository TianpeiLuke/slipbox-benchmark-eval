# Aurora Over the Glasgow, Montana Weather Office

Meteorologist Angel Enriquez photographed pink and green aurora above a round observatory at the National Weather Service office in Glasgow, northeast Montana, while working the evening shift during the November 2023 storm.

He was alerted by a colleague who was already outside. "A fellow coworker was out gathering her own pictures when she texted 'GO OUTSIDE NOW,'" Enriquez told Space.com. "I took the tripod to the darkest spot of the office, which happens to be next to the radar, and took some memorable photos."
