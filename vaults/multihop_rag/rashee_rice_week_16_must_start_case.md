# Rashee Rice Week 16 Must-Start Case

CBSSports.com's Dec. 19, 2023 Week 16 wide receiver preview argued that whether or not to start Rashee Rice should not be one of the many difficult decisions facing fantasy managers in what was the Fantasy Football semifinals in most leagues: if Rice was on your roster, he was in your starting lineup.

The grounds begin with recent form. While Rice was just WR29 on the season, that was mostly due to a slow start, and his hot stretch had pushed him into must-start, borderline top-12 territory: the Chiefs' star rookie had four straight games with at least nine targets and had scored at least 14 PPR fantasy points in all of them, and since Week 12 only Deebo Samuel and CeeDee Lamb had scored more PPR fantasy points than Rice. The apparent objection — that on paper the matchup did not look great, because the Raiders had been above average at limiting the fantasy production of wide receivers — is answered directly: this hot stretch started against the Raiders, when Rice scored 24.7 PPR fantasy points against them in Week 12, and they played a style of defense that limits big plays but allows throws underneath, which fits Rice and his 4.3 average depth of target perfectly. While the Raiders had surrendered the 11th-fewest passing yards that season, they had given up the fourth-most yards after the catch. The supporting stat: 69.9% — nearly 70% of Rice's receiving yards came after the catch, tops among wide receivers with at least 50 targets.
