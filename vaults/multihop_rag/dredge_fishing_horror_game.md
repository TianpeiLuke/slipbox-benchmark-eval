# Dredge (Fishing Horror Game)

Dredge, developed by Black Salt Games for Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, Windows PC, Xbox One, and Xbox Series X, is ranked 17 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31. Polygon describes it as a Lovecraftian horror experience masquerading as a simple fishing game.

The open ocean is filled with terrible creatures that can and will damage or destroy your boat, and each of the major islands you visit comes with its own evil sea beasts that must be dealt with if you want to progress the story or fish peacefully. The game ultimately tells a dark parable about loss, and how obsession can own you if you are not careful.

Critic RG argues what makes it one of the best games of 2023 is that under its foreboding story and twisted environments is a fishing game that grows more complex with every outing, centered on an upgrade system that feels amazing to progress through: as the dangers around you grow, so do your ship's capabilities, and adventuring into battles — metaphorical and otherwise — with the seas' most dastardly critters always yields upgrades that let you catch even better fish and build an even bigger boat. By 10 or so hours in, you feel like a commercial fisherman who happened upon something bigger and more foreboding than they could have imagined, having stared deep into some kind of black abyss only to escape forever changed.
