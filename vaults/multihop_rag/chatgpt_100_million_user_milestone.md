# ChatGPT's 100 Million User Milestone

To say that ChatGPT was well-received would be to say that the Titanic suffered a small fender-bender on its maiden voyage, Engadget writes: it was a polestar, magnitudes bigger than the hype surrounding DALL-E and other image generators, and people flat out lost their minds over the new AI and its CEO, Sam Altman. Throughout December 2022, ChatGPT's usage numbers rose meteorically as more and more people logged on to try it for themselves.

By the following January, ChatGPT was a certified phenomenon, surpassing 100 million monthly active users in just two months — faster than both TikTok or Instagram, and still the fastest user adoption to 100 million in the history of the internet.
