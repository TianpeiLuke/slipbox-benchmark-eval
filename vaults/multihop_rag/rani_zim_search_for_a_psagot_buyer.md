# Rani Zim's Search For A Psagot Buyer

Zim has started to look for someone to buy his entire stake in the investment house, or at least part of it — a reversal of what he said on acquiring the business, and one whose prospects Globes treats as doubtful.

Immediately after acquiring Psagot's business, which mainly consisted of management of mutual funds and customer portfolios, Zim told "Globes": "Our headline at Psagot is to restore its former glory. We haven't come to make a killing in one or two years, and I don't want to start selling the business on to other entities… we'll invest in the people, in the activity, in the organization, in the market, and we'll form collaborations with many entities in the economy. We want to bring new activities and a different aroma to the market." But, as reported six months ago, he began seeking a buyer; one of the names mentioned in the press as someone who could participate in such an acquisition is Group Psagot CEO Yaniv Bender, who currently holds 7.4% of the company. When he put Psagot up for sale a few months ago, Zim was looking for a valuation of around NIS 400 million, similar to the valuation at which he acquired it — but in light of the current share price, and since market conditions in Israel generally indicate it will be hard to find a buyer who wants to do a deal at this time, it is not certain a deal is in the offing. At any rate, Group Psagot recently announced that Rani Zim was stepping down as chairperson, to be replaced by Daniel Leventhal.
