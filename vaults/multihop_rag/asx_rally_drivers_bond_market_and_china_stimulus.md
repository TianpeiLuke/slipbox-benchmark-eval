# What Drove The ASX Rally On 10 October 2023

TMS Capital portfolio manager Ben Clark attributed the Australian sharemarket's strength to three things acting together: a calming bond market, a positive lead from Wall Street, and positive news out of China.

He set out how each fed through. "The bond market is retracing," he said. "There's also been strength in resource stocks amid rumours that the Chinese government might launch a substantial stimulus package." On the rates channel, Clark said interest-rate sensitive sectors climbed on increased probabilities of an easing in interest rates, because "Consumer discretionary and information technology companies are winners in lower-interest rate environments." Looking ahead, as more annual general meetings kicked off over the following weeks, Clark said he would be looking at trading updates, especially of companies that did not give solid guidance in their last update.
