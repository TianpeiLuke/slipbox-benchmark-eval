# The Terms Of A Separation Agreement Determine Its Outcome

Relationship expert Susan Winter argues that what happens during a separation depends almost entirely on what the couple agreed the separation was for, so the same events — including an outside relationship — can lead to opposite outcomes. Applied to the Smiths, the point is pressed by a gap in the public record: outsiders now know that Jada Pinkett Smith and Will Smith have been separated since 2016, but the timeline of her relationship with August Alsina remains fuzzy and the terms of the separation are still unknown to the public. The Independent notes that in some cases having separate relationships can give couples the clarity they need about their marriage.

Winter's grounds are the two different agreements a separating couple might have made, which carry different implications for an affair: "What's pivotal here is, what was the agreement? Did we agree to separate to work on ourselves for the purpose of coming back together again? Did we agree to separate and just be alone for a while and not have that discussion? And in the second case, if you have an affair, sometimes it can bring you back to the original person. You can get whatever you need to achieve or gain a different perspective, allowing you to decide if you want to come back or leave for good."
