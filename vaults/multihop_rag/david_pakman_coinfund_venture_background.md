# David Pakman's Venture Background At CoinFund

On TechCrunch's Chain Reaction podcast, Jacquelyn interviewed David Pakman, managing partner and head of venture investments at CoinFund.

Before CoinFund, Pakman spent 14 years at the venture capital firm Venrock. He also led the Series A and B rounds at Dollar Shave Club, which was acquired by Unilever for $1 billion. And in 1991 he co-created Apple Music when he was part of Apple's system software product marketing group.
