# Govee LED Strip Light M1 Matter Deal

Govee's Matter-ready LED Strip Light M1 went on sale for an all-time low of $44.99 at Amazon — $15 off, down from $60, a 25 percent discount — when buyers clipped the on-page coupon, and could also be bought for $3 more, at $48, direct from Govee through October 31st, which The Verge called still one of its better prices to date. The customizable 6.56-foot (2m) strip can display millions of colors and shades of white and can create light patterns that sync with music.

The Verge framed the timing on October 28th, 2023 around the calendar: Halloween is not the only holiday on the horizon as October draws to a close, since once the clock strikes midnight on October 31st, Mariah Carey will defrost, peppermint mochas will inevitably return, and the 2023 holiday season will have officially begun. While handy throughout the year, the light strip is particularly convenient during Halloween and other holidays — the bright, tunable LEDs can flash and blink to the beat of your music and act as festive decor that adds a pop of color to holiday parties. What is particularly great about this strip, the writer noted, is that it supports the newer Matter protocol, which allows you to control the lights with a wide array of smart home platforms.
