# Angus Hastie AFL Draft Prospect 2023

Angus Hastie, profiled in Kevin Sheehan's top 40 for the 2023 AFL Draft as reported by The Roar on Nov. 15, 2023, is an athletic medium defender who sets the play up from defence with his run and ball use. State: Victoria Country. State League Club: Geelong Falcons. Community Club: Geelong West Giants. Date of Birth: 19/09/05. Height: 190cm.

He has shown a preparedness to back himself and take the game on to create opportunities for his team, and his intercepting ability is also a strength, which saw him represent Victoria Country in all four matches at the National Championships. He played 13 matches with the Geelong Falcons in the Coates Talent League that year, averaging 20 disposals and 4.6 marks; his standout performance came in the preliminary final defeat to the Sandringham Dragons, where his dash from defence was exceptional. His elite lateral movement was on display at the national Draft Combine, where he produced the third best time (8.20 seconds) in the agility test. Weapon: Line-breaking dash. AFL player comparison: Mason Redman (Essendon).
