# Holdover Performance Of Hunger Games, Trolls, Wish And Napoleon

On the first weekend of December 2023 the holdover titles behind "Renaissance" were led by Lionsgate's "The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes," which fell to second place in its third weekend with an estimated $14.5 million and had by then earned over $121 million domestically.

"Trolls Band Together" landed in fourth place in its third weekend with $7.6 million, bringing its domestic total to $74.8 million. Fifth place went to Disney's "Wish," which fell 62% from its underwhelming first weekend, taking $7.4 million from 3,900 locations for a global total of $81.6 million. Disney's other major film in theaters, "The Marvels," was also winding down in its fourth weekend with a disastrous global tally of $197 million against the reported $300 million it cost to make and market the superhero film. In its second weekend, Ridley Scott's "Napoleon" earned an estimated $7.1 million from 3,500 locations; produced by Apple Original Films and distributed by Sony Pictures, the film starring Joaquin Phoenix had then made $45.7 million domestically against a $200 million budget.
