# CyberGhost, Best For Cross-Platform Accessibility

CyberGhost was named the best VPN for cross-platform accessibility. Specifications: 7 simultaneous connections across 7 devices; Windows, Mac, Android, iOS and Linux; Chrome and Firefox browser extensions; dedicated IP addresses available with an additional purchase; 2 free months and a 45-day money-back guarantee; $2.11/month at CyberGhost.

Cross-platform support is not always necessary, because several of the best VPN services connect to routers — connecting a VPN to your home router lets you cover unlimited devices in your household, as long as they all access the internet through that router. But if you use VPNs on the go and across several devices, being able to connect to a wide range of platforms is indispensable.

CyberGhost offers simultaneous connectivity on up to seven devices for $2.11 to $12.99 per month depending on subscription term, and supports several types of gadgets like routers, computers, smart TVs and more. It is similar to the support ExpressVPN offers, but CyberGhost provides detailed instructions on how to set up the cross-platform connections, making it a bit more user-friendly for those purposes. It also just expanded its network of servers from 91 to 100 countries, adding Bolivia, Ecuador, Peru, Uruguay, Laos, Myanmar, Nepal, Guatemala and the Dominican Republic.

On security, CyberGhost completed an independent security audit by Deloitte earlier this year, runs a vulnerability disclosure program and provides access to a transparency report explaining requests for its data, with an updated version going live in August. It passed all of the tests, though the reviewers had trouble connecting to servers in the United Kingdom and had to run the gaming test through an Ireland-based server instead.
