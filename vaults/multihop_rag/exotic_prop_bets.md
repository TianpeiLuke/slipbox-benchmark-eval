# Exotic Prop Bets

An exotic prop bet is a non-traditional prop that is not focused on scenarios during a game; instead it can be as out of the ordinary as the sportsbook wants. What distinguishes it from an ordinary prop is precisely that detachment from in-game events.

In the US the most exotic bets appear during the Super Bowl, the most prominent sports betting day of the year, which offers hundreds of props. These can range from the number of songs sung during the halftime show, to the result of the coin toss, to the color of the sports drink dumped on the winning coach at the end of the game.

The availability of such bets is limited. For the most part you will not come across many exotic props at US sportsbooks, as many are simply not legal bets; it is at the largest sporting events, such as the Super Bowl or March Madness, that sportsbooks offer a wider range of props and more exotic ones appear.
