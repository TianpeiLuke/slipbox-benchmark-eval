# AliveCor's EKG Import Ban And The PTAB Patentability Ruling

The Masimo case is not the only import ban Apple faces: in February the Biden administration also declined to veto another ITC import ban and cease-and-desist order stemming from a dispute with AliveCor over the Apple Watch's EKG features — but that ban has had no impact, because the Patent Trial and Appeal Board ruled that AliveCor's EKG tech isn't actually patentable.

Like Masimo, AliveCor argued that Apple stole its tech to develop the wearable EKG feature and thereby infringed its patents. AliveCor would have to win its appeal of the PTAB ruling before any Apple Watches with EKG features would be pulled from the market for that reason. The Verge cautioned against reading one case as a guide to the other: "Every case is different, every patent is different," said Ben Levi. "Different complainants who bring up a case have got their own businesses, their own products, and those implicate a whole different set of facts."
