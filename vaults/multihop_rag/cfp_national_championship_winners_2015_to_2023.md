# CFP National Championship Winners, 2015 To 2023

The College Football Playoff National Championship has been taking place since 2015, and its winners through the 2022-23 season were Georgia twice, Alabama three times, Clemson twice, LSU once and Ohio State once.

The full list of previous winners was: 2022-23 season, Georgia Bulldogs; 2021-22 season, Georgia Bulldogs; 2020-21 season, Alabama Crimson Tide; 2019-20 season, LSU Tigers; 2018-19 season, Clemson Tigers; 2017-18 season, Alabama Crimson Tide; 2016-17 season, Clemson Tigers; 2015-16 season, Alabama Crimson Tide; and 2014-15 season, Ohio State Buckeyes.
