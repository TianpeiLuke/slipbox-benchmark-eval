# Chicago's Third-Period Comeback Against Pittsburgh

The Blackhawks scored three unanswered goals in the third period of the Oct. 10, 2023 opening-night game in Pittsburgh, turning a 2-1 deficit into a 4-2 win.

The period began at 10:14 p.m. with the Penguins holding a one-goal lead. Chicago went to its third power play at 10:17 p.m. when Matt Nieto was called for hooking 2:28 in; Corey Perry had two point-blank chances in front at 10:19 p.m. but put the puck into Tristan Jarry's midsection both times. Pittsburgh killed the Nieto penalty at 10:20 p.m. only for Kris Letang to take a cross-checking minor seconds after returning to even strength, and by 10:24 p.m. Chicago had failed to convert again, dropping to 0-for-4 on the power play with Pittsburgh ahead 2-1 and 13:43 left.

The equalizer came at 10:31 p.m.: Cole Guttman found a soft spot in the Penguins defense in the slot, took a pass from Jones and beat Jarry by the glove to make it 2-2 with 9:55 left in regulation. Petr Mrazek then kept it level at 10:36 p.m. with another sensational save on Jake Guentzel, pushing over to snag a one-timer set up by an excellent Erik Karlsson backhand pass to the dot — the blog called him the best player on either side that night. At 10:40 p.m. Chicago took its first lead through Jason Dickinson: Nick Foligno dropped a pass on a zone entry to Perry, whose shot attempt deflected to the middle for Dickinson to snap home, leaving Pittsburgh — who struggled with third-period leads the previous year — down one with 4:33 to go.

Mike Sullivan pulled Jarry for the extra attacker with over two minutes left at 10:47 p.m., and at 10:48 p.m. Perry found Foligno for the empty-net goal that iced the game with 1:27 to go, with Jarry going back to the bench again for the extra attacker. At 10:50 p.m. the Sporting News summed it up: the Blackhawks scored four unanswered goals to come from behind on opening night, Bedard was as advertised in picking up his first NHL point, and the third-period struggles carried over from last season for the Penguins.
