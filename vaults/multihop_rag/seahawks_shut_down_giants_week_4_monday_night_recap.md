# Seahawks Sack Daniel Jones 11 Times In A 24-3 Monday Night Win

Seattle beat the New York Giants 24-3 on "Monday Night Football" on Monday, Oct. 2, 2023 at MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, New Jersey, closing Week 4 with a dominant defensive performance. In addition to sacking Daniel Jones 11 times, the Seahawks defense forced New York's quarterback into three turnovers — a fumble and two interceptions — the most notable being rookie Devon Witherspoon's 97-yard pick-six that turned the game on its ear.

The quarter-by-quarter line was Seahawks 7-7-7-3 for 24 and Giants 0-3-0-0 for 3. The team statistics showed Seattle with 13 first downs to New York's 17, third downs of 3-for-12 versus 6-for-16, fourth downs of 0-for-2 versus 2-for-4, total yards of 281 to 248, passing of 15-of-26 for 160 yards versus 27-of-34 for 136 yards, rushing of 23 attempts for 121 yards versus 29 for 112, penalties of 8-74 versus 6-45, zero turnovers versus three, and time of possession of 24:00 versus 36:00.

Kickoff was 8:15 p.m. ET / 5:15 p.m. PT, the customary "MNF" kickoff time that year. The win moved the Seahawks to 3-1 with a perfect 2-0 record away from home, while the Giants dropped to 1-3 and had been outscored 64-3 in two primetime home losses.
