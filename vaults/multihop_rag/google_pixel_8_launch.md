# Google Pixel 8 Launch

Google unveiled the Pixel 8 and Pixel 8 Pro at its annual hardware event in the week covered by TechCrunch's October 7, 2023 Week in Review, and TechCrunch's hands-on verdict — reaffirmed a week later — was that the phone's cameras were its standout upgrade.

The event was Google's annual hardware showcase, and Christine's roundup of the news covered updates to the Pixel 8 and Pixel 8 Pro, Pixel Fold, Android 14, Pixel Buds, Google Assistant, Bard, Pixel Watch 2 and other goodies. Darrell took the newly unveiled Pixel 8 Pro for a whirl and liked what he saw: while very similar to last year's Pixel 7 Pro, its improved cameras, brighter screen and enhanced AI-powered features made it enough of an upgrade to potentially warrant a purchase — with the underutilized temperature sensor the exception. A full review was still to come at that point.

By the October 13, 2023 issue, TechCrunch had taken a deeper look and found the Pixel 8 delivered solid performance and design upgrades, with the camera drawing a particularly enthusiastic reception: Darrell declared the Pixel 8 Pro camera the new mobile photography champ, and Brian waxed lyrical about the phone's picture-snapper in "The camera's still the thing."
