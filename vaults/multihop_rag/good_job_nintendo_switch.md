# Good Job! (Nintendo Switch)

Good Job! casts you as the new CEO of a large corporation whose wisdom is required to help employees stay on task. It appears in Polygon's 2023-10-20 list of the 35 best couch co-op games for Nintendo Switch.

Polygon says the game has a lot in common with Untitled Goose Game.

In this isometric game you are given a list of tasks — like bringing a projector into the conference room — but, as Polygon notes, no one said you could not do it by launching the projector like a projectile through the window.
