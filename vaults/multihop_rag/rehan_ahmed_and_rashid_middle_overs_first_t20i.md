# Rehan Ahmed And Adil Rashid Through The Middle Overs

England's spinners were taken apart and then struck back through the middle of West Indies' chase in the first T20I, with Rehan Ahmed conceding nineteen in his first over before removing Nicholas Pooran.

In the seventh over Rehan bowled to Kyle Mayers, whose first ball was dragged down and launched over the midwicket boundary for six, followed by a supreme lofted drive down the ground for six more; Rehan came back with two sharply turning leg breaks before Shai Hope smeared a length ball over mid-on for the third six of the over, leaving West Indies 78-1. In the ninth over Tymal Mills replaced Rehan and started with another wide before a series of slower balls and back-of-the-hand trickery restricted Pooran and Hope, with the final ball top-edged over Buttler's head for a one-bounce four — 87-2, and West Indies needing 85 from 66 balls. In the tenth Adil Rashid was given another over after an excellent first: Pooran spotted the googly and drove it down the ground for six, singles were exchanged, and a short ball was panned into the deep for a couple, leaving West Indies 99-2 and needing 73 from 60. In the eleventh Rehan returned and had Pooran caught and bowled for 13, taking a nifty tumbling catch to his left at 100-3; Shimron Hetmyer opened his account with a single and Rehan got out of the over with three dots. In the twelfth just a single and a wicket came from the over as Hope could not break the legspin shackles, with West Indies captain Rovman Powell the new man and Buttler cranking the pressure by inserting a slip.
