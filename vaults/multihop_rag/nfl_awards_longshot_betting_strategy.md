# Why Bettors Should Consider NFL Award Longshots — And Bet Them Small

Sporting News argued on Dec. 5, 2023 that with five weeks left in the regular season there was plenty of time for an NFL award longshot to become a contender, so bettors should consider them — but should keep those bets small.

The grounds for taking longshots seriously are that they are easy to overlook because each award has a favorite or favorites: a longshot may be playing well, but other players have commanded the spotlight, making it easier for them to go unnoticed, and whatever the reason, they have not gotten the credit they deserve. The timing argument follows: if you wait too long before placing a bet, your payout will be a lot less than if you were to roll the dice on a longshot right now.

The counterweight, and the warrant for the sizing advice, is that there is always a reason why a player or several players have relatively short odds — they have been the best at their position throughout the season — and consequently there is just as good a reason why longshots have odds as long as they do. So the recommendation is to keep your bets small, or use a bonus bet if one is available, on longshots; if they do go on to win, you can smile all the way to the bank while telling everyone, "I told you so."
