# Alabama's Preferred Championship Weekend Scenarios

Sporting News argued on Dec. 1, 2023 that Alabama had options with a victory against Georgia, but that a specific set of results in the other four championship games would be the preferred scenario for the Crimson Tide.

The framing premise is the size of the field: the Crimson Tide were one of eight Power 5 teams with one loss or less heading into conference championship weekend, and no two-loss team had ever made the four-team College Football Playoff. Each preferred result therefore works by pushing a rival into the two-loss pile or freeing a spot.

Washington beating Oregon in the Pac-12 championship: Alabama got this piece in their favor, since Washington beat Oregon on Friday, which knocked the Ducks into the two-loss pileup and out of the CFP picture. Oklahoma State beating Texas in the Big 12 championship: this would make life easier in a lot of ways for Alabama, because Texas had a 34-24 head-to-head non-conference victory against the Crimson Tide — the Longhorns would have two losses at that point, so there would be nothing to discuss. Iowa beating Michigan in the Big Ten championship: while this was the most unlikely result, it could knock the Big Ten out of the CFP; Alabama was ranked behind No. 6 Ohio State, a one-loss team, but the Crimson Tide would leap-frog them with a victory against Georgia, and the committee would also take an SEC champion over a one-loss Michigan team. Louisville beating Florida State in the ACC championship: every one-loss team was rooting for the Cardinals to knock off the Seminoles, which would free up one of the four spots in the CFP.
