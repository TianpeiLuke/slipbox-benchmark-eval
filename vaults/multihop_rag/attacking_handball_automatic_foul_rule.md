# The Attacking Handball Automatic Foul Rule

By rule, any contact by the ball with the arm or hand of an attacking player is an automatic foul, regardless of arm position, intent, or any other factor. Sporting News cited this on Nov. 6, 2023 as the reason a Chelsea goal at Tottenham was disallowed.

The rule's discriminating feature is that it removes the usual mitigating considerations — where the arm was, whether the player meant it, or anything else — for an attacking player. Once contact with the arm or hand is established, the foul follows and the goal is ruled out.
