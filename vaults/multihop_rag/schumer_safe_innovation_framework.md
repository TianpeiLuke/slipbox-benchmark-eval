# Schumer's SAFE Innovation Framework

The SAFE Innovation Framework is Senator Schumer's AI policy program, debuted by his office in June, and the AI Insight Forums are part of it — the forums are the framework's convening mechanism rather than free-standing events.

The framework's defining premise is one of geopolitical standard-setting. As its program announcement reads: "The US must lead in innovation and write the rules of the road on AI and not let adversaries like the Chinese Communist Party craft the standards for a technology set to become as transformative as electricity."
