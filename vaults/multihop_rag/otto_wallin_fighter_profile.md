# Otto Wallin Fighter Profile

Otto Wallin, as characterized by CBSSports.com on Dec. 23, 2023, is a 33-year-old native of Sweden considered much more a live dog than Joseph Parker — that is, a genuinely plausible upset threat rather than a safe opponent.

His discriminating features are size and stance: standing only half an inch shorter than Joshua's 6-foot-6 frame, Wallin is a southpaw. His reputation was extremely bolstered in 2019, when he lost a title shot against Tyson Fury despite opening up a cut that required 47 stitches above Fury's eye and coming close to giving Wallin a stoppage win.
