# The OPEC Letter Pressuring Members Against Phase-Out Language

There were hopeful signs the week before COP28's close, when draft documents included options to incorporate language calling on countries to phase out fossil fuel use — alongside an option not to include such a clause — until the Organization of the Petroleum Exporting Countries sent a letter to its member states pressuring them to "proactively reject any text or formula that targets energy i.e. fossil fuels rather than emissions."

Following that letter, language that had previously called on countries to phase out fossil fuels vanished from subsequent draft texts. Backlash was swift: "This obsequious draft reads as if OPEC dictated it word for word," former US vice president Al Gore posted on X on the Monday. "COP28 is now on the verge of complete failure."
