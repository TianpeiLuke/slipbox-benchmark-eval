# Downward Revision of the Solar Corona's Size

A study published in November 2023 revealed that the outer edge of the sun's atmosphere, or corona, does not extend out quite as far as previously thought, making the sun likely somewhere between 0.03% and 0.07% smaller than had been assumed.

Until recently, the best way to measure the sun's corona was to observe it during a solar eclipse, when it becomes clearly visible around the moon. New technologies have allowed scientists to measure the oscillations, or waves, that travel through the corona, and those waves do not travel quite as far as expected. The difference between the new findings and previous estimates is relatively small, but scientists say it could make a big difference in how the star is studied.
