# UTSA's Transfer Portal Absences For The Frisco Bowl

UTSA played the Frisco Bowl without its best defensive player, EDGE Trey Moore, who was looking to jump to a Power 5 school; Moore had led the conference in sacks, so he was a star-level absence for the unit, per Sporting News on Dec. 18, 2023.

Four UTSA players did not play in the Frisco Bowl, all of them transfer portal entrants: Trey Moore, EDGE; Syrus Dumas, DB; Xavier Spencer, CB; and Kyle Eaves, WR.
