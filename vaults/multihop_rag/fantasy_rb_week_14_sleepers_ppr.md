# Week 14 PPR Running Back Sleepers

Sporting News welcomed Tyjae Spears (@ Dolphins) into its Week 14 PPR top 25 and built a sleeper group around backs whose touches would rise through injuries ahead of them, publishing on Dec. 7, 2023.

Spears had averaged 5.1 yards per carry entering the previous weekend and stepped up in a major way for Tennessee when Derrick Henry sustained a game-ending hit to the head area against Indy, contributing 20 yards on the game-tying drive in the fourth quarter and another 27 yards in overtime, though Mike Vrabel's squad ultimately lost. In what should be a high-scoring game against the Super Bowl-hopeful Dolphins, Sporting News liked Spears to be busy as rookie quarterback Will Levis's top check-down option — with the explicit condition that Henry (head/neck) be out.

Ezekiel Elliott (@ Steelers) was another potential fill-in who should produce at least flex-worthy stats, since with Rhamondre Stevenson (ankle) on the shelf Elliott could see 20 touches. Similarly, Kenneth Gainwell (@ Cowboys) and Roschon Johnson (vs. Lions) could see big upticks in touches if D'Andre Swift and D'Onta Foreman could not suit up, but Sporting News was not as confident those backs could produce given their matchups. Najee Harris (vs. Patriots) could confidently be taken out of the doghouse for good — Sporting News still liked Jaylen Warren better overall — because Harris had been running with purpose and his role in the passing game continued to make him a high-ceiling RB2 in a run-first offense that would be without Kenny Pickett (ankle) that week; New England might be banged up but was still a tough defense to get chunk yardage against through the air, so Harris should see lots of screens and dump-downs in a rough-and-tumble Thursday night game in Pittsburgh. Finally, Baltimore's Keaton Mitchell (vs. Rams) had the potential to smash Los Angeles at home: the speedster had quietly racked up over 300 yards and two touchdowns over the Ravens' past four games, and with Lamar Jackson facing Aaron Donald and a still-talented pass rush, John Harbaugh's squad might turn to the rookie to move the chains between the 20s — all it would take was one or two big chunk gains.
