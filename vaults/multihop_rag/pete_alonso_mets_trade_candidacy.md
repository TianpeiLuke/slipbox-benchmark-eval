# Pete Alonso Mets Trade Candidacy

Pete Alonso was ranked No. 25 on Yardbarker's Nov. 13, 2023 list of the top 25 MLB offseason trade candidates: he had been the subject of early offseason trade speculation, and with the Mets projecting a less active offseason and placing a priority on 2025, it was not out of the question he could be available. Alonso was projected for a $22M arbitration salary and would be a free agent after the following year.

New York had subsequently walked back some of its statements about a possible retool and was still aiming to compete in 2024, even while signaling it would not blow away the top of the free agent market. First-year president of baseball operations David Stearns had twice publicly said he anticipated Alonso being the Opening Day first baseman in Queens. That would not stop teams from trying, but an offseason deal looked decidedly unlikely.
