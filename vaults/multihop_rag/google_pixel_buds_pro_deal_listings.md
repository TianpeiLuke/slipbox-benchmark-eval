# Google Pixel Buds Pro Deal Listings

The Google Pixel Buds Pro appear in The Verge's December roundup at around $119.99 ($80 off) at Amazon, Walmart, and Best Buy, and in WIRED's Prime Day roundup at a price matching historic lows that don't come around very often.

The Verge calls them another good pair of noise-canceling earbuds, particularly for Pixel phone owners, with terrific sound quality and battery life plus useful features like multipoint Bluetooth connectivity; Pixel phone owners also get exclusive features like head-tracking spatial audio and easy access to the settings menu. WIRED (9/10, WIRED Recommends) calls them truly excellent earbuds, particularly for Android phone owners, comfortable and available in a wide array of colors at this price.
