# Be'eri Kibbutz Casualties And Displaced Residents

The kibbutz of Be'eri lost "112 murdered and so many, many more kidnapped" out of a population of about 1,200, and its surviving residents are now refugees living in hotels across Israel, Thomas Hand told Fox News Digital in an interview published on 17 November 2023.

Hand himself has been homeless since Be'eri was savaged: "I'm a refugee. I have no home to go back to." Of the community he said: "Your whole community was devastated. Yeah, we're all refugees. We're all living in various hotels all over the country."

Yehudit Weiss was one of the residents of Be'eri taken hostage on 7 October; Israeli Defense Forces reported on the Thursday before publication that they found her body in a building adjacent to the Al-Shifa Hospital in Gaza, which Israel says Hamas had been using as a headquarters.
