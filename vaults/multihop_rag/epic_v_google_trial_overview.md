# Epic v. Google: What The Trial Is

Epic v. Google is the antitrust case in which Fortnite publisher Epic Games drags a tech giant into court alleging that its mobile app store is an illegal monopoly — and although Epic's parallel claims against Apple already got their day in court, the similar lawsuit against Google never did, which is why the case goes to trial on November 6th, a mere 1,180 days after Epic originally sued.

The déjà vu is the point of confusion the piece sets out to clear up: Epic already went to trial with Apple, resulting in a ruling where Apple mostly won, and its Llamacorn legal gambit failed to #FreeFortnite years ago. The distinction is that the Apple claims were heard and the Google ones were not.

An update dated December 15th, 2023 notes that the case is basically over by then.
