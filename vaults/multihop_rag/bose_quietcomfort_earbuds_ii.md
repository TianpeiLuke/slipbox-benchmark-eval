# Bose QuietComfort Earbuds II Deal Prices

The Bose QuietComfort Earbuds II were $199 for October Prime Day 2023 — an all-time low, but Prime-members-only — and returned to $199 for Black Friday with the Prime requirement dropped, Engadget reported across three roundups.

Engadget's 10 October 2023 Prime Day list called $199 the best price ever seen, available only to Prime members, and said they are currently the best wireless earbuds you can get if you want as much noise cancellation as possible, with a smaller, more comfortable fit than the previous generation and good audio quality. Its headphone-specific list the same day quoted "$199 $279 Save $80", calling it an all-time low and roughly $75 off their average street price, and adding context: the pair has technically been replaced by Bose's new QuietComfort Ultra Earbuds, which add a spatial audio mode, but that model was not discounted at the time and the two are largely similar otherwise; the QC II's chief selling point is ANC performance still among the most powerful on the market, so saving $99 to buy the older pair may still make sense. The caveats: no multi-device pairing, no wireless charging, and bulky earpieces. Engadget gave the QC IIs a review score of 87 the previous year.

By 19 November 2023 Engadget reported them down to $199 again, "the same as they went for for October Prime Day", but noted that "during that sale you needed to be a Prime member to save, but now anyone can take advantage of the discount". They retail for $299 but had sold for $279 for the past few months, so this was still an $80 discount.
