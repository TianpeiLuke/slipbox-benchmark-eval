# Week 13 Saints-Lions: Detroit's Pass Defense Is The Exploitable Weakness

CBS Sports argues that the Lions' pass defense is the decisive Fantasy variable in the Week 13 Saints-Lions game, while injuries on both sides push the matchup toward the run for Detroit and toward Alvin Kamara for New Orleans. The grounds: since their Week 9 bye the Lions have been mostly great against the run — 3.1 yards per carry allowed, with just one running back reaching 15-plus PPR points — but they rank bottom-six in completion rate allowed (68.4%), yards per attempt allowed (8.0), passing ADOT against (8.84 yards) and defensive Expected Points Added per dropback (-0.40, league-worst). Of the second-most eight passing touchdowns they have given up in three games, five came on passes of 15-plus Air Yards, tied for most in the league.

The article then attaches scope conditions from the injury report. Detroit should catch a break because field-stretcher Rashid Shaheed (thigh) is missing the matchup for New Orleans, while Chris Olave looks like he will play. After the injuries to Shaheed and Olave last Sunday it was Alvin Kamara who predictably led the way in targets per routes run at 30.8%, and Kamara gets a boost in both the pass game and the run game with Lions linebacker Alex Anzalone missing action with a hand injury. For the Lions offense, the run game is expected to get a bit more focus with Saints linebacker Pete Werner sidelined and All-Pro defensive end Cameron Jordan questionable to play. The verdicts: STARTS — Jared Goff, Amon-Ra St. Brown, Jahmyr Gibbs, Alvin Kamara, Chris Olave (conditional on passing concussion protocol), David Montgomery, Sam LaPorta and Taysom Hill; SITS — Derek Carr and both defenses; SLEEPERS — Juwan Johnson.
