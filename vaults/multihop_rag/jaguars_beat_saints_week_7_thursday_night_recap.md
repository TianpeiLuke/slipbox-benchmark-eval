# Jaguars Survive Late Saints Rally To Win 31-24 On Thursday Night Football

Jacksonville beat New Orleans 31-24 in the Week 7 "Thursday Night Football" game on Thursday, Oct. 19, 2023, surviving a late scare at the Caesars Superdome. Doug Pederson's crew improved to 5-2 and extended its winning streak to four games, while the loss dropped the Saints to 3-4.

The Jaguars controlled most of the game, building an early first-half lead and entering the fourth quarter up 24-9, at which point the Saints offense was completely out of sync — Derek Carr had just thrown a pick-six to Foye Oluokun and was struggling to connect with any key weapon other than running back Alvin Kamara. But Trevor Lawrence and the Jaguars failed to put the game away, and New Orleans scored two fourth-quarter touchdowns to tie the game with less than six minutes to go, with the momentum fully shifted to the home side. Christian Kirk then broke loose for a 44-yard touchdown after an exchange of punts, retaking the lead for Jacksonville with 3:08 remaining. New Orleans nearly tied it in the final seconds: tight end Foster Moreau had a touchdown in his fingertips on third-and-goal but could not hold the catch, and a fourth-down pass to Chris Olave was broken up. The quarter-by-quarter line was Jaguars 7-10-7-7 for 31 and Saints 3-3-3-15 for 24.

Kickoff was 8:15 p.m. ET (7:15 p.m. CT), the normal start time for all of Amazon's "TNF" games in 2023; because the game was in New Orleans, in the Central time zone, local viewers saw it at 7:15 p.m. Historically the Saints had the Jaguars' number: in seven meetings between the franchises New Orleans held a 5-2 record and a four-game winning streak, and the previous meeting, in 2019, was a 13-9 Saints win in a defensive battle.
