# Apple Watch Series 9, SE And Ultra 2 Holiday 2023 Deals

WIRED's two 2023 holiday guides — the Walmart Cyber Monday roundup of 27 November and the Apple Black Friday guide of 25 November — covered three discounted Apple Watches: the Series 9, the second-generation SE and the second-generation Ultra, with an explicit recommendation against upgrading from a recent Series 8 or 7.

On the Series 9 (7/10, WIRED Recommends), the Walmart guide says it is not enough of a leap forward to justify replacing an Apple Watch Series 8 or even a Series 7, but that buyers entering the Apple Watch ecosystem for the first time or replacing an even-older smartwatch will find a host of spiffy new features such as daylight tracking and haptic gesture control. The Apple guide describes the same watch as the latest Apple Watch, offering faster performance, improved battery life and a brighter display to upgraders, plus a new Double Tap feature that triggers functions like playing music or hanging up a call when you tap your index finger and thumb, and a second-generation ultra-wideband chip that lets you precision-locate your iPhone and AirPods.

For buyers who do not need the Series 9's in-depth capabilities, WIRED calls the second-generation Apple Watch SE a great alternative: it still tracks crucial health and fitness features, and its S8 chip brings Crash Detection and the redesigned Compass app, with WatchOS 10 support for the latest software updates. The second-generation Apple Watch Ultra (8/10, WIRED Recommends) packs a brighter 3,000-nit display, the latest S9 chip and the new ultra-wideband chip for precision-locating your phone, while retaining the useful Action button, the three-mic array and precise GPS; WIRED says its gigantic size makes it the Apple Watch with the best battery life.
