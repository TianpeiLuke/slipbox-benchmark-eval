# Teenage Engineering's Post-Release Firmware Update Record

As Engadget wrote its closing thoughts on the K.O. II, the second official firmware update (v1.1.1) had just been released — nothing spicy like motion control or sampling the radio, but confirmation that Teenage Engineering adds features after a product hits the shelves.

The reviewer cited two precedents: the OP-1 Field's vocoder synth, which landed over six months after release, and the fairly substantial 1.2.38 update for the OP-Z, which came almost three years into that device's life. The same passage discusses the "magic" factor Teenage Engineering sometimes hides in its products — small, cute and often slightly hidden features that aren't necessary but are tons of fun, such as the inclusion of FM radio on the OP1/Field and OB-4, or the video making tool in the OP-Z's app. There was a brief moment of excitement when the reviewer spotted "loop mode from OB-4" on the K.O. II's product page, hoping the two devices would interact somehow, but it appears that is just a way of describing the looping feature borrowed from the OB-4.
