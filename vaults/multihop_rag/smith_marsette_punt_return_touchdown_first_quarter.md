# Smith-Marsette's Punt Return Touchdown In The First Quarter

Ihmir Smith-Marsette returned a punt 79 yards for a touchdown at 8:36 p.m. on Nov. 9, 2023, giving Carolina a 7-0 lead over Chicago against his former team; on a night that could be defined by bad offense, the Panthers got a huge early boost from their special teams unit. The quarter ended Panthers 7, Bears 3.

The return came after Bagent threw incomplete on third down at 8:35 p.m., sending the Bears out to punt for the second time that night. Chicago's opening possessions had been cramped: the Bears started with the ball at 8:17 p.m., Donte Jackson broke up a third-down pass at 8:21 p.m. to force a punt, and Johnny Hekker pinned the Bears at the one at 8:29 p.m. Darnell Mooney had picked up a first down at 8:33 p.m. after a nice D'Onta Foreman run set up third-and-2.

Chicago answered with a long field goal. At 8:40 p.m. the Bears rattled off two first downs on their first three plays, with Moore and Foreman catching passes from Bagent to take them over midfield; at 8:41 p.m. Foreman ripped off an 11-yard run to reach 43 scrimmage yards in the quarter, making him the Bears' best weapon of the night. The drive fizzled at 8:43 p.m. into a 49-yard field goal try, a false start by Cody Whitehair erased what would have been a good 49-yarder at 8:44 p.m., and at 8:45 p.m. Cairo Santos drilled a 54-yard kick anyway to cut the deficit to 7-3. Carolina's early offense ran through Bryce Young's legs: at 8:23 p.m. he ran for a first down on third-and-7 with five extra yards added on an illegal contact penalty against Chicago, and at 8:25 p.m. he scrambled for a first down on a second straight third down; at 8:26 p.m. he missed a wide open Terrace Marshall Jr. by sailing the pass too high, and at 8:28 p.m. he was forced to throw it away, sending the Panthers to punt from just past midfield. At 8:50 p.m. Young hit Mike Strachan for a 44-yard gain — Strachan's first catch as a Panther, described in the accompanying clip as 45 yards — to put Carolina in business in Bears territory. The game was carried on Prime Video.
