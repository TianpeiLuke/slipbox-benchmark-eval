# RB Leipzig 3-2 Borussia Dortmund

RB Leipzig beat 10-man Borussia Dortmund 3-2 on Dec. 9, 2023 to tighten their grip on fourth place and put a further dent in their opponents' title hopes.

It started badly for the hosts, with Mats Hummels sent off for a last-man foul at the edge of the box in the 15th minute, and a Ramy Bensebaini own goal put the visitors in front just after the half-hour. Dortmund equalised with the last kick of the first half when Niklas Süle volleyed in from the far post, but it was only a matter of time until Leipzig scored again, and they did so through Christoph Baumgartner in the 54th minute. With Dortmund taking risks in search of an equaliser, Leipzig launched a counterattack and Yussuf Poulsen rifled in, before Niclas Füllkrug scored deep in stoppage time for some last-gasp drama. Leipzig were fourth on 29 points with a four-point lead over Dortmund in fifth.
