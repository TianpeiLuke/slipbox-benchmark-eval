# Lego Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga

Polygon says the Lego games are always a great laugh and have reliable couch co-op modes — Lego Marvel Super Heroes 2 being another example — and that The Skywalker Saga is no different. It appears in the outlet's 2023-10-20 list of the 35 best couch co-op games for Nintendo Switch.

They are accessible, family-friendly, and give a silly spin to some of the most recognizable films of our time.

The Skywalker Saga's scale is the draw: Lego versions of a whopping nine movies from the behemoth franchise, and 200-plus minifigs to unlock.
