# Overlord (2018 Nazi Zombie Film)

Overlord is a slept-on genre-mashing gem that places a cast of excellent actors about to do bigger things — Jovan Adepo, Wyatt Russell, John Magaro, Pilou Asbæk, and more — and up-and-coming director Julius Avery, of The Pope's Exorcist, behind Nazi lines during World War II, where the Nazis are up to more than usual: there be zombies in these labs. It was the Oct. 24 pick in Polygon's 2023-10-10 Halloween Countdown, listed on Paramount Plus and Pluto TV.

On the eve of D-Day, a group of Allied paratroopers are sent into Germany on a covert mission. Things go wrong instantly when their plane crashes, and they soon discover twisted Nazi experiments after connecting with the residents. Things get bloody fast, with great action from second unit director Shaun O'Dell, of The Woman King and Brotherhood of the Wolf, and stunt coordinator Jo McLaren of Heart of Stone.

Polygon's Pete Volk calls it a gleeful, intentionally ridiculous Nazi-zombie-bashing time with tight direction and a fantastic group of actors, recommended for anyone who loves the Wolfenstein games, Call of Duty's Zombies mode, or B-movies with A-movie production.
