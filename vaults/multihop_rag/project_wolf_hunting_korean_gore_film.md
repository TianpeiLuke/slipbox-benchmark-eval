# Project Wolf Hunting (Korean Gore Film)

Project Wolf Hunting is a Korean monster mash built out of an extremely fun idea: what if a bunch of dangerous criminals rebelled on the cargo ship they were being transported on, only to discover the ship is also transporting a monster? Polygon summarizes the premise as essentially "Con Air meets Predator, with a bit of The Last Voyage of the Demeter." It was the Oct. 16 pick in the 2023-10-10 Halloween Countdown, on Hi-Yah! and available to rent on Amazon, Apple, and Vudu.

Critic Pete Volk concedes the story is necessarily light and the characters thinly drawn, although the effective costuming and makeup work go a long way — but that is not what viewers are there for.

What they are there for is gory violence, and there is a lot of it: the director has bragged that the team used 2.5 tons of fake blood while filming, and it shows. Volk calls it one of the goriest movies he has ever seen, with the gore augmented by terrific sound design that builds the monster into something well beyond the realm of humankind by making its movements sound metallic — a machine designed for killing, in a movie designed for a bloody good time.
