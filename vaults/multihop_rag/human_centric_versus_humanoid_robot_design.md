# Human-Centric Versus Humanoid Robot Design

Agility Robotics argues that the goal should be capability in human environments, not resemblance to humans. "The intention is not to start from the beginning and say, 'Hey, we're trying to make a robot look like a person,'" said Jonathan Hurst, co-founder and chief robot officer at Agility Robotics. "We're trying to make robots that can operate in human spaces."

Amazon's chief technologist for robotics, Tye Brady, endorsed the same ordering of priorities after the company showed Digit off at a media event in Seattle: "The mobility aspect is more interesting than the actual form."
