# Destiny Udogie's Tackle Escapes A Red Card

Tottenham were lucky to remain with 11 players in the 18th minute against Chelsea on Nov. 6, 2023, as Destiny Udogie went in with a horrible two-footed tackle on Raheem Sterling out wide and escaped with only a yellow card.

Udogie saw yellow initially from Michael Oliver, and there was a subsequent VAR check to review for a possible red card — for good reason, since the tackle was extremely dangerous and he lunged in with a leg-breaker. He escaped without further punishment for one reason: he missed. Just like Kai Havertz of Arsenal earlier that weekend, who also remained on the pitch against Newcastle for a similar incident, Udogie's tackle did not reach the necessary threshold for a straight red because he did not make full contact with Sterling.

NBC commentator Jon Champion, who has real-time access to the VAR communication, said the review ultimately determined the tackle was an "upper-level yellow card" that did not quite meet the qualifications for a red.
