# The Amazon Prime Membership Requirement For Prime Day

Prime Big Deal Days is an event for Amazon Prime members, meaning most of its deals are for subscribers only: as WIRED puts it, if you want to take the ride you need to buy the ticket — in this case $15 a month, which also brings free two-day shipping and a whole bunch of other Prime perks.

The boundary cases matter as much as the rule. You can sign up for a 30-day Amazon Prime trial, registering before the event and cancelling right when the trial ends, to take advantage of the deals. And membership is not strictly necessary to shop the period: there are a lot of discounted products available to people who are not Prime subscribers, while retailers like Best Buy and Walmart price-match some items or run their own competing sales.
