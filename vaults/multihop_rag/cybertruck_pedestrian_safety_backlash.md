# The Cybertruck Pedestrian Safety Backlash

The Tesla Cybertruck had been out for a little more than a week when The Verge wrote on December 12, 2023, and people were already ready to declare it a safety nightmare.

"Guideless missile" and "death machine" were among the loaded phrases being tossed around. Safety experts were "raising concerns" about the truck's crumple zones — or lack thereof — and TikTok and other social platforms were abound with videos highlighting the poor sight lines and lack of visibility for drivers and passengers.
