# Nate Caddy AFL Draft Prospect 2023

Nate Caddy, profiled in Kevin Sheehan's top 40 for the 2023 AFL Draft as reported by The Roar on Nov. 15, 2023, is a pack marking forward with a vice-like grip overhead who has a real physical presence with his impact inside 50. State: Victoria Metro. State League Club: Northern Knights. Community Club: Yarrambat. Date of Birth: 14/07/05. Height: 193cm.

He has also shown he can provide a lift for his team with his ability to win the contested ball as a midfielder when the occasion arises. He had an outstanding year with the Northern Knights, averaging 16.3 disposals and 6.2 marks across nine matches to earn selection in the Coates Talent League Team of the Year, and kicked 39 goals across all matches in the talent pathway that year, including games for Victoria Metro and the Australia under-18 team. He tested extremely well at the national Draft Combine, with 3.02 seconds on the 20m sprint along with 6:35 for the 2km time trial indications of his athletic prowess. He is the nephew of two-time Richmond premiership star Josh Caddy. Weapon: Contested marking and goalkicking. AFL player comparison: Taylor Walker (Adelaide Crows).
