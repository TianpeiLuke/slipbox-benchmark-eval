# The CFP National Championship: Format And Schedule

The CFP National Championship is the final game of the NCAA Division I Football Bowl Subdivision season, crowning the country's best team, and is contested by the winners of the two CFP semifinal games, usually on a Monday in early January.

The game started in 2014 after replacing the Bowl Championship Series, and attracts millions of viewers — a popularity that also makes it one of the most-wagered-on sporting events in the country; sportsbooks post odds to win the national title that a bettor can click through to open an account and bet. The scheduled dates for the following years are Jan. 8 in Houston for 2023-24, Jan. 20 in Atlanta for 2024-25 and Jan. 19 in Miami for 2025-26 — a schedule visibly pushed later into January once the CFP field expands to 12 teams in 2024-25.
