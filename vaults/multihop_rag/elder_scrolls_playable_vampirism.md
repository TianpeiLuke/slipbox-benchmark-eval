# The Elder Scrolls: Vampires Everywhere

The Elder Scrolls games hide vampires beneath a conventional surface, and in keeping with the open-ended role-playing philosophy the series espouses, you can also become a vampire — which Polygon says kicks ass.

The fantasy of the Elder Scrolls games often presents as very straightforward: with the exception of Morrowind, each game starts out as a pretty up-the-middle fantasy setting with nary a twist of any kind. But a big reason the series has an enduring fan base is that beneath the surface there is always weird stuff to find, vampires included.
