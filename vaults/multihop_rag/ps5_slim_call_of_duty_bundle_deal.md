# PS5 Slim Call Of Duty Bundle Deal

Call of Duty: Modern Warfare III came free with a $499 PS5 Slim bundle for Black Friday 2023 — roughly $60 off the price of buying each separately, and available at multiple retailers.

Sony announced this new svelte version of its console the previous month and it had been popping up sporadically across the internet since then. Depending on the model chosen, it'll be up to 24 percent lighter than the standard PS5 and will take up about 30 percent less space overall.
