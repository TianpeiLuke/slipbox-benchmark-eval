# Samsung microSD Card Deals

For a new microSD card for a Nintendo Switch, Steam Deck or GoPro, a trio of Samsung microSD cards Engadget recommends fell to their lowest prices to date for Black Friday 2023: the 128GB version of the Samsung Pro Plus down to $11 at Amazon, B&H, Best Buy and others, with the 256GB and 512GB models down to $18 and $32 respectively; the 256GB Samsung Evo Select on sale for a new low of $15; and the 256GB Samsung Pro Ultimate available for $25.

The Pro Plus is the top overall pick in Engadget's microSD card buying guide, as it consistently ranked among the fastest cards tested despite its relatively affordable price tag. The Pro Ultimate offers faster read speeds, so it could be worth the step up for anyone who often moves files between card and computer, while the Evo Select is the "best value" pick: slower than the other two but cheaper, with losses that won't be hugely noticeable in devices that can't take advantage of the extra performance, such as the Switch. Each card comes with a 10-year warranty.
