# WIRED Black Friday 2023 MacBook Pro Deals

WIRED's Apple Black Friday guide of 25 November 2023 covered discounted MacBook Pros across three chip generations: the just-released M3 model, a nearly year-old M2 Pro configuration, and two M3 Pro machines.

The M3 MacBook Pro had been out for less than a month when it went on sale, and it replaces the 13-inch MacBook Pro that Apple no longer sells through its stores. It carries the same mini LED screen, 120-Hz refresh rate, brighter display and 1080p webcam as the high-end versions, plus HDMI, two USB-C ports with Thunderbolt 4, an SD card slot, a high-impedance headphone jack and a MagSafe charging port. WIRED recommends the M3 for anyone who wants some of the Pro features without dropping almost $2,000 to get them.

The M2 Pro MacBook Pro (9/10, WIRED Recommends) is less than a year old, and WIRED attaches an explicit price condition: it only recommends the machine if you can find it for under $1,800. It packs all the same features as the latest version, the M2 Pro is more than capable of handling graphically demanding and intensive tasks, and this is the lowest price WIRED has tracked for that specific configuration. The M3 Pro model on sale is similar to the other discounted machine but with a larger screen, a more powerful CPU and slightly better graphics. WIRED explains the tiering: Apple's brand-new M3 chips come in three MacBook tiers — M3, M3 Pro and M3 Max (9/10, WIRED Recommends) — all included in its Best Laptops guide, with the M3 Pro falling in the middle for processing power and speed while still being blazing fast; WIRED recommends opting for the Pro if you are a serious productivity user or content creator, and says this deal matches the best price it has seen so far.
