# Day Of Reckoning Predictions And Picks

CBSSports.com's Dec. 23, 2023 preview picked Wilder via KO8 and Joshua via UD12, arguing that even though both men had signed up for fights they could very well lose, things were more likely to go to plan for the big-money investors.

The grounds start with the card and the stakes: although the rest of the super card featured recognized names, including 2022 fighter of the year Dmitry Bivol, the majority of matchups were one-sided on paper; and given the amount of money at stake in the pending two-fight series between Joshua and Wilder, and the lack of finishing power from both Wallin and Parker, the favorites should prevail — those investors having spared no expense to make the event happen and to continue Saudi Arabia's plans to be a new global hub for big-time combat sports events. For Wilder specifically: even with the layoff, he has the power and conditioning to eventually catch Parker, even if the former champion is able to outbox him leading up to a possible late stoppage, and for as much as Wilder gets criticized for his technical disadvantages despite being a former U.S. Olympic bronze medalist, he has long shown the patience to make opponents pay for even the slightest mistake late in a bout. Joshua would likely have to work harder: he committed too much to being a boxer in both losses to Usyk, when playing the role of physical and mauling puncher could have been a more sound strategy, and was both passive and boring for the majority of his fight against Helenius before delivering a perfect knockout blow that reminded fans of his power and natural finishing skills. As long as Joshua commits to playing the role of the bigger man, he likely does enough to take a decision — the same result that would be difficult to see Wallin get the nod for winning unless he does so in dominant fashion, given the financial commitment the region has made to employing Joshua's services as a global star. While it may be a bit more of a dicey walk for Joshua than Wilder on this day, both bouts were expected to be competitive.
