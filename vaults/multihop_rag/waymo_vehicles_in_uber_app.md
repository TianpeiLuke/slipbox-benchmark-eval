# Waymo Vehicles In The Uber App

Waymo driverless vehicles are now available through the Uber app, starting with Phoenix, TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-30. The launch comes five months since the two companies announced a multi-year agreement for the autonomous vehicle service to be accessed via the Uber app.
