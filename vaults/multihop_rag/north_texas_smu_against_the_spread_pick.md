# The Against-The-Spread Pick In North Texas-SMU

Sporting News picked SMU against the spread on Nov. 7, 2023, on the ground that its own models projected a bigger margin than the market. The game opened at Mustangs -14 with an exorbitant total of 71.5 in Vegas and had shifted to 16 and 67.5 respectively 24 hours later; with two of the outlet's three models having SMU winning by 22 and 18 points, the spread grabbed its attention.

Three supporting reasons follow. At home the Mustangs average 0.3 giveaways per game, among the top seven teams in the country, while on the road North Texas coughs it up nearly twice a game, among the bottom third of Division I. SMU is also one of 14 programs that are the best-balanced squads in the country, passing and running between 49 and 51 percent of the time — the balance coaches pray for. And the SMU defense, top 10 in key categories, would keep quarterback Chandler Jones and the Mean Green at bay.
