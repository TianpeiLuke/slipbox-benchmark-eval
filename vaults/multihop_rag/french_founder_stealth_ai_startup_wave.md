# The French Founder Stealth AI Startup Wave

Beyond the companies already public, a wave of new French-founder AI startups was forming as of November 9, 2023. Earlier that week, The Information reported that Adaptive, an AI startup based in Amsterdam and co-founded by French AI researcher Julien Launay, was about to raise a seed round at a $100 million valuation; Adaptive helps companies iterate on their AI applications without relying on external data or annotations. A source confirmed to TechCrunch that Index Ventures is leading the Adaptive round, with Iconiq and Motier Ventures participating, and another source said Index Ventures is also leading a seed round for an AI startup with a team split between Paris and London.

Several are being built by founders of earlier consumer successes. Steeve Morin, a key member of the team behind Zenly, a popular consumer app acquired by Snap, is creating an AI startup called ZML, whose team will focus on improving machine learning inference and other low-level tools. Maxime Germain, the founder of mental health startup Jour, which was acquired by Alan, is leaving Alan to start Heart Hands, an AI startup developing a "second brain in your pocket," as an investor described it.

Pierre Valade, the founder of Sunrise and Jumbo, is putting together a team for an AI startup currently called Chief of Staff. And Hugo Mercier is working on an AI startup in stealth mode as well.
