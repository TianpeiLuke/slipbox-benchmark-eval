# Fossil Fuel Lobbyist Access At COP28 In Dubai

The UN's decision to hold the summit in the United Arab Emirates, a major oil and gas producer, wound up giving the fossil fuel industry unprecedented access: there were more fossil fuel lobbyists in Dubai than at any climate conference in the 28 years the United Nations has convened it.

Industry representatives for fossil fuels outnumbered the delegations from every country present at the talks except the United Arab Emirates and Brazil. According to an investigation by the BBC and the Centre for Climate Reporting, COP28 president Sultan Ahmed Al Jaber even used his position to lobby for oil and gas deals with other governments.
