# Advances in Deep-Sea Sensory Ecology

Sensory ecology has been an area of many interesting papers over the past five years, Jon Copley says: researchers are finding out a lot more about how deep sea animals interact with each other and their environment.

That work concerns how animals perceive their environment, how they respond to it, and how they avoid being seen by predators, and Copley says it was nice to bring some of those findings together in a couple of chapters of his new book.
