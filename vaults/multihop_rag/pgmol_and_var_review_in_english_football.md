# PGMOL: The Body That Apologises For Refereeing And VAR Mistakes

The Professional Game Match Officials Limited (PGMOL) is the body responsible for referees in English football, and it has apologised on multiple occasions for mistakes made during matches, Sporting News explained on 2023-09-30. The article's purpose is to keep an updated list of those incidents — specifically, all the apologies carried out by the PGMOL for wrong decisions later accepted to have been wrong.

The occasion for the update was that referees once again took the headlines on the opening matchday of the 2023/24 Premier League season, following an incorrect VAR decision in Manchester United's fixture against Wolves.
