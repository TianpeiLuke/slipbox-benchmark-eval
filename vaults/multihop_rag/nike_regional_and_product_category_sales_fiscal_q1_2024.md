# Nike Regional and Product Category Sales Fiscal Q1 2024

Nike saw sales jumps in every region besides North America, its largest market by revenue, in the fiscal first quarter ended 31 August 2023. North American sales fell 2% from the year-ago period to $5.42 billion, just above the $5.39 billion analysts had expected, according to StreetAccount. In Europe, the Middle East and Africa, sales were up 8% at $3.61 billion, against the $3.51 billion analysts expected. Sales in the Latin America and Asia Pacific unit came in 2% higher at $1.57 billion, just shy of the $1.59 billion expected.

The Converse brand fell well short of expectations for a second quarter in a row, with sales of $588 million, down 9% compared with the year-ago period, against analyst expectations of about $660 million according to StreetAccount.

By product category, footwear sales rose 4% to $8.4 billion, making up about 68% of Nike's total sales, while apparel was down 1% at $3.4 billion.
