# Anatomy of a Fall (Justine Triet)

Anatomy of a Fall is Justine Triet's Palme d'Or-winning procedural starring Sandra Hüller, Swann Arlaud, and Milo Machado-Graner, ranked 20 on Polygon's best movies of 2023 list of 2023-10-07 and playing in theaters at the time.

Polygon notes that the premise could set up a tacky 1990s courtroom drama or erotic thriller — a woman's husband falls from a balcony and dies while she was in the house, and the question is whether she did it — and says the film is not one of those movies, but it is not not one of those movies either. It is not a thriller, but it is thrilling.

Even though Triet stubbornly refuses to pick any of the obvious ways her film could resolve itself, it achieves a deep and lingering payoff anyway. Critic OW calls it a hypnotic well of a movie in which the truth of what happened only seems to recede the deeper you dive into it — a bold, almost sadistic choice — while the sleek presentation, sharp script, and riveting performance by Sandra Hüller, perhaps the year's best by any actor, keep you on the hook.
