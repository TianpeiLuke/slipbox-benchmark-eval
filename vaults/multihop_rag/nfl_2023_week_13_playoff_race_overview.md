# NFL Playoff Race Heading Out Of Week 13 Of The 2023 Season

The NFL playoff race was heating up as the calendar flipped to December, the penultimate month of the 2023 regular season, with only six weeks of games left through Week 18 and 14 teams looking to position themselves well as either division leaders or wild-card hopefuls, per Sporting News on Dec. 3, 2023.

Although there were some dominant teams headed to a return to the playoffs, most scenarios were in flux with seeding and potential entrants. Sporting News framed its updated NFL playoff picture around a 13-game slate with six teams on bye, now after Week 13.
