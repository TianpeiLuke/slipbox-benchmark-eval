# Chicago Fire Beat Inter Miami 4-1 Before A Record Soldier Field Crowd

The Chicago Fire beat Inter Miami 4-1 on Oct. 4, 2023, with braces from Xherdan Shaqiri and substitute Maren Haile-Selassie. Without Lionel Messi, Inter Miami looked exceedingly mortal, putting in a 90-minute performance littered with individual mistakes that the Fire made them pay for.

The goalscorers were Shaqiri in the 50th and 73rd minutes and Haile-Selassie in the 62nd and 66th, with Josef Martinez scoring Inter Miami's only goal in the 53rd. Shaqiri's brace was the biggest talking point for the hosts: the former Liverpool midfielder had not always been at his best since joining MLS, but he rose to the occasion in front of a record Chicago Fire crowd at Soldier Field.

At the 78th minute a crowd of 62,124 was announced, blowing out the old Chicago Fire record and setting a new club record at Soldier Field. Those fans did not get to see Lionel Messi play, but they did see their home team put on a rousing performance.
