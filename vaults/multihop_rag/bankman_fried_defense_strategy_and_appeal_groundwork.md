# Bankman-Fried's Defense Strategy And Appeal Groundwork

Bankman-Fried's defense can itself introduce risks for people who dealt with him, because his lawyers have several simultaneous objectives: first and foremost proving their client not guilty, but also — just in case they don't get the outcome they want — laying the groundwork for appeals and sentencing arguments.

The consequence is procedural: any piece of evidence they want to introduce for those two purposes has to come into play during the trial. Christopher LaVigne notes that Bankman-Fried's lawyers are already marking arguments that his constitutional rights are being violated by his pretrial incarceration because, among other things, his internet connection wasn't good enough for him to plan his defense — which could be grounds for an appeal.
