# Tech Layoffs In September 2023

September 2023's largest tech layoff was Epic Games, which announced on September 28 that the Fortnite maker was laying off 16% of its workforce, amounting to 870 people.

The month's other cuts ran across streaming, edtech, gaming and analytics. Roku announced on September 6 that it planned to lay off approximately 10% of its workforce, representing over 300 employees. Sensor Tower announced on September 8 that it had laid off approximately 40 people out of the 270-plus at the company, including C-suite executives. Divvy Homes announced on September 11 that it was laying off 94 employees. Roblox confirmed to TechCrunch on September 21 that it had let go of 30 employees in its talent acquisition organization, with no other teams impacted. Byju's announced on September 26 that it planned to cut 5,000 jobs due to restructuring efforts, the startup having eliminated more than 10,000 positions in the past two years. An impacted employee confirmed on September 27 that at least 140 people, if not more, were affected by Talkdesk's third round of layoffs in less than 14 months. Naughty Dog was reportedly cutting at least 25 contract developers, two sources familiar with the situation told Kotaku. In a regulatory filing published September 29, the cybersecurity startup IronNet said it had ceased all business activities as it prepared for Chapter 7 bankruptcy and had laid off its remaining staff.
