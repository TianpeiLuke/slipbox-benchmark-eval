# Reece James Return From Injury Chelsea

Reece James was a constant menace to Blackburn's defence as he started for the first time since the opening day of the season on his return from injury, and Mauricio Pochettino admitted he was delighted to have his captain back, The Guardian reported on Nov. 1, 2023.

"He is one of the best full-backs in the world and to have been without him for so long has been difficult," Pochettino said. "But we need to be careful and little by little he will get back to full fitness."
