# Alexander Mattison Vikings Hot Seat

Yardbarker's Nov. 30, 2023 NFC hot-seat roundup named running back Alexander Mattison as the Minnesota Vikings' hottest seat: he had floundered in his first season as the starter.

The grounds are production and volume together — zero rushing touchdowns on a career-best 13.2 attempts a game, and a rank of 22nd among qualified backs in yards per carry at 3.8. He was still getting more snaps than second-year RB Ty Chandler, but the Vikings would likely look for a replacement that offseason if Mattison did not show more. The warrant is that career-high usage without production leaves nothing to point to in defense of the job. The entry was written by Mike Santa Barbara.
