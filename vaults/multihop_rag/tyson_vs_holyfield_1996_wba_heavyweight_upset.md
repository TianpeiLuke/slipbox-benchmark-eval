# Holyfield TKO 11 Tyson: The 1996 WBA Heavyweight Upset

Evander Holyfield stopped Mike Tyson in the eleventh round on November 9, 1996 at the MGM Grand in Las Vegas to take the WBA heavyweight title, dominating throughout and decking Tyson in round six before finishing him in the eleventh.

The fight had been anticipated for years. In the early 1990s it was considered the biggest heavyweight title matchup since Muhammad Ali and Joe Frazier clashed two decades earlier, but when Tyson was jailed for rape in 1992 the division had to move on without him. While Tyson was incarcerated, Holyfield's career went through peaks and troughs: he lost his championship to Riddick Bowe in one of the greatest heavyweight title fights of all time and regained it in a rematch, but a loss to Michael Moorer, a second defeat to Bowe and an underwhelming win over Bobby Czyz had many convinced "The Real Deal" was shot. Tyson was released in March 1995 and quickly resumed his reign of terror, regaining the WBC title seven months into his comeback by stopping Britain's Frank Bruno in three rounds and then splatting Bruce Seldon in one to pick up the WBA version. "The Baddest Man on the Planet" was back and everyone was terrified — almost everyone.

On fight night, Holyfield, unlike the vast majority of Tyson's opponents, came to the ring with a smile on his face. The challenger was not intimidated by the Tyson mystique and turned in the greatest performance of his career.
