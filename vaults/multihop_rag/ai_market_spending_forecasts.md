# AI Market Spending Forecasts Reported In October 2023

Total global spending on AI was expected to reach $154 billion by the end of 2023, a 27% increase from 2022, with the following three years also expected to see a compound annual growth rate of 27% — putting annual AI spending in 2026 at over $300 billion. These figures were cited in TechCrunch's 13 October 2023 survey of venture capitalists active in AI investing, as context for how much room startups have in the enterprise market.

Alongside the spending numbers, McKinsey reports that generative AI alone can add up to $4.4 trillion in value across industries through writing code, analyzing consumer trends, personalizing customer service, improving operating efficiencies, and more. Ninety-four percent of business leaders agree AI will be critical to all businesses' success over the next five years, and eighty-two percent of business leaders believe the integration of AI solutions will increase their employee performance and job satisfaction.

A comparative observation accompanies the totals: despite cloud computing remaining critical, AI budgets are now more than double that of cloud computing. The conditions under which these figures were offered matter — they were presented as evidence that startups should expect a high level of desire for and experience with AI solutions in their future customers. Within the enterprise sector there is a clear recognition of the value of AI, but many enterprises lack the internal capabilities to develop AI solutions, and that gap is what is said to present a significant opportunity for startups specializing in AI to engage with enterprise clients. As the business landscape matures, proficiency in leveraging AI is becoming a strategic imperative.
