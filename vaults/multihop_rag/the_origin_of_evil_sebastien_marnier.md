# The Origin of Evil (Sébastien Marnier)

The Origin of Evil is a French thriller by Sébastien Marnier starring Laure Calamy, Suzanne Clément, and Dominique Blanc, ranked 45 on Polygon's best movies of 2023 list of 2023-10-07 and available for digital rental/purchase.

Polygon calls it the kind of twisty story that is hard to sum up without giving something away — and says that is part of the appeal. Marnier, director of the shocking 2018 prep school thriller School's Out, steers the film away from a neo-noir tone even though the plot is pure noir, right down to the character types.

The setup is that a woman played by Laure Calamy insinuates herself into a rich family, claiming she is a lost daughter to the patriarch Serge, played by Jacques Weber; viewers will suspect she has more of an agenda than she is letting on, but Serge and the rest of his caustic family members have agendas too. Polygon's Tasha Robinson writes that unpacking every lie and scheme takes every minute of the run time and that audience sympathies are guaranteed to shift half a dozen times in the process — a gem as a crime story, and even better as a character story.
