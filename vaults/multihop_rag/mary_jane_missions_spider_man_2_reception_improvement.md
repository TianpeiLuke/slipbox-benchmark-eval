# Mary Jane's Missions In Spider-Man 2 As An Improvement On Their Reception

Essentially Sports argued on 1 November 2023 that Mary Jane's arc in Marvel's Spider-Man 2 received an impressive elevation over the poorly received missions featuring her in the original 2018 game: the claim is that she can now be called the third unofficial superhero in the latest PS5 exclusive, and the warrant is that the developers gave her the means to fight back, which converts her missions from movement exercises into ones players have a reason to be invested in.

The starting point is the reception problem. In the first title, stepping into MJ's shoes was a great concept, but the developers somehow couldn't execute the plan flawlessly, and missions featuring her as a playable character were heavily criticised by most fans, with nothing too special about those quests whatsoever. Insomniac's sequel, by contrast, has witnessed huge appreciation from fans for delivering a memorable sequel, alongside the epic portrayal of Peter Parker and Miles Morales fighting some of the strongest villains.

The grounds for the improvement are mechanical. Being a journalist at The Daily Bugle, her character is bound to go places to investigate mysteries; the first game had such situations, but the sequel improved on them, giving MJ a lot more strength instead of making her someone who always needs rescuing at the last moment. Compared with the lack of interactivity in 2018's Spider-Man, where all she could do was move from one point to another, MJ is now capable of tackling the enemies around her and making pretty good and lethal moves: the developers have given her a little firepower, if not in too much capacity, so she has weapons instead of being clueless in moments of vulnerability against armed enemies. She has no superpowers, but she has other means to help herself out. Because of that involvement, players now have a reason to be more invested in a given MJ mission, and she can handle circumstances in her own manner, breaking free from the linear approach of just trying to hide and escape. The article notes that Marvel's Spider-Man 2 is available on PlayStation 5 worldwide.
