# Many Apps Over One Shared Social Graph

The Verge's writer argued that we'll be better off with a hundred different apps for Snapchat or Instagram or X instead of just one, a dozen companies competing to build the best moderation tools, and an app store filled with different ways to follow and be followed by other people on the internet.

The grounds are the absurdity of the current arrangement: it doesn't make sense that we have a dozen usernames, a dozen profiles, a dozen sets of fans and friends. The warrant is a claim about ownership — all that stuff should belong to the user, who should be able to access it and interact with it anywhere and everywhere — which is what makes competition among many apps over one shared identity and graph preferable to one app per network.
