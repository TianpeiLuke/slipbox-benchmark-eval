# Sheffield United vs Manchester United Preview, October 2023

Sheffield United, rock bottom of the Premier League with just one point on the board, hosted Manchester United at 8:00pm on 21 October 2023 live on talkSPORT, with talkSPORT noting the previous day that their hopes could be looking up given Erik ten Hag's men's disappointing season.

Sheffield United's season could not be going much worse. Manchester United sat tenth in the table, having won four and lost four of their opening eight games. Their most recent Premier League victory, over Brentford, may nonetheless give the Red Devils hope that their season is on the up — a dramatic win secured before the international break.
