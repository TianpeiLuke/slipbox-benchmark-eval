# Hijack (Apple TV Plus)

Hijack, created by George Kay and Jim Field Smith and starring Idris Elba, Neil Maskell, and Archie Panjabi, is almost exactly what it sounds like — a show about a hijacking — but Polygon says that simplicity of premise belies a much richer, much more fun story that reads more like a breezy blockbuster thriller of yore than another drop in the streaming TV bucket. It appears in the outlet's 2023-10-25 roundup of the best new TV series on every streaming service.

Sam Nelson, played by Idris Elba, is one of many passengers just trying to make it home when his plane is suddenly hijacked by a group of terrorists. Without arousing suspicion from the gunmen closely watching the plane, Sam and his fellow travelers have to figure out how to secure the plane and get everyone home safely.

Critic ZM calls the show taut, making every moment count even in unexpected ways: little details that feel brushed past are returned to, and things that first seem like plot holes get filled in, exploited by the passengers or the hijackers. It is told in close to real time, covering every minute and every decision made by those on the plane and on the ground as British law enforcement mobilizes. It is twisty and neat, and it involves Idris Elba ducking around a plane — something Elba said he really had to do, since he is very tall and they filmed in an actual plane. In a world drowning in watchlists that grow larger by the day, the critic calls it a breath of fresh air: easy, competent, and a pure pleasure.
