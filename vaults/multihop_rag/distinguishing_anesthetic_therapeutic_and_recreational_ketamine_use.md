# Distinguishing Anesthetic, Therapeutic And Recreational Ketamine Use

It is important for people to recognize the distinction between different uses of the drug, according to Prashad, because the same molecule is used in ways that are not comparable.

The features that separate the three uses are dose and frequency: "In reality, the ways in which ketamine is used for anesthesia versus depression versus abuse are all very different in terms of dosage and frequency."
