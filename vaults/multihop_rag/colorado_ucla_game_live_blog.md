# Colorado-UCLA Live Blog: Four Bruins Turnovers, A Targeting Ejection, And A 28-16 Finish

The Oct. 28, 2023 Colorado-UCLA game at the Rose Bowl swung from a Colorado 6-0 start built on turnovers to a 28-16 UCLA win. Colorado scored first: Shedeur Sanders escaped a sack to find Javon Antonio for 12 yards at the UCLA 47 (7:52 p.m.), and Alejandro Mata converted a 31-yard field goal at 7:54 p.m. Travis Hunter then picked off Ethan Garbers at the Bruins' 36 — upheld on review — at 7:59 p.m., and Colorado settled for a 39-yard Mata field goal at 8:05 p.m. for 6-0 with 7:44 left in the first quarter. Jordan Domineck sacked Garbers to force 4th & 11, but UCLA went for it and Garbers converted himself on a 13-yard run (8:22 p.m.); the first quarter ended 6-0.

UCLA took the lead early in the second quarter when Garbers found Carson Steele on a three-yard pass after two quarterback scrambles, making it 7-6 with 14:20 left (8:29 p.m.). Colorado's defense kept forcing turnovers it could not convert: Steele ran 18 yards but lost the ball on the tackle, with Carter Stoutmire recovering after a Shilo Sanders hit (8:40 p.m.); Collin Schlee, in for Garbers, was intercepted by Hunter, giving Colorado the ball at its own 41 with just over six minutes left in the half (8:51 p.m.); Colorado went three-and-out and Garbers returned with a 17-yard run to the UCLA 37 (8:58 p.m.); Shilo Sanders was ejected for targeting after a hit on Carsen Ryan and was out for the rest of the game (9:03 p.m.); RJ Lopez's 24-yard field goal attempt hit the left upright, keeping UCLA's lead at one with 1:37 left in the second quarter (9:07 p.m.); and Steele fumbled again on a run, recovered by Cam'Ron Silmon-Craig in Colorado territory for the Bruins' fourth turnover with 20 seconds left in the half — the Buffaloes having scored only three points off turnovers. Halftime was 7-6 UCLA.

UCLA pulled away after the break. Garbers found Logan Loya and Moliki Matavao on two plays for a touchdown at 9:40 p.m., making it 14-6. Laiatu Latu, who had multiple sacks on the night, took down Sanders for UCLA's sixth sack (10:03 p.m.), and Mata's 34-yard field goal cut the deficit to five with four minutes left in the third (10:06 p.m.). A Garbers pass to Loya was ruled complete after review (10:20 p.m.), and the third quarter ended 14-9. TJ Harden capped an 80-yard drive with a short touchdown run at 10:23 p.m. for 21-9 with 14 minutes left, then went 25 yards with a hurdle (10:43 p.m.), and Collin Schlee ran seven yards into the end zone at 10:45 p.m. for 28-9 with six minutes left. Colorado finally reached the end zone at 10:58 p.m. when Sanders found Jimmy Horn Jr. on an 18-yard toss for his first touchdown of the night; the Buffaloes attempted an onside kick with two-and-a-half minutes left, but the Bruins recovered. Final: UCLA 28, Colorado 16.
