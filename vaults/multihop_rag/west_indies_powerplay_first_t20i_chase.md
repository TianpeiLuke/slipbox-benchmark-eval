# West Indies' Powerplay In The First T20I Chase

Chasing 172, West Indies finished the powerplay of the first T20I on 59-1, having come out of the traps as well as England did.

Brandon King got the home side off to a blistering start in the first over, sending Sam Curran down the ground for four and swiping a brace of sixes over mid-wicket to reach 16-0. In the second over, Will Jacks dropped short and Kyle Mayers murdered it onto the roof of the stadium, Mayers cut for three behind point and King skipped out of his crease to skim the ball for four down the ground, taking it to 30-0. In the third over Chris Woakes replaced the beleaguered Curran and struck immediately: Ben Duckett flew to his left at backward point for a spectacular diving catch off a full-blooded cut shot to dismiss King for 22, leaving West Indies 32-1; Shai Hope came in and dug out a Woakes yorker, crunching it away down the ground for four to make it 36-1. In the fourth over Tymal Mills bowled for England for the first time in eighteen months, starting with a couple of wides slanted down the leg side before Mayers pulled a short ball onto the roof for six, and Duckett then shelled a chance that smashed into his palm as he leapt to his right — West Indies 47-1. In the fifth over Hope clubbed a Woakes length ball down the ground for six to reach 56-1, and in the sixth Curran returned and conceded just three runs, with a single each to Mayers and Hope, to complete the powerplay at 59-1 with Mayers on 22 and Hope on 13.
