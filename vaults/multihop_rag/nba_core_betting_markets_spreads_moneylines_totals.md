# The Three Staple NBA Bet Types: Point Spreads, Moneylines And Totals

For anyone new to NBA betting the staple wager types are point spreads, moneylines and over/unders. NBA point spreads bet on games via margins of victory or loss: if the New York Knicks are -7.5 favorites over the Miami Heat, the Knicks need to win by 8 points to win a spread bet, while the Heat can lose by up to 7 points or win outright. NBA moneylines are the simplest form of betting — you pick the winner of the game and are paid based on the odds when you finalize your bet slip, with favorites shown at negative odds such as -135 and underdogs at positive odds such as +165. NBA over/under bets, also known as totals, focus on the combined point total of the two teams, with winners and losers irrelevant: if the over/under in Knicks vs. Heat is 211.5 points, the over needs the teams to combine for 212 or more, while the under needs 211 or fewer.

These sit inside a much wider menu. With legal sports betting available in over 30 states, the NBA gives bettors plenty of games, teams and players across hundreds of odds markets — moneylines, totals and point spreads, plus player props for scoring, rebounding and assists, and futures bets such as odds to win the Eastern Conference or individual awards like NBA MVP. Bettors will want to find the best NBA betting lines night after night as well as futures markets from NBA Finals odds to Rookie of the Year odds, and any online betting site will offer plenty of odds to choose from.
