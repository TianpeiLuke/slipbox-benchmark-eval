# Son Heung-min's Criticism Of Tottenham's Softness

Tottenham captain Heung-Min Son rejected any reading of the 7 December 2023 defeat to West Ham as bad luck or a surprise: his counter is that the result was unacceptable and self-inflicted, because Spurs knew what was coming and were soft anyway.

"It's unacceptable," Son said. "I think players should take responsibility, this is unacceptable. The fans don't deserve this, especially when you play at home, when you go 1-0 up early on you should play with even more energy but we didn't, and that's why we lost."

He then closed off the excuse that the opponent caught them out: "West Ham have always been tough to play against so we knew what was coming. But we were soft, and especially in the Premier League, even when you're winning 2-0, 3-0, you don't know what's going to happen in the end. 1-0 is not enough, you have to try to kill the game."
