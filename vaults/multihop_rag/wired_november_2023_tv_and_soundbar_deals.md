# WIRED's Pre-Black Friday TV And Soundbar Deals

WIRED's TV and audio picks in this roundup are the Samsung S95C, the LG C3, and a Polk soundbar with subwoofer.

The Samsung S95C (8/10, WIRED Recommends) is one of WIRED's favorite TVs thanks to its quantum dot OLED panel, which gives perfect black levels and a ridiculously bright image, and it comes with an excellent centered pedestal mount that makes it easy to place in a living room. For gamers, the LG C3 is WIRED's top TV pick: beyond the usual benefits of LG's OLED panels, like perfect black levels, it has gaming-friendly features including a 120-Hz refresh rate for super smooth gameplay and a 9.2 millisecond response time to reduce input lag in fast-paced games. On soundbars, WIRED argues one of the best upgrades you can make is replacing a TV's mediocre built-in speakers, and this Polk model is one of its favorites, coming with a subwoofer that boosts all those deep, booming notes Christopher Nolan likes so much and several audio presets that tune the sound to what you're watching.
