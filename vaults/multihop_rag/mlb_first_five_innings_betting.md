# First 5 Innings (F5) Betting In MLB

First 5 Innings, or F5, bets concern the outcome of just the first five innings of a Major League Baseball game, and are ideal for bettors who prefer to focus on the early portion of a game because they often take out the uncertainty of relief pitchers replacing the starters.

You will find F5 bets in the MLB tab of your online sportsbook or betting app, where you can browse the available odds and lines for those particular wagers. The same staple markets apply — you can still place bets on moneylines, run lines or totals — but focused only on the first five innings of play.

The boundary to keep in mind is pricing: the odds and lines will likely differ from those for the full game, since they are concentrated on the first half of the game only.
