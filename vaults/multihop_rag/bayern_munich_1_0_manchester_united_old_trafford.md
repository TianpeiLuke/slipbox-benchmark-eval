# Bayern Munich 1-0 Manchester United At Old Trafford

Kingsley Coman scored the only goal of Bayern Munich's 1-0 Champions League win at Old Trafford just after the hour, from a delightfully sneaky round-the-corner pass by Harry Kane.

Bayern held the ball nicely and probed in neat, clipped, mannered attacks, with a lot seeming to hinge on how desperately they felt the need to twist that scalpel and whether they could really be bothered to take the thing to the wall. United defended well and their waves of pressing drew waves of applause; everyone there seemed to be trying. But with 58 minutes gone Copenhagen scored and United's hopes began to slip beneath the waves, and just over 10 minutes later Bayern made it 1-0 in Manchester: Coman was granted a huge expanse of time and space in front of the United goal, enough to set himself as though taking a penalty, and belted the ball into the corner. After all that pressure and all that tender hope, it looked a bit like a training goal.
