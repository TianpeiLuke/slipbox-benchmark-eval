# D'Andre Swift And The Eagles' Run Game In The Showdown Lineup

Sporting News took D'Andre Swift ($8,000) in the FLEX of its Week 15 2023 Eagles-Seahawks Showdown lineup, betting that Philadelphia would re-emphasize the run after three weeks in which Swift had no impact.

The message to the Eagles from the fans had been clear: run the ball. It was part of what made the team so hard to beat earlier in the season, but they had become a pass-happy offense in recent history, relying mostly on the legs and arm of Jalen Hurts to do everything. After getting smacked by the Cowboys the previous week, the Eagles were not afforded the opportunity to establish the run. The column expected that to be a point of emphasis this time around, especially since Hurts was dealing with an illness, and argued Swift's dormant stretch needed to change if the Eagles wanted to get back on track.
