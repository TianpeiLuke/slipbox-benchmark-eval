# The Lions-Packers Rivalry Turned In 2022, And Week 4 2023 Is The Rematch

Sporting News argues on 2023-09-26 that the long-standing direction of the Lions-Packers rivalry began to reverse in 2022, and that this makes the Week 4 meeting a genuine battle for NFC North supremacy. The framing: for Detroit it's all about courage — a team once down and out, much like their city, is now once again on the rise; the Lions are supposed to be the kings of the jungle and hope to become the NFL's version in 2023, since in the food chain lions are the hunter rather than the hunted, yet it has been the other way around for far too long as 31 other teams feasted on a once proud franchise. Their motors have been restored and the team rebuilt with an attitude and moxie that would make even the most "football guy" of "football guys" blush — a combination of quiet confidence and downright arrogant confidence that draws people in to root for, or against, an underdog.

The grounds for the turn: while the Packers have packed up the Lions for years, that began to change in 2022, Aaron Rodgers is no longer in Green Bay, a new love interest is under center in Jordan Love, and it was these Lions who ended the Rodgers era and swept Green Bay the previous season. Now they meet again in prime time on one of the league's most hallowed grounds; the tundra won't be frozen at Lambeau Field at this point in the season, but the game is important. The DFS read that follows: last week was all about defense, this week is all about offense. The Lions feature a potent offense whose three-headed monster of Goff, St. Brown and Gibbs should be able to produce, while on the Green Bay side Love will hopefully have his top option back in the backfield — that duo cooked the Bears defense in the opener — so if Jones is healthy and suits up he's a must-have after the clunkers A.J. Dillon has posted for the last two weeks; otherwise, pivot to a pass-catcher.
