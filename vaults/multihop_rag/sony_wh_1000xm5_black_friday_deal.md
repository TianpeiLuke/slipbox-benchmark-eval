# Sony WH-1000XM5 Black Friday Deal

The Sony WH-1000XM5 came down to $328 at Amazon, B&H and other retailers for Black Friday 2023 — a $72 drop from its $400 list price. Engadget's headphone roundup calls that a tie with the lowest price it has seen; the wider Black Friday roundup describes it as not an all-time low but about $70 off Sony's list price and $20 lower than the headphones' usual recurring sale price.

The XM5 is the top recommendation in Engadget's guide to the best wireless headphones and earned a review score of 95 last year. Its active noise cancellation is among the better options tested, its design is comfortable to wear over extended periods, its call quality is crisp and its battery lasts a solid 30 or so hours per charge. By default its sound goes heavy on the bass, which should please fans of hip-hop, EDM and pop, though the EQ curve can be customized through Sony's app, and like many Sony headphones it comes with useful bonus features such as "Speak-to-Chat," which automatically pauses your music when you start speaking.
