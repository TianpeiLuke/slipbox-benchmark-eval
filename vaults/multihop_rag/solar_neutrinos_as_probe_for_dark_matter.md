# Solar Neutrinos as a Probe for Dark Matter

A pre-print paper published in August 2023 suggested that the sun may be harboring hidden particles of dark matter in its fiery guts, and that neutrinos — the "ghost particles" spat out by the sun — could be the key to finding them.

Dark matter is an elusive type of matter whose identity is still a mystery to scientists; it rarely interacts with regular matter or light, but when it does it is theorized to emit neutrinos, which have almost no mass and no electrical charge. The researchers hypothesized that the sun's core may have a high concentration of dark matter, and predicted that if this is the case the sun will occasionally emit slightly more neutrinos than normal. That prediction is the test: so far, these extra neutrinos have not been detected.
