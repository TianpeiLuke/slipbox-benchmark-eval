# 14-Inch MacBook Pro M3 Holiday Deal

Apple released its new M3-powered MacBook Pros just before Halloween, and the notebooks were already on sale for Black Friday and Cyber Monday 2023 at Amazon, B&H and Best Buy. The base model 14-inch MacBook Pro with the new M3 chip, 8GB of memory and 512GB of storage was $1,449, or $150 off Apple's list price.

Engadget's caveat, after testing the new chip, is that the base model with 8GB of RAM may quickly get bogged down — the M3 chip may quickly begin to push the limits of what 8GB of RAM can handle — so if it's within budget the recommendation is the model with 16GB of RAM, also $150 off at B&H Photo. Upgrading to a model with the M3 Pro chip for an even speedier experience carried a $200 discount, at $2,199 at B&H Photo; the wider Black Friday roundup lists an M3 Pro version with 18GB of RAM at $200 off at $1,799, and an M3 Pro version of the larger 16-inch Pro at $200 off at $2,299. Engadget gave the new M3 MacBook Pros an appreciative review and named them the best option for creatives in its buyer's guide.
