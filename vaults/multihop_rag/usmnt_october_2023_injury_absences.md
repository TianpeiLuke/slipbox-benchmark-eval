# Who The USMNT Was Missing Through Injury In October 2023

Gregg Berhalter largely had his entire first-choice player pool available for the October 2023 friendlies, with only a few injuries to work around. Goalkeepers Zack Steffen and Sean Johnson remained out of action injured; Josh Sargent was sidelined with a long-term ankle injury; Daryl Dike was out after tearing his Achilles; and Antonee Robinson was missing with what Berhalter described as a sports hernia — Robinson had played through it in the previous camp and recently for his club, but it was affecting him, so rest was judged the best option.

Two midfield situations went the other way. Johnny Cardoso, who had pulled out of the September camp with an ankle injury, had returned to action with Brazilian club Internacional, indicating a return to fitness, and was included in the squad. And with Tyler Adams injured, social media noise calling for Heidenheim midfielder Lennard Maloney to be brought in was answered with a call-up: the 23-year-old was born in Berlin to an American father, had appeared for the U.S. at U-20 level and had stated his desire to reach the USMNT senior squad.
