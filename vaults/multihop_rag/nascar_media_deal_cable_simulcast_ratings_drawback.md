# NASCAR Media Deal Cable Simulcast Ratings Drawback

Against the view that NASCAR's media rights deal is straightforwardly good news, Sports Business Journal's Adam Stern, quoted by Essentially Sports on Dec. 1, 2023, attacked the premise that the money automatically helps the teams — because part of the deal moves races off network television and onto cable, where ratings will probably be lower.

Stern's specific point: "When you look at the deal with Warner Bros. Discovery, those races are going to be simulcast not just on MAX streaming but also on TNT and TNT is cable, not a free-to-air channel." He continued: "You have that on the cable subscription. So, the number of races that are going down that have been on the network, that's what teams are concerned about because the ratings will probably be lower." This matters because NASCAR has been struggling to keep teams happy on profitability — since the introduction of the Next Gen cars it has only become more difficult for Cup Series teams and teams in other competitions, and having taken a commercial hit they have piled pressure on NASCAR to help. With not every fan having access to all platforms, it is a tricky situation for both fans and NASCAR; the sanctioning body has nonetheless made its move, and it remains to be seen how execution works out — for now, it looks like NASCAR has secured itself well by bringing more money to the table.
