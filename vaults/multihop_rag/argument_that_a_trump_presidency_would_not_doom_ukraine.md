# Argument That a Trump Presidency Would Not Doom Ukraine

Kurt Volker challenges the premise that a Trump election victory would mean abandonment of Ukraine, while conceding that it would make future funding uncertain. "I doubt that even if Trump were elected that he would abandon support for Ukraine overall, because it would be a disaster for U.S. interests, and it would appear to be a failure," he told CNBC. "You'd have these images of Russians overrunning places, and brutality and so forth, so I don't think he wants that."

The attack is on the inference from Trump's past relations with Moscow to a wholesale cut-off, not on the observation that his intentions are unclear: "But it's not clear exactly what he would do to try to end the war." For his part, Trump has said he would be able to resolve the Ukraine war "in one day" if reelected, saying he would convince the leaders of Ukraine and Russia to make a deal.
