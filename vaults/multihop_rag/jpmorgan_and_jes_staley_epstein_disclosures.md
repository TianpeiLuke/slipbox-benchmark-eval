# JPMorgan And Jes Staley: The Epstein Disclosures

Emails that surfaced in the litigation revealed Epstein's unusually close relationship with Jes Staley, a top banker at JPMorgan Chase & Co. who went on to run Barclays Plc — one of the secrets the world may never have glimpsed without those cases, along with the depths of Epstein's influence in the Caribbean.

The blame is contested on both sides. JPMorgan alleged in court that Staley failed to alert the bank of all he knew about Epstein. Staley — who swapped sexually suggestive emails about young women with Epstein and visited Little St. Jeff, according to emails filed in court — hasn't been accused of wrongdoing by the authorities, and lawyers for Staley claim he had no decision-making authority over Epstein's JPMorgan accounts.
