# OpenAI's Nonprofit Governance And Capped-Profit Structure

OpenAI's for-profit subsidiary, OpenAI Global, LLC, is fully controlled by OpenAI's nonprofit: the subsidiary is permitted to commercialize its technology, but it is subject to the nonprofit's mission of attaining artificial general intelligence (AGI), which OpenAI defines as AI that can "outperform humans at most economically valuable work."

The discriminating feature of this structure is where the power over AGI sits. The nonprofit OpenAI's board has the power both to determine when the company has achieved AGI and to exclude that AGI from IP licenses and other commercial terms — including terms with Microsoft, one of OpenAI's biggest investors and a steadfast integrator of OpenAI's technologies. OpenAI was founded as a nonprofit in 2015 but restructured in 2019 as a "capped-profit" company in order to raise capital, an acknowledgement of the huge costs associated with training cutting-edge AI systems. Altman underlined the point in an interview with the Financial Times in the week of his firing, saying he "hoped" Microsoft would increase its investment to help pay for "huge" imminent model training costs.
