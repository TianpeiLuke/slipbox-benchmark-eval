# Vincent Namatjira: Australia In Colour Survey Exhibition

Vincent Namatjira turned 40 in June 2023 and launched a monograph and major survey exhibition, Australia in Colour, in October as part of Tarnanthi festival, the Art Gallery of South Australia's annual celebration of Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander art, The Guardian reported on 16 October 2023.

Collating this formative decade of his work, the exhibition's title alludes to "the blue skies, the red deserts, the green hills, the yellow fields, the blue oceans, the white sands, the creek beds" seen in the backdrop of his portraits, but it is also a more visceral reflection on a continent thrown into stark contrast. "Australia in Colour is gut-wrenching painting, and hard work," he says — in an interview that took place before Saturday's failed referendum vote.

Vincent Namatjira: Australia in Colour opens on 20 October at the Art Gallery of South Australia before touring nationally; Desert Songs is showing until 28 October at Yavuz Gallery in Surry Hills, Sydney; and the monograph Vincent Namatjira (Thames & Hudson) is out now.
