# Meta DSA Obligations as a Very Large Online Platform

Compliance with the [Digital Services Act](digital_services_act.md) took effect for Meta in August 2023 as owner of designated very large online platforms. The Commission designated 19 VLOPs in April 2023, including Facebook and Instagram.

Penalties for non-compliance reach 6% of global annual turnover, which in Meta's case could run to multiple billions.

The DSA's crisis response mechanism did not become operational until February 17 of the following year, because it requires an advisory body — the European Board for Digital Services — to be operational so it can recommend the Commission adopt such a mechanism. The Board's establishment in turn awaited Member States appointing national Digital Services Coordinators, the bodies overseeing DSA compliance for non-VLOPs.

Meta was not alone in being warned: X received both an urgent warning and a formal request for information, and TikTok also received a warning about DSA content risks related to the conflict.
