# Michigan Expected To Face No Big Ten Penalty Before The Season's End

Sources at four different Big Ten schools said they do not expect the conference to levy any sort of punishment against Michigan before the season ends, despite frustration from all corners of the conference, as reported by The New York Times on 3 November 2023.

The explanation offered by a source briefed on the coaches' call places the commissioner in a familiar bind: "It feels like (former commissioner) Kevin (Warren) taking over and then COVID. Tony's walking into this situation, and people are calling for the league to make a statement before they have all the facts."

Michigan's own position is recorded too. On the Monday, coach Jim Harbaugh met with reporters and said "the people that know us the most think the most of us" as Michigan faces the NCAA investigation; he has denied knowledge of the alleged scouting.
