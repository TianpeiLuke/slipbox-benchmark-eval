# Broadcast Rights Limits on Twitch Sports Streams

Livestreams of major league games are less common to find on Twitch because of broadcast rights, Polygon reported on 2023-10-20 — though some leagues, nationally and globally, have experimented with streaming games in recent years.

The NBA signed a deal with Twitch in 2017 to stream minor league games while giving fans the chance to interact with each other, and the two teamed up again in 2019 for a separate deal to exclusively stream USA Basketball games globally.

When Amazon began airing Thursday Night Football games the previous fall, Nielsen reported a total viewership figure of 13 million for its first exclusive game, a figure that included U.S. Prime Video subscribers who streamed on Twitch.
