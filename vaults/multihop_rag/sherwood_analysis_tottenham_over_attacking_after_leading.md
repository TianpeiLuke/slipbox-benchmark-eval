# Sherwood's Analysis Of Tottenham Over-Attacking After Leading

Speaking on Optus Sport after the 7 December 2023 defeat, ex-Spurs boss Tim Sherwood diagnosed the losing run as a game-management failure: the claim is that Tottenham keep trying to win a game they have already won, and the warrant is that in doing so they leave themselves open to being exploited.

His grounds start with the pattern rather than the single match: "They're quietly having a bad run, it's five without a win now. They've led in every game and I've said when they take the lead, I wouldn't say the game is over. It's not good enough."

Sherwood was careful to separate style from outcome, crediting the change under Postecoglou before faulting it: "But they've changed. The last three or four years they've been watching some dross there but it's better to watch. It's game management." He then spelled out the mechanism and the evidence from this match: "They go ahead and they're already winning the game but they try and win it again and again and leave themselves open. Jarrod Bowen was outstanding out there and you can really exploit them. They leave space and they make poor decisions on the ball and it's costing them at the moment."
