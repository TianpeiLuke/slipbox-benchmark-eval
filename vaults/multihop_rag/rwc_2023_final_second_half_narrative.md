# The Second Half Of The 2023 Rugby World Cup Final

New Zealand's only try of the 2023 Rugby World Cup final, scored by Beauden Barrett after a Mark Telea break, left them trailing by a single point when Richie Mo'unga missed the conversion — and that lone point proved the difference as the Springboks celebrated a remarkable victory.

The half turned on discipline and missed chances. Minutes into it Siya Kolisi was off after cannoning into Ardie Savea; he later returned, much to the anger of the New Zealanders in the crowd who were calling for justice. Sensing an opportunity to strike against 14 men, the All Blacks kicked for the corner on a couple of occasions out wide, and it came back to haunt them as their usually silky and composed skill sets failed them.

That pattern held when the TMO intervened after Aaron Smith thought he had a try in his final Test, only for an error at the lineout to cancel out the five-pointer. Eventually the All Blacks did have a try: Mark Telea, their best and biggest attacking threat all night, got on the outside and drew in two defenders before offloading to Beauden Barrett — but Mo'unga missed the conversion, leaving them a point behind.
