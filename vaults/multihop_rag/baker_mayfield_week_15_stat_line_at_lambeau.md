# Baker Mayfield's Week 15 Stat Line At Lambeau Field

Baker Mayfield completed 22 of 28 passes (78.6%) for 381 yards and four touchdowns with no interceptions in Tampa Bay's 34-20 Week 15 win at Green Bay on Dec. 17, 2023.

He kept the good times rolling by shredding Joe Barry's Packers defense to keep the Bucs strumming along toward a divisional crown. It was the finest display of Mayfield's season and one of the best of his career, and one of the greatest showings in recent Lambeau Field history: according to ESPN Stats and Info, and as posted by Rob Demovsky, Mayfield was just the sixth visiting quarterback ever to throw for at least 350 yards and four touchdowns there, with Kirk Cousins in 2018 the last to do it. In terms of individual days it was a career one — he recorded the second-most single-game yardage and second-most single-game touchdown totals of his professional career, and StatMuse noted it was his third career four-touchdown, zero-interception game and his first as a Buc — and he did it against a side fighting tooth and nail with Tampa for a playoff spot. Chris Godwin was Mayfield's favorite battery mate, catching 10 passes for 155 yards, by far his best showing of the year; but even when Godwin was not open Mayfield got creative, and whether it was Mike Evans, Rachaad White, David Moore or even Ko Kieft, it seemed that whenever Mayfield wanted someone to get the ball, he got it to them without a hint of discomfort.
