# Whether Gastroparesis Persists After Stopping GLP-1 Drugs

For the cases of stomach paralysis in the study, the researchers could not assess whether the condition was temporary or permanent -- the data did not settle the question either way.

What exists beyond the study data is anecdotal and secondhand. "There are reports from other journalists where they have met patients whose symptoms have not gone away despite stopping the drugs," study co-author Dr. Mahyar Etminan, associate professor in the Departments of Ophthalmology and Visual Sciences and Medicine at UBC, told Fox News Digital.

He added that there are medications that can be used to help with the condition.
