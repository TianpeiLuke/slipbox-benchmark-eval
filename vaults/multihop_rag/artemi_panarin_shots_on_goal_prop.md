# Artemi Panarin OVER 3 Shots On Goal

Sporting News' first pick for the Nov. 7, 2023 NHL slate was Artemi Panarin, LW, Rangers vs. Red Wings — OVER 3 shots on goal. The base rate supports it: Panarin had registered at least three shots on goal in nine of 11 games that season, going over three in six of them, while up to his usual antics and seeing extra ice time under new head coach Peter Laviolette.

The complication is the Rangers' makeshift lineup. The absences of Adam Fox and Filip Chytil are a big deal for Panarin specifically, since he loses Fox, who quarterbacks the power play, and Chytil, who centered his line at even strength. Even so, Panarin is still all over the ice for the Blueshirts and is seeing 18.8 percent of his shifts start in the offensive zone, a welcome sight after the previous two seasons under Gerard Gallant.

The opponent completes the case. The Red Wings had allowed the 11th-most shots on goal that season (371) and the fourth-most power play opportunities (46), so if the Rangers can play with the man advantage Panarin should hit the number with ease — and he is enough of a factor at even strength to like the OVER anyway.
