# M3 Max MacBook Pro Battery Runtime Test

In TechCrunch's video rundown test the 16-inch M3 Max MacBook Pro ran for 26 hours and 45 minutes — the reviewer's judgment being that the battery might be the most exceptional bit of the machine.

The test conditions: the first thing done upon receiving the system, after setup and charging, was a video rundown, far and away the most lengthy part of testing. A single movie — Bill & Ted Face the Music — was looped in Apple TV with brightness and volume at their default settings. It was kicked off at 4:45PM on Tuesday and ran until 7:30 the following evening. As the reviewer puts it, this thing is going to get you through your next flight, no problem.
