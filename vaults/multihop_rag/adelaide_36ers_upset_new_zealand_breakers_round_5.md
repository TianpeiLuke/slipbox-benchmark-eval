# Adelaide Upset The Breakers As Vasiljevic Takes Over

The Adelaide 36ers upset the New Zealand Breakers in NBL round 5, going on a 10-0 run straight after Zylan Cheatham went off injured, moving in front for the first time since the opening quarter, and never trailing again as they clinched the final quarter 27-14.

Sunday Dech nailed a three-pointer to give what was then the 36ers' biggest lead of the day at seven points. Mid-season recruit Dejan Vasiljevic (24 points, five assists) took centre stage after Cheatham went off, the two-time NBL champion sinking his 200th three-pointer in the first half and hitting three more in the fourth quarter as the Sixers broke away. "He's a tough SOB," 36ers coach CJ Bruton said. "When it comes down to it, from the juniors, to the seniors, to the pros, that's who he is and he carries that. He's got respect from everyone, not only our team, but the league." The 36ers, tipped as possible wooden spooners in the pre-season, had now won three of their past four games.

Sixers big man Isaac Humphries, who starred in the upset defeat of Perth in the previous game, had been instrumental earlier: the centre (17 points, 11 rebounds) used his size advantage to dominate the paint and help Adelaide remain in the game despite 10 first-half turnovers as a team, a tally that finished at a staggering 23.

For New Zealand, any long-term injury would be troublesome for last season's runners-up, who had begun the campaign with only one win from four games. The Breakers were left to rue wayward free-throw shooting — 12 from 20 attempts — which meant they were never able to open up a double-digit lead, and former Golden State Warriors forward Anthony Lamb (25 points) is acclimatising quickly to the NBL though he had too little help as he notched nine fourth-quarter points.
