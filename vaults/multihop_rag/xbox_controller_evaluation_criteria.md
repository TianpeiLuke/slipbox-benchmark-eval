# How The Verge Evaluates Xbox Controllers

The guide judges each controller against five criteria, applied in order. Connectivity: is it wireless, does it support Bluetooth, or is it wired only — and if wired, is the cable replaceable? Quality: does it feel cheap or nicely made, and were there any glitches or reliability issues while testing?

Extra features: does it have handy extras like remappable buttons, or extra ones? Customization: can you remap buttons or tune it with custom software profiles, and are there fun cosmetic flares to its design?

Value: is it priced affordably, about average, or does it come at a steep premium?
