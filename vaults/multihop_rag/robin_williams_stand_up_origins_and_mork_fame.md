# Robin Williams' Stand-Up Origins And Mork Fame

Robin Williams began his career in stand-up comedy in the 1970s and elevated his fame after appearing in an episode of "Happy Days" as an alien named Mork, a role that led to his own TV series "Mork and Mindy", FOX News Entertainment reported on 19 October 2023.

The account comes from Vice's "The Dark Side of Comedy" documentary series, whose second-season premiere was the Williams episode; the report frames it as friends and colleagues giving an inside look at the years before his 2014 death by suicide, which shocked the world. Williams also landed a role in the film world with "Popeye".

His friends noted that while he loved being around people, he also "cherished" his alone time, and they began to see him struggle with his level of fame. "Robin's highs were so high and his lows were often low," his "Mork and Mindy" co-star Gina Hecht said during the documentary.
