# The Verge's Black Friday Deals Under $25

Every Black Friday and Cyber Monday there is a ton of hype about discounts on big-ticket items — deals on OLED TVs, game consoles, and tablets — but it never fails that many of the ultra-cheap items make for some of the most clutch deals, because spending very little on a small accessory you end up using all the time can be the most gratifying.

So throughout the annual shopping weekend, The Verge keeps an eye out for the best tech deals on little gadgets and gizmos under $25. It also has roundups for those working with $50 or $100 budgets, but the items in this one are pretty much as cheap as you can get in the tech world without simply buying absolute crap destined to end up as e-waste.

The budget gems include fully compatible MagSafe chargers for the latest iPhones, a colorful smart speaker, Apple's handy AirTag trackers, and some staff favorites.
