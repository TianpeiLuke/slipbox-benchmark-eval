# Cat Quest 2

Cat Quest 2 is a criminally underrated action game that puts you in the shoes of a cat fighting their way through the pun-filled land of Felingard — and player two gets to be a dog. It appears in Polygon's 2023-10-20 list of the 35 best couch co-op games for Nintendo Switch.

Playing is a simple grind in this approachable and silly RPG.

Players fight their way through dungeons, meet necromancer cats, and try on lots of outfits.
