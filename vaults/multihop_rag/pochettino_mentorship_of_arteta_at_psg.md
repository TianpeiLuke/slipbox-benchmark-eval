# Pochettino's Mentorship Of Arteta At PSG

Mauricio Pochettino took the teenage Mikel Arteta under his wing at Paris Saint-Germain in 2001, when Arteta had arrived on loan from Barcelona's B side and the two new recruits lived together in a hotel for three months — a period Arteta described on 20 October 2023, ahead of their first meeting as head coaches, as key to his career.

Arteta was struggling to come to terms with the transition to his first experience in the first team of a top-flight side, and Pochettino, ten years his senior, provided the support. "It was my first professional opportunity in Paris and we arrived at the same time and lived together in a hotel for three months," Arteta said on the Friday. "He was critical, he has been one of the most influential people in my career, firstly as a player, he took me under the arm and looked after me like a little child, a little brother. He was a big part of the success I had in Paris. It was because of him because he really looked after me, gave me a lot of confidence and a lot of advice." He expanded on the circumstances: "I went from playing Ligue 1 to a week later Champions League in Milan, I was all over the place. I didn't speak the language, it was my first professional club and I needed someone to be close to me, give me confidence, give me support, because I was thrown in there and they said 'play, you're in the starting XI'. So he was coaching me, talking to me. Really, really inspirational. He was really supportive. I was living with him for 2-3 months so that period was key in my career to be able to make it."

Arteta described the method as a mixture of encouragement and discipline: "He's an expert at giving you confidence and then keeping you on your toes all the time and being really harsh on me every time I was doing something: 'Where have you been last night?!' He really looked after me. The way he understood the game was phenomenal. I used to have him at my back and he was constantly coaching me. Very proud of what he has done and the way he has done it throughout his coaching career. I'm so grateful for what he did for me and the way he guided me in those early years. He got something else out of me that was needed in that period to make it and stayed with me in my football career as a player and coach." Speaking ahead of Arsenal's trip to Stamford Bridge, Arteta — who labelled Pochettino's early work at Chelsea "phenomenal" — said that influence continued from those early days through his playing career and into coaching. Pochettino has equally fond recollections: "Amazing memories. We share values and the passion for football. He's part of my family from day one. We arrived in Paris together and we spent time together with our families, first of all in the hotel and then we lived very close. It was an amazing history. He's not surprised me with what he's doing because he was a coach already at 17!" The two knew each other inside out before meeting as head coaches for the first time on the Saturday, with the Argentinean saying "He's part of my family."
