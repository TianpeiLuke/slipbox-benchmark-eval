# Sherrone Moore's Turn As Michigan's Acting Head Coach

Sherrone Moore filled in for the suspended Jim Harbaugh as Michigan's coach for the Penn State game and, by Sporting News' reckoning, turned it into a fitting audition for a head-coaching job of his own. The Wolverines rushed for 227 yards — including 32 straight run plays — in grinding out a top-10 victory against the Nittany Lions at Beaver Stadium, one of the toughest environments in the FBS.

Moore was emotional afterward, complete with NSFW language. In the Fox postgame interview with Jenny Taft he made Harbaugh ever-present in the moment: "I want to thank the Lord and I want to thank Coach Harbaugh," Moore said. "I f------ love you man. I love the s--- out of you, man. I did this for you, for this university, the president, our AD. We've got the best players, best university, and the best alumni in the country. I love these guys. These f------ guys right here. These guys right here. These guys did it."

Sporting News judged that Moore would emerge as a leading candidate for some jobs as a result, a huge victory for one of the nation's top assistants.
