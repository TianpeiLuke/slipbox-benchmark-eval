# Which Team-Level Props To Play In Chiefs-Jets Week 4

Sporting News assessed four team-level "Prop Stars" markets for the Week 4 Chiefs-Jets game on 2023-09-28, passing on three and taking one long shot. "Seeing Red: Chiefs to Win by 22+" at +380 — pass: for the bet to cash, Wilson would have to be atrocious again considering New York's defense isn't a pushover, the Jets often like to make games close even when they seemingly have no business doing so, and even if they are steamrolled a garbage-time score is more than enough to ruin the wager. "Running Away Together: Chiefs to have 150+ Total Rushing Yards" at +420 — pass: the strength of the Jets roster is along the defensive line while the Chiefs are a pass-first team that loves to light it up through the air; this is the first time Patrick Mahomes is playing in the New York area, he'll want to put on a show, Andy Reid isn't going to hold him back, and the Chiefs shouldn't start running until it's time to chew the clock.

"Plot Twist: Zach Wilson to have 300+ Passing Yards" at +1800 — pass: if you asked the average Jets fan what is holding this roster back, the overwhelming answer would be Wilson, and "I'm the problem, it's me" would probably qualify as his walk-up song; the Jets need to let him sling the ball around instead of worrying about bad turnovers, because they aren't winning this game 14-10 and aren't winning anything if they can't let their quarterback be who he is — but the writers are absolutely not betting it. "Exes & 0s: NY Jets to Score 0 Total Points" at +3400 — bet on it, with a small wager: the Jets are back in a familiar place as the laughingstock of the NFL, and while shutting out NFL teams is no easy task, the Chiefs defense is much improved and the Jets look like a team that doesn't have a quarterback; it is not considered likely to hit, but +3400 wagers hardly ever are, so consider it a fun bet — unless you're a Jets fan — and see how long Kansas City can keep New York off the scoreboard.
