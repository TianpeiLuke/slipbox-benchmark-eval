# Line Shopping: Comparing Sportsbooks To Maximize Your Return

"Line shopping" means researching several different sportsbooks to compare odds, so you find the best price for the bets you were already planning to make and maximize your potential return. Not all sportsbook betting odds are created equal, so if you are willing to put in some legwork you can shop for the best lines.

The procedure has two routes. You can use an odds feed — such as an NBA odds page — to find the best lines for a prospective bet, or you can open accounts at multiple online sportsbooks or betting apps and do the shopping yourself, comparing odds across them.

The reason prices differ is that lines move. Movement depends on multiple factors including team news, injuries, public betting, recent play, and even the number of games a team plays in a row, and oddsmakers use all available information to craft their lines — which is where the value in shopping around comes from. The trade-off is time: this can be a time-consuming process, but it helps boost your bankroll and give yourself the largest possible profit.
