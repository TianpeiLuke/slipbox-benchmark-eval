# Zenly's Acquisition And Shutdown By Snap

Much of the hype and anticipation surrounding Amo's launch comes from its founders' history. Amo was co-founded by Antoine Martin, who was the co-founder of Zenly with Alexis Bonillo. Zenly was a popular social app focused on location sharing that encouraged you to spend more time with your friends and discover new places.

Snap spent more than $200 million to acquire Zenly and kept the same team to iterate on it, as a separate app. Under Snap's ownership, Zenly became one of Europe's biggest social apps of all time; at its peak the company had 18 million different users opening the app every day.

And then it disappeared. As part of Snap's cost-cutting efforts, the company decided to shut down Zenly altogether. From what TechCrunch's writer has heard, this move even triggered discussions between French politicians at the highest level and Snap CEO Evan Spiegel.
