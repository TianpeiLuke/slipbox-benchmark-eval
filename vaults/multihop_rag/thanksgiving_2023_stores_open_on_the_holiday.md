# Thanksgiving 2023: Stores Open on the Holiday

A number of US retailers stayed open on Thanksgiving Day 2023, generally on reduced or location-dependent hours: Whole Foods, Wegmans, Starbucks, Dollar General, most CVS locations, and Dunkin' Donuts.

Whole Foods was set to be open during the holiday season, but running on a limited schedule with hours of operation that may vary, so shoppers were advised to check their local store; Whole Foods stores were also open on Black Friday, with hours depending on location. Wegmans was open on Thanksgiving with limited hours — one Wegmans store in Brooklyn, New York, closed at 4pm instead of 11pm as it usually does — and opening and closing times vary by location; most Wegmans stores were expected to reopen at 6am on Black Friday, again with location-specific times.

A spokesperson for Starbucks told Country Living earlier in November that stores would be open on Thanksgiving Day; its usual hours are 6am to 9pm, but holiday opening and closing times vary by location, and it was expected to run 6am to 9pm on Black Friday with store-specific variation. Dollar General, the low-priced retailer, was open from 7am to 10pm on Thanksgiving Day, with times potentially varying by location and details available on the store's website; it was expected to keep its usual hours on Black Friday, again location-dependent. A representative with CVS told ThePioneerWoman.com that most, but not all, CVS locations would be open on Thanksgiving, with shoppers directed to the CVS website to check their local store; on Black Friday, CVS locations were open during regular hours. Dunkin' Donuts was open on Thanksgiving Day but with limited hours that vary by location, and reopened on Black Friday with its usual hours based on each store.
