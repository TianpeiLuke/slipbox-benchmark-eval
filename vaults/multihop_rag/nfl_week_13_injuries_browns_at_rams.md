# NFL Week 13 Injuries Browns At Rams

In the Week 13 final injury reports published by CBSSports.com on Dec. 1, 2023, with the Rams listed at -3.5 against the Browns, Cleveland ruled out QB Dorian Thompson-Robinson (concussion), WR Marquise Goodwin (concussion) and CB Denzel Ward (shoulder), and listed C Nick Harris (knee) as questionable; the Rams ruled out S Quentin Lake (hamstring) and listed OLB Michael Hoecht (knee) as questionable.

The Browns officially ruled out Thompson-Robinson due to a concussion he suffered the previous week against Denver, which would thrust veteran Joe Flacco in as the starter for Cleveland in Los Angeles. For the Rams, Hoecht had not practiced the last two days after being a limited participant on Wednesday.
