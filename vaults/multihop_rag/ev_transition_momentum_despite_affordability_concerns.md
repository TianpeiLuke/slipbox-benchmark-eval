# EV Transition Momentum Despite Affordability Concerns

The Verge argued that nothing is likely to stop the EV train now that it's in motion, despite affordability remaining a major concern.

The grounds start by defusing the affordability objection: vehicle prices are going up across the board, both for EVs and for gas vehicles, and automakers are still losing billions of dollars each year on EVs, with their gas-vehicle profits redirected into the enormous investments needed to fund the transition. The warrant for the momentum claim is the alignment of the actors who could stop it: the automakers are committed, and UAW leadership wants to ensure that workers get to share the profits. China looms large over everything, producing the bulk of the components that go into EVs — a major concern of the union.
