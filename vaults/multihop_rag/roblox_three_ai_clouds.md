# Roblox's Three AI Clouds

David Baszucki described Roblox's AI work as three big AI clouds that accelerate creation and support the platform. The first has been there for two or three years and no one knows about it: safety, automatic translation, moderation, and efficiency, with roughly 70 AI pipelines driving the efficiency and quality of the business. The middle cloud, where a lot of the energy is right now, is generative: making creation easy for everyone. His example is fashion — in addition to competing on Project Runway using digital scissors and a digital sewing machine, a creator would also use prompts, asking for a blue shirt with buttons of a certain color and having AI start to generate it. Generative AI is interesting for avatars, clothing, and 3D experiences to bring creation to everyone, and Roblox is deep in on it, having already shipped AI code generation to help creators create and AI material generation; a company with a lot of user data flowing, he said, can help support and train those types of models.

The third cloud is the far-off one. "There's a third cloud way out there, which is starting to imagine having a virtual doppelganger," Baszucki said — someone to take your place, or to meet another person for five minutes before a meeting just to figure things out. That is not just generative AI but generative AI that might look and act like you in a virtual space, which he called the more sci-fi future. Asked whether that meant an AI trained on his Roblox persona and data to be like him, he said, "That's right. If you so choose and you so want that, then that could be an opportunity."

The framing came at the Code Conference, where AI was a theme; The Verge summarised his position as support for AI being woven all through Roblox's business, starting with largely invisible functions like efficiency, translation, and safety, while he also sees a lot of opportunity for generative AI to help content creators in the not-so-distant future. The interviewer's reference point was the last season of Westworld, whose main character has a job talking to a computer to create virtual worlds as a narrator — something Baszucki had earlier said he sees happening on Roblox before long.
