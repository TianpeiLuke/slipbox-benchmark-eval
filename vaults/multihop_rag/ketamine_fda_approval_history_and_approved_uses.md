# Ketamine's FDA Approval History And Approved Uses

Ketamine is not a new or unstudied drug: it has been used for depression, post-traumatic stress disorder and pain for more than 10 years, according to Dr. Patrick Sullivan, medical director of Initia Nova Medical Solutions in New Jersey.

The FDA approved ketamine for anesthesia in both humans and animals since 1970, the doctor noted.

After successful clinical trials, the FDA approved the drug in 2019 as a medication for treatment-resistant depression. "There are hundreds of studies showing it to be safe and effective in the outpatient setting," Sullivan told Fox News Digital.
