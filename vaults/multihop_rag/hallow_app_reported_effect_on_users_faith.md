# The Hallow App's Reported Effect on Users' Faith

Jonathan Roumie reported in December 2023 that many people had told him they cultivated a relationship with Jesus through prayer because they heard him talk about the Hallow app, or because they watched "The Chosen" and then started seeing the app pop up in other places.

"And they've gotten introduced to the app and to a more deeply cultivated prayer life — and in some cases it's been absolutely transformative for people," he said. "Some people who've never been to church started going to church. Some people who haven't been to church for 20 years have come back to the church. People have converted — and all because they've fallen in love with the richness, the depth and the tradition of the Catholic Church."

Roumie also set out the app's confessional scope: "And obviously [the Hallow app] is authentically Catholic, but it is for all Christians — Christians of all denominations. There's something there for everybody, which is what makes the app so universally appealing."
