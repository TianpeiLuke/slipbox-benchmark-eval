# Owen Teale's Family And Recent Villain Roles

Owen Teale is a Welsh character actor whose recent parts are a run of menacing men — Lars Hutton in The Rig, Ser Alliser Thorne in Game of Thrones, Chief Inspector Philip Osborne in Line of Duty and the villainous sorcerer Peter Knox in A Discovery of Witches — The Sydney Morning Herald reported on 20 October 2023.

Reflecting on the chance encounter that sent him to drama school, he says: "I was going nowhere really. If I hadn't met those two young women I don't know what would have happened. But there have been so many points in my career, moments of pure chance, when things went my way. And if you can survive the moments when things don't go your way, you can make it." If you are not a diehard Game of Thrones fan his name may not be familiar, but his pale face, intense eyes and imposing physicality — he is 188cm in his socks — will be; he is a Zelig-like figure whose frequent stage and screen appearances invariably guarantee two things: the tale will be well told and bad stuff is going to happen.

He spoke from Leeds, where he was visiting his younger daughter Gracie, 21, who is studying art and design at the city's university; Eliza, 25, his other daughter with actor wife Sylvestra Le Touzel, is following in her parents' footsteps, and his son Ion, from his first marriage, recently made him a grandfather. Earlier that day he had been in Edinburgh shooting scenes for the second season of the supernatural TV thriller The Rig, in which his character Lars Hutton — an irascible bully and the man most likely to be chucked off the oil rig — has somehow made it back for the next instalment of the Amazon Prime drama.
