# Vudu Digital Movie Storefront

Vudu is like a digital Blockbuster: a storefront with a huge catalog of films you can rent or buy, ranging from new releases such as Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles: Mutant Mayhem to beloved classics such as The Nightmare Before Christmas.

Its distinguishing feature as a gift is that, unlike some other gift cards on the market, Vudu's do not expire — so you can take your time and save them for a rainy day. Polygon frames them as compelling gifts for the cinephile in your life.
