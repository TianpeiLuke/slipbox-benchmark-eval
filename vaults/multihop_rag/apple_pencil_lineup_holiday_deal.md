# Apple Pencil Lineup Holiday Deal

Apple now makes three different Apple Pencils: the original recharges via Lightning, the second generation recharges wirelessly, and the new USB-C model looks a lot like the first generation but charges via USB-C instead of Lightning. Each one has different iPad compatibility, which Engadget calls confusing, though the pencil itself is rather elegant and one of the best accessories for an iPad.

Over Black Friday 2023 the 1st gen pencil was on sale for $73 at Amazon, the 2nd generation stylus was down to $89, and the USB-C model, with an MSRP of $79, was down to $71 at Amazon. By Cyber Monday the 2nd generation stylus was down to $80, with the USB-C model still at $71 at Amazon.
