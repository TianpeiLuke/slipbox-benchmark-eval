# Snapchat Design Features Alleged To Enable Drug Sales

The lawsuit against Snap claims that Snapchat's features facilitate practices like drug sales by connecting dealers to young customers while promising them safety from legal repercussions through anonymity.

Chief among those designs, according to Matthew Bergman, the lead attorney on the case, is the promise that a message will disappear not only for fellow users but also on the software's back end. That is the ground that turns a product feature into an enabler of crime: it prevents law enforcement officials from seeing the activity of a dealer even after the dealer has been identified.

The complaint names other features as problematic too: notifying individuals when another person screenshots their post, the ability to geolocate fellow users, and algorithms that suggest new connections based on demographics.
