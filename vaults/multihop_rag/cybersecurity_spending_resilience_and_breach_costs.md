# Cybersecurity Spending Resilience And Breach Costs

Security continues to be a huge priority for enterprises and smaller businesses, and it is one area where companies have returned to spending even when other IT categories have remained frozen or constrained.

The cost trend is the driver. A McKinsey report from the previous year notes that breaches are on track to collectively cost $10.5 trillion annually by 2025, a 300% increase on 2015 figures. While a lot of companies clamped down on spending and IT budgets over the previous couple of years, security budgets came back. "For end customers, security is still a big business risk, so budgets are back in action and we're seeing sales picking up in Q3 and Q4," one source told TechCrunch. "Security companies will want to tap into this opportunity aggressively."
