# The Beatles' "Now And Then" 2023 Release

The Beatles' CMG-issued comeback single "Now And Then", released in November 2023, recently hit No.1 in multiple territories including the UK, Germany and Austria. Michelle Jubelirer says she would never in a million years have thought she would be involved in releasing a brand-new single from The Beatles: she and Arjun Pulijal got a preview of the song from Jeff Jones at Apple Corp earlier in the year and began planning and strategizing on a global level with him, along with UMG UK and Universal Music Enterprises President and CEO Bruce Resnikoff and his team.

She calls the whole experience a "pinch me" moment, saying The Beatles mean the world to her as a massive lifelong fan and are such an important part of the company's DNA, and noting that she was thrilled to sign Paul McCartney directly to Capitol in 2016, that he is an important frontline artist who continues to make amazing music and has achieved some of his biggest solo success in recent years including back-to-back #1 albums. The campaign goal for "Now And Then" was to highlight the storytelling around the creation of the song while also ensuring it was treated like a brand-new record, albeit 50 years in the making, that should become an important part of the cultural conversation. Jubelirer describes it as surreal to see a new Beatles song in 2023 as the cover of New Music Friday on Spotify, trending creations on short-form platforms, played across all current formats of radio, synced across network TV and topping single charts, and says she is proud that Capitol helped present it to the world and that her team, led by Arjun, was integral to creating a global campaign worthy of the historic nature of the release; being involved has been a real career highlight for her.
