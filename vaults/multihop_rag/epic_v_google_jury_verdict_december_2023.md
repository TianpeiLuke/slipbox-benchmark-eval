# Epic's Jury Verdict Against Google, December 2023

On Monday, a federal jury sided with Fortnite maker Epic Games in its fight to break Google's control over Android apps, surprising the world. The nine-person jury decided that Google has an illegal monopoly over Android app distribution and in-app payment systems, and that Google illegally tied its Google Play billing system to its app store.

The outcome was striking because "walled garden" rival Apple almost entirely won a similar case two years earlier. For Tim Sweeney, this was, at last, a win.
