# Travis Etienne Jaguars Midseason MVP

Yardbarker's Nov. 9, 2023 AFC midseason team MVP list named running back Travis Etienne the Jacksonville Jaguars' MVP: quarterback Trevor Lawrence had not quite taken the step forward everyone thought he would, but fortunately for the Jags, Etienne was emerging as one of the top backs in the league.

The grounds tie his scoring to the team's wins: he had rushed for multiple touchdowns in three of his past four games and scored a touchdown in five of the team's six wins, ranking third in the NFL in rushing touchdowns with seven and sixth in rushing yards with 583. The warrant is that with the quarterback's development stalled, the back who scores in nearly every win is the one carrying the team.
