# Britney Spears And Kevin Federline: Marriage And Divorce

Britney Spears and Kevin Federline married in September 2004 after just three months of dating, in a surprise ceremony in Studio City, California, and Spears filed for divorce in November 2006 citing "irreconcilable differences." The two had met at a club in 2004 — Joseph's, in Hollywood — while Federline was working as a backup dancer for Justin Timberlake. Federline recalled the meeting to People in 2008: "I met her at a club in Hollywood, Joseph's. Our eyes met and that was it. We just hit it off right away. I learned real fast how much of a whirlwind the press and everything was." He was 26 at the time of the wedding; the "Toxic" and "Gimme More" singer was 22. Earlier that same year Spears had married her childhood friend Jason Alexander, a marriage annulled after 55 hours.

The couple's first child, son Sean Preston, was born weeks before their first wedding anniversary, and their second son, Jayden James, arrived in 2006. Federline was already a parent to daughter Kori Madison and son Kaleb Michael with ex-fiancée Shar Jackson — reported as 20 and 18 in the 18 October account and as 21 and 19 in the 20 October one. Spears filed for divorce just eight weeks after the birth of their second child. The initial divorce settlement, finalised in July 2007, granted the pair joint legal and physical custody of their two children, and relations appeared cordial until 2018, when Federline requested an increase of $20,000 a month in child support payments from Spears.
