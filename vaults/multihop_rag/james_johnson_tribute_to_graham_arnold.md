# James Johnson Tribute To Graham Arnold

Football Australia CEO James Johnson paid tribute to Graham Arnold in a statement marking the coach's milestone, calling him one of the greatest contributors to the Socceroos, as reported by The Roar on Nov. 13, 2023.

"Graham is one of the greatest contributors to the Socceroos," Johnson said. "He's been a constant in the Australian football landscape for more than 30 years as both a player and coach, being a part of some of the Socceroos most memorable moments. Anyone who knows Arnie will tell you he bleeds green and gold. His love for the Socceroos and football in Australia is both infectious and inspiring. That energy and passion is a huge element to the way he's able to lead and inspire our nation's best male footballers in some of our sport's most challenging arenas. On behalf of Football Australia, I'd like to congratulate Arnie on everything he's achieved in reaching this milestone and we look forward to him continuing to build on what is already an exceptional legacy."
