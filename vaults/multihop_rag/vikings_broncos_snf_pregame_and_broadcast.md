# Vikings-Broncos Sunday Night Pregame And Broadcast

Before kickoff on Nov. 19, 2023, Vikings starting running back Alexander Mattison was confirmed active for Sunday Night Football after going through concussion protocol that week, as Adam Schefter reported. Broncos legend Peyton Manning showed some love to Vikings star receiver Justin Jefferson ahead of the game; Jefferson remained out with a hamstring injury.

The broadcast aired on NBC nationally, with viewers also able to stream the game on Fubo, which offers a free trial, in the U.S., and on DAZN, which carries NFL Game Pass, in Canada. Mike Tirico called play-by-play, with Cris Collinsworth as analyst and Melissa Stark reporting.
