# Twilight (1998)

Robert Benton's Twilight is not the sparkle-vampire film of the same name but a celebrity-crammed, steamy crime drama, and Polygon's Tasha Robinson recommends springing it on a Noirvember buddy or viewing group at the last minute with the line "Hey, we're watching Twilight tonight!" It streams free with a library card on Hoopla and is rentable on Amazon, Vudu, and YouTube.

Her joke about the risk — that if they do not break your neck and throw you under a train, Double Indemnity-style, they will probably thank you afterward — sets up the actual recommendation: a surprisingly winning and winningly surprising crime drama starring Paul Newman, Susan Sarandon, Gene Hackman, Reese Witherspoon, Stockard Channing, James Garner, Giancarlo Esposito, Liev Schreiber, and Margo Martindale, among others. It is, she writes, the kind of movie where you can play "Oh wow, they're in this movie too?" in almost every scene.
