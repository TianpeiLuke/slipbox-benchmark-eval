# Cale Makar OVER 2.5 Shots On Goal

Sporting News' fourth pick for the Nov. 7, 2023 NHL slate was Cale Makar, D, Avalanche vs. Devils — OVER 2.5 shots on goal. Makar had gone over the number in each of the last two games, though fans would want to see him shoot a little more after going under in five of the previous six before that hot streak.

The matchup argument is that facing a Devils team reeling from injuries should help, with Colorado able to control the play in the Mile High City. Makar is the team's top defenseman and a key piece of its power play, so barring something unforeseen he will have plenty of opportunities to find three shots within the 60 minutes — and with 5.9 shot attempts per game, it is only a matter of time before they start finding their way on goal.
