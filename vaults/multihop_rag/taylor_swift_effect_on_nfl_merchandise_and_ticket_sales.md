# The Taylor Swift Effect On NFL Merchandise And Ticket Sales

Since Taylor Swift attended the Chiefs game on 24 September 2023 there was a 400% increase in Travis Kelce jersey sales, according to Front Office Sports figures cited in CBS Sports' timeline published 12 October 2023.

The same set of figures, dated 25 September, records that Kelce's podcast went No. 1 overall on Apple, he gained over 380,000 Instagram followers, and 24.3 million people watched the game, making it the No. 1 game of that week. There was also triple the "Chiefs" searches on the web and triple the Chiefs ticket sales on StubHub.

The effect extended to other teams' gates. On 26 September the timeline notes that the defending Super Bowl champions were already one of the more popular teams in the league but were becoming the hottest ticket in town, no matter what town that was: "Since Swift's appearance at the Chiefs game on Sunday, the Jets have sold more tickets for their game against the Chiefs in a single day than since the season started -- by more than double," a representative at StubHub told Town & Country magazine.
