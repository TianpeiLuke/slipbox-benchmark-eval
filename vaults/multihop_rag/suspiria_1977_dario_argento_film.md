# Suspiria: Argento's Witch Academy Defined By Colour And Goblin's Score

Suspiria, directed by Dario Argento and starring Jessica Harper, Stefania Casini and Flavio Bucci, is available on Tubi and Kanopy. Polygon calls it one of the best-looking movies of all time with one of the best soundtracks of all time. It tells the story of Suzy Bannion, an American dancer who moves to Germany to study at the prestigious Tanz Akademie — an academy that just so happens to be run by witches; as the facade of the school unravels, Suzy's fellow students slowly start going missing or dropping dead in increasingly bizarre and horrible ways.

The discriminating feature is not the plot but the presentation. While the plot is interesting, what really makes the movie great is how it looks and how it sounds: everything about the production design, the costumes and the colours is eccentric in ways no other horror movie has ever matched. Couple that with the incredible and haunting soundtrack from European rock band Goblin and Suspiria becomes an unforgettable horror classic that everyone should see at least once.
