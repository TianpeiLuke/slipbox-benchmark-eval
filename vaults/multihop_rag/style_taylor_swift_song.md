# Style: Named For Harry Styles, Explained As A Song About Fashion

Style is an epic aural painting of windswept coastlines and propulsive wanderlust, supposedly named for Harry Styles, with whom Taylor Swift had a brief relationship in 2012 — many of 1989's songs were supposedly inspired by that relationship. Swift's own account differs: she says the song was inspired by fashion, and how certain items like a black dress or red lipstick will "never go out of style", which prompted her to write about the people who'll always be part of your life even after the flame of romantic love wanes. The BBC notes that this all sounds like a classic piece of deflection to a probing journalist.

Whichever interpretation you prefer, the song marks a shift in Swift's psychology, as she briefly admits to having been unfaithful — the discriminating feature that separates it from her earlier writing. "I would never have said anything like that on a previous album," she told Ryan Seacrest. "My previous albums have always been, 'I was right, you were wrong'. What happens when you grow up is you realise that the rules in a relationship are very blurred. It gets very complicated, very quickly."
