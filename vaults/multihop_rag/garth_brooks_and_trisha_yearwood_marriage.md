# Garth Brooks And Trisha Yearwood's Marriage

Garth Brooks and Trisha Yearwood married in 2005 and had been together 18 years as of the November 2023 article, with Yearwood becoming a bonus mom to his three grown daughters from his first marriage. The couple have been candid about the union, and its defining contrast is with Brooks's earlier marriage: the "Friends in Low Places" singer told Billboard of his first marriage, to Sandy Maul, which ended in a messy divorce in 2000, "I sucked at being a husband, I was horrible at it. I was horrible at being a dad. I had to get my s--- together."

Each partner offers a different account of why this marriage works. Brooks attributes it to the choice of person: "The person you're with [does make] a huge difference in your life." Yearwood, the "She's in Love With the Boy" singer, told E! News on the ACM Awards red carpet that the secret to their long-lasting marriage is that the relationship is "always a first date, you know? It just really is."
