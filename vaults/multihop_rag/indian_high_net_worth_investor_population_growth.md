# Growth In India's High-Net-Worth Investor Population

According to recent wealth reports cited by Business Line on 28 October 2023, the population of Indian high-net-worth individuals holding assets worth $1 million or more, which was 7.9 lakh in 2022, will rise to 16.5 lakh, a 107 per cent increase in five years.

The observation is offered as the consequence of a cause: India's remarkable economic growth has resulted in a notable increase in the country's high-net-worth investors. The figure is a projection from wealth reports rather than a realised count, and its horizon is five years from the 2022 base.
