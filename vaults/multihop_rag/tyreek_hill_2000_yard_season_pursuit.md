# Tyreek Hill 2,000-Yard Season Pursuit

CBSSports.com's Dec. 19, 2023 Week 16 wide receiver preview reported that after missing the previous week's game, Tyreek Hill needed to average almost 153 yards per game — 152.67 — in his final three games to reach his stated goal of 2,000 receiving yards that season.

The adjacent note in the same list recorded that Puka Nacua needed 18 more catches to set the rookie record for most catches in a season.
