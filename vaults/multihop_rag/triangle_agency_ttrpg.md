# Triangle Agency

Triangle Agency is a TTRPG in which you play super-abled agents investigating and stopping ontological anomalies from destroying reality, with mechanics its Polygon contributor calls the platonic ideal mix of analytic crunch and grand narrative weirdness. Their capsule verdict, recorded 10 minutes after a first session, was "I laughed so hard my face hurt."

The materials set the tone: written in the form of a cheerful, slightly threatening employee handbook, the Field Agent Manual guides you through creating your supernaturally powered character and then readies you to find and contain Anomalies — after your team's morning meeting, of course. In play, the mix of mundane life, weird occurrences, and corporate BS created a fast-paced experience that was playful and easy to understand, got every player invested in their characters and each other even in a group of total strangers, and let the whole table lean into the strange and ridiculous nature of life. The contributor frames 2023 as a personal tabletop gold mine — Brennan Lee Mulligan's first official 5e homebrew arrived, they discovered the meetup hub StartPlaying.Games, and they played a lot of the party game As You 'Wich — but calls Triangle Agency the fresh grand dame of their tabletop world, a gleaming, razor-sharp new TTRPG that feels like a cross between The X-Files, Annihilation, and playing Control with the Robert Anton Wilson mod turned on. On availability: the full game ships early in 2024, but the digital rules can be had right now for "whatever you think is appropriate," and the art and design put it on the same shelf as Wanderhome and Mörk Borg. The contributor discloses they picked up the player guide in beta form earlier that year to prep for writing a published adventure for the game.
