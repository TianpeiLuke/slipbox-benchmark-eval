# Prosecution And Defense Arguments On Day Two Of The SBF Trial

On the second day of the trial of Sam Bankman-Fried and FTX, the prosecution argued that Bankman-Fried knowingly committed fraud, and it grounded that claim in motive: he did so, prosecutors said, to achieve great wealth, power and influence.

The defense countered with the opposite reading of the same conduct. Its claim was that the FTX founder acted in good faith, never meant to commit fraud or to steal, and basically got in over his head — an argument whose warrant is that inexperience and overreach, rather than intent, explain what happened. The two sides therefore contest intent rather than the underlying events, and the reporting on this exchange comes from Rebecca and Jacquelyn in TechCrunch's Week in Review of October 7, 2023, covering day two of the trial only.
