# Waking Art Cullagh With The Battered Lute

To wake Art Cullagh in Baldur's Gate 3 you must retrieve the Battered Lute from the undead surgeon Malus Thorm at the House of Healing near Moonrise Towers, and the sequence only begins once you have reached the Last Light Inn in the Shadow-cursed Lands.

The steps run as follows. First, head back to your camp and talk to Halsin, who tells you the tale of a young man named Thaniel, a Tiefling who is the root of the curse taking over the Shadow-cursed Land. Then return to the Last Light Inn and visit the bunks on the first floor, just to the right of the entrance, where Art Cullagh lies in bed with a swirly animation above him; talking to him gets him muttering about Thaniel, and walking through the dialogue reveals that he needs some kind of personal item to wake him. Next, return to Halsin in camp; the Druid decides he needs to see the sleeping man himself, so jump back to the Last Light Inn and talk to Art again with Halsin's help. Read Art's last orders letter and a quest marker near Moonrise Towers appears; make your way there. In the town, near the Road to Baldur's Gate Waypoint, you find the House of Healing and the cemetery connected to it. Inside you are directed to the operating theater and the "doctor" on staff. Make your way into the large room at the back of the hospital to meet Malus Thorm and his crew of assistants — Thorm being an undead boss and a "surgeon" torturing a patient. Talk to him enough and combat begins; kill Thorm and his cronies, loot his body once combat is over to take the Battered Lute he carries, and return to Art back in Last Light.
