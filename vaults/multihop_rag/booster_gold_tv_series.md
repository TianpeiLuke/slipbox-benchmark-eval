# Booster Gold TV Series

DC's Booster Gold TV series brings the comics' fan-favorite comic-relief character to the screen for his own adventures; as of Polygon's 2023-10-24 report the company had not said much about it.

The company's logline for the show is just "Booster Gold uses basic technology from the future to pretend to be a superhero in present day."

Polygon notes that this logline is pretty much Booster Gold's traditional comics depiction as well.
