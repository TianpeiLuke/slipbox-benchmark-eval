# FanDuel Sportsbook In Kentucky

FanDuel is a daily fantasy operator turned major sports betting player, offering a wide variety of markets, sports and leagues, and it is distinguished among Kentucky books by having no customer rewards program, as described by CBS Sports on 13 October 2023.

Its positive features are breadth and usability. FanDuel claims to be "America's #1 sportsbook" and offers a large menu of bet types; its promotions include odds boosts, parlay insurance, big win bonuses and more; its wagering menu is complex, similar to chief competitor DraftKings; and live betting markets and same game parlays are often enhanced with specific profit boosts, with an interface that is one of the cleanest and easiest to use among the offerings.

The boundary case is the reward gap: unlike its competitors, FanDuel does not offer a rewards program for its customers at this time.
