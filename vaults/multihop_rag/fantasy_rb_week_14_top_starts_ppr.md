# Week 14 PPR Running Backs To Start

Sporting News kept Christian McCaffrey atop its Week 14 PPR running back rankings on Dec. 7, 2023, arguing he had given no reason to stop believing in him as the best overall player in the NFL, in a week the outlet framed as the final week of the fantasy football regular season, when running back decisions in PPR leagues could very well impact playoff fate.

The season context was that the year had not been all holly, jolly — a plethora of injuries to stud running backs over the previous few weeks — with one major positive being that only two teams were on bye that week, a welcome relief after the six-team byepocalypse that was Week 13; still, injury-ravaged teams would have trouble replacing their studs and deep squads would have difficult start 'em, sit 'em decisions.

Austin Ekeler (vs. Broncos) had started to give some pause, averaging fewer than 3.0 yards per carry since coming off IR eight games earlier, but a juicy home matchup against Denver should be his get-right game: the Broncos had surrendered the most PPR points in the NFL to running backs, and the Chargers faced them twice between then and fantasy title week. With Jacksonville playing very well over the past month, Sporting News was all-in on Travis Etienne Jr. (@ Browns) even in a tough matchup, reasoning that Trevor Lawrence and his high-flying offense should dominate time of possession against the Joe Flacco-led Browns, leaving Etienne plenty of opportunities to add to his per-game PPR average of 17.4 points, third best in the NFL entering Week 13.

De'Von Achane (vs. Titans) went right back to roughing up the opposition in his Week 13 return from a knee injury that had sidelined him one week prior, putting up 103 yards and two touchdowns on the Commanders, so Sporting News placed him and running mate Raheem Mostert in the top five despite a somewhat-tough matchup with Tennessee: Mostert had more PPR points than everyone but McCaffrey, while Achane ranked third in PPR points per game at 20.6, two spots behind McCaffrey. Detroit's Jahmyr Gibbs and David Montgomery (@ Bears) also made the top 10, having ravaged Chicago when they last met a few weeks earlier by combining for 193 yards and two touchdowns, with Dan Campbell's squad having just put 142 rushing yards and 33 points on the Saints in the Bayou. Alvin Kamara (vs. Panthers) had been a source of light for an otherwise-dismal Saints offense, and with Derek Carr suffering a brutal blow in the Week 13 loss to Detroit, Kamara would likely shoulder the load against Carolina and its atrocious run defense — entering Week 13, only two teams had allowed more PPR points than the Panthers at 28.9 fantasy points per game. Sporting News also loved Saquon Barkley (vs. Packers) against the Packers' leaky run defense and Breece Hall (vs. Texans) in what should be a bounce-back game, since entering Week 13 Houston had allowed 112.9 scrimmage yards per game and nine total touchdowns to running backs on the year.
