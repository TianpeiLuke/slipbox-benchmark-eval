# Dillon Danis's Feud With The Paul Brothers Predates The Logan Paul Fight

Dillon Danis had been going back and forth with the Paul family long before his fight against Logan Paul scheduled for October 14th, Essentially Sports reported on 2023-10-07. At that moment Danis was preparing hard for his Misfits contest, having tried to get in the head of his opponent leading up to the bout — an effort that had earned 'El Jefe' over a million followers on X. He had been trying to dig up the past relationships of Logan Paul's fiancée, Nina Agdal, as a way to create turmoil in Paul's mindset during the fight.

The article's key claim is about origin: this beef was first started by Logan Paul's younger brother, Jake Paul, who called out Danis on his Instagram story in 2020 by taking a hit at his rumored ex-girlfriend, Savannah Montano. That means Danis's targeting of Agdal could have a revenge angle that many did not know about. Even Logan addressed the topic of Montano and Jake during their first press conference.
