# ICC World Cup 2023: Top Ten Run Scorers

As listed on 18 November 2023, the top ten run scorers of the ICC Men's ODI World Cup 2023 were: 1. V Kohli (IND), 11 matches, 765 runs; 2. RG Sharma (IND), 11 matches, 597 runs; 3. Q de Kock (SA), 10 matches, 594 runs; 4. R Ravindra (NZ), 10 matches, 578 runs; 5. DJ Mitchell (NZ), 10 matches, 552 runs; 6. DA Warner (AUS), 11 matches, 535 runs; 7. SS Iyer (IND), 11 matches, 530 runs; 8. KL Rahul (IND), 11 matches, 452 runs; 9. HE van der Dussen (SA), 10 matches, 448 runs; 10. MR Marsh (AUS), 10 matches, 441 runs.

The accompanying player notes add detail, and in several cases quote run totals slightly different from the table. Virat Kohli, described as one of the best batsmen ever to grace the sport, topped the list with 765 runs in 11 matches, with five fifties and three centuries and a highest score of 117. Indian captain Rohit Sharma had been on all-out attack since the start of the campaign, smashing 597 runs in 11 matches with one century and three fifties. Quinton de Kock, a pivotal part of the South African top order, scored 594 runs in 10 matches and had the most centuries in the tournament with four. The 23-year-old all-rounder Rachin Ravindra, called the biggest find of the year's World Cup, smashed three centuries and two fifties for 578 runs in 10 matches. Daryl Mitchell, a powerhouse for the Kiwis in the middle order, is described as scoring 520 runs in nine matches with a high score of 130. David Warner, one of the most destructive openers of the modern era, is described as scoring 528 runs in ten innings with a highest score of 163 plus two centuries and two fifties. Shreyas Iyer, called the backbone of the Indian middle order by head coach Rahul Dravid, is described as scoring 526 runs in 10 matches with three fifties and two centuries. KL Rahul, the Indian number five, scored 452 runs with one century and two fifties. Rassie van der Dussen, a support system for the Proteas middle order and sometimes inconsistent, accumulated 448 runs in 10 innings with two centuries, two fifties and a high score of 133. Mitchell Marsh, playing as opener in the absence of Travis Head, accumulated 441 runs in 10 matches while providing good starts.
