# The FTX Trial's Stakes For The Wider Crypto Industry

The Verge argued that the trial of Sam Bankman-Fried is likely to be more consequential than just whether the man himself is found guilty: depending on what evidence is introduced, it could be rough for the entire crypto industry.

The grounds are the unpredictability of what a trial surfaces, voiced by Yesha Yadav, a law professor at Vanderbilt University: "How much damage can this trial do to the already beaten-down reputation of the industry at this point? This trial is going to be an excruciating moment for the industry because no one knows what kind of evidence might come out." The warrant joining that uncertainty to industry-wide stakes is that the industry's reputation is already beaten down, so evidence introduced against one exchange's founder lands on the whole sector rather than on him alone.
