# Final Fantasy 16

Final Fantasy 16, developed by Square Enix for PlayStation 5, is ranked 16 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31. Polygon's verdict is blunt: it kicks ass, taking players on a lavish, unadulterated Game of Thrones-esque adventure.

You play as a broody Clive Rosfield, a young man whose life's work is to protect his little brother Joshua; the story begins when Clive's life takes a turn for the worse and he vows to destroy the monster who ruined his and his family's legacy. Developed by Creative Business Unit III, Square Enix's internal team behind the MMORPG Final Fantasy 14, the game leans into patchwork territories of fantasy genre fare — palace intrigue, a whole lot of sex, and endless war between nations — then sprinkles in Final Fantasy elements like mother crystals, dazzling kaiju fights between summons known as Eikons in this iteration, and of course Chocobos.

Critic Ana Diaz notes the quality of the story in this long and linear character-driven RPG waxes and wanes, but calls the action combat among the best she has ever played: the gameplay grips you from the very beginning as Clive smoothly dashes, parries, and swings his giant sword and varied magic with a dazzling amount of style, and it did not just help her stick with the game but allowed her excitement to bubble over every time she took on a new mission.
