# Lions-Packers Week 4: Fourth Quarter Log

The fourth quarter ended Lions 34, Packers 20, per Sporting News' live log of 2023-09-28. At 10:36 p.m. Jordan Love scampered into the end zone from short distance to cut the Detroit lead, though he was held short of the end zone on the ensuing two-point effort — Lions 27, Packers 17, with Green Bay firmly back in it. At 10:43 p.m. the Lions began to salt the game away with strong running through the tackles, Montgomery and Gibbs creeping through the crevices to push Detroit down the field, plus an impressive toss from Goff into the outstretched hands of Reynolds, leaving the Lions on the doorstep of the red zone. At 10:50 p.m. Quay Walker leapt over a blocker on Riley Patterson's field goal attempt, prompting an unsportsmanlike penalty that put Detroit on the cusp of another score pending a Packers defensive rally. At 10:54 p.m. that penalty proved vital as Montgomery found pay dirt from a couple of yards out on fourth down, a gutsy call by Campbell that paid off in spades and extended the lead to 17 — Lions 34, Packers 17.

At 11:04 p.m. came an interception: Love missed his target and instead found the mitts of Jacobs for his second pick of the day, a strong showing by the Detroit defense that should have put things to bed. At 11:08 p.m., not so fast — a quick three-and-out gave Green Bay a glimmer, brightened by an impressive return by Keisean Nixon, with 3:30 left. At 11:11 p.m. Carlson's field goal try from distance made the deficit two possessions. At 11:12 p.m. the onside kick spilled out of bounds, with the Packers holding one timeout but Montgomery's fiery run through the tackles set to make it obsolete. At 11:19 p.m. the Lions completed yet another fourth down, officially putting the game on ice.
