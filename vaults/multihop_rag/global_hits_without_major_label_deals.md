# Global Hits Without Major Label Deals

Alejandra Olea argues that beyond Spanish-speaking artists there is something bigger at play: all around the world, more and more artists are able to reach massive levels of success, not only in their own markets but internationally, without the need for major label support and traditional label deals, and she sees this trend getting bigger and gaining more markets. The question that prompts the claim cites RAYE enjoying global success independently with "Escapism" and Oliver Anthony going to No.1 on the Billboard Hot 100 in the US earlier in 2023.

Her grounds are Believe's own roster across several territories. Iñigo Quintero is a fantastic example, but there have been similar success stories in other markets, notably France, where the Top 5 albums of the week of 16 June were all by artists signed to Believe directly or through their labels. Elsewhere in Europe, Blanco has been skyrocketing in the UK, while RAF Camora and HoodBlaq in Germany peaked at #1 in June 2023 in the Official German Album Charts with RAF Camora's "XV RR", a re-edition of his album "XV". Similarly, Indonesia's Nadhif Basalamah's "Penjaga Hati" was the #4 global viral song on Spotify in the week of 16 October and Idgitaf peaked at #1 on Spotify's Viral Charts. Another example is the Regional Mexican powerhouse Grupo Frontera, whose album "El Comienzo", released early August, peaked at #1 on Spotify's Top Album Debut USA, Top Album Debut Global, Top Songs Debut Global and Top Songs Debut USA, with over 1.6 billion streams on Spotify to date. The warrant is that these cases span markets and genres, so the pattern is not a Spanish-language peculiarity but a general shift.
