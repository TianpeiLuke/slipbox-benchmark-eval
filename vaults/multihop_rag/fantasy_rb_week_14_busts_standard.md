# Week 14 Standard-League Running Backs To Downgrade

Sporting News pushed back on several running backs whose Week 14 standard-league appeal looked better than the matchups warranted, publishing on Dec. 7, 2023.

The premise it attacks in each case is that a back's usual role guarantees production regardless of opponent. Sporting News was a tad worried about Buffalo second-year back James Cook (@ Chiefs), because Kansas City's front seven tends to be stout against speedsters but below-average against power backs; managers probably were not in a position to bench Cook, but should not expect massive numbers from a player who averaged just 2.7 yards per carry against the Eagles his last time out.

Zach Charbonnet (@ 49ers) faced a tough matchup and had a bruise on his knee, so Sporting News did not expect very fruitful results from the rookie if he started in Santa Clara. It also did not love Jerome Ford or Kareem Hunt (vs. Jaguars), since Jacksonville entered Week 12 allowing just 12.3 standard fantasy points per game to running backs, the third lowest in the NFL. And the standing verdict on Carolina held: do not start Chuba Hubbard or Miles Sanders (@ Saints) — it was even worse than usual that week, as New Orleans had a pretty hard-hitting front seven and the Bayou is no place for below-average offenses.
