# Team News For Jamaica vs Canada, First Leg

Both sides named 4-4-2 lineups for the CONCACAF Nations League quarterfinal first leg in Kingston. Jamaica (right to left) started Blake in goal — Leigh, Pinnock, Bernard, Lembikisa — Decordova-Reid, Latibeaudiere, Johnson, Bailey — Antonio, Nicholson. Canada (right to left) started Borjan in goal — Laryea, Vitoria, Miller, Adekugbe — Buchanan, Kone, Eustaquio, Davies — Larin, David.

Each team was missing a player. Jamaica manager Hallgrimsson described his side as being on a mission but faced the tie without New York Red Bulls forward Cory Burke because of a groin problem. Canada were without attacking midfielder Liam Millar, out with a calf injury; interim head coach Biello also said his players were on a "mission" to progress, adding: "We know that these matches will be against a solid opponent in difficult conditions. That is why we decided on an experienced and seasoned group of players who are used to high-pressure games."

There was one debut call-up and one piece of history in the buildup. Karoy Anderson, who plays for English club Charlton Athletic, was called up for the first time and told the Addicks' website: "I was just shocked. I was looking at it for a long time just taking it in and I told my mum and she started crying." The last time the two teams played was a momentous occasion for Canada — Les Rouges won 4-0 and qualified for the 2022 World Cup in the process, their first trip to the FIFA tournament in over 30 years.
