# Billie Jean King's Case That Women's Sports Are At An Investment Tipping Point

"We're kind of at a tipping point," Billie Jean King said. "People are actually looking at women's sports like a great investment." Her ground for the claim is the change she observes in who is putting money in: "We're really lucky to be with this investment group. The male allies we've had through the years have made such a difference. They have the money and the power. But we're getting there, getting more and more women investors, particularly in soccer."

She keeps the claim qualified rather than triumphant. Her test question still lands awkwardly: "I'm asking CEOs and everyone now — 'do you invest as much in women as you do in men?' Then it usually gets quiet. But I must say it's better than it used to be." And the progress is presented as hard-won: "Women's sports, we've all been fighting for it."
