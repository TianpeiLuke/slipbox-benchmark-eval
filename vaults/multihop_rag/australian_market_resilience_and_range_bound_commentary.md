# Australian Market Resilience And The Range-Bound Reading

Novus Capital senior client adviser Gary Glover argued that the Australian sharemarket was surprisingly resilient following a negative lead from Wall Street and the latest inflation data, with markets starting to wake up to the fact that interest rates could stay higher for longer. His ground was the mismatch between the overseas move and the local one: "Considering the damage overnight in the US, Australian markets held on pretty well. I thought it would be a bigger down day across the board."

Glover characterised the market as volatile but quite range-bound, drawing an analogy to previous periods of high inflation in the 1940s and 1970s. The Wall Street lead he referred to was severe: Wall Street's ugly September got even worse on Tuesday as a sharp drop for stocks brought them back to where they were in June, with the S&P 500 tumbling 1.5 per cent for its fifth loss in the last six days, the Dow Jones dropping 1.1 per cent, and the Nasdaq composite losing 1.6 per cent.
