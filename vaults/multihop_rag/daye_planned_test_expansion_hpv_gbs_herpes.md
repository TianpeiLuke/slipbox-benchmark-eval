# Daye's Planned Test Expansion To HPV, GBS And Herpes

Daye says HPV is next on its list of tests to add — an STI which has been linked to increased cervical cancer risk.

Another on the list to add "soon" is GBS screening for pregnant patients. "GBS is a common bacteria often carried in the intestines or lower genital tract. While usually harmless in adults, it can cause severe infections in newborns if transmitted during delivery. Testing pregnant women for GBS allows for the timely administration of antibiotics during labour, significantly reducing the risk of neonatal infections," Daye told TechCrunch. The startup also plans to offer herpes screening for everyone down the line.
