# America Growing Less Religious And More Secular

Trust in God has been a foundational principle of the United States since the days of colonial settlement, but survey after survey, along with countless anecdotal examples, paint what Fox News Lifestyle calls an unmistakable picture of an America growing less religious and more secular. The article's summary judgement, as of 26 September 2023: the United States, it appears, no longer trusts God.

The piece characterises what has taken its place: trust in God in recent decades appears to have been replaced by false idols of celebrity, technology and government.
