# Craig Morgan On Military Service And The Recruiting Shortfall

Craig Morgan argues that Americans should serve because recruiting is "worse than it's ever been" and because military service is one of the few places where pride and humility coexist, in an interview published by Fox News Digital on 16 October 2023.

His grounds are the recruiting numbers as he states them onstage: "Less than 1% of the people in this nation – and I say this every night upstate – less than 1% of the people in our nation serve in this military and right now, recruiting is worse than it's ever been. So, we're going to do everything we can to try to encourage people to serve our country and to understand that it's not about self, and it's not even really about government."

The warrant is what joining offers in return: being part of something "so much bigger than yourself". "There's a sense of pride and humility, two words that are very seldom able to coincide. And I tell people all the time, I think the military is one of the few places where that takes place. Law enforcement service to our country is where you can be very proud and humble at the same time. It's a different kind of pride, not an arrogant pride. It's a sense, a sense of humility, and I'm humbled beyond words to be able to be a part of that again," Morgan said.
