# May December (Todd Haynes)

May December is Todd Haynes' film starring Natalie Portman, Julianne Moore, and Charles Melton, ranked 11 on Polygon's best movies of 2023 list of 2023-10-07 and streaming on Netflix.

Elizabeth, played by Natalie Portman, arrives in Savannah to prepare for a new role based on Gracie, played by Julianne Moore — a grown woman who slept with 13-year-old Joe, played by Charles Melton, and subsequently married him. Elizabeth comes with an open mind, ready to inhabit Gracie and fully understand her, and yet across the film nothing is as simple as Elizabeth, or the audience, thinks: Gracie is always more than meets the eye, more emotional, more aware, more monstrous, and Haynes expertly teases out the two women's mysteries as they each try to craft an identity around the other.

Critic Zosha Millman notes the film has callbacks to other classic movies about selfhood but says it feels most in line with something like The Rehearsal, which also has a lot on its mind about how blurry the line between real life and performance can be, and particularly how traumatic it is to ask kids to behave like adults. That is what Melton's Joe is tragically caught in the middle of, and as Gracie and Elizabeth obsess over little details, the film lets Melton make the case for the quiet, seething corruption at its heart.
