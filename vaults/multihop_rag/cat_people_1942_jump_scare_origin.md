# Cat People (1942) and the Origin of the Jump Scare

Cat People is the movie that originated the modern jump scare, and Polygon recommends it as a way to be a smarter horror fan — knowledge to impress people with at Halloween parties. It was the Oct. 14 pick in the 2023-10-10 Halloween Countdown, listed on Max.

Beyond its historical role, Polygon calls it a delightfully dark and sexy time positively radiating with atmosphere. The film follows a woman, played by Simone Simon, caught between her desire for a new man in her life, played by Kent Smith, and her belief that she is cursed to turn into a panther if she becomes aroused. At 73 minutes it is one of the shorter horror classics available.

The jump-scare origin is a specific sequence: one character stalks another, and director Jacques Tourneur lets the tension build to an unbearable level before surprising the audience with the sudden appearance of a bus. Often considered the first example of the modern jump scare, it was called the "Lewton Bus" after producer Val Lewton, who used the technique again in later movies.
