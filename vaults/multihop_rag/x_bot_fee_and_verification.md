# X Anti-Bot Strategy: Fee Plus Verification

X charges new users $1 per year to write, while reading remains free. The fee is live only in test markets — New Zealand and the Philippines — and it is one component of a broader anti-bot strategy rather than the whole of it.

Director of engineering Eric Farraro described the intended layers: payment verification, phone verification and ID verification, alongside traditional heuristics and models for detecting fake accounts and engagement. He stated these approaches "are not mutually exclusive."

The stated goal is explicitly not elimination but cost imposition: "to make it difficult and expensive enough that it's less and less viable." Farraro noted that creating accounts by human or script currently costs pennies, so even $1 makes the process substantially more expensive. Musk said the fee "won't stop bots completely, but it will be 1000x harder to manipulate the platform," and that each new bot requires another new payment method.

The strategy is premised on a forecast: Farraro argued that within years AI will mimic human interaction well enough to solve CAPTCHAs and generate media "undetectable by human or AI countermeasures," so current detection methods must evolve. "If you can avoid getting identified as a bot, why can't an intelligent AI do the same?"
