# Morbius In Spider-Man: The Animated Series

Morbius, the Living Vampire, is what Polygon calls hands down the ballsiest Spidey villain to adapt in a cartoon for children — a network whose censors would not even allow the words "sinister" or "kill," so that he frankly had no business being in a Saturday morning lineup.

The clever folks behind Spider-Man got around the strict content standards with a loophole Polygon suggests might be even creepier than how vampires normally feed: they gave him little suckers on the palms of his hands that drain victims of "plasma." The same trick came back later. A creative application of vampirism is how the show would go on to adapt another villain normally too violent for kids TV — Carnage, a serial killing symbiote who now "drained" his victims' "life force" instead of cutting them to bits.
