# The Delta SkyMiles Gold Business Welcome Bonus

The Delta SkyMiles Gold Business Card's welcome bonus is 75,000 Bonus Miles after you spend $6,000 in purchases on the new Card in your first 6 months of card opening — described by Fortune on 2023-12-12 as a big benefit of the card.

The bonus sits alongside a $0 intro annual fee for the first year (then $99). Its value is amplified by how the miles can be spent: cardholders who use their miles to purchase flights receive a 15% discount when they buy Delta flights with miles on the Fly Delta app or the Delta website.
