# The Detroit Lions' Defensive Improvement, 2023

The New York Times argued on 16 October 2023 that the Lions' defensive improvement is real but not yet testable: Detroit is 11.3 EPA per game better on defense than the previous season, the second-largest gain for any team, yet the true measure will have to wait for the postseason.

The qualifications are built into the grounds. The schedule has something to do with the gain, and the 2022 defense was so bad that it couldn't stop Carolina, so the baseline was low. Looking forward, the remaining schedule features so many weak offenses — including Chicago twice, Green Bay, Denver and Las Vegas — that the improvement cannot be properly assessed before January. The writer nonetheless expects Detroit to win the NFC North and return to the playoffs, which will make the season successful no matter what happens from there.

An executive endorsed the team on the grounds of self-knowledge rather than personnel: "Yes, I do buy them. This is a league full of teams who do not really know who they are week to week. Detroit knows who they are, and they play to their identity. Anytime you know who you are and play to that style, you have a chance to be dangerous."
