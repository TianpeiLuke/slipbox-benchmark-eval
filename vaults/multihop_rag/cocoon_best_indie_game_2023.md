# Cocoon Wins Best Indie Game, 2023

Cocoon won Best Indie Game during the 2023 Game Awards, and Polygon reports it is a personal favorite among much of its staff.

The game is the latest title from a studio founded by Jeppe Carlsen, the person responsible for the unsettling narrative platformers Limbo and Inside, and Polygon describes it as a psychedelic odyssey.
