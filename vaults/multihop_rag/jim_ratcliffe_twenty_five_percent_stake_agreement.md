# Jim Ratcliffe's Twenty-Five Per Cent Stake Agreement

Sir Jim Ratcliffe had an agreement in principle to buy a 25 per cent share in Manchester United, with a £1.3billion offer due to be high on the agenda when United's board of directors met on the Thursday following talkSPORT's 18 October 2023 report; the chemical billionaire moved into position after fellow bidder Sheikh Jassim withdrew his offer.

The terms carry several open conditions. Ratcliffe had originally wanted a much larger up-front stake before settling on the 25 per cent agreement in principle. The exact timescale for completion remained vague, and how much it would cost him overall could depend on United's sporting and commercial success in the coming seasons. Thursday's board meeting was pre-arranged, and while his proposal was expected to be discussed in detail, sources close to United indicated to talkSPORT that it was far from guaranteed the matter would be put to a vote, with several details still to be thrashed out. Even once a final deal is agreed, it is likely to take at least a couple of months for Ratcliffe to pass the Premier League's owners and directors test, which has been made more stringent since Portsmouth became the first top-flight side to go into administration in 2010.

Ratcliffe has supported the club since childhood and is Manchester born; his move to Monaco in 2020 may have been bad news for the British taxpayer, but not for Manchester United fans. talkSPORT framed the deal as potential progress if he follows through with his vision to revolutionise the club, and raised the questions of how the situation had been reached, what happens next, and what the future could look like at Old Trafford.
