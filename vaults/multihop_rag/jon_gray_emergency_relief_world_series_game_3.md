# Jon Gray's Surprise Relief Outing In World Series Game 3

Jon Gray threw three scoreless innings in a surprise relief appearance in Game 3 of the 2023 World Series on Oct. 30, striking out three and allowing just one hit before Bruce Bochy turned to right-hander Josh Sborz for the bottom of the seventh at 10:00 p.m.

Gray needed little time to settle in. At 9:07 p.m., in the bottom of the fourth, he struck out Gabriel Moreno, got Christian Walker to ground out to third and Tommy Pham to fly out to center for an easy 1-2-3 inning. By 9:28 p.m., in the bottom of the fifth, he was cruising: despite the impromptu appearance he had sent down the first six batters he faced, with Lourdes Gurriel Jr. and Alek Thomas both grounding out and Evan Longoria popping out to first in foul play.

Gray was still rolling when he was removed, and the live blog described the outing as fantastic — three scoreless innings with three strikeouts and just one hit allowed.
