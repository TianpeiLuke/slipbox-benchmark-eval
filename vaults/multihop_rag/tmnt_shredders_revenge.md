# TMNT: Shredder's Revenge

TMNT: Shredder's Revenge is a return to the halcyon days for any TMNT fan and, in Polygon's words, a truly excellent beat-'em-up brawler. It appears in the outlet's 2023-10-20 list of the 35 best couch co-op games for Nintendo Switch.

Players can choose between the four Turtles — Leonardo, Michelangelo, Donatello, and Raphael.

The roster also includes April O'Neil, Master Splinter, and Casey Jones.
