# Handicap Betting In Tennis And Golf

In individual sports the point spread survives as a handicap denominated in the sport's own units: at tennis betting sites it is usually based on games or sets and is termed a "game handicap" or "set handicap," while in golf it appears as "stroke handicap" betting, akin to point spreads but measured in golf strokes.

For tennis, Sporting News uses a match between Serena Williams and Maria Sharapova: if Williams is a -4.5 favorite, she must win by a 5-game margin for her bettors to win. A match score of 6-4, 6-2 for Williams gives a 6-game margin and so covers the -4.5 spread. Betting on Sharapova at +4.5 wins if she wins outright or if Williams wins by fewer than 5 games. Set handicaps work similarly but focus on the number of sets won by each player.

For golf, betting often centers on outright winners or head-to-head matchups, with stroke handicap as the spread-like alternative. In a head-to-head bet between Tiger Woods and Jordan Spieth, if Woods is given a -2.5 stroke handicap as the favorite, he would need to outplay Spieth by 3 strokes or more to win the bet; betting on Spieth at +2.5 wins if he wins outright or if Woods wins by just 1 or 2 strokes.
