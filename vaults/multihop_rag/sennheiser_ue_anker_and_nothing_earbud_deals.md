# Sennheiser, UE, Anker And Nothing Earbud Deals

Four more sets round out The Verge's roundup: Sennheiser's Momentum Wireless 4 at $269.89 at Amazon (and $30 more at Best Buy) against a typical $379.95; the UE Fits at $149.99 direct from Ultimate Ears, normally $199; Anker's Soundcore Liberty 3 Pro at $99.99 at Amazon and $10 more from Walmart, regularly $169.99; and Nothing's Ear Stick at $59, $40 off, from Nothing.

For noise-canceling headphones with superb battery life, the Momentum Wireless 4 are the best option, lasting up to 60 hours on a single charge; they sound terrific, offer multipoint Bluetooth connectivity, and match the Sony WH-1000XM5 for comfort, though they lack physical controls and the stylish, retro-inspired design of their predecessor and their improved noise cancellation still isn't quite on par with Sony's or Bose's. The UE Fits were called the most comfortable consumer earbuds on the market in The Verge's review, because they permanently mold to the shape of your ears in just 60 seconds — a great choice for anyone worried about buds coming loose — with good sound quality and eight-hour battery life; the listing shows $150 against $199, 25 percent off, at Ultimate Ears. Anker's Soundcore Liberty 3 Pro are relatively affordable noise-canceling earbuds with long battery life, powerful sound with LDAC support, multipoint Bluetooth, IPX4 water resistance, and a wide selection of ear and wing tips. Nothing's Ear Stick is a cheaper alternative for anyone drawn to the transparent look of the new Beats Studio Buds Plus: not as capable and lacking premium features like active noise cancellation, but stylish thanks to a see-through design and tube-like charging case, with good sound from the open-style design plus improved battery life and better voice call quality than their predecessor; the listing shows $59 against $99, 40 percent off.
