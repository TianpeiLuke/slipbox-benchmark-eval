# Penalties Sought in the Letitia James Civil Fraud Lawsuit

Attorney General Letitia James is seeking $250 million in penalties and a ban on Trump doing business in New York, his home state. She has asked Engoron to bar Trump and his three eldest children from ever again running a company based in New York, and to bar Trump and the Trump Organization from entering into commercial real estate acquisitions for five years, among other sanctions. The $250 million figure is the estimated worth of benefits derived from the alleged fraud, she said.

The procedural position as of 26 September 2023 was that Engoron's ruling, in a phase of the case known as summary judgment, resolves the key claim in James' lawsuit while six others remain. He was slated to hold a non-jury trial starting 2 October before deciding on those claims and any punishments he may impose, and has said the trial could last into December.

Manhattan prosecutors had looked into bringing a criminal case over the same conduct but declined to do so, leaving James to sue and seek penalties that could disrupt Trump's and his family's ability to do business in the state. James' lawsuit does not carry the potential of prison time, but could complicate Trump's ability to transact real estate deals and could also stain his legacy as a developer.
