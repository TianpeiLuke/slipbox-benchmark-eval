# SoftBank's "Beyond Japan" Strategy Via The Cubic Partnership

SoftBank Corp's route to international expansion in this deal runs through a stake in a partner rather than through network build-out or carrier acquisition: it sees an opportunity to expand internationally not through costly builds of more network, or by acquiring other carriers, but by taking a stake in a partner it sees getting strong traction as an IT partner globally.

The relationship pre-dates the investment. The pair have been working together in Japan, where Cubic Telecom has been integrating SoftBank's wireless network to provide connectivity to connected cars. The strategic target is the area where telecoms meets technology — a holy grail-type goal for carriers, especially as their networks become further commoditized.

The model is meant to extend beyond cars. Cubic plans to continue building more connectivity for vehicles, Sorohan said, but he added that the company's infrastructure and partnerships can work in a number of other verticals, such as the agricultural industry, where national and multinational companies are now using a multitude of connected tooling and autonomous equipment in remote areas.
