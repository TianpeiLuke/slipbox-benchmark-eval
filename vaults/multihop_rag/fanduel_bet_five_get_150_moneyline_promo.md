# FanDuel Bet $5, Get $150 Moneyline Promo

FanDuel's welcome offer, as presented by Sporting News on Nov. 29, 2023, is bet $5 on any moneyline and get $150 in bonus bets if your team wins — claimed through an exclusive FanDuel promo code link rather than a typed code.

The defining condition is that the payout is contingent on the wager winning: you need to win the wager to receive $150 in bonus bets. There are no odds restrictions on which moneyline you pick, which is why Sporting News advises it would be prudent to bet a big favorite over an outmatched underdog on the college basketball schedule.
