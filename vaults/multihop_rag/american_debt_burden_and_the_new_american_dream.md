# The American Debt Burden And The New American Dream

TechCrunch argues that the American Dream is no longer the promise that anyone can get rich if they just work hard enough, but the hope that maybe one day MrBeast will film you living in terrible conditions for a few months so you can pay off the debt you accumulated by simply going to school or getting sick.

The grounds are the scale of household debt. The total amount of student debt has almost tripled in the last 15 years to over $1.77 trillion, and according to a study from Kaiser Health News and NPR, 41% of American adults have some form of medical debt.

The warrant is a question about rational choice under those conditions: if getting sick can render you bankrupt, why not live inside a grocery store for 45 days if it could eliminate your financial burdens? The argument concedes a limit on the Squid Game analogy while making it — contestants on Netflix or in MrBeast videos are participating voluntarily and are not in mortal danger.
