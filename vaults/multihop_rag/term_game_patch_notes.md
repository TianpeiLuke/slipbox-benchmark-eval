# Game Patch Notes

## Definition

Neither corpus article uses the phrase "patch notes," but both document the same underlying pattern: a studio bundling a slate of changes — new content, rebalancing, and quality-of-life fixes — under a single named or numbered update tied to a release date.
Polygon's Diablo 4 coverage lists that pattern for "Season 2"/"Season of Blood": leveling speed, new endgame bosses, a Nightmare Dungeon rework, mount handling, and Renown/gem-Stash changes, all bundled to arrive together on Oct. 17 (doc_0533).
Polygon's games-of-the-year piece shows the same pattern for Cyberpunk 2077's named "Patch 2.0," a systems-revamping update shipped alongside — but distinct from — the paid Phantom Liberty expansion (doc_0213).

## Context

The corpus's clearest example is Diablo 4's Season 2 update, "Season of Blood," reported by Polygon on 2023-10-04: alongside new vampire powers, Blizzard shipped a broad slate of quality-of-life changes covering leveling speed, endgame bosses, Nightmare Dungeon design, mount handling, and the Renown and gem/Stash systems, all set to arrive together on Oct. 17 as one seasonal patch.

Polygon's broader 2023 games-of-the-year piece shows the same pattern in a second game: Cyberpunk 2077's Phantom Liberty expansion arrived "alongside the sweeping Patch 2.0," which revamped the base game's role-playing progression systems, improved enemy AI, and "course-corrected a litany of other details" — a patch bundled with, but distinct from, the paid DLC content it accompanied.

## Key Characteristics

- **Versioned or seasonal framing** — changes are grouped under a single identifier (Patch 2.0, Season 2/"Season of Blood") rather than released piecemeal, per doc_0533 and doc_0213.
- **Mix of change types** — a single patch bundles new content (Diablo 4's five endgame bosses), rebalancing (faster leveling, reworked Nightmare Dungeon affixes), and quality-of-life fixes (Renown carryover, gems no longer taking bag space), per doc_0533.
- **Developer-explained rationale** — studios attach commentary to justify changes, as when Diablo 4's game director Joe Shely and associate director Joe Piepiora told Polygon the quality-of-life focus came from finally having time to react to player feedback, unlike the rushed first season (doc_0533).
- **Distinct from paid content** — a patch can ship independently of or alongside paid expansions; Cyberpunk 2077's Patch 2.0 revamped core systems while Phantom Liberty (the paid DLC) added new story content, and reviewers credit the patch as much as the DLC for the game's improved reception (doc_0213).

## Background (external)

Outside the corpus, this kind of update bundle has a generic industry name: release notes (also called patch notes).
Per Wikipedia, release notes "describe important information about a distribution of a hardware or software product" and typically include "a list of what is new in the release, including bug fixes and new features" (Wikipedia, "Release notes," accessed 2026-09-02).
