# Christian Watson Packers Hot Seat

Yardbarker's Nov. 30, 2023 NFC hot-seat roundup named wide receiver Christian Watson as the Green Bay Packers' hottest seat: the popular choice to emerge as the team's No. 1 wideout had underachieved in his second season.

The grounds are comparative and statistical. He was outshined by fellow 2022 draftee Romeo Doubs, with seven touchdowns, and rookie Jayden Reed, with five. Watson had his best game in Week 12 — five receptions, 94 yards, one TD — but was nearly invisible before that, recording 16 catches for 257 yards and two touchdowns over seven games. The warrant is that being outproduced by same-draft and rookie teammates removes the justification for the expected No. 1 role.
