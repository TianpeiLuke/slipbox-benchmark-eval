# Emily Hand's Dancing Talent And Beyoncé Routines

Emily Hand "just loved life and music" and had a gift for memorising whole dance routines — Beyoncé was a favourite she would copy — her father told Fox News Digital in an interview published on 17 November 2023.

Emily was only 2½ years old when her own mother died of breast cancer, and she has been raised by her dad and by others in their tightly knit kibbutz in the years since. "Beyoncé is a favorite because Beyoncé is a beautiful singer and a very good dancer. And so Emily would copy her," her father said. "She had this gift that she could memorize movements, she could just memorize whole routines, and she would do it within a very short time."

Her natural dancing talent was apparent to everyone in Be'eri, not far from the border of Gaza: "Every holiday they'd have performances of little kids and older kids, so she was always in it. But she was always there front and center because she would always remember the moves if the kids at the back didn't."
