# Why Spanish-Language Music Keeps Growing Globally

Asked what is driving the ability of Latin artists, particularly Latin American artists, to have global hits on distribution agreements without signing even licensing deals with major record labels, Alejandra Olea answers that Spanish-speaking music, from Spain and Latin America, just keeps getting bigger, and that Spanish and Latin artists have many more options to develop their audiences and monetize their music at each stage of their career.

The mechanism she gives has two linked parts. First, the world is one where music discovery and consumption are now primarily digital, artists have understood that, and they look for partners who get it too and whose expertise will allow them to leverage digital platforms to reach new and existing audiences wherever they are; artists like Iñigo Quintero and labels like Acqustic are prime examples of this profound shift within the Latin and the wider music industry. Second, the effect is strongest in Spanish-speaking markets, where music fans are among the most engaged audiences in the world, whether on digital platforms or on social media, and where music content is consumed with more frequency, in different formats, across multiple platforms and through different aspects related to the track.
