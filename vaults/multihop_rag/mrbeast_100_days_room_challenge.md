# MrBeast's 100-Day Room Challenge

Suzie Taylor and Bailey Stanfield, the two contestants in MrBeast's newest video as of 21 December 2023, successfully completed a challenge to live with a stranger for 100 days, winning $185,000 each.

The conditions were austere. A woman in a blue tracksuit is on the floor of a bright asylum-like room, stretching in a pigeon yoga pose, when she turns to the stranger she plans to live with for 100 days; at that point the pair had been cohabitating for nearly three weeks, isolated from the rest of the world, eating only canned food, with nothing to entertain themselves but a deck of cards they made themselves. Stanfield sits in his bed, where he has made a canopy with a sheet to keep the light out since the bright lights never turn off, with stacks of dollar bills atop the canopy and 15 more briefcases of money around him that he will only get if he can stick it out for another few months.

Their answers to "what are you going to do with this money?" were about obligations rather than luxuries: "I was probably going to pay a lot on the house," Stanfield replies; "I want to pay off my parents' debt," Taylor says. "That would be like, my ideal world." What TechCrunch finds intriguing about their reality-TV-like experience is that this trade-off has become a new normal on social media: if you agree to suffer for content, you might just be able to pay off your parents' debt.
