# Craig Morgan On The Military's Time Demands On Families

Craig Morgan told Fox News Digital, in an interview published on 16 October 2023, that the biggest challenge of active duty was the time commitment: there were moments when he was away from his family for a year at a time.

When he was serving, it was not only his dedication that mattered but his family's as well. "It not only requires a lot of dedication on the soldier's part, but dedication on the family's part. It's tough. It's very tough," he explained.

Despite the challenges of joining the military, to Morgan the reward is worth it: "The reward, again, is being around people that I think are some of the greatest Americans in the world."
