# Jordan Poyer's Versatile Safety Role With The Buffalo Bills

The Buffalo Bills have what The New York Times described on 15 December 2023 as a new safety: Jordan Poyer, the same player who had been with the team the previous six seasons, but changed by ayahuasca and deployed in a far wider range of roles.

He sometimes plays close to the line of scrimmage, even lining up in the gaps and banging helmets with interior linemen. The Bills use him as a hybrid linebacker in a three-safety dime package, and he has played free safety, strong safety, outside cornerback, inside cornerback, left linebacker and right linebacker — and more. Physically and mentally he is being challenged, but he is grateful, content and all in.

His record establishes the standing behind the role: he is the only player in the NFL to have 500 or more tackles, 20 or more interceptions and 10 or more sacks over that six-season time frame, a Bills captain for the fourth time, an Ed Block Courage Award winner in 2017, a Pro Bowl safety the previous year and an All-Pro the year before that.
