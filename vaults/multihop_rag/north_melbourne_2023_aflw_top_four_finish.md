# North Melbourne's 2023 AFLW Top-Four Finish

North Melbourne secured a top-four spot for the 2023 AFLW finals with a one-way win over the Western Bulldogs, played almost entirely in the Roos' attacking half, The Age reported on 3 November 2023.

Star midfielder Ash Riddell had 36 touches and seven clearances and said she was excited to go up against Melbourne or Adelaide in the first weekend of finals depending on other results that round, as they were the only two sides who beat North that year. "I'm excited like I think the group's coming together really nicely and where really well-prepared compared to previous years," Riddell told Fox Footy. "So, I can't wait I think it's an exciting time for us." She credited her side's "team performance" after two losses to the Dees and Crows.

For the Western Bulldogs it was another tough day out as they notched their ninth loss of the season; they won just one game that year, an eight-point victory the previous weekend over 17th-placed West Coast.
