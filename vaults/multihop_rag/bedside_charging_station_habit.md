# The Bedside Charging Station Habit

News writer Jess Weatherbed got a charging station as a birthday present from her partner after several months of arguing over their sleeping arrangements — something about her trailing the charging cables for her devices in the bed being "dangerous" and "extremely uncomfortable."

After begrudgingly acknowledging her poor charging habits, she admits that having it on her desk has had benefits beyond not garroting herself mid-slumber: her iPhone, Apple Watch, and wireless earbuds rarely run out of juice, because she no longer falls asleep before plugging them in.

She has also taken to using the charger's upright positioning for phones to her advantage — serving as a desk clock, a tiny display for Slack or Discord, and a hub to remotely control the various smart devices around her home. It helps her separate the device from being her phone, and instead builds the habit of it being another tool to boost her productivity.
