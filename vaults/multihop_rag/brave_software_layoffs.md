# Brave Software Lays Off 9% Of Its Workforce

Brave Software, the maker of Brave Browser and Brave Search, confirmed that it had laid off 9% of its workforce, with the cuts falling across departments rather than being concentrated in one team. TechCrunch reported the confirmation in its 2023-10-13 startups roundup.

The company itself confirmed the figure, so the 9% is Brave's own number rather than an outside estimate. The source gives the scope of the cut and its cross-departmental spread; it does not give a headcount, a date for the layoffs themselves, or a stated reason.
