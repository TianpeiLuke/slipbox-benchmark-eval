# Joint Security Area (Park Chan-wook)

Joint Security Area is Park Chan-wook's breakout hit, a tightly wound thriller starring Lee Yeong-ae, Lee Byung-hun, and Song Kang-ho, and the film Polygon identifies as the point his career traces back to.

The plot starts with the killing of two North Korean soldiers in the DMZ and a Swiss Army major sent to investigate the killings. As more is learned, JSA becomes a movie about an unlikely friendship and about the ways constructs like borders divide people and put them in deadly opposition to each other.

Its importance is both commercial and career-making: JSA was the highest-grossing movie in Korean history at the time, and it helped launch Park's career as well as those of its stars Lee Young-ae, Lee Byung-hun, and Song Kang-ho, all of whom Polygon's Pete Volk calls excellent in bringing a complicated and intense story to life. Volk adds context on the director — Park's most recent movie, Decision to Leave, was his own No. 1 film of 2022, and Park is one of his very favorite filmmakers working today, with many excellent movies over the course of his career.
