# Moyes On West Ham's Win At Tottenham

West Ham manager David Moyes called his side's 7 December 2023 win at Tottenham a "huge performance because of the quality of the opposition," a result The Roar reported as lifting West Ham to ninth, just three points behind Spurs.

Moyes described a match his team survived rather than controlled: "We were up against it tonight. We had to dig in. Thankfully we just about scraped it. The quality Tottenham showed in the first half, I don't know if we touched the ball for eight or nine minutes."

He then explained the balance his side struck once they settled, and what it says about them: "We gained a bit of confidence once we got some passes. We wanted to get after them a bit more but by the same breath we were cautious about them picking us off. We are a team who can give some of the good teams a bloody nose, we have that in us."
