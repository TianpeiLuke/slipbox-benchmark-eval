# Jaguars-Saints Pregame: Jamaal Williams Activated, Saints Tackles Out

Before the Oct. 19, 2023 Jaguars-Saints "Thursday Night Football" game, the headline roster news was that New Orleans activated running back Jamaal Williams off injured reserve and expected him to play, per Adam Schefter at 6:52 p.m.; Williams had not played since Week 2 after a hamstring injury. At 7:56 p.m. the live blog noted that Williams' return marked the first time all season that both he and Alvin Kamara were in the Saints backfield, since Kamara had missed the first three games of the season due to a suspension.

The inactives, posted at 7:19 p.m., showed Demario Davis notably active for the Saints — a boost to New Orleans' defense — but both starting tackles out. Trevor Lawrence was officially active for Jacksonville.

On records, the blog noted at 7:43 p.m. that the Jaguars entered Week 7 with a perfect 2-0 mark as the away team, while the Saints were 1-1 at home at the Caesars Superdome.
