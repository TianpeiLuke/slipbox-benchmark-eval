# Day Of Reckoning Undercard Results: Bivol, Dubois And Kabayel

Dmitrii Bivol defeated Lyndon Arthur by unanimous decision on the Day of Reckoning undercard in Riyadh, with all three judges scoring it a 12-round sweep for the IBO light heavyweight and WBA super light heavyweight belts, as reported by The Roar on 23 December 2023.

The early card also included five knockouts. After Jai Opetaia's win, Daniel Dubois (20-2-0) registered a 10th-round TKO of Jarrell Miller (26-1-1) with eight seconds left in the final round. Agit Kabayel (24-0-0) scored a fourth-round TKO of Arslanbek Makhmudov (18-1-0) in a scheduled 10-round heavyweight bout. Filip Hrgovic (17-0-0) stopped Mark De Mori (41-3-2) with a first-round TKO, and Frank Sanchez (24-0-0) opened the night with a seventh-round knockout of Junior Fa (20-3-0).
