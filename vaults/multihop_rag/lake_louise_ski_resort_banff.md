# Lake Louise: The Largest Banff Resort, With Mount Assiniboine Views

Lake Louise is the largest of the resorts in Banff National Park. It lies a little further from Banff than Sunshine Village but offers a larger ski area and some of the most amazing views in the park, including that of the famed Mount Assiniboine. Beginners will find the area around the foot of the hill a good start, while the area around the Grizzly Express and Larch Express lifts contains good beginner runs and some top-to-bottom greens; much of the rest suits intermediates, while the Back Bowls are good for the more advanced.

The practical trade-off is the drive. Most people will choose to base themselves in Banff, though the 45-minute journey to Lake Louise may put some off; a lift pass to the "Ski Big 3" area covers Lake Louise, Sunshine Village and nearby Mount Norquay, and a stay in Lake Louise itself is a great option for a relaxing week split between skiing, the odd visit to Banff and seeing what the charming village has to offer.

Another Fairmont offering is the magnificent Chateau Lake Louise, a historic hotel located right next to the shores of the lake. Crystal Ski offers the most affordable package there, with an elegant double room boasting delightful views of the Rockies; you are five minutes from town, with the ski area accessible via a free 10-minute shuttle bus. From £1,904pp, including seven nights' room-only accommodation, return flights from London Heathrow to Calgary and transfers, departing 27 January 2024.
