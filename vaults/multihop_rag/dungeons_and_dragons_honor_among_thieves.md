# Dungeons & Dragons: Honor Among Thieves

Honor Among Thieves, directed by Jonathan Goldstein and John Francis Daley and starring Chris Pine, Michelle Rodriguez, and Justice Smith, is described by Polygon as far from the definition of great, ambitious filmmaking but nevertheless a pretty perfect romp for fantasy fans, whether or not they know the RPG or care about its rules. It ranked 27 on the best movies of 2023 list of 2023-10-07 and was available on Prime Video, Paramount Plus, MGM Plus, or for digital rental/purchase.

The film is funny, fast-paced, and freewheeling, full of visual gags for the in-the-know crowd but broad and action-driven enough to be comprehensible to people who have never picked up a d20.

Polygon's Tasha Robinson singles out Chris Pine and Michelle Rodriguez's companionable bard-and-barbarian relationship as a lovely model for how to include a romance-free mixed-gender friendship in a story — still a frustratingly rare thing for mainstream movies.
