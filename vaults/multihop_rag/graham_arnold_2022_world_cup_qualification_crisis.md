# Graham Arnold 2022 World Cup Qualification Crisis

In March 2022 Graham Arnold seemed a dead man walking — the Socceroos' World Cup qualification campaign was lurching towards failure and the manager was being battered from all sides, The Roar recounted on Nov. 13, 2023.

Arnold's team had finished seven points off automatic qualification for Qatar after a shocking run of one win in seven games, leaving them facing an Asian playoff against the United Arab Emirates and then another against Peru to make the finals. While rumours swirled and ex-players and fans bayed for his blood, Football Australia took the pragmatic course and stuck by Arnold — a decision that likely had more to do with the lateness of the hour and financial considerations than any overwhelming faith in Arnie to turn around the fortunes. But he did just that, with qualification and an excellent run at the finals, bowing out to eventual champions Argentina after nearly taking them to extra time. He also got to answer critics like Mark Bosnich and Craig Foster, bluntly rejecting their negativity with a dismissive "who cares?" — which would have tasted sweet.
