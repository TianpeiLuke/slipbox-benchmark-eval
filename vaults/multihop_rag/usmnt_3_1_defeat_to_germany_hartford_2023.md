# USMNT 1-3 Germany, Hartford, October 2023

The United States lost 3-1 to Germany at Rentschler Field in Hartford on Oct. 14, 2023, having appeared well positioned to fight with a European power through 45 minutes before the house of cards came crashing down after the break.

The U.S. entered the interval level, as a Christian Pulisic stunner was cancelled out by Ilkay Gundogan's poaching. After halftime, as Gio Reyna was withdrawn from his first start back under Gregg Berhalter to build his fitness, the U.S. midfield was overrun and the defense committed a multitude of errors, allowing the Germans to gain full control of proceedings. Goalkeeper Matt Turner summed it up afterwards: "It's frustrating because it's just little moments. Little moments made a big difference at the World Cup, and it's kinda the same story here."

The U.S. started in a 4-3-3, right to left: 1. Turner in goal; 19. Scally, 4. Richards (replaced by Carter-Vickers, 66'), 13. Ream and 2. Dest across the back; 7. Reyna (de la Torre, 46'), 6. Musah and 8. McKennie (Cardoso, 75') in midfield; and 21. Weah (Aaronson, 66'), 20. Balogun (Pepi, 66') and 10. Pulisic (Paredes, 75') in attack.
