# The Social Media Reaction To Gellar's Roblox Feud

Many users on X, formerly known as Twitter, were surprised by Sarah Michelle Gellar's unexpected feud with the online gaming platform. Responding to her Instagram Story, one person said: "This is the most important beef of 2023." Another joked: "Buffy vs Roblox is where all this Millennial vs Gen Z discourse has been heading."

Reactions were not uniformly sympathetic. While one user agreed that "being hacked on Roblox is life ruining," another questioned why Gellar's 11-year-old son had access to his mother's credit card in the first place: "Oh please. That's not that bad. And I don't know why parents let their children use their debit/credit cards on games, like, make them pay themselves!"
