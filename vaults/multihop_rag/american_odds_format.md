# The American Odds Format

American odds express a price as either a negative (-) or a positive (+) number, and they are the format most listings on the best online sports betting sites predominantly use — one of three types of odds, alongside decimal and fractional, that bettors encounter when researching and handicapping across platforms.

The negative numbers, which can seem perplexing at first, point to the team, player or option considered the favorite to win, and can also represent wagers that are less than even money; the negative figure tells you how much you need to bet to earn a profit of $100. Positive odds conversely signal the underdog, or bets offering more than even money, and the figure shows how much profit you will make on a $100 wager.

A worked moneyline example makes the arithmetic concrete. Imagine that on a top NFL betting site the New England Patriots are listed as -110 favorites while the New York Jets stand at +250. If you back the favorite and bet $110 on the Patriots and they win, you end up with $210 — your initial $110 stake plus the $100 profit you earned.
