# Echo Frames Battery Life And Charging Stand

Battery life on the third-gen Echo Frames has been improved and held up well across a week of testing: with a commute of around 60–80 minutes depending on how much the MTA wants to test the reviewer, they got about five hours of media playback and calls and still had about 30 percent battery left.

A roughly 50-minute call drained the battery about 20 percent, and charging from zero to 100 percent takes about 2.5 hours. The reviewer was not a fan of the charging stand's design: it took a hot second to work out that the glasses are supposed to be placed on the stand vertically, lenses pointed straight up at the ceiling, when the stand's design suggested plopping them in horizontally. They missed the charging case of Meta's smart glasses, which killed two birds with one stone and was a much more elegant charging solution overall.
