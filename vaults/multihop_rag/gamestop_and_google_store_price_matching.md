# GameStop And Google Store Price Matching

GameStop does not mention an official price-matching policy on its website. Last year, however, GameStop tweeted that the store will price-match items sold and shipped by Amazon as long as they are available online or in-store, and although the tweet mentioned only Amazon, others have reported the store will price-match items purchased from Target and elsewhere.

The Google Store's policy is explicit but narrow. Google will match online pricing from select retailers — specifically Amazon, Best Buy, Walmart, B&H Photo, and Target — if a request is made within the standard return period. It does not match preorder pricing from other retailers or carrier deals; the product must be identical, right down to the color and configuration, and must be in stock; and you need to provide a public link to the promo page or product listing to verify the rival's price. You may also get the difference if a product purchased from the Google Store drops in price within the standard return period. To get the price match or correction, contact Google. The Verge published this summary on October 25th, 2023.
