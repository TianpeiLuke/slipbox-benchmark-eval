# The Roland Doe Case and The Exorcist

Perhaps the most famous instance of purported demonic possession connected to Ouija board usage was the case of "Roland Doe", a young teenage boy from Maryland, who underwent numerous major exorcisms in 1949, said Fr. Dan Reehil.

Doe's story was adapted into the 1971 book "The Exorcist" by William Peter Blatty, which was then made into the 1973 movie starring Ellen Burstyn and Linda Blair.
