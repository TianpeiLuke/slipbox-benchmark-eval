# Verdane's Stake In Meltwater Via Fountain Venture

Verdane, a Norwegian private equity firm that earlier in 2023 closed a $1 billion-plus fund to invest in scaling tech companies, is taking an 11% stake in Meltwater at a company valuation of €542 million ($592 million), valuing the stake at around $65 million.

The structure is unusual: the investment comes by way of Verdane taking a substantial stake in Fountain Venture, the investment vehicle controlled by Meltwater's founder and current chairman Jørn Lyseggen, rather than investing directly in Meltwater — because the plan is to partner with Fountain to make future investments together in startups working in areas like AI. Meltwater, which first made its name around media monitoring and then got active in business intelligence using AI and big data analytics techniques, was until earlier that year traded publicly on the Norwegian stock exchange; Lyseggen oversaw the company going private again in a deal with two private equity firms, Altor and Marlin, holding his remaining share via Fountain, and that take-private deal was the last disclosed valuation and the one Meltwater currently cites.

The deal also brings a second asset. Joakim Kjemperud, a principal at Verdane, said it gives his firm a stake in an HR firm, Jobylon, although Meltwater is by far the bigger asset. "The deal here is that it's very much a portfolio transaction," he said. "We're buying into Jørn's investment company and acquiring an implied direct stake in Meltwater and Nordic HR firm Jobylon, but Meltwater is the biggest asset in the portfolio." Jobylon's ARR is around €5 million, while the ARR for Meltwater — founded in Norway but now headquartered in San Francisco — is around €500 million, he added.
