# US Weekend Box Office Top Ten, Early December 2023

Comscore's estimated Friday-through-Sunday ticket sales at U.S. and Canadian theaters for the first weekend of December 2023 ranked "Renaissance: A Film by Beyoncé" first at $21 million, ahead of "The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes" at $14.5 million and "Godzilla Minus One" at $11 million.

The remainder of the top ten ran: 4. "Trolls Band Together," $7.6 million; 5. "Wish," $7.4 million; 6. "Napoleon," $7.1 million; 7. "Animal," $6.1 million; 8. "The Shift," $4.4 million; 9. "Silent Night," $3 million; 10. "Thanksgiving," $2.6 million. These were estimates only — final domestic figures were to be released Monday.
