# Manchester United's Form Slump Before The Fulham Trip

Manchester United went into their November 2023 trip to Fulham needing to get back to winning ways after two major defeats, sitting 8th in the Premier League table with 5 wins and 5 defeats. They had been on a 3-match winning run in all competitions when they faced Man City in their previous Premier League match; there was little hope of winning that match considering their form, and the fears came true as they lost 3-0 in the Manchester derby.

They had some hope in their next match, the EFL Cup round of 16 tie against Newcastle United, especially since they had defeated the Magpies the previous term in the final of the EFL Cup. However, the reigning Carabao Cup champions could not defend their title any longer, losing by another 3-0 margin to Newcastle United.

It still seemed that coach Erik Ten Hag had the backing of the management; however, he would still have to pull his team up from the doom as soon as possible. United's Premier League form read L W L W W L, and their form in all competitions read L W W W L L.
