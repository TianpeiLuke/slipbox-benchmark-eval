# Jawan (Shah Rukh Khan)

Jawan, directed by Atlee and starring Shah Rukh Khan, Nayanthara, and Vijay Sethupathi, is the biggest Indian movie of the year — a genre-bending, crowd-pleasing spectacle that rests on the reliably charismatic talents of its star. Polygon ranked it 25 on its best movies of 2023 list of 2023-10-07 and listed it on Netflix.

The cleanest description of the premise is "Charlie's Angels meets Robin Hood"; another would be "everything you want in a blockbuster movie," combining some of the year's best action, romance, and dance numbers within a compelling revenge story with a strong "power to the people" message.

Critic PV names Shah Rukh Khan as the key to the film's joyful success. One of the biggest movie stars in the world, he returned to blockbuster cinema after a brief hiatus with Pathaan and this movie; both are very fun and make good use of his irrepressible charisma, but Jawan shines to a different degree, a reminder of what blockbuster movies can be — fun and exciting, but with plenty on its mind. It is the highest-grossing Indian release of the year and the second-highest-grossing Hindi film ever.
