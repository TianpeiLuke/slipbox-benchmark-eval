# NFL Week 11 Power Rankings, 17-24

Sporting News' Week 11 rankings of Nov. 13, 2023 placed the New Orleans Saints 17th at 5-5 (previous week: 15), heading into a bye without knowing what to do at quarterback: Derek Carr further hurt his right shoulder and needed to be checked for a concussion, and while Jameis Winston provided a spark it was too little too late at Minnesota, leaving a precarious division lead with poor quarterback play overall. The Los Angeles Chargers were 18th at 4-5 (17), having got well offensively with Justin Herbert, Keenan Allen and Austin Ekeler against the Lions before the defense went back to crumbling against big pass plays and wilting against the run, putting the pressure right back on Brandon Staley with a trip to Green Bay next. The Indianapolis Colts were 19th at 5-5 (19), stopping their streak of scoring at least 20 points in every game during an ugly, run-heavy victory in Germany against the Patriots, though Jonathan Taylor was doing his normal things and the defense was playing well again while Gardner Minshew looked meh.

The Tampa Bay Buccaneers were 20th at 4-5 (23), with Baker Mayfield and the offense back on track to move the ball well after a brief midseason slump — not lighting it up, but playing smart complementary football with a rebounding defense and perhaps a long-term quarterback answer. The New York Jets were 21st at 4-5 (18): Aaron Rodgers could not come back soon enough and a December miracle might be too late after two straight key losses to the Chargers and Raiders, with Zach Wilson always capping the potential of a talented offense that keeps making mistakes, and back-to-back division games against the Bills and Dolphins possibly deciding their season.

The Denver Broncos were 22nd at 4-5 (24), having sandwiched their Week 9 bye with upsets over the Chiefs and Bills, with the offense hitting a balanced groove under Russell Wilson and the defense making a lot more plays. The Washington Commanders were 23rd at 4-5 (21), pleased with Sam Howell and an offense showing more fight with additional weapons and better all-around running backs, but with a reeling defense and pass protection that remained un-winnable assets. The Atlanta Falcons were 24th at 4-5 (20), forced into another quarterback change because of injury; they found a few more offensive answers but that position still made things an overall mess, and the defense did not seem well prepared for different styles of offense.
