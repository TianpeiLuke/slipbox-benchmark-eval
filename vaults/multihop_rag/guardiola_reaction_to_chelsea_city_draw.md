# Guardiola Reaction To Chelsea City Draw

Manchester City coach Pep Guardiola said the 4-4 scoreline was a fair reflection of the game, The Roar reported on Nov. 12, 2023.

"The Premier League has quality players with the ability to drive the ball from deep. I'm happy for the result. We were close. I think it's a fair result. We were controlled enough," he said. "I always have to think about what we can do to get better. It's not going to happen that we come here and win 7-0. They are trying to rebuild. We have no complaints." He described the midfield battle as "like chess. They had Enzo, Gallagher and Caicedo. They are so dangerous. Liverpool couldn't do it. They were much better than Arsenal. It's Chelsea. They have speed, physicality and a bench. We go into the international break top of the league and I didn't expect that after the Arsenal defeat."
