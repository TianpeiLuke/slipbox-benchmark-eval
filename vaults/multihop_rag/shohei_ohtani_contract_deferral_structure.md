# The Deferral Structure Of Ohtani's Dodgers Contract

Ohtani will defer $680 million of his $700 million Dodgers contract, receiving only $2 million per year during the deal, according to a Dec. 11, 2023 report by Fabian Ardaya of The Athletic.

Under that structure the two-time MVP will receive $68 million per year, without interest, from 2034-43, making the present-day value of the contract much less than it originally appeared. The deal will still count as $46 million annually toward the luxury tax thresholds for Los Angeles, but Ohtani is nonetheless giving the organization much more flexibility than expected over the course of the contract.
