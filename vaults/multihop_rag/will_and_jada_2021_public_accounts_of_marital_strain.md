# The Smiths' 2021 Public Accounts Of Marital Strain

In late 2021 both Will Smith and Jada Pinkett Smith described the difficulty of their marriage in public, first on her show and then in his memoir. In an October 2021 episode of Red Table Talk, Pinkett Smith said it "takes work" to maintain a spark in the bedroom: "It's hard... the thing Will and I talk about a lot is the journey. We started in this [relationship] at a very young age, 22 years old... I think you expect your partner to know — especially when it comes to sex. It's like: 'Well, if you love me, you should know. If you love me, you should read my mind.' That's a huge pitfall."

The following month Will Smith's memoir was released, detailing several problems the couple had had over the years. He wrote that they were "suffering the brutal death of our romantic fantasies, the burning away of the idealistic illusion of the perfect marriage and the perfect family. Neither of us wanted a divorce; we knew we loved each other, and some aspects of our union were magical. But the structure of the life that we had established was strangling both of us."
