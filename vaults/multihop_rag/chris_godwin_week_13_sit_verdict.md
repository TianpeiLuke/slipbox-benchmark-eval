# Chris Godwin Week 13: Sit Him, A Slot Receiver Who No Longer Plays The Slot

CBS Sports' Week 13 verdict on Chris Godwin is Sit Him, and the explanation offered is a change in where he lines up. Godwin was once known for being a super-reliable slot receiver; this season he has lined up wide on 68% of his snaps, and the article cannot help but think that has caused the massive change in his production this year — that, and the fact that his quarterback isn't a serial slot-targeter, or a Hall of Famer. The production evidence: Godwin has been under 60 yards in five straight games, has not had a game with even 10 PPR points since Week 8, and has one touchdown on the year despite being tied for 12th among all NFL players in end-zone targets with nine.

The matchup makes it worse rather than better: the Panthers offense can't score and their run defense is so bad that opponents are left with pass-negative game scripts, and just two teams — the Saints and Dolphins — have rostered multiple receivers with 10-plus PPR points against the Panthers in 2023. The conclusion is to sit Godwin for Josh Downs, Brandin Cooks, Christian Watson and Curtis Samuel, especially in PPR. Scope: Godwin is a Tampa Bay wide receiver, #14, age 27 with 7 years of experience; the weekly breakdown lists the matchup versus Carolina with Tampa Bay favored by 5.5 and an over/under of 37, the opponent 15th versus wide receivers, projected points of 12.5 and a WR rank of 32nd; year-to-date he has 53 receptions on 82 targets for 606 receiving yards, 1 touchdown and 11.2 Fantasy points per game, with projections powered by SportsLine.
