# SuperDraft's Multiplier-Based DFS Scoring Format

SuperDraft's DFS scoring involves point multipliers as opposed to traditional salary-capped or tiered contests: each player is assigned a scoring multiplier based on their value as determined by SuperDraft, and users can roster any player in their lineup with no restrictions, per Sporting News in December 2023.

The consequence for lineup construction is that a winning lineup will likely feature a strong mix of stud fantasy contributors with 1x-1.6x boosts, as well as matchup-based sleepers with larger multipliers. Because there is no salary cap, the constraint that shapes a roster is the multiplier attached to each name rather than a budget.

The most notable scoring rules for SuperDraft NFL contests are half-point PPR, four-point passing touchdowns, and two-point bonuses for 100 rushing yards, 100 receiving yards and 300 passing yards. Sporting News listed its favorite DFS plays on SuperDraft alongside its FanDuel and DraftKings picks that season, with some of the picks and analysis overlapping.
