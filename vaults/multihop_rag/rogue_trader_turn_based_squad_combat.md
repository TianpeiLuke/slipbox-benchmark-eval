# Rogue Trader: Turn-Based Squad Combat

Combat in Rogue Trader is turn-based and grid-based, which Polygon's reviewer finds deeply satisfying and compares to Baldur's Gate 3 or XCOM 2: some positions provide cover while others are out in the open.

Friendly fire is a very real concern — an arc of auto-fire from a bolter, or a Navigator's third eye opening, can harm friend as well as foe. The reviewer's own Rogue Trader is a sniper who would be lost without her Senechal taking the front lines. There is a lot of firepower at the player's disposal, and it is mostly quite satisfying to use, even if the reviewer occasionally shreds her poor Senechal with a devastating AOE.
