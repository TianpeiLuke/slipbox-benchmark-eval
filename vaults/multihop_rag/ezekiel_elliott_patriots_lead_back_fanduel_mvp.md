# Ezekiel Elliott As The FanDuel MVP Pick

Sporting News made Ezekiel Elliott ($12,000) the MVP pick — worth 1.5x points — in its Week 14 2023 Patriots-Steelers FanDuel single-game lineup, built under a $60,000 budget requiring at least one player from each team.

The reasoning was that it did not matter whether Bailey Zappe, Mac Jones, Bill Belichick or Robert Kraft ended up taking snaps on Thursday night: the Patriots' offense would continue to be tough on the eyes. Points would be at a premium between these teams and controlling the clock was definitely important, which is where Elliott came in. He was set to start with Rhamondre Stevenson (ankle) sidelined for the foreseeable future, meaning New England's offense would be hoping for a vintage performance from the former Cowboy, and the column expected him to be fed a ton. Whether that would result in a touchdown remained to be seen, but if anyone was scoring one on the Patriots roster that night, it was Elliott.
