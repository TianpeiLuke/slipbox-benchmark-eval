# Travis Kelce's WSJ Magazine Interview on Swift

In an interview with WSJ Magazine published on 20 November 2023, Travis Kelce described how much he admires Taylor Swift's skills as a musician and said he was not letting media attention affect the relationship.

"I've never been a man of words. Being around her, seeing how smart Taylor is, has been f***ing mind-blowing. I'm learning every day," he explained. Kelce acknowledged the public attention his personal life had received because he has "never dated anyone with that kind of aura around them", but specified that he was not letting the media affect his relationship.

"But at the same time, I'm not running away from any of it," he said. "The scrutiny she gets, how much she has a magnifying glass on her, every single day, paparazzi outside her house, outside every restaurant she goes to, after every flight she gets off, and she's just living, enjoying life. When she acts like that I better not be the one acting all strange."
