# Argentina's Second Half And Messi's Introduction Against Paraguay

Lionel Messi entered in the 53rd minute for Julian Alvarez in Argentina's Oct. 12, 2023 qualifier against Paraguay, taking the captain's armband from Nicolas Otamendi with a chuckle as Otamendi put it on him despite Messi telling him not to give it up — and hit the woodwork twice without changing the 1-0 scoreline.

The half opened with Paraguay's first substitution, Robert Rojas coming on at right-back for Ivan Ramirez, and with Messi warming up on the touchline; Alexis Mac Allister was fouled from behind seconds after the restart. In the 50th minute Antonio Bareiro picked up what the blog called a hilarious yellow card: he fouled Cristian Romero and got away with it, Romero reacted off the ball by bumping him, and Bareiro threw himself dramatically to the turf, with the reaction earning the booking while Romero was lucky to escape his brainless moment. Paraguay's best attacking move of the match came in the 53rd, when Ramon Sosa brought down a Route One ball and fired a vicious shot that Emiliano Martinez turned inches wide with a fingertip save. The Sporting News noted it was odd that Scaloni turned down the chance to bring Messi on at halftime without burning a substitution window, only for him to enter eight minutes later.

Messi's arrival did not lift the performance. By the 63rd minute the blog observed that Argentina had dropped their level since he came on — not explicitly his fault, but the whole team had been sloppy, and Paraguay looked more confident pressing and caused problems in possession. In the 57th Ramon Sosa escaped a booking for barging extremely late into Rodrigo de Paul, arriving well after the ball; the referee had a chat with him instead. De Paul forced a save from Coronel from an extremely tight angle almost on the end line in the 68th, winning a corner that was wasted short. In the 70th Junior Alonso, already on a yellow, fouled Lautaro Martinez hard and got a talking to, and Paraguay made a double change with Antonio Sanabria and Braian Ojeda replacing Bareiro and Campuzano. In the 75th, with Messi set to take a corner, Alonso went down needing treatment — what first seemed a cramp turned into evident pain, and he was strapped to a stretcher and replaced by Mathias Villasanti, signalling a potential change of formation.

The closing stretch brought Messi's two efforts and a flashpoint. In the 77th he floated an in-swinger directly from a corner that sailed over a helpless Coronel and clanged the far top corner of the woodwork, close to an olimpico, and Enzo Fernandez was withdrawn for Leandro Paredes. In the 84th Lautaro Martinez, in acres of space atop the box and cutting onto his left, rifled a shot inches over the crossbar — the first real open-play chance for either side in some time, in a game mucked up by substitutions. In the 86th came controversy: Antonio Sanabria drew Messi's ire, rarely seen, because replay showed Sanabria had showered Messi with spit from behind; the officials missed it but Messi obviously did not. And in the 90+2nd, after Messi baited Gustavo Gomez into a foul just atop the box — dispossessed but first back to the loose ball, shielding it expertly until Gomez barged in from behind — he hit the woodwork again from the free kick, with Coronel rooted to the spot as it struck the outside of the left post. Gio Lo Celso came on for de Paul, who had a very good match.
