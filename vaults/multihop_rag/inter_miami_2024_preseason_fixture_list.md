# Inter Miami's 2024 Preseason Fixture List

Sporting News compiled on Nov. 30, 2023 all the known Inter Miami preseason matches confirmed by the club directly or widely reported by media ahead of the 2024 season, which begins in February 2024.

The context is a long break: with the 2023 MLS season closing earlier than expected for Inter Miami, eliminated from playoff contention, Lionel Messi would have significant time off before the new campaign. Preseason friendlies exist because players need game time to build their bodies to the point where they can play full 90-minute matches, especially when contesting multiple games a week — and Inter Miami's preseason would be watched with extra observation because Messi draws considerable attention wherever he goes, home or abroad.

Two items headline the list. An anticipated two-match tour of China was cancelled, the trip proving too difficult logistically to arrange, and was replaced by a friendly against fellow MLS side NYCFC to honor Messi's Ballon d'Or. The biggest confirmed match is the meeting between Messi and rival Cristiano Ronaldo, as Inter Miami announced a pair of fixtures in Saudi Arabia including one against Ronaldo's Al Nassr on February 1. Among the unconfirmed but reported fixtures is Inter Miami vs. River Plate at AT&T Stadium in Dallas, Texas on Saturday, Jan. 20, 2024, with a rumored kickoff time still to be determined.
