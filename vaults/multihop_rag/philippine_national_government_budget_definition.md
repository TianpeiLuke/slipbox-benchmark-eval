# Philippine National Government Budget Definition

The Department of Budget and Management (DBM) defines the government budget as "the financial plan of the government…which shows what its resources are, and how they will be generated and used over the fiscal period. It refers to the income, expenditures and sources of borrowings of the National Government (NG) that are used to achieve national objectives, strategies and programs."

Its constitutional basis is Section 22, Article VII: "The President shall submit to the Congress within 30 days from the opening of every regular session, as the basis of the general appropriation bill, a budget of expenditures and sources of financing including receipts from existing and proposed revenue measures."

The National Government budget is the totality of the budgets of all government entities: the National Government itself, the non-financial government-owned and -controlled corporations (usually only the 14 major GOCCs), government financial institutions, local government units, the social security institutions (GSIS, SSS, military pensions, PhilHealth and others), the Oil Price Stabilization Fund, the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas, and the Central Bank-Board of Liquidators. It is allocated for the implementation of government programmes and projects, the operation of government offices, payment of salaries of government employees and payment of public debts, with expenditures classified by expense class, sector and implementing unit of government.

Appropriations come from two sources: new general appropriations legislated by Congress for every budget year under the General Appropriations Act, and existing appropriations previously authorised by Congress. Under Article VI, Section 29 of the Constitution, no money can be withdrawn from the Treasury except in pursuance of an appropriation made by law.
