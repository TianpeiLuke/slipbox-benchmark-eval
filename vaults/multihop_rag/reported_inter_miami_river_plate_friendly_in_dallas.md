# The Reported Inter Miami-River Plate Friendly In Dallas

In late November 2023 it was reported that Inter Miami had arranged a friendly against Argentine giants River Plate in Dallas, Texas in late January — but the reported date collides with a confirmed fixture, so the match could not happen as described unless the club splits its squad.

According to Argentine journalist German Balcarce of River Plate publication RMG, the match would take place on January 20 at AT&T Stadium, the home of the Dallas Cowboys. However, the El Salvador friendly, which is confirmed by the club, happens literally the day before in El Salvador, making it impossible for Inter Miami to play this match as reported unless the squad is divided.

The match would supposedly be the second leg of a two-part trip for River Plate, who will also meet Liga MX club Deportivo Guadalajara — formerly Chivas — on January 17 at the same venue. Sporting News said it would update the report with any additional information on whether the match would be moved to another day or nixed entirely.
