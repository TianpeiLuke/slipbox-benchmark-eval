# ICC ODI World Cup 2023: Knockout Stage And Final

Australia won the final of the ICC Men's ODI World Cup 2023, beating India by six wickets on Sunday, 19 November 2023 at the Narendra Modi Stadium in Ahmedabad. The two semi-finals that produced that pairing were India beating New Zealand by 70 runs on Wednesday, 15 November at the Wankhede Stadium in Mumbai, and Australia beating South Africa by three wickets on Thursday, 16 November at the Eden Gardens in Kolkata. Before the final was played, it was previewed as two-time World Champions India locking horns with five-time World Champions Australia on 19 November in Ahmedabad.

India's route into that semi-final was already secure earlier in the round-robin. On 11 November 2023, Rohit Sharma-led India were preparing for their ninth round-robin match, against the Netherlands at the M Chinnaswamy Stadium in Bengaluru on Sunday 12 November, having already booked a semi-final place by thrashing Sri Lanka by 302 runs at the Wankhede Stadium in Mumbai on 2 November 2023; that semi-final against New Zealand at the Wankhede on Wednesday 15 November was India's seventh appearance in the last-four stage of the 50-over World Cup.
