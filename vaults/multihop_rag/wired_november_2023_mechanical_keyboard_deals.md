# WIRED's Pre-Black Friday Mechanical Keyboard Deals

WIRED's keyboard picks are the NZXT Function, the Roccat Vulcan 121, the Logitech G915, a board with two LEDs per switch, and a NumPad-friendly keyboard whose discount requires clicking the coupon button at checkout.

For anyone interested in mechanical keyboards but unsure where to start, the NZXT Function (8/10, WIRED Recommends) is a great jumping-off point, with a stylish design, configurable switches and keycaps at purchase, and included tools to modify the keyboard later. For those who like keyboards loud enough to register on remote seismic detectors, the Roccat Vulcan 121 is the pick: its switches are illuminated from below and semi-clear, giving a bold look to match staggeringly loud clicky switches, in a good way. The Logitech G915 is one of the writer's personal favorites, in no small part due to Logitech's powerful G Hub software, which makes automating tasks for productivity dead simple; designed for gamers, it's also useful for creative tools like DaVinci Resolve, Premiere, Blender, Cinema 4D and other high-end media-production software. The double-LED board is one of WIRED's favorites, with twice as many LEDs as usual — two per switch — giving cosmetic benefits like smoother color animations and occasional functional ones, like indicating when a mute toggle is enabled. The last keyboard is specifically great for fans of the NumPad: high-quality plastic, hot-swappable switches, and satisfying tactile switches; the deal is pretty modest, but this keyboard rarely goes on sale.
