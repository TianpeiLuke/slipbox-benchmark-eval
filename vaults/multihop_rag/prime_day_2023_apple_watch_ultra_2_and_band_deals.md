# Prime Day 2023 Apple Watch Ultra 2 And Band Deals

WIRED's Apple Watch picks are the Apple Watch Ultra 2, the Silver Milanese Loop band, an elastomer Ultra band, and a microfiber desk mat.

The Apple Watch Ultra 2 (8/10, WIRED Recommends) is only a month old but already on sale, and it is WIRED's favorite smartwatch for outdoor sports: a new, bright 3,000-nit display, the latest S9 chip for faster processing, and a new ultra-wideband chip for precision-locating your phone, alongside the useful Action button and three-mic array carried over from last year's version — a great option for anyone heavily into outdoor sports who wants a rugged, full-functioning smartwatch, and the Apple Watch with the best battery life thanks to its massive size. The Silver Milanese Loop is a great metal band for when you're not using the watch for workouts: stainless steel, with extremely flexible thin mesh and a magnetic clasp that makes sizing easy; the deal applies to the 41-mm size in silver and graphite, with the 45-mm band also on sale. The elastomer band with a titanium buckle is the best band for the Apple Watch Ultra in WIRED's Best Apple Watch Accessories guide — stretchy, bright, fitting small wrists, easy to wash, and showing dirt much less easily than fabric bands — and it is compatible with the large 45-mm Apple Watch Series 8. The microfiber mat keeps a desk looking nice and clean and doubles as a giant mousepad; it's pretty minimalist and only comes in black, but at this sale price that's all WIRED needs.
