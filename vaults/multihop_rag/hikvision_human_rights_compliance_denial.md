# Hikvision's Human Rights Compliance Denial

Hikvision's answer to the allegations is a blanket denial of intent and a claim of full legal compliance, which attacks the inference that its products' presence in the West Bank makes it a knowing participant in abuses.

The company did not respond to Amnesty's report on the use of its products, nor did it provide an on-the-record response in time for the Guardian's publication. Its position therefore rests on an earlier statement, in which Hikvision told the Guardian that it "has strictly followed all applicable laws and regulations in the UK and all countries where we operate to ensure complete compliance."

The same statement makes the intent claim explicit: "Hikvision has never knowingly or intentionally committed human rights abuses itself or acted in willful disregard and will never do so in the future."
