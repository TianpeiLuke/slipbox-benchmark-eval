# Corporate VTubing Remains Viable (Counterpoint)

Against the claim that agency VTubing is a dead end, independent VTuber NagamiMugi told Polygon on 2023-10-19 that "corporate VTubing" is still a viable avenue for those looking to make a career out of the medium that can support content creation full-time.

The premise she does not dispute is that the content has gone flat: "I think VTubing content has become kind of stale," she says.

But she locates the problem in content range rather than in the corporate model, noting that a lot of VTubers tend to be boxed into specific kinds of content — playing Minecraft or popular FPS games like Valorant. "In order for VTubing to continue to grow, VTubers need to branch out and create more content in line with other influencers," she says.
