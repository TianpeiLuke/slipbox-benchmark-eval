# Ari Schoenmaker AFL Draft Prospect 2023

Ari Schoenmaker — pronounced Show-en-maker — profiled in Kevin Sheehan's top 40 for the 2023 AFL Draft as reported by The Roar on Nov. 15, 2023, is a left-footed tall defender who reads the game exceptionally well, is a good interceptor and a prodigious kick of the ball. State: Tasmania. State League Club: Tasmania Devils. Community Club: Launceston. Date of Birth: 07/01/05. Height: 194cm.

He had a very impressive back half of the year, which included being selected for the Allies in their final two matches of the National Championships. He also showed the ability to play on the wing, and averaged 27 disposals at 75 percent efficiency as well as 5.9 marks and 9.6 rebound 50s in his eight matches with the Tasmania Devils in the Coates Talent League to be one of their most impressive performers. Weapon: Intercept ability and long kick. AFL player comparison: Callum Wilkie (St Kilda).
