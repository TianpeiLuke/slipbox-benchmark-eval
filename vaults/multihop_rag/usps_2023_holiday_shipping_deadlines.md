# USPS 2023 Holiday Shipping Deadlines

USPS's 2023 holiday cutoffs run: December 20th for First-Class Mail and USPS Ground Advantage, and December 21st for Priority Mail and Priority Mail Express.

Thursday, December 21st, is the final day to send packages through USPS via Priority Mail Express and have them arrive by Christmas Eve. Packages can also be delivered by December 23rd via Priority Mail. First-Class Mail packages, and those intended for USPS Ground Advantage, must be sent by Wednesday, December 20th, to arrive in time.
