# NFL DFS Week 15 Defense And Special Teams Values

Sporting News' headline Week 15 2023 D/ST play was the Los Angeles Rams against the Commanders (DK $3,100, FD $4,300).

The Rams had been destroyed by MVP candidate Lamar Jackson and the high-flying Ravens in Week 14, but the column urged readers to push that out of their memory banks: before that game Sean McVay's squad had surrendered 20 or fewer points in four straight contests, and Aaron Donald and company had 17 sacks and six takeaways over their past six games, so they would be licking their chops looking across the line at Sam Howell — no quarterback had more interceptions than Howell, who had also taken 10 more sacks than any other signal-caller that season. More solid Week 15 D/ST plays listed were the Chicago Bears at Browns (DK $3,300, FD $4,100); the New York Jets at Dolphins (DK $2,700, FD $3,600); and the New England Patriots vs. Chiefs (DK $2,300, FD $3,200).
