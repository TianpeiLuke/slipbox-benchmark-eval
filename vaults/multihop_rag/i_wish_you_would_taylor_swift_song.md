# I Wish You Would: A 2am Song Whose Real Story Runs In Reverse

I Wish You Would is one of Taylor Swift's many 2am songs, powered by a choppy, unrelenting guitar line that mimics her thoughts as they race uncontrollably in the middle of the night. In the lyric she is pining for an ex, absent-mindedly driving down their street and wishing she had never hung up the phone or broken things off.

In real life, though, the story was reversed — that inversion is the song's defining twist. Swift told Rolling Stone the song was "about an ex who bought a house two blocks from hers", while a hidden message in the CD booklet said: "He drove past her street each night." The BBC's verdict is that the mystery only deepens.
