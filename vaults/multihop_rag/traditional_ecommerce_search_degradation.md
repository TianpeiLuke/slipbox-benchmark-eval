# Why Traditional E-Commerce Search Has Degraded

Deft co-founder Zach Hudson told TechCrunch that there are a few plaguing problems with the traditional search experience that prevent users from finding a suitable item — that is, the claim is that search has gotten worse, not that users have gotten worse at searching.

He offers three grounds, each a different actor degrading the result set: "You have companies like Google and Amazon that continue to push the most relevant listings down so they can make more advertising dollars. Some easy searches are now hard because marketers have learned how to game these search engines. Plus, now you have AI content that is putting the burden on users to separate fact from fiction," he said.

The warrant tying them together is that each mechanism moves cost onto the user: platform ad incentives bury relevance, SEO gaming inflates the noise, and AI-generated content shifts verification work to the person searching.
