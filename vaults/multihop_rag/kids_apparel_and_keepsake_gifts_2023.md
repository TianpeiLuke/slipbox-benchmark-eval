# Kids' Apparel And Keepsake Gifts 2023

The Verge's kids guide lists five apparel and keepsake gifts: Pokémon Matching Family Pajamas at $19.99 (Hanna Andersson), Custom Face Socks at $24+ (DivvyUp), a Chatbooks Subscription Photo Book at $21+ (Chatbooks), Parchie watches at $55.25+ (Amazon, Parchie, Hodinkee), and the Keith Haring Kids' Chair at $116.10+ (The Shop at The Broad, MoMA Design Store).

On the pajamas: if your little ones are engrossed in the Pokémon world, dress-up is a fun way to engage with the hobby — who needs cheesy red flannel and holiday PJs when you can wear the many expressions of Pikachu, which makes for much cuter family photos. DivvyUp's socks let you choose up to three faces to plaster across a pair in up to 14 colors, needing only photos with decent resolution and good, consistent lighting. Chatbooks addresses the hurdle that dooms most family photos to a life dormant in cloud storage — the time to make albums — by letting you take photos from your phone and assemble them into a 30-page photo book delivered to your door with minimal effort. Giving a kid a watch once they can tell time is framed as a small acknowledgment of growing responsibility, and while many children's watches are cheesy, cheap garbage, Parchie's waterproof timepieces are fun, adequately sized for smaller wrists, and built to last without costing a small fortune. The Keith Haring chair, kid-sized and constructed of lacquered beech wood, makes the artist's playful and uplifting work a decorative yet functional part of the home, adding whimsy to any room and looking adorable pulled up to a small desk for arts and crafts.
