# Nocturne's Supporting Cast: Maria, Edouard And Annette

Nocturne reworks a number of Castlevania's classic supporting characters to brilliant effect — Maria, who fights using reimagined versions of the Four Sacred Beasts, and empathic singer Edouard (Sydney James Harcourt) — but the standout among the show's old-but-new faces is ferrokinetic Vodoun practitioner Annette (Thuso Mbedu).

What distinguishes Annette is how strongly the show introduces her as a woman whose Haitian identity is what makes her able to understand the situation in France with an exacting kind of clarity. It is through Annette and the show's exploration of Afro Caribbean syncretism that Nocturne expands the boundaries of Castlevania's magical mythology, a thread touched on heavily throughout the season's eight episodes. Small nods to the video games and gnarly beheadings abound, particularly in the Powerhouse Animation-produced show's bigger hyperkinetic brawls between its heroes and hordes of horrific creatures.
