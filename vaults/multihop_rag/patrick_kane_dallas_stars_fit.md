# Patrick Kane's Fit With The Dallas Stars

Dallas is a dark horse for Kane in the sense that the interest runs mostly one way: the Stars look like one of the best teams in the Western Conference and there were reports Kane would love to end up there, but the rumblings were that the player is much more interested in the team than the other way around.

The obstacle is roster depth. Dallas already has tons of forward depth — arguably the deepest top-nine forward group in the league on paper — so adding Kane would push someone like Mason Marchment or Evgenii Dadonov to the fourth line, and neither is a fourth-line player. The Stars are also up against the cap and would have to move money around to make it work financially.

Elliotte Friedman summarised the mismatch: "I think Kane would love to play there, I'm just not sure Dallas thinks it's the best idea for them. I think they feel if they're going to add, it's going to be on defense." Sporting News' own verdict was that while adding a player who wants to be there is appealing, you don't fix what ain't broke.
