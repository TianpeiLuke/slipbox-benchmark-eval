# ActivityPub And Rival Social Protocols

ActivityPub is the infrastructure typically underlying the fediverse: a decade-old protocol overseen by the World Wide Web Consortium, the group more or less in charge of how the internet works.

It is not alone in the category — there are other similar protocols such as Bluesky's AT Protocol, Nostr and Farcaster — and The Verge's writer said he would bet heavily that ActivityPub becomes the default choice over time. But the boundary he draws is that the identity of the winner matters less than that there is one: it doesn't matter so much which protocol wins as long as one of them does, because we don't need two internets and we don't need two social protocols. What is needed is one thing both simple enough and big enough to handle all the ways we connect with each other online — something no centralized platform has ever been big enough to be, and which the fediverse can be.
