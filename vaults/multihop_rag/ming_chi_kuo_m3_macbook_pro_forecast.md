# Ming-Chi Kuo's M3 MacBook Pro Forecast

Apple analyst Ming-Chi Kuo has suggested that "M3 series MacBook Pro" will be the focus of the event, indicating the company will have more than one new SoC to reveal — so there is a chance of seeing an M3 Pro and maybe even an M3 Max during the Scary Fast showcase.

Kuo does not expect a large inventory. He previously did not anticipate Apple would start selling M3-powered laptops before the end of the year, due to limited supply of between 400,000 and 500,000 units, and said that unless demand drops, supply may remain tight until early 2024.

His explanation for shipping anyway is commercial. MacBook sales have waned this year, mainly due to "the limited M2 computing power upgrade," Kuo wrote on X — which might be why Apple is willing to start shipping M3 MacBook Pros now even with constrained supply. Announcing new models just a few days before reporting quarterly earnings could help give investors confidence in the company, and recent increases of Apple TV+, Apple Arcade and Apple News+ prices could play into that too.

If M3-powered models do not lead to a boost in sales, Kuo suggests it is more likely that a MacBook Pro with a new design will arrive in 2025, and that Apple may try to beef up the division with a more budget-friendly MacBook carrying an annual sales target of between 8 million and 10 million.
