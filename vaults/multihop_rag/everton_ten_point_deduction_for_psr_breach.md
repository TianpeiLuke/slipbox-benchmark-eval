# Everton Ten Point Deduction For PSR Breach

An independent commission imposed a 10-point penalty on Everton for breaching Premier League financial rules — the biggest sporting sanction in Premier League history, for a £19.5m overspend in 2021-22 — as reported by The Guardian on Nov. 24, 2023.

Manager Sean Dyche said he was shocked and aggrieved by the decision, describing the deduction as both disproportionate and unjust. He was ill while on holiday in Dubai when informed of the punishment by Everton's director of football, Kevin Thelwell. Dyche said he was confident Everton's season would not be derailed by the penalty, but warned his players that they could not rely on the club's appeal to rescue them from another relegation battle.
