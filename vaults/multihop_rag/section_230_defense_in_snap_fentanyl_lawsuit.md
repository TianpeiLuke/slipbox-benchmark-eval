# The Section 230 Defense In The Snap Fentanyl Lawsuit

Snapchat's answer to the parents' complaint attacks its legal foundation rather than its facts: the company filed a motion to dismiss citing Section 230 of the Communications Decency Act, which protects online platforms from being held responsible for the illegal actions of their users. A hearing on that motion was scheduled for 18 October.

The plaintiffs' rebuttal concedes the statute but disputes that Snap qualifies for its shelter. Section 230 only offers immunity to companies that have acted responsibly and taken precautions to prevent illegal activity from taking place on their platforms — something lead attorney Matthew Bergman says Snap has not done.

"It's the world's largest open-air drug market," Bergman said. "It was clearly designed with the intention of allowing and encouraging nefarious activity with a lack of record."
