# Havertz And Jorginho's Return To Stamford Bridge

The 21 October 2023 derby would be the first time Kai Havertz and Jorginho had gone back to play at Stamford Bridge since leaving Chelsea for Arsenal, Sky Sports reported the day before.

"I'm sure they'll be excited," said Mikel Arteta. "They were there and they talked really highly about the club. I'm sure it'll be a special day for them."

Havertz would command particular focus after his early struggles at Arsenal: with just one goal in 12 appearances for the club, there were questions about his form and role.
