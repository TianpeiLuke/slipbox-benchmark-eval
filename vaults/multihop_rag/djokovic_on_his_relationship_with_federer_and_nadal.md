# Djokovic On His Relationship With Federer And Nadal

Novak Djokovic said in an interview with 60 Minutes, reported by The Roar on 19 December 2023, that he and Roger Federer and Rafael Nadal have not gotten along very well off the field during their careers and are not friends, because they are rivals.

His stated reason is the difficulty of closeness between opponents: "We're not friends because we're rivals and it's difficult to be close as opponents. Some things you share about your life or how you feel can be used against you."

He paired that with an assertion about respect and a hope for the future, careful to limit the claim to his own side: "We have shared the stage for many years and I think there has always been the utmost respect, at least on my part, towards them. I hope that one day we will be closer, to be able to sit and talk and reflect. It would be truly incredible."
