# Walmart Cyber Monday 2023 Over-Ear Headphone Deals

WIRED's Walmart Cyber Monday roundup of 27 November 2023 recommended six discounted over-ear headphones: Sony's WH-1000XM5 and WH-1000XM4, Bose's QuietComfort Ultra, Sennheiser's HD 660S2, a cheaper pair of Sony noise cancellers and Soundcore's Life Q30.

As Sony's priciest wireless headphones, the WH-1000XM5 had a lot to live up to after the remarkably popular WH-1000XM4, but WIRED says they deliver with upgraded noise cancelling, a posh new design and a more refined sound signature — especially on sale. Bose's QuietComfort Ultra (9/10, WIRED Recommends) arrived in October 2023 with only one notable problem, their very high price; the discount makes it easier to get their class-leading noise cancelling, great sound and luxuriously comfy, feature-loaded design. Sony's older WH-1000XM4 (9/10, WIRED Recommends) have been a stalwart for years, offering excellent noise cancelling, warm and detailed sound and a ton of modern features in a comfy design that puts them among the best travel headphones you can buy.

WIRED's only complaints about Sennheiser's HD 660S2 (7/10, WIRED Review) — bass-enriched audiophile headphones — were the high price and stiff clamping force, and at this price it can live with the tighter fit given gloriously clear and richly detailed sound. Sony's "working-class" noise cancellers offer solid performance, clear sound and impressive battery life at a much more reasonable price than the brand's top offerings, and with this sale WIRED calls them some of the best value in their class from one of the top names in the business. Soundcore's Life Q30 are already impressive at full price thanks to noise cancelling that punches well above its class, so the deal makes them something of a steal, with accessible and adjustable sound quality, good features and great battery life.
