# Barbie (Greta Gerwig)

Barbie is Greta Gerwig's film starring Margot Robbie, Ryan Gosling, and Will Ferrell, ranked 9 on Polygon's best movies of 2023 list of 2023-10-07 and available for digital rental/purchase before streaming on Max.

Polygon concedes that any description of the film's big themes — toxic masculinity, how Barbie branding affects young girls, women as playthings, the commodification of girl power — makes it sound preachy and stilted, but says Gerwig and co-writer Noah Baumbach never hold still long enough for viewers to feel they are sitting through a Gender Studies 101 class. They package these ideas into a giddy satire full of bright and winning performances, pointed jokes aimed at Mattel and the corporate world, terrific casting with Issa Rae as President Barbie, Simu Liu as one of many Kens, and Kate McKinnon as Weird Barbie as standouts, and endless cultural gags, plus bright poppy meta-humor musical numbers including Ryan Gosling turning "I'm Just Ken" into 2023's top movie anthem.

Critic Tasha Robinson calls it perky, playful, and deceptively caustic, one of just a few films — like 1995's The Brady Bunch Movie and 2007's Enchanted — that gleefully satirize a cultural staple while also treating it with real affection. Margot Robbie makes a perfect Barbie, whether in perky-and-plastic mode or slowly revealing her underlying humanity as fears of death and dying leak into the endless party of Barbieland. There is so much going on in this patter-filled, joke-a-second comedy that it feels like 2023's Everything Everywhere All at Once, right down to the big life messages smuggled into all the goofery — a high-speed joke-fest that does not take Barbie any more seriously than she deserves but does pay solemn homage to all the ways, positive and negative, that Barbie fandom makes people feel.
