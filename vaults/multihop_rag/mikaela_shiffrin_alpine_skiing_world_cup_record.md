# Mikaela Shiffrin's Alpine Skiing World Cup Record

Mikaela Shiffrin has made history by achieving the most World Cup wins by any male or female skier ever, as reported by Essentially Sports on 3 November 2023.

Her wider honours are listed alongside that record: two Olympic golds, an impressive five overall World Cup victories, and four world championship wins, and she is the youngest slalom champion in Olympic Alpine skiing history.

The article frames her as a long-dominant force to be reckoned with in the world of Alpine skiing, describing her descending a towering snow-capped mountain with grit and precision like no other, hair billowing in the wind and skis gliding on the glistening ice as if afloat — one with the snow and the mountain. It adds that while her legacy stands tall, her roots run deep as well, which is the article's route into her family.
