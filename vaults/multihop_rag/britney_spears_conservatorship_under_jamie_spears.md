# Britney Spears' Conservatorship Under Jamie Spears

Britney Spears' conservatorship was a legal arrangement, entered into in February 2008, under which her father Jamie Spears took complete control over her assets. It followed a mental breakdown in 2008 that saw her placed on a 5150 psychiatric hold and left Kevin Federline with sole custody of their sons. The defining feature of the arrangement was the transfer of control not only over her finances but over her personal life, and it lasted until November 2021, when Spears was granted her freedom from it.

The turning point was Spears' testimony at her conservatorship hearing in June 2021, when she spoke publicly about her experience for the first time and called for those involved to be "put in jail." Federline expressed support for her after the testimony, saying he "only hopes the best for her because when the best for her is achieved, it's the best for their kids." His lawyer added in a statement at the time: "The best thing would be for their mom to be healthy and happy. And if either of those things aren't true, it doesn't provide for the best setting for custody to be exercised."
