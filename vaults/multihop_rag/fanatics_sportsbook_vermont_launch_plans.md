# Fanatics Sportsbook's Vermont Launch And Its Past Welcome Offers

Fanatics Sportsbook was already scheduled to launch in Vermont in Jan. 2024, Sporting News reported on 2023-09-26, though word was still awaited on what the Fanatics Sportsbook VT welcome offer would be; the site was already live in several other states.

In the past, Fanatics has featured different welcome offers for new sign-ups, including variations on the "bet-and-get" welcome offers described elsewhere in the article. Fanatics has featured a bet $5, get $200 in Bonus Bets offer before, and has also structured the same offer differently by inviting new users to make five bets of at least $10 over five days and receive Bonus Bets each time they do, again totaling $200. Fanatics Sportsbook has also taken advantage of the company's other major business as an official sports apparel and gear provider that partners with all the major leagues and teams: drawing on that connection, Fanatics has sometimes made a free jersey — or, rather, money to spend in the Fanatics Store — part of a welcome offer to new users. The article closes by saying it will stay tuned to see what happens with Fanatics Sportsbook VT.
