# The Fuzzy Cat Bed Office Companion

Senior reporter Elizabeth Lopatto's cat Jeeves loves to be in the office with her while she works — Lopatto assumes she is supervising — so to make her cozier she got her a fuzzy cat bed.

At first Jeeves was afraid of it, being extremely shy around new people and objects.

Now, a year later, it is her favorite place to sit that is not Lopatto's lap, and she usually spends the afternoon snuggled up in it.
