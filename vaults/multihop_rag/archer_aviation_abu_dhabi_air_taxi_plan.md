# Archer Aviation's Abu Dhabi Air Taxi Plan

EVTOL company Archer Aviation plans to start air taxi operations in Abu Dhabi in 2026, TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-22. From there, Archer says it will expand across the UAE as part of a memorandum of understanding with the Abu Dhabi Investment Office.
