# Google Pixel Buds Pro Across The 2023 Sale Season

The Google Pixel Buds Pro were repeatedly discounted through late 2023, from $120 at October Prime Day down to a new low of $115 at Wellbots for Black Friday, and are described across reviews as the best wireless earbuds for Android users.

The Verge's 28 October 2023 post listed them at $117 against $200 (42% off), available to Verge readers at Wellbots for $116.99 — $83 off — with offer code VERGE83, and said that though they launched the previous year they continue to impress with terrific sound quality and good noise cancellation, plus multipoint connectivity, long battery life and a good transparency mode; Pixel phone owners get exclusive access features such as head tracking spatial audio and Google's Clear Calling, which reduces background noise during calls. They are Google's first earbuds to include active noise cancellation, combining impressive sound, great battery life and good comfort without the connection issues of earlier models. Engadget's Prime Day roundups of 10 October 2023 put them at $120 — a new low, $80 off Google's $200 list price — calling them Google's best wireless earbuds yet with punchy bass, good ANC, reliable touch controls and wireless charging, and noting an upcoming firmware update promising improved call quality and automatic pausing when you speak.

Engadget's Black Friday coverage moved the price lower: on 19 November 2023 it reported $115 at Wellbots, "the best price we've ever seen", and on 24 November 2023 it called $115 a new low, about $50 off their average street price, with other retailers like Amazon and Best Buy $5 higher. Engadget's verdict across those pieces is that the Pixel Buds Pro are the "best for Android" pick due to tight integration with the OS and Google's Pixel phones, that their ANC and V-shaped sound are solid if not superlative, that battery can last up to 11 hours depending on ANC use, and that the fit may be tricky for those with smaller ear canals; the pair scored 87 in Engadget's review the previous year.
