# BG3 Ranger: The Beginner Ranged Class

Polygon names Ranger the best ranged class for beginners in Baldur's Gate 3. Rangers are agile hunters who focus on Dexterity as their primary Ability and combine melee, stealth, skills, and magic — a mix that also makes the class harder to get the most out of.

Rangers get Light and Medium Armors and some of the more basic weapons. They also make a lot of choices during character creation and levelling that determine how they play: at level 1 you choose a Favored Enemy — a preferred quarry — and a preferred environment that grants some magical spells. At level 3 you choose a Ranger subclass from Beast Master, Gloom Stalker, or Hunter. Beast Masters get a Companion animal that fights alongside you, Gloom Stalkers focus on stealth, and Hunters get to specialize their melee abilities. Hunter is the best subclass for a Ranger, with the supporting reasoning and the skill and feat picks left to Polygon's separate Ranger build guide.
