# Colby McKercher AFL Draft Prospect 2023

Colby McKercher, profiled in Kevin Sheehan's top 40 for the 2023 AFL Draft as reported by The Roar on Nov. 15, 2023, had an outstanding year which included winning the Coates Talent League's Morrish Medal and finishing runner-up in the Larke Medal at the National Championships. State: Tasmania. State League Club: Tasmania Devils. Community Club: Launceston. Date of Birth: 12/04/05. Height: 180cm.

He uses his dazzling speed to break the lines and kicks the ball with pinpoint accuracy on his preferred left foot. He averaged 32.8 disposals and 5.8 clearances across four matches for the Allies at the National Championships to earn All Australian honours, and was equally dominant for the Tasmania Devils in the Coates Talent League, having more than 30 disposals in seven of his nine matches and kicking 12 goals. He showed he could match it against more experienced opponents by having 20 disposals and three clearances in Australia's win over Carlton's VFL team. Weapon: Dazzling speed and ball-winning ability. AFL player comparison: Zac Merrett (Essendon).
