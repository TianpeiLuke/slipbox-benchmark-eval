# 8BitDo Ultimate 2.4G Controller

8BitDo has released a lot of controllers in recent years, but Polygon says the name of its Ultimate controller is fitting: it carries many of the features from the company's best gamepads plus some extras that should settle any sticker shock at its $49.99 price.

Its asymmetrical analog stick layout, overall shape, and face button labels should feel right at home for people who love the shape of the Xbox controller, and all of its buttons, triggers, and sticks feel just as good to use as any modern controller from a console manufacturer. Like several other 8BitDo controllers it can be thoroughly customized with the company's Ultimate software on mobile devices or on PC, where you can remap controls, assign buttons to its two rear paddle buttons, tweak the actuation sensitivity of its triggers, and adjust stick settings; it allows up to three custom control schemes, switchable with its middle "star" button. It ships with a charging dock, and nesting inside that dock is a 2.4 GHz transmitter that makes connecting to a PC fast and easy. The dock is a convenient way to charge the controller, and a switch on the controller's back lets you move between DirectInput and XInput depending on the requirements of each PC game.
