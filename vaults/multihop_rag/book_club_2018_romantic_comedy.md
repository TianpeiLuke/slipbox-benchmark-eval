# Book Club (2018): The Raunchy Late-Life Romantic Comedy

Book Club is a 2018 romantic comedy directed by Bill Holderman, running 1h 44m, starring Diane Keaton, Jane Fonda, Candice Bergen and Mary Steenburgen as a group of best friends who have long belonged to a book club. Each of them is successful in her career but dealing with a crisis of life or love.

The film's hinge is the group's choice of reading: when one of them picks Fifty Shades of Grey as the next book they will all read together, it opens the group up, in what Polygon calls a lovely story of personal acceptance and self-realisation, no matter what stage of life you find yourself in. The reviewer describes the film as delightful and raunchy. It is available to stream on Paramount Plus and FuboTV, or for digital rental or purchase on Amazon, Apple TV, Google Play and Vudu.
