# Liverpool's Attacking Form: Núñez And Salah

Liverpool's progress in October 2023 had arrived in attack: Darwin Núñez's influence had improved considerably since his match-winning cameo for the 10 men of Liverpool at Newcastle, and Mohamed Salah had scored or assisted in 14 consecutive Premier League matches.

The side stood on the verge of a club-historical marker, able to emulate their predecessors from 1980-81 by scoring three or more goals in the opening four home league games. Stopping goals, however, had been the problem, with Liverpool conceding first and cheaply on occasions.

Klopp insisted he had not built another formidable attack at the expense of a resilient defence; it was, in his framing, just another step Liverpool had to make.
