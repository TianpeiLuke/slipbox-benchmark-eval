# Breathe Battery Technologies' Charging Software

Breathe Battery Technologies is a startup that has developed a piece of software which can be slipped into just about any lithium-ion battery in use today, giving that battery either faster charging speeds or greater longevity; Tim covered it in TechCrunch's Week in Review of October 7, 2023.

The claim that makes the item notable is the breadth of the retrofit: the gain is delivered in software rather than in new cell chemistry or new hardware, and it is described as applying to lithium-ion batteries already deployed. The two benefits are presented as alternatives — a battery gets faster charging or longer life — not as a simultaneous improvement in both.
