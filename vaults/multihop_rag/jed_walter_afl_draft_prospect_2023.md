# Jed Walter AFL Draft Prospect 2023

Jed Walter, profiled in Kevin Sheehan's top 40 for the 2023 AFL Draft as reported by The Roar on Nov. 15, 2023, is a strong and powerful tall forward who has taken all before him across the past two years for the Gold Coast Suns Academy and Allies, winning All Australian honours at under-18 level in both seasons. State: Queensland. State League Club: Gold Coast Suns Academy. Community Club: Palm Beach Currumbin. Date of Birth: 08/06/05. Height: 194cm.

He has a real physical presence up forward with his appetite for the contest, along with his second efforts and tackling pressure. He was a threatening target up forward for the Allies during that year's National Championships, averaging 13 disposals and seven marks as well as kicking 11 goals in four matches to be a vital cog in the title-winning team; overall he kicked 29 goals in eight matches that season to round off a splendid underage career. The draft-night expectation attached to him: he will receive a bid from a rival club very early, which the Suns will gladly match. Weapon: Aerial ability and second efforts at ground level. AFL player comparison: Charlie Curnow (Carlton).
