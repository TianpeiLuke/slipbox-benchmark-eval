# Best Buy Holiday 2023 Shipping And Pickup Cutoffs

Best Buy's free-shipping cutoff for arrival by December 23rd is December 21st at 11:30AM ET, with free same-day delivery available for orders placed on December 23rd or December 24th by 12PM local time for delivery by 7PM.

My Best Buy Plus and My Best Buy Total subscribers get free two-day shipping with no order minimum; other customers need to clear the $35 order threshold, calculated before taxes but after coupons, and certain items are available for next-day and standard shipping. Free next-day delivery is available on select items through December 23rd. Shoppers can also order online by 5PM local time on Christmas Eve for an in-store pickup at 7PM. The Verge notes Best Buy's shipment arrival dates may vary depending on the kind of item, and that major appliances may take longer to be delivered at this point.
