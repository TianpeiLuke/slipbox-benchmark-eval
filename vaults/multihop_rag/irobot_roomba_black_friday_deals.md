# iRobot Roomba Black Friday Deals

Shoppers could save $300 on the Roomba Combo j5+ robot vacuum and mop at Amazon for Black Friday 2023, spending $500 for the machine and its self-emptying base, while the more affordable Roomba 694 was $115 off.

The Combo machine lets you vacuum and mop with one machine and comes with a base into which the vacuum dustbin empties its contents after every job. The cheaper Roomba 694 is one of the best robovacs for people on a budget thanks to its slim design, strong suction power and easy-to-use mobile app.
