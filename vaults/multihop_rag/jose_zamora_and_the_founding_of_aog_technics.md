# Jose Zamora And The Founding Of AOG Technics

Jose Zamora, who is Venezuelan, founded AOG Technics in 2015 from a rented terraced house in Hove, about 70 kilometres south of London on the UK's South Coast, having got his start in aviation in 2010 as an account manager at AJW, an engine maintenance business, according to Bloomberg.

Business associates who worked with him outside AOG and who were willing to speak to The Telegraph describe a savvy operator who was fun to be around; a keen techno DJ, he was also gigging as recently as 2018 according to the Daily Mail. The company appeared small scale in its early years, its registered address moving between four homes in the following three years, shifting from Hove to London in 2017, after which it was registered at serviced offices in central London. Friends say they have not been in contact with Zamora in a number of months as he begins his defence in London's High Court. AOG's website appears offline, but a cached version says the company offered "a diverse portfolio of quality products and services" and claimed: "With our head office in the UK and warehouse operations in London, Frankfurt, Miami and Singapore, we are uniquely positioned to support our customers around the world – 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. We keep our clients flying." The company's phone line is still connected but offers only endless hold music.
