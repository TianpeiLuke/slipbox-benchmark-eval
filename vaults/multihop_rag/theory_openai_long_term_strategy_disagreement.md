# Theory: A Long-Term Strategy Disagreement Over OpenAI's Economics

The proposition is that Altman and the board fell out over when OpenAI's money-losing growth strategy has to end. Despite being the hottest tech company in the world and everyone talking about ChatGPT, OpenAI is not exactly a sound business: it is shoveling money into the furnace as fast as it can by serving, by all accounts, a fantastically expensive product at bargain-bin prices. That is all well and good for a year or two, but at some point that strategy changes from a growth hack to an existential liability — so could Altman and the board have had irreconcilable differences on where that point lies?

TechCrunch judges this unlikely, and gives the disconfirming evidence: the company has been very deliberately pursuing this very publicly, confidently and on a long-term basis. Altman and the board seem to be in sync on this, at least for the present.
