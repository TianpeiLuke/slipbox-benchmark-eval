# The Value Of Humanoid Research Beyond The Final Form

All the attention — and money — poured into making ungainly humanoid machines might make the whole enterprise seem like a futile hobby for wealthy technologists, but for some pioneers of legged robots the justification is what you learn along the way rather than the machine you end up with.

Marc Raibert, the co-founder of Boston Dynamics — best known for its dog-like robots named Spot — enumerates what that learning covers: "Not only about their design and operation, but also about how people respond to them, and about the critical underlying technologies for mobility, dexterity, perception and intelligence."
