# Charlotte Wood's October 2023 Novel

Charlotte Wood's novel, published by Allen & Unwin at $32.99 on 3 October, is another reckoning with a mother's death — this time in fiction — in which a woman arrives at some sort of religious community having turned her back on her husband, their life and the metropolis, The Sydney Morning Herald listed on 1 October 2023.

The questions the book poses are what she is fleeing and what her demons are. It is not all peace and tranquillity in the community, which has collective crises to deal with, while her previous life will not leave her alone.

Written in spare prose, the reviewer says, "the consistently brilliant Wood delivers yet again".
