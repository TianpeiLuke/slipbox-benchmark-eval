# Warriors Recent Draft Class Outcomes

Golden State's original sins run deeper than the 2020 and 2021 drafts, Yardbarker reported on Nov. 29, 2023, reviewing the team's recent draft history: Kevon Looney, picked in 2015, is the only indisputable success, and beyond that Jordan Poole is the only other draft pick to have signed another contract with the Warriors beyond his rookie deal.

Poole, at 28, helped power the Warriors to the 2022 championship but was in equal measure part of the problem the next season — and based on his season in Washington, his 2022 playoff run was looking more and more like an outlier. Four draft picks were by then on other teams: Patrick Baldwin, Wiseman, Poole and Damian Jones; and four were no longer in the league: Nico Mannion, Justinian Jessup, Eric Paschall and Jacob Evans. June's 19th overall pick Brandin Podziemski seemed like a base hit at that point, but it had only been a month — the writer recalling the brief flashes seen from Paschall.
