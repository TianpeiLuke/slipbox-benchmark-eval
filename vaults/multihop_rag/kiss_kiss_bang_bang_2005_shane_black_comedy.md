# Kiss Kiss Bang Bang (2005): Shane Black's Neo-Noir Black Comedy

Kiss Kiss Bang Bang is Shane Black's 2005 neo-noir black comedy crime thriller, running 1h 42m, starring Robert Downey Jr., Val Kilmer and Michelle Monaghan, and partially based on Brett Halliday's 1941 novel Bodies Are Where You Find Them. Downey plays Harry Lockhart, a petty thief who, through a series of extraordinary circumstances, is mistaken for an actor and whisked from the back alleys of New York to the twinkling lights of Los Angeles for a screen test — where he becomes ensnared in a murder mystery involving his childhood crush (Monaghan), a sarcastic private detective (Kilmer), and a retired actor (Corbin Bernsen) with a terrible secret to hide.

Polygon's reviewer calls it one of if not the funniest and most effortlessly cool movie he has ever seen: relentlessly meta, wickedly funny, and boasting one of the coolest opening title sequences of its time. He positions it as the rough-and-tumble blueprint for Black's 2016 film The Nice Guys, and by all degrees the better of the two. It is available for digital rental or purchase at Amazon, Apple and Google Play.
