# Portal: Companion Collection on Switch

The Portal Switch remaster includes both Portal and Portal 2, which Polygon calls seminal games well worth the revisit; it appears in the outlet's 2023-10-20 list of the 35 best couch co-op games for Nintendo Switch.

In these games you explore and solve puzzles by using a portal gun, and Polygon says experimenting within the game's physics is even more fun with a friend in tow.

There is also a mechanical reason the co-op mode differs: when playing local co-op, the number of portals that can be placed is doubled.
