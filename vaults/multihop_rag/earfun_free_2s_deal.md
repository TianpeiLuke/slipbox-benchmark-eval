# EarFun Free 2S Deal

The EarFun Free 2S came down to $24.59 at Amazon for Black Friday 2023 with a Prime-only discount and the checkout code FREE2SPD1 — an all-time low.

The Free 2S is the "best under $50" pick in Engadget's budget wireless earbuds guide. It has a hugely bass-forward sound out of the box, which some may enjoy with hip-hop or EDM, and the profile is customizable through the EarFun app; the earpieces are comfortable and fully waterproof, the battery lasts around seven hours, and the suite of touch controls work reliably, which isn't a given in this price range. There's no ANC, multi-device connectivity or wear detection, however, and the built-in mic will pick up wind noise on a call outside.
