# Boston Dynamics On Generative AI's Role In Robotics

Boston Dynamics CTO Aaron Saunders argues that foundation models will ultimately extend past language and vision into robotic planning and control. Saunders has been with the company for more than 20 years, most recently serving as its vice president of Engineering, and gave this answer in a TechCrunch robotics Q&A published on 9 December 2023.

He frames the claim with an explicit limit on foresight: the current rate of change makes it hard to predict very far into the future. His grounds are near-term evidence — foundation models represent a major shift in how the best machine learning models are created, and impressive near-term accelerations are already visible in natural language interfaces, which offer opportunities to create conversational interfaces to robots, improve the quality of existing computer vision functions and potentially enable new customer-facing capabilities such as visual question answering.

The warrant joining those to the larger claim is scalability: these more scalable architectures and training strategies are likely to carry over, and being able to interpret the world around a robot will lead to a much richer understanding of how to interact with it. "It's a really exciting time to be a roboticist!" he says.
