# Jacksonville Jaguars' Contender Readiness, 2023

The New York Times argued on 16 October 2023 that the Jaguars, more than the other AFC contenders, seem early in their life cycle and still finding their way — which makes them a little harder to trust in big spots but also leaves greater space for growth.

The immediate scope condition is Trevor Lawrence's knee injury, the big concern coming out of that Sunday: if he is out, expectations shift dramatically, and the writer hopes the test results come back clear.

An executive gave the positive case, resting on coaching pedigree and experience rather than current form: "I like Jacksonville. Doug (Pederson) has a championship pedigree. This team is relatively young. They are scrappy. The fact that they have been there before gives them a chance. I think the quarterback is growing."
