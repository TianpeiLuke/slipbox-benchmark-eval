# France Beat Italy To Top Rugby World Cup Pool A

France reached the Rugby World Cup quarter-finals on 6 October 2023 with a rout of a demoralised Italy, the unthinkable outcome of a pool-stage exit for Fabien Galthié's side never on the cards from the moment Damian Penaud scored the first of eight French tries in the second minute. It was France's 14th consecutive win over Italy, a run stretching back to 2014.

Galthié framed the eight-try display in purely functional terms: "Our goal is to win the game. There are several boxes to tick. The important thing is that at the end of the game, we end up ahead. We are not here to show off, or to look good. We're here to win." He added that he expected the injured Antoine Dupont and Julien Marchand to play during the knockout stages.

Asked about the following Sunday's opponents, flanker Charles Ollivon was interrupted by his head coach: "We can't know our opponent. Have you played tomorrow's game already? There is a Scotland v Ireland game that may modify the quarter-finals. If you are talking about our quarter-final opponents, it's quite simple. It's a World Cup final for us."
