# Panthers' Fourth-Quarter Bid And Pineiro's 59-Yarder

Carolina's fourth-quarter comeback attempt against the Bears on Nov. 9, 2023 ended with Eddy Pineiro's 59-yard field goal falling short at 11:06 p.m., and 16 points turned out to be all Chicago needed. After Pineiro had cut the deficit to three early in the fourth quarter with a good kick at 10:42 p.m., Frank Reich put his trust in Pineiro again with the game on the line, sending him out for a 59-yard attempt on fourth-and-10 in the final minutes rather than trusting the offense to pick up the 10 yards; the gamble failed.

The drive that produced the first fourth-quarter field goal was built on conversions. At 10:37 p.m. a neutral zone infraction pulled the Panthers closer and they lined up to go for it on fourth-and-6; at 10:38 p.m. Young found Tremble for the first down on that fourth-and-6, turning a first-and-30 into a fresh set of downs. At 10:41 p.m. Young's third-down pass to Marshall was incomplete against good coverage, bringing on the field goal try that Pineiro converted to make it Bears 16, Panthers 13.

The final drive followed the same laborious pattern. At 10:46 p.m. Bagent found Cole Kmet for a first down and more — Kmet had become a trusted target for Bagent across the rookie's four starts — before Deion Jones dropped Roschon Johnson for a three-yard loss at 10:49 p.m. and the Bears punted, giving Carolina a chance to tie. At 10:52 p.m. the tush-push failed as Chuba Hubbard went nowhere on third-and-1; at 10:54 p.m. Young ran to the right on fourth-and-inches from deep in his own territory to extend the drive; at 10:56 p.m. Hayden Hurst dropped a pass on second-and-3; at 10:57 p.m. Thielen made a catch on the run for another first down; and at 11:00 p.m. Miles Sanders got wide open on fourth down to extend the drive, leaving the Panthers 3-for-3 on fourth down for the night. At 11:04 p.m. Young was very nearly intercepted on third-and-10 and Carolina sent Pineiro out for the 59-yarder to tie. After the miss, Bagent hit Mooney for a first down on third-and-7 at 11:08 p.m., leaving the Panthers unable to stop the clock. The game was carried on Prime Video.
