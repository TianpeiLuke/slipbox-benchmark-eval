# Week 13 Packers-Chiefs: Spagnuolo's Blitz Wave Should Undo Jordan Love's Improvement

CBS Sports argues that Jordan Love's recent improvement will not survive the step up in competition to the Chiefs, and that Kansas City's coverage will also cap Green Bay's receivers in Week 13. The grounds on Love: he has posted 20-plus Fantasy points in three straight games, but it is his past two where there has been real improvement in his play, including a low 8.3% off-target rate to go with a 68.1% completion rate and 8.2 yards per attempt. The rebuttal is the opponent — the step up in competition will clearly impact him and his offensive line, which played better in their games against the Chargers and Lions. The mechanism: Steve Spagnuolo's defense has been blitzing its tails off over the past two weeks, at nearly 50% of their snaps against the Eagles and Raiders, so the hunch is they will apply maximum pressure to that offensive line in an effort to make Love more off-target than he has been and, hopefully, get some turnovers.

A second strand caps the Packers pass-catchers: only one receiver has had more than 15 PPR points against the Chiefs in their past four games — Jakobi Meyers last week — making it tough to expect grand games from Love's favorite targets, and shutdown cornerback L'Jarius Sneed is expected to line up across from Christian Watson the most and Jayden Reed the least. The verdicts: STARTS — Patrick Mahomes, Travis Kelce, Isiah Pacheco, Rashee Rice (worth a look as a WR2), Jayden Reed (low-end WR2/high-end PPR flex) and the Chiefs DST; SITS — Jordan Love (low-end QB1 at best), Christian Watson (bye-week replacement at best), A.J. Dillon (flex at best), Romeo Doubs and the Packers DST.
