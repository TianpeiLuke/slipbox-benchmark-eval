# Panthers Offense Struggles Under Bryce Young

Through the first nine games of the 2023 NFL season the Carolina Panthers' offense failed to come together around rookie quarterback Bryce Young, leaving the team at the bottom of the NFC South with one of the worst records in the league.

Sporting News reported on Nov. 6, 2023 that the Panthers had entered the season shrouded in optimism, having moved on from Matt Rhule — who proved to be an ineffective NFL coach — and added a potential franchise quarterback in Young, with the hope that those moves would make Carolina a contender in the wide-open NFC South. Instead they sat at 1-7. Young had not yet looked comfortable in the offense and was coming off one of his worst games as a starter against the Colts, throwing three interceptions including two pick-sixes to Kenny Moore in a 27-13 defeat. The rest of the offense had not been much better: Adam Thielen looked rejuvenated in his new home, but running back Miles Sanders had been a bust acquisition, and the offensive line had allowed 74 pressures on the season, the 10th-most in the NFL.

Three days later, in the Nov. 9 loss to the Bears, the pattern held. Young got little help from receivers who failed to get open all night, and his struggles — including multiple passes that were nearly intercepted — were a reminder that the offense was still a long way from resembling anything like the unit owner David Tepper envisioned when the franchise traded for the No. 1 pick in March. As of 10:06 p.m., outside a 45-yard completion to Mike Strachan, Young was 7-of-16 for 24 yards, and Sporting News found it tough to recall any positive moments for the offense aside from that one play, concluding Carolina had serious questions to answer. Earlier, Young had been left lining up in his own end zone after Miles Sanders was dropped for a six-yard loss, and less than a minute into the second half Carolina gained a grand total of one yard — minus four including a delay of game penalty — before punting again.
