# The Naimi Park Office Development At Messubim

Property developer Miki Naimi, who is building the large office park near the Messubim Interchange, does not sound bothered by the war: he says they are continuing to progress, that real estate is not for a year, that he is not afraid of the situation and the war will also end, and that they are currently in advanced talks with three companies that will join them in Naimi Park.

On the scale of the project, Naimi says they have built 140,000 square meters out of 350,000 square meters and are now developing another 140,000 square meters of office space with 100,000 square meters of parking lots designed by MYS architects, and that their location is the most central in the country, so he is not afraid to plan and build four more buildings. The first building to be occupied, with an area of 35,000 square meters, is mostly marketed with occupancy already set: the first company to move in, as early as January, is Audicodes, which has leased 10,500 square meters and is implementing finishing works; in March-April additional companies will enter, and Sheba Medical Center Tel Hashomer will also enter there, having leased 2,600 square meters and set to increase to 5,300 square meters.
