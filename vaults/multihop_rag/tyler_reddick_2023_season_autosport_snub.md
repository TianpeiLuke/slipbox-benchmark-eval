# Tyler Reddick 2023 Season Autosport Snub

Essentially Sports argued on Dec. 21, 2023 that Tyler Reddick belonged among the major exclusions from Autosport's Top 50 drivers of 2023, having recorded his best-ever finish in the Cup Series and been among the dark horses for the title.

The grounds start with team context: 2023 was a year of milestones for 23XI Racing, which had two drivers in the Cup Series playoffs for the first time since its inception, and one of the major reasons was #45 Toyota Camry driver Tyler Reddick. Alongside team owner Denny Hamlin, Reddick was the pick of the drivers in the first round of the playoffs; the 27-year-old California native showcased his road course prowess with a win at COTA earlier that year and carried the momentum into the playoffs with a win at Kansas in the Round of 16 to gain traction for his championship push. He also had one of the best average starting positions among the drivers, 10.167, despite having only two poles. Reddick finished sixth in the final standings with two wins, ten top-fives and 16 top-10s, and led 470 laps through the season — certainly holding the potential to win the competition.
