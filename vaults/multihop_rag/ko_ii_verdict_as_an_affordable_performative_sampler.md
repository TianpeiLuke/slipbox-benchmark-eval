# The K.O. II Verdict As An Affordable Performative Sampler

Engadget concluded that the K.O. II represents an opportunity for Teenage Engineering to do the unthinkable and create a series of more capable instruments that don't cost Field-series levels of money.

The grounds are a scoped assessment of what the sampler is for: as a sampler it's great for beginners or those who love a more performative style, and it is not nearly as detailed and in-depth as something like Roland's SP 404 or Native Instruments' Maschine — but, the reviewer argues, it was never going to be a rival to anything really, so that comparison isn't the standard it should be judged by. The warrant is who the company has been serving lately: fadergate aside, this is a promising product from a company that has tested the loyalty of its fans more than usual in recent years.
