# Jesse Bradley and The Power of the Second Thought

"The Power of the Second Thought" is Pastor Jesse Bradley's second book, and its central idea is that the first thoughts we have are not always the best — so it is wise to take some time and get to the "second thoughts", which can yield greater understanding. Bradley said he wrote it to help people gain habits to strengthen their mental health.

Bradley, of Auburn, Washington, runs Grace Community Church and has ministered to many people struggling with a variety of faith dilemmas and issues in their lives. In October 2023, as Americans grappled with a variety of issues, he addressed the notion of positive thinking and shared with Fox News Digital what it would "look like to change your thinking and shift in a new direction."

The concept has a personal source. Bradley graduated from Dartmouth College and was playing professional soccer when he became ill — something that took 10 years to fully recover from. He said finding hope during that time was challenging but is how he changes his "hopeless first thoughts": "I didn't have a roadmap for finding hope or the tools to handle the intensity of my loss and pain," he said.
