# Falun Gong And Li Hongzhi's Teachings

Falun Gong — or Falun Dafa, as some followers call it — is a kind of personal development movement started by Li in China in 1992, combining tenets of Buddhism and Taoism, whose followers practise through meditation and flowing breath and movement exercises and by studying Li's teachings.

What distinguishes it from an ordinary wellness practice is the status accorded to its founder. To his followers, Li is a God-like figure who can levitate, walk through walls and see into the future.

The content of his teaching is described as ultra-conservative and controversial: it includes a rejection of modern science, art and medicine, and a denunciation of homosexuality, feminism and general worldliness.
