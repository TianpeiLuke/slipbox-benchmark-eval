# NHTSA Investigation Of Cruise Pedestrian Incidents

The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) has opened an investigation into GM subsidiary Cruise's AV system following several incidents involving pedestrians in San Francisco, TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-22.

The most recent of those incidents left a woman stuck under a Cruise robotaxi after being hit by a human-driven vehicle.
