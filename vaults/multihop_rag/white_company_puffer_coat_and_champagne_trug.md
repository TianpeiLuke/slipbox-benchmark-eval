# The White Company's Puffer Coat And Champagne Trug

The final section of the guide covers the stretch of Christmas when everyone loses track of time — unsure what day it is, or whether it is acceptable to eat mince pies and brandy butter for breakfast — but where there is still time for a visit to old friends, a quiet walk in the frosty woods, and the fizz and sparkle of New Year parties.

For getting out of the house on the coldest mornings, the guide picks the Down Filled Oversized Puffer Coat, made from recycled fibres with a detachable hood, described as embracing the wearer in a pillowy cuddle. For New Year's celebrations it picks the Champagne Trug as a party essential: hand-soldered in nickel-plated stainless steel, it chills Champagne to perfection once filled with ice, and doubles as a statement piece announcing that yours is the place to go back to during the festive period and beyond.
