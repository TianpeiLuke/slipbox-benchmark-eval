# Recommended Feats For A BG3 Warlock

Every four levels a Baldur's Gate 3 character can either increase stats or choose a Feat — a special talent that adds features to the character — and Polygon names four Feats that Warlocks benefit from once you are happy with your stats.

Actor increases your Charisma by 1, to a maximum of 20, and doubles your Proficiency Bonus for Deception and Performance Checks. Elemental Adept makes your spells ignore Resistance to a damage type of your choice, and when you cast spells of that type you cannot roll a 1. Moderately Armored grants Armor Proficiency with Medium Armor and shields and increases your Strength or Dexterity by 1, to a maximum of 20. Spell Sniper teaches you a cantrip and reduces by 1 the number you need to roll a Critical Hit while attacking, an effect that can stack.
