# 49ers 42-19 Eagles, Week 13 2023

Brock Purdy threw for 314 yards and four touchdowns and Deebo Samuel scored three touchdowns as the San Francisco 49ers won 42-19 at Philadelphia on Sunday, Dec. 3, 2023, with Eagles quarterback Jalen Hurts checked for a concussion during the game.

The fiery NFC championship game rematch also featured 49ers touchdowns on six straight possessions and a pair of ejections that showed this year's title game may not run again through Philly after all. The 49ers (9-3) had left Philly 10 months earlier battered in the NFC championship game behind injuries to Purdy and his backup, never standing a chance in a 31-7 loss; they spent an offseason chirping at the Birds — Samuel called one Eagles cornerback "trash" — and then entered the Linc as a rare betting favorite against a 10-win team at home. The Eagles (10-2) suffered a scare when Hurts jogged off the field in the fourth quarter and went straight to the locker room; it was not immediately clear when he was injured. He was cleared to play and returned with about 10 minutes left, by which time last season's NFL MVP runner-up found the Eagles trailing 35-13 and on their way to their first home loss of the season. Hurts hit DeVonta Smith for a two-yard touchdown on his return drive and finished 26-of-45 passing for 298 yards, while Purdy put the finishing touches on the win with a 46-yard touchdown to Samuel.
