# The End Of Honda e Production

By December 2023 Honda said it would cease production of the Honda e in January 2024. The little buggy had debuted in Europe and Japan in 2020 with a 100-mile range and a $36,000–$43,000 price tag.

The car charmed reviewers with its city-friendly size and sweet-yet-vacant stare, but as far as sales went it proved to be a total flop, thanks in no small part to its weak price-to-range ratio. Honda never brought the e to other locales.
