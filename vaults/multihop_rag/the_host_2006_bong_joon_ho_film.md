# The Host: Bong Joon-ho's Han River Monster Movie As Political Commentary

The Host is Bong Joon-ho's monster film starring Song Kang-ho, Byun Hee-bong and Park Hae-il, watchable on Max, Roku Channel, Hoopla and Kanopy. It was his follow-up to the smash success serial killer drama Memories of Murder, and was both a critical and commercial success: it became the highest-grossing South Korean film ever after its release and won Best Film at the Asian Film Awards and the Blue Dragon Film Awards.

The premise: years after chemicals are dumped into the Han River, a huge mutated fish monster emerges and kidnaps a young girl; her father (Song Kang-ho) sets out to find and rescue her, before being kidnapped by the American scientists responsible for the creature's existence. What distinguishes it as horror is its double register — a fun monster thriller that doubles as insightful commentary on U.S. intervention, ecological disasters, and much more — and Polygon calls it a high mark in Bong's impressive filmography.
