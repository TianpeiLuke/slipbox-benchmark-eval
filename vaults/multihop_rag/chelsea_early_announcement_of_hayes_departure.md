# Chelsea's Early Announcement Of Hayes' Departure

Chelsea announced Emma Hayes' departure on Saturday 4 November 2023, at 11 a.m. ET in the U.S., before her contract with U.S. Soccer was finalized and while board approval was still needed — the main visible wrinkle in the process, as reported by The New York Times on 15 November 2023.

The club's statement noted she would leave at the end of the season "to pursue a new opportunity outside of the WSL and club football." The club feared the news was starting to leak and wanted to share it on its own, which began the race to confirm Hayes had been selected as the USWNT head coach. Talks between U.S. Soccer and Hayes' representatives continued even after the press release, and the federation's board convened late that same Saturday to approve the selection, even without the final details of the contract settled or signed.

Hayes' own response was about process rather than substance: "I believe in private conversations. Of course, I'm disappointed to hear things being said in the press. I want to make sure I maintain my own professionalism in everything I do." The report adds that U.S. Soccer's rollout of their new head coach has not been an easy one for the federation's communications staff, considering Hayes is essentially unavailable for any formal ceremonies or media appearances until her time with Chelsea is complete.
