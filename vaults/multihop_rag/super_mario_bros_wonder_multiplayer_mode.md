# Multiplayer In Super Mario Bros. Wonder

Super Mario Bros. Wonder shines as a solo experience but, like other recent 2D Mario games, supports up to four players together — a mode Polygon describes as both a delight and utter chaos. The character roster divides into two kinds. The humanoid characters (Mario, Peach, Toad and so on) are "like M&Ms": different exteriors that effectively play the same.

The critters are the discriminating case, intended for players who want a little extra help: Nabbit and Yoshi won't take damage, and the Yoshis come with their familiar tongue attack and floaty jump.
