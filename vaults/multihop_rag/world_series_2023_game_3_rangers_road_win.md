# Rangers Win World Series Game 3, 3-1, For Ninth Straight Road Playoff Win

Texas beat the Diamondbacks 3-1 in Game 3 of the 2023 World Series on Oct. 30, 2023, picking up its ninth consecutive road victory of the playoffs and taking a 2-1 series lead. The Sporting News summed the Rangers up as continuing to be unstoppable road warriors.

The Diamondbacks' offense struggled to string together hits and could not cash in when they had runners in scoring position. Even though Arizona out-hit the Rangers, its bats hardly put any pressure on Texas' pitching.

The line score shows all the Rangers' scoring came in one frame: Texas put up three runs in the third inning and none in the other eight, while Arizona's only run came in the eighth — Rangers 0-0-3-0-0-0-0-0-0 for 3, Diamondbacks 0-0-0-0-0-0-0-1-0 for 1.
