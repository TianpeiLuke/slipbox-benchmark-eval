# Liam Paro's World Title Ambitions

Liam Paro argues that one good performance can put him right back at the front of the queue for a world title, despite having slipped from number one in the division with the WBO to 11th in line — a casualty of his inactivity.

His grounds are his record at the top level and his intent for the fight ahead. "I'm going to get my title shot," he said. "I've proved it time and time again that I belong at the top level and I intend to do that on December 9, too, and stamp my way into a world title into an opposite corner and win it when I get my shot."

The plan beyond that is volume, driven by how much the layoff cost him: "2024, I want a busy year. I want to fight three times next year. I've only had one round in two years. I missed the sport and it's lit a new fire in my belly."
