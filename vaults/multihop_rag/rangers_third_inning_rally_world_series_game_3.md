# The Rangers' Three-Run Third Inning In World Series Game 3

All three Texas runs in Game 3 of the 2023 World Series came in the third inning, powered by two-out magic: Marcus Semien finally found his bat with an RBI single before Corey Seager demolished a two-run home run 421 feet to right field, making it Rangers 3, Diamondbacks 0.

The rally started at 8:36 p.m. when Nathaniel Lowe got the Rangers going right away with a double to right-center field. At 8:39 p.m. Brandon Pfaadt struck out Josh Jung swinging and got Leody Tavares to ground out to second, but Lowe advanced to third — 90 feet away with two outs and Semien coming to the plate. After Semien's single and Seager's homer, Pfaadt got Adolis Garcia to strike out swinging at 8:45 p.m. to end the inning, limiting the damage to three runs.

Arizona did not answer right away. At 8:51 p.m. Max Scherzer got through the bottom of the third with little resistance, striking out Evan Longoria and getting Geraldo Perdomo to fly out to right before issuing a two-out walk to Corbin Carroll; Carroll advanced to second on a wild pitch, but Scherzer got Ketel Marte to fly out to left to end the frame.
