# Apple Watch SE Holiday Deal

The most affordable smartwatch in Apple's lineup, the Apple Watch SE, came down to $179 across the 2023 holiday sales — a record low and $70 off its usual price. It has a list price of $249 but has regularly sold for $220 lately, so this is still a significant deal.

Engadget called it the best smartwatch money can buy in its review when it came out in 2022. It lacks an always-on display and a few of the more specialized health sensors, like the blood oxygen app, but the fitness features and tracking data are well-rounded, and the notifications and integrations are particularly convenient for iPhone users. It may not have all of the advanced health tracking features of the Series 9, but it has all of the basics you'd want in a smartwatch — smart notifications, all-day activity tracking, and perks like Fall and Crash Detection and Emergency SOS — making it a great option as an entry point into the Apple Watch space or a gift for someone who has never worn a smartwatch.
