# Hyperscaler Partnerships with OT and Simulation Vendors

The third trend is partnership rather than vertical integration: in the last few years cloud hyperscalers such as AWS and Microsoft Azure have introduced digital twin platforms — AWS IoT TwinMaker and Azure Digital Twins — that allow interconnecting various data sources and building digital twin topology, but these companies realise they cannot deliver end-to-end digital twin solutions by themselves, and are therefore partnering with OT and simulation companies to add capabilities to their networks.

The exchange with OT companies runs in both directions. OT companies provide industrial data management capabilities and connections to millions of local physical assets; by partnering with hyperscalers they can reach broader audiences looking for digital transformation solutions, gaining typical cloud benefits like storage and computation power. In 2021, Siemens and AWS announced an expansion of their partnership with digital twin technology as an area of focus, aiming to accelerate Siemens Xcelerator adoption and democratise new digital twin solutions using AWS IoT TwinMaker, a service intended to make creating digital twins that incorporate multiple data sources faster and easier.

The exchange with simulation companies follows the same logic. Simulation companies bring specialised expertise in creating accurate, high-fidelity models of physical entities — machinery, buildings or entire ecosystems — and by partnering with a hyperscaler they can harness cloud data storage and computational power to create more robust, responsive and accurate data twins that scale and integrate seamlessly into broader IT ecosystems. In 2023, Ansys and Microsoft announced a partnership to help customers envision digital twins on a large scale; in cooperation with Tata Consultancy Services they integrated Ansys's Twin Builder physics-based simulation capabilities with Azure Digital Twin and IoT data, letting Twin Builder users run scenarios and obtain predictions on how their systems will behave.
