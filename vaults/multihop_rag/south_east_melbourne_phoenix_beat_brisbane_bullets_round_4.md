# South East Melbourne Phoenix Beat Brisbane Bullets 96-73

South East Melbourne Phoenix made it three straight wins by beating the Brisbane Bullets 96-73 in the other NBL match on the Saturday of round 4, with Alan Williams leading the way against a side looking lost without the suspended Aron Baynes.

Williams produced 21 points and six rebounds for South East Melbourne. Brisbane would still be without Baynes for the following weekend's double-header away to Perth and at home to Tasmania, and needed to find some answers.

The Bullets' own assessment focused on the defensive end: "Then giving them 96 is way too many as well so we have a lot of stuff that we still have to work on."
