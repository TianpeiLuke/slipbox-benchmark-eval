# The EU AI Act's Training Data Disclosure Rule

The European Union's proposed AI Act contains a rule that would require AI developers to disclose whether their AI had been trained on copyrighted materials — going some, if not all, of the way toward the transparency regime the NMPA sought.

As of MBW's 2023-11-30 report the Act was still the subject of back-and-forth negotiations between the European Commission and the European Parliament, so the rule was proposed rather than in force. Transparency in the use of AI is also becoming a rule on social media sites where AI-generated content is likely to appear: both TikTok and YouTube have passed rules requiring AI-generated content to be labeled as such.
