# The House GOP's Narrow-Majority Vote Math

With Republicans in majority control of the House 221-212, any speaker candidate can lose only a few detractors: a clear majority of 217 votes is required to win the gavel. That arithmetic is what made Jim Jordan's position hopeless — he had to pick up most of his GOP foes to win.

The second ballot on Wednesday, 2023-10-18, showed the math failing: 199 Republicans voted for Jordan and 212 for Democratic leader Hakeem Jeffries of New York, leaving no candidate with a clear majority because 22 Republicans voted for someone else. By 2023-10-20 the same constraint had generalized beyond Jordan — it appeared there was no Republican at present who could win the 217 votes needed to become speaker.
