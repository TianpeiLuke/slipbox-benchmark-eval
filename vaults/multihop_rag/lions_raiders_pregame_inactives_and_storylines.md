# Lions-Raiders Pregame: Carlson And St. Brown Active, Hoyer Inactive

The pregame log for the Oct. 30, 2023 Raiders-Lions "Monday Night Football" game reported two questionable players cleared to play and one backup quarterback ruled out. At 6:25 p.m. it noted, citing Adam Schefter and a league source, that Raiders kicker Daniel Carlson would play despite being listed as questionable with a groin injury — with the added question of whether the injury would change Josh McDaniels' decision-making. At 6:45 p.m. Lions receiver Amon-Ra St. Brown was officially active after being listed as questionable with an illness. At 6:49 p.m. the blog reported Brian Hoyer inactive, leaving Aidan O'Connell as the Raiders' backup quarterback behind Jimmy Garoppolo.

Two matchup storylines followed. At 7:56 p.m. the blog observed that Josh Jacobs had not averaged more than 3.6 yards per carry in a single game that season and asked whether he could get going against a strong Lions run defense, noting it would be tough for Las Vegas to win without at least some production from the former first-rounder. At 8:07 p.m. it relayed the broadcast's point that the game was a homecoming for Michigan native Maxx Crosby, and that his play and that of fellow Michigan native Aidan Hutchinson would be pivotal.
