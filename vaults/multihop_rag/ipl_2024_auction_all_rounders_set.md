# IPL 2024 Auction: The All-Rounders Set

The first set of all-rounders was announced at 1:50 PM IST, with Wanindu Hasaranga first up at a base price of Rs 1.5 Cr; Sunrisers Hyderabad bid at the base price and, with no other interest, Hasaranga went to SRH for 1.5 Cr, sending him to a new franchise.

The set's results were: Wanindu Hasaranga to SRH for 1.5 Cr; Rachin Ravindra to CSK for 1.8 Cr; Shardul Thakur to CSK for Rs 4 Cr; Azmatullah Omarzai to GT for Rs 50 lakh (also listed as "To SRH for Rs 4 Cr" in the blog's running summary); Gerald Coetzee to MI for Rs 5 Cr; Harshal Patel to Punjab Kings for 11.75 Cr; Daryl Mitchell to CSK for 14 Crore; and Chris Woakes to PBKS for 4.2 Cr.

The sequence ran as follows. At 1:55 PM IST Rachin Ravindra came up with CSK as opening bidder, and CSK, DC and PBKS entered a bidding war that took the Kiwi all-rounder past 1 Cr. At 2:00 PM IST Shardul Thakur came up with CSK jumping in; SRH and CSK fought for him before SRH pulled out, giving CSK their all-rounder for Rs 4 Cr in what the blog called a homecoming, and Azmatullah Omarzai went to GT for Rs 50 lakh. At 2:15 PM IST Gerald Coetzee drew a contest between CSK and MI, with LSG joining and CSK pulling out, before he breached Rs 5 Cr with MI picking up the all-rounder. At 2:19 PM IST Harshal Patel drew a contest between GT and PBKS that breached 9 Cr, with LSG joining at 11 Cr, and at 2:25 PM IST Punjab Kings took him for 11.75 Cr. Daryl Mitchell came up the same minute with DC and PBKS in a bidding war that breached Rs 10 Cr; CSK joined at Rs 12 Cr at 2:35 PM IST and took him for 14 Crore at 2:40 PM IST. At 2:43 PM IST PBKS took Chris Woakes for 4.2 Cr.
