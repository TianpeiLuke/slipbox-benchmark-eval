# Why Vermont Sportsbook Promos Will Target Boston Teams And College Sports

Vermont has no teams in the four major US pro sports leagues, Sporting News noted on 2023-09-26. However, state residents have been known to support Boston-based teams: the Red Sox in MLB, the Celtics in the NBA, the Bruins in the NHL, and the Patriots in the NFL.

Because of those teams' proximity to the state, you can expect Vermont online sportsbooks to run related promos — NFL betting promos, for example — to attract fans. College sports are big too, which means several betting promos should also surround local college teams.
