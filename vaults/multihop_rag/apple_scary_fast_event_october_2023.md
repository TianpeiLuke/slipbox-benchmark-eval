# Apple's Scary Fast Event, October 2023

Apple is hosting an event on October 30, the night before Halloween, dubbed "Scary Fast" — a strong indicator that the company will have some speedy new hardware to show off. Unusually, Apple is holding this event in the evening: it starts at 8PM ET.

This year's iPhones and Apple Watches are already out in the wild, but Apple has a bunch of other product categories it could focus on, and there is always the chance of iPad updates. If rumors are to be believed, though, Apple's focus during the Scary Fast event will be on Macs and a fresh batch of chipsets to power them.
