# Reece James' Touchline Outburst And FA Charge

Reece James lost his cool with an official in the tunnel after Chelsea's 1-0 home defeat to Aston Villa on 24 September 2023, and a Football Association charge for abusive conduct followed three days later, making a one-match ban feel like only a matter of time.

The context was frustration at not being able to play. Named by Mauricio Pochettino as Chelsea captain, James had not played since the opening-weekend draw with Liverpool at Stamford Bridge, when he injured a hamstring, and he seethed from the sideline as his team lost to Villa in a game where his replacement, Malo Gusto, was controversially sent off. It was Chelsea's second home Premier League defeat on the spin after the one against Nottingham Forest, and they were booed off again.

The ban did not arrive in time for Chelsea's visit to Fulham on 2 October, when James was still injured — that would have been too simple — but rather for their next game at Burnley on 7 October, when, according to Pochettino, James was back in contention for selection.
