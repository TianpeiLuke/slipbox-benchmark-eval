# Pet Fit For Life Plush Wand

Audience manager Kaitlin Hatton's dog Trudee — otherwise known as My Boss — is notoriously insistent on being the focus of her attention: at any given moment Trudee has 20 toys strewn about the apartment, but she will not play with them unless Hatton is watching, literally with no distractions, sitting within feet of her, just staring at her chew on her toys for hours on end.

The problem is not affection but enrichment: Hatton loves staring at her dog, but Trudee does not get the enrichment she deserves when her activity levels are tied directly to Hatton's ability to give her undivided attention.

That changed when she bought the Pet Fit For Life Plush Wand, which has a toy attached to a long chewable rope suspended from a metal pole. Now Hatton not only tosses the toy about the room from the comfort of her couch, but Trudee is so distracted by chasing it that she does not notice if Hatton is not making direct eye contact. It is a win-win.
