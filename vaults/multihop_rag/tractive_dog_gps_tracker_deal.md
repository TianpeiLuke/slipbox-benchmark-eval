# Tractive Dog GPS Tracker Black Friday Deal

The Tractive dog GPS tracker was $29.99 at Walmart for Black Friday 2023, a $20 saving, Mashable reported on 25 November 2023.

It is described as a solid dog tracking device for pet parents on a budget: it lets you track your dog's live location within the companion app, and allows you to set up a virtual fence and be notified when your pet steps out of — or enters — the safe area.

It is not perfect, the roundup adds: "the battery is just so-so and the live tracking can be a bit glitchy — but for the price, it's hard to beat."
