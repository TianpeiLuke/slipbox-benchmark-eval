# Week 13 Cardinals-Steelers: Arizona's Run Defense Makes Najee Harris A Start

CBS Sports argues that Pittsburgh should lean on the ground game in Week 13 because Arizona's run defense is among the league's worst, making Najee Harris and the Steelers' rushing attack the play. The grounds: Pittsburgh's biggest scheme change last week involved Kenny Pickett throwing over the middle much more than before, with Pat Freiermuth benefitting, but the Steelers also ran the ball effectively at Cincinnati and Najee Harris had one of his best games this year. Against Arizona, more of the same is expected. The Cardinals rank in the bottom five in pretty much every single run defense metric except yards after contact per rush, where they are league average; a running back has scored 17 or more PPR points in four of their past five games, and eight in 12 games have hit at least 15 PPR points. A further condition reinforcing the run lean: with Pickett nursing an ankle injury, the Steelers could run the ball aplenty.

The tight-end read is split: Arizona has allowed five touchdowns to tight ends over its past five games but has actually been relatively efficient defending them otherwise. The verdicts: STARTS — Najee Harris, Kyler Murray, Trey McBride, Marquise Brown, Pat Freiermuth, Diontae Johnson (PPR flex), Jaylen Warren (PPR only) and the Steelers DST; SITS — Kenny Pickett, James Conner, Rondale Moore and the Cardinals DST; SLEEPERS — Greg Dortch (PPR only) and George Pickens.
