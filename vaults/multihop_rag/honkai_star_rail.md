# Honkai: Star Rail

Honkai: Star Rail, developed by Hoyoverse for Android, iOS, PlayStation 5, and Windows PC, is ranked 21 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31. Polygon's critic calls it Hoyoverse's answer to Genshin Impact burnout.

The critic, JL, describes being a pretty die-hard Genshin Impact player since launch — rolling for every character, grinding them out, 100%-ing the exploration, completing every quest, doing all the stuff that would burn anyone out.

What makes Star Rail work as a daily game is its slower-paced turn-based combat, the ability to auto-battle if your teams are strong enough, and smaller maps, letting the critic funnel interest into it without feeling exhausted. The characters are fun, flashy, and easy to latch onto — whether to main the sneaky but elegant Kafka, who uses damage-over-time skills to whittle opponents away, or the brooding Blade, who unleashes huge attacks at the cost of his HP — though, as JL notes, the wallet decides in the end, since it is a gacha game.
