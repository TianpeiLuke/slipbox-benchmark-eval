# Pokémon Merchandising For Grown-Up Fans

The diversification naturally included merchandising aimed at an audience that had aged: many of the 10-year-olds who played the initial games were now adults with families of their own, which led to the launch of monpoke, a special line of Pokémon-themed items for infants, as well as the now-discontinued Pokémon Shirts, which sold button-up shirts and formal workwear based on various pokémon.

With each of these efforts, alongside the launch of Pokémon Go — which gave lapsed older audiences an accessible reentry point that could fit around their busy schedules — the franchise was suddenly less of a gaming curio and more an ingrained facet of modern life.
