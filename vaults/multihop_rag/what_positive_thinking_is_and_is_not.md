# What Positive Thinking Is and Is Not

Pastor Jesse Bradley defines positive thinking as realistic, full of truth and love, and aligned with heaven and scripture — and he marks its boundaries as sharply as its content.

The excluded cases are four. It is not "denial and pretending that there are no challenges". "Positive thinking is not flattery and frivolous compliments." "Positive thinking is not self-consumption, self-absorption or selfishness." And "positive thinking is not creating unrealistic expectations for other people and putting extra stress on them to be perfect."

Its positive content is relational and practical. "Positive thinking is a vital aspect of loving your neighbor, nourishing your mind, discerning between light and darkness, good and evil and right and wrong," Bradley said. "Positive thinking leads to taking action and improving situations. Positive thinking brings out the best in the people around you. Positive thinking rejects excuses, takes responsibility, and overcomes setbacks."
