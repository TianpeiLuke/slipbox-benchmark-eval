# Fintech Funding Roundup, October 2023

TechCrunch's October 8, 2023 fintech roundup collected a batch of funding rounds, acquisitions, product launches and executive appointments across payments, insurance, banking and equity management. The items below are the roundup's own headline entries.

Funding rounds and recapitalizations reported in the roundup: open banking led to a fintech boom, and as Brite raised $60 million, account-to-account payment grew; a recapitalization and a $60 million Series D supported the growth of e-commerce financier Clearco; Stitch raised a $25 million Series A extension led by Ribbit Capital, bringing the round's total to $46 million; Resy and Eater co-founder raised $24 million for Blackbird, a restaurant loyalty platform; logistics company Loop raised $35 million to modernize the supply chain; Reserv raised $20 million for AI-driven insurtech software; Vyzer raised a $6.3 million seed round for an AI-powered wealth management platform; Kafene added another $12.6 million to its Series B round; fintech firm Revio boosted community bank growth with $2.5 million in funding; and AP automation fintech Stampli announced a $61 million round led by Blackstone.

Acquisitions in the roundup: Shift4 acquired a SpotOn unit for $100 million, and SkyWatch acquired Droneinsurance.com.

Products, partnerships and people: the great Zelle pool scam; the Public platform offering everyday investors a slice of the "Shrek" catalog with quarterly payouts; Stockpile and Green Dot partnering on debit cards for minors; Amex piloting biometrics in online checkout; Pie Insurance appointing Audra Foglietta as chief financial officer; Monzo picking Cash App veteran Conor Walsh for US CEO; Folsom-based EV Life launching an electric car loan to lower monthly payments; CLEAR launching a reusable KYC solution; Sparx wanting to do for enterprise what Truebill did for consumer recurring bills; and Ten Key Labs wanting to simplify managing equity for startups.
