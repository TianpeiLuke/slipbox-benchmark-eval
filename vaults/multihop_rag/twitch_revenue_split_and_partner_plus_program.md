# Twitch's Revenue Split And Partner Plus Program

The default subscription revenue split for Twitch Partners is 50/50, but select streamers were offered a premium 70/30 deal until the previous year, when Twitch cut that deal in favor of the platform's ad revenue program — angering many of its most loyal creators.

Partners grandfathered into the old agreement were still paid a 70% cut of the first $100,000 they earned, and 50% of any revenue after that. Twitch then rolled out a new Partner Plus program in 2023, which allowed qualifying streamers to apply for the same deal given to streamers grandfathered into the 70/30 split. Its steep requirements exclude the majority of Twitch Partners and do not count gifted subs toward the minimum subscription requirement, further straining the relationship between the platform and its creators. Twitch adjusted the program so that higher tier subscriptions count for more points toward the minimum subscription requirement, but that was not the change streamers were requesting.
