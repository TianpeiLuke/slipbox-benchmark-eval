# Kirby and the Forgotten Land

Kirby games tend to be perfectly chill and incredibly adorable, and Polygon says Forgotten Land is no exception. It appears in the outlet's 2023-10-20 list of the 35 best couch co-op games for Nintendo Switch.

In Forgotten Land you and another player run, float, and fight through 3D levels.

In addition to Kirby's classic inhale powers, the game introduced "mouthful mode," which lets Kirby stretch like a glove over enormous objects like cars and huge lightbulbs. Polygon adds that Kirby Star Allies is also fun with local co-op.
