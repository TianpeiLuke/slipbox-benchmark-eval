# Portable Bluetooth Speaker Deals

Three portable Bluetooth speakers were discounted around October Prime Day. Sony's four-inch-tall SRS-XB13 was back to $35, matching its sale price from July's Prime sale, though you need a Prime membership to get it; this is an older model of the tiny portable, replaced by the SRS-XB100 earlier this year, which was itself seeing a 20 percent discount. Both are rated for water and dust resistance and can be paired for stereo sound.

Anker's Soundcore Motion+, one of the top Bluetooth options in Engadget's speaker guide, gives out good sound for its price point: its $100 MSRP was discounted to $70 if you clipped the on-page coupon — a 30 percent discount and an all-time low. It has a bright and bassy output, offers EQ customization in the app, and is water-resistant, so you can take it to the park or on a hike.

The Tribit StormBox Micro 2 is another small speaker Engadget likes, putting out decent volume for its size; for Prime Day it went down to $48, $12 off its usual going rate, and afterward it was $60 with an on-page 15 percent coupon bringing it back to that low. It has a strap on its back that works well on belts, backpacks or bike handlebars, and "on the go" is where it performs best — it is more about portable volume than pure fidelity.
