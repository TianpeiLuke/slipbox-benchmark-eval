# Liam Paro vs Montana Love, December 2023

Back fit again, Liam Paro faces Ohio's Montana Love in San Francisco on December 9 — December 10 in Australia — on the undercard of the WBC super lightweight title bout between Devin Haney and Regis Prograis. Australian fans will recognise Love as the man who lost by disqualification when he threw Stevie Spark out of the ring in frustration the previous October. Paro hoped to put a difficult run behind him and kick off a world title charge with an emphatic win on a big card.

His game plan is pressure. "I'll just bring the fight to [Love], he likes a slow, controlled fight, a slow pace," Paro said. "We've seen with the [Spark] fight, a bit of pressure and he folds, so bring the fight to him. He's obviously weak in the head a little bit and actions speak louder than words, you've seen that in his fight. So we're going to bring the fight to him. Smart pressure, smart boxing. I honestly believe I'm better than him in every aspect, except wrestling."
