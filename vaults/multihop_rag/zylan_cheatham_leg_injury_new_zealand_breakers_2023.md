# Zylan Cheatham's Leg Injury For The New Zealand Breakers

The New Zealand Breakers faced a nervous wait after losing import Zylan Cheatham to a leg injury in their 97-85 upset loss to the Adelaide 36ers at Auckland's Spark Arena on the Thursday night of NBL round 5.

Without a hand being laid on him, the ex-NBA forward pulled up sore changing direction early in the fourth quarter, was unable to put weight on his right leg as Breakers staff helped him from the floor, and played no further part in the match, with no immediate update on his condition after full-time. "It's too early. We'll pray for good news," said Breakers coach Mody Maor. By the following Saturday's game the injury was reported as a broken foot, with the Breakers missing him against South East Melbourne, where Alan Williams duly proved a beast in the low block against the undersized Anthony Lamb.

The injury proved a turning point in the Adelaide game. The Breakers had maintained a slim lead for most of the night as Cheatham (17 points, eight rebounds) combined with guard Will McDowell-White to strong effect. "Zylan's a focal point for us in everything that we do, he's also one of our leaders, one of our best competitors," Maor said. "To see him go down like this, I think deflated us. New team, new people, new leaders, these are the kinds of things that we need to learn to deal with."
