# PC Shipment Decline Slows In Q3 2023

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-13, in reporting by Ron, that the decline in PC shipments slowed in Q3 2023, while Apple's sales plunged over 23%.

The newsletter reads the two figures together as a sign that mobile devices may be starting to cannibalize sales of personal computers. Note the direction of each number: shipments are still declining, and what changed in Q3 2023 is the rate of that decline, not its sign. Apple's drop of more than 23% runs against the industry-level slowdown in the same quarter.
