# Mayfield Against Zone Coverage And At All Four Levels

Per Next Gen Stats, Baker Mayfield did the brunt of his damage in the Buccaneers' 34-20 win over the Packers on Dec. 17, 2023 against zone coverage, going 20-of-24 for 361 yards and three touchdowns against it.

He whipped arrows beyond the Packers secondary time and time again. He also became the first player in seven years to complete a touchdown pass at all four levels of the field — the line of scrimmage, 1-10 yards, 11-20 yards, and 20-plus yards. Next Gen Stats noted it was the third time in Mayfield's career he had thrown a touchdown pass to all four levels, the most by any quarterback since 2016.
