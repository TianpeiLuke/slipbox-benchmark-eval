# Deebo Samuel Yards Per Target Pace

CBSSports.com's Dec. 19, 2023 Week 16 wide receiver preview reported that Deebo Samuel was on pace to become just the fourth player since 1992 to average more than 11 yards per target with at least 60 targets in multiple seasons — the note's headline number being 11.4.

The other three players to have done it are Tyreek Hill, Tyler Lockett and DeSean Jackson. The same set of notes recorded that nearly 70% of Rashee Rice's receiving yards had come after the catch, tops among wide receivers with at least 50 targets.
