# Epic's Post-Verdict Remedy Strategy And Global Cases

Told that Google has said it will challenge the verdict, Tim Sweeney said Epic is not going to wait: it will do absolutely everything it can as quickly as it can to start changing the world, rather than pausing for the appeal.

His reasoning for the reach of the win is the market Epic established at trial — a market worldwide excluding China — so this is, in his framing, a worldwide verdict, and Epic presumes any remedies would be worldwide too. Beyond the US case, he pointed to the European DMA, to Epic v. Apple and Epic v. Google cases coming up in Australia, and to another in the UK. He also stressed that the push no longer depends on Epic alone: a lot of legislators, a lot of regulators, and other litigation are all pushing in the direction of openness. On whether the Google verdict helps Epic's Apple appeal, Sweeney said there is no linkage between the cases in law and no legal connection between the two, so it would come down only to whether the court is in any way following current events — to the decisions of the humans involved, the justices and support teams considering the appeal.
