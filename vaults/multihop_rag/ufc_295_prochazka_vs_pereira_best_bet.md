# UFC 295 Best Bet: Over 1.5 Rounds In Prochazka vs Pereira

CBS Sports' best bet for the UFC 295 main event, Jiri Prochazka versus Alex Pereira, is Over 1.5 rounds at -150. The grounds: only three of Prochazka and Pereira's nine UFC fights have ended in under 1.5 rounds. Both men are dynamic and dangerous, with the ability to finish a fight in a flash, but both are also tough as nails and highly skilled.

The warrant is explicitly epistemic rather than purely statistical. In such an even fight, it's hard to throw a bet at either man straight up or on any method of finish unless you feel you have a truly good read on the bout, and the writer does not have that feeling; what he does expect is that the fight happens at a high pace and lasts more than one and a half rounds before either man takes over or gets the finish. The stated exception: barring someone landing an early bomb, this feels like the best available line for the fight.
