# Supergirl: Woman of Tomorrow

Supergirl: Woman of Tomorrow is billed as "a science fiction adventure" that "presents a Supergirl viewers are not used to seeing" — the Supergirl counterpart to what Superman: Legacy is meant to do for Superman in the DCU.

The movie will be based on Tom King's celebrated Supergirl comics.

Polygon presumes it will not touch on anything from the six-year run of Supergirl on The CW starring Melissa Benoist. The project was undated as of the 2023-10-24 report.
