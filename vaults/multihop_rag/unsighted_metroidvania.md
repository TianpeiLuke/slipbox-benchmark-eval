# Unsighted (Metroidvania)

Unsighted casts you as an automaton that needs a gem substance to survive, fighting against the clock to keep your friends from becoming "unsighted" and turning into shells of their former selves. It appears in Polygon's 2023-10-20 list of the 35 best couch co-op games for Nintendo Switch.

The Metroidvania has an incredible map with three interwoven floors of levels, and tons of Zelda-like puzzles.

Polygon's co-op note is that a couch co-op buddy makes these challenges much more surmountable.
