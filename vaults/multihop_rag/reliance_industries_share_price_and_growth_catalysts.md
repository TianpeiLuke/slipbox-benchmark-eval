# Reliance Industries Share Price and Growth Catalysts

The stock of India's most valued company, Reliance Industries (RIL), has trebled over the last six years after a decade-long underperformance from 2007 to 2017. On a three-year basis, however, RIL has delivered 11 per cent CAGR returns including the value of demerged Jio Financial Services, underperforming the Nifty 50's CAGR of a little over 14 per cent over the same period.

Business Line argued on 9 December 2023 that this is nothing to fret about, because the stock has typically tended to underperform whenever the company has been in a heavy investment phase — something it has embarked on in its green energy ventures.

The catalysts behind the six-year outperformance were the launch of Jio's data services in 2016, the maturation of the retail business, and the subsequent stake sales in FY21. Although the markets have already assigned a significant re-rating in valuation to both businesses, the article holds that scope for further outperformance from them remains, given the opportunity ahead.
