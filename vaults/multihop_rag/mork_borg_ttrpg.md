# Mörk Borg

Mörk Borg was released in 2020, but writer, content creator and DesiQuest executive producer Jasmine Bhullar only stumbled on it in 2023 and returned to it repeatedly — the game she kept coming back to among everything she played that year.

The book has a stunning and distinct art style that immediately transports you into the doom metal fantasy of the setting, but Bhullar argues that is only a small part of its allure. Unlike many TTRPGs one merely aspires to play someday, Mörk Borg does everything in its power to make the game easy to run: it is a short read in which no line is wasted, and even item descriptions drip with hints at the nefarious deeds one must have done to acquire them. The optional classes are a delight for anyone who savours playing the unsavoury — from esoteric introverts who make their hovel far from prying eyes to unfortunate discarded-at-birth cutthroats scratching out a living — and while you can always create something more tailor-made, she appreciates that the book offers beginner-friendly ideas and archetypes that fit the gritty setting. In putting a game together she also found a glut of free online tools, from dungeon generators to NPC character sheet generators, that made prep a breeze. She notes she is not alone in returning to it, given the number of Mörk Borg-compatible games that sprang up that year, from Pirate Borg to Chris Lockey's upcoming Abyss of Hallucinations.
