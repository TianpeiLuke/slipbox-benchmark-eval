# Prime Day 2023 Android Tablet Deals

WIRED's Prime Big Deal Days Android tablet picks are the OnePlus Pad and Google's Pixel Tablet.

The OnePlus Pad (8/10, WIRED Recommends) is one of the few Android tablets WIRED considers a worthy iPad alternative, offering good performance, great battery life, and an excellent 144-Hz, 11.6-inch LCD display; WIRED strongly recommends OnePlus' magnetic keyboard, also on sale for $100 ($50 off) — though buying directly from the company's website gets the keyboard bundled for free. Google's Pixel Tablet (7/10, WIRED Recommends) is part tablet, part smart display: it comes with a charging dock that doubles as a speaker, so you can pump out tunes and get answers from Google Assistant at a moment's notice, and taking it off the charger gives a full Android tablet experience.
