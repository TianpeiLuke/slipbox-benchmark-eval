# The Early Favourites In The 2024 NBA MVP Market

As of Oct. 4, 2023, the Sporting News profiled four leading candidates in the 2024 NBA MVP market: Nikola Jokic, Luka Doncic, Giannis Antetokounmpo and reigning winner Joel Embiid.

Jokic, having fallen short of a third consecutive MVP the previous season, steered Denver to its first NBA championship through an impressive playoff showing and emerged over the summer as a favored MVP candidate, with the Nuggets maintaining their key lineup for another championship bid as he consistently demonstrates his game prowess. Doncic, among the preseason favorites for the past three years, has seen his prospects decline due to the Mavericks' limited support in Dallas, as underscored by their recent playoff absence — nobody doubts his skills, but questions about the Mavericks' contention capabilities persist.

Antetokounmpo's case rests on a rebound. Milwaukee's surprising first-round departure against the Heat the previous season was notably influenced by his absence for much of the series, and the two-time MVP — arguably the league's premier two-way player — is poised to rebound, at 28 and in his prime, with his resolve stronger than ever after the Bucks' premature playoff exit. Embiid, the reigning MVP, remains a prominent figure among top contenders: despite the Sixers' playoff setbacks he dominated the regular season, leading in scoring and showcasing superior rim defense, and if he remains injury-free he is poised to vie for his MVP status again, hopefully with a strengthened supporting cast.
