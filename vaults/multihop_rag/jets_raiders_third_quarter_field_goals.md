# Jets-Raiders Third Quarter Field Goals

The third quarter of the Nov. 12, 2023 Jets-Raiders game produced one score — Daniel Carlson's 40-yard field goal at 10:29 p.m. that tied the game 9-9 with 2:36 left in the period, keeping the night's tally at all kicks.

New York opened the half from its own 25 on a touchback at 10:02 p.m. and immediately hurt itself: at 10:04 p.m. a low incompletion from Zach Wilson to Garrett Wilson and a two-yard Hall pickup started the drive slowly, and although Wilson appeared to convert with a 12-yard throw to Gipson, Carter was flagged for a chop block that pushed the Jets back to third-and-21 at their own 14 — a sixth penalty for 68 yards. At 10:05 p.m. Conklin's reception would have set up fourth-and-4, but a Jets holding penalty was accepted by the Raiders instead of the punt; a quick pass to Malik Taylor gained six and New York punted, with DeAndre Carter returning it six yards from the Raiders' 25 to the 31.

Las Vegas built the tying drive out of Josh Jacobs and field position. At 10:11 p.m. Quinnen Williams made an impressive solo tackle on Jacobs for a three-yard loss, Tre Tucker's end-around gained nine, and O'Connell scrambled for five to convert before a holding flag brought up third-and-14; at 10:14 p.m. O'Connell had to spike a tight end screen to Mayer, and Gipson returned the punt five yards to the Jets' 15. At 10:19 p.m. the Jets went three-and-out — an incompletion, a one-yard loss for Hall against Butler, a false start making it third-and-16, and Wilson's 13-yard scramble stopping three short — and Carter returned the punt 31 yards from the Raiders 23 to the Jets 46. From there, at 10:25 p.m. Meyers made a catch for eight yards and a pop pass to Jacobs went three yards for a first down at the Jets 34; at 10:26 p.m. O'Connell took his second bad sack of the day back to the Jets 42 before Jacobs turned a lateral into a 12-yard carry for a manageable third-and-6; at 10:27 p.m. a quick pass to Hunter Renfrow came inches short and the Raiders went for it, Jacobs finding a hole on the right side for the first down at the Jets 23. The drive then stalled at 10:29 p.m. as Jacobs was blown up for a loss of four, gained five on a toss, and O'Connell way overthrew Adams, who was furious there was no penalty, leaving Carlson's 40-yarder.

The quarter closed with the Raiders threatening. At 10:34 p.m. Gipson was brought down at the Jets 13 on a short kickoff return; at 10:35 p.m. a Wilson-to-Wilson gain of five was followed by a sack that jarred the ball loose, recovered by Mekhi Becton, and a deep shot to Taylor that sailed over his head, so New York punted and Carter returned it 10 yards to the Raiders' 46. At 10:41 p.m. Mayer dropped a first-down pass before Jacobs exploded 40 yards to the Jets' 13 — Carter punched the ball out at the end of the run but it rolled harmlessly out of bounds — and Jacobs took the next carry for five yards to end the third quarter with the Raiders at the Jets' 8.
