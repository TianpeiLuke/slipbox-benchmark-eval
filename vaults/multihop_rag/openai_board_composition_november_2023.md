# OpenAI's Board Composition In November 2023

The OpenAI board that fired Sam Altman consisted, the company said, of OpenAI's chief scientist Ilya Sutskever plus three non-employees.

Those three were Quora CEO Adam D'Angelo, tech entrepreneur Tasha McCauley, and Helen Toner of the Georgetown Center for Security and Emerging Technology.
