# Eddie Jones Denies Talks Over The Japan Head Coach Role

Eddie Jones rejected the reported claim that he had interviewed for the Japan head coach role, denying it again on Tuesday, Oct. 16, 2023 having previously also denied the story.

The claim he was attacking was reported during the World Cup campaign: that Jones had an interview for the Japan head coach job. His denial targeted the factual premise rather than the inference drawn from it.

Pressed on multiple reports of conversations with sources in Japanese rugby, Jones would say only: "I haven't been speaking to anyone, mate."
