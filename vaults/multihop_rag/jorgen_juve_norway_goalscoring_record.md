# Jorgen Juve's Norway Goalscoring Record

Norway's is the longest-standing outright individual goalscoring record in the world among the 213 FIFA-affiliated nations, set by Jorgen Juve, who ended his international career in 1937 and later became a renowned sports journalist in his home country, as reported by The New York Times on 17 November 2023.

The record itself is modest but the circumstances are not. Juve scored 33 goals in 45 matches for Norway, including five hat-tricks, a tally made remarkable by the fact that he played as a centre-forward in less than half of those games; he was otherwise positioned in defence, from where he captained his nation to the bronze medal at the 1936 Olympics.

That positional shift explains the dating of the record: his final international goal came three years before his final cap, in June 1934, so it is likely that by the time Haaland scores six more goals it will be around 90 years since Juve reached the 33-goal mark.
