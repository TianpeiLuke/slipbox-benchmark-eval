# Jordan Poyer's Handpan And Sound Healing

Jordan Poyer heard a handpan for the first time in Costa Rica, was drawn to it, and now plays his frequently, The New York Times reported on 15 December 2023.

A handpan is a steel musical instrument that generates sound through vibrations, and sound healers and meditators often play it.

The change it marks is illustrated at home. Not long ago Rachel returned home at about 10 p.m. and was tidying up the kitchen when, in the peace of the night, she heard something coming from the outside balcony upstairs — ethereal, haunting sounds. "It was kind of like something you would hear in a dream," Rachel says. It was music the old Jordan Poyer never could have made.
