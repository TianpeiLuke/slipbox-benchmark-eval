# Daye's STI Diagnostic Tampon Service

Daye, a U.K. femcare-turned-gynaecological-health startup, has expanded its tampon-based home screening service to add STI testing — a "non-invasive screening" service for sexually transmitted infections that the startup bills as a "world first."

The "STI Diagnostic Tampon" service uses Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR) testing technology to detect the presence of pathogens. Daye is launching with the ability to test for five STIs — Chlamydia, gonorrhoea, trichomonas, mycoplasma and ureaplasma — which were selected because they are the most common STIs. More tests are planned.
