# Reliance Industries Sum-of-the-Parts Valuation Case

Business Line's conclusion on 9 December 2023 is that RIL still leaves some money on the table at current levels, so long-term investors can accumulate it on dips. The four grounds it gives are reasonable valuations; pole position in India's high-growth digital and retail business; potential for large-scale value creation in renewable energy similar to the digital and retail forays, not reflected in current valuations; and a stable O2C business.

On the multiple, Reliance trades at 24 times trailing twelve-month earnings. That is above RIL's pre-Covid three-year average valuation of around 20 times, but given the growth delivered by the telecom and retail businesses post-Covid, the article sees scope for further re-rating.

The sum-of-the-parts arithmetic supplies the warrant. Reliance Retail Ventures, the flagship, is currently valued at about $100 billion (₹8.3 lakh crore) based on recent transactions, with RIL holding an 83 per cent stake. Reliance Jio was valued at $63 billion back in 2020, and the market-assigned value is in the $80–90 billion range, 10 to 11x one-year forward EBITDA, which is not expensive; RIL holds 67 per cent. Adding RIL's stakes in those businesses to around $75 billion for O2C — a conservative figure assuming no change in value in the last five years — gives an enterprise value of around $214 billion, excluding the E&P and media businesses and some monetisable real estate, and assigning zero value to green energy. With net debt of around $15 billion, the debt-adjusted valuation is around $200 billion, which matches the current market cap. Adding the value of E&P and the high potential from green energy leaves money on the table for long-term investors. The article closes by noting that at a time when small and mid-cap stock valuations are trading at dizzying levels, large caps like RIL may be safer bets for the long term, summarising its case as growth engines intact, valuation reasonable, and potential value of the green energy business not reflected.
