# OpenAI's Safety Advisory Group And Board Veto Process

OpenAI routes model-risk decisions through a new cross-functional Safety Advisory Group that sits on top of the technical side, and gives the board the power to reverse leadership's shipping decisions. The premise is that only medium and high risks are to be tolerated one way or another, but that the people making the models are not necessarily the best ones to evaluate them and make recommendations.

The Safety Advisory Group reviews the technical teams' reports and makes recommendations from a higher vantage point, which OpenAI hopes will uncover some "unknown unknowns" — though by their nature those are fairly difficult to catch.

The process then requires these recommendations to be sent simultaneously to the board and to leadership, understood to mean CEO Sam Altman and CTO Mira Murati plus their lieutenants. Leadership makes the decision on whether to ship a model or fridge it, but the board is able to reverse those decisions.
