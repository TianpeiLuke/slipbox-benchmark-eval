# Crude Oil Prices And Gaza Supply Risk In October 2023

Rising crude oil prices have put extra pressure on inflation since the summer, and they were volatile again on Thursday 12 October 2023: after jumping early in the day, a barrel of benchmark US crude slipped US58¢ to settle at $US82.91, while Brent crude, the international standard, rose US18¢ to $US86 a barrel. After jumping since the summer and then regressing a couple of weeks earlier, crude prices have been shaky following the latest fighting in Gaza, with the worry being that the violence could lead to disruptions in the supply of petroleum.

The report notes the wider transmission: higher oil prices add costs across the economy, and airlines are particularly affected because fuel is one of their biggest expenses. The passage also carries a remark from a market commentator that "the question around whether or not there will be one more interest rate hike is yet to be answered".
