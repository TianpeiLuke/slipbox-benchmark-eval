# Rachel Claire, A Photographer Returning To The Family Home

Job insecurity and financial practicality encouraged Rachel Claire, a 32-year-old photographer, to move back in with her parents in 2019, The Age reported on 25 November 2023: "The great thing about living with your parents when you're an adult yourself is that you don't take any of it for granted."

She and her parents have always been close. When she first moved out of home at 22, her parents would always find reasons to pop by, whether a quick visit after work or to fix things or mow her lawn — "I could say they struggled with becoming empty-nesters, but I think it's more accurate to say we've just always enjoyed each other's company." With her career as a photographer she was in and out of the country a lot, spending money on rent when she was not often at home, which she calls crazy in an industry with very little job security. Eventually her parents suggested she move back home for a while so she could come and go with minimal financial impact while establishing herself.

She was not sure initially: she loved her freedom and struggled with the idea of falling back into traditional parent/child roles in her late 20s. But when she started thinking about the benefits — particularly her parents' home security systems, which are very handy when you have expensive gear — she accepted and moved back in.
