# Logistics And Check-Ins During A Holiday Family Visit

Beyond the initial conversations, relationship experts told The Independent that two practical steps remain when bringing a partner home for the holidays. The first is settling logistics in advance: if you are travelling, you may need to decide on sleeping arrangements — potentially staying somewhere other than a parent's house — in order to minimise family stress. "It would be really helpful to have conversations beforehand, so it's clear what you are agreeing or not agreeing to," said Saba Lurie. "For example, if your family isn't comfortable with you bringing someone home and sleeping in the same bedroom, then you and your partner may stay elsewhere. But those would be conversations that you all need to have together. It really depends on your relationship with your family and with your partner."

The second is checking in with your partner during the visit itself, including some quality time together, so that any family drama that arises does not damage the relationship. "I'd recommend pulling your partner aside and saying: 'How are we doing?' Like a temperature check," Lurie added. "Those are points where the two of you can come and remind each other: 'We are a team in this.' Or, the two of you could go on a walk and separate from the family, just for a little bit of time to reset within the relationship."
