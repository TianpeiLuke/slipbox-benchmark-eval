# Laurell K. Hamilton: Vampire Verdict

Polygon's verdict on Laurell K. Hamilton, author of the Anita Blake: Vampire Hunter series (1993-present), is: not a vampire.

Her books are described as the final evolution of an undead Pokémon. Where Anne Rice made vampires sexy but still took pains to remind readers they were fundamentally the stuff of nightmares, Hamilton's Anita Blake series is more like the merrily obscene aesthetic of a 1970s grindhouse flick: her characters bash, slaughter, and jump one another's bones, animated or otherwise, but it is all in good fun, so the sexy and violent vampire has reached a Looney Tunes stage of madcap romp that audiences cannot get enough of. That is exactly why Polygon acquits her. Nothing serves the vampire cause less than undermining the air of looming menace vampires have been cultivating since the 15th century — Vlad the Impaler worked for that nickname, and the last thing they need is an airport novel making a stake through the heart look fun. So Hamilton is not among the denizens of the undead; she is the one thing they have come to fear this millennium: a good punchline.
