# Travis Kelce and Taylor Swift Dating Rumours in 2023

Speculation that Travis Kelce and Taylor Swift were dating began in July 2023, when Kelce confessed that he had tried and failed to give Swift a friendship bracelet bearing his phone number at one of her Eras Tour concerts; he later invited her on his podcast to one of his games at Arrowhead Stadium, an invitation she famously accepted on 24 September 2023, and she had attended three more of his games by 25 November 2023.

Kelce first told the bracelet story to his brother on their podcast back in July. "I was disappointed that she doesn't talk before or after her shows, because she has to save her voice for the 44 songs that she sings," he explained. "So I was a little butthurt I didn't get to hand her one of the bracelets I made for her. If you're up on Taylor Swift concerts, there are friendship bracelets and I received a bunch of them being there, but I wanted to give Taylor Swift one with my number on it."

The NFL star officially addressed the dating rumours during an episode of The Pat McAfee Show on 21 September 2023, in which he mocked his brother Jason Kelce for joking that he "100 per cent" believed his younger sibling was dating Swift. "It's hilarious how much traction this has actually gotten, right now it's like a game of telephone," Kelce said. "Where everybody's whispering in each other's ears and hearing random stuff. No one actually knows what's going on, especially when you got Jason Kelce on live television telling people just both sides - he doesn't know, it's true, this and that." He also said he remained open to pursuing a relationship with Swift, revealing that he had subtly asked whether she would attend one of his games: "I threw it out there, I threw the ball in her court. I told her, you know, I've seen you rock the stage in Arrowhead [Stadium], you might have to come see me rock the stage in Arrowhead and see which one's a little more lit. So, we'll see what happens in the near future."
