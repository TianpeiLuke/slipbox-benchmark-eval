# Roblox's China Strategy And Tencent

Asked at the Code Conference where Roblox stands in China — a big part of its growth narrative at its IPO, mentioned many times in the prospectus via a joint venture with Tencent that was shut down without explanation less than a year after the company went public — David Baszucki said Tencent continues to be an amazing partner and that Roblox is being very, very careful in China. The dynamics there have gone to the point where, rather than envisioning a fully connected-type network, the company has to imagine an autonomous network: it is modernizing its infrastructure to the point where it can literally print a copy of Roblox in China and bring it to market there.

Asked what an "autonomous version" of Roblox means, Baszucki said that as with any other platform or social media-type company, looking back over the last three to four years shows less and less information going back and forth between the US and China, to the point where he thinks the future will be very little going back in a China situation. Asked directly whether Roblox has given up on China, he answered: "Absolutely not. No." The interviewer also noted that Roblox's head of China is now its head of Japan.
