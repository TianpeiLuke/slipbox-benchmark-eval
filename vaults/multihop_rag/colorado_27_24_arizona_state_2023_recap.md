# Colorado 27, Arizona State 24: Week 6 2023 Recap

Colorado beat Arizona State 27-24 in Week 6 of the 2023 college football season, winning on Alejandro Mata's 43-yard field goal with 12 seconds left after a 43-yard pass from Shedeur Sanders on the Buffaloes' final drive set it up. It was Colorado's first Pac-12 win of the season and snapped an eight-game conference losing streak.

Sanders had a mediocre day by his lofty standards, but his 239 yards and a touchdown were enough in what the Sporting News called perhaps Colorado's most crucial game so far. The win brought Colorado to 4-2; had the Buffaloes lost, it would have been much harder for them to finish the season bowl eligible, and the victory gives them a leg up in a competitive conference, particularly with four ranked teams ahead of them down the stretch. The report's overall judgment was that neither team showed its worst but neither showed its best either: Colorado needed a bounce-back win and got one, while Arizona State kept struggling and fell to 1-5.

The scoring by quarter was Colorado 7-7-0-13 for 27 and Arizona State 7-10-0-7 for 24, with the final confirmed as Colorado 27, Arizona State 24.
