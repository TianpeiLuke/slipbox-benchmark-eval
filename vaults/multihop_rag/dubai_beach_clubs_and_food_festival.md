# May In Dubai: Beach Clubs, Al Fresco Dining And Two Festivals

May is a great time for a trip to Dubai, particularly for sunseekers, with average temperatures around 30 degrees — blue skies, turquoise seas and golden shores made for sunseekers. It is the month to make the most of dining al fresco, in a city filled with beachside eateries and outdoor terraces: at Akira Back, which serves Japanese dishes prepared with Korean and international flavours, you can enjoy incredible views across Dubai Marina from its huge terrace, or try Fish Beach Taverna for must-experience Aegean seafood in a coastal setting.

Whether you fancy soaking up the day's rays in a luxury cabana or floating in an infinity pool overlooking the surrounding sea, there is no shortage of beach clubs to get your sunshine fix in style: melt into a cosy daybed at SĀN Beach, or head to DRIFT Beach Dubai for infinity pool rest, relaxation and chic French fare.

Two festivals anchor the month. Foodies should coincide their trip with The Dubai Food Festival, the ultimate celebration of the local culinary scene, held at the beginning of the month; for those looking to add some culture to the mix, the Dubai Comedy Festival takes place later in May, bringing together some of the best names in comedy.
