# Born To Kill (1947)

Robert Wise's Born to Kill is an openly Hitchcockian drama that Polygon's Tasha Robinson says feels like a matchup for Suspicion, with terrific plotting built around the idea that it is always exciting when the audience knows a lot more than the characters. It is rentable on Amazon and Vudu.

Helen and Sam, played by Claire Trevor and Lawrence Tierney, are involved in different ways with the same double murder but do not realize it; when they meet and are attracted to each other, viewers see the threat looming ahead even while both characters remain oblivious. More murder, blackmail, cover-ups, and a great deal of double-dealing follow — enough, Robinson writes, to require a scorecard to follow the action. She calls it a terrifically tense story in which there is no mystery about who did the killing, only about what or who the audience should root for: whether these two crazy kids have a future together, whether either deserves a future at all, and how many innocent bystanders they will take down with them.
