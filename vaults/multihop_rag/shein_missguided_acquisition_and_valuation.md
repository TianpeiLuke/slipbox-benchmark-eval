# Shein's Missguided Acquisition and Valuation

As ASOS faced huge losses, it was reported in the week to 1 November 2023 that the Chinese-founded retailer Shein had acquired the UK company Missguided, with plans to "reignite" the online retailer — which had itself only been bought out of administration by Mike Ashley's Frasers Group a year and a half earlier.

Despite calls for boycotts from anti-fashion campaigners, Shein was at that point valued at around £53bn, having expanded globally and bought many of its rival brands. In 2022 it was the most-googled fashion brand in the world and made a reported £18.9bn in revenue, suggesting that fast fashion is not falling out of favour with consumers who want to find entire outfits on a budget.
