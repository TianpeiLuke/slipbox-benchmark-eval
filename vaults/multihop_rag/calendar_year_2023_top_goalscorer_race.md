# The 2023 Calendar-Year Top Goalscorer Race

Al Nassr striker Cristiano Ronaldo looked set to be the top scorer in the 2023 calendar year after his Boxing Day double against Al Ittihad. He was not the only star in great form, with players across Europe banging them in for fun.

The Portugal international had been battling with Kylian Mbappe, Erling Haaland and Harry Kane for the title of the best goalscorer of 2023.
