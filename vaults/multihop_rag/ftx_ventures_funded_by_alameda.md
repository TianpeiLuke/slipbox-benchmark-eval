# FTX Ventures Was Funded By Alameda

Caroline Ellison testified that the money behind FTX Ventures came from Alameda Research, even though Bankman-Fried announced the fund publicly without naming its source: on January 14th, 2022 he tweeted, "We're launching a $2b venture fund: FTX Ventures!" She said he did not want to go public with where the funds came from, telling her he thought Alameda's brand was less good and that he did not want his name associated with it.

The Verge reported the episode on October 10th, 2023 as part of Ellison's testimony, framing it as FTX keeping cozy private ties with Alameda while publicly holding it at arm's length — an echo, the reporter wrote, of Bankman-Fried and Ellison's romantic relationship. The same distancing appears in another transaction: Alameda bought Robinhood shares for Bankman-Fried, who moved them to a vehicle called "Emergent Fidelity Technologies" to avoid association with Alameda.
