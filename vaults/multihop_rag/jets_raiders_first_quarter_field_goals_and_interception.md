# Jets-Raiders First Quarter Field Goals And Interception

The first quarter of the Nov. 12, 2023 Jets-Raiders game produced three field goals and an interception, leaving the Jets ahead 6-3 as Breece Hall picked up a yard to end the period at 8:58 p.m.

The Raiders started the game with the ball at their own 25 at 8:23 p.m. and punted after O'Connell missed Davante Adams and then connected for gains of six and three, Xavier Gipson calling a fair catch at the Jets' 22 at 8:25 p.m. New York scored first: after two Hall carries at 8:27 p.m. and a six-yard threaded throw to Garrett Wilson at 8:28 p.m., the Wilson-to-Wilson connection went explosive at 8:29 p.m. for 41 yards down the right sideline to the Raiders' 26; at 8:30 p.m. Zach Wilson was brought down and flipped the ball as he fell, ruled intentional grounding for second-and-20; and at 8:31 p.m., after an impressive seven-yard Dalvin Cook run and a dropped third-down pass to Gipson, Greg Zuerlein kicked a field goal for a 3-0 lead.

Las Vegas answered at 8:39 p.m. DeAndre Carter returned the kickoff to the Raiders' 25 at 8:35 p.m.; at 8:37 p.m. Jacobs' first carry was stuffed for two before the first big O'Connell-Adams connection went 42 yards over the middle to the Jets' 31; at 8:38 p.m. Adams was flagged for offensive pass interference on a swing pass to Jacobs, bringing up first-and-20; and the drive ended in a 41-yard field goal to tie it at 3-3 after an impressive one-handed eight-yard Adams grab and a seven-yard Mayer catch that came up just short.

The Jets took the lead back at 8:51 p.m. Starting at their own 25 with 6:15 left at 8:44 p.m., they moved on big strikes: a 24-yard Wilson pass to Gipson down the right sideline to the Raiders' 45 at 8:45 p.m., then five- and eight-yard Garrett Wilson catches to the Raiders' 32 at 8:47 p.m.; at 8:49 p.m. Garrett Wilson took an end around for 11 yards but C.J. Uzomah's holding penalty pushed it to second-and-18; and at 8:51 p.m., after Garrett Wilson could not hang on to a threaded pass and Tyler Conklin made a five-yard grab, Zuerlein kicked another field goal for a 6-3 lead. Las Vegas' next drive ended in disaster: after a touchback at 8:54 p.m., O'Connell muffed the snap under center and fell on the ball for a one-yard loss at 8:55 p.m., then at 8:56 p.m. threw an interception right at Jordan Whitehead, who returned it to the Raiders' 26; the Raiders challenged, but since all turnovers are reviewed their timeout was used. Earlier, at 8:10 p.m., the blog noted that Aaron Rodgers might be looking better and better each week but would not be playing against the Raiders that night.
