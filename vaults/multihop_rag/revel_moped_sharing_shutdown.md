# Revel's Moped-Sharing Shutdown

Revel — the Blackrock- and Shell-backed moped-sharing business — dumped its two-wheeled EVs in 2023 in favor of regular-old electric cars.

The company said its moped ridership peaked in July 2020, at nearly 600,000 rides that month. In the years that followed, Revel's moped ridership collapsed amid several deaths involving Revel mopeds, as e-bikes gained ground in cities like New York and San Francisco.
