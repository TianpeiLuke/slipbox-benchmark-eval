# Wingwomen

Wingwomen, an action film available to stream on Netflix in early November 2023, runs 1 hour 56 minutes and is directed by Mélanie Laurent, who is both behind and in front of the camera; the cast includes Laurent, Adèle Exarchopoulos, and Isabelle Adjani.

Polygon describes it as an action comedy about women thieves on the run looking to pull off one last job, and notes Laurent's earlier appearance in Inglourious Basterds. Netflix's own synopsis, carried in Polygon's November catalog listing, adds detail: tired of life on the run, a pro thief decides to retire — but not before one easy last job with her partner in crime and a feisty new getaway driver.
