# Mario Barrios Claims The Interim WBC Title With A Wide Decision Over Yordenis Ugas

Mario Barrios claimed the interim WBC welterweight title with a wide decision over Yordenis Ugas on the Canelo-Charlo card, Sporting News reported in its live coverage of 2023-09-30, with scores of 117-108, 118-107 and 118-107. The bout was introduced at 9:00 p.m. ET as Yordenis Ugas (27-5, 12 KOs) versus Mario Barrios (27-2, 18 KOs) for the WBC interim super welterweight championship, with Ugas back in action over a year after suffering an eye injury in defeat against Errol Spence Jr. and Barrios having his third fight at 147 pounds.

The live entries tracked the fight's turning points. At 9:26 p.m. ET, Barrios had scored a knockdown at the end of round two to make the fight very interesting — Ugas walked into a stiff jab and hit the canvas, and although he wasn't terribly hurt, his punch resistance over the duration became the question. At 9:50 p.m. ET, heading into the ninth, there was clear damage on Ugas' right eye, the same orbital bone broken by Errol Spence Jr. the previous year; he had fought a decent fight and appeared to have rebounded after the early knockdown but would have to finish strong to get a decision. At 10:00 p.m. ET the condition of that right eye continued to worsen heading into the championship rounds, and the coverage suggested this could be the beginning of the end for Ugas' career if things did not turn around in the final six minutes. At 10:08 p.m. ET there was added drama down the stretch as Barrios dropped Ugas, who appeared to be on the verge of getting stopped; Ugas spit out his mouthpiece on two occasions to slow down the beating but lost a point in the process. It didn't matter, as Barrios was well ahead and secured the decision and the title.
