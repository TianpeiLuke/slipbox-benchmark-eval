# Bose QuietComfort 45 Headphones Deal Prices

The Bose QuietComfort 45 over-ear headphones fell to $229-$230 for October Prime Day 2023 and then to a record-low $199 in Amazon's Cyber Monday sale, according to Engadget and Mashable roundups across the season.

Mashable's Cyber Monday post of 27 November 2023 said there is "only one pair of Bose over-ear headphones you can grab for just under $200, and that's the QuietComfort 45s at a record-low price of $199", crediting the classic pair with top-of-the-line ANC, 20 hours of battery life and all-day comfort "for less than half of the AirPods Max's sale price". Engadget's 10 October 2023 Prime Day roundup put them at $230, about $80 cheaper than their usual selling price, and said they earned a spot in its best wireless headphone guide thanks to their comfortable if a little dated design, excellent ANC and balanced audio.

Engadget's headphone-specific Prime Day list, also 10 October 2023, listed them at $229 against a $329 list price (save $100), noting that $229 is $30 more than the lowest price it had tracked but still $80 below their average street price. That piece adds that this is another noise-cancelling set Bose recently replaced, but the differences between the QC 45 and the new QuietComfort Headphones appear small, so the older model is worth a look when discounted; Engadget gave the QC 45 a review score of 86 in 2021 and still likes its lightweight design, upper-tier ANC and easygoing sound. The caveats: you cannot manually adjust ANC intensity, unlike the newer model, and Sony's XM5 and XM4 provide longer battery life and a more versatile feature set for those willing to pay up.
