# Art Cullagh And The Shadow Curse Quest In Baldur's Gate 3

Art Cullagh is a sleepy man in the Last Light Inn of Baldur's Gate 3's second act, and he is the key to breaking the Shadow Curse that plagues the Shadow-touched lands.

Once you make it into act 2 and reach the central hub of the Last Light Inn, the quest chain opens up: you wake Art, locate Thaniel, defeat Oliver, and finally recruit Halsin as a real companion for your party. All of that takes place during a lengthy quest that takes you all across these new lands and to the edge of Moonrise Towers.
