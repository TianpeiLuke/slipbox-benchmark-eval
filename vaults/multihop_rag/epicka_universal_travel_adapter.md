# Epicka Universal Travel Adapter

The Epicka universal travel adapter was $20, down from $25 — 20 percent off at Amazon. It is an all-in-one adapter that includes four different plugs covering over 150 countries, and it is senior reviewer Victoria Song's pick.

In her youth she forgot to pack plug adapters for international trips one too many times, and buying them once you have landed in another country is not always easy; besides, her relatives in rural Korea do not always have more than one plug for American devices, in which case she would have to share with her six other cousins. So she never leaves the country without a universal travel adapter.

It is basically six plug adaptors in one, and depending which one you get it might come with USB ports so you can charge multiple devices in one outlet. It is bulkier than buying one or two specialized adapter plugs, but for a multi-continent itinerary it is a game-changer; what she likes about the Epicka is that it comes with a spare fuse in case things go sideways with voltage.

The caveat is the word "universal": it is technically only the four most common types of plugs, which will get you by in most countries but is not a guarantee in places like Brazil, South Africa, or India. Even so, she will take this over price gouging at airport electronics shops or taking time out of her schedule to visit a local hardware store.
