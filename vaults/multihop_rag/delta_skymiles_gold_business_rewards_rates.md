# Delta SkyMiles Gold Business Card Rewards Rates

The Delta SkyMiles Gold Business American Express Card earns 2x miles in four categories and 1x mile everywhere else: 2x on purchases made directly with Delta, 2x on U.S. purchases for advertising in select media, 2x on U.S. shipping purchases, 2x on dining at restaurants, and 1x mile on every eligible dollar spent on other purchases.

Fortune's 2023-12-12 framing is that these higher rewards rates apply to select purchases that might be helpful to small business owners. The rates accompany a $0 intro annual fee for the first year, then $99.
