# Rogue Trader: The Companion Cast

Between battles the player can chat with companions and learn about their pasts: most have deep and dark secrets that time or patience will uncover, and fascinating stories to tell.

Polygon's reviewer runs through the cast. Abelard, her Senechal, is a man who sucks morally but backs her to the absolute hilt; she grew to love hearing about his days in the Imperial Navy and calls him the one guy she felt she could trust. Augusta, a Sister of Battle, starts as a one-note zealot, but cracks form in that facade as her past and doubts emerge. Cassia and Pasqal each represent a sub-faction of 40K and have much to share about the Navigators and the Tech-Priests. Her absolute favorite is Marazhai Aezyrraesh, a dark space elf who feeds off the suffering of sentient beings — cruel, depraved, and an absolute hoot, and though he may flay a few too many people for her tastes, the best companion to bring to a party.
