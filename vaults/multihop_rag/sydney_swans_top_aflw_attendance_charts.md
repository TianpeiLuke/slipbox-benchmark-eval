# Sydney Swans Top The AFLW Attendance Charts

The Sydney Swans sat top of the AFLW attendance charts on 26 October 2023, less than two years after their first match in the women's league, with an average attendance of 4,365 from four games — 124 more than the next best club, Adelaide.

They were due to play their final home match of the regular season that weekend, a marquee pride match against Collingwood at Henson Park on the Sunday, where a crowd in excess of 4,000 was expected. Earlier in the season, 5,474 fans were at North Sydney Oval to watch the Swans' first AFLW victory.

Swans forward Bella Smith said the players were proud of the club's attendance record and grateful for the support: "We get there two hours before the start of the game and the hill [at Henson Park] is bare. When you come out for the first team warm-up, the hill is slowly filling and then by the time the first bounce gets around, it is full. You can hear it, the atmosphere is amazing."
