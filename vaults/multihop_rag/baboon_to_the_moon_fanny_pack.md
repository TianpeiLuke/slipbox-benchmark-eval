# Baboon To The Moon Fanny Pack

Senior reviewer Victoria Song had always been stymied by the need for a bag smaller than a backpack or knapsack but larger than a dinky clutch, and was wary of the whole "wear a fanny pack as a mini crossbody bag" trend — until she caved earlier this summer and bought the Baboon to the Moon 3L Fannypack. Now she cannot go back.

The bag easily fits her wallet, phone, house keys, car keys, hand sanitizer, and chapstick — everything she needs when she takes walks or runs errands. It has a quick-release buckle, so she can stick a carabiner on it and schlep a water bottle on hot days. The material is incredibly durable, spill-proof, and easy to clean, and the inside has some dividers but nothing too complicated.

What she probably likes most about this fanny pack, and the brand in general, is how colorful the bags are. She got one in lavender, stuck some enamel pins on it, and now there is absolutely no mistaking it is hers — she can see it easily from a distance, which also makes it harder to lose. The mix between colorful whimsy and practical functionality is something she wishes she saw more often; two of her friends went out and bought the same bag in different colors as soon as they saw her wearing it, and they are all immensely happy with their purchases.
