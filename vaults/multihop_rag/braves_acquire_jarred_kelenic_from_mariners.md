# Braves Land Jarred Kelenic And Marco Gonzales From The Mariners

Atlanta traded its No. 7-ranked prospect, right-hander Cole Phillips, and right-hander Jackson Kowar to the Mariners in exchange for outfielder Jarred Kelenic, left-hander Marco Gonzales, first baseman Evan White and cash on Sunday, Dec. 3, 2023, per Ryan Divish of the Seattle Times as carried in Sporting News' winter meetings tracker.

Kelenic, 24, was the headliner of the deal as a former No. 6 overall pick in the 2018 MLB Draft and a top five-ranked prospect in 2021. He had not found any consistency in his MLB career thus far, hitting .206 with a .656 OPS through three tumultuous seasons, with 32 home runs, 109 RBI and 24 stolen bases in 974 plate appearances.

Gonzales, 31, was also a decent rotation arm, with 65 wins, 665 strikeouts and a 4.14 ERA over 893.0 innings pitched in his nine-year career.
