# Kevin Durant Career Accolades And Scoring Ranks

Kevin Durant, as Essentially Sports characterized him on Dec. 1, 2023, stands as one of the most exceptionally skilled players in NBA history — a transcendent superstar across an illustrious 17-year career whose stellar performances have endeared him to millions of fans, yet who regularly faces disregard from both the media and fans, making him one of the most polarizing figures in basketball.

The discriminating feature is his physical and technical profile at entry: Durant rose to prominence in 2007, when the Seattle SuperSonics drafted him with the No. 2 pick, and the NBA had never witnessed a player possessing his unique skill set — a 6'11" forward renowned for his exceptional jump shot and lethal three-point accuracy, epitomizing the concept of a "Unicorn" in its truest sense. The record makes the disrespect more perplexing still: Durant has amassed 27,393 points in the regular season, ranking 11th in NBA history, and has contributed an additional 4,878 points in the playoffs, placing him 7th on the all-time list. He is a surefire future first-ballot Hall of Famer with a resume few can match — two NBA titles, two NBA Finals MVP awards, four scoring titles, and multiple selections to the All-Star and All-NBA teams.
