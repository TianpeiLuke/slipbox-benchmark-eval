# The 2023-24 Offseason Starting Pitcher Trade Market

Most of the star power on the 2023-24 trade market was in arms, with Dylan Cease and Tyler Glasnow the pitchers most likely to be dealt that offseason, per Jeff Passan of ESPN on Friday, Dec. 1, 2023 as carried in Sporting News' winter meetings tracker.

Passan noted the free agency pool outside of Ohtani, Cody Bellinger, Blake Snell and Yoshinobu Yamamoto was a bit lacking in star power, but that the trade market could ensure several big-name players were on the move. Beyond Cease and Glasnow, Cleveland could also hold a pair of intriguing trade chips in closer Emmanuel Clase and former Cy Young winner Shane Bieber, and the Brewers had begun trade talks for former Cy Young winner Corbin Burnes.

The market developed over the meetings. On Sunday, Dec. 3, Jon Morosi reported three teams — the Dodgers, Braves and Orioles — were among several involved in discussing a deal for White Sox ace Dylan Cease as the meetings began. On Tuesday, Dec. 5, with MLB largely in a holding pattern awaiting news on Ohtani, Morosi reported the Rays could wind up trading Tyler Glasnow shortly after Ohtani signs, listing the Cubs and Reds as top contenders for the ace, and that Yamamoto could sign following Ohtani's deal with the Red Sox having him among their top priorities. On Wednesday, Dec. 6, Jon Heyman of the New York Post reported one rival believed there was "a decent chance" Corbin Burnes was traded that winter — the 2021 NL Cy Young winner was a free agent after the 2024 season and could be one of the most expensive pitchers to sign — and that Miami was at least believed to be listening to trade packages for its young starters including Jesus Luzardo, Braxton Garrett and Edward Cabrera, having made a run to the postseason the previous year on the strength of that young pitching.
