# The Epic v. Google Witness Lineup

A whole cast of intriguing people were set to be called as witnesses during the trial, going straight to the top of the org chart: Google CEO Sundar Pichai and Epic CEO Tim Sweeney are absolutely expected to attend.

Beyond the two chief executives, Android co-founder Andy Rubin might be deposed, and Google says the court will hear from current Android — and Chrome, and Chrome OS — boss Hiroshi Lockheimer. Google also says representatives from Apple, Netflix, Motorola, and AT&T should testify.
