# NFL Week 13 Injuries Lions At Saints

In the Week 13 final injury reports published by CBSSports.com on Dec. 1, 2023, with the Lions listed at -4 at the Saints, the headline uncertainty was New Orleans wide receiver Chris Olave's concussion status.

On the Detroit side, the Lions had just designated Hooker for return — he had been rehabbing a torn ACL suffered at Tennessee the previous year — so the rookie quarterback was not expected to be activated that week; Anzalone did not practice at all that week, so his prospects looked bleak for Week 13; and Jackson was a full participant on both Thursday and Friday. Head coach Dennis Allen told reporters Friday that despite Olave being a full participant in Friday's practice he was still in concussion protocol though had a chance to play: the wideout still had another hurdle to pass to be cleared for Sunday, so his status remained very much up in the air.
