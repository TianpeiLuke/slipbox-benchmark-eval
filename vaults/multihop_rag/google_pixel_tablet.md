# Google Pixel Tablet Deal Prices

The 128GB Google Pixel Tablet fell to $409 for Amazon's October 2023 Prime Big Deal Days and then to an all-time low of $399 for Black Friday, Engadget reported on 10 October and 19 November 2023.

At Prime Day the 128GB model was $409 — $90 off and a new low — with the 256GB model at $509. By 19 November it was $399 at several retailers including Amazon, Target, Best Buy and Google's own online store, which is $100 off the 11-inch slate's list price and $10 less than the Prime Big Deal Days price; the 256GB version was also $100 off at $499.

Engadget gave the Pixel Tablet a review score of 85 in June: its crisp/sharp LCD display, clean software, speedy Tensor G2 chip and easy-to-hold design weighing just over a pound make it a solid Android tablet in its own right, but its biggest selling point is the included charging speaker dock — plop the tablet onto it and it becomes a Nest Hub Max-style smart display and smart home hub. The October piece adds that Engadget thinks Samsung's Galaxy Tab S9 works better purely as a tablet, and that Android still is not as optimised for large screens as iPadOS, but if the extra functionality sounds useful the Pixel Tablet is worth a look; the Galaxy Tab S9 was not on sale at that writing.
