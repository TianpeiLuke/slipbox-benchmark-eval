# Georgia's First-Half Touchdown Drives: Beck And Edwards

Georgia scored three quick first-half touchdowns against Ole Miss on Nov. 11, 2023, all set up or delivered by Carson Beck, twice pulling level and twice taking a seven-point lead.

Trailing Ole Miss 7-0, the Bulldogs got on the board at 7:31 p.m. after Beck tossed to Rara Thomas for a 44-yard gain at 7:29 p.m. that gave them a first down at Ole Miss's 13; Beck then flicked it to Daijun Edwards for a one-yard touchdown, tying the game in Athens with 7:28 on the clock in the first quarter. At 7:45 p.m., needing only a minute, Beck found Ladd McConkey for a 29-yard touchdown to put the Bulldogs seven points up with 3:09 left in the first frame.

After Ole Miss levelled at 14-14, Edwards ran in his second touchdown of the night at 8:07 p.m., following a 41-yard connection from Beck to McConkey, giving Georgia its second seven-point lead of the evening with just less than 10 minutes remaining in the first half.
