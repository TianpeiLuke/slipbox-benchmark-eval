# Abdul Karim Abdullah's Path into the Music Business

Abdul Karim Abdullah's first experience in the music industry came when a member of his fraternity, Phi Beta Sigma Fraternity Inc., booked Big Sean at his alma mater, Syracuse University. "I remember how much I enjoyed the experience because I was fascinated by how everything came together and the process of creating and managing an event," he told Music Business Worldwide in October 2023.

From that experience he got his big break as a stage manager for a concert he pitched to W&R Productions for El and Joey B, two major Ghanaian artists, at the Gramercy Theatre. Those early experiences, he says, gave him the confidence he needed to create, plan and execute his own event, leaving only the question of how to fund his big ideas. From his first event to establishing AfroFuture, he says he has gained a tremendous amount of knowledge, and that it has been the driving force behind everything he does to create sustainable change not only for Ghana but for Africa at large.
