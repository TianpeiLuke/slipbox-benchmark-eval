# Reese Witherspoon On Letting Children Fail

Reese Witherspoon argues that learning from failure "is actually a valuable tool that you can't take away from kids" — "You rob them if you don't let them sit in the discomfort of the experience" — speaking on the "Good Inside with Dr. Becky" podcast as reported by FOX News Entertainment on 10 October 2023.

Her grounds are her own upbringing. "I see this a lot with parents – I don't know when we stopped letting our kids fail. Like I learned so much from the paper I didn't turn in or the demerits I got, so I got detention. I was suspended from school," she recalled, adding: "And my parents didn't say, 'Uh, she didn't deserve that,' and take me out of school. They actually let me sit in it and feel uncomfortable." The Oscar winner, who shares her eldest children Ava and Deacon with ex-husband Ryan Phillippe and her youngest, Tennessee, with ex-husband Jim Toth, said she wants to see kids live with failure a little more.

The warrant is that discomfort teaches something removal cannot, including self-knowledge about limits. The "Walk the Line" star remembered comforting her daughter after she lost a basketball game while not shying away from the lesson: she said, "'Yeah, I know. I know that probably feels really bad,'" then added, "'You know what also? Maybe you're not good at basketball?'" Her daughter was slightly upset in the moment, but Witherspoon continued: "It's actually really important to learn what you're not good at."
