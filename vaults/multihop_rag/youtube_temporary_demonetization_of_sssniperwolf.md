# YouTube's Temporary Demonetization of SSSniperwolf

On Oct. 20, a week after the doxxing incident, YouTube and Alia Shelesh both made public statements, Polygon reported on 2023-10-25.

YouTube posted a tweet confirming Shelesh had received "a temporary monetization suspension per Creator Responsibility policies." The tweet also criticized "the behavior on both sides" and expressed hope the incident would "move this convo to a better place." TeamYouTube's post read: "Confirming SSSniperWolf has received a temporary monetization suspension per Creator Responsibility policies. Off platform actions that put others' personal safety at risk harm our community & the behavior on both sides isn't what we want on YT."

Shelesh made a statement on X apologizing to "Jacksfilm, YouTube, the entire creator community, and my incredible fans for not being a better example of conflict resolution." She has since posted a handful of new videos to her YouTube account, with the comment sections disabled.
