# The November 2023 G3 Geomagnetic Storm

A strong geomagnetic storm peaked as a G3 on Nov. 5, 2023 at 12:40 p.m. EST (1740 GMT) on the 5-grade scale used by the U.S. National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) to assess the severity of space weather events, triggering colourful aurora displays reported as far south as Greece and Turkey.

Geomagnetic storms are disturbances to Earth's magnetic field caused by solar material from coronal mass ejections (CMEs) — large expulsions of plasma and magnetic fields from the sun's atmosphere. This particular storm was triggered by not one but two CMEs that slammed into Earth on Nov. 4 and Nov. 5, according to spaceweather.com. Skywatchers worldwide were treated to a magnificent aurora light show over that weekend.
