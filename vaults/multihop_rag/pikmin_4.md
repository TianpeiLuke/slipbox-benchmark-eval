# Pikmin 4

Pikmin 4, developed by Nintendo EPD and Eighting for Nintendo Switch, is ranked 5 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31, and Polygon calls it the perfect amalgamation of Pikmin games.

The synthesis is specific: there are fun mini-dungeon caverns, but they are not filled with terrible bomb traps; there are new types of Pikmin, but not at the cost of getting rid of the old ones; and there is a dog now, who helps corral the Pikmin and make operations run even more efficiently.

Critic JL calls the entirety of the game a whimsical joy to play — even completing every Dandori Challenge, which got pretty damn hard, and collecting every item never became a drag or a grind, and it might actually be impossible to be angry while playing. Hearing the little Pikmin hum as they carry a huge peach, which they call a "mock bottom," across the map is enough to melt anyone's heart, and JL was sad when the game ended. The game is beautiful and, thanks to its quasi real-world setting, makes the critic see the world differently, as though there is something special to be found everywhere, even in the tiniest corners. Even for someone who has never played a Pikmin game, it is the perfect place to start, with the rest conveniently ported to the Switch.
