# Budget Smart Home And Networking Deals

Four cheap smart home and networking items made the under-$25 list. The Meross Smart Wi-Fi Plug Mini (MSS110) was $18, down from $23 — 22 percent off for a two-pack with an on-page coupon; with solid performance and support for all the major smart home platforms, this affordable Wi-Fi smart plug gives you the basics you need to control non-smart devices for less, and while it lacks Matter or energy monitoring, that is alright at this price.

The Amazon Smart Plug was $15, down from $25 — 40 percent off at Amazon and Best Buy. It does not require a hub and lets you add Alexa functionality to any outlet, so you can control a range of devices with just your voice.

The Amazon Echo Glow was $17, down from $30 — 43 percent off at Amazon. It is a multicolored smart lamp for children that can cycle through colors and function as an Alexa-enabled sleep timer if needed.

TP-Link's RE315 Wi-Fi extender was on sale for just $24.99, $25 off, matching its best price to date — a good option if you are looking to extend network coverage in your home or add ethernet connectivity to another room.
