# Josh Harris Commanders Hot Seat

Yardbarker's Nov. 30, 2023 NFC hot-seat roundup named owner Josh Harris as the Washington Commanders' hottest seat, arguing he would need a better plan that offseason once "I'm not Dan Snyder" stopped working.

The grounds are his record and the standings: he oversaw the controversial "Process" as owner of the Philadelphia 76ers, and at 4-8 the Commanders were floundering in his first season of ownership. The warrant is that goodwill purchased purely by not being the previous owner is a depreciating asset — it has to be replaced by results. The entry was written by Bruce Ewing.
