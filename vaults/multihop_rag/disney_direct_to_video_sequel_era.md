# The Disney Direct-To-Video Sequel Era

Disney once pumped out cheap direct-to-home-video sequels based on every beloved Disney movie from The Fox and the Hound to The Lion King — and it is an era the studio tries not to remember.

After John Lasseter took over as Disney's chief creative officer in 2006, he canned the whole DTV department a year later, reportedly denouncing the way cheaply made sequels diminished the Disney brand. Polygon's observation is that even though Disney loves to celebrate its past, the studio rarely references this era.
