# The Franke Defense That Hildebrandt's Influence Drove The Abuse

Franke's attorneys at Winward Law did not dispute that the abuse occurred; they attacked the premise that it originated with Franke's own moral judgment, saying in a statement on the Friday before the plea that the abuse occurred while Franke was influenced by a relationship counselor who led her to "a distorted sense of morality."

"Ruby Franke is a devoted mother and is also a woman committed to constant improvement," the statement said. It framed her as having initially believed that her co-defendant Jodi Hildebrandt "had the insight to offer a path to continual improvement," but said that Hildebrandt "took advantage of this quest and twisted it into something heinous." The statement stops short of denying culpability: during Franke's incarceration, "she has actively engaged in an introspection that has allowed her to reset her moral compass and understand the full weight of her actions. Ms. Franke is committed to taking responsibility for the part she played in the events leading up to her incarceration."
