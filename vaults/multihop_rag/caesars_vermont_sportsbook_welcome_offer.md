# How Caesars Sportsbook's Up-To-$1,000 First Bet Offer Would Work In Vermont

Caesars Sportsbook has become one of the most popular online sportsbooks in the US in several states and should prove a favorite in Vermont as well, Sporting News wrote on 2023-09-26, with the expectation that Caesars will offer the same offer in Vermont as elsewhere: new sign-ups eligible to receive up to a $1,000 Bonus Bet if their first bet loses.

The steps: when registering your new Caesars Sportsbook account, enter the bonus code NEWS1000; place a first bet within 30 days, and if it loses, receive back the equivalent amount in the form of a Bonus Bet worth up to $1,000; after that first bet has been settled, you'll see the Bonus Bet in your account within two days; use your Bonus Bet within 14 days or else it will expire; and if you win with your Bonus Bet, you only receive the winnings and not the bet amount back. On the product itself, Caesars covers a wide range of markets and different bet types while featuring a terrific live betting platform, offers frequent odds boosts and other bonuses, and enrolls new sign-ups in the famed Caesars Rewards program that can earn them more site bonuses among other benefits.
