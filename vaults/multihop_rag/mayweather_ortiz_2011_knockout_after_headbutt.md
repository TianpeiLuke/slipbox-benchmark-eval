# Mayweather vs Ortiz, 2011: The Knockout After The Headbutt

Floyd Mayweather knocked out Victor Ortiz in 2011 with two cracking hooks landed while Ortiz had his hands by his side, in what talkSPORT called on 15 November 2023 the most bizarre finishing sequence of Mayweather's 50-bout pro career.

Ortiz had blatantly headbutted "Money", was docked a point, then wanted to apologise by hugging it out. An angry Mayweather was having none of it. It was also his last KO against an actual boxer, discounting Conor McGregor and Deji.

The ending sparked an argument between Mayweather and HBO analyst Larry Merchant, which finished with the 80-year-old proclaiming his desire to kick Floyd's ass.
