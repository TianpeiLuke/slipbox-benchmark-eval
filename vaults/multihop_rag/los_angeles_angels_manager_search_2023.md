# The Angels' Manager Search Hinges On What Shohei Ohtani Decides

The Angels' managerial seat stood vacant after Phil Nevin's 2024 club option was rejected, Essentially Sports reported on 2023-10-30. The sequence: after Joe Maddon was relieved of his managerial duties, Nevin signed up as interim manager for the remaining 106 games of 2022, then signed a one-year contract to manage the Angels in 2023 with the interim tag lifted. The Halos rejected his 2024 option after partial two seasons of 119-149 (.444) play under his supervision, and since this was non-negotiable, the seat stands vacant.

The biggest challenge is that Angels star Shohei Ohtani had yet to reveal which team he would be joining. If the Angels re-sign the two-way phenom, the manager would get to head a roster with the 2021 MVP and Mike Trout — a once in a lifetime opportunity. On the flip side, if they lose Ohtani, the roster would need a solid revamp; the Angels are also infamous for their below-par farm system and a playoff drought spanning 9 seasons. As for options: Ray Montgomery could be one, considering he began his tenure with the Angels; Walt Weiss could potentially be a decent fit, having been part of a successful team in the Atlanta Braves as their bench coach since before the 2018 season; and among other options are Ron Washington and Clayton McCullough. Though these options are open, no solid information had been revealed as to who would fill the position.
