# The Platform Versus Point Solution Pendulum In Security

In the world of IT the pendulum swings between giant all-in-one platforms and customers opting for best-in-class point solutions, and in cybersecurity — considering the number of acquisitions being reported — the swing is definitely in the direction of large platforms, which is what the Talon and Dig deals represent.

The open question is integration rather than direction: how well those doing the buying are digesting these companies, and how that gets presented to end users. There is evidence the digestion is not going smoothly. A source told TechCrunch that customers parting ways with Palo Alto are doing so in part because "they feel like the product is disconnected" — the company now has more than 30 products and services and has made 17 acquisitions in total. If these two deals close, the question will be how Palo Alto Networks' CEO Nikesh Arora plans to present a holistic view of tackling what remains a very fragmented and dangerous threat landscape.
