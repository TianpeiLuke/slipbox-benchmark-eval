# Alanna Rizzo's Attack On The Report: The Clubhouse Is 'A Sacred Space'

The biggest backlash to Jake Mintz's report came from MLB Network's Alanna Rizzo, who on "High Heat" attacked the premise that a remark shouted in the clubhouse was fair game for publication — and attacked Mintz's standing to report it at all. She said she was irritated by "bloggers or podcasters" going into the clubhouse and "ruining it for the rest of us" when "some jackoff comes in at the end of the season that gets a credential, God only knows why," called the clubhouse "a sacred place," and defended the players who were upset by the reporting of Arcia's comments before naming Mintz.

"First of all, this guy, Jake Mintz, that's not even a reporter," Rizzo said. "That's taking away from true reporters and true journalists. Where were you on the random Tuesday in April in Cincinnati when this team was playing in Cincinnati? Where were you on a rain delay, and you have to sit in the press box for all of this time? It's ridiculous." The clip was circulated by Awful Announcing on October 12, 2023.

She was not alone in the objection. Players including the Blue Jays' Kevin Gausman decried the situation as a mistake by Mintz to report comments that were said out loud in the locker room rather than in an interview setting, while others defended it on the grounds that anything in the clubhouse is open to be reported on — the reporting proved divisive across the baseball community. Following a BBWAA statement, Rizzo apologized to Mintz both publicly and privately for her reaction.
