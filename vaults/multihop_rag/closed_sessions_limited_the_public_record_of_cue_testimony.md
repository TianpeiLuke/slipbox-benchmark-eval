# Closed Sessions Limited The Public Record Of Cue's Testimony

The Verge cautioned that what it reported was all the testimony it saw: like so many things in this trial, the star witness was kept mostly under wraps thanks to complaints and worries about revealing confidential numbers and corporate secrets.

Even so, the questions put to Eddy Cue were the ones the DOJ is going to keep asking: is Google really the best search engine, or is it just the one writing the biggest checks — and if those checks went away, what would the search engine market look like? On that counterfactual, Cue said Apple has never really thought about it, Google said Apple would be silly to do so, and the Justice Department thinks it's about time Apple starts doing so.
