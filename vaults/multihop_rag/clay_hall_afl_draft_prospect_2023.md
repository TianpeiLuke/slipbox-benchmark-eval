# Clay Hall AFL Draft Prospect 2023

Clay Hall, profiled in Kevin Sheehan's top 40 for the 2023 AFL Draft as reported by The Roar on Nov. 15, 2023, is a prolific disposal winner who uses the ball effectively on his preferred left foot and excels at stoppages, using his clean hands and vision to create for his team. State: Western Australia. State League Club: Peel Thunder. Community Club: Harvey Brunswick Leschenault. Date of Birth: 12/05/05. Height: 189cm.

He has already played nine matches at senior level with Peel Thunder, averaging 18.9 disposals, and starred when representing Western Australia at the National Championships, averaging 27.2 disposals, 11 contested possessions, six clearances and five tackles to win All Australian honours. The eligibility boundary worth noting: he is the son of former West Coast Eagles and Geelong Cats forward Derek Hall, but is not eligible as a father-son to either club, given Derek played only two games for the Eagles and 74 matches for the Cats. He did not test at the national Draft Combine due to injury. Weapon: Neat kicking and ball-winning ability. AFL player comparison: Jack Macrae (Western Bulldogs).
