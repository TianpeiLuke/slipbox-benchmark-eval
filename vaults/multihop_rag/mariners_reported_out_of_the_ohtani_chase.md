# The Mariners Reported Out Of The Ohtani Chase

Daniel Kramer of MLB.com reported on Nov. 17, 2023 that "Industry sources told MLB.com this week that landing Ohtani doesn't appear to be within the Mariners' realistic agenda this offseason."

Sporting News found that surprising, because Seattle seemed to check a lot of boxes for Ohtani: they are set up to win for a long time with a bunch of young starting pitchers and a co-superstar in Julio Rodriguez; Ohtani would get to be part of the quest for the franchise's first World Series title; he would wear the same uniform as his baseball hero, Ichiro; and he has lived there in the offseason. The inference drawn was that it must be about the money and Seattle's reluctance to even get in the conversation — which, the piece added, has to be super disappointing for Mariners fans starved for a title.
