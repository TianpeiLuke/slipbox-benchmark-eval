# ASX Sector Movers On 4 October 2023

On the small-cap front, Melbourne-based biotech Dimerix more than doubled its share price to 16¢ on the Thursday after revealing it had inked an exclusive licensing deal to commercialise its treatment for a kidney disease called Focal Segmental Glomerulosclerosis in Europe, the UK, Switzerland, Canada, Australia and New Zealand.

Among the laggards, energy companies were down 0.9 per cent and among the weakest on the index as the oil price slumped overnight, with heavyweights Woodside and Santos shedding 0.8 per cent and 0.4 per cent respectively and Ampol dropping 2.8 per cent. Miners were also weaker: lithium prospector IGO fell 4 per cent and Mineral Resources 2.9 per cent, which the report lists among the biggest large-cap advancers despite the falls. Iron ore giants Rio Tinto, down 1.5 per cent, and BHP, down 0.7 per cent, slipped after the iron ore price fell 1.1 per cent, while Aristocrat Leisure, down 3.7 per cent, dragged the consumer discretionary sector down 0.3 per cent.
