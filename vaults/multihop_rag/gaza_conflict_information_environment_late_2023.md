# The Gaza Conflict's Information Environment, Late 2023

Israel has retaliated against Hamas' October 7 attack with unprecedented force against the Palestinian territory, claiming over 9,000 Palestinian lives according to Gaza Health Ministry numbers reported by the Associated Press, as of November 3, 2023.

Reporting capacity in the territory has been degraded on two fronts. The Committee to Protect Journalists reports that at least 30 journalists have been killed since Israel's bombardment and total blockade of the region began, and journalists in Gaza say that without consistent access to food, water and power it is becoming "impossible" to continue reporting.

At the same time, misinformation about the escalating violence is rampant on social media — so the shortfall in professional reporting coincides with an unreliable information supply.
