# Broadcast Rights For Argentina vs Paraguay, October 2023

Broadcast rights for Argentina's home World Cup qualifiers in the United States were secured by Telemundo and Univision following an agreement at the 11th hour of the September 2023 international break, so U.S. viewers could watch the Oct. 12 match against Paraguay on Universo, with streaming on Fubo as well as the company's premium subscription services.

The defining boundary of this arrangement is its incompleteness: the match was not set to be available globally to regions including Australia, Canada and the United Kingdom, because CONMEBOL had yet to sell broadcast rights to a provider in those territories. The published grid shows blanks for the UK, Australia, Canada, India, New Zealand, Singapore and Hong Kong, with entries only for the USA (Universo on TV; Fubo, Telemundo Deportes en Vivo and Universo NOW for streaming) and Malaysia (Astro Supersport on TV; Astro Go and sooka for streaming).

Other territories with broadcast options for this match included Malaysia (Astro Supersport), Argentina (TyC Sports), Brazil (SporTV 2), France (L'Equipe Web), Spain (Movistar) and Saudi Arabia (SSC).
