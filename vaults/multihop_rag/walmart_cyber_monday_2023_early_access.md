# Walmart's Cyber Monday 2023 Early Access For Walmart+ Members

Walmart's official Cyber Monday sale started on Sunday 26 November 2023 at 7 p.m. ET, exclusively on Walmart.com and the Walmart app, with Walmart+ members able to access it three hours early at 4 p.m. ET — the first time Walmart had given Walmart+ members early access to its Cyber Monday sale, Mashable reported on 25 November 2023.

Walmart usually does grant that early access for Black Friday, the roundup notes, making Cyber Monday the new extension of the practice.

The Cyber Monday sale promised deals on top gifts with savings of up to 80% on apparel, beauty, tech, toys and more, with Mashable promising the best deals from the sale in the coming days.
