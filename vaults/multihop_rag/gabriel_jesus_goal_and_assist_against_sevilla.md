# Gabriel Jesus' Goal And Assist Against Sevilla

Two grand moments from Gabriel Jesus either side of half-time decided Arsenal's win at Sevilla on 24 October 2023: an exceptional assist that broke the game open in the blink of an eye, and a shot that flashed past Ørjan Nyland into the far corner. Asked afterwards which was better, Mikel Arteta replied "both", adding: "I would like to see a replay of the first in slow motion because it was so sharp. He came for a reason: this is our level now and we need players like that."

The opener came at the very end of the first half, when the game had begun to tilt slightly away from Arsenal and an initially timid Sevilla had started to think they could get a goal of their own. Superb footwork from Ocampos had enabled him to get away from Bukayo Saka, nicking the ball through the England player's legs and running at the defence with space opening before him, but Dodi Lukebakio's cross was cleared and Sevilla were suddenly exposed, stripped bare by Jesus. A lovely turn let him evade two players and slide the ball into a huge gap where Gabriel Martinelli was dashing free, alone from the halfway line. After a long run right through the middle, Nyland came to him — the second time the two had met, the Norwegian having denied Martinelli when Jorginho released him after 10 minutes — but this time Martinelli slowed, stepped around the keeper to the right and rolled the ball into an empty net.

If Sevilla were encouraged forward by that, it proved their downfall on the verge of half-time, and soon after the break they conceded again: Jesus received on the left, cut inside Jesús Navas, stepped onto the sweet spot for a shot and bent a brilliant finish into the far corner.
