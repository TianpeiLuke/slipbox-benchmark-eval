# Playoff And Draft Implications Of Patriots-Broncos

Denver's Christmas Eve 2023 loss put the Broncos' playoff hopes on ice at 7-8 — though the team was not formally eliminated quite yet — while New England moved to 4-11 and hurt its own draft positioning in the process.

Coming into the game, New England had played the Chiefs tough but lost at home to fall to 3-11, improving its chances of landing the No. 1 pick in the next year's draft. Denver had aimed to get back to the right side of the win column after being blown out by the Lions in an all-around abysmal performance.
