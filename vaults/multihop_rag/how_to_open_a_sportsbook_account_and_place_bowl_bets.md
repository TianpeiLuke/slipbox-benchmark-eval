# How To Open A Sportsbook Account And Place Bowl Bets

Sporting News outlines five ordered steps for betting on NCAA football bowl games: choose your sportsbook, register for an account, collect your welcome bonus, make a deposit, and start betting.

The precondition is simply having access to one of the online betting sites that have sprouted up across the country, which the article says has made betting on bowl games easier than ever; creating an account with any of the best sports betting apps is a quick process. Step one is to choose your sportsbook — if unsure which is the right fit, browse the reviews for the pros and cons of each as well as current offerings for new customers, then click a link from the site to visit the chosen sportsbook's homepage. Step two is to register: on the homepage you will find a button that says "sign up" or "register," which takes you to a form for personal information such as your name, birth date, email address and the last four digits of your Social Security number, and you will also be required to accept the sportsbook terms and conditions, which the article recommends reading. Step three is to collect your welcome bonus, which as a new customer you will receive; it varies from sportsbook to sportsbook, so do some homework before deciding where to register and read through the playthrough requirements for any bonus or promo so you are aware of any restrictions. Step four is to make a deposit to fund the account — online betting sites offer a variety of ways to fund an account or make withdrawals. Step five is to start betting: click on a bet to add it to your bet slip, adjust how much you want to wager, and then finalize the bet.
