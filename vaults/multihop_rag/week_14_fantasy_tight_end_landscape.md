# The Week 14 Fantasy Tight End Landscape

Sporting News framed Week 14 of the 2023 season as a week that might not matter for many fantasy owners — some had secured a postseason spot, others were well out of contention — but that really mattered for those in must-win situations, where a hole at tight end was the last thing a lineup needed.

Its Week 14 tight end PPR rankings were offered as the fix. Despite two tough byes and some big injuries to notable tight ends, there were several solid sleepers and streaming options. Because it is always tough to know who to trust at this volatile position, the column broke down which tight ends had higher floors for owners favored to win comfortably and which had higher ceilings for those who needed to shoot for big points.
