# PS5 Spider-Man 2 Bundle Deal

A Black Friday 2023 bundle paired the $500 PS5 console with the new and critically acclaimed Spider-Man 2 game for no extra cost.

The PS5 remains one of the best gaming consoles you can get right now, Engadget says, and it found the open-world game to be even better than its predecessor in its review: players can swap between playing as Peter Parker and Miles Morales, and it includes expanded combat mechanics.
