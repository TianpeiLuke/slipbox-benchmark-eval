# Newcastle vs Crystal Palace Preview, October 2023

Newcastle hosted Crystal Palace live on talkSPORT 2 at 3pm on 21 October 2023 on an impressive run of form across all competitions, having knocked last year's treble winners Manchester City out of the Carabao Cup and beaten Paris Saint-Germain 4-1 in the Champions League, talkSPORT previewed on 20 October.

The obstacle was the opponent's recent record against them: Crystal Palace proved unbeatable for Newcastle last season, with goalless draws in all three of their meetings across all competitions. Palace had been defensively impressive that term, keeping four clean sheets, and sat ninth in the table, just one place behind Newcastle, so they could vault the Magpies with a win.

On team news, Newcastle were without Joe Willock and Harvey Barnes through injury, but Sandro Tonali was available despite allegations of illegal betting that led to him being sent home from Italy's national team squad, and despite fears that he could be banned.
