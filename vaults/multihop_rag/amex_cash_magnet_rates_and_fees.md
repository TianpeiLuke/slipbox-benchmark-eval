# Amex Cash Magnet Rates And Fees

The American Express Cash Magnet Card carries a 0% intro APR for the first 15 months from the date of account opening, after which the APR is 19.24%–29.99% variable. The purchase APR is 19.24%–29.99% variable. Balance transfers get 0% APR for the first 15 months from account opening on transfers requested within 60 days of opening, after which that rate and any other balance transfer requests move to 19.24%–29.99% variable; the balance transfer fee is either $5 or 3% of the amount of each transfer, whichever is greater, with a minimum transfer amount of $100 and a maximum that is less than your credit limit but may not be more than $7,500. Cash advances carry 29.99% APR plus a fee of either $10 or 5% of the amount of each advance, whichever is greater. A penalty APR of 29.99% variable may be applied to new transactions if you make one or more late payments or your payment is returned by your bank.

The remaining fees are a $0 annual fee, a foreign transaction fee of 2.7% of each transaction after conversion to US dollars, a late payment fee of up to $40, and a returned payment fee of up to $40. The intro offer gives new cardmembers 15 months at 0% APR on purchases and balance transfers completed within the first 60 days of opening an account — more than a year to make payments without interest.
