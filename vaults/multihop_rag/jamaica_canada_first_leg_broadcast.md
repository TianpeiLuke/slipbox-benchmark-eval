# How To Watch Jamaica vs Canada, First Leg

Coverage of the CONCACAF Nations League quarterfinal first leg was streaming-only across the major regions listed by Sporting News. In the USA the game was available for streaming via Paramount+. In Canada, fans could watch on Fubo Canada, OneSoccer and via CONCACAF's Go app and YouTube channel.

Elsewhere the match ran through CONCACAF's own platforms. In the UK and Australia it was available to stream via CONCACAF's Go app and YouTube channel, as it was in India, Hong Kong, Malaysia, Singapore and New Zealand; no television channel was listed for any of those regions.
