# December 2023 Earnings Week Secondary Reporters

Beyond the headline names, Seeking Alpha's December 3, 2023 week-ahead listed four further batches of companies as "also reporting" during the December 4-8 stretch; the source gives these only as grouped "also reporting" lists, without attaching a date to each group.

One batch comprised Science Applications International Corporation (SAIC), Fusion Fuel Green PLC (HTOO), IDT Corporation (IDT), JOANN (JOAN) and RGC Resources (RGCO), among others. A second and much longer batch listed MongoDB (MDB), SentinelOne (S), Asana (ASAN), The J. M. Smucker Company (SJM), AutoZone (AZO), Stitch Fix (SFIX), Box (BOX), AeroVironment (AVAV), Yext (YEXT), Signet Jewelers Limited (SIG), Dave & Buster's Entertainment (PLAY), Ocuphire Pharma (OCUP), Hovnanian Enterprises (HOV), Designer Brands (DBI), HealthEquity (HQY), Ferguson (FERG), G-III Apparel Group (GIII), The Descartes Systems Group (DSGX), Phreesia (PHR), Core & Main (CNM), J.Jill (JILL), Daktronics (DAKT) and Lands' End (LE), among others.

A third batch listed ChargePoint Holdings (CHPT), C3.ai (AI), Veeva Systems (VEEV), Chewy (CHWY), Campbell Soup Company (CPB), Toll Brothers (TOL), Brown-Forman Corporation (BF.B), Powell Industries (POWL), United Natural Foods (UNFI), Ollie's Bargain Outlet Holdings (OLLI), Semtech Corporation (SMTC), Braze (BRZE), Verint Systems (VRNT), Sprinklr (CXM), Argan (AGX), Sportsman's Warehouse Holdings (SPWH), Korn Ferry (KFY), John Wiley & Sons (WLY), Amtech Systems (ASYS), Couchbase (BASE), Vera Bradley (VRA), Barnes & Noble Education (BNED) and Oxford Industries (OXM), among others. A fourth, much shorter batch named Johnson Outdoors (JOUT) and Canadian Western Bank (OTCPK:CBWBF), among others. Each list is explicitly open-ended — the source closes every batch with "and more" — so none should be read as exhaustive.
