# Pochettino's Assessment Of Arteta As A Coach

Mauricio Pochettino argued on 20 October 2023 that Mikel Arteta is one of the best coaches in the world and could become one of the greatest managers in the world, and that his age is irrelevant to that judgement.

The grounds Pochettino gives are twofold: what Arsenal are producing on the pitch, and the range of skills he sees in Arteta. "What Arsenal are showing is because he's one of the best," Pochettino said. "I don't care if he's young or not young - he's one of the best coaches in the world." He had also recalled that Arteta "was really good with how he saw football. He can be one of the greatest managers in the world."

The warrant is Pochettino's view of what management actually requires, which extends well beyond the tactical: "It's not only about being good tactically - it's about how you manage people, the media, the owners, the sporting director. It's not only about being clever." On that standard, Arsenal's showing is evidence of the whole skill set, which is why Pochettino thinks the Gunners boss is destined for the top.
