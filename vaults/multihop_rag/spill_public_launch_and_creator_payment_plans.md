# Spill's Public Launch And Creator Payment Plans

Spill is set to exit beta and open to the public in 2024, and the app has plans to pay creators for their cultural contributions to the platform — something Alphonzo Terrell frames as especially important for Black creators in the creator economy.

"We don't get credit for all the contributions that we make," Terrell said of Black artists in the space. Despite a robust first year, he frames the story for Spill as just beginning, with AfroTech reminding him of how far the team has come but also how far they have to go: "To be able to make something for the community, it's just really beautiful."
