# Tony Wilson vs Steve McCarthy, 1989: The Shoe Intervention

Tony Wilson was declared the winner of his 1989 British light-heavyweight fight with Steve McCarthy after Wilson's 62-year-old mother entered the ring in a floral dress and whacked McCarthy with her shoe, talkSPORT recalled on 15 November 2023.

McCarthy had scored a third-round knockdown and was looking to finish the fight when, as the pair exchanged haymakers, the even more dangerous combatant climbed in. McCarthy celebrated an apparent victory, but strangely this was not over: referee Adrian Morgan insisted the fight must continue, and McCarthy refused, citing his head wound from the shoe, which would later require stitches.

Wilson was therefore declared the winner — via a massive assist from his mother.
