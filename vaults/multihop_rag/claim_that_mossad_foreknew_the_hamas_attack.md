# Claim That Mossad Foreknew the Hamas Attack

The counter-claim collected by Business Today on 7 October 2023 attacks the premise that the attack was a surprise at all. Some users speculated that Mossad knew what was going to happen but let Hamas make a mistake so that Israel could launch a ferocious attack on it. One wrote: "Mossad was not so ignorant that it did not know about this #attack. Israel wanted to invade Palestine, but they had no excuse, stupid #Hamas gave them the reason. Hamas is an excuse, new lands are wonderful."

Reacting to that, a user posting as timm said that knowing the proficiency of the Mossad he found the idea of a "surprise attack" to be a willing lie. Journalist Ben Muasya framed the same doubt as a question: "Mossad is overrated. How come they never knew Hamas militants planned a sudden attack on Israel? Or was it a trap to get a reason to retaliate heavily? We wait to see how it goes but it will be messy." Another user argued that no one can carry out such a major attack against Israel without intelligence support, asking "Who gave Hamas Intelligence? It was as if the entire Israeli army and the Mossad were asleep."

What these comments share is a refusal of the inference from "Mossad did not stop it" to "Mossad did not know". Where the failure argument treats the outcome as incompetence, this line treats Mossad's reputation for proficiency as evidence that foreknowledge, or deliberate permission, is the more plausible explanation.
