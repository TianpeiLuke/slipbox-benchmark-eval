# 2023/24 Champions League Round Of 16 Qualifiers And Draw Rules

Sixteen teams qualified for the 2023/24 Champions League Round of 16: Bayern Munich, Real Madrid, Real Sociedad, Inter Milan, Manchester City, RB Leipzig, Atletico Madrid, Lazio, Borussia Dortmund, Barcelona, Arsenal, PSV, PSG, Porto, Copenhagen and Napoli, per Sporting News on Dec. 12, 2023.

The draw for the Round of 16 would be made in Nyon, Switzerland at UEFA's headquarters on Monday, December 18, 2023 at 12 noon CET, which is 6 a.m. ET.

The draw rules were that the teams would be divided into group winners and second-place finishers, with each runner-up drawn against a group winner, and that no team could be drawn against anyone from the same country until at least the quarterfinals. Third-place finishers in each group would drop into the Europa League playoff round.
