# Apple's USB-C Accessory Transition

Rumors point to Apple unveiling more USB-C accessories as it shifts away from the Lightning port, with Gurman suggesting USB-C versions of the Magic Trackpad, Magic Mouse and Magic Keyboard will appear at the event.

This makes a lot of sense given the rest of the lineup. Apple ditched the Lightning port in all iPhone 15 models, started adopting USB-C on iPads several years back, and recently unveiled an Apple Pencil with a USB-C charging port.

The driver is regulatory: the change was prompted by European Union rules requiring a standard charging port on certain devices. Having the same port on all Apple devices and accessories will mean consumers have fewer types of cables to worry about.
