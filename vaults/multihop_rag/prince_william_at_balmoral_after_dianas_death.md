# Prince William at Balmoral After Diana's Death

It is true that William and Harry initially stayed at Balmoral with their grandmother following Diana's death, though it is not known whether William went missing as The Crown depicts.

In the drama, after learning of Diana's death from his father at the royal residence of Balmoral in Scotland, William (Rufus Kampa) is left heartbroken with grief and angry at his family's response to it. At one point Charles is informed that the prince is not in his room and that "no one can find him", prompting Charles and Prince Harry to look for him and a subsequent hunt across the grounds for the young royal. He is not located, but later walks back to the house of his own accord, drenched in rain. "14 hours, that poor boy was gone," the Queen later says.

William himself has spoken about that period. During a visit to Scotland in 2021, he recalled the experience as one of the "saddest" moments of his life. "I was in Balmoral when I was told that my mother had died. Still in shock, I found sanctuary in the service at Crathie Kirk that very morning," he recalled. "And in the dark days of grief that followed, I found comfort and solace in the Scottish outdoors. As a result, the connection I feel to Scotland will forever run deep."
