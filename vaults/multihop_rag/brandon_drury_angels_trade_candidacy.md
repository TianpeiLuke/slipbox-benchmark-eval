# Brandon Drury Angels Trade Candidacy

Brandon Drury was ranked No. 20 on Yardbarker's Nov. 13, 2023 list of the top 25 MLB offseason trade candidates: the Halos had signed him to a two-year free agent deal the previous winter, and the righty-hitting infielder turned in a nice season, connecting on 26 home runs with a .262/.306/.497 line across 523 plate appearances.

His $8.5M salary for the following year looked like strong value, particularly in an offseason where the supply of up-the-middle players was thin. The Angels probably were not kicking off a rebuild; they could do a shorter-term retool if Shohei Ohtani walked, or they could simply look to move Drury for pitching while turning second base over to Luis Rengifo and/or an external acquisition.
