# Xbox Series X And Diablo IV Bundle Deal

The Xbox Series X bundled with Diablo IV dropped to $439 at Walmart for Black Friday 2023 — an all-time low for the bundle — reported by Engadget on 19 November 2023 and Mashable on 25 November 2023.

Engadget's framing: the console itself has a list price of $500 and the game is typically listed between $40 and $70, "so this is like getting a $60 discount on the console plus a free copy of the huge action-RPG". It also noted that Target was selling the same bundle with a $75 store gift card for $450.

Mashable's Walmart doorbuster roundup put the same $439 figure as the new all-time low, saying "you're basically getting a free copy of Diablo IV on top of a $60 discount on the Series X (which typically sells for $499.99 on its own)". The only other retailer matching it at the time of writing was the Microsoft Store itself, whose markdown "seems to have popped up recently in response to Walmart's doorbuster": a post on Xbox Wire had previously pegged its Black Friday price there at $509.99.
