# 10 Minute School's Pre-Series A Investor Syndicate

10 Minute School's new pre-Series A round, reported on 2023-10-12, came from a combination of private and government investors and was led by Conjunction Capital. Participation came from returning investor Peak XV's Surge (formerly Sequoia Capital India), the Bangladesh government's sovereign venture fund Startup Bangladesh Limited, CRED founder and CEO Kunal Shah, MyAsiaVC managing partner Sajid Rahman, and several local angel investors.

The mix of a sovereign fund, a regional scale-up program, individual founder-operators and local angels is what marks the round out. Conjunction Capital managing partner Kirill Kozhevnikov framed the investment as an entry into a new market, saying in an investor quote, "This marks our first venture into the Bangladesh market, and we are confident that this partnership will redefine the education landscape in the country."
