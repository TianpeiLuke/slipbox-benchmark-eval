# Cowboys-49ers First Half: Two Kittle Touchdowns Build A 21-7 Lead

San Francisco led Dallas 21-7 at halftime of the Oct. 8, 2023 "Sunday Night Football" game, with George Kittle catching two touchdowns from Brock Purdy and Christian McCaffrey adding a third score. The 49ers started with the ball after a slightly late kickoff (8:23 p.m.), moved past midfield on a facemask penalty and a nine-yard pass to Brandon Aiyuk (8:25 p.m.), survived a near-fumble by Deebo Samuel that was nullified by a Dallas offsides penalty (8:27 p.m.), and scored at 8:30 p.m. when Purdy found a wide-open Kittle in the end zone to cap a well-scripted opening drive by Kyle Shanahan.

Dallas could not respond early. Three plays and two incompletions forced a punt (8:36 p.m.); Johnathan Hankins deflected a Purdy third-down pass with his helmet to force a 49ers punt (8:40 p.m.); Tony Pollard was dropped for a loss to start Dallas' second drive (8:44 p.m.); and after Lamb's first catch of the night, Nick Bosa sacked Prescott to end that drive (8:46 p.m.). Jayron Kearse recorded the Cowboys' first sack at 8:50 p.m., but Deebo Samuel made up 11 of the 12 needed yards on the next play, and Kearse then lined up offsides to extend the 49ers drive (8:54 p.m.). Purdy flipped a 3rd & 1 pass to Kyle Juszczyk (8:56 p.m.) before the drive fell apart on a delay of game penalty and a five-yard loss by McCaffrey on 3rd & 15 (8:59 p.m.). At 9:01 p.m. Pollard was stripped deep in Dallas territory and San Francisco recovered — confirmed at 9:04 p.m. — but at 9:10 p.m. Jourdan Lewis took the ball out of McCaffrey's arms to trade the fumble back. The first quarter ended 7-0.

In the second quarter Purdy hit Kittle again after some trickery at 9:14 p.m. for a 14-0 lead, set up when Fred Warner sacked Prescott to force a punt from the Cowboys' own end zone (9:13 p.m.), giving Warner a sack and a forced fumble. Dallas finally earned a first down after more than 17 minutes (9:20 p.m.), then Prescott completed passes to Brandin Cooks (9:22 p.m.) and a 20-yard third-down conversion to Jake Ferguson (9:24 p.m.) before hitting KaVontae Turpin on a 26-yard touchdown at 9:27 p.m. to make it 14-7. Pollard had just 10 yards on six touches at that point (9:23 p.m.). San Francisco answered with Aiyuk's 15-yard catch (9:31 p.m.), a 19-yard McCaffrey screen (9:35 p.m.) and Samuel's run to the one (9:37 p.m.), and McCaffrey pushed in at 9:38 p.m. for his 14th consecutive game with a touchdown and a 21-7 lead. Dallas' next drive ended on a short third-down pass to Ferguson (9:45 p.m.); the 49ers failed to convert with a low third-down throw to Aiyuk (9:52 p.m.); edge rusher Drake Jackson walked off after medical attention (9:56 p.m.); and Dre Greenlaw sacked Prescott at 9:58 p.m. as Dallas ran out the half, with the Cowboys due to receive to start the second half.
