# Kevin Sheehan 2023 AFL Draft Top 40 Prospects List

AFL Talent Ambassador Kevin Sheehan's top 40 prospects list is his ranking of the leading players available ahead of the 2023 AFL Draft, announced in a press release by the AFL on the Thursday, as reported by The Roar on Nov. 15, 2023.

The 2023 draft would be held on November 20 and 21, with the rookie draft to follow a day later. The list's scope is defined by the combines: Sheehan profiled all 129 players who attended the 2023 AFL Draft Combine or 2023 AFL State Draft Combine the previous month, and his full list can be found at the AFL Draft Hub — so the top 40 is a selection from that combine-attending cohort rather than from every eligible player.
