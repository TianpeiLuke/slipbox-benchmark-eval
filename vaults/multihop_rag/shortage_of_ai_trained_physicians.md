# The Shortage Of AI-Trained Physicians

A growing number of health care organizations are hiring individuals in high-level AI roles, but filling them could be a challenge, according to Dr. Harvey Castro, a Dallas, Texas-based emergency medicine physician and AI expert.

"We will likely see a huge increase in these roles, but may not have enough AI doctors to fill this space," Castro told Fox News Digital.

He put a number on the available pool: approximately 5,000 U.S. doctors have AI and data science knowledge after undergoing formal training in these fields, he estimated.
