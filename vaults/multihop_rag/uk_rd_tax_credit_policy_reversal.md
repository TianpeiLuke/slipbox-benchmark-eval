# The UK R&D Tax Credit Policy Reversal

The second consistency demand is a consistent tax policy, argued from the importance of research and development (R&D) tax credits: one of the most critical things for startups, this was a lifeline that gave small, research-intensive companies months of budget runway every year, as it effectively saw them get a third of the money spent in R&D back.

The grounds are the reversal itself and how narrowly it was contained: the government announced it would be scrapping that last year, and it is only thanks to a lobbying effort spearheaded by the Startup Coalition that, at the last minute, a portion of that tax credit was able to be preserved.
