# Cleveland-Cliffs Q3 2023 Earnings Preview

Cleveland-Cliffs was scheduled to announce Q3 earnings results on Monday 23 October 2023, after market close, with analysts expecting profit to surge by more than 50% while revenue was projected to go down slightly.

The stock carried a Hold rating from Seeking Alpha's Quant Rating system, a more cautious view than the consensus Buy rating from Wall Street analysts. Investing group leader Jonathan Weber said: "Cleveland-Cliffs is well-positioned to perform well over time, thanks to its strong position when it comes to supplying automobile companies and due to energy cost advantages compared to competitors from markets such as Europe."

Consensus EPS estimates were $0.44 and consensus revenue estimates $5.53B. On the earnings insight, the company had beaten EPS expectations in 3 of the past 8 quarters and revenue in 5 of those reports. Also reporting that Monday were Logitech International, Koninklijke Philips, Packaging Corporation of America and more.
