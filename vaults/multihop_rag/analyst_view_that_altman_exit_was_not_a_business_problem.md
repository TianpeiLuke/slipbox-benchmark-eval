# The Analyst View That Altman's Exit Was Not A Business Problem

Forrester analyst Rowan Curran speculated that Altman's departure, "while sudden," did not likely reflect deeper business problems — attacking the inference from a shocking exit to trouble in the company's technology or commercial position.

"This seems to be a case of an executive transition that was about issues with the individual in question, and not with the underlying technology or business," Curran said.
