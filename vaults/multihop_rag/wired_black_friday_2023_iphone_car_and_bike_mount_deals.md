# WIRED Black Friday 2023 iPhone Car And Bike Mount Deals

WIRED's Apple Black Friday guide of 25 November 2023 recommended three discounted phone mounts: iOttie's Velox Pro Magnetic Wireless Cooling Charger for dashboards and windshields, a one-handed clamping car mount, and a Peak Design mount for bikes and scooters.

The iOttie Velox Pro is WIRED's pick for people who prefer a dash or windshield mount and have an iPhone 12 or later. Its suction cup attaches to a windshield or dashboard pad and proved secure in WIRED's testing, and the telescopic arm delivers a wide range of movement for the ideal position. Its stated limits and extras: charging maxes out at 7.5 watts, the USB-C charging cable can be removed once the iPhone is fully charged, and a built-in fan helps keep the temperature down when it is hot out.

What WIRED likes best about the second mount is one-handed use: the arms close automatically around the phone when you press it against the trigger button, and pressing the release bars frees it again. It also has a telescopic arm and ball joint for tweaking placement and angle, and a clever magnetic tab that attaches the charging cable to the back of the mount so you do not have to fish around for it; WIRED calls it super sturdy even on bumpy roads. The Peak Design mount is WIRED's favourite way to attach an iPhone to a bike, a scooter or pretty much anything that is a rod; it sits secure, and it must be paired with a Peak Design SlimLink case for added security, after which WIRED says a MagSafe iPhone will not fly off even over a deep pothole. Other MagSafe accessories from the company are also on sale, including the Car Vent Mount, which WIRED also really likes.
