# Retailer Sales Competing With Amazon Prime Day

Amazon Prime Day has inspired many imitators, and shoppers will find sales from competitors like Best Buy, Target, and the like.

WIRED's assessment is mixed: some of these sales are great, and it flags them when they are, but others are worth holding off on until Black Friday and Cyber Monday — when, it jokes, WIRED will again be the Internet's only website with deals posts.
