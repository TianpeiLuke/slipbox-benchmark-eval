# Claiming Bet365's Two Welcome Offers With Bonus Code TSNCO

Bet365 bonus code TSNCO unlocked a choice of two welcome offers for the Week 15 Eagles vs. Seahawks Monday Night Football game — First Bet Safety Net up to $1,000, or Bet $5, Get $150 in bonus bets — per Sporting News on Dec. 18, 2023.

The precondition is being a new Bet365 customer and using the exclusive Bet365 promo code TSNCO. New customers can then choose between the two offers. Under the First Bet Safety Net offer, a new customer receives a refund of up to $1,000 in bonus bets if their first Bet365 bet is a loss. Under the Bet $5, Get $150 new user offer, you receive $150 in bonus bets after placing your first bet of at least $5.

The code varies by state: use Bet365 bonus code VATSN if you are betting in Virginia and NJTSN if you are in New Jersey.
