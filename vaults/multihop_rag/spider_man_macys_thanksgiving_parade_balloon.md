# Spider-Man's Macy's Thanksgiving Parade Balloon

Spider-Man is the only Marvel character to rate a balloon in the Macy's Thanksgiving Parade: other Marvel characters have appeared on floats, but only Spider-Man has been inflated to 80 feet and pulled through the Upper West Side.

Polygon offers this as the real-life counterpart to the comics, which have themselves dabbled in seeing what Thanksgiving looks like when you are young and arachnid-themed. It adds the parenthetical distinction that the balloon also makes him the only Marvel character to have had his head horrifically torn open by a tree branch.
