# Netflix's Licensed Library Additions, November 2023

Polygon's November 2023 Netflix guide lists a large block of licensed films and series joining the service that month, alongside the platform's own originals.

The films include 13 Going on 30; 13 Hours: The Secret Soldiers of Benghazi; The Addams Family; The Big Lebowski; The Change-Up; Desperado; Downsizing; Drag Me to Hell; Madea's Family Reunion; The Mummy (2017); Paul Blart: Mall Cop; Paul Blart: Mall Cop 2; Pitch Perfect; The Scorpion King; Scott Pilgrim vs. the World; Sherlock Gnomes; Sixteen Candles; The Social Network; Ted 2; Whiplash; Downton Abbey; Harriet; and Lone Survivor. The series and seasons include 60 Days In: Season 5; Love in the Wild: Season 1; Six Feet Under: Seasons 1-5; Victorious: Season 3; Erin & Aaron: Season 1; The Amazing Race: Season 17; The Amazing Race: Season 31; Lopez vs. Lopez: Season 1; Face Off: Seasons 4-5; Team Ninja Warrior: Season 2; Laguna Beach: Season 3; First Wives Club: Seasons 1-3; Million Dollar Decorators: Season 1; Shahs of Sunset: Seasons 1-2; Love Island USA: Season 3; and School Spirits: Season 1.
