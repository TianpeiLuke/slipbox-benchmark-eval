# Hello Group (MOMO) Q3 2023 Earnings Preview

Hello Group (NASDAQ:MOMO) was due to release its Q3 earnings before the opening bell on the Friday of the week following Seeking Alpha's December 3, 2023 preview, having guided to a year-over-year revenue decline of between 10.3% and 7.2% after upbeat Q2 results.

The two ratings systems split, and Seeking Alpha's had moved recently: its Quant Ratings shifted to a Hold recommendation just days before the earnings announcement, while Wall Street analysts maintained a Buy on the stock. Consensus estimates going into the print were EPS of $0.35 and revenue of $413.43M. The Earnings Insight records a strong recent track record — the company had beaten EPS and revenue estimates in seven of the past eight quarters — which sits against the softer Q3 the company itself signalled after the stronger Q2.
