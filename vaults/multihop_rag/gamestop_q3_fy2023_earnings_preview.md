# GameStop Q3 Earnings Preview, December 2023

Video game retailer GameStop (GME) was scheduled to post its Q3 earnings after the closing bell on the Wednesday of the week following Seeking Alpha's December 3, 2023 preview, with analysts expecting losses to narrow year over year on relatively flat revenue.

The two rating systems disagreed, and in the harsher direction than for most names in the same preview: Wall Street analysts recommended selling GME stock, while Seeking Alpha's Quant rating system advised only a Hold. Consensus estimates were EPS of -$0.08 and revenue of $1.18B — consistent with the narrowing-loss, flat-revenue expectation. The Earnings Insight is the notable qualifier: GameStop had beaten EPS estimates in only three of the past eight quarters and revenue expectations in five of those reports, so a beat was far from the base case going in.
