# Flat-Rate Cash Back Alternatives To The Fifth Third Card

There are plenty of other cash back credit cards that can compete with the Fifth Third 1.67% Card, many of which don't require you to have a previous relationship with the bank to secure an approval. Two are singled out as the best to consider if the Fifth Third 1.67% Card isn't doing it for you, and both carry no annual fee.

The first offers an unlimited 1.5% cash back return — slightly below the Fifth Third 1.67% Card — but makes up for the deficit in other ways: 5% cash back on hotels and rental cars booked through Capital One Travel, $200 after spending $500 in the first 3 months from account opening, and six months of free Uber One membership through Nov. 14, 2024 (otherwise $9.99 per month). The second earns 1% back when you make a purchase and 1% back when you pay off that purchase — 2% everywhere, notably higher than the Fifth Third 1.67% Card.
