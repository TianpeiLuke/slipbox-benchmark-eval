# The SSSniperwolf and Jacksfilms YouTube Conflict

The debate over "reaction" content on YouTube has been brewing for years, but a recent incident between two creators refueled the urgency of the conversation, Polygon reported on 2023-10-25.

The debate centers on Alia "SSSniperwolf" Shelesh, a YouTuber with over 34 million subscribers, and Jack "Jacksfilms" Douglass, a YouTuber with 4.9 million subscribers. The two had been feuding online over the past year.

It came to a head that weekend: Douglass said Shelesh showed up to his home and filmed the outside of his residence on Saturday, and Shelesh shared a video of the incident as an Instagram story saying "let's talk like adults," according to a report from NBC News. On Oct. 20, YouTube said Shelesh's account would be temporarily demonetized.
