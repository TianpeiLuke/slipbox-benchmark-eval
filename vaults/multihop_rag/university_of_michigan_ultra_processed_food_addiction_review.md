# The University Of Michigan Review Of Ultra-Processed Food Addiction

A new study from the University of Michigan, reported on October 19, 2023, suggests that a craving for salty chips could actually be a sign of addiction: reviewing 281 studies from 36 different countries, the researchers found that 14% of adults and 12% of children showed signs of addiction to ultra-processed foods, according to the Yale Food Addiction Scale.

That level is close to the addiction levels seen for alcohol and tobacco, noted the study article, which was published in the journal BMJ.

The study suggests that some people's consumption of these foods may meet the criteria for substance use disorder, resulting in the "biopsychological mechanisms of addiction and clinically significant problems."
