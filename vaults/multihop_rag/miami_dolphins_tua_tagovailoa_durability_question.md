# The Miami Dolphins And Tua Tagovailoa's Durability Question

The New York Times was cautiously optimistic on 16 October 2023 about Tua Tagovailoa's physical well-being, arguing that watching him this season had not brought the worry that last season did, while expecting games in which defenses muddy the picture sufficiently to create turnovers.

The grounds are the writer's own contrast between seasons — it was tough watching Tagovailoa the previous season without worrying for his physical well-being, and that feeling has been absent so far this year — plus a forward-looking test: the writer would feel even better if Tagovailoa played a full game against Philadelphia in Week 7. The scope condition is the schedule ahead, since New England, Kansas City, the Jets (twice), Washington, Tennessee, Dallas, Baltimore and Buffalo will all get their shots at him.

An executive raised the separate question of quality of opposition beaten: "Interesting team. They put up 70 on Denver and should have put up 70 on the Giants. No one can keep up with that kind of firepower. But they also haven't really beaten anyone. They were down 14 points to Carolina. Do you think they are coming back from 13 down against Philly? In my opinion, no."
