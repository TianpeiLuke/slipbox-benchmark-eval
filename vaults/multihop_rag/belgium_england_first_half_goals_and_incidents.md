# Belgium-England: First-Half Goals And Incidents

The first half in Leuven on 31 October 2023 finished 2-2: Laura De Neve curled Belgium ahead in the ninth minute, Lucy Bronze and Fran Kirby put England in front late in the half, and Tessa Wullaert levelled in first-half injury time, Sport Grill reported.

Having battled to a narrow home win the previous Friday, 27 October, England began on the front foot with a fourth-minute free-kick cleared by Belgium's Nicky Evrard. Belgium drew first blood in this Halloween encounter after Chloe Kelly was penalised for handball just outside the box, allowing De Neve to curl her ninth-minute free-kick straight into the near-top right corner beyond the reach of an outstretched Mary Earps. England struggled to mount a response as Belgium continued to soak up their pressure and hit them on the counter attack, until Alex Greenwood and Jassina Blom banged heads in the 18th minute as they tried to meet an aerial pass, halting play.

That lengthy delay reignited England's intensity, which paid off as Bronze drew a 36th-minute foul from Marie Detruyer and headed in from Kelly's subsequent free-kick two minutes later to restore parity. Kelly then fired wide in the 41st minute as Belgium were forced to substitute De Neve due to injury, which unsettled their defence; while Belgium's defence adjusted, Bronze's header a minute later from Keira Walsh's cross was fired wide on the rebound by Alessia Russo. England continued to press and got their reward in the 44th minute as Lauren Hemp was unleashed up the left flank by Walsh and found Kirby on the pullback to rifle in beyond Evrard and turn the tie around. England's lead lasted only seven minutes into 14 minutes of first-half injury time, as Wullaert was played through on the counter-attack by Janice Cayman to slot into the far left corner and pull Belgium level at the break.
