# The Last-Minute Digital Gifts And Subscriptions Guide

The Verge's December 24, 2023 guide is built around the category of digital gifts — gift cards, subscriptions, and other things you can buy as late as Christmas Day itself — aimed at readers who have yet to buy a present the day before Christmas.

Its defining selection criteria are personal use and organisation by interest: the list is curated from digital gifts the staff have either used themselves or gifted to friends and family, and it is organized by interests so the reader can find the right present whether their loved one is into the arts, exercise, or something else entirely. The boundary the guide sets for itself is thoughtfulness relative to the obvious fallback — the aim is to gift something more thoughtful than a generic Amazon, Best Buy, or Walmart gift card, even though The Verge grants those are still totally viable options.
