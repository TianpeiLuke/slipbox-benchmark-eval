# Duran SD 12 Barkley: A 37-Year-Old Takes A Fourth World Title

Roberto Duran beat Iran Barkley by split decision over 12 rounds on February 24, 1989 at Convention Hall in Atlantic City, New Jersey, taking the WBC middleweight title in a fight that had the makings of a "passing of the torch" moment for the other man.

The pre-fight picture favoured the champion heavily. Duran was 37 and had not scored a significant victory since an eighth-round stoppage of Davey Moore for the WBA super welterweight title in June 1983; in five and a half years he had posted seven wins against three losses and had looked a shell of himself in a split decision over the unheralded Jeff Lanas in his most recent fight. The 28-year-old Barkley had already conquered one of The Four Kings, spectacularly knocking out Thomas Hearns in three rounds to win the WBC middleweight title — foreboding for Duran fans who had once watched Hearns obliterate their hero in two rounds. Barkley was a natural middleweight and at 6-1 towered over the 5-7 Duran.

Motivated by the chance to become the first Hispanic fighter to win four world titles in as many weight classes, Duran had whipped himself into excellent fighting shape. The ex-champ stunned Barkley with a right in the opening round, absorbed everything the champion could throw, and punctuated an astonishing performance with a pulverizing combination knockdown in round 11. It was close, but Duran took the decision and the championship.
