# Logan Thomas As A Week 6 2023 Start At Atlanta

CBS Sports listed Logan Thomas under "Start Him" for Week 6 of the 2023 season at Atlanta: the claim is that streaming Thomas is worth the risk considering how the Falcons have let up numbers to tight ends, and the warrant is that Washington values the position heavily, Thomas sees the most throws from Sam Howell, and Atlanta's expected heavy zone coverage should produce a bunch of underneath throws for him to take in.

The opponent grounds come first. The Falcons see the third-most targets per game to tight ends, 9.0, which has made them bottom-five in raw stats like total catches and yards allowed, though they float around league average in catch rate allowed (77.8%, below league average) and yards per catch (8.9, better than league average); however, no team has allowed more first-down receptions to tight ends, 17. The column adds the scope caveat that this defense hasn't seen tight ends better than Evan Engram, Sam LaPorta or Dalton Schultz. On coverage, Atlanta usually plays a lot of zone early and sticks with it for the whole game if it works, as in Week 5; in Weeks 3 and 4 against the Lions and Jaguars they played much more man, especially when they needed a fourth-quarter turnover — and because Howell is much less explosive against zone looks, the author expects the Falcons to stick with zone much more this week.

The usage grounds carry a health caveat. Washington throws to its tight ends on 26.3% of its throws, fourth-most in football, but that hasn't always meant a huge target share for Thomas: in Weeks 1 and 5 he saw at least 22.9% of Howell's targets, but he left Week 2 after suffering a concussion, was absent in Week 3, and was not much part of the game plan in Week 4, with a 7.7% target share against Philadelphia — perhaps all health related, and perhaps his Week 5 volume will stick. That Week 5 volume also came with a game-script asterisk: Thomas was a big-time beneficiary of Washington chasing points against Chicago's heavy zone, seeing 10 of his 11 targets once the Bears built a 17-point lead in the first half, with a 24-yard catch on the final play of the half against prevent defense not hurting either. The breakdown lists Washington at Atlanta, the Falcons favoured by 2.5, an over/under of 42.5, an opponent ranked 31st versus tight ends, 8.4 projected points, an 11th tight end ranking, and year-to-date totals of 18 receptions on 25 targets for 183 yards, two touchdowns and 11.6 fantasy points per game. The stated hope is at least seven targets, which is possible; the verdict gives Thomas the nod over Jake Ferguson, Dalton Schultz and Tyler Conklin, and calls it close in full PPR over Kyle Pitts.
