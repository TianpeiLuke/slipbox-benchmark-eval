# The Crown: The Fact-Versus-Fiction Criticism

The criticism levelled at The Crown attacks the premise that a dramatisation set close to living memory can be relied on to read as fiction: as Netflix's acclaimed royal drama moves closer to the present day, the line between fact and fiction feels ever more blurred.

The objection does not deny that Peter Morgan's show has always presented a dramatised take on real-life events. It targets the inference that the dramatised framing is therefore self-evident to viewers — many have argued that viewers struggle to tell the difference between the real royal family and the events on the show.

The proposed remedy, and Netflix's refusal of it, is part of the same dispute: when season five arrived in 2022 there were calls for the show to include a "fictional explainer", something Netflix did not do.
