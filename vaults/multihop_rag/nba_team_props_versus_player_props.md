# NBA Team Props Versus Player Props

There are two main prop bets you can make on the National Basketball Association — team props and player props — and the distinction is whether the wager depends on the team as a whole or on an individual's performance.

Team props, also known as "game props," are typically focused on statistical accomplishments by a particular team and rarely on the game's result, and they don't rely on individual players. Common NBA team props are based on a team's points, such as the first team to reach 20 points, the first team to score in a game, or whether the total points by both teams in a contest will end up being odd or even.

Player props, on the other hand, are driven by individual performance and focus on statistical milestones during a game. The most popular typically focus on points, rebounds, assists and three-point field goals, and you can also bet on combination wagers such as points and rebounds together. The practical boundary is roster depth: in most cases you can bet on traditional statistics for the five starters on each team.
