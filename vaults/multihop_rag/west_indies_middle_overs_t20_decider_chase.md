# West Indies' Middle Overs In The T20 Decider Chase

Adil Rashid took two wickets through the middle overs of the decider chase — Johnson Charles for 27 and Sherfane Rutherford for 30 — but West Indies still moved to 95-4 with the run rate under control.

In the seventh over Rehan Ahmed's first over disappeared for 12: four singles and a two off the first five balls, then a no-nonsense straight six from Charles, taking West Indies to 51-2. Rashid struck straight away in the eighth, accidentally bowling a very wide, dipping full toss that Charles clunked low to Buttler at short extra cover, 54-3. Moeen replaced Rehan in the ninth and rushed through four singles. In the tenth, West Indies knew how dangerous Rashid was and were content to sit on him — the live blog thought that had been Charles' plan too before he saw a low full toss and his brain went rogue — and at drinks West Indies needed 71 from 60. England reviewed an LBW appeal against Rutherford off a Rashid legbreak that had hit him on the pad in front of middle and leg, but it was comfortably missing leg stump and England lost the review. In the eleventh, after 23 balls without a West Indian boundary, Rutherford lifted Moeen handsomely over extra cover for four. In the twelfth England appealed unsuccessfully for caught behind when Rutherford missed a swipe at Rashid and decided not to risk their last review — a good job, as there was nothing on UltraEdge — before Rutherford launched a full ball down the ground for six. In the thirteenth Rehan returned, perhaps hoping to tempt Rutherford into one big shot too many, but Hope, happily playing the anchor role, faced five of the six deliveries as England ran out of time. In the fourteenth Rutherford hooked new bowler Sam Curran for six more in an over worth 11, with Rutherford on 30 from 23 balls and Hope 19 from 28, both playing their roles to perfection. Buttler then brought Rashid back for his final over knowing it was now or never, and got an immediate reward as Rutherford smashed a drive towards short extra where Curran dived to his left for a superb catch, 95-4.
