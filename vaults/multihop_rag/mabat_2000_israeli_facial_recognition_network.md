# Mabat 2000, The Israeli Facial Recognition Network

Mabat 2000 is an Israeli police-run facial-recognition surveillance network that spans the entire city of East Jerusalem, and Amnesty investigators said the Hikvision cameras they identified are at high risk of being connected to it.

The capability question is separated from the connection question in Amnesty's findings. The report identified specific products deployed in the West Bank that come enabled with human and vehicle recognition features out of the box, but Amnesty could not confirm definitively that facial recognition had been activated in all the products identified; its investigators said they were "concerned" that the cameras "are potentially being used together with facial recognition tools."

What makes the connection consequential is what the network does: the vast surveillance system "enables Israeli authorities to identify protesters and keep Palestinians under constant observation, even as they go about their ordinary daily activities."
