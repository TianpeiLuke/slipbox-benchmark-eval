# Will Smith's Response To Jada Pinkett Smith's Memoir Worthy

Will Smith broke his silence on his wife's memoir Worthy on 16 October 2023 in an interview with The New York Times, saying the book "kind of woke him up" and that he had come to realise she is more "resilient, clever and compassionate than he'd understood." He added: "When you've been with someone for more than half of your life, a sort of emotional blindness sets in, and you can all too easily lose your sensitivity to their hidden nuances and subtle beauties." The statement was framed as support for his partner and her success.

Before giving that statement he had appeared to mock the publicity storm surrounding the couple in a video titled "Official statement" posted on Instagram. "So, here's the thing. My opinion is... uh…" he said, before sneezing into the crook of his arm; as he sneezed, the video comically zoomed out to show several different landscapes including lakes and mountains.
