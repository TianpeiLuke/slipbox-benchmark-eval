# Kia EV9 Reservations

Kia has started taking reservations for its EV9 full-sized SUV, TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-22. Reservations are $750 and can be applied to the purchase of the company.

This is one of the first vehicles Kia has allowed customers to reserve in advance.
