# The MEDLIFE Mediterranean Lifestyle Index

MEDLIFE is a Mediterranean lifestyle index designed and developed by Mercedes Sotos-Prieto and her team, using a questionnaire that calculates an individual's adherence to a Mediterranean way of life across three primary components: food consumption, dietary habits, and physical activity — the last including rest, social habits, and conviviality.

The index exists because of a specific analytic gap. Scientists stress that the Mediterranean diet should be understood more as a way of life than a mere dietary pattern, with scientific evidence acquired over the last four decades establishing its benefits on life expectancy, quality of life, and the prevention of several chronic diseases; the challenge lies in deciphering which elements contribute most significantly to improved health. This type of analysis allowed the researchers to dissect the impact of each of the three components, even drilling down to specific items like "hours of sleep" or "socializing with friends," and the MEDLIFE index could also help capture synergistic effects of multiple components that may not be apparent when examining individual components in isolation.
