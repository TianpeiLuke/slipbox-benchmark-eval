# Quviviq's NHS Approval As An Insomnia Medication

A new insomnia drug called Quviviq was approved by the NHS in the week before The Independent's 1 October 2023 report on insomnia. Sleep therapist Dr Lindsay Browning is optimistic about the "exciting" new medication, explaining that it acts in a very different way to other sleeping pills, which "knock you out" but cause people to develop a tolerance.

She places the drug alongside rather than above therapy: "As much as CBTI is the gold standard across the world, there might be some people who need a bit of [medicinal] help as well, a bit like depression."
