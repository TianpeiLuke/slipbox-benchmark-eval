# Germany's Squad For The October 2023 North America Friendlies

Julian Nagelsmann's first Germany squad numbered 26 players and was heavy with faces he already knew: five hail from his former club Bayern Munich, including the critical midfield duo Josh Kimmich and Leon Goretzka, who were instrumental in his setup at the Allianz Arena.

The shape of the selection was unusual at the back. Nagelsmann oddly selected five centre-backs compared to just two natural full-backs in David Raum and Robin Gosens, both left-sided players, possibly indicating a desire to play a three-centre-back system — although it is hard to know for sure, since he preferred a 4-2-3-1 for much of his club managerial career. One of the centre-backs is Mats Hummels, who had not been called in since 2021 and was dropped for the entirety of Hansi Flick's time in charge; his recall came at the expense of his Borussia Dortmund teammate Nico Schlotterbeck. First national team call-ups went to Kevin Behrens of Union Berlin, Chris Fuhrich of Stuttgart and Robert Andrich of Bayer Leverkusen. Up front Nagelsmann had a choice to make between Niklas Fullkrug, prolific in his short national team stint, and Thomas Muller, an experienced veteran still performing at a high level. Injuries to work around included Serge Gnabry, out with a broken arm, and Timo Werner, touch and go at RB Leipzig with a back problem.

Two lineups were projected, both in a 4-2-3-1: the predicted starting XI of ter Stegen in goal, Kimmich, Rudiger, Sule and Raum across the back, Gundogan and Goretzka in midfield, Sane, Musiala and Brandt ahead of them, and Fullkrug up front; and a second-choice side of Leno, Gross, Tah, Thiaw, Gosens, Andrich, Kimmich, Hoffman, Wirtz, Fuhrich and Muller. The full squad list, with clubs, caps and goals, was: goalkeepers Oliver Baumann (Hoffenheim, 0 caps, 0 goals), Bernd Leno (Fulham, 9, 0), Marc-Andre ter Stegen (Barcelona, 36, 0) and Kevin Trapp (Eintracht Frankfurt, 7, 0); defenders Robin Gosens (Union Berlin, 18, 2), Mats Hummels (Borussia Dortmund, 76, 5), David Raum (RB Leipzig, 18, 0), Antonio Rudiger (Real Madrid, 62, 2), Niklas Sule (Borussia Dortmund, 47, 1), Jonathan Tah (Bayer Leverkusen, 17, 0) and Malick Thiaw (AC Milan, 2, 0); midfielders Robert Andrich (Bayer Leverkusen, 0, 0), Julian Brandt (Borussia Dortmund, 44, 3), Chris Fuhrich (VfB Stuttgart, 0, 0), Leon Goretzka (Bayern Munich, 53, 14), Pascal Gross (Brighton, 2, 0), Ilkay Gundogan (Barcelona, 69, 17), Jonas Hoffman (Bayer Leverkusen, 22, 4), Joshua Kimmich (Bayern Munich, 80, 6), Jamal Musiala (Bayern Munich, 23, 1), Leroy Sane (Bayern Munich, 55, 13) and Florian Wirtz (Bayer Leverkusen, 10, 0); and forwards Kevin Behrens (Union Berlin, 0, 0), Niklas Fullkrug (Borussia Dortmund, 9, 7), Kai Havertz (Arsenal, 39, 13) and Thomas Muller (Bayern Munich, 123, 45).
