# Spy (2015): Paul Feig's Espionage Comedy

Spy is Paul Feig's 2015 send-up of espionage movies, running 2h, starring Melissa McCarthy, Rose Byrne and Jason Statham. McCarthy plays a desk worker for the CIA who is forced into active duty when her partner (Jude Law) is killed by the daughter of an arms dealer (Byrne). Around them Polygon lists scene-stealing turns from Statham as an overconfident Bond parody, Allison Janney as McCarthy's sceptical boss, and appearances by Bobby Cannavale, Peter Serafinowicz and Miranda Hart, with terrific action shot by Day Shift director J.J. Perry.

Polygon frames the film as a rare exception to a trend: as the biggest blockbusters in the world have become more and more comedy-oriented, there has been less space for true comedies in theatres. Spy is one that got made, and it became more broadly available to watch at home after its addition to the Max catalog. It streams on Max, or is available for digital rental or purchase on Amazon, Apple TV, Google Play and Vudu.
