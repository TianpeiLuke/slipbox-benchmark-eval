# 49ers Rout Cowboys 42-10 On Week 5 Sunday Night Football

San Francisco beat Dallas 42-10 at Levi's Stadium in Santa Clara, California on Sunday, Oct. 8, 2023, confirming that a 49ers team that had ended the Cowboys' season in each of the previous two years remained a level above its NFC counterpart. Jerry Jones had said earlier in the week that the game would show him how the Cowboys stacked up against the NFL's best; the answer, per Sporting News, was "not very well."

The 49ers carved up Dallas' defense on the opening drive and never let up. Brock Purdy threw four touchdowns in the rout, including three to George Kittle, and Christian McCaffrey scored for a 14th consecutive game as all of San Francisco's offensive weapons got involved. Defensively nobody had a larger impact than Fred Warner, who finished with an interception, a sack and a forced fumble. Dallas' offense looked much as it had in the previous season's playoff exit — uncomfortable and out of sorts — sluggish through the first half before unraveling when Dak Prescott threw three second-half interceptions. It got out of hand quickly enough that Sam Darnold entered the game for the 49ers with more than 12 minutes remaining. The quarter-by-quarter line was Cowboys 0-7-3-0 for 10 and 49ers 7-14-7-14 for 42.

Kickoff was 8:20 p.m. ET, which was 5:20 p.m. local time in Santa Clara. Going in, the two franchises had a long history, but San Francisco had won each of their last two meetings by knocking Dallas out of the playoffs, so a win would have carried psychological value for the Cowboys. Afterward, questions about whether Prescott and Dallas could get over the hump against the class of the NFC persisted, and although their wins had been dominant, Dallas already sat two games behind the Eagles in the NFC East. San Francisco moved to 5-0 with all of its major offensive weapons healthy and its defense living up to its billing; Kyle Shanahan's team would not face a team with a winning record until Week 10, though the Browns, Vikings and Bengals were described as far from automatic wins.
