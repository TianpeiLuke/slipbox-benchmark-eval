# Razer Basilisk V3 Gaming Mouse

The Razer Basilisk V3 was down to $44 — a few bucks more than its all-time low, but still $6 less than its usual street price.

It is the "best for most" pick in Engadget's guide to the best gaming mouse, because its sturdy shape should be comfortable for each grip type and all but the smallest hands, provided you are right-handed. It has 11 customizable buttons, including a "sensitivity clutch" for more precise aiming, and its thumb wheel supports both left-right tilt and a faster free-spin mode.

The drawbacks: that wheel is somewhat noisy, and the whole thing is too heavy for competitive-minded players — but it still tracks accurately and glides smoothly.
