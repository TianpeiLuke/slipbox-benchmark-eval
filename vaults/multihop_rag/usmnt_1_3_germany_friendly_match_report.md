# USMNT Blow An Early Lead And Lose 3-1 To Germany In East Hartford

The USMNT blew an early lead and fell 3-1 to Germany in East Hartford, Connecticut on Saturday, Oct. 14, 2023. Gregg Berhalter viewed the match — quite possibly the most challenging friendly the U.S. would play between then and the 2026 World Cup in North America — as a significant opportunity to learn about his team, and what was learned above all was that there needs to be an alternative approach to the midfield whenever Tyler Adams is unavailable.

The U.S. led through Christian Pulisic's brilliant 27th-minute goal, in which he drove to his right and blasted a curving shot as four defenders converged, with ace goalkeeper Marc-Andre Ter Stegen watching it sail into the upper right-hand corner. Earlier a ball played over the top of Germany's defense to Pulisic was stopped by an offside call that both player and coach disputed. Berhalter was pleased with the pressure applied in the first half: "I think we created some problems. And then just the threat we had a lot in the first half was really good. It put Germany on their heels at times. But to me, it's also about controlling the tempo of the game, and that's what we really need to learn."

Germany's goals came from Niclas Fullkrug in the 58th minute and Jamal Musiala three minutes later, with plenty of blame to share. On the first, Sergino Dest allowed the ball to deflect off his foot into dangerous territory at the top of the box, Luca de la Torre became a spectator with a too-great view, and central defender Chris Richards missed two opportunities to shut down the threat; McKennie, central defender Tim Ream and right back Joe Scally all bore some responsibility for the second. "When you watch the goals back — I had the opportunity to do that in the coaches' office — it's really these fine moments, these split seconds where you need to be well-positioned, not open up any gaps in your back line," Berhalter said. "And that's where it went wrong."
