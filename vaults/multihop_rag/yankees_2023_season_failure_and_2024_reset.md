# The Yankees' Failed 2023 And Their Plan To Reset For 2024

The baseball season for the New York teams did not turn out to be their most successful one, Essentially Sports reported on 2023-10-10: both the New York Mets and the New York Yankees failed to make the playoffs and came under scrutiny as the highest spenders in the league. The level of accounting has been higher for the 27-time winners because of the highest expectations.

The management comprehended the situation and was actively seeking to make changes for the 2024 season — be it trimming the squad and releasing a few players, including superstar Gleyber Torres, they were looking to bring about changes. But given that they are already short on options, the article raises the question of whether the move is justified.
