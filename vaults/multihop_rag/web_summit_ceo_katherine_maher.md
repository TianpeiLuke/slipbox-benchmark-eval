# Katherine Maher Appointed Web Summit CEO

Former Wikimedia Foundation CEO Katherine Maher has been appointed as the new CEO of Web Summit, TechCrunch reported on November 3, 2023.

The appointment follows the controversial departure of its previous leader, Paddy Cosgrave.

Maher's appointment comes as the tech conference faces a challenge to rebuild relationships and trust within the tech community.
