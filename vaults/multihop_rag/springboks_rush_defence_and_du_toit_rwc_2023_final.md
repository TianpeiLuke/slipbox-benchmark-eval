# Pieter-Steph Du Toit And The Springboks' Rush Defence In The Final

Pieter-Steph du Toit, the 2019 World Rugby player of the year, played his best match in four years in the 2023 World Cup final, spearheading the Springboks' physical onslaught with 28 tackles, each as bone-crunching as the next.

The collective effect was the Springboks' rush defence, which caused the All Blacks problems all evening, with Damian de Allende and Jesse Kriel forcing their opponents into errors.

Its most visible victim was one of New Zealand's most dangerous attackers: Will Jordan, the impressive young outside back who scored eight tries in the tournament, had a difficult night and was replaced midway through the second half.
