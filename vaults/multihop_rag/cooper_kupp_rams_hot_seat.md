# Cooper Kupp Rams Hot Seat

Yardbarker's Nov. 30, 2023 NFC hot-seat roundup named wide receiver Cooper Kupp as the Los Angeles Rams' hottest seat: at his peak he was one of the best offensive players in the league, but age (30) and injuries — appearing in 16 of a possible 28 games the past two seasons — had hurt his production.

The immediate grounds are recent form and replacement: he had been a non-factor over the past five games, with 12 catches for 127 yards, while young receivers Puka Nacua and Tutu Atwell stepped up. The warrant is that when a declining, injury-prone veteran's snaps are already being absorbed productively by cheaper younger players, his standing on the roster is what comes into question.
