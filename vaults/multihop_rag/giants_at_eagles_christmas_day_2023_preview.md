# Giants At Eagles, Christmas Day 2023: Storylines To Watch

New York Giants (5-9) at Philadelphia Eagles (10-4), at 4:30 p.m. ET, was the middle game of the Christmas Day tripleheader, Yardbarker previewed on 2023-12-24. The first question is whether the Eagles defense can shake off the rust: appointing Matt Patricia as defensive play-caller didn't change much, with the defense giving up the third-most points in the NFL over their three-game losing streak, 95. Although the unit had reached its lowest point, there were reasons for optimism — New York ranked 31st among teams in points and yards per game that season and had surrendered a staggering 76 sacks, the third-most in a single season in NFL history, and the annual trip to Philadelphia had essentially been a scheduled loss for the Giants, whose last win at Lincoln Financial Field came in 2013.

The second question is whether quarterback Jalen Hurts and the Eagles offense can finally bounce back. The offense had struggled mightily over the last three games with Hurts' ineffectiveness playing a significant role: after averaging the third-most points in the NFL over the first 11 games at 28.2, the Eagles had scored four offensive touchdowns during the skid, three of which came via the "tush push." They might have difficulty throwing against a solid Giants pass defense but could alleviate some woes by running the ball efficiently, since New York gives up 131.6 rushing yards per game, the fourth-most in the league. The third question is whether Giants quarterback Tommy DeVito has any magic left: the Giants may have a terrible offense, but the unit has operated at its best under DeVito, an undrafted rookie and native of Cedar Grove, New Jersey, who had thrown for 858 yards, seven touchdowns and one interception in five games since being named starter, posting the 13th-highest passer rating in the NFL during that stretch at 97.3.
