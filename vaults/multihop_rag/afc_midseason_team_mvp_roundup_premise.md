# AFC Midseason Team MVP Roundup Premise

Yardbarker's Nov. 9, 2023 feature named a most valuable player for each of the 16 AFC teams at the season's midpoint — a team-by-team MVP, meaning the player most valuable to his own club rather than a league-wide award candidate.

Of the 16 AFC team MVPs, five were quarterbacks, and at least two of them should be in the running for league MVP after the season. Meanwhile, a wideout who might be on his way to the greatest individual season at the position was his team's MVP through nine games. The piece pointed readers to a companion list of Yardbarker's NFC team MVPs.
