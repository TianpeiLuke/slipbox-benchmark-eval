# Week 14's Biggest Potential Quarterback Busts

Sporting News flagged Matthew Stafford, Geno Smith and Josh Dobbs as the biggest potential quarterback busts of Week 14 in the 2023 season, all risky against some of the league's better pass defenses.

Stafford (at the Ravens) faced a sound Baltimore stop unit that had entered its Week 13 bye allowing the second-fewest fantasy points per game to quarterbacks. There was a chance the Vikings would opt to start Nick Mullens over Dobbs (at the Raiders) after his debacle the previous Monday night against the Bears, but if Minnesota stuck with Dobbs, his rocky Week 12 outing was a concern against a Raiders defense that had been one of the more efficient pass defenses since Week 5, sitting eighth in dropback EPA. For Smith (at the 49ers), after an elite Week 13 effort in Seattle's one-possession loss to Dallas, fantasy owners might go looking to chase points, but replicating that showing against San Francisco was a completely different ask for a quarterback who had underwhelmed that season.
