# Weaning Off Ketamine And Gradual Dose Titration

"Weaning off ketamine is typically not too difficult, as the medicine has a half-life of three to four hours, and most is eliminated by the body in a single day," said Johnson.

The procedure at both ends of treatment is the same: gradual dose titrations of ketamine -- in which the amounts are slowly increased or decreased -- are recommended for starting and stopping the medication, the expert noted.
