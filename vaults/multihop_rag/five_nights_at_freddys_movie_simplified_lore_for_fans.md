# The Movie's Simplified Lore Cuts Against the "For the Fans" Framing

Polygon's 2023-10-26 guide pushes back on the premise that the Five Nights at Freddy's movie was made purely to please longtime fans: fans hungering for an exploration or expansion of the game series' continuing mythology should prepare themselves to see something more simplified.

As is typical enough for an adaptation, some of the more complicated lore has been stripped out, though it could probably be reintroduced in any sequel.

The consequence undercuts the producer's stated approach directly: this does make the movie more accessible for franchise newcomers, whether Blum wanted that or not. Polygon adds a second disappointment for fans — they might expect more screen time than they actually get for the core animatronics team of Freddy, Bonnie, Chica, Foxy, and Carl the Cupcake.
