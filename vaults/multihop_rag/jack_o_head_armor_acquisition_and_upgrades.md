# Jack-o'-Head Armor Acquisition and Upgrades

To get the Jack-o'-Head armor in Monster Hunter Now's Halloween event, players must first earn 10 Pumpkin Tickets, as Polygon set out on 2023-10-25.

Upgrading the armor then requires further Pumpkin Tickets, which can only be sourced from the Halloween event.

Developer Niantic has said that once you have spent 70 Pumpkin Tickets on Jack-o'-Head armor upgrades, further improvements will then require only regular crafting materials — so 70 is a good number to aim for if you prefer to continue upgrading after the event ends.
