# Quasi-Periodic Pulsations in Solar Radio Bursts

Scientists in 2023 finally identified what causes the mysterious heartbeat-like signals coming from solar flares: studying patterns produced during a 2017 solar flare, they found the patterns were created by fluctuations within invisible fields of electrical current that run across loops of plasma on the solar surface.

For more than a decade, scientists had pondered the origins of these heartbeat-like patterns within streams of electromagnetic radiation that shoot out of the sun during solar flares. The streams, known as solar radio bursts, normally flow non-stop out of the sun, but sometimes there are fluctuations or gaps in the stream, known as quasi-periodic pulsations, which when viewed on a graph look similar to an electrocardiogram (ECG or EKG) of a human heart.
