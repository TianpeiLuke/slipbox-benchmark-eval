# Danny Elfman as Jack Skellington in The Nightmare Before Christmas

Danny Elfman joined Tim Burton early in the development of The Nightmare Before Christmas, plussing up the loose animation pitch into a musical worthy of the Disney Renaissance, Polygon reported on 2023-10-13.

After recording demos for Jack Skellington's big numbers, Elfman became the obvious pick to record the final versions, while The Princess Bride's Chris Sarandon was hired to match Elfman's baritone vocals in the spoken dialogue.

The rest is history: Elfman went on to win awards for his Nightmare work, and the soundtrack became a cross-holiday staple every October through December.
