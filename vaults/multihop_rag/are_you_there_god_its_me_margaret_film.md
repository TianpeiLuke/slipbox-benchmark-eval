# Are You There God? It's Me, Margaret

Kelly Fremon Craig's adaptation of Judy Blume's coming-of-age novel, starring Abby Ryder Fortson, Rachel McAdams, and Benny Safdie, is called a gem by Polygon. It ranked 29 on the outlet's best movies of 2023 list of 2023-10-07 and was available on Starz or for digital rental/purchase.

Polygon notes the book's standing: Blume's novel is one of the most challenged in the country because of its direct discussion of puberty, but it is also a staple of children's literature.

Fremon Craig previously wrote and directed The Edge of Seventeen, so she knows her way around crafting a relatable coming-of-age movie. Critic PR writes that as Margaret's parents, Rachel McAdams and Benny Safdie steal scenes, but Abby Ryder Fortson's earnest and endearing performance as Margaret anchors the whole film.
