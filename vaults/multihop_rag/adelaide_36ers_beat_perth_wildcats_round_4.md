# Adelaide 36ers Beat Perth Wildcats 89-78 In Round 4

The Adelaide 36ers told each other some hard truths before responding in style with an 89-78 victory that handed the Perth Wildcats a third straight defeat, reported on Oct. 19, 2023.

Sitting in bottom spot with one win from five games coming into the match, and having lost at home to the Phoenix on the Thursday night, the 36ers players got together to draw a line in the sand. It worked: their effort, energy and desperation were dramatically improved against Perth, as was the execution and a focus on getting Isaac Humphries more involved inside. Humphries put up 26 points and eight rebounds in a match-winning performance, and coach CJ Bruton's shortening of the rotation to just eight players worked too. "We met as a playing group yesterday and talked through a lot of stuff," Humphries said. "We just wanted to come out today and put into action everything we talked about. We executed what the coaches needed from us tonight and we executed what we, as a playing group, wanted to execute as well."

The third straight defeat left the Wildcats struggling at 2-4 for the season. Coach John Rillie was focused on knowing they aren't playing well and that the improvement needs to start offensively: "We're not playing good basketball at this point in time. An area we need to improve on is our offence. That's where we have to find some type of rhythm and we've yet to do that in these last couple of games. The game's played at both ends and you need to find a rhythm to generate some natural enthusiasm, and we haven't been able to do that for good stretches."
