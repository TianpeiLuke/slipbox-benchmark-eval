# Anker Soundcore Space Q45 Deal

For a wallet-friendly pair of noise-canceling over-ear headphones, Anker's Soundcore Space Q45 was worth a look at its Black Friday 2023 price of $100. The $50 discount matches the lowest price Engadget has tracked and was available at both Amazon and Anker's online store, using the on-page coupon for the latter.

For that amount, the pair's better-than-average ANC, softly padded design and multi-device pairing make for a strong value. Its V-shaped sound profile isn't especially detailed, but it can work well enough for pop music and is customizable through Anker's app; call quality, however, is mediocre.
