# IMF Assessment of India and Global Growth Divergence

The IMF, in its latest update as reported by Zee Business on 14 October 2023, said India is one of the large emerging economies that has been doing better than expected — that it has been doing better for a while now, and is in fact one of the growth engines in the world economy at this point.

The IMF also said important divergences are appearing. The slowdown is more pronounced in advanced economies than in emerging markets and developing economies. Among advanced economies, the US has been revised up on resilient consumption and investment, while the euro area has been revised down as tighter monetary policy and the energy crisis took a toll. There is divergence among emerging markets and developing economies too, with China facing growing headwinds while Brazil, India and Russia are revised up.
