# Amazon Echo Buds (2023) Deal Prices

The 2023 Amazon Echo Buds were $35 — a $15 drop and an all-time low — for both October Prime Day and Black Friday 2023, Engadget reported, recommending them in its budget wireless earbuds guide for people who hate the feeling of traditional in-ear headphones.

Engadget's 10 October 2023 list quoted "$35 $50 Save $15" and noted that Prime members could get the price then. The Echo Buds have a semi-open design that lets in outside noise but does not insert directly into the ear canal; they can sound solid for the price with a bit of EQ tweaking, and support features rarely seen under $50, such as wear detection and multipoint connectivity, with Alexa baked in.

By 24 November 2023 the same $35 price returned for Black Friday, again matching the all-time low. Engadget's caveats are consistent across both: the semi-open design lets in a good amount of outside noise and cannot produce deep bass, there is no wireless charging, battery life of five or so hours per charge is mediocre, the IPX2 sweat-resistance rating is too weak for the gym, and Amazon's companion app is rather bloated — "But those shouldn't be deal-breakers for $35." Engadget gave the Echo Buds a review score of 77 in July.
