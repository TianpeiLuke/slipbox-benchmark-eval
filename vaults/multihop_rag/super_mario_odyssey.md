# Super Mario Odyssey

In a sea of truly excellent Mario games, Polygon says Super Mario Odyssey stands out as the choice for the Switch, because it was released specifically with this console in mind.

The game perfectly mixes classic, whimsical Mario platforming with the expansiveness of a large world. You run around tons of different maps, some of which reference past installments of the franchise, and which include what the writer calls their favorite metropolitan area in any Switch game, New Donk City. There are lots of power-ups and critters to turn into courtesy of the partner character Cappy — at one point you play as a T-Rex — plus hidden collectibles for the exploration inclined. The objective is to collect Power Moons and save the day.
