# Barriers To Truly General-Purpose Robots

Boston Dynamics CTO Aaron Saunders argues that many hard problems stand between today and truly general-purpose robots. Purpose-built robots have become a commodity in the industrial automation world, but the field is only now seeing the emergence of multi-purpose robots.

He names the specific conditions such robots would have to meet: to be truly general purpose, robots will need to navigate unstructured environments and tackle problems they have not encountered; they will need to do this in a way that builds trust and delights the user; and they will have to deliver that value at a competitive price point.

His grounds for optimism are about the field rather than any single technology: there is an exciting increase in critical mass and interest, children are exposed to robotics early, and recent graduates are helping drive a massive acceleration of technology. The warrant tying present work to that future is commercial — today's challenge of delivering value to industrial customers is paving the way toward tomorrow's consumer opportunity and the general purpose future.
