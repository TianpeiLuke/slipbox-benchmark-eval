# American Pie's Billboard Record and Taylor Swift

"American Pie" held the record for longest No. 1 song on the Billboard 100 for nearly half a century, until it was broken in November 2021 when "All Too Well (10 Minute Version) (Taylor's Version)" by Taylor Swift hit No. 1 at a length of 10 minutes and 13 seconds.

Don McLean's recent tours over the two years to December 2023 have celebrated the now 50-plus anniversary of "American Pie", the 8½-minute folk-rock ballad and cultural touchstone about the loss of innocence among the early rock 'n' roll generation. After spending eight weeks on the charts, the song hit No. 1 on the Billboard chart on Jan. 15, 1972, as noted by the History Channel's website.

McLean took the loss of the record graciously. "There is something to be said for a great song that has staying power," he told Billboard at the time.
