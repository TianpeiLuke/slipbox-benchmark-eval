# Merrill Kelly's Seven-Inning World Series Start

Merrill Kelly was the player of the game in World Series Game 2 on 28-29 October 2023, throwing 7.0 innings with 3 hits, 1 earned run and 7 strikeouts — a dazzling performance after which it even felt like he could have stayed in longer if necessary.

The Guardian's live blog stressed how much of the margin was his: despite the final score, this was a much closer game until the Rangers bullpen imploded late, and Arizona's big hero was the starting pitcher.

The outing was also historically unusual. As Tyler Kepner noted on October 29, 2023, in a world full of bullpen games and pitching "openers" it is rare for a starter to get this deep in a postseason game — Kelly became the first pitcher to work seven innings in a World Series game in the 2020s, the last to do it being Stephen Strasburg with 8.1 innings and Gerrit Cole with seven innings twice, both in 2019.
