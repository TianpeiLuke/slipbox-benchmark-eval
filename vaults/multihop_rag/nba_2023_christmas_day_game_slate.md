# NBA 2023 Christmas Day Game Slate

The 2023 NBA Christmas Day slate was loaded, with games in New York, Los Angeles and Miami, Essentially Sports reported on Dec. 25, 2023 — a tradition that predates even the Super Bowl.

NBA superstars including LeBron, Steph, Giannis, Luka, Jokic and Durant were scheduled to take the court that night. The league strategically plans matchups featuring elite teams and players, as these games are broadcast to over 200 countries and translated into more than 50 languages. The Milwaukee Bucks and the New York Knicks got the festive proceedings underway at Madison Square Garden, and the Warriors were scheduled to face the reigning champions, the Denver Nuggets; apart from those two matchups, the league also scheduled Lakers vs Celtics, Heat vs Sixers, and Suns vs Mavs.
