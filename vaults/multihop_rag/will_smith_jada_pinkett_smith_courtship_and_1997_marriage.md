# How Will Smith And Jada Pinkett Smith Met And Married In 1997

Will Smith and Jada Pinkett Smith met when she auditioned for the role of his girlfriend on The Fresh Prince of Bel-Air; she did not get the part, but the two hit it off — even though Smith was married to Sheree Zampino at the time, with whom he shares son Trey. Smith later described the moment of recognition on Pinkett Smith's Red Table Talk in 2018: "I had a realisation I wasn't with the person I was supposed to be with. I was sitting in a [bathroom] stall and I was crying and laughing uncontrollably, and I knew [Jada] was the woman I was supposed to be with."

The pair only began seriously dating in 1995, after Smith finalised his divorce from Zampino. He proposed in November 1997 and they married just one month later, in Baltimore. Pinkett Smith was not initially enthusiastic about marrying at all: she told People in 2019, "I never wanted to get married. But my mother was like: 'You have to get married' — she's so old-school — and Will wanted a family. So I said: 'All right, maybe it's something I should do.'"
