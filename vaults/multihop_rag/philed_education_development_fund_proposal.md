# The PhilEd Education Development Fund Proposal

PhilEd, the proposed Philippine Education Development Fund, is defined by its purpose: its role would be to fund the education dreams of all Filipinos, especially the neediest and most deserving.

The definition is drawn by analogy to a corporate finance role. When Isidro Consunji, President of DMCI Holdings, Inc., was asked by his officers what the role of his Chief Financial Officer would be, he gave a three-word answer: "Fund my dreams." PhilEd is cast in the same terms for the country's education ambitions.
