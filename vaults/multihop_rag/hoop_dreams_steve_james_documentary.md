# Hoop Dreams (Steve James)

Hoop Dreams is Steve James' documentary featuring William Gates and Arthur Agee, which Polygon calls the greatest basketball movie and one of the greatest American documentaries ever made. It was recommended on 2023-10-25 as a free Tubi watch, timed to the NBA playoffs having just started with a bang.

The film embeds itself with two high school basketball stars who want nothing more than to make it in the NBA. Polygon stresses that it is not just a basketball movie: James paints an in-depth portrait not only of his subjects' lives but of the fallibility of the American dream and who gets left behind.

Polygon's Pete Volk calls it one of the defining movies of the nation and a compelling and moving must-watch for all, not just basketball fans.
