# December 2023: Dr Death Season Two, Reacher And Prisoner's Daughter

Dr Death returns on December 22 for a second serve of malpractice and monstrous medical egos — the reviewer still hasn't got over the nightmarish sounds of a spinal surgery going very wrong in the first season. The American true crime drama is now an anthology, with every season covering a different doctor whose abuse of privilege posed a criminal risk to their patients; the new season covers Paolo Macchiarini, an Italian surgeon and researcher accused of laboratory fraud and manipulative behaviour, with Edgar Ramirez playing Macchiarini and Mandy Moore (This is Us) as Benita Alexander, the journalist who got dangerously close to the truth. November highlights on that service included the final season of the groundbreaking Canadian comedy Sort Of, the tight Australian crime thriller Scrublands, and the science-fiction spin-off Orphan Black: Echoes.

The top Amazon Prime recommendation is Reacher (December 15). Star Alan Ritchson, despite looking like an absolute unit in the first season, decided he needed to add even more muscle for the new episodes to convincingly depict Jack Reacher. Adapted from Lee Child's wildly successful novels about an off-the-grid former US military policeman — a role the diminutive Tom Cruise somehow played in a pair of movies — Reacher is an uncomplicated guy who fixes wrongs with two fists and zero concerns; if anything the first season was too subtle, so an even larger Reacher who drops deadpan one-liners while having access to a missile launcher seems promising.

Also on Amazon Prime, Prisoner's Daughter (December 5) is a bet on talent. The domestic drama has a familiar outline: an ageing and terminally ill criminal has his sentence commuted on the grounds that he lives with his estranged daughter, a single mother and Las Vegas waitress struggling to make ends meet and connect her son with a positive role model. They are obviously going to belatedly bond in the face of increasingly fraught difficulties, but how that plays out will be guided by the performances of Brian Cox (Succession) and Kate Beckinsale (Laurel Canyon) as father and daughter, and the direction of Catherine Hardwicke, who found something genuine in the first Twilight movie before being replaced for the sequels.
