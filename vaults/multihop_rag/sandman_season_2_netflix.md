# Sandman Season 2 (Netflix)

Netflix announced in November 2022 that its live-action series Sandman, based on Neil Gaiman's seminal horror-fantasy comic, had been greenlit for a second season. No date had been announced as of Polygon's 2023-10-24 report.

Polygon's timing estimate is that, given the scale of the visual effects involved in the first season, it is a fair bet audiences will not see the second season until late 2024 or early 2025 at the earliest.

Polygon also predicts the content. Because the first season covered the arcs of the first two Sandman graphic novels, Preludes and Nocturnes and The Doll's House, with side stories from the third volume, Dream Country, it is a fair bet that season 2 will continue with book four, Season of Mists — the heavily foreshadowed confrontation between Morpheus, played by Tom Sturridge, and Lucifer, played by Gwendoline Christie.
