# Google Pixel 7a

The Google Pixel 7a was down to a new low of $399 for the October Prime Day sale event.

Engadget calls it the best midrange phone you can get right now, thanks to its speedy Tensor G2 chip, smooth 90Hz display and support for wireless charging.

The 7a also has solid cameras and an IP67 water-resistant design.
