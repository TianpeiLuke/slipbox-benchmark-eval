# BG3 Warlock Subclasses: The Fiend, The Great Old One, The Archfey

The Warlock has three subclasses — The Fiend, The Great Old One, and The Archfey — all unlocked at the beginning of the game. Like a Sorcerer's, the Warlock subclass is picked during character creation, when you must choose the otherworldly patron you have decided to serve; the patron is themed around where your magic comes from and determines some of the spells you can use.

The Fiend Warlock has struck a deal with a hellish fiend who strives for corruption and destruction, giving hell-themed spells and the exclusive feature Dark One's Blessing, which grants four temporary hit points whenever you defeat a hostile creature; its two exclusive spells are Burning Hands and Command. Those serving The Great Old One are bound to a mysterious being from the Far Realm who might not even know you exist — a more Lovecraftian horror theme — and gain Mortal Reminder, which frightens a creature and any nearby enemies after you land a critical hit unless they succeed on a Wisdom saving throw; its exclusive spells are Dissonant Whispers and Tasha's Hideous Laughter. The Archfey draws power from a lord or lady of the fey, a creature from before the time of mortal races, and grants the action Fey Presence, which charms or frightens nearby foes and is the Warlock's only action, exclusive to this subclass; its exclusive spells are Faerie Fire and Sleep. One oddity in the source: the class-selection guide's recommendation line for the Warlock reads "Great Old One is the best subclass for a Sorcerer," pointing readers to the Sorcerer build guide.
