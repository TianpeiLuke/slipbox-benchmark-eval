# OpenAI Share Sale And Valuation Risk After The Firing

The planned sale of OpenAI employee shares that would have valued the startup at about $86 billion was put in jeopardy by Altman's firing.

The Information, speaking to three sources formerly with the company, reported that they no longer expected the sale — led by Thrive Capital — to happen, or, if it did happen, expected it to come with a lesser valuation because of the recent turn of events. Money was also implicated in the causes as well as the consequences: at the time of the firing it was unclear what missteps Altman might have made helming OpenAI, but they evidently related to his relationship with OpenAI's unusual board makeup and corporate governance structure — and perhaps to OpenAI's active talks to raise substantial new capital.
