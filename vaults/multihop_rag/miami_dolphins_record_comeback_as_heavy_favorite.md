# The Dolphins' Record Comeback As A Heavy Favorite

The Miami Dolphins were the only team since 2000 to fall behind by 14 or more as a 13.5-point-or-greater favorite and still win and cover the spread, The New York Times reported on 16 October 2023.

The base is 223 teams since 2000 favored by at least 13.5 points in a regular-season or playoff game. Seventeen of those 223 fell behind by 14 or more, and six of the 17 won anyway. Only the Dolphins both won and covered the spread, which was 13.5 for that game — a new way to win a game, on top of the many that already existed.
