# The Rangers' Early Explosion In World Series Game 4

Texas built a 10-0 lead in the first three innings of Game 4 of the 2023 World Series on Oct. 31, scoring five runs in the second and five more in the third against a Diamondbacks bullpen game.

The game got underway at 8:05 p.m. with Joe Mantiply pitching to Marcus Semien. Mantiply worked a scoreless first: Geraldo Perdomo made a great defensive play to retire Semien on a full count at 8:08 p.m., Seager swung at the first pitch and was caught comfortably for the second out, Mitch Garver worked a two-out walk, and Evan Carter flew out at 8:12 p.m. Andrew Heaney — who had been hit hard in the first inning during the ALCS — answered for Texas, striking out Corbin Carroll at 8:18 p.m. and Gabriel Moreno at 8:21 p.m. as the Diamondbacks made him work, and at 8:23 p.m. Jonah Heim nabbed Ketel Marte on a stolen base attempt to end the inning after Marte's leadoff single, a quick momentum shift back to the Rangers.

Arizona stuck with Mantiply to start the second at 8:25 p.m., which did not work: Josh Jung belted a double into the gap at 8:27 p.m. for a leadoff runner in scoring position. Mantiply struck out Nathaniel Lowe at 8:29 p.m. for the first out and that ended his day; Miguel Castro came in at 8:31 p.m. to face Heim with Jung at second. Heim's chopper moved Jung to third at 8:33 p.m. while Heim was out at first, and at 8:35 p.m. Jung scored on a wild pitch when Castro's pitch in the dirt took a bad bounce away from Moreno with no play at the plate: Rangers 1, Diamondbacks 0. At 8:37 p.m. Leody Taveras walked and Travis Jankowski singled up the middle to put runners on first and second with two outs and Semien up with a chance to extend the lead; the score moved to 3-0 and then to 5-0, and at 8:44 p.m. the inning ended on a nice throw by Emmanuel Rivera after what the blog called a five-run inning for Texas. Heaney notched two quick outs to start the bottom of the second at 8:49 p.m., Lourdes Gurriel Jr. hit a two-out single at 8:51 p.m. to give the Diamondbacks a pulse, and Alek Thomas flew out to left at 8:53 p.m. to end the inning.

The third produced five more Texas runs. Jung hit a one-out single at 8:58 p.m. for his second hit of the night, Lowe laced a single up the middle at 8:59 p.m. to put two on with one out and end Kyle Nelson's night, and at 9:03 p.m. Christian Walker had a chance to turn two but could not hold onto the ball, loading the bases with one out. Luis Frias struck out Taveras at 9:06 p.m. with a chance to wiggle out against Jankowski, but at 9:08 p.m. Jankowski — in an inning the blog headlined "Who needs Adolis Garcia?" — came through with a massive two-run double to put the Rangers up 7-0, with Texas all over the Diamondbacks' bullpen, and the score reached 10-0.
