# Bazella's Mistaken Birth Mother, 1983

At 23, Diane Bazella looked through public birth records and tracked down the woman she thought was her birth mother — and then spent the next 39 years trying to bond with that woman, and with those she thought were her biological family, on the basis of the name that appeared on her birth certificate.

"I'm thinking I found my birth parents and my half-siblings," Bazella said. "It's like the story was kind of over in 1983." The woman welcomed Bazella into her fold, and Bazella was able to learn intimate details about her birth, including that she was originally named Kelly Jean.

The bond never took hold. Looking back, Bazella said she never had a strong bond with the woman she thought was her biological mother, according to those birth records she had found. The woman died in 2000. "She was raising younger children," Bazella said of her. "She had a lot going on. There just wasn't that bond." Of the eventual outcome of the whole search, Bazella said: "I finally feel at peace with everything, which is really nice."
