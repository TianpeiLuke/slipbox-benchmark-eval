# Michael Gawenda's Exploration Of His Jewish Identity

Michael Gawenda's book, published by Scribe at $35 on 3 October, is a powerful, moving and personal exploration by the former Age editor-in-chief — a left-wing secular Jew — of his roots and the meaning of his life in Australia today: "the Jew I was, the Jew I am, and the Jew I am becoming", The Sydney Morning Herald listed on 1 October 2023.

It begins with a frank account of why he ended his long friendship with former publisher Louise Adler over an essay she commissioned about the so-called Israel Lobby, and a public letter about coverage of Israel.

But there is much more in this brave book, the reviewer says — memoir, history and literature.
