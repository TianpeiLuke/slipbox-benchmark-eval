# Jeremy Howe Played Out The 2023 Grand Final With Bruised Ribs

Collingwood defender Jeremy Howe played out the 2023 AFL grand final in pain after a bone-crunching knock to the ribs from Lions forward Charlie Cameron during the last quarter. Cameron crashed into Howe as he cleared the ball from Collingwood's defence, leaving the high-flying Magpie gasping on the ground with badly bruised ribs; Howe got up and played out the match, helping Collingwood hold on to a four-point win in one of the great grand finals.

This was not the type of "car crash" injury Howe has suffered before, but it was brutal nonetheless. He is accustomed to extreme pain, having broken his arm in six places after a sickening midair collision in the opening round of the season — leading into the finals he said the bone had protruded from his arm when he crash-landed in round one — and he came back from a horrific knee injury in 2020. This pain was tolerable by comparison.

"The incident late in the game with Charlie ... He managed to hit me relatively late, it was probably in play, [but I] got a down-field free kick," Howe told Channel Nine on the Sunday after the match. "The ribs are incredibly sore but I think if we lost they'd be sorer. I've got six months to get them right. I can tolerate the pain for now. I bounced out of bed regardless." The injury did not prevent him celebrating Collingwood's premiership.
