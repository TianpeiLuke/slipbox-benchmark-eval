# Certified Copy (Abbas Kiarostami)

Certified Copy is a romance directed by the late Abbas Kiarostami, whom Polygon calls one of the most masterful directors of his time; the film stars Juliette Binoche and William Shimell and was available to watch for free on Tubi as of the 2023-10-25 roundup.

The film follows two people: a French antique dealer, played by Binoche and described as arresting as always, and a British writer played by famed opera baritone William Shimell, who meet in Tuscany while the latter is promoting his new book. That book, also titled Certified Copy, puts forth the argument that there is no such thing as a "copy" in art, because even reproductions are original works. The two debate this idea as the question of what is "real" gets called into question — even the very nature of their own relationship.

Polygon's Pete Volk describes it as a gorgeous film led by two unforgettable performances and the kind of masterpiece that will linger for years, depicting humanity at its most impenetrably complex in a way that lays bare fears and hopes while remaining warm, charming, and delightfully funny. He calls it one of a kind but also in conversation with many other films about love and art — after all, every copy is its own original work.
