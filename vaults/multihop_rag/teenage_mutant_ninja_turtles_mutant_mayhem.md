# Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles: Mutant Mayhem

Mutant Mayhem, directed by Jeff Rowe with Seth Rogen as executive producer and voiced by Micah Abbey, Shamon Brown Jr., Nicolas Cantu, and Brady Noon, asserts itself as one of the most visually impressive animated films of the post-Into the Spider-Verse era. Polygon ranked it 28 on its best movies of 2023 list of 2023-10-07 and listed it on Paramount Plus or for digital rental/purchase.

A modern reboot of the classic TMNT origin story, the film emphasizes the fact that everyone's favorite anthropomorphic reptilian warriors are, well, teenagers, with all the stupid, fun-loving shenanigans that come with being an adolescent.

Polygon's Toussaint Egan writes that from the character designs and lighting to the action sequences and soundtrack, everything in the film pops with its own unique oddball charm that breathes new life into a beloved franchise — a certified banger whether you are new to the Turtles or a longtime fan.
