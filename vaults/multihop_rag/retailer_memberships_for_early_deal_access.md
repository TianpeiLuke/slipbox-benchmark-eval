# Retailer Memberships For Early Deal Access

To get early access to Black Friday and Cyber Monday deals — or in some cases to get access at all — some retailers require paid memberships, and Polygon's position is that these memberships will pay for themselves eventually.

Amazon Prime is the familiar case: it offers a free 30-day trial and unlocks special prices on top of free shipping and other perks. Costco is another popular long-standing example, whose $60 membership pays for itself quickly with holiday purchases, especially if you buy food products in bulk. Walmart Plus costs $99 per year, $50 for a limited time, and serves as a ticket to get in on deals before other Walmart shoppers plus other perks — with an important catch: you will not get early access to deals with its free 30-day trial, since you must be a paid member to earn access, so hit "Skip trial for Early Access" when signing up. Best Buy offers My Best Buy Plus and Total memberships at $49.99 and $179.99 respectively, with Best Buy's own page detailing what each offers and the exclusive deals that can help you quickly recoup the cost.
