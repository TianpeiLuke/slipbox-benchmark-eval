# The Rangers' Final At-Bats In Game 2

Texas went down in the bottom of the ninth of World Series Game 2 on 29 October 2023 with Mitch Garver lining out to third for the final out, sealing a 9-1 Diamondbacks win.

Andrew Saalfrank started the inning hoping to end it quickly. Marcus Semien led off, hit a booming foul flyball, then snuck a ground ball through the defenders for a single — his first hit of the game. Corey Seager followed with a long flyball caught in centerfield, leaving the Rangers two outs from defeat. Luis Frías then came in to get the final two outs, possibly against pinch-hitter Robbie Grossman, and struck Grossman out after an opening ball, bringing Texas to its final out.

Frías could have ended it against Adolis García but fell behind 2-1 — not worrisome with an eight-run lead — and eventually walked him, while Semien took second on defensive indifference, leaving runners on first and second with two outs. Garver's only goal was to avoid making the final out of a World Series game, and Frías helped by throwing two straight balls; but after two strikes, he lined out to third and made the final out anyway.
