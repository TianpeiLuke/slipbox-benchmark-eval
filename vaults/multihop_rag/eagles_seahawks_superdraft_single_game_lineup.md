# The Eagles-Seahawks SuperDraft Single-Game Lineup

Sporting News' SuperDraft lineup for the Week 15 2023 Monday night Eagles-Seahawks game went all-in on both passing attacks: DK Metcalf as Champion at a 1.8x multiplier, plus Geno Smith (1.2x), Jalen Hurts (1x), A.J. Brown (1.05x) and DeVonta Smith (1.25x) in the Super FLEX spots.

The Champion slot carries +50% points. The column carried its Smith-Metcalf stack over to the SuperDraft side, and explained that the lack of a budget allowed it to add Hurts along with Brown and the Eagles' Smith to stack the opposite sideline too. It envisioned plenty of production through the air despite both teams' desire to run the ball.
