# Robin Williams' Cocaine Use At The Comedy Store

Friends interviewed for Vice's "The Dark Side of Comedy" and reported by FOX News Entertainment on 19 October 2023 describe Robin Williams' cocaine use as extreme enough that filmmaker and comedian Mike Binder concluded: "With drugs, he was a monster."

Because of Williams' high-energy performance onstage, many speculated he had turned to cocaine, and some said they would see him head to the back of The Comedy Store, where drugs were prevalent. His longtime friend Allan Stephan recalled a moment that made him worry: "He said, 'Know anybody with any blow? I have to go on. I can't go on without the blow.' And I sat down and said, 'I'm going to help you.' He goes, 'You have blow?' I go, 'No, are you out of your f---ing mind? You're Robin Williams!'" After that, Stephan says, "I think after that he wouldn't get high when he had to perform" — though Williams continued to use drugs after his stand-up shows. "When you did have that kind of fame, drugs were just given to you," Gina Hecht noted.

Binder recalled Williams' reaction to "a gram of coke" while the two were out in Hollywood in the '80s: "Robin said, 'Let me take that. Do you mind if I grab a hit off that in the bathroom?' He came back, and it was empty. It was like, 'Whoa.' It was like 8:15 at night." Called out for taking the entire gram, Williams responded, according to Binder: "It was an accident, I'm sorry."
