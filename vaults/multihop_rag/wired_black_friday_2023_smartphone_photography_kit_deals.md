# WIRED Black Friday 2023 Smartphone Photography Kit Deals

WIRED's Apple Black Friday guide of 25 November 2023 recommended two discounted kits for shooting content on a phone: a Lume Cube all-in-one creator bundle and Peak Design's Creator Kit of mounting hardware.

The Lume Cube kit ships with a Lume Cube light, a compact tripod that opens up to 55 inches in length, a shotgun mic with a windscreen and a universal phone mount with clips to attach all of those accessories. WIRED presents it as a great and convenient all-in-one solution for anyone who shoots content with a smartphone.

The Peak Design Creator Kit plays a different role: it acts as the link between your phone and various mounting solutions. It comes with a magnetic locking mount that works with the Peak Design SlimLink phone case, plus several mounting options including a Peak Design Arca quick-release plate, a GoPro mount adapter and a 1/4-inch tripod mount, so you can mount the phone on almost any tripod. WIRED notes that a phone in the SlimLink case slots easily into the mount and stays secure.
