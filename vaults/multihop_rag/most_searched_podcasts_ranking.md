# The Most-Searched Podcasts Ranking

PodBam released a list of the 40 most-searched podcasts, and The Joe Rogan Experience was number one, averaging 135,000 monthly searches on Google. Call Her Daddy followed with 106,000 and This American Life with 81,000. The Verge, reporting the list on October 25th, 2023, noted it was no surprise that Rogan came first.
