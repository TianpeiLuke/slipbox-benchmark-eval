# Player Injury Report

## Definition

A player injury report is the periodic status listing an NFL team publishes during game week (and updates live on gameday) that tags each hurt or ailing player with a practice participation note and a game-status designation such as questionable, doubtful, or out, so broadcasters and fantasy managers know who is likely to play.

## Context

The corpus shows the report operating on two timescales: the pregame weekly cycle and the in-game live update. In the weekly cycle, CBS Sports' Week 13 injury roundup (doc_0289) and Sporting News' Week 10 fantasy injury updates (doc_0288) track players through the week's practice sessions — a player who is a full participant, limited participant, or a "DNP" (did not practice) on Wednesday, Thursday, and Friday accumulates a running designation that the team finalizes before kickoff. Ja'Marr Chase's Week 10 status is a clear case: he was listed as a DNP on Wednesday, logged back-to-back limited practices, and his final active call was left until a Sunday morning pre-game workout (doc_0288). During the game itself, CBS Sports' live-blog roundup shows teams issuing real-time in-game designations — "questionable to return," "doubtful to return," "ruled out" — when a player is hurt mid-contest, as with Cooper Kupp's ankle injury and Kenneth Walker III's oblique injury in the Week 11 roundup (doc_0113). The designations also interact with roster mechanics: a player can be activated off injured reserve to play (doc_0035) or, conversely, be placed on injured reserve, as De'Von Achane was during Week 6 (doc_0149).

## Key Characteristics

- **Tiered designations** — the standard tags observed in the corpus are full participant/limited/DNP during the week, collapsing into questionable, doubtful, or out for the final pregame report (doc_0289, doc_0288).
- **Live in-game updates** — beyond the pregame report, teams issue real-time calls like "questionable to return" or "ruled out for the remainder of the contest" when a player is injured during the game itself (doc_0113).
- **Roster-status linkage** — the report intersects with injured reserve moves: players can be activated off IR to play (doc_0035) or placed on IR after a lingering issue (doc_0149).
- **Coach commentary supplements the tag** — reporters attach head-coach quotes (e.g., Achane is "day-to-day"; it would be "a little aggressive" for Jefferson to play) to the bare designation to give fantasy audiences more color than the tag alone conveys (doc_0113, doc_0288).
- **Deadline-driven finality** — some designations resolve only hours before kickoff, e.g. Justin Jefferson needing activation by a Saturday 4 p.m. ET deadline to be eligible (doc_0288).
