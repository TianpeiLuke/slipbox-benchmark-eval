# NFL Week 11 2023: Tyreek Hill Returns From A Hand Injury, Darius Slayton Does Not

CBS Sports' Week 11 injury roundup of 2023-11-19 records two wide receivers going down with contrasting outcomes. Tyreek Hill of the Dolphins briefly left Miami's game against the Raiders while nursing a hand injury and was ruled questionable to return, but he took the field again soon afterward, securing seven catches for 80 yards and a score before halftime. The roundup notes the pattern that Hill has left multiple games that year, only to return in all of them.

Darius Slayton of the Giants had a big impact against the Commanders, hauling in a deep score from Tommy DeVito, before exiting with an arm injury. He was ruled out for the remainder of the contest halfway through the third quarter.
