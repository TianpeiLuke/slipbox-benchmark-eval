# The 49ers Trade For Chase Young At The 2023 Deadline

San Francisco traded a 2024 third-round pick to the Commanders for pass rusher Chase Young ahead of the 2023 NFL trade deadline, reuniting him with his college teammate and fellow No. 2 pick Nick Bosa.

Young was among the most notable players dealt ahead of the deadline. The No. 2 pick in the 2020 NFL Draft was in the midst of a breakout season, totaling five sacks in eight games and generating a solid 18 pressures while getting into the backfield often for Washington. As the Commanders jettisoned their top two defensive ends — Young and Montez Sweat — the 49ers found themselves positioned to pounce.

The Sporting News framed the move alongside the previous year's splash, when San Francisco made the biggest move ahead of the 2022 deadline by acquiring running back Christian McCaffrey from the Panthers. Young should provide a boost to a 49ers defense that ranked just 22nd that season in sacks per game at 2.3, but many were wondering whether he would end up being a rental or more of a long-term investment.
