# Canelo vs Charlo: Sporting News' Round-By-Round Unofficial Scorecard

Sporting News' live round-by-round coverage of Canelo Alvarez versus Jermell Charlo for Canelo's undisputed super middleweight championship, on 2023-09-30, gave every round to Canelo and closed at 120-107 on its unofficial scorecard. Round-by-round coverage began at 11:34 p.m. when the main event was announced as next. Round One: the two circled each other with no punches thrown for a minute; Canelo missed a huge body punch, closed the distance with still no shots landed by either fighter, then landed a short left hook, while Charlo began pumping the jab to back Canelo off — nothing but pressure and not a whole lot else. Scorecard 10-9 Canelo. Round Two: a hard right hand landed for Canelo, who backed Charlo into the ropes and landed a body shot; Charlo continued to jab and landed a body shot, Canelo answered with a right hook to the body and bounced a body shot and a hook off Charlo, with Charlo giving him nothing to respect. Scorecard 10-9 Canelo, 20-18. Round Three: Charlo opened with jabs that did not land as Canelo pressured him into the ropes without a punch thrown, added another right to the body, and rocked Charlo with a straight right; Charlo's uppercut was his best punch but not enough to win the round. Scorecard 10-9 Canelo, 30-27. Round Four: the writer did not understand Charlo's strategy — Canelo walked through the jab, nearly took his head off with a hook, banged away with no concern about what was coming back, landed a hard right hand and a left to the body, tenderizing the body with hooks while Charlo gave rounds away. Scorecard 10-9 Canelo, 40-36. Round Five: more body work started to wear on Charlo, who landed a combination and went to the body before looking content to survive; Canelo's combination moved him around the ring. Scorecard 10-9 Canelo, 50-45.

Round Six: Charlo did nothing but let Canelo bully him around the ring, taking a hard left hook to the body and ducking into an uppercut as the round ended. Scorecard 10-9 Canelo, 60-54. Round Seven: Charlo opened up with some offense, but Canelo lit him up with a body shot and a hook and down went Charlo; Charlo got up, Canelo looked for the finisher, Charlo landed a left hook trying to get away, and Canelo stalked him and landed a left hook. Scorecard 10-8 Canelo, 70-62. Round Eight: a hard jab opened the round, Canelo kept sinking in body shots, Charlo landed a right hand and Canelo trotted forward, more body shots and a landed combination as Canelo tore up the body — one-way traffic. Scorecard 10-9 Canelo, 80-71. Round Nine: Canelo continued to pressure and ripped Charlo with a short uppercut; Charlo missed a combination, landed a jab, and took another combination — getting ridiculous. Scorecard 10-9 Canelo, 90-80. Round Ten: Canelo popped Charlo with a pair of jabs, landed an uppercut and a wicked left to the body, barely missed an uppercut, and Charlo landed a combination that meant nothing — if you really wanted to find a round to give Charlo you could give him this one. Scorecard 10-9 Canelo, 100-89. Round Eleven: more of the same, jabbing and looking for one big shot, more body punches, with Charlo appearing to have checked out of winning and checked into the survival hotel. Scorecard 10-9 Canelo, 110-98. Round Twelve: Canelo chopped Charlo with a short right and landed an uppercut to the body as more people headed for the exit; Charlo landed a left hook and continued to throw hands but ate a body shot, and Canelo looked upset as the fight ended. Scorecard 10-9 Canelo, 120-107.
