# Supercars Drivers In 2023 NASCAR Races

Two drivers who featured in both the 2023 NASCAR season and Autosport's Top 50 Drivers list were Supercars racers Shane van Gisbergen and Brodie Kostecki, Essentially Sports reported on Dec. 21, 2023.

While the pair battled for the championship in the Australian racing circuit, they also raced at the Indianapolis road course race earlier that season. Van Gisbergen had earlier created history by winning on his NASCAR debut at the inaugural Chicago Street Race.
