# The K.O. II Super Segment Hybrid Display

Teenage Engineering calls the K.O. II's screen a "Super segment hybrid display": its icons are fixed rather than made up of pixels, fusing a digital-watch-style segment readout with a bunch of colorful, cute custom icons that light up to tell you when certain modes or features are activated.

Engadget's reviewer compared it to the old Game & Watch handhelds, where you can see where all the icons are and they are simply switched on or off as needed; the K.O. II springs to life with a flourish of those icons across its display once the fader is checked and the batteries are in. Some of the icons are pretty abstract, but there is a guide on Teenage Engineering's website explaining them — for example, the red umbrella means undo.
