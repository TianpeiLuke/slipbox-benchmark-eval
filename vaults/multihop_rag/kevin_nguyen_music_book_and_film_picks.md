# Kevin Nguyen's Music, Book And Film Picks

Asked to share a few things he is into right now, Kevin Nguyen picked a record, a novel and a film.

The record is Surround by Hiroshi Yoshimura, a recently released ambient album originally made to be the music of prefab homes. It reveals so much on closer listen — shimmering drone, swirls of pleasing synths, a touch of humor — but if you are lazy, as he is most days, you can just put it on while you work and let it wash over you.

The book is Same Bed Different Dreams by Ed Park. He is halfway through Park's long-awaited second novel, and so far it is a funny, genre-busting saga deeply obsessed with Korean history — for anyone wanting a big, immersive read who is usually daunted by a big, immersive book.

The film is Anatomy of a Fall, a courtroom thriller he reviewed when it premiered at the New York Film Festival and which you can now catch in select cities: a dark, twisty murder mystery that surrenders itself to a much richer set of questions than your usual whodunit, and the best thing in theaters at the moment. In general, he recommends going to the theater — replace your screen time with the biggest-possible-screen time.
