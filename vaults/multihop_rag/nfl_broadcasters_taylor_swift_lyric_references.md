# NFL Broadcasters' Taylor Swift Lyric References

On 17 September 2023 Travis Kelce scored a touchdown against the Jaguars, prompting CBS announcer Ian Eagle to say "Kelce finds a blank space for the score" — a reference to Swift's 2014 hit Blank Space — and NFL Network's Rich Eisen followed with a slew of lyric references in a preshow, The Age reported on 26 September 2023.

The suspicion that Swift and Kelce were spending time together in New York was seemingly confirmed in September when it was reported they had been "quietly hanging out" for months — the first, albeit tentative, confirmation from the media rather than from amateur Swiftie sleuths on social media. It was only a matter of time before the story entered the cultural zeitgeist, and it did so very quickly. Eisen's preshow references included "shake it off", "anti-hero" and "all too well", and Kelce acknowledged the tongue-in-cheek banter, commenting on a montage video of the preshow that Eisen posted to Instagram: "Well played Rich … Well played."

Suddenly football fans and Swifties were living through this surprising collision — Swift lyrics were infiltrating NFL game commentary, and her fans were furiously becoming football experts. All that needed to happen, the timeline says, was for Swift herself to infiltrate a game, determining once and for all whether there was anything to the rumours.
