# Jonathan India Reds Trade Candidacy

Jonathan India was ranked No. 11 on Yardbarker's Nov. 13, 2023 list of the top 25 MLB offseason trade candidates because the Reds' infield surplus had fueled trade speculation on him going back to the deadline.

Cincinnati had not seemed inclined to make that move midseason, pointing to India's role as a clubhouse leader. While that was certainly still a factor, he did not hit well down the stretch and finished the year with a middling .244/.338/.407 batting line, and he was a well below-average keystone defender. There was an argument the Reds were better off relying on an up-the-middle tandem of Matt McLain and either Noelvi Marte or Elly De La Cruz, and that the offseason was a better time to consider moving India for a back-end starter. The former Rookie of the Year was projected for a $3.7M salary and had three seasons of remaining arbitration control.
