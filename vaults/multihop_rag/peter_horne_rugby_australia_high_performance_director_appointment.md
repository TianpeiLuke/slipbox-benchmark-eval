# Peter Horne's Appointment As Rugby Australia High-Performance Director

Rugby Australia was moving closer to naming World Rugby's director of high-performance Peter Horne as its new director of high-performance, a move The Roar reported on 11 December 2023 could also help land highly-regarded coach Joe Schmidt for the vacant Wallabies job.

The Roar had reported the previous week that Horne emerged as the leading candidate, and it is believed he edged out former Sydney University, Australian Sevens and Harlequins director of rugby Billy Millard for the role. The finer points of the negotiations were being worked out, with an announcement likely over the following week once Horne wrapped up a crucial series of meetings with World Rugby — he was leading World Rugby meetings with tier-two nations that week in Sydney, with former Fiji coach Simon Raiwalui in attendance before officially joining World Rugby. His appointment reflects Rugby Australia's desire to go down an administrative path rather than choose a former coach of any standing, as Millard would have offered, and Horne will sit at the top of the high-performance tree with the next Wallabies coach reporting into him.

His background is given in some detail. Horne joined World Rugby in 2015 as a general manager of high-performance before moving into his current role, and during that tenure the Sydneysider played an important role in setting up competitions, high-performance structures and pathways across developing countries. He also played a role in helping Super Rugby integrate Fiji Drua and Moana Pasifika into the competition. Those successes played out at the recent Rugby World Cup, with Chile, Fiji, Georgia and Portugal delivering encouraging performances that positively reflected World Rugby's desire to spread and build the game. He briefly worked at Saracens as a general manager.
