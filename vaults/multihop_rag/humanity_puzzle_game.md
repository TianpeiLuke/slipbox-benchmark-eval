# Humanity (Puzzle Game)

Humanity, developed by tha for PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, and Windows PC, is ranked 24 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31. It looks like a modern art project, recalls memories of early PlayStation oddity, and comes in part from the team behind Rez Infinite.

Its most impressive feat, critic ML writes, is the way it gradually shifts genres: starting as a classic puzzle game, then taking on tower defense and shoot-'em-up traits as it evolves into an all-out war.

The picture he offers is Braveheart, with a shiba inu general leading a troop of faceless, brainless low-polygon figures against a group of angry, lightsaber-wielding, even lower-polygon foes — the kind of design subtlety that stays in its lane yet builds on you, and before you realize it ends very differently than it began.
