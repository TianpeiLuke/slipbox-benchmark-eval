# Melbourne United Beat Illawarra 96-84 In Round 5

Chris Goulding fired off a season-high seven three-pointers — more than any other player in a game that season — to steer NBL ladder leaders Melbourne United to a 96-84 win over the Illawarra Hawks at John Cain Arena, finishing with 33 points.

Melbourne operated without injured guards Ian Clark (hamstring) and Matthew Dellavedova (concussion), and suffered a first-half scare when Luke Travers was forced off after a knock to his left knee as visiting Illawarra surged as much as 10 points clear in a high-scoring first half. Goulding's hot hand kept United in the contest with 21 first-half points including five three-pointers, and the team's tough defence in the second half carried them home. Shea Ili (17 points) and Tanner Krebs (14) also reached double figures. The result extended United's winning streak to six games for an 8-1 record on top of the ladder.

Import forward Gary Clark (24 points) and Justin Robinson (20) tried to lift Illawarra, but Tyler Harvey managed just 10 points three days after his clutch three-pointer sealed a thrilling win over Cairns. In a sloppy start United committed five turnovers in the first period and trailed at quarter-time for the first time that season, with Clark's eight points on perfect shooting leading Illawarra to a 29-24 advantage; his tally swelled to 16 by the main break and the Hawks' lead reached double figures soon after United temporarily lost Travers, before Goulding caught fire to give United a 52-51 lead at halftime.

Defence was key in the second half as both sides tightened up, with Melbourne holding the visitors to just 15 points in the final period. Travers returned after halftime to finish with six points and a game-high 11 rebounds, while Ariel Hukporti retained his spot in Melbourne's starting five ahead of Jo Lual-Acuil Jr and impressed with seven points and 10 rebounds, four of them on the offensive end.
