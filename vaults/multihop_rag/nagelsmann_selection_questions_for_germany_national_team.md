# The Selection Questions Facing Nagelsmann With Germany

The Sporting News argues that the biggest question raised by Julian Nagelsmann's first 26-man Germany squad is which formation he can actually play, because there are no natural right-backs on the roster.

The grounds are that every available fix costs something: he will either have to pigeonhole a centre-back — Antonio Rudiger or Niklas Sule — out wide, sacrifice Josh Kimmich's presence in midfield to move him back, or move Pascal Gross back, and none of those are ideal options. The warrant limiting the damage is that the shape matters less than the method: whether Nagelsmann deploys a 4-2-3-1 or a 3-2-4-1, the tactical approach will likely remain the same.

Two further selection tasks follow. Getting the best out of young superstar Jamal Musiala will be one of the most important aspects of Nagelsmann's tenure, as Musiala has blossomed into a world-class talent at Bayern Munich but the 20-year-old has just one goal in 23 international caps to this point. Nagelsmann will also look to introduce a few overlooked players, starting with Robert Andrich, who could factor into the midfield setup as a traditional No. 6, especially as Ilkay Gundogan enters the twilight of his career — and he will want to raise Kimmich's level of play back to old heights, as the ever-present Bayern star has seen his level drop in recent years for both club and country but remains a special talent.
