# Echo Frames Audio Leakage In Quiet Spaces

The third-gen Echo Frames leak a fair amount of audio, and the leakage only becomes a problem in quiet places: no one asked The Verge's reviewer to turn things down while commuting, because trains and city noises are loud, but at the same volume in the office all of their co-workers could hear that they were listening to something.

Co-workers might not have been able to discern the song, but it was loud enough to be distracting. The review's caption states the finding plainly: audio definitely leaks on these in quieter spaces.
