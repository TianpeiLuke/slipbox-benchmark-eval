# Beats Studio Pro Headphones

The Beats Studio Pro went on sale for $180, down from $350 — a new low and a sizable $170 discount. Two days later, after Prime Day officially ended, they were still $180, a dollar more than their Prime Day price and still a $170 discount.

Engadget gave these wireless over-ears a review score of 81 when they came out in July, and cannot recommend them over the top picks in its headphones guide — they did not push any of those picks off their pedestals. They are not bad, though: they offer a pleasant, even-handed sound, helpful ANC, and the ability to stream higher-quality audio over a USB-C connection. Like most Beats headphones they also play nice with Android, even though Beats is now owned by Apple.

The drawbacks are multi-device pairing not working with iOS, no wear detection, and a mostly plastic design that is not super premium. But at this price you may not care, and the Studio Pro could make sense if you really dig the Beats aesthetic.
