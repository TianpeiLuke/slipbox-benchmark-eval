# Possessor (Brandon Cronenberg)

Possessor is Brandon Cronenberg's psychological horror film, which Polygon's critic came to after Infinity Pool on the insistence of friends and calls an unforgettable entry into the psychological horror canon. It was the Oct. 27 pick in the 2023-10-10 Halloween Countdown, listed on Hulu.

The critic's route to it is part of the recommendation: his first Brandon Cronenberg — yes, David's son — movie was Infinity Pool, the director's latest messed-up meditation on the rich and powerful, and when he told friends how exciting and debauched it was, their response was that clearly he had not seen Possessor. He rectified that the same night and found it was all that and more, and has not been able to get it out of his brain since, which he notes is fitting given the movie.

Andrea Riseborough, of Mandy and To Leslie, plays an elite corporate assassin able to inhabit other people's bodies, getting closer to her victims by posing as people already in their lives. Critic Pete Volk calls it a haunting premise the film takes full advantage of, both in the moments of action and in the assassin's contemplation about what her identity truly even is, and one that brings a little sci-fi into Halloween celebrations.
