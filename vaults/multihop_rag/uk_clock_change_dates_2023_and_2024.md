# UK Clock Change Dates For 2023 And 2024

The UK clocks went backwards one hour at 2am on Sunday 29 October 2023, giving an extra hour in bed as the country prepared for the colder months when the sun sets earlier and the days get significantly shorter. "Winter is coming," as The Independent put it on 24 October 2023.

The clocks were not due to go forward again until Sunday 31 March 2024.
