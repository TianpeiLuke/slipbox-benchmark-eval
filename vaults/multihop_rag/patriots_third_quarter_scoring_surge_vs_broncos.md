# The Patriots' Third-Quarter Surge Against Denver

New England turned a one-score deficit into a 23-7 lead during the third quarter of the Christmas Eve 2023 game with three scores in roughly half an hour of clock time.

The live blog logged the run: at 9:54 p.m. Ezekiel Elliott took it up the middle to the Denver 28; at 9:59 p.m. Elliott took it in for a 15-yard touchdown, complete with some hurdling action, to put the Patriots on top; at 10:01 p.m. Courtland Sutton was ruled out for the Broncos with a concussion; at 10:29 p.m. Bailey Zappe hit Mike Gesicki for an 11-yard touchdown; and at 10:31 p.m. Cody Davis recovered a fumble for a 1-yard touchdown.
