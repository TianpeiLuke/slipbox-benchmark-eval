# United's Shot Dominance In The Win Over Chelsea

Manchester United's December 2023 win over Chelsea felt a lot tighter than it really was: United hung on inexplicably at the end despite enjoying 28 shots and an xG tally of 4.07.

For long periods United played some of their most authentically thrilling football of the season, a doctrine of relentless attack that had Diogo Dalot flying forward from right-back even as the minutes ticked down. It was also a game that felt more chaotic than it really was — the slippery, slingy nature of the play was largely by design, with United hollowing out their midfield to throw bodies forward and Chelsea hollowing out theirs to leave men up for the breakaway. It was a game that hung on United's determination to keep allowing Chelsea counterattacks and Chelsea's determination to keep messing them up, and above all on the curious main character energy of Scott McTominay.
