# Sample Size Critique of the Jellyfish Learning Result

Björn Brembs, who studies the nervous systems of animals at the University of Regensburg in Germany, views the results more cautiously, pointing to the small number of jellyfish tested — attacking the inference from a handful of animals to a general claim that jellyfish learn.

"I want this to be true, as it would be so very cool," he says of the results. But it will take tests with more jellyfish to convince him that the animals really do learn.
