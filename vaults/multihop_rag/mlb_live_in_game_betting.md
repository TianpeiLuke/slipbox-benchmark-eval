# MLB Live In-Game Betting

Live betting means you can bet on a Major League Baseball game in progress, right down to what the next batter will do with the next pitch or which team will win the inning, and the best sports betting apps offer it. It is available at top betting sites such as DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM and Caesars.

The key to live betting is using a betting app to make your wagers, because apps let you track all the changes — both on the diamond and in the odds — as they happen; you will not find that response time at a brick-and-mortar sportsbook.

Common MLB in-game betting lines include next team to score, run line and moneyline odds, total runs scored, winner of the next inning, and player props such as total hits or strikeouts.
