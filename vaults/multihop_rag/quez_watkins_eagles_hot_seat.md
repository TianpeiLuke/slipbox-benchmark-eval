# Quez Watkins Eagles Hot Seat

Yardbarker's Nov. 30, 2023 NFC hot-seat roundup named wide receiver Quez Watkins as the Philadelphia Eagles' hottest seat, on the claim that Philadelphia's supposed No. 3 receiver had produced a measly four catches for 21 yards.

The mitigating fact is acknowledged — he missed five games with a hamstring injury — but the grounds that carry the argument are comparative: Olamide Zaccheaus and Julio Jones combined for 132 yards receiving and three touchdowns. The warrant is that when replacement-level options at the same spot substantially outproduce the nominal starter, the nominal starter's roster place is what is at risk.
