# The Patriots-Steelers SuperDraft Single-Game Lineup

Sporting News' SuperDraft lineup for the Week 14 2023 Patriots-Steelers game used its first "Co-Champ," pairing Najee Harris as Champion (1.9x multiplier, +50% points) with Ezekiel Elliott as Co-Champ (1.4x multiplier, +25% points).

The Super FLEX spots went to quarterback Mitch Trubisky of the Steelers (1.15x), tight end Pat Freiermuth of the Steelers (1.35x), kicker Chris Boswell of the Steelers (1.8x) and kicker Chad Ryland of the Patriots (1.8x). With the ground game being a point of emphasis, the column focused on the running backs for the two top spots, then filled in the lineup with both kickers alongside a Trubisky-Freiermuth stack.
