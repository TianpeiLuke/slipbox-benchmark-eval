# Beats And AirPods Holiday 2023 Deals

WIRED's Walmart Cyber Monday roundup of 27 November 2023 and its Apple Black Friday guide of 25 November 2023 together covered four discounted Apple-owned audio products: the Powerbeats Pro, the Beats Studio Pro headphones, the Beats Fit Pro and the newest AirPods.

Both guides make the Powerbeats Pro (8/10, WIRED Recommends) their pick for the most secure fit: the buds came out in 2019 but remain WIRED's top recommendation for the most intensive workouts, with an IPX4 water-resistance rating that protects them from sweat and excellent sound quality. The Apple guide adds that they pack nine hours of battery life with an extra 18 hours in the case, and that many features will not work with an Android phone although the core features will.

The Beats Studio Pro Wireless Headphones (7/10, WIRED Review) pack excellent noise cancelling, good sound quality, great wireless range, a stable fit and a compact design; WIRED notes they are missing a few key features including multi-band EQ and sensors for auto-pause, but the improved sound and active noise cancelling plus features for both Apple and Android devices make them a good option, especially on sale — and this deal is $10 cheaper than Prime Day, the lowest price WIRED has tracked. The Beats Fit Pro (9/10, WIRED Recommends) deliver great sound and a comfortable fit, and Apple's proprietary H1 chip makes them work like AirPods with an iPhone while still working well with Android, complete with an app for customising controls and running a fit test; they have dipped as low as $159 in the past, but WIRED still calls this a good discount. Finally, the AirPods (9/10, WIRED Recommends) that Apple launched in September 2023 were already on sale: identical to the old pair on the outside but with internal upgrades including slightly improved sound and a new Conversation Awareness mode that turns the music down and boosts Transparency mode so you can hear someone talking to you.
