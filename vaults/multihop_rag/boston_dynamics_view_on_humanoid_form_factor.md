# Boston Dynamics On The Humanoid Form Factor

Boston Dynamics CTO Aaron Saunders argues that humanoids are not necessarily the best form factor for all tasks, while still seeing great potential in them for general-purpose robotics.

His grounds are his own company's experience with Stretch. Boston Dynamics originally generated interest in a box-moving robot from a video it shared of Atlas moving boxes, but as Saunders puts it, just because humans can move boxes does not mean we are the best form factor to complete that task — and the company ultimately designed a custom robot in Stretch that can move boxes more efficiently and effectively than a human.

The scope of the concession is bounded: with that said, the company sees great potential in the long-term pursuit of general-purpose robotics, and the humanoid form factor is the most obvious match to a world built around our form. Boston Dynamics has always been excited about the potential of humanoids and is working hard to close the technology gap.
