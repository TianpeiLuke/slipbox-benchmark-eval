# iPad 9th Generation Holiday Deal

Apple's ninth generation standard iPad dropped to a new all-time low of $249 during Amazon's October Prime Day sale and then hovered at that price for the next few weeks, before falling to $230 at Amazon and Target for Black Friday 2023. For a few hours during the Black Friday sales it went as low as $229 at Amazon; by Cyber Monday that deal appeared to have sold out, though the prior generation standard Apple slate was still $80 off the MSRP from Walmart — about the same as it had sold for over the past few months, and still a good buy.

It is the recommended budget pick in Engadget's guide to the best Apple tablets because it's powerful enough for couch surfing, playing Apple Arcade games and watching Netflix, and it's the most affordable way to get an iPad. It earned a review score of 86 in 2021 and is the last iPad to use Apple's old design language, so it has thick bezels, a Lightning port, a Home button and a non-laminated display, and this variant comes with a paltry 64GB of storage. At this price it's hard to do better if all you want is a large screen for streaming video, playing games and browsing the web: its 10.2-inch display remains a step up from cheaper slates, its A13 Bionic chip is plenty fast for casual media consumption, its battery still lasts around 10 hours per charge, and most importantly it's the cheapest route into Apple's huge app library, customer support and years of software updates. If you need more storage space, the 256GB model was also down to a low of $380.
