# Hipgnosis Songs Fund Board Changes September 2023

On 28 September 2023, Hipgnosis Songs Fund confirmed that Andrew Sutch, 73, the Chair of its board since 2018, is stepping down both as HSF board Chair and as a director of the company.

HSF said it had begun a process to recruit a new Chair, and that Sutch will leave his post as and when that person is hired, or in any case before HSF's AGM the following year, in Q4 2024. In addition, Andrew Wilkinson, 72, informed the HSF board of his intention to retire as a board director, a departure that will reduce the company's board to five directors.
