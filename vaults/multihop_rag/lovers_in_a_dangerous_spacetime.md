# Lovers in a Dangerous Spacetime

Lovers in a Dangerous Spacetime has players scramble with friends to operate a spaceship hurtling through outer space. It appears in Polygon's 2023-10-20 list of the 35 best couch co-op games for Nintendo Switch.

The constraint that drives it: each player can only pilot one of the ship's eight total control panels at any given time.

Polygon's warning is social — you had better like your partners, because you will be shouting directions and trying not to lose it as you work together.
