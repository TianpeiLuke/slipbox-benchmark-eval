# Rangers Beat Diamondbacks 11-7 In World Series Game 4

Texas beat Arizona 11-7 in Game 4 of the 2023 World Series on Oct. 31, 2023, taking a 3-1 series lead and moving one win away from the franchise's first World Series title. The Sporting News framed the stakes by recalling that the Rangers had been one strike away from a championship twice in 2011 and could not finish the job, and that on Wednesday night they would have a chance to avenge that painful finish.

The offense came from two Rangers. Corey Seager hit his third home run of the series, and Marcus Semien drove in five of the Rangers' first 10 runs, including a three-run blast that put Texas up by double digits. Arizona, which had earned a reputation for not backing down, lived up to it: the Diamondbacks scored six runs over the final two innings, putting together competitive at-bats despite the dramatic deficit and forcing the Rangers to go to closer Jose Leclerc for the final out. It still was not nearly enough, and Arizona's best hope was that the late surge would carry over into Game 5, where they would face elimination for the third time in the postseason.

The line score was Rangers 0-5-5-0-0-0-0-1-0 for 11 and Diamondbacks 0-0-0-1-0-0-0-4-2 for 7. Looking ahead, the Rangers needed one more win and would have Nathan Eovaldi making his sixth start of the postseason in Game 5, while Zac Gallen — less than sharp in October but a Cy Young contender in the regular season — would start for Arizona with everything on the line.
