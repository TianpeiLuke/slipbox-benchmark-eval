# Andre Russell's Comeback In The First T20I

Andre Russell was named Player of the Match in the first T20I against England, taking 3-29 and smashing 29 not out off just 14 deliveries in his first T20I in over two years.

He seemed very pleased to be back in maroon, saying: "Life is so funny. Since when I got selected for the West Indies two weeks ago I've been dreaming of being man of the match." His bowling figures at the end of England's innings were 3-19, which the live blog described as balancing the attack perfectly on his comeback. As Tim Wigmore put it, Russell at his best adds so much to the West Indies side — with him at seven, and Holder, Shepherd and Hosein to follow, West Indies bat incredibly deep.
