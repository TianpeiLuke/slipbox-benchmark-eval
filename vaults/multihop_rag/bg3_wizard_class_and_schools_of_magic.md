# BG3 Wizard: Magic Damage Across Eight Schools

Polygon names Wizard the best class for magic damage in Baldur's Gate 3. Wizards are the archetypical caster: the only Intelligence-based class in the game, with the most spells and the most spell slots to cast them of all the casters.

Wizards get no armor and are limited to daggers and quarterstaffs in combat, so they rely on magic to do any damage. A Wizard's subclass is chosen at character creation from eight schools of magic: Abjuration, Conjuration, Divination, Enchantment, Evocation, Illusion, Necromancy, and Transmutation. Each subclass gets an extra ability themed to its school. Broadly, Abjuration is about protection and defense; Conjuration creates something from nothing; Divination foresees and changes rolls; Enchantment charms and controls minds; Evocation shapes and controls spells; Illusion is about trickery; Necromancy controls life and death; and Transmutation focuses on alchemy and changing the world around you. Evocation is the best subclass for a Wizard, with the reasoning and the skill and feat picks left to Polygon's separate Wizard build guide.
