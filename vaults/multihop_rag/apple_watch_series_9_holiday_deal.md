# Apple Watch Series 9 Holiday Deal

The new Apple Watch Series 9 saw a $70 discount over the 2023 holiday sales, bringing it to $330 at Amazon, Target and Walmart — a new low for the wearable that debuted alongside the iPhone 15 back in September. The wider Black Friday roundup lists it at $329 at Amazon, Walmart and Target, noting that is the price for the 41mm model while the 45mm models dropped to $359 with the same $70 discount.

The big change this time around is a new SiP (system in package) chip that allows for a Double Tap feature, letting you tap your thumb and forefinger together to answer calls and more, and that also allows for onboard processing of Siri requests, making simple demands like starting a timer or a workout happen faster. The Series 9 has an always-on display and, when paired with the Sport Loop, is a carbon neutral product. As has been the case for many years, the Apple Watch is an excellent fitness companion offering comprehensive health and activity data, and the Series 9 took the crown of the best smartwatch you can get right now in Engadget's guide.
