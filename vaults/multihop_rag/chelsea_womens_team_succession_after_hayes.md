# Chelsea Women's Team Succession After Hayes

Chelsea has received several applications regarding Emma Hayes' replacement but has not yet started an official recruitment process, with Hayes due to meet the club's technical directors once a week to create a succession plan and with a say in who takes the job after her, as reported by The New York Times on 15 November 2023.

How the news reached the club is recorded precisely: a few staff members were told on the morning of 4 November before Chelsea's away game at Aston Villa kicked off at 12.30 p.m. UK time, while most other staff members found out with the players in the post-match meeting, minutes before the official club statement — which Hayes had no hand in writing and did not even see before publication — was released at 3 p.m.

Two forward-looking possibilities are noted. There is the possibility of Hayes retaining a connection to the club via some sort of ambassadorial role, though it's likely contingent on a lack of conflict with the USWNT role and responsibilities; under American Todd Boehly's co-ownership, expanding Chelsea's profile and reach in the U.S. would make sense, especially with USWNT internationals Catarina Macario and Mia Fishel playing their club football there and CBS Sports holding WSL rights. For now there is an immediate task: Chelsea face Real Madrid on the Wednesday for their first match of the UWCL Champions League group stage, and her full American arrival will not come for another half a year after that.
