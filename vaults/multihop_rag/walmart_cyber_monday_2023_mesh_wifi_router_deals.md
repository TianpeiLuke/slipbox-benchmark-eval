# Walmart Cyber Monday 2023 Mesh Wi-Fi Router Deals

WIRED's Walmart Cyber Monday roundup of 27 November 2023 highlighted two mesh Wi-Fi systems: TP-Link's budget Deco X20 and the premium Google Nest Wi-Fi Pro.

The Deco X20 is a solid Wi-Fi 6 mesh system from TP-Link that appears in WIRED's Best Mesh Routers guide as the budget pick. It is a dual-band system running on 2.4-GHz and 5-GHz, with parental controls and basic security, and it performs dependably well. WIRED adds a pricing scope condition: the Deco X20 is frequently discounted and usually hovers between $160 and $180, and WIRED had previously seen it this low only during Lightning deals.

The Google Nest Wi-Fi Pro (7/10, WIRED Recommends) is one of WIRED's favourite mesh systems for people who want an elegant way to bring Wi-Fi to every nook and cranny of the home. It is easy to set and forget, and it employs the 6-GHz band for backhaul; each unit covers 2,200 square feet and can connect up to 100 devices. The stated drawback is that the Nest Wi-Fi Pro units are not backward compatible with older Nest routers.
