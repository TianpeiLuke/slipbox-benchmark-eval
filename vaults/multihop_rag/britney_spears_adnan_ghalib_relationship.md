# Britney Spears And Adnan Ghalib

Britney Spears met the British paparazzo Adnan Ghalib in 2007, shortly after she decided to shave her hair off in front of approximately 70 photographers, and the two dated for about a year before the relationship ended in 2008. They split after Ghalib reportedly pulled a pregnancy test-buying stunt for publicity. In 2009 Jamie Spears filed a restraining order against Ghalib, ordering the photographer to stay away from the singer and her family until 2012.

Ghalib spoke about the romance in the 2021 Netflix documentary Britney vs Spears, claiming: "It was a very simple, very easy relationship, you know? But between her fans and everybody else, it was like I was the bad guy."
