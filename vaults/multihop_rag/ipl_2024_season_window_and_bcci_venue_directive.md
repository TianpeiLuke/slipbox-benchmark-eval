# IPL 2024 Season Window And The BCCI Venue Directive

The IPL 2024 window starts on 22 March, as reported at 12.07 PM during the auction day live blog, and the early start to the tournament might force Ranji Trophy venues to shift.

Relatedly, as per a BCCI directive reported at 12.10 PM, all the IPL venues cannot hold any domestic game in February, and need to hand over the stadium to teams in the first week of March.
