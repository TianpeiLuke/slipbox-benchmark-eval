# NFL Week 11 2023: Joey Bosa Carted Off And Talanoa Hufanga Lost For The Season

CBS Sports' Week 11 injury roundup of 2023-11-19 records two defensive stars leaving their games. Joey Bosa of the Chargers appeared to injure his right leg or foot while held in a block during a first-quarter pass-rushing attempt against the Packers; he limped to the sidelines, was carted to the locker room, and was then ruled questionable to return.

Talanoa Hufanga of the 49ers, who emerged as a ballhawk for San Francisco's secondary in 2022, left early against the Buccaneers with a knee injury. Coach Kyle Shanahan confirmed to reporters on the Monday that the All-Pro suffered a season-ending ACL tear.
