# Jim Jordan's Political Profile And Record

Jim Jordan is known more as a chaos agent than a skilled legislator, which is what made his political climb toward the speakership steep and raised questions about how he would lead. First elected in 2006, he has few bills to his name from his time in office. The stakes of that thin legislative record were high: Congress faced daunting challenges, risking a federal shutdown at home if it failed to fund the government, and fielding President Biden's requests for aid to help Ukraine and Israel in the wars abroad.

He also faces questions about his past. Some years ago, Jordan denied allegations from former wrestlers during his time as an assistant wrestling coach at Ohio State University who accused him of knowing about claims they were inappropriately groped by an Ohio State doctor. Jordan has said he was never aware of any abuse.
