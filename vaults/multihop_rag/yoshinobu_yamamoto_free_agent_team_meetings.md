# Yoshinobu Yamamoto's Free Agent Meeting Tour

Between Dec. 10 and Dec. 19, 2023, Yoshinobu Yamamoto met face-to-face with a long list of suitors — the Giants, Mets, Yankees, Dodgers, Phillies, Red Sox and Blue Jays — while reporting indicated he would not decide until after Christmas.

ESPN's Buster Olney reported on Monday, Dec. 11 that the Giants had met with Yamamoto on Sunday, Dec. 10; the tracker described him as the biggest fish on the free-agent market, pursued by a handful of big-market teams including the Yankees and Dodgers, and noted the Giants had missed out on some of the most prominent free agents over the previous two offseasons, giving them incentive to make a massive offer to stay relevant in the NL West after the Dodgers' Ohtani signing. On Tuesday, Dec. 12, Bally Sports' Russell Dorsey reported the Dodgers met with Yamamoto, extending his free agent tour, and MLB.com's Mark Feinsand reported he would meet with both the Red Sox and Blue Jays in the coming days, having already met the Mets, Giants and Yankees with the Dodgers also believed to be involved — an indication the sweepstakes were not on the verge of a conclusion. On Wednesday, Dec. 13, The Athletic's Fabian Ardaya reported the Dodgers sent Ohtani, Mookie Betts, Freddie Freeman and Will Smith to meet with Yamamoto, trying to convince the starter to join the National League superpower, a signing that would be a major boost for a club that still had a need at starting pitcher. On Thursday, Dec. 14, Jon Heyman reported Yamamoto met with the Phillies, who were not among the more frequent names connected to him but who had re-signed ace Aaron Nola to pair with Zack Wheeler and would gain one of baseball's best rotations by adding him. On Sunday, Dec. 17, Joel Sherman of the New York Post reported the Mets hosted Yamamoto for dinner Saturday night at owner Steve Cohen's Connecticut home — at least the second face-to-face meeting after Cohen and other top Mets officials flew to Japan — and that the bidding for the 25-year-old was "supposed to intensify this week" with the Yankees, Dodgers, Giants, Red Sox, Phillies and Blue Jays also in the mix; Heyman reported the same day that Yamamoto asked to meet the Yankees a second time, a day after the Mets dinner. On Tuesday, Dec. 19, The Athletic's Andrew Baggarly reported the Giants met with him in San Francisco the previous week and had been willing to "blow teams out of the water" to land him, though Baggarly believed the crowded picture could make it harder for the Giants to win the sweepstakes, while the Newark Star-Ledger's Bob Klapisch reported Yamamoto was not expected to decide until after Christmas, possibly running until around Jan. 1-4.
