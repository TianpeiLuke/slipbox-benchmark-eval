# Dak Prescott As A Week 6 2023 Sit At The Chargers

CBS Sports listed Dak Prescott under "Sit Him" for Week 6 of the 2023 season at the Los Angeles Chargers: the claim is that trusting Prescott is a tall task even in what looks like a favourable matchup, and the warrant is that his collapsed throwing depth, a compromised offensive line and a former coach who knows his tendencies leave the favourable matchup unable to produce fantasy points.

The core grounds are Prescott's depth-of-target profile. His completion rate this season is fine at 69.4% and his off-target rate is low at 8.1%, but his average depth of target is 6.5, ranking 28th among 33 qualifying quarterbacks, and he is second-to-last in pass attempts of 10-plus air yards, with only Daniel Jones throwing fewer. The scary part, the column notes, is that his average depth of target had been over a yard lower at 5.4 going into the previous week's game, so the Cowboys tried to let him air it out and the plan completely backfired: he got aggressive on downfield throws early and missed until hitting KaVontae Turpin for a 26-yard touchdown, then had to throw deep to climb back and got sloppy on two interceptions, with a third bouncing off Michael Gallup's hands; he did not play the last 12 minutes and Dallas was blown out. The offensive line is offered as the underlying cause — injuries dating back to Week 2 plus declining play from Tyler Biadasz and Terence Steele may be why Prescott, not wanting to get hit, gets the ball out as fast as he can; even against the 49ers he sped up his motion and overshot targets, and the offense isn't adjusting well enough. The breakdown lists Dallas at the Chargers, the Cowboys favoured by 2.5, an over/under of 51, an opponent ranked 21st versus quarterbacks, 18.9 projected points, a 15th quarterback ranking, and year-to-date totals of 1,061 passing yards, 45 rushing yards, five touchdowns, four interceptions and 13.8 fantasy points per game.

The matchup evidence cuts both ways, which is what makes the sit a judgement call rather than a projection. The Chargers traditionally play a good amount of zone but have used more man this season, including against Miami in Week 1, and despite headliners in the front seven and 16 sacks in their past three games they rank fourth-worst in pass-rush pressure rate at 29.5% while blitzing at about the league average — in theory not a difficult matchup for Prescott. Every quarterback except Raiders rookie Aidan O'Connell has taken at least 22 fantasy points off the Chargers. Against that, the column raises Kellen Moore, Prescott's quarterback coach and playcaller for the prior five seasons, who will be coaching the Chargers offense on Sunday and is sure to have spilled the beans on Prescott's tendencies — offensive coordinators come back to wreck their old teams all the time. It also expects Dallas to pivot to a slower approach with Tony Pollard handling the run game more than he has recently, which could take pressure off Prescott without meaning he will unload statistically. The author would rather start Sam Howell, Jared Goff and Matthew Stafford.
