# Seahawks-Giants Pregame: Jamal Adams Returns, Saquon Barkley Ruled Inactive

Before the Oct. 2, 2023 Seahawks-Giants "Monday Night Football" game, the two headline availability items were Jamal Adams' return for Seattle and Saquon Barkley's absence for New York. At 6 p.m. the live blog reported that the three-time All-Pro safety would take the field for the first time since Week 1 of the 2022 NFL season — almost poetically, returning in the place he called home for the first three years of his career as a member of the Jets — and at 7:50 p.m. it confirmed Adams was back on the field warming up, a welcome sight for the 12s.

Barkley, listed as doubtful with a high ankle sprain, had no concrete availability update with less than two hours to kickoff, and barring a last-minute change did not appear likely to suit up (6:25 p.m.). At 6:55 p.m. he was officially ruled inactive, joined on the Giants' inactive list by safety Bobby McCain, safety Gervarrius Owens, tackle Andrew Thomas and defensive lineman Jordon Riley. Seattle's inactives were cornerbacks Coby Bryant, Tre Brown and Artie Burns, linebacker Nick Bellore, guard McClendon Curtis, tackle Charles Cross and guard Ben Brown.
