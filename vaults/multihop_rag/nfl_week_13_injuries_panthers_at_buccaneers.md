# NFL Week 13 Injuries Panthers At Buccaneers

In the Week 13 final injury reports published by CBSSports.com on Dec. 1, 2023, with the Buccaneers listed at -5.5 against the Panthers, Carolina risked being down two tight ends and Tampa Bay came in banged up, particularly on defense.

Carolina ruled out Hurst — the third straight game he would miss — and listed Tremble as doubtful. The team would also be without Bell and would likely look to rookie Alex Cook to start. Corner CJ Henderson (concussion) was a full participant on Friday and carried no designation heading into the weekend. Tampa Bay would be without key pieces David, Dean and White when it hosted Carolina, while Godwin did not practice Friday after being added to the injury report on Thursday with a neck injury.
