# Bad Times At The El Royale (2018)

Drew Goddard's Bad Times at the El Royale is a star-studded, charisma-packed murder-thriller told out of order, in the kind of layered "and here's what was going on with this other character at the same time" narrative that hangs plenty of Chekhovian guns on the wall for the final act. Polygon's Tasha Robinson calls it an underrated blast. It streams free with ads on FreeVee and is rentable on Amazon, Vudu, and YouTube.

The film features Cynthia Erivo in a star-making role, Chris Hemsworth in a part that makes the most of his sex appeal, and plenty of other familiar names — Jon Hamm, Dakota Johnson, Nick Offerman, Jeff Bridges — doing what they do. Robinson describes it as a powder keg of a movie that assembles a motley crew of unrelated guests, or are they, at a seedy motel and then keeps revealing secrets in a deliriously dizzy way as it all comes together.
