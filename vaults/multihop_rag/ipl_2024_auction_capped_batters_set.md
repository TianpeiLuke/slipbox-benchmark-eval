# IPL 2024 Auction: The Capped Batters Set

The first set of the IPL 2024 auction, announced at 1:15 PM IST, was the capped batters, and only three batters were sold from that first round — Travis Head, Rovman Powell and Harry Brook — with no one breaching the 10 Cr mark, as noted at 1:41 PM IST.

The results were: Rovman Powell to RR for ₹7.4 Cr; Harry Brook to DC for 4 Cr; Travis Head to SRH for 6.8 Cr; and Karun Nair, Manish Pandey, Rilee Rossouw and Steve Smith all unsold. Powell's sale at 1:22 PM followed a bidding war between KKR and RR that broke the Rs 7 Cr mark at 1:20 PM. Harry Brook came up at 1:22 PM with a base price of Rs 2 Cr, prompting a Delhi Capitals and Rajasthan Royals bidding war that passed Rs 4 Cr before Delhi took him. Travis Head came up at 1:28 PM in a direct contest between CSK and SRH, breached 6 Cr at 1:30 PM with SRH leading, and went to Sunrisers Hyderabad at 1:35 PM. Steve Smith came up at 1:37 PM with a Rs 2 Cr base price, the same minute Karun Nair went unsold, and Manish Pandey went unsold at 1:39 PM.

At 1:42 PM IST Anil Kumble explained the Powell purchase: "RR don't need any bowlers and they needed an overseas batter who can finish games to complement Hetmyer. They wanted some solidity and Powell gives RR the batting hold in the side."
