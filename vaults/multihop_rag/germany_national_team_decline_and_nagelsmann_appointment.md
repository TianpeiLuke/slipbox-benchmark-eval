# Germany's Decline And The Appointment Of Julian Nagelsmann

Germany entered their October 2023 friendly against the United States in a transitional phase, with Julian Nagelsmann having taken over as new head coach after Hansi Flick was unable to steer the team to safer waters in Qatar.

Germany are considered one of the preeminent national team programs in the world, but recent years have humbled Die Mannschaft. They had been eliminated in the group stage of two straight World Cup tournaments — an unthinkable slip in stature given they had never been bounced from any World Cup that early in history prior to 2018.

The Sporting News set that decline alongside the United States' own position: with the U.S. set to test themselves against one of Europe's most powerful sides, the opponents were entering a transitional phase as well.
