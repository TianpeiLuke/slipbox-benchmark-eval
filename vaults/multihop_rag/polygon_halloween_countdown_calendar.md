# Polygon's Halloween Countdown Calendar

For the past three years as of 2023-10-10, Polygon has put together a Halloween countdown calendar: the staff selects 31 of its top horror-themed or Halloween-adjacent picks across movies and TV throughout the month of October, all of them available to watch at home. The outlet brought the format back again for 2023, this time with a whole new batch of films and shows to choose from.

The mechanics are daily: every day for the month of October, Polygon adds a new recommendation to the countdown and tells readers where they can watch it. The invitation is to curl up on the couch, dim the lights, and grab some popcorn for a marathon of horrific delights.

The countdown sits alongside Polygon's year-round horror coverage rather than replacing it — the outlet also runs lists of the best horror movies you can stream at home, the best horror movies on Netflix, and the best horror movies of the year ranked by scariness. The stated reason for the separate seasonal format is that Halloween is an especially spooky time of year and warrants special attention and celebration.
