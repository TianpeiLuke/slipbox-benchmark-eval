# Sony Headphone And Earbud Deals During Prime Big Deal Day

Sony's headphones and earbuds were discounted at Best Buy during the anti-Prime Big Deal Day sales, led by the flagship WF-1000XM5 earbuds at $280, down from $300 — 7% off.

The over-ear Sony WH-1000XM4 was $250, down from $350 — 29% off; they can last up to 30 hours on a single charge and provide a comfortable listening experience thanks to their plush ear pads. The WF-1000XM5 earbuds improve upon the previous model with richer sound quality, slightly more powerful ANC, and vastly improved comfort thanks to their reduced size and weight. Sony's LinkBuds, which normally retail for $179.99, were $129.99 at Best Buy; the wireless earbuds work great for making calls and offer a unique "open-style" donut-shaped design that keeps the wearer aware of their environment. The Sony LinkBuds S were also on sale for $129.99, $70 off, at Best Buy; unlike the LinkBuds, these offer active noise cancellation, support for the LDAC lossless audio codec, and a slightly more conventional design. Sony's WH-CH720N were down to $99.99, $50 off, at Best Buy; the over-ear headphones may not offer all the bells and whistles of Sony's premium noise-canceling headphones, but they offer decent ANC and up to 35 hours of battery life.
