# Disinformation on X After the Hamas Attacks

Following the October 7, 2023 Hamas attacks on Israel, graphic videos apparently showing attacks on civilians circulated on X, alongside posts that fact-checkers identified as false.

Documented examples include footage from the video game Arma 3 posted with the false claim that it showed Hamas missile attacks on Israel; footage filmed the previous month in Egypt presented as from the conflict; and a false claim that Israel had bombed a church in Gaza, which went viral after multiple verified accounts repeated it without evidence.

Elon Musk himself recommended that people follow accounts that had previously posted antisemitic comments and false information, then deleted the post.

A Wired report described the situation as "The Israel-Hamas War Is Drowning X in Disinformation." Eliot Higgins argued that Musk "has created a fundamental issue with Twitter's credibility in moments of crisis."

X reported more than 50 million posts globally over two days referencing the attacks. By that Thursday afternoon the attacks had killed more than 1,200 people per the Israeli Military, and at least 1,537 people had been killed in Gaza by Israel's retaliatory strikes per the Gaza Ministry of Health.
