# The White Company's Advent Calendars And Snowflake Jumper

The White Company offers two Advent calendars in its Christmas range, aimed at different audiences. The Snowy Advent Calendar is pitched at younger members of the family: a charming penguin with 25 numbered pockets to fill with treats and gifts, made from super soft velboa faux fur so that no one can resist cuddling it once the pockets are empty. The second is an adult version packed with daily indulgent treats in 25 drawers — The Independent reports having had a sneak peek and reveals it contains candles, shower gels, nail creams and more seasonal scents and wellness goodies, described as 25 moments of joy.

The section frames these as part of the small rituals that make the season distinctive — counting down the days, making your own decorations, or digging out your favourite festive fashions. For that last category the guide picks the Sparkle Snowflake Embroidered Jumper: scattered with embroidered snowflakes for a subtler nod to the season than the standard Christmas jumper, with a comfort fit and a dash of cashmere adding snugness to its elegant style.
