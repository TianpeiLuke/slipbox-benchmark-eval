# Apple's Budget iPad And iPad Mini

Apple's most affordable tablet, the 10.2-inch iPad, was on sale for $249. It may have an old-school design, but it is certainly the best iPad for people on a budget and has most of the crucial features you would expect in an Apple tablet: it earned a score of 86 from Engadget for its solid performance, sharp screen, TouchID-capable Home button and good battery life. The only major downside to this model is that it still sports Apple's old Lightning port rather than USB-C.

The Apple iPad mini dropped to $400 for October Prime Day. It is the best small tablet on the market right now and really your only option if you want a true one-hand-friendly iPad.

It is powered by Apple's A15 Bionic chip and sports a Touch ID-toting power button, solid cameras, good battery life and a USB-C port for charging.
