# 2024 Mock Draft: Seven Offensive Linemen Projected In Round 1

CBS Sports' 2024 NFL Mock Draft of 2023-11-17 projects seven offensive tackles or linemen in the first round, each tied to a specific roster problem. At Pick 4, Arizona takes Olumuyiwa Fashanu, OT, Penn State, a junior at 6'6" and 317 lbs, ranked 3rd prospect and 1st at the position: when a new homeowner moves in they often have a different vision of how to decorate the space, and Jonathan Gannon and Monti Ossenfort are the new homeowners who could move on from D.J. Humphries, saving money toward the salary cap while likely upgrading. At Pick 6, Green Bay takes Joe Alt, OT, Notre Dame, a junior at 6'7" and 322 lbs, 7th prospect and 3rd at the position — the David Bakhtiari experience may be over in Green Bay, but there is a tantalizing off-ramp with Alt. At Pick 7, the L.A. Rams take JC Latham, OT, Alabama, a junior at 6'6" and 360 lbs, 6th prospect and 2nd at the position, because Los Angeles' offensive tackles have had two of the highest beaten rates in the NFL that season according to TruMedia.

At Pick 12, the N.Y. Jets take Amarius Mims, OT, Georgia, a junior at 6'7" and 340 lbs, 23rd prospect and 4th at the position: the top-10 selections were not kind to a New York team that desperately needs offensive tackle help, and Mims, young and inexperienced, upgrades the ceiling of their protection. At Pick 23, Pittsburgh takes Taliese Fuaga, OL, Oregon State, a junior at 6'6" and 334 lbs, 17th prospect and 1st at the position — Pittsburgh probably has some offensive soul-searching ahead, but more protection is a sound investment. At Pick 28, San Francisco takes Kingsley Suamataia, OT, BYU, a sophomore at 6'6" and 325 lbs, 37th prospect and 7th at the position, as Stage 1 of a transformation that needs multiple changes to its offensive line, replacing Colton McKivitz. At Pick 30, Detroit takes Graham Barton, OT, Duke, a senior at 6'5" and 314 lbs, 35th prospect and 6th at the position: Jonah Jackson and Halapoulivaati Vaitai are slated to hit free agency, and if one or both leave it creates a void, with Barton having played left tackle for the Blue Devils but projecting inside.
