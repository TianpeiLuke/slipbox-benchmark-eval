# Why DraftKings' Vermont Offer Beats Rivals — And Why The Deposit Match Is Only For High Rollers

Sporting News argues on 2023-09-26 that DraftKings Sportsbook outdoes other sites by having multiple offers rather than just one. The $200 total in Bonus Bets you can receive just for depositing and wagering $5 is described as the best deal around, and you don't even have to win or lose your first bet; the deposit match bonus offer is unique as well, although not all new users will likely try to take advantage of it.

The advantages listed: there are multiple welcome offers and not just a single offer; you don't have to risk a lot, just $5, to receive a total of $200 in Bonus Bets; and you don't have to worry about winning or losing your first wager to get a bonus, as you do with other sites' offers. The counterweights are stated just as plainly: the 20% deposit match bonus worth up to $1,000 might sound good, but with a 25x wagering requirement it is truthfully only useful to high-stakes bettors; you only get a week to use the $150 in Bonus Bets awarded for making your first wager of at least $5; and you only receive winnings and not the bet amounts when you use Bonus Bets.
