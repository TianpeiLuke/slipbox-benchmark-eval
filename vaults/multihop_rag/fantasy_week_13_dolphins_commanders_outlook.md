# Week 13 Dolphins-Commanders: Miami's Pass Rush Should Pass Its Post-Phillips Test

CBS Sports argues that the Dolphins pass rush, even after losing edge rusher Jaelan Phillips, should handle a leaky Commanders offensive line without giving up much in the way of deep completions. The grounds: with Phillips, the Dolphins ranked third in sacks with 38 and sixth in pass rush pressure rate at 39.9%, though both numbers were boosted by the defense's effort against a sagging Jets offense last Friday. Adding veteran Jason Pierre-Paul is described as not a cure-all, but he will help keep the Dolphins from being forced to blitz a ton week in and week out. On the other side, the Commanders offensive line has had some ugly showings lately — four sacks and a 49% pass rush pressure rate allowed against the Cowboys, four sacks and 32.7% pressure allowed against the Giants, and three sacks with 46.8% pressure allowed against the Seahawks — and, somehow, these results are better than what they were allowing through much of the first two months of the year.

The warrant tying that to Sam Howell's Fantasy value is that getting the ball out faster has been a positive for pass protection but a negative for his numbers in three of his past four games, with the one game he did really well in described as sort of fluky. The verdicts: STARTS — Tyreek Hill, Jaylen Waddle, Raheem Mostert, Tua Tagovailoa, Terry McLaurin (low-end WR2), De'Von Achane (low-end RB2) and the Dolphins DST; SITS — Sam Howell (low-end starter at best), Brian Robinson (desperation RB starter), Logan Thomas (PPR bye-week replacement), Antonio Gibson (PPR bye-week replacement) and the Commanders DST; SLEEPERS — Curtis Samuel (especially PPR) and Jahan Dotson (non-PPR only).
