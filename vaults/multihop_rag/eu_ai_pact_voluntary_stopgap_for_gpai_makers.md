# The EU AI Pact As A Voluntary Stopgap For GPAI Makers

The AI Pact is a voluntary stop-gap the Commission has been working on with industry for some months, and it confirmed at the time of the AI Act deal that the Pact is intended to plug the practice gap until the AI Act comes into force.

Its function follows from the Act's long lead time: while the full regulation will not be up and running for a few years, the EU will be pushing for GPAIs to abide by codes of practice in the meanwhile, so AI giants will be under pressure to stick as close to the hard regulations coming down the pipe as possible, via the bloc's AI Pact.
