# Tottenham's Premier League Record Of Failing To Win After Leading

OptaJoe recorded on 7 December 2023 that Tottenham had become the first side in Premier League history to fail to win five consecutive games despite going 1-0 up in each match, and the first to lose three consecutive home games despite going 1-0 up in each — a run the post summed up as "Spursy."

The Roar's report places that record in the table. Spurs had rebounded from three consecutive defeats with a stirring 3-3 draw at Manchester City over the weekend, but after this result they sat outside the Champions League qualification spots in fifth place, level with Manchester United.
