# Sphere: Venue And Display Specifications

Sphere is a $US2.3 billion Las Vegas venue whose defining feature is an interior 16K x 16K spherical video surface — the highest-resolution LED screen ever made, with 268,435,456 pixels — inside what is otherwise a conventional concert space, The Sydney Morning Herald reported on 19 October 2023.

Its discriminating features are technical: besides the interior screen, the exterior is covered by 1.2 million hockey-puck-sized LEDs; there are 157,000 ultra-directional speakers, which "the boffins call 'beam-forming acoustics'"; and there are "infrasound haptics", meaning the soundwaves are shaped to create both vibrations and waves of air pressure. In U2's show the audience journeys through a succession of environments referencing the band's creative history, while for other projects such as Darren Aronofsky's Postcard From Earth the sound system uses haptics to make the audience feel the sounds.

The boundary case is how ordinary the rest of it is. In many ways it is a conventional space — tiered seating inside, wallet-bruising bar snacks and merchandise outside, the audience seated in rows and the band on a single central stage — and some parts of the rock concert experience will never change. But when the lights go down everything changes: Sphere splits open around you to reveal a succession of environments, each more stunning than the one before, and the effect is overwhelming. Like the ABBA Voyage show in London, the reviewer writes, it is hard to capture in words.
