# Monster Hunter Now Halloween Event Schedule

Monster Hunter Now's Halloween event runs between Wednesday, Oct. 25, at 9 a.m. and Tuesday, Oct. 31, at 11:59 p.m. local time, and has two phases, as Polygon reported on 2023-10-25.

From the start of the event onwards, Pumpkin Tickets and Halloween Pumpkin Hunt Quests are available, active until the end of the event. From Saturday, Oct. 28, at 9 a.m. local time onwards, pumpkin rock Kulu-Ya-Ku appears in the wild and Halloween Medal Acquisition Quests become available, also active until the end of the event. In summary, players can begin earning Pumpkin Tickets at any time through the questline, but it is not until Saturday morning that they can encounter pumpkin rock Kulu-Ya-Ku and earn the Halloween medal.

Alongside those quests, monsters, and currencies, the game's various arenas also get a Halloween makeover, with the world map, encounter screen, and battle arena becoming adorned with pumpkins.
