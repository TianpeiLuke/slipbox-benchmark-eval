# Everton Case Implications For Manchester City And Chelsea

Sean Dyche declined to comment on the implications of Everton's punishment for Manchester City and Chelsea, The Guardian reported on Nov. 24, 2023.

Manchester City had been charged with 115 financial breaches by the Premier League and deny any wrongdoing; Chelsea were also under investigation by the football authorities.
