# Drawbacks Of Prop Bets

Against the case for props, Sporting News presses three objections: props are difficult to research, they can tie up your bankroll, and exotic props are a guessing game. The first attacks the assumption that a bet's specificity makes it tractable. Prop bets are so specific you would think they would be easier to research than other bet types, but sports are extremely volatile and you can never truly predict how an athlete or a team will perform, which makes smart props very hard to nail down — you might bet a player to score "over" a point threshold and lose because he had an off-night, even though he would have won that bet easily in other recent games.

The bankroll objection targets the role props should play in a strategy. Sporting News would not recommend using prop bets as your main bet type: use most of your budget on more stable bet types such as moneylines, spreads or totals, and focus only a small percentage of your bankroll on props.

The third objection singles out the exotic props that arrive with big events. When large events like the Super Bowl roll around you are inundated with exotic and entertaining props; these are almost impossible to research, and pure guessing is not good for anyone's bankroll or betting strategy.
