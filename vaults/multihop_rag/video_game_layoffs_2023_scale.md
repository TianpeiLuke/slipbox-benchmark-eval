# The Scale Of Video Game Layoffs In 2023

It's estimated that more than 9,000 people in video games were laid off in 2023, and the firings affected teams of all sizes — what Engadget called a crisis amount of cuts.

The scale is clearest by comparison: in 2022, just 1,000 video game jobs were lost, according to layoffstracker.com. Engadget framed the year's cuts as the post-acquisition power dynamic playing out in public and in real time.
