# Proprietary Data

## Definition

In the corpus, proprietary data is the data a niche AI startup collects and controls that makes its application's output specific and relevant, and that stops others from replicating the final product.

## Context

The term surfaces in TechCrunch's 13 October 2023 survey of six VCs on how AI startups can capture and defend market share, in the answer on what lets industry-specific startups that tune generative models for a niche prove defensible: startups must prioritize collecting proprietary data, integrating a sophisticated application layer and assuring output accuracy, because the application is only as sophisticated as the data it is fed, and in-house middle-layer capabilities that harness this data provide a competitive edge alongside the foundational models themselves.
A companion TechCrunch piece, published six days earlier, surveyed 10 investors on the future of AI and names the same idea from the investor side: among the topics they raised was where startups still stand a chance, where oligopoly dynamics and first-mover advantages are shaping up, and the value of proprietary data.

## Key Characteristics

- **Exclusivity is the source of value** — proprietary data is necessary for specific and relevant insights and, critically, to ensure others cannot replicate the final product.
- **It is a defensibility lever, not the whole defense** — the VC framework pairs proprietary-data collection with a sophisticated application layer and assured output accuracy as the three conditions for a defensible niche AI startup.
- **It compounds with the application layer** — in-house middle-layer capabilities that are fed proprietary data provide a competitive edge while still harnessing the power of foundational models.
- **Investors treat it as a scarce strategic asset** — VCs surveyed separately about the next big thing in AI flagged the value of proprietary data alongside oligopoly dynamics and first-mover advantage as forces shaping which startups stand a chance.

## Background (external)

Outside this corpus, "proprietary data" is a general business and legal term for data embodying trade secrets developed at private expense, or commercial or financial information that is privileged or confidential — typically marked with a restrictive legend, with carve-outs when the data is already public, independently developed, freely shared by its owner, or required to be disclosed by law (Law Insider, "Proprietary Data Definition," accessed 2026-09-02).
