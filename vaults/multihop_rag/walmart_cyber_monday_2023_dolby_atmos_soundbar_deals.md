# Walmart Cyber Monday 2023 Dolby Atmos Soundbar Deals

WIRED's Walmart Cyber Monday roundup of 27 November 2023 listed ten discounted soundbars, most of them Dolby Atmos systems: Bose's Smart Soundbar 600 and Smart 900, a multi-piece JBL system, the Klipsch Cinema 600, Polk's Audio Signa 4, Sony's HT-A5000, the Sonos Arc and Beam (Gen 2), a luxury Sennheiser model and Samsung's HW-Q800C.

Bose's Smart Soundbar 600 is described as a Mighty Mouse Dolby Atmos bar offering clear and expansive sound from a strikingly small frame, and unlike a lot of soundbars at its price it includes real up-firing drivers for immersive overhead effects, alongside good musicality, relatively punchy bass and great network streaming features. The JBL multi-piece system (8/10, WIRED Recommends) offers some of the best Dolby Atmos immersion you can buy plus fully wireless surround speakers that can sit behind you or double as Bluetooth speakers before reconnecting to the main bar for charging and playback. The Klipsch Cinema 600 is built from a speaker-like MDF cabinet paired with a massive 10-inch subwoofer driver, giving clear and powerful sound that is as good for music as for cinematic punch. Polk's Audio Signa 4 (7/10, WIRED Recommends) was already one of the most affordable Dolby Atmos soundbars of its kind, with true up-firing drivers, a wireless subwoofer for beefy bass and generally pleasing performance. Sony's HT-A5000 is pricey at full cost but a killer deal here, adding engulfing surround expansion, next-gen HDMI with VRR support for PS5 or Xbox Series X, multiple Wi-Fi streaming options, Dolby Atmos and DTS:X support and hi-res audio.

The Sonos Arc (9/10, WIRED Recommends) is WIRED's favourite soundbar for big home theatres, with three tweeters and eight mid-woofers for deep bass and full Dolby Atmos that bounces sound off walls and ceilings to mimic a surround setup; it was on sale at the same price during last year's holiday sales, but this is the lowest WIRED has tracked. The Sonos Beam (Gen 2) offers enhanced sound with Dolby Atmos support (with compatible TVs and streaming apps), a fast processor, an HDMI eARC port for high-definition audio, support for Alexa, Google Assistant and AirPlay 2, and pairing with other Sonos speakers for surround. Bose's updated Smart 900 adds better bass extension to an already great sound profile, with true up-firing drivers delivering expansive Dolby Atmos and virtual surround from a single unit, and this is the first time WIRED has seen its price drop. The luxury Sennheiser model is at the lowest price WIRED has seen — a hefty unit with no separate subwoofer, but with high-flying virtual surround and Dolby Atmos tricks, punchy grooves, sweet and smooth musicality and conveniences like Wi-Fi streaming and an analogue input. Samsung's HW-Q800C offers a lot from a two-piece system, mixing up-firing Dolby Atmos speakers with side-firing drivers for an engaging cinematic experience while its wireless subwoofer adds a hefty punch to effects and streaming music.
