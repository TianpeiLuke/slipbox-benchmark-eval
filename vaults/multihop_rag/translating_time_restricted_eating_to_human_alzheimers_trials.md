# Translating Time-Restricted Eating Into Human Alzheimer's Trials

The researchers hypothesize that time-restricted eating will carry over from mice to people, and they hope that these findings will lead to human trials; the goal is to obtain funding to start a pilot study of patients next year, which is the test that would confirm or disconfirm the proposition.

The reason they expect it to translate is practical rather than mechanistic. "Accessible interventions like this have great translational potential, as they are available and are usually very well-tolerated," Desplats said.

The proposition is stated in conditional terms, and it identifies the population where the payoff would be largest: "This may have special importance for the elderly, as time-restricted eating does not require limitation in calories or change in the diet, but may offer important benefits ranging from metabolic and sleep regulation to potential cognitive improvement."
