# NFL Week 11 Power Rankings, 9-16

In Sporting News' Week 11 rankings of Nov. 13, 2023, the Jacksonville Jaguars were ninth at 6-3 (previous week: 4) after an NFC wake-up call against the 49ers, who were much more prepared coming off a Week 9 bye; Jacksonville needed to forget that debacle quickly, with a division double against Tennessee and Houston before facing Cincinnati. The Dallas Cowboys were 10th at 6-3 (11), having taken advantage of a "no contest" against the Giants at home with Dak Prescott continuing to ball as a passer and runner, needing to sustain the defense and running game in bigger games. The Seattle Seahawks were 11th at 6-3 (13), needing every ounce of their offense around Geno Smith to rally past the Commanders in a get-well performance that worked for him, Kenneth Walker and the receivers, with a defense that could be more consistent.

The Minnesota Vikings were 12th at 6-4 (14): new arrival Josh Dobbs kept producing the same offensive results the team had enjoyed with Kirk Cousins, thanks to more help from the running game for Kevin O'Connell and the defense overall under Brian Flores, making their 0-3 and 1-4 starts distant memories as they sought a sixth straight win in Week 11 against Denver. The Houston Texans were 13th at 5-4 (16), looking great with C.J. Stroud leading the offense and a running game now emerging, their defense making just enough plays to win high-scoring games — a legitimate playoff contender under rookie coach DeMeco Ryans.

The Cincinnati Bengals fell to 14th at 5-4 (7), battling key injuries that left little room for error, with Joe Burrow making key mistakes and the defense wilting against run and pass at home in the loss to the Texans. The Buffalo Bills were 15th at 5-5 (12) after falling into a trap game against the Broncos at home, losing because of more mistakes from Josh Allen, which their injury-hit defense could not tolerate. The Las Vegas Raiders jumped to 16th at 5-5 (22), hot after two consecutive wins for interim coach Antonio Pierce that brought flashbacks to the playoff push under Rich Bisaccia in 2021, putting them back in the AFC wild-card race with the Dolphins, Chiefs and Vikings ahead.
