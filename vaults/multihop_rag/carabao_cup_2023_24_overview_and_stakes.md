# What The Carabao Cup Is And What Winning It Is Worth In 2023/24

The Carabao Cup is the League Cup, named after its sponsor; its 64th edition got underway on August 8 and had reached the semifinal stage by Dec. 19, 2023, per Sporting News.

Its defining place in the calendar is that, after the Community Shield won by Arsenal on August 6, the League Cup would be the first domestic trophy of the 2023/24 season awarded, with the final to take place on Sunday, February 25, 2024 at Wembley Stadium in London. Manchester United were the holders of the trophy after defeating Newcastle United in the 2023 final; the previous season's finalists met in round four after being drawn against one another, with the Magpies gaining revenge.

The competition carries a second prize beyond silverware: the winner of the EFL Cup earns a place in the 2024/25 UEFA Conference League playoffs, with a chance to qualify for the group stage of that European cup competition.
