# Balderton's Investment Thesis On Scalable Capital

Balderton general partner Rana Yared is joining Scalable Capital's board with the December 2023 round. In a statement she said: "Scalable's one-stop, digital-first, wealth building and generating platform brings a suite of top-class financial products to individuals across Europe, and is unparalleled in the market. We've been impressed by Erik, Florian and team's vision and execution to date and are delighted to be supporting them in this next chapter."

TechCrunch noted at publication that it hoped to hear from Yared directly later that day and would update the piece afterwards.
