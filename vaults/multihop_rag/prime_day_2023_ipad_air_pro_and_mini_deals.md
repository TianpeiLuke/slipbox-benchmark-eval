# Prime Day 2023 iPad Air, Pro And Mini Deals

WIRED's iPad picks are the iPad Air, the 12.9-inch iPad Pro, and the iPad Mini.

The iPad Air (8/10, WIRED Recommends) is recommended as an upgrade over the iPad (9th Gen), with a 10.9-inch LCD screen, a USB-C port, slim bezels, Touch ID built into the top power button, and the same M1 processor as the 2020 MacBook Air, plus support for the second-generation Apple Pencil, Smart Keyboard Cover, and Magic Keyboard Cover; it dipped lower to $460 in July, but WIRED still thinks this is a solid deal. The iPad Pro (7/10, WIRED Review) packs only incremental upgrades over its predecessor but is the best option for the ultimate iPad: a large 12.9-inch LCD screen with ProMotion for a 120-Hz refresh rate and smoother scrolling, Face ID, four speakers, more microphones to pick up your voice clearly, an M2 chip — the same one powering the MacBook Air and Mac Mini — and an exclusive Hover Mode for the second-gen Apple Pencil that previews what the stylus will do when hovering over the screen. The iPad Mini (8/10, WIRED Recommends) is WIRED's go-to tablet for travel, small enough to slip into a backpack and fitting perfectly on an airplane tray table; its 8.3-inch display is great for watching movies, reading ebooks, sketching and note-taking with the second-generation Apple Pencil, and its A15 Bionic processor — the chip in the 2021 iPhone 13 — handles taxing apps and games with ease and should last many years. Apple is rumored to be launching a next-gen version soon, but it remains a solid tablet if you don't need the latest and greatest.
