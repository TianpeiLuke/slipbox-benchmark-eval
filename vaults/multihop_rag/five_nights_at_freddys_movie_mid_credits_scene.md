# Five Nights at Freddy's Movie Mid-Credits Scene

The Five Nights at Freddy's movie does have a mid-credits scene: a brief one that rolls less than a minute after the initial cut to black. Polygon reported this on 2023-10-26.

The scene is not integral to the movie's lore and does not feel like a sequel tease, though it certainly could be elaborated on in a sequel. It is more of a comic scene involving YouTuber and horror-game aficionado Cory DeVante "CoryxKenshin" Williams, though he does not seem to be playing himself.

As for a true post-credits scene that actually plays at the tail end of the credit roll, the film does not have one — just a brief, not especially exciting audio-only Easter egg.
