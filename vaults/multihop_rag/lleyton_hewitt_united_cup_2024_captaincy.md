# Lleyton Hewitt's United Cup 2024 Captaincy

Australia's Davis Cup captain Lleyton Hewitt will return to captain the Australian team at the United Cup, as reported by The Roar on 19 December 2023.

The tournament details given are that eighteen nations will contest it in Perth and Sydney, starting on 29 December and ending on 7 January. The event began the previous year with Hewitt and Sam Stosur at the helm for Australia.

The report also names one other returning captain: David Witt, who helped lead the United States to the 2023 trophy, returns in 2024 for the Americans' title defence.
