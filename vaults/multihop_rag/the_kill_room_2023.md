# The Kill Room

The Kill Room premiered on VOD in early November 2023, available to rent on Amazon, Apple, and Vudu. The dark comedy thriller runs 1 hour 38 minutes, is directed by Nicol Paone, and stars Joe Manganiello, Samuel L. Jackson, and Uma Thurman.

Manganiello plays a hitman turned artist in what Polygon calls a funny comedy about how the worlds of fine art and high crime are not so separated after all: when he turns to art as a method of laundering money, the hitman becomes an unexpected overnight sensation in the high-art scene.
