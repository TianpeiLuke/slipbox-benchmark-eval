# Paw Patrol: The Mighty Movie

Paw Patrol: The Mighty Movie became available to rent on Amazon, Apple, and Vudu in early November 2023. The action adventure runs 1 hour 35 minutes, is directed by Cal Brunker, and features the voices of Mckenna Grace, Taraji P. Henson, and Marsai Martin.

In this sequel to the first movie, the pups get superpowers in their quest to stop Mayor Humdinger from destroying Adventure City.
