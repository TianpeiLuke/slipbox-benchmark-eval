# Cole Palmer's Goal Against Manchester City In The 4-4 Draw

Cole Palmer marked his goal against Manchester City with a shrug rather than a celebration, out of respect for the club where he spent 15 years. "I spent 15 years at the club," he noted. "I can't really go and celebrate how I would celebrate if I have scored a 95th-minute equaliser because it would have been disrespectful." Perhaps it summed up Palmer's inability to feel intimidated by anything; there was no getting carried away.

The alternative explanation is that Palmer has scored so many major goals in the last few months that this was merely another. He struck in the Community Shield and the European Super Cup for City, and against Arsenal and Tottenham for Chelsea. That big-match temperament may equip him for tournament football in an England shirt — and so, too, may his nerveless penalty-taking.
