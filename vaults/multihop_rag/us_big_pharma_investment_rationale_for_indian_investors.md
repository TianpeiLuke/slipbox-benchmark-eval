# Why Indian Investors Should Look To US Big Pharma

For cutting-edge tech and pharmaceutical exposure, the US is the place for Indian investors to look to, because Indian markets already give them access to growth in manufacturing, banking, commodity, services and IT, but not to that frontier. The grounds are structural: the US is the largest pharmaceutical market in the world, and by virtue of its size, its ideal IP protection laws and its deep-pocketed insurers, most of the cutting-edge innovation in pharma tends to be concentrated there. The warrant is that for investors and general readers alike one should be abreast of the latest pharmaceutical innovation taking place overseas, since the open questions of the field, whether a weight loss pill will be a reality, when HIV will be cured, and what the future of cancer treatments is, are being answered in that market.

The article's scope is set out with the argument: it highlights the largest unmet needs in medicine currently being addressed by US Big Pharma such as Merck, Gilead and Eli Lilly, and also by big players based in Europe, namely Novo Nordisk and Roche, and it lays down the basic framework for investing in or assessing US pharma and the stocks currently in focus.
