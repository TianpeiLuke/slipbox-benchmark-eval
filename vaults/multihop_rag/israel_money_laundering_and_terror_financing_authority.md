# Israel's Money Laundering And Terror Financing Prohibition Authority

IMPA is an intelligence body that conducts the most complex international economic investigations and fights terror financing routinely; it has been headed by Adv. Ilit Ostrovitch-Levi since May 2022, and since October 7 most of its work has focused on identifying and blocking terror money pipelines.

Ostrovitch-Levi describes the pivot: "Already on that Saturday night, with Israel under heavy rocket fire, we talked about preparing ourselves for the campaign we're now entering, the fight against terror financing channels. Dozens of our staff have been called up to reserve duty, in large part managers working regularly in the fight against terror financing." The working method is deliberately reactive and continuous: "We work very reactively – we see a fundraising campaign, try to deter it, and stop it from happening again. There are no breaks, because they are constantly active. It's a daily struggle. Our analysts have been sitting on the social networks from day two of the war, just manually monitoring visible sources of information on these networks to locate fundraising campaigns for terrorist organizations operating in Gaza." Three days after the war began, IMPA also issued a call to all global regulated entities for increased vigilance towards terrorism financing campaigns in response to the ongoing war and state of emergency, and to report all activities that raise suspicion of terror support and terror financing in an effective and immediate manner.
