# Community Notes

## Definition

Community Notes is X's (formerly Twitter's) crowdsourced fact-checking feature, which lets platform users add context to questionable posts: users vote to label disputed tweets, and the aggregated result may be appended to the post as a contextual note.

## Context

In the corpus the feature recurs as X's central, and contested, answer to disinformation risk after Elon Musk's takeover.

X has pushed Community Notes as its main response to disinformation risks, including during the surge of over 50 million posts referencing the Israel-Hamas war in October 2023.

The feature is also a formal object of EU scrutiny: the European Commission's first DSA infringement proceeding against X names "the effectiveness of measures taken by X to combat info manipulation" with a specific focus on "the effectiveness of its 'Community Notes' system of crowdsourcing fact-checking to aggregated views of users" as one of the areas under investigation.

X itself has cited Community Notes activity in its own defense, telling the EU that more than 700 unique Community Notes appeared on posts related to the Ukraine conflict, with a "Notes on Media" feature that matches notes on an image or video to other posts containing matching media, extending coverage to over 1,200 additional posts.

Reddit co-founder Alexis Ohanian, discussing social media's difficulty in arbitrating truth, singled out Community Notes as "the best measure I've seen yet," while also noting the endorsement is bounded — he separately rejected the idea that a platform like X can function as a neutral "town square."

## Key Characteristics

- **Crowdsourced, not staff-adjudicated** — labeling disputed content is done by aggregating votes from platform users rather than an in-house fact-checking team.
- **Rebranded legacy feature** — it is described as an existing crowdsourced fact-checking mechanism that Musk pushed and rebranded as Community Notes, not a new invention.
- **Positioned as a substitute for cut moderation capacity** — it is X's promoted alternative after Musk slashed in-house content moderation and enforcement teams.
- **Multi-language, locally targeted** — per X's own account, notes are written in numerous languages "specifically for local audiences."
- **Extended via automated matching** — the "Notes on Media" feature automatically matches an existing note on an image or video to other posts carrying matching media, propagating a single note's context across duplicate posts.
- **Object of regulatory effectiveness testing** — the EU's DSA investigation explicitly targets whether the Community Notes system is effective, not merely whether it exists.
