# Anker 737 Power Bank Deal Listings

Both of WIRED's October 10, 2023 Prime Day roundups list this power bank, which WIRED had just added as the top upgrade pick in its guide to the Best Portable Chargers — pricey, but made more accessible by the deal.

It charges from zero to completely full in an hour and boasts a whopping 24,000-mAh capacity, powerful enough to charge laptops and tablets as well as phones and other gadgets, and it has a built-in display for monitoring stats like temperature and remaining battery percentage.
