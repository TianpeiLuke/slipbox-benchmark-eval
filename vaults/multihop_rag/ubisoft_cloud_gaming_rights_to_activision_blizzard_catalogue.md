# Ubisoft's Cloud Gaming Rights To The Activision Blizzard Catalogue

Ubisoft holds the cloud gaming rights to every Activision Blizzard game, past and future, for 15 years — a concession Microsoft had to agree to in order to get its acquisition past UK regulators, who were worried about the company dominating the cloud industry.

The defining feature of the arrangement is its breadth in one dimension and its limit in another: it covers the entire Activision Blizzard catalogue, including games not yet made, but only the cloud gaming rights, and only for a fixed 15-year term. Ubisoft is perhaps best known as the developer and publisher of the stealthy action-adventure franchise Assassin's Creed.

Cloud gaming, as the BBC defines it, is when people stream games over the internet — the much-touted "Netflix for games." The big subscription services in gaming at the time included Ubisoft+, as well as Sony's PlayStation Plus and Xbox Game Pass.
