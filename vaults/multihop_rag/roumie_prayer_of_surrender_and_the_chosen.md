# Roumie's Prayer of Surrender and "The Chosen"

Jonathan Roumie describes a decisive moment five years before this December 2023 interview in which he prayed a simple prayer of surrender, and says that three months later he was called to do "The Chosen".

Asked whether there was a larger message in the experience he has described — praying about leaving all of his anxieties and worries aside and instead trusting that God would take care of things, after which came the role of Jesus in "The Chosen" — Roumie answered: "I think it all comes down to the simple act of surrender to God in a person's life. And I went through this in a very visceral, substantial and transformative way in my life five years ago. I literally was on my knees and I said, 'Jesus, I surrender myself to you. Take care of everything.' And He did."

He drew the interpretation himself: "And then three months later, I got called to do 'The Chosen.' It was a point that I think my life was building to when that happened — and I realized that trying to micromanage God was not something that was working in my favor. And once I let him take the wheel, everything changed. The decisive moment was that simple prayer of surrender." He summarised the general lesson as: "It's a matter of tapping into [our] faith and allowing Him to be more involved in our lives — and surrendering is the first step to getting there."
