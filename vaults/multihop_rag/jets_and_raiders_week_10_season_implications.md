# Jets And Raiders Week 10 Season Implications

The Raiders' Nov. 12, 2023 win over the Jets was their second in as many games since the firing of Josh McDaniels, giving interim coach Antonio Pierce an undefeated 2-0 start to his head coaching career. It also vaulted Las Vegas into a deep AFC playoff race, joining what were now five AFC wild-card contenders with at least five wins.

For the Jets the same game marked a second straight outing without a touchdown and a second consecutive loss, coming before they hit the road to face the Bills in Week 11.
