# Wheels On Meals (1984): The Jackie Chan Action Comedy

Wheels on Meals is a 1984 Hong Kong martial arts action comedy directed by Sammo Hung, running 1h 38m, starring Jackie Chan, Hung and Yuen Biao. Polygon frames it as a standout within a standout body of work: few creative teams have ever managed the consistent level of excellence that Chan, Hung and Yuen did with their 1980s action comedies, and this is one of the best of an outrageously good group of films. Set and shot in Barcelona, it centres on Thomas (Chan) and David (Yuen), cousins who run a food truck — with skateboarding tricks to boot — and become enamoured with a local woman (Lola Forner); when they run into a somewhat incompetent private investigator (Hung) who is also looking for her, the group bands together to save her after she is suddenly kidnapped.

Its defining attraction is the fighting. The film features some of the very best fight scenes of Chan's prolific filmography, as he squares off against legendary kickboxer Benny Urquidez — then among the most prominent and successful fighters in the world, and an opponent Chan would face again in Dragons Forever. Polygon says the whole movie is worth your time, but that their six-minute marathon fight session, findable on YouTube, is among the best things in this world, and recommends Project A, released a year earlier, for one of the most daring stunts of Chan's career. Wheels on Meals streams free with ads on Plex and FreeVee, or is available for digital rental or purchase on Amazon.
