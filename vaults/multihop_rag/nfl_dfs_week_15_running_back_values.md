# NFL DFS Week 15 Running Back Values

Sporting News' headline Week 15 2023 running back DFS plays were Devin Singletary of the Texans at Tennessee (DK $5,400, FD $6,000, SD 1.35x) and Antonio Gibson of the Commanders at the Rams (DK $5,200, FD $5,600, SD 1.4x).

With C.J. Stroud in the league's concussion protocol and Nico Collins (calf) joining Tank Dell (fibula) on the Texans' injury list, Houston suddenly found itself without many impact skill-position players; Singletary, however, had been pretty good and had shown the previous week that he was the main man over Dameon Pierce in DeMeco Ryans' backfield, taking 13 carries for 65 yards and a touchdown against the Jets while Pierce totaled just five touches for seven yards. The column argued not to be scared of Tennessee's run defense because it was not what it used to be under Mike Vrabel — Raheem Mostert and De'Von Achane had just posted 35.1 PPR points against the Titans, Chuba Hubbard had 20.2 a few weeks earlier, and Rachaad White had 17.8 against them in Week 10. For Gibson, the reasoning was that Aaron Donald and friends would be attacking the pocket early and often in pursuit of Sam Howell, who had been sacked 10 more times than any other quarterback, making dump-offs and screens plentiful for a back available at a major discount, at least in PPR formats. More solid Week 15 running back values listed were Ezekiel Elliott, Patriots vs. Chiefs (DK $5,800, FD $6,500, SD 1.35x); Jerick McKinnon, Chiefs at Patriots (DK $5,100, FD $5,500, SD 1.45x); Jordan Mason, 49ers at Cardinals (DK $4,600, FD $4,200, SD 1.6x); and Tyler Allgeier, Falcons at Panthers (DK $4,400, FD $5,700, SD 1.6x).
