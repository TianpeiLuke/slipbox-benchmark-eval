# Max Scherzer's Back Injury And Removal From The Rangers' World Series Roster

Max Scherzer was pulled from Game 3 of the 2023 World Series on Oct. 30 with a lower back injury after three scoreless innings, and by Game 4 the next day he was off the World Series roster for Texas altogether, replaced by Brock Burke.

Scherzer started Game 3 for the Rangers and worked cleanly early. At 8:15 p.m. in the bottom of the first he got Corbin Carroll to ground out to short and walked Ketel Marte to give the Diamondbacks the game's first baserunner, then almost immediately avoided trouble by forcing Gabriel Moreno to ground into a 5-4-3 double play two pitches later to end the inning. At 9:03 p.m., before the bottom of the fourth, came what the live blog called an interesting development: Scherzer was being pulled after just 36 pitches and three scoreless innings. He tried to take the mound for the fourth but appeared to be bothered by a lower back injury; it was unknown whether the injury was correlated to the comebacker he took off his lower back in the second inning. FOX Sports reported that Scherzer left the game with back tightness. Right-hander Jon Gray got the ball in relief and was credited with the win, delivering three scoreless innings with three strikeouts and just one hit allowed in a surprise appearance.

The following day, at 7:18 p.m. before Game 4 on Oct. 31, the Sporting News reported that Scherzer was also off the World Series roster for Texas, having been replaced by Brock Burke.
