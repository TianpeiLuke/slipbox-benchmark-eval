# Robot Labor Supply And The Artificial General Intelligence Goal

Geordie Rose argues that supplying general labor requires general intelligence, not a set of task-specific machines. "We want to be able to provide labor to the world, not just for one thing, but for everybody who needs it," Rose said.

The inference he draws is explicit about what that demands of the systems: "The systems have to be able to think like people. So we could call that artificial general intelligence if you'd like. But what I mean more specifically is the systems have to be able to understand speech and they need to be able to convert the understanding of speech into action, which will satisfy job roles across the entire economy."
