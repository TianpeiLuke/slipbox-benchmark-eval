# A Federal Judge Throws Out Sonos' $32.5 Million Win Against Google

In the week reported by TechCrunch on 2023-10-13, a federal judge threw out the $32.5 million win that the audio company Sonos had scored against Google a while back, after Sonos' patents were deemed unenforceable and invalid.

The sequence matters: the award came first and stood for some time, and the reversal followed from a ruling on the patents themselves rather than on the size of the damages. With the patents held unenforceable and invalid, the judgment they supported did not survive.
