# Gen Z Workplace Purpose And ESG Priorities

By 2030 Gen Z will account for nearly one-third of the U.S. workforce, but they are already radically redefining the meaning of work. Wanting a sense of purpose, they prioritize environmental, social, and governance (ESG) considerations in the workplace — which for them encompasses their companies taking accountability for their sustainability and environmental impacts, providing education and awareness for social issues, and ensuring diverse and inclusive boards and teams.

The behavioral evidence matches the stated priority: roughly two-thirds of Gen Zers say they frequently speak about important societal issues while at work, according to the Edelman report. They are also influencing their older coworkers when it comes to areas like work-life balance, fair pay, and employers' involvement on social issues.
