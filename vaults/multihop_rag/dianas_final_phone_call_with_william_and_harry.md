# Diana's Final Phone Call With William and Harry

Episode three of The Crown season six depicts what the show presents as Princess Diana's final conversation with her sons, and many viewers called it the most devastating moment in the entire series.

In the episode, Diana's partner Dodi Fayed (played by Khalid Abdalla) surprises her with a quick trip to Paris, France. The princess is keen to return home to the United Kingdom and eager to speak to her sons over the phone; a mob of paparazzi following the couple delays Diana's scheduled phone call with William and Harry, until she is able to catch them again later that evening. William, then 15, and Harry, then 12, ask over the phone when their mother will be coming back home from Paris. "I'm coming home tomorrow, and I'm seeing you both tomorrow night," Diana replies, in an episode that also takes place on the same day as her fatal car crash later that evening.

The boys then ask Diana whether she plans on marrying Dodi, given that their relationship has been plastered all over the tabloids. "Well, you know better than to believe the papers," she tells William, before adding: "I'm emphatically not going to marry Dodi. To be honest, I can't wait to come home." When William asks his mother if she is doing "okay", Diana tells her eldest son: "I'm okay. It's just a bit mad here. I don't really understand how I ended up here. Mummy just needs to make some changes to her life, that's all. But that's not your problem, that's mine." Before saying goodbye she reminds them she will see them the next day — a day that never comes: "I'll see you both tomorrow night. Just the three of us."

The moment tugged on the heartstrings of many viewers, who took to social media to share their reactions. "The last phone call between Harry, William and Diana broke my heart," one fan wrote on X, formerly known as Twitter. "That precious phone call between Diana and her sons," said someone else. "Don't mind me, I'm just gonna cry in one corner." A third user commented: "The last call between Harry, William and Diana is heartbreaking."
