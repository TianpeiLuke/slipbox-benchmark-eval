# Spider-Man 2: Venom Origin and Miles Morales' Story

Juggling two Spider-Mans requires a different kind of narrative deftness, and in Marvel's Spider-Man 2 two villains new to the series take center stage — Kraven the Hunter and Venom — alongside a returning cast of nearly every villain from the previous two games. Polygon reported this on 2023-10-16.

Without spoilers, a large majority of that returning cast is sidelined early on, leaving Kraven and Venom as the principal antagonists, with large roles for the Lizard, who made a brief appearance in Miles Morales, and Mister Negative. Others besides them should delight comic fans, setting up future conflicts for expansions or the presumed Spider-Man 3. Miles and Peter return from their individual outings for a dramatic team-up, their stories threaded together to allow randomized encounters where one might show up while you are playing as the other, tag-teaming a random criminal — and yes, they do the Spider-Man-pointing-at-Spider-Man meme in-game, charming the first time if maybe less so the second. The main story sometimes swaps Spider-Mans mid-mission, as in the dramatic opening where Sandman coats the city in dust while Peter and Miles rush to help firefighters and first responders, with eerie echoes of 9/11; side missions often allow either hero, but the main story beats require one or the other so each is allowed his full particularity.

Peter's plot involves a retelling of the Venom origin story, with beats recognizable to anyone even passingly familiar with the Spider-Man mythos. If the game is one of retellings, Peter's story clings most closely to the canon — anyone who has seen Sam Raimi's Spider-Man 3 will know the gist of what to expect when the symbiote finds its way to Peter — and it is not until the late game that his story starts pushing more strongly against canonical memories into something more unique to Insomniac.

Miles' story takes more liberties. Hailey Cooper, his love interest from his solo game, returns in more of an MJ-esque role while carving out her own identity separate from Miles'. American Sign Language features heavily throughout Miles' missions because of Hailey's presence, feeling as natural and integral to the story as anything from the canon. One Miles-specific side quest involves learning about the artistic history of Harlem, entwining the real-life histories of Josephine Baker, Langston Hughes, and Charlie Parker with the fictional history of SHIELD and other Marvel properties, and an early mission shows off Insomniac's technical chops with a section that cleverly borrows a trick from Ratchet & Clank: Rift Apart. Of the two Spider-Mans, it is Miles who feels more alive with the possibility to break free of expectation and canon.
