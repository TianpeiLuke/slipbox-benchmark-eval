# The Humanitarian Corridor Proposal For Gaza

A humanitarian corridor, as called for in Gaza by international aid groups and European Union officials, means a protected path specifically for civilians, aid workers and necessary basic items to pass through safely back and forth from Gaza to Israel and Egypt. Its defining features are protection and restricted purpose: it is not a general opening of the border but a designated safe route for particular people and goods.

As of 2023-10-13 the proposal remained only a proposal — there were no clear plans for such a protected pathway.
