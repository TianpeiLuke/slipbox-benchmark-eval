# Colts -2.5 At Panthers, Week 9

Sporting News took the Colts at -2.5 against the Panthers in Week 9 of 2023, with odds courtesy of Caesars Sportsbook, predicting a final score of Colts 27, Panthers 17. Bryce Young had looked much better once settled into his rookie season, but Carolina still seemed like a work in progress, while the Colts had quietly been running a pretty decent offense — much better than their three-game losing streak might suggest, having scored 65 points between Weeks 7 and 8, 29 more than the Panthers over the same span.

The rest of the case is a quarterback comparison and a matchup edge. In a Gardner Minshew-Bryce Young battle the column took "the 'Shew," with the backfield committee of Jonathan Taylor and Zack Moss against Carolina's abysmal run defense as icing on the cake: during their 1-7 start the Panthers had allowed the most rushing touchdowns (14), the third-most points per game (28.4) and the fourth-most rushing yards per game (139.4). The conclusion was that the Colts would cruise.
