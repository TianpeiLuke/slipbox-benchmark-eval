# Mobile's Share Of Thanksgiving 2023 E-Commerce

Mobile devices had a standout Thanksgiving: Adobe said some $3.3 billion was spent over mobile devices on the day, up 14% and an all-time record for Thanksgiving.

The split between devices depends on time of day and on what is being measured. Overall, desktop sales still exceed other kinds of screens when it comes to conversions and number of items purchased, but mobile is the standout device in the evening hours, when it accounted for a whopping 59% of all online sales — likely because people were using that time to socialize and buy on the sly. Salesforce said that for the day mobile accounted for 79% of all online traffic globally and 82% in the U.S., and that mobile wallets were really making a mark for reducing some of the buying friction on the smaller devices, with Apple Pay transactions up by 47% compared to 44% up for all mobile wallets overall. Social on mobile — Instagram links, TikTok and Snapchat — has become an influencer in itself, collectively accounting for 13% of all traffic referrals to sites, though notably these are not where the purchases are being made for the most part. Salesforce added that online traffic in general to e-commerce sites, including browsing, was up too, but again only in single digits: 4% globally and 6% in the U.S.

"Mobile traffic and sales are soaring as people are on the go once again this holiday weekend," said Salesforce VP and GM, Retail, Rob Garf, in a statement. "Consumers are embracing mobile wallets to break down friction between discovering on social and purchasing on mobile."
