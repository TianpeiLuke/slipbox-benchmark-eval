# Comparing "Renaissance" And "The Eras Tour" As Concert Films

The two 2023 concert films sit at different scales and, in Comscore analyst Paul Dergarabedian's framing, should not be compared too closely: "Renaissance" opened at $21 million against the $92.8 million debut of "Taylor Swift: The Eras Tour" in October, yet both figures dwarf the previous concert-film ceiling, and the tours behind them differ by roughly a factor of three in gross.

The structural relations are these. No one expected "Renaissance" to match "The Eras Tour," which was wrapping up its theatrical run with over $250 million globally; prior to Swift, the biggest concert film debuts — titles held by Miley Cyrus and Justin Bieber for their 2008 and 2011 films — had not surpassed the unadjusted sum of $32 million, so $21 million remains a very good start for a concert film. On the touring side, the 39-city, 56-show "Renaissance" tour kicked off in Stockholm, Sweden in May and ended in Kansas City, Missouri in the fall, making over $500 million and attracting over 2.7 million concertgoers, while Swift's ongoing 151-date "Eras Tour" was expected to gross some $1.4 billion. "To have two concert films topping the chart in a single year is pretty unprecedented," Dergarabedian said. The limit of the comparison is his own: "Taylor Swift was a total outlier and the result of a very specific set of circumstances. These two films are similar in genre only."
