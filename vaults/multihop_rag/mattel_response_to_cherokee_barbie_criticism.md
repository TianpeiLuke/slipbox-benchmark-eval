# Mattel's Response To The Cherokee Barbie Criticism

Mattel spokesperson Devin Tucker said the company is aware of the problem with the Cherokee syllabary on the doll's packaging and is "discussing options." Tucker also noted that Mattel worked on the doll's creation with Wilma Mankiller's estate, which is led by her husband, Charlie Soap, and her friend, Kristina Kiehl.

Cherokee Nation Principal Chief Chuck Hoskin Jr described the company's private response as constructive: "After some of the problems were revealed, we had very good conversations with Mattel. And I think they responded internally to us in a very thoughtful way and expressed some regret for not engaging us."
