# Don McLean's Upcoming Albums and Touring

Don McLean told Fox News Digital in December 2023 that he is working all the time: another new album of his, "American Boys," was coming out in February 2024, and he was getting ready to tour again in the U.S.

He suggested that his touring schedule in the year ahead may take him to the Far East. "As you may have remembered, the president of South Korea [Yoon Suk Yeol] sang 'American Pie' to our president in the White House this year — it was a big story, and he got a guitar signed by me," McLean said of the state dinner event held by the Biden White House on April 26.
