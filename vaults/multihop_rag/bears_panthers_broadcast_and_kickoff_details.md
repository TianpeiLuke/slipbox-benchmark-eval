# Bears-Panthers Broadcast And Kickoff Details

The Chicago Bears vs. Carolina Panthers "Thursday Night Football" game on Thursday, Nov. 9 kicked off at 8:15 p.m. ET (7:15 p.m. local time) from Soldier Field in Chicago, and was aired nationally on Amazon Prime Video, with DAZN carrying it in Canada.

Local over-the-air coverage was available in the two home markets: WFLD (Channel 32) in Chicago and WSOC (Channel 9) in Charlotte, so fans in those markets could watch the Amazon broadcast over the air. Those in Canada could stream it on DAZN, which carries every NFL game all season.

The Amazon broadcast was spearheaded by play-by-play announcer Al Michaels and color commentator Kirk Herbstreit, with sideline reporter Kaylee Hartung offering sideline updates throughout the game. At 9:00 p.m., to start the second quarter, Sexiest Man Alive Jason Kelce joined Michaels and Herbstreit in the booth.
