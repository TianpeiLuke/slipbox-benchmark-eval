# The VAR Intervention Window Before Play Restarts

VAR can only intervene in an incident before play restarts; once the game has resumed, the official cannot act on it. Sporting News invoked this window on Nov. 6, 2023 to explain why one passage of play in Tottenham vs. Chelsea was not reviewed at all.

It was unclear exactly why the incident went unreviewed, but the assumption offered was that the VAR official was likely not aware of it until play had already restarted, at which point he cannot intervene. The boundary condition Sporting News stresses is practical rather than legal: such incidents have to be caught before play restarts, which is extremely difficult to do as the game continues to flow.
