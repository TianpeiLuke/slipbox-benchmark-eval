# Jordan Poyer's Ego And Perspective Shift

Ayahuasca gifted Jordan Poyer perspective, The New York Times reported on 15 December 2023: in previous seasons he believes his responses to successes and failures were too ego-driven — football was him, and he was football — but that is no longer the case.

Ego is a powerful force in professional athletes; it helps them ascend and often hastens their downfalls. Poyer has an NFL logo tattooed on his left shoulder. The change shows in how he frames a hard season: "We still have games left that I'm excited about, but I knew some way, somehow, this universe was going to test me this year," he says. "We lost DaQuan Jones, Matt Milano and Tre'Davious White for the season. It's hard. This s— is hard. And it's not just (the losses). I'm 32, not this young spring chicken anymore. You know, s— hurts all the time. But my perspective is I get to play a game that I love, and I'm healthy enough to play. Every time you come out of a game, that's a win in its own way."

His commitment has not softened: last season, when a rib injury precluded him from airplane travel, he took a 32-hour round-trip drive to play in a game in Kansas City, and he wants to lift a Lombardi Trophy as badly as ever — but if it doesn't happen, he believes he can handle the disappointment better. On Fridays that season, Poyer, fellow safety Taylor Rapp and assistant strength coach Will Greenberg play crystal bowls at Bills headquarters. "The different tones that you can tune into can enhance chakras within you and help you heal," he says.
