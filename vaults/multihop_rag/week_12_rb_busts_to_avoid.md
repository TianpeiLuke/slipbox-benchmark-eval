# Week 12 RB Busts To Avoid

Sporting News' Week 12 bust list, published Nov. 21, 2023, pushes back on starting Derrick Henry with confidence, on the Rams' committee backs Darrell Henderson and Royce Freeman, on Khalil Herbert, and flatly on Chuba Hubbard and Miles Sanders — each entry attacking the assumption that name value or a soft matchup is enough.

On Henry against the Panthers, the column concedes it does not quite have the intestinal fortitude to bench him, but insists expectations must be tempered: over the past two weeks he has tallied just 64 total yards on 23 touches, 2.8 yards per touch. Carolina served as Tony Pollard's get-right game the week before, so fire King Henry up if you have him — but if he falters against the Panthers you had better have a backup plan, starting with picking up Tyjae Spears as insurance. On Henderson and Freeman at the Cardinals, both would be decent dart throws in a week filled with byes, but their floors in a zero-bye week seem too low to trust if Kyren Williams is back at 100 percent; the matchup is juicy, but with no idea what to expect from a touch-share perspective they are no better than desperation, touchdown-dependent flexes.

On Herbert at the Vikings, his return to the field for Chicago against the Lions the previous week left a lot to be desired: he finished with just 35 yards on 16 carries and was vastly overshadowed by Justin Fields' 100-yard rushing effort, while D'Onta Foreman scored the Bears' lone rushing touchdown on the afternoon. On Hubbard and Sanders at the Titans, the verdict is unconditional — it does not matter what your team looks like, you should not be starting them. Neither was awful against Dallas the previous week on a per-touch basis, but neither finished with more than 12 touches, and no Carolina back has enjoyed a 60-yard rushing game since Oct. 15: bad offensive line, negative game scripts, awful coaching.
