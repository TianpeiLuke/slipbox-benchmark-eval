# Flexport And Convoy Technology Acquisition Talks

Flexport is in talks to acquire the technology of Convoy, the once buzzy digital freight startup that abruptly shuttered after failing to find a buyer, TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-30. The possible deal was reported by WSJ and came without other details, but still made TechCrunch's writer raise an eyebrow.

If Flexport acquires the technology, the company plans to restore Convoy's trucking services for as many customers and partners as possible, according to WSJ's source — which, TechCrunch notes, is going to cost money, prompting the question of whether Petersen's reign of financial frugality is already over.
