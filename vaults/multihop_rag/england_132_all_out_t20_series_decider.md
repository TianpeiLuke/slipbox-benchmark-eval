# England 132 All Out In The T20 Series Decider

England were bowled out for 132 in the deciding fifth T20I on Dec. 21, 2023, less than half the 267-3 they had made on the same surface two days earlier, leaving West Indies needing 133 to win the series.

The Guardian's live blog set the contrast starkly: Tuesday at Tarouba, England 267-3 with 19 sixes; Thursday, England 132 all out with five sixes. England assembled exactly the same players in exactly the same place and exactly the same situation — losing the toss, put in to bat — but this was an entirely different performance, on a wicket once so true that turned untrustworthy. The report suggested that as a tour enters its final days thoughts often turn to home, and perhaps the focus was not as laser-sharp as it might have been; whatever the reason, the pyrotechnics of the two previous matches never sparked. It took a fabulous delivery from Gudakesh Motie to end Phil Salt's run of absurd form with his score a modest 38, but some of the other wickets seemed a little careless: in the fourth over Jos Buttler casually turned a Jason Holder delivery to fine leg where Oshane Thomas was positioned, hands cupped, and Harry Brook tried to paddle-sweep Motie but sent the ball only a few feet into the air for an easy Nicholas Pooran catch, making it 70 for four one ball into the ninth over, at which point Liam Livingstone and Moeen Ali had to focus on disaster prevention rather than entertainment. Akeal Hosein's four overs went for just 20 and brought the wickets of Will Jacks and Moeen — the latter undone by a relay catch on the boundary after he and Livingstone added 40 for the fifth wicket — and Livingstone followed precisely two overs later, prodding the ball limply back to Motie, at which point things really fell apart, the last five wickets surrendered in 20 balls for just 11 runs. England were 109 for four after 14 overs and then the house fell down: in the 19th over Andre Russell, who bowled really well, finished with two for 25 and would have had three wickets in four balls had Hosein taken a tricky low chance at third man to dismiss Sam Curran; Adil Rashid was run out for 1 at 132-9, sacrificing himself just as in the 2019 World Cup final so that Curran could stay on strike with four balls remaining; and Curran clouted the next ball to long-off, caught by Rutherford off Holder for 12, to end the innings.
