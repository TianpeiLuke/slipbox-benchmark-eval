# Sly

Sly, a Netflix documentary about Sylvester Stallone, was available to stream on Netflix in early November 2023. It runs 1 hour 35 minutes, is directed by Thom Zimny, and features Sylvester Stallone, Arnold Schwarzenegger, and Quentin Tarantino.

Polygon describes it as a close look at the life of one of the great American movie stars and film writers. Netflix's own synopsis, reproduced in Polygon's November catalog listing, frames it around origins and arc: his love of film began as an escape from a rocky childhood, and from underdog to Hollywood legend, Stallone tells his story in the documentary.
