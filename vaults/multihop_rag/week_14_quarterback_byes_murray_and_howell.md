# Week 14 Quarterback Byes: Murray And Howell

Week 14 of the 2023 season was the last week of byes, with the Cardinals and Commanders the last two teams to take their mandatory time off, putting starting-caliber quarterbacks Kyler Murray and Sam Howell on the shelf.

Their absence slightly impacted the quarterback pool, but Sporting News judged the bigger losses came on the defensive side of the ball: fantasy owners would not be able to start quarterbacks going up against two subpar pass defenses in Washington and Arizona. Starting any quarterback facing the Commanders' swiss-cheese defense had yielded strong returns, and Arizona had been just as inefficient, entering Week 13 ranking 30th in dropback EPA.
