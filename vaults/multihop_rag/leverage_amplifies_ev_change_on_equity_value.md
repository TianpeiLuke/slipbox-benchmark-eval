# Leverage Amplifies The Effect Of An EV Change On Equity Value

In EV-based valuation, any change in enterprise value impacts only the equity value, and leverage scales that impact. Take a company whose EV is ₹100 with ₹50 of debt: the value of its equity is ₹50. If EV increases by 10 per cent, the value of equity increases by 20 per cent, and the reverse is also true.

The relation generalises in one direction: the higher the leverage, the more the impact of a change in EV on the value of the stock, and this holds both ways, on the upside and the downside.
