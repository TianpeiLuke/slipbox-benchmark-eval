# Zipcar's NHTSA Recall Fine

Zipcar is getting hit with a fine from the NHTSA for "renting vehicles with open, unrepaired recalls," TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-22.

One recall concerns 2015-2017 Ford Transit Vans, which continued to appear on Zipcar's platform despite safety issues with the vehicles.
