# AI As A Clinician's Ally, Not A Replacement

Patel argues that AI's role in health care is to be an ally: while its capabilities are "immense and ever-growing," it strengthens rather than substitutes for the human relationship at the center of care.

His grounds are the parts of care that cannot be modeled. "Humans caring for humans is the bedrock of health care -- AI strengthens that foundation, but doesn't replace it," he said. "AI can offer insights and assist in decision-making, but human touch, intuition and empathy cannot be replicated by algorithms."

The warrant is a posture rather than a prohibition: while AI promises a "new frontier" in health care, Patel calls for a balance of "enthusiasm and caution." "By recognizing AI's limitations and actively working to address them, we can harness AI's potential while safeguarding the core values of our health care system," he said.
