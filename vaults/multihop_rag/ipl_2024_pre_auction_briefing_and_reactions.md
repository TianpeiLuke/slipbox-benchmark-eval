# IPL 2024 Pre-Auction Briefing And Reactions

In the hours before the IPL 2024 auction began, the live blog logged arrivals, franchise messages and released-player lists. At 12:40 PM IST, after his homecoming at KKR, mentor Gautam Gambhir arrived at the auction centre.

At 11.54 AM a message came in for Sourav Ganguly, from Parth Jindal (@ParthJindal11) on December 19, 2023: "Will miss being at the auctions today but have no doubt that under the leadership of Kiran and @SGanguly99 we will build a very strong squad for @IPL – all the best @DelhiCapitals – I know a lot of hard work has gone into today". At 11.47 AM, the blog noted that if Aakash Chopra was to be believed, Coetzee could get well over INR 10 cr.

At 11.06 AM the released players from CSK were listed — Akash Singh, Ambati Rayudu, Ben Stokes, Bhagath Varma, Dwaine Pretorius, Kyle Jamieson, Sisanda Magala and Subhranshu Senapati — with the question of whether they would go for Rachin. Earlier, at 9.20 AM, came news for Delhi Capitals fans that Rishabh Pant would be part of the auction and would make a comeback for the next year's IPL.
