# Justin Jefferson's Week 10 Injury Status

Justin Jefferson (hamstring) was officially ruled OUT for the Vikings' Week 10 game against the Saints, with NFL Network's Tom Pelissero reporting that Minnesota would not activate him before Sunday. He had been listed as "questionable" ahead of the 1 p.m. ET kickoff, and fantasy owners did not have to wait long for clarity, because to be eligible to play he needed to be activated by 4 p.m. ET on Saturday.

The expectation had been cautious. Earlier in the week head coach Kevin O'Connell said it would be "a little aggressive" for Jefferson to get back on the field, though three straight limited practices were enough for the team to at least weigh the decision.

The fantasy read came with a caveat about the quarterback. Jefferson's outlook is always through the roof, but it was an open question whether that changed with Josh Dobbs under center instead of Kirk Cousins. K.J. Osborn (concussion) was also listed as "questionable," with his status steadily improving from DNP on Wednesday to full practice on Friday. If Jefferson were back he would be a must-start; with him out and Osborn returning, both Osborn and Jordan Addison were worth using.
