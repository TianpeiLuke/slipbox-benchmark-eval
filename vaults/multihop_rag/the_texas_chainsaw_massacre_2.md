# The Texas Chainsaw Massacre 2

The Texas Chainsaw Massacre 2 is nothing like its predecessor, and Polygon recommends it anyway: it was the Oct. 26 pick in the 2023-10-10 Halloween Countdown, listed on Max.

Polygon's account of the original is that The Texas Chain Saw Massacre is a nauseating smoothie of rural horror, outré indie experimentation, and wannabe snuff film — its murders are not real, but you would be forgiven for being convinced the actors are in genuine trouble, since from the budget film stock to the filthy sets it all feels off. The result is not just a great horror flick but a monumental work that originated many tropes of its genre and, most recently, earned a coveted spot in the Sight and Sound list of the greatest films of all time.

The sequel is a different proposition: you will not see its name in a film school syllabus, and the films it inspired can be counted on one hand. Critic CP nonetheless finds something commendable in the gall of Tobe Hooper, the original director, returning to his series with the energy of a middle-aged man on his first ayahuasca retreat — Leatherface gets in a high-speed car battle, eventually standing on the roof of a speeding sedan; Dennis Hopper dual-wields chainsaws; a radio station office is obliterated like a novelty rage room. Sometimes you want a steak and sometimes you want a hamburger, and this one is a damn juicy burger.
