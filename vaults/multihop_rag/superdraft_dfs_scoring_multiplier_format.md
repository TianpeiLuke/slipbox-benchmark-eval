# SuperDraft's Multiplier-Based DFS Scoring Format

SuperDraft's DFS scoring involves point multipliers rather than traditional salary-capped or tiered contests: each player is assigned a scoring multiplier based on their value as determined by SuperDraft, and users can roster any player in their lineup with no restrictions.

The discriminating feature against salary-cap formats is that price is expressed as a boost rather than a cost, so the sleepers who carry lower salaries on DraftKings and FanDuel instead have higher boosts on SuperDraft. A winning lineup will likely feature a strong mix of stud fantasy contributors with 1x-1.6x boosts as well as matchup-based sleepers with larger multipliers. The most notable scoring rules for SuperDraft NFL contests are half-point PPR, four-point passing touchdowns, and two-point bonuses for 100 rushing yards, 100 receiving yards and 300 passing yards. Sporting News noted across its December 2023 columns that it had begun incorporating SuperDraft into its weekly DFS coverage that year, listing favorite weekly plays on the platform alongside DraftKings and FanDuel.
