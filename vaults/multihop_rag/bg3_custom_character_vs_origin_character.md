# Custom Character Versus Origin Character In Baldur's Gate 3

An Origin character in Baldur's Gate 3 is one of the NPCs from your party that you can simply choose and play as right from the start, whereas a custom character is one you build yourself by picking a race and background.

The discriminating feature is the trade between setup effort and narrative freedom. Playing an Origin character is a perfectly fine way to play if you would rather not go through the process of picking a race and background, but you are then limited to that character's predetermined story. A custom character is a bit more work at the beginning, yet gives you far more freedom to experience the entire story of Baldur's Gate 3. Polygon presents this as the decision to settle before moving on to the class rankings themselves.
