# NFL Week 13 Injuries Colts At Titans

In the Week 13 final injury reports published by CBSSports.com on Dec. 1, 2023, with the Colts listed at -1 at the Titans, Indianapolis ruled out cornerback JuJu Brents (quad) and running back Jonathan Taylor (thumb), while Tennessee listed wide receiver Treylon Burks (concussion) as questionable.

Indianapolis ruled out Brents and Taylor after both missed the entire week of practice. Taylor underwent surgery on his thumb that week and was expected to miss at least a couple of weeks, which would thrust Zack Moss into a more prominent role in the Colts backfield. Burks was the only Titan with an injury designation entering the weekend, and the wideout was a full participant in Friday's practice, but he would need to clear the final stages of concussion protocol in order to play.
