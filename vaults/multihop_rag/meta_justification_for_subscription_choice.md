# Meta's Justification For The Subscription Choice

In its blog post Meta claims the choice — to pay it for privacy, or else agree to being tracked — "balances the requirements of European regulators while giving users choice and allowing Meta to continue serving all people in the EU, EEA and Switzerland."

The claim's grounds are the three things it says the offer reconciles: regulatory requirements, user choice, and continued service across the region. TechCrunch registers its scepticism of the warrant joining them in one line: "But, well, it would say that."
