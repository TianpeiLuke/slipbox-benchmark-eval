# Console Subscription And Store Gift Cards For Gamers

If you're not sure which type of games your giftee prefers, The Verge suggests an Xbox, PlayStation, or Nintendo subscription: these memberships grant access to free digital titles and come with perks such as online multiplayer and cloud-based saves.

For a PlayStation 4 or PlayStation 5 owner, PlayStation Plus grants access to free titles and discounts every month, online play, and cloud-based backups; memberships start at $9.99 a month, and you can subscribe directly via PlayStation or buy a three-month subscription with a PlayStation Plus gift card, available at Amazon, Best Buy, and Target for $30. Nintendo Switch lovers might enjoy an annual Nintendo Switch Online subscription starting at $19.99 a year (Amazon, Best Buy, and GameStop), which lets them play more than 70 retro games from the NES, SNES, and original Game Boy eras, play online with friends, and use cloud saves. Spending more buys the annual Nintendo Switch Online + Expansion Pack, which adds Game Boy Advance, Sega Genesis, and Nintendo 64 games plus DLC content for some titles: individual plans cost $49.99 (Amazon, Walmart), while a family plan covering up to eight accounts is currently available via gift card at Amazon and Walmart for $71.89, $8 off. Alternatively, a gift card to a store like GameStop (Amazon, GameStop) is useful if you don't know which console your giftee prefers or want to give them the option of buying accessories and games.
