# Amazon Kindle And Kindle Paperwhite

The standard Kindle dropped to $75 for October Prime Day. This model saw a lot of improvements when it was updated in 2022: capacity was doubled to 16GB and the screen resolution jumped to 300 ppi for much clearer text. Engadget named it the best budget option in its ereader guide because it is a perfect entry point to reading and borrowing ebooks and audiobooks.

The Kindle Paperwhite was down to $95 for an 8GB model — $5 more than it went for during July's Prime Day event, but still $45 off its MSRP.

The Paperwhite adds a few extra perks over the standard Kindle, including a larger 6.8-inch screen, a more comprehensive front light and IPX8-rated waterproofing. The deal applies to the ad-supported version, which displays ads for Kindle books and services on your lock screen; if you want no ads on your device you will need to pay $20 more.
