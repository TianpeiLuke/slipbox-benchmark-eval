# Ole Miss-Georgia: Late Scoring And The Final Whistle

The closing stretch of Georgia's 52-17 win over Ole Miss on Nov. 11, 2023 saw the Bulldogs pull further clear before the game ended at 10:24 p.m., a statement win for the SEC East champions that extended their win streak to 27.

At 9:59 p.m. Ole Miss receiver Dayton Wade made an incredible one-handed catch on a five-yard pass from Spencer Sanders — one the live blog said you would have to see to believe — with the score at Ole Miss 14, Georgia 45. At 10:14 p.m. Andrew Paul ran in a 4-yard touchdown to push Georgia's lead to 52-14, with four minutes and 12 seconds remaining on the clock. A 29-yard Caden Davis field goal, logged in the blog at 8:05 p.m., added three more points for the Rebels, who still trailed Georgia heavily. The final score stood at Ole Miss 17, Georgia 52.
