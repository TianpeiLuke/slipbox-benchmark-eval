# TP-Link Kasa Smart Plug Mini Deal

The smart plug Engadget recommends for most homes is TP-Link's Kasa Smart Plug mini, and a four-pack was on sale for $35 for Black Friday 2023 — about $3 more than it sold for during Amazon's October Prime Day sale, but still a decent $15 savings.

Plugs like these are a simple way to add some smart capabilities to any home, letting you turn on lights with just your voice and set automated schedules and routines triggered by other activities. Engadget suggests they would make a great stocking stuffer for anyone curious about smart home connectivity who hasn't yet taken the plunge.
