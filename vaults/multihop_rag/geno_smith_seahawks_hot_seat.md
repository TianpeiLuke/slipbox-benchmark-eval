# Geno Smith Seahawks Hot Seat

Yardbarker's Nov. 30, 2023 NFC hot-seat roundup named quarterback Geno Smith as the Seattle Seahawks' hottest seat: the Seahawks passed on drafting a QB in 2023 and re-signed Smith, but he had taken a significant step back.

The grounds are his form and his contract. Entering Thursday's game against Dallas, his completion percentage was down from 2022, from 69.8 to 65.4 percent, despite the impressive offensive weapons around him. Seattle also had an out in his contract after that season that would carry only a $17.4 million dead-cap number, per Spotrac. The warrant joining them is that declining play plus a cheap exit makes the incumbent easy to move on from. The entry was written by Adam Gretz.
