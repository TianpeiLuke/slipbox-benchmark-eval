# John J. Ray III Running The FTX Estate

What's left of FTX is now being led by John J. Ray III — the man who cleaned up Enron, and who has said FTX is worse than Enron.

Just before the trial began, the FTX lawyers filed suit against Bankman-Fried's parents, saying they should give back millions of dollars they got from their kid. Ray has also referred to Bankman-Fried's conduct as "really just old-fashioned embezzlement."
