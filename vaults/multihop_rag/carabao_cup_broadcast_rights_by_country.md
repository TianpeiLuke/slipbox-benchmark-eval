# Carabao Cup 2023/24 Broadcast Rights By Country

The Carabao Cup was carried on Sky Sports in the UK, ESPN+ in the USA, DAZN in Canada and beIN Sports in Australia in 2023/24, per Sporting News on Dec. 19, 2023.

The listed grid was: UK — TV channel Sky Sports, streaming Sky Go and NOW TV; USA — no TV channel listed, streaming ESPN+; Canada — no TV channel listed, streaming DAZN; Australia — TV channel beIN Sports, streaming beIN Connect and Kayo Sports.

The accompanying notes said Sky Sports was the exclusive broadcaster of the Carabao Cup in the UK; that ESPN's streaming platform ESPN+ was the home of the competition in the USA, with select matches from early rounds available to be streamed followed by all matches from the Round of 16 through to the final; that DAZN had the Carabao Cup streaming rights in Canada with select matches carried from the early rounds; and that the broadcaster for fans in Australia was beIN Sports.
