# Dallas Goedert As The Eagles' Tight End Showdown Pick

Sporting News took Dallas Goedert ($6,200) in the FLEX of its Week 15 2023 Eagles-Seahawks Showdown lineup, calling him one of the players who should help Philadelphia get right.

Goedert was healthy once again, having returned against the Cowboys the previous week and settled in nicely by collecting all four of his targets for 30 yards. The column had noted Philadelphia's lack of a true WR3, and argued that is essentially Goedert's role given the lack of receiver depth; as a trusted option in the passing game, he should find success against a defense that had been shredded by tight ends in back-to-back weeks.
