# England vs South Africa: 2023 Rugby World Cup Semi-Final

England faced defending champions South Africa in the Rugby World Cup semi-final in Paris from 8:00pm on 21 October 2023, having secured victory over Fiji in the quarter-finals, with talkSPORT carrying updates from all the action.

Victory would take England into their second consecutive Rugby World Cup final. Owen Farrell would be looking to inspire England to victory against the Springboks. The fixture formed one half of a double crunch point for England against South Africa on the same day, as they also met in the ODI Cricket World Cup — talkSPORT framed it as crunch time for England in the World Cups.
