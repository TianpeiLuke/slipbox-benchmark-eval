# 2023/24 Champions League Group Stage Overview

The UEFA Champions League reached the end of its group stage with 16 precious places in the first knockout round claimed, per Sporting News on Dec. 12, 2023.

Manchester City were looking to go back-to-back after their triumph in the previous season's Champions League, potentially becoming the first English club since Nottingham Forest in 1980 to successfully defend the trophy. The likes of Bayern Munich, Real Madrid and Barcelona had also enjoyed strong group-stage campaigns and would surely be in the mix towards the sharp end of the competition, while Atletico Madrid and Real Sociedad completed the Spanish contingent in the next round.

Manchester United and Newcastle United would play no further part in Europe, having been eliminated by finishing bottom of their groups; Paris Saint-Germain would be in the Round of 16. Sporting News reviewed the group stage ahead of its final match night and examined what teams from each group needed to qualify for the Round of 16.
