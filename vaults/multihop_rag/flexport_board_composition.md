# Flexport's Board Composition

Flexport's board was made up largely of investors who were betting on the founder — a composition that matters because Clark's first quarterly board meeting as sole CEO was June 1 and his second was Aug. 31, days before he was forced out.

The board included Founders Fund's Trae Stephens, who had helped start defense-tech firm Anduril Industries, and Michael Ronen, who left SoftBank in 2020. Andreessen Horowitz was represented by Bob Swan, an operating partner at the firm and former CEO of Intel.
