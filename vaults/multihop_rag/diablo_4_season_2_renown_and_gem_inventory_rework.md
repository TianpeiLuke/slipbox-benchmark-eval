# Diablo 4 Season 2 Renown and Gem Inventory Rework

Two of Diablo 4's other biggest pain points — gems and Renown — are also being addressed in season 2, as Polygon reported on 2023-10-04.

The grind to accrue Renown, the resource players get for completing activities in each area, is no longer something players will need to partake in every season: much like the Altars of Lilith change in season 1, players who have fully unlocked their Renown track with one character will never have to do it again. Previously players had to max out Renown — which all but required doing nearly every dungeon and most side quests in the game — every season in order to get all available Skill and Paragon Points. Unlike with the Altars of Lilith carryover, players will not need to first log into a character that has completed the Renown track in order to propagate that data to the rest of their account; associate game director Joe Piepiora said all of that data migration should happen without that extra step in season 2.

Polygon calls this yet another massive 180 on the Renown system from Blizzard in barely five months' time. Asked about the shift, Piepiora said players simply were not having fun redoing the grind every season, hence the move — as with the Altars last season — to alleviate the pressure on players to only play "efficiently."

As for gems, they will no longer take up bag space, as they are now considered crafting materials. That change comes alongside a massive upgrade to the Stash itself: Stashes now appear in more locations, including all minor towns with a Waypoint in them, and players can search their Stashes for specific items and affixes.
