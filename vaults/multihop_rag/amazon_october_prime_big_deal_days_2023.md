# Amazon's October Prime Big Deal Days 2023

Prime Big Deal Days is Amazon's second Prime Day-like sale event for 2023, a storewide sale for Amazon Prime members that kicked off in the wee hours of October 10 and officially ended on Wednesday. It brought back many of the same steep discounts seen during the summer's Prime Day as well as new sales, and Engadget put together category roundups for deals on headphones, tablets, TVs, video games and other gadgets.

The framing is early holiday shopping: it is a great opportunity to get holiday shopping done early, or to make up for missing out on any of the deals from Prime Day in July, and a good time to start checking off a gift list so you are not pressed for time or funds when Black Friday and Cyber Monday come around next month.

Membership is not a universal requirement. A few deals are just for Prime members, but many are open to anyone looking to save; some, but not all, of the headphone offers are only accessible with a Prime subscription.

The discounts also did not all end with the event. Although the sale officially ended on Wednesday, a few deals were still live heading into the weekend: while Amazon returned most of its own devices, like Echos and Kindles, to full price, a handful of products from other brands like Google, Samsung and Anker maintained the lows they hit on Prime Day. The headphone roundup's highlights included new lows on the AirPods Pro, Sony WF-1000XM5, Beats Studio Pro and Amazon Echo Buds.
