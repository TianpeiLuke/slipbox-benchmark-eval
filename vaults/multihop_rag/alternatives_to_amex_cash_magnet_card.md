# Alternatives To The Amex Cash Magnet Card

Two alternatives are offered if the American Express Cash Magnet Card isn't the best choice for you. The Blue Cash Everyday Card from American Express is similar to the Cash Magnet Card in its fees and APR offers but has a different rewards structure: 3% cash back on up to $6,000 spent on groceries per year, 3% cash back on up to $6,000 spent on U.S. online retail purchases per year, 3% cash back on up to $6,000 spent at gas stations per year, and 1% cash back on all other purchases.

The Discover it Cash Back Card, like the Cash Magnet, doesn't have an annual fee, but it has a very different rewards system than the Amex card: rather than earning a flat cash back rate, you can earn 5% cash back on up to $1,500 in combined quarterly purchases in various categories throughout the year, then 1%.
