# PlayStation DualSense Controller Deal

Amazon, Walmart, PlayStation Direct and several other retailers discounted a bunch of PlayStation DualSense controllers ahead of Black Friday 2023, including many of the colorways that typically go for $75: Cosmic Red, Starlight Blue, Nova Pink and other models could be picked up for $50 each, just about a record-low price.

These controllers work with the PS5 as well as PC, and they sport adaptive L2 and R2 triggers, haptic feedback and a built-in mic array.
