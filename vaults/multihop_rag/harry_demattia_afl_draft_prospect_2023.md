# Harry DeMattia AFL Draft Prospect 2023

Harry DeMattia — pronounced De-matt-e-ya — profiled in Kevin Sheehan's top 40 for the 2023 AFL Draft as reported by The Roar on Nov. 15, 2023, is a midfielder/forward with a combination of speed and endurance which enables him to cover the ground and compete in the air and at ground level. State: Victoria Country. State League Club: Dandenong Stingrays. Community Club: Edithvale-Aspendale. Date of Birth: 11/09/05. Height: 185cm.

A left footer, he brings real pressure with his ability to lay run down tackles against the flow of play. He shared the role of Victoria Country co-captain with Harley Reid and performed consistently, and had an exciting year with the Dandenong Stingrays averaging 19 disposals and 4.8 tackles to earn selection in the Coates Talent League Team of the Year. He recorded a top 10 finish in the 20m sprint at the national Draft Combine with a slick 2.99 seconds. He is a dual sports athlete who was a member of Australia's under-19 cricket program. Weapon: Speed and endurance. AFL player comparison: Jordan Dawson (Adelaide Crows).
