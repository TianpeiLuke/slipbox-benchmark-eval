# Eddie Hearn On Opetaia Emulating Usyk And Unifying Cruiserweight

Promoter Eddie Hearn argued in December 2023 that Jai Opetaia has the potential to replicate Oleksandr Usyk's career — unifying the cruiserweight division before moving up to the heavyweights: the claim is that Opetaia will do the exact same thing as Usyk, and the warrant is that he is a pound-for-pound talent whom every fight man already recognises as the No. 1 cruiserweight in the world.

"Every fight man knows," Hearn said of Opetaia, adding: "I believe Jai's going to do the exact same thing (as Usyk) … we're talking about a pound-for-pound talent."

The report attaches a near-term scheduling detail to that path: Opetaia's next fight could be on the Tyson Fury-Oleksandr Usyk card in Riyadh on 17 February.
