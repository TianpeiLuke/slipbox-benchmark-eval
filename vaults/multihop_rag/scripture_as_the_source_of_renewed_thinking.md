# Scripture as the Source of Renewed Thinking

Pastor Jesse Bradley's claim is that Scripture, and the relationship with God it opens, is the source of the new thoughts that replace unhelpful first ones — turning to Jesus for a deeper meaning and learning not to take his "first thoughts" for fact was, he said, crucial to his overall mental health.

The grounds are claims about what God's Word does and what daily attention to it produces. "The Word of God is living and active, bringing strength, courage, wisdom, and protection," he said. "God's thoughts and ways are higher than ours, but when we spend time, each day reading His Word and listening to the voice of the Good Shepherd, we are filled with new thoughts that change the trajectory of our lives."

The warrant tying Scripture to the practice of positive thinking is a claim about Jesus himself: Jesus brought more positivity than anyone — which is why it is important to keep faith aligned with that practice. "Jesus exudes a trustworthy positivity that is full of meaning, faithfulness, and transformation," Bradley noted. "God renews our thinking and vision, empowers us to make intentional choices about our focus and perspective and trains us how to win the battle of our minds," he added. And because the source is a person rather than a technique, the conclusion is relational: "Positive thinking is relational, and there is no greater source of joy in our lives than God."
