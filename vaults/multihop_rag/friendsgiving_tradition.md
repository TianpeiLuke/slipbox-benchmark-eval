# The Friendsgiving Tradition

Friendsgiving is a popular tradition held around Thanksgiving that consists of a celebration with close pals, arising because some Americans do not have the opportunity to return home for the holidays.

Its defining feature is that it is not fixed to the holiday itself: Friendsgiving can be held before, during, or even after the 23rd of November in 2023. Over the years, people have celebrated it in a similar vein to Thanksgiving, with everyone sitting around the table for one big feast, and it is often done potluck style, with each guest bringing their own dish to contribute.

The big meal is a convention rather than a requirement — you do not need to have one to celebrate your Friendsgiving, as there are many ways to change up the usual plans. The tradition sits alongside Thanksgiving itself, which remains a day filled with food and family, as Americans write their menus and buy ingredients for their lavish feasts.
