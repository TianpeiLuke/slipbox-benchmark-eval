# Istanbul 1993 Defeat As Formative Experience

Mike Phelan, who started in central midfield in Istanbul in 1993, argued that the defeat galvanised the squad — the claim being that a team sometimes has to go through such nights to realise what it takes, as reported by The Guardian on Nov. 27, 2023.

The supporting fact is that six of United's squad that failed in 1993 went on to win the competition six years later. "It did galvanise us," Phelan said. "Winning the Cup Winners' Cup took us to another level and we felt pretty comfortable with each other and felt we could develop into a serious team, and I think going into the Champions League was one of those moments where we expected to do well – the experiences along the way were great and took us to the next level further down the line." The warrant he offers is about the breadth of what has to be experienced: "I think that it is important sometimes that you have to go through those moments to realise what it takes. There are elements that happen in a football match and elements that happen in the buildup to a football match, so you have to experience all those things if you want to be successful."
