# CM Punk WWE Return Speculation Ahead Of Survivor Series 2023

CM Punk's next move after his exit from AEW was one of the most talked about subjects in the pro wrestling fandom, with fans particularly inclined to think he might return to his old stomping ground, WWE, as reported by Essentially Sports on 21 November 2023.

The circumstances given are that the Second City Saint was released from the Jacksonville-based promotion back in September 2023 due to his backstage altercation with Jack Perry at AEW All In 2023. Following this there were multiple speculations about what might be the former WWE star's next move, and he had been dropping multiple hints sparking rumours.

The specific trigger for the Survivor Series speculation is location: the latest Premium Live Event, Survivor Series, would be held in his hometown of Chicago, Illinois. While the possibility of his return exists, the article notes there are a few WWE stars he might lock horns with following such a return.
