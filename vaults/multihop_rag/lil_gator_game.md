# Lil Gator Game

Lil Gator Game, developed by MegaWobble for Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, Windows PC, Xbox One, and Xbox Series X, is ranked 35 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31.

Critic Chris Grant acknowledges that in an unprecedented year for game releases, readers may be surprised to see it this high on the list — and says he was just as surprised to find, in a moment of introspection, that it was one of his own favorite games of 2023.

At the risk of diminishing its accomplishments, he offers a comparison that should cut to the quick for interested parties: this is the most A Short Hike-alike he has discovered yet, a singular high he has been chasing for years. His recommendation is for a lazy weekend afternoon spent playing — not gaming, but playing, in the joyous, unstructured sense of a day in the park.
