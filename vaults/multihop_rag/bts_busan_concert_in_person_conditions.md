# Conditions At The BTS Busan Concert

Polygon's writer, who attended the Yet to Come show in Busan, reports that getting 50,000 people into a 20-year-old stadium was not simple: by the time BTS took the stage, most attendees had been in line for hours with limited access to bathrooms, water, or information about what was going on.

Once the concert started, conditions in the 3,000-strong standing areas got intense as fans pushed toward the stage, crowding people at the front and prompting BTS to alter plans to come down into the audience for their performance of their 2018 hit "IDOL." The writer notes this was not her first chaotic, stressful in-person stadium concert experience and doubts it will be her last, and states the scope condition plainly: she is able and willing to make that trade for the chance to see her favorite musical group perform, but not everyone is, even if they could get tickets.
