# The Cargo Shorts Photograph With Clinton And Blair

During Adam Yedidia's testimony, the court was shown a photograph of former President Bill Clinton and former Prime Minister Tony Blair onstage in suits next to Bankman-Fried, who was in a rumpled T-shirt and cargo shorts. The defense counsel tried to elicit testimony from Yedidia that the cargo shorts were evidence of Bankman-Fried's humility.

The Verge's reporter read the image the other way. Dressing down is a staple of Silicon Valley startup culture — and a sign of its swagger — and the photograph seemed to signify the opposite of humility: that the man in cargo shorts is so important he does not need to dress up, even to meet former world leaders. Her closing observation, from the October 26th, 2023 dispatch: now, in court, Bankman-Fried is wearing a suit.
