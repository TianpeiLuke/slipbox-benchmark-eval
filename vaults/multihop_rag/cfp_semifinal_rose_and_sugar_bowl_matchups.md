# CFP Semifinal Matchups: Rose And Sugar Bowls

The College Football Playoff semifinals were Alabama vs. Michigan in the Rose Bowl presented by Prudential and Texas vs. Washington in the Allstate Sugar Bowl.

The Rose Bowl game takes place at the Rose Bowl in Pasadena, CA, with kickoff scheduled for 5 p.m. ET, broadcast on ESPN. The Sugar Bowl takes place at the Caesars Superdome in New Orleans, LA, with kickoff scheduled for 8:45 p.m. ET, also broadcast on ESPN.
