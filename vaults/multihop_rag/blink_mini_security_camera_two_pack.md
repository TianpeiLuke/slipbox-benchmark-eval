# Blink Mini Security Camera Two-Pack

A two-pack of Blink Mini security cameras dropped to $40 for Prime Day. That is about $10 more than they went for at the previous Prime Day, but still a decent 38 percent discount.

These cameras are wired, unlike the standard wireless Blink cameras.

They have most of the same features as those wireless models, including 1080p video recordings and support for two-way talk and motion alerts.
