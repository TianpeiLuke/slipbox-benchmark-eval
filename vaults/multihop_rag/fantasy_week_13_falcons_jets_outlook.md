# Week 13 Falcons-Jets: A Leaky Atlanta Defense Lifts Garrett Wilson And Breece Hall

CBS Sports argues that the Falcons defense, despite one strong showing, is still weak enough to give better-than-normal numbers to the Jets' best skill players in Week 13. The grounds: Atlanta's defense finally had a strong showing against the Saints last week, but got a little lucky because so many New Orleans players left with injuries; in the three games prior, the unit had a knack for making mediocre offenses look amazing — Will Levis' breakout game, Josh Dobbs' first game with the Vikings and Kyler Murray's first game with the Cardinals. The warrant is bounded rather than sweeping: the author is pretty certain they are not bad enough to make Tim Boyle look like Tim Tebow or even Tim Hasselbeck, but wonders if they are juuust bad enough to boost Garrett Wilson and Breece Hall. A second supporting ground is that reinforcements along the offensive line should help the Jets' cause against a Falcons defense that has blitzed at a league-average rate, is eighth-lowest in pass rush pressure rate and is fifth-lowest in sacks with 28.

The verdicts follow from that split judgment — the skill players are playable while neither quarterback is: STARTS — Bijan Robinson, Garrett Wilson, Breece Hall, Drake London, the Jets DST and the Falcons DST; SITS — Kyle Pitts, Tyler Conklin, Tyler Allgeier and both quarterbacks.
