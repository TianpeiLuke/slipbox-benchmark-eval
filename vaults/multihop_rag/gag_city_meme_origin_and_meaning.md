# Gag City: The Meme's Origin And Meaning

Gag City is the pink metropolis inhabited by stans and brands alike — a dreamy false utopia ruled by Nicki Minaj and her Barbz that broke through stan Twitter to become a viral meme in the days leading up to the release of "Pink Friday 2," Minaj's fifth studio album and sequel to her debut record "Pink Friday," which dropped on Friday 8 December 2023. In that period Twitter was flooded with AI-generated images of pink-toned cityscapes, and brand accounts immediately used the meme for their own marketing, promoting Minaj's album for free.

The name comes from gay slang. Minaj and her Barbz started referring to the album's release as "Gag City," NBC News reports, referencing slang for being so amazed that you are at a loss for words — one might be gagged by witnessing a stunning outfit change, or by listening to a perfect record.

The imagery traces to September 2023, when Minaj teased the album's cover art online: the image features Minaj on a pink subway car, drifting through pink clouds with a futuristic and obviously pink city skyline in the background. Leading up to the release, stans started posting AI-generated images of a pink concrete jungle, joking that fictional characters and celebrities were arriving in Gag City in anticipation of the album; in one of the first, posted on 1 December according to Know Your Meme, a fan account shared an image of a pink plane labeled "Gagg City" flying over a similarly pink skyline. In the days before release Minaj told fans to "prepare for landing" and teased a description of her pink utopia, and Barbz replied with AI-generated renditions of the descent into Gag City.
