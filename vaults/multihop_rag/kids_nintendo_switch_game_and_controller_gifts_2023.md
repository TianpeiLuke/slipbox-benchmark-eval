# Kids' Nintendo Switch Game And Controller Gifts 2023

The Verge's kids guide picks four Nintendo Switch-related gifts: Disney Illusion Island at $39.88+ (Walmart, Target, GameStop), the 8BitDo SN30 Pro Special Edition at $44.99+ (Amazon, Walmart, 8BitDo), the Nintendo Switch Lite in Isabelle's Aloha Edition at $199.99 (Target), and Samba de Amigo: Party Central at $14.99+ (Amazon, Best Buy, GameStop).

Disney Illusion Island is described as a terrific intro to 2D side-scrolling "Metroidvania"-style games for kids, with a cute and fun art style, co-op for up to four players, and an in-game hug that restores a struggling player's vitality when they get dangerously low on health. The 8BitDo SN30 is more than a small controller suited to tiny hands: it oozes nostalgic vibes evoking the original SNES while working with modern games on PC or Switch, and its see-through color schemes are sure to entice just about everyone. On the Switch Lite, the guide argues the Steam Deck may have ushered in a new era of handheld gaming but is a bit pricey and overbearing for a child, whereas nothing substitutes for the simplicity of the Switch Lite and its travel-friendly 5.5-inch display — and this Animal Crossing-inspired design comes with a digital copy of New Horizons. Samba de Amigo: Party Central lacks the original Dreamcast game's maracas controllers, but all you need to get shaking are Joy-Cons; the rhythm game is loaded with modern pop from the likes of Ariana Grande and Charli XCX plus classics like Ricky Martin's "The Cup of Life," with Sonic the Hedgehog cameos to marvel at.
