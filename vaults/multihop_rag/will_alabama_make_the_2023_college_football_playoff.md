# Will Alabama Make The 2023 College Football Playoff?

Sporting News concluded on Dec. 1, 2023 that Alabama's path was real but not clean: Oregon had lost, and seeing at least one of Texas or Florida State lose would be helpful for the Crimson Tide.

The supporting grounds are institutional rather than purely statistical. There is the power of the SEC at work, and the emotional response by the committee to seeing Alabama beat Georgia might be enough on its own; the SEC is the only conference that has put a team in the CFP every single year.

The counterweight is the head-to-head and unbeaten logic. If Alabama beat Georgia, the arguments around those other conferences would get heated — yet Florida State as an unbeaten team would still theoretically have the drop on the Crimson Tide, and so would Texas based on the head-to-head victory. That could heat up quickly knowing Michigan likely would not lose in the Big Ten.
