# ID: Amo's Creative Social App

ID is a social app that launched on iOS on November 19, 2023, letting you connect with your friends in a creative way. It comes from Amo, a small team based in Paris that had been working on it for the better part of 2023.

What distinguishes it from the current crop of social products is its lineage rather than its feed: in many ways it feels like the early days of blogs, Myspace's highly-personalized profile pages and the golden era of Tumblr.

The premise behind it is that TechCrunch's writer is not the only one who has noticed that social networks have been slowly drifting away from their original purpose.
