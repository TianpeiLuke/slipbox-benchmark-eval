# Bank of Israel Thirty Billion Dollar FX Intervention

On the Monday of the week in which this 12 October 2023 interview was published, the Bank of Israel announced a dramatic plan to sell $30 billion of its foreign currency reserves to stabilise the shekel. By the time of the report the shekel had in fact recovered slightly and was trading at around NIS 3.96/$.

Leo Leiderman endorsed the move: "It's important to welcome the Bank of Israel's decision to intervene, as necessary, in the foreign exchange market, in order to ensure the market's regular operation." He also treated leadership continuity as stabilising, citing the decision to extend the term of Amir Yaron as governor at least for the duration of the war, and in his hope beyond it. He added that the banking system is stable with enough liquidity in the system, and that the behaviour of the investment community in Israel, including the financial institutions, indicates high confidence in the economy's resilience and financial strength — an "asset" he said a level-headed, correct economic policy must preserve.
