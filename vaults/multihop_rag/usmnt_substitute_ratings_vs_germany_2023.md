# USMNT Substitute Ratings Against Germany, October 2023

The Sporting News rated the United States' six substitutes against Germany on Oct. 14, 2023 as Luca de la Torre 5, Cameron Carter-Vickers 7, Brenden Aaronson 7, Ricardo Pepi 5, Johnny Cardoso 6 and Kevin Paredes 5.

De la Torre, brought on to replace Gio Reyna at halftime, was unable to stem the German tide despite being a more defensive-minded option than Reyna in what should have been a swap to shore up the midfield. Carter-Vickers, on at the 65th minute, remains a top-tier centre-back in the U.S. player pool and a clear starter when healthy, with Tim Ream and even Chris Richards struggling at various stages; he came off the bench due to recent hamstring troubles but was stout in his 25 minutes, recording 22 touches, giving away possession just twice, completing two dribbles and making two clearances, and was not targeted by Germany nearly as much as the other three defenders on the pitch — a strong nod to his abilities. Aaronson, also on at 65 minutes, was probably the U.S. substitute to make the biggest impact, bright in attack, dazzling with his dribbling and helping usher the U.S. forward amid a second-half onslaught — a good showing from the Union Berlin winger who needs confidence at the moment.

Pepi entered at the same time in a far from ideal situation, coming on with the U.S. shell-shocked from the quickfire Germany double; he provided very little up front with just eight touches in 24 minutes, featured his usual pressing and came close to scoring once on a messy chance that Germany just barely cleared, but otherwise did not cause the opposing back line problems to open spaces for teammates. Cardoso, on at 75 minutes, had only 15 minutes to showcase his abilities, losing possession just once in his 10 touches and completing a tackle while also being dribbled past a few times — which begged the question why he or Lennard Maloney weren't introduced at halftime instead of de la Torre. Paredes, also on at 75 minutes, had 10 touches in 15 minutes at the end of a mucked-up game of numerous substitutions, losing possession four times and completing four passes; the Sporting News found his time tough to evaluate but concluded he didn't stand out.
