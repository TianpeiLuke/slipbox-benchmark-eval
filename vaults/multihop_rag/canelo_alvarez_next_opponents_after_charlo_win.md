# Who Canelo And Charlo Fight Next After The 2023 Super Middleweight Bout

After suffering a hiccup when he fell short in attempting to gain light heavyweight gold against Dmitry Bivol, the 33-year-old Canelo Alvarez had won three fights in a row following the Charlo win and would wait to see what's next, Sporting News reported on 2023-09-30. The obvious choice for many was a megafight with undisputed welterweight champion Terence Crawford; however, Crawford would need to move up 21 pounds for that fight to happen, and he was engaged in a potential rematch with Errol Spence. Other options appeared to be David Benavidez and Jermell's older brother, Jermall Charlo.

As for Jermell Charlo, he still owned the IBF, WBA and WBC championships and could return to the 154-pound division to defend those titles. Charlo called out Crawford, but after this performance it was judged unlikely that Crawford would be interested.
