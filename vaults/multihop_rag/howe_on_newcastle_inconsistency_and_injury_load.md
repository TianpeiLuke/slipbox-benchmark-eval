# Howe On Newcastle's Inconsistency And Injury Load

Eddie Howe accepted after the 3-0 defeat at Everton on 7 December 2023 that Newcastle have to do better, while attributing the inconsistency to an inability to rotate: the claim is that there are reasons behind every performance, and the warrant is that the quality of players unavailable makes it harder the longer the run goes on.

The Roar frames the pattern Howe was answering for: his side are proving one of the most inconsistent in the league, albeit with a lengthy injury list. In the previous month they had defeated Manchester United, Arsenal and Chelsea, and drawn at French heavyweights PSG in the Champions League, but had now lost comfortably to two cellar dwellers in Everton and Bournemouth.

"We have to do better," Howe said. "There are reasons behind every performance and we have struggled to change players. That is a big miss with the quality of players out. The longer you do it, the harder it gets. I am disappointed, a missed opportunity, we did not grab it." He also located the turning point within the match itself: "The game was there for us to win in the second half. The first half was even and we had them penned in in the second half. We conceded the first goal and it changed the momentum of the match. Frustrating and a chance missed."
