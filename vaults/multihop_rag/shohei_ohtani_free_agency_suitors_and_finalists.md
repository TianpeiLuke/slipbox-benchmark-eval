# Shohei Ohtani's Free Agency Suitors And Finalists

Between Dec. 1 and Dec. 5, 2023, reporting narrowed Ohtani's field of suitors to the Dodgers, Blue Jays, Angels and Giants, with the Cubs — long viewed as one of his likeliest destinations — losing optimism and the Rangers, Red Sox and Mets already out.

Many of the top teams were expected to be in the mix, with the Dodgers and Cubs among those aiming to sign the two-time AL MVP and plenty of others expected to compete through the end. On Dec. 1, ESPN's Jeff Passan reported that the Rangers, Red Sox and Mets appeared to be out of the running while the Dodgers, Cubs, Blue Jays and Angels remained in the mix, with the Giants considered an unknown. On Dec. 4, MLB Network's Jon Morosi reported that the Braves were "engaged" in talks with Ohtani's camp, raising the prospect of a lineup with Ohtani alongside Ronald Acuna Jr., Ozzie Albies, Austin Riley and Matt Olson. The Athletic's Ken Rosenthal reported the same day that Ohtani was "believed" to have met with the Blue Jays at the team's complex in Florida, far from the meetings in Nashville, with Toronto pursuing both Ohtani and Juan Soto in hopes of striking a deal for one of the two stars, even as an earlier report that Monday indicated Ohtani was not expected to sign before the end of the Winter Meetings. On Dec. 5, USA Today's Bob Nightengale reported one high-ranking executive believed the Cubs' optimism about signing Ohtani had "now significantly wanted," leaving the Dodgers, Blue Jays, Angels and Giants as the remaining finalists; Jon Heyman of the New York Post reported that Ohtani had a "positive feeling about Toronto" before even meeting the Blue Jays on Monday, along with other cities and teams including the Dodgers, having been linked to Toronto and Los Angeles throughout the process. Dodgers manager Dave Roberts told reporters on Tuesday, Dec. 5 that the team had met with Ohtani for two to three hours over the weekend, that Ohtani was "clearly" the Dodgers' top priority and that the team was confident he would pitch again, though Roberts could not offer a timeline for the decision.
