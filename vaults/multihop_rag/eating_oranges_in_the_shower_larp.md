# Eating Oranges In The Shower

Eating Oranges in the Shower, by Hazel Anneke Dixon, is a LARP about exactly what it sounds like, picked in Polygon's 2023 roundup by Sam Dunnewold of the Dice Exploder podcast, who says he has not been able to stop thinking about it.

It came out of the Golden Cobra challenge, which Dunnewold calls a deep well of exciting and free new larps from the bleeding edge of the hobby. Players take the roles of members of a group chat who discover the /r/ShowerOrange subreddit, a real community dedicated to the "liberating" act of eating an orange while taking a shower; each player decides to try it before returning to compare notes, and because it is a LARP, you actually go and do the thing. Dunnewold finds the principal act compelling on its own — it feels forbidden somehow, even as it is obviously harmless — but argues the game's real achievement is capturing something ephemeral and nostalgic about being in a niche online community and group chat, and the magic of making discoveries in those spaces, an experience he says has become rare on an increasingly centralized internet.
