# Emily Hand's Ninth Birthday In Gaza Captivity

Emily Hand, an Israeli girl born with a gift for dance, spent her ninth birthday — that Friday — held captive in "the tunnels of Gaza", her father Thomas Hand told Fox News Digital in a New York City interview published on 17 November 2023.

She should have been bouncing to her favourite Beyoncé songs, her father said. Instead: "No party. No friends. She won't even know if it's day or night. There's no light down there. So she won't know it's her birthday."

He is also haunted by the fear Emily has suffered since 7 October and on what should have been a joyous birthday celebration embraced by the love of their kibbutz. "My daughter, she doesn't know if I was killed or kidnapped or somewhere else," Hand said. "She's in terror every day."
