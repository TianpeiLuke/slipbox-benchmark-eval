# Trenton Bourguet's 335-Yard Effort In Arizona State's Narrow Loss

Trenton Bourguet passed for 335 yards and a touchdown in Arizona State's narrow Oct. 7, 2023 loss to Colorado, playing as one of only two healthy quarterbacks on the Sun Devils' roster and putting all he could into the game.

Arizona State had a real shot at its first conference win. As the game came to a close Bourguet seemed roughed up and slow, though he still drove the team 94 yards in 13 plays for the game-tying touchdown with 50 seconds left — a drive Colorado then answered to win.

The schedule ahead cut both ways for the Sun Devils: they had a bye the following week, which the Sporting News called lucky given Bourguet's condition, but what was not so lucky was that they would then play three ranked teams over the following three weeks.
