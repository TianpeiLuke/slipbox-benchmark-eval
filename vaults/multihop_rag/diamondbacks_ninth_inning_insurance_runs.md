# The Diamondbacks' Ninth-Inning Insurance Runs

Arizona added two more runs in the top of the ninth of World Series Game 2 on 29 October 2023, stretching the lead to 9-1 on a two-run single by Emmanuel Rivera with runners on second and third and two outs — though Rivera was thrown out at second to end the inning.

The rally came against Martín Pérez, back out essentially in mop-up duty. Christian Walker singled to center for his first hit of the World Series. Jace Peterson, getting a pinch-hitting opportunity in what the live blog compared to a basketball game where the coaches empty the bench, worked a full count before hitting into a fielder's choice that erased Walker, leaving one down with Peterson on first. Lourdes Gurriel Jr. singled to centerfield, advancing Peterson, and Alek Thomas grounded out on two pitches with two on and one out, moving the runners into scoring position for Rivera's two-run single.
