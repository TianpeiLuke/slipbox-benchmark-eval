# Mitchell Starc's Record Rs 24.75 Cr Signing By KKR

Kolkata Knight Riders bought Mitchell Starc for Rs 24.75 Cr at the IPL 2024 auction, confirmed at 3:40 PM IST. Before the auction, at 8.49 AM, the live blog had flagged that all eyes would be on Starc, who could be back in the IPL fold after 2015 and could just be the biggest buy of the day.

The bidding escalated in stages. At 3:28 PM IST Starc came up with an all-out bidding war between MI and DC. At 3:32 PM IST it was Rishabh Pant leading the bid for DC, wanting Starc at any cost — not long before, he had been up against Starc in Australia, facing his fiery deliveries. At 3:35 PM IST, KKR and GT joined the race at 13 Cr as DC and MI dropped out and RCB stayed awfully silent, and the same minute KKR and GT took Starc beyond the Rs 20 Cr mark.

At 7:12 PM IST Starc spoke about playing under Shreyas Iyer: "I am really excited to play under Shreyas Iyer captaincy. He understands his thought process. I'm really happy to finally back at the best league in the world."
