# Adolpho Washington vs Virgil Hill, 1993: The Camera Stoppage

Adolpho Washington's 175lb world title challenge against Virgil Hill in 1993 ended when he went to sit down in his corner at the end of round 11 and his swollen eye hit an intrusive ringside TV camera, opening a cut, talkSPORT recalled on 15 November 2023.

The gash was so bad that the fight was called off, Hill was declared a technical decision winner, and Washington was denied the chance to go all-out in round 12.

It may not have changed the end result — chances are Hill was winning anyway — but stoppage via aggressive TV camera is surely unique in boxing history, and Washington can forever hold a grudge against ringside cameras.
