# Walmart Cyber Monday 2023 Smart Lighting And Digital Frame Deals

WIRED's Walmart Cyber Monday roundup of 27 November 2023 picked an Aura digital picture frame plus two Nanoleaf smart lighting kits — the hexagon panels and the Nanoleaf Lines — as its decor-oriented smart home deals.

On frames, WIRED's position is that Aura makes the best digital picture frames: photos look great without being too bright and obvious, because you want a digital frame to look like decor rather than a smart display. Aura has a few options and they are discounted regularly; WIRED points readers who like the mat look to that model, and notes that Aura's newest frame, the 15-inch Walden, is $249 ($50 off).

The hexagon panels top WIRED's smart lighting panels guide and are described as a fun way to add ambient lighting to any room: each hexagon is an individual light, they connect in whatever pattern you want, and the app controls the colour scheme, sets the panels to react to music, or sets schedules — with the caveat that you should prep and use a level to mount them straight on the wall. The Nanoleaf Lines (8/10, WIRED Recommends) are modular light bars that install on a wall and can be arranged into whatever design you want, again with the caveat to prep and plan properly; WIRED calls them great for subtle ambient light but notes they can also sync to music or mirror your screen to add immersion to gaming, and says this is the cheapest it has seen the starter kit.
