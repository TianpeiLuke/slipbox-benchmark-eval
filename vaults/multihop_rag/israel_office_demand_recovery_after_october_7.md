# Israeli Office Demand Recovery After October 7

Natalie Marshall, owner of Marshall Strategic Real Estate marketing, reports restored interest in offices: she had expected a longer stoppage, and there was a stoppage of office marketing for a month, but about two weeks before the 3 December 2023 article movement recommenced, noting too that there is seasonality in the office sector, with a stoppage every year in July-August and during the holidays. She adds that, ironically, the public has now realised that there is nowhere else to go and only one country; in the last six months, with the protests and the reform, people did not know what to do and even thought of leaving, but all understood that they have one home and must keep it as the best possible place. She sees movement of people interested in renting offices in the last two and a half weeks with the market alive and kicking, many meetings and tours of the area, and companies searching for 10,000 or 15,000 square meters.

Newmark Natam VP real estate services Or Ben Zvi Klein says there are even companies interested in the southern region and a market for deals and factories there: his firm managed deals for lawyers, small high-tech companies, call centres and warehouses moving from Ofakim and Sderot towards Beer Sheva and Kiryat Gat on short contracts, so that as soon as the area is restored they will return; in the north he is marketing a building in Nof Hagalil, while Metula, Kiryat Shmona and similar areas are not areas for offices. He thinks that as in other crises, when the crisis breaks everything halts but then buds of everything returning to work appear, and adds that volumes are low but no different than before the war. In the first two weeks of the war people were in the market but the market was at a standstill, reminding him of Covid when there were ghost offices; as time goes by people come back to the offices, wanting to leave home and get to work, and most offices have emergency shelters and are relatively protected compared with residential areas, which is why, living in Tel Aviv, his children came to the office with him when schools were closed because the house only has a stairwell. In the last three weeks he feels the market is returning to activity, but the market was already stuttering when the war began, with no huge deals because there is no huge hiring in high-tech or big deals of tens of thousands of meters for the big companies; there are small and medium-sized deals and quite a few inquiries from high-tech and service companies looking for 1,000-2,000 square meters who want to expand, in Haifa, Tel Aviv and Rehovot. There is some revival in the market, but property owners have not cut rents.
