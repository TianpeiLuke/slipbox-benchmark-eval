# Corbin Burnes And Willy Adames Brewers Trade Candidacy

Corbin Burnes and Willy Adames were ranked No. 2 and No. 3 on Yardbarker's Nov. 13, 2023 list of the top 25 MLB offseason trade candidates, and the piece tied the two Brewers together with good reason: each was one year from the open market and projected for a noteworthy arbitration salary — $15.1M for Burnes, $12.4M for Adames — while Milwaukee was reportedly open to offers on the majority of the roster.

A former Cy Young winner, Burnes may have been the best pitcher available in trade. The right-hander posted a sub-3.00 ERA each season from 2020 to 2022; his 3.39 mark across 193 2/3 innings in the past season was not quite so dominant but was still high-end production. He struck out just under 26% of opposing hitters, his lowest mark since his rookie season but still an above-average figure.

Adames was coming off a less impressive year: the 28-year-old shortstop hit .217/.310/.407 through 638 trips to the plate, slightly below-average offense, although he still connected on 24 home runs. He was an excellent defensive shortstop with plus power in an offseason where there were essentially no free agent shortstops of note, so even with a mediocre on-base percentage he would generate plenty of interest.
