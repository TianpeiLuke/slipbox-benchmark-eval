# Trump's Other Criminal and Civil Cases 2023

Letitia James' lawsuit is one of several legal headaches for Trump as he campaigns for a return to the White House in 2024. He has been indicted four times in the six months to September 2023 — accused in Georgia and Washington, D.C., of plotting to overturn his 2020 election loss; in Florida of hoarding classified documents; and in Manhattan of falsifying business records related to hush money paid on his behalf.

Separately, the Trump Organization was convicted of tax fraud in 2022 in an unrelated criminal case for helping executives dodge taxes on extravagant perks such as Manhattan apartments and luxury cars, and the company was fined $1.6 million. One of the executives, Trump's longtime finance chief Allen Weisselberg, pleaded guilty and served five months in jail; he is a defendant in James' lawsuit and gave sworn deposition testimony for the case in May.
