# Epic Games Store Growth And Diversification Beyond Games

Asked what changed after a period when Epic seemed interested in expanding outside games with acquisitions like Houseparty and Bandcamp, Tim Sweeney said Epic has gotten so much traction with Fortnite in so many different ways, and with the Epic Games Store, that it is going to continue investing heavily there.

He called the Epic Games Store "the untold success story in the background": since it launched in 2018 it now has 80 million monthly active users, against Steam's 120 million, which he read as catching up fast. He also claimed Fortnite has more concurrent users right now than all Steam games combined, and promised dramatic changes that benefit all developers over the next few years. The Verge appended an editor's note correcting the concurrency claim: Epic spokesperson Natalie Muñoz confirmed Sweeney had misinterpreted a Polygon story about Fortnite hitting 7.6 million players at a time when the entirety of Steam had 10.1 million; these days Steam generally peaks at over 30 million with valleys of 20 million, while Fortnite's valleys are closer to 3.8 million.
