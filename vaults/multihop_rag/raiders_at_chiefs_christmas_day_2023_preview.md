# Raiders At Chiefs, Christmas Day 2023: Storylines To Watch

Christmas Day isn't just for the NBA — the NFL aired an exciting tripleheader featuring fierce division clashes and a highly anticipated matchup that could recur on Super Bowl Sunday, Yardbarker previewed on 2023-12-24. The opener was Las Vegas Raiders (6-8) at Kansas City Chiefs (9-5) at 1 p.m. ET. The first storyline is a new chapter in the Patrick Mahomes-Maxx Crosby rivalry: this hasn't been much of a rivalry between the teams lately, with Kansas City holding a 10-1 record over the Raiders since Mahomes became its starting quarterback, yet it has sparked an exciting feud between the two-time Super Bowl MVP and the two-time Pro Bowler Crosby, who has five sacks in nine career games against the Chiefs; the two might not like facing one another, though there is clear mutual respect, which only makes the rivalry more intriguing.

The second storyline is whether Raiders quarterback Aidan O'Connell can earn next season's starting job. After leading the offense on seven touchdown-scoring drives in the previous week's 63-21 win over the Chargers, the rookie fourth-rounder aimed to establish himself as a serious contender for Las Vegas' starting job in 2024; despite that encouraging performance he had been inconsistent across six games as full-time starter, throwing for 1,300 yards, seven touchdowns and five interceptions, and a stellar showing against the NFL's fifth-ranked pass defense, allowing 202.5 yards per game, would benefit his bid. The third is Chiefs tight end Travis Kelce: his girlfriend would be in town for the holiday, and Kelce was averaging 89.6 yards per game with global popstar Taylor Swift in attendance that season, though coming off his worst outing in her presence with five catches for 28 yards. Even so, his 81.3 average receiving yards per game against the Raiders since 2019 suggested the All-Pro tight end was in line for a productive outing and could potentially snap his four-game scoring drought.
