# EU AI Act Political Agreement, December 2023

European Union lawmakers clinched a political deal on a risk-based framework for regulating artificial intelligence late on Friday, December 8, 2023, after marathon "final" talks which stretched to almost three days — meaning a pan-EU AI law is definitively on the way.

The file was originally proposed back in April 2021, and it took months of tricky three-way negotiations to get a deal over the line. Giving a triumphant but exhausted press conference in the small hours of Friday night/Saturday morning local time, key representatives for the European Parliament, Council and the Commission — the bloc's co-legislators — hailed the agreement as hard fought, a milestone achievement and historic, respectively. Taking to X to tweet the news, the EU's president Ursula von der Leyen — who made delivering a regulation to promote "trustworthy" AI a key priority of her term when she took up the post in late 2019 — lauded the political agreement as a "global first": "The EU AI Act is a global first. A unique legal framework for the development of AI you can trust. And for the safety and fundamental rights of people and businesses. A commitment we took in our political guidelines – and we delivered. I welcome today's political agreement."

The deal is billed by the bloc as the world's first comprehensive law for regulating artificial intelligence, and the Commission has confirmed that it includes powers for the Commission to adapt the pan-EU AI rulebook to keep pace with developments in the cutting-edge field.
