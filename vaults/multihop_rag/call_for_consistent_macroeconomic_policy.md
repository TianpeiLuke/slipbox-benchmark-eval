# The Call For Consistent Macroeconomic Policy

The first of the author's three consistency demands is a consistent macroeconomic policy, on the grounds that it has a major impact on startups raising and deploying capital into climate R&D.

The evidence is present conditions and their reach: the global economic contraction has made it difficult for these businesses to raise any money, and overall approaches to the economy have trickle-down effects on how businesses make money and run their operations — right down to salaries, which alone are hard to keep up with inflation-based pay rises.
