# Opening And Funding An Online Sportsbook Account

The first step to betting on NBA props or MVP futures is opening an account at an online sportsbook — a process the Sporting News describes as simple, free and quick, running in six ordered steps: choose your sportsbook, register, accept the terms and conditions, claim your bonus, make a deposit, and start betting.

Choose your sportsbook first. Any state with legal online sports betting has a variety of sportsbooks, and not all are created equal — some are easier to use than others and some offer more incentives — so research and read reviews to find which fit your needs, remembering you can have an account with more than one. Following a link from the review site takes you to the sportsbook homepage and qualifies you for the best new customer welcome bonus. Then register for an account: at the top of the homepage you will find a button that says "register" or "sign up", which opens a form asking for personal information such as your name, phone number, email address, and the last four digits of your Social Security number. Before finalizing your account information you must accept the sportsbook's terms and conditions, which the Sporting News recommends reading through to understand the rules your sportsbook will enforce regarding bets — especially for the sports you intend to wager on — and which remain available on the site to read at any point.

With the account set up and the terms accepted you can claim your welcome bonus as a new customer; if a bonus code is needed you input it during the registration process, and you should read through the bonus requirements before accepting anything so you are aware of any restrictions or other rules. Next, make a deposit to start funding your bankroll: most legal sportsbooks offer a variety of ways to add funds, such as through your bank or an e-wallet, and many deposit options will work for withdrawals as well. Finally, start betting — go to the basketball section of your sportsbook and select the NBA, where you can choose individual games and make bets like money lines, point spreads, totals, props, futures and more; NBA MVP futures sit under the basketball tab, and you click the bet you want to add it to your bet slip, adjust how much you're wagering, and finalize the bet.
