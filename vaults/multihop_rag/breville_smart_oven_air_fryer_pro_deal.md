# Breville Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro Deal

Breville's Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro was 29 percent off and down to $320 for Black Friday 2023 — the best price Engadget has seen since the start of the year on one of its favorite air fryers.

This Breville machine is much more than an air fryer: it has 13 cooking functions and a capacity that can handle a full turkey, so it's more like a mini convection oven that sits on a countertop. It's best for people who love to cook and have big families, or those who don't have a traditional oven and want a versatile, relatively small appliance for their kitchen.
