# Sony LinkBuds S Earbuds

The Sony LinkBuds S were on sale for $128, down from $200 — a $72 saving. It is a price Engadget has seen in the past, but it still takes about $30 off the pair's average street price.

Engadget highlights the LinkBuds S as an honorable mention in its wireless earbuds guide, liking the compact design, decent noise cancellation, handy bonus features and warm sound.

The comparison that places them: they cannot match the ANC strength or battery life of the WF-1000XM5, but they also cost a fair bit less, which helps.
