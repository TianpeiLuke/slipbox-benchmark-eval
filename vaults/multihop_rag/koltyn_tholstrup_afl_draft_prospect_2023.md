# Koltyn Tholstrup AFL Draft Prospect 2023

Koltyn Tholstrup — pronounced Foal-strup — profiled in Kevin Sheehan's top 40 for the 2023 AFL Draft as reported by The Roar on Nov. 15, 2023, is a talented player with an elite work rate who often plays as a high half forward and sometimes goes through the midfield. State: Western Australia. State League Club: Subiaco. Community Club: Newtown Condingup. Date of Birth: 28/06/05. Height: 186cm.

He is a fearless competitor with excellent game sense and a penetrating kick. He played the final three games for Western Australia at the National Championships after missing the first with injury and did not miss a beat, averaging 18.7 disposals. He has a sharp competitive edge which has seen him already play 10 senior matches for Subiaco in the WAFL. He tested impressively at the national Draft Combine, recording the second quickest time on the agility test (8.19) and a top ten finish in the 2km time trial (6:17). Weapon: Power and clean hands. AFL player comparison: Cameron Rayner (Brisbane Lions).
