# NFL Week 11 2023: Three Starting Quarterbacks Hurt, All Three Stayed Or Returned

In Week 11 of the 2023 NFL season, as reported by CBS Sports on 2023-11-19, three starting quarterbacks took injury scares and none of them ended the day sidelined for good. C.J. Stroud of the Texans left for a single play against the Cardinals after absorbing a hard hit, with backup Davis Mills entering in relief; Stroud did not receive an injury designation and returned after a quick check-in from trainers. Sam Howell of the Commanders received medical attention on the sidelines after enduring several hard hits to the upper body on a touchdown run against the Giants, was quickly cleared of a potential concussion and remained in the game, with veteran journeyman Jacoby Brissett listed as Washington's backup.

The most consequential case was Geno Smith of the Seahawks, who suffered an injury to his throwing arm after guiding a 16-7 lead over the Rams, showing frustration while trying to stretch the arm after a hit from Aaron Donald. Seattle quickly ruled him questionable to return with an elbow issue, and backup Drew Lock took over under center with 18 seconds left in the third quarter. Smith did return for the final drive of the game, in a losing effort. Head coach Pete Carroll said Smith will "make it back," and when asked whether that meant for Thursday's matchup against San Francisco, the coach said, "I would think so." The wider Week 11 context given by the same roundup: the week provided plenty of drama, from the Bears hanging with the Lions to the Giants getting a rebound from emergency quarterback Tommy DeVito, alongside another wave of unfortunate injuries.
