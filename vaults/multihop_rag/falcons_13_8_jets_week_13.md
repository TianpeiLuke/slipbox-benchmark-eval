# Falcons 13-8 Jets, Week 13 2023

Desmond Ridder threw a 20-yard touchdown pass to MyCole Pruitt as the Atlanta Falcons took sole possession of first place in the NFC South by slogging their way to a 13-8 win over the New York Jets in Week 13 of the 2023 season.

The Falcons (6-6) had back-to-back wins for the first time since opening the season with victories over Carolina and Green Bay; Atlanta entered the week tied atop the division with New Orleans, who lost to Detroit. The game appealed only to fans of sloppy play, punting and penalty flags. The Jets (4-8) got an early safety but could not get anything going on offense again, losing their fifth straight game; New York even pulled quarterback Tim Boyle and replaced him with Trevor Siemian in the fourth quarter to try to provide a spark, but it was more of the same misery in what appeared a half-filled MetLife Stadium.
