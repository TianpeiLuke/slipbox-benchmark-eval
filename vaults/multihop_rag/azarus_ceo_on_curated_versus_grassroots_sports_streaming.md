# Azarus CEO on Curated Versus Grassroots Sports Streaming

Alex Casassovici, founder and CEO of Azarus — a company that works with online creators to enhance the streaming experience — told Polygon for its 2023-10-20 story that livestreaming sports and sports-related content on platforms like Twitch creates a more intimate experience for streamers and fans alike.

He frames the landscape as a split between two models.

"I think what we're looking at is either a very curated kind of managed channel, and more grassroots-like, creator-led experiences," Casassovici says. "That's clearly something that can be differentiated when it comes to streaming."
