# Apple Watch Ultra 2 Holiday Deal

The Apple Watch Ultra 2 hit a new low of $730 at Amazon over Black Friday 2023 — $69 off its $799 price and cheaper than it was for October's Prime Day, available by clipping an on-page coupon, or without the coupon at Best Buy for $739. By Cyber Monday it was $739 at both Amazon and Best Buy, $60 off; Engadget noted it sold for $9 less during Black Friday and is one of the few Apple products not to maintain its pricing for Cyber Monday.

The Ultra 2 has the same new chip as the Apple Watch Series 9, so it can perform the new Double Tap trick, but also sports a more robust build and outdoor-specific features that make it suited to adventurers: a siren to alert people to your presence, a dive meter, waypoint setting capabilities and night mode to preserve low-light vision. It is Engadget's recommended Apple Watch for adventurers.
