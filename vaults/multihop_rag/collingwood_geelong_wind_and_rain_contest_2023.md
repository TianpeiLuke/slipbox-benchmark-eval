# Collingwood-Geelong's Wind And Rain Contest, 2023

Collingwood beat Geelong by six points at Victoria Park in a round-eight AFLW clash decided less by which side was better overall than by which could adapt best to the blustery wind and rain, The Age reported on 20 October 2023.

The Magpies survived a fourth-quarter comeback to stay firmly in finals contention, while the Cats now faced an uphill battle to finish in the eight in two weeks' time. Geelong scored just one point in the first quarter and had only 1.3 on the board at three-quarter-time before kicking two goals to none in the final term to close in. Magpies ruck Sabrina Frederick starred with 39 hit-outs, using her height to bring the ball to ground to the advantage of her midfielders, and said her side had been practising for games like this: "Even in the chaos, it's quite comfortable because we train it. We train what scenarios to do in what moments, and even though it's a bit crazy, it felt so calm. We put players in place and we know what we're doing. But close games are good ones, for everyone. We knew they were going to come out like that [in the last quarter]. When they're down, they're just going to give us everything." Frederick admitted at times she "couldn't see" and felt like she could be "blown away".

Geelong coach Daniel Lowther said the strong winds made it hard for his side to clear the ball out of their defensive 50-metre zone and to move the ball with any certainty around the ground. "At one point the rain hit our box, it was flowing," Lowther said. "Both teams had to fight in the conditions, but it didn't help the game. Our marking contest wasn't too flash, our ground ball representation was a bit hit and miss, so our defence were under a bit more pressure than they should have been, but they fought back really well." Collingwood next play a Sydney line-up including former Magpies star Chloe Molloy, while Geelong take on Richmond at Ikon Park.
