# Liverpool vs Everton: Merseyside Derby Preview, October 2023

Liverpool went into the first Merseyside derby of the season, live on talkSPORT at 12:30pm on 21 October 2023, sitting in a comfortable fourth place in the Premier League with five wins from their opening eight games, while Sean Dyche's Everton went in as heavy underdogs.

The bad news for Jurgen Klopp was that the Reds lost to Tottenham and only managed a draw with Brighton just before the international break. Klopp also had a new worry: Liverpool were without Andy Robertson after the left-back suffered a shoulder injury while on international duty with Scotland, and his absence could open up a gap in their defence for Everton to exploit.

Everton were winless in their first five games but had since picked up two much-needed wins. They were nonetheless up against the odds, having won just one of the last 25 previous Merseyside derbies. The fixture opened a Saturday packed full of huge matches as the Premier League returned, with talkSPORT getting the action started at Anfield.
