# Second Period: Bedard's First NHL Point

Connor Bedard picked up his first NHL point in the second period of the Oct. 10, 2023 opening-night game in Pittsburgh, taking a secondary assist on Ryan Donato's goal, with the Penguins still ahead 2-1 at the second intermission.

The period began at 9:15 p.m. Jeff Carter gave Chicago an early power play at 9:19 p.m. when the Penguins veteran was called for holding Kevin Korchinski's stick, but Pittsburgh killed it off by 9:21 p.m. as Chicago made it fairly easy, putting just one shot on net and spending most of the man advantage passing around the perimeter. At 9:23 p.m. Reilly Smith, traded to the Penguins that offseason from the Golden Knights, was denied by Petr Mrazek's glove off the rush, and at 9:27 p.m. Tristan Jarry kept his shutout going with two strong saves on Tyler Johnson — a tipped point shot and then a leg stop on the loose puck.

Pittsburgh doubled its lead at 9:42 p.m. Sidney Crosby had sent a loose puck into a wide-open net off a give-and-go with Jake Guentzel at 9:39 p.m., and Chicago challenged for offside on the grounds that Marcus Pettersson had to reach for the puck along the blue line; officials determined the play was onside, so Crosby's first of the year stood with 8:04 to go, and the unsuccessful challenge also gave Pittsburgh a delay-of-game power play. Chicago killed that off by 9:46 p.m., with the blog noting that for all the star power on the top unit the Pittsburgh power play had not been as crisp as expected.

Chicago got on the board at 9:47 p.m.: Alex Vlasic walked in off the wall and put a low shot on Jarry that was saved, and Ryan Donato pulled the rebound around and past Jarry's leg from the top of the crease with 4:23 left, with Bedard credited a secondary assist for his first NHL point — a sequence that began with his zone entry. At 9:54 p.m. an awful Blackhawks line change let Evgeni Malkin send Smith in on a breakaway, but Mrazek made the stop. At 9:56 p.m. the period closed with both teams on the board but Pittsburgh still ahead, and with Bedard looking to be improving every shift.
