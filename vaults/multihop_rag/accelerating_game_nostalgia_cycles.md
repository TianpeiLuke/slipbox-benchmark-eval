# Accelerating Game Nostalgia Cycles

TechCrunch argues on 2023-10-30 that gaming's nostalgia cycles are speeding up, with relaunches and remakes coming on quick — much like the revolutions of a giant oversized hamster hurtling down from Fortnite's rave cave.

The ground is a direct comparison of lookback distances: World of Warcraft Classic recreated a 15-year-old version of the game, but Fortnite's own blast from the past seeks to capture the magic of a relatively very recently bygone era. The warrant is that the shrinking gap between a game's original state and its nostalgic re-release is itself the measure of the cycle's acceleration.
