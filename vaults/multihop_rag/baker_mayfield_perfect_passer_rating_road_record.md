# Mayfield's Perfect Passer Rating And Road Record

Baker Mayfield hit the perfect 158.3 passer rating in Green Bay on Dec. 17, 2023, becoming the first visiting quarterback ever to record such a mark at Lambeau Field.

The film told a similar story to the numbers: Mayfield looked composed in the pocket, letting loose as he slung the hazel-tinted chamber in all sorts of different directions, whipping whistlers beyond Green Bay surveillance with a variety of targets, and five different Buccaneers wide receivers recorded at least 40 receiving yards on the day. The NFL's post summarised the day as 381 yards, four touchdowns, zero interceptions and a 158.3 passer rating. He also made a bit of league history: per NFL345, Mayfield became just the third quarterback in NFL history with 375-plus passing yards (381), four-plus touchdown passes and a 158.3 passer rating — the highest attainable mark — in a road game, joining Nick Foles (Nov. 3, 2013 at Oakland) and Ken O'Brien (Nov. 2, 1986 at Seattle).
