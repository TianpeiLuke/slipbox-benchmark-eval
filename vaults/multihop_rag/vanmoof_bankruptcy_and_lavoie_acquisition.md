# VanMoof's Bankruptcy And Lavoie Acquisition

Dutch e-bike startup VanMoof declared bankruptcy in the summer of 2023, pausing sales as co-founder Taco Carlier left the firm. Electric scooter maker Lavoie acquired what was left of VanMoof in August for "tens of millions," pulling it out of bankruptcy; months later, it seems the acquirer has yet to restart VanMoof production or resume repairs.

VanMoof had ridden the high of the COVID-19-era bike boom into a $128 million Series C funding round, proclaiming at the time that it was the "most funded e-bike company in the world," with Carlier boasting that the company was "reinventing, redesigning, and re-engineering every component of the bicycle."

TechCrunch attributes the collapse to that same growth model: VanMoof's rapid growth and focus on proprietary parts led to its downfall, and by 2023 the company reportedly lost money on each bike due to costly repairs and struggled to raise additional funds to stay afloat.
