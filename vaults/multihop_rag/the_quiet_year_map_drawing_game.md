# The Quiet Year

The Quiet Year is a repeat entry on Polygon's list — it appeared twice on the previous year's roundup, praised by Jay Dragon for its elegance and noted by Keerthi Sridharan for the way their game group used it as a prologue, a practice popularized by Friends at the Table and The Adventure Zone — and Em Friedman, associate professor of English at Auburn University and Polygon contributor, also uses it as a standard early in her TTRPG class.

Her reason for naming it the standout of 2023 is an unplanned test of the design. After running it for her niece, then 4, and nephew, 8, her nephew asked her to run "the skulls game" for his entire class. Though not intended for a dozen grade schoolers, the mechanics turned out to be a perfect framework for imagining how to work together: they ensure everyone gets to take their turn unimpeded by the commentary of others, they ritualize dissent and discussion, and they supply a glorious sheet of blank paper on which to draw the zombie chicken monster friend of your dreams. She adds that it fits in her pocket.
