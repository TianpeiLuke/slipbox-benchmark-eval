# Windscribe, Best Free VPN

Windscribe was chosen as the best free VPN because of its high security and wide range of server locations compared with other free VPNs. Signing up for the free plan with an email gets you 10GB per month of data, unlimited connections and access to more than 10 countries. Specifications: unlimited simultaneous connections and devices; Windows, Mac, Android, iOS and Linux; Chrome and Firefox browser extensions; no dedicated IP addresses; no trial and no money-back guarantee; $0 at Windscribe.

Its infrastructure is broad: over 500 servers in over 60 countries, according to the company, and it can be configured to routers, smart TVs and more on top of the usual operating systems.

On security, Windscribe does not have a recent independent security audit, but it does publish a transparency report showing it has complied with zero requests for its data, runs a vulnerability disclosure program encouraging researchers to report flaws, and offers multiple protocols for users to connect with.

It is also easy to use: setup is intuitive and it passed the geoblock, streaming and gaming tests. The paid version costs $5.75 to $9 each month depending on the plan, and includes unlimited data, access to all servers and an ad/tracker/malware blocker; alternatively, for $1 per location per month, users can build a plan tailored to the VPNs they want to access. Windscribe is still expanding its reach, having added Japan to its list of available spots.
