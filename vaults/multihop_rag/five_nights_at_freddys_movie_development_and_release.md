# Five Nights at Freddy's Movie Development and Release

The possessed-animatronics horror video game Five Nights at Freddy's is less than a decade old, but a movie version had been in the works almost since the game launched — a protracted development process Polygon recounted on 2023-10-26.

Warner Bros. bought the movie rights in 2015, and the project moved over to Blumhouse a couple of years later. Game creator Scott Cawthon wrote and rewrote the script, while kid-horror filmmakers including Gil Kenan of Monster House and Chris Columbus of Gremlins and the first Harry Potter and Percy Jackson movies signed on and jumped off the project.

Finally, under director Emma Tammi, the film hit theaters simultaneously with a Peacock streaming debut. Polygon framed its accompanying Q&A as extremely spoiler-light and aimed at people other than the franchise's most hardcore fans, deliberately not digging into the considerable FNAF lore and focusing instead on who the film is for and what to expect.
