# Paranormasight: The Seven Mysteries of Honjo

Paranormasight: The Seven Mysteries of Honjo, developed by Square Enix for Android, iOS, Nintendo Switch, and Windows PC, is ranked 23 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31 — an excellent riff on the visual novel penned by a beloved storyteller whose best series has never appeared in the U.S.

Polygon's Chris Plante sets it against Square Enix's habits: the publisher has plenty of mega-franchises to fill its time and coffers, with new entries for Octopath Traveler and Final Fantasy that year and new Dragon Quest and Kingdom Hearts games not far off, and yet it cannot help bombarding players with surprising, interesting, sometimes great, often good-enough experiments — in 2022 an English-language remake of lost gem Live A Live, the surprisingly enjoyable tactical RPG DioField Chronicle, a bonkers Final Fantasy spinoff featuring the musical stylings of Limp Bizkit, and a pair of oddball card games lathered in lore; this year, the Avengers of rhythm games, Theatrhythm Final Bar Line, and Paranormasight.

Plante says the ideal amount to know before playing is nothing, but concedes the basics: it is a mystery, and a horror mystery at that, taking you to 1980s Japan, specifically the Tokyo neighborhood of Honjo, located not far from the modern Tokyo Skytree — hard to imagine that modern landmark towering alongside these streets filled with shadows and lethal curses. Anyone with even a passing interest in urban legends, spooky folklore, cults, and deadly rituals, or who has enjoyed series like Zero Escape and Danganronpa, will find it an easy recommendation, as will anyone who just enjoys a good yarn and has access to basically any screen and $15; it runs as well on console and PC as on iOS and Android.
