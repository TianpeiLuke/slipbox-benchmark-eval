# Scorsese's 21st-Century Filmography

In the 21st century alone, Martin Scorsese has made nine feature-length narrative films: Gangs of New York, The Aviator, The Departed, Shutter Island, Hugo, The Wolf of Wall Street, Silence, The Irishman, and Killers of the Flower Moon. Polygon set this out on 2023-10-26.

Polygon still identifies his stretch from 1976's Taxi Driver through 1990's Goodfellas as his peak run, the films that defined his style and his perspective.

But its claim about the later work is that decades from now, the nine features made since 2000 will not be looked back on as half-baked, indulgent afterthoughts from a diminished artist who lost his fastball — a fate some great auteurs suffer in old age. Instead it is a fertile, vital body of work, virtuosic in entirely new ways, and as important in contextualizing his greatness as all the incredible work that preceded it.
