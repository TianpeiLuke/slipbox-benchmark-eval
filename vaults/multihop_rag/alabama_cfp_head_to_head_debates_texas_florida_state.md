# The Head-To-Head Arguments Alabama Would Face Against Texas And Florida State

Sporting News argued on Dec. 1, 2023 that if the preferred scenario unfolded the Crimson Tide would be in the CFP, and that even if Michigan won, Alabama would have a case with a Florida State loss — but that other results would force head-to-head arguments Alabama might not win.

Alabama (12-1) vs. Texas (12-1) was described as the scenario everyone could be talking about: Washington, Florida State and Michigan win, and Texas wins too, which means there are three unbeatens and the committee must pick among the Longhorns, Crimson Tide and Bulldogs for the fourth spot. The decisive ground against Alabama is that Texas beat Alabama at Bryant-Denny Stadium on Sept. 9; no matter how you try to talk around that, it might not happen for the Crimson Tide.

Alabama (12-1) vs. Florida State (13-0) was the fallback: if Saban could not win the argument with Texas, would he try to target Florida State instead? The Seminoles might be playing with a backup quarterback in Tate Rodamaker, but they beat LSU in Week 1, and that victory would be worth cashing in at this point.
