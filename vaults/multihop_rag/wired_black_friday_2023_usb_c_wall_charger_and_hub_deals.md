# WIRED Black Friday 2023 USB-C Wall Charger And Hub Deals

WIRED's Apple Black Friday guide of 25 November 2023 listed six discounted USB-C charging and connectivity products: Anker's 67-watt Prime GaN charger, a four-port desktop charger, Apple's dual USB-C 35-watt adapter, a folding two-port charger, Satechi's four-port hub with an SSD slot, and Anker's compact 30-watt iPad adapter.

WIRED likes Anker's chargers and notes its Prime range uses GaN technology to shrink their size; the 67-watt model has fold-out prongs, two USB-C ports and one USB-A, supports fast charging for iPhones as well as Pixel and Samsung Galaxy phones, and can also charge a MacBook. The four-port charger has enough power to juice up two MacBook Pro models simultaneously and suits a desk full of devices, with a little stand to prop it up, a short cord to the outlet, and four USB-C ports outputting 100 watts each; this is the lowest price WIRED has tracked. Apple's dual USB-C port power adapter is called an essential accessory for people who forget to pack chargers: its two USB-C ports recharge a MacBook and iPhone simultaneously, and at 35 watts it is powerful enough to recharge an iPhone 15 Pro Max or iPad Pro. The folding charger packs two USB-C ports, one of which outputs 65 watts when used alone, dropping to 45 watts on the top port and 20 watts on the bottom when two devices are plugged in.

Satechi's four-port hub is, in WIRED's view, a little too large for the number of ports it offers, but for a reason: popping off the aluminium cover reveals a slot for an M.2 SATA solid-state drive, giving access to an external SSD alongside two USB-A jacks, one 100-watt USB-C pass-through charging port and a 4K HDMI at the same time. WIRED is explicit that it did not have an M.2 SATA drive to test, that a specific 1-terabyte card should work, and that the hub does not support NVME M.2 cards. The last pick is a tiny 30-watt Anker adapter for iPads: WIRED notes Apple has not nixed the charging adapter from its iPads yet but probably will before long, and this compact, fold-away plug can fast-charge the iPad Pro, making it a good spare or travel option.
