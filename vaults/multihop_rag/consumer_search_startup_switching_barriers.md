# Consumer Search Startups And Switching Barriers

The premise attacked is that a better search product wins users. The cautionary case is Neeva, founded by former Google execs, which tried to convince people to switch to paid and private search with additional features; earlier in 2023 the startup shut down its consumer efforts and eventually got acquired by Snowflake.

Adii Pienaar, an investor in Deft, said that Deft has to give compelling reasons to users to switch to its service. His grounds are behavioral rather than technical: "Users' natural behavior for search is to follow the path of least resistance, i.e. most people just pick up their phone and just use the default search engine."

The conclusion he draws sets the bar Deft must clear: "For Deft to change that behavior, they will need to create a high-value proposition."
