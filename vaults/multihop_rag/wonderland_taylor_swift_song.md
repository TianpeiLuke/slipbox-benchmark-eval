# Wonderland: An Alice-Themed Bonus Track Swift Rarely Plays

Wonderland is an EDM-tinged banger in which Taylor Swift returns to Lewis Carroll's Alice In Wonderland, having dressed as the Mad Hatter for an Alice-themed performance at the 2013 Grammy Awards. "It's all fun and games til someone loses their mind," it opens; the song describes a fling with a "green-eyed" man — Harry Styles again, perhaps — with whom she "fell down a rabbit hole" where "nothing's as it seems" until, in the end, "we both went mad".

The track marks the boundary of 1989's quality: originally a deluxe edition bonus track, it doesn't add anything new or insightful to the mix, and even Swift appears to agree it is a minor entry in her songbook, having played it only seven times in concert to date. It nonetheless follows the album's general rule, which she stated to Radio 1: "Any song [on 1989] that is touching on a relationship issue is looking back on it, and discussing what I learned, rather than you did this and now I feel this way. There aren't any accusations made. There aren't any outright diss tracks."
