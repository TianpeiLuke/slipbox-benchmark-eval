# Nyad

Nyad, a biographical sports drama available to stream on Netflix in early November 2023, runs 2 hours 1 minute, is directed by Jimmy Chin and Elizabeth Chai Vasarhelyi, and stars Jodie Foster, Annette Bening, and Rhys Ifans.

Polygon's weekend roundup describes it as the — questionably — true story of swimmer Diana Nyad, who swam from Cuba to Florida at 64 years old, among many other swimming accomplishments. Netflix's own synopsis, reproduced in Polygon's November catalog listing, gives different particulars: it calls the film the remarkable true story of an athlete who, at the age of 60 and with the help of her best friend and coach, commits to achieving her life-long dream of a 110-mile open ocean swim from Cuba to Florida.
