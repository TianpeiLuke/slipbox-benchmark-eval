# American Public Climate Worry Polling

A 2022 poll found that almost two-thirds of Americans say they have been affected by extreme weather they believe was at least partially due to climate change, and about 27% of Americans say they are "very worried" about climate change while another 27% just avoid the subject as best they can.

One in 10 Americans reported feeling symptoms of anxiety or depression over climate change. The article introduces these figures to make the point that climate change is weighing not only on scientists but also on everyday Americans.
