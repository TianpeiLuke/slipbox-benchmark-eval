# Ravens At 49ers, Christmas Day 2023: A Possible Super Bowl Preview

Baltimore Ravens (11-3) at San Francisco 49ers (11-3), at 8:15 p.m. ET, was easily the most hyped game of the week, Yardbarker previewed on 2023-12-24 — the top seed in each respective conference squaring off in what could be a Super Bowl LVIII preview as well as the decisive clash for the MVP Award. On the 49ers side, running back Christian McCaffrey: if Brock Purdy weren't playing at such a high level, and if the MVP weren't a quarterback-driven award, it would be difficult to argue against McCaffrey taking home the honors; he seemed poised to win Offensive Player of the Year, leading the NFL in rushing with 1,292 yards and in total yards per game at 128.6, and tied for first in touchdowns with 20. On the Baltimore side, tight end Isaiah Likely thriving in the starting role: the Ravens offense hadn't lost a step since losing Mark Andrews (ankle) in Week 11, largely due to Likely's emergence — the second-year tight end had averaged 64.3 yards over his last three games, particularly noteworthy since Andrews averaged 54.4 yards per game before his injury.

Should a defensive battle be expected? While both teams boast offenses ranking in the top five in yardage and scoring, this may not turn into a shootout: the Ravens defense had allowed the fewest points per game that season at 16.1 and the second-fewest total yards at 4,030, while San Francisco's unit ranked second in points at 16.7 and 10th in yardage at 310 per game. The 49ers defense might be slightly inferior statistically, but it could have more impact in this matchup — San Francisco had racked up an NFL-leading 25 turnovers and given up the second-fewest rushing yards at 1,252, which should help slow down the team with the league's most effective running game.
