# Murray And Rune's Brisbane International Doubles Entry For 2024

Andy Murray added the doubles at the Brisbane International to his Australian Open warm-up schedule, partnering 20-year-old Holger Rune, as reported by The Roar on 19 December 2023.

Murray, a former world No. 1, had already entered the singles draw at the tournament, and the doubles entry gives the pair a 16-year age gap. It will be the first time Murray and Rune play together.

The report adds two details of context: Rune is coached by Boris Becker and is world ranked No. 8, while Murray is trying to restart his momentum after a disappointing 2023 in which he slipped to world No. 42.
