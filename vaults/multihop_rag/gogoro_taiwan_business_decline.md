# Gogoro's Declining Taiwan Business

Gogoro's growth is stagnant and even dropping in Taiwan, as CEO Horace Luke admitted to TechCrunch in December 2023, despite the company's dominant position there: its battery-swapping system captures 93% of all electric vehicles in Taiwan, of which about 80% use Gogoro's own branded vehicles, and it is not limited to two-wheelers at home, also offering swappable-battery technology to players running auto-rickshaws.

The financial picture matches. In its recent earnings report, the Nasdaq-listed company reported a 10.2% year-on-year drop in revenue to $91.8 million, resulting in a net loss of $3.1 million, down from net income of $56.4 million in the same quarter a year earlier.

Luke gave two reasons for the decline: a lobbying effort against the speed of electric vehicle adoption after the 2020 elections, and the time the country took to recover from the COVID-19 pandemic. He also reframed the home market's role: "Taiwan has always been a pilot for us. It has always been a market where we used to develop the technology, develop the system so that when we do come to India, we're ready. And so that's where the pivot point now is." He further noted that gasoline in Taiwan has been subsidized to the level that it is available at an average price of 0.85 cents per liter, significantly less than the average price of $1.4 per liter in India, which is above the global price of $1.22.
