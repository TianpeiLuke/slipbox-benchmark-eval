# Role Player Signings And Trades At The 2023 Winter Meetings

Three secondary moves landed during the 2023 winter meetings — Victor Caratini to the Astros, Marco Gonzales re-routed from the Braves to the Pirates, and Wade Miley back with the Brewers — per the reporting Sporting News carried in its tracker.

On Tuesday, Dec. 5, Chandler Rome of The Athletic and Mark Feinsand of MLB.com reported veteran catcher Victor Caratini was headed to Houston on a two-year deal worth $12 million; the 30-year-old would serve as backup to new starter Yainer Diaz now that Martin Maldonado had moved on. Caratini had filled a similar role in Milwaukee the previous two years, slashing .259/.327/.383 with seven home runs in 62 games in 2023.

Also on Tuesday, Dec. 5, Jeff Passan of ESPN reported Marco Gonzales — acquired by the Braves in the trade that helped them land Jarred Kelenic on Sunday — was being re-routed to Pittsburgh for cash and a player to be named later. Gonzales, 31, was a decent rotation arm with 65 wins, 665 strikeouts and a 4.14 ERA over 893.0 innings pitched in his nine-year career. On Monday, Dec. 4, Ken Rosenthal reported that amid a turbulent offseason the Brewers would keep one piece of their rotation intact, as Wade Miley agreed to return on a one-year deal for 2024 with a mutual option for 2025; per ESPN's Jesse Rogers, Miley would make a base salary of $7 million the next season with the option at $12 million and a $1.5 million buyout. The 37-year-old lefty started 23 games for Milwaukee that year, going 9-4 with a 3.14 ERA.
