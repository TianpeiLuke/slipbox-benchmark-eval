# Gen V (Prime Video)

Gen V, run by showrunners Michele Fazekas and Tara Butters and starring Jaz Sinclair, Chance Perdomo, and Lizze Broadway, is set in the universe of The Boys and appears in Polygon's 2023-10-25 roundup of the best new TV series on every streaming service.

Becoming a superhero is hard — those origin stories are not all sunshine and rainbows — but it is doubly true in this world, where your powers manifest with your first period and then kill your mom, as happened to Marie, played by Jaz Sinclair. She eventually winds up at Godolkin University, the only university for supes in particular, in the land of Vought International, and trouble ensues from there.

Critic Zosha Millman says what makes the show compelling is how deftly it weaves its themes together: the latest conspiracy smoothly brings Marie and her cohort together, leapfrogging tropes and letting the story flow. Gen V manages the impossible — making superhero TV feel like more than a collection of origin story tropes and letting its characters feel very grounded and like people — and though it is The Boys, so a giant dick or a puppet massacre is guaranteed, that is worth the buy-in.
