# Grading The 2022 NFL Draft Quarterback Class

Sportskeeda graded the nine quarterbacks of Purdy's draft class and concluded that the last pick has outperformed all eight taken before him, awarding Purdy an A+ against grades ranging from B down to F for the rest. The grounds are each player's current standing on an NFL depth chart; the warrant joining grounds to claim is that a quarterback's grade should track whether he has won and held a starting job.

Working from the top: Kenny Pickett, Pittsburgh Steelers, Pick 20, grade B — the starting quarterback for the Steelers, a home-grown University of Pittsburgh talent whom the fans are behind, and while he has not done anything special in the NFL just yet, he is one of just four QBs in the 2022 class currently an undisputed starter. Desmond Ridder, Atlanta Falcons, Pick 74, grade C — he earned the starting job ahead of the 2023 season and has not been spectacular, but at least he is starting games at the highest level. Malik Willis, Tennessee Titans, Pick 86, grade D — the QB2 or QB3 in Tennessee depending on who you ask, which is interesting given that some draft analysts thought him the most naturally gifted QB in the class; he is fighting a losing battle after the franchise drafted the highly rated Will Levis, and a "D" might even be generous. Matt Corral, Carolina Panthers, Pick 94, grade E — the Panthers have mainly ignored him, electing to trade up to number one for Bryce Young in the 2023 draft, and he left the franchise ahead of his sophomore season.

Continuing: Bailey Zappe, New England Patriots, Pick 137, grade C- — Mac Jones' backup plays for the worst Patriots team of the past twenty years, not a dream spot for the Western Kentucky alum. Sam Howell, Washington Commanders, Pick 144, grade B — the franchise quarterback for the usually trigger-happy Commanders, which shows the new ownership group has faith in his ability, and he gets a positive grade for that sole reason. Chris Oladokun, Pittsburgh Steelers, Pick 241, grade F — yet to make a start in the NFL and rated an F until that changes, though it will not be on the team that drafted him as the Steelers waived him a while ago. Skylar Thompson, Miami Dolphins, Pick 247, grade D — the third-string QB heading into 2023, who could be eligible for a Super Bowl ring if the Dolphins go all the way. Finally, Brock Purdy, San Francisco 49ers, Pick 262, grade A+ — arguably the best "Mr. Irrelevant" of all time, who, if he stays healthy in San Francisco, can become one of his generation's finest QBs; the article suggests, clichéd as it is, that we might have the next Tom Brady in front of us.
