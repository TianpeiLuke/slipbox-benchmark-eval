# Power Bank For USB-C Laptops

A growing number of laptops recharge over USB-C, which makes it possible to use one charger for a laptop, a phone, and a Nintendo Switch — Apple's entire MacBook lineup allows it, as do several popular Windows laptops such as the Dell XPS 13 and Razer's Blade gaming laptop. The catch is that different laptops have different power requirements, so one power bank may not work well across multiple machines.

For a 13-inch MacBook Air or Pro, or a similarly sized Windows laptop, the chances are very good that a 65 W power bank is fast enough to recharge the machine as you use it, though Polygon advises checking your power supply's wattage to be sure. The scope condition: if you are trying to play games or do other resource-intensive tasks, the battery may recharge slowly, or continue to discharge, if your power bank is not powerful enough. For more powerful laptops there are bigger batteries that supply up to 140 W, currently a top-of-the-line spec costing significantly more than the other options — and reaching that 140 W top speed additionally requires a compatible USB-C-to-USB-C cable and a laptop that supports the PD 3.1 USB-C charging spec. One of Polygon's recommendations can match the speed of Apple's 140 W wall charger included with 16-inch MacBook Pro laptops, while on the Windows side most laptops do not go above 90 or 100 W at peak USB-C recharging speed, so its picks should suffice. Polygon concedes it might be tough to justify spending $100 to $150 on a power bank just to charge a laptop, but argues it is easier to justify if you frequently travel with multiple power-guzzling devices you would like to recharge simultaneously, since these are among the few that can manage that multitasking.
