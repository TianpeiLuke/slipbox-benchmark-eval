# Microsoft's OpenAI Investment And Early Capability Tests

The first look at the disruptive potential that generative AI offers came when ChatGPT managed to pass a series of law school exams — albeit by the skin of its digital teeth, Engadget notes.

Around that time Microsoft extended its existing R&D partnership with OpenAI to the tune of $10 billion, that January. Engadget's aside on the figure: that number is impressively large and likely why Altman still has his job.
