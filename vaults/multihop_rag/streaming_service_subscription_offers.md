# Streaming Service Subscription Offers

Two streaming subscriptions made the under-$25 Black Friday list. Max offered new and returning customers a six-month subscription to its ad-supported plan for $2.99 a month instead of $9.99 a month until November 27th — $18 in total against a $60 list, 70 percent off. The base plan provides access to all the same programming as the pricier tiers, including shows like The Last of Us and films like Barbie.

Hulu's ad-supported annual subscription was $12 against $80 — 85 percent off. Until November 28th at 11:59PM PT you could get a year of ad-supported Hulu for $11.88, which works out to 99 cents a month and a saving of $68 compared with buying the $79.99 annual plan.

Eligibility for the Hulu offer is specific: new and returning subscribers who canceled their Hulu subscription at least a month ago, as well as existing Disney Plus and ESPN Plus standalone subscribers.
