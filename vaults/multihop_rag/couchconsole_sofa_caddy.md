# CouchConsole Sofa Caddy

The CouchConsole is an organizational caddy to keep around your sofa, recommended by Polygon because crumb-covered seats and missing controllers cause frustration when you want to relax.

This snack- and controller-protecting companion has a modular design that can be tweaked to fit your needs, and it includes a convenient self-balancing cup holder to avoid beverage spills when you get a bit too excited. It also features a charging port, so phones, remotes, and controllers can stay juiced regardless of the marathon movie session you plan to undertake with friends.
