# Amex Cash Magnet Rewards Structure And Exclusions

Earning rewards with the Cash Magnet Card is simple: you earn an unlimited 1.5% cash back on every purchase you make.

The exclusions define the boundary of "every purchase." No rewards are earned on balance transfers, cash advances, purchases of traveler's checks, purchases of prepaid cards or reloading card balances, or person-to-person transfers such as Venmo transactions. One further condition applies to the whole billing period: American Express doesn't allow cardholders to earn rewards during a billing period if the cardmember hasn't paid the minimum payment due by the due date.
