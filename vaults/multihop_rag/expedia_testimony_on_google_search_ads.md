# Expedia's Testimony On Google Ad Costs And Organic Listings

On November 1, 2023, Barry Diller, chair of Expedia and IAC, testified about his concerns that the increasing number of ads in search results was having an impact on organic listings. Quoting a letter he had written to Google back in 2019, per Bloomberg: "I must say I'm on the edge of revolt now that Google's actions are so punitive, not just for Expedia but also for IAC and all the players that depend upon something of a level playing field." Google CEO Sundar Pichai fired back that Google's travel listings was one of the most popular experiences the company built.

Expedia executives also testified about the cost of ads and how increases had no impact on search results. On October 19, 2023, Expedia's former chief operating officer, Jeff Hurst, told the court that the company's ad fees increased tenfold, from $21 million in 2015 to $290 million in 2019, and yet Expedia's traffic from Google did not increase. The implication was that this was due to direct competition from Google itself; Hurst pointed out that Google began sharing its own flight and hotel data in search results in that period, according to The Seattle Times.
