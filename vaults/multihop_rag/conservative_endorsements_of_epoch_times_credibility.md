# Conservative Endorsements Of The Epoch Times' Credibility

Prominent conservatives argue that The Epoch Times is credible journalism rather than a propaganda operation, and the outlet's own strategist argues that this standing was deliberately built and achieved.

The grounds offered are personal endorsements from figures with reach. Anti-vaccine activist and presidential candidate Robert F. Kennedy Jr. calls The Epoch Times a daily read and among his most trusted news sources, telling NBC News in the summer of 2023: "They have a real bias against China, but on other reporting, they're very courageous and it's real journalism." In July, Rep. Ralph Norman, R-S.C., recited the history of The Epoch Times into the congressional record, saying "This is all about one word: freedom."

The warrant joining those endorsements to the claim of success is supplied by Brendan Steinhauser, a Texas-based Republican strategist hired by The Epoch Times in 2018 to open doors to conservative politicians and players: "They achieved the goal," he said, explaining that a series of interviews and introductions with politicians and media figures at that year's Conservative Political Action Conference offered access to a wider conservative audience — and The Epoch Times was a "supporting sponsor" of this year's conference. "They take advantage of every opportunity," Steinhauser, who is not currently working for The Epoch Times, continued. "They studied digital marketing. They learned how to work the system. And they did it. They're smarter than people give them credit for, and they've got the money to back it up."
