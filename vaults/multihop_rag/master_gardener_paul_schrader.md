# Master Gardener (Paul Schrader)

Master Gardener is the third film in Paul Schrader's thematic "Man in a Room" trilogy, starring Joel Edgerton, Quintessa Swindell, and Sigourney Weaver. Polygon ranked it 40 on its best movies of 2023 list of 2023-10-07 and listed it on Hulu or for digital rental/purchase.

It follows a reformed former white supremacist, played by Joel Edgerton, who works as a horticulturist on a grand Southern estate; when the young Black relative of the estate's owner, played by Quintessa Swindell and Sigourney Weaver respectively, arrives, the two form an unlikely bond.

Critic PV calls it a gorgeous movie filled with fields of flowers and excellent performances from Edgerton and Swindell, and identifies what sets it apart from many other "reformed racist" movies: Edgerton's character is already distanced from his past self, rather than the film relying on his relationship with a Black person to spur that change. He is a new person, but that does not erase his history — a wonderful coda to a superb trilogy.
