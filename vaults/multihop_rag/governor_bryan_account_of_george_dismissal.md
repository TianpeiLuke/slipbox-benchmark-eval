# Governor Bryan's Account Of George's Dismissal

After George was dismissed in December 2022, Governor Albert Bryan Jr.'s office said her dismissal was unrelated to her pursuit of JPMorgan — directly contesting the premise that she was fired for going after the bank.

Bryan later gave a different reason, saying he couldn't have an attorney general launching lawsuits he didn't know about. He was in any case clear about wanting the matter closed: "If we never mention Jeffrey Epstein again, that would be good for me," Bryan told The St. Thomas Source. His office didn't respond to requests for comment.
