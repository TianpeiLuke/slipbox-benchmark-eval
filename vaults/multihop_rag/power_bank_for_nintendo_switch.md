# Power Bank For The Nintendo Switch

The Nintendo Switch console with detachable Joy-Cons can receive a maximum of 18 W through its USB-C charging port, and the Switch Lite is slightly less demanding — so the very same compact power bank Polygon recommends for phones and tablets can be used to charge a Switch.

Polygon repeats its cable advice here: buy more charging cables than you will immediately need and stow one permanently in the bag you frequently use. It points to a two-pack of 100 W-ready, six-foot-long USB-C-to-C cables at Amazon for $15.99.
