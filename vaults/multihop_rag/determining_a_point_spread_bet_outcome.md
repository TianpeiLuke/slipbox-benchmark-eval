# Determining A Point Spread Bet Outcome

To settle a point spread wager, compare the final margin of victory against the spread: the favorite covers by winning by a margin greater than the specified point spread, while the underdog covers by either winning the game outright or losing by fewer points than the spread. Those are the potential outcomes of a point spread wager as Sporting News sets them out.

Applied to a favorite that covers, suppose an NBA betting site has set the point spread for a Los Angeles Lakers-New York Knicks game at Lakers -7. This means the Lakers are the favorites and are expected to win by 7 points, so a spread wager on the Lakers requires them to win by more than 7. If the final score is Lakers 110, Knicks 100, the Lakers won by a margin of 10 points; since that is more than the 7-point spread, a bet on the Lakers wins.

Applied to a favorite that fails to cover, suppose an NFL betting site has set the point spread for a Chicago Bears-Miami Dolphins game at Bears -4, indicating the Bears are the favorites and are expected to win by 4 points; betting the Bears means betting they will win by more than 4. If the final score is Chicago Bears 24, Miami Dolphins 23, the Bears have won by only a 1-point margin. Since that is less than the 4-point spread, a bet on the Bears loses — despite winning the game, they failed to "cover" the 4-point spread, so those who wagered on them lost.
