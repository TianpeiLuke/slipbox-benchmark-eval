# England 171 All Out In The First T20I

England were bowled out for 171 with three deliveries to spare in the first T20I in Barbados, an innings of two halves that ran from 77 for no loss to all out.

Tymal Mills got a gossamer-thin edge through to Pooran to end the innings, completing what the live blog called a turnaround for West Indies, who had been staring down the barrel with England flying at 77-0 after six overs. The closing wickets came in quick succession: Liam Livingstone chopped a cutter from Andre Russell onto his stumps for 27 with England at 167-7; Rehan Ahmed followed shortly after for 1, trying to smear a length ball down the ground and finding Powell lurking ten yards in from the sponge, leaving England 169-8 and giving Russell two in the over to finish his spell — and his first T20I in two years — with figures of 3-19; and Adil Rashid went for 0, swiping at Joseph in the final over and hacking the ball onto his own stumps to make it 170-9. As Aatif Nawaz posted, England's recent white-ball approach was a variable one yielding mixed and often frustrating results.
