# CRED's 255% Operating Revenue Growth To $168.1 Million

CRED grew its operating revenue by a staggering 255% to $168.1 million in the financial year ending March, as the fintech startup found rising adoption of its lending and commerce offerings among India's affluent individuals.

The Bengaluru-headquartered startup, which garners an unusually high level of attention, had a total income of about $50 million in the financial year ending March of the prior year, and $11.4 million in the year before that.

The figures were reported by Manish Singh.
