# Notre Dame At Louisville: The 2023 Week 6 Betting Line

No. 10 Notre Dame was favoured by 6.5 points at No. 25 Louisville, according to CBS Sports' Week 6 lines published 1 October 2023.

Notre Dame arrived off two consecutive dogfights: the Fighting Irish had their hearts broken by Ohio State in Week 4 before playing the role of heartbreaker at Duke in Week 5. Louisville remained unbeaten a month into the Jeff Brohm era.

The line's rationale is experience rather than statistics. There isn't a ton separating these teams statistically on either side of the ball, but Notre Dame is the more battle tested of the two, so it's not a shock to see the Irish checking in as a touchdown favourite. This is Louisville's first time facing ranked competition in 2023, and it should give the best idea yet of what Brohm's squad is truly made of.
