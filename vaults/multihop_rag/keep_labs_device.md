# The Keep, A Lockable Smart Storage Container For Medicine

The Keep is a lockable storage container for medicine that both secures its contents and tracks their use, and it works regardless of how the medication is packaged — bottles, boxes or dime bags alike. Keep Labs launched in 2019, won and then lost an innovation award at CES 2020, and, in the week of TechCrunch's 4 October 2023 report, started shipping its first product to consumers.

What distinguishes the device from a plain lockbox is the combination of consumer-product design and sensing. The unit looks more like a smart speaker than a secure box: there is a clock on the front and a soft matte finish. Clicking a button in the app opens the lid, revealing a space that holds about six bottles of pills; with secure mode disabled, a double tap on the front opens the device without needing a smartphone at all. Usage tracking runs off weight — the Keep uses a sophisticated scale to detect bottles that were returned just a little bit lighter, and records that in the app. The inverse case is handled too: if a dose is not taken, the Keep knows, and can trigger a smartphone notification on the user's phone or a companion's.

The boundary the founders draw is that the Keep is not merely a secure box. To them it is a solution to harm reduction and medication management, which is why the tracking behaviour, and not just the lock, is treated as the core of the product.
