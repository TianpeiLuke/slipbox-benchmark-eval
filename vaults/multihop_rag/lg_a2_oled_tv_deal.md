# LG A2 OLED TV Deal

Engadget expected many more TV deals closer to Black Friday 2023, but as of 2023-11-19 one standout was LG's 48-inch A2 OLED TV on sale for a new low of $550 at Best Buy.

This is an entry-level model from 2022, so it's limited to a 60Hz refresh rate, lacks HDMI 2.1 ports and doesn't support other gaming-friendly features like VRR. That said, it's still an OLED TV, so it delivers deep contrast, bold colors, wide viewing angles and low input lag; at this price it should be a nice step-up option for smaller or secondary rooms — though it can't get as bright as higher-end OLED sets, so it's best situated away from direct sunlight.
