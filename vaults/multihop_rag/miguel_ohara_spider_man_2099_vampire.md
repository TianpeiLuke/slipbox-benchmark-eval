# Miguel O'Hara, Spider-Man 2099

Miguel O'Hara, the Spider-Man of 2099, is one of Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse's big enigmas — and one of the few things the film reveals about him is that he is kind of a vampire.

Comics readers will know plenty about his whole deal, but the movie does not really explain much, except for a brief scene showing he has fangs and an offhand reference to the vampirism. Polygon's aside is that he is a jerk but still kind of cool, which it says is hard not to be when you are a vampire — "that's kind of the whole quandary with vampires as a metaphor."
