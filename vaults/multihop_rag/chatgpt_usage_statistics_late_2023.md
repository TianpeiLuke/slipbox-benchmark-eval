# ChatGPT Usage Statistics, Late 2023

ChatGPT still gets a lot of attention a year after launch — at least if third-party statistics are anything to go by, TechCrunch notes on November 30, 2023, flagging that these figures come from outside measurement firms rather than OpenAI.

According to Similarweb, the web metrics company, OpenAI's ChatGPT web portal saw 140.7 million unique visitors in October 2023, while the ChatGPT iOS and Android apps have 4.9 million monthly active users in the U.S. alone.

Data from analytics firm Data.ai suggests that the apps have generated nearly $30 million in subscription revenue — a hefty amount, TechCrunch observes, considering that they launched just a few months earlier.
