# Kevin Federline's Marriage To Victoria Prince

Victoria Prince is Kevin Federline's wife, a volleyball player he married in 2013 after moving on from his relationship with Britney Spears. The couple share two daughters. The two Independent accounts, published two days apart in October 2023, give their names and ages slightly differently: the 18 October piece calls them Jordan, 10, and Peyton, eight, while the 20 October piece gives their full names as Jordan Kay, 12, and Peyton Marie, nine.

The marriage is presented as the post-divorce chapter of the Spears-Federline story: after the divorce, the two appeared to be co-parenting amicably, seen supporting their boys at soccer and football games, and Federline moved on to Prince.
