# Spill's User Reception And In-Person Community

Spill has come to be a bright spot in the post-Twitter era, with users organizing in-person meetups in Atlanta, New York and "Spillicon Valley."

One user's account illustrates the retention claim: Anne Griffin, a product manager based in New York, signed up for Bluesky, Spill and Threads the previous year, and out of those three Spill is the only one she is still consistently using. "It feels much more rooted in authentic connection," she said of the app, praising its product features. "In a world where many social media algorithms focus on engagement at any cost, it's refreshing to have an app that is focused on engagement around community in culture."

At AfroTech, Spill gave out customized tea bags created with the help of Nigerian-American tea sommeliers, while lines formed to use a Spill-branded 360 camera at a house party — inspired by an actual trend on the app where people share photos of themselves on Fridays.
