# West Indies' Death Overs Win In The First T20I

West Indies won the first T20I by four wickets when Andre Russell smashed the winning runs off Sam Curran — a length ball spanked over point for a one-bounce four — to go 1-0 up in the series.

The closing overs swung twice. In the thirteenth Rehan Ahmed had two balls left, the first flayed for four by Powell behind point and the last a dot, leaving West Indies needing 60 from 42. In the fourteenth Liam Livingstone came on instead of Rashid and kept it to singles as England tried to take the game as deep as possible and hope West Indies fluffed their lines at the death, with 55 needed from 36. Rehan then struck twice: Shai Hope, who had just hit him for six, holed out to Brook for 36 at 123-5, and Shepherd poked a leg break to Curran at slip first ball for 0 at 123-6, putting Rehan on a hat-trick. Russell faced the hat-trick ball and patted a decent delivery on the stumps back, then worked a couple; Ahmed finished with 3-39 from his four overs, leaving 47 needed from 30. In the sixteenth Livingstone conceded four singles before Rovman Powell crunched consecutive sixes down the ground for sixteen off the over — 31 needed from 24. In the seventeenth Tymal Mills conceded sixteen, including a mighty top-edged six from Powell and a short, wide ball sent to the fence, leaving 15 needed from 18. In the eighteenth Rashid was recalled and Russell smashed his googly clean out of the ground for six, then drove a four, eleven off the over leaving West Indies needing four.
