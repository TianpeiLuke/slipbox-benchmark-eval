# Diablo 4 Sorcerer Firewall Endgame Build

This Sorcerer endgame build, based on Northwar's guide at MaxRoll.gg and published by Polygon on 2023-10-18, is a general endgame build capable of finding success in all activities. It assumes you are over level 50, have access to all 58 skill points, and are making progress on your Paragon Boards; readers wanting detail on item affixes or the reasoning behind the Legendary powers are pointed to the original MaxRoll guide. The build uses Firewall as its core skill and main means of dealing damage, plus Fire Bolt, Inferno, Teleport, Ice Armor, and Flame Shield.

Because all 58 points are needed, the order of selection does not matter as long as the prerequisite points exist in earlier Skill Tree tiers. The allocation is: Fire Bolt / Enhanced Fire Bolt / Glinting Fire Bolt; Fireball / Enhanced Fireball / Greater Fireball; Potent Warding (rank 1-3); Devastation; Elemental Dominance (rank 1-3); Flame Shield / Enhanced Flame Shield / Shimmering Flame Shield; Teleport (rank 1-4) / Enhanced Teleport / Shimmering Teleport; Ice Armor / Enhanced Ice Armor; Glass Cannon (rank 1-3); Precision Magic (rank 1-3); Align the Elements; Mana Shield (rank 1-3); Protection (rank 1-3); Firewall (rank 1-5) / Enhanced Firewall / Wizard's Firewall; Inner Flames (rank 1-3); Crippling Flames (rank 1-3); Inferno / Prime Inferno / Supreme Inferno; Fiery Surge; Endless Pyre; and Combustion.

The two Enchantment Slots take the Firewall Enchantment and Fireball. Northwar recommends five Vampiric Powers — Anticipation, Prey on the Weak, Ravenous, Flowing Veins, and Metamorphosis — and to slot those powers you need these Pacts available on your armor: 9 Ferocity, 5 Divinity, and 4 Eternity.

The build also needs powerful Legendary Powers and Unique items. Northwar's recommendations by slot are: Helm — Shared Misery (dungeon drop) or Godslayer Crown (Unique); Chest — Snowveiled (dungeon drop) or Raiment of the Infinite (Unique); Gloves — Edgemaster's (dungeon drop), Conceited, or Storm Swell (dungeon drop); Pants — Disobedience (dungeon drop); Boots — Ghostwalker (dungeon drop), Bounding Conduit (dungeon drop), or Flickerstep (Unique); Wand — Edgemaster's (dungeon drop), Conceited, or Storm Swell (dungeon drop); Focus — Edgemaster's (dungeon drop), Conceited, or Storm Swell (dungeon drop); Amulet — Control (dungeon drop); Ring 1 — Prodigy's (dungeon drop) or Retribution (dungeon drop); Ring 2 — Engulfing Flames or X'Fal's Corroded Signet (Unique). Stat priorities are left to Northwar's original guide.

For the Paragon Board system, which Polygon calls very complex, Glyphs must be carefully paired with specific boards: Starter Board / Reinforced Glyph; Burning Instinct / Destruction Glyph; Searing Heat / Adept Glyph; Static Surge / Control Glyph; Enchantment Master / Exploit. Glyphs should be leveled to 15 in this specific order: Destruction, Control, Adept, Exploit, Reinforced. Players can path their own way through the boards following these basic guidelines, but for the best results should follow Northwar's path exactly.
