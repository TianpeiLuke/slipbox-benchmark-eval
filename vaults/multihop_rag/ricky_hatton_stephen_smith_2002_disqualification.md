# Ricky Hatton vs Stephen Smith, 2002: The Disqualification

Ricky Hatton's 2002 fight with Stephen Smith — his 30th — ended in a disqualification after Smith's father and cornerman, Darkie, piled into the ring and began shoving referee Mickey Vann, talkSPORT recalled on 15 November 2023.

Hatton was doing a number on Smith when his elbow caught his opponent and opened a cut. It looked like an accident, but not to Darkie Smith, who entered the ring when his son went down.

Considering it happened in front of 15,000 fans in Manchester, the Smiths did well to make it back to the dressing room, being pelted only with cups filled with (hopefully) beer. "Boxing used to be a clean and fair sport; it isn't any more," moaned Darkie. It was, as talkSPORT put it, an eventful night for Hatton in 2002.
