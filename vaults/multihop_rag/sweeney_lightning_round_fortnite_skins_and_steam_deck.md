# Sweeney's Lightning Round: Fortnite Skins And The Steam Deck

In a lightning round closing The Verge's post-verdict interview, Tim Sweeney named Jellie — the jellyfish, "you know, the big tentacles" — as his favourite Fortnite skin, answered a question about ever announcing Unreal Tournament 3 X with "Forever in motion, the future is," and explained that Fortnite is still not playable on Steam Deck because of the Linux problem and a shortage of programmers.

On the Steam Deck he was complimentary: he loves the hardware, said Valve has done an amazing job, and wished it would reach tens of millions of users, at which point supporting it would actually make sense. Asked whether Epic is in the "aggressive pursuit model" the company internally floated in October 2019 for the Epic Games Store — aggressively paying more for exclusive games to pull in more users — he said no, Epic is in a different model called the "Ridiculously aggressive pursuit model": if it's a 10-slide deck, that's on slide 11. Asked whether he got a bingo on his Verge bingo card, he said it was almost a complete shutout with only one cell on the entire board missing, and singled out "lick the cookie" as the entry that impressed him, a phrase from the 1999 US v. Microsoft antitrust trial.
