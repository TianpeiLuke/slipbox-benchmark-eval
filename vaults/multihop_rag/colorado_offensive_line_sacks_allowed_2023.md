# Colorado's Offensive Line Is A Bowl-Eligibility Problem

The Sporting News argues that if Colorado wants to solidify a bowl game it will have to fix many things, one of them being the offensive line — the grounds being that in the Oct. 7, 2023 Arizona State game the line allowed five sacks on Shedeur Sanders, bringing the Buffaloes' season total to a nation-leading 31.

The warrant is that a nation-leading sack total is a durable structural weakness rather than a one-game aberration: the count is cumulative across the season, and it was already conspicuous mid-game. At 9:10 p.m. the live blog recorded the fourth sack allowed by the Colorado offensive line, noting that the Buffaloes then led FBS with the most sacks allowed this season at 30 — so the 31st, allowed later in the same game, extended a lead Colorado already held nationally.

The same passage supplies a contrast that sharpens the point: Colorado's own defense picked up five sacks of its own to maintain pressure on Trenton Bourguet. The problem is therefore located specifically in pass protection, not in the team's ability to generate or withstand pressure generally.
