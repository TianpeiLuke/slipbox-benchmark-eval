# The Week 14 Fantasy Quarterback Landscape

Week 14 of the 2023 season was the final week of the fantasy football regular season, and although fantasy's highest-scoring position had been beaten and battered that year, Sporting News judged there were serviceable signal-callers capable of leading a team to victory in a pivotal week.

The column's Week 14 quarterback rankings were framed as help for start-'em, sit-'em moves to cap off the regular season. The complicating conditions it named were that byes were still in play and that there had been two more quarterback injuries the previous week; even so, the position remained deep with high-scoring studs and potential sleepers. It also noted the season had reached the point where weather could factor into start-sit decisions, but that finding quarterback production should not be a problem in 10- and 12-team leagues.
