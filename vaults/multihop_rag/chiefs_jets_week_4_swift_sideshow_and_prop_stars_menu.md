# The Chiefs-Jets Week 4 Swift Sideshow And FanDuel's "Prop Stars" Menu

Ahead of the Week 4 Chiefs-Jets Sunday Night Football game, Sporting News wrote on 2023-09-28 that one thing was certain when it came to the media, social media, newspapers, football fans, Swifties and just about everyone else within the 50 states and U.S. territories including Puerto Rico and Guam: "You need to calm down." Kelce and Swift were taking their tour on the road to Broadway, a place that puts on all the great shows; the traditional circus hadn't been in town for a few years, simply because the New York Jets exist and there was only room for one. It seemed like ages since Aaron Rodgers marched out of the MetLife Stadium tunnel holding an American flag, but this was Zach Wilson's team now, whether you like it or not.

One of the most hyped Sunday Night Football games of the year had been expected to feature Rodgers against Patrick Mahomes for the first time; instead, viewers were more likely to be treated to non-stop split screens involving Swift and Rodgers, two people who wouldn't be on the field. There would be some actual football, even if it was just a sideshow to the event everyone couldn't stop talking about — and fans paying ridiculous prices for a glimpse of Ms. Swift were told to make sure their phones were charged. The betting hook: for bettors, where there is hype there's also an opportunity to make some money, and FanDuel released a special prop bets menu, cleverly called "Prop Stars," for the Swift-inspired wagers.
