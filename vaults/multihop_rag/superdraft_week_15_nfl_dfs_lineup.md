# SuperDraft Week 15 NFL DFS Lineup

Sporting News' Week 15 SuperDraft NFL DFS lineup, published Dec. 13, 2023, was: QB Matthew Stafford, Rams (multiplier 1.3x); RB Rachaad White, Buccaneers (1.2x); RB Bijan Robinson, Falcons (1.25x); WR Puka Nacua, Rams (1.25x); WR Deebo Samuel, 49ers (1.2x); WR Rashee Rice, Chiefs (1.35x); TE Tyler Conklin, Jets (1.7x); and FLEX RB Derrick Henry, Titans (1.2x).

The stated construction logic was that the lineup aimed to keep the Rams stack together with Stafford and Nacua while adding other players with high floors and ceilings like White, Samuel and Henry. Considering the matchups, Sporting News liked taking shots on Robinson, Rice and Conklin, especially with the multipliers working overtime to bring in an even better score.
