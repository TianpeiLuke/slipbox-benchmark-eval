# C.J. Stroud Texans Midseason MVP

Yardbarker's Nov. 9, 2023 AFC midseason team MVP list named quarterback C.J. Stroud the Houston Texans' MVP, arguing he was having one of the best statistical rookie seasons of all time.

The grounds are his league ranks and his error rate: he ranked in the top 10 in passing yards with 2,270, touchdown passes with 14 and passer rating at 102.9, and had thrown only one interception. The warrant is stated as a counterfactual — the Texans, at 4-4, were not close to a .500 team without him.
