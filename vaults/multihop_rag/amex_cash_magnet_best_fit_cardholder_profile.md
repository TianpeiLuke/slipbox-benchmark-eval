# The Amex Cash Magnet Best-Fit Cardholder Profile

The American Express Cash Magnet Card is best for those who are looking for a simple and easy-to-use card they can use for all of their purchases.

The grounds are the two features that make it undemanding to hold: with no annual fee and 1.5% cash back on every purchase, it's a no-frills, no-hassle addition to your wallet.
