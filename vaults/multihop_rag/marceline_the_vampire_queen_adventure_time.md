# Marceline The Vampire Queen

For 11 episodes Adventure Time was a show with lots of weird stuff but no vampires; then the episode "Evicted!" introduced Marceline the Vampire Queen, and the show changed forever.

Polygon credits her with being key to what Adventure Time went on to do so well: using its goofy sensibilities to tackle existentially dark and frightening things quite seriously without losing its edge, and playing with familiar tropes to create a fantasy like no other — now with vampires.
