# Chicago Fire-Inter Miami First Half: Goalless, With The Fire Creating The Better Chances

The first half of the Oct. 4, 2023 Chicago Fire-Inter Miami match at a sold-out Soldier Field finished 0-0, with the Fire having the better chances but neither side taking a firm hold for an extended period. The game was billed at kickoff as a huge match in the MLS playoff race, played at the NFL venue in the Windy City.

Both teams began sloppily in midfield with needless giveaways; Inter Miami held most of the possession but struggled with bad turnovers, and Chicago could not take advantage (5th minute). The Fire's first big chance came in the seventh minute, when good work from Fabian Herbers down the right produced a cross into a two-against-one, but Brian Gutierrez could not control it and Gaston Gimenez, under pressure, sizzled his shot inches over the crossbar. Robert Taylor had Inter Miami's first shot on target in the 11th, a simple low save for Chris Brady; Chicago native and Ted Lasso actor Brendan Hunt was shown on the video board to applause. Inter Miami looked cleaner in attack by the 14th minute, when Farias was dispossessed at the top of the area with teammates streaking on goal. In the 15th, Chicago somehow failed to score: Drake Callender misread the flight of a corner and undercut the ball but recovered to keep out a shot at the near post, and Benjamin Cremaschi got a massive block on the follow-up to send the ball over the crossbar.

The rest of the half traded near-misses. In the 21st minute two Fire players combined down the left to beat DeAndre Yedlin, but the cross to a leaping Herbers — attempting a karate kick — went wide. In the 23rd Noah Allen's vertical ball sliced the Fire defense to find Yedlin at the far post; Yedlin slid to keep it in play, Brady spilled it, but Josef Martinez could not quite reach the loose ball. Kamal Miller made an exquisite block on Gutierrez in the 29th. By the 35th the hosts had a foothold and earned several consecutive corners — four straight went begging — and Chicago screamed for a penalty when the ball hit Kryvtsov on the arm, but VAR did not intervene because his arm was in a natural position down by his side. In the 38th an exceptional run by Georgios Koutsias produced a shot that deflected off Kryvtsov's foot and rolled toward goal, wrong-footing Callender, only to hit the inside of the post and come out. Sergio Busquets took the match's first yellow card in the 42nd for a foul on Gutierrez near the center circle. At the break Martino told the Apple TV+ broadcast: "We have to play further forward, we played side to side too much."
