# Travis Kelce at the Eras Tour in Buenos Aires

Travis Kelce showed his support for Taylor Swift by attending her Eras Tour show in Buenos Aires, Argentina, on 11 November 2023, where Swift gave a shout-out to the Chiefs player by changing her "Karma" lyrics to reflect their relationship.

"Karma is the guy on the Chiefs, coming straight home to me," she sang, letting out a laugh as fans in the audience screamed in response. The "All Too Well" singer was later seen running into Kelce's arms and kissing the NFL player following the concert, as he whisked her away backstage.
