# Week 14 Bye Week Impact On Fantasy Wide Receivers

The final set of bye weeks in the 2023 season saw only the Commanders and Cardinals idle in Week 14, resulting in the losses of Terry McLaurin, Marquise Brown, Jahan Dotson, Curtis Samuel, Rondale Moore and Greg Dortch, per Sporting News on Dec. 7, 2023.

Even worse for fantasy managers, Washington and Arizona sat as two of the weakest pass defenses in the league, so owners would not be able to count on big stats from their receivers going against the Commanders and Cardinals either.
