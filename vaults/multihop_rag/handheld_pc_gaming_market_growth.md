# Handheld PC Gaming Market Growth

The argument is that the Steam Deck's own success created the component supply that made the OLED model possible. According to the developers Engadget spoke with at Valve, this is the definitive version of the Steam Deck — the HDR OLED model is what they would have released the first time around, if the technology had existed before 2022.

The grounds are the market's pace: the handheld market has evolved quickly, and in just over a year the Steam Deck proved there's widespread demand for handheld PCs, with its success helping drive chip makers and display manufacturers to build hardware specifically for high-fidelity mobile play. The warrant is that this supply-side shift benefits the whole category, not only Valve: this is great news for Valve and the Steam Deck, and also the other handhelds coming out of companies like Ayaneo, ASUS, Ayn, Logitech, Retroid and Razer — and, joking, maybe even Playdate will get an HDR OLED display one day, though Playdate is literally perfect.
