# James Cook's Surge In Buffalo's Rushing Workload

Before Week 15 of the 2023 season James Cook had never rushed more than 17 times in a game; he then carried 25 times against Dallas for 179 yards and earned AFC Offensive Player of the Week, making Bills fans hopeful the offense would keep giving him more than 20 rushes a game.

The preview argued that with the Bills we often see several versions and levels of a team through the year, and with Buffalo it largely boils down to their ground game: Ty Johnson and Latavius Murray are serviceable running backs but lack the game-breaking speed and big-play ability Cook offers. Cook's 179-yard outburst coupled with backup Ty Johnson's shoulder injury in Week 15 were both expected to contribute to Cook garnering 20-plus touches in Week 16.
