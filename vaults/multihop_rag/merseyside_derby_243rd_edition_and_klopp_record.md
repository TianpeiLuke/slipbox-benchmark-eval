# The 243rd Merseyside Derby And Klopp's Record

The Merseyside derby of October 2023 was the 243rd edition of the most played derby in English football, and The Guardian argued on 20 October that it offered an ideal opportunity for Liverpool to grant Klopp's request for improvement, given how one-sided the fixture had been on his watch — regardless of another early kick-off after an international break playing havoc with preparations.

Klopp's record in the fixture was overwhelming: he had lost one of his 17 encounters with Everton, Liverpool one of the past 23 Premier League meetings at Anfield, and one of the past 25 league derbies home or away.

After the trials at Tottenham and the frustration at Brighton, The Guardian's assessment was that Klopp needed Liverpool to embrace the intensity of the Merseyside derby when they arrived home.
