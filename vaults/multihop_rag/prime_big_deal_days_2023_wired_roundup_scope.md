# WIRED's Prime Big Deal Days 2023 Roundup Scope

WIRED's October 10, 2023 roundup covers Amazon Prime Day Part II — technically called Prime Big Deal Days, though like most people WIRED thinks of it as Prime Day Deux — and collects the best discounts on laptops, tablets, kitchen and home gear, headphones, and plenty more.

Two scope conditions define the list. Access: as usual, most of these Prime Day deals require a Prime membership, though a 30-day free trial is available to make the most of the event. Method and maintenance: WIRED tests products year-round and handpicked these deals; products sold out or no longer discounted as of publishing are crossed out, and the guide is updated regularly throughout Prime Day by adding fresh deals and removing dead ones.
