# Canada vs Jamaica Second Leg: Kickoff And Broadcast

The CONCACAF Nations League quarterfinal second leg at BMO Stadium kicked off on Tuesday, Nov. 21, 2023 at 7:30 p.m. local time in Toronto — 7:30 p.m. Eastern, 6:30 p.m. Central, 5:30 p.m. Mountain and 4:30 p.m. Pacific.

Coverage differed by country. In Canada the match was on OneSoccer, which is available to stream on Fubo. In the USA there was no television channel and the game streamed on Paramount+; English-language broadcasts of the entire CONCACAF Nations League, with the exception of USMNT matches, are found in the United States exclusively on the CBS streaming service.
