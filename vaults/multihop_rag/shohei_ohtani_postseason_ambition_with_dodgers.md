# Shohei Ohtani's Postseason Ambition With The Dodgers

Shohei Ohtani has never played a postseason game in his big-league career, and joining the Dodgers gives him the opportunity to add collective hardware to his trophy case, The New York Times noted on 9 December 2023.

His individual brilliance was not enough to lift the Angels into the postseason. The Dodgers, by contrast, have won the National League West in 10 of the past 11 seasons, topped 100 victories in five of the past six full seasons, and won the World Series in 2020.

As the Angels foundered in recent years, Ohtani became more vocal about his desire to play for a winner. Only one stage remains for him, and he now has the chance to make a different kind of history.
