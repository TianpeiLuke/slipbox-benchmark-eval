# The Apple Watch ITC Import Ban And Apple's Preemptive Sales Halt

Earlier in 2023 the US International Trade Commission ruled that Apple had infringed on two patents from medical device maker Masimo, and said it would impose an import ban on the Apple Watch Series 9 and Ultra 2 starting December 26th — and Apple responded by preemptively pulling the watches from its online store starting December 21st at 3PM ET, with the models disappearing from Apple Stores after the 24th.

The Verge wrote on December 19th that it had been hard to believe the ban would come to pass, since Apple gets sued all the time and rarely faces dramatic consequences even when it loses — but that this was one of those times, with experts saying that barring a Christmas miracle it is unlikely Apple will find a way to escape. Apple's decision to pull the watches shocked everyone. "I think Apple sees the writing on the wall and they're preparing themselves," said Ben Levi, a partner at Levi Snotherly & Schaumberg, which has experience litigating ITC disputes. The Verge's framing: it's rare to see Big Tech lose, and with such tangible consequences — and while there are still ways Apple can keep the watches on sale, it'll likely take a lot of waiting or a lot of money.
