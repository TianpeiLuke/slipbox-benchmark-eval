# The Kelce Brothers' Criticism Of NFL Swift Coverage

Travis and Jason Kelce said on 6 October 2023 that the NFL was doing too much with its Swift-Kelce coverage, a counter aimed not at showing celebrities at all but at the amount of it, according to CBS Sports' timeline published 12 October 2023.

Travis conceded the underlying value before rejecting the execution: "I think it's fun when they show who's at the game. I think it brings a little bit more to the atmosphere, brings a little bit more to what you're watching. But at the same time, they're overdoing it a little bit."

Jason attacked the league's inexperience by comparison with another sport: "I just think the NFL is not used to celebrities coming to the games. Like basketball has it all figured out. They're all courtside, they're sitting there. They show 'em once or twice, but then they get back to the game."
