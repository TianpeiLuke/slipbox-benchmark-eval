# What Drivers Can Do To Reduce Tire Pollution

Given that most of the relevant research is happening at an industry level, it is easy to feel helpless about the role we all play in contributing this pollution when we are just trying to get to work — but Molden suggests three things drivers can do in the interim: drive more slowly, drive more smoothly, and choose the right tires.

On the third, the rule of thumb is to match the tire to the car rather than to the price. According to Molden the tires your car was outfitted with when new will provide the best wear characteristics: "It's a bit like going to a flight in restaurants, and each course has a wine match to the food," he says. Perfectly paired tires may cost more than whatever you can get at a local discounter, but they will provide better wear and, ultimately, less pollution.
