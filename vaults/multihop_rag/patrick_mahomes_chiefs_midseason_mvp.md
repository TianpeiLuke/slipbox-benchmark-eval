# Patrick Mahomes Chiefs Midseason MVP

Yardbarker's Nov. 9, 2023 AFC midseason team MVP list named quarterback Patrick Mahomes the Kansas City Chiefs' MVP: a shaky receiving corps had not fazed him.

The grounds are his league ranks — third in the NFL in passing yards with 2,442, fourth in touchdown passes with 17, and third in QBR at 73. The forward claim is that the 28-year-old might capture his third MVP, warranted by market evidence: oddschecker noted he was favored to win the award again.
