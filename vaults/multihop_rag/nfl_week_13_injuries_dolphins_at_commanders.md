# NFL Week 13 Injuries Dolphins At Commanders

In the Week 13 final injury reports published by CBSSports.com on Dec. 1, 2023, with the Dolphins listed at -9.5 at the Commanders, every player Miami listed as questionable for Sunday's game against Washington was able to practice on a limited basis on Friday.

That included Cotton and Lamm, who had both been DNPs earlier in the week. Rookie running back De'Von Achane was a full participant in Friday's practice and head coach Mike McDaniel said he would play that week after missing time with a knee injury. Washington could be without its starting center as it hosted Miami: Larsen, who missed Wednesday's practice due to a knee injury, was limited on Friday and officially listed as questionable. The Commanders would also play Week 13 without Forbes Jr., who had been sidelined since suffering an elbow injury against the Giants in Week 11 — head coach Ron Rivera called the injury "significant" earlier that week.
