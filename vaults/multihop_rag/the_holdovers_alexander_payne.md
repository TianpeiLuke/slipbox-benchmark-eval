# The Holdovers (Alexander Payne)

The Holdovers is Alexander Payne's film starring Paul Giamatti, Da'Vine Joy Randolph, and Dominic Sessa, ranked 3 on Polygon's best movies of 2023 list of 2023-10-07 and available in theaters or for digital rental/purchase. Polygon pitches it as a guaranteed surprise for anyone who hates sentimental, clichéd "abandoned kid and aging curmudgeon create found family" movies.

Scripted by Kitchen Confidential creator and writer David Hemingson, the film reunites Payne, director of Election and Nebraska, with his Sideways star Paul Giamatti, playing a buoyantly stuffy professor at a '70s private boys' prep school where he is tasked with overseeing "the holdovers" — the kids staying at the school for Christmas while their more fortunate classmates head home or out on adventurous holidays.

The film is full of sudden twists, mostly backstory reveals suitable for a particularly startling stage play, but critic Tasha Robinson says the real surprise is how personal and specific it becomes, with excellent writing and acting helping it dodge the expected parameters. It eventually settles into a three-hander between Professor Hunham, his troubled adolescent student Angus — played by Dominic Sessa in an intense star-making performance — and Mary, played by Da'Vine Joy Randolph, the school's head cook, an older Black woman mourning her son's recent death in the military. All three characters get room to develop and reveal themselves, handled with sensitivity and real warmth that goes far beyond the usual sentimental holiday feel-good fare; if anything this is a pretty dark story, and a beautifully calibrated, touching drama about starting over when there is no other choice available.
