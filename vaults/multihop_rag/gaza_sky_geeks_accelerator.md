# Gaza Sky Geeks Accelerator

Gaza Sky Geeks is an Alphabet-backed initiative based in Gaza that provides pre-seed investments, training and technology resources to Palestine's Gazan population, and has been a beacon of entrepreneurship in the region.

It is the largest tech hub in Palestine, providing a wide range of tech training at scale: in 2022, 5,000 coders and developers from across the West Bank and Gaza graduated from the program.
