# Day Of Reckoning Riyadh Boxing Card

"The Day of Reckoning" was a blockbuster pay-per-view boxing card staged on Dec. 23, 2023 at Kingdom Arena in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, headlined by Anthony Joshua against Otto Wallin with Deontay Wilder against Joseph Parker in the co-main event, as CBSSports.com set out that day.

Joshua (26-3, 23 KOs) welcomed former title challenger Otto Wallin (26-1, 14 KOs) in the main event, live on DAZN beginning at 11:30 a.m. ET, while Wilder (43-2-1, 42 KOs) returned from a 14-month layoff to face former champion Joseph Parker (33-3, 23 KOs) as Saudi Arabia continued to pour money into the sport to make the kind of fights that have been difficult in the past. Joshua and Wilder were not fighting each other that weekend, but each entered separate tests that could be the final hurdle before the former heavyweight champions finally shared the same ring in early 2024. The undercard was loaded with other top heavyweights looking to make a splash and stay in title contention as Tyson Fury and Oleksandr Usyk prepared to crown an undisputed champion in early 2024; a win could put any of these men in line for a shot at one of the four recognized titles sometime the following year.

The full card and odds: Anthony Joshua -455 vs Otto Wallin +345, heavyweight; Deontay Wilder -650 vs Joseph Parker +460, heavyweight; Dmitry Bivol (c) -3500 vs Lyndon Arthur +1350, WBA light heavyweight title; Daniel Dubois -220 vs Jarrell Miller +180, heavyweight; Jai Opetaia -2400 vs Ellis Zorro +1150, cruiserweight; Arslanbek Makhmudov -1000 vs Agit Kabayel +650, heavyweight; Frank Sanchez -2000 vs Junior Fa +1050, heavyweight; Filip Hrgovic vs Mark De Morio, heavyweight. Date: Dec. 23. Location: Kingdom Arena, Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. Start time: 11:30 a.m. ET. How to watch: DAZN PPV, price $39.99.
