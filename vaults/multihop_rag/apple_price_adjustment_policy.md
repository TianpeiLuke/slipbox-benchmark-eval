# Apple Price Adjustment Policy

Apple does not offer a price-matching policy: The Verge noted on October 25th, 2023 that while there have been some claims Apple will price-match up to 10 percent for products bought at certain major retailers, there is no official policy on the company's website.

What Apple does offer is a price adjustment on its own reductions. If Apple reduces its price on an Apple-branded product within 14 days from when you received your item, you can visit an Apple Store or contact the Apple Contact Center at 1-800-676-2775 to request a refund or get credit for the price difference. The exclusion that bounds it: this does not apply to special sales like Black Friday or Cyber Monday. Separately, buyers can still price-match Apple devices like the 15-inch MacBook Air elsewhere, assuming it is on sale.
