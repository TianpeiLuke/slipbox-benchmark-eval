# Logitech G535 Wireless Gaming Headset

The Logitech G535 was back down to $80, from $130 — a $50 listed saving, and about $25 below its average street price. It held that price through the end of the Prime Day event.

This is an honorable mention in Engadget's gaming headsets guide: the appeal is its lightweight design, Bluetooth support and agreeable, relatively well-balanced sound.

The drawbacks are that it forces you to crank the volume to reach a listenable level, its mic makes voices sound a little too thin, and it does not work with Xbox consoles. But if you really want a wireless gaming headset for less than $100, it is well worth a look.
