# Arnold Schwarzenegger's "Drill Instructor" Discipline

Arnold Schwarzenegger recently admitted he had a "drill instructor" approach to parenting when his children were young, telling People while promoting his new book that he once burned his daughter Katherine's shoes in front of her, FOX News Entertainment reported on 10 October 2023.

Schwarzenegger, who shares four children with ex-wife Maria Shriver and also shares a son with Mildred Baena, the family's former housekeeper, recalled a moment when Katherine brought over her 3-year-old daughter Lyla and he saw the lesson passed down: "Katherine comes over with Lyla, and she says, 'Lyla, I told you already not to put the shoes there. Keep your shoes on, or you put them away, but you don't leave them there by the stand in front of the fireplace because you know what Daddy did? When I left my shoes there twice? The third time, he burned them in front of me and I cried,'" he said. Schwarzenegger said the story was "absolutely" true and took pride in knowing that Katherine uses the same methods, "which she cried over, and she complained about", with Lyla as well as her other daughter Eloise, both of whom she shares with husband Chris Pratt.

The 76-year-old described similar approaches with his eldest son Patrick. When Patrick was 9 and had not made his bed, Schwarzenegger threw his mattress off the balcony: "I opened up the door to the balcony, picked up the mattress and threw it down with the bedsheets, the pillows, everything. I said, 'Don't ever make someone come in and clean your room, clean your shower or make your bed… Because I taught you how to make the bed.'" He also battled over shower time with Patrick, feeling he was taking too long, and installed a timer similar to ones he had used in Munich: "And so when he got the shower on, and he was whistling and singing in there and thinking Daddy's not around, all the sudden the ice-cold shower came on. And all of a sudden, we heard downstairs the scream."
