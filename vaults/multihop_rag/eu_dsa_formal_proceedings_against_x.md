# The EU's First Formal DSA Proceeding, Against X

On 18 December 2023 the European Commission announced the opening of a "formal proceeding" against Elon Musk's X, making it the first confirmed investigation opened by the European Union under its rebooted digital rulebook, the Digital Services Act (DSA). The investigation will look at whether the social networking platform may have breached rules linked to risk management, content moderation, dark patterns, advertising transparency and data access for researchers.

The Commission set out the evidentiary basis for the step: "On the basis of the preliminary investigation conducted so far, including on the basis of an analysis of the risk assessment report submitted by X in September, X's Transparency report published on 3 November, and X's replies to a formal request for information, which, among others, concerned the dissemination of illegal content in the context of Hamas' terrorist attacks against Israel, the Commission has decided to open formal infringement proceedings against X under the Digital Services Act."

The EU added that "if proven, these failures would constitute infringements of Articles 34(1), 34(2) and 35(1), 16(5) and 16(6), 25(1), 39 and 40(12) of the DSA," and said it will now carry out an in-depth investigation "as a matter of priority." Internal market commissioner Thierry Breton announced the same day that the proceedings covered a suspected breach of obligations to counter illegal content and disinformation, a suspected breach of transparency obligations, and suspected deceptive design of the user interface.
