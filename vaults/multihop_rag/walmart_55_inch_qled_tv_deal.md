# Walmart's 55-Inch QLED TV Black Friday Deal

A 55-inch QLED TV took an extra $50 discount in Walmart's Black Friday 2023 sale, pushing it below $350, Mashable reported on 25 November 2023.

The best Walmart Black Friday deals typically include giant rollbacks on TVs, and that tradition continued in 2023 — although many popular 4K TVs sold out well before Black Friday officially began, leaving "solid cheap 4K TVs lingering".

The set was already affordable at full price, and thanks to its extra layer of quantum dots buyers will "be able to appreciate rich colors and deep blacks even during the day".
