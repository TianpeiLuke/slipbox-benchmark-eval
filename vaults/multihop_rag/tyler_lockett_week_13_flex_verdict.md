# Tyler Lockett Week 13: A Flex Starter Facing Another Lean Night

CBS Sports' Week 13 verdict on Tyler Lockett is Flex Starter — playable as a WR3 but likely to be quiet. The grounds: Lockett has seven-plus targets in four of his past five games, but he still does not have as many as Metcalf has gotten in those games, and he does not see nearly as many deep throws — literally two targets in his past two games traveled 15 or more Air Yards. The article grants the upside case, that he always has potential to pop off for a big game like he did against a tough Browns defense five weeks ago, then sets against it that recent matchups against the Niners, Rams and Ravens were not kind to Lockett, and were even less kind to Geno Smith, with this week not expected to be much different. Added to that is the tough assignment of Stephon Gilmore in coverage — with the stated caveat, unless the Cowboys use shadow coverage for the first time this year — leaving Lockett in for another lean night.

The resulting ranking: the author does not mind him as a WR3 over Drake London, DeAndre Hopkins and Diontae Johnson, but would start Rashee Rice and Adam Thielen over Lockett. Scope: Lockett is a Seattle wide receiver, #16, age 31 with 9 years of experience; the weekly breakdown lists the matchup at Dallas with Dallas favored by 9 and an over/under of 47.5, the opponent 10th versus wide receivers, projected points of 10.6 and a WR rank of 31st; year-to-date he has 54 receptions on 79 targets for 575 receiving yards, 4 touchdowns and 12.5 Fantasy points per game, with projections powered by SportsLine.
