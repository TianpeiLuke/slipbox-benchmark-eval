# Travis Kelce's Moustache Remark And New York Visit

In August 2023 Travis Kelce hinted on the New Heights podcast that Taylor Swift would like his moustache — a clear suggestion he had made contact with her — and shortly afterwards took a week off training to visit New York City while Swift was re-recording her 1989 tracks there, The Age reported on 26 September 2023.

The brothers were discussing the moustache Kelce grows every year in honour of the Chiefs' head coach, Andy Reid. Asked if he knew what Swift thought of the 'stache, he remained cheekily confident: "We're not going to bring up Taylor Swift in this episode," he says, "But something tells me she's going to like it."

The New York trip was reported by Vulture, and coincidentally happened to be while Swift was re-recording her 1989 tracks in the city.
