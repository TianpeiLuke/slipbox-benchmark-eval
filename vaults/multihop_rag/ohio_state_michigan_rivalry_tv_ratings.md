# Television Ratings For The Ohio State-Michigan Rivalry

With or without Harbaugh, Sporting News expected The Game to draw ridiculous television ratings, and reported on Nov. 11, 2023 that the previous year's matchup drew an 8.05 Nielsen rating with 17.1 million viewers. According to Sports Media Watch, only three college football games had drawn a Nielsen rating of 5.0 or better that season: Penn State-Ohio State (5.3), Colorado-Oregon (5.2) and Ohio State-Notre Dame (5.1). Michigan-Penn State was expected to join that list on Saturday, but the intrigue around The Game could double all of those numbers.

The draw came from what was on the line. Michigan had won the last two meetings and had a chance to win three in a row in the rivalry for the first time since 1995-97; Ohio State was No. 1 in the College Football Playoff rankings; Harbaugh (2-5) and Ryan Day (1-2) both had losing records in the matchup; and the Big Ten East, the CFP and perhaps a national championship were at stake. Sporting News judged that a 10 Nielsen rating and 20 million viewers was not out of the question, with enough WWE-like promos leading up to the game to get both fan bases frothing.
