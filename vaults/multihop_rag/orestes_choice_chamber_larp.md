# Orestes' Choice

Orestes' Choice is a LARP by Susanne Vejdemo that Tim Hutchings names his best game experience of 2023 — the first heavy-duty chamber LARP he had played since the pandemic, one that he says reset his brain and helped him remember what he values in play.

In it, players continuously swap roles as they play through a very queer Greek myth in which young Orestes returns home and must decide whether to kill his treacherous mother and father-in-law. Hutchings' summary line: never had he planned a murder with such erotically charged practice stabbing.
