# Jim Ratcliffe Completes His 25% Stake In Manchester United

Jim Ratcliffe completed the formalities around his investment into Manchester United, taking control of 25% of his boyhood club after a year of negotiations, per Sporting News on Dec. 13, 2023.

Sporting News reported that with the deal done the pressure on Erik ten Hag would be piled on even more — Ratcliffe wanted United back at "the very top of world football."

Against that backdrop, Sporting News laid out all the options should Man United move on from Ten Hag and look for a new beginning, in the hopes of salvaging the 2023/24 season.
