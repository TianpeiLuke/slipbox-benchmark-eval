# Kylian Mbappe's 52-Goal 2023 And His PSG Contract Situation

Kylian Mbappe scored 52 goals in the 2023 calendar year for Paris Saint-Germain and France, tied for second on TalkSport's list of the year's top scorers. PSG's all-time record scorer with 233 goals, the ex-Monaco man is still just 24 years old.

He added even more goals to his tally for Les Parisiens in the current term, while firing in ten times for the French national team; his overall tally of 46 goals for Les Bleus is just ten shy of Giroud's record. In the final year of his contract at the Parc des Princes, Real Madrid are favourites to secure a free transfer the following summer, but they face competition from Liverpool.

PSG had no games left to play in 2023 due to the French winter break, leaving Mbappe unable to catch Ronaldo in the calendar-year scoring race.
