# India's 2011 World Cup Semi-Final Against Pakistan

India beat Pakistan in the second semi-final of the 2011 World Cup at the IS Bindra Stadium in Mohali on 30 March, bowling Pakistan out for 231 in 49.5 overs after posting 260 for 9 in 50 overs. The World Cup had returned to India after 1996, and fans from India and Pakistan could not have expected a better occasion than a World Cup for a meeting of the two teams.

Man-of-the-Match Sachin Tendulkar rose to the occasion with 85 runs, Virender Sehwag scored 38 and Suresh Raina remained unbeaten on 36; Pakistan seamer Wahab Riaz took 5 for 46 in 10 overs. In reply Misbah-ul-Haq with 56 runs and Mohammad Hafeez with 43 showed some resistance, but the lack of a big innings lower down the order hit Pakistan's prospects badly. India moved to the final and beat Sri Lanka to win their second World Cup title.
