# Rnf43-Daam1 Frizzled Degradation Mechanism

The mechanism the team established is that Rnf43 requires the protein Daam1 in order to dampen Wnt signalling by degrading the Wnt receptor Frizzled. Rnf43 is a known regulator that tempers the activity of the Wnt signalling pathway — a role initially discovered by Bon-Kyoung Koo — and was previously recognised for its ability to flag Frizzled for destruction.

The open question was how it does so and what controls it: "We wanted to know how Rnf43 works, and also what – in turn – controls Rnf43 and helps it to regulate Wnt signalling," the research team explains. Past studies had established that Rnf43 could not effectively degrade Frizzled by itself, since Frizzled is embedded in the cell's plasma membrane. "In our project, we used biochemical assays to identify which proteins interact with Rnf43," and they discovered that Daam1 is a crucial collaborator.

The steps of the relation are specified: "We found that Daam1 is required for Rnf43 to be active, so for Rnf43 to regulate Wnt signaling at all. Further work in cells showed Rnf43 needs Daam1 to move the Wnt receptor Frizzled into vesicles called endosomes. From the endosomes, Frizzled is shuttled to the lysosomes where it is degraded, dampening Wnt signaling," Colozza elaborates.
