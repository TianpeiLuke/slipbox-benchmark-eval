# The USWNT's Interim Transition Under Twila Kilgore

U.S. Soccer's transition plan keeps current interim head coach Twila Kilgore in the role through May, after which she will remain as a permanent assistant coach under Emma Hayes, with Kilgore and the rest of the technical staff working on a handoff plan, as reported by The New York Times on 15 November 2023.

The gap Kilgore covers is substantial. Unless the situation changes drastically, Hayes will only have two camps, including four friendlies, with the USWNT ahead of the 2024 Olympics in Paris, and she will miss three international windows between the Tuesday announcement and her planned start date, including the 2024 CONCACAF Gold Cup in February and March. The particulars around timing and the plan moving forward have been one of the areas of discussion between Hayes and her representatives, Chelsea and U.S. Soccer that continued through the start of November, and her appointment will have an immediate impact even if she's not immediately present.

Matt Crocker framed the arrangement: "This is a unique situation, but the team is in safe hands with Twila. Her stewardship will be crucial during this period as we are focused on success at the Olympics. Emma has endorsed Twila, she will be a key part of Emma's staff when she arrives and moving forward, and we are excited for what's to come with our USWNT program." The turnaround remains extremely tight: Hayes has 18 days to get situated with the team ahead of the tournament, across two international windows from 27 May to 4 June and then 8 to 16 July, with the Olympic tournament starting on 25 July.
