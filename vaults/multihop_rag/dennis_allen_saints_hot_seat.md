# Dennis Allen Saints Hot Seat

Yardbarker's Nov. 30, 2023 NFC hot-seat roundup named head coach Dennis Allen as the New Orleans Saints' hottest seat, concluding that he needed a playoff appearance or there could be big changes in New Orleans.

The grounds are threefold: New Orleans' 5-6 record against the league's easiest schedule was bad; running back Alvin Kamara made a concerning claim after a 24-15 loss to the Falcons that the team did not have an identity, which the piece called worse than the record; and Allen carried a 20-44 record as head coach of the Raiders and Saints. The warrant is that a weak record made worse by a favorable schedule, plus a public sign that the locker room has lost its bearings, plus a long career track record of losing, together leave only a playoff berth as a defense.
