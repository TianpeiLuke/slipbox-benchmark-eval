# Simply Homes' Social Mobility Mission

Simply Homes' founders argue that providing well-maintained affordable homes to the people most in need is both an unmet market need and a lever on poverty. "We're helping to solve the affordability crisis for people who struggle with housing stability the most," said CEO and co-founder Brian Bagdasarian. "While there are groups that have attempted to tackle programmatic buying of homes in the past — to varying degrees of success — the reality is no one is operating in our market, providing well-maintained affordable homes to the people most in need."

The warrant connecting housing to life outcomes is a claim about mobility: the opportunity to help people overcome poverty and improve their chances for social and economic mobility is what attracted Bagdasarian and co-founder and CFO Robert Kavanagh to build the model, and Bagdasarian cites the figure that "children that are able to move into lower-poverty neighborhoods can see a 31% increase in lifetime earnings."

The pair are firm believers that you can still make money and do good at the same time.
