# MLB 2023 Postseason Results Bracket

The Sporting News' 2023 postseason bracket, published alongside its World Series live blogs, records every result from the Oct. 3 opening games through the scheduled World Series dates, ending with Texas leading Arizona 2-1 after Game 3 on Oct. 30 and Games 4 through 7 still to be played.

The opening series on Oct. 3-4 produced sweeps: Rangers 4, Rays 0 and Twins 3, Blue Jays 1 on Oct. 3, then Rangers 7, Rays 1 and Twins 2, Blue Jays 0 on Oct. 4. On the other side, Oct. 3 gave Diamondbacks 6, Brewers 3 and Phillies 3, Marlins 1, and Oct. 4 gave Diamondbacks 5, Brewers 2 and Phillies 7, Marlins 1.

The next round ran Oct. 7-11. Texas beat Baltimore 3-2 on Oct. 7, 11-8 on Oct. 8 and 7-1 on Oct. 10 to win 3-0, while Houston beat Minnesota 6-4 on Oct. 7, lost 6-2 on Oct. 8, won 9-1 on Oct. 10 and closed it out 3-2 on Oct. 11 to win 3-1. In the other bracket Philadelphia beat Atlanta 3-0 on Oct. 7, lost 5-4 on Oct. 9, won 10-2 on Oct. 11 and 3-1 on Oct. 12 to win 3-1, while Arizona beat the Dodgers 11-2 on Oct. 7 and 4-2 on both Oct. 9 and Oct. 11 to win 3-0.

The Rangers-Astros series went the distance on Fox and FS1: TEX 2, HOU 0 on Oct. 15; TEX 5, HOU 4 on Oct. 16; HOU 8, TEX 5 on Oct. 18; HOU 10, TEX 3 on Oct. 19; HOU 5, TEX 4 on Oct. 20; TEX 9, HOU 2 on Oct. 22; and TEX 11, HOU 4 on Oct. 23 for a 4-3 Texas win. The Diamondbacks-Phillies series on TBS also went seven: PHI 5, ARI 3 on Oct. 16; PHI 10, ARI 0 on Oct. 17; ARI 2, PHI 1 on Oct. 19; ARI 6, PHI 5 on Oct. 20; PHI 6, ARI 1 on Oct. 21; ARI 5, PHI 1 on Oct. 23; and ARI 4, PHI 2 on Oct. 24, giving Arizona the series 4-3.

The World Series schedule, all on Fox at 8:03 p.m. ET, listed Diamondbacks at Rangers on Oct. 27 (TEX 6, ARI 5) and Oct. 28 (ARI 9, TEX 1, tied 1-1), then Rangers at Diamondbacks on Oct. 30, Oct. 31 and Nov. 1, and Diamondbacks at Rangers on Nov. 3 and Nov. 4, the last four marked with asterisks as if necessary. In the Oct. 30 edition of the bracket the Oct. 30 game was still blank; in the Oct. 31 edition it is filled in as TEX 3, ARI 1, putting Texas ahead 2-1, with Oct. 31 onward still unplayed.
