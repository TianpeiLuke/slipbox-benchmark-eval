# DK Metcalf Week 13: Start Him On Volume, Not On Efficiency

CBS Sports' Week 13 verdict on DK Metcalf is Start Him, but explicitly as a volume bet rather than an efficiency bet. The grounds for starting: Metcalf has at least nine targets in each of his past three games and in five of his past six, and that kind of usage, especially in a game where the Seahawks figure to pass a bunch, makes him a borderline must-start. The grounds against: on the 12 targets he has seen that traveled at least 15 Air Yards over the past four weeks, Metcalf has caught just three, and more alarming, five of those targets were knocked away by defenders who were right on him when the ball arrived — he is not really zooming past anyone these days and is not winning contested catches regularly either. The matchup compounds it: he is expected to line up across from DaRon Bland for much of the game, since Bland usually plays right cornerback and Metcalf primarily lines up at left wide receiver, and Bland has allowed one touchdown and just a 52.5% catch rate with 13 passes defensed and seven interceptions, so it could be a rough one.

The warrant that resolves the tension is stated plainly: it's the volume you're banking on this week. The ranking that follows is start Metcalf in PPR over Diontae Johnson and Cooper Kupp, while liking the upside for Rashee Rice and Courtland Sutton just a little bit more. Scope: Metcalf is a Seattle wide receiver, #14, age 26 with 5 years of experience; the weekly breakdown lists the matchup at Dallas with Dallas favored by 9 and an over/under of 47.5, the opponent 17th versus wide receivers, projected points of 11.1 and a WR rank of 22nd; year-to-date he has 43 receptions on 80 targets for 678 receiving yards, 3 touchdowns and 12.9 Fantasy points per game, with projections powered by SportsLine.
