# The US Copyright Office's 2023 Study Of AI And Copyright

The US Copyright Office is spearheading one of the key US efforts to address AI and copyright: in the summer of 2023 it issued a call for submissions on the issue of AI and copyright, with the goal of putting together a study to "help assess whether legislative or regulatory steps in this area are warranted."

The call for submissions came as legislators and regulators around the world grappled with the many issues that had emerged since AI technology "went mainstream" about a year earlier. Numerous businesses involved in AI, and those with substantial copyright holdings, submitted their thoughts on the issue — including Universal Music Group and AI developer Anthropic.
