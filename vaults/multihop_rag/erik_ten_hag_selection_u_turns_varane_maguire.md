# Erik Ten Hag's Selection U-Turns On Varane And Maguire

Erik ten Hag recalled Raphael Varane against Bayern Munich on 12 December 2023 after a 10-game absence during which both Luke Shaw and Jonny Evans had been preferred at centre-back to a World Cup winner, one of a series of selection reversals The Independent recorded that night.

Varane, summoned from what the report calls his inexplicable exile, performed with an assurance that made his omission all the odder. He is not the only case: with Harry Maguire and Scott McTominay, Ten Hag is compiling a group of players who were first out of his plans and then back in. During the match Maguire's injury forced him off the pitch, with 36-year-old Jonny Evans replacing him.

The significance attached to the pattern is about the manager's self-presentation: for a manager who likes to project an image of strong, decisive leadership, Ten Hag has had to reverse course rather too often — but then, the report concludes, his team are going backwards.
