# Apple's 2023 Top iPad App And Game Lists

Apple's year-end App Store feature also includes separate lists of the top iPad apps and games, both free and paid, as well as top Apple Arcade games — the latter offering the only glimpse into Apple's subscription gaming store, which is otherwise a black box because it does not feature a Top Charts section like the larger App Store does.

There is some overlap with the iPhone lists, though streaming apps make a better showing among the free iPad apps: that list includes, in order, Max, YouTube and Netflix in the top three spots, with Disney+, Prime Video and Peacock at No. 5, 8 and 10 respectively. The full free iPad list ran: Max: Stream HBO, TV, & Movies; YouTube: Watch, Listen, Stream; Netflix; Google Chrome; Disney+; Goodnotes 6; TikTok; Amazon Prime Video; Temu: Shop Like a Billionaire; and Peacock TV: Stream TV & Movies.

The top paid iPad apps were Procreate, Shadowrocket, Nomad Sculpt, forScore, Toca Life: Hospital, Bluebeam Revu for iPad, Teach Your Monster to Read, AnkiMobile Flashcards, Endless Paper and ToonSquid. Top iPad games also include more kid-friendly titles, such as Toca Life: Hospital at No. 5 and Teach Your Monster to Read at No. 7.

Among games, the top free iPad titles were Roblox, Magic Tiles 3: Piano Game, Subway Surfers, Royal Match, Among Us!, Duet Cats: Cute Cat Games, Stumble Guys, MONOPOLY GO!, Bridge Race and Gardenscapes, while the top paid iPad games were Minecraft, Geometry Dash, Bloons TD 6, Stardew Valley, MONOPOLY, Five Nights at Freddy's, Red's First Flight, Poppy Playtime Chapter 1, Plague Inc. and Garten of Banban 2.
