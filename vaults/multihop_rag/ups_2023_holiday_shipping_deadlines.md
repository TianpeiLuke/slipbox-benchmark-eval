# UPS 2023 Holiday Shipping Deadlines

UPS's 2023 holiday cutoffs run: December 19th for UPS 3 Day Select, December 20th for UPS 2nd Day Air, and December 21st for UPS Next Day Air.

With UPS you have until Thursday, December 21st, to ship via Next Day Air and have your package arrive by Friday, December 22nd; packages shipped via Next Day Air after Thursday won't arrive until after Christmas. Wednesday, December 20th, is the last day to ship via 2nd Day Air for arrival by Friday, December 22nd, and 3 Day Select can be used until Tuesday, December 19th, to arrive by the 22nd. Before then, the normal pickup and delivery service will get packages where they need to be on time.
