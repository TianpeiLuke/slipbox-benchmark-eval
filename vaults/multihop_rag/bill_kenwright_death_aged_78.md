# Bill Kenwright Dies Aged 78 After Liver Cancer Surgery

Everton chairman Bill Kenwright, an acclaimed West End theatre and film producer, died at the age of 78, his family announcing on Tuesday that he "passed away peacefully" on Monday night "surrounded by his family and loved ones". The BBC reported the death on 24 October 2023.

The medical context: Kenwright had surgery to remove a cancerous tumour from his liver eight weeks before his death. On 12 October the Premier League club said the tumour had been removed six weeks earlier and that the operation was "completely successful", but complications meant Kenwright required a "prolonged period in an intensive care unit".

Everton said it was "in mourning" following the death of the club's longest-serving chairman for more than a century, adding: "The club has lost a chairman, a leader, a friend, and an inspiration." His family's statement read: "Bill was driven by his passions and devoted his life to them; his deep love of theatre, film, music and his beloved Everton, and the families they created. He impacted the lives of thousands, whether that be through the launching of careers or his unending loyalty, generosity and unfaltering friendship and support." They added: "We will remember him with huge love and admiration - the shows will of course go on, as he would have wished, and his towering legacy will continue."
