# Dontayvion Wicks Week 16 Waiver Target

CBSSports.com's Dec. 19, 2023 Week 16 wide receiver preview listed Dontayvion Wicks, WR, Green Bay, No. 13, among its Week 16 waiver adds, arguing he should certainly be started in more leagues than he was currently rostered in.

The claim carries a condition: if Christian Watson and Jayden Reed remained out, then Wicks might just be the best option available. The grounds are a rising role — his seven targets in Week 15 were a career high and part of a trend, and his snap share had increased for three weeks in a row. His weekly breakdown at age 22, rookie: at CAR, GB -5, O/U 37.5, opponent vs. WR 22nd, WR rank 35th, rostered 15%; year-to-date 31 receptions, 49 targets, 491 receiving yards, 1 TD, 6.5 fantasy points per game.
