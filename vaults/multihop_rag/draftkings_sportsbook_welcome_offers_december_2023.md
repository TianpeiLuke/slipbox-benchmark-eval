# Claiming DraftKings' Three Welcome Offers Worth Up To $1,200

DraftKings offered new customers three welcome offers, worth up to $1,200 in total, claimable through the Sporting News DraftKings promo code link for the Eagles vs. Seahawks Monday Night Football game, per Sporting News on Dec. 18, 2023.

The three offers are: deposit a minimum of $5 and receive a $50 bonus bet; bet a minimum of $5 and get $150 in bonus bets; and a 20% first deposit match up to $1,000. To claim the new user offers, first click the DraftKings promo code link and sign up for a new account. Then make your first deposit of at least $5 and you will receive a $50 bonus bet. When you make your first bet of $5 or more, your account will also be credited with $150 in bonus bets.

The limiting condition on the largest component is scale and playthrough: to claim the maximum 20% deposit match of $1,000 you will need to deposit $5,000, and there is also a 25x playthrough requirement to claim the full amount.
