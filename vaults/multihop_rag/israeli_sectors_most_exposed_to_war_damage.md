# Israeli Sectors Most Exposed to War Damage

Asked which industries the war would hit, Leo Leiderman put private consumption — particularly of services — at the centre: "A substantial decline can be expected in private consumption, particularly of services."

His mechanism runs through sentiment as well as income. The decline is not just because of damage to output and income but also for psychological reasons: distress, uncertainty and the possibility of prolonged reserve duty by some of the public will all affect consumer sentiment.

The exposed sectors he names are tourism, commerce, transport services, entertainment and restaurants. He also identified a vulnerable class of firm by analogy with an earlier shock: as in the Covid pandemic, the self-employed and small and medium-size businesses may be hurt, particularly those that operate with high leverage.
