# How To Claim DraftKings Sportsbook Vermont's Prelive Or Multi-Part Welcome Offer

DraftKings offers Vermont users a choice between a prelive bonus and a multi-part welcome offer worth up to $1,200, and taking the prelive one forfeits the other, Sporting News explained on 2023-09-26. DraftKings is likewise already known to Vermont sports fans thanks to its popular DFS site, and it already had a prelive offer available — but be aware that those who accept the prelive offer will not be eligible for DraftKings' welcome offer later on. The prelive path: use the links and sign up for a DraftKings Sportsbook VT account between then and the moment the site goes live on Jan. 11, and you will receive $200 in Bonus Bets, issued as eight $25 Bonus Bets; you'll have to wager these rather than withdraw them, and you must do so within 7 days or they'll expire.

The welcome-offer path is a multi-part offer worth up to $1,200, comprising a deposit match bonus worth up to $1,000, a $50 Bonus Bet just for depositing at least $5, and another $150 in Bonus Bets for making a first wager of at least $5. The steps: sign up for a DraftKings Sportsbook account via the links on the page; make a first deposit of at least $5 and get a $50 Bonus Bet; when you make that first deposit, DraftKings will give you a 20% deposit match bonus worth up to $1,000 in Bonus Bets — be aware this bonus comes with a 25x wagering requirement; finally, when you place an initial bet of at least $5, you get $150 more in Bonus Bets, delivered as eight $25 Bonus Bets that you'll have seven days to use. If you go for the full deposit match bonus you can receive as much as $1,200 in Bonus Bets all told, and when you wager with Bonus Bets, also known as DK Dollars, and win, you only receive the winnings and not the wager amounts. When DraftKings Sportsbook Vermont launches, the site will feature the same multi-part welcome offer it has in other states, and what you can win depends on how much in Bonus Bets you gather and on placing successful wagers with them.
