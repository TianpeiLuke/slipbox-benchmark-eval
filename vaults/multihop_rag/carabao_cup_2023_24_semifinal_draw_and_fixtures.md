# Carabao Cup 2023/24 Semifinal Draw And Fixtures

The semifinal draw for the Carabao Cup was carried out at Anfield following Liverpool's quarterfinal thrashing of West Ham, pairing Middlesbrough with Chelsea and Liverpool with Fulham, per Sporting News on Dec. 19, 2023.

These ties are two-legged matchups. The first legs would be played in the week commencing January 8, 2024, with the return legs in the week beginning January 22, 2024.

The fixtures were: week commencing Jan. 8, 2024 — Middlesbrough vs. Chelsea and Liverpool vs. Fulham; week commencing Jan. 22, 2024 — Middlesbrough vs. Chelsea and Liverpool vs. Fulham.
