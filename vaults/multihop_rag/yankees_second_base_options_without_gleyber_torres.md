# The Yankees Have No Real Second-Base Alternative To Gleyber Torres

Essentially Sports argues on 2023-10-10 that trading Gleyber Torres is not worth it for the Yankees because there is no adequate replacement, in-house or on the market. Torres is the sole option for the Yankees at second base, and with limited options available in the market the team would be in circumstances to spend more. If the Yankees go for other infielders, they may have to reshuffle to make room in the squad, including shifting Oswaldo Peraza from third base to second — but Peraza could not do well this season, even after all the positives, and his involvement in unique positions could be a risk for the team.

The free-agent alternatives are no better: Whit Merrifield and Adam Frazier have not done well. The contact argument is the strongest strand: given that the Yankees finished second last in total hits and had over 1,400 strikeouts, losing a player with the best record in both parameters would not be ideal — Torres has a 14.6% strikeout rate, with only a handful of players recording a lower rate, and he also covered a lot of bases this season to finish second to Judge. All these factors make him a player the Yankees should keep as they look for a change in 2024. With the roster not in the best shape, losing Torres could haunt the Yankees further; whether the most successful team can find genuine replacements on the market, only time will tell. His trading is judged not worth it, as it would further reduce the options in the outfield, and with rookies and the farm not compensating for the demand it would be a big ask for them to fulfill — the article trailing off mid-sentence while naming Jasson Dominguez, Anthony Rizzo and DJ LeMahieu.
