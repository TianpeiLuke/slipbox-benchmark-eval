# Apple Watch SE Second Generation Deal Listings

Both of WIRED's October 10, 2023 Prime Day roundups pick the second-generation Apple Watch SE as the top choice for most people, at the lowest price tracked yet, with the larger 44-mm version also on sale for $229 ($50 off).

WIRED's general roundup notes it is the most affordable of what Apple touts as its first carbon-neutral products — when bought in conjunction with the new sport loop — and that it is compatible with WatchOS 10, where many new health and wellness features show up. The Apple roundup frames it as the option for anyone who doesn't need all the extensive capabilities of the Apple Watch Series 9: it still tracks important health and fitness features, and with the latest S8 chip it adds the redesigned Compass app and Crash Detection, plus WatchOS 10 support so you won't miss the latest software updates.
