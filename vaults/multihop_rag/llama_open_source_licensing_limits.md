# Llama's Open Source Licensing Limits

The premise under attack is Meta's claim that Llama is open source: despite Meta's claims, its Llama-branded family of LLMs are not as "open source" as it would like people to believe.

The grounds are two restrictions in the license. Yes, the models are available for both research and commercial use cases, but the license forbids developers to use Llama for training other models, and app developers with more than 700 million monthly users must request a special license from Meta which it may grant based on its "sole discretion."

The consequence TechCrunch draws is that the openness is selectively withheld from exactly one class of user: basically, anyone but Meta's Big Tech brethren can use Llama sans permission.
