# Echo Frames Physical Controls And Button Actions

The Echo Frames' controls are easy to navigate and split across the two arms: the left arm carries a self-explanatory volume rocker, and the right side has two action buttons.

The right-side action buttons are used to take or decline calls, put the device into pairing mode, or mute Alexa; pressing the back action button twice launches a personalized playlist on the music streaming service of your choice. The Verge's reviewer noted two caveats from use. First, habit: they found themselves reaching for the volume rocker on their phone far more often, since habits are hard to break, especially when the phone is often in hand anyway. Second, the double-press playlist did not personalise well — the reviewer expected a list full of K-pop and sad indie artists like Phoebe Bridgers and Mitski, but the glasses played a list full of artists they didn't know and songs they would never listen to. A photo caption notes the right side's two action buttons, the volume rocker on the left, and some of the directional speakers and mics.
