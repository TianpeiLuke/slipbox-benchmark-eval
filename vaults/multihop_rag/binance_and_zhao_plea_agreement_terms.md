# Binance And Zhao's Plea Agreement Terms

Under Zhao's plea agreement, he will agree to the recommendation that the court impose a $150 million fine to the CFTC, and will not make any statements "contradicting his acceptance of responsibility," according to a separate filing from Monday, November 20, 2023.

Binance's plea agreement sets out an ordered set of obligations. The company will accept the resignation of Zhao and prohibit him "from any present or future involvement in operating" the business, a bar that begins from the plea acceptance and "ends three years from the date a monitor is appointed," the Monday filing stated. The company will also "maintain and enhance" its compliance program and appoint an independent compliance monitor during that three-year period.

The crypto exchange did not respond to multiple requests for comment from TechCrunch on the charges.
