# Britney Spears' 55-Hour Marriage To Jason Alexander

Jason Alexander was Britney Spears' first husband: the two married at a wedding chapel in Las Vegas in 2004 and annulled the marriage only 55 hours after the ceremony. Alexander returned to the news in 2022 when he tried to attend Spears' wedding to Sam Asghari, and could be heard in a video saying he was going to crash the nuptials. "She's my first wife, my only wife," he reportedly told security. "I'm her first husband, I'm here to crash the wedding."

Alexander was arrested for trespassing, vandalism, and battery over the incident. A security guard later revealed in court that when Alexander crashed the wedding he also tried to enter Spears' bedroom.
