# Whistler Blackcomb: North America's Largest Ski Resort

Whistler Blackcomb is undeniably Canada's most famed ski resort and the largest in North America, with over 8,100 acres of skiable terrain and over 200 runs, and a legendary reputation as an excellent ski area for all levels of skier. Some 55 per cent of the runs are marked as intermediate, though there are great beginner areas at the base of Blackcomb and mid-way up Whistler, plus several excellent advanced areas including the Flute Bowl, West Bowl and Spankey's Ladder, with the longest runs offering a 1,500-metre vertical.

The town of Whistler was purpose-built and has quickly developed into one of the world's premier resorts. There are plenty of non-skiing activities available and several options for kids, while the apres is among the best in North America, centred around venues like Merlin's, Dusty's and BrewHouse. Heidi offers packages to The Westin Resort & Spa, two minutes from the slopes and just over five from the town centre, with spacious, modern rooms and excellent views beaten only by those from the terrace and grill restaurant — from £3,073pp including seven nights' room-only accommodation, return flights from London Heathrow to Vancouver and airport transfers, departing 13 January 2024.

The Independent's separate luxury round-up (13 October 2023) frames the same resort at the top end: sitting at the base of the Whistler and Blackcomb Mountains, it is legendary for its steep off-piste trails, peak-to-peak gondola ride and high-end après bars, and besides the sparkling layer of snow from November to April, prestige chalets house world-class spa facilities. Expect over 200 piste runs, oyster menus and live music sessions. Inghams offers seven nights at the prestigious chateau-style Fairmont Chateau Whistler, below Blackcomb Mountain, from £1,559pp based on four adults sharing, including return flights from London Heathrow, access to the hotel's health club and a shuttle into Whistler Village, departing 1 December 2023.
