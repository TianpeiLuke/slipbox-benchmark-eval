# Generative AI For Robot Design And Path Planning

Asked about Daniela Rus's enthusiasm for using generative AI to literally design robots, Amazon Robotics chief technologist Tye Brady endorsed the direction and extended it to motion: "The dynamics of the robots, to literally move the robots — path planning to actually figure out how to get the right angles — generative AI is incredible at that."

He added that Amazon is seeing a lot of promise with that today, placing the capability in present practice rather than in prospect as of the 2023-10-28 interview.
