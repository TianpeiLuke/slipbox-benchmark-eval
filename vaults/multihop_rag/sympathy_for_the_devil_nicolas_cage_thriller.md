# Sympathy For The Devil

Sympathy for the Devil became available to stream on AMC Plus in early November 2023. The psychological thriller runs 1 hour 30 minutes, is directed by Yuval Adler, and stars Nicolas Cage and Joel Kinnaman.

Polygon describes it as a largely two-person movie that sounds a bit like Collateral, with Cage as a passenger who holds a driver, played by Kinnaman, hostage on a long car trip.
