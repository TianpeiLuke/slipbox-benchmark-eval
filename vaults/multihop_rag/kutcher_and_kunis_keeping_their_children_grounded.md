# Kutcher And Kunis Keeping Their Children Grounded

Mila Kunis said she keeps her and Ashton Kutcher's children's privilege in check by reminding them that "Mommy and Daddy have money, but they're broke all the time", in a 2020 interview on "The Late Show with Stephen Colbert" reported by FOX News Entertainment on 10 October 2023.

The couple have been married since 2015 and share two children, daughter Wyatt, 9, and son Dmitri, 6. "That is not their bedroom, it is my bedroom that I'm letting them use, so they better take good care of it," she said with a laugh, continuing: "I say this in jest, but really they don't have things … they're raised on hand-me-downs from girlfriends' kids' clothing; we garden."

Kunis noted her kids are lucky to be born into a famous family and said she wants to ensure they are grateful: "Our kids are super-fortunate. Regardless, they were born at the right time to the right people, and I love them, but they're not going to be entitled a--holes."
