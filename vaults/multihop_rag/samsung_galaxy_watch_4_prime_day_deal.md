# Samsung Galaxy Watch 4 Prime Day Deal

Samsung's Galaxy Watch 4 was on sale at Best Buy starting at $149.99 — $50 off — in the Bluetooth-enabled, 40mm configuration during the anti-Prime Big Deal Day sales.

The older smartwatch includes health features like EKG, built-in GPS and body composition measurements. Two caveats apply: its battery life is not great, and it lacks features Samsung's newer Galaxy watches offer, such as a body temperature sensor.
