# Google's Black Summit Offensive Notebook Incident

People who attended Google's K&I Black Summit in August 2023 were given third-party notebooks containing highly insensitive language, in what TechCrunch called another Google PR blunder. Reporter Dominic-Madori wrote that the inside of the notebooks was printed with the phrase "I was just cotton the moment, but I came back to take your notes."

It goes without saying that this would not have been well received by the mostly Black audience in attendance. Google pledged to "avoid similar situations as [it engages] with [merchandise] vendors going forward."
