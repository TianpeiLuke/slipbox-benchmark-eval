# Prediction: AI-Generated Content Will Overwhelm Search

Daron Babin's proposition is that Google results will soon be even worse, dominated entirely by AI-generated crap designed to please the algorithms, produced and published at volumes far beyond anything humans could create — far beyond anything we had ever seen before.

The mechanism he gives is cost collapse at the bottom of the market: "All the assholes that are out there paying shitty link-building companies to build shitty articles now they can go and use the free version of GPT."

The prediction's strong form is that the volume is unstoppable: "They're not gonna be able to stop the onslaught of it," he said, then laughed and laughed, thinking about how puny and irrelevant Google seemed in comparison to the next generation of automated SEO. "You can't stop it!" What would falsify it is exactly that — Google proving able to stop the onslaught, so that results are not dominated by machine-produced content.
