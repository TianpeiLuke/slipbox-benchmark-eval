# Luis Robert Jr. White Sox Trade Candidacy

Luis Robert Jr. was ranked No. 24 on Yardbarker's Nov. 13, 2023 list of the top 25 MLB offseason trade candidates with a pointed caveat: he had the most trade value of any player on the list and may also have been the least likely to move, since White Sox GM Chris Getz had called him a franchise building block the previous week and noted he had no plans to proactively shop Robert, even as he left open the possibility of listening to offers on anyone.

It was not hard to understand why. Robert was an elite defensive center fielder who connected on 38 home runs while hitting .264/.315/.542, in the first season he stayed healthy for a 162-game schedule — a year that would get him down-ballot MVP support. He was a prime-aged superstar and, unlike Juan Soto or Corbin Burnes, nowhere near free agency: he would make $12.5M the following year, $15M in 2025, and was controllable via $20M team options for 2026-27. Trading Robert would signify a complete teardown that the Sox probably did not want, yet the haul would be astronomical if they decided to go in that direction.
