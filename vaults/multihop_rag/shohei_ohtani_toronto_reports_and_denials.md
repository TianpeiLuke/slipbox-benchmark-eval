# The Toronto Reports And Denials Of Dec. 8, 2023

On Friday, Dec. 8, 2023, a cascade of conflicting reports had Ohtani picking the Blue Jays and flying to Toronto, only for other reporters to place him at his home in Southern California with no decision made.

The sequence began with Dodgers Nation's J.P. Hoornstra reporting that Ohtani had selected the Blue Jays, who had reportedly won the sweepstakes; official details were not reported, though the report indicated the deal would shatter Mike Trout's record 12-year, $426.5 million contract. Sportsnet's Ben Nicholson-Smith countered that Ohtani had not yet made his decision and that there was no timeline, while noting Toronto was a finalist. MLB Network's Jon Morosi then reported that Ohtani, while not having officially picked a team, was headed to Toronto, after previous reports that he had picked the Blue Jays had since been denied. Hours after Morosi's report that the two-way star was headed to Toronto for a Friday meeting, USA Today's Nightengale and Sportsnet's Nicholson-Smith reported that Ohtani was at his home in Southern California; the Blue Jays remained finalists but his landing spot was not official. Morosi also reported that Ohtani was expected to make his decision soon, possibly as early as that Friday — coincidentally, Ohtani had made his original decision to sign with the Angels on Dec. 8, 2017, six years to the day.
