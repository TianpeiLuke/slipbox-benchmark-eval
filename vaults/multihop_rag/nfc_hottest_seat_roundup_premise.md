# NFC Hottest Seat Roundup Premise

Yardbarker's Nov. 30, 2023 feature identified the hottest seat on each NFC team as determined by Yardbarker NFL writers — a "hot seat" here meaning the person on a roster or in an organization whose position is most precarious, not necessarily the head coach.

The premise stressed that even the Philadelphia Eagles and Dallas Cowboys — two of the NFL's best teams — have hot seats. No seats, however, may have been more sizzling than those occupied by two head coaches in the NFC South, one in the NFC North and another, surprisingly, in the NFC West.
