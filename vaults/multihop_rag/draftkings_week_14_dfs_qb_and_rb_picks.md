# DraftKings Week 14 DFS Quarterback And Running Back Picks

Sporting News built its Week 14 DraftKings DFS lineup around quarterback Justin Fields of the Bears ($6,800) and running backs Christian McCaffrey ($9,200) and Alvin Kamara ($8,200), publishing on Dec. 7, 2023.

The framing was that most season-long fantasy leagues were entering their final week of the regular season, and if a team was not going to qualify, DFS was where every week is a new season: managers do not have to sweat injury reports, make difficult trades, or concern themselves with bad matchups, and instead get to enjoy each week as a new opportunity.

Fields (Bears vs. Lions) was picked because the Lions might have control over the NFC North but their defense was losing steam as the season progressed — Detroit appeared well on their way to a dominant victory over New Orleans until they let the 2023 Saints march back into it, alarming with that version of Derek Carr leading the charge — and Fields recently had success against the divisional foe, rushing for 104 yards and tossing a touchdown in Week 11, so the dual-threat quarterback should be in line for a great day at home.

McCaffrey (49ers vs. Seahawks) was the splurge: fantasy's best running back had been a little rich all season, but the 49ers were coming off an emotional win over the Eagles after stomping the Seahawks on Thanksgiving and were trying to track down the top spot in the NFC while leaning on their stars; McCaffrey had just rushed for over 100 yards and two touchdowns against that same defense two weeks earlier, and his floor remained at the penthouse with a ceiling stretching to the moon. Kamara (Saints vs. Panthers) was backed despite the Saints' quarterback problem and an offense stuck in quicksand: Derek Carr would likely be out with a concussion, leaving Jameis Winston to start, and Kamara remained a fixture as arguably the team's top runner and receiver thanks to his work as a security blanket for whichever quarterback was under center; with the Panthers remaining terrible at defending running backs, Sporting News penciled him in for at least 100 total yards and a touchdown.
