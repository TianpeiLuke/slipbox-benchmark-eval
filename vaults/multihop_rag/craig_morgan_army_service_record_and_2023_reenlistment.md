# Craig Morgan's Army Service Record And 2023 Re-Enlistment

Craig Morgan was sworn in again to the U.S. Army Reserve in July 2023 at age 59, on stage at the Grand Ole Opry in front of a sold-out audience, Fox News Digital reported on 16 October 2023.

The "That's What I Love About Sunday" singer previously served in the Army and Army Reserve with the 101st and 82nd Airborne Divisions as a staff sergeant and fire support specialist, and holds airborne, air assault and rappel master certifications.

He has also worked with the USO, and has earned the Army's Outstanding Civilian Service Medal and the USO Merit Award.
