# Installer Reader Game And App Picks

Installer's readers sent in five recommendations this week — Installer invites readers to email installer@theverge.com with recommendations for anything and everything, and features favorites each week.

On games: Nick recommends RetroAchievements, "a website that adds trophies to old-school games. It's a great excuse to go back and play games from your childhood or games you never got around to playing." Drake notes that Fluttermind, the Moonring dev, has not ported that game to Mac yet, but he discovered some of their other games and started playing Spellrazor, "a very interesting haunted arcade game." Bahadir is playing Chants of Sennaar, "unlike any other puzzle / logic game I have played. You wake up in a tower with different classes of people on each level, and they all speak different languages (and obviously, you don't know any of them). You try to learn each language through context from people or environment. It is super chill but challenging."

On apps: Rich stumbled on the Beli app for tracking and discovering restaurants and calls it "by far the best option for that sort of thing, far better than a crowded Google Maps or noisy FourSquare," with a lot of potential and features you keep discovering as it learns your preferences. Jonathan has for over 15 years made a regular pilgrimage to Kriegs.net for the wallpaper posted by the start of each month, usually themed to the season and available with a calendar in the image — "it has always felt like a little secret that only I know about, which I am now willing to share."
