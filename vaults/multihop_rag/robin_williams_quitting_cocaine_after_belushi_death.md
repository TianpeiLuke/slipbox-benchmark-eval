# Robin Williams Quitting Cocaine After John Belushi's Death

John Belushi's death in 1982 "threw cold water" on Robin Williams, who had been close to the comedian, and — together with the birth of Williams' first son in 1983 — really helped him clean up his drug addiction, according to the Vice documentary reported by FOX News Entertainment on 19 October 2023.

Belushi died from a drug overdose in a bungalow at the Chateau Marmont hotel in West Hollywood, California.

The documentary presents the two events of 1982 and 1983 together as the turning point in Williams' addiction.
