# Boston Dynamics AI Institute Research Pillars

The Boston Dynamics AI Institute lists three key pillars of its research: intelligence, dexterity and mobility — effectively making robots that are smarter, better at mobile manipulation and move more dynamically. Those three axes define the institute's scope as of TechCrunch's 2023-10-01 report.

The name is a known boundary problem. Raibert admits that "Boston Dynamics AI Institute" might not be the most instructive name, both with regard to confusion between itself and Boston Dynamics the company, and because it suggests a focus on AI over robotics. A name change is apparently coming, but nothing has been decided just yet.
