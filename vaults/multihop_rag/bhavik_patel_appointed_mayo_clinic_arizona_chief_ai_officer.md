# Dr. Bhavik Patel Appointed Chief AI Officer For Mayo Clinic Arizona

Radiologist Bhavik Patel, M.D., has been named chief artificial intelligence officer, or CAIO, for Mayo Clinic Arizona, an appointment reported on October 10, 2023 as artificial intelligence gains an ever-widening role in the medical field.

Patel came to the role from academic medical centers: before joining the clinic in 2021, he practiced at Duke University Medical Center and Stanford University Medical Center.

Dr. Richard Gray, CEO of Mayo Clinic Arizona, announced the hire on LinkedIn, noting the organization has only "begun to scratch the surface of AI's potential in medicine."

In his new role, Patel will lead Mayo Clinic's Advanced AI and Innovation Hub, and he will focus on expanding AI-based solutions throughout the organization, according to a press release.
