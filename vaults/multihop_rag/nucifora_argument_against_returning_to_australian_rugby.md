# Nucifora's Argument Against Returning To Australian Rugby

David Nucifora told The Roar the previous year that he would be unlikely to consider returning to Rugby Australia when he leaves his post with Ireland following the 2024 Olympics: the claim is that it would not be wise to revisit, and the warrant is that once you have had a go at something and it did not work, you move on rather than repeat it.

"I don't think it'd be wise to revisit," Nucifora said, giving the general principle behind it: "I think that you have a go at something once, you do your best and then if it's not good enough or it didn't work you move on, do something else."

He also questioned whether the situation had changed enough for him to add anything, while separating that judgement from his feelings about the game: "What's changed back there that I could think that, what I can offer, can change what they're doing still? I'll always love rugby. I'll always love Australian rugby. Could I see myself working in Australia again? I doubt it."
