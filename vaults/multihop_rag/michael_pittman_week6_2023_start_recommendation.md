# Michael Pittman As A Week 6 2023 PPR Start At Jacksonville

CBS Sports listed Michael Pittman under "Start Him In PPR" for Week 6 of the 2023 season at Jacksonville: the claim is that Pittman is best as a low-end WR2 in PPR leagues but no better than a flex in half- and non-PPR formats, and the warrant is that the case rests on him and Gardner Minshew landing on the same page a lot, plus a Jaguars run defense good enough to stop Indianapolis running out the clock.

The quarterback grounds are the pivot. Minshew is clearly more of a developed downfield thrower than Anthony Richardson and has thrown at Pittman on 26.4% of his routes run, including four red-zone targets and an end-zone target; even the previous week, when Minshew played less than a half, Pittman saw a target once every five routes he ran with him. That matters because Pittman's production had faded: since Week 1 he has been above 14 PPR points only once, in Week 3 at Baltimore, a game Minshew started, and over the past two weeks he totalled just 12 targets against the Rams and Titans for six catches and 67 yards, a far cry from Week 1 — when he came alive in the second half against these same Jaguars and finished with a season-high 97 yards on eight catches including a well-documented 39-yard catch-and-run touchdown.

The coverage and matchup grounds complete it. Jacksonville ranks fifth with a 77.7% zone coverage rate and has consistently played a lot of zone in every game; they were busted up by outside receivers, at over 17.0 yards per catch, both last week against Buffalo and two weeks earlier against the Texans, and Pittman tends to play outside more than in the slot. An outside receiver has scored at least once against the Jaguars in every game this season, Pittman included back in Week 1. Pittman tends to do better against zone than man, though he was much better last year than this; this year he has been about even in catch rate and yards per catch against both, but has a higher explosive play rate against zone, 9.7% versus 6.7% against man, which tracks with last year, when his explosive play rate was nearly twice as high against zone. The Jaguars also aren't exactly terrible against the run, allowing 3.6 yards per carry, meaning the Colts can't easily dominate on the ground for four quarters. The breakdown lists Indianapolis at Jacksonville, the Jaguars favoured by 4, an over/under of 45.5, an opponent ranked 18th versus wide receivers, 13.1 projected points, a 21st receiver ranking, and year-to-date totals of 31 receptions on 46 targets for 297 yards, one touchdown and 13.7 fantasy points per game. The format-dependent verdict is explicit: the author would start Zay Flowers, Tyler Lockett and Amari Cooper over Pittman in non-PPR, but not in PPR.
