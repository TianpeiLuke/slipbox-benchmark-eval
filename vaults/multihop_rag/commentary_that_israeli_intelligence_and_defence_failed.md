# Commentary That Israeli Intelligence and Defence Failed

The dominant online argument on 7 October 2023, as collected by Business Today, was that the attack represented a massive failure of Israeli intelligence and defence. Its grounds are the gap between Israel's reputation and the outcome.

Journalist Rania Khalek said the strategic planning that must have gone into this against "one of the most high-tech occupation and surveillance systems in the world" will be studied by many, adding: "The Israelis must be totally losing it right now." Tarik Cyril Amar wrote: "And the intel, and the special forces training... both areas where Israel mythology will be very upset indeed." A user posting as Mjoachim said Israel's defence forces have lost their fame, noting that the IDF lost an army base and a general was captured right out of his house: "Invincible 'Merkava MK4 MBT's captured, and destroyed...what is wrong with the Israeli security and defence forces? And (what) MOSSAD did?"

Dr Jaswant Gandhi, a professor of management, drew a lesson for another country: the attack on Israel was a "great Mossad failure", and India had to learn a lot and keep all intelligence agencies duly greased and internecine conflict-proof. "Israel_under_attack Great #Mossad failure. GoI has to learn a lot and keep all Intelligence Agencies duly greased and internecine conflict-proof. BHARAT is not immune to #terrorism and internal enemies," he wrote on X. The warrant common to these comments is that a surveillance and special-forces apparatus of this reputation should have detected preparations on this scale.
