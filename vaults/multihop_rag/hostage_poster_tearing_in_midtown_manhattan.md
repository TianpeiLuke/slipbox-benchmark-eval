# Hostage Poster Tearing In Midtown Manhattan

Minutes after Thomas Hand gave an interview about his abducted daughter at Central Synagogue in Midtown Manhattan, a woman walked past the posters of Israeli kidnap victims mounted on a fence outside and began tearing them down, FOX News Lifestyle reported on 17 November 2023.

Hand had shared stories of his beautiful girl and the ugly circumstances of her abduction inside the synagogue; he and Michael Levy were then standing outside looking at the posters when the woman began tearing them off. The report calls it a shocking indignity following the pain they had just shared, and a visceral example of the intolerance suffered by Israelis in the Middle East and in the United States.

The father, already deeply pained, was incensed. He shouted out angrily while the woman moved quickly down the sidewalk, raising a hand behind her as if to mock the suffering father's pain.
