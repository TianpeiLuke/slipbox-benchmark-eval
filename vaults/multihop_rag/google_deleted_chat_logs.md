# Google's Deleted Chat Logs Under A Litigation Hold

During Pichai's testimony, the DOJ touched on Google's policy of deleting internal chat messages despite the company being subject to a litigation hold. In February the DOJ accused Google of systematically destroying chats through its history-off option, which deletes messages every 24 hours unless a user manually changed the setting.

The condition that makes the observation matter is the litigation hold itself: the deletion is not alleged as an ordinary retention practice but as destruction of material Google was already obliged to preserve, and the mechanism alleged is a default — history-off — rather than any individual act of deletion.

The same stretch of testimony also recorded a proposal Apple turned down. The way Pichai pitched it would have been a win-win for both companies: Google gets more people searching on its platform — not to mention all that juicy data — and Apple would get more revenue, as a result of the lucrative search agreement the two signed. For whatever reason, Cook didn't take the bait. Apple doesn't preload third-party software onto its devices, and it wasn't going to make an exception for Google.
