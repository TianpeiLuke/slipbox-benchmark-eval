# Climate Policy Reversal's Impact On EV Businesses

The argument is that a five-year delay in the net-zero timeline lands directly on individual companies' revenue plans, not just on national targets. The author frames the common cause: his company is trying to move industry away from a reliance on fossil fuels to make chemicals, among other things.

The grounds are the mechanism for a specific business type: the impact of these political spasms and contradictions in climate commitments on, say, an EV battery business — which has just seen the market demand for its output slide down the road by five years — isn't hard to appreciate. The warrant is asymmetry of resilience: if giants like Ford are feeling the frustration, imagine the mood at a small green tech startup.
