# Shadow (Zhang Yimou)

Shadow is a wuxia film by Zhang Yimou starring Deng Chao, Sun Li, and Zheng Kai, which Polygon calls one of the best action movies of the century and a master class in framing bodies in motion.

Polygon's Pete Volk frames Zhang as a director no one in the world surpasses at making beautiful action movies. Zhang's latest at the time, the political comedy Full River Red, is a fun and strange twisty thriller that trades some of his usual love for soaring action for fast-paced dialogue and a highly complicated narrative, but Zhang is perhaps best known for his wuxia work — Hero, House of Flying Daggers, and Shadow, all described as breathless displays of cinematic excellence.

Like Full River Red, Shadow is rich in political intrigue, but it does not skimp on action: the film's signature umbrella is made of blades, and there are many outstanding action sequences using that unconventional weapon. Though shot in color, Shadow makes great use of black-and-white imagery in every element, from stark costuming and set design to lighting that brings out the contrast of black and white. It was available to watch for free on Tubi as of Polygon's 2023-10-25 roundup.
