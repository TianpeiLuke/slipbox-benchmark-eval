# Defense And Kicker Plays For A Low-Scoring Patriots-Steelers Game

Sporting News filled the rest of its Week 14 2023 Patriots-Steelers FanDuel lineup with both defenses and a kicker — the Steelers D/ST ($10,000), the Patriots D/ST ($9,000) and Chris Boswell ($8,500) — on the expectation of very few yards and even fewer points.

For the Steelers D/ST, the case was that Pittsburgh can get after the quarterback and force plenty of turnovers in any given game, and were facing an offense that had posted 13 points in the past three weeks — not encouraging for fantasy purposes; the column saw a good chance of a defensive score, and judged that even though the Steelers can be vulnerable against the pass, the Patriots were not a team capable of exploiting that weakness. For the Patriots D/ST, most of the same logic applied: Trubisky would have to try to move the ball against a team that had not allowed more than 10 points in three straight weeks, and the column could not envision the Steelers scoring 20 points on offense; New England's problem was obviously the offense, so the defense that continued to give them chances to win every week should not be overlooked. For Boswell, the column asked whether another field goal fest was coming — the previous week's contest in New England saw the Chargers kick two field goals en route to a 6-0 victory — and reasoned that with Pittsburgh already starting a backup quarterback and having a tough time scoring against everyone anyway, it was safe to assume field goals, not touchdowns, were on the menu again.
