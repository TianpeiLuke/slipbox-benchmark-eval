# The Michigan Sign-Stealing Scandal In Outline

The Michigan sign-stealing scandal is an NCAA investigation into the Wolverines for in-person scouting and sign stealing, a saga that began in October 2023 and led to the resignation of staffer Connor Stalions on Nov. 3. The affair centred on an alleged illegal sign-stealing scheme run out of the football program, and it evolved by the hour, with one update after another adding a further wrinkle. On the field Michigan was in the midst of what could be a historic season; off it, the focus was rarely on the team's success.

The investigation produced conference discipline before it produced any NCAA finding. The Big Ten suspended coach Jim Harbaugh for three games on Friday while the team was on a plane en route to Penn State; Michigan had hoped to block the suspension but the Wolverines were left without their coach on the sidelines in Happy Valley. Under the Friday ruling Harbaugh was allowed to coach during the week but not to be on the sideline, and Michigan did not obtain a ruling on a temporary restraining order ahead of the showdown against No. 10 Penn State.

Michigan still won that game 24-15 under interim coach Sherrone Moore, but Sporting News judged that the fallout from the scandal would continue to have a far-reaching impact on the Big Ten, on Michigan and on college football beyond that year's College Football Playoff race.
