# Maxx Crosby Raiders Midseason MVP

Yardbarker's Nov. 9, 2023 AFC midseason team MVP list named defensive end Maxx Crosby the Las Vegas Raiders' MVP: despite a weak supporting cast, Crosby thrived.

The grounds are his share of the team's pass rush and his league standing — 9.5 of the Raiders' 24 sacks, and a tie for the league lead in tackles for loss with 13. The forward claim is conditional: if the two-time Pro Bowler kept dominating, he would generate more Defensive Player of the Year buzz.
