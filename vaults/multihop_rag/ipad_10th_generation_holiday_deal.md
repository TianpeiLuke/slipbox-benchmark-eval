# iPad 10th Generation Holiday Deal

The tenth generation iPad was on sale for $349 at Amazon, Best Buy and Target across the 2023 holiday sales, $100 off the list price; it had often sold for $399 lately, so this was still $50 off the typical street price, and the Black Friday roundup called $349 for the 64GB model in all four colors its lowest price ever — $100 off and $30 less than the previous low.

Engadget doesn't think it's a wild improvement over the previous generation, though it does orient the camera to the landscape edge, which is much better for video calling. Compared to the 9th-gen model, the new version ditches the Home button and comes with a larger, 10.9-inch display, and its USB-C charging is more modern than the Lightning connector on the 9th gen iPad — but for some reason this model still works only with the first generation Apple Pencil, even though the slate came out after the second-gen stylus. Engadget gave the revamped standard iPad an 85 in its review.
