# Asteroid City (Wes Anderson)

Asteroid City is Wes Anderson's ridiculously meta story-within-a-story sci-fi film about an alien encounter, starring Jason Schwartzman, Scarlett Johansson, Tom Hanks, and many more. Polygon ranked it 6 on its best movies of 2023 list of 2023-10-07 and listed it on Peacock or for digital rental/purchase.

Polygon distinguishes the outside view of Anderson from the inside one: to people who do not know his work well, he is a known quantity easily replicated and parodied — a lot of fast-talking people responding with unemotive calm to extraordinary events, against a backdrop of meticulously designed pastel sets — but fans see a lot of nuance within that formula as Anderson's voice, especially his sense of humor, develops from film to film.

In Asteroid City that voice hones in on the question of art and creativity: who it is for, what it brings the artist and the audience, why any form of recognition or acclaim is good enough for one creator while another strains to find connection and resonance in their work. Critic Tasha Robinson notes the film moves so quickly, and with so little attempt to hold the audience's hands and tell them what to feel, that it takes some work to scratch the surface — but it is worth diving into its connections and themes as a who's who of actors, many from Anderson's usual stable and some debuting here, bounce off each other looking for meaning in an isolated desert setting. The cast, including Tom Hanks, Scarlett Johansson, Jason Schwartzman, Jeffrey Wright, Sophia Lillis, and Edward Norton, navigate familial death, meaningless plaudits, and that alien visitor with the same straight-faced aplomb; the film may not be designed for passionate emotional response, but as usual for Anderson it is remarkably specific, idiosyncratic, beautifully assembled, and absolutely intentional.
