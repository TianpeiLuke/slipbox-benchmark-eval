# The Verge Staff Favorites Black Friday Roundup

Black Friday and Cyber Monday may be in the past, but some sales are still hanging around — although maybe not for long — and The Verge points readers to the rest of the Cyber Monday deals still available for more leftovers across all categories.

The roundup's premise is a recurring feature: every month or so The Verge asks its staff about their favorite stuff — pet toys, travel aids, kitchen gadgets, or straightforward tech — and the results are usually very different, very interesting, and a lot of fun.

In celebration of the annual post-Thanksgiving sales, the staff looked through recent "favorites" articles and found deals on a lot of the tech, kitchen tools, travel aids, and pet toys they like — listed in case readers had previously thought "well, that sort of sounds good, but it's a bit pricey," or "that's pretty cheap, but maybe I'll wait until the price goes down some more." Much of the gear is still discounted from Cyber Monday.
