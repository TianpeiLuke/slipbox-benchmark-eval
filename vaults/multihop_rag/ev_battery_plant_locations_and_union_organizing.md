# EV Battery Plant Locations And Union Organizing

What often goes unsaid about autoworkers' attitudes to EVs is where the money is flowing: right now, much of it is going to factories and plants outside their strongholds in the Midwest.

Ford, for example, is building a massive battery plant in Tennessee as a joint venture with Korean battery maker SK Innovation. Meanwhile, workers at a General Motors joint venture battery plant in Ohio overwhelmingly voted in favor of unionizing under the UAW late last year, in a major win for the union. The Verge draws the conditional: if more factories follow their lead, and more of the EV transition is meted out on the union's terms, worker attitudes are likely to shift in favor of electrification — but right now most of the work on electric vehicles is being done by non-union labor, and experts say that needs to change for opinions to soften.
