# HP Price Match Policy

According to HP's website, the company will match the current pre-tax price for new comparable PCs and for identical HP printer, display, and accessory model numbers from nationally recognized online retailers such as Dell, Amazon, Newegg, and Best Buy. For computers the specs have to be the same, but the product model number does not need to be identical. The claim must be made at the time of purchase by calling 1-800-888-0229 — you cannot request a price match via email or chat.

The policy does not extend to items bought during promotional periods, and it does not apply to membership stores like Costco and Sam's Club.

There is a separate post-purchase route: if you find a comparable product at a lower price during the return period, you can email HP at myhpsales@hp.com. That applies only to branded HP products and not to products purchased during certain promotional events, including Green Monday and products on sale during the week of Thanksgiving and the week after. The Verge published this summary on October 25th, 2023, illustrating it with HP's Spectre x360 14.
