# MBenes: Boron-Based Nanosheets

MBenes are a related family of nanosheets made from MXenes in which the "X" layer is made up of boron atoms, and the Riverside group's new paper points to them as materials that may prove good CO2 sponges.

They are harder to make than MXenes, so most research on them has been done with math or computer models. Ozkan's team finds that MBenes could have unique mechanical and electrical traits, and those just might help them last through many, many cycles of CO2 capture and release.
