# Goodbye Volcano High

Goodbye Volcano High, developed by KO_OP for PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, and Windows PC, is ranked 41 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31. It is a visual novel with rhythm game elements that takes place at the precipice of the end of the world.

The game centers on a group of teenage dinosaurs entering their senior year of high school — a perfect balance, critic N. Carpenter writes, between high school dramatics and the grim future of Earth as a meteor rockets toward the planet. Though the rhythm game elements can feel a bit finicky and do not seem to matter much in terms of progressing the game, the music only adds to the dimensional, raw experience created by worker-owned studio KO_OP.

Carpenter's central point is that it is rare to find a game that takes the teenage experience seriously, and this one does: it is a time in your life where you feel so, so much, and that earnestness shows in a teenage experience where everything is a big, huge issue — sometimes to the point of cringe — tied up in the big, global issue of the meteor looking to destroy everything.
