# NFL Thanksgiving Day Games 2023 Viewership

The NFL's Thanksgiving Day games in the 2023 season made history, Essentially Sports reported on Dec. 25, 2023: across all three games the NFL recorded an average viewership of 34.1 million, surpassing the previous year's record of 33.6 million.

Furthermore, the combined unduplicated audience for all three games reached 133 million, marking the second-highest total audience on Thanksgiving, surpassed only by the previous year's numbers. Despite the impressive viewership numbers of the NBA's Christmas Day games, the NFL's Thanksgiving figures eclipse them by a considerable margin.
