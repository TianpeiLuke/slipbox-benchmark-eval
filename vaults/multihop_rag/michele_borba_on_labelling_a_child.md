# Michele Borba on Labelling a Child

Parenting expert and child psychologist Dr. Michele Borba, commenting on the blue bucket controversy, told Fox News Digital that it is "always advisable not to label a child — unless it's for a positive reason."

Her ground is the effect of labels on the child: "Negative labels can impact a child's self-confidence," said the California-based psychologist. But she attaches an immediate limiting clause that keeps the judgment with the family: "But no one knows a child better than a parent."

The decision rule she proposes is therefore comparative rather than categorical: parents should consider whether the label hurts or helps their child interact with others. "For instance, is the child speech delayed? Does he have difficulty interacting with others or expressing his needs? Are others (including his peers) aware of the child's challenges?" she questioned. And the criterion that settles it is the child's own experience, not onlookers': "The ultimate goal is for the child to have a fun Halloween. The parents should never be concerned about others' responses; only do what is best for [the] child."
