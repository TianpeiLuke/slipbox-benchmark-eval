# Ireland: The Greatest Team Yet, Still Without A Knockout Win

This is the greatest Ireland team of all time, and may still even be the best team in the world — but on this day, the quarter-final curse, not to mention an inspired All Blacks side, ruled once more. The claim is that quality and knockout success have come apart: being the best team available is compatible with going out at the same stage as every Ireland side before them.

The consequence the argument draws is reputational. Instead of being feted as the heroes to finally overcome that insurmountable obstacle, the Ireland players will still have to answer endless questions about the heavy burden of previous knockout failures, and fans will endure at least four more years of choking jokes at their expense.
