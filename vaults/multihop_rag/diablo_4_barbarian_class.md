# The Barbarian Class in Diablo 4

Barbarians are a relatively simple class in Diablo 4 — they smash stuff — but, as Polygon notes in its 2023-10-19 guide, Diablo 4 is underscored by math so complex that even the simple act of smashing stuff becomes a dizzying equation.

Barbarians are big, muscular, and powerful. They excel at using Bleed to kill their enemies over time, and their unique weapon-swapping system means they get to have more Legendary powers at their disposal than any other class.

Polygon's guide gathers and simplifies the best Barbarian builds for season 2, linking out to the more detailed versions of those builds and to their original creators for readers who need more on item affixes or other complex ideas.
