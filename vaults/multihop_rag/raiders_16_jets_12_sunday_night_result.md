# Raiders 16, Jets 12 On Sunday Night Football

The Las Vegas Raiders beat the New York Jets 16-12 on "Sunday Night Football" on Nov. 12, 2023, in an old-school, defense-heavy primetime clash that featured nothing but six field goals through the first three quarters. By quarters the line was Jets 6-3-0-3 for 12 and Raiders 3-3-3-7 for 16.

The game's only touchdown came 50 seconds into the fourth quarter, when Michael Mayer made a leaping grab in the end zone to put the Raiders up 16-9. In such a low-scoring game that touchdown felt, even that early, as though it could be the game-winner — and it was, as the Raiders held the Jets to just a field goal the rest of the way and picked off Zach Wilson late to stymy New York.

It was not the quarterback showdown many might have expected in the preseason, when the game appeared to be Aaron Rodgers vs. Jimmy Garoppolo. Instead Wilson went 23-for-39 for 263 yards with no touchdowns and a pick, while rushing four times for 54 yards; Aidan O'Connell completed 16-of-27 passes for 153 yards with a touchdown and an interception.
