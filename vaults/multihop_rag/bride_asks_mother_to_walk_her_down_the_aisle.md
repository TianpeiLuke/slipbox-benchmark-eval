# Bride Asks Her Mother to Walk Her Down the Aisle

A bride-to-be was defended online after choosing her mother to walk her down the aisle instead of her stepfather, following a post the mother shared to the popular "Am I The A**hole?" Reddit forum.

The mother — who goes by the username u/PopShort7064 — asked whether she was in the wrong for agreeing to walk her child down the aisle, in addition to doing a mother/daughter dance at the wedding. She specified that she also has a husband, who has been a stepfather to her daughter since the daughter was 10.

The request dated back years. "She was 15 when she told me if she ever got married she would want me to walk her down the aisle and for us to do a mother/daughter dance instead of a father/daughter one," the parent wrote. "I told her we could dance to the song her dad used to sing to her. She said she loved the idea." The Reddit user expressed that she "always assumed things would change" down the line, with her daughter either choosing to walk alone or with her brother, "as a representation of her dad". However, when her daughter got engaged she "asked [her mother] right away" to walk her down the aisle. "She brought up our past conversation and told me she wanted exactly that. I told her I would be honoured," she wrote. "We cried tears of joy together. I told her that her dad would be so proud."
