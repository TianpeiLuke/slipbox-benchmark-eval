# Argentina's Squad And Starting XI Against Paraguay, October 2023

Lionel Scaloni's Argentina started 4-3-3 against Paraguay on Oct. 12, 2023: E. Martinez (GK) — Molina, Romero, Otamendi, Tagliafico — De Paul, Mac Allister, E. Fernandez — J. Alvarez, L. Martinez, N. Gonzalez.

Scaloni had named a bloated 36-man squad for that month's matches against Paraguay and Peru, but Angel Di Maria, Paulo Dybala and Juan Foyth were forced to withdraw through injury, and Lisandro Martinez was also unavailable after undergoing another foot operation. Nicolas Tagliafico, who started both September matches, retained his starting spot even though Marcos Acuna was back with the team following an injury. Lionel Messi's club teammate Facundo Farias was included on the roster, but it would have been surprising had he seen the field.

The absences sharpened a generational shift. Messi was helping usher in a youth movement up front, with Julian Alvarez and Alejandro Garnacho considered the future of the forward line, and with Di Maria not fit for duty that month those two were even more in the spotlight against Paraguay and in the follow-up in Peru.
