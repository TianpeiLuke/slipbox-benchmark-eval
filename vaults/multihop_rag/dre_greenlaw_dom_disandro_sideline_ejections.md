# The Greenlaw-DiSandro Sideline Ejections

A third-quarter scrap on the Eagles sideline during the 49ers-Eagles game of Dec. 3, 2023 got both San Francisco linebacker Dre Greenlaw and the Eagles' chief security officer, Dom DiSandro, thrown out.

Hard feelings from the pre-game buildup spilled into the game and were highlighted by the incident. DiSandro, a constant presence on the Eagles' sideline, pulled Greenlaw off Smith after a reception. Greenlaw popped up and reached over two officials to touch DiSandro's face with a closed fist, and was ejected. DiSandro, a cult hero in Philly for his ever-present backup when Eagles are out in public, was also told to leave and walked to the locker room to a roaring ovation. FOX Sports' NFL account posted that Greenlaw had been disqualified from the game after the incident on the Eagles sideline.
