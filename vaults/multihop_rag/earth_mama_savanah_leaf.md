# Earth Mama (Savanah Leaf)

Earth Mama is Savanah Leaf's film starring Tia Nomore, Erika Alexander, and Doechii, ranked 36 on Polygon's best movies of 2023 list of 2023-10-07 and available on Paramount Plus, Showtime, or for digital rental/purchase.

Gia, a pregnant single mother in the Bay Area, seeks to recover her two children from foster care.

Polygon's Chris Plante identifies the close-up as Leaf's flex: she uses the shot with a confident abundance that few contemporaries dare, moving in close on each character like a friend at a party with a secret to tell under the loud music. When that intimacy is shared with Gia herself, what differentiates Earth Mama comes into focus — the audience is not observing this woman from a distance but sitting beside her, listening closely and hoping for the best.
