# Mont Tremblant: Quebec's Premier Resort With Old Quebec Charm

Tremblant is Quebec's premier ski resort, sitting at the foot of the eponymous mountain, whose pistes cater well to all abilities. Beginners can start at the edge of the village before progressing onto a series of long greens including the Flying Mile; intermediates have a number of blues, especially around Le Soleil and the Lowell Thomas lift; and advanced skiers can enjoy several terrain parks and steep black runs on both the north and south side of the mountain.

Its distinguishing character is the village. The idyllic, purpose-built settlement has been deliberately built in the style of Old Quebec, with plenty of colourful facades and French-Canadian charm, and offers lively apres and nightlife, often bolstered when locals flock in from Montreal. Non-skiing activities include ice skating, ice fishing and long walking trails, plus a village casino.

British Airways Holidays has trips to the Fairmont Tremblant, one of the more grand hotels in the resort, with an excellent ski-in/ski-out location just a few metres from the lifts, plus impressive spa, pool and sauna facilities, a large terrace, restaurant and outdoor hot tub. From £1,329pp, including seven nights' room-only accommodation, return flights from London Heathrow to Montreal and airport transfers, departing 20 January 2024.
