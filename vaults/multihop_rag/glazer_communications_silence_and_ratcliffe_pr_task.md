# Glazer Communications Silence and Ratcliffe's PR Task

The Guardian's argument on 25 December 2023 is that opening and maintaining a regular line into supporters is a "tap-in" for Sir Jim Ratcliffe — an easy win he should not pass up. The grounds are the Glazers' record: they treat a microphone or TV camera as United fans do Liverpool or Manchester City, as a fierce enemy, and no family member has given a press conference or interview during their 18-year tenure. The family have little relationship with Manchester United's fans.

The warrant that makes this Ratcliffe's problem specifically is his own positioning. He has arrived partly on a ticket of being the local lad made good — Failsworth, where he was born, is next to Newton Heath, United's birthplace — so if he proves as silent as the Americans then this will be, in the paper's words, "a) poor PR; and b) just plain poor."

The stakes are set by the state of the relationship he inherits: football fans can be classed as the most loyal citizens of any society, but even the faith of United enthusiasts has been stretched by the cold relationship created by the Glazers' stance on communication.
