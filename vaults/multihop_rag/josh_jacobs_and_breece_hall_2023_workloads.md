# Josh Jacobs' And Breece Hall's 2023 Workloads

In the Nov. 12, 2023 Raiders-Jets game the two backfields diverged sharply: the Raiders largely turned to Josh Jacobs, who set season-best marks with 27 carries for 116 yards, while the Jets' duo of Breece Hall and Dalvin Cook was held to just 17 rushes for 54 yards.

Nine days later, in the Nov. 21 Week 12 running back rankings, both Jacobs and Hall were still trusted. Rounding out the top 10, the trio of Travis Etienne at the Texans, Josh Jacobs against the Chiefs and Breece Hall against the Dolphins were all looking to bounce back from disappointing Week 11 outings on the ground; Sporting News noted that all three backs had been mostly solid and that none had much to worry about in terms of backfield competition.
