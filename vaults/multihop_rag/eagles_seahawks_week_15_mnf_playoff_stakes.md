# Playoff Stakes Of The Eagles-Seahawks Monday Night Football Game

The Eagles and Seahawks came into their Monday Night Football meeting on troubling losing streaks, and the NFC matchup carried important playoff implications, particularly for Philadelphia, per Sporting News on Dec. 18, 2023.

Philadelphia's two-game losing streak had dropped the Eagles to second place in the NFC East behind the Cowboys, who were also 10-3 but held the tiebreaker thanks to their slightly better divisional record. For a good stretch of the season the Eagles had appeared unstoppable and had been a favorite to return to the Super Bowl: after a surprising loss to the Jets in Week 6 — their first of the season — Philadelphia went on a five-game winning streak, impressively taking down top teams like the Dolphins, Cowboys and Chiefs. Most of those games were gritty, close wins, but the Eagles then dropped back-to-back games in blowout fashion to the 49ers and Cowboys, raising alarm bells among the Philly fanbase.

The Eagles were looking to rebound against a Seahawks team that had been reeling even worse, having lost four straight. Sitting four games behind the 49ers in the NFC West despite a disappointing season, Seattle still had a shot at a wild card playoff spot. After the Monday night game the Seahawks' schedule eased up a bit, with winnable games against the Titans, Steelers and Cardinals, but they could really have used a victory to give them confidence going into the final stretch.
