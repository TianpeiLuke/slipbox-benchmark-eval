# Seahawks-Giants First Half: Penalties, A Geno Smith Knee Scare, And A 14-3 Lead

Seattle led the Giants 14-3 at halftime of the Oct. 2, 2023 "Monday Night Football" game, in a first half marked by penalties, reversed calls and a knee injury scare for Geno Smith. Seattle won the coin toss and deferred; New York fair-caught the kickoff and started at its own 25 (8:15 p.m.). Jamal Adams' return was quickly threatened when he took a knee to the helmet and needed assistance getting off the field (8:22 p.m.), and he was ruled out for the remainder of the game at 8:35 p.m. New York's opening drive ended in a turnover on downs when Jones was stopped on a fourth-and-1 quarterback sneak from the Seahawks' 27 (8:25 p.m.).

The first quarter produced several oddities. Kenneth Walker III ran what appeared to be a 73-yard touchdown but, ruled not down, the play was called back and became a two-yard loss (8:28 p.m.). Seattle went three-and-out on a Kayvon Thibodeaux sack, and the Giants muffed the ensuing Michael Dixon punt but recovered it, taking over at Seattle's 34 (8:34 p.m.). Four straight Seahawks penalties left a drive starting first-and-20 (8:47 p.m.). Then at 8:54 p.m. Mario Edwards sacked Jones at the Giants' 15 and forced a fumble that Seattle recovered and returned to the New York 7. As time expired in the first quarter (8:56 p.m.), Smith rolled out and found DK Metcalf for his second touchdown of the year, and the extra point made it 7-0.

The second quarter brought fireworks and an injury. At 9:11 p.m. Smith caught his own batted pass and was pulled down out of bounds by Giants linebacker Isaiah Simmons; taking exception, Smith came back on the field and had words for safety Xavier McKinney, and he appeared slightly hobbled after the hit. Graham Gano made a 55-yarder at 9:23 p.m. to put New York on the board — since the start of the previous season Gano was 10-for-11 from 50 yards or longer. At 9:26 p.m. Smith was revealed to be in the medical tent with an undetermined injury, with Drew Lock taking snaps; by the two-minute warning Lock had moved Seattle to its own 48 while Smith left the tent for the locker room for further testing on his knee, his status questionable (9:30 p.m.). Lock then hit Noah Fant for a 51-yard completion to the one-yard line, Fant toeing the sideline (9:35 p.m.), and Kenneth Walker III punched it in from a yard out at 9:37 p.m. for his second touchdown of 2023, followed by a scrum between linemen producing offsetting penalties on Seahawks guard Anthony Bradford and Giants defensive end Leonard Williams. The half closed with New York committing its third and fourth special teams penalties of the night, handing Seattle the ball back after an intended fourth-and-4 punt; the Seahawks ran out the clock as boos rained down, and Seattle was also set to receive the second-half kickoff (9:47 p.m.). By halftime, Seattle fans could breathe a sigh of relief that their top signal-caller would be back under center, having avoided serious injury.
