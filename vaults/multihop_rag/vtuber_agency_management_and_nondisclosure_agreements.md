# VTuber Agency Management and Nondisclosure Agreements

Agencies for VTubers manage their talents, often assigning a manager who helps organize interviews, collaborations, paid promotions, and ad content on streams, as Polygon described on 2023-10-19.

Sometimes those managers can be financial advisors, but that is not always the case, as money made through agencies may include subscription or merchandise sales.

A defining constraint is contractual silence: the source at Nijisanji notes that all talents within the agency are under nondisclosure agreements, which prevents them from sharing information with their audiences that could compromise their identity, or even from informing viewers why they might be graduating — effectively retirement for VTubers.
