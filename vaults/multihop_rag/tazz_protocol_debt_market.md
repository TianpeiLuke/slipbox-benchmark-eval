# Tazz, Debt Market For Protocols

Tazz is a seed-stage decentralized lending protocol founded by Mark Cinali, Alan Hampton and Anthony Fahden, presented in Alliance DAO's All11 cohort on 2023-10-18. It aims to make buying and selling debt tied to digital assets more efficient, letting users trade debt through the decentralized exchange Uniswap V3, and according to its website it accepts any type of crypto collateral.

During his demo day presentation, CEO and co-founder Alan Hampton said the company is in "discussions with a very large protocol to issue $5 million in debt." Tazz was raising a seed round at the time.
