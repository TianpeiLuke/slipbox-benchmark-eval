# The Case For Raising The Inflation Target

Olivier Blanchard, a former IMF chief economist, has long argued in favor of raising the inflation target, and former European Central Bank Vice President Vitor Constancio has also embraced the idea. The case comes with a strict precondition: it is a controversial view and only possible from a position of credibility, which means central banks would likely have to get inflation back to 2% first.

The supporting ground is that the environment itself has changed. Global trends suggest inflation will be stronger than in the past, with former Bank of England Governor Mark Carney among those saying rates won't return to pre-pandemic lows.
