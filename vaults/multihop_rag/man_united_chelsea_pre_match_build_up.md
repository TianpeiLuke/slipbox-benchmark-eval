# Pre-Match Build-Up To Manchester United vs Chelsea, December 2023

Sporting News' countdown to kickoff at Old Trafford on Dec. 6, 2023 recorded the team-news surprise that Marcus Rashford started from the bench, along with Ten Hag's pre-match message about Chelsea's defending.

At 1 hour 30 mins until kickoff the blog noted Chelsea had won three of their last four Premier League away games but were without a league win at Old Trafford since 2013. At 1 hour until kickoff the team news was in: the eyebrow-raising decision from Erik ten Hag was that Marcus Rashford started from the bench, while Mauricio Pochettino started the former Man City man Cole Palmer. At 45 mins until kickoff the blog noted Raheem Sterling had failed to score in any of his 24 matches against Man United, and asked whether the night might be the ex-Liverpool and Man City player's.

At 30 mins until kickoff the warm-ups were underway at Old Trafford on a chilly night, and Ten Hag had just told MUTV that Chelsea do not "like to defend" and that United must take advantage of that. At 15 mins until kickoff the teams had headed down the tunnel at the Theatre of Dreams.
