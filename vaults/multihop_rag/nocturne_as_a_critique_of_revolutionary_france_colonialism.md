# Nocturne As A Critique Of Revolutionary France's Colonialism

The Verge argued that what makes Nocturne's deep dive into alternate history a worthwhile watch is the way it presents France not just as a crumbling monarchy in the throes of a heroic social uprising, but also as a colonial nation whose pivot to democracy at home can't be divorced from its history of committing unfathomable atrocities abroad.

The grounds for calling the show faithful as well as pointed are narrative: its focus on another brooding Belmont and its depiction of late-18th-century France as a country overrun by literally vampiric aristocrats drunk on the promise of a new messiah make the series feel true to the essence of Castlevania. The warrant is that the vampire metaphor is not merely decorative — the same conceit that supplies the franchise's atmosphere is what carries the critique of a revolutionary republic's colonial record.
