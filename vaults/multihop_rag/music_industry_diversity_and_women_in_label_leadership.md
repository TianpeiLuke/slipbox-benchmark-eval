# More Diversity And More Women In Label Leadership

Asked what single thing she would change about today's music business, Michelle Jubelirer chose one issue out of several she could name: the industry needs more diversity at all levels and within all facets, particularly on the label side. Her ground is stated as a claim about capability rather than fairness alone, that diversity is a superpower, and she describes it as an ongoing process she has put a lot of focus on, along with doing what she can to increase the number of women at her company.

The supporting evidence is the scarcity at the top: there just are not enough women, particularly within the most senior ranks at the labels. Within UMG, Nashville Chair and CEO Cindy Mabe and Jubelirer herself run standalone label groups, and she says she knows Lucian agrees with her that there need to be more. She commits to continuing to do what she can to increase representation at Capitol Music Group and within the music business at large.
