# Console Gift Card Black Friday Deals

A $50 Xbox gift card could be picked up for $45 at Amazon and Target for Black Friday 2023 — a digital item Engadget suggests would make a good gift for a gamer who's otherwise hard to shop for, usable on game downloads, in-game purchases, membership costs like Xbox Game Pass and more.

Other digital gift cards were on sale for players loyal to a different console: a $70 PlayStation Plus gift card was going for $63, while a 12-month Nintendo Switch Family plan was slightly discounted from $35 to $32.
