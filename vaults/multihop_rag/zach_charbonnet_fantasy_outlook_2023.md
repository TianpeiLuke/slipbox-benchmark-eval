# Zach Charbonnet's Fantasy Outlook In 2023

Sporting News' view of Zach Charbonnet reversed across nine days in late 2023: on Nov. 21 the rookie was welcomed into the Week 12 top 25 running backs on upside, and by Nov. 30 he was a sit for Week 13 on the evidence of what he had actually produced.

The Nov. 21 case rested on opportunity and prior form. With Kenneth Walker III's oblique injury came opportunity for the rookie, who already looked strong in the month leading up to Seattle's narrow Week 11 loss to the Rams; his 69-yard effort in Los Angeles was a bit underwhelming, but the ranking loved his upside with a full game as the Seahawks' bell-cow, in a Week 12 matchup against the 49ers.

The Nov. 30 case turns on the results of that opportunity and on the matchup. The advice is to be wary of anyone who has rushed for just 47 yards two straight weeks despite getting significant duty with Walker on the shelf; Charbonnet is also not doing much in the passing game, and the Cowboys are as bad a matchup as San Francisco was the week before — so you should do better even in a big bye week.
