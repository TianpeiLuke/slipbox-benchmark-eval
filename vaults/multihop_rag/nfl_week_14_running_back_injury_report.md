# Week 14 Running Back Injury Report

Derrick Henry, Rhamondre Stevenson, Kenneth Walker III, Aaron Jones, D'Onta Foreman and D'Andre Swift were all questionable at best to suit up in Week 14, with Stevenson known to be out, per Sporting News on Dec. 7, 2023; Jonathan Taylor had undergone thumb surgery and would miss the next few weeks.

Two-time rushing champ Derrick Henry (head) suffered a huge blow to the head and neck area in Tennessee's Week 13 loss to the Colts, immediately leaving the game for the locker room, and it was confirmed he would not return with a head injury; Sporting News reasoned it would not make much sense for a four-win team to rush back its franchise player, so the king might give up his throne to Tyjae Spears. Patriots dual-purpose back Rhamondre Stevenson (ankle) was hurt on a nasty hip-drop tackle in New England's Week 13 loss to the Chargers, and Bill Belichick and company had already noted he would miss multiple weeks, which pushed Ezekiel Elliott way up against the Steelers. D'Andre Swift took a hard hit and exited Philadelphia's game late against the 49ers, though it was tough to know whether he was actually hurt or was kept out because of the lopsided score; if Swift could not go in Week 14 against Dallas, Kenneth Gainwell and Boston Scott would split touches, with Gainwell likely garnering flex value.

Jonathan Taylor underwent thumb surgery and was expected to miss a few weeks, which was tough for his managers down the home stretch though they could get him back for the end of the fantasy playoffs; the injury highlighted the importance of handcuffs, as Zack Moss had put up multiple top-10 performances with and without Taylor that season and would resume his role as Indy's super-sub lead back. Seattle's Kenneth Walker III (oblique) missed Weeks 12 and 13 and Sporting News was not confident in his ability to suit up against the hard-hitting 49ers, while backup Zach Charbonnet exited early with a knee injury against the Cowboys, putting his status in doubt too — if both were out, DeeJay Dallas would start and lead the backfield in touches. Green Bay's Aaron Jones had a sprained MCL and sources revealed the Packers continued to consider him week-to-week: he logged only four carries before sustaining the injury in Week 11, then sat for the Packers' Thanksgiving Day tilt with the Lions and their Sunday Night Football win over Kansas City. D'Onta Foreman (ankle) missed Chicago's game before the bye and his status remained unclear, and if healthy he would likely start in Chicago's backfield, though it was anyone's guess how carries would be divvied up after Roschon Johnson surprisingly led the way in Week 12. Sporting News said it would be updating the rankings all week.
