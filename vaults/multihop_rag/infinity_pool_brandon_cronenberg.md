# Infinity Pool (Brandon Cronenberg)

Infinity Pool is Brandon Cronenberg's third film, starring Alexander Skarsgård, Mia Goth, and Cleopatra Coleman, ranked 41 on Polygon's best movies of 2023 list of 2023-10-07 and available on Hulu or for digital rental/purchase.

Its premise is that death does not have to be real in the world of the film — at least not if you are rich enough to turn it into a fun night out. Polygon says the film, from the director of 2020's stellar Possessor, gazes into the bizarre void of identity and wonders what happens when it gazes back.

Polygon's Austen Goslin calls it queasy and fascinating, and perhaps 2023's most nihilistic view of wealth, while naming the real selling point as the go-for-broke central performances by Mia Goth and Alexander Skarsgård.
