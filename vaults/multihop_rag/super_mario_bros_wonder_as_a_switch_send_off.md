# Super Mario Bros. Wonder As A Send-Off For The Switch

Polygon argues that Super Mario Bros. Wonder would be a fitting last big release for the Nintendo Switch, on the grounds that the game embodies the same combination that defined the console. With rumours that the Switch's successor could arrive early the following year, the reviewer says he would be fine if Wonder were the console's final major title. The historical grounds: to survive after the Wii U, Nintendo had to bring its console and handheld designers together and create something unlike anything on the market without sacrificing what had got the company to that point — and as the sun sets on the Switch, after an unprecedented run of success and arguably the best entries in many of Nintendo's biggest franchises, Wonder arrives. "Like the Switch itself, Wonder is a collision between the traditional and the new. A game that's the same as it ever was and nothing like Mario has ever been."

The reviewer sets aside the fan question of whether Wonder is the unofficial Super Mario Bros. 5 that "dorks like me have long wanted," leaving that to the forums, and rests his praise on the experience instead: "What a joy it is to play a Mario game and not already know how to read it, to not have my muscle memory do more work than my brain." The scope conditions of the review are stated: Super Mario Bros. Wonder was released on 20 October 2023 on Nintendo Switch, and was reviewed on that console using a pre-release download code provided by Nintendo.
