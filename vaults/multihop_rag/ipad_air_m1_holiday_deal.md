# iPad Air M1 Holiday Deal

The base model of the latest generation iPad Air has an MSRP of $599 and came down to $499 over the 2023 holiday sales — around $500, a record low, with all color options discounted — a price it hit quite a few times in 2023.

This is the iPad Engadget recommends for most people because it's speedy, has a great screen and works with current-generation accessories, including Apple Pencil 2 support: it can act as a rather powerful entertainment option for games and streaming, but is powerful enough to serve as a productivity device when paired with a stylus or mouse and a keyboard, and it offers good battery life and a slim design. One caveat about timing: now that the M3 chip has been loosed upon the world, the iPad Air, which houses the M1 chip, is primed for an update, which will likely come next year along with a potential price increase — Engadget expects to see a fresh lineup with the latest M-series processor debut early next year.
