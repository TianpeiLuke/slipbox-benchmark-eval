# The Smiths' Denials Of Open-Marriage, Divorce And Infidelity Rumours

Will Smith and Jada Pinkett Smith have each publicly rejected the recurring claims made about their marriage — that it was an open relationship, that they were divorcing, and that either had been unfaithful. Pinkett Smith attacked the open-relationship premise directly on Facebook, after a quiet decade and a half spent making films and raising their children: "Here is how I will change my statement... Will and I BOTH can do WHATEVER we want, because we TRUST each other to do so. This does NOT mean we have an open relationship... this means we have a GROWN one." Her counter is that mutual freedom follows from trust, not from an open-marriage arrangement, so the inference from one to the other does not hold.

Will Smith answered the separation-and-divorce rumours in a mocking Facebook post: "Under normal circumstances, I don't usually respond to foolishness. But, so many people have extended [to] me their 'deepest condolences' that I figured — 'What the hell… I can be foolish, too!' So, in the interest of redundant, repetitious, over and over-again-ness… Jada and I are… NOT GETTING A DIVORCE!!!!!!!!!!!!!" In an interview on CBS Sunday Morning in October 2023 he also denied the infidelity premise outright: "Never. There's never been infidelity in our marriage... And we have never surprised one another with anything ever." He added that he had decided public chatter about his life "can be of a benefit to people," since "chatter is the first stage to having a real conversation and being able to truly explore if some of the things in your heart are loving, or poisonous."
