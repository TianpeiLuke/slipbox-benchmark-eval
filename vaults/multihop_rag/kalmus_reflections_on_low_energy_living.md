# Kalmus's Reflections on Low-Energy Living

Months after returning "kicking and screaming" to the "quote-unquote real world," Kalmus was still mulling why the Possibility Alliance's "low energy" lifestyle had felt so meaningful and what was missing from modern life, concluding: "Everything is too fast. It's obvious to a lot of people, but it's a new revelation to me. There's nothing more important in life than relationships."

Maine, like anywhere else, is not immune to climate change, but there at least he had people who understood his angst, who could share music and joy, who were willing to make their own sacrifices, and who were trying to prepare their community in the way they thought best. He was planning a return trip to the Possibility Alliance over the winter holidays.
