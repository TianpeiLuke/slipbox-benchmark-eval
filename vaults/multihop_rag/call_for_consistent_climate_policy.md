# The Call For Consistent Climate Policy

The third and most important demand is a consistent climate policy, and the author argues the impact of not having one is playing out in real time. Sunak's public U-turn doesn't just undermine science and play into the hands of the climate skeptics; it also plows headlong into the future of any climate tech company whose launch plans have to be aligned around decarbonization timelines. The predicted consequences are stated in sequence: this will damage economic confidence, people will lose jobs, and action will be further delayed.

The warrant is compressed into a single line — inconsistency breeds uncertainty — and the ask follows with a scope condition attached: it is possible for the sector to succeed in spite of bad policies, but to thrive it needs consistent resolutions and behavior from a government that actually cares about leading in this critical fight. Given the colossal stakes, which were at least recently taken seriously, the author asks whether that is really asking too much.
