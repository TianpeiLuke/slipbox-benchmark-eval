# Cecile de Jongh's Role And Her Deposition Denial

During her deposition as part of the JPMorgan litigation, Cecile de Jongh was asked about seeking enrollment in English classes for women at Epstein's request — the question implying knowledge of what those arrangements served.

She denied both awareness and complicity: she said she never saw anyone under distress and "would never do anything to harm anyone, or aid and abet anyone in harming someone."
