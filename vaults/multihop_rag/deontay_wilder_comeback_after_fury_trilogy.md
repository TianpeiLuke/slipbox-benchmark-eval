# Deontay Wilder Comeback After Fury Trilogy

After coming up empty in the final two fights of his trilogy with Tyson Fury, both of which ended via violent stoppage, Deontay Wilder sat out a full year before returning the previous October to knock out Robert Helenius in Round 1, CBSSports.com recounted on Dec. 23, 2023.

Wilder went on to enjoy a bit of a spiritual rebirth after attending an Ayahuasca retreat in South America and had been noticeably happier as a result — a far cry from the more combative side of Wilder that shared more than a few conspiracy theories as to why he lost to Fury, and even fired co-trainer Mark Breland for throwing in the towel to save Wilder from further punishment in his 2020 Fury rematch. "My mindspace is in a wonderful place. It's in an over-happy place," Wilder said. "I'm always smiling nowadays. It's a joy to be able to enjoy life. Life passes us by so quickly because we are living in borrowed time, nothing is promised. You have to enjoy every moment."
