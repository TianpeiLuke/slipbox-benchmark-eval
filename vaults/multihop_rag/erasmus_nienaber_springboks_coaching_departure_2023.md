# Erasmus And Nienaber Depart After The Springboks' 2023 Title

The 2023 World Cup final victory was the perfect sign-off for the Springboks' management, with director of rugby Rassie Erasmus set to follow head coach Jacques Nienaber out — a duo The Roar says will go down as the greatest coaching pair in rugby history, having led South Africa to back-to-back World Cup triumphs.

Nienaber framed the win as the endpoint of a long plan and thanked the country: "We came a long way with the players. We always planned for this World Cup, since 2018. Massive credit to our fans. They were special from South Africa, 62 million people, the videos we had to play from them. It was unbelievable."

The departures were not only from the coaching box: veteran back-rower Duane Vermeulen would also call time on his illustrious career.
