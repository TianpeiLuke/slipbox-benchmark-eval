# Fifth Third 1.67% Card Redemption Options

Redeeming rewards on the Fifth Third 1.67% Card is deliberately plain — it doesn't have many tricks up its sleeve. The first option is to apply earned points toward a statement credit on the card to offset the balance, which can be done immediately after the rewards are posted to the account; unlike many cash back cards, which require you to earn a specific amount before redeeming ($25 is a common threshold), this card has no such minimum.

Alternatively, you can request a direct deposit into your Fifth Third checking or savings account — the route to take if you don't currently have a balance on your credit card, or if you'd like to use the funds in some way other than lowering your credit card debt.

A third option removes the step entirely: enroll in auto-redemption, which empties the full rewards balance into your account each month.
