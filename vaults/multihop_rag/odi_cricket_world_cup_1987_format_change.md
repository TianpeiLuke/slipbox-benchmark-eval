# The 1987 ODI World Cup Format Change

The 1987 ODI World Cup brought two prime changes in the way the tournament was organised: a shift of venue from England to the Indian subcontinent, and games being played in 50 overs instead of 60.

The reason given for the shorter innings is climatic rather than sporting — the change in the number of overs was attributed to shorter daylight hours in Asia in comparison to England.

The 1987 tournament also had a new winner in Australia, who went on to defeat England by seven runs.
