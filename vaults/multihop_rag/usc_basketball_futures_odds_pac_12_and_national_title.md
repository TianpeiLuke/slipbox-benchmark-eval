# USC's Pac-12 And National Title Futures Position

USC was ranked as the second choice to win the Pac-12 at most books, behind No. 1 Arizona, per Sporting News on Dec. 9, 2023.

The resume context was neutral: USC had not done much to enhance or tarnish its resume in the season's first few weeks. A loss to UC Irvine did not help its at-large chances but was not a deal-breaker either, and a solid showing in Pac-12 play should see the Trojans enter the NCAA Tournament field of 68 comfortably.

On the conference market, while UCLA was not expected to be as formidable as it had been in years past, Arizona was ranked No. 1 nationally and was basically an even money choice to win the Pac-12 as a clear favorite, leaving USC as the second choice behind the Wildcats. On the National Championship market, USC's price varied depending on where you shopped, and Sporting News judged that there was definitely an opportunity to cash in on them if you were bullish on their ability to improve and blossom into a title contender.
