# Champions League 2023/24 Group H: Barcelona And Porto Qualify

Barcelona's 2-1 home win over Porto sealed their place in the Round of 16 from Group H of the 2023/24 Champions League, and they were joined by the Portuguese side, with Shakhtar Donetsk taking the Europa League spot and Antwerp bottom, per Sporting News on Dec. 12, 2023.

The final table read: 1. Barcelona (Q) 6 games, 4 wins, 0 draws, 2 losses, +6 goal difference, 12 points; 2. Porto (Q) 6, 3, 0, 2, +7, 12; 3. Shakhtar Donetsk 6, 3, 0, 3, -2, 9; 4. Antwerp 6, 1, 0, 5, -11, 3. Porto qualified after beating Shakhtar Donetsk 5-3 in a matchday-six thriller.

The results were: Sept. 19, Barcelona 5-0 Antwerp; Sept. 19, Shakhtar Donetsk 1-3 Porto; Oct. 4, Antwerp 2-3 Shakhtar Donetsk; Oct. 4, Porto 0-1 Barcelona; Oct. 25, Barcelona 2-1 Shakhtar Donetsk; Oct. 25, Antwerp 1-4 Porto; Nov. 7, Shakhtar Donetsk 1-0 Barcelona; Nov. 7, Porto 2-0 Antwerp; Nov. 28, Shakhtar Donetsk 1-0 Antwerp; Nov. 28, Barcelona 2-1 Porto; Dec. 13, Antwerp 3-2 Barcelona; Dec. 13, Porto 5-3 Shakhtar Donetsk.
