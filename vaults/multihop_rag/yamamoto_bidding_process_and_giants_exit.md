# The Yamamoto Bidding Process And The Giants' Exit

Formal bidding for Yoshinobu Yamamoto was slated to begin on Monday, Dec. 18, 2023, and by Thursday, Dec. 21 the Giants had been notified they were out of it.

ESPN's Jeff Passan reported on Dec. 18 that despite plenty of speculation about offers sent to Yamamoto, he had yet to receive any formal offers — but that would start to change that Monday, with the bidding slated to begin. On Dec. 21, NBC Sports Bay Area's Alex Pavlovic reported that San Francisco, which had made a hard push to land Yamamoto, had been notified it was out of the bidding for the Japanese ace. Pavlovic added that it was very likely Yamamoto would end up either in Los Angeles with the Dodgers or in New York with the Mets or Yankees.
