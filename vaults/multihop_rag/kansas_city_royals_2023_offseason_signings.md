# Kansas City Royals' December 2023 Signings

The Royals spent aggressively in December 2023, agreeing with reliever Will Smith (one year, $5 million, Dec. 10), starter Seth Lugo (three years, $45 million, Dec. 12) and, on Friday, Dec. 15, outfielder Hunter Renfroe and right-hander Michael Wacha — bringing their total offseason commitments to $82 million.

ESPN's Jeff Passan reported the Smith agreement: the reliever had spent the past season with the Rangers and had won the World Series in each of the last three seasons, though Kansas City's expectations in 2024 did not quite extend to a championship; instead the Royals hoped the veteran, who had solid underlying numbers in 2023, could serve as a reliable late-inning arm after both Scott Barlow and Aroldis Chapman were traded before that season's deadline. MLB.com's Anne Rogers reported the Lugo contract as being finalized: the Padres had stretched Lugo back into a full-time starter in 2023, and the 34-year-old posted a 3.57 ERA across 146.1 innings, with Kansas City needing plenty of rotation help after a dismal 2023 season and Lugo figuring to be one of its top arms in 2024 and perhaps beyond. Rogers also reported the Dec. 15 pair: Renfroe on a one-year, $5.5 million deal with a player option for 2025 worth $7.5 million, and Wacha on a two-year, $32 million deal with an opt-out after 2024. Those moves followed earlier signings of Lugo, Smith and Garrett Hampson.
