# HP Business 2-In-1 Laptop Black Friday Deal

An HP business 2-in-1 laptop was available for under $500 in Walmart's Black Friday 2023 sale while supplies lasted, Mashable reported on 25 November 2023.

The total discount is not the biggest rollback the writers had seen, but they call it "a deceptively good deal on an underpriced laptop".

With an Intel Core i5 processor, the 2-in-1 "will be more than capable of handling your day-to-day work (and after-work streaming and surfing)".
