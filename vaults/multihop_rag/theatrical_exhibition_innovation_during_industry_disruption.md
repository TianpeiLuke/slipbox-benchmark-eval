# Theatrical Exhibition Innovation During Industry Disruption

Paul Dergarabedian, senior media analyst for data firm Comscore, argues that disruption is itself the engine of change in exhibition: "Innovation comes out of challenging times in this business. We're seeing a lot of changes, some subtle, some not so subtle." His reading of the current moment is that the old constraints have lapsed — "It seems like, right now, there are no rules when it comes to being successful."

The conclusion he draws for theaters is a widening of what belongs on their screens: "It really opens up the idea that other types of content can play really well in a movie theater."
