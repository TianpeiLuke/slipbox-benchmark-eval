# Amex Cash Magnet Approval Odds And Credit Limits

American Express doesn't disclose its minimum credit score requirement for its cards, but generally you need good credit — according to Equifax, a score of 670 or higher — to qualify for the Amex Cash Magnet Card or other American Express cards. Amex does have a card pre-qualification tool you can use to find out the likelihood of getting approved without affecting your credit score.

Credit limits are similarly undisclosed: some cards list their minimum spending limit in their terms and conditions, but American Express doesn't list the minimum for the Cash Magnet Card. Card users reported limits as low as $1,000, while others reported significantly higher limits, with some receiving limits over $10,000. Unlike the American Express Platinum Card, which is a metal card with a heavier weight than most cards, the Cash Magnet is a standard plastic credit card.
