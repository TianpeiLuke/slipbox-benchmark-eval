# 2023 Bowl Season Betting Trends

Bowl season had been going the way of the underdog until a Tuesday triple header in which all three favorites won straight up and against the spread and the OVER went 3-0 on the day.

At that point in the season favorites were 11-11 straight up and 7-15 against the spread, and the Over was 13-9. Sporting News suggested bettors could be seeing a new trend developing and that they would find out on Wednesday, starting with the Military Bowl at 2 p.m. ET.
