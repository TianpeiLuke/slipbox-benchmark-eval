# Pregame Setup For World Series Game 3, 2023

Game 3 of the 2023 World Series between the Rangers and Diamondbacks was played on Monday, Oct. 30 at Chase Field in Phoenix, AZ, with first pitch set for 8:03 p.m. ET (5:03 p.m. MST) and the stadium roof confirmed open for the night.

The roof update came at 1:23 p.m., when the Sporting News relayed that the Chase Field roof would be OPEN for Game 3. Lineup news followed. At 3:10 p.m. the Diamondbacks were reported to be returning Corbin Carroll to the top of the lineup after leading off with Ketel Marte in Game 2, with right-hander Brandon Pfaadt getting the ball to start.

At 3:29 p.m., after only scoring one run in Game 2, the Rangers were reported to be making a slight tweak to their lineup: Adolis Garcia would slide up to the No. 3 spot with Evan Carter hitting clean-up. As Texas PR noted, Carter is the fifth-youngest player in World Series history to bat fourth, joining Juan Soto, Ty Cobb, Miguel Cabrera and Claudell Washington.
