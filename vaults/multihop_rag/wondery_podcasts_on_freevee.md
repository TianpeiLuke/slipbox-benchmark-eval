# Wondery Podcasts On Freevee

Wondery, which is owned by Amazon, will make many of its podcasts available on three new channels on Freevee, the ad-supported video streaming service also owned by Amazon that was formerly known as IMDb TV. On October 31st, 2023 Freevee will launch three dedicated Wondery channels: a flagship channel focused on entertainment programming such as Baby, This is Keke Palmer, Business Wars, and American Scandal; Exhibit C, featuring true crime shows including This is Actually Happening, Dr. Death, and Morbid; and Wondery Sports, with shows including Don't Call It a Comeback and Gladiator. The visuals will be a mix of on-camera recordings, animations, and show art — but The Verge's writer would not bank on that being the main draw.

She read it as an interesting strategy for discovery, though she was not sure how replicable it is beyond Amazon. If it succeeds, she could see other ad-supported video services licensing podcast programming, since it is certainly cheaper than video — but Amazon is in the unique situation of owning the whole pipeline, so there is not much risk for it.
