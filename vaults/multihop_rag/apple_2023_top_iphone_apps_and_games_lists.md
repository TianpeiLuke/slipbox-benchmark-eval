# Apple's 2023 Top iPhone App And Game Lists

Apple's 2023 U.S. iPhone lists, released on 12 December 2023, ranked the top free apps in this order: Temu: Shop Like a Billionaire; CapCut – Video Editor; Max: Stream HBO, TV, & Movies; Threads, an Instagram app; TikTok; Instagram; Google; YouTube: Watch, Listen, Stream; WhatsApp Messenger; and Gmail – Email by Google.

The top paid apps list showcases a different market, more often of smaller developers or those building useful productivity tools, creative apps or utilities that users are willing to pay for. The top 10, in order, were Shadowrocket, HotSchedules, Procreate Pocket, The Wonder Weeks, 75 Hard, AutoSleep Track Sleep on Watch, Goblin Tools, TonalEnergy Tuner & Metronome, SkyView and AnkiMobile Flashcards.

The top free games included several of last year's winners, like Roblox, Call of Duty: Mobile, Subway Surfers and Parking Jam 3D, as well as some new additions. The No. 1 was MONOPOLY GO!, followed by Roblox (up from No. 3 last year), Royal Match, Subway Surfers (down from No. 2 last year), Gardenscapes, Call of Duty: Mobile (up from No. 8 last year), Block Blast!, Makeover Studio: Makeup Games, Parking Jam 3D (ranked the same as last year) and Survivor!.io.

The top paid games in the U.S. were led by Microsoft-owned Minecraft, and also included Heads Up!, Geometry Dash, Bloons TD 6, MONOPOLY, Papa's Freezeria To Go!, Plague Inc., Red's First Flight, Five Nights at Freddy's — which became a movie this year too — and Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas.
