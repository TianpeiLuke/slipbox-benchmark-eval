# Paul Mitchell's Recruitment Career Record

Paul Mitchell is one of football's most respected executives, a recruitment specialist who has held head-of-recruitment or sporting-director roles at MK Dons, Southampton, Tottenham, RB Leipzig and Monaco, and who recently left the principality of Monaco — where he could well return for meetings with Sir Jim Ratcliffe, who is planning to appoint a sporting director, talkSPORT reported on 16 October 2023.

What distinguishes Mitchell is a scouting net cast far and wide and an eye for players before they are established. A former player in England's lower leagues, starring mainly for Wigan and MK Dons, he was moved upstairs at MK Dons and made his first big move by spotting future England regular Dele Alli after being named their head of recruitment in 2010. Two years later he joined Southampton in the same role, where Sadio Mane, Dusan Tadic, Graziano Pelle, Dejan Lovren and Victor Wanyama all went on to secure the Saints a bright future, make the club a fortune, become Champions League regulars and deliver a Premier League best finish — earning Mitchell a promotion to Tottenham in 2014. The north London side had just unsuccessfully splurged their world-record £85million from Real Madrid for Gareth Bale, so it looked like he could not have picked a worse time; yet Alli, Heung-Min Son, Toby Alderweireld, Eric Dier and Kieran Trippier set the side up for a bright future.

The boundary case in his record is Tottenham's boardroom. Mitchell left what he called his "dream job" after less than two years, reportedly due to chairman Daniel Levy's over-involvement in negotiations and the recruitment process — something that was not an issue at his next club, Leipzig, where Ralf Rangnick landed him and signings such as Dani Olmo, Christopher Nkunku and Amadou Haidara showed what he can do with the shackles off. Many of those signings starred in a run to the Champions League semi-finals in 2020, and in 2022 the club won its first ever trophy with a DFB Pokal. He left only to reach the pinnacle of his job, finally stepping up from head of recruitment to sporting director at Monaco. He was previously linked with Chelsea and Liverpool and, much like Ratcliffe, is from Greater Manchester; he remains good friends with Rangnick from their time together at Leipzig and last season made headlines when he was spotted at a youth team game while the German was interim coach at Man United.
