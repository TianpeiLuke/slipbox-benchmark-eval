# Kick's Platform Model And Controversy History

Kick has lured disenchanted Twitch streamers away from that platform with non-exclusive streaming deals, light moderation and a coveted 95/5 revenue split.

The platform is young, but its first year has been checkered with controversy — starting with its ties to a crypto gambling site also owned by Kick CEO Eddie Craven. A more recent scandal was an offensive fake interview between white supremacist sympathizer Adin Ross, accused sex trafficker Andrew Tate and a Kim Jong Un impersonator. The sex worker stream is a wake-up call for many of its streamers, especially women who question whether the platform is safe for them at all: Bob, a Kick creator, acknowledged the string of controversies and said that seeing Craven laughing at the sex worker was "more than enough to stamp out any hope that the platform intends to improve any of its policies."
