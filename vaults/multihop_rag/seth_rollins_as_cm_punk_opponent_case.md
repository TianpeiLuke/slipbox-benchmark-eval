# Seth Rollins As A CM Punk Opponent

Essentially Sports argued on 21 November 2023 that Seth Rollins is a candidate opponent for a returning CM Punk not mainly because he holds the WWE World Heavyweight Championship but because of real animosity from outside the ring: the claim is that Rollins' public comments will be the setting stone for their rivalry, and the warrant is that those comments express genuine hostility that a Survivor Series return would immediately activate.

The grounds are a history that turned. When CM Punk made his return to WWE Backstage in 2019, many saw this as a precursor to his return to the company, Rollins included, who invited him to step into the ring with the Messiah. By January 2023, in an interview promoting WWE 2K23, The Visionary made his feelings clear after Punk's backstage antics in AEW, referring to him as a "cancer" and stating that he should stay away from him and WWE. The following week the current WWE World Heavyweight Champion shed some light on his intentions: referring to Punk as 'Chicago Phil', he said he is as selfish as another WWE talent, Logan Paul, adding that Punk had particularly helped him during his career but had also taken a lot from the promotion; he believes Punk might not be in the right headspace but has to contribute somehow, and that if he is not going to provide for the industry he does not deserve to be a part of it.

The projected payoff is modest and specific: if CM Punk returns at Survivor Series, fans might get a minor confrontation or possibly a verbal confrontation between the two, given the very real animosity. The article adds that it would be a neat wrinkle in their character arcs considering CM Punk brought forth The Shield, the faction that brought Rollins to prominence.
