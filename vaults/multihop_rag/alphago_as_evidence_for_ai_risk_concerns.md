# AlphaGo As Evidence For AI Risk Concerns

Rationalists, effective altruists and others who worried about the risks of AI claimed the computer's win at Go validated their fears. The evidence cited is the pace of progress rather than the game itself: "This is another indication that AI is progressing faster than even many experts anticipated," wrote Victoria Krakovna, who would soon join DeepMind as an "AI safety" researcher, in a blog post.

The consequences inside DeepMind followed the same worry. Its founders were increasingly concerned about what Google would do with their inventions and in 2017 tried to break away from the company; Google responded by increasing the salaries and stock award packages of the DeepMind founders and their staff, and they stayed put. The ethics board never had a second meeting.
