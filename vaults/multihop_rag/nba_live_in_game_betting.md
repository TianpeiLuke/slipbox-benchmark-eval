# NBA In-Game Betting: Wagering While The Game Unfolds

In-game betting, also known as live betting, allows you to bet on a game while it is unfolding, and it has exploded nationwide thanks to the increased popularity of online sports betting apps. The action is fast and the odds are constantly shifting, making for a new way to enjoy the flow of a game while staying engaged as a bettor.

Depending on the sportsbook there is usually a strong variety of live betting opportunities. These include game and player prop bets such as whether the next bucket will be worth one, two or three points; bets on the team leading at the end of the current quarter; bets on whether there will be a comeback victory; and wagers boiled down to the results of the next possession.

The most popular sportsbook apps — DraftKings, FanDuel and BetMGM — offer NBA in-game betting, though each has its own lines and betting opportunities.
