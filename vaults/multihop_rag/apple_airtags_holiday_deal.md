# Apple AirTags Holiday Deal

A four-pack of Apple AirTags was $80 at Amazon over the 2023 holiday sales thanks to a 19 percent discount — about $5 more than the pack's record-low price. They go for $29 each at full price, so the deal saves $9 apiece, and a single AirTag could be had for $24, a $5 discount, at Walmart and Target.

AirTags are Engadget's top picks for Bluetooth trackers for iPhone users, as they tap into Apple's disturbingly vast Find My network, using other Apple mobiles to find lost items: they are small Bluetooth trackers you can keep in a bag or luggage or attach to items like keys to track their location, see where your stuff is in the Find My app, and even force the gadgets to ring so you can find your belongings when within close proximity. The only catch is that these only work with Apple devices as their host device, so Android users will have to pick a tracker from another brand to get similar benefits.
