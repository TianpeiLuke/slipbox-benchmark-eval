# PSG 2-1 Nantes In Ligue 1

Randal Kolo Muani's late goal gave Paris Saint-Germain a 2-1 win over Nantes on Dec. 9, 2023, their eighth consecutive Ligue 1 win, extending their lead at the top to six points.

Bradley Barcola opened the scoring in the 41st minute, finding space in the six-yard box after a solo run from the left. Mostafa Mohamed equalised for the visitors in the 55th minute from a corner, heading home from close range. Kolo Muani restored the lead seven minutes from time by capitalising on a rebound after a save by the visitors' keeper following a set piece. PSG moved to 36 points, six clear of Monaco, who beat Rennes 2-1; third-placed Nice were on 29 points and would host Reims on the Sunday.
