# BetRivers Sportsbook: Alternate Lines, An Integrated Casino, And iRush Rewards

BetRivers is distinguished by its iRush Rewards loyalty program and by an app that integrates sports betting with online casino games under a single account. For Major League Baseball it presents bettors with a wealth of stats and insightful tips on upcoming games to aid decision-making, and alongside standard betting options offers a vast selection of alternate lines and prop bets tailored to baseball. Loyal MLB bettors accumulate iRush Rewards points as they place bets, redeemable for exclusive VIP gifts, MLB event tickets, BetRivers branded merchandise and a range of special offers; on the NBA side the same program lets users earn points as they bet on sports, with the added flexibility of spending accumulated points on BetRivers' online casino.

Dedicated promotions are a hallmark of the book, and its engagement with NBA fans is evident. A past promotion dubbed "NBA Wednesdays" allowed users to wager up to $25 on any NBA game scheduled on a Wednesday, with a 20% profit boost if the bet was successful.

New accounts typically can claim a second-chance bet that could reach up to $500, although the amount might differ based on the state.
