# How To Claim A Vermont Sportsbook Bonus

Claiming a bonus from a Vermont online sportsbook usually takes one step, Sporting News explained on 2023-09-26: many times, all you'll need to do is sign up for a new account through the links on the page. Sometimes you may need to enter the promo or bonus code, but those will all be listed on the same page.

Two further conditions may apply after registration: you may need to place a qualifying bet, or meet specific wagering requirements, to get your hands on the bonus. All of this will be outlined in the terms and conditions attached to any Vermont online sportsbook bonus offer.
