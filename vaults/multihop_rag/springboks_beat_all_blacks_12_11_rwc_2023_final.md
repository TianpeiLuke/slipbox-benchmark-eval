# Springboks Beat All Blacks 12-11 To Win A Record Fourth World Cup

South Africa beat New Zealand 12-11 at the Stade de France on Oct. 28, 2023 to claim a record fourth Rugby World Cup, moving into uncharted territory by leapfrogging the All Blacks — and doing it, as The Roar noted, twenty-eight years after the famous Ellis Park win at which Nelson Mandela handed the William Webb Ellis Cup to Francois Pienaar.

The conditions and the setting could not have been more different from 1995: instead of the blue skies over Johannesburg and the Boeing 747 that sent shivers down spines in attendance, rain and dampness poured down on the Stade de France, though nothing dampened an evening into which 80,065 fans poured. It was, the report says, that kind of evening, where the margins were tiny and the outcomes almighty.

The margin itself came down to one kick. A missed Richie Mo'unga conversion following Beauden Barrett's 58th-minute try — the only five-pointer of the match — ultimately proved the difference, as Handre Pollard's four three-pointers led the Springboks to World Cup glory. The win was South Africa's third one-point knockout victory in a row, and the second straight tournament they had won in France, having beaten England at the same venue in 2007.
