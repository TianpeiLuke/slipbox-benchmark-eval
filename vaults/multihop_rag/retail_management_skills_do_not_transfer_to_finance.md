# Retail And Real Estate Management Skills Do Not Transfer To Finance

A capital market player argues that Zim's difficulties are not merely bad luck but a category error: "There's a considerable difference between commercial centers and the world of finance. People think that if they are good managers, they can manage anything, but that isn't necessarily so."

The grounds are specific to what the capital market requires: "To succeed in the capital market you have to be able to bring in the best investment managers and create DNA in the organization. There are many elements that are not similar to real estate or retailing, and the mere fact that a person has financial insight doesn't mean success on the capital market." The warrant is a caution against appearances: "It looks sexy and glittering from the outside, but you have to understand how this sector works."
