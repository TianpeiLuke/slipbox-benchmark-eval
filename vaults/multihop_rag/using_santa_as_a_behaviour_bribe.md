# Using Santa as a Behaviour Bribe

Hannah Keeley argues that using a message of "be good or else" before Santa Claus comes down the chimney lowers parenting standards — a tactic she calls "stooping to Santa". "Don't lower your parenting standards to bribery, especially around a season and a character that is supposed to symbolize peace and goodwill on Earth," she said.

Two grounds support the claim. First, the bribe distorts the meaning of the holiday: the Santa bribe turns the focus of Christmas into "selfish gain on the part of your children". Second, it fails as parenting practice: "It's also lazy parenting, which never pays off in the long run," she said. "Being a mom is a professional career and deserves to be treated accordingly."

The alternative she prescribes follows directly: "Love your kids enough to learn how to manage their behavior appropriately and teach them self-discipline."
