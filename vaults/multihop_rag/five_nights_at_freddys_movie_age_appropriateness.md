# Age Appropriateness of the Five Nights at Freddy's Movie

Polygon's conclusion, published 2023-10-26, is that the PG-13 rating's 12-or-13 age cutoff is probably about right for the Five Nights at Freddy's movie — though, as with any such judgment, every kid is different.

The question arises because, for an ostensible horror franchise built on nostalgia for and subversion of animatronic characters dating to the 1980s, the FNAF games have a surprising number of young fans. Their kid appeal echoes a time when playgrounds were alight with elementary school kids describing the antics of Freddy and/or Jason, whether witnessed firsthand or pieced together from rumors. Unlike A Nightmare on Elm Street or Friday the 13th, however, Freddy's is rated PG-13 rather than R.

The grounds for leniency are that the movie is far from an unrelenting thrill ride: it is not as intense as fellow PG-13 horror movies The Ring or Drag Me to Hell, the most ghoulish aspects of the Freddy's backstory are kept off screen or entirely unmentioned, none of its set-pieces seem like pure nightmare fuel, and several of the titular five nights pass without much incident at all.

The grounds for caution are that there are plentiful cheap music-sting jump scares and a few gruesome bits of junior-level gore. Violence-wise this is a harder PG-13 than the average superhero movie — something Polygon expects will only entice its younger YouTube-versed fans more.
