# Why Communicating Deep-Sea Science Matters

Jon Copley argues that communicating deep sea science matters because the deep sea is not somewhere people think about every day: we can go out at night and look at the sky and wonder what is going on up there, but you cannot glance into the deep sea in the same way, so it has become a realm of myth and darkness.

Even the names of the deepest parts — the abyssal plains and the hadal zone — evoke that kind of underworld, he says. The conclusion he draws is that it is nice to be able to shine a light on that for people and to highlight how our lives are connected to it.
