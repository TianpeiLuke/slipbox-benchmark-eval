# Coming To America (1988): Eddie Murphy's Romantic Comedy

Coming to America is the 1988 romantic comedy directed by John Landis, running 1h 56m, starring Eddie Murphy, Arsenio Hall and James Earl Jones. Murphy plays Akeem Joffer, the crown prince of the fictional African country of Zamunda who, tired of his mother and father's meddling in his love life, travels to the borough of Queens in New York City with his personal aide Semmi (Hall) to search for a wife. The film is based on a story by Murphy.

Polygon's write-up highlights the density of quotable performances — Samuel L. Jackson, James Earl Jones, Louie Anderson, John Amos, and both Murphy and Arsenio Hall in multiple roles — and calls it an absolute riot front to back, an enduring classic for good reason and one of Murphy's finest films. It is available to stream on Netflix, or for digital rental or purchase on Amazon, Apple TV, Google Play and Vudu.
