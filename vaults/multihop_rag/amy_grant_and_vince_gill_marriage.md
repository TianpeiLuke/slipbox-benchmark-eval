# Amy Grant And Vince Gill's Marriage

Amy Grant, the Queen of Christian Pop, and the Grammy Award-winning country artist Vince Gill have been married since 2000 — 23 years as of the November 2023 article — and share a daughter named Corrina. Their shared love of music is what brought them together.

The episode the profile centres on is Grant's recovery from a bike accident the previous July, in which she suffered a traumatic head injury: the "Baby, Baby" singer was knocked unconscious for up to 15 minutes after hitting a pothole and tumbling to the ground, and had to postpone her fall concert tour. She credited Gill with providing a "grounding" place to heal. "He has just been so patient," Grant told Fox News Digital at The Kennedy Center Honors. "Vince has a kind of way of grounding the space that we're in even without saying a word. I think early on I said, 'What if I'm different, what if I'm not the same?' and he said, 'Hey, every day we wake up a little different, and we love each other, and it's good.'" After recovering, the "El Shaddai" singer shared the stage with her husband for several Christmas-season performances.
