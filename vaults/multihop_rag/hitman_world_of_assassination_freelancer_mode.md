# Hitman World of Assassination: Freelancer Mode

Hitman World of Assassination's Freelancer mode, developed by IO Interactive for PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, Windows PC, Xbox One, and Xbox Series X, is ranked 27 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31. Debuting in January, it puts a roguelike twist on Agent 47's globe-trotting murder-for-hire missions.

Polygon warns it is not for the faint of heart: it demands constant improvisation to a degree that can challenge even veteran Hitman players, and repeated failures can make it feel more frustrating than fun.

Critic Samit Sarkar counters with what roguelike fans know — these high-stakes experiences have the capacity to deliver a sense of exhilaration like nothing else. Pulling off multiple daring kills, hiding the evidence or going out with a bang, and trying to make it out alive with all the gear you brought in is tense and thrilling every time; every move could be your last, since all it takes to ruin a flawless run is a single ill-considered plan or one seemingly minor slip-up. With the abyss of failure forever yawning beneath 47, playing Freelancer can feel like tiptoeing along the top of a barbed-wire fence, and the exultation of safely reaching the final exfiltration point after defeating a crime syndicate is a high the critic keeps chasing.
