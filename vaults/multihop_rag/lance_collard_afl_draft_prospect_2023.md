# Lance Collard AFL Draft Prospect 2023

Lance Collard, profiled in Kevin Sheehan's top 40 for the 2023 AFL Draft as reported by The Roar on Nov. 15, 2023, is a small forward who is extremely dangerous around goals. State: Western Australia. State League Club: Subiaco. Community Club: North Beach. Date of Birth: 26/03/05. Height: 180cm.

He booted 39 goals in 16 matches in Western Australia's pathway that year, playing predominantly at Colts level, and also represented his state in three games at the National Championships, which gave him a taste of playing against the nation's best. He then highlighted his potential by kicking five-goal bags in three of the final five games of the WAFL Colts season. He showcased his athletic prowess at the national Draft Combine, producing the quickest time on the agility test (8.16 seconds) and a top ten finish on the 20m sprint (2.92 seconds). The eligibility condition attached to him: he is a member of the West Coast Eagles' Next Generation Academy, meaning they can match any bid after selection 40 in that year's draft. Weapon: Speed and innate goal sense. AFL player comparison: Charlie Cameron (Brisbane Lions).
