# Saltburn (Emerald Fennell)

Saltburn is Emerald Fennell's startling, gleefully dark follow-up to Promising Young Woman, starring Barry Keoghan, Jacob Elordi, and Rosamund Pike. Polygon ranked it 24 on its best movies of 2023 list of 2023-10-07, when it was in theaters and coming to Prime Video Dec. 22.

Like Fennell's previous film, it is a story about toxic obsession; this time the central figure is a social climber, played by a mesmerizing Barry Keoghan, trying to weasel his way in among a rich family in 2000s England.

Polygon calls it one of the year's most polarizing films — critics either applauded its daring or dismissed it as empty provocation, on account of its graphic use of nudity, sexual imagery, and extreme behavior. Critic Tasha Robinson rejects the second reading, arguing that "empty provocation" is an odd insult to levy at a movie from someone who so thoroughly thinks her movies through, and describes Saltburn as visually luscious and emotionally gripping, a real feast for the senses, grimly funny and full of shocks, and a smarter, more insinuating take on the growing eat-the-rich subgenre.
