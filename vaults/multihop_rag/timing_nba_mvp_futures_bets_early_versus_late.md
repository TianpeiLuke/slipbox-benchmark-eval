# Timing An NBA MVP Futures Bet: Early Versus Late

You can bet on the regular-season NBA MVP award well before the season starts, and the Sporting News argues the best time to do so comes down to your tolerance for risk versus reward — there is no single correct answer.

The grounds are informational. The odds are so favorable when the lines for NBA Most Valuable Player are first released because the oddsmakers, like you, have very little information to work with: they are basing their odds on past performance and expected future accomplishments, as well as the quality of the team around the top candidates for the award. The warrant is that price tracks information — while you wait for more, such as a player's performance during the regular season, the lines shift and become less favorable, because the oddsmakers are gathering information and applying new data and analytics to make their lines as accurate as possible.

The conclusion is therefore a choice rather than a rule: as a bettor you must decide which approach works best for your strategy — wager when the lines are released with little information at your disposal but the best potential return, or wait until you have more solid information to craft your MVP speculation.
