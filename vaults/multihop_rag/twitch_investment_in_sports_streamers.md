# Twitch's Investment in Sports Streamers

Twitch seems to see value in investing in sports streamers, Polygon reported on 2023-10-20.

A few years earlier, the platform launched the Sports Accelerator Program to support sports content creators wanting to get into "sports talk."

That covers formats including podcasts, talk shows, reaction streams, sidecasts, and watch-alongs, and the program works through live workshops and by providing opportunities to network and expand viewership.
