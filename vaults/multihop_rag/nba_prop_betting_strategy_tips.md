# Three Strategy Tips For NBA Prop Betting

Every bettor should develop their own strategies, but the Sporting News offers three tips for NBA props: pay attention to player and team news, keep an eye on usage and minutes, and shop for the best lines.

First, pay attention to player and team news. Oddsmakers use various information to develop their lines for games and players, and you should too — keep tabs on player news and injuries, when top players are being rested, how they performed in recent games, and more. Value in player and team props comes from gathering information from multiple sources and tracking how players and their teammates perform; for example, if one of the starting guards is questionable for a game, you might consider the other guard for increased points or assists since they will likely handle a heavier load.

Second, keep an eye on usage and minutes. Even slight increases to a player's average minutes can make a difference when betting player props, especially later in the season when aging veteran players tend to rest more. Stay informed on usage and minutes trends for the players you're considering, and use them to your advantage — some of the best players in the NBA were once on the bench for most games until small increases in playing time gave them a chance to shine.

Third, shop for the best lines, a tip that applies to every bet you make. Using multiple sportsbook accounts lets you find the best odds for every NBA prop bet, and even the smallest differences — one sportsbook at +125 and another at +135 — can make a difference in your profit.
