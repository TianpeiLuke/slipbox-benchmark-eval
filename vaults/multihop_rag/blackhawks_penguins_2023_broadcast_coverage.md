# Blackhawks vs. Penguins 2023: Broadcast Coverage

ESPN carried the Blackhawks-Penguins opening-night game in the United States, with Sportsnet and TVA Sports holding Canadian rights; U.S. streams ran on ESPN+ and Fubo, and Canadian cord-cutters could watch on Sportsnet+.

The listing's fields are TV channel (U.S.) ESPN, TV channel (Canada) Sportsnet and TVA Sports, live stream (U.S.) ESPN+ and Fubo, and live stream (Canada) Sportsnet+. The Pittsburgh-Chicago contest was one of three games the network had on opening night.

The commentary assignment marks the occasion: ESPN's "A-team" was on the call for Bedard's debut, with Sean McDonough on play-by-play, Ray Ferraro as analyst and Emily Kaplan reporting. For viewers without cable, the game could be streamed through ESPN+ or with Fubo, which offers a free trial, while in Canada Sportsnet brought the action to TVs and TVA Sports carried it for the French-speaking audience.
