# Benavidez vs Andrade: Date, Venue, Odds And How To Watch

David Benavidez faced Demetrius Andrade on Saturday, Nov. 25, in a 168-pound clash at the Michelob Ultra Arena at Mandalay Bay in Las Vegas, broadcast on Showtime PPV, as previewed by CBS Sports on 2023-11-25. Andrade, 35, was finally getting his shot at the super elite, and potential recognition among the pound-for-pound best in the sport, against former two-time super middleweight titleholder David Benavidez, who entered at 27-0 with 23 KOs. The framing offered for the matchup is that when two great fighters have been avoided by top opponents for so long, it inevitably makes sense for them to fight one another in order to get to the top — and the preview cautions readers not to let the ambitious hopes of both fighters, and their collective lust for one day sharing the ring with boxing's biggest star, suggest they are overlooking one another.
