# Daniel Sprong OVER 2.5 Shots On Goal

Sporting News' second pick for the Nov. 7, 2023 NHL slate was Daniel Sprong, RW, Red Wings @ Rangers — OVER 2.5 shots on goal. Despite not being featured in the top six, Sprong was holding down the fort on the third line and averaging five shot attempts per game through the first 12, having hit the over in five straight with 18 shots on goal compared with just eight combined for his two linemates.

The reasoning rests on volume and opportunity. Sprong sees some work on the second power play unit but is clearly willing to fire a lot of pucks at the net with his shoot-first mentality on the third line, and with the Rangers still figuring out how to deal with the loss of Adam Fox there should be openings for him to find at least three shots that night.
