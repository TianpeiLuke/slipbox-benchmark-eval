# Jeff Shiffrin's Death In 2020

Jeff Shiffrin sustained a head injury in an accidental fall and died on 2 February 2020, as reported by Essentially Sports on 3 November 2023.

The circumstances given run in sequence. Over the years Jeff had seen his daughter win one downhill, one super-G and place fourth in a second downhill; she was at the pinnacle of her career with her sights set on a fourth consecutive overall title. After spending some much-needed family time together in the resort town of Lake Garda, Italy, following the Bansko races, the family said goodbye and promised to see each other in five weeks. Two days later, while Mikaela was training in Italy, she received a concerned call from her brother Taylor asking to speak urgently with their mom; Taylor told them Jeff had fallen and that they needed to return home immediately. The family spent Jeff's last days together by his side before he passed away.

The report records the aftermath as shattering, leaving a huge void in the family's hearts. Eileen described the pain as gut-wrenching: "We learned quickly that people have their crosses to bear, and they aren't everyone else's crosses; they're the ones we have to bear ourselves. You learn to suffer in silence".
