# Amazon Prime Big Deal Days October 2023 Dates

Amazon's second big sale event of 2023 runs from Tuesday, October 10 through Wednesday, October 11, ending at 2:59 am ET on October 11 (11:59 pm PT).

WIRED described the second and presumably final Amazon Prime Day 2023 as happening right about now: Prime Day deals started flowing at 3 am EST on October 10 and would continue through October 11.
