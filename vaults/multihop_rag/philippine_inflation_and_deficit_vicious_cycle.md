# Philippine Inflation and Deficit Vicious Cycle

The cycle described is mutual reinforcement between deficits and inflation: some economists say that deficit budgets and their remedies — monetary, through borrowings that increase money supply, and fiscal, such as raising taxes — increase inflation, and stubborn inflation further entrenches structural deficits in the system. Business World calls this a vicious cycle, and prefaces it by noting that whomever one believes, inflation "independently says it as it is."

The measurement anchor given is the Philippine target arithmetic at the time. While inflation in the Philippines had been on a downward trend since January, the country needed to achieve a 3.7% average inflation rate for the next five months to hit the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas forecast of 5.5% inflation for 2023, Assistant National Statistician Rachel Lacsa said.
