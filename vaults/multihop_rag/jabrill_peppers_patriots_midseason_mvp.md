# Jabrill Peppers Patriots Midseason MVP

Yardbarker's Nov. 9, 2023 AFC midseason team MVP list named safety Jabrill Peppers the New England Patriots' MVP: there had not been much to get excited about in New England, but the versatile defensive back was a bright spot and a respected voice in the locker room.

The grounds are his production line — 54 tackles, three for loss, one interception, one forced fumble and a team-leading six passes defended. The warrant is stated as a forward-looking judgment: for a team facing as many uncertainties as the Patriots were, Peppers was a solid piece to build around in the short term. The entry was written by Colum Dell.
