# Cards And Officiating Controversy In The 2023 Rugby World Cup Final

The 2023 Rugby World Cup final was a match of controversy in which, as The Roar put it, cards of yellow and red were handed out quicker than a game of Uno, with thundering shots both legal and illegal put on by rugby's two greatest sides.

The decisive card fell on the All Blacks captain. Sam Cane was forced to watch the match from the sidelines for 53 minutes after his yellow card for a high shot on Jesse Kriel was upgraded to red. The report describes the incident as the biggest error of the match: Kriel stepped back into the defence and Cane failed to drop his body height, clocking the centre in the jaw. Cane did not return.

South Africa also lost players to the bin. Siya Kolisi, Cane's opposite in the number seven jersey, was later shown a yellow card for a high shot on Ardie Savea but returned. Cheslin Kolbe — the winger who had sealed South Africa's World Cup triumph four years earlier against England — could not bear to watch from the sidelines after being shown a yellow card for a deliberate knockdown in the 73rd minute.
