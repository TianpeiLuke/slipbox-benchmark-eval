# Mysterious Canine Respiratory Illness Of 2023

The mysterious canine respiratory illness of 2023 is an unexplained respiratory disease that may have sickened scores of dogs across the country and that researchers say could be caused by a new type of bacterial infection that may be very good at evading the canine immune system. Some dogs have died from the illness.

Its clinical opening is distinctive: the illness starts with a cough that can last for weeks, along with runny eyes and sneezing. That weeks-long cough is the feature owners and veterinarians encounter first.

The boundaries of the category are unusually loose, and that looseness is part of the definition. Scientists are not even sure yet whether the same bug is making dogs sick across the nation, so "the illness" may not be a single disease entity at all. Many researchers have wondered whether the pathogen is bacterial or viral. The one thing veterinarians do know is that the germ is something they do not recognize — the illness is defined, for now, by the absence of a familiar cause rather than by the presence of an identified one.
