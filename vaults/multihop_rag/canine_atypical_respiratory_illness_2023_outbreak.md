# Canine Atypical Respiratory Illness of 2023

The mysterious respiratory illness that may have sickened scores of dogs across the country could be caused by a new type of bacterial infection that may be very good at evading the canine immune system, according to researchers. Some dogs have died from the illness.

The clinical picture that distinguishes it is a cough that can last for weeks, along with runny eyes and sneezing. The illness is described as starting with that cough.

The boundaries of the category are unsettled in two important ways. Scientists are not even sure yet whether the same bug is making dogs sick across the nation, so "the illness" may not be a single disease entity at all. Many researchers have wondered whether the responsible pathogen is bacterial or viral. The one thing veterinarians do know is that the germ is something they do not recognize.
