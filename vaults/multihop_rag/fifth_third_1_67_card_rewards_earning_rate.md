# The Fifth Third 1.67% Card's Rewards Earning Rate

The Fifth Third 1.67% Card offers a unique fixed return rate for all purchases: 1.67%, uncapped. The odd-looking number ties in with the bank's "Fifth Third" motif — 5/3 = 1.67. This is an above-average return for a no-annual-fee cash back credit card with a flat earning rate; more common are offers between 1% and 1.5%.

Technically the card earns "Rewards Points," redeemable at a rate of 1 cent per point, and point earnings on each purchase are rounded up or down to the nearest whole number — spend $6 and you earn 10.02 points, which rounds to 10 points. Rewards can be redeemed as a direct deposit into a Fifth Third checking or savings account, or as a statement credit applied to the card. The card does not currently offer a welcome bonus to new members.
