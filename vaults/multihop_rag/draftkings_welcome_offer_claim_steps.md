# DraftKings Welcome Offer Claim Steps

Claiming DraftKings' $1,200 in bonus bets, as laid out by Sporting News on Nov. 29, 2023 for Tennessee vs. North Carolina odds, takes four steps: click the exclusive DraftKings promo code link; register as a new user by supplying personal information including name, email, the last four digits of your SSN and mailing address; deposit $5 to activate the $50 bonus bet; then bet $5 and get $150 in bonus bets.

The offer being claimed bundles three components for new users — a 20% deposit match up to $1,000, a $50 bonus bet with a $5 deposit, and a Bet $5, Get $150 in Bonus Bets promo — while all customers also receive a "No Sweat SGP" daily.

The deposit-match component carries its own preconditions: to max out DraftKings' deposit match at $1,000, a user must make a minimum deposit of $5,000 and meet the 25X playthrough requirement.
