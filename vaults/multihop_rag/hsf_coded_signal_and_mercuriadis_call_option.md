# The Announcement As A Coded Signal About Mercuriadis' Call Option

MBW argues that it is a fair bet that the HSF board, which the previous week appointed activist investor Christopher Mills as a non-executive Director, is making something of a hidden statement to shareholders with the 19 December announcement. Specifically, it may be a coded attempt by the board to gnash teeth towards the call option held by HSM and Merck Mercuriadis, which, as MBW has pointed out before, leaves the executive with the exclusive power to sweep in and acquire HSF's portfolio under several different circumstances.

The grounds are textual clues in the statement itself, including what the article calls its rare and conspicuous mention of HSM being "majority-owned by funds managed and/or advised by Blackstone". The warrant is that a board would not otherwise volunteer that ownership detail in a statement about a valuation, so its inclusion signals the Blackstone-linked acquisition route as the board's real concern. The claim is offered as an inference about intent, hedged as a fair bet and a possible coded attempt, not as a stated position of the board.
