# Prime Day 2023 Soundbar And TV Lighting Deals

WIRED's home-theater picks are Nanoleaf's 4D Kit, the JBL Bar 1300X, Yamaha's SR-C20A, the Roku Streambar, a large dual-subwoofer system from Japan, a Sennheiser luxury soundbar, and a Yamaha soundbar with Alexa built in.

Nanoleaf's 4D Kit (9/10, WIRED Recommends) can turn any movie or video game into a beautiful, immersive experience, coming with a trimmable light strip that fits TVs as large as 65 inches and a 4D camera that captures the screen's colors and projects them onto the wall behind the TV; the larger size is on sale too, for TVs between 65 and 85 inches. The JBL Bar 1300X (8/10, WIRED Recommends) has detachable wireless speakers you can pop off the sides of the main bar and place anywhere without worrying about power outlets or hiding cables, plus some of the best Dolby Atmos immersion WIRED has heard at this price — the only downside being that the speakers need charging between uses. Yamaha's SR-C20A is WIRED's favorite affordable soundbar, cheap enough to tack onto even a very modest TV budget, with 100-watt forward-facing drivers that easily outperform most TV speakers. WIRED is a fan of nearly everything Roku offers, including the Streambar, which upgrades sound and streaming in one device and is small enough for homes where space is at a premium while still sounding good. Reviewer Jaina Grey has spent a few years with the very large dual-subwoofer system: two huge subwoofers deliver earth-shattering bass while side and rear speakers give more lifelike reproduction than soundbars that bounce sound off walls, with support for all major object-based surround formats like DTS:X and Dolby Atmos — great for large spaces where the extra bass feels more cinematic, and at the lowest price tracked so far. For the luxury-minded, Sennheiser's extravagant soundbar is WIRED's top pick, with massive speakers and some of the best audio quality of any soundbar tested; it's also one of the most expensive recommended, and its price very rarely dips this low. Yamaha's Alexa-equipped soundbar cuts down on separate smart speakers by controlling a host of other smart home gadgets; its price has fluctuated a fair amount recently, sometimes as low as $180, but this is still nearly the cheapest ever seen.
