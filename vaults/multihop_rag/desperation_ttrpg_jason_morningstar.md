# Desperation

Desperation, by Jason Morningstar, is a card-driven TTRPG built on one mechanic that game designer and academic Tim Hutchings calls marvelous: players draw a card carrying a bit of spoken text and then assign those words to one of the characters in play.

Hutchings, who names Desperation and Susanne Vejdemo's Orestes' Choice as a tie for the best game he played in 2023, argues the mechanic is so smart that it makes for rich yet tight storytelling, since the cards force new contexts for the actions of the characters. The game ships with two dark American Gothic-flavored scenarios: a starving Kansas town locked in a brutal winter, and a cursed fishing boat lost in the north Pacific. He adds that the faux woodcuts by Jabari Weathers and Brennen Reece are wonderful to look upon and convey the game's tone as much as the flavorful card text.
