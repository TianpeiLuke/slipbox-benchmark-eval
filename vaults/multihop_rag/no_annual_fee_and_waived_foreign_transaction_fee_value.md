# The Value Of No Annual Fee And Waived Foreign Transaction Fees

The Fifth Third 1.67% Card waives foreign transaction fees, where many credit cards tack on up to 3% of total overseas spending — which makes it a good companion for international trips. That is the first of two fee-side arguments for holding the card.

The second concerns what happens after the card stops being your main one. It doesn't charge an annual fee, which means it can continue to benefit your credit profile even if your credit card strategy evolves away from using it regularly. A big factor in your credit score is "length of credit history," determined by the average age of your accounts, so by keeping the card open your length of credit history will grow even if you don't use it — though you should swipe it once every six months to avoid account closure due to inactivity.
