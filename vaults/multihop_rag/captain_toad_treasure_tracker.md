# Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker

Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker is a Wii U game whose Switch port Polygon calls full of charm, included in its 2023-10-20 list of the 35 best couch co-op games for Nintendo Switch.

You play together in local co-op as Captain Toad and Toadette, exploring and solving your way through a series of bite-size puzzles.

Each self-contained course has little nooks and crannies to pop in and out of, and Koopas to navigate around.
