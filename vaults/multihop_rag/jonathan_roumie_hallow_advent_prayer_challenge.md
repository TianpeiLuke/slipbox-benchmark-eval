# Jonathan Roumie and the Hallow Advent Prayer Challenge

Jonathan Roumie — perhaps best known for his role as Jesus Christ in "The Chosen" — paired up with Hallow, the No. 1 prayer app in the world, for a special Advent prayer challenge for the 2023 season of preparation for Christmas. The 25-day prayer challenge began on Monday 4 December 2023, and as part of the effort both he and actor Liam Neeson guide people through a variety of prayers, meditations, Bible readings and more.

In a phone interview with Fox News Digital on that Monday, the Los Angeles-based actor spoke of his Christian faith, his prayer life and his hope that others may find peace and serenity during the Advent season of 2023 — because "any opportunity to connect to Jesus is a welcome one," he said.

The involvement is not merely a professional engagement for Roumie. He is a committed Christian and Catholic, with his own personal story of growing closer to God and continuing to do so each day, and he presents the Hallow app and especially this Advent challenge as a beautiful way to kickstart that, especially for a person who has not ever tried praying consistently before.
