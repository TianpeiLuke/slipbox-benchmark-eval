# Apple's Generative AI Investment Stance

Apple CEO Tim Cook pushed back against the notion that the company was behind in AI on Apple's Q4 earnings call with investors, highlighting technology developments Apple had made recently that "would not be possible without AI."

Cook also said that Apple was working on generative AI technologies.

TechCrunch reads that statement as corroboration for a specific spending figure: it lends credence to reports suggesting the company is on track to spend $1 billion per year on developing generative AI products.
