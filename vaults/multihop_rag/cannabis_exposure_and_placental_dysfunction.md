# Cannabis Exposure and Placental Dysfunction

The poor pregnancy outcomes studied have been tied to dysfunction of the placenta, the organ that supplies a growing fetus with nutrients and oxygen while removing waste from the womb — which is the proposed route from cannabis exposure to those outcomes.

The supporting relations come from two levels of evidence. Research in monkeys has suggested that cannabis exposure can alter gene activity in the placenta, and it is also linked to reduced oxygen and blood flow through the organ. In humans, cannabis exposure has been linked to slower-than-typical growth of the fetus, which can be related to problems with the placenta.
