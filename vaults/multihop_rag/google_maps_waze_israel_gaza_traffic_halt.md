# Google Maps And Waze Halt Israel-Gaza Traffic Data

Google Maps and Waze stopped live traffic updates in Israel and the Gaza Strip at the request of the Israel Defense Forces, TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-30. A Google spokesperson said the ability to see live traffic conditions and business information was halted temporarily out of "consideration for the safety of local communities."

There is precedent: Google did something similar in 2022 amid the Russian invasion of Ukraine, because the apps were being used to track military movements.
