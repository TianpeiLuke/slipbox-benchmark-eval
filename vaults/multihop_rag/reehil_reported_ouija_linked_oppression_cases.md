# Reehil's Reported Ouija-Linked Oppression Cases

Fr. Dan Reehil told Fox News Digital that he has personally seen six cases of demonic oppression that stemmed from Ouija board usage.

"The victims were left with night terrors, suicidal ideation, despair — and one man was impaled with chicken bones in his leg that flew across the kitchen," he said.

He also reported the outcome of intervention in those cases: "Once the deliverance prayers were prayed, and the participants renounced all participation with the demons, the demonic activity ceased."
