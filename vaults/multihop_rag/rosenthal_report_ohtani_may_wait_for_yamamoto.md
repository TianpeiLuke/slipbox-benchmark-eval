# Rosenthal's Report That Ohtani May Wait For Yamamoto

Ken Rosenthal of The Athletic reported on Nov. 20, 2023, at the bottom of a piece about what an Ohtani contract might look like — incentives, opt-outs and all sorts of stuff — that Shohei Ohtani might be better off waiting for Yoshinobu Yamamoto to sign before making his own decision.

Rosenthal's reasoning was that an agreement at the winter meetings in Nashville the first week of December would be the ideal outcome for MLB but not necessarily for Ohtani. Yamamoto, the Japanese right-hander, was expected to be posted for free agency as soon as that Monday, triggering a 45-day window for teams to negotiate with him. Losing out on the 25-year-old Yamamoto, who could command more than $200 million, might put certain teams on tilt, improving the landscape for Ohtani. Sporting News' comment was simply: "Interesting, certainly."
