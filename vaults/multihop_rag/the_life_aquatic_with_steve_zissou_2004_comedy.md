# The Life Aquatic With Steve Zissou (2004): Wes Anderson's Ensemble Comedy

The Life Aquatic with Steve Zissou is Wes Anderson's 2004 eccentric ensemble comedy, running 1h 58m, starring Bill Murray, Owen Wilson and Cate Blanchett. It is dedicated to Jacques Cousteau and is a loving, hilarious homage to the legendary French oceanographer. Murray plays Steve Zissou, an oceanographer and documentarian who loses his best friend to a shark attack while working on a project and sets out on his next one: to find and kill the shark, and film the whole thing.

Polygon lists the ensemble's roles as part of the appeal: Anjelica Huston as Zissou's estranged wife who finances his projects, Willem Dafoe as an emotionally insecure German first mate, Owen Wilson as a Zissou super-fan who believes he is Zissou's son, and Jeff Goldblum as Zissou's rival, a more successful oceanographer. With a soundtrack of Portuguese David Bowie covers by Brazilian singer-songwriter Seu Jorge and Anderson's typical attention to detail in composition, the reviewer calls it a cinematic feast of the senses. It is available free with a library card on Hoopla, or for digital rental or purchase at Amazon, Apple and Google Play.
