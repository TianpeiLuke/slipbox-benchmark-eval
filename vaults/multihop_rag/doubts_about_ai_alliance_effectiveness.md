# Doubts About The AI Alliance's Effectiveness

TechCrunch's counter to the AI Alliance's promise attacks the premise that a broad coalition can act at all: without the participation of so many major AI industry players — and lacking deadlines or even concrete objectives — can the AI Alliance succeed, and what would success even look like?

Two further grounds are offered. First, the vast number of competing interests, from healthcare networks such as the Cleveland Clinic to insurance providers such as Roadzen, will not make it easy for the Alliance's members to coalesce around a single, united front. Second, for all their talk of openness, IBM and Meta are not exactly the poster children for the future the Alliance's release depicts, which casts doubt on their sincerity.

The conclusion is held open rather than asserted: perhaps the writer is wrong and the AI Alliance will be a smash success, or perhaps it will crumble under mistrust and its own bureaucracy — time will tell.
