# The Badge System In Super Mario Bros. Wonder

The badge system is Super Mario Bros. Wonder's mechanism for optional special abilities: each unlockable upgrade or special move is assigned to a badge that can be found or purchased, and one collected badge can be selected before each stage. The abilities range from helpful (a high, floating leap) to playful (a Piranha Plant grappling hook) to silly (a triple jump tied to a musical rhythm), and Polygon's reviewer describes rotating them by need — opting for the Safety Bounce badge once later stages added instant-death bottomless pits, so that plunging into an abyss has the darkness spit you back up "like a piece of hamburger caught in its throat." The badges are worn by Prince Florian, a little royal bug who joins the journey and provides most of the conversation on the road trip, alongside very talkative flowers sprouting across every corner of the Flower Kingdom, eager to offer a tip for a metatextual wink.

Badges sit inside a wider design of considerate rather than patronising assistance: Wonder Seeds can add a splash of difficulty, but help is perpetually available — time limits are gone and extra lives can be purchased cheaply with the game's abundant purple currency. The balance question the system raises is answered by escalation: the reviewer expects many players to brag that their favourite badge is "the overpowered one," which is sort of always true, since whichever you use you will feel you have an unfair advantage as you float over chunks of the stage or vacuum coins through walls — so the designers layered on new challenges to keep Wonder inside the Goldilocks window, not too hard and not too easy.
