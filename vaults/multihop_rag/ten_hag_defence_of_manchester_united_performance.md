# Ten Hag's Defence Of Manchester United's Performance

Erik ten Hag tried to put a positive spin on the defeat to West Ham on 23 December 2023 by claiming to the BBC that his side were the better team before the first goal: the claim is that the loss came from a single lapse rather than a systemic failure, and the warrant is that United created the better chances and had recently matched the top of the league.

His grounds for the first part are the shape of the match: "I think it was a decent 72 minutes where we didn't take our chances. We should've gone in the lead but we didn't and then it was one moment of switching off."

His grounds for the second part are the previous fixture, which he uses to set the standard he says the team must reproduce: "At this moment we are not top, but we have seen last week against the top of this league we were head-to-head. We didn't score, we had the best opportunities of the game." He added: "There we defended very well for 90 minutes, that has to be always our standard like what we did there. You have to bring that to every game on the pitch."
