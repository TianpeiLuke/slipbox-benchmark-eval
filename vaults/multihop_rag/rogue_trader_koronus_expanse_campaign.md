# Rogue Trader: The Koronus Expanse Campaign

After the events of the tutorial, Polygon's reviewer reports that their beautiful voidship is run down and their staff is struggling, so the newly anointed Rogue Trader must trek around the various planets of the Koronus Expanse to get a new Navigator, fix the ship up, and avoid any major diplomatic incidents with the locals.

The scale of the role is part of the point: the voidship is the size of a modern city, making the player as much a governor as a captain, responsible for managing the ship, its cargo, and its many occupants. In the process of getting back on their feet, the reviewer uncovers a nefarious cult and a deep conspiracy. It is not an easy job, but they are blessed with a handful of companions from the Imperium who can be called on to unlock doors or perform other environmental checks, and who come most in handy in combat.
