# Super Mario Bros. Wonder

Super Mario Bros. Wonder, developed by Nintendo EPD for Nintendo Switch, is ranked 4 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31 — the first side-scrolling 2D platformer in the Super Mario Bros. line since New Super Mario Bros. U in 2012, and both a faithful rendition of the classic format and a complete reinvention of the series' irresistible formula.

When you activate the game's delightful whimsy via the aptly named Wonder Flower, it becomes more than a 2D platformer: a musical, a quiz show, a race, or a hidden object game, where pipes become inchworms, Piranha Plants burst into song, and Yoshi becomes a dragon. Critic N. Carpenter writes that each and every level finds some new way to delight and surprise, constantly introducing new ideas, enemies, and wrinkles only to pull back on them just when it is on the verge of becoming rote — like everything else in the Flower Kingdom, a few enemies shift and change in bizarre ways when the Wonder Flower is activated, such as Hoppo, the bouncy, round hippo equivalent that grows in size to create chaos. All of this weirdness, combined with Nintendo's platforming expertise, makes it an entrancing romp alone, with friends, or with strangers online, and the freshest Mario has felt in decades.

Polygon's 2023-10-20 couch co-op roundup adds the local multiplayer detail: Wonder brings together platforming shenanigans, silly power-ups, and lots of map secrets, and the best part is that four people can play local multiplayer, picking from classic characters including Mario, Luigi, Princess Peach, Princess Daisy, Toad, and Toadette.
