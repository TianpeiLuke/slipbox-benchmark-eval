# Resident Evil 4 Remake

Capcom's Resident Evil 4 Remake, for PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, Windows PC, and Xbox Series X, is ranked 11 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31. The claim Polygon makes is simple: it turns out Capcom is good at remaking games.

The track record supports it. The original Resident Evil remake all but set the bar for the format in 2002 with sleeker controls, more nuanced graphical details, and whole new areas to explore in the iconic Spencer Mansion; the Resident Evil 2 remake changed the entire perspective of its source material without sacrificing the focus on horror and survival; and Resident Evil 3's remake, as forgettable as it was, still brought the design conceits of the original, warts and all, to a modern audience.

Critic M. Mahardy writes that in this reimagined version of the 2005 action-survival-horror game, Capcom has erased many of the blemishes on one of the most beloved games in the series, if not all time. The remake is full of new flourishes and extra details in each of its three sprawling areas, making it less of a remake and more of a dramatic reinterpretation, and it adds even more survival elements to the original's action-centric combat without sacrificing the camp and cheese that have made it enduring. A lesser game would have shrunk in the face of such intimidating source material, but this one achieved the balancing act in spades.
