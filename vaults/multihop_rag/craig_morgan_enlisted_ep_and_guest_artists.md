# Craig Morgan's Enlisted EP And Its Guest Artists

Craig Morgan's EP "Enlisted" was set to debut on 20 October 2023, a six-song project featuring a handful of his "famous friends" — Trace Adkins, Luke Combs, Gary LeVox, Jelly Roll, Blake Shelton and Lainey Wilson — Fox News Digital reported on 16 October 2023.

The EP contains renditions of some of his biggest hits as well as two new songs. Morgan told Fox News Digital he initially did not want to re-record his 2005 "Redneck Yacht Club", but his new song "Raise the Bar" with Billy Dean inspired him: "In the process of doing the demo to pitch to Luke Combs, I sang it and I thought, 'Man, I'm not sure I want to pitch this to somebody else,'" he said. "Then I thought, 'Well, maybe I'll pitch to look and see if he would want to do it with me.'"

That idea, Morgan said, inspired him not only to re-record some of his biggest hits but to bring in new artists to give his older songs a fresh twist.
