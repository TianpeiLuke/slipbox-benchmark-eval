# Google Nest Hub Smart Display Deal

For those partial to the Google Assistant, Google's Nest Hub was on sale for $50 at Target and Walmart for Black Friday 2023 — $10 higher than its all-time low but still $50 off Google's list price.

The Nest Hub is the top overall pick in Engadget's smart display buying guide and earned a review score of 89 back in 2021. It has a 7-inch screen, so it's a bit bigger than the Echo Show 5 but should still be compact enough for a bedroom or small office; it lacks a built-in camera, which may be a selling point for those especially sensitive to their privacy, though no smart display is truly privacy-conscious. Whether a Nest Hub or Echo Show is worth getting depends on which services you use: if you own a bunch of Nest devices and depend on apps like Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Photos, Google's display will make sense — and for a larger model with a webcam and stronger speakers, the 10-inch Nest Hub Max was also on sale for $129.
