# Flipboard's Social Magazine Platform

The Flipboard app's primary purpose has been to curate into social "magazines" news and information found on the web, including links to articles, photos and other social posts. Because of that dependence on web and social sources, it had relied on Twitter as one of its sources of information — which changed in 2023, when Flipboard shifted its Twitter integration over to Mastodon and another alternative social app, Bluesky, set up its own Mastodon server, and began curating news across the fediverse via editorial "desks" focused on improving the discovery of news on Mastodon.

As of December 2023, Flipboard has over 10,000 publishers of social magazines on its app and over a quarter million individuals curating content using the app. Given that Mastodon then had around 1.5 million monthly active users, this could be a notable bump for the fediverse when Flipboard's integration fully rolls out, assuming Flipboard's users do not opt out.

Despite all the changes, Flipboard is not in need of raising funds to support its new developments: it is running off the profits of its own business as it moves in this direction.
