# Week 14 Tight End Injury Report

Beyond Dallas Goedert (forearm) and Dalton Schultz (hamstring), the tight end injuries to monitor for Week 14 of the 2023 season were Darren Waller (hamstring) and Tyler Higbee (neck).

Waller could be back that week, though the Giants really had no reason to bring back their star tight end if they did not feel he was 100 percent; it was something to monitor throughout the week, as Waller (vs. Packers) always has a high ceiling as the Giants' de facto No. 1 receiver. Higbee had been banged up the previous week and was no sure thing to suit up against Baltimore; even if he did, he was not a recommended play against the Ravens' top-10 tight end defense.
