# Gaza's Internet Blackout And Connectivity

Internet connectivity in Gaza took a severe hit as the conflict between Israel and Hamas continued: Palestinian internet service provider NetStream reportedly collapsed due to a severe shortage of fuel supplies, while other providers such as Paltel and Mada Al Arab also faced serious connectivity issues. Although the situation remained precarious as of November 3, 2023, connectivity eventually was partially restored, and there are indications that the U.S. government is putting pressure on Israel to restore internet connectivity in Gaza. TechCrunch frames the stakes: when you think "essential services" you might think water, electricity and hospitals, but internet connectivity is increasingly important as the infrastructure that ties together the fabric of society.

The blackout was visible on Snap Map in real time. Some are concerned that internet access in Gaza, partially restored after Israel imposed a communications blackout, will be cut again. TikTok creator divergentredhed posted that she watched the blackout happen in real time on Snapchat: "Right there in Gaza it was like a hotspot of Snapchat Stories. It was yellow and green and red … all of it just went dark. The entire Gaza Strip went dark before my very eyes," she said. "I'm just going to spend the next few days uploading all of the content I have from Snapchat, from the people themselves."
