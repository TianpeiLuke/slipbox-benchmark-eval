# The Newell's Old Boys Friendly And Messi's Rosario Roots

After their Asia trip, Inter Miami will host a friendly at DRV PNK Stadium against Newell's Old Boys — a team from Argentina that holds a special place in the hearts of both Lionel Messi and coach Gerardo Martino.

Both men are from the city of Rosario, where Newell's are based. Messi developed at the club's youth academy, and Martino played more than 400 senior games for the club before becoming head coach in 2012. Messi signed for Barcelona after spending six years as a youth player at Newell's, during which time he reportedly scored 234 goals.

"I am delighted to welcome my beloved Newell's to our home here in Miami. It will be a special match due to everything Newell's Old Boys means to me," said the Inter Miami coach. "It will also be a good opportunity to prepare for what will surely be an exciting season."
