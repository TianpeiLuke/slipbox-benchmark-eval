# Dolphins +110 vs Chiefs In Germany

Sporting News took the Dolphins at +110 against the Chiefs in the Germany game in Week 9 of 2023, predicting Dolphins 26, Chiefs 24. It conceded the objections up front — Miami had yet to beat a true contender that season and Kansas City were the reigning champions — but countered that the Chiefs had just had their doors blown off by Russell Wilson and the lowly Broncos in a game in which Patrick Mahomes and company suffered five turnovers and three sacks.

Preparation was a central ground. The column expected a statement win from Mike McDaniel's team on neutral ground, noting the Dolphins had been in Frankfurt since early that week, having flown overnight on Monday to give themselves multiple days to acclimate to the time difference and surroundings, while the Chiefs waited until after Thursday's practice to take the 11-hour trip.

Two individual angles complete the case. It was a revenge game for former Chiefs receiver Tyreek Hill, on pace for multiple single-season receiving records and poised for his first Offensive Player of the Year award. And Raheem Mostert, who led the NFL with 10 touchdowns and ranked second behind only Breece Hall in yards per carry (5.4), should be licking his chops against a Kansas City defense that had surrendered 135.6 rushing yards per game over the previous three weeks.

Other Week 9 moneylines the column liked were Vikings (+170) at Falcons, Bengals (-130) vs. Bills and Cowboys (+140) at Eagles.
