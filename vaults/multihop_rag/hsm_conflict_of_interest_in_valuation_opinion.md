# HSM's Conflict Of Interest In Opining On The Valuation

Christopher Brown expressed surprise that HSF asked Hipgnosis Song Management to opine on the valuation given HSM's conflict of interest, and MBW spells out what that conflict consists of. If HSM had turned around and said that HSF's valuation is 25% too high and should be brought down, then it would produce a new valuation for HSF that may allow Blackstone, or the Blackstone-controlled Hipgnosis Song Capital, to launch an acquisition bid for the HSF portfolio, or at least part of it.

The warrant is that the party asked to judge the value is also a party that stands to buy at that value, so a lower opinion serves the adviser's own side of a prospective trade. The article adds the price implication: the likely price for this hypothetical Blackstone bid would be smaller than the failed $440 million, or $418.5 million post-costs, bid that Hipgnosis Songs Capital, via HSM, made for a chunk of the HSF portfolio earlier that year.
