# Anker Soundcore Space A40 Earbuds Deal Prices

The Anker Soundcore Space A40 was $54 at both October Prime Day and Black Friday 2023 — matching its all-time low — and is the top pick in Engadget's guide to the best budget wireless earbuds.

Engadget's 10 October 2023 Prime Day roundups said $54 matches its all-time low and comes in $25 less than usual, and that the Space A40 tops the budget guide because it includes features that belie its price: adaptive ANC, multi-device pairing, wireless charging, a transparency mode and up to 10 hours of battery life. Its audio quality and ANC are not quite as powerful as more expensive pairs but are more than effective enough for the money, and unlike many budget wireless headphones its default sound does not completely blow out the bass. The headphone-specific list quoted it as "$54 $100 Save $46".

For Black Friday, Engadget reported on 19 November 2023 that it was down to $54, matching the lowest price seen, describing it as comfortable, supporting wireless charging and multi-device pairing, lasting up to 10 hours a charge, with ANC "remarkably effective for a sub-$100 pair" and bassy sound customisable through a clean companion app. Its 24 November 2023 piece called it one of the best values in wireless earphones at its normal price of $79, now $54, tying the lowest price tracked, and said it delivers most of the features expected from headphones costing twice as much. Across all four write-ups the same two downsides recur: middling call/mic quality and a lack of wear detection, so it will not auto-pause when you remove an earbud.
