# 2024 Mock Draft: Six Defensive Backs Projected In Round 1

CBS Sports' 2024 NFL Mock Draft of 2023-11-17 projects five cornerbacks and one safety in the first round. At Pick 14, the L.A. Chargers take Kool-Aid McKinstry, CB, Alabama, a junior at 6'1" and 195 lbs, ranked 9th prospect and 2nd at the position: J.C. Jackson never became what the Chargers had hoped when they signed him, his spot in the lineup has not been filled with a long-term candidate, and McKinstry is good value midway through the first round. At Pick 16, Las Vegas takes Nate Wiggins, CB, Clemson, a junior at 6'2" and 185 lbs, 19th prospect and 3rd at the position — the thought of Las Vegas taking another cornerback in the first round has to be terrifying for Raiders fans, but it remains a position of need for the AFC franchise despite adding Patriots castoff Jack Jones that week. At Pick 17, Buffalo takes Cooper DeJean, CB, Iowa, a junior at 6'1" and 207 lbs, 38th prospect and 7th at the position; it was learned on the Wednesday that DeJean's season, and potentially his Iowa career, is over, and Buffalo adds him to fill out its secondary.

At Pick 25, Miami takes Denzel Burke, CB, Ohio State, a junior at 6'1" and 190 lbs, 36th prospect and 6th at the position, because injuries have plagued Miami's cornerback room. At Pick 26, Seattle takes Tyler Nubin, S, Minnesota, a senior at 6'2" and 210 lbs, 44th prospect and 2nd at the position, adding a ballhawk who can help capitalize on the chaos created by a surprisingly active defensive front over the past month — the Seahawks having already invested in the unit with Riq Woolen and Devon Witherspoon in back-to-back years. At Pick 29, Baltimore takes Kalen King, CB, Penn State, a junior at 5'11" and 190 lbs, 8th prospect and 1st at the position, because Marlon Humphrey and the entire Baltimore secondary have struggled to stay healthy.
