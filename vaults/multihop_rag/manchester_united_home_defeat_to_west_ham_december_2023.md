# Manchester United's Defeat To West Ham In December 2023

Goals from West Ham's Jarrod Bowen and Mohammed Kudus beat Manchester United on 23 December 2023, extending United's miserable run in the English Premier League, as reported by The Roar that day.

Bowen bundled home at the second attempt in the 72nd minute for his 11th league goal of the season, breaking the deadlock, and Kudus struck his fourth goal in as many games to settle matters, finishing after a defensive howler from Manchester United — a goal Kobbie Mainoo would not want to see back, and one created by an assist from Lucas Paquetá.

The result is recorded as another worrying setback for manager Erik ten Hag: United had now suffered a 13th defeat in 26 games in all competitions.
