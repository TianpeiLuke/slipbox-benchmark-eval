# Time-Restricted Eating Versus Calorie-Restricted Fasting

Desplats argues that the UC San Diego approach has a practical advantage over fasting regimens that cut calories: it may be more sustainable and safer for older people, whose metabolisms are already impacted by aging changes.

Desplats, lead author of the UC San Diego study, told Fox News Digital that Longo's studies are on a similar track, demonstrating the connection between metabolism, fasting and brain health.

The distinction she draws is about what the intervention requires of the participant: "The main difference is that we tested an approach that does not require calorie reduction, which may be more sustainable and safer for older people whose metabolisms are already impacted by aging changes," she said.
