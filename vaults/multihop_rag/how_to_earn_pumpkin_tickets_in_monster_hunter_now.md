# How to Earn Pumpkin Tickets in Monster Hunter Now

Collecting Pumpkin Tickets is how players source the event-exclusive Jack-o'-Head armor piece in Monster Hunter Now's Halloween event, as Polygon set out on 2023-10-25.

There are two ways to get them. The first is completing Pumpkin Hunt Quests in the Special tab during the Halloween event — 18 tickets in total, one per quest in the first step and five per quest in the second. The second is defeating pumpkin rock Kulu-Ya-Ku, available from Saturday, Oct. 28, at 9 a.m. local time until the end of the event.

The timing consequence is that until Saturday morning players are capped in how many Pumpkin Tickets they can earn, until pumpkin rock Kulu-Ya-Ku makes its debut. Both sources are available only to players who have reached Hunter Rank 11 and above.
