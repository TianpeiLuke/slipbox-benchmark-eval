# Hi-Fi Rush

Hi-Fi Rush, developed by Tango Gameworks for Windows PC and Xbox Series X, is ranked 30 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31. You play as Chai, a guy who must escape the factory of a villainous corporation; in a workplace accident Chai's iPod gets punched into his chest, making him sensitive to sound and staying on beat, and what follows is a joyful action-rhythm game where you explore, climb, and fight to the beat of the music.

Critic N. Clark calls it a remarkably inviting take on a genre not exactly known for being accessible.

The accessibility is mechanical: the game does away with hardcore precision in exchange for gameplay that always subtly nudges you back toward keeping in time. Failing to attack on a drum stroke does not mean failing a fight, you just do not get a combo multiplier, and the musical score never gets jarring as a punishment — punchy notes always play in time with the rhythm even if you hit a button at the wrong time. The result is a game that evokes the thrill of feeling like you are acing it and getting into a flow, no matter your skill level.
