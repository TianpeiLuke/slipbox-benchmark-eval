# Player Reactions To The Bears-Browns Hail Mary Finish

Players on both sides described the suspended moment as the Hail Mary deflected from Bear to Brown, with Cleveland tight end David Njoku telling The Athletic's Zac Jackson, "I damn near s— myself, dog," per Sporting News on Dec. 17, 2023.

Darnell Mooney, who nearly made the catch, said afterward: "It would've been crazy. I mean, how many times you see a Hail Mary work and actually fall into, being able to make the catch? It's tough. I wish I could've had it, but I mean it is what it is." Justin Fields said that as the ball began to fall into Mooney's hands the Chicago sideline began to erupt before realizing the ball would not be ending in Bears' hands; the Bears' sideline appeared convinced it had won.

Fields' counterpart Joe Flacco could only watch on the jumbotron as the play unfolded, having just driven the Browns to the go-ahead score the drive before. In his 16 years in the NFL he had seen a lot, and he knew to hold off on any celebration until the game was official: "When you watch and or are a part of all the games that go on every week, you know not to get excited until that clock hits zero. There's so many things that can happen, whether we've been a part of it or whether like I said you've seen it on Sundays just watching TV so believe me I was over there and guys want to come up and congratulate and things like that and I'm just sitting there waiting for that clock to hit zero." Browns edge rusher Myles Garrett was on the field as one of the few players at the line of scrimmage, focusing more on pressuring Fields than picking him off, and once Fields got the throw off he too became a spectator: "Shoot, too good of a view. He kinda looked at me just made sure I was far enough and then tossed it up and I was like, 'Please, Lord, don't let us be on one of these YouTube compilations.'"
