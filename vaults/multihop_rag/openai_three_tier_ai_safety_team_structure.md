# OpenAI's Three-Tier AI Safety Team Structure

OpenAI divides safety work across three teams distinguished by how far along a model is. In-production models are governed by a "safety systems" team, which handles, for example, systematic abuses of ChatGPT that can be mitigated with API restrictions or tuning. Frontier models in development get the "preparedness" team, which tries to identify and quantify risks before the model is released. Finally there is the "superalignment" team, which is working on theoretical guide rails for "superintelligent" models — which, as TechCrunch notes, we may or may not be anywhere near.

The discriminating feature between the tiers is thus the model's lifecycle stage: shipped, in development, or hypothetical future superintelligence.
