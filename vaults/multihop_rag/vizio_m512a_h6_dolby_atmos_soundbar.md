# Vizio M512a-H6 Dolby Atmos Soundbar

One of the highlights of going to the cinema is the booming surround sound that envelops the theater, and Polygon says the Vizio M512a-H6 — a 5.1.2-channel soundbar setup — attempts to replicate that feeling at a much smaller scale in your home, and succeeds, in a highly configurable package.

The mechanism is upward-firing drivers: while the soundbar unit looks like most others, it has built-in speakers aimed upward to bounce sound off your ceiling, simulating the theater effect. That, plus its two included satellite speakers, allows Dolby Atmos-ready content to sing. Polygon also notes the speakers look great, with a sleek, low-profile design.
