# Ford Racing Success As A Brand Marketing Engine

Mark Rushbrook described Ford's motorsport marketing as a chain that starts on the racetrack: success on track is the foundation of successful marketing programs, and everything downstream — credibility, fan engagement, pride of ownership and global road-car promotion — depends on it, as reported by Essentially Sports on 6 November 2023.

The first link is winning itself and its internal effect. "Yeah, winning is everything for us, just as a point of pride. It's certainly going to be a celebration in Dearborn. My phone has been blowing up with Ford family members and our senior executives already so excited about it. It means a lot internally to the customer because motorsports is so important to us." The second link is the credibility mechanism, which Rushbrook states as a precondition rather than a benefit: "That is the basis for successful marketing programs; it is success on the racetrack. So you have to win the races, you have to win championships to have that credibility, and for fans to engage with your brand and make them proud to have a Ford parked in their driveway or in their garage."

The third link is the transfer from race car to road car, and it holds across series rather than only in NASCAR. Ford's global marketing strategy leverages the Mustang's success in NASCAR as a tool to promote its road cars worldwide, and as the seventh-generation Mustang continues to be sold globally as a road car it races not only in NASCAR but in various international series, including Australia Supercars, Mustang GT3, Mustang GT4 and Dark Horse R with a Spec Challenge. In essence, Ford takes the benefits from any Mustang's racing success and uses it to market their cars globally — the report calls it a win-win situation for both the brand and racing enthusiasts, while noting that all this might not have come to pass were it not for a timely intervention by "The Captain."
