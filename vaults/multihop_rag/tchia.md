# Tchia

Tchia, developed by Awaceb for PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, and Windows PC, is ranked 48 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31. It is an open-world adventure game set in a fictional version of the island nation New Caledonia, inspired by Awaceb's co-founder's childhood in the country.

Everything is filtered through the titular main character's eyes — eyes with a special power that allows her to transform into any animals or objects in her environment, whether birds, dolphins, a camera, or rocks.

Critic Nicole Carpenter notes the game is clearly inspired by The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild but ends up standing on its own because of the innovative shapeshifting mechanics. It is not as technically polished as a Nintendo title with hundreds of developers — Awaceb has a team of roughly a dozen — but it is hard to innovate in such a ubiquitous genre, and Awaceb managed to do just that.
