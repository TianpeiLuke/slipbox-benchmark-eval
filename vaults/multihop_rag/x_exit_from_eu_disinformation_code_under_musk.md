# X's Exit From The EU Disinformation Code Under Musk

Twitter, now X, was an original signatory to the EU's Code of Practice on Disinformation, but Elon Musk took the platform out of the initiative in May 2023, as critical scrutiny of his actions dialled up in the EU.

The exit came shortly after EU lawmakers warned that policy shifts executed after Musk's takeover were boosting Kremlin propaganda and criticised the company for patchy reporting. The EU's top diplomat also took a public shot at Twitter for ending free access to its APIs for researchers, saying the action could threaten the study of disinformation. The previous year the Commission had warned Musk over the "huge work" ahead if the platform was to avoid falling foul of the incoming DSA. In September 2023, Jourova drew attention to early analysis conducted by some of the remaining signatories which she said had found X performed the worst for disinformation ratios.
