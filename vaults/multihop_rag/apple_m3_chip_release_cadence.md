# The M3 Release Cadence Hypothesis

The proposition is that it is time for Apple to move to the next generation of silicon, and that the Scary Fast event is where the M3 arrives: Apple will not be talking about speed advancements without having new hardware to discuss, and that could all start with upgraded chipsets.

The supporting interval is that it has been nearly 17 months since Apple's M2 system on a chip debuted, and many manufacturers have an annual cadence for their chipsets. Shoving two M2 Max chips together does not count as a new generation.

The precedent that would confirm the timing is the previous gap: the M2 series debuted 19 months after the M1, so if Apple announces the M3 at the Scary Fast event, the latest SoC would be arriving after a similar period of time. An announcement without a new chip generation would be what falsifies the expectation.
