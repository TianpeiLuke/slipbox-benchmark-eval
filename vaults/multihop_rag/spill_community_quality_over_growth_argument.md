# Spill's Community-Quality-Over-Growth Argument

Alphonzo Terrell's case for Spill is that experience quality drives growth, so a differentiated product beats a race for scale: "If you have a great experience, you're going to grow."

The grounds are about user choice in a crowded market: "Especially in a world where you have so many networks — there are all these choices that people conceivably have — you're gonna go to the one where you have the greatest experience and want to invest your time." The warrant joining that to a strategy is a claim about how this market will settle: "People are looking for something new. I think things that have really defined, unique value propositions are going to win over the long term — it might not be like there's one winner-take-all."
