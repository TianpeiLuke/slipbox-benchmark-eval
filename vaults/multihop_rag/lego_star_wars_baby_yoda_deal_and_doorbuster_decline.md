# LEGO Star Wars Baby Yoda Deal And The Decline Of The Doorbuster

Mashable's Walmart Black Friday roundup of 25 November 2023 singled out a full 50% discount on a LEGO Star Wars building kit of The Child, aka Baby Yoda, precisely because such discounts have become rare: "In days gone by, Walmart's Black Friday sale was full of 40% or higher discounts, but the era of the doorbuster deal is ending."

That decline is why the writers found the 50% cut on a popular item "so refreshing".

The LEGO Star Wars line, the roundup adds, includes tons of great gift ideas for fans of all ages, from Jedis to Padawans, and this kit "is just begging to live on your office desk".
