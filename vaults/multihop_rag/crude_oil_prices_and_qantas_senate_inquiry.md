# Crude Oil Prices And The Qantas Senate Inquiry

Crude oil prices rose in the session reported on 2023-09-26, adding to worries about inflation: a barrel of benchmark US crude climbed 71¢ to $US90.39, and Brent crude, the international standard, added 67¢ to $US93.96 per barrel.

The day's quote came from the Qantas hearings. "The Senate committees have the power to summons witnesses within Australia but have no enforceable powers for witnesses who are overseas," said Senator Bridget McKenzie, after former Qantas boss Alan Joyce chose not to front the Senate select committee into the federal government's decision to reject extra flights from Qatar Airways, citing "personal commitments."
