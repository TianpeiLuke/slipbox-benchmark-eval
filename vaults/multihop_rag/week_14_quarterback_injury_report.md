# Week 14 Quarterback Injury Report

The biggest quarterback injuries going into Week 14 of the 2023 season were Kenny Pickett (ankle) and Derek Carr (head, shoulder, back), both of whom exited their contests the previous week; Sporting News replaced them in its rankings with backups Mitchell Trubisky and Jameis Winston, as early indications pointed toward both missing time.

Leading into Week 13, Dorian Thompson-Robinson (concussion) had been the lone quarterback injury situation being closely monitored; even if he cleared concussion protocol in time for Week 14, the column expected Joe Flacco to make his second start as a Brown. An update added that Trevor Lawrence suffered an ankle injury on Monday, which would likely keep him out for at least a week and open the door for C.J. Beathard (vs. Browns) to start.
