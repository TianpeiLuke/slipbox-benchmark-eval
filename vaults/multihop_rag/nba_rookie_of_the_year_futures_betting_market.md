# The NBA Rookie Of The Year Futures Market

NBA Rookie of the Year is a futures market: bettors across the country can wager on which player they believe will win the coveted award as part of NBA futures betting.

Like other NBA futures odds, it can be bet on before the start of the NBA regular season and throughout the year, and the best NBA betting apps make placing that wager simple and convenient. Sporting News publishes the latest Rookie of the Year odds as posted at online sportsbooks alongside guidance on how to bet on them.
