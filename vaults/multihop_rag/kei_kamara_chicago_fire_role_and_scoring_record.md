# Kei Kamara Sat On The Bench One Goal Short Of Landon Donovan

Chicago Fire striker Kei Kamara could have tied Landon Donovan on the all-time Major League Soccer scoring list with a goal against Inter Miami on Oct. 4, 2023: his 144 goals were just one behind the former USMNT great, with Chris Wondolowski's 171 goals leading the league's career chart. The note came 45 minutes before kickoff, while lineups were running exceedingly late.

Kamara did not start. He was on the bench for Chicago, with Georgios Koutsias slotted in up front just as he had been at the weekend; Mexican playmaker Jairo Torres also sat, with Fabian Herbers deployed on the right wing.
