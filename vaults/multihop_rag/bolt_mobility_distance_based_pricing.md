# Bolt Mobility's Distance-Based Pricing

Bolt Mobility is introducing distance-based pricing for some of its micromobility vehicles, TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-22. The aim is to incentivize riders to slow down and ride more safely, rather than racing the clock.
