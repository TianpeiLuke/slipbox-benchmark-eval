# San Francisco Giants' December 2023 Signings

The Giants made two notable additions in December 2023: Korean outfielder Jung-Hoo Lee on a six-year, $113 million contract agreed Tuesday, Dec. 12, and catcher Tom Murphy on a two-year, $8 million contract reported Monday, Dec. 18.

The New York Post's Jon Heyman reported the Lee agreement, framing it as San Francisco reeling in a significant fish after struggling to land big-name free agents over the previous two offseasons; Lee can opt out after 2027. Lee was just 25 and had been an on-base machine in Korea, batting .340 in his KBO career, and while his power was inconsistent between seasons he was a sought-after bat in free agency who drew heavy interest from the Padres as well. The Athletic's Ken Rosenthal reported the Murphy deal, adding catching depth: Murphy appeared in only 47 games for the Mariners in 2023 but had a standout year at the plate, slashing .290/.335/.538 with eight home runs. He joined a catching room that also featured breakout 2023 rookie Patrick Bailey and Joey Bart; Bart was out of minor-league options and could be on the outside looking in for a 2024 Opening Day roster spot if the Giants decided to carry only two catchers on the active roster.
