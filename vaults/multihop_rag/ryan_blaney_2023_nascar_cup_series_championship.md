# Ryan Blaney's 2023 NASCAR Cup Series Championship

Ryan Blaney secured his first-ever championship by manoeuvring his Ford Mustang to victory at Phoenix in the NASCAR Cup Series Championship Race the previous Sunday, as reported by Essentially Sports on 6 November 2023.

He held off late challenges from Chevrolet drivers Kyle Larson and William Byron. The report records the manufacturer-level significance: the achievement marks a significant moment for Ford and its NASCAR program, as it made a clean sweep by winning championships in the Truck, Xfinity and Cup Series.

The framing offered around it is that the victory was a moment of pride and celebration for Ford, but that for the manufacturer it's not just about winning races or championships — it's about marketing and maintaining brand loyalty, as a Ford insider reveals.
