# City Of Winter

City of Winter is a TTRPG with lightweight rules but a heavy emphasis on the kind of ephemera usually reserved for much crunchier games, recommended in Polygon's 2023 roundup by Ennie Award-winning entertainment journalist Linda Codega, who says she and her friends still talk about the weekend they spent playing it.

Because of that emphasis, creating space for the game is almost as important as who you choose to play it with. Characters travel along a cloth map illustrating a landscape with only the barest indications of topography; the focus instead relies on themes of assimilation, diaspora, and — most importantly to the narrative — baggage. As a family of immigrants, bound by blood or by choice, players describe their home and their traditions, and then, as a darkness comes from the west side of the map, they leave it all behind, choosing what traditions and rituals they pick up from the places they visit and what they hold on to from their homeland. Codega notes that very few games require such strict fidelity to the road laid out before you, but argues City of Winter uses that structure to facilitate the kind of deeply resonant character arcs and development many role-players crave. Her verdict: an astoundingly complex game in which bringing your best stories to the table leaves you with a sense of loss and belonging that seem to walk together, hand in hand.
