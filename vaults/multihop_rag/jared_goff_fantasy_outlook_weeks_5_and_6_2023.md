# Jared Goff's Fantasy Outlook In Weeks 5 And 6 Of 2023

CBS Sports flipped its Jared Goff call between consecutive weeks in 2023: he was a "Sit Him" for Week 5 at home against Carolina because any quarterback with upside was a better pick, then a "Start Him" for Week 6 at Tampa Bay despite a tougher matchup, on the warrant that a favourable game script caps Goff's upside more than a hard matchup caps his floor.

The Week 5 case against Goff, published 4 October 2023, is that he shouldn't have to do too much to help the Lions pull down a win. The weekly breakdown lists Detroit at home versus Carolina, the Lions favoured by 10, an over/under of 45, an opponent ranked 4th versus quarterbacks, 20.8 projected points, an 11th quarterback ranking, and year-to-date totals of 1,029 passing yards, 12 rushing yards, seven touchdowns, three interceptions and 19.6 fantasy points per game. The column expects temptation to test Jameson Williams deep and opportunities for Sam LaPorta, Josh Reynolds and hopefully Jahmyr Gibbs, but calls a modest home game with a couple of scores and maybe 225 yards more likely; it gives Goff the nod over Dak Prescott and Joe Burrow while ranking Jordan Love, Trevor Lawrence, Josh Dobbs and C.J. Stroud as better bets. Goff's previous week fit that shape — after an interception he marched downfield for a Lions touchdown, then gave way to David Montgomery to pound the Packers into submission, finishing with season lows in attempts (28) and yards (210) and just one touchdown for the third time in four games. The Panthers, at 27.9%, were dead last in pass-rush pressure rate through four weeks, their only pressure game coming against a beat-up Seahawks offensive line, while Detroit's line was near full strength and had allowed five sacks in four games. Quarterbacks had failed to top 20 fantasy points against Carolina all season, including Kirk Cousins the week before, who threw 19 times in a win for two touchdowns and just 13 fantasy points; only twice had a quarterback thrown more than twenty passes against the Panthers. Running backs, by contrast, had clobbered Carolina, with a rusher scoring at least twice in each of the first three weeks and, in Week 4, Alexander Mattison (5.6) and Cam Akers (8.0) posting uncharacteristically high rushing averages for a Vikings team that had operated as if allergic to running the ball until it played the Panthers. The one upside precedent cited is Goff's single game last year without Amon-Ra St. Brown: 26 of 39 for 378 yards and four touchdowns with an interception in a mad comeback against the Seahawks in a 48-45 loss — a scoreboard pressure the Panthers were unlikely to reproduce.

The Week 6 case for Goff, published 12 October 2023, is hedged the other way: if the game were in Detroit the author wouldn't hesitate to call Goff a must-start, but the Buccaneers defense deserves credit for holding up against opponents and Goff's two big games came against bad secondaries and suspect pass rushes. The breakdown lists Detroit at Tampa Bay, the Lions favoured by 3, an over/under of 43.5, an opponent ranked 3rd versus quarterbacks, 16.6 projected points, a 16th quarterback ranking, and year-to-date totals of 1,265 passing yards, 12 rushing yards, 11 touchdowns, three interceptions and 22.4 fantasy points per game. Goff is rated ahead of Trevor Lawrence, Sam Howell and Geno Smith but behind Matthew Stafford and Brock Purdy. In Week 5 he exploited the Panthers for 236 yards and four total touchdowns on 28 attempts even without two of his most explosive targets, and inside the five — where David Montgomery might have been expected to get the ball — Goff threw two scores and dove for another. Tampa Bay had been zone-heavy in three games but went man-to-man trying to get back into its Week 3 game against the Eagles, its only loss, and blitzes at the third-highest rate in football (41%) without generating much pressure. Goff has been slightly better against man than zone this season though fine against both, with a passer rating over 105 against each, a better touchdown rate and more downfield attacking against zone; when blitzed his completion rate stays sensational either way, over 68%, with better numbers unblitzed by a noticeable but not detrimental margin. The counterweight the column dislikes raising is road form: since arriving in Detroit, Goff has topped 20 fantasy points, at six points per passing touchdown, twice in 16 road games. Against the Buccaneers this season only Jalen Hurts had posted over 20 fantasy points, and only Kirk Cousins, on 44 attempts, had thrown two touchdowns against them.
