# What Makes A Good PC Gaming Controller

Finding the best controller for playing PC games is a more personal journey than it sounds, so Polygon frames the choice around fit and feature set rather than a single winner.

The baseline requirement is comfort: the controller has to be comfortable to use, with every button, stick, and trigger easily in reach. Beyond that, it is important that you get the features you want, which Polygon lists as easy setup on PC, good battery life, multi-platform compatibility, and accessibility options such as the ability to remap buttons and adjust stick or trigger sensitivity. The recommendations that follow are collective rather than editorial-single-voice: Polygon staff play games on a lot of consoles and platforms, both new and old, and have a wide variety of preferences and needs, so they came together to share their favourite PC-ready controllers.
