# Responsible Credit Card Use During The Holidays

The first choice is to avoid the card altogether — Plauché, already paying her student loan debt every month, is avoiding using her credit card this holiday season so as not to carry more debt — but if using your credit card is your only option, it is important that you use it wisely.

Credit Karma's Courtney Alev's steps are these: if you struggle with overspending during the holidays, set a budget for each of your credit cards; if you cannot pay your balance in full every month, make sure you understand when your payment is due and have a plan to avoid paying a lot of interest. The same caution extends to "buy now, pay later" services, which Alev says are best used very intentionally: "It's easy to get caught up. It's easy to use it a lot and have several different payments that will come down the road."
