# Shetland: Britain's Most Northerly Point, Closest To The North Pole

Shetland is the most northerly point in all of Britain and lies closest to the North Pole, which makes it a location where locals can expect to see the Northern Lights a few times every winter. Over 100 miles from the mainland, the remote isle is home to plenty of tranquil countryside and coastal scenery, providing dozens of spots where you can potentially watch the lights away from the light pollution of nearby settlements.

The season here runs from mid-October to mid-March, with winter again the best time to try to see the phenomenon — there are fewer than six hours of daylight during the shortest days. Light shows are not guaranteed during a stay, but wildlife spotting, walking and exploring the towns more than make up for any disappointment should the aurora fail to materialise.
