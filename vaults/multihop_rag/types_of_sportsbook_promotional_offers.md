# The Nine Categories Of Sportsbook Promotional Offer

The welcome bonuses and ongoing promotions coming from new online betting sites in Vermont fall into nine categories, Sporting News set out on 2023-09-26. Deposit match bonuses: get up to 100% of your first deposit matched in bonus funds, with sportsbooks limiting the bonus size you can claim and attaching wagering requirements. Referral bonuses: you share a personal referral code with friends and family, and if they use it to sign up, deposit and bet, you both get a bonus, with wagering requirements attached. First-bet offers: you get a refund if you lose your first bet, with the size of the refund limited, and most refunds being bonus bets or site credit rather than cash — meaning wagering requirements are attached and payouts don't include the stake. Odds boosts: you receive better than the usual odds on specific bets, with daily odds boosts available, though they provide no value if the boosted bet isn't a winner.

Bet insurance: get a refund if you lose a specific bet or your parlay comes up one leg short, with the refund size limited and most refunds being bonus bets or site credit, so wagering requirements are attached and payouts don't include the stake. Bet & get bonuses: you get a bonus when you place a specific bet, and most bet & get bonuses are bonus bets or site credit, so again wagering requirements are attached and payouts don't include the stake. App-only offers: place a specific bet with one of the best sports betting apps to receive a bonus, which won't be available on the sportsbook website. Special event offers: get a bonus when you place a particular bet on special event odds like World Series odds, Super Bowl betting lines, or March Madness, with these bonuses often being bonus bets or site credit and so carrying wagering requirements and excluding the stake from payouts. Loyalty offers and programs: earn points with every bet, redeemable for rewards like cash and bonus bets.
