# The Seven, Chris Hammer's Crime Novel

Chris Hammer's latest crime novel, published by Allen & Unwin at $32.99 on 3 October, brings back Nell Buchanan and Ivan Lucic for a murder case involving one of the seven families who rule the Yuwonderie district like a fiefdom, The Sydney Morning Herald listed on 1 October 2023.

Its place in his output is the roundup's framing: it is hard to believe Scrublands, Hammer's first crime novel, was published only five years earlier, and since then the former journo has followed the crime writer's timetable of a book a year. The returning detectives were met in Treasure & Dirt and Tilt.

The reviewer's expectation of the genre is stated plainly: "You can expect dark secrets to emerge and herrings to be red."
