# Sennheiser HD 560S Open-Back Headphones

The Sennheiser HD 560S was worth a look at $150, down from $229 — a $79 saving. Not an all-time low, but about $35 below the pair's typical street price. The roundup otherwise sticks mostly to wireless headphones, since that is the way the market has moved, so this is the entry for anyone who wants a quality wired model.

Engadget recommends the HD 560S in its guide to the best gaming headphones. Its open-back design lets in and leaks a ton of noise, but it sounds spacious, detailed and well-balanced, which makes it a treat for critical listening, and it is comfortable to wear for extended periods.

Just do not expect much in the way of deep sub-bass response.
