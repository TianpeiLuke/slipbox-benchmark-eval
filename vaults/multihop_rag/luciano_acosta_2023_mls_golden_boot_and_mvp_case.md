# Luciano Acosta's 2023 Golden Boot Chase And MVP Case

FC Cincinnati forward Luciano Acosta was one goal behind Denis Bouanga for the MLS goal scoring lead with two matches left in the 2023 season, sitting on 16 for the year, and was an MVP candidate. With 13 assists alongside those goals, his 29 total goal contributions were the best in the league, above Thiago Almada's 27 and Hany Mukhtar's 25, and he ranked as the second-highest rated player by WhoScored across all of Major League Soccer that season.

He started against Inter Miami on Oct. 7, 2023 despite the match being meaningless for FC Cincinnati from a tangible standpoint — understandable given his Golden Boot chase — even though Sporting News wondered before the lineups whether he would be rested with the Supporters' Shield and the No. 1 seed locked up, since an injury in a meaningless game would be devastating. Acosta had been partially rested midweek, coming off the bench to score the team's only goal against the Red Bulls in 32 minutes; Alvaro Barreal, who led the team in minutes, was also rested midweek and logged just 32 minutes on the Wednesday. In the same lineup, Ian Murphy came in for Yerson Mosquera at the back and Aaron Boupendza was rotated out with Brandon Vazquez and Sergio Santos starting together up front.

FC Cincinnati goalkeeper Roman Celentano offered his own verdict: "Luciano Acosta's the MVP... He's been an unbelievable player for us this year."
