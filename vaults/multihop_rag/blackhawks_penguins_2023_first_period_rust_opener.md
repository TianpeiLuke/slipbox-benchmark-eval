# First Period: Bryan Rust Opens The Scoring

Pittsburgh led Chicago 1-0 after the first period of the Oct. 10, 2023 opening-night game on a Bryan Rust tip-in, with the Blackhawks out-shooting the Penguins 17-13 in a frame full of shot attempts.

Bedard and Sidney Crosby squared up for the opening faceoff at 8:17 p.m. to start the debut. Bedard's first shift at 8:19 p.m. was uneventful as the Penguins controlled most of the play in the Blackhawks' zone, and through the first four minutes at 8:23 p.m. it was a back-and-forth affair, with neither team sustaining pressure and most of Chicago's attempts coming from the outside. Lars Eller was called for goalie interference at 8:26 p.m. for clipping Petr Mrazek's leg in the crease, giving Chicago the game's first power play about five minutes in; the top unit showed well by 8:28 p.m. with four shots on goal, including a Bedard one-timer stopped by Mrazek's pad — as the blog put it, Bedard and Co. could not capitalize.

Pittsburgh scored right after killing the Eller minor. At 8:29 p.m. Kris Letang threw a shot on net that Rust tipped up and over the shoulder of the Blackhawks goaltender; it was not originally called a goal on the ice but stood after review, making it 1-0 with 12:56 remaining. Crosby nearly doubled the lead on a breakaway moments later at 8:30 p.m., his backhand attempt stuffed by Mrazek's pad, and at 8:34 p.m. the blog noted Rust opening the scoring to start the year has become a trend — he had potted the first goal in the team's opener in each of the last three seasons.

The rest of the period was end-to-end. At 8:36 p.m. Evgeni Malkin made a beautiful saucer pass to Guentzel cutting to the net but the winger could not elevate it over Mrazek's shoulder, while at the other end Taylor Raddysh put a feed from behind the net into Jarry's midsection. At 8:41 p.m. Wyatt Kaiser tripped Malkin in the neutral zone for Pittsburgh's first power play — the first time seeing Erik Karlsson run the power play for the Penguins, taking Letang's place on the top unit with Crosby, Malkin, Guentzel and Rickard Rakell — and at 8:43 p.m. Karlsson danced by Cole Guttman at the blue line and ripped one from the slot, only for Mrazek to make the glove stop. Chicago killed the Kaiser minor by 8:46 p.m. having allowed three shots. At 8:47 p.m. Noel Acciari and Tyler Johnson collided in the neutral zone, leaving Johnson leaking from the bridge of his nose; Acciari was initially assessed a double minor for high-sticking, but after review no penalty was called as officials deemed the stick was not above the shoulders. At 8:51 p.m. an excellent Bedard shift nearly produced his first goal — he showed off his elite release by zipping a long-range shot that Jarry turned aside, then got two whacks at a loose puck in front without beating the goaltender. At 8:56 p.m. the period ended with Rust's tally the only one on the scoreboard and a strong showing from Bedard, who had four shot attempts with two on net.
