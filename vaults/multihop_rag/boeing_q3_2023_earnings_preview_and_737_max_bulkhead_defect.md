# Boeing Q3 2023 Earnings Preview and 737 MAX Bulkhead Defect

Boeing was preparing to report Q3 results after the closing bell on Wednesday 25 October 2023. Shares of the now-Virginia-based industrial and defence giant had dropped over 7% over the previous six months, attracting a cautious Hold from Seeking Alpha's Quant Rating system on valuation concerns, while Wall Street analysts rated the stock a Buy.

During the third quarter, Boeing and its supplier Spirit AeroSystems determined that the aft pressure bulkhead on many Boeing 737 MAX airplanes had erroneously drilled fastening holes. At the 2023 Industrial Conference, CFO Brian West claimed the issue would affect 75% of the 220 planes in inventory, or 165 planes. Despite persistent 737 manufacturing problems, the firm reiterated its ambition to boost 787 production from three to four per month by the end of 2023. Boeing delivered 27 commercial jets to customers in September, raising its yearly total to 371 planes.

Consensus EPS estimates were $-2.53 and consensus revenue estimates $18.37B. Boeing had beaten EPS expectations just once in the past 8 quarters and revenue just twice in that span. Also reporting were IBM, QuantumScape, ServiceNow, General Dynamics, Automatic Data Processing, Baker Hughes, KLA Corporation and more.
