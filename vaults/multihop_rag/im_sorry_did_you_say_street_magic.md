# I'm Sorry Did You Say Street Magic

I'm Sorry Did You Say Street Magic, by Caro Asercion, is a GM-less city-building game played by two to six people with a stack of index cards and your favorite writing utensils. Madison Durham, writer and journalist and staff writer at Reviewed, recommends it on the grounds that some of the most joyful moments in tabletop gaming come from creative collaboration — the magic of making something new with friends, be it a narrative, a character, a world, or in this case a city.

The whimsically illustrated rulebook lays out the tools by which you lay bricks for your city, weaving together your ideas and your fellow players' to establish Landmarks, Residents, and Neighborhoods, all on a quest to find the city's true names. Durham calls it a beautifully simple game, guided by a Compass which can be anything — literally — and says it is almost impossible to capture its magic in words because each session was so entirely unique, from the verdant turtle-city in the sky her group built out in stages to the haunted shell of a city they later used as their D&D setting. Her verdict: a perfect game full stop, but especially for those seeking to rekindle their creativity or simply to sit down and create with friends.
