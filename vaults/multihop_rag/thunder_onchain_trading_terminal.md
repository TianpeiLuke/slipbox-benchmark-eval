# Thunder, Onchain Trading Terminal

Thunder is a seed-stage onchain trading terminal founded by Jackson Denka and presented at Alliance DAO's All11 demo day on 2023-10-18. A product of Eversify, it is an onchain "intent-based" trading terminal that, according to its website, aims to give users "an unfair advantage in every way possible."

Its distinguishing feature is breadth of venue support: the platform aims to provide users with support for major blockchains and exchanges including OpenSea, Uniswap, Base, Polygon, Blur and Solana, among others. At the time of the demo day it was looking for strategic investors for its seed round.
