# Apple AirPods Pro 2nd Gen Holiday Deal

The second-generation AirPods Pro with USB-C — revealed when Apple announced the iPhone 15 and its conversion to USB-C charging — dropped to $190 across the 2023 holiday sales, available at Amazon and Target on Cyber Monday and at Amazon, Target and Best Buy over Black Friday, with Engadget's headphone roundup also listing Walmart. That price matches an all-time low and comes in $60 below the pair's usual going rate, or $60 less than buying from Apple directly; they had gone as low as $189 recently, so it was within a dollar of that. The Lightning version was on sale for $169 at Walmart — a new low — and $185 at Verizon.

Other than the port, the hardware hasn't changed, so buyers still get the earbuds Engadget named the best pick for iPhone users in its guide. Beyond pairing well with Apple's handsets, the buds offer one of the best transparency modes on the market along with solid ANC and richer, fuller sound than the previous generation Pros, plus Apple-specific features including hands-free access to Siri, quick pairing with iPhones and device tracking through Apple's Find My network, and a new "adaptive audio" mode that can adjust the earphones' noise control settings based on surroundings. The caveats: battery life is just average at six-ish hours per charge, and you really have to use an iPhone to get anything out of them. Engadget gave the Lightning-based version of the second-generation AirPods Pro a review score of 88 last year, and this new iteration is almost identical, only it comes with a USB-C charging case and has a higher IP54 dust-resistance rating.
