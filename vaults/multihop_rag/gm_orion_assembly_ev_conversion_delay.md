# GM's Orion Assembly EV Conversion Delay

General Motors is delaying its $4 billion plan to convert the Orion Assembly plant into an EV truck factory to late 2025, TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-22. The stated cause is softening EV demand, with TechCrunch guessing the ongoing UAW strike is also a factor.
