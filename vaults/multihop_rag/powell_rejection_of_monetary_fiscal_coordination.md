# Powell's Rejection Of Monetary-Fiscal Coordination

Fed Chair Jerome Powell, sitting to Christine Lagarde's right at the panel discussion, signaled he wasn't ready to rely on the kind of cooperation between monetary and fiscal policy she described as sometimes helpful.

His stated ground rejects the premise that the central bank's mandate is contingent on what fiscal authorities do: "Our assignment is to deliver price stability kind of regardless of the stance of fiscal policy."
