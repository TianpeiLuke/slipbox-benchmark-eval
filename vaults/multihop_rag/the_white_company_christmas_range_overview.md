# The White Company's Christmas Range: Overview

The White Company's Christmas collection, available from October 2023, is a range of new products pitched either as an extra cosy touch for the home or as affordable gifts to place beneath the tree — from wreaths and garlands that beckon guests through the door to warming scents that put everyone in the mood for Christmas, so that a household is ready to welcome friends and family throughout the coming months.

The organising claim of the collection, as presented in The Independent's festive guide, is that the products not only make good presents but can also help place your home at the centre of the celebrations. The brand is described as known for carefully crafted products and an attention to detail that make it one of Britain's most cherished lifestyle brands, and the article picks out a few favourite products from an extensive range.
