# T.J. Hockenson's Week 10 Injury Status

T.J. Hockenson (ribs) was officially ACTIVE for the Vikings' Week 10 game against New Orleans, with NFL Network's Ian Rapoport reporting Sunday morning that he was expected to play. He had been listed as "questionable" but had already said he planned to play, and although he dealt with his share of injury woes that season he had yet to miss a game; after three limited practices, Sporting News trusted he was ready, especially since he had continued playing through the ailment in Week 9 as a big part of the offense with Josh Dobbs under center.

The fantasy verdict was unequivocal. As long as Hockenson is playing he is not being benched in any fantasy format: he projected as a top option at the position and remained the Vikings' leader in targets with 80 on the season, while Dobbs had enjoyed targeting tight ends in Arizona and continued that trend in his Minnesota debut — making Hockenson's floor, as the piece put it, equivalent to that of a rooftop bar.
