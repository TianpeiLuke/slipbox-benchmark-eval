# Vivek Ramaswamy's TikTok Launch

Republican presidential candidate Vivek Ramaswamy — who proposed raising the voting age to 25 — launched his TikTok presence with an endorsement from Jake Paul, the YouTuber turned boxer who built a content empire by marketing to children, in what TechCrunch described on 2023-10-02 as an appeal to younger voters. Ramaswamy is the only Republican presidential candidate to engage with TikTok and one of the few in his party to even have an account.

His Silicon Valley roots as a biotech entrepreneur and millennial upbringing set him apart from the other candidates, but he faces an uphill battle in convincing Gen Z to like him, much less show up at the polls: his videos receive hundreds of thousands of views while his comments are overrun with sex jokes. His first videos portray him as the one candidate who cares about America's youth, and while his Republican colleagues have largely shunned TikTok, he presents himself as one of the Cool Politicians who will actually use it. "We have a generation of politicians that is badly out of touch," Ramaswamy said in his inaugural video, which did not describe his policy stances. Jake Paul's endorsement post read: "Getting Vivek on Tik Tok because i believe our politicians of the future should connect with gen z and milennials on social where we all live and breathe. Its bizarre that in this day and age our presidents have no connection with us via social. Only the occasional tweets."

Establishing himself as the millennial politician who's cool enough to use TikTok but anti-woke enough to play in the divisive culture war isn't working in his favor. His account, which amassed over 50,000 followers in the weeks since he joined, has been barraged with comments either criticizing his positions or trolling him; he has been the butt of Gen Z's relentless comments about getting off to his content, with the top comments on his videos consistently jokes about edging, and he also incited the wrath of witchtok creators who filmed themselves casting hexes upon him and other conservatives.
