# Artists Now Seek Control, Transparency And Ownership Of Their Masters

Alejandra Olea argues that artists today are much more conscious of their career trajectory and look for better deals, with more freedom, more transparency and more options: they want to keep ownership of their masters and get better economics. Her closing claim gives the leading group, that for many years now Latin artists have been paving the way for deals that bring them more control, transparency and better economics.

The warrant connecting that demand to Believe is agility of structure. This level of agility is at the very core of Believe's structuring, from self-releasing services with TuneCore to more premium services with Believe Label and Artist Solutions or Artist Services Solutions, and the company prides itself on being able to accompany talents like Iñigo at all stages of their development, from emerging acts all the way to becoming top charting artists.
