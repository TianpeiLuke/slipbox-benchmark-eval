# USC's Player Of The Year Candidates: Isaiah Collier And Boogie Ellis

Isaiah Collier (+3500) and Boogie Ellis both had odds on them in the college basketball Player of the Year race, with Purdue's Zach Edey basically an even money favorite since he won the award the previous season and was off to a terrific start, per Sporting News on Dec. 9, 2023.

Collier, a freshman, had lived up to the hype, averaging 17.3 points, 3.0 rebounds and 4.5 assists through his first eight games. He had scored in double figures each time out and had set a personal best with six assists in each of the last two games. One concern was that he went just 2-for-6 from the free-throw line in the loss to Gonzaga, but he had shot the ball reasonably well beforehand, so his off night may have just been an aberration.

Ellis was described as one of college basketball's best scorers, a shot creator who can get hot from the perimeter, so he should also enjoy a strong season in conference play.
