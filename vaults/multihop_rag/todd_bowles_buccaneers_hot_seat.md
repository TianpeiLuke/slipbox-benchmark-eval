# Todd Bowles Buccaneers Hot Seat

Yardbarker's Nov. 30, 2023 NFC hot-seat roundup named head coach Todd Bowles as the Tampa Bay Buccaneers' hottest seat: at 4-7 the Bucs were only a game back of first in the division, but they were fading fast, going 1-6 in their past seven games.

The most concerning ground is specific to Bowles: the team's lapses on defense, allowing 363.7 yards per game and ranking 27th, in what was supposedly his area of expertise. The warrant is that a coach cannot lean on divisional proximity when the unit he is personally identified with is the one failing. The entry was written by Eric Smithling.
