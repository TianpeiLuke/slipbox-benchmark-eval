# Market Linked Debentures In India

Market Linked Debentures are non-convertible debt instruments whose returns are tied to a specific financial market benchmark, such as the Nifty 50, the BSE Sensex or the 10-year G-Sec, with maturity periods varying from one year to five years. For HNIs looking to invest part of their investible surplus in debt, MLDs can be a prudent option.

The boundary case is instructive: although the minimum investment for MLDs is ₹1 lakh, which makes them accessible even to small investors, they are more popular with HNIs owing to the complex structure of these debentures. Unlike traditional fixed-income securities, MLDs do not provide periodic interest payments before maturity. Income multipliers and principal protection are common features of equity-linked MLDs: an income multiplier lets the MLD generate a return that is a multiple of the underlying index's return, which increases the likelihood of a higher overall return for a smaller market movement, while principal protection safeguards the principal up to the face value of the debenture in the event of an adverse market movement, a feature that allows even investors with moderate risk tolerance to participate in the market easily.
