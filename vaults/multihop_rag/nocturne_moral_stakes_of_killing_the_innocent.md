# Nocturne's Moral Stakes Of Killing The Innocent

The Verge argued that amid all the bloodshed, Nocturne still finds time to make its characters — and by extension the viewer — think about what it means to slaughter technically innocent people for a genuinely noble cause, and that this is what makes the season as a whole work beautifully.

The argument is careful about the strength of its claim: that beat isn't entirely original, and Nocturne is mindful about leaning on it too hard as it begins to weave Annette and Edouard's stories together with Richter and Maria's in France. The warrant is a claim about form matching theme — the restraint creates a resonance between the show's action and the concepts it's tackling, so the fighting carries the argument rather than interrupting it.
