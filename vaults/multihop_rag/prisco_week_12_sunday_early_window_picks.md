# Prisco's Week 12 Sunday Early Window Picks

Pete Prisco's Week 12 picks for the Sunday 1 p.m. ET window, published by CBS Sports on 2023-11-23, run through six games with a reason attached to each. In the Saints-Falcons divisional game on Fox and fubo, he called it a big game in a division with one team at a .500 record, the Saints; both teams were coming off a bye, but the Saints had some injury issues, and because the quarterback situation isn't great for the Falcons he expected the Saints to win behind their defense. Pick: Saints 24, Falcons 20. In the Steelers-Bengals game on CBS and Paramount+, he reasoned that the Bengals without Joe Burrow will be a different team, that Jake Browning starts with no real idea what he can do, that the Steelers defense is good, and that Pittsburgh had fired offensive coordinator Matt Canada that week hoping to liven up the offense — Kenny Pickett needs to play better now, and Prisco said he will here. Pick: Steelers 23, Bengals 21.

In the Jaguars-Texans game on CBS and Paramount+, played for first place in the division, he noted the Texans have owned the Jaguars in recent years, winning 10 of 11 and blowing them out earlier that year, but the Jaguars got back on track the previous week with Trevor Lawrence playing well, and he expected that to continue as the Jaguars take command of the division. Pick: Jaguars 27, Texans 23. In the Buccaneers-Colts game on CBS and Paramount+, he weighed the Bucs playing a second straight road game against the Colts coming off a bye — a big edge for the Colts — but judged the Bucs the better team, with Baker Mayfield playing well and Gardner Minshew not, and the Bucs winning although banged up some on defense. Pick: Bucs 30, Colts 21. For New England Patriots (-3) at New York Giants on Fox and fubo, he described two of the bottom teams in the league, both with quarterback issues: Tommy DeVito did play well for the Giants the previous week, but Bill Belichick has had great success against rookie quarterbacks, and while the Patriots quarterback situation isn't good either, he thought the Patriots find a way to win. Pick: Patriots 21, Giants 17. Finally, the Titans-Panthers game on Fox and fubo he labelled the dog game of the week, featuring two rookie passers struggling behind bad lines — Bryce Young and Will Levis have been brutalized at times, and nothing suggested that would change; the Titans defense is better, so they get the best of it in what should be an ugly game. Pick: Titans 23, Panthers 12.
