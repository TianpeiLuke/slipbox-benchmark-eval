# The Zone of Interest (Jonathan Glazer)

The Zone of Interest is Jonathan Glazer's film starring Christian Friedel, Sandra Hüller, and Ralph Herforth, set mere feet from the walls of Auschwitz at the home of commandant Rudolf Höss and his family. Polygon ranked it 22 on its best movies of 2023 list of 2023-10-07, when it was in theaters.

The Höss family builds their life in this small estate, with a fancy house and Mrs. Höss's carefully curated garden, while the horrible sounds and black smoke of the concentration camp constantly seep over their protective walls. Glazer's camera never really goes inside the camp, or shows the prisoners huddled there or their actual fates, and Rudolf is careful never to speak about his job while at home.

Polygon's Austen Goslin anticipates the objection that this sidelines the tragedy and horror of the Holocaust by centering the culprits rather than the victims, and answers it: Glazer's carefully measured detachment lets the situation speak for itself as the quiet part gets louder and more horrific throughout the film, creating a completely different kind of Holocaust film from almost any seen before, and one that is essential to understanding the scale and depth of its evils.
