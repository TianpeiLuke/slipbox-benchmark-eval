# ZeniMax Workers United, The Game Industry's Largest Union

Microsoft is currently the home of the video game industry's largest union, with representation for more than 300 quality assurance workers at ZeniMax Media — the parent company of Bethesda, id Software and Arkane, which Microsoft bought for $7.5 billion in 2021.

Microsoft formally recognized the ZeniMax union in January 2023, and the parties started negotiating in April. In December, Microsoft announced it would hire 77 contract workers as full-time employees under the ZeniMax Workers United-CWA union, in a deal guaranteeing a pay raise, paid holidays and sick leave, and a copy of Starfield, the game they helped ship. "We are now stronger at the bargaining table and are working to secure a fair contract for all workers — direct employees and contractors," union member Chris Lusco said. "We are all a part of ZeniMax Studio's success and we all deserve our fair share. We hope to set a new precedent for workers across Microsoft and the entire gaming industry so that all workers, regardless of their employment status, are able to improve their working conditions through collective bargaining." Engadget frames unionization as one approach that can help protect the livelihoods of people in the industry, and notes there was progress on this front in 2023, with developers at multiple studios — from small indies to AAA powerhouses — now having union support.
