# Man United Vs Fulham, 4 November 2023: The Fixture

Fulham hosted Manchester United on 4 November 2023 at 6:00 PM IST, in what was United's 11th Premier League match of the season. Manchester United travelled to Greater London for the fixture, with Fulham hoping to finally beat them for the first time since 2019.

It did not seem Fulham would get a better chance, considering Manchester United's horrible run of form. However, the Red Devils had come away as winners in both of their last games on the road, so the job would not be easy for Fulham.
