# Azrieli Group's Office Portfolio In Q3 2023

Azrieli Group (TASE: AZRG) is one of the biggest companies in Israel's income-producing property sector, with 15 office buildings comprising 632,000 square meters of space and 97% occupancy.

In the week before 3 December 2023 Azrieli published its financial results for the third quarter of 2023, saying the company had not been impacted by the war. The company added a caveat: due to the uncertainty about the length of the war and its development, it could not assess the future impact of the war on business activities in Israel in general and on the company in particular.
