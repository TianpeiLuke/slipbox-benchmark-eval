# Brand Accounts Co-Opting The Gag City Meme

Never one to miss out on an easy trend, brand accounts started joining in on the Gag City hype in December 2023. Chili's posted an image of rosy smoke billowing from its restaurant, which prompted the TechCrunch writer to wonder whether air pollution exists in Gag City.

Wheat Thins, Baskin-Robbins, Dunkin' Donuts, Pizza Hut, Red Lobster, Oreo, Bing, the Empire State Building and countless others posted their own versions of Gag City.
