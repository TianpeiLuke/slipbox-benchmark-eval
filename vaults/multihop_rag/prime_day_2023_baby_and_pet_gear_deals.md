# Prime Day 2023 Baby And Pet Gear Deals

WIRED's baby and pet picks are the Willow Go breast pump, the Elvie Stride, a stroller occasionally seen below $300, a Diggs pet carrier with code WOOF25, a large pet bed with a $100 discount, and Basepaws cat DNA testing.

WIRED's favorite wearable breast pump is easy to use, easy to clean, and easy to bring anywhere; what's not easy is the Willow Go's usual price tag (8/10, WIRED Recommends), though right now it's priced similarly to a standard portable pump, making it much more worth the splurge. For baby gear generally, a good deal goes a long way given how much you need before the little one arrives, and while insurance covers some of the cost of pumps it's often not all of it — depending on your plan, this deal on the Elvie Stride (7/10, WIRED Recommends) may get you closer to the price your insurance will cover. WIRED's favorite stroller is all-around great, with an adapter for infant car seats, great suspension without bulk, and a fold that goes surprisingly close to flat; it's already a great deal at regular price. The Diggs carrier draws compliments every vet trip, works for cats or smaller dogs, feels high-end with lots of pockets, and has safety features including seat belt clips and a buckle strap, plus crash testing and a five-star rating from the Center for Pet Safety; the one dislike is that you can't really see through the mesh to check on your pet, which the company says gives nervous pets privacy and calmness. A full review of the pet bed is coming, but the short version is that it's super comfy with a soft, machine-washable cover and big enough for adults to relax in — expensive, so the $100 discount is welcome. On Basepaws: dog owners tend to know exactly their pups' genetic makeup, cat owners not so much, and WIRED received PDF reports around 70 pages long detailing breed percentages and stating whether a cat is a carrier of or at risk for several health issues.
