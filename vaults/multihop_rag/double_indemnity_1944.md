# Double Indemnity (1944)

Billy Wilder's Double Indemnity, another of his films told in flashback, is one of the great genre-defining femme fatale movies, with Barbara Stanwyck as the lovely lady leading hapless chump Fred MacMurray into an insurance fraud scheme that involves murdering her husband. It is rentable on Amazon, Vudu, and YouTube.

Polygon's Tasha Robinson also rates it one of the great twist movies: everybody in the film is a potential double-crosser, and MacMurray, as an insurance investigator, is simultaneously tasked with solving the murder he committed and trying to cover it up. Her verdict is that there is enough going on to keep anyone guessing up to the last minute.
