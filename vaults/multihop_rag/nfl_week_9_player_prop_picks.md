# NFL Week 9 Player Prop Picks

Sporting News made three SuperDraft player prop picks for Week 9 of 2023: Rashee Rice OVER 45.5 receiving yards, Austin Ekeler OVER 29.5 receiving yards and Jahan Dotson OVER 45.5 receiving yards.

Rice (Chiefs vs. Dolphins in Germany) popped out as the first prop of the first game on Sunday morning, 9:30 a.m. in Frankfurt. The column loved the rookie receiver's big-play ability, consistently flashed over the previous month, during which he averaged over 55 receiving yards per game despite Kansas City's games being decided by an average of 11.8 points. It expected a tightly contested, high-scoring game with heavy playoff implications — potentially an AFC Championship preview — keeping Rice busy through all four frames, against a Dolphins defense that had allowed the fifth-most receptions and 12th-most yards to wideouts that season.

Ekeler (RB, Chargers @ Jets) was backed because the Chargers finally had their best player back to full health: he exploded for 123 yards and a touchdown against the Bears, a whopping 94 of them through the air as the PPR god gashed Chicago for chunk yardage several times. More of the same was expected from Justin Herbert and his back against a Jets defense allowing 46 receiving yards per game to running backs, sixth-most in the NFL.

Dotson (WR, Commanders @ Patriots) came full circle after making the preseason sleepers column, fading into early-season obscurity and re-emerging over the previous two weeks: with Curtis Samuel clearly not right the week before, the second-year wideout caught 8 of 10 targets for 108 yards and a touchdown, a stellar encore to his Week 7 line of five catches for 43 yards. The banged-up Patriots defense would focus primarily on stopping Terry McLaurin, which should open plenty of opportunities for Dotson.
