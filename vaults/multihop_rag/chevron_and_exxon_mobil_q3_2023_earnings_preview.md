# Chevron and Exxon Mobil Q3 2023 Earnings Preview

Oil giants Chevron and Exxon Mobil were set to announce their Q3 earnings before the market opened on Friday 27 October 2023. Despite both stocks lagging behind the broader market index, Seeking Alpha's Quant Rating system approached them cautiously with a Hold rating, while Wall Street analysts expressed optimism, giving both a Buy rating.

The expectations differed between the two: given the prevailing market conditions and the upward trend in crude oil prices, analysts anticipated year-on-year growth in earnings per share and revenue for Chevron, while expecting a decline for Exxon Mobil.

Also reporting that Friday were WisdomTree, AbbVie, Phillips 66, Colgate-Palmolive, Sanofi, T. Rowe Price Group, Stanley Black & Decker, Charter Communications, Newell Brands, Xcel Energy, Aon, AutoNation, CBRE Group and more.
