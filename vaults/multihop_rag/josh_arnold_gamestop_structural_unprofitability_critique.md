# Josh Arnold's GameStop Structural Unprofitability Critique

Seeking Alpha author Josh Arnold argued in the December 3, 2023 week-ahead that GameStop is a structurally unprofitable company, writing: "GameStop faces fundamental headwinds, including poor margins and declining sales, making it a structurally unprofitable company."

The premise this attacks is that GameStop's problems are cyclical or fixable within the current business — the assumption behind any bullish read of the shares. Arnold's grounds are two specific fundamental headwinds, poor margins and declining sales, and the word doing the work is "structurally": the claim is not that the company is losing money this quarter but that the loss-making is built into its economics, so a better quarter or a stronger tape would not overturn it. Scope conditions: this is one named Seeking Alpha author's stated view as quoted in a December 3, 2023 preview, offered without supporting figures in the passage.
