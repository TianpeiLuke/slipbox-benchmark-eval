# NCAA Coach-To-Player Helmet Communication For Bowl Season

CBSSports.com reported on Oct. 20, 2023 that the NCAA rules committee is giving teams the option of coach-to-player helmet communication to relay plays from the sideline during bowl season that year. Sporting News expected tablets to follow inevitably, and called this the sliver of good that would come from the Michigan sign-stealing scandal.

The outlet's verdict was blunt: "It's about damn time. Death to sandwich-board playcalling, please. Bring on those playsheets where coaches simply have to cover their mouth." This, it argued, is one instance where emulating the NFL game is overdue.
