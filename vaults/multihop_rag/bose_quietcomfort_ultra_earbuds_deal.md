# Bose QuietComfort Ultra Earbuds Deal

The new Bose QuietComfort Ultra Earbuds came down to $249 at several stores for Black Friday 2023 — another $50 discount and an all-time low.

Engadget gave these in-ears a review score of 88 in September and recently named them the "best for noise cancellation" pick in its wireless earbuds buying guide. As that title suggests, the main reason to consider this pair over the Sony WF-1000XM5 is its ANC performance, which is the strongest Engadget has tested from a true wireless model, and it also enjoys Bose's spatial audio tech. The drawbacks: the earpieces are on the larger side, the call quality and battery life at six-ish hours are just OK, and there's no support for multi-device pairing.
