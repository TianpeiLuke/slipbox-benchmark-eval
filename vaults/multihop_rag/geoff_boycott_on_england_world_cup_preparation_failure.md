# Boycott: England Failed To Prepare For The 2023 World Cup

Sir Geoff Boycott argued in The Telegraph, in reaction reported on Oct. 26, 2023, that England were paying the price for poor preparation in the lead-up to the World Cup as well as shoddy team selection extending back to The Ashes earlier that year — leading a scathing UK reaction from a swathe of English cricket greats.

His grounds are historical and specific. "It is an old saying: 'By failing to prepare, you prepare to fail'," he wrote, adding that history shows England rarely bat well in India where the ball spins: "Our guys are okay on flat pitches but if it turns they have a problem." The warrant connecting that record to the remedy is that exposure to difficult conditions is what sharpens a team — "batting and bowling against stiff opponents sharpens your game and puts you on your mettle" — so the best way to overcome the spin problem would have been for the squad to get to India early and play four or five warm-up matches.

What England actually did, Boycott wrote, was plan only two warm-up games, one of which was rained off, which he called not smart planning; against Sri Lanka they were once again exposed. He extended the charge to the players' approach: "Too many of our guys just want to play crash, bang wallop all the time irrespective of the type of pitch or opposition. Again not smart."
