# Upshield, Web3 Security Platform

Upshield is a seed-stage, full-stack security platform for web3 protocols, assets and communities, founded by Paul Vijender and Preston Thornburg and presented in Alliance DAO's All11 cohort on 2023-10-18. It aims to be user friendly for people building applications who are not security experts.

Co-founder and CEO Paul Vijender said during his presentation that the startup launched two weeks earlier after two months in beta mode and currently has an ARR of $120,000. Its engine has over 100 web3 threat detectors plus on-chain and off-chain coverage for projects across smart contracts, application front ends and social channels, among others, and it can work with web3 applications built on eight blockchains and networks including Coinbase's Base, Ethereum, Avalanche, Binance and Polygon. It was raising a seed round.
