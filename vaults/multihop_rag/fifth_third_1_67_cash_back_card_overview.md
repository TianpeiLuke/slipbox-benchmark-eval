# The Fifth Third 1.67% Cash/Back Card: Overview

The Fifth Third 1.67% Cash/Back Card is a mostly vanilla cash back credit card with one of the simplest rewards structures you will find: a fixed 1.67% earning rate on all purchases, with the option to redeem for either a direct deposit or a statement credit on the card. Its distinguishing features are an unlimited 1.67% return rate on all purchases at an abnormally high rate for its class, a simple rewards structure, and a $0 annual fee.

Its limits are the mirror image of that simplicity. The card has no welcome offer and no bonus spending categories, and applicants must already be a Fifth Third customer to qualify. The super-simple approach may be a big draw for some but a deterrent for others: its one-dimensional design means it is impossible to get outsized value for the rewards.
