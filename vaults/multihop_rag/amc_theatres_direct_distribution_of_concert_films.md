# AMC Theatres As Direct Distributor Of Concert Films

Both Beyoncé and Taylor Swift chose to partner with AMC Theatres to distribute their concert films rather than going through a traditional studio, an arrangement in which both are reported to be receiving at least 50% of ticket sales.

The parallels run further: both superstars have been supportive of one another, making splashy appearances at the other's premieres, and both had previously released films on Netflix — "Miss Americana" for Swift and "Homecoming" for Beyoncé. The direct-distribution model also came with premium pricing. Movie tickets to the Beyoncé show were more expensive than average, around $23.32 versus Swift's $20.78, according to data firm EntTelligence.
