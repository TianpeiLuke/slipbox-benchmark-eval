# QI Tech's Series B: Brazilian Banking-As-A-Service

Brazilian startup QI Tech, a banking-as-a-service platform, has raised $200 million in a Series B funding round.

That makes it the largest raise in Brazil in 2023 across all sectors, as of November 3, 2023.

The latest funding round brings QI Tech's total capital raised to $262 million.
