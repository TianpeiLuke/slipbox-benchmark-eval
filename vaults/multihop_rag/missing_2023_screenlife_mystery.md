# Missing (2023 Screenlife Mystery)

Missing, directed by Nicholas D. Johnson and Will Merrick and starring Storm Reid, Nia Long, and Ken Leung, is the twisty and fun spiritual sequel to 2018's Searching. Polygon ranked it 49 on its best movies of 2023 list of 2023-10-07 and listed it on Netflix or for digital rental/purchase.

The film is a web of clues and a mess of mystery, all played out within the computer screen of June, played by Storm Reid, who is left to figure out what happened when her mom, played by Nia Long, does not return from a vacation with her boyfriend.

Polygon's Zosha Millman frames it against the appetite Knives Out demonstrated for mysteries on the big screen, arguing that the mystery genre deserves more movies that feel big and meaty, and that whether splashed across the big screen or just a computer one, Missing manages to bring its A-game.
