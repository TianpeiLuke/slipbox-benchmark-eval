# Shadow Gambit: The Cursed Crew

Shadow Gambit: The Cursed Crew, developed by Mimimi Games for PlayStation 5, Windows PC, and Xbox Series X, is ranked 18 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31, and the critic calls it the most bittersweet entry on the list.

The bittersweetness is that it is both one of the best games released in 2023 and the swan song of Mimimi Games, one of the most underappreciated studios making games over the last decade: Mimimi announced its planned closure only two weeks after releasing its final game, making The Cursed Crew both its farewell and its magnum opus.

Set in a tropical world of zombie pirates, religious fanatics, and talking ships, the game has you traveling freely across an archipelago as you revive your undead crew, deploy them on dioramic worlds constituting some of the finest level design in video games to date, and dispatch enemies with a mixture of tactical stealth and supernatural abilities. Critic M. Mahardy concedes the mixture is undeniably niche taken at a distance, but says that seen up close it is as potent a creation as anything since 2016's Shadow Tactics: Blades of the Shogun or 2020's Desperados 3, which respectively established and cemented the studio's brilliant design chops.
