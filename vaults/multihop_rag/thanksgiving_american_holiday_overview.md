# Thanksgiving: The American Holiday

Thanksgiving is a national holiday in the United States, celebrated every fourth Thursday in November — falling on Thursday 23 November in 2023 — and it is a cultural holiday that symbolises peace, thankfulness, and the beginning of the holiday season. Although Thanksgiving may originally have had religious significance, the day has become a mostly secular holiday.

Food is arguably the main component of the day. Americans start preparing their menus weeks ahead, with the requisite turkeys and stuffing ingredients likely already purchased, and on the day itself they are hard at work preparing dinner, with foods on the menus ranging from a roasted turkey to a green bean casserole. Aside from the dishes served in the lavish feast, which can vary based on families' cultures and traditions, the holiday is a beloved time of year when Americans come together to celebrate what they are thankful for, either with family or friends, and to commemorate all that they are grateful for.

Most Americans consider the holiday a day to gather and express their thanks through food, family, and football — with multiple NFL teams playing on the holiday. During some Thanksgiving celebrations, people write down what they are thankful for and then read aloud from the pieces of paper. Following Thanksgiving, the month-long shopping season for the winter holidays begins, with Black Friday kicking it off.
