# Amex Cash Magnet Welcome Bonus Terms

New customers can qualify for a $200 statement credit by spending $2,000 in the first 6 months of card ownership. Once the spending threshold is reached, American Express credits the $200 to the account as a statement credit — though it can take eight to 12 weeks after reaching the spending requirement to receive the credit.

The distinguishing feature of the offer is its length rather than its size. Many cards require cardmembers to reach the spending threshold within three months, so unless you spend a significant amount of money each month with your card you're unlikely to reach it. With six months to reach the threshold, even someone using the card only for routine expenses — utility bills, streaming services, gas for the car, groceries — could likely reach the spending requirement and earn the $200 cash back bonus.
