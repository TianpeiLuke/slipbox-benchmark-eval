# Roblox Fashion Design And Direct-To-Avatar

Asked at the Code Conference by Cathy Hackl — a tech and gaming executive at Journey, a Roblox player, and mother of an 11-year-old Roblox developer who makes about a hundred dollars a month from his builds — about his RDC prediction that a top fashion designer will be discovered on Roblox without any experience in physical fashion, David Baszucki said the role of fashion in Roblox's future and in direct-to-avatar is huge. He cited Karlie Kloss being on the platform and a partnership with Parsons School of Design, noting that at RDC they showed a couple of gowns done in concert with some of the students there, which were absolutely amazing. One could imagine, he said, reducing the friction of creativity for a fashion designer to virtual design and ultimately AI-supported design.

He also framed Roblox as a prototyping environment for the fashion industry: you can try a lot of things more quickly than building the physical article, you can get crowd feedback, and there will someday be experiences where early designs are voted on — Roblox has had things like that in a more simplistic way — and from there you can imagine almost predicting what types of designs from top designers would be welcomed.
