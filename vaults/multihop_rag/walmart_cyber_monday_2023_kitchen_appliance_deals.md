# Walmart Cyber Monday 2023 Kitchen Appliance Deals

The kitchen picks in WIRED's Walmart Cyber Monday roundup of 27 November 2023 were a 4.5-quart stand mixer, the Keurig K-Express single-serve coffee maker, a budget espresso machine and the Ninja Creami ice cream maker.

WIRED notes that mixers have not changed much since they were introduced over a century ago, which it treats as the mark of a great tool; the discounted 4.5-quart bowl model is slightly less powerful than some, but still strong enough to knead dough, cream butter and whip up big batches of holiday cookies. The K-Express is pitched at people low on both caffeine and counter space: it is a mere 6.5 inches wide and 12 inches deep, yet its 36-fluid-ounce removable reservoir holds enough water to make a round of coffee for you and your friends without constant refilling, and WIRED asks readers to use Keurig Reusable Pods to cut down on single-use plastic waste.

The Stilosa is WIRED's favourite among the rare good budget espresso machines, with a solid portafilter, a reliable steam pump and a steaming wand in a fairly compact body; unlike other cheap machines the team tried, it showed no signs of slowing down, and reviewer Medea — who was gifted it after getting married — reports it is easy to use for brewing espresso or steaming milk and takes up very little counter room. The Ninja Creami (6/10, WIRED Review) looks like a coffee maker but makes ice cream; WIRED calls it inessential but a fun kitchen tool for experimenting with your own sorbets and ice cream recipes, and notes Ninja sells a $17 Creami recipe book separately, which WIRED wishes were included instead.
