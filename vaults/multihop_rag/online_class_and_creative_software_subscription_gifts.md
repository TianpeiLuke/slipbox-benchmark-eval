# Online Class And Creative Software Subscription Gifts

For the creative type, The Verge's rule is simple — buy them something to help them create, whether an online course or access to a new tool — and it lists several subscriptions and gift cards that can be bought last minute.

A MasterClass membership, starting at $10 a month, provides access to classes taught by world leaders and other subject matter experts including screenwriters, musicians, and business experts; the company is currently throwing in a second membership free when you sign up, so you can gift it to another person on your list. An Adobe Creative Cloud subscription at $59.99 per month suits both aspiring and experienced creative professionals, providing access to popular services like Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro, InDesign, and other Adobe apps. For someone learning to code, a subscription to Codecademy at $29.99 per month offers online courses, a community, fun events, cheat sheets, and other resources to build a portfolio, and there's a career-change plan that adds technical interview help, currently $120 a year — half off — with promo code BYE2023. For giftees into arts and crafts, a Craftsy membership at $113 a year grants access to more than 2,000 live and on-demand expert-led classes covering everything from baking and cake decorating to woodworking and painting, plus the Craftsy community and live events.
