# Shaqiri Before Facing Inter Miami: Preparation Unchanged, And Team Messi

Ahead of the Chicago Fire's Oct. 4, 2023 match with Inter Miami, Xherdan Shaqiri said the Fire's preparation would not change regardless of whether Lionel Messi played. Speaking in his pre-match availability on the Monday, and reported 90 minutes before kickoff, Shaqiri said: "The preparation is not going to change, we're going to prepare as normal as always. Everybody is excited to play against Inter Miami. If Messi is injured or not, personally I am not thinking too much about this. Of course Inter Miami is a different team when Messi is playing or not playing, you see that in the last games when Messi is in the team they are better, but we prepare like normal for every game." Sporting News noted that many teams had indicated their preparation for Inter Miami varied wildly depending on Messi's availability, while Shaqiri played his cards close to his chest.

Shaqiri also framed the stakes of the match itself: "These are the best games to play all the time. As a player, you want these kinds of final games and to play for something. We put ourselves in this position, and we have a chance to do it, so now we have to perform."

Asked "Messi or Ronaldo?", Shaqiri confirmed he was Team Messi: "I'm always a big Messi admirer, if I have to choose now I will go with Messi. He won the WC which was the only thing he missed. So for sure Messi." He also said: "For me it's clear, Messi and Ronaldo are the best players in the world… these guys are among the best athletes in the history of sport, along with Michael Jordan and Tom Brady."
