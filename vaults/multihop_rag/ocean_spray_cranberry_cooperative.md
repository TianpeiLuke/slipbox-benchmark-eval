# Ocean Spray, The Cranberry Cooperative

Ocean Spray is the farming cooperative Marcus Urann created with fellow cranberry farming leaders John Makepeace and Elizabeth Lee, still headquartered in Massachusetts today and synonymous with cranberry juice and cranberry sauce, FOX News Lifestyle reported on 23 November 2023.

Its defining feature is scale and reach: it is one of the world's most successful farming cooperatives, and it has helped spread cranberry farming around the country and around the world while putting cranberry juices and sauces into international markets.

Its signature product remains cranberry sauce. The company first sold its cranberry sauce nationally in 1941, its ridged cranberry gel a familiar sight on Thanksgiving tables around the United States.
