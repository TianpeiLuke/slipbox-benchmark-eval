# IronNet Shutdown

IronNet, a once-promising cybersecurity startup founded by a former NSA director, has shuttered and laid off its remaining staff following its collapse, TechCrunch reported in its Week in Review of October 7, 2023.

The Virginia-based company was founded in 2014 by retired four-star general Keith Alexander and had raised more than $400 million in funding. It went public in August 2021 but failed to gain traction afterwards; its stock price spiked initially and then continued to decline steeply in the wake of that spike.
