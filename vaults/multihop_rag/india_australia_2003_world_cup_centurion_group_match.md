# India vs Australia In The 2003 World Cup Group Stage

The 2003 group-stage meeting produced what the article calls India's biggest batting collapse in the history of the ODI World Cup. India won the toss and chose to bat first, but crumbled within 125 runs in 41.4 overs, with Brett Lee and Jason Gillespie picking up three wickets each for Australia.

Matthew Hayden and Adam Gilchrist gave Australia a fiery start in the second innings and reached the target in 22.2 overs, losing just one wicket. Australia won the match by nine wickets with 166 balls remaining.
