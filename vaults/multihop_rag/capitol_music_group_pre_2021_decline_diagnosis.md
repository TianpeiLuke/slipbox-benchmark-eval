# Jubelirer's Diagnosis Of Capitol's Pre-2021 Decline

Asked in December 2023 about the turbulence she had to right, Michelle Jubelirer said that before she was named CEO and Chair in 2021 CMG had undergone a few leadership changes within a short period of time, and that this turmoil was compounded by the economic volatility of the pandemic; as a result the company faced eroding market share, low morale and a stagnant artist roster. The contrast drawn by the interviewer is that CMG is now in significantly stronger shape than it has been for a while and feels meaningfully influential in pop culture, with several tracks in TikTok's end-of-year and most popular playlists.

Jubelirer attributes her fitness for the repair job to tenure: because she had spent almost a decade at the company, she was uniquely situated to bring a fresh perspective to the job and build a modern culture within the company while still honouring Capitol's legacy in profound ways. In her framing that means CMG could be a company that excels at launching and developing Ice Spice and at the same time brings real innovation and full-throttle commitment to the release of a new single from The Beatles, and everything in between.
