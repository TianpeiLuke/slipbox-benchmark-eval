# Tencent's Investment Web Across The Game Industry

Tencent is bigger than both Sony and Microsoft: not a console manufacturer and so not a household name among most players, but one of the largest companies in the world and the biggest investor in games, wielding a ridiculous amount of financial power through thousands of tendrils across the industry.

Its holdings come in three degrees. It owns a portion of Bloober Team, Bohemia Interactive, Don't Nod, Epic, Paradox Interactive, PlatinumGames, Remedy Entertainment, Roblox and Ubisoft, among others; it has a majority stake in Supercell, Grinding Gear Games, Klei Entertainment, Tequila Works, Techland, Yager Development and others; and it fully owns Riot Games, Funcom, Sharkmob, Turtle Rock Studios and others. It also runs multiple internal development companies, including the Level Infinite and Tencent Games publishing labels. Engadget illustrates the reach with Epic: Sony has a stake in Epic, but Tencent's is bigger, and that investment alone means that any time you buy a game built on Epic's Unreal Engine, Tencent — and Sony — is getting a cut. The summary: if you played something this year, Tencent was probably involved.
