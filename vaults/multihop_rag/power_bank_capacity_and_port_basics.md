# Power Bank Capacity And Port Basics

A power bank, also commonly called a portable charger, is a battery that recharges your gadgets one or more times over a USB connection — Polygon calls it an essential purchase for anyone on the go with a phone, tablet, or a gaming handheld such as a Nintendo Switch or a Steam Deck, so you can keep talking, texting, or gaming throughout the day.

The features that separate one power bank from another are also what set its price: how compact it is, what kind of ports it has, how much battery capacity it contains — typically measured in mAh — and how quickly it can recharge your devices.
