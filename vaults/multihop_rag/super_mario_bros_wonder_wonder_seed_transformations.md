# Wonder Seeds And The Transformations They Trigger

A Wonder Seed is the object that gives Super Mario Bros. Wonder its name: a Mario-sized ball of botany hidden inside each Wonder stage that, when found, morphs the environment into something quite wonderful. Choosing to seek it out — being "not too precious about video game tradition," as Polygon puts it — floods the atmosphere with lava-lamp plasma, sends gravity into convulsions, or transforms the hero into any variety of creatures or even inanimate objects, so that for a moment Mario gets to be the background. The second level of the game illustrates the range: it can become a musical, depending on whether you pursue the "Super Mario Bros." of the title or the "Wonder."

The defining property, in the reviewer's reading, is that the choice is one of "and," not "or" — the traditional and the topsy-turvy coexist. Across six worlds the creators offer a menu of familiar stages that can, if you like, be prepared with extra spice, or paired with the family-friendly equivalent of ayahuasca; the drug comparison is not flippant, since at one point Toad licks a Wonder Seed and is suddenly riding a dragon. Sometimes the effects go further and the levels themselves collapse and contort, disobeying laws established by decades of Mario games: giant, sudsy, rainbow-tinted bubbles fill a stage and burst through the ceiling, and a colossal snowball knocks down a flagpole, extending the stage beyond its official conclusion.
