# The Mombies' Origin and "Dance to Donate"

The Mombies started out in 2016, when Terry Davis — a Fairfield mom and human resources executive — got together with a group of other moms to surprise the neighbourhood with a flash mob on Halloween.

The initial motive was not fundraising. As the account given to Fox News Digital puts it: "She wanted to create a unique surprise for the kids to show them we're fun and to show them that we can be exciting, too."

The charitable purpose was added the following year: "After the first year, she said, 'Why don't we attach a little bit of goodness to it?' And we started 'Dance to Donate,' which has created this big movement. We've been dancing to fund the Cancer Couch, which is an organization founded here in Fairfield."
