# They Cloned Tyrone (Juel Taylor)

They Cloned Tyrone is Juel Taylor's Blaxploitation-inspired romp starring John Boyega, Teyonah Parris, and Jamie Foxx, ranked 44 on Polygon's best movies of 2023 list of 2023-10-07 and streaming on Netflix.

The film follows a drug dealer, a pimp, and a prostitute who inadvertently stumble upon a clandestine government facility that has been secretly experimenting on their neighborhood from the shadows. Faced with the horrifying reality of their situation, the trio band together to do the only thing they can — find a way to spread the truth and fight back against their oppressors.

Polygon's Toussaint Egan calls it a wild, weird, and genuinely funny comedy anchored by strong leading performances, especially Foxx's charismatic and foul-mouthed turn as Slick Charles.
