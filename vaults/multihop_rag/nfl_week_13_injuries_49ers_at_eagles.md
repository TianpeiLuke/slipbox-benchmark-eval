# NFL Week 13 Injuries 49ers At Eagles

In the Week 13 final injury reports published by CBSSports.com on Dec. 1, 2023, with the 49ers listed at -3 at the Eagles, each of San Francisco's questionable players had been limited Friday after not practicing the previous two days, and Armstead had stated that he would suit up on Sunday.

Philadelphia saw the return of tight end Dallas Goedert at practice on Friday: he was limited for the session and officially listed as doubtful after practicing for the first time since suffering a fractured forearm against the Cowboys on Nov. 5. Cox was questionable despite not practicing Friday due to a groin injury.
