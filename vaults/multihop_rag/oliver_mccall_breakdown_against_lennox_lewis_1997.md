# Oliver McCall's Breakdown Against Lennox Lewis, 1997

Lennox Lewis' 1997 attempt to avenge his upset defeat by Oliver McCall ended with McCall crying, no longer throwing punches, and eventually being stopped for simply not trying, talkSPORT recalled on 15 November 2023.

The iron-chinned McCall had battled drug addiction in the build-up to the hotly anticipated heavyweight world title fight and was arrested for throwing a Christmas tree across a hotel lobby. He was clearly not in the right mindset to trade leather with Lewis, as showed when he began wandering around the ring in a world of his own.

Referee Mills Lane eventually just waved the bizarre spectacle off.
