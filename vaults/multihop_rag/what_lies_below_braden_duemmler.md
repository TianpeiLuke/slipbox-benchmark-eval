# What Lies Below (Braden Duemmler)

What Lies Below is Braden R. Duemmler's thriller starring Ema Horvath, Haskiri Velazquez, and Trey Tucker, recommended by Polygon on 2023-10-25 as a free watch on Tubi.

Horvath plays Libby, a 16-year-old girl who returns home to her mother's lake house after summer camp to discover that her mother has taken on a new boyfriend, John, played by Trey Tucker. Initially accepting of her mom's new beau, Libby gradually begins to suspect that something is off about John that he is trying to conceal. With no one but her friend Marley, played by Haskiri Velazquez, to turn to for help, Libby must expose the truth of John's sinister nature before it is too late to save her mother and herself.

Polygon's Toussaint Egan describes the film as filled with mounting tension, creepy ethereal visuals, and a mind-bogglingly bizarre ending, concluding that it has more appeal than might at first appear on its surface.
