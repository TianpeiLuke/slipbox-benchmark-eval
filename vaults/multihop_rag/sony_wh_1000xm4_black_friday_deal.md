# Sony WH-1000XM4 Black Friday Deal

For those who don't want to pay up for Sony's highest-end noise-canceling headphones, Amazon, Adorama and B&H had the older Sony WH-1000XM4 down to $248 for Black Friday 2023. While that isn't an all-time low, it's $100 off Sony's list price and a little lower than the usual sale price seen over the course of the year.

The XM4 delivers similar ANC and battery performance as its successor and has nearly the same feature set, and unlike the XM5 it can fold up for easier storage. However, its mics aren't as clear for calls, it's a little bulkier though still comfy, and it sounds a bit boomier out of the box — but at this price it's still a strong value. Engadget gave it a 94 in its review back in 2020.
