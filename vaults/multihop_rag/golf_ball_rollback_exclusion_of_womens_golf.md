# Golf Ball Rollback Exclusion Of Women's Golf

Although the R&A and USGA each organize an LPGA major — the Women's Open and the U.S. Women's Open respectively — the rollback rules will apply only to men's golf, Essentially Sports reported on Dec. 3, 2023.

The reason given is that the average driving distance on the LPGA Tour is still well within the accepted norm of the two regulatory bodies. No noise had been heard from the female circuit, even though Lexi Thompson shot a 316-yard drive at the Shriners Children's Open. It was the PGA Tour pros and LIV Golf pros who chimed in with their thoughts.
