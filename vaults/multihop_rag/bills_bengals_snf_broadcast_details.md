# Bills At Bengals Broadcast Details

Bills at Bengals kicked off at 8:20 p.m. ET on Sunday, Nov. 5, 2023 at Paycor Stadium in Cincinnati, carried by NBC, which has broadcast Sunday Night Football since 2006 and will continue to do so through at least 2033. The game featured the usual broadcast group of Mike Tirico on play-by-play and Cris Collinsworth on color commentary, with Melissa Stark reporting from the sidelines.

Several live-stream options were available. NBCSports.com carried a broadcast for fans with a cable login, Peacock carried NBC's broadcast, as did Fubo, which offers a free trial, and fans in Canada could stream the game live with DAZN.
