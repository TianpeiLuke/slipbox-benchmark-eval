# Efe Ajagba vs Curtis Harper, 2018: The Walkout

Efe Ajagba beat Curtis Harper in the first second of round one in 2018 without throwing a punch, because Harper waited for the opening bell, then calmly stepped between the ropes and strolled back to his dressing room, talkSPORT recalled on 15 November 2023.

The then 5-0 heavyweight prospect, a baffled Ajagba, celebrated his "win" and moved to 6-0 with an official KO1 at 0:01.

Harper later explained he was upset over a contract situation — which leaves the question of why he bothered with the ring walk and referee's instructions. He has not fought since.
