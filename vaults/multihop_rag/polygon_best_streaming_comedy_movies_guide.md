# Polygon's Guide To The Best Comedies On Streaming

Polygon argues that comedy is an ignored genre in modern moviemaking, and that the remedy for viewers is to look to streaming rather than to new studio releases. The grounds are a comparison across genres: horror was having a fantastic 2023, thrillers come out on a weekly basis, and even action movies had seen stellar recent releases, while Hollywood has been in a comedic rut with fewer and fewer notable releases from big studios.

The warrant joining the complaint to the recommendation is that the good comedies still exist, just not in cinemas — so the site compiled a list of the best comedy movies to watch at home, scraping streaming services including Netflix, Hulu, Prime Video and HBO Max, as well as free services, "to find the best of the best." The list deliberately spans registers, from romantic comedies that make your heart sing to gut-busting laugh-out-loud comedies. Polygon maintains a separate companion list for the best comedies on Netflix alone, and notes that its latest update added The Road to El Dorado as an editor's pick.
