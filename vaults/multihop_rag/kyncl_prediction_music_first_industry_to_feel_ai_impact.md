# Kyncl Prediction: Music Is the First Industry to Feel AI's Impact

Robert Kyncl's proposition, put at the Code Conference in September 2023, is that the music business will be out ahead of other industries in exploiting the potential of AI, and — out of necessity — also ahead on the question of how to regulate and monetise the use of AI by non-rights-holders. He attached a date to it: asked when that sea change would occur, he said "I would say within the next year you will see lots of evolution around AI… What you will likely see is increasing quality at a very fast pace."

His reasoning is structural rather than accidental. "Music – because it's so broadly distributed and it's so well aligned with the internet, because it's short format, it lends itself to recommendations, it's on all platforms – is generally first in most transformations and most innovations. So it digitizes first." He generalises from the previous cycle: "Whatever happened to the music industry 20 years ago is starting to happen in the movies and TV shows now. In the meantime, music emerged out of [digitization] better and stronger and more resilient. So, I would imagine the same will happen here, which is we'll likely be first."

The hypothesis is falsifiable on two fronts as stated. It fails if other industries, rather than music, lead on both exploiting and regulating AI; and it fails on its own timetable if the year following September 2023 does not bring the predicted rapid evolution and fast-rising quality in AI. Because the argument rests on music's broad distribution, short format and recommendation-friendliness, an AI transformation that arrives first in a long-format medium would also count against the mechanism he proposes.
