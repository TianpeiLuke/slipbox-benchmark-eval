# Cole Palmer Stoppage-Time Penalty Vs Manchester City

Cole Palmer, who left Manchester City for Chelsea in a deal worth around $A82 million in September, showed supreme composure to fire past Ederson in the dying stages of the 4-4 draw at Stamford Bridge, scoring from the spot in the 95th minute, The Roar reported on Nov. 12, 2023.

In his post-match interview he said of penalties: "I'm not going to say I work on them, because I don't. I just trust in my natural abilities."
