# Iran's State Funding Of Hamas

"The most significant way to finance terrorism is to finance countries, not fundraising campaigns," Ostrovitch-Levi says: campaigns have intensified greatly over the last month, but the core of the budget for Hamas, Hezbollah, the Houthis and Palestinian Islamic Jihad is Iran, in particular the Quds Force, the special forces of the IRGC.

The dependence is presented as decisive: Hamas has all kinds of additional sources of self-financing — businesses, associations, investments, and all kinds of arrangements it has tried to set up over the years — but "They need state funding, and without Iran they have no ability to exist." On scale: "As far as we know, the budget from Iran to Gaza is in the order of $100 million a year. Gaza could do very nice things with this money, make it even more beautiful than Dubai. But from what we know, most of this budget goes to the military wing of Hamas." Qatar is a distinct case: "Qatar transferred suitcases of cash to Gaza through Israel. Everyone knows this. This money allegedly went to pay salaries and civilian needs (education, health, infrastructure), and therefore Israel transferred it, so it's incorrect to say that it all went to fund terrorism." IMPA is charged with rooting out the sources of funding for terrorism, working together with the National Bureau for Counter Terror Financing of Israel (NBCTF) in the Ministry of Defense, which coordinates all activities to thwart terror financing, along with the Shin Bet, Military Intelligence Directorate (AMAN), and all other relevant intelligence and security agencies.
