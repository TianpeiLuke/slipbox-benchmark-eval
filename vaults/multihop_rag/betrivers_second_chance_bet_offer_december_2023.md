# Claiming BetRivers' Second Chance Bet With Bonus Code SPORTSRIV

BetRivers bonus code SPORTSRIV let new users claim a Second Chance Bet worth up to $100, $250 or $500 for the Eagles vs. Seahawks Monday Night Football game, with the cap depending on the bettor's state, per Sporting News on Dec. 18, 2023.

The steps are: click the exclusive link, use the promo code SPORTSRIV, register the new account, and then make a deposit. Place your first bet and, if it loses, BetRivers will refund the amount of the wager in bonus bets worth up to $100, $250 or $500, depending on your state.

The state boundaries are explicit: the Second Chance Bet is worth up to $100 for users in AZ, IA, OH and WV; $250 in CO and NJ; and $500 if betting from IL, IN, LA, MD, MI, PA and VA.
