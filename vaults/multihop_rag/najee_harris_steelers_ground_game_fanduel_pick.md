# Najee Harris And The Steelers' Ground Game

Sporting News took Najee Harris ($14,000) in the FLEX of its Week 14 2023 Patriots-Steelers FanDuel lineup, calling him its best bet for success in an old-fashioned defensive struggle between two of the worst offenses the NFL had to offer.

Much like the Patriots, the Steelers offense would want to establish the ground game, and that starts with Harris. It had not been long since the running back was the subject of major criticism for looking slow and ineffective, but that narrative had flipped in a big way: with an average of 15 carries per game over the previous five, the column expected at least that and maybe more from a Pittsburgh team starting Mitch Trubisky that Thursday night. As an added bonus, Harris was also the preferred goal-line back, having scored in three of those games, and the Patriots' defense did not give up much.
