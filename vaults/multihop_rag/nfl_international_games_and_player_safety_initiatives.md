# The NFL's International Games And Its Concussion Initiatives

Amid the media frenzy surrounding the two lovebirds, other issues get thrown out of the window, Essentially Sports observed on 2023-10-25 — Kaepernick, who? Deflategate, what? Two of those overlooked efforts are its international expansion and its player-safety work.

The NFL had scheduled 5 games abroad that season, organized outside American borders with a view to garnering international appeal. It was also addressing the concussion controversies, having recently added both new concussion rules and a new $100 million initiative called "Play Smart, Play Safe" to make the sport safer for players — and, the article concludes, all the effort is showing.
