# Reliance Green Energy Investment Programme

Reliance Industries has committed a whopping ₹75,000 crore investment into building a comprehensive green energy and green material ecosystem, having identified green energy as its next growth opportunity as the world moves away from conventional fossil-based energy sources towards cleaner and sustainable ones.

The bulk of the money is going into one site: at Jamnagar the company is building a green energy complex spread over 5,000 acres of land which, according to the company, will be among the largest integrated renewable energy manufacturing facilities globally, at a cost of ₹60,000 crore. The balance ₹15,000 crore is being invested in partnerships, future technologies and value chain in order to build an integrated renewable energy ecosystem. Reliance has already made several investments in companies working on green energy technologies, such as battery technologies, solar cells and panels, clean mobility solutions and software tools for solar energy.

Business Line frames the investment case around market pricing rather than certainty of success: historically, any business RIL has put its weight behind has ended up being one of the largest players in the field and created a lot of shareholder wealth, but whether green energy will reap the same dividends as the telecom and retail forays is up in the air. The good thing for investors, the article argues, is that the market is hardly assigning any value to this business at current levels, so there is scope for significant upside down the years if the company repeats its earlier success.
