# Desmond Ridder Falcons Hot Seat

Yardbarker's Nov. 30, 2023 NFC hot-seat roundup named quarterback Desmond Ridder as the Atlanta Falcons' hottest seat, concluding that the clock was ticking on Ridder's time as an NFL starter even though he would finish the season leading the Falcons.

The grounds are the gap between opportunity and play: the Falcons had a golden path toward a division crown, but Ridder's two-interception performance against the Saints in Week 12 was another reminder Atlanta was not a legitimate contender with him. The warrant is that a team likely to get a home playoff game will not tolerate a quarterback who caps its ceiling. The claim is scoped — he keeps the job through that season, and the pressure lands afterward.
