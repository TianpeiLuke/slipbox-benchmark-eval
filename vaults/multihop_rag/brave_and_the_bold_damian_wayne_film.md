# Brave and the Bold (Damian Wayne Film)

Brave and the Bold, which takes its title from a silver age DC comic, will introduce Batman's biological son Damian — the fifth of Batman's many Robins — to the DCU, with a story inspired by Grant Morrison's interpretation of the character in the Batman and Son run of the Batman comic.

This will be Damian's first appearance in a live-action film, though the character has appeared in animated form in TV and movies.

Its continuity placement is explicit: the film will be separate from Matt Reeves' planned The Batman trilogy, and is meant to establish the new DCU continuity for Batman and Robin. Polygon reported this on 2023-10-24.
