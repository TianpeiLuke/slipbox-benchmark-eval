# 2024 Mock Draft: Five Wide Receivers Projected In Round 1

CBS Sports' 2024 NFL Mock Draft of 2023-11-17 projects five wide receivers in the first round. At Pick 3, New England takes Marvin Harrison Jr., Ohio State, a junior at 6'4" and 205 lbs, ranked 1st prospect and 1st at the position; the mock calls him arguably the best talent available in the 2024 NFL Draft but asks whether that matters if he is catching passes from Mac Jones or Bailey Zappe, adding that the Patriots have to be hopeful they find themselves in a position to draft one of the two top quarterback prospects. At Pick 15, Indianapolis takes Malik Nabers, LSU, a junior at 6'0" and 200 lbs, ranked 13th prospect and 2nd at the position, because Michael Pittman Jr. is scheduled to be a free agent at season's end and, if the two parties cannot come to terms, Nabers is a good partner for Josh Downs.

At Pick 18, Cincinnati takes Keon Coleman, Florida State, a junior at 6'4" and 215 lbs, ranked 16th prospect and 3rd at the position, since Tee Higgins and Cincinnati will likely part ways after the season, creating a need; Coleman is described as a big-bodied receiver who can win jump balls down the field. At Pick 22, Dallas takes Emeka Egbuka, Ohio State, a junior at 6'1" and 206 lbs, ranked 20th prospect and 4th at the position, retooling at wide receiver with CeeDee Lamb and Egbuka, because the franchise has struggled to find a reliable complement to Lamb since the departure of Amari Cooper. At Pick 27, Jacksonville takes Rome Odunze, Washington, a junior at 6'3" and 215 lbs, ranked 31st prospect and 6th at the position, clearing the deck and starting anew with Calvin Ridley and Odunze — though the Jaguars have to figure out their protection issues that offseason.
