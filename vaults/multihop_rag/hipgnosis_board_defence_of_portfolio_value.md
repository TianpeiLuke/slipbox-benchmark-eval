# Hipgnosis Board Defence of Portfolio Value

In its note to shareholders on 28 September 2023, the Hipgnosis Songs Fund board argued that the fund's share price understates the worth of what it owns. It reiterated its belief — and Hipgnosis Song Management's belief — that HSF owns "a unique portfolio of iconic, culturally significant songs that will deliver strong long-term value as they benefit from the structural tailwinds in the music industry".

The board's second ground is the adviser's method rather than the assets alone: "the [HSF] Board believes that the Investment Adviser's approach to Song Management should enable the Company to outperform the wider music market." Its evidence for that claim is the $440 million transaction — "This has been evidenced by the 44% total return, including right to income, transaction fees and expected taxes, realised since acquisition on the [$440 million] Transaction."

The warrant joining evidence to conclusion is a commitment rather than a further fact: "the Board and the Investment Adviser are committed to ensuring that this value is achieved for shareholders by a re-rating of the share price and are determined to deliver on the ongoing opportunity of the Company." The argument is therefore that quality of assets plus adviser performance, demonstrated by a realised 44% total return, justifies expecting the market to close the gap.
