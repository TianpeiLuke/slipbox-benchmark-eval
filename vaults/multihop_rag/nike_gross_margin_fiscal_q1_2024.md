# Nike Gross Margin Fiscal Q1 2024

Nike's gross margin fell about 0.1 percentage points to 44.2% during its fiscal first quarter ended August 2023, but came in higher than the 43.7% analysts had expected, according to StreetAccount. The company attributed the decline to higher product costs and currency exchange rates, and said those trends were offset by price increases, which contributed to the earnings beat. CNBC appended a correction noting that the gross margin fell 0.1 percentage points, an earlier version of the story having misstated the figure.

The result mattered because of what investors were watching. They had been laser focused on Nike's recovery in China, its relationship with its wholesale partners and how the resumption of student loan payments would impact sales, and were keen to see margins recover after bloated inventories, high promotions and supply chain woes contributed to lower profits over the previous few quarters.
