# Ketamine As An Anesthetic And A Dissociative Drug

Ketamine is primarily used as an anesthetic during surgical procedures, but in recent years it has been used as a remedy for treatment-resistant depression.

Its defining pharmacological character is dissociation: the Alcohol and Drug Foundation describes ketamine as a "dissociative drug," which means it causes people to feel "separated or detached" from their bodies or physical surroundings.

The drug's boundary case is its illicit use. While it is also used illegally as a recreational drug, experts say ketamine is generally not dangerous when used as prescribed.
