# The Dodgers' Pitching Need Versus Ohtani's 2024 Elbow Injury

Essentially Sports argued on 18 November 2023 that it would not be advisable for the Dodgers to hire Shohei Ohtani until 2025: the claim is that the pitching value that makes him attractive is unavailable in 2024, and the warrant is that the team already has the hitting he would otherwise provide.

The need grounds are real. The Dodgers have been in dire need of pitchers for a few seasons — their current highest strikeout total by a pitcher in a single postseason is 137 by Clayton Kershaw, followed by 119 by Bobby Miller and 117 by Julio Urias — so Ohtani's high strikeout rate of 167 that season would make him an invaluable addition.

The hiccup is availability, and the fallback is redundant. Ohtani will not be available to pitch throughout the 2024 season due to an elbow ligament injury he is recovering from, so given that unavoidable absence the article says it would not be advisable to hire him until 2025. Ohtani does have magnificent hitting form as well, but the team already has hitters like Freddie Freeman and Mookie Betts, who recorded 211 and 179 hits respectively that regular season.
