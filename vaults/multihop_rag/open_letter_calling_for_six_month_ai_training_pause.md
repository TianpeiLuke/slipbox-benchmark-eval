# Open Letter Calling For A Six-Month AI Training Pause

More than 1,100 signatories — including Elon Musk, Steve Wozniak and Tristan Harris of the Center for Humane Technology — signed an open letter calling on "all AI labs to immediately pause for at least 6 months the training of AI systems more powerful than GPT-4."

The grounds the letter offers are that contemporary AI systems are now becoming human-competitive at general tasks, and that this raises questions no one has answered: "Should we let machines flood our information channels with propaganda and untruth? Should we automate away all the jobs, including the fulfilling ones? Should we develop nonhuman minds that might eventually outnumber, outsmart, obsolete and replace us? Should we risk loss of control of our civilization?"

The warrant that joins those grounds to the demand for a pause is a claim about legitimacy and about the burden of proof: "Such decisions must not be delegated to unelected tech leaders," and "Powerful AI systems should be developed only once we are confident that their effects will be positive and their risks will be manageable." On that principle, capability that has already reached human-competitive general performance is exactly the point at which development must stop until confidence is established.
