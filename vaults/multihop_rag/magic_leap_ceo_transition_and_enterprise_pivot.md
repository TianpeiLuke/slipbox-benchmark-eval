# Magic Leap's CEO Transition And Enterprise Pivot

Magic Leap, an augmented reality hardware company, has replaced its CEO Peggy Johnson with Ross Rosenberg, marking the completion of its enterprise pivot.

Johnson joined Magic Leap in August 2020 and led the shift in business strategy.

Her successor previously served as an executive at Belden and First Solar, and will now guide the company through its next period, as it faces the daunting challenge of achieving profitability.
