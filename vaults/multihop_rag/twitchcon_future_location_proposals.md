# TwitchCon Future Location Proposals

Lowco, Moni and multiple other Twitch users commenting on Moni's UserVoice post all questioned why TwitchCon couldn't take place in San Diego again, TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-26. Some have floated hosting both a West Coast and East Coast TwitchCon, if capacity is a concern — a precedent exists in VidCon, which has traditionally been held in Anaheim, California and recently launched a second convention earlier this year in Baltimore, Maryland.

Lowco stated the criterion attendees want applied: "Ultimately, TwitchCon is a community celebration. We are there to meet up with our viewers and fellow streamers. Let's pick places that make it easy for us to gather."
