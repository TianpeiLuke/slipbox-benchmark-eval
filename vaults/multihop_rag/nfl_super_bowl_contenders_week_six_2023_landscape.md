# The NFL Super Bowl Contender Landscape, Week 6 Of 2023

The New York Times' Pick Six column of 16 October 2023 addressed the key questions facing the ten teams with the shortest Super Bowl odds — the Chiefs, Eagles, 49ers, Bills, Bengals, Cowboys, Ravens, Lions, Dolphins and Jaguars — with one-third of the 18-week regular season nearly complete.

The prompt for the exercise was a weekend that changed the picture at the top. The San Francisco 49ers entered Week 6 riding a 15-game winning streak and looking unstoppable with a young quarterback, Brock Purdy, who was making the game look easy; they exited with a 19-17 defeat at Cleveland, injuries to key players and, for the first time that season, a question mark or two. The previously unbeaten Philadelphia Eagles also lost ugly that Sunday.

The season to that point had produced a set of contradictions the column set out to test. The Miami Dolphins had set NFL records for offense, only to lose by four touchdowns in their lone game against a true contender. The Kansas City Chiefs had won with defense, sometimes in spite of their offense. The Buffalo Bills had incurred devastating injuries. For each of the ten teams the column ran through what the writer thinks and what NFL insiders have to say.
