# Amazon Prime Video As The Home Of Thursday Night Football

Amazon Prime serves as the home of "Thursday Night Football" and will be for the next decade: the NFL agreed a deal with the streaming giant making it the home of the package for 11 years, so anyone watching "TNF" nationally needs an Amazon Prime subscription. That was the arrangement described for the Oct. 19, 2023 Jaguars-Saints game.

There are two carve-outs. Viewers in the local markets of the two teams playing can watch without an Amazon subscription on local affiliates — for that game, CBS affiliate WJAX (Channel 47) carried it in the Jacksonville area and NBC affiliate WDSU (Channel 6) carried it in the New Orleans market. Viewers in Canada also did not need Amazon Prime to access "Thursday Night Football" in 2023; they could watch on DAZN.

Amazon's "Thursday Night Football" broadcast crew consisted of Al Michaels on play-by-play, Kirk Herbstreit on color commentary and Kaylee Hartung as sideline reporter, the trio in their second season working together.
