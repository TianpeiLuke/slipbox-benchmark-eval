# Octopath Traveler 2

Octopath Traveler 2, developed by Square Enix and Acquire for Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, and Windows PC, is ranked 13 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31.

Polygon's frame is the first game's near miss: the original Octopath Traveler was as enjoyable to play as it was painful — enjoyable because so much of it kicked ass, painful because so much of it dragged the positive aspects down — standing on the precipice of excellence without quite crossing the line.

The sequel leaps across that boundary. In place of the original's repetitive level design, monotonous narrative structure, and sometimes awkward characterization, critic M. Mahardy writes, it demonstrates an expert ability to challenge your expectations at every turn. The general goal is still to recruit eight playable characters and follow each of their separate plot threads to their conclusions, participating in turn-based battles and side quests along the way, but those plots vary greatly from character to character, and players can, if they choose, see a handful of characters through several major plot points before recruiting the whole gang — finely toeing the line between the comfort food-esque repetition of the best JRPGs and the subversive nature of great genre storytelling.
