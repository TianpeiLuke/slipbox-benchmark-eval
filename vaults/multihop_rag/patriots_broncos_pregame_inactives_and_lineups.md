# Patriots-Broncos Pregame Inactives And Lineups

New England ruled out seven players for the Christmas Eve 2023 game at Denver, including special teams captain Matthew Slater with a hamstring injury, while the Broncos listed seven inactives of their own.

The Patriots inactives were Slater (hamstring), DT Sam Roberts, third/emergency quarterback Nathan Rourke, WR JuJu Smith-Schuster (ankle), RB Rhamondre Stevenson (ankle), safety Jabrill Peppers (hamstring) and Hunter Henry (knee). The Broncos inactives were TE Nate Adkins, OLB Nik Bonitto, QB Ben DiNucci, C Alex Forsyth, DE Elijah Garcia, OLB Thomas Incoom and S JL Skinner. In warmups, Tyquan Thornton was aligned in Slater's spot as a gunner on the punt coverage unit, and the Patriots offensive line appeared to be LT Vederian Lowe, LG James Ferentz, C David Andrews, RG Sidy Sow and RT Mike Onwenu. Nathan Rourke impressed in the pregame with some one-handed grabs — Mike Reiss posted that Rourke and Mac Jones made one-handed grabs in warmups — and Bill Belichick arrived at Mile High.
