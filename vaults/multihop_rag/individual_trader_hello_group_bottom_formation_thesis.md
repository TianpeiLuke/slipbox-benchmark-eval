# Individual Trader's Hello Group Bottom-Formation Thesis

Individual Trader, a Seeking Alpha author, argued in the December 3, 2023 week-ahead that MOMO's recent downtrend may be concluding, pointing to signs of a bottom forming, and that the stock is an appealing investment option.

The argument runs on two distinct kinds of ground. The first is technical: signs of a bottom forming, offered as evidence that the recent downtrend may be ending rather than continuing. The second is valuation and income: the analyst added that the combination of cheap earnings and a high dividend yield makes MOMO appealing. The warrant joining them is that a stock trading on cheap earnings with a high yield is worth owning once the price trend stops working against it — the technical read supplies the timing, the valuation read supplies the reason to hold.

Scope conditions: this is one Seeking Alpha author's view as relayed on December 3, 2023, and it is hedged in the original — the downtrend "may" be concluding, with signs of a bottom rather than a confirmed one.
