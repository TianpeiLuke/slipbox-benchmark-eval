# India-Israel Bilateral Trade Exposure In 2022-23

In 2022-23, Israel's share in exports from India stood at 1.87 per cent, with India shipping goods worth around $8 billion to Israel; petroleum products accounted for 65 per cent of those exports, followed by gems and jewellery at 15 per cent and electrical machinery at 3 per cent. These figures were reported by Business Line on 14 October 2023 as the trade-exposure channel through which the Israel-Hamas conflict could reach India even where broader market impact is limited.

Two disruption conditions are attached to the observation. Trade can be adversely impacted if operations at the Israeli ports are disrupted. Separately, fertiliser movement from Israel's Port of Ashdod accounts for 3 per cent of global potash supply, and any disruption on that front will also be a dampener.
