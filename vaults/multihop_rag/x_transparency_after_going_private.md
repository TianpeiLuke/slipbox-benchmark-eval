# Platform Transparency After X Went Private

A DSA investigation would give the public significantly more transparency about how X is used and abused, beyond its direct regulatory purpose.

The grounds are that since Musk took Twitter private, the company no longer produces quarterly earnings reports and is not held to account as closely as a result. Regulatory proceedings would therefore become a substitute disclosure channel for information that public-company reporting previously supplied.
