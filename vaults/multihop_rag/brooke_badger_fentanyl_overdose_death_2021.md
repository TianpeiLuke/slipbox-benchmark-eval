# Brooke Badger's Fentanyl Overdose Death, 2021

Brooke Badger, 17, died at her California home on 17 June 2021 after taking what she believed was Roxicet bought from a dealer on Snapchat; the substance was a counterfeit pill that held a lethal dose of fentanyl.

Her mother, Hanh Badger, was working from home that morning. She went to the kitchen to grab a second cup of coffee and noticed her daughter's bedroom door was still shut, and found Brooke pale and motionless in bed. The sheriff arrived soon after and immediately administered Naloxone, a nasal spray that reverses the effects of an opioid overdose. Badger, a pharmacist, was confused: Brooke was a talented student who could not wait to begin college that fall. "She had the whole world in her hands," Badger said through tears. "There was no way in my mind that [Brooke] had died of a drug overdose."

The evidence of what happened came from the account itself. In the ensuing days, Badger's husband and son gained access to Brooke's computer and with it her Snapchat account, where they found screenshots of what looked like a menu of narcotics and conversations with a drug dealer showing that Brooke had purchased what she believed to be Roxicet, a prescription medication containing acetaminophen and oxycodone typically prescribed for pain relief.
