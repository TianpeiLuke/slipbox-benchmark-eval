# Mandy (Panos Cosmatos)

Mandy is Panos Cosmatos' 2018 action horror film starring Nicolas Cage, Andrea Riseborough, and Linus Roache. It features what many considered one of Cage's best performances of the past decade, and — as revealed in a recent episode of The Late Show With Stephen Colbert, per Polygon's 2023-10-25 write-up — is one of Cage's favorite movies he has ever worked on.

The story follows a lumberjack who embarks on a conquest of revenge to kill the monstrous Gwar-like cult members who murdered his beloved girlfriend, played by Andrea Riseborough. The film has been praised for its psychedelic brutality, over-the-top performances, and a prog-rock-inspired score by the late composer Jóhann Jóhannsson.

Polygon's Toussaint Egan pitches it to readers yearning for more Nicolas Cage-fueled insanity after Renfield and The Unbearable Weight of Massive Talent, itemizing the attractions: Cage drinking vodka in his underwear while wailing hysterically in a pink-tiled bathroom, going head-to-head against a hulking monster named "Fuck Pig," snorting cocaine off a glass table, and getting into a chainsaw fight. Egan's verdict is that Mandy is a blast and anyone who has not seen it is missing out.
