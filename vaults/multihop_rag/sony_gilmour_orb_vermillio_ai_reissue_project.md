# The Sony, Gilmour, The Orb And Vermillio AI Reissue Project

Kooker cited as an example a recent generative AI-powered project around a reissue and remix of an album; although he did not name it specifically, Sony Music Entertainment had recently announced a generative AI project with The Orb and David Gilmour, a partnership between Sony Music Entertainment, Legacy Recordings and AI company Vermillio.

"These artists are known for their cutting-edge experimentation in music," Kooker said. He described how the deal came together: "About the time that we started the discussion with this artist, we had begun informal talks with a generative AI start-up company whose business model focused on working with IP rightsholders the 'right way'. In other words, they respect intellectual property rights and want to work with rights holders in ways that enhance and protect the copyrighted works."
