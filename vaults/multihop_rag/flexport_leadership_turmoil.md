# Flexport Leadership Turmoil

Dave Clark, the former Amazon executive who was ousted as CEO of Flexport just a year into the job, fired back at the company's founder and board, calling recent reporting on the logistics company "deeply concerning."

Clark made the comments on Monday of the week covered by TechCrunch's October 7, 2023 Week in Review, in a lengthy post on the social media site X. The post followed a CNBC report that had provided new information about his last days at Flexport. Flexport is described as a freight forwarding and customs brokerage startup valued at $8 billion.
