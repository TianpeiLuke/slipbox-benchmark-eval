# Pittsburgh Pirates' December 2023 Signings

The Pirates made three December 2023 additions: Rowdy Tellez on a one-year, $3.2 million deal (Dec. 12), left-hander Martin Perez on a one-year, $8 million deal (Dec. 18), and franchise icon Andrew McCutchen on a one-year, $5 million deal (Dec. 19).

FanSided's Robert Murray reported the Tellez agreement, which included an additional $800,000 in incentives; Tellez hit 35 home runs as a centerpiece of the Brewers' lineup in 2022 but took a significant step back in 2023 after starting the season on a roll, and between first base and the DH role the Pirates figured to give him near-everyday playing time in hopes his power bat found its form. The Pittsburgh Post-Gazette's Jason Mackey reported the Perez deal as Pittsburgh continued to add to its rotation: the former Ranger was used as a swing man in 2023, making 20 starts and 35 total appearances with a 4.45 ERA, 93 strikeouts and 49 walks in 141.2 innings en route to winning a World Series ring. The Pirates also added another left-hander who pitched in 2023 for an AL West team, Marco Gonzales, though he had an offseason stop in Atlanta in the middle. Mackey also reported the McCutchen re-signing, bringing back a franchise icon after a solid 2023 return to Pittsburgh in which he slashed .256/.378/.397 with 12 home runs in 112 games — his first season in Pittsburgh since 2017.
