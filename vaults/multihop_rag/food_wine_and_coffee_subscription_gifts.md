# Food, Wine And Coffee Subscription Gifts

For the diehard foodie, wine connoisseur, or caffeine addict, The Verge's last-minute guide lists subscriptions and gift cards spanning wine clubs, kitchen stores, ice cream, coffee and food delivery.

Eater's Wine Club is listed at $70: a subscription that automatically sends either two or four bottles of wine chosen by an Eater expert every month, with past boxes showcasing wines from all over the world, including holiday-friendly picks like Domaine Lattard Gamay and Milan Nestarec Youngster Rosé. A Sur La Table gift card (Sur La Table, Kroger, Staples) suits the beloved chef in your life, usable for kitchen purchases and for online cooking classes where live instructors help students make everything from chicken piccata to tiramisu, starting at $29 per household with each class running 90 to 120 minutes. For a sweet tooth, Goldbelly's monthly ice cream subscription delivers up to six pints of ice cream or 24 ice cream sandwiches a month sourced from small creameries all over the country, though it is pricey — a three-month plan goes for an eye-watering $299.85. A Starbucks gift card (Amazon, Best Buy, or Target) covers the caffeine fix whenever and wherever, since it feels like there's a Starbucks everywhere you look. And a gift card to a food delivery service like DoorDash (Amazon, Target) means a foodie can nab crab rangoons from their favorite Chinese restaurant without leaving home.
