# WIRED's Pre-Black Friday 2023 Deal Window

WIRED's October 19, 2023 roundup covers the lull between sales events: with the latest Amazon-created Prime-themed sale day behind us and Black Friday slowly approaching, the deals world feels a bit like Pippin waiting on the edge of battle.

The point of the list is that the lull is not empty — there are still sales to score while waiting for winter to come — and its scope is the best deals right now on TVs, gaming gear, and other electronics.
