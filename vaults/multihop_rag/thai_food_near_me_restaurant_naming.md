# Thai Food Near Me: A Restaurant Named For A Search Query

Thai Food Near Me is a New York restaurant named after a literal Google search, betting it can bring in customers with the power of SEO — the practice of making a business, website, or content more findable in search engine results. The defining feature is what it optimizes for: the restaurant is built for the digital platforms diners use to find places nearby, not for the person walking past on the street or getting a recommendation from a friend.

Even to its owners the name seemed silly and unserious, and some of them were embarrassed to tell friends about it at all for fear of it sounding too ridiculous. Teddy Jirapraphanan, one of the four owners, thought back to his own experience using Google to find restaurants: "Everywhere I go, I'm craving Thai food. I have to search 'Thai food near me' all the time." He is exactly the kind of customer the restaurant hopes to scoop up. His account of why the name works is competitive: "When you have a million restaurants close by, you will be in the bottom [of rankings] if it's a random name. But [when] we used Thai Food Near Me, people started knowing us." Customers told him they had been searching for the exact phrase and stumbling upon the restaurant.

The Verge, writing on October 26th, 2023, framed the restaurant — located on a busy street in the Murray Hill neighborhood of Manhattan — as a small but powerful symbol of Google's far-reaching impact on businesses over the past two decades and of the lengths owners will go to in optimizing their operations for the company's platforms. The name is both notable and obvious: anyone who has spent time searching for things online understands the reference immediately, and the turn is that 25 years after Google Search first arrived, the name says the quiet part out loud.
