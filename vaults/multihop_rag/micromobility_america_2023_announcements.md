# Micromobility America 2023 Announcements

The trade show Micromobility America took place in the week before TechCrunch's 2023-10-22 roundup, which listed two announcements from it. The team at Ride Review launched the Rider's Choice Awards again, for those who want to vote, with winners to be announced January 25.

The folks behind Micromobility America also launched the Electric Rider Alliance, a 501c6 membership organization set up to "create a level playing field in the transportation industry for the small electric vehicle ecosystem through standards, lobbying and governance."
