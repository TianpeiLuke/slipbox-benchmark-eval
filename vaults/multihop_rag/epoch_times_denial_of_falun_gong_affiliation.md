# The Epoch Times' Denial Of A Falun Gong Affiliation

Epoch Times representatives deny that the paper is affiliated with Falun Gong, and have previously defended it against critical reporting by labelling accounts of its conservative mission and its penchant for misinformation "smears" — a denial that attacks the very premise on which the reporting rests, that the newspaper is an arm of the religious movement.

The article rebuts that denial with the organizational and financial record. The Epoch Times' board members and most of its staff are Falun Gong practitioners. The nonprofits behind The Epoch Times and Friends of Falun Gong, the movement's advocacy organization, share executives and provide grants and services to each other, according to tax filings. And the newspaper, together with a digital production company and the heavily advertised dance troupe Shen Yun, makes up a nonprofit network that the leader of the religious movement himself calls "our media."

The denial also comes without direct engagement: neither the chairman nor the editor-in-chief of The Epoch Times returned multiple requests for comment.
