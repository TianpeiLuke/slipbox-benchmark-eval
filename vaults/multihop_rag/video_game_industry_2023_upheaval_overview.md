# Video Game Industry 2023: An Upheaval Overview

Engadget's year-in-review found that 2023 was a year of upheaval in video games, with three factors molding the landscape — consolidation, layoffs and unionization — each feeding into the others, so that the industry shrank even as it grew financially.

The upheaval was not all down to Microsoft's lengthy acquisition of Activision, Blizzard and King. While Xbox executives were defending the legality of a $69 billion deal that would create the third-largest video game studio in the world, smaller companies were firing staff and shutting down entire teams, even amid fervent collective-bargaining efforts. Engadget's summary of the past 12 months: "It's been a wild ride."
