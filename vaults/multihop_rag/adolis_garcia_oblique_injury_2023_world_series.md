# Adolis Garcia's Oblique Injury Ends His 2023 World Series

Adolis Garcia left Game 3 of the 2023 World Series on Oct. 30 with an oblique injury and, before Game 4 the following day, Texas learned his season was over — he was taken off the World Series roster with a moderate oblique strain and replaced by Ezequiel Duran.

The injury surfaced during Game 3. At 10:19 p.m., in the bottom of the eighth, the Sporting News reported Garcia was leaving the game with an oblique injury, with Travis Jankowski taking his place in right field, and posted video of him coming out following an apparent injury.

The roster consequences unfolded across Oct. 31. At 5:10 p.m. Garcia was not in the lineup for Texas, with Jankowski again taking his place, although Garcia told ESPN's Buster Olney in the clubhouse that he was "good." At 6:25 p.m. Bruce Bochy said he was anticipating making a roster decision on Garcia that night, and that Garcia had a moderate oblique strain. At 7:07 p.m. it was reported that Garcia was off the World Series roster and his season was over, with Ezequiel Duran added in his place. Texas nonetheless did not miss a beat with a talented lineup, erupting for five runs in the second inning and five more in the third to bludgeon Arizona's bullpen and take a 10-0 lead in the blink of an eye.
