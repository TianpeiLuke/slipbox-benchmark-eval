# Crucial MX500 Internal SSD

The Crucial MX500 internal drive was on sale for $46 during October Prime Day, and was still at that price after the event ended.

It has been an Engadget favorite for a while thanks to its sequential read speeds of 560MB/s.

Its standard 2.5-inch design should make it easy to swap in for your desktop's or laptop's used-up drive.
