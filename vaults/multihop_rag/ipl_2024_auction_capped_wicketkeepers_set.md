# IPL 2024 Auction: The Capped Wicketkeepers Set

The capped wicketkeepers set at the IPL 2024 auction produced only two sales, both at Rs 50 lakh: K.S. Bharat to KKR and Tristan Stubbs to Delhi Capitals. Phil Salt, Josh Inglis and Kusal Mendis all went unsold.

The sequence ran: at 3:08 PM IST Tristan Stubbs went to Delhi Capitals for Rs 50 L and Phil Salt went unsold; at 3:10 PM IST KS Bharat came up next and went to KKR for Rs 50 L; at 3:12 PM IST the Australian Josh Inglis was next at a base price of Rs 2 Cr and remained unsold; and at 3:13 PM IST Kusal Mendis also went unsold.
