# Sporting News' Frisco Bowl Pick: UTSA, Even Laying A Big Number

Sporting News picked UTSA to beat Marshall in the Frisco Bowl, under the headline that Frank Harris would lead the Roadrunners past the Herd, per its Dec. 18, 2023 preview.

The grounds are tempo and efficiency. Even with the transfer portal hitting this game hard, the UTSA offense would be too much for Marshall to keep up with: the Roadrunners push the pace in a way that Marshall cannot replicate, and the Thundering Herd are not efficient enough to make the most of what few scoring opportunities they will have. The Thundering Herd could find themselves down several scores by the end of the first frame if Pennington turns the ball over, as he is prone to do.

The warrant is a talent-mismatch generalization: UTSA is a well-oiled machine against teams with similar or worse talent, and Marshall is in that latter category. The stated reservation is the size of the spread — "it's a big number to lay" — but the pick was still UTSA.
