# Diamondbacks Win World Series Game 2

The Arizona Diamondbacks won Game 2 of the 2023 World Series on the back of an impressive pitching performance by Merrill Kelly, as reported at 03.08 GMT on 29 October 2023, tying the series 1-1 heading to Arizona for Monday's Game 3.

The Guardian's closing assessment was that a 1-1 split is the ideal situation for the casual fan because it usually portends a longer series — but a less than ideal one for the Rangers, whose weaknesses were exposed on the day and who knew their two-game home stand could have gone worse, having needed plenty of late-inning drama just to pull off a come-from-behind win in Game 1.

What happened next was anybody's guess: the World Series headed to Arizona for Monday's Game 3, with the Rangers' Max Scherzer and the Diamondbacks' Brandon Pfaadt penciled in as the probable starters.
