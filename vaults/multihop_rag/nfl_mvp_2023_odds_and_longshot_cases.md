# 2023 NFL MVP Favorites And The Longshot Cases For Goff And Stroud

Brock Purdy, Dak Prescott and Jalen Hurts were the current favorites for 2023 NFL MVP after Week 13, with C.J. Stroud and Jared Goff the longshots Sporting News argued bettors should consider, per its Dec. 5, 2023 awards-odds piece.

The reasoning for the board is that the MVP often ends up being someone, typically the quarterback, from one of the top-seeded teams, which is why Purdy, Prescott and Hurts sat atop betting boards after Week 13. But by that same logic, Sporting News argued, Jared Goff should be in the conversation: he had played well that season, his Lions were 9-3, described by Sporting News as poised to win the NFC South and in contention for the No. 1 seed in the conference — and compared to the 49ers, Cowboys and Eagles, Goff and the Lions may have had the easiest schedule to finish the season.

The case for C.J. Stroud rests on production and value. He led the league in passing yards, was one of four quarterbacks who had started the whole season with a passer rating over 100, and his touchdown-to-interception ratio was the best in the NFL at 4 to 1. As for the value of his play, Stroud had taken one of the worst teams in the NFL — 11 total wins over the previous three seasons — and turned it into a playoff contender and potential division champion.
