# Hipgnosis Songs Fund NAV-to-Share-Price Discount

As of 14 September 2023, Hipgnosis Songs Fund's market cap stood at approximately GBP £1.125 billion, which represented a 40% discount to the firm's 'operative Net Asset Value' of USD $2.316 billion as of the end of March that year. That operative NAV was calculated by an independent valuer, Citrin Cooperman, using a discount rate of 8.5%.

Music Business Worldwide framed this gap, rather than trading performance, as the source of shareholder frustration. The fundamental revenue performance of HSF did not appear to be under scrutiny from shareholders: the company posted its best-ever revenues in the 12 months to the end of March, while streaming 'pro-forma' revenue was up 14.8% year-on-year in calendar 2022. Where HSF shareholders — as well as its management — were finding frustration was in the gulf between the operative NAV and the company's value on the public market, that is, its share price.
