# Spill's Tea Party Feature And Celebrity Users

At AfroTech, Spill CEO Alphonzo Terrell revealed a new app feature called Tea Party, which is similar to Instagram Live; the first user to host a Tea Party was actress Kerry Washington.

Terrell took the AfroTech stage twice, once to interview Mitch and Freida Kapor, the founders of Kapor Capital, and a second time for the Tea Party reveal. His next dream Tea Party host is Keke Palmer, who is already on Spill, as are other cultural icons including Questlove, Ava DuVernay and Janelle Monáe. "Tea Parties reflect the communities that we have always spoken very clearly that we are here to center," Kenya Parham, Spill's Global VP of Community and Partnerships, told TechCrunch. "Black communities, queer communities, women, non-binary and femme stories are all the way at the forefront, and the community is just eating it up."

Washington joined the platform organically; Spill has "not paid for a single acquisition to date," Parham said, which speaks to the influence and respect the app's leadership has established in the entertainment and tech industries. Before Spill, Terrell led marketing teams at HBO and Showtime, and Parham ran her own strategic communications consultancy working with a laundry list of major production studios. Spill has already wooed advertising partners including Netflix, Lionsgate, VH1, Showtime and Sony Pictures.
