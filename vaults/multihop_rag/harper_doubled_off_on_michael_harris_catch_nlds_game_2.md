# The Play: Harper Doubled Off To End NLDS Game 2

The whole controversy begins with the final play of NLDS Game 2, in which Bryce Harper was doubled off at first to end the game. Harper led off the top of the ninth in a one-run game with a walk. J.T. Realmuto flew out to center for the first out, and on the next play Nick Castellanos lifted a deep fly into right-center that appeared to be extra bases — if Harper took off, he would score the tying run.

The problem for the Phillies was that Michael Harris made a leaping grab up against the wall to rob Castellanos of the hit. Harper had well since rounded second on his way to third. The throw from Harris missed both cut-off men by second, but Austin Riley ran in, scooped the ball and made an impressive throw to Matt Olson to nab Harper and end the game.
