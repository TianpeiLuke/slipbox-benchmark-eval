# Cooper Simpson AFL Draft Prospect 2023

Cooper Simpson, profiled in Kevin Sheehan's top 40 for the 2023 AFL Draft as reported by The Roar on Nov. 15, 2023, is an ultra professional midfielder with pace, composure and great finishing skills. State: Victoria Country. State League Club: Dandenong Stingrays. Community Club: Mt Martha. Date of Birth: 13/02/05. Height: 181cm.

He also has a neat step through traffic and runs hard both offensively and defensively, and has shown the ability to perform when challenged and in the big moments when the game is on the line. He started the season in good touch with the Dandenong Stingrays in the Coates Talent League but suffered an ankle injury in Victoria Country's opening game of the National Championships and was unable to play any further matches at that level. He made an impressive return for the final four matches with the Stingrays, including 28 disposals against the Tasmania Devils, which reminded scouts of his class and AFL potential. Weapon: Composure and step through congestion. AFL player comparison: Adam Cerra (Carlton).
