# Are Dillon Danis And Savannah Montano Still Together? The Available Signals

As of the article's publication on 2023-10-07, Essentially Sports reported it was not completely clear whether Dillon Danis and Savannah Montano were still together. They had made no claims of a breakup and had been silent about their relationship with each other. The only thing that had changed was Savannah's Instagram feed: she had stopped posting pictures with Danis, which were quite prevalent back in 2020.

The other hint pointing toward a breakup came from Danis's first press conference for the Logan Paul fight, where Logan Paul brought up the topic of Montano and Danis pointedly mentioned their breakup. Paul said, "Yo you've been so salty ever since Savannah rejected your a** to go hook up with real athletes. You don't gotta take that s**t out on me because my brother hooked up with your ex." Danis scoffed at Maverick's claim and clapped back, "At least I got rid of the sl*t, you're marrying one." The article reads that exchange as telling fans that Danis is no longer together with Montano, and suggests fans could look out for her attendance at Danis's first-ever boxing debut on October 14 at the AO Arena, Manchester; although there were rumors about Danis pulling out, the fight still appeared to be going ahead, and her presence at ringside may make things even more complicated.
