# Apple AirPods Pro, Second Generation

The second-generation AirPods Pro dropped to $189 for Prime Day. The USB-C version was down from $239 — $50 off at Amazon, and $60 off Apple's list price, a new low — and the Lightning version was available for the same price.

Apple made a ton of small but noticeable improvements to these models, so they have better sound quality, noise cancelation and Transparency Mode. Engadget's verdict is that there remains no competition if you have an iPhone and live within the Apple ecosystem — these are the best earbuds to get if you fall into that category, and they are the "best for iOS" pick in its buying guide. Both models deliver strong ANC, a superb transparency mode and a pleasingly warm sound, along with Apple-specific features including hands-free Siri access, Find My tracking and fast pairing with other Apple devices. A recent update added an "Adaptive Audio" mode that dynamically blends ANC and transparency based on your surroundings, and the new USB-C model comes with improved dust resistance alongside the new charging port.

The caveats are ecosystem lock-in and endurance: you really need to be committed to the Apple ecosystem to get the most out of these, and the six-hour battery life is just OK. Engadget gave the AirPods Pro a score of 88 last year.
