# Justice Hill As A Week 6 2023 PPR Sneaky Sleeper At Tennessee

CBS Sports listed Justice Hill as a "Sneaky Sleeper in PPR" for Week 6 of the 2023 season at Tennessee: the claim is that the call is a little gutsy because Hill has been inefficient for much of the year, and the warrant is that the Titans' recent vulnerability to zone-scheme runs matches the exact scheme in which Hill is Baltimore's best back, while his high-value touch share supplies the floor.

The opponent grounds are the trigger. Tennessee's run defense started the season strong but got smashed the previous week by Zack Moss and the Colts to the tune of 6.3 yards per carry and four explosive runs, having allowed two in total before that game, with Moss beating a stacked box on a 56-yard touchdown scamper; even when Anthony Richardson was off the field the Colts averaged 4.7 yards per run against Tennessee. Across their past two games, against the Bengals and Colts, the Titans allowed at least 2.79 yards before contact per rush in each and were decimated by zone-scheme runs, conceding at least 6.0 rushing yards per carry to running backs. Baltimore has called zone runs half as often as power runs even though the Ravens average half a yard more per carry on zone, so the column argues they'd be smart to do more of it this week — and Hill has the team's best rushing average and highest rush EPA on zone runs.

The role grounds supply the PPR floor. In the three games in which he played the most snaps — Weeks 1, 2 and 5 — Hill played 24 of a possible 43 third-down snaps (56%) and 14 of 20 snaps inside the 10; he is smaller than Gus Edwards but faster and more versatile, which has earned him extended opportunities for high-value touches. The breakdown lists Baltimore at Tennessee, the Ravens favoured by 4, an over/under of 41, an opponent ranked 14th versus running backs, 7.5 projected points, a 29th running back ranking, and year-to-date totals of 115 rushing yards, seven receptions, 25 receiving yards, three touchdowns and 9.3 fantasy points per game. The hoped-for outcome is modest and explicit: just over 12 PPR points for the second week in a row, achieved by the Ravens outsmarting the big size of the Titans' defensive line and using Hill for his speed along the edges and in the passing game. The author would take that chance over low-upside running backs like Gus Edwards, Antonio Gibson and Jonathan Taylor, adding that Taylor is low-upside for now.
