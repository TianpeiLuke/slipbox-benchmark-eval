# Annie Wu Henry On Gen Z Authenticity

Annie Wu Henry, the digital strategist behind Fetterman's TikTok stardom, argues that younger voters who are very online tend to value raw, unfiltered authenticity — and that they don't see it in many older politicians, and especially not in the Republican party. Her claim, given to TechCrunch for its 2023-10-02 piece, explains the gap in behavioural terms rather than technical ones.

Her grounds are what the platforms themselves reward. "Gen Z does not put up with bullshit," Henry said. "That's so much of what platforms like TikTok and Twitch cater to, and why they thrive on those platforms, because you can just pull out your phone and be talking while you're getting ready with the worst angle possible. Republicans in general, it goes against so much of how they act otherwise and young people know that." The warrant is detectability: the informal, low-production register these platforms select for is incompatible with how the party otherwise presents itself, and young users notice the mismatch.
