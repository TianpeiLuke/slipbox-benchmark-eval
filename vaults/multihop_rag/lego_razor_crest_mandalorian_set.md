# LEGO Razor Crest Mandalorian Set

The massive 6,187-piece Lego version of Din Djarin's iconic ride from The Mandalorian was matching its Black Friday price on Amazon as of 2023-12-09, on sale for $419.99, down from $599.99.

Polygon calls it an ideal gift for any fan of the show, brimming with detail such as a full-size cargo compartment complete with carbonite prisoner storage. Besides minifigs of Mando and Grogu, the set includes a Lego version of Kuiil riding on his Blurrg.
