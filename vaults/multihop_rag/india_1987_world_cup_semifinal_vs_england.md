# India's 1987 World Cup Semi-Final Against England

India lost the 1987 semi-final to England by 35 runs at the Wankhede Stadium, Mumbai, on 5 November. Four years after 1983 the World Cup was being played outside England for the first time and India were hosting it, and in a coincidence India were up against England in the semi-finals once again.

England's Man-of-the-Match skipper Graham Gooch smashed 115 runs in 136 balls to lay a solid foundation for his team's fighting total of 254 for 6 wickets. India, one of the favourites to win the tournament, were very much on the path to victory while Mohammad Azharuddin, who top-scored with 64 runs, was at the crease; but with the fall of his wicket the Indian lower order collapsed like a house of placards.
