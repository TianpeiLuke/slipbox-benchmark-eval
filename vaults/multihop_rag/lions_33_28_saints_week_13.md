# Lions 33-28 Saints, Week 13 2023

The Detroit Lions beat the reeling New Orleans Saints 33-28 in Week 13 of the 2023 season to improve to 9-3, their best record through 12 games since they went 10-2 in 1962.

Sam LaPorta had career highs of nine catches for 140 yards, including an early touchdown and a crucial first-down snag in the final minutes. The Saints lost quarterback Derek Carr in the fourth quarter to back, shoulder and head injuries; Carr was hurt on a penalized hit by Bruce Irvin. New Orleans fell to 5-7, behind first-place Atlanta in the NFC South.
