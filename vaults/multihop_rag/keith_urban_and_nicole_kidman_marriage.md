# Keith Urban And Nicole Kidman's Marriage

Keith Urban and the Hollywood actress Nicole Kidman, both Australian, met in 2005 and had been married for 17 years as of the November 2023 article; they are parents to Sunday Rose and Faith Margaret. Kidman had previously been married to the actor Tom Cruise, with whom she adopted two children over the course of that marriage.

The couple's stated rule is a priority ordering rather than a fixed schedule. Urban, who acknowledged that managing his career alongside his personal life is not always easy, told Fox News Digital ahead of the 2023 ACM Awards that "it's always family first," and described the balance as something maintained by correction rather than achieved: "It's balanced, so it means it goes out of balance sometimes, and we just put it back in balance. It's never perfectly in balance, but we get it back on track."
