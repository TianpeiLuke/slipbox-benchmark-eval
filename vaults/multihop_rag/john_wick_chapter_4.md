# John Wick: Chapter 4

John Wick: Chapter 4 is Chad Stahelski's fourth entry in the saga about the boogeyman of assassins in a world full of them, starring Keanu Reeves, Donnie Yen, and Ian McShane. Polygon ranked it 12 on its best movies of 2023 list of 2023-10-07 and listed it on Starz or for digital rental/purchase.

Polygon's premise summary is that John Wick has only ever wanted to fuck off and retire, and no one seems to get the message. The film reaches a staggering crescendo, somehow managing to run faster and hit harder than three previous movies dedicated to constant escalation.

Facing off against action legends Donnie Yen and Scott Adkins, Keanu Reeves pushes John Wick harder and farther than before, in a film that critic JR describes as raging against the untouchably wealthy and their expectation that the world be in their service.
