# Party Animals

Party Animals, developed by Recreate Games for Windows PC and Xbox Series X, is ranked 46 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31. Critic Julia Lee's summary is that the Gang Beasts-esque wiggly physics mixed with the cute characters makes for a perfect party game of fluffy fighting.

She says she does not know how Recreate Games managed to render some of the cutest animals she has ever seen.

Nor does she know how they made her completely OK with picking up those cute animals and flinging them into black holes, poison clouds, or freezing tundras: the second the match starts, all those cute fluffy corgis, rabbits, kitties, and ducks become her enemies, and she will beat them with a bat until they cannot wake up anymore without thinking twice about it.
