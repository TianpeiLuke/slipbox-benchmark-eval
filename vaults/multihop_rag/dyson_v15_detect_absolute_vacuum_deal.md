# Dyson V15 Detect Absolute Vacuum Black Friday Deal

Walmart's Black Friday 2023 deal on the Dyson V15 Detect Absolute saved 27% and made it $100 cheaper than the Dyson V12 Detect Slim, which has a smaller dust bin and less suction power, Mashable reported on 25 November 2023.

The V15 Detect is a cordless stick vacuum with a fancy LCD screen and a bright built-in laser that illuminates tiny crumbs, pet hairs and dust particles on floors, "so you can be sure you're actually cleaning them thoroughly".

The premium Absolute variant adds a shiny gold finish, HEPA filtration, and an extra dusting/crevice tool.
