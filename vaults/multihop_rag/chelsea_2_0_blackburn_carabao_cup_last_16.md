# Chelsea 2-0 Blackburn Carabao Cup Last 16

Chelsea beat Blackburn 2-0 at Stamford Bridge to secure a Carabao Cup last-eight spot, with goals from Benoît Badiashile and a rejuvenated Raheem Sterling, The Guardian reported on Nov. 1, 2023.

Even 2-0 up and cruising, Mauricio Pochettino was a bundle of nerves throughout what turned out to be a comfortable victory in the end — no wonder he spends most of his time watching Chelsea on the edge of the touchline with his hands clasped tightly in his pockets and a tense expression. Blackburn certainly caused problems even after the hosts made their intentions clear from the kick-off, when Nicolas Jackson forced a corner with a blocked shot. Travis was very lucky not to concede a penalty when he slid in against Sterling after 10 minutes, with no VAR to overrule what looked like a clear and obvious error from referee Tim Robinson. It took a good save from Leopold Wahlstedt to deny James, but Blackburn then enjoyed a good period of possession in their opponents' half; James was inches away midway through the first half but his shot just eluded the far post, before Enzo Fernández forced Wahlstedt to palm away his effort. The Swedish goalkeeper was at fault when Chelsea finally scored, palming Gallagher's cross — after he had combined with Palmer — straight to the feet of Badiashile, who calmly sidefooted home. Blackburn's supporters then felt aggrieved when a Callum Brittain cross struck Gallagher's trailing arm, but once again Robinson waved away strong appeals for a penalty.

The visitors showed no intention of surrendering when Harry Leonard caught Axel Disasi napping and raced onto a long ball at the start of the second half, only to curl his shot wide; not for the first time, Robert Sánchez was then far too casual as the Chelsea goalkeeper's pass was almost intercepted. Almost 15 minutes passed before Fernández had Chelsea's first effort of the second half, and Palmer came closer from a tight angle; having cleared the ensuing corner, Blackburn shot themselves in the foot trying to play out, as Brittain's pass was intercepted by Palmer and Sterling curled home from the edge of the area to give Pochettino welcome breathing space. Jackson should have made it more comfortable but blazed over from close range, before the post denied the outstanding Palmer a deflected goal. Sterling could and probably should have added to his tally after finding space in behind more than once late on. But it was Palmer who was afforded a standing ovation when withdrawn at the end, as Chelsea's supporters celebrated only their fourth home win of the season — three of them in this competition.
