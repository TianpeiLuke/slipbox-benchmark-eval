# Iñigo Quintero's "Si No Estás" Breakthrough

Spanish artist Iñigo Quintero's "Si No Estás" rose to the top of Spotify's Global Top 50 in late October 2023 and has refused to leave the upper reaches of digital charts since; even amid the early December onslaught of Christmas standards in Spotify's flagship global chart, the Spanish-language track remained at No.7 as of the 7 December 2023 article. Released only on 23 September, it had by then racked up over 330 million plays on Spotify and over 100 million on YouTube, making it the global music business's biggest breakthrough independent hit of the past few months.

From a music industry perspective the track is especially interesting because it provided Believe, the Paris-headquartered indie business giant, with its first-ever chart-topper on Spotify's Global Top 50. Quintero, who released his follow-up track "Lo Que Queda de Mí" the week before the article, was signed to Believe in March via the indie label and aggregator Acqustic.
