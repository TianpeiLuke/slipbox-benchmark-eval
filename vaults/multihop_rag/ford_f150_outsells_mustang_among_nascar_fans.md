# Ford F-150 Outsells The Mustang Among NASCAR Fans

Mark Rushbrook, Global Director for Ford Performance, said Ford sells more F-150s than Mustangs to NASCAR fans even though the car it races is a Mustang, in the post-race media conference reported by Essentially Sports on 6 November 2023.

He was asked whether winning the championship has any significance for the manufacturer as a whole in terms of marketing the brand globally. Ford's racing program, featuring the Mustang, certainly boosts sales of that iconic sports car, but Rushbrook revealed: "For us, NASCAR, even though we're racing a Mustang, we certainly sell Mustangs because of it. But we sell more F-150s to NASCAR fans. This is a proof point for them."

The report adds the supporting context: the Ford F-150 truck, known for its ruggedness and reliability, has garnered a dedicated following among NASCAR enthusiasts, and winning the championship is not only about showcasing the Mustang but also the F-150, with which Ben Rhodes won his second Truck Series championship on the Friday — a testament to the power of NASCAR in influencing car-buying decisions.
