# Keenan Allen Chargers Midseason MVP

Yardbarker's Nov. 9, 2023 AFC midseason team MVP list named wide receiver Keenan Allen the Los Angeles Chargers' MVP, describing the 31-year-old as the centerpiece of the offense.

The grounds are team-leading volume and independent grading: he led the team in receiving yards with 720 and receptions with 62, and through Week 9 Pro Football Focus gave him a receiving grade of 84.6, eighth among wide receivers. The entry was written by Clark Dalton.
