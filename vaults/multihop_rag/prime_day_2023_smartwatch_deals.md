# Prime Day 2023 Smartwatch Deals

WIRED's Prime Big Deal Days smartwatch picks are the second-generation Garmin Epix Pro, the Withings Steel HR, and the TicWatch Pro 5.

The Garmin Epix Pro holds the title of Best Outdoor Watch in WIRED's Best Fitness Trackers guide and is one of its favorite sports watches (8/10, WIRED Recommends), with a bright AMOLED display, battery life that can last through a week of camping, redshift mode for training outside at night, a flashlight, and an endurance feature alongside Garmin's other proprietary metrics that assess what kind of shape you're in; the deal applies to the 51-mm size, with the 42-mm and 47-mm sizes also on sale. WIRED liked but didn't love the Withings Move, and a chief complaint was its lack of heart rate monitoring — an issue the Withings Steel HR resolves, adding that crucial feature to an already elegant, subtle smartwatch with typical analog hands, a smaller monochrome display for basic data, and restraint about buzzing your wrist for every notification. For Android owners, WIRED really liked the TicWatch Pro 5, mostly for a battery that stands out among competitors like Google, Samsung, and Apple — easily three days of average use, stretched further by Mobvoi's unique dual-display technology — powered by Qualcomm's newer, more efficient Snapdragon W5+ Gen 1 chipset, with barely any hiccups operating this Wear OS 3 watch.
