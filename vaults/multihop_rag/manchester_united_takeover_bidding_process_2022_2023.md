# The Manchester United Takeover Bidding Process, 2022-2023

The sale process for Manchester United ran for roughly eleven months from November 2022, when the Glazer family announced they were open to offers, to mid-October 2023, when the withdrawal of Qatari bidder Sheikh Jassim bin Al Thani left Sir Jim Ratcliffe clear to complete a partial takeover, as reported by talkSPORT on 18 October 2023.

It was in November 2022, while Qatar was hosting the controversial 2022 World Cup, that the deeply unpopular Glazers revealed they were looking to cash in all or at least some of their shares, a decision that had United fans rejoicing. The move was mainly influenced by the fact that Raine had brokered a deal with Todd Boehly to commit £4.5bn to take control of Chelsea, which gave the Glazers grand ideas that they could rake in as much as double that for a global sporting institution like United. After several rounds of bidding the race narrowed to a straight shootout between Oldham-born Ratcliffe, who had previously offered £4bn to buy Chelsea, and Sheikh Jassim bin Al Thani, who professed to being a lifelong Red Devils fan and set up the "'92 Foundation" in tribute to the famous class of '92 of David Beckham and company.

It had initially been hoped the process would conclude in time for the summer transfer window, but the longer it dragged on — prompting several large-scale protests from United fans — the more apparent it became that Joel and Avram were reluctant sellers, as talkSPORT revealed in March. Sheikh Jassim's public statement promising to restore United "to former glories" was also seen as a thinly veiled swipe at the Glazers, which did not help his negotiating hand. The Qatari banker's patience finally snapped, and his withdrawal at the end of the preceding week left the pathway clear for Ratcliffe to complete what he has always viewed as his dream purchase. At times the saga resembled an X-Factor style judges' houses audition.
