# ESPN's 2023 Monday Night Football Broadcast, Manningcast And Streaming

"Monday Night Football" in 2023 was called by Joe Buck and Troy Aikman with Lisa Salters providing sideline reports, and was carried by the ESPN family of networks with simulcasts on ABC. For the Oct. 2 Seahawks-Giants game the listed TV channels were ABC, ESPN and ESPN2, with the game simulcast on ABC and ESPN and the Buck-Aikman crew on the call; Sporting News named the same Buck-Aikman-Salters trio for the Oct. 30 Raiders-Lions game.

Week 4 also marked the return of the "Manningcast," with Eli and Peyton Manning offering an alternate broadcast presentation on ESPN2.

Streaming options in the United States were ESPN+ (subscription required, ESPN's proprietary service), NFL+ (also subscription-required), and Fubo for cord-cutters. Fubo carries the ESPN family of networks plus ABC, NBC, CBS and NFL Network — meaning nationally televised games throughout the 2023 season — and offers a free trial. Viewers in Canada could sign up for DAZN, which carries NFL Game Pass.
