# Shohei Ohtani's Elbow Surgery And 2024 Pitching Absence

Shohei Ohtani will not pitch in 2024 as he recovers from a September 2023 operation to repair his right elbow's ulnar collateral ligament, The New York Times reported on 9 December 2023, and he will attempt to return to the mound in 2025.

He had already undergone Tommy John surgery in 2018. Neither Ohtani, who has not taken questions from reporters since August, nor his agent Nez Balelo, nor the Angels have disclosed the exact nature of the second surgery, though the Los Angeles Times has reported it was a second Tommy John procedure.

Balelo has stressed that Ohtani remains committed to both pitching and hitting in the future — "Shohei loves to pitch," he told reporters in September. His camp has not revealed at what point Ohtani would consider giving up his dual career and focusing on learning a different position; since he was a teenager he has ignored suggestions that he focus on only one pursuit. Significant questions linger about his future.
