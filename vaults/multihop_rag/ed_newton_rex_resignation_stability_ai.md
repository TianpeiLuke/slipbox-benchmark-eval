# Ed Newton-Rex's Resignation From Stability AI

Ed Newton-Rex — a generative AI pioneer, but also a published composer — quit his role at Stability AI over concerns about the company's position on "fair use" in its own recent US Copyright Office filing.

Stability AI had made an argument similar to Anthropic's on fair use and AI training, and MBW reported that the company's position on the issue of fair use concerning AI and copyrighted content resulted in the resignation of the prominent generative AI executive. Both reports were published in the second half of November 2023.
