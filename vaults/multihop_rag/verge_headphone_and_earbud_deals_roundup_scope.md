# The Verge's Headphone And Earbud Deals Roundup Scope

The Verge's December 7, 2023 roundup is a curated list of the best headphone and earbud deals available, covering sales on all kinds of earbuds and over-ear headphones, each with its own strengths and weaknesses.

The rationale for curating rather than listing everything is the shape of the category: when it comes to headphones and true wireless earbuds there are a ton of brands and products to choose from, and the sheer range of models makes them difficult to shop for, especially since many cater to different lifestyles, budgets, and priorities — some better suited for long-haul flights and vigorous workouts, others letting you tune out noise so you can focus better. For readers who want to research further before buying, The Verge points to its guides to the best wireless earbuds and best noise-canceling headphones.
