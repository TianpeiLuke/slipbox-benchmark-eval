# The Spectacular Spider-Man Thanksgiving Finale

Not all Spider-Man Thanksgiving dinners fall apart: the first season of The Spectacular Spider-Man ends with one that succeeds in reminding Peter he is not alone.

The setup is bleak. One of Peter's closest pals, Eddie Brock, has become Venom and has threatened everyone Peter holds dear; this comes just after Peter himself was infected by the symbiote and went through the now-iconic throes of pushing everyone away; Aunt May has just gotten out of the hospital; and even noted jerk "Flash" Thompson has given Peter hell for how awful he has been acting. Attempting to make things right, Peter opts to cook the Thanksgiving meal all by himself and mostly succeeds in ruining the kitchen — but Gwen Stacy and her father, along with a recuperating Aunt May and her doctor, all help out, and the episode concludes with a pleasant dinner. There are no big turns or twists aside from Aunt May revealing she is publishing a cookbook. Polygon calls it simple, earned solace in a life marked by chaos, and notes that Peter even gets a kiss from Gwen on his porch, in a sequence conducted with a John Hughes sense of satisfying romantic flair.
