# Blockcast, Decentralized Broadcast Delivery Network

Blockcast is a Berkeley-based, seed-stage decentralized content delivery network and marketplace founded by Omar Ramadan and Lisa Li, presented at Alliance DAO's All11 demo day on 2023-10-18. It aims to help broadcasters get more traffic and reach wider audiences by bridging the gap between television and online streaming through tokenizing un-utilized TV spectrum to deliver content, according to co-founder, CEO and CTO Omar Ramadan.

The cost claim is the pitch's load-bearing element. Ramadan said streaming a 4K movie to 10,000 viewers typically costs about $1,000 through servers like Cloudflare, but with Blockcast that cost can be reduced by 96%. The company was raising a seed round at the time of the demo day.
