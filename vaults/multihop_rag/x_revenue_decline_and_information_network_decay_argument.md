# X's Revenue Decline And The Information-Network Decay Argument

Charging all users for access to X could be the final nail in the coffin of the platform as a real-time information network, because it risks triggering a further and final exodus of the remaining legacy users whose knowledge and engagement lent Twitter its original value.

The grounds are financial. Subscription revenues to the revamped Twitter Blue paid offering, now called X Premium, do not appear to have been substantial enough to plug the advertising gap, which may explain Musk's recent suggestion that he plans to charge all users a small fee. The warrant joining that to the conclusion is a claim about what makes an information network work: a bona fide information network depends on a critical mass of genuine users to pay attention and contribute value and vitality — its life-blood. Remove the paywall-averse legacy users and disinformation and spammers may rush in to fill the gap, generating a parody of activity; without the genuine users, X really will mark the spot of Twitter's demise.
