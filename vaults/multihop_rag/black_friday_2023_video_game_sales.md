# Black Friday 2023 Video Game Sales

A truckload of PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X/S and Nintendo Switch games dropped in price for Black Friday 2023: both Sony and Microsoft rolled out holiday sales on their respective digital storefronts, while a number of physical games fell to new lows at Amazon, Best Buy and others.

Engadget says there are frankly too many deals to list them all, but highlights for PlayStation and Xbox owners include Assassin's Creed Mirage and the dramatic action-RPG Final Fantasy XVI for $40 each, God of War Ragnarök for $35, Gran Turismo 7 for $30, the rhythm action game Hi-Fi Rush for $22, Marvel's Spider-Man: Miles Morales and Horizon Forbidden West for $20 each, and Halo: The Master Chief Collection for $10 — many of which make Engadget's lists of the best PS5 games and best Xbox games. On the Switch side, the turn-based strategy game Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope was on sale for $15, Xenoblade Chronicles 3 and Luigi's Mansion 3 were each down to $40, and the excellent 2D platformer Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze was down to a new low of $30, with plenty more games on sale beyond those and more PC game deals expected over the course of the week.
