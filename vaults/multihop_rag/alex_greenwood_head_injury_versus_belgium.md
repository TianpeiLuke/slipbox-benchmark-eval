# Alex Greenwood's Head Injury Versus Belgium

Alex Greenwood was stretchered off after a clash of heads with Belgium's Jassina Blom on 31 October 2023, an incident that forced a 13-minute delay for medical treatment, Sport Grill reported.

The impact resulted in both players falling to the ground. Blom was eventually able to continue after concussion checks, while Greenwood was taken from the field on a stretcher.

England tweeted at half-time that Greenwood was conscious and talking to medical staff under observation: "Alex Greenwood is conscious and talking, and is being monitored by our medical staff," the Lionesses posted on October 31, 2023.
