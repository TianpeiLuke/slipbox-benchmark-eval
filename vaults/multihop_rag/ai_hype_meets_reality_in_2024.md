# AI Hype Meets Reality In 2024

TechCrunch's framing prediction for 2024, published on 19 December 2023, is that it will be the year when the hype runs full-steam into reality as people reckon with the capabilities and limitations of AI at large.

The premise is the year just ending: 2023 was a banger for AI as the technology went from niche to mainstream about as fast as anything ever has. The proposition would be falsified if that reckoning did not arrive — if capability claims went untested and limitations remained unexamined — and the article sets out a few specific ways the reckoning is expected to play out.
