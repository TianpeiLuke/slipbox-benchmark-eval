# Quincy Williams Jets Midseason MVP

Yardbarker's Nov. 9, 2023 AFC midseason team MVP list named linebacker Quincy Williams the New York Jets' MVP, noting that while his brother Quinnen may be the better-known Jets defender in the family, Quincy was also making a name for himself.

The grounds are his October honor and his statistical standing: he was the AFC Defensive Player of the Month for October, the fifth-highest-rated linebacker in the NFL by Pro Football Focus, tied for 17th in tackles with 74 and tied for ninth in tackles for loss with nine among all defenders. The warrant is that recognized month-level dominance plus top-of-position grading and volume makes him the most valuable Jet at the midpoint.
