# Klarna's IPO Preparation And Fintech Signals

Swedish fintech Klarna is taking steps toward an eventual IPO, Mary Ann and Alex reported on November 11, 2023. The company has initiated a process for a legal entity restructuring to set up a holding company in the U.K., which a Klarna spokesperson tells TechCrunch+ is an important early step in its plans for an initial public offering.

The move sits within a wider read on the sector. On Equity that week, the crew dove deep into the encouraging signs from the fintech startup market, starting with Klarna's Q3 results.

From there they looked at buy now, pay later consumer behavior and fintech fundraising results with a 2021 flavor.
