# Target Price Match Policy

As of October 22nd, 2023, Target will match the price of any item purchased online or in-store from Target that goes on sale through December 24th. It will also match the price of items purchased from Amazon, Best Buy, and a select group of competitors if you ask for it at the time of purchase or within 14 days of it.

To claim a competitor match in person, bring the original ad or listing — whether digital or in print — along with your receipt into the store. For online purchases you can instead call 1-800-591-3869 or chat online via contactus.target.com.

The exclusions are specific. The current policy does not apply to products you bought from one Target store and found for less at another; it excludes doorbusters and competitors' lightning sales, like those Amazon offers; and it does not apply to products that require you to log in to see the price, or to "Marketplace" items from third-party sellers.
