# Fediverse Onboarding Barriers And Mozilla's Proposed Fixes

Mozilla's model of why the fediverse has not taken off names three obstacles that have prevented users from joining and participating so far: the technical hurdles around onboarding, finding people to follow, and discovering interesting content to discuss.

The remedies map onto those barriers in order, and O'Hara chains them into a sequence: "The baseline is just better onboarding, better connecting with accounts and communities. You might be interested in spaces where you can have the kinds of conversations that you want to be able to have; trust and safety practices that make you feel safe in order to participate. And I think also then access to content that spurs those conversations and seeds those conversations. And it all being kind of a delightful user experience — but we're at the early days of that," she adds. So onboarding enables connection, safety enables participation, and content seeds the conversations that make participation worthwhile.

A further avenue of thought is to create spaces within the fediverse where consumers can have civil and direct conversations within smaller communities, instead of shouting into the void. The range over which that idea holds is undefined: as to what it will look like is less clear — will it be different instances, or just new ways of forming communities by building out features or experiences? O'Hara could not say, only noting that experimentation will be needed.
