# Vincent Namatjira's Call For A Foster Care Apology

Vincent Namatjira told The Guardian on 16 October 2023 that the government should apologise for taking young Indigenous people into foster care: "To be honest, I would like the government to apologise [for] taking these young Indigenous people into foster care."

His grounds are cultural loss rather than individual harm alone: "In reality, we are losing much more of where we're originally from, and our background, and our history. We have lost a lot of things, our culture, languages and our tradition."

The warrant is a claim about voice and standing, made from the country he lives in: "And around here, this is the hard country, we work hard to be who we are today. Indigenous people, we want to be heard and to take a stand. To show our tradition and culture, and art – also to show the rest of the world that Indigenous people, we need to be heard."
