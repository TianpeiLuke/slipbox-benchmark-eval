# North Texas At SMU: The 2023 Matchup And Efficiency Picture

SMU entered its Friday meeting with North Texas at 7-2 and 5-0 in its final go-round in the American Athletic Conference, having guaranteed a fifth consecutive winning season — success not seen in Dallas since the years that led to the program's Death Penalty, which suspended it in 1987 and '88. North Texas came in at 3-6 and 1-4, having dropped four of its past five games and allowing 37.4 points per game, ninth-worst in the nation. The Mustangs had a national ESPN2 audience for the 9 p.m. ET kickoff.

The recent past gave a clear window into what to expect: over the previous four seasons SMU beat North Texas 48-10, 35-12, 65-35 and 49-27, covering the spread each time and comfortably in all but one.

The efficiency numbers, as reported Nov. 7, 2023, pointed the same way. SMU had a top-10 defense allowing only 0.251 points per play, stiffening to 0.185 at home, while the North Texas defense sat 12th from the Division I basement at 0.501 points per play allowed and an even looser 0.533 on the road. SMU's 20th-rated offense (0.483) sharpened to a ridiculous 0.662 at home — the only squads more efficient in their own yards being USC (0.780), LSU (0.762), Oregon (0.712) and Washington (0.690) — which Sporting News called the definition of élite.
