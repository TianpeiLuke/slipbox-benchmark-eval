# Joelinton's Reinvention In Newcastle's Midfield

Joelinton, the misfit forward reinvented as a talismanic midfielder, gave Newcastle a deserved lead against AC Milan on 13 December 2023 with a rasping, rising shot from the edge of the box that flew past Mike Maignan — one of the great success stories of Eddie Howe's reign, The Independent wrote.

He had taken Miguel Almiron's pass and teed himself up before thumping the ball home. His initial non-celebration may have reflected his own surprise at the unerring accuracy of the strike.
