# Meta Platforms Q3 2023 Earnings Preview

Facebook parent Meta Platforms was scheduled to announce its Q3 results after the close on Wednesday 25 October 2023. After exceeding expectations in Q2, the social media giant had forecast Q3 revenue in the range of $32B-$34.5B.

SA contributor Dair Sansyzbayev, extremely optimistic about the company's near-term prospects, said: "Meta Platforms stock remains undervalued despite a tripling price since November 2022. Quarterly earnings show revenue growth, profitability, and solid cash flow, while Meta's expansion and large user base present potential revenue streams." Meta's cost-cutting actions and a rebound in ad income had boosted the stock's performance that year, which had climbed by about 150%, and the stock was rated Strong Buy by both sell-side analysts and Seeking Alpha's Quant Rating system.

The preview also noted that Alphabet's unit Google, Meta Platforms and Apple had recently received approval from US regulators to deploy a new set of mobile AR/VR devices, including goggles and in-car connections. Consensus EPS estimates were $3.60 and consensus revenue estimates $33.43B; Meta had topped EPS expectations in 4 of the past 8 quarters, beating revenue estimates in 5 of those reports.
