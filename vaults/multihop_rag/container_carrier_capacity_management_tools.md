# Container Carriers' Capacity Management Tools

To manage capacity in the short term, the main tools at container carriers' disposal are canceling individual voyages, or suspending services entirely on trade lanes where demand is weak.

In prolonged slumps they can also go further: letting charter contracts expire, idling some ships, or selling old ones in the scrap steel market. The second set applies only when the downturn persists — it is the escalation beyond short-term voyage management.
