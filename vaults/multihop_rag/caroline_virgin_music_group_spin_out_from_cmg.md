# Caroline And Virgin Music Group's Spin-Out From CMG

The indie distribution and services company Caroline, later Virgin Music, was in recent years spun out of Capitol Music Group, forming the foundation for the global UMG priority venture known today as Virgin Music Group. Michelle Jubelirer calls it one of her most successful gambits and describes the spin-out as one of the structural fun and games she had to contend with in her first two years in charge, and the interviewer frames it as a decision that was probably not in her control.

Her account of the pros and cons is that Virgin, first as Caroline, was a major priority since the launch of Capitol Music Group in 2012; CMG prioritised the area and oversaw the transition from Caroline into Virgin directly within the company, and was fortunate to have the talent and friendship of now-Virgin Music Group President Jacqueline Saturn and her team for many years. Because CMG was so successful in building that end of the business, it was only a matter of time before other labels within UMG would want to grow a similar business within their groups; since it would not have made sense to have multiple indie label and artist services divisions within UMG, the plan to globalise Virgin and scale it to work with all the labels made sense. Jubelirer says she misses having Saturn and her team a few steps away, but is proud of the crucial roles they have played in Virgin's growth and evolution and works as closely with them as ever.
