# Jake Ferguson As A Week 5 2023 Start Against The 49ers

CBS Sports listed Jake Ferguson under "Start Him" for Week 5 of the 2023 season: the claim is that the only reason to give Ferguson the nod is that he is a safety valve for Dak Prescott to lean on, and the warrant is that San Francisco's expected pass rush with zone coverage behind it should push Prescott into short throws even though the 49ers are the league's hardest matchup for tight ends.

The grounds are a volume play, stated as such. Starting Ferguson means counting on him to catch six or seven balls and average around 10 yards per catch, which makes him much more appealing in PPR than in non-PPR. The precedent is the previous week, when Ferguson benefited from Prescott taking what the Patriots defense gave him, used mostly as a short-area target to fire the ball to when pressure came quickly off the snap, with a couple of throws where Prescott read the defense well and found him for pretty good gains; all told Ferguson led Dallas with seven targets and seven catches for 77 yards in three quarters of work. This week the 49ers figure to unleash their pass rush on Prescott with zone coverage behind it, which only makes sense given how Prescott has handled pass-rush pressure this year, and that should mean more short throws for Ferguson.

The counter-evidence is San Francisco's coverage of the position. The 49ers are tops in football in catch rate allowed to tight ends at 48.6% and in yards after catch per reception allowed at 1.29 yards, and are top-five in yards per catch at 7.1 — numbers made more remarkable by tight ends averaging 8.8 targets per game against them this season, third-most in football. Tyler Higbee and Darren Waller were completely humbled by them, though Zach Ertz managed 11 PPR points the previous week and nearly caught a touchdown, and Pat Freiermuth did catch a touchdown in Week 1 for seven PPR points. The verdict is therefore format-sensitive: the author is OK starting Ferguson over Dallas Goedert and Kyle Pitts.
