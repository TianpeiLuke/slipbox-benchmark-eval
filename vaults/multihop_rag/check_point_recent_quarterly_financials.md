# Check Point Recent Quarterly Financials

Sales at Check Point have grown on a yearly basis amid the increase in demand for cybersecurity. For the most recently reported quarter as of 18 November 2023, non-GAAP earnings of $2.07 per share beat the average analyst estimate by 5 cents, while revenue of $596.3M beat by $3.78M.

That performance resulted in an upgrade late the previous month at Piper Sandler, with signs of recovery on the horizon.
