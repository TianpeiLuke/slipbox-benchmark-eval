# Bernard Hopkins vs Robert Allen, 1998: The Referee's Fall

Bernard Hopkins' 1998 middleweight title fight against Robert Allen ended when referee Mills Lane's attempt to physically pull the clinching pair apart went badly wrong, sending an off-balance Hopkins through the ropes and onto the floor, talkSPORT recalled on 15 November 2023.

It was an ugly, mauling fight, and a nasty spill: Hopkins, lying ringside, claimed he was too injured to fight on. talkSPORT's aside — "Maybe you just should have shouted 'break', Mills."

B-Hops joked he would "sharpen my wrestling skills" for the rematch a year later, but this time he knocked out Allen in round seven. The duo got to fight again in 2004.
