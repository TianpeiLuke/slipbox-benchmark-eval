# Microsoft's Game Studio Portfolio

Microsoft owns nearly 40 developers, having bought a chunk of them in the past five years: the Xbox umbrella covers 343 Industries, Arkane Studios, Bethesda, Compulsion Games, Double Fine Productions, id Software, Infinity Ward, Mojang Studios, Ninja Theory, Playground Games, Tango Gameworks and Turn 10, among dozens more.

Engadget notes the scale marker that distinguishes the Activision deal from the rest of the portfolio: at $69 billion it was the biggest acquisition in Microsoft's history — the previous record being its $26 purchase of LinkedIn in 2016 — but it was far from the company's first video game acquisition.
