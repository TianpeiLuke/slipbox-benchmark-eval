# Nike Direct-to-Consumer Channel Performance

Nike's direct channel, which includes its owned stores and its digital channel, led the retailer's growth during the fiscal first quarter ended 31 August 2023, up 6% compared with the prior year. In June the company had noticed shoppers shifting towards its stores over its digital channels, signalling that consumers are getting closer to pre-pandemic shopping habits.

Finance chief Matthew Friend described the engagement behind that growth: "We continue to see that consumers want to connect directly and personally with our brands and in fact, member engagement within our direct business is up double digits versus the prior year with increasing average order values."

He also detailed the physical side of the shift: "Our stores delivered an especially strong quarter with traffic up double digits from last year, and members driving an increasing share of our business as consumers shifted from our digital to physical channels... Our team was nimble in transitioning inventory to capture higher full-price sales across our entire store fleet."
