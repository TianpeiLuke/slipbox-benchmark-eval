# Romelu Lukaku's 40-Goal 2023 At Roma And With Belgium

Romelu Lukaku scored 40 goals in the 2023 calendar year for Roma and Belgium, placing him fifth on TalkSport's list of the year's top scorers. After reuniting with Jose Mourinho at Roma in the summer, Lukaku found his goalscoring form once again.

The Belgian has been firing back in Serie A following another loan move from Chelsea, netting in almost all of his starts this season. He also found his shooting boots for his country, most notably scoring four in a 5-0 win over Azerbaijan in November, and ended the Euro 2024 qualifying campaign as top scorer. Lukaku's career seems to have reignited, and that has seen him close to claiming a place as one of the highest goalscorers of the year.
