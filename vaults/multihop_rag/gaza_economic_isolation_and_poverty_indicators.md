# Gaza's Economic Isolation And Poverty Indicators

More than 53% of Gaza residents were considered below the poverty line in 2020, and about 77% of Gazan households receive some form of aid from the United Nations and other groups, mostly in the form of cash or food. The Palestinian enclaves of the West Bank and Gaza — generally lumped together in economic analyses — both have small economies that together run a massive deficit of US$6.6 billion in losses each year, as the value of the imports they receive greatly outweighs the value of the items they produce and sell elsewhere.

Gaza's weak economy is caused by a number of complex factors, but the largest is the blockade and the economic and trade isolation it creates.
