# SBF Political Access and Donations

[Sam Bankman-Fried](sam_bankman_fried.md) had close ties to U.S. regulators and government officials, many of whom he donated to.

Michael Lewis said he met Bankman-Fried ahead of a meeting with Mitch McConnell to discuss funding political candidates who sought to offset Donald Trump's influence in the Republican Party. Lewis also claimed Bankman-Fried had devised a plan to pay Trump not to run for president again: "Like how much would it take to get an answer? There was a number that was kicking around… this was $5 billion." Lewis said Bankman-Fried was never sure whether that figure came directly from Trump, and whether any money was ever sent is not established.

Bankman-Fried also sought to back candidates on the Democratic side.
