# UK Games Industry Impact Of The Activision Acquisition

For the UK, the Microsoft-Activision deal means more British studios coming under the control of Microsoft: Activision Blizzard alone has studios in London, Guildford and Warrington, and the business also owns King, the Stockholm and London-based makers of Candy Crush Saga.

Activision Blizzard itself owns many developers, including the maker of Candy Crush Saga, so the transfer of ownership reaches well beyond the parent company's own offices.

The industry body's view is that this is welcome. Ukie, which represents games companies, told the BBC that this kind of international investment was "a crucial part" of the UK industry, and according to a spokesperson, 89 percent of the total investment in the UK games industry between January 2017 and June 2022 came from non-UK companies. From the side of simply paying for games to be made, the BBC concludes, this kind of money makes a big difference.
