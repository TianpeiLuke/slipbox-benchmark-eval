# Jake Ferguson Week 13: Sit Him, Because The Game Script And Seattle's Tight End Defense Cap His Upside

CBS Sports' Week 13 verdict on Jake Ferguson is Sit Him, on the grounds that starting him amounts to hoping for a touchdown grab or for a ton of targets in a come-from-behind effort — neither of which the circumstances favor. The Cowboys are expected to lead this matchup pretty handily, which can squash the upside for a lot of yardage from Ferguson. On his touchdown chances, the article concedes a genuine positive: 37% of his targets, 7 of 19 over the past five weeks, have come in the red zone, which is good. The rebuttal is the opponent — the Seahawks have allowed one touchdown to a tight end all season, David Njoku in Week 8, and while Mark Andrews had a great game against Seattle, even he did not score. The warrant is a scheme expectation: the author suspects the Seahawks will have a plan to try to cover Ferguson near the end zone without having to sacrifice too much elsewhere.

The conclusion is that he is a low-upside play, one the author would sit for Juwan Johnson, Gerald Everett and Logan Thomas. Scope: Ferguson is a Dallas tight end, #87, age 24 with 2 years of experience; the weekly breakdown lists the matchup versus Seattle with Dallas favored by 9 and an over/under of 47.5, the opponent 1st versus tight ends, projected points of 8.3 and a TE rank of 11th; year-to-date he has 40 receptions on 58 targets for 421 receiving yards, 4 touchdowns and 9.6 Fantasy points per game, with projections powered by SportsLine.
