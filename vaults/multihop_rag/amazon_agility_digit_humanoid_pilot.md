# Amazon's Agility Digit Humanoid Pilot As A Test Case

TechCrunch argues on 2023-10-28 that Amazon's pilots with Agility's Digit systems are a decisive test for humanoid robots as a category — not because failure would end Agility, but because success would change how the rest of the industry treats the form factor. As the author puts it, it's not that he thinks it's the end of Agility if Amazon opts not to extend a contract; it's more that if Amazon decides to pursue it further, it's going to cause a lot more companies to take bipedal/humanoid robots a lot more seriously.

The warrant is Amazon's own conservatism as a signal. The company operates at such an unfathomable scale that it truly needs to feel absolutely confident before it begins implementing new technologies into its workflows, so its continued adoption would function as evidence others can rely on. The author places himself in the same position as Amazon: he has been saying the whole time that he's holding off on judging the efficacy of humanoids until we see more in the field, and Amazon clearly feels the same way. The pilots were the first of three topics in his conversation with Amazon Robotics chief technologist Tye Brady, and he had written about them the previous week, including a piece titled "Humanoid robots face a major test with Amazon's Digit pilots."
