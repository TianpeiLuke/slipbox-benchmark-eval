# Notre Dame's Third Quarter Against USC, Oct. 14 2023

Both teams scored a touchdown in the third quarter of Notre Dame's Oct. 14, 2023 game against USC — a 31-yard MarShawn Lloyd run for the Trojans and a Sam Hartman deep ball to Chris Tyree for the Irish — leaving Notre Dame ahead 31-13 with Audric Estime finishing the quarter on a 14-yard run.

The half opened at 9:25 p.m. with Notre Dame starting at its own 22 after a 17-yard Devyn Ford return. Hartman's play-action deep shot to Mitchell Evans was broken up by Christian Roland-Wallace, the second time he had defended a pass to Evans; Evans was injured on the play and, though able to walk off, had to go to the blue tent. Hartman then threw short to Holden Staes for three and missed on third down, forcing a punt after a three-and-out. Branch boomed a 50-yard punt and hopped back on it so USC retained possession, starting at its own 30, but the Trojans went three-and-out quickly — an incompletion, a pass for a loss of four and another incompletion — with the Sporting News noting the Trojans still looked lost on offense. Tyree returned the punt two yards to the Notre Dame 25, where a short Faison gain and a four-yard Love run set up third-and-5 at the Notre Dame 30; another short Hartman pass was met by a great open-field tackle from Calen Bullock after just a yard, Notre Dame punted, and Branch returned it 21 yards to the USC 43.

USC then moved into scoring position through a stretch of trading penalties. Mario Williams was flagged for USC's second offensive pass interference of the night after a 10-yard Washington catch, creating first-and-25, and Notre Dame's Benjamin Morrison was then called for pass interference — his third penalty of the night — on a deep shot to Rice, moving USC back to its own 43. Branch got back to the line of scrimmage after a reverse, Williams found Singer down the right sideline for 11 before a sack for a loss of 10 gave the yardage back, and Williams then bailed USC out of second-and-long with a 21-yard completion to McRee on the run. At 9:45 p.m. USC scored its first touchdown of the evening: after a four-yard Williams rush, Lloyd took a pitch from Williams, carved through the left side and broke loose for a 31-yard score, making it Notre Dame 24, USC 13.

Notre Dame responded on the next drive. Ford's return gave the Irish the ball at their own 32, Estime ran for seven, four and three yards, Evans returned from injury to catch a short pass and fight for eight yards to the USC 46, and at 9:53 p.m. Hartman uncorked a deep ball down the middle to the speedy Tyree, who ran the remaining 15 yards into the end zone to push the lead back to 18: Notre Dame 31, USC 13. USC started its reply at its own 25, with Williams hitting Washington for 13 on the sideline; a batted pass and a four-yard Lloyd run brought up third-and-6 from the USC 32 and Notre Dame used its first timeout of the half. Williams found Jackson over the middle for 10 and a first down, but Marist Liufau sacked him for a loss of eight back to the USC 44, and then Rylie Mills and Nana Osafo-Mensah brought him down for another eight-yard loss, leaving third-and-28. Notre Dame conceded a 10-yard wide receiver screen to Jackson, and a false start preceded Eddie Czaplicki's punt, which went out of bounds at the Notre Dame 7 with eight seconds left in the quarter. Estime then ended the third quarter with a run to the left, hitting the hole and being brought down at the Notre Dame 21 by Zion Branch after a gain of 14.
