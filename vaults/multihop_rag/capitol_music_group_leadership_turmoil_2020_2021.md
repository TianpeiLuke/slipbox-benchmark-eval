# Capitol Music Group's Leadership Turmoil, 2020-2021

Capitol Music Group, the Los Angeles-based label group that is a key pillar of Universal Music Group, went through two leadership handovers in about a year before Michelle Jubelirer took charge. Jubelirer was widely tipped as the natural successor to Steve Barnett when the British executive stood down as head of CMG in 2020, having been by Barnett's side throughout a run of success at CMG over multiple years, via artists like Sam Smith, Halsey, Paul McCartney and Lewis Capaldi, as well as transformative deals like a joint venture between Motown, then part of CMG, and Quality Control.

Her immediate ascension was not forthcoming. Barnett was succeeded in December 2020 as Chairman/CEO of the label group by Jeff Vaughn, a fast-rising executive who had previously impressed as a data-and-socials-savvy A&R at Mike Caren's Artist Partner Group. Vaughn entered CMG mid-pandemic with bold ideas, but there was a culture clash, and within a startlingly short 12 months he was gone from CMG; he has since launched his label joint venture, Signal Records, with Sony's Columbia. In the wake of this corporate untidiness Jubelirer was named Chair & CEO of Capitol Music Group, with the most pressing brief of calming everything down and getting the Good Ship Capitol out of the eye of the hurricane. Two years before the December 2023 interview, the writer had sat down with Jubelirer and some of her closest allies for a Chinese meal on a chilly winter's night in London just after her promotion, a celebratory mood imbued with knowledge of a tough road ahead, mixing nervous energy with a healthy confidence.
