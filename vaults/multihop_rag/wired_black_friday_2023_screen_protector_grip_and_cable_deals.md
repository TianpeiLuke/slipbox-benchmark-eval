# WIRED Black Friday 2023 Screen Protector, Grip And Cable Deals

WIRED's Apple Black Friday guide of 25 November 2023 rounded up five small-accessory deals: Apple AirTags, Zagg's Glass Elite Privacy 360 screen protector, Nomad Lightning cables, a MagSafe phone grip and the USB-C cable that tops WIRED's cable guide.

Apple's AirTags are called a handy way to keep track of your stuff, with the caveat that you should be careful to make sure you are not being tracked without your permission; WIRED notes they often go on sale, so if you are itching to pick up a pack, now is as good a time as any. The Glass Elite Privacy 360 blocks anyone from seeing your display content from the sides, feels great and is easy to apply; WIRED's only complaint is that Zagg's screen protectors are typically expensive, and it notes this one has hit as low as $24, while calling it a good protector for an iPhone 14 — with other sizes for the iPhone 14 Plus, iPhone 14 Pro and iPhone 14 Pro Max also on sale.

The Nomad Lightning cables are for people with an iPhone 14 or earlier, on the logic that you can never own too many Lightning cables; they are durable, offer a choice of USB-C or USB-A at the other end, and come in different lengths. The MagSafe phone grip is one of WIRED's favourites: it sticks magnetically to the back of an iPhone, pops out for extra stability, and comes off so you can charge the device wirelessly. The last pick is the cable currently sitting at the top of WIRED's Best USB-C Cables guide because it can do it all — 100 watts of power, data transfer up to 40 Gbps, and driving an 8K display or two 4K displays, with support for DisplayPort Alt Mode, USB Power Delivery and PPS and backward compatibility with all earlier standards; it is pricey even with the discount but rarely dips this low, and this deal is for the longest size, with the shorter one also on sale.
