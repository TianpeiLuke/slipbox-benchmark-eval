# Cloud Stock Multiples And Startup Valuations

The current bear market in cloud stocks has led to a reversal of revenue multiples from earlier in 2023, making it difficult for late-stage startups to accrue value, Alex writes on TC+.

The effect is not uniform across stages: early-stage startups that are growing rapidly may still be able to raise funds and demand a better valuation.

The trend points toward a favorable environment for startups prioritizing growth over cash preservation.
