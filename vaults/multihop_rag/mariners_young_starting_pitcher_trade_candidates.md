# Mariners Young Starting Pitcher Trade Candidates

Yardbarker's Nov. 13, 2023 top 25 MLB offseason trade candidates list filled its No. 16 spot not with a single player but with the Seattle rotation in general: Bryan Woo was initially slotted there, but it could just as easily have been Bryce Miller or Emerson Hancock, so the piece cheated and named the group.

Seattle was almost certainly not going to trade George Kirby, and it would take a lot to pry away Logan Gilbert. Moving any of Woo, Miller or Hancock could be more appealing for a front office looking for ways to add long-term offensive upside. None of that trio had even reached one year of major league service; Hancock had only three big league starts, while Miller and Woo spent more time in the rotation that year, starting 25 and 18 games respectively, and both turned in mid-rotation results. Other clubs probably had differing opinions on which right-hander they preferred — the Mariners likely had their own slight preferences — and all three would have significant value if president of baseball operations Jerry Dipoto decided to move one for a bat.
