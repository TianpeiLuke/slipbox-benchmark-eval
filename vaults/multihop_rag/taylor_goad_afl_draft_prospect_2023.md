# Taylor Goad AFL Draft Prospect 2023

Taylor Goad, profiled in Kevin Sheehan's top 40 for the 2023 AFL Draft as reported by The Roar on Nov. 15, 2023, is a developing ruckman who only began to focus on a potential AFL career that year after initially applying his natural talent to basketball. State: South Australia. State League Club: South Adelaide. Community Club: Willunga. Date of Birth: 28/04/05. Height: 206cm.

He showed splendid improvement throughout the season playing at under-18 level for his state and club, and was particularly impressive in South Adelaide's premiership campaign, where he averaged 10.4 disposals, 25 hitouts and 2.2 marks in 13 matches. Both his standing vertical jump of 74cm and 20m sprint time of 2.965 seconds ranked in the top 10 at the national Draft Combine, giving distinct indications of his elite athletic tools. Weapon: Height and speed. AFL player comparison: Max Gawn (Melbourne). As one assessment put it, "He's got the most upside of any player in our state."
