# Holiday Price Matching Policies, 2023

Some major retailers ensured price protection on purchases during the 2023 holiday season, but the terms differ sharply by retailer, so whether you get price matching depends on where and when you shop.

Amazon does not price match, which Polygon calls annoying but tolerable since it often has the lowest prices of all retailers anyway; Amazon only protects the price on pre-orders carrying its "Pre-order Price Guarantee," which ensures you pay the lowest amount a product sells for before it launches. Best Buy not only matches prices from "qualified competitors" but says on its site that most products bought between the article's publication and December 30 can be price-matched through January 13, 2024. Walmart's policy says it will not price match with competitors, nor does it price match for products purchased online that later decrease in price. Target says purchases made between October 22 and December 24 can be price-adjusted if the cost falls within that span and you show proof of purchase; eligibility exists, but the rules for submitting documentation vary depending on whether you shop online or in store.
