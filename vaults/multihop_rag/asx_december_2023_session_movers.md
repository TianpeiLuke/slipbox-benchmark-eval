# ASX December 2023 Session Movers

On the Australian sharemarket session reported by The Sydney Morning Herald on 5 December 2023, Perpetual was the standout gainer, climbing 6.3 per cent after announcing a strategic review of its three-pronged corporate structure that might see it demerge its corporate trust and wealth management businesses and create a more focused asset manager. Endeavour shares lifted 2.4 per cent after the company forecast that earnings before interest and tax in its hotels division would grow by more than $150 million over the next five years.

Utilities and energy stocks were the weakest performers on the local bourse, rising just 0.6 per cent and 0.8 per cent respectively. Only five large-cap stocks declined outright. Evolution Mining sank a mammoth 13 per cent after raising $525 million by selling new shares to institutional investors at $3.80 a share, although the mining sector as a whole still managed to finish in positive territory. Gold miner Newmont was down 1.25 per cent, Origin Energy fell 0.4 per cent, Mercury NZ declined 0.3 per cent and Argo Investments traded 0.1 per cent lower.

Moomoo market strategist Jessica Amir attributed the local market's outperformance of global equities on the Wednesday session to the National Accounts figures, which pointed to the Reserve Bank potentially hitting pause on lifting interest rates. "We know economic growth is slowing, Australian unemployment is likely to pick up next year and inflation is likely to fall," Amir said, adding that this "trifecta" pointed towards the RBA stopping rate rises and then potentially moving to cut, which was what the market had priced in that day.
