# Believe's Marketing Playbook For "Si No Estás"

Believe has been working with Iñigo Quintero since March 2023, and Alejandra Olea describes the campaign as a sequence. In the initial discovery phase, the team developed the audience of the track with the label mostly through Believe's expertise in marketing on Spotify. Second, thanks to Believe's short-form video trends detection technology, the team noticed acceleration in the propagation of the track and consequently leveraged its partnership with TikTok, as well as coordinated promotion from all its teams around the world with Spotify. The precondition that makes the sequence work is Believe's global partnerships with both TikTok and Spotify, which allowed marketing and promotion campaigns to be combined in each service at local level in the key early markets of Spain, Latin America and France, as well as globally. Alongside TikTok on the social side, the company leveraged Instagram to drive engagement around Iñigo and the song globally, and its teams, which have robust video expertise for maximising content across platforms, built Iñigo's YouTube channel from the ground up, optimising it and directing traffic to it by creating the official lyric video as its first video so music fans could find and listen to his music on the platform.

The results reported are that the track has been included in over 180 playlists across all digital music partners and in over 79 playlists with over 97 million followers on Spotify alone, including reaching #1 of Spotify's Top 50 - Global for the first time ever for a Believe track, being featured on Today's Top Hits, Spotify's biggest playlist, and topping Spotify's Viral 50 and Top 50 playlists around the world; the TikTok partnership has resulted in more than 5 million creations according to MediaMatch; and the lyric video for "Si No Estás" has reached 100 million views to date, currently ranked #13 on YouTube's Top 100 Global Music Videos. The final step, in progress at the time of the interview, is a new phase to deepen and expand the success by promoting the track on more traditional media in key countries, where it is rapidly gaining radio airplay and unlocking other promotional opportunities.
