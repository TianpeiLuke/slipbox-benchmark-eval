# Britney Spears' Memoir Barbs At Timberlake And Federline

The Guardian's 24 October 2023 summary of *The Woman in Me* notes that Spears, while seeming "too pure of heart for active malice", lands piercing characterisations of both Justin Timberlake and Kevin Federline.

She writes that Timberlake's band 'NSync were big hip-hop heads who sometimes "tried too hard to fit in" with Black artists, and recounts Timberlake spotting Ginuwine at an event and getting "all excited" — "Oh yeah fo shiz, fo shiz! Ginuwiiiine! What's up, homie!" Her account of being encouraged to abort their pregnancy at home, lest anyone see them going in and out of hospital, is described as horrifying: Britney, with no pain relief, is writhing on the floor, and Timberlake "proves to be the last person you'd call in a crisis" — "At some point he thought maybe music would help, so he got his guitar and he lay there with me, strumming it."

Almost as embarrassing, she writes, was husband Kevin Federline's attempt to start a music career: "He really thought he was a rapper now… Bless his heart – because he did take it so seriously."
