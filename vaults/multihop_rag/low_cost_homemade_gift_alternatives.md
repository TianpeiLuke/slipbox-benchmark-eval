# Low Cost And Homemade Gift Alternatives

There is more than one way to find the "perfect" gift for your family and it does not always involve spending a lot — making gifts, or buying used, is the route Fortune's 2023-12-23 piece recommends for anyone whose gift budget has been squeezed.

Marisa Johnson, in her quest to find gifts that do not cause her financial stress, has been getting creative: "I've definitely been trying to save or just make gifts because I don't really have the disposable income to buy other people gifts or even buy myself a Christmas gift." She has been looking into gifting a used book to her dad, and because she loves painting and already has all the tools, she is working on a painting for her mom. Other affordable alternatives the article lists are sending handwritten notes to your loved ones, buying from thrift stores, hosting a cookie bake-off, or simply spending quality time with your family.
