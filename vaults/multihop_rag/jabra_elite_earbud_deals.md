# Jabra Elite Earbud Deals

The Verge lists three Jabra sets: the Elite 10 at $199.99 (Amazon, Best Buy, Jabra) against a regular $249.99; the Elite 8 Active at $149.99 (Amazon, Best Buy, Jabra) against a normal $199.99; and the Elite 4 Active at $69.99, $50 off, at Amazon and Walmart.

The Elite 10 are Jabra's most premium wireless earbuds, with a new semi-open design that should be more comfortable and Dolby Spatial Audio with head tracking; The Verge is still testing them, but Jabra says they offer its most powerful active noise cancellation yet, and their new drivers should provide more detailed and richer sound while retaining wireless charging, multipoint Bluetooth support, and an IP57 water-resistance rating. The Elite 8 Active are fitness-focused successors to the excellent Elite 7 Active — terrific for runs, with more robust IP68 water resistance than their predecessor and adaptive ANC — and, unlike similar offerings from Beats and Anker, they achieve a secure fit without added hooks or fins, while keeping essentials like multipoint and wireless charging. The more affordable Elite 4 Active are also geared toward working out, with IP57 water resistance and active noise cancellation that can't be adjusted, and no wireless charging.
