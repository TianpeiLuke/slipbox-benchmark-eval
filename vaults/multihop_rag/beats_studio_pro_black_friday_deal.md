# Beats Studio Pro Black Friday Deal

The Beats Studio Pro came down to $170 for Black Friday 2023 at Amazon, Target and Best Buy — topping the pair's previous low by $10 and falling about $130 below its average street price in recent months.

The Studio Pro isn't a top pick in any of Engadget's guides, but it earned a solid review score of 81 in July: its sound is largely pleasant and even-handed, its ANC is strong enough to be useful, and it can stream higher-quality audio over a USB-C cable, working well with both iOS and Android — though it doesn't support multi-device pairing on iOS, there's no auto-pausing when you remove the headphones, and the design is mostly plastic. If you like the Beats style, those flaws should be a little easier to accept at this price.
