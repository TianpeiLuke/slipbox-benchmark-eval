# Taylor Swift And Joe Alwyn's Relationship And Breakup

In April 2023 it was revealed that Taylor Swift and British actor Joe Alwyn had broken up after six years of dating. The former couple were notoriously private: the Conversations with Friends star had previously said he did not want to discuss the relationship because doing so "feeds into a weird part of the culture."

Even so, the relationship generated constant rumours about Swift's personal life — enough that her publicist, Tree Paine, was forced to publicly deny some of them. Swift, arguably more famous now than ever before, said the constant attention and swarms of paparazzi would not stop her from living her life.
