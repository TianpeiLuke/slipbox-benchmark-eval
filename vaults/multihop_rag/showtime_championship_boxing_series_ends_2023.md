# Showtime Championship Boxing Ends After 37 Years With A December 2023 Finale

December 16, 2023 was set to be the final broadcast of the Showtime Championship Boxing series, on a card where Cuban super middleweight David Morrell defends his WBA "regular" super middleweight title against Sena Agbeko at the Armory in Minneapolis. It is not the main event that will bring a lump to ringsiders' throats, but the end of the beloved series itself.

Showtime Boxing began in 1986 and hosted some of the greatest and most famous fighters of all time, including Marvelous Marvin Hagler, Thomas Hearns, Roberto Duran, Sugar Ray Leonard, Mike Tyson, Evander Holyfield and Floyd Mayweather. Among the elite-level professionals who contributed in a broadcasting capacity are Tim Ryan, Gil Clancy, Al Bernstein, Steve Farhood, Barry Tompkins, Jim Gray, Mauro Ranallo and many others; the platform has been an institution and will be greatly missed.

Showtime Sports executive Stephen Espinoza framed the decision in a statement to staff: "The company's decision is not a reflection of the work we have done in recent years, nor of our long and proud history. Unfortunately, in a rapidly evolving media marketplace, the company has had to make difficult choices allocating resources, resetting priorities, and reshaping its content offering. While today's news is certainly difficult and disappointing, it is entirely out of our control." Sporting News agreed on the "long and proud history," arguing that — just as when HBO departed from boxing in 2018 — the achievements of the fighters and of the individuals who gave them the platform should be celebrated, and it looked back at Showtime Boxing's five greatest fights.
