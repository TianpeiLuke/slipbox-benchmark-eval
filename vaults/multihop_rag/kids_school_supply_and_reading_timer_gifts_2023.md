# Kids' School Supply And Reading Timer Gifts 2023

Two school-oriented picks appear in The Verge's kids guide: Arteza Gel Ink Pens in a 60-pack at $17.84+ (Amazon, Target, Arteza) and the Mark-My-Time digital bookmark at $8.95+ (Amazon, Mark-My-Time).

Some kids will always complain about school and homework, the guide allows, but the right set of pens can make both a teeny-tiny bit more enjoyable: Arteza's long-lasting gel pens are great for writing, drawing, coloring, or just doodling, with metal tips and a wide range of colors including metallic, pastel, and glitter finishes. Mark-My-Time's colorful bookmark has a timer built into its top for tracking a child's daily reading sessions — you can set a countdown timer with an alarm so children know when they're done, or set cumulative time to track multiple sessions if you're curious how long it takes them to finish a particular book or assignment.
