# Sony Music's Deepfake Takedowns And Support For The No FAKES Act

Dennis Kooker reported in his November 29, 2023 speech that Sony Music Entertainment has to date sent "close to 10,000 takedowns to a variety of platforms hosting unauthorized deepfakes that SME artists asked us to take down."

He openly discussed the company's efforts to get unauthorized AI content removed from platforms online, and named the obstacle: "platforms are quick to point to the loopholes in the law as an excuse to drag their feet or to not take the deepfakes down when requested." Kooker commended the No FAKES Act policy drafted by senators earlier in 2023, which he explained "would create a federal property right in one's voice or visual likeness and protect against unauthorized AI-generated replicas."
