# Decentralization As A Free Market, Not An Anti-Capitalist Project

The Verge rejected the framing that decentralizing social media is a kumbaya anti-capitalist manifesto — "It's about openness and sharing, not capitalism, man!" — arguing that in practice it is the opposite: a truly free market approach to social networking.

The premise attacked is that decentralization is hostile to large commercial success. Mastodon may not be interested in becoming a trillion-dollar company, the argument concedes, but there's no reason there can't be plenty of trillion-dollar companies built on the fediverse. What changes is the winning strategy rather than the size of the prize: in a fediverse-dominated world, the way to win is not to achieve excellent lock-in and network effects — the only way to win is to build the best product.
