# Week 14's Top Fantasy Quarterback Starts

Sporting News made Josh Allen its QB1 for Week 14 of the 2023 season, ahead of Tua Tagovailoa, Dak Prescott, Jalen Hurts, Patrick Mahomes, Lamar Jackson, Jared Goff and C.J. Stroud.

Despite Buffalo being on the outside looking in for a playoff berth, Allen (at the Chiefs) had come through in fantasy; the Chiefs' defense had done its part in keeping quarterbacks in check that season, entering Week 13 allowing the fifth-fewest fantasy points per game to the position, but Allen had performed well against Steve Spagnuolo's defense in the past, averaging 276.4 passing yards per game with a strong 14:2 touchdown-to-interception ratio over five career games against Kansas City. Not far behind sat Tagovailoa (vs. Titans), who had shredded the Commanders' defense for 280 yards and two touchdowns in Sunday's blowout, with another juicy matchup awaiting against a lackluster Tennessee coverage unit. Prescott (vs. Eagles) remained a top-three play in the rematch against a team he had torched for 374 yards and three touchdowns in a narrow 28-23 loss in Week 9. Hurts (at Cowboys) and the Eagles looked to get back on track in a pivotal NFC East clash in Dallas, facing a Cowboys defense that had let Geno Smith post top-end QB1 numbers the previous week. Mahomes (vs. Bills) was in line for strong numbers in a potential shootout, and although his receiving corps had issues, the reigning MVP was still a top-five play. Jackson (vs. Rams) faced an exploitable Los Angeles secondary, while Goff (at Bears) looked to build off a dominant Week 13 effort against an ascending Chicago stop unit. Stroud (at Jets) saw a slight dip in his Week 14 ranking in a tough road test, but the Rookie of the Year frontrunner still profiled as a set-it-and-forget-it QB1.
