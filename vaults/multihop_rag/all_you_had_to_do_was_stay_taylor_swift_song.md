# All You Had To Do Was Stay: A Track Five That Breaks The Pattern

Traditionally, track five on a Taylor Swift album is the record's most raw and vulnerable moment, but All You Had To Do Was Stay is no Dear John or All Too Well — it is instead a big disinterested "nope" to an ex who comes back begging for a second chance. That inversion of the track-five convention is the song's discriminating feature.

Its origin was a dream in which her ex turned up at the door and, instead of giving him a piece of her mind, all that came out was a high-pitched plea to "stay". When she woke, she recorded the sound into her phone and decided to base a song around it — only this time, she got to say everything she wanted.
