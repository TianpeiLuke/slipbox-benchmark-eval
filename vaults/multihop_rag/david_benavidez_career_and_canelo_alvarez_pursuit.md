# David Benavidez: Anointed Successor To Canelo, Still Waiting For The Call

Fresh off a resounding decision win over former champion Caleb Plant in their March PPV clash, the 26-year-old David Benavidez — born in Arizona to parents of Mexican and Ecuadorian descent — had seemingly been hand-picked by boxing's foundational Mexican-American fan base as the eventual successor to Canelo Alvarez, CBS Sports reported on 2023-11-25. Like Andrade, Benavidez hoped a breakthrough victory against such a tricky opponent would be the final rung on the ladder before finally getting the call to challenge Alvarez for his four-pack of world titles in the division. On Alvarez, Benavidez told "Morning Kombat" the previous month: "To be honest, I don't know what that man is doing. I feel like every time I say something, people say that I'm just looking for a payday. I have been the mandatory fighter for the past two years. I won the title eliminator first and then the WBC interim, which guarantees you a spot. So, the fact that the fight hasn't happened is really crazy to me." He added: "This man is just making it worse for himself because I am gaining so much experience. I'm getting very comfortable and I'm not going nowhere. As soon as I get in the ring with Canelo, all of those belts will come to me."

The preview notes that Benavidez may previously have been his own biggest enemy in a career that saw him become the youngest champion at 20 in divisional history back in 2017, only to twice be stripped of his WBC title — once for a failed drug test for cocaine and a second time mid-pandemic for missing weight. But the maturity he has shown in recent years has been noteworthy, as was the performance against Plant, where Benavidez entered in top physical shape and simply wore his smaller opponent out over 12 rounds. In his own words before the Andrade fight: "I'm trying to prove to the world that I'm the best super middleweight on this planet. I'm going to definitely show that because I have had a tremendous training camp. I have been working for three months already and have been getting some great rounds of sparring. And this is the final Showtime [pay-per-view] so we have to go out with a bang." He added that these are the type of fights that interest him because Demetrius Andrade is a great fighter, no doubt about that, but that he gets to test his heart and spirit in front of everybody — and that when people make him reach deep down, the best of himself comes out.
