# Chiefs-Jets Second Half: Wilson's Fumble Decides A 23-20 Final

The second half of the Oct. 1, 2023 Chiefs-Jets "Sunday Night Football" game turned on a Zach Wilson fumble, and Kansas City closed it out 23-20. New York opened the half with a seven-play, 75-yard drive: Wilson found tight end Ty Conklin for 25 yards and Allen Lazard for 12 to pick up back-to-back first downs at 10:09 p.m., then hit Lazard for a 10-yard touchdown at 10:13 p.m.; Wilson scrambled and dove into the end zone for the two-point conversion at 10:14 p.m. to tie the game at 20.

Momentum stayed with the Jets briefly. At 10:19 p.m. the Chiefs went three-and-out when Mahomes failed to connect with receiver Marques Valdes-Scantling on a deep 3rd & 16 pass, and New York took over at its own 47 after an 11-yard Xavier Gipson punt return. The Jets punted back with a three-and-out of their own at 10:24 p.m., pinning Kansas City at its own 12 with 7:28 left in the third. Breece Hall broke a 43-yard run at 10:32 p.m. to reach the KC 42, but Kansas City's defense stopped New York on 3rd & 11 at 10:36 p.m. and forced a punt. The third quarter ended tied 20-20 with the Chiefs having gained 28 yards on six plays and starting the fourth on their own 40.

Kansas City then took the lead and kept it. Mahomes completed seven passes on a drive that reached the Jets' 13 (10:45 p.m.), and Harrison Butker hit a 26-yard field goal at 10:48 p.m. to cap a 14-play, 80-yard drive, making it 23-20 with 10:51 to play. At 10:55 p.m. Wilson dropped back before he had full control of the snap and fumbled; Chiefs defensive tackle Tershawn Wharton recovered, giving Kansas City the ball at the NYJ 47 with 7:24 remaining. Mahomes converted a 3rd & 22 with a 24-yard run up the middle at 11:02 p.m. At 11:06 p.m. the Jets intercepted Mahomes on 3rd & 20, but a controversial defensive holding call returned the ball to Kansas City with an automatic first down, drawing angry protests from Jets head coach Robert Saleh with 4:29 left. The Jets had burned all their timeouts by the two-minute warning (11:13 p.m.), with Kansas City facing 3rd & 8 at the NYJ 11. At 11:16 p.m. Mahomes scrambled for a first down and, able to walk into the end zone untouched, elected to slide at the one-yard line to end the game.
