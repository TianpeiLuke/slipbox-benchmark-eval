# Noir Viewing Tiers And Extra Recommendations

Polygon organises its Noirvember recommendations into progressive tiers, each with its own overflow list of extra titles: the seminal basics, the after-the-basics picks, and the deep cuts for people who have seen everything else.

The first tier is a few seminal noirs, the basics you will want to hit to understand the genre and its conventions; more in that category are Le Samouraï, Rififi, Laura, The Third Man, Touch of Evil, The Big Sleep, In a Lonely Place, Chinatown, and Night of the Hunter. The second tier is where noir fans' mileage varies most, depending on which aspects they like — the mysteries, the fraught relationships, the twisty stories and unexpected reveals, or just the overall mood — and Polygon recommends its picks regardless of subgenre preference; more in that category are Strangers on a Train, Ace in the Hole, Murder My Sweet, The Asphalt Jungle, The Killing, The Set-Up, and Scarlet Street. The third tier is what you see from anyone who has been at the Noirvember game for a while: titles that lack a Billy Wilder or Humphrey Bogart to bring them into the cinematic limelight but have plenty of their own memorable charms; more in that category are Force of Evil, Nocturne (1946), Female Jungle, Killer's Kiss, Abandoned (1949), No Man of Her Own, and Nightmare Alley (1947).
