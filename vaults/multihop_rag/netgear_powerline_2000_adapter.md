# Netgear Powerline 2000 Adapter

The Netgear Powerline mitigates slow console download speeds and severely pixelated images during a film's emotional climax by creating a power plug-based tether between your router and your devices — a fix aimed at people who primarily use Wi-Fi for their home theater devices.

Each Powerline adapter features an ethernet port, letting you use your home's electrical lines as an internet pathway. In practice you plug one in near your router and connect an ethernet cable, then use another adapter near the device you want to hard-wire.
