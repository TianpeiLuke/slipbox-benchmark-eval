# First Half Live Updates From Manchester United 2-1 Chelsea

The first half at Old Trafford ended 1-1, with Scott McTominay putting United ahead in the 19th minute and Cole Palmer levelling in the 45th, per Sporting News' live blog of Dec. 6, 2023, which called it a hectic first half.

The visitors got the game underway. In the 3rd minute Dalot picked out Fernandes on the edge of the box, who blasted well over the bar with a first-time strike; in the 4th Sanchez made a great save to deny Hojlund the opener after he was well found by Luke Shaw. In the 7th minute United were rewarded for their bright start with a penalty as Antony was brought down by Enzo Fernandez in the box — Chris Kavanagh originally waved play on, but VAR instructed him to take a look at the monitor and the referee gave the penalty. In the 9th minute it was saved: a woeful penalty from Fernandes and an easy diving save to his right by Sanchez. In the 15th minute it had all gone a bit wild as neither side appeared to have a midfield capable of dictating the play, with Chelsea having two glorious chances but Mudryk unable to beat Onana and Fernandes making a brilliant recovery run to prevent the other attack. In the 18th minute Silva's block denied Antony the opener; he had not scored at Old Trafford since his debut.

In the 19th minute came the goal for 1-0 United, Scott McTominay finishing off a scrappy period of play for a deserved lead. In the 25th minute there was a penalty appeal for Chelsea as Sterling dropped to the floor after very minimal contact with Victor Lindelof, but neither the referee nor VAR bought his theatrics. In the 32nd minute McTominay should have had his second — Fernandes picked him out beautifully from the left but the Scotsman headed directly at Sanchez, and either side of the Chelsea goalkeeper would have given United a two-goal lead. In the 36th minute Mudryk found himself in the box on his weak foot but dragged his effort wide. In the 45th minute came the equaliser: the former City man found the bottom corner with a delicate stroke past Onana, a wonderful goal from Cole Palmer to pull level right before the break.
