# NBA MVP Futures And Other NBA Futures Markets

A futures bet in any sport is a wager on an event usually weeks or months away, settled at the end of a season or a tournament — and predicting the next NBA MVP is a popular futures option at the best sports betting sites.

Every sports betting site will have NBA futures markets, including the NBA Most Valuable Player award, Rookie of the Year odds, regular-season win total OVER/UNDER bets and more; MVP odds are posted at top online sportsbooks and the MVP is far from the only NBA futures bet available.

Futures sit within a broad NBA betting menu. The NBA is the second-most popular sport in the US for betting, behind only the NFL, so the bet types run deep: in addition to pregame NBA betting lines such as moneylines, point spreads and totals, you can bet on NBA player and team props, parlays, teasers and more with legal and regulated online sportsbooks.
