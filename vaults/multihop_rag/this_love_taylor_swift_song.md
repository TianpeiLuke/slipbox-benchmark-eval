# This Love: A Journal Poem Turned Into A Vocal Experiment

This Love began as a poem in Taylor Swift's journal before it became a song. In an interview with the Scholastic book club she recalled: "I just wrote it down, and closed the book and put it back on my nightstand, like, 'Oh, that was fun'. Then all of a sudden in my head, I started hearing this melody - and that's when I realised it was going to be a song."

Its distinguishing feature on 1989 is vocal: Swift was beginning to experiment with different vocal styles, and the multi-tracked, semi-whispered performance here foreshadows the delicate sounds of the Folklore and Evermore albums. The effect is almost aquatic, as though she is drowning in the high tide she depicts in the opening verse — which suits the song's theme of cutting someone adrift because they are not ready for a full relationship.
