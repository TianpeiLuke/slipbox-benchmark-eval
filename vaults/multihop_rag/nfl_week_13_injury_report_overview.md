# NFL Week 13 Injury Report Overview

CBSSports.com's Dec. 1, 2023 roundup of the final Week 13 injury reports led with Cleveland's quarterback situation: veteran Joe Flacco was named the starter against the Rams with Dorian Thompson-Robinson ruled out with a concussion.

The framing was that Week 13 was in full swing and teams were starting to truly jockey for playoff position as the regular season rocketed down the stretch run. With it being so late in the year, injuries continued to be a predominant storyline to monitor heading into each slate, and that weekend was no different — the Cleveland quarterback turn was just one situation the piece would monitor. All NFL odds quoted in the piece came via SportsLine Consensus odds.
