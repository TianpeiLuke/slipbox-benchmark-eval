# Chelsea Janes' Defense: Reporting An On-The-Record Shout Is Not Wrong

Chelsea Janes argued that Jake Mintz was not in the wrong to report Orlando Arcia's taunt, posting a thread defending him after Alanna Rizzo's comments drew heat from the baseball journalism world. Her grounds were the circumstances of the remark: cameras and recorders were recording, and Arcia yelled the comments while they were recording and with more than a dozen reporters around. On that basis, reporters abstaining from writing about his comments would be akin to "protecting players from themselves."

She added that what Arcia said was not a slur or hateful, and that the only reason anyone was concerned about it was because "they know it would motivate Harper to beat [the Braves]. Which implies the Braves do not feel they can handle a motivated Bryce Harper and are willing to suggest that is why they lost the other day."

"I didn't say something sooner because I thought this was overblown," Janes continued. "And frankly, I thought it wouldn't help. But I am genuinely disgusted by the way some media members are acting like a reporter was in the wrong for this. He wasn't. And if the Braves let this be the story of their series, that's on them. Win and it goes away. Period."
