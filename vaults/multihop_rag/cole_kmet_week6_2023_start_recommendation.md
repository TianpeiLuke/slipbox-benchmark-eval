# Cole Kmet As A Week 6 2023 Start Against Minnesota

CBS Sports listed Cole Kmet under "Start Him" for Week 6 of the 2023 season against Minnesota: the claim is that starting Kmet means buying into his role as Justin Fields' change-up to D.J. Moore and, this week, a change-up to Chicago's depleted run game, and the warrant is his red-zone role and touchdown opportunities, which survive a matchup that suppresses tight end yardage.

The role grounds are recent and specific. Really since the Bears told Chase Claypool to take a hike, Kmet has become a focal point of the offense: over the past two games he has averaged seven targets per game with a 24.6% target share along with four red-zone targets and three end-zone targets, second to D.J. Moore in all of those categories except red-zone targets, where he is first by a lot, with no one else — Moore included — having more than one. In Week 5 he kept rolling as Fields' second-favourite target, turning five throws into a 5-42-1 line against a Commanders defense that had shut down the Cardinals, Bills and Eagles tight ends to start the season. This week Chicago may need him as a short-area target to help make up for the run game, being expected to be without Khalil Herbert and rookie Roschon Johnson; in an effort not to overwork backs who won't have it easy against the Vikings, Kmet could handle more short-area throws than normal, and his average depth of target over the past two games, 7.64, isn't far from his season average of 6.69.

The matchup grounds cut against the yardage but not the scoring. The Vikings allow the fewest yards per catch to rival tight ends, 5.9 yards, even keeping a lid on Travis Kelce the previous week at 6.7 yards per catch, and they have seen the fourth-lowest average depth of target from tight ends, 5.0 yards per target, almost as if teams know they won't succeed on downfield shots against them, with zero completions of 20-plus yards allowed to tight ends. Those numbers are tough, but if Kmet gets the target volume he has had over the past couple of weeks he can still put up palatable numbers — and tight ends have scored three times in the Vikings' past three games. The verdict is ready to chance it with Kmet over Kyle Pitts, George Kittle and Dallas Goedert.
