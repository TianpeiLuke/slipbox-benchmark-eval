# Jordan Morgan's Senior Day At Arizona Stadium

Fifth-year senior captain Jordan Morgan, who had missed his opportunity to play in Senior Day the previous season due to injury, said that being able to compete and win in his final game at Arizona Stadium "means so much." The Tucson native was playing at home for the last time in his career.

"I finally got to finish off with a bang like this at home for the last time in my career, so really enjoyed it," Morgan said.
