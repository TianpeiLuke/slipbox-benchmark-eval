# ODI Cricket World Cup Winners List

Australia are the most successful nation in ODI World Cup history with five titles, ahead of India and the West Indies, who have two each, and England, Sri Lanka and Pakistan with one apiece.

The full roll of finals runs: 1975 in England, West Indies beat Australia by 17 runs; 1979 in England, West Indies beat England by 92 runs; 1983 in England, India beat West Indies by 43 runs; 1987 in India and Pakistan, Australia beat England by seven runs; 1992 in Australia and New Zealand, Pakistan beat England by 22 runs; 1996 in Pakistan, India and Sri Lanka, Sri Lanka beat Australia by seven wickets; 1999 in England, Australia beat Pakistan by eight wickets; 2003 in South Africa, Australia beat India by 125 runs; 2007 in the West Indies, Australia beat Sri Lanka by 53 runs (DLS); 2011 in India, Sri Lanka and Bangladesh, India beat Sri Lanka by six wickets; 2015 in Australia and New Zealand, Australia beat New Zealand by seven wickets; and 2019 in England and Wales, England beat New Zealand on boundary count after a Super Over.

By country the titles are: Australia 5 (1987, 1999, 2003, 2007, 2015); India 2 (1983, 2011); West Indies 2 (1975, 1979); England 1 (2019); Sri Lanka 1 (1996); and Pakistan 1 (1992). The list is current as of the Oct. 1, 2023 article, published before the 2023 tournament was played.
