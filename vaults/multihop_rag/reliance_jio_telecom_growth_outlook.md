# Reliance Jio Telecom Growth Outlook

Business Line's model of Reliance Jio's prospects starts from market structure: Jio has emerged as the largest telecom services company in what is largely a duopolistic Indian market, given survival issues at Vodafone Idea.

The growth mechanism runs from technology to pricing. 5G will drive advances over the next few years, and new 5G-enabled use cases will create guzzling growth in data consumption, providing scope for higher ARPUs. A second mechanism is breadth of offering: Reliance Jio has offerings across the spectrum of wireless, wireline/broadband, enterprise/cloud services and allied retail and enterprise digital offerings, which make its services sticky for customers.

The relation the article draws from these is durability of position and margin: Jio's dominant position in telecom, along with second player Bharti Airtel, is likely well entrenched for the foreseeable future in India's digital ecosystem, and these factors position it advantageously to sustain or increase its operating margins in a growing industry.
