# Roblox's Position On AAA Gaming

Asked at the Code Conference by The Verge's Andrew Melnizek about leaked internal emails from Microsoft's Xbox CEO Phil Spencer on the future of AAA game publishing and development — that it is just really expensive, that people have to take bigger bets to succeed, and that it is getting harder and harder — David Baszucki said: "Internally, we have nothing near a bull's-eye on AAA gaming."

His stated engineering constraint is the opposite end of the hardware range: internally there is always a struggle to have all of Roblox's engineers working on midrange Android phones as a primary and very difficult platform, so the focus is on performance on midrange and low-end devices rather than the high end. Over time, he allowed, you could imagine even those low-end devices starting to support more and more realism, with easier deployment on the cloud and an existing social network in place, so there may be some natural evolution toward higher fidelity — but definitely nothing deliberate on Roblox's part.
