# Verdict On The 16-Inch M3 Max MacBook Pro

The 16-inch MacBook Pro with M3 Max is the most powerful Apple laptop ever, and is best thought of as a portable desktop — big, heavy, expensive and powerful — rather than as a travel machine.

The grounds are experiential and comparative. TechCrunch's reviewer ported everything over from the 15-inch Air used daily for several months, and the 16-inch Pro felt like a different species: a beast in pretty much every sense, with a bright display, massive battery life and impressive power under the hood. The M3 Max is plenty powerful for even most professional uses, and the chip affords the laptop the ability to power up to four external displays, making it a fine workstation option for many. But the reviewer planned to leave it at home for a cross-country flight — partly due to an ongoing back injury, but frankly because as good as the Pro's battery life is (very good, for the record), the Air is a much better travel companion: thin, light, with more than enough processing power for 95% of the things 95% of people need, and it will not wrestle you for the arm rest.

The recommendation is therefore segmented by need. The 15-inch Air continues to be the best MacBook for the vast majority of users and remains the pick if you fly a lot; the 14-inch Pro is worth looking at if gaming is important, though the reviewer is still a long way off from recommending any Macs as pure gaming machines; the 16-inch is the pick for those who really need to up their professional game but still need some portability; and for pure desktop power the Mac Studio and Mac Pro with the M2 Ultra is still king — until the M3 Ultra rolls out. You will still get more performance out of the M2 Ultra in the Mac Studio, but for most intents and purposes this is the desktop of laptops.
