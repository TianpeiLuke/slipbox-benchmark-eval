# NHL Opening Night 2023 Schedule

The Blackhawks-Penguins game was sandwiched between two other matchups on NHL opening night 2023, with Predators-Lightning first on the docket and Golden Knights-Kraken closing the evening.

Tampa Bay started the year without star goaltender Andrei Vasilevskiy, who was expected to miss the first two months of the season after undergoing back surgery.

Before the Vegas game, the Golden Knights would hold a ceremony to raise their 2023 Stanley Cup banner.
