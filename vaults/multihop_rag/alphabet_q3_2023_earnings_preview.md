# Alphabet Q3 2023 Earnings Preview

Alphabet was set to announce its Q3 results during Tuesday 24 October 2023's post-market session, with the search-engine giant having outperformed the broader market index at a 55% growth rate that year, surpassing the S&P 500's 12%.

Following the company's upbeat Q2 results, investment firm Bank of America said it was expecting "favorable" headlines, due in part to strength in YouTube, search and artificial intelligence. Just a few days before the earnings report, Seeking Alpha's Quant Rating system changed its view from Strong Buy to Hold, while Wall Street analysts maintained a bullish view with a Strong Buy rating.

Consensus EPS estimates were $1.45 and consensus revenue estimates $75.73B. On the earnings insight, Google had topped EPS expectations in 4 of the past 8 quarters, beating revenue estimates in 5 of those reports.
