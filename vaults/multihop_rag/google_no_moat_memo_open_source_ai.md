# Google's "No Moat" Memo On Open Source AI

A leaked internal memo from Google earlier in 2023 seemed to express fears that despite the huge advances made by proprietary LLM models from the likes of OpenAI, open source AI would ultimately trump them all. Its line: "We have no moat, and neither does OpenAI."

The memo's grounds were a specific event. It was written in reference to a foundation language model initially leaked from Meta in March 2023, which gained a fair bit of steam in a short period of time.

The warrant TechCrunch draws from that episode is about the development model rather than any single release: it highlighted the power and scalability of a more open approach to AI development, because openness enables collaboration and experimentation on a level that is not so easy to replicate with closed models.
