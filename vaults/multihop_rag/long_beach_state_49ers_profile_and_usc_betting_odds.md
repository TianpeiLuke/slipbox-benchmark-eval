# Long Beach State's Profile And The USC Betting Odds

USC was a 14.5-point favorite over Long Beach State with a total of 159.5, and Sporting News warned bettors to tread lightly on Bronny's debut because the 49ers were plenty capable of pulling off an upset at the Galen Center, per its Dec. 9, 2023 preview.

The listed college basketball betting odds were: spread LBSU 14.5 (-110), USC 14.5 (-110); total 159.5, OVER/UNDER -110. Sunday's game against Long Beach State represented USC's last home game until conference play began in January.

Long Beach State came in at 6-4 with wins over major conference squads in DePaul and Michigan. The 49ers upset the Wolverines in Ann Arbor and had beaten Iona, Montana State and Sacramento State since then, losing to Illinois State and Louisiana. Highly respected head coach Dan Monson led a solid team.
