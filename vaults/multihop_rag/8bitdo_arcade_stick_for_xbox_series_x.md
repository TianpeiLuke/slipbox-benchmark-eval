# 8BitDo Arcade Stick For Xbox Series X

As of 2023-12-09 the 8BitDo Arcade Stick for Xbox Series X was still available at Woot for $74.99, which Polygon called the best price yet for the $119.99 controller — a 38% discount, at prices taken at time of publishing.

Polygon frames the massive gamepad as a must for fighting game fans, whether you had recently picked up Street Fighter 6 or Mortal Kombat 1 or were looking forward to the launch of Tekken 8 in February. Its pitch is that fighting sticks can be really pricey, and that this stick for the Xbox and PC is a great yet affordable option for getting into fighting games.
