# Inter Miami Lose 1-0 To FC Cincinnati And Fall Out Of The Playoff Race

Inter Miami lost 1-0 to FC Cincinnati at DRV PNK Stadium in Fort Lauderdale, Florida on Oct. 7, 2023, with Alvaro Barreal's 78th-minute goal the only score. The defeat, coupled with CF Montreal's 4-1 win over Portland, meant Inter Miami were officially out of the postseason running.

The match had every hallmark of what made Inter Miami a flawed side when Lionel Messi arrived. Inter Miami utterly dominated the first half but could not find the breakthrough, hitting the woodwork three separate times in the opening half-hour and four times overall. At the other end Drake Callender was nothing short of sensational in goal, but even his heroics were not enough to stymie the electric counter-attack FC Cincinnati possess. Inter Miami were made to rue their early missed chances.

Kickoff was 7:39 p.m. ET / 4:39 p.m. PT — a slight delay from the advertised 7:30 p.m. time — and the referee was Rubiel Vazquez.
