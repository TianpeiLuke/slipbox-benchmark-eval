# People-Centered Robotics Philosophy At Amazon

Asked what role people play in the picture of increasingly automated operations, Amazon Robotics chief technologist Tye Brady answered with a claim of principle: "People will always be at the center of a robotics universe."

His ground for it is an observed relationship rather than a value statement alone: "We know more robots, more jobs that we see through the productivity increases that we have." The warrant is that productivity increases from robots generate the work that people do.
