# The 2023 Power Five Conference Championship Game Slate

The 2023 conference championship weekend slate, as listed by Sporting News on Dec. 1, 2023, ran across five Power Five title games, one of which — the Pac-12 final — had already been played.

Pac-12: No. 3 Washington 34, No. 5 Oregon 31. Big 12: No. 7 Texas vs. No. 18 Oklahoma State, Saturday at 12 p.m. on ABC. SEC: No. 1 Georgia vs. No. 8 Alabama, Saturday at 4 p.m. on CBS. ACC: No. 4 Florida State vs. No. 14 Louisville, Saturday at 8 p.m. on ABC. Big Ten: No. 2 Michigan vs. No. 16 Iowa, Saturday at 8 p.m. on FOX.
