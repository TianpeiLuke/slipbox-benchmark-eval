# Pregame Notes: Colorado At Arizona State, Oct. 7 2023

Before kickoff of the Oct. 7, 2023 Colorado-Arizona State game, the Sporting News live blog reported a channel change, a Colorado personnel update, and Arizona State's losing streak — the game would begin on the Pac-12 Network Arizona because the Washington State vs. UCLA game was running long.

On personnel, at 6:10 p.m. starting safety Trevor Wood was reported out for Colorado, with the good news that Shilo Sanders would start. At 5:50 p.m. Colorado was reported to be debuting new helmets in Tempe, and at 5:20 p.m. the blog noted that the Buffaloes had entered the chat.

On the Arizona State side, at 6:05 p.m. the blog noted that the Sun Devils were looking to end their eight-game losing streak against FBS teams — ironically, their last FBS win had come against Colorado, in the game where Trenton Bourguet set a school record for most passing yards in a career start. Bourguet was expected to be Arizona State's starting quarterback that night.
