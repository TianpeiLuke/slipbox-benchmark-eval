# Roku Streambar Pro

If you are in the market for both a streaming device and a soundbar, Polygon says there is precisely one product it can recommend that does both: the Roku Streambar Pro.

Plugged into your TV's HDMI port, it delivers sound quality that defies its petite size, and its streaming capabilities are up there with the best of them, providing 4K HDR so movies and TV shows look their best. The setup also comes with a universal remote featuring voice controls.
