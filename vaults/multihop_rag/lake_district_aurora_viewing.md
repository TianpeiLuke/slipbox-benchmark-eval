# The Lake District: Rarer Lights, More Dramatic Backdrops

The Lake District, better known as a destination for walking, hiking and simply enjoying the peace and tranquility of the surrounding nature, is also one of the best places in England to see the Northern Lights. The area is an exceptional place for stargazing too, and is currently seeking to become an International Dark Sky Reserve.

The trade-off is frequency against setting: the lights are rarer here than in Scotland, but the lakeside backdrops make for particularly dramatic surroundings if they do appear, with excellent viewpoints at Grizedale Forest, Wasdale, the Langdale Valley and Derwentwater.
