# Group Psagot's Share Price Collapse

Just over two years since the deal that was meant to move Rani Zim up a league, Group Psagot for Finance and Investments (TASE: GPST), which holds the investment house that was once the biggest in Israel, has a market cap of just NIS 97 million — after a 75% decline in its share price in the past year and 80% since the acquisition.

Zim has so far invested nearly NIS 130 million of his own capital in buying control of Psagot, including in a rights issue the group made in the summer of 2023. The 62% stake he holds in Group Psagot — the public company, formerly called Value Capital, through which he bought the investment house — is currently worth just NIS 64 million, meaning he lost about half his investment within a short time.
