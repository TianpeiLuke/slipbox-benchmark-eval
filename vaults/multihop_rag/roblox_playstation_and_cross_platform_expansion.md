# Roblox's PlayStation And Cross-Platform Expansion

At Roblox's annual developer conference in San Francisco two weeks before the Code Conference interview published on October 12th, 2023, the biggest crowd reaction in the opening keynote was for PlayStation support. Asked what took so long, CEO David Baszucki said the company had put enormous focus on mobile quality over the last couple of years because mobile is its hugest market: Roblox started as a PC product, went to Mac, and always held the vision of connecting people around the world on every platform. With mobile in really good shape and Xbox in good shape, it started rolling out new platforms. The creator community loves PlayStation because all of their existing creations will work there, so for creators making a living and building new businesses it is an immediate expansion of their business.

Asked whether anything was unique about PlayStation from an economics or business partnership perspective, or whether the delay was all technical, Baszucki said nothing too unique: every platform has been very thoughtful about when to start allowing social cross-platform everywhere, and five or 10 years ago, when Roblox started this notion, Xbox and PlayStation were arguably a little more walled garden-ish. The vision of connecting and communicating in a 3D space no matter where you are, with the best user interface, the best camera, and the best motion — whether on a phone or a console — has, he said, really come to bear right now.
