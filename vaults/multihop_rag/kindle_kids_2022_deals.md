# Kindle Kids 2022 Deals

Amazon's 2022 entry-level Kindle arrived alongside a new Kindle Kids, which is identical to the standard model but comes with a handful of accessories and provides age-appropriate content for younger readers who prefer digital books. Like the last-gen Kindle Kids, the new model retails for $20 more than the base model, bringing the MSRP to $119.99.

The edition consists of four items: the device, a case, a two-year extended replacement guarantee in the event the device breaks, and one year of Amazon Kids Plus. The last is the biggest selling point aside from the kid-friendly patterns, since it lets parents grant their child access to a digital library of kid-friendly books like Percy Jackson and the entire Harry Potter series at no additional cost.

As of October 16th, 2023, there were no deals available: The Verge had recently seen the Kindle Kids drop as low as $79.99 for Black Friday, but at the time of writing it could only be bought at Amazon and Best Buy for its full retail price of $119.99.
