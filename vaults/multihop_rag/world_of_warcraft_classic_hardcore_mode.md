# World of Warcraft Classic: Hardcore and Season of Discovery

World of Warcraft Classic, developed by Blizzard Entertainment for Mac and Windows PC, is ranked 40 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31, and critic Oli Welsh argues it is quietly, and paradoxically, where Blizzard is doing its most forward-thinking work.

Something very interesting started happening in the retro version of WoW that year. While Blizzard has been content to march one half of the game's community forward through its history of expansions, it started to come up with creative ways to keep the other half — the half that wants to stay in the game as it was at launch — engaged.

The first of these was Hardcore, a brilliant permadeath mode that instantly made this aging game both more dangerous and more social, resurrecting the spirit of its 2004 servers. The second, just launched at the time, is the wild Season of Discovery, which remixes and restructures the original WoW experience — interpolating staggered level caps, shuffling class roles — in ways Welsh says might just change MMO design forever.
