# Dolphins-Eagles First Half: Philadelphia Leads 17-10 Behind Goedert And The Tush Push

Philadelphia led Miami 17-10 at halftime of the Oct. 22, 2023 "Sunday Night Football" game, with Dallas Goedert and Jalen Hurts scoring the Eagles' touchdowns on National Tight Ends Day. The Eagles started with the ball (8:23 p.m.) and rode D'Andre Swift early — three carries in three snaps for a first down (8:26 p.m.), five touches before anyone else saw the ball (8:28 p.m.) — then Hurts hit Goedert on back-to-back passes for 45 yards into the red zone (8:30 p.m.). Three straight runs in the red zone drew unhappy reactions from Eagles fans (8:32 p.m.), and Jake Elliott made a 24-yard field goal at 8:33 p.m. for a 3-0 lead.

Miami answered by trading punts and then capitalizing on a fumble. Tyreek Hill opened with a 15-yard catch (8:38 p.m.), Haason Reddick dropped Raheem Mostert for a five-yard loss (8:39 p.m.), and the Dolphins punted after a short 3rd & 15 pass (8:40 p.m.). David Long deflected a Hurts second-down pass (8:44 p.m.), then Jaelan Phillips and Bradley Chubb knocked the ball out of Hurts' hands at 8:46 p.m., giving Miami possession just outside the red zone. Jaylen Waddle went airborne for a third-down catch to set up first-and-goal (8:48 p.m.), but the Eagles lost yardage on three different plays in the quarter and Miami faced 3rd & Goal from the 12 (8:50 p.m.); a Tyreek Hill touchdown was erased by holding (8:51 p.m.) and Jason Sanders tied it with a field goal at 8:52 p.m. Boston Scott's return set Philadelphia up at its own 39 (8:56 p.m.) and DeVonta Smith's eight-yard grab reached Dolphins territory (8:57 p.m.) as the first quarter ended 3-3.

The Eagles took control in the second quarter. Hurts converted 4th & 1 with a two-yard sneak (9:03 p.m.), a Christian Wilkins roughing-the-passer penalty pushed Philadelphia to the edge of the red zone (9:05 p.m.), and Goedert took a catch-and-run into the end zone at 9:06 p.m. for a 10-3 lead. Miami punted after another Tagovailoa incompletion (9:12 p.m.). Julio Jones made his first catch as an Eagle, a three-yard gain (9:15 p.m.); DeVonta Smith caught a dart into Miami territory (9:19 p.m.); Hurts was fortunate that Smith rather than a defender came down with a pass thrown while he was hit (9:22 p.m.); and on fourth down Hurts escaped heavy pressure and found A.J. Brown for what was initially called a touchdown but placed the Eagles at the goal line (9:23 p.m.). Hurts was then tush-pushed in on 1st & Goal at 9:24 p.m. for 17-3. Jaylen Waddle walked back to the locker room and was questionable to return with a back injury (9:30 p.m.). Tagovailoa rescued a stalling drive with a 29-yard pass to Cedrick Wilson at the two-minute warning (9:33 p.m.), catches by Braxton Berrios and Hill reached the Eagles' 29 (9:37 p.m.), rookie Nolan Smith sacked Tagovailoa with 44 seconds left (9:38 p.m.), and Tagovailoa hit Hill in the end zone through double coverage at 9:39 p.m. for 17-10. Hill was up to 65 yards on eight catches, with no other Dolphins receiver above two catches and Hill at 879 yards on the season (9:43 p.m.). A Jaelan Phillips sack of Hurts ended Philadelphia's last hope before halftime (9:45 p.m.).
