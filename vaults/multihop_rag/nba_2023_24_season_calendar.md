# The 2023-24 NBA Season Calendar

Sporting News listed the key dates of the 2023-24 NBA season for bettors.

The regular season begins October 24; the in-season tournament runs November 3 to December 9; All-Star Weekend 2024 is February 16-18; the regular season ends April 14; the play-in tournament runs April 16-19; the playoffs begin April 20; and NBA Finals Game 1 is June 6.
