# The 2023 Senate Hearing On Bank Capital Rules

At the annual Senate hearing reported on 2023-12-06, executives from the biggest financial institutions and legislators clashed over new regulations that would require the big banks to keep larger capital reserves: the banks argued the rules would limit their ability to lend to lower and middle income buyers, while committee chair Senator Sherrod Brown (D-Ohio) disagreed and said any reduced lending would instead be due to banks prioritizing riskier investments with higher returns.

The hearing featured eight executives in all, including the four retail bank CEOs Menendez questioned. Brown put his objection bluntly in his opening remarks: "You'd rather fund risky trading and derivatives bets than boring bread and butter small business lending."
