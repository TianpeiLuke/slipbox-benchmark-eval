# Ashton Moya AFL Draft Prospect 2023

Ashton Moya — pronounced Moy-ya — profiled in Kevin Sheehan's top 40 for the 2023 AFL Draft as reported by The Roar on Nov. 15, 2023, is a medium forward who really caught the eye the previous year with his goal kicking and marking ability, including a haul of four majors against Western Australia at the National Championships. State: South Australia. State League Club: Glenelg. Community Club: Plympton. Date of Birth: 15/04/05. Height: 187cm.

He was slightly hampered by a hip injury at various parts of the 2023 season but still managed to kick 23 goals in 17 matches in the talent pathway and showed glimpses of his elite potential. He was fully fit at season's end and excelled at the national Draft Combine, with his time of 8.24 seconds on the agility test in the top bracket. Weapon: Marking on the lead and goalkicking accuracy. AFL player comparison: Bayley Fritsch (Melbourne).
