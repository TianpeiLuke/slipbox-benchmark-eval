# Sports Betting As An Investment Mindset

Sporting News' best bets column argues that sports betting is not a gamble: with the right mindset, strategies and preparedness it is an investment, and the column said on Nov. 3, 2023 that it was loving its return on investment through seven weeks. On that view the activity is not a hobby but "a second job that allows us to punch in while we're sitting on our couch."

The grounds are the work that goes into it. Because the reader's goal is to boost their bankroll, the column's top priority is to help achieve that by putting in a ton of statistical research, attacking all the angles, uncovering betting trends and pinpointing value across all the sports betting sites each week — which is how it arrives at its best moneyline, spread, over/under and SuperDraft player prop bets for a slate it describes as featuring a plethora of lucrative opportunities.
