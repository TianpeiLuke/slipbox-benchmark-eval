# Love & Friendship (2016): Whit Stillman's Austen Adaptation

Love & Friendship is Whit Stillman's 2016 adaptation of Jane Austen's Lady Susan, running 1h 30m, starring Kate Beckinsale, Xavier Samuel and Emma Greenwell. Beckinsale plays Lady Susan, a young widow looking to secure appropriate matches for both her daughter (Morfydd Clark) and herself, flirting and scheming her way through the film to the delight of the audience and the frustration of her suitors and friends. Polygon calls it one of Beckinsale's richest and most hilarious roles.

The supporting elements Polygon singles out are Chloë Sevigny as Susan's supportive best friend, Tom Bennett as a hilariously dense wealthy fool, Stillman's characteristic biting dialogue, and attention to detail in sets and costuming — but the whole is held together by Beckinsale's transcendent performance, which the reviewer offers as a recent example of how comedic roles get ignored during awards season. It is available to stream on Prime Video.
