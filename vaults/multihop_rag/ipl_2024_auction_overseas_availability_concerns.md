# IPL 2024 Auction: Overseas Availability Concerns

Overseas players who cannot serve a full IPL season should expect their price to suffer, because availability — not just ability — determines what a franchise is buying. At 10.40 AM the live blog put the case directly: there will be a lot of teams eyeing the English players, but they won't be available for the playoffs, and this could go against them in bagging a big contract.

The same reasoning was applied at 12.17 PM to one of the most talked-about players, Josh Hazlewood, who might not be available till the last leg of the tournament — leaving open the question of whether teams would target him at all.
