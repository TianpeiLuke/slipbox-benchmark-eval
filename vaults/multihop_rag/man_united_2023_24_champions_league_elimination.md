# Manchester United's 2023/24 Champions League Elimination

Manchester United were out of Europe altogether after a limp display against Bayern at Old Trafford, where Kingsley Coman sealed a 1-0 win for Thomas Tuchel's side on the final day of Champions League group-stage play, per Sporting News on Dec. 12 and Dec. 13, 2023.

The Red Devils finished bottom of their four-team group, leaving them eliminated from Europe in their worst performance in the competition since 2005. On top of that, they set the record for most goals against in a Champions League group stage by an English club, with 15 conceded in their six games.

Their defensive record had collapsed as the season went on: from a Premier League standpoint they sat eighth in the English top flight, but their -5 goal differential was the worst of any club in the top 10.
