# MLS Season Pass On Apple TV+ As The Only Route To These Matches

Both October 2023 Inter Miami matches covered by Sporting News were available only by streaming on Apple TV+ through MLS Season Pass, with no TV channel carrying them. For the Chicago Fire versus Inter Miami match on Wednesday, Oct. 4, 2023 at 8:30 p.m. ET / 5:30 p.m. PT, the listing gave the TV channel as "— None —" and streaming as Apple TV+ (MLS Season Pass).

The Inter Miami versus FC Cincinnati listing on Oct. 7, 2023 was the same in substance, giving Apple TV+ (MLS Season Pass) as the global streaming option.
