# Everton Under Sean Dyche As A Derby Threat

The Guardian argued on 20 October 2023 that Sean Dyche's Everton posed a genuine threat in the Merseyside derby, because Liverpool's defensive concerns were matched against a visiting side whose overall performance had improved even if its scoring had not.

The grounds run both ways. Liverpool's defensive concerns were heightened by the loss of Andy Robertson for up to three months with a shoulder injury, but were offset by the visitors' troubles in front of goal: a 3-0 defeat of Bournemouth last time out was only the eighth time Everton had scored more than once in a Premier League match in the past 12 months. Against that, the overall improvement in Everton's performance, coupled with three wins in the past four games, raised Dyche's hopes of capitalising on any post-international fatigue in the opposition ranks.

Klopp's compliments towards Dyche and his local rivals were of the back-handed nature, and they supply the warrant — the threat is stylistic, not a matter of form alone. "Everton are in a good moment," he said. "Burnley in their best moments [under Dyche] were a super-difficult team to play against and you can see that again; it's similar with different players. We have to be ready for second ball fights, runs in behind, a compact defence. It will be a tough one. Burnley games were always tough. We have to have football understanding, patience, put a proper shift in and take the fight."
