# Steam Game Awards Winners Sale, 2023

In the weekend following The Game Awards, Steam was discounting many contenders from the 2023 Game Awards along with winners from the past nine years.

Polygon framed it as a fantastic opportunity to supplement your library with some of the biggest releases of 2023, naming Diablo 4 and Starfield, and also to pick up essential titles you may have missed, such as Valiant Hearts, Her Story, and Celeste. Its own coverage highlighted some of the 2023 winners while also featuring a larger list of winners from previous years.
