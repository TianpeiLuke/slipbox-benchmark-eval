# Tyler Adams' Hamstring Injury Left The USMNT Without A Recognized No. 6

Captain Tyler Adams was the notable absence from the USMNT's October 2023 camp, sidelined with a frustrating long-term hamstring injury. He was initially injured playing for Leeds United back in March, and although he had finally recovered enough to make his Bournemouth debut in the EFL Cup in late September, he played just 20 minutes off the bench before suffering a setback that sidelined him again for the foreseeable future.

The absence mattered because Adams is the one proven defensive midfielder — a No. 6, in soccer jargon — on the USMNT roster. He had been absent from the national team since injuring his hamstring in the spring, and by the Oct. 14 friendly against Germany it was possible he would need a second surgery to address the issue.

Asked about him after that match, Berhalter told reporters: "Tyler's a great player. It's not nice to talk about guys that aren't here, though. I'd rather talk about the guys that are on the field."
