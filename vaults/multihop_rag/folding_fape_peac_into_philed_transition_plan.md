# Folding FAPE And PEAC Into PhilEd

The authors propose that the FAPE/PEAC structure and organization be moved to PhilEd, which operationally means folding into PhilEd the roles and responsibilities of FAPE and PEAC as well as their financial resources. The asset transfer step covers FAPE's funds of P277 million as of 31 May 2022, plus the assets and liabilities of PEAC amounting to P567 million as of the same date.

The programs step moves the DepEd programs presently managed by PEAC and lodged in the DepEd budget into PhilEd: Educational Service Contracting at P9.3 billion in School Year 2021-2022, the Senior High School Voucher Program at P20.3 billion in School Year 2021-2022, and the Teachers' Salaries Subsidy at P793 million in School Year 2021-2022. The governance step keeps the existing people in place: the present Board of Trustees of the Fund, consisting of the Secretary of Education as Chairman with the Director-General of the National Economic and Development Authority and the three Presidents of the Association of Christian Schools, Colleges and Universities, the Catholic Educational Association of the Philippines, and the Philippine Association of Colleges and Universities, shall also constitute the proposed Board of Trustees of PhilEd, and the management team of PEAC will be the incoming management team of PhilEd.
