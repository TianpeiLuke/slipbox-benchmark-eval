# Devin Singletary Week 13: Start Him, Last Week's Carry Count Was An Aberration

CBS Sports' Week 13 verdict on Devin Singletary is Start Him, arguing that his disappointing last game was uncharacteristic of the Texans offense rather than evidence of a diminished role. The grounds: Singletary disappointed because he did not get many carries last week, but that is uncharacteristic of the Texans offense; some of it was made up for with receptions, giving him weak yardage totals without a touchdown. The role evidence is the core of the claim — Singletary is still clearly Houston's lead back, having played 81% of their snaps and dominated third downs and snaps inside the 5, even though it appeared Pierce rotated drives with him starting with the third one.

The matchup grounds: the matchup is pretty good, as Denver's run defense is the only element that hasn't improved much this season. The article handles the obvious counter-evidence directly — while it is true that the Broncos have given up just one rushing touchdown in their past six games, it is also true that they have let up 5.4 yards per carry in those same six games. The conclusion is to bank on the Texans giving Singletary more work on the ground to pace their offense, and to start him over Jerome Ford, Javonte Williams and Joe Mixon. Scope: Singletary is a Houston running back, #26, age 26 with 5 years of experience; the weekly breakdown lists the matchup versus Denver with Houston favored by 3.5 and an over/under of 47, the opponent 22nd versus running backs, projected points of 8.7 and an RB rank of 19th; year-to-date he has 489 rushing yards, 18 receptions, 113 receiving yards, 3 touchdowns and 8.8 Fantasy points per game, with projections powered by SportsLine.
