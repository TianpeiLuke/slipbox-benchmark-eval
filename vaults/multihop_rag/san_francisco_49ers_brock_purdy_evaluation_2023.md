# Evaluating Brock Purdy And The San Francisco 49ers, 2023

The New York Times argued on 16 October 2023 that Brock Purdy is a good, young quarterback well-suited to run Kyle Shanahan's offense at a high level by making quick decisions when given time to find open receivers — but not the kind of quarterback who can carry the 49ers when the supporting cast is missing.

The grounds start with what the 49ers do not have in Purdy: an AFC North-certified, weatherproof Ben Roethlisberger clone able to overpower the elements, break away from defensive linemen and thrive in the conditions Purdy faced against Cleveland. The warrant is that Purdy's effectiveness is conditional on time and on receivers getting open, which is a property of the system around him rather than of improvisation.

An NFL executive made the same point about the system's dependencies: "The 49ers knew they needed a good quarterback to win the Super Bowl, and they knew they could help Purdy be that. Give him a great receiving tight end, two good receivers and a great halfback who fits the system, and they'll get someone open on every pass play. But then Deebo Samuel gets hurt, Christian McCaffrey gets hurt, and now we don't have as many guys open. Especially when you face a defensive coordinator who says, 'You show me you can beat me throwing the ball outside the numbers — best of luck, and to do that, you are going to have to take a five-step drop and I'm going to have Myles Garrett chasing you every play.'"
