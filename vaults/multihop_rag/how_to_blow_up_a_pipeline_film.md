# How to Blow Up a Pipeline (Daniel Goldhaber)

How to Blow Up a Pipeline is Daniel Goldhaber's unconventional adaptation of the controversial 2021 nonfiction book, starring Ariela Barer, Lukas Gage, and Forrest Goodluck. Polygon ranked it 5 on its best movies of 2023 list of 2023-10-07 and listed it on Hulu or for digital rental/purchase.

The film applies the ethos of the book — which argued that sabotage is a necessary part of environmental activism — to a fictional scenario: a group of people from different walks of life, students, disillusioned activists, service workers, and punks, gather in Texas with a plan to blow up an oil pipeline and finally enact some real change.

Critic Pete Volk describes the construction: the movie takes the best parts of the heist thriller genre — a likable crew who each bring their own specialized skills, a worthy cause, a focus on the process, a tense finale — and discards the rest, notably any major police or investigator B-plot, making a perfect marriage of the genre and the movie's radical politics without sacrificing either. It is also impeccably crafted, with strong location work that makes the most of America's vast landscapes and hints at past beauty ruined by industrialization, supported by great performances from emerging stars, especially Forrest Goodluck of The Revenant as a quiet, awkward, self-taught explosives expert. One of the buzziest and most controversial movies of the year, it should not be mistaken for an empty vehicle for controversy: Volk calls it a thoughtful, masterful work, and says none of 2023's many great movies feel as urgent and timely.
