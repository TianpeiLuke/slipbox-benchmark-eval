# Gemini Pro's Factual Errors And Confabulation

Gemini Pro failed to get basic facts right about the 2023 Oscar winners in tests reported on 7 December 2023. A user, Vitor de Lucca, posted on X that he was "extremely disappointed with Gemini Pro on Bard," saying it still gave very bad results to questions that should not be hard anymore with RAG, and noting that a simple question with a simple answer still came back wrong. In that exchange Gemini Pro claimed incorrectly that Brendan Gleeson won Best Actor last year, rather than Brendan Fraser, the actual winner.

The TechCrunch reporter asked the model the same question and, bizarrely, got a different wrong answer. In that response the errors were that "Navalny," not "All the Beauty and the Bloodshed," won Best Documentary Feature last year; "All Quiet on the Western Front" won Best International Film; "Women Talking" won Best Adapted Screenplay; and "Pinocchio" won Best Animated Feature Film — a lot of mistakes in one reply.

Science fiction author Charlie Stross found many more examples of confabulation in a recent blog post; among other untruths, Gemini Pro said that Stross contributed to the Linux kernel, which he never has.
