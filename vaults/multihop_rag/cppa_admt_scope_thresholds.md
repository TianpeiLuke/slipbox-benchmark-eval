# CPPA ADMT Scope Thresholds

Not every use of ADMT will be in scope of the CCPA's proposed rules. The draft regulation proposes a threshold covering three cases: a decision that produces legal or similarly significant effects concerning a consumer, for example decisions to provide or deny employment opportunities; profiling a consumer who is acting in their capacity as an employee, independent contractor, job applicant, or student; and profiling a consumer while they are in a publicly accessible place.

Three further cases sit on the boundary, undecided. The Agency says the upcoming consultation will discuss whether the rules should also apply to profiling a consumer for behavioral advertising; profiling a consumer the business has "actual knowledge is under the age of 16," that is, profiling children; and processing the personal information of consumers to train ADMT.

Those three open questions are exactly the ones that determine the regime's reach over the biggest firms: their unsettled status indicates it is not yet confirmed how much of the planned regime will apply to, and potentially limit the modus operandi of, adtech and data-scraping generative AI giants.
