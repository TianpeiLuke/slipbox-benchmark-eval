# Shikho, A Bangladeshi Edtech Competitor

Shikho is a Bangladeshi edtech company focused on elementary school learners and one of 10 Minute School's competitors. Before 10MS's $5.5 million pre-Series A, Shikho held the record for the highest seed-stage funding by a Bangladeshi startup, having raised a total of $5.3 million in seed funding as of March 2022 — a coincidence the reporting notes, since the previous record holder was also an edtech.

The boundary between the two is one of age, size and grade coverage. 10MS's COO Beg says 10MS has the advantage of being older and larger, and of teaching a wider range of grades, whereas Shikho's focus sits at the elementary school level.
