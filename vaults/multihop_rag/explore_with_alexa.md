# Explore With Alexa

"Explore with Alexa" is a feature on Amazon's Echo devices that lets kids have interactive conversations with an AI-powered Alexa. It was first announced in September and, as reported by TechCrunch on 25 October 2023, launched as an addition to the Amazon Kids+ content subscription, allowing children to have kid-friendly conversations with Alexa, powered by generative AI, but in a protected fashion designed to ensure the experience remains safe and appropriate.

The discriminating feature is the combination of generative AI, conversation, and an audience of children under 13. Some AI experiences already cater to younger users — the AI chatbots from Character.ai and other companies, including Meta — but Amazon is among the first to specifically look to generative AI to develop a conversational experience for kids under the age of 13. The two qualifiers that bound the concept are the subscription it lives inside (Amazon Kids+) and the protective framing: this is not an unbounded chatbot but a conversational experience deliberately constructed to stay safe and age-appropriate.
