# E-Commerce Search Startup Viability Challenges

Amir Konigsberg, an investor and founder of e-commerce search startup Twiggle, which shut down, attacks the premise that a better search experience is the binding constraint. His startup wanted to solve ChatGPT-type interactions in the shopping world, and he said the main problem for startups in this space is the go-to-market strategy and fundraising.

His grounds are that the incumbents' advantage is not in the interface at all: "The problem with competing with e-commerce players is not just about user experience, it's also about logistics infrastructure and supply chain. So a startup catering to consumer search has to invest a lot of money into making it a viable business," Konigsberg told TechCrunch. He also added that it will be difficult for a startup like Deft to build user loyalty while competing against Google and Amazon.

The remedy both investors converge on points away from a pure consumer product: both Pienaar and Konigsberg said Deft will have to involve retailers in the mix, either as partners or as customers of their search product. Either way, TechCrunch notes, it is going to be interesting to see if Deft can build a user base on its own or sell its search feature to e-commerce retailers.
