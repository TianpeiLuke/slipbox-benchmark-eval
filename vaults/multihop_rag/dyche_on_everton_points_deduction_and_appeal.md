# Dyche On Everton's Points Deduction And Appeal

Everton manager Sean Dyche argued after the 7 December 2023 win over Newcastle that his side's real standing is better than the table shows: the claim is that without the ten-point deduction Everton would be in an amazing position, and the warrant is that a growing team mentality, not the points total, is the measure that matters while the appeal is pending.

"Without those 10 points, we'd be in an amazing position," Dyche said, giving the comparison that makes the claim concrete: "20 points would be amazing relatively to the last two seasons here."

He then set expectations about the appeal and shifted the emphasis onto the squad's resilience: "We'll see what the appeal brings. The mentality is key for me, we took a knock but there have been a lot of knocks here over the last couple of years. But the team mentality is growing. I can't emphasise it enough, the commitment to each other and the connection is a powerful thing because I know we've got quality."
