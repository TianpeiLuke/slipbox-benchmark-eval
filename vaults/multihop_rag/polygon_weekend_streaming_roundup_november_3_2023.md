# Polygon's Weekend Streaming Roundup, November 3 2023

Polygon's new-releases roundup for the weekend of 2023-11-03 named Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse, then streaming on Netflix, as the week's biggest debut.

Netflix also took Insidious: The Red Door, the fifth installment in the Insidious horror franchise, and Jawan, one of the biggest Indian action movies of the year. Elsewhere, A Haunting in Venice arrived on Hulu, the Italian superhero movie Freaks vs. the Reich landed on Prime Video, and The Kill Room and Outlaw Johnny Black, from director-star Michael Jai White, premiered on VOD.
