# Indie Games Suit The Switch's Portability

Polygon's second category of starter recommendations rests on a claim about platform fit: with so many celebrated indie games it can be hard to know which platform is best to play them on, and the Switch's portability makes it the right one for these titles.

On that basis the guide offers three of its absolute favorite indie games for the Switch. The argument is left compact in the source — the grounds are the abundance of acclaimed indies and the reader's difficulty choosing a platform, and the warrant is the Switch's portability, which the individual picks then illustrate.
