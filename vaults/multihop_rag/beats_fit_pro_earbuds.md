# Beats Fit Pro Earbuds

The Beats Fit Pro was down to $160, from $200 — a $40 saving off MSRP, at a price Engadget has seen multiple times in recent months.

The Fit Pro is the "best for workouts" pick in Engadget's wireless earbuds guide, because it offers most of the Apple-friendly perks you would get from a set of AirPods — Beats is a subsidiary of Apple — in a sportier, more stable design. Its punchy yet refined sound is a plus, and it uses physical control buttons instead of touch panels.

The omissions are multi-device pairing and wireless charging, and its ANC is a step down from the AirPods Pro. Engadget gave the Fit Pro a review score of 87 in 2021.
