# The FanDuel Single-Game Lineup For Lions-Packers Week 4, And Why

Sporting News' FanDuel single-game DFS lineup for the Week 4 Lions-Packers Thursday night game, published 2023-09-26, is built on the premise that this will be a high-scoring game, and it fills a $60,000 budget under FanDuel's most notable scoring rules: four-point passing touchdowns and half-point PPR. MVP, at 1.5x points: QB Jared Goff, Lions ($15,500). The reasoning: the Lions might not be the Super Bowl contenders once expected, but Detroit is finding its footing in 2023 and should have success against the Packers. On the surface Green Bay's pass defense is around league average, allowing 207.3 passing yards per game — No. 18 — while Pro Football Focus grades them the No. 8 team in coverage at 81.5; but beneath the surface, the Packers' first three games came against the No. 15, 29 and 31 ranked teams in passing yards per game, or the No. 21, 29 and 32 passing offenses as graded by PFF. The Lions love to throw and have a quarterback that rarely turns it over, so Goff should be in position for a solid night against a defense that might again be without star corner Jaire Alexander (back).

FLEX QB Jordan Love, Packers ($16,000): from one quarterback to another, the writers like this game to feature a lot of points, something that doesn't happen often on "Thursday Night Football" but should change this week, because the Lions seem incapable of playing in low-scoring, grind-it-out games; Love has seven passing touchdowns through the first three weeks and faces a bottom-10 passing defense per PFF, plus Detroit's banged-up secondary just beginning life without C.J. Gardner-Johnson (pec). FLEX WR Romeo Doubs ($10,500): Christian Watson appears primed for a return, but after being held out for multiple weeks with a hamstring injury his workload is questionable, so the pick is Doubs — tied for the team lead in targets with the most touchdowns at three, and along with Jayden Reed a favorite of Love's in the red zone, the duo having combined for 12-of-16 targets to receivers. FLEX TE Sam LaPorta, Lions ($10,000): rookie tight ends aren't typically fantasy-relevant right away, but LaPorta has become a favorite target of Goff on an upward trajectory — five targets in Week 1, six in Week 2, 11 in Week 3, for 39, 63 and 84 yards respectively with a touchdown in the most recent game; the Packers are middle-of-the-road against tight ends but haven't faced one in a passing offense at LaPorta's level, and on a short week with banged-up players he should be a heavily involved security blanket. FLEX TE Luke Musgrave ($8,000): coming off his best game of the season with six catches for 49 yards, he gets a matchup against the second-worst defense against tight ends — the Lions giving up 14.8 fantasy points per game to the position according to FantasyPros — and if Watson does return it could help the rookie as Detroit's defense focuses elsewhere.
