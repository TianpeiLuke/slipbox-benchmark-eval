# Savannah Montano's Denial Attacks The Premise That She Knew Jake Paul

Savannah Montano's rebuttal targets the specific inference that answering Jake Paul's call implied a relationship with him, Essentially Sports reported on 2023-10-07. Paul's claim went beyond the Instagram Stories: he also told TMZ, "Dillon, I know you're watching this … Just remember you're still a bitch and I hooked up with your girlfriend. Savannah, how you doing? What's good?" Montano completely denied any relation with the influencer boxer whatsoever, responding to Paul's stories through a video giving her side of the story. The premise she attacked was the assumption of prior contact: "I'm dead confused at some of you guys going, 'Oh, but why did you answer his call?' As if I have his f**king number saved in my phone! It was literally a random number. You can literally see in the video, I'm like, 'Who is this?'" She denied the accusations of hooking up with Paul and even felt disgusted by the speculations.

The surrounding facts weaken the callout's premise as well: it didn't help much, as Jake Paul never could finalize his fight with Dillon Danis, instead taking a fight against former Olympic MMA artist Ben Askren in 2021, which he again won by knockout, while many fans still thought Danis was smart not to respond to the YouTuber-turned-boxer. Still, Paul tried his best to involve Montano in their non-existent tussle. The dispute remains unresolved on the article's own account: her current relationship status is unknown, and even three years later many followers are still not convinced about her breakup with Dillon Danis. An accompanying image credit shows Jake Paul fighting Tyron Woodley in a rematch of an eight-round cruiserweight bout at the Amalie Arena on December 18, 2021 in Tampa, Florida.
