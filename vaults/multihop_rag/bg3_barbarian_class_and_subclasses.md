# BG3 Barbarian: The Damage Class

Polygon names Barbarian the best class for damage in Baldur's Gate 3. Barbarians are all about combat: they get light and medium armor and shields, but they also have the option to keep their AC up even without wearing armor.

For weapons, Barbarians get a wide range of heavy hitters such as battleaxes and warhammers. Every Barbarian gets the Rage ability, which lets them hit even harder and reduces damage during combat — and only during combat. At level 3 a Barbarian chooses from the Wildheart, Berserker, or Wild Magic subclasses. Wildheart lets you modify Rage with animal-themed tweaks such as healing, jumping, or a stampede; Berserker is a lot more straightforward and focuses on hitting extra things and on throwing; Wild Magic adds a magical flair to Rage with a random magical effect plus improved saving throws against magic for you and your allies. Berserker is the best subclass for a Barbarian, with the reasoning and the skill and feat picks left to Polygon's separate Barbarian build guide.
