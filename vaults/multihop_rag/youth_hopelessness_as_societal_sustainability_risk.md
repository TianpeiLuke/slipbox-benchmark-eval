# Youth Hopelessness As A Societal Sustainability Risk

Dayo Abinusawa, founder of London's Awa Business School and a former lecturer at Cambridge University's Judge Business School, warned that "a society where the young have little to no hope for the future is not a sustainable one." The grounds he offers are the material blockage facing young people: "How can young people build careers or wealth if they don't have jobs, and prices of goods and services continue to increase?"

The warrant joining the two is that a generation deprived of the means to build careers or wealth loses hope, and a hopeless young cohort undermines the society that produced it. The article leaves the consequence open: it remains to be seen what Gen Z will do when they come into decision-making positions in the workplace — perhaps they'll channel their rage and cynicism effectively, as some have already demonstrated.
