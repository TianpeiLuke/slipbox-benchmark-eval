# The Angels' 2023 Offseason: Ohtani And Trout Availability

The Los Angeles Angels faced the impending exit of their star two-way player Shohei Ohtani after a heartbreaking 2023 season, and were also open to trading nine-time Silver Slugger hitter Mike Trout should he request it, as reported by Essentially Sports on 18 November 2023.

The report frames this as a huge miss: a team that couldn't do well even with two of the league's biggest talents on its roster may lose those stars at the same time, leaving open the question of whether they will choose to stay in Los Angeles.

It also sets up the Dodgers angle. The Halos' cross-town rivals, the Los Angeles Dodgers, have been eyeing the dynamic Troutani duo for a long time; however, despite fans' expectations, the Dodgers' Ohtani-Trout conquest might not take place — and for good reasons.
