# Barber Shop Near Me And Felix Silva

Felix Silva decided on the name Barber Shop Near Me after considering more than 20 other options for his Coral Springs, Florida, store in 2019. The name is meant to be neutral and memorable — another contender was "The Barber Shop" — but Silva fully leaned into the Google joke: the logo is a red location pin resembling Google's own, with a blue, white, and red barber pole pattern in the middle. "Before we opened, we just had a logo [on an Instagram account]," Silva says. "And we had some clients then tell us, 'Oh, man, before you guys opened we thought Google was coming to town.'"

Like Jirapraphanan of Thai Food Near Me, Silva had seen firsthand how a business's Google presence could help or hurt its chances of success. He frequently searches for keywords like "vegan" to find restaurants, and one time Google returned results for a restaurant that was not vegan but had a customer review containing the keyword, which was highlighted. "It was a light bulb moment… like, 'Okay, this is the path I need to take. I really need to dial in and focus on the online presence, specifically Google, because that is going to be the foundation of the business,'" Silva says.
