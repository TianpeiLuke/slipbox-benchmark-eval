# Carbon Capture Cannot Replace Emissions Cuts

Carbon absorbers, including MXenes or MBenes, are never going to replace the need for reducing emissions of CO2 and other greenhouse gases, because each year human activities spew tens of billions of tons of CO2 into the air — equivalent to the weight of tens of billions of elephants — and absorbers cannot catch it all.

The argument stops short of dismissing capture, however. Given the urgency of climate change, even a quick or partial fix such as carbon capture cannot be ignored: "At this point in time," says Per Persson, "we need to look at all the possible solutions." MXene-like materials are emerging as potentially important candidates.
