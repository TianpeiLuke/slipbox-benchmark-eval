# Hypothesis: A McGregor Fight Would Lift Canelo's Net Worth To $240 Million

Essentially Sports proposes on 2023-10-18 that if a Canelo versus McGregor fight ever materialises, it could help Canelo take a significant leap toward Floyd Mayweather's net worth. The proposition is quantified: Canelo's biggest fight purse to date is $45 million from the third encounter with Gennady Golovkin, so a fight with McGregor could earn him anywhere from $55 to $60 million, adding to his $180 million, meaning his total net worth would jump up to $240 million. The reasoning behind the estimate is that, if Canelo's past earnings are any evidence, this fight will be bigger than ever before, with the financial windfall from such a humongous clash being substantial.

The hypothesis carries its own stated conditions and limits. It is conditional on the fight happening at all — "only time will tell if the fight can ever materialize" — and the article concedes that matching Mayweather's net worth is no easy feat, while asserting that if anyone can do it, it is Canelo Alvarez, who has inched closer to Floyd's billionaire status over the years despite that gap. The projection would be falsified by the fight never being made, or by a purse outside the $55-$60 million band that fails to move his total to roughly $240 million. The piece closes by putting the open questions back to readers: do you think McGregor versus Canelo can happen, and who would win the fight?
