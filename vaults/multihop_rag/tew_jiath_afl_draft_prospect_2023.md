# Tew Jiath AFL Draft Prospect 2023

Tew Jiath — pronounced T-ow Gee-ath — profiled in Kevin Sheehan's top 40 for the 2023 AFL Draft as reported by The Roar on Nov. 15, 2023, emerged in the back half of the Coates Talent League season with some dazzling performances for the Gippsland Power, including a 29-disposal effort during the Wildcard Round victory over the Dandenong Stingrays. State: Victoria Country. State League Club: Gippsland Power. Community Club: Morwell. Date of Birth: 02/03/05. Height: 188cm.

He is a fine intercept mark and displays fantastic speed away from the contest, averaging 17.7 disposals in 10 matches for the Power to catch the attention of AFL scouts. He is the brother of Hawthorn star Changkuoth Jiath and a member of the Hawks' NGA program. He performed solidly during testing at the state Draft Combine in Melbourne, with 3.03 seconds on the 20m sprint and 6:32 for the 2km time trial both impressive results. Weapon: Intercept marking. AFL player comparison: Will Day (Hawthorn).
