# Andre Onana's Goalkeeping Errors In The 2023 Champions League

Andre Onana was criticised for his role in Galatasaray's second goal, allowing Hakim Ziyech's shot to spill under him and into the net, and was blamed again for United's concession from the Bayern Munich winner in December — a strange season for Erik ten Hag's primary signing that only continues, per talkSPORT on 29 November 2023 and The Independent on 12 December 2023.

Aston Villa legend Gabby Agbonlahor insisted to talkSPORT that Onana needs to take a significant chunk of the blame for United's collapse on the Wednesday night against Galatasaray. "I feel sorry for Onana," Agbonlahor said. "There's a clip of him throwing his gloves to the ground after the game. He's angry with himself. But those two free-kicks... you can't score those. They're easy saves and he's cost his team. If Onana doesn't make those two mistakes, I think United go on to win 4-1." He added: "He's so laid back on the ball that he can't switch on, he's late to dive for the free-kicks." Ten Hag's own view was that collectively the defence isn't up to scratch.

Two weeks later, at the Bayern defeat, the finish was almost too easy for the scorer and the question was why Onana stayed so close to his line — if that sounds like the set-up for a joke, you can write your own punchline. The scope condition matters: this is the current reality for United, who obviously have many more issues than the goalkeeper, as illustrated by the goal itself. United's problems combined, with so many errors leading to the goal, to go with all of those that have characterised the entire European campaign.
