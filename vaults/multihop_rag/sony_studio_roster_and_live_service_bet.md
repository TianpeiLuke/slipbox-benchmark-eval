# Sony's Studio Roster And Its Live-Service Bet

Sony is bigger than Microsoft by revenue and owns 21 development teams — including Bungie, Guerrilla Games, Haven Studios, Housemarque, Insomniac Games, Media Molecule, Naughty Dog and Sucker Punch Productions — even though Microsoft is more often the face of the game-studio acquisition spree.

Sony has been expanding its roster over the past three years, more subtly than Microsoft, and has also made heavy investments in studios like Epic Games and FromSoftware. With that lineup it is betting heavily on ongoing games: it has 12 live-service titles in production right now, on top of Bungie's Destiny franchise, including Haven's Fairgame$ and a multiplayer Horizon title from Guerrilla. Sony Interactive Entertainment president and CEO Jim Ryan set out the rationale in 2022: "By expanding to PC and mobile, and… also to live services, we have the opportunity to move from a situation of being present in a very narrow segment of the overall gaming software market, to being present pretty much everywhere."
