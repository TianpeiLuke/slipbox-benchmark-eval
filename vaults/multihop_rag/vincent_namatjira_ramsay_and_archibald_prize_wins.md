# Vincent Namatjira's Ramsay And Archibald Prize Wins

Vincent Namatjira, 40 at the time of The Guardian's 16 October 2023 interview, has won some of Australia's most high-profile art prizes: the Ramsay art prize in 2019 for a Cook-inspired work, the Archibald prize in 2020 for his portrait of the AFL footballer Adam Goodes, and an Order of Australia medal.

His Archibald-winning portrait of Goodes is titled Stand Strong for Who You Are. Many more prizes followed the classroom prize he won as a teenager.

He describes the change in his life in his own words: "I've become rocketing. Before I ever picked up a paintbrush, I was just a normal Aboriginal fella. And here I am today, popping up everywhere – it's completely changed my life."
