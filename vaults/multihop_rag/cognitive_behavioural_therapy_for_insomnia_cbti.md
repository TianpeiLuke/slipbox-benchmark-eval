# Cognitive Behavioural Therapy For Insomnia (CBTI)

Cognitive Behavioural Therapy for Insomnia (CBTI) is, according to sleep therapist Dr Lindsay Browning, the most effective therapy for insomnia — she calls it a "complete cure" that a lot of people do not know about. As with many other mental health disorders, therapy can often provide long-term relief from insomnia, and CBTI is the form of it designed for this condition.

What distinguishes CBTI from adjacent therapies is its target: "Different from CBT for depression or CBT for anxiety, CBTI works by addressing the negative thoughts and worries that perpetuate insomnia." The object of treatment is the perpetuating worry, not the original trigger.
