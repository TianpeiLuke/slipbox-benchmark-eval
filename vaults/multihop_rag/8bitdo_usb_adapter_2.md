# 8BitDo USB Adapter 2

8BitDo's $19.99 USB Adapter 2 is a cross-platform dongle that lets controllers work on machines they were never paired with — most notably, it lets you use an Xbox controller on your Nintendo Switch.

Beyond Switch support, the adapter easily connects an Xbox controller to other devices such as Mac, Raspberry Pi, and more. It also works perfectly with the DualSense, letting you play Switch games with Sony's pad once it is paired; Polygon's writer preferred to play Splatoon 3 with the PS5 controller's analog stick arrangement, and notes that the adapter even supports gyroscopic camera movement, just like the official Switch controllers.
