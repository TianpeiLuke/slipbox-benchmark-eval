# Jim Harbaugh's Legacy And Future After The Suspension

Sporting News argued that the suspension leaves Harbaugh's standing genuinely two-sided: he could serve his second suspension within one season with further NCAA punishment still to come, or he could emerge as a martyr for Michigan fans, given that there is reportedly no proof yet that he knew of Stalions' rule-breaking.

The record complicates both readings. It is an inescapable fact that Harbaugh is 31-3 — and Michigan 35-3 — since the program bottomed out at the end of the 2020 season. While it is almost impossible to quantify how much the alleged in-person scouting and sign-stealing would have helped the Wolverines over the past two seasons, the violations are now on the Big Ten record, which will be a tough stain to erase.

What Harbaugh does next is left open. Sporting News called it almost impossible to predict given his combative personality: he might sign a contract extension and continue to fight the NCAA, or finally take the parachute to the NFL — questions bound to come up at the end of the 2023 season.
