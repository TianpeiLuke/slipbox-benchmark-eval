# Friendsgiving Alternative Activity Ideas

For a Friendsgiving that is not a sit-down dinner, the options below can be chosen instead of hosting a big meal; the enabling precondition throughout is that Friendsgiving is not necessarily held on the Thanksgiving holiday itself, which frees the date and the venue.

Visit a holiday market. While Christmas is still over a month away, many cities in the US already have their holiday-themed markets up and running, so a Friendsgiving held off the holiday can be used as an opportunity to check out new shops. In New York City, Bank of America hosts its annual Winter Village at Bryant Park in Manhattan, where a wide variety of food vendors lets a group pick dinner and dessert, and vendors also sell goods from ornaments to jewellery to scarves — a chance to do some early holiday shopping.

Host a movie marathon at home. This is the budget-friendly indoor alternative to a big dinner: with the help of friends, make a list of any horror, romance or comedy films to watch for the day, and top the activity off by supplying an assortment of snacks or cooking a meal together. Options can match the Thanksgiving theme, with films like A Beautiful Day in the Neighborhood (2019), Toy Story (1995), Addams Family Values (1993) and A Charlie Brown Thanksgiving (1973), and Christmas films can be added to the mix, including Elf (2003), It's A Wonderful Life (1947) and Steel Magnolias (1989).

Go on a hike — this one is weather-dependent, so check the conditions outside first. Whether at a local park or a trail an hour or so away, hiking is the perfect daytime activity for a friend group of any size.

Bake or decorate treats. This is another easy indoor activity: make a pie from scratch or simply frost ready-bake sugar cookies, which gives the group something interactive to do together. Treats can be chosen on theme with the fall and winter seasons, from baking an apple crisp to decorating gingerbread cookies.

Run a Turkey Trot. Whether before, during, or after Thanksgiving Day, many cities in America host an annual Turkey Trot run, and the length varies — from a 5K to a half marathon — so the group can pick what it prefers. In New York City in 2023, one Turkey Trot was held at Flushing Meadows-Corona Park on 25 November, with participants running for the Rolling Thunder organisation, a non-profit "for mainstream athletes who are developmentally disabled or have other special needs".

Host an at-home wine tasting. A night out at the bars can get expensive depending on the venue, and a trip to a winery can rack up a hefty bill and is not the most enjoyable activity in cold weather — which is the reason to host your own instead. Everyone brings a different type of wine, from red to white, and the group tries a few sips from each bottle; a big wine fan may end up discovering a new favourite brand. A homemade charcuterie board, filled with different meats and cheeses, can be prepared to pair with the wine.

Play an outdoor game. If the friends have a fun, competitive side, the group can host the event in any big outdoor space — a local park or a friend's backyard. Football in particular meets the typical theme of Thanksgiving, as a beloved tradition on the actual holiday is to watch a sports game with the family.

Hold an early gift exchange. The winter holidays are still weeks away, but Friendsgiving can be used to host the annual exchange: many friend groups in the US opt for the budget-friendly Secret Santa format, where each person is randomly assigned someone to give a gift to and, during the exchange, reveals that they are that person's "secret Santa" before handing over the present.
