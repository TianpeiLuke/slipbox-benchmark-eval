# ASX REITs And Company News, 24 October 2023

Real estate investment trusts (down 2 per cent) dropped further following the latest inflation data, with Mirvac (down 3.7 per cent) and Dexus (down 3.3 per cent) sliding lower, as the Australian sharemarket shook off a positive lead from Wall Street after hotter-than-expected inflation data raised the prospect of another interest rate rise at the Reserve Bank's next meeting.

Interest-rate sensitive sectors, including REITs and consumer companies, were among the weakest on the local bourse as investors digested the news. Dexus chief executive Darren Steinberg announced on Wednesday that he would step down next year after 11 years at the helm, and at the annual meeting in Sydney the same day the company's remuneration report received a first strike after failing to pass the 25 per cent voting threshold. Consumer staples (down 1.4 per cent) were also weaker, with Woolworths losing 2 per cent despite sales across the company rising 5.3 per cent in the 14 weeks to October, as it vowed to pass on easing costs for protein and fresh produce amid rising mortgage and rental pressures. In other company news, shares in embattled funds manager Magellan dropped 3.5 per cent after the abrupt departure of its chief executive, David George. Infratil (down 3.6 per cent), Meridian Energy (down 2.7 per cent) and Resmed (down 2 per cent) were among the biggest large-cap decliners.
