# Diablo 4 Season 2 Nightmare Dungeon and Travel Changes

Nightmare Dungeons remain places to farm for better items and push builds to their limit in Diablo 4 season 2, but they are becoming much simpler and less grindy: the design team has reworked several Nightmare Dungeon affixes to make them less obnoxious, and Nightmare Sigils now teleport players directly into the dungeon instead of to the door. Polygon reported this on 2023-10-04.

Teleporting to Nightmare Dungeons is one thing, but players still need to ride their horses around the world to reach events like Helltides and the new seasonal activity, Blood Harvest.

Mounts have therefore also been improved in season 2: horses are now faster, and their dashes allow them to break through barricades in the roads. Game director Joe Shely told Polygon the mounts should be more intelligent as well, and will not get stuck on objects in the world as easily as before.
