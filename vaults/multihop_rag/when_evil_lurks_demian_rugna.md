# When Evil Lurks (Demián Rugna)

When Evil Lurks is Demián Rugna's gory possession movie starring Ezequiel Rodríguez, Demián Salomón, and Luis Ziembrowski, ranked 33 on Polygon's best movies of 2023 list of 2023-10-07 and available on Shudder, AMC Plus, or for digital rental/purchase.

Polygon argues the film is not just the usual barrage of religious imagery and religious anxiety: it is much more concerned with bigger, sadder thoughts on the ways institutions keep failing people, and the ways people fail each other and themselves.

Ezequiel Rodríguez gives a mesmerizing performance as Argentinian farmer Pablo, a man estranged from his wife and in denial about his own responsibilities; when a demonic presence starts stalking his rural county, he runs to his ex-wife and the city and brings the curse along with him. Polygon calls it a shocking film that crosses lines in unusual ways for horror, particularly around horrors visited on children and animals, but says what really makes it land is how well realized all the human drama is, more so than the demon drama.
