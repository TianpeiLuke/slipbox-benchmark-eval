# Criticism Of The Focus On Far-Off AI Risk, And The FTC Probe

Some computer scientists have criticized the focus on far-off risks — the existential dangers Altman called attention to — as distracting from the real-world limitations and harms of current AI products. The premise attacked is that future catastrophic risk is the most urgent thing to attend to.

The concrete present-day case is already before a regulator: the U.S. Federal Trade Commission has launched an investigation into whether OpenAI violated consumer protection laws by scraping public data and publishing false information through its chatbot.
