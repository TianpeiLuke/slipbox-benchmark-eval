# Russell Wilson's Performance Against The Patriots

Russell Wilson finished the Christmas Eve 2023 game against New England 25-of-37 for 238 yards with two touchdowns, in a Broncos performance whose 16 fourth-quarter points came a hit too late.

It took the Broncos a while once more to put points on the board, with 16 of them arriving in the fourth quarter in a comeback effort that fell short. Wilson had seemed to be experiencing a bit of a resurgence in his first year playing under head coach Sean Payton, but there had continued to be some ups and downs. He faced a Patriots defense that was among the better ones in the league — hardly like its offense had been for the better part of the year, ranking 28th in the league heading into the game — though the Patriots had one of their better showings on that side of the ball, totaling 289 yards and 23 points.
