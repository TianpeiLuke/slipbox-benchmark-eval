# Denise George: Profile And Epstein Case Role

Denise George was the driving force behind three high-profile legal battles involving Jeffrey Epstein — the Svengali who courted a wide circle of rich and powerful men, from Bill Gates to Prince Andrew, even after he was branded a sex offender. Those landmark cases reverberated like a thunderclap through the power corridors of business and politics.

George, 64, told her story on a rain-drenched Tuesday in the Caribbean, sitting inside a poolside restaurant at a flamingo-pink hotel overlooking the rooftops of St. Thomas, mid-summer in the tropics and low season, the place empty. On the silver chain round her neck hangs a gift from her husband: a pendant in the shape of the scales of justice. Even four years after Epstein died by suicide in a Manhattan jail cell, it is hard to shake the feeling that a code of silence, a sort of omerta, hangs over his affairs there.
