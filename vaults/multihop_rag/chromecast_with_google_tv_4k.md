# Chromecast With Google TV (4K)

Polygon recommends the latest Chromecast as a compact and easy-to-use streaming device that will not cost much, providing 4K HDR playback and a voice-enabled remote.

The problem it solves is that smart TVs tend to come with a range of downloadable apps built in, but performance is often middling at best — even in high-end models. The voice remote is singled out because it helps you get to your favorite movies and shows faster than fiddling with on-screen keyboards.
