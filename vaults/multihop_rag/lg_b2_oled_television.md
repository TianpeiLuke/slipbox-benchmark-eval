# LG B2 OLED Television

The LG B2 OLED TV was down to $847 for a 55-inch model — a new low, and roughly $150 off its average street price in recent months.

The B2 is the step-down model from the C2, which was also on sale at $1,097 for a 55-inch set: it is not as bright or color-rich with HDR content, runs on a slower processor, and has two fewer HDMI 2.1 ports. That said, its contrast, colors and motion performance will still be a significant upgrade if you are coming from a more basic LED TV.

LG's new B3 TV promises a cleaner webOS interface but is priced a good way higher as of this writing. If you want an OLED TV for less than $1,000, the B2 should be a better value.
