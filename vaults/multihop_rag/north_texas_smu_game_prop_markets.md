# North Texas-SMU Game Prop Markets

Player props for North Texas vs. SMU had not been made available at top sportsbooks as of Nov. 7, 2023, but BetRivers was offering several game props on the matchup.

Among them were a pair of team combo specials pairing the first touchdown with the game's outcome: SMU to score first and win was priced Yes -165, No +123; North Texas to score first and win was Yes +950, No -5000.
