# Alliance DAO All11 Mentor Roster

Mentors for Alliance DAO's All11 cohort, as reported on 2023-10-18, include Kain Warwick, founder of Synthetix; Jason Yanowitz, co-founder of Blockworks; Anatoly Yakovenko, co-founder of Solana; Ilja Moisejevs, co-founder and CEO of Tensor, who came through Alliance's own ALL9 cohort; and David Vorick, co-founder of Sia and lead developer of Glow.

The roster mixes protocol founders, media operators and an alumnus of an earlier Alliance batch, indicating the program recycles its own graduates back in as advisers.
