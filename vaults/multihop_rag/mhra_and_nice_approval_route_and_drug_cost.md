# MHRA and NICE Approval Route and Drug Cost

Both drugs are to be considered for approval in the UK next year in a two-stage sequence: the Medicines & Healthcare products Regulatory Agency (MHRA) will first decide if they are safe and effective, and then the National Institute for Health and Care Excellence (NICE) will rule on whether they offer value for money.

Cost and delivery are the practical constraints on that route. Both medicines are expensive — lecanemab costs about $25,000 (£19,700) a year — and are given by regular intravenous infusions. "That is a challenge from a health-service point of view, because you need to find the space and time to put someone in an infusion suite to treat them," added David Thomas.
