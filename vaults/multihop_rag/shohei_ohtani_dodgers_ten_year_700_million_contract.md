# Shohei Ohtani's 10-Year, $700 Million Contract With The Dodgers

Shohei Ohtani announced on Saturday, Dec. 9, 2023 that he had agreed to a 10-year, $700 million contract with the Los Angeles Dodgers, a deal ESPN's Jeff Passan reported shattered the MLB record for both total value and average annual value.

Ohtani made the announcement on Instagram, spurning the Blue Jays — to whom rumors had connected him the previous day — and remaining in the city where he became a sensation with the Angels. The contract carried "significant deferrals," so while Los Angeles would not pay him $70 million per year, the franchise would be paying him beyond those 10 seasons. The Dodgers formally announced the signing on Monday, Dec. 11, calling Ohtani a "once-in-a-generation talent and one of the most exciting professional athletes in the world," and the club's own post said it had signed the two-time American League Most Valuable Player to a 10-year contract. In a statement Ohtani said, "Dodger fans, thank you for welcoming me to your team. I can say 100 percent that you, the Dodger organization and I share the same goal - to bring World Series parades to the streets of Los Angeles." The signing paired the two-time MVP with two former MVPs already in the Dodgers lineup, Mookie Betts and Freddie Freeman, both of whom were NL MVP finalists in 2023; while Ohtani would not pitch again until 2025, his power and on-base ability gave Los Angeles the most dangerous trio of hitters in the sport.
