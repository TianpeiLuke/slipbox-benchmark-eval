# Spelunky 2

Spelunky 2 is not for the faint of heart, Polygon warns in its 2023-10-20 list of the 35 best couch co-op games for Nintendo Switch, where it is called one of the best platformer roguelikes.

Players work with a friend to excavate cave levels, avoid the dangers within, and ration supplies.

In local co-op, if one of you dies the other can bring them back to life by finding a coffin. Ragdoll physics — not to mention turkey riding — add challenge and humor.
