# Week 16 Wide Receivers Ruled Out

CBSSports.com's Dec. 19, 2023 Week 16 wide receiver preview listed two receivers who were not being projected to play Week 16 at that time, with a note on what each absence meant for fantasy managers.

Nico Collins, WR, Houston, No. 12, age 24 and in his third year: the Texans offense looked like one to avoid as long as C.J. Stroud was out. Ja'Marr Chase, WR, Cincinnati, No. 1, age 23 and in his third year: Tee Higgins was a No. 2 receiver and Tyler Boyd a fine flex in his absence.
