# Notorious (1946)

Alfred Hitchcock's Rebecca is the defining noir classic in his filmography, but Polygon's Tasha Robinson argues that fans who have not dug past his topline roundup of classics should not miss Notorious, a thriller-romance featuring some of his most touching character work. It streams free with ads on Tubi, IndieFlix, and FlixFling.

The plot: Alicia Huberman, played by Ingrid Bergman and the daughter of a German war criminal, is asked to use that connection to seduce and spy on a prominent Nazi figure, played by Claude Rains, for the American government. In the thinking of the time, Robinson notes, this involves ruining herself for good men like her handler and love interest, played by Cary Grant, by having sex with another man. In modern eyes she reads it as the stuff anime romance dramas and fanfic are made of: deferring her own desires for her country, Alicia willingly enters a love triangle and invites plenty of tsundere, leaving open whether duty wins over love, whether she gets any good spying done, and whether Cary Grant is the biggest self-righteous stuffed shirt imaginable in the movie.
