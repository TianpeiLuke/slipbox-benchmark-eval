# The First Half Of Jamaica 0-1 Canada

Canada led 1-0 at halftime in Kingston through Jonathan David's goal in the second minute of added time, and were good value for it having taken 11 of the 16 attempts at goal in the half and four of the five on target — though it was not all plain sailing, especially when Leon Bailey struck a post. Conditions were glorious after the previous night's washout, with sunshine and a picturesque, tree-filled backdrop, Jamaica in their familiar yellow and green home strip and Canada in dark blue.

Jamaica made the better early chances and did not take them. At 2 minutes Bailey cantered along the left flank and sent in a dangerous low cross that Canada cleared, with Bailey adjudged offside. At 4 minutes he was through on goal from a long ball but produced a tame, rushed finish that dropped comfortably wide of Milan Borjan's goal with the keeper well off his line, drawing frustration from manager Heimir Hallgrimsson on the sideline while Bailey looked sheepish. At 15 minutes an aerial ball down the flank bounced kindly for the Aston Villa attacker inside the left of the penalty area, deceiving Steven Vitoria, but he dragged his first-time shot wide of the far post, with Hallgrimsson applauding as Bailey's runs continued to cause Canada problems. Canada's best chance came at 9 minutes when David broke free inside the Jamaica area on the right, sending Greg Leigh off balance, but could only poke a low shot gently at Andre Blake. Jamaica loaded the box from a free kick beyond halfway at 12 minutes and Bailey could only nod it on for Borjan to scoop up.

Jamaica then lost Michail Antonio. At 18 minutes the West Ham United forward was down for an extended period and asked to be substituted, rising to hobble down the tunnel with medical staff in what was speculatively a hamstring issue; Trivante Stewart replaced him, and by 21 minutes Ismael Kone had sent a fierce shot from outside the area wide for Canada. Stewart turned and tried his luck from distance at 24 minutes without success, the sides yet to find their range. At the 27-minute drinks break Hallgrimsson wore a back-to-front white baseball cap while instructing Stewart among others, and a man in a marvellous yellow wig and black-and-yellow tracksuit was visible in the somewhat sparsely populated stands.

Canada had the better of the action after the break in play, though the pace was ponderous at times. Alphonso Davies' free kick from the right channel at 33 minutes bobbled through almost everyone before Jamaica cleared; at 36 minutes his corner failed to beat the first defender, but Canada found him again on the same flank and his cross reached David at the far post, whose looping header back across goal was claimed with reasonable ease by the backtracking Blake. At 39 minutes Jamaica could not have come closer: Bobby De Cordova-Reid's corner to the far post found Bailey, who beat his man with his run and sent a low first-time strike against the near post and away. At 41 minutes Borjan received extended treatment while clutching his left calf, and Shamar Nicholson, the apparent culprit, exchanged a hug with him but was still booked. De Cordova-Reid zipped a shot from long range into Borjan's arms at 44 minutes; at 45 the play was end-to-end, with Ethan Pinnock stopping David from getting a shot away and Blake punching Davies' cross clear from a crowded box, while at the other end Sam Adekugbe showed strength and speed to stop Stewart racing onto a long ball.

The goal came in stoppage time. Cyle Larin responded to a bouncing ball inside the box by turning smartly to befuddle his two markers and scythed a low delivery across the face of goal, and David gratefully tapped in at the far post. Sporting News noted it would be an excellent result for Canada's prospects of qualifying for Copa America 2024 with the home leg still to come.
