# Connor O'Sullivan AFL Draft Prospect 2023

Connor O'Sullivan, profiled in Kevin Sheehan's top 40 for the 2023 AFL Draft as reported by The Roar on Nov. 15, 2023, is an athletic tall defender who was outstanding for the title-winning Allies at the National Championships, winning their MVP award and All Australian honours. State: NSW/ACT. State League Club: Murray Bushrangers. Community Club: Albury. Date of Birth: 19/05/05. Height: 198cm.

He averaged 17.8 disposals at 86 percent efficiency as well as 8.2 marks, providing great rebound to lead the Allies defence. He has an exciting natural leap, good speed and elite endurance, highlighted by a top 10 placing in the 2km time trial at the national AFL Draft Combine with a time of 6:11. He also showed he can go forward and have an impact during his time with the Murray Bushrangers. Weapon: Rebound ability and athleticism. AFL player comparison: Sam Taylor (GWS Giants).
