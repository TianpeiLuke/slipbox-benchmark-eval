# French AI Startups: Poolside, Dust And Nabla

Mistral is not the only high-profile AI company in France, and the three named alongside it each occupy a distinct application niche. Poolside, a company that recently raised a $126 million seed round, plans to use AI to make it drastically easier to write code; co-founded by Jason Warner, the former CTO of GitHub, and Eiso Kant, a serial entrepreneur who worked on Athenian and source{d}, the company has picked Paris as its home base.

Dust is working on integrating large language models with internal corporate data from GitHub, Notion or Slack, and also took the stage for a demo at the November 8, 2023 meetup. Nabla is another French AI startup, focused on helping doctors leverage large language models, and co-founded by former FAIR engineers.

The clustering itself is the observation TechCrunch draws out: at Dust's office warming party its writer realized that Dust shares its beautiful office building with Nabla, which proves once again that an AI ecosystem is emerging.
