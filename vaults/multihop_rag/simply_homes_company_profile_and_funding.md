# Simply Homes: Company Profile And Funding

Simply Homes is a Portland, Maine-based startup that buys single-family homes in blighted neighborhoods, renovates them and then rents them out to very low-income families, the elderly and the disabled — that is, Section-8 voucher holders. In December 2023 it secured $22 million in funding to further those efforts.

Its structure separates operations from ownership: Simply Homes operates in an operating company/property company structure, with the operational company using its technology platform and operational teams to source, acquire, renovate and manage the properties, while the property management company holds them long term.

Its revenue model combines rent with fees. Simply Homes collects the rent on its properties, which helps cover the cost of managing them, and it takes a transaction fee plus a 3% fee on an ongoing basis to manage the portfolio.

Gutter Capital and Watchung Capital co-led the $22 million round, which included participation from Village Global, Ambush Capital, RavenOne Ventures, Neil Parikh, Gabe Flateman, Luke Sherwin and others.
