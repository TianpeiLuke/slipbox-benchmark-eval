# The iPad Pro Refresh Outlook

Gurman may have poured cold water on the idea, but Engadget's hunch was that at least one iPad would appear at Apple's event — and with the focus likely on M3-powered products, the chances are any new iPad would be of the Pro variety with one of those chips inside.

The case against a full refresh is timing. It has only been a year since the most recent iPad Pro debuted, and other than a few small tweaks Apple only really swapped in the M2 in place of the M1. The next iPad Pro will probably need more changes to make it palatable for anyone who picked up the previous two models, which means Apple might need more time to get it ready — in which case the company might be content to give the iPad Pro a spec bump for the time being.

Other iPads could also get a refresh at the event, just in time for the holidays. Engadget promised full coverage of everything from the Scary Fast event on Monday evening.
