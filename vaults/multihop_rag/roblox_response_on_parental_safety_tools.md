# Roblox's Response On Its Parental Safety Tools

Roblox rejected the premise of Sarah Michelle Gellar's complaint that it lacks parental help, telling The Independent that such tools already exist and that the company was investigating her case. "We are looking into this case and we reached out directly to the user to help. We are committed to ensuring that our users have a positive experience on the platform," a spokesperson said.

The substance of the rebuttal is the provision the company says is already in place: "In partnership with safety groups, we've developed tools and resources like our Parent & Caregiver Guide and other helpful resources for parents to have conversations with their kids and teens about staying safe, spending controls and protecting their account. Our support team works with parents and users directly 24/7 and can be contacted with questions here," the spokesperson added, including a link to the company's support page.
