# Ohtani's Camp Secrecy And The Reporting Blackout

Little news was expected to emerge from Shohei Ohtani's free agency because his camp had made publicity costly for suitors: ESPN's Jeff Passan reported that "If visits between Ohtani and a team are reported publicly, it will be held against the team."

Sporting News' conclusion on Nov. 27, 2023 was that the baseball world would probably not hear a ton, though that would not stop the speculation, nor stop the outlet from passing along what precious few nuggets were scattered about.
