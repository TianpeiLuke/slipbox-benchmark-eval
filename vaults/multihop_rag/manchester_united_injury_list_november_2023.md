# Manchester United's Injury List Before The Fulham Match

Manchester United were yet to recover from their large number of long-term injuries ahead of the November 2023 Fulham fixture, and had been fighting a battle to line up their best XI as players fell victim to one injury after another. Casemiro, who got injured during the international break, got back on the field against Newcastle United in the EFL Cup match, but was taken off at half-time with a new hamstring issue and would probably remain sidelined for weeks.

Other than Casemiro, Tyrell Malacia and Amad Diallo were suffering from knee issues, Lisandro Martinez was nursing a foot injury, Jadon Sancho was out due to disciplinary issues, and Luke Shaw was yet to recover from a muscle problem. Adding to that, Victor Lindelof was suffering from a bug that could see him miss the weekend's match.

On the other hand, Raphael Varane, who missed the previous match due to an illness of his own, could be back. Aaron Wan-Bissaka was also able to participate in the last match, coming on as a substitute, so he should be able to make a comeback. Marcus Rashford, Rasmus Hojlund and Bruno Fernandes could all see themselves in the starting lineup.
