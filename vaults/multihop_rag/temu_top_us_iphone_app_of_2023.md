# Temu As The Top US iPhone App Of 2023

Temu, the Chinese e-commerce company, is Apple's most downloaded free app in the U.S. for 2023. Apple released an App Store feature on 12 December 2023 showcasing the top iPhone apps of the year, free and paid, as measured by app installs — its proxy for overall popularity. In that ranking, apps from Chinese-based companies have taken over spots previously held by U.S. tech giants like Meta and Google, with CapCut and TikTok, both from Beijing-based ByteDance, coming in at No. 3 and No. 5 respectively.

TechCrunch suggests the negative attention TikTok received during the year may account for its slip from being the No. 1 app last year to No. 5 this year, but says U.S. consumers have not sworn off Chinese apps by the looks of things, given Temu's No. 1 position.

Engagement data supports the point: figures from Apptopia, reported by Bloomberg, noted that consumers were spending 18 minutes per day in Temu's app compared with 10 minutes in Amazon, with younger users spending even more, at 19 minutes per day on average. While Temu attracts users with in-app games, its real draw may be its bargain prices, which have more appeal during an economic downturn, and it is also a heavily marketed app, just as TikTok was in the months leading up to its No. 1 ranking last year.
