# Alameda Research As The Alleged Conduit For FTX Customer Funds

In the prosecution's account offered by Thane Rehn, the key to Sam Bankman-Fried's alleged scheme was Alameda Research, a crypto hedge fund that Bankman-Fried also owned. The structure the government described works as follows: Caroline Ellison — Bankman-Fried's on-again off-again girlfriend and the CEO of Alameda — served as a front for the fund, and through that arrangement Bankman-Fried had "secret access" to customer money, both cash and crypto.

Two further relations complete the alleged mechanism. Bankman-Fried is said to have directed employees to conceal the flow of money into FTX's coffers, and to have forged financial documents distributed to lenders and investors. "The defendant lied to the world," Rehn alleged. These are the government's allegations as presented in its opening statement, not established findings.
