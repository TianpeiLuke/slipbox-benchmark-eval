# Rory McIlroy Support For Golf Ball Rollback

Rory McIlroy backed the rollback proposal for elite-level golfers, arguing it would help the overall professional game, in comments to the No Laying Up Podcast reported by Essentially Sports on Dec. 3, 2023.

What makes the endorsement notable is that his own average of 326.7 yards puts him on top of the PGA Tour's driving distance leaderboard — so the five-time major winner is backing a change that costs him an advantage. His grounds are about the range of skills the game asks for: "I think it's going to help the overall professional game. I think making guys hit some long irons again and some mid irons and being able to hit every club in your bag in a round of golf." The scope is explicit: he backs it for elite-level golfers.
