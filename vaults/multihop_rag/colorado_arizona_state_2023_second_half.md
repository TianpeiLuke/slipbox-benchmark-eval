# Colorado's Second Half Against Arizona State, Oct. 7 2023

Colorado scored 13 unanswered points before Arizona State tied the game late, and then won it 27-24 on Alejandro Mata's 43-yard field goal with 12 seconds left in the Oct. 7, 2023 Week 6 meeting. Neither side scored in the third quarter, which ended Colorado 14, Arizona State 17.

The scoreless third quarter still produced two notable moments: at 8:52 p.m. Cam Skattebo was heating up under the sun, adding 24 rushing yards on an Arizona State possession, and at 9:13 p.m. Sun Devils defender Ed Woods was ejected for targeting, a penalty that also cost him the first half against Washington two weeks later. Colorado took the lead early in the fourth quarter: at 9:26 p.m. Shedeur Sanders found Javon Antonio for a nine-yard touchdown, Antonio's first of the year, making it Colorado 21, Arizona State 17 with 14 minutes to go.

Colorado then extended the margin. At 9:35 p.m. Xavier Weaver returned the ball 51 yards, stopping only after tripping himself up and leaving Colorado 25 yards from another touchdown, and at 9:38 p.m. Mata nailed a 42-yard field goal to push the lead to seven with ten minutes left. At 9:48 p.m. Carter Stoutmire blocked a 52-yard field goal attempt from Dario Longhetto to deny a Sun Devils score.

Arizona State refused to fold. At 10:11 p.m. Trenton Bourguet found Troy Omeire on a 15-yard pass to tie the game at 24-24 with only 50 seconds remaining. Colorado answered immediately: at 10:14 p.m. Sanders hit Antonio on a 43-yard pass to take over at the Arizona State 25, and at 10:17 p.m. Mata made a 43-yard field goal look easy to give Colorado the lead with 12 seconds on the clock — final score Colorado 27, Arizona State 24.
