# USMNT Attacking Ratings Against Germany, October 2023

Christian Pulisic was rated 9 out of 10, Tim Weah 8 and Folarin Balogun 7 for the United States' front three against Germany on Oct. 14, 2023 — though Germany carved the U.S. defense to pieces, the American attacking threat remained strong, and almost all the danger came from the wings.

Pulisic once again backed up his nickname of 'Captain America', and there is no doubting who remains the most electric and incisive U.S. player. The first-half stunner was emblematic of his performance, but beyond that he constantly proved a threat to Germany's out-of-position right-back Jonathan Tah. He completed 27 of 29 passes — a tidy haul for a player of his attacking intent — and created two chances in addition to scoring. His corners remain awful, and it's a wonder why he is still on duty for that role, but otherwise Pulisic was a menace.

Weah was equally devastating down the right while Pulisic starred on the left, dazzling with electric pace and good link-up. His biggest moment came when he scorched Robin Gosens on the wing in the first half, knocking the ball past the German defender and then beating him with blistering speed to get back on it; his statistical haul doesn't quite do him justice, but he was a threat throughout his 66 minutes.

Balogun's rating reflects the effort to feed him: U.S. fans are salivating for him to get more touches up front, and as Gregg Berhalter foreshadowed pre-match, the team made a clear attempt to do that, with Gio Reyna and Tim Weah doing most of the work in that regard.
