# BG3 Warlock: Pact Casting For Beginners

The Warlock is one of the twelve classes in Baldur's Gate 3, and Polygon names it the best magic class for beginners. Warlocks are casters who made a deal with a higher power for their magic — extreme knowledge-seekers willing to form a pact with otherworldly beings to gain ancient secrets that bolster their magical arsenal.

Because dealing with such powerful creatures demands strong mental power — you have to make a deal with the devil and survive — the Warlock's primary ability score is Charisma. They play a lot like Sorcerers but with a slight emphasis on more academic Skills and fewer spells, and they get Light Armor, so they are a little less squishy than Sorcerers. The defining trade is that Warlocks sacrifice the number of spells they know for sheer power: every Warlock spell slot is the same — the highest level available — and slots recharge on a short rest instead of a long rest. They are not as versatile as other caster classes, but what they do, they do well. Polygon's recommendation is that Warlock suits you if you want a dedicated caster without the prep work a class like Wizard requires: you still use a lot of magic, but you do not have to manage a binder full of spells every morning.
