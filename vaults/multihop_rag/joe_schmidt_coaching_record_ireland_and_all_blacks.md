# Joe Schmidt's Coaching Record With Ireland And The All Blacks

Joe Schmidt took Ireland to the top of World Rugby's rankings while his Irish sides bombed out in successive World Cup quarter-finals under his leadership, and he later helped transform an All Blacks team that reached the 2023 World Cup final, as reported by The Roar on 11 December 2023.

His Ireland tenure is credited with more than results: Schmidt played an instrumental role in not just seeing them become one of the world's best teams but helped usher through transformational change off the field, as Ireland moved to a centralised system.

The New Zealander briefly exited the international coaching scene following the 2019 World Cup, citing a desire to move home to New Zealand to be closer to his family. After initially moving back into the coaching fray with the Blues in Super Rugby, he joined the All Blacks ahead of the previous year's Rugby Championship, as New Zealand Rugby shook up the All Blacks coaching team following their historic series loss to Andy Farrell's Irish. His expertise proved telling: he and Jason Ryan transformed the All Blacks and helped Ian Foster's side reach the final of that year's World Cup after stunning wins over Ireland and Argentina in the knockout stages.
