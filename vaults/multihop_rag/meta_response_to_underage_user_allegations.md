# Meta's Response To The Underage-User Allegations

Meta's rebuttal attacks the evidentiary basis of the unredacted complaint rather than the legal standard. In statements, the company said the suit "mischaracterizes our work using selective quotes and cherry-picked documents."

On the substantive allegation about under-13 accounts, Meta asserted that it does act on them once identified: "we have measures in place to remove these [i.e. under-13] accounts when we identify them." The premise it disputes is therefore inaction, not the existence of underage users.

Its defense on why identification is imperfect is a claim about the state of the art rather than about its own effort: "However, verifying the age of people online is a complex industry challenge."
