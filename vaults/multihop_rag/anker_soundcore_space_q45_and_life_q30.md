# Anker Soundcore Space Q45 And Life Q30

The Anker Soundcore Space Q45 was on sale for $100, down from $150 — a $50 discount and an all-time low. While not as nice as most of the pricier models in the roundup, it is a fine value if you cannot spend more than $100: its adaptive ANC is better than average and can be set to a manual mode if needed, it supports multi-device pairing, and its design is comfortable to wear for hours at a time. Sound quality is where the price is most evident, being a little too uneven in the treble, but its bass-heavy profile should work for fans of hip-hop and EDM, and you can tweak the sound somewhat through the EQ tools in Anker's Soundcore app.

For as little as possible, the Anker Soundcore Life Q30 is also worth considering, down to $56 from $80 — a $24 saving, which is $24 less than usual.

The Life Q30's tradeoffs are clear against its sibling: it is not built as well as the Space Q45, nor does it sound as nice out of the box, massively inflating the bass and degrading when ANC is active, and call quality is worse too. That said, it is comfortable, its battery can last more than 40 hours, and the ANC itself is excellent for less than $60 — and you still get multi-device pairing and a customizable EQ.
