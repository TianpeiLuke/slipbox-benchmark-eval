# Kermit Cintron vs Paul Williams, 2004: The Ring Exit

Kermit Cintron's 2004 fight with Paul "The Punisher" Williams ended when Cintron dived head-first between the ropes in round four, crashed into a table and hit the floor, and could not continue, talkSPORT recalled on 15 November 2023.

The two got tangled up in the fourth, and while Williams fell to the canvas, Cintron went out of the ring entirely — pure momentum or oddly deliberate, the account asks. Either way, when the Puerto Rican could not continue, the bout went to the scorecards, Williams winning while Cintron was carted away on a stretcher in a neck brace.

Cintron had a pretty good career, crowned by a short stint as a welterweight world champion, but most boxing fans mainly recall him for just this one thing.
