# The Vegas Line As The Odds Benchmark

The Vegas line is the reference price the rest of the market is built on: Las Vegas sportsbooks set the standard for lines and odds across the country, and when a sportsbook copies a line it is usually copying the Vegas line. Most online betting sites do not stray far from it, using it as the foundation for setting all lines in every major sport.

Departures from that benchmark carry information. When those lines do shift, it is usually because of increased betting on one side of a bet — so shopping lines locally lets a bettor take advantage when sportsbooks stray from the Vegas line.
