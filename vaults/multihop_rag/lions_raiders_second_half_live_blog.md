# Lions-Raiders Second Half: A Goff Pick-Six, Then Gibbs Puts It Away

Las Vegas briefly closed to within two points on a Marcus Peters pick-six in the third quarter of the Oct. 30, 2023 "Monday Night Football" game, but Detroit pulled clear to win 26-14. Leading 16-7 at the break, the Lions began the half by shutting the Raiders down: Davante Adams unsuccessfully sought a pass interference call to make it 3rd & 9 (9:55 p.m.), and Julian Okwara sacked Garoppolo on third down to force a punt (9:56 p.m.). Jahmyr Gibbs broke tackles for a 24-yard run to reach 97 scrimmage yards (10:00 p.m.), but at 10:01 p.m. Marcus Peters intercepted a Goff pass that Amon-Ra St. Brown did not have his eye on and ran it back 74 yards for a touchdown, cutting the lead to 16-14.

Detroit answered but kept leaving points on the field. Two early first downs pushed the Lions past midfield with St. Brown at 108 yards (10:06 p.m.); a measurement confirmed a Craig Reynolds first down and Reynolds then caught a 12-yard pass (10:09 p.m.); Gibbs picked up another first down at 116 scrimmage yards to set up first-and-goal (10:11 p.m.); but at 10:13 p.m. Maxx Crosby ripped the ball out of Reynolds' arms for a fumble, upheld despite Lions fans arguing Reynolds was down (10:16 p.m.). Las Vegas could not capitalize — a low third-down throw was dropped by Adams and the Raiders punted (10:18 p.m.). An 18-yard pass interference call took Detroit well into Raiders territory (10:23 p.m.) and Gibbs then took off for a 27-yard touchdown at 10:25 p.m., reaching 149 scrimmage yards and a two-possession lead at 23-14. Michael Mayer's 19-yard catch — the rookie's first of the night — started the Raiders' next drive (10:28 p.m.), but Alim McNeill sacked Garoppolo for an eight-yard loss to end it, with Garoppolo at just 62 yards through nearly three quarters (10:31 p.m.). Gibbs turned a stuffed run into a first down with 126 rushing yards as the third quarter ended 23-14 (10:32 p.m.).

The fourth quarter was one-sided. Gibbs reached 179 scrimmage yards (10:36 p.m.), Goff was pushed forward on 4th & 1 to keep a drive alive (10:43 p.m.), a slightly-behind throw to Kalif Raymond brought on the kicker (10:45 p.m.), and Riley Patterson made a 52-yarder at 10:46 p.m. for a 26-14 lead. Garoppolo again missed a wide-open Adams downfield (10:51 p.m.), hit Jakobi Meyers for 19 (10:53 p.m.), then was sacked on back-to-back plays by Alex Anzalone and Alim McNeill to face 3rd & 28 (10:56 p.m.) and sacked again by Anzalone (10:58 p.m.). Craig Reynolds reached 66 yards near the end zone (11:00 p.m.), a holding penalty erased a Reynolds touchdown (11:01 p.m.), and Patterson missed a 26-yard attempt after making his first four of the night (11:04 p.m.). Garoppolo again failed to connect with Adams, who had beaten Cameron Sutton repeatedly, on what would have been a game-changing play (11:07 p.m.), and was sacked a sixth time to face 4th & 17 (11:08 p.m.). Detroit took the ball back to ice it (11:09 p.m.) and won 26-14 to move to 6-2 (11:15 p.m.).
