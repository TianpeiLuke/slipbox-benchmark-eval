# Growth In Gogoro's Battery-Swapping Service Revenue

Although Gogoro's overall revenue dropped significantly in the last quarter reported before December 2023, its battery-swapping service continued to grow, with revenue of $33.6 million, up 10.4% year-on-year.

TechCrunch reads this as the basis for the India strategy: as the company looks to collaborate with other vehicle manufacturers in India for its battery-swapping technology, it could be a mutually beneficial move for both Gogoro and automobile players, because Indian automotive players actively seek solutions to reduce charging time and offer efficient alternatives to ICE (internal combustion engine) vehicles. By providing its technology to vehicle manufacturers, Gogoro can tap into this need and increase its battery-swapping service revenues.
