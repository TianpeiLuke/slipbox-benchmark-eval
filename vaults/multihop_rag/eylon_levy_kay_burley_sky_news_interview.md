# Eylon Levy's Kay Burley Sky News Interview

Eylon Levy received his greatest exposure in an interview with UK Sky News presenter Kay Burley, who asked whether the fact that Israel is willing to release 150 Palestinian prisoners in exchange for 50 Israeli hostages indicates that Israel thinks Palestinian lives are worth less. Levy's spontaneous, shocked response saw his eyes open wide with surprise and his eyebrows shoot up, but within a second he regained his composure and explained that Israel would release one Palestinian for each Jewish hostage if it could: "We did not choose to release these prisoners who have blood on their hands. That's a disgusting accusation." Levy, who has degrees from both Oxford and Cambridge, had in recent weeks gained massive exposure worldwide, and the Israeli public embraced the eloquent and quick-witted spokesperson articulating Israel's point of view with his English accent.

The question aroused anger and criticism worldwide, but Burley did not apologise and justified her question on X, formerly Twitter, saying interviewers sometimes present one side of an argument so that those being interviewed can refute it, and that she was presenting a controversial opinion she had heard to allow the interviewee to respond. Levy does not accept this explanation, saying it is true that the role of the media is to challenge speakers with controversial opinions but it is also to filter out the bullshit and to know when you have been told nonsense that requires no further comment. He posted the clip on his Twitter account, where it received 16 million views and 15,000 shares from the Tweet alone, and his followers soared from 24,000 before the war to 140,000 after the Sky News interview, while his look of astonishment and raised eyebrows became a popular meme and WhatsApp sticker; he says that if told several weeks earlier that people would make memes of his face he would not have believed it, but that they are there to explain the story of Israel abroad and if along the way there are amusing moments that let people enjoy a funny moment amid the darkness, then let the joke be on him.
