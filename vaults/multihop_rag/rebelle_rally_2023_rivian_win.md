# Rebelle Rally 2023 Rivian Win

At Rebelle Rally 2023, a 2,120-kilometer off-road and navigation competition in its eighth year, one Rivian team took first place in the 4x4 class — the first time an all-electric vehicle was on the top podium. The first place finishers, driver Lillian Macaruso and navigator Alexandra Anderson, are both employees of Rivian.

The event's conditions are unusual: it has become a proving ground of sorts for the 65 all-women teams who participate as well as for stock manufacturer vehicles, and GPS and other electronic devices are strictly prohibited. There were 10 vehicles out of the 65 that fell into the electrified category, such as the Jeep Wrangler Rubicon 4xe, and four of those — all of them Rivian R1T pickups — were electric. TechCrunch's Kirsten reported the result on 2023-10-22, noting she went looking at EVs, tech and, believe it or not, green hydrogen at a seemingly non-tech event.
