# BG3 Druid: The Nature Caster

Polygon names Druid the best class for magic in Baldur's Gate 3. Druids are the game's nature-based casters: most of their magic is nature- or animal-themed, and they get Skills to match.

What Druids lack in armor proficiencies — they only get Light and Medium Armor — they make up for with the ability to shapeshift into a badger, wolf, spider, or cat. At level 3 a Druid picks from the Circle of the Land, Circle of the Moon, and Circle of Spores subclasses. Circle of the Land grants some extra magic and the ability to cast more spells throughout the day; Circle of the Moon adds a bear to the repertoire of animal shapes you can shift into; Circle of Spores adds extra fungus- and decay-based magic. Circle of the Moon is the best subclass for a Druid, with the reasoning and the skill and feat picks left to Polygon's separate Druid build guide.
