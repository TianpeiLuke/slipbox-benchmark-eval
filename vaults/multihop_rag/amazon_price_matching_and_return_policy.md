# Amazon Price Matching And Return Policy

Amazon does not offer price matching — full stop. You can contact customer support, but even if you are an Amazon Prime customer they are not likely to help you out.

The workaround Amazon's own rules allow is its return policy, which The Verge described on October 25th, 2023 as pretty liberal: you can often return an item you purchased from Amazon if it becomes available for less elsewhere, or at Amazon itself, and then simply repurchase the product wherever it is cheapest.
