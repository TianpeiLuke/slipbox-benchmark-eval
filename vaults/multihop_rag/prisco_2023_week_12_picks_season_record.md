# Prisco Crosses .500 Against The Spread Before Week 12 Of The 2023 Season

Writing on 2023-11-23, CBS Sports' Pete Prisco reported that he had made it over .500 for the season picking NFL games against the spread — "I did it," he wrote, and asked to celebrate. The climb had taken a few weeks: after going 8-6 the previous week, his season record against the spread stood at 80-78-6. Straight up, he went 10-4 the previous week, improving that mark to 99-65 for the season.

He also set out the week's conditions and his own targets. Week 12 had all 32 teams playing, which he called a tougher challenge than in recent weeks. Even so, he said he liked the card; it was Thanksgiving Week, he had a lot to be thankful for, and next week, he told readers, they would be thanking him. His stated goal for the week was an 11-5 record against the spread and a 12-4 record straight up.
