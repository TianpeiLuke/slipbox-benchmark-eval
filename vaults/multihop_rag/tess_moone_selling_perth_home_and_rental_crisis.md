# Tess Moone: Selling The Perth Home And The Rental Crisis

Tess Moone, a 31-year-old travel writer, sold her Perth home to go travelling and could not get back into the market: what was meant to be a three-to-four-week stay with her husband Dane's parents "has stretched out month after month", The Age reported on 25 November 2023.

She and Dane became home owners at 21 and bought a house 10 minutes from Perth's CBD, where she was working — "We thought we were doing all the right things" — but after the pandemic they began questioning their lifestyle choices, asking whether they really wanted to kill themselves to pay a million-dollar mortgage in the city when they were leaning towards a tree change in Margaret River. They sold in February and spent six months travelling around Europe and Japan, planning to stay at Dane's parents' house while looking for a rental in Margaret River. They did not realise how bad the rental crisis in the region is: they have been offering more than the asking price and/or more rent upfront, but because there is a preference to rent to families or those who have rented before, they keep getting knocked back. They are living in one of the spare rooms with their boxes stored in another.

There have been upsides: they spend a lot of quality time with her parents-in-law, who are lovely about giving them space, often booking weekends away so the couple get the house to themselves for a couple of days, and they have worked out a system for sharing the load, with the parents cooking dinner half the week and the couple the other half. The most challenging aspects are that her in-laws are retired and home a lot, and that she works from home while Dane works in an office, so she gets lots of invitations from her mother-in-law to join her and her friends for lunch or coffee when she is supposed to be working — "probably not the worst thing to complain about". Despite their love for his parents, their anxiety has increased over time, so much so that they have agreed to move into a friend's holiday home sight unseen, even though they will have to move back in with Dane's parents when the friend wants to use it, such as at Christmas: "It isn't ideal, but it's one step back towards independence. If we'd known then what we know now, we'd never have sold our home."
