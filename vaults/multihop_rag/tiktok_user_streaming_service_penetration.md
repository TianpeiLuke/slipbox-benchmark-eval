# TikTok Users' Streaming Service Penetration

Including both paid and ad-supported streaming services, roughly nine out of 10 TikTok users in each market Luminate studied used at least one music streaming service.

In the US, 89% of TikTok users subscribed to at least one music streaming service, compared to 74% of overall consumers; in the UK, 85% of TikTok users had a streaming service account, compared to 62% of consumers overall. The study also found a correlation with engagement: "In the global and US markets, TikTok user engagement metrics are strongly associated with streaming volumes. In other words, higher TikTok engagement — whether that's likes, views or shares — corresponds with elevated streaming volumes," the Luminate report stated.
