# Week 14's Top Fantasy Tight End Starts

Travis Kelce topped Sporting News' Week 14 2023 tight end rankings, with T.J. Hockenson, Sam LaPorta, David Njoku and Dalton Kincaid not far behind.

Kelce (vs. Bills) held the top spot as usual. LaPorta (at Bears) and Njoku (vs. Jaguars) both had great matchups against defenses that entered the previous week in the top 10 in fantasy points per game allowed to tight ends, and the column noted we know what Kelce, Hockenson (at Raiders) and Kincaid (at Chiefs) do every week in PPR formats. George Kittle (vs. Seahawks) did not have a great matchup — especially remembering he had just three catches for 19 yards against Seattle in Week 12 — but remained a must-start given his high ceiling in any matchup.
