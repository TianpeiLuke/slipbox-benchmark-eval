# Road to AfroFuture Geographic Brand Expansion

'Road to AfroFuture' is the experiential popup activation AfroFuture launched in 2022 to catapult its brand into the Sub-Saharan market, and since that launch the brand has expanded its presence to the UK, South Africa, Côte d'Ivoire and France. Co-founder Abdul Karim Abdullah said in October 2023 that as the events gain momentum and popularity, the organisation will aim to explore additional locations and expand its reach to more diverse markets.

Asked about running events in South Africa and New York and whether the brand would enter other markets, Abdullah confirmed the intention to expand AfroFuture's brand and events to additional Francophone African nations and other markets, in order to attract more partnership opportunities and reach a wider, more diverse audience. He cited incredible success with the South Africa events, with over 1,000 attendees in both 2022 and 2023, and said that in New York the team has partnered with brands including Positive Vibes Only (PVO) and Days Like This (DLT) to create fresh and fun events for its audiences in the States.

The wider ambition he describes is to continue strengthening the brand's narrative within its primary pillars of food, art, fashion and music, while striving towards a united diaspora.
