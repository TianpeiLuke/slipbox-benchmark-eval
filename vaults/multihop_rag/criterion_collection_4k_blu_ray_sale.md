# Criterion Collection 4K Blu-ray Sale

Through December 22, 2023, The Criterion Collection offered discounts on selections from its catalog of 4K UHD Blu-Rays and box sets, including pre-orders.

Polygon notes that many Criterion titles feature bespoke box art, making them excellent additions to any collection of physical media, and rounded up highlights including Trainspotting, Time Bandits, The Godzilla Showa-Era Box Set, and Moonage Daydream, pointing readers to the Criterion deals page for the dozens of other titles on offer.
