# ASOS: Online Fashion Retailer Profile

ASOS is an online fast-fashion retailer, launched in 2000, whose distinguishing features are next-day delivery and an easy returns service — the reason a shopper with a friend's wedding three days away and nothing to wear turns to it rather than dragging themselves around high-street shops.

At launch the retailer was considered revolutionary: it was like a supersize department store at your fingertips, and became the first online retailer that UK customers could visit and find hundreds of dresses for £30 all in one place.

Its original scope was much narrower than its present one. ASOS originally stood for As Seen On Screen and initially traded with the tagline "Buy what you see on film and TV", selling exclusively imitations of clothing worn by celebrities. That business model quickly expanded away from the world of replica products: ASOS now offers 850 affordable to mid-range high-street brands and ships to 196 countries.
