# The Cranberry Wet-Harvesting Method

Wet harvesting works because of a physical property of the fruit: each cranberry is filled with four chambers of air, so the berries float easily — which let Marcus Urann replace hand-picking with flooding, as FOX News Lifestyle reported on 23 November 2023.

The relation runs in three steps. Previously the tiny berries were usually plucked from dried vines by migrant workers. Urann realised the bog could be floated, the berries agitated from their vines, and then easily rounded up while floating in the water.

The method is one of several innovations in cranberry farming that Urann pioneered.
