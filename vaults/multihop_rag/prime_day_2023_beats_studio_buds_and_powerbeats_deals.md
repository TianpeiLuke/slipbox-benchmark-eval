# Prime Day 2023 Beats Studio Buds And Powerbeats Deals

Two Beats sets appear in WIRED's Apple roundup: the Studio Buds, at the same price seen on Prime Day in July and the lowest the price has dipped, and the Powerbeats Pro.

WIRED associate editor Parker Hall says the Studio Buds fit better than most buds he has ever tried, and likes their extremely useful physical buttons on the outside for playing and pausing audio and controlling noise cancelation; they stay put even when you're breaking a sweat and sound as good as Apple's AirPods Pro. The Powerbeats Pro (8/10, WIRED Recommends) came out in 2019 but remain WIRED's top recommendation for earbuds with the most secure fit, making them excellent for the most intensive workouts, with an IPX4 water-resistance rating to protect against sweat, excellent sound quality, and an impressive nine hours of battery life plus an extra 18 hours in the case — though many features won't work with an Android phone, the core functions will.
