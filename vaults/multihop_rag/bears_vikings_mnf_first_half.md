# The First Half Of Bears 12, Vikings 10

The Bears and Vikings went to halftime tied 3-3 on Nov. 27, 2023 in what the live blog called a mess of a game, with a scoreless first quarter and two Dobbs interceptions. Chicago started with the ball on the road looking to build early momentum: Fields found D.J. Moore for a short gain and the first first down of the night, the Bears strung together short gains into Vikings territory, and Fields threw on the run to Roschon Johnson for a first down and then 13 yards to Khalil Herbert without an incompletion — before rookie Ivan Pace Jr. dropped him for a five-yard loss to force 3rd & long, and Cairo Santos missed a 48-yard field goal to end a great start with zero points.

Montez Sweat then did what Chicago acquired him to do, sacking Dobbs on third down for a quick three-and-out. Fields took off for 14 yards and hit Roschon Johnson for 22 to get back into Vikings territory, but multiple Vikings defenders wrapped him up for a 12-yard loss, making it 2nd & 22 and then 2nd & 27; Chicago made up 17 yards on two plays and lined up to go for it on 4th & 10, and Fields beat intense Vikings pressure to find a wide-open Cole Kmet for 24 yards. The first quarter ended 0-0, and the drive fizzled out with the Bears kicking on 4th & 3 for a 3-0 lead, having left plenty of points on the board.

The second quarter was a catalogue of mistakes on both sides. At 8:50 p.m. Jaylon Johnson intercepted a Dobbs throw that hung up, though a taunting penalty after the play hurt Chicago's field position; Fields then overthrew Darnell Mooney on third down, Mooney getting destroyed in a collision, and Minnesota got the ball back still trailing by only three. A roughing-the-passer call gave the Vikings breathing room and another penalty gifted them a first down, but at 9:04 p.m. Dobbs was picked off again — this one not his fault, as Jordan Addison saw it bounce off his hands and Jaquan Brisker took it — leaving Dobbs with two interceptions and just nine passing yards. Fields came under pressure and missed Moore, starting to miss receivers after a hot start; Addison's apparent 17-yard catch was cut to four yards on replay with his knee clearly down; Dobbs was nearly intercepted by Jaylon Johnson again; and Fields hit Moore for 22 before nearly being picked by D.J. Wonnum, with neither offense stringing together positive plays since Chicago's opener.

Minnesota still had a chance before the break. Fields had all day on third down and could not find anyone, leaving Chicago 0-for-6 on its last six third downs and punting after the two-minute warning. Brandon Powell then turned around and made a 28-yard grab while falling to the ground — the Vikings' biggest offensive play of the night — and another first down by K.J. Osborn pushed Minnesota into Bears territory, with a Kyler Gordon interference call on T.J. Hockenson leaving them 13 yards from the end zone with 38 seconds left. The Bears' defense then morphed back into an elite unit, forcing intentional grounding by Dobbs and pressuring him heavily to set up 3rd & 24, and at 9:32 p.m. Greg Joseph drilled a 34-yarder to tie it at three entering the half.
