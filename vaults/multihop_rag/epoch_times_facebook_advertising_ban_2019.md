# The Epoch Times' Facebook Advertising Ban, 2019

In 2019, following reporting by NBC News, Facebook found that The Epoch Times had "leveraged foreign actors posing as Americans to push political content" and banned it from future advertising, citing violations of its policies including trying to circumvent review systems.

By that year the outlet had gone mostly digital and was spending millions of dollars building a network of Facebook pages and groups and running aggressive pro-Trump ad campaigns. That move toward explicit support of Republicans ran against Li's teachings to stay away from U.S. politics, but it had been foreshadowed by Li's comments at a Falun Gong conference the year before.

The Epoch Times also employed a tactic more often associated with fake news content farms and scammers than with news organizations: it created a network of inspiring and cute-content pages, along with fake accounts, to inflate its reach.
