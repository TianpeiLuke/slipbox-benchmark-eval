# WIRED Black Friday 2023 iPhone And iPad Case Deals

WIRED's Apple Black Friday guide of 25 November 2023 covered case discounts from Otterbox, Apple, a Nomad and Peak Design collaboration, Totallee and Incipio, plus an iPad folio case and a wool desk mat.

Otterbox is described as the first name in super-protective phone cases, featuring in WIRED's Best Galaxy S23 Cases and Best iPhone 15 Cases guides among many others; several people on the WIRED Gear Team have owned and tested Otterbox cases and can speak to their reputation for durability, and Otterbox also makes one of WIRED's favourite Apple 3-in-1 chargers. The scope of that deal is site-wide: everything on Otterbox's site is 25 percent off. Apple's official MagSafe case has accurate cutouts for ports, clicky buttons and protection for the entire bottom edge of the phone, comes in a variety of colours, and select colours are also on sale for the iPhone 15 Pro Max at the same price. The Nomad and Peak Design collaboration brings the rugged design of Nomad's cases together with Peak Design's SlimLink MagSafe mount system, which WIRED calls a great combo giving more security when mounting an iPhone 14 Pro or iPhone 14 Pro Max.

Totallee's cases will not give you the most protection, but they are some of the thinnest on the market and will keep a handset safe from everyday scuffs without adding bulk; they are available for a variety of iPhone models. WIRED is also a fan of Incipio cases, which appear in its Best iPhone 15 Cases and Best Samsung Galaxy S23 Cases guides, and for Cyber Monday the company is offering up to 50 percent off a wide selection of cases and accessories. On tablets, WIRED named one folio the best iPad case it has ever tested: it is durable, with a lip around the edges of the screen that protects the expensive glass if dropped, solid but easy-to-use buttons, and a magnetised cover that stays securely shut and reverses into a stand with eight different angles; it is sold for the 2022 10.9-inch iPad but there are versions fitting lots of iPads, even several years old, most of which are also on sale. The roundup also includes a wool desk mat that WIRED called the best overall desk mat on the market: reviewer Brenda Stolyar found it easy to clean while testing it underneath her laptop, keyboard, notebook and coffee mug, it offers a lot of real estate and comes in a variety of colours, and a pricier cork-bottomed version exists if you think the felt-bottomed one will slip on a glass table.
