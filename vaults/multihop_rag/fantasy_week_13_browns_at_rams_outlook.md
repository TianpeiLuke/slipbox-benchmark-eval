# Week 13 Browns-Rams: A Soft Cleveland Run Defense And Two Banged-Up Rams Receivers Point To A Low-Scoring Game

CBS Sports argues that the Rams should lean on the run against a Browns defensive front that is no longer what it was, and that Los Angeles's two top receivers are compromised enough to expect a low-scoring game. The grounds on the run defense: in their past three games the Browns gave up 4.1 yards per rush to the Ravens with just 6.3% of runs going 10-plus yards, 7.8 yards per rush to the Steelers thanks to a long touchdown run by Jaylen Warren with 23.8% of runs going 10-plus yards, and 4.6 yards per rush to the Broncos with 18.5% going 10-plus yards. The article pre-empts the obvious rebuttal that Cleveland was simply on the field too long because the Browns offense has been inept — that is not the reason, because they struggled with the run in the first half against all three teams, allowing over 5.3 yards per carry against each. A caveat is retained: the Rams offensive line should not be expected to handle the Browns' front the way it handled the Cardinals' last week.

On the passing side, the article concedes it is silly to say the Rams passing game is struggling when Matthew Stafford just threw four touchdowns, but notes Cooper Kupp looked like he played hurt last week and Puka Nacua didn't look that much better. The warrant: if they are ailing, the Rams would be smart to use the run game early and often, and a low-scoring game should follow. The verdicts: STARTS — Kyren Williams, Amari Cooper, Puka Nacua, David Njoku and Jerome Ford; SITS — Cooper Kupp (flex at best), Kareem Hunt, Tutu Atwell, Tyler Higbee, Royce Freeman, both quarterbacks and both defenses as low-end options; SLEEPERS — Elijah Moore (PPR flex).
