# OpenAI Board's Merger Approach To Anthropic

During the leadership crisis, OpenAI's board of directors approached Dario Amodei, the co-founder and CEO of rival large-language model developer Anthropic, about a potential merger of the two companies, according to a report by The Information.

The approach was part of an effort by OpenAI to persuade Amodei to replace Altman as CEO. Amodei quickly turned down the CEO offer.
