# Taylor Swift Re-Records 1989 To Regain Control Of Her Masters

On Friday, Taylor Swift released a new version of 1989 — the biggest-selling album of her career and the record that definitively turned her into a pop star — re-recorded as the latest instalment of an ongoing campaign to regain control of her work after an investment company bought her master tapes in 2019. The BBC reported this on 13 October 2023, with the article itself noting a publication date of 27 October for the album's return.

This re-recording is described as the biggest and riskiest part of the project. Earlier remakes in the campaign were largely made with a live band, whereas 1989's pop landscapes are full of squelchy, processed synths and treated vocals, which makes them harder to reproduce faithfully. Alongside the re-recorded album, fans can hear five new tracks from the star's vault, expanding on the themes and relationships she explored on the original. The artwork for the re-recorded version has a beach vibe, in contrast to the Polaroid aesthetics of the original.
