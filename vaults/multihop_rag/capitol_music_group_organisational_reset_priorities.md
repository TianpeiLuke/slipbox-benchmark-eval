# Capitol Music Group's Organisational Reset Priorities

Michelle Jubelirer describes the reset she ran from day one as Chair of Capitol Music Group as an ordered set of priorities. First, signing a truly diverse array of new artists, with specific attention on building a strong presence in hip-hop and R&B; President of A&R Jeremy Vuernick has been crucial in this regard as well as overseeing the making of the records, and the precondition that made the work necessary was that the company had not been consistently competitive in these areas for a very long time, with Motown having been spun off on its own long before she took the reins, in early 2021, before returning to CMG in early 2023. Second, the company took a hard look at every aspect of the business, revamped its entire organisational structure, reset its value system and even replaced certain terminology that was outdated, all to meet the needs of the modern music business; President of Capitol Music Group Arjun Pulijal, whom Jubelirer calls her partner in all of this and a brilliant creative marketer equally adept at strategizing innovative paths for artists ranging from Ice Spice and Offset to Troye Sivan and Paul McCartney, was key to this process, and his expertise drove a new dynamic in how marketing, creative, international, digital and commerce now interact.

The operating rule that came out of the reset is that literally every plan at CMG is tailored for a specific artist or project rather than pulled from a well-worn playbook, with the focus on finding and creating opportunities that will have an impact no matter where an artist is in their development; the artist's vision still lies at the heart of every plan, but the company listens to an audience that is smarter and savvier than ever to determine whether the marketing direction is working and whether artists are properly engaged with their audience. The company strives to be ideas-driven, to have honest constructive conversations with artists and each other, to run the business efficiently, and to think constantly about where it finds real value for its artists. The remaining to-do item is prioritisation, which Jubelirer defines against the usual industry usage: companies often use the word to mean focusing on one group of artists and ignoring all the others, whereas for CMG it means defining what success can uniquely mean for each artist and then delivering it, identifying which artists will benefit from the close attention of a small group of people who bring passion and nuance to marketing and development and which others clearly need the full power of the organisation firing on all cylinders from the get-go.
