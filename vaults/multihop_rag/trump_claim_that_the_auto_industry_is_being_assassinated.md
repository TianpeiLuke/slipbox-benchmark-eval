# Trump's Claim That "The Auto Industry Is Being Assassinated"

In his Wednesday speech, Trump tried to play up the division between autoworkers and environmentalists, heaping blame on Biden and the Democrats for the uncertainty around EVs: "The auto industry is being assassinated," he said, according to Detroit News. "If you want to buy an electric car, that's absolutely fine. I'm all for it. But we should not be forcing consumers to buy electric vehicles they don't want to buy."

He also cast the union's strike as largely pointless and — in what The Verge called a major head-scratcher — said autoworkers would likely be out of work in a few years anyway, so why bother: "You're all on picket lines and everything. But it doesn't make a damn bit of difference what you get because, in two years, you're all going to be out of business." The Verge's assessment is that whether Trump's plan to drive a wedge between autoworkers and environmentalists will pay off isn't clear.
