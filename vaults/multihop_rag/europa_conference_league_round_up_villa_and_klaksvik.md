# Europa Conference League Round-Up: Villa And Klaksvik

The Guardian's Europa Conference League round-up of 26 October 2023 led on Aston Villa's statement 4-1 win at AZ Alkmaar, KI Klaksvik becoming the first Faroe Islands club to win in the group stages of a European competition, and Aberdeen throwing away a two-goal lead at home to PAOK Salonika.

Ollie Watkins continued his red-hot form as Villa cruised in the Netherlands, netting his 10th goal of the season and fourth in Europe. Leon Bailey and Youri Tielemans — the latter his first for Villa — put them in control at the AZ Stadium inside 25 minutes; Vangelis Pavlidis wasted the hosts' best chances, and Watkins and John McGinn added more goals after the break before Ibrahim Sadiq grabbed a consolation. It was the second time in two games Villa had scored four, after their 4-1 Premier League win over West Ham on the Sunday.

KI Klaksvik's 3-0 success against Olimpija Ljubljana was a stunning first for a Faroe Islands club in a European group stage, with Rene Joensen, Pall Klettskard and Jakup Andreasen the heroes at the 6,500-capacity Torsvollur national stadium. Aberdeen's hopes of navigating a route through the group stage suffered a major blow on a rollercoaster night at Pittodrie: having weathered heavy pressure in a goalless first half, Barry Robson's men were two goals to the good by the hour mark through Bojan Miovski and Dante Polvara, but a stunning comeback saw the Greek side take three points through substitutes Kiril Despodov and captain Vieirinha, plus a stoppage-time penalty from Stefan Schwab awarded after a VAR check.
