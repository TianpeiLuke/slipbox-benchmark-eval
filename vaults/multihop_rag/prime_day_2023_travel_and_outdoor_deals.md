# Prime Day 2023 Travel And Outdoor Deals

Three travel and outdoor picks appear in WIRED's roundup: the Travelpro Maxlite suitcase, Hydro Flask insulated water bottles, and a Wing electric bike.

Matt Jancer, who has wheeled, lifted, and slung around a lot of budget bags over dozens of trips across the globe, recommends the Maxlite as the best budget suitcase: lightweight at 5.4 pounds, reasonably well made compared with the competition, and able to stand up to aircraft cargo bellies with nothing worse than scuff marks; for a carry-on he prefers the two-wheel rollaboard, which offers more interior space than a four-wheel spinner. Hydro Flask routinely makes some of WIRED's favorite insulated water bottles: most insulated travel mugs these days keep ice water cold and hot coffee warm, but what makes Hydro Flask stand out is the durability of the powder coating — Jancer has knocked several around for years in gyms, at the base of outdoor rock climbing walls, and rolling around his old car's floorboards without managing to scratch or dent one. The electric bike has good looks, strong acceleration, and a better price point than the competition, especially during Wing's major sale, with a built-in headlight and taillight, comfortable hand grips, and a nicer seat than you'd expect — checking a lot of boxes at its higher price point.
