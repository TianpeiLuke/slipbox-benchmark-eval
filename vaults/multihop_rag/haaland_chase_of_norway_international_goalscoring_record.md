# Haaland's Chase Of Norway's International Goalscoring Record

Erling Haaland was just six goals away from Norway's international goalscoring record, making it surely inevitable that he will soon be his nation's most prolific striker ever, as reported by The New York Times on 17 November 2023.

The report notes the two ways of reading the timing: on one hand it will have happened remarkably quickly, since Haaland only scored his first goals for Norway in 2020; on the other, this has been an incredibly long time coming. At this point Norway appear to have the most prolific striker in Europe, and it's not unreasonable to consider where Haaland might end up in the all-time international goalscoring charts worldwide, never mind just among his compatriots.

His rate is the striking figure, with a caveat attached. Haaland is averaging nearly a goal a game for his country, which will inevitably be difficult for the 23-year-old to sustain over his career, but is impressive even at this early stage: excluding countries who have never qualified for a World Cup, only Jorgen Juve, Gigi Riva, Ferenc Puskas and Poul Nielsen, plus Japan's Kunishige Kamamoto, hold their nation's international goalscoring records while also boasting a rate of 0.75 goals per game or more. Even Cristiano Ronaldo, the most prolific international goalscorer of all time with 127 for Portugal, boasts "only" 0.63 goals per game, a lower rate than Romelu Lukaku, Harry Kane and Aleksandar Mitrovic, which owes to his early days as a winger rather than a central striker. Considering how many hat-tricks Haaland scores for Manchester City, it's not beyond the realms of possibility that he could score six goals during this international break to move level with Juve, particularly given Norway's first fixture is a home friendly against the Faroe Islands that Thursday before a European Championship qualifier against Scotland in Glasgow on Sunday — though the Faroes' defence is less leaky than you might expect, having conceded more than three times in a game only twice in their last 22 outings.
