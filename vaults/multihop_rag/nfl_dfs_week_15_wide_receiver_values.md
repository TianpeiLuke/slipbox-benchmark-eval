# NFL DFS Week 15 Wide Receiver Values

Sporting News' headline Week 15 2023 wide receiver DFS plays were Rashee Rice of the Chiefs at New England (DK $6,100, FD $6,500, SD 1.35x), Romeo Doubs of the Packers against Tampa Bay (DK $5,300, FD $6,400, SD 1.45x) and Demarcus Robinson of the Rams against Washington (DK $3,600, FD $5,600, SD 1.7x).

With Isiah Pacheco (shoulder) banged up, Rice seemed like Kansas City's best skill-position player this side of Travis Kelce; he was certainly the most sure-handed wideout on the Chiefs and had electric after-the-catch playmaking ability, useful given that Patrick Mahomes had struggled with deep-ball accuracy, and the rookie had quietly posted 57.3 PPR points over the previous three weeks. Since Bill Belichick's defense typically makes a point of neutralizing the opposition's top threat — obviously Kelce that weekend — the column looked for Rice to bust chunk-yard plays through the screen game and dump-downs. Doubs had been one of the more dependable offensive weapons in Green Bay's offense and Tampa Bay's secondary had been utterly abysmal all season; Doubs had a good floor, logging 11-plus DraftKings points eight times, and a decent ceiling with at least 18.3 three times, in what should be a pass-heavy matchup at Lambeau. Robinson had become one of Matthew Stafford's most trusted receivers, drawing a 21.8-percent target share over the past two weeks and collecting seven catches for 101 yards and two touchdowns in that span, against a Commanders team terrible at defending the pass — even worse than earlier in the season after shipping out multiple impact pass-rushers — having surrendered the most FanDuel points and second-most DraftKings points to wide receivers that season. More solid Week 15 values listed were Amari Cooper, Browns vs. Bears (DK $6,000, FD $7,200, SD 1.35x); Garrett Wilson, Jets at Dolphins (DK $5,800, FD $6,900, SD 1.4x); Brandin Cooks, Cowboys at Bills (DK $5,200, FD $6,300, SD 1.45x); and A.T. Perry, Saints vs. Giants (DK $3,500, FD $4,700, SD 1.7x).
