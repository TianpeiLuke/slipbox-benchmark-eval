# Welcome To New York As A Song About Creative And Personal Freedom

Welcome To New York is, in the BBC's reading, the sound of creative and personal freedom — "It's a new soundtrack, I could dance to this beat forever more." Taylor Swift moved to the city in 2014 and was amazed to discover she could go grocery shopping largely incognito, and that shift registered physically: "My friends and people who know me have commented that I'm physically different since being here," she told NYC radio station WKTU.

The song's defining feature, in Swift's own framing, is its orientation towards possibility rather than any particular relationship: "That song is about the endless hopefulness and endless possibilities that New York seems to present," she said in an interview with Access Hollywood. A distinguishing fact about the track, setting it apart from the rest of the album, is that all proceeds from it are donated to New York City's Public Schools.
