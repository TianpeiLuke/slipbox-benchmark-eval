# The Nobel Prize Nomination And Selection Procedure

The Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences, which awards the physics, chemistry and economics prizes, follows an ordered procedure. First it asks for nominations a year in advance from thousands of university professors and other scholars around the world. Then a committee for each prize discusses candidates in a series of meetings. Finally that committee presents one or more proposals to the full academy for a vote.

The confidentiality rule bounds the whole process: the deliberations, including the names of nominees other than the winners, are kept confidential for 50 years.
