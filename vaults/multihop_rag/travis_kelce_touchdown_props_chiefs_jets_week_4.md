# Which Travis Kelce Props To Play In Chiefs-Jets Week 4

Sporting News worked through FanDuel's Kelce-specific "Prop Stars" markets on 2023-09-28, recommending two of five. "Top of the Charts: Travis Kelce to have the Most Receiving Yards in the Game" at +140 — pass. The reasoning: the Jets know Kelce is trouble long before he even walks in and must slow down No. 87 to have a chance; New York's defense has been very good, though not quite great, over the last year and continues to improve, with Robert Saleh's troops holding opponents in check and giving their anemic offense a fighting chance, but the one thing Gang Green struggles to defend is the tight end — they were giving up the fifth-most receiving yards to the position that season at 56.3 and had just allowed Pharoah Brown a long 58-yard touchdown. Even so, +140 is a nice price for the best pass-catcher in the game, but there's too much blowout potential to warrant a flier. "Opening Act: Travis Kelce First TD Scorer" at +400 — bet on it, and the writers' favorite of all the specials: the Chiefs will likely score first whether they get the opening kickoff or not, while the Jets have an offensive coordinator who somehow underrated Micah Parsons and a team that refuses to take the training wheels off their former gunslinging quarterback, with Wilson turned into "Checkdown Charlie" by conservative coaching and no change expected early. "FriENDZONE: Travis Kelce to NOT score a TD" at +120 — pass, obviously, given the previous recommendation.

"Seemingly Ranch: Travis Kelce Last TD Scorer" at +550 — pass: ranch is a fine condiment beloved by many, which makes this wager something that should be seen as an insult to ranch fans everywhere; unless the Jets make it a close game or send it to overtime, it is hard to believe Kelce will even be on the field to score the last touchdown. "Platinum Performance: Travis Kelce to Score 3+ TDs" at +2500 — bet on it, with a small wager: when it comes to Kelce's greatest hits he prefers to save them for division rivals like the Raiders and Chargers, and he could be schmoozing in the suite when the second half begins rather than throwing blocks and catching footballs; but if the Jets keep it close they are prone to being exploited by players like Kelce, and with receivers lacking on the Chiefs sideline the tight end might put on a show typically only found in show business — having hosted "Saturday Night Live" back in March, Kelce is familiar with how to tell a story. Since the writers already have him scoring once, what's another two times to cash a monster long shot; don't count on it, but don't rule it out either, and bet responsibly.
