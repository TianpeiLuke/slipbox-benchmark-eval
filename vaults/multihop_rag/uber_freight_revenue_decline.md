# Uber Freight's Revenue Decline

While food delivery and scooting humans about town were profitable for Uber in the third quarter of 2023, the final major portion of its business had a more lackluster quarter: freight continues to drag on Uber.

While Uber's ride-hailing and delivery businesses saw an uptick in gross bookings, Uber Freight experienced a 27% drop year over year, and revenue had a similar fall. The business unit reported revenue of $1.3 billion in the third quarter, a 27% drop from the same period the prior year; on a quarter-over-quarter basis, Uber Freight had a 1% gain in revenue. The results do not get better on net income: on an adjusted basis, Uber Freight lost $13 million in the third quarter compared to a $1 million profit in the same quarter the year before. Uber said the dismal year-over-year revenue results for its freight business were driven by lower revenue per load and volume, both consequences of the challenging freight market cycle.

Uber Freight is not alone. Other more recent entrants to the freight and logistics industry such as Flexport and Convoy have struggled in 2023; in Convoy's case the business was forced to shut down, with its assets gobbled up by Flexport. Uber Freight continues to plug along despite these economic headwinds, and the question is whether Uber believes in the long-term income potential of freight.
