# Anker Nano Magnetic Power Bank Deal Listings

Both of WIRED's October 10, 2023 Prime Day roundups list the Anker Nano, a power bank that clicks right into the bottom of a phone via a built-in connector, with the 22.5-watt USB-C version for the iPhone 15 or Android phones at $22 ($10 off).

WIRED's general roundup calls it one of its favorite portable power banks, particularly for phones, available in fun colors, with the listed model carrying a 12-watt Lightning connector; both connectors fold away when not in use, and you'll likely be able to charge most phones fully once before recharging the bank itself via the included USB-C cable. The Apple roundup adds that it's great if you don't want to worry about carrying a cable, thanks to the built-in rotating USB-C or Lightning connector, which is MFi certified, and that it offers 5,000 mAh of energy with a USB-C port for recharging and a short cable included. Both versions carry the same caveat: it may not work if you use a particularly thick case.
