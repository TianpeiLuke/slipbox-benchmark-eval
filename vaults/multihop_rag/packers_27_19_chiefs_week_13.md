# Packers 27-19 Chiefs, Week 13 2023

The Green Bay Packers beat the Kansas City Chiefs 27-19 in Week 13 of the 2023 season, picking up their fourth win in five games against the defending Super Bowl champions.

The victory came on Sunday night, and the 6-6 Packers were in the NFC wild card places afterwards.
