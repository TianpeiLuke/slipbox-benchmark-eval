# The AI Alliance's Case For Open AI Development

The AI Alliance's stated case is that openness produces faster, more inclusive innovation and better risk detection than concentration does. Its release argues: "[M]ore collaboration and information sharing will help the community innovate faster and more inclusively, and identify specific risks and mitigate those risks before putting a product into the world. This stands in contrast to a vision that aims to relegate AI innovation and value creation to a small number of companies with a closed, proprietary vision for the AI industry."

TechCrunch grants an obvious objection before the argument is made: if that sounds a lot like what the inaugural members of the Alliance were already doing independently, you are not wrong. The Alliance's answer in the release is that its work — whatever form it ultimately takes — is intended to be complementary and additive rather than needlessly duplicative.
