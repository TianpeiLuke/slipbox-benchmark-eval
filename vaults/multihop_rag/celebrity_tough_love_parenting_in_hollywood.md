# Celebrity Tough-Love Parenting In Hollywood

"Tough-love parenting" is FOX News Entertainment's 10 October 2023 label for the harsher styles some celebrities have described in interviews, prompted by the fact that Hollywood is a tough place to raise kids.

The category has two discriminating strands. The first is disciplinary severity: stars like Arnold Schwarzenegger, who admitted to being like a "drill instructor" when his kids were young, and Reese Witherspoon, who said she wants kids to deal with failure more directly.

The second is a wealth-and-gratitude strand: others, like Ashton Kutcher and Mila Kunis and Kristen Bell and Dax Shepard, are focused on keeping their kids grateful for growing up in famous families with wealth and privilege at their fingertips.
