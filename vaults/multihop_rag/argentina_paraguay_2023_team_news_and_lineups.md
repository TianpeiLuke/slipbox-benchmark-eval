# Team News And Lineups: Argentina vs Paraguay, October 2023

Argentina started their Oct. 12, 2023 World Cup qualifier against Paraguay in a 4-3-3 with Lionel Messi on the bench and Lautaro Martinez and Julian Alvarez starting together for the first time ever, while Paraguay set up in a 5-4-1.

The Argentina XI, right to left, was 23. E. Martinez in goal; 21. Molina, 13. Romero, 19. Otamendi and 3. Tagliafico; 7. De Paul, 20. Mac Allister and 8. E. Fernandez (replaced by Paredes, 80'); and 9. J. Alvarez (Messi, 53'), 22. L. Martinez and 15. N. Gonzalez. The 12 substitutes were Armani (GK), Musso (GK), Quarta, Montiel, Acuna, Ocampos, Paredes, Lo Celso, Pezzella, G. Rodriguez, Garnacho and Messi. Paraguay lined up, right to left, as 1. C. Coronel in goal; 14. I. Ramirez (R. Rojas, 46'), 15. G. Gomez, 5. Balbuena, 6. Alonso (Villasanti, 76') and 4. Espinoza; 20. R Sanchez, 8. Campuzano (Ojeda, 70'), 10. Almiron and 19. Sosa; and 21. Bareiro (Sanabria, 70'), with substitutes Rojas (GK), Espinola (GK), R. Rojas, Alderete, Avalos, Sanabria, Villaba, Galarza, M. Rojas, A. Romero, Gimenez and Ojeda.

Squad context filled out the picture an hour before kickoff. Lionel Scaloni named a bloated 36-man squad for the matches against Paraguay and Peru that month, but Angel Di Maria, Paulo Dybala and Juan Foyth were forced to withdraw due to injury, and Lisandro Martinez was unavailable after another foot operation. Nicolas Tagliafico, who had started both September matches, retained his left-back spot despite the return of Marcos Acuna from injury. Messi's club teammate Facundo Farias was also included on the roster, though it would have been surprising if he saw the field.
