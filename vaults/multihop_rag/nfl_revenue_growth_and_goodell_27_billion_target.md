# The NFL's Revenue Lead And Goodell's $27 Billion Target For 2027

From the league's humble beginnings in 1920 to the $18 billion juggernaut it is today, the NFL's ride has been rocky with more than just a few small bumps along the road, Essentially Sports wrote on 2023-10-25 — and NFL Commissioner Roger Goodell had set an ambitious target for 2027, raising the question of whether there is a new strategy in the 104-year-old league's master plan to dominate the world sporting scene.

The scale of its lead is put in comparative terms. According to Joe Pompliano, "The NFL will bring in $20 billion in revenue this year, more than the Premier League, La Liga, Bundesliga, Serie A, and Ligue 1 combined." The NFL is already the world's richest league, and calling that an incredible feat would be a gross understatement: the English Premier League, broadcast in over 212 territories, has a revenue of just $6 billion, while football games are primarily viewed in America only. But the NFL does not want to stop at $18 billion — it wants to become a $27 billion league in 4 years, though it must address a few skeletons in its closet before reaching that goal. The strategic question raised is whether it can take a leaf out of the NBA's playbook and reinvigorate its appeal as it aims to conquer international audiences.
