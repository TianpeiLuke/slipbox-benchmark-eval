# Partial Liver Transplants As A Way To End Wait List Mortality

A partial liver transplant is when either a living or deceased donor's liver is donated to two different recipients, and Mazariegos is determined to eliminate pediatric "wait list mortality" through its use.

The problem the technique targets is a recurring annual toll: each year, dozens of children in the U.S. die while awaiting a new liver.
