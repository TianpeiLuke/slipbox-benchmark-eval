# Meta Quest 3 And The Affordable End Of AR/VR Hardware

In its 2023-10-13 startups roundup, TechCrunch reported that the Meta Quest 3 takes a step closer to mainstream AR/VR at the affordable end of the market, in reporting by Brian.

The newsletter frames that report against an argument its author had made a while back: that Apple's new AR headset is a game-changer for startups. The author's assessment at the time of writing is that the game-changer claim seems likely to hold at the high end of the market, while the Quest 3 is what moves the category toward mainstream adoption in the more affordable range. The observation is a snapshot of the newsletter's read on consumer AR/VR hardware as of that date, with the high-end and affordable segments treated separately.
