# Blackhawks vs. Penguins 2023: Start Time And Arena

The Blackhawks-Penguins opening-night game was scheduled for puck drop at 8 p.m. ET (7 p.m. CT) at PPG Paints Arena in Pittsburgh, Pa., with the date listed as Tuesday, Oct. 11.

The listing's fields are date and time, given as Tuesday, Oct. 11 and 8 p.m. ET (7 p.m. CT), with the Sporting News noting the puck-drop time in both the Eastern and Chicago clock.

The venue is the one boundary worth keeping straight: although the time is quoted for Chicago as well, the game itself is played at PPG Paints Arena in Pittsburgh.
