# Mainstream Platform Adoption Of ActivityPub

Flipboard is only one of several companies that had embraced decentralized social media by December 2023. X rival Instagram Threads began testing ActivityPub the week before Flipboard's announcement.

Other tech companies are moving in the same direction. Automattic made it possible for all WordPress.org and WordPress.com blogs to become federated, and said it is working on doing the same with Tumblr in 2024. Medium and Mozilla have also set up their own servers, and Mozilla backed a Mastodon client called Mammoth.
