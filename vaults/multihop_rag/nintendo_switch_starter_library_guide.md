# Six Games To Play First On A New Nintendo Switch

Polygon's guide for new Switch owners answers one question — where to start — with six recommendations ranging from modern Nintendo classics that cannot be played on any non-Nintendo console to indie games that are perfect for the Switch.

The premise is that the console has a robust, delightful library, that you can play those games on a television screen or monitor and also take the console with you wherever you go, and that there are tons of cute Joy-Con colors, a great pro controller, and lots of themed peripherals for fans of all stripes. The hardest part, Polygon says, is sorting through the options. It also flags that narrowing to six was genuinely difficult, and points readers wanting more toward its lists of the 22 best Nintendo Switch games, the 36 best couch co-op games for Nintendo Switch, and the best Nintendo Switch games for grown-ups.
