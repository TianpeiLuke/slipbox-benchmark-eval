# Rogue Trader: Verdict And Release Details

Polygon's verdict is that Rogue Trader is a dense, vast game, much of it clearly crafted with love for the expansive lore of the 40K canon, and that while there are small annoyances and clunky features along the way, the political intrigue, cast of characters, and moral choices have the reviewer hooked.

The claim for its audience is specific: for 40K fans this is a rare treat — a game that digs past the heroic facade of bolters and battles and taps into the grimdark dystopia that makes this particular sci-fi setting so compelling. On release details, Warhammer 40,000: Rogue Trader was set to launch December 7 on Windows PC, and Polygon discloses that the game was reviewed using a pre-release download code provided by Owlcat Games, and that Vox Media has affiliate partnerships which do not influence editorial content though the company may earn commissions on products purchased via affiliate links.
