# Tubi's Free Ad-Supported Streaming Movie Catalog

Tubi is Fox's ad-supported free streaming service, and it holds one of the most vast libraries of any streaming service, with thousands upon thousands of movies available to watch at home. The defining features are that it is free, ad-supported, owned by Fox, and enormous in scale.

The size of the catalog is what makes curation difficult. Writing for Polygon on 2023-10-25, the author reported that when building a list of the best movies on the service, they looked up their "liked" films on Letterboxd that were currently on Tubi and more than 100 showed up — an illustration offered explicitly as not an exaggeration. The response was to narrow the field down to a group of excellent movies drawn from a variety of moods, cultures, and eras.

The catalog is treated as a living list rather than a fixed one. Polygon said it would continue to update its Tubi picks over time, building out a full catalog of excellent free movies to watch at home; the latest update at the time added Planet Terror as an editor's pick.
