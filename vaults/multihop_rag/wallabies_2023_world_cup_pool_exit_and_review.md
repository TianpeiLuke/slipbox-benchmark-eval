# The Wallabies' 2023 Pool Exit And The Review That Followed

Eddie Jones' Wallabies became the first Australian side to bow out of a Rugby World Cup in the pool stages in 2023, and Jones accepted that a review would be required into the flop — a statement that, as The Roar put it, puts the ball firmly in Rugby Australia's court as far as his future goes.

Jones was named Wallabies coach at the start of the year as replacement for Dave Rennie, arriving with grand statements that his team could win the Rugby Championship, the Bledisloe Cup and make a smash and grab on the World Cup. None of those targets were achieved, and Jones changed his tune about building for the future when he named a young team for the doomed Cup campaign. He finished the year with a 2-7 win-loss record, the only two victories coming against lowly Georgia and Portugal.

On the review process, Rugby Australia had so far said it intended backing Jones, and he said he believed he had official support, while refusing to answer a question on potential break clauses in his contract. He said the team — including coaches and players — had already completed their own review of the World Cup disaster, and that the next one would be "a formal review to be done by independent consultants" in which he was ready to participate. He also rejected any suggestion the job was a soft landing: "I didn't come back to Australia to have a holiday – sit down at Coogee Beach, eat fish and chips, have a nice flat white. It was always going to be a battle. When you've got a team that hasn't done well for a long period of time, it's always a battle."
