# Outlook For Home Robots Beyond Vacuums

Boston Dynamics CTO Aaron Saunders predicts that we may see additional introduction of robots into the home in the next decade, but only for very limited and specific tasks — like Roomba, other clear value cases will be found in daily life — and that we are still more than a decade away from multifunctional in-home robots that deliver value to the broad consumer market.

He offers a test for when the prediction would be overturned, framed as a question about willingness to pay: when would you pay as much for a robot as you would for a car? His answer is when it achieves the same level of dependability and value we have come to take for granted in the amazing machines we use to transport us around the world.
