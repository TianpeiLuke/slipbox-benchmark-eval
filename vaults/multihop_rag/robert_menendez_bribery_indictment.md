# Robert Menendez's Bribery Indictment

In September 2023 the Justice Department charged Sen. Bob Menendez (D-N.J.) and his wife Nadine with accepting bribes and allegedly using his Senate seat to improperly aid the Egyptian government; Menendez pleaded not guilty.

A raid of Menendez's house turned up $100,000 in gold bars and bundles of cash stuffed into jacket pockets. The charges were pending in December 2023 when Menendez, as Fortune reported on 2023-12-06, gave Wall Street bank executives a pop quiz about their own corporate misbehavior that they all failed — an irony the reporting made explicit, since Menendez's own adherence to the law happened to also be in question.
