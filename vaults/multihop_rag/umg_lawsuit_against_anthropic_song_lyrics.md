# The UMG, Concord And ABKCO Lawsuit Against Anthropic Over Song Lyrics

Universal Music Group, Concord Music Group and ABKCO filed a lawsuit against AI developer Anthropic seeking potentially tens of millions of dollars for the alleged "systematic and widespread infringement of their copyrighted song lyrics."

Music Business Worldwide noted on 2023-11-30 that the fair use argument Anthropic had advanced in its submission to the US Copyright Office could yet come into play in the company's legal battle with Universal.
