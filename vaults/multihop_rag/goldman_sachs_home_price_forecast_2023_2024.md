# Goldman Sachs' Forecast That Home Prices Keep Rising Slowly Through 2024

Goldman Sachs managing director Roger Ashworth predicted that home prices will rise by 1.8% by the end of 2023 and by 3.5% by the end of 2024, and he is not hopeful that prices will drop anytime soon. The proposition is stated with an explicit escape condition: "Absent any negative shocks to the broader economy that would either boost excess supply of homes on the market or fuel an uptick in unemployment, we continue to expect home prices to rise at a slow pace."

That conditional is what would falsify the forecast. A negative shock to the broader economy that pushed excess supply of homes onto the market, or one that fueled an uptick in unemployment, is by his own statement the circumstance under which the expectation of slowly rising prices would no longer hold.
