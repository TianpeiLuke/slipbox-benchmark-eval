# Preparing For A Startup Co-Founder Breakup

The precondition for the advice is that startup co-founders do not always make it — the breakup is a live possibility on any startup journey, not an exception to plan around after the fact.

The step it prescribes is to act early: in her TC+ piece, Rebecca explores what you can do to prepare today, should a co-founder breakup be on your startup journey's path.

The framing TechCrunch gives it: hope for the best, prepare for the worst.
