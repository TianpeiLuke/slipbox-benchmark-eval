# Week 13 Wide Receiver Injury Report

Entering Week 12, Tee Higgins (hamstring), Noah Brown (knee), Treylon Burks (concussion) and Kadarius Toney (ankle, hip) all failed to suit up due to nagging injuries. For Week 13, published Nov. 29, 2023, Sporting News placed Higgins and Brown in its rankings list and omitted Burks and Toney. It was also watching the Chargers' Josh Palmer (knee), eligible to come off IR that week but probably not suiting up against New England.

Four more receivers left their games hurt the previous week: Chris Olave (concussion), Amari Cooper (ribs), Demario Douglas (concussion) and Rashid Shaheed (thigh). All four were left out of the initial Week 13 list, with their teams' injury reports to be monitored closely to gauge their playing prospects.
