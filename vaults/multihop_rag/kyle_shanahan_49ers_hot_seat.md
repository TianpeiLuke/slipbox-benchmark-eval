# Kyle Shanahan 49ers Hot Seat

Yardbarker's Nov. 30, 2023 NFC hot-seat roundup named head coach Kyle Shanahan as the San Francisco 49ers' hottest seat while explicitly limiting the claim: his job was in zero danger, but his reputation may be.

The grounds are that the 49ers had done almost everything possible under Shanahan except win a Super Bowl, and that fair or not, failing to win a title with a stacked roster — especially on offense, with Christian McCaffrey, Deebo Samuel, Brock Purdy and George Kittle — would be a massive disappointment. The warrant is that for a coach whose employment is secure, the thing actually at stake is standing rather than the job itself.
