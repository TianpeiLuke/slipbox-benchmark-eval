# The onn. Budget Tablet Black Friday Deal

Walmart's Black Friday 2023 sale included an onn. tablet in the $20 range, which Mashable's 25 November 2023 roundup framed as beating the "best tablets under $100" category outright: "Try best tablets under $30."

The tablet may not have all the functionality of an iPad, the roundup allows, "but if you're looking for a super basic tablet to watch videos or read on, you'll be hard-pressed to find anything in the $20 range".

The comparison offered is with Amazon's own devices: "Even Fire tablets have only dropped down to $40 this Cyber Monday."
