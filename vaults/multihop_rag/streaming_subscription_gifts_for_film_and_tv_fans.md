# Streaming Subscription Gifts For Film And TV Fans

For movie buffs and avid sports fans alike, The Verge's last-minute gift guide points to subscriptions that grant a giftee access to a wide range of content, including some catered toward anime diehards, horror lovers, and those looking for something more niche.

The example it details is the Disney Bundle at $15: a monthly, ad-supported subscription that grants access to Disney Plus, Hulu, and ESPN Plus for $14.99 a month, listed as $15 at Disney. The Verge's pitch for it is breadth of household appeal — sports fans can watch all the latest games while the rest of the family streams Percy Jackson and the Olympians, Ahsoka, and The Bear.
