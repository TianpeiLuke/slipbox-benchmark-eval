# Week 14 Tight End Byes: Logan Thomas And Trey McBride

Week 14's two-team bye in the 2023 season removed Logan Thomas and Trey McBride from fantasy lineups, a loss Sporting News judged more problematic than most would have expected that late in the season.

Losing both this late could have some fantasy owners scrambling. Fortunately, both the Commanders and Cardinals have above-average tight end defenses, so taking those two opponents off the board only helped the position that week.
