# Why The Springboks Were Predicted To Beat England At The Stade De France

Sporting News predicted South Africa would beat England in the 2023 Rugby World Cup semi-final at the Stade de France, because the match required a huge step up in quality from Steve Borthwick's side while South Africa had already shown they could match and beat the world's best.

The grounds are England's record read closely. Their tournament form was perfect at face value — Argentina, Japan and Chile, plus Samoa and Fiji, had all provided highlights, and England beat all of them, with Los Pumas also reaching the semi-finals. But only Japan and Chile were defeated with bonus points, and the narrow wins over Samoa and Fiji in the last two games showed that England were certainly gettable.

The recent head-to-head reinforced the point. The last time the sides met was a tumultuous period for English rugby: South Africa's dominant 27-13 victory at Twickenham proved to be the last game in charge for Eddie Jones, the coach who had taken England to the 2019 World Cup final, and Borthwick was hired in his place — now one game away from repeating that feat.
