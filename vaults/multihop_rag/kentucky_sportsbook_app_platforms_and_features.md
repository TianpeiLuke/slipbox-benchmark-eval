# Kentucky Sportsbook App Platforms And Features

The Kentucky sportsbook apps differ on two axes — which platforms they run on and which features they carry — as tabulated by CBS Sports on 13 October 2023.

On platforms, Fanatics is listed with iOS and Android apps only, while FanDuel, DraftKings, BetMGM, Caesars and bet365 are each listed with iOS and Android apps plus desktop and mobile web.

On features, the listed sets are: Fanatics — FanCash Rewards, bettable search, Discover page, cash outs, easy sign up, secure deposit and a Withdrawal Tracker; FanDuel — quick bets, secure deposits, horse racing, casino, cash outs and fast payouts; DraftKings — casino, fast withdrawals and secure deposits; BetMGM — easy deposits and withdrawals, 24/7 access and safe, secure transactions; Caesars — Caesars Rewards, casino and Quick Picks; and bet365 — cash outs, personalized display, alerts, edit bets, My Teams, search and sports stats.
