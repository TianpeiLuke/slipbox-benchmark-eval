# Women's Nations League Group A1 Qualification Scenarios

After Belgium's 3-2 win over England, the Lionesses needed to win their last two group games and hope that both the Netherlands and Belgium slipped up to stand any chance of qualifying for the knockout stages, Sport Grill reported on 31 October 2023.

Belgium would meet Scotland on Friday 1 December at Den Dreef, Leuven, in an 8:30pm CET (7:30pm) kick-off, with the Belgian Red Flames knowing that a win would put their fate in their own hands heading into their final group match away to the Netherlands.

England meanwhile would host the Netherlands that same evening at Wembley Stadium, London, in a 7:45pm GMT kick-off.
