# Bose QuietComfort Headphone And Earbud Deals

The Verge lists six Bose sets on sale: QuietComfort Headphones at $249 ($100 off) from Amazon, Best Buy, and Bose; QuietComfort 45 at $229 ($100 off) from Amazon, Best Buy and Target; QuietComfort Ultra Headphones at $379 ($50 off) from Amazon, Best Buy, and Bose; Noise Cancelling Headphones 700 at $279 ($100 off) from Best Buy and Bose; QuietComfort Ultra earbuds at $279 ($20 off) from Amazon, Best Buy, and Bose; and QuietComfort Earbuds II at $199 ($80 off) at Amazon, Best Buy, and direct from Bose.

Earlier in the fall Bose rehauled its lineup, replacing the QuietComfort 45 with the QuietComfort Headphones, which add adjustable noise cancellation levels, custom modes, and a green colorway. The Verge hasn't tested them, but headphone reviewer Chris Welch says they're otherwise extremely similar to the QuietComfort 45: foldable, exceptionally comfortable, with 24-hour battery life and a transparency mode that helps when you need to be more aware of your surroundings, like when about to cross the street. The QuietComfort Ultra Headphones add higher-quality Bluetooth codecs and an immersive audio mode — essentially Bose's version of spatial audio — and improve on the Noise Cancelling Headphones 700 with a foldable design, better call quality, and more effective noise cancellation, plus new physical controls like a volume slider; the improved call quality is notable given the 700s were already exemplary in that respect. Bose also replaced the excellent QuietComfort Earbuds II with the QuietComfort Ultra wireless earbuds, which outperform all other earbuds on the market for noise cancellation and add immersive audio, better voice call quality, and new silicone stabilizer wings that are easier to apply, while remaining very similar in design, fit, and sound to their predecessor — but they still lack multipoint support and don't come with a built-in wireless charging case. The Verge notes the Ultra earbuds recently dropped to $249, though at the time of writing $279 is the available price.
