# Week 14 Standard-League Running Back Sleepers

Sporting News argued on Dec. 7, 2023 that Tyjae Spears (@ Dolphins) should be the hottest waiver-wire pickup of Week 14 in standard leagues, heading a group of sleepers whose value came from injuries and workload uncertainty ahead of them.

The Spears case rests on Derrick Henry's concussion and Tennessee's identity: Tennessee is a run-first offense and King Henry might be sidelined with a head injury, so Spears could very well pick up where he left off against Indy the previous weekend, when the young back averaged 4.7 yards per carry — just shy of his 5.1 YPC entering the week — en route to 88 yards. Another backup getting a boost was the Patriots' Ezekiel Elliott (@ Steelers), who would assume lead-back duties after Rhamondre Stevenson went down with a pretty bad ankle injury in Week 13; Zeke might put in a vintage performance considering James Conner had just gone back to Pittsburgh and hung 105 yards and two touchdowns on his old squad.

Keaton Mitchell (vs. Rams) was floated as a possible RB2 for the Ravens, on the reasoning that Los Angeles might find itself spying Lamar Jackson and Gus Edwards so often that the speedy rookie busts a couple of big runs. The qualification is distribution: Mitchell had racked up 330 yards and two touchdowns since breaking out four games earlier, but the majority of that damage — 134 yards and a touchdown — came in the initial breakout against Seattle, and he had still yet to see 12 touches in a game, something Sporting News could not imagine still being true by the end of the regular season. Roschon Johnson (vs. Lions) could have a chance to take over lead-back duties in Chicago, as both Khalil Herbert and D'Onta Foreman had battled injuries and inconsistent touches; it was a tough matchup, so expectations should be tempered, but there was some high-floor flex appeal considering Alvin Kamara and Taysom Hill averaged 5.2 yards per touch and found the end zone three times via the ground against Detroit the previous week.
