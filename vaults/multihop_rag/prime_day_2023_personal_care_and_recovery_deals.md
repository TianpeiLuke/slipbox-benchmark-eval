# Prime Day 2023 Personal Care And Recovery Deals

WIRED's personal care picks are Therabody's Smart Goggles, the TheraFace Pro, the Philips Multigroom Series 9000, the Dyson Supersonic at $300, a flat iron, a multi-styling tool, the Gravity Move massage gun, and reusable ear plugs.

Therabody's Smart Goggles (9/10, WIRED Recommends) use heat, vibrations, and light pressure to massage eyes and temples, with an app to customize sessions and track heart rate to help reduce stress and anxiety; they feel amazing but are very expensive, as all Therabody products are, and the writer noticed a small hole in the eye pad after a while that hasn't grown. WIRED is testing Gravity's competing eye massager, which is not app-controlled but much cheaper and not hated so far. The TheraFace Pro is a very expensive self-care tool WIRED liked, finding it exfoliated away blackheads, cleared up stuffy sinuses from allergies, and even minimized fine lines, alongside buzzwordy functions like microcurrent that may or may not work; it hasn't gone on sale often in its yearish shelf life, though the hot and cold heads cost an additional $99. On trimmers: trimming and edging a beard can be as aggravating as edging a lawn, and most trimmers make do with too many plastic guides of different lengths yet not enough attachments for fine detailing, whereas the Multigroom Series 9000 comes with an adjustable 1-to-3-millimeter guide plus a mini-foil shaver, narrow-width head, T-shaped head, ear/nose attachment and more, with stainless steel construction rare among consumer trimmers that feels solid in the hand. The Dyson Supersonic (8/10, WIRED Recommends) is one of the best hair dryers you can buy and extremely expensive, so buy it on sale rather than at full price. The flat iron, recommended in WIRED's Best Hair Straighteners guide, has never dropped this low: easy to use and comfortable to hold, wanting more temperature settings but best for finer, wavier hair. The multi-styling tool, also in that guide, comes with round and paddle-brush attachments and matches the lowest price tracked. The Gravity Move (8/10, WIRED Recommends) is one of WIRED's favorite massage guns — compact but able to work sore muscles with ease, with four attachment heads including a heated one that feels heavenly, though the case only fits the device and one head at a time. Adrienne has two pairs of the ear plugs, Best for Sleep in WIRED's Best Earplugs guide: stylish, with different-sized ear tips, staying put much more easily than foam ones from a giant can, and reusable.
