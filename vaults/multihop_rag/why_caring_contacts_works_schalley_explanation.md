# Why Caring Contacts Works, According To Schalley

Schalley credits the success of Caring Contacts to a multitude of factors rather than to any single mechanism, and the factors she names are about normalization, skills and relationship.

On normalization and treatment adherence: "The program normalizes the struggles and challenges of mental health and stresses the importance of ongoing treatment," she said. "It provides positive coping skills and words of encouragement."

On relationship, the program keeps a person, not just a service, in the young patient's life: it "provides the ability for the youth to maintain a positive, trusting relationship with the social worker," said Schalley.
