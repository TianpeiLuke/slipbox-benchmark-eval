# Novel Electric Two-Wheeler Concepts, 2023

TechCrunch flagged two unusual small electric vehicles on 2023-10-22. Infinite Machine's first product, the P1, is an e-scooter that looks like a Cybertruck, and is on sale now.

The second is Shane, a two-wheeled EV concept described as a space-pod-looking thing, from the creator of the original hoverboard, Shane Chen.
