# Britney Spears On Motherhood And Postpartum Depression

Spears writes in *The Woman in Me*, per The Guardian's 24 October 2023 account, that she suffered severe postpartum depression after her sons were born — depression she only recognised later — and that it left her suicidal: "I'll admit it, I felt that I couldn't live if things didn't get better."

When she became pregnant with her first son she initially thought it might protect her — "I wanted everyone to stay away: stand back! There's a baby here!" — but the paparazzi became more aggressive, particularly once she had her children in quick succession, becoming pregnant a second time three months after giving birth to her first. She felt hopeless about her ability to protect her sons: "I got a little depressed once I was no longer keeping them safe inside my body … I wanted them back inside me so the world couldn't get at them."

She has a keen sense of injustice about a contract with the public and the press that she never signed: "They just kept acting like I owed it to them to let the men who kept trying to catch me looking fat take photos of my infant sons." Her postpartum body and shots of her without makeup were treated as "some kind of a sin – as if gaining weight was something unkind I'd done to them personally, a betrayal. At what point did I promise to stay 17 for the rest of my life?" What she knows now, she writes, "is that every part of normal life had been stripped from me – going out in public without becoming a headline, making normal mistakes as a new mother of two babies, feeling like I could trust the people around me. I had no freedom and yet also no security."
