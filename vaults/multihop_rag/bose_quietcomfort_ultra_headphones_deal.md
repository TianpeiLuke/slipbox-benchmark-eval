# Bose QuietComfort Ultra Headphones Deal

Bose's new flagship noise-cancelling headphones, the QuietComfort Ultra, were $50 off at Amazon, Walmart, Bose direct and other retailers for Black Friday 2023, bringing the over-ear cans down to $379 from a $429 full price. Bose released them only the previous month, and outside of a very brief drop a few weeks back that may have been a pricing error, this was the first significant discount Engadget had seen.

Engadget gave the pair a score of 86 in its review, praising its predictably comfy design, outstanding ANC, above-average call quality and bassy default sound with "increased clarity and enhanced warmth" compared to previous generations of the QC cans; one of its concerns in the review was the higher MSRP, so the deal takes some of the sting out. Engadget still recommends the Sony XM5 to most, as that pair offers a wider feature set and the ability to be used passively — without being turned on — but the QuietComfort Ultra may still be worth a look for especially powerful noise cancellation. The new, non-Ultra QuietComfort Headphones were also $100 off, down to a new low of $249; Engadget hasn't reviewed that model, the follow-up to 2021's QuietComfort 45 which it generally liked, but other reviews suggest a more bass-heavy sound and it expects a slight step down from the Ultra across the board.
