# Tottenham's Win Over Everton With Richarlison Scoring, December 2023

Richarlison scored against his old club Everton for a third consecutive match as Ange Postecoglou's Tottenham won 2-1 at home on 23 December 2023, ensuring they would be in the Premier League top four at Christmas, as reported by The Roar that day.

The goals are recorded in order: Richarlison's opener, described in the report as pure Ange-ball with Spurs passing their way through Everton, then Son Heung-min adding to it, and Andre Gomes scoring a later consolation for the visitors.

The report flags one decisive intervention against Everton: Sean Dyche's visitors crucially had a Dominic Calvert-Lewin effort ruled out by VAR in the 51st minute, which could have changed the complexion of the match.
