# Biden's Picket Line Visit And EV Worker Messaging

Biden became the first modern president to visit a picket line in late September 2023, standing alongside striking UAW members in Wayne County, Michigan, and telling workers they "deserve a significant raise," citing the record profits made by the Big Three automakers — Ford, GM, and Stellantis.

The Verge judged that his efforts to frame the EV transition as good for workers as well as for the environment remain a work in progress, and that he has his work cut out for him as he seeks to shore up support among working-class families. Striking UAW members may be skeptical about whether EVs are affordable or practical enough for themselves, but that reflects widely held attitudes about the rapid shift to electric — skepticism that can't be ignored, which is why the Biden administration is prioritizing billions in federal funding for the installation of a robust EV charging network.
