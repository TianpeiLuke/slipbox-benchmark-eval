# Second Half Live Updates From Manchester United 2-1 Chelsea

United won the second half 1-0 and held on for a crucial three points, with McTominay's 70th-minute header the decisive goal, per Sporting News' live blog of Dec. 6, 2023.

Both sides made a change at the break in the 46th minute: Sergio Reguilon came on for Lindelof and, much to the delight of the Chelsea fans, Reece James came on for Cucurella. In the 49th minute Jackson had a glorious chance at the back post to make it 2-1 but could not keep his header down. In the 54th minute lightning did not strike twice for Garnacho, whose latest acrobatic effort went well wide. Antony's trivela was deflected for a corner in the 61st minute, and it may have been going wide. In the 62nd minute there was a penalty appeal for United when Shaw rocketed an attempt goalwards and it looked as though Colwill may have blocked it with his hand; VAR took a quick look and play continued. Antony tried one of his trademark curling efforts from the right after cutting in on his left in the 69th minute, but it was wide.

In the 70th minute came the goal for 2-1 United and McTominay's brace: a great ball to the back post by Garnacho, met by McTominay's header from inside the six-yard box, putting him in terrific goalscoring form and making him United's top scorer that season. In the 74th minute an insane recovery run and block by Reece James denied Garnacho from doubling United's lead after he was picked out superbly by Onana to kickstart a rapid counter by the hosts. In the 80th minute Garnacho so nearly made it 3-1 but his delicate finish toyed with the goalline. In the 90th minute Broja hit the post — so close to the equaliser for Chelsea — with four minutes of added time to come. At full time United could breathe a sigh of relief and hold onto a crucial three points.
