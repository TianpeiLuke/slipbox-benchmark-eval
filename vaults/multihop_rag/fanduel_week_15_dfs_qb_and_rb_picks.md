# FanDuel Week 15 DFS Quarterback And Running Back Picks

Sporting News built its Week 15 FanDuel DFS lineup around quarterback Matthew Stafford of the Rams ($7,200) and running backs Rachaad White ($7,600) and Breece Hall ($7,500), publishing on Dec. 13, 2023.

The framing was that the fantasy regular season had closed: the best part of fantasy football is watching the team you draft have a chance to win a championship, the worst is watching it get injured and plummet — and just because the regular season had come to an end did not mean managers could not still search for ways to win, which is why the outlet headed to FanDuel to craft a Week 15 lineup to root for.

Stafford (Rams vs. Commanders) returned to the friendly confines of SoFi Stadium after dealing with the elements in Baltimore the previous week; after nearly pulling off the upset over the Ravens he had a nice landing spot against a defense Sporting News likened to training-camp props, since Washington could not slow anyone down and had only gotten worse since trading away two of its best defenders at the deadline. Stafford does not have the rushing upside, but he is more than capable of having a good day with his arm.

Rachaad White (Buccaneers @ Packers) had rewarded his backers with eight straight games of at least double-digit fantasy performances, including three straight games with at least 100 total yards, and Tampa had handed him the ball 60 times over that stretch, a rate ticking up each week; as arguably the top option in the Bucs' offense, Sporting News would be crazy to stand in his way against a vulnerable Packers defense. Breece Hall (Jets @ Dolphins) was backed on the reasoning that New York's coaches appeared to have stopped coaching scared: with the season essentially over barring a miracle, the staff took the handcuffs off Zach Wilson and unleashed an offense that had been run-run-pass-punting its way to irrelevance, and in doing so Hall was a weapon as a receiver, posting over 100 total yards for the first time since Week 5. The condition attached is that if the Jets continue their willingness to take chances downfield and let Wilson stop being a game manager, Hall is in line for another big day.
