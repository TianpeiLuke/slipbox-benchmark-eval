# Prediction: Generative AI Disinformation In The 2024 Election

TechCrunch predicts that influence mongers will use every tool in the box to move the needle in the 2024 U.S. presidential election, including AI in whatever form is convenient. How the election itself will play out is anyone's guess, with too many things up in the air to make any real predictions beyond that.

The mechanisms are named specifically. Expect bot accounts and fake blogs to spout generated nonsense 24/7: a few people working full time with a text and image generator can cover a lot of ground, generating hundreds of social media and blog posts with totally fabricated images and news. "Flooding the zone" has always been an effective tactic, and AI now acts as a labor multiplier, allowing more voluminous yet also targeted campaigns, with both false positives and false negatives in a concerted effort to confuse the narrative and make people distrust everything they see and read — a win state for politicians who thrive in chaos. Organizations will also tout "AI-powered" analyses to back up purges of voter rolls, challenges to vote counts and other efforts to suppress or interfere with existing processes.

Generated video and audio will join the fray, and though neither is perfect, both are good enough to be believable given a bit of fuzzing: the clip does not have to be perfect, because it will be presented as a grainy zoomed-in cellphone capture in a dark room, or a hot mic at a private event, after which it becomes a matter of "who are you going to believe, me or him?"

The prediction on mitigation is pessimistic: there will likely be some half-hearted efforts to block generated content from being used this way, but these posts cannot be taken down fast enough by the likes of Meta and Google, and the idea that X can or will effectively monitor and take down such content is implausible.
