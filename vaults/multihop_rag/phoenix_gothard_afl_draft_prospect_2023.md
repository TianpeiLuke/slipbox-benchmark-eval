# Phoenix Gothard AFL Draft Prospect 2023

Phoenix Gothard, profiled in Kevin Sheehan's top 40 for the 2023 AFL Draft as reported by The Roar on Nov. 15, 2023, is a small forward who came to prominence with his creative forward play for the Allies in their unbeaten National Championship-winning run. State: NSW/ACT. State League Club: Murray Bushrangers. Community Club: Albury. Date of Birth: 07/09/05. Height: 178cm.

In that campaign he was a livewire at ground level, averaging 10.3 disposals, three tackles and a goal a game. He was also impressive for the Murray Bushrangers in the Coates Talent League, averaging 16 disposals and 3.7 tackles across 10 matches and booting nine goals. His high natural leap and pace were both prominent at the national Draft Combine, with his standing vertical leap of 78cm ranked second overall and his time of 2.96 seconds on the 20m sprint ranked in the top ten. Weapon: Elusiveness and creativity. AFL player comparison: Tom Papley (Sydney Swans).
