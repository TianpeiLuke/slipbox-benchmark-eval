# Patriots-Steelers Week 14 Thursday Night Context

Sporting News framed the Week 14 2023 Thursday night game — the Patriots heading to Pittsburgh — as possibly the worst Thursday night game of the season, following what was arguably the best.

Whatever sport the teams would be attempting to play would probably end up setting the sport back 100 years, so much so that even the biggest supporters of defensive battles would be begging for some offense. The NFL probably did not envision Mitch Trubisky facing off against Bailey Zappe when the schedule was released back in the spring, but sometimes the worst games on paper also bring the biggest profit opportunities — which is why the column was once again building a FanDuel DFS lineup, so it had something to root for all night long.
