# Carol of the Bells and Its Biblical Roots

Although the lyrics of "Carol of the Bells" make no deep theological statement about the birth of Jesus, the biblical inspiration for the carol has Old Testament roots, according to GodTube.

Three passages are cited. Zechariah 14:20 reads: "And on that day there shall be inscribed on the bells of the horses, 'Holy to the Lord.' And the pots in the house of the Lord shall be as the bowls before the altar." The verses in Exodus 28:33-35 talk about the bells ringing on Aaron's robe as he ministers in the holy place before the Lord. And Psalm 150 says, "Let everything that has breath, praise the Lord."
