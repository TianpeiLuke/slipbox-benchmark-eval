# Taylor Swift's Comment On The Viral CD TikTok

Taylor Swift liked and commented on the viral TikTok of three young fans struggling to open her "1989 (Taylor's Version)" CD, writing "Wait, this is amazing", FOX News Lifestyle reported on 11 November 2023.

Since it was uploaded the video had garnered over 2.7 million views and 5,952 comments — one written by Swift herself. "It's so wild to think our video went viral and got Taylor Swift's attention!" Lilly told Fox News Digital. "Going to her concert was amazing, but having her notice our video is something I still can't wrap my head around." Annika said she never imagined that millions of people would find the video so comical, let alone the "1989" singer: "Who would have thought that getting a CD while sporting a broken wrist would lead to something so huge? I figured maybe a few people would see our video, but then, bam — Taylor Swift saw it. She actually saw me and liked it. It's crazy."

It was the comment from the A-list singer that really got the girls' attention. "That was the best surprise ever — my year is made," Henslee told Fox News Digital, and Tia commented, "Her like/comment is something the girls and this Swiftie mama will always remember." As Schmidt put it: "Their delightful encounter with a seemingly antiquated piece of technology, the CD, has captured the attention of Taylor Swift herself, transforming their genuine moment into an unforgettable event."
