# Colorado's First Half Against Arizona State, Oct. 7 2023

Arizona State led Colorado 17-14 at halftime on Oct. 7, 2023 in Tempe, after the teams traded first-quarter touchdowns and the Sun Devils closed the second quarter with a Cam Skattebo touchdown and a Dario Longhetto field goal on the final play of the half.

Arizona State started quickly. At 6:47 p.m. the Sun Devils scored a touchdown on their first drive behind Elijhah Badger's 48 yards to lead 7-0 just a few minutes in. Colorado's reply stalled — intentional grounding was called on Shedeur Sanders on first down at 6:54 p.m. and the drive ended in a three-and-out, which Arizona State immediately matched at 7:00 p.m. Anthony Hankerson then picked up two first downs for the Buffaloes at 7:06 p.m. on a four-yard run and a 12-yard run, and at 7:17 p.m., needing one yard on fourth down, Sanders handed off to Xavier Weaver, who weaved into the end zone to tie it 7-7 in Tempe with two minutes left in the first quarter. The quarter ended at 7-7.

Arizona State's defense stayed prominent early in the second quarter, recording its third sack of the day at 7:30 p.m. to force fourth down for the Buffaloes. On the other side, after scoring on its opening possession, Arizona State had three straight three-and-outs as of 7:34 p.m. A Colorado offsides call at 7:41 p.m. gave the Sun Devils a first down at the Buffaloes' 48; at 7:45 p.m. Andre Johnson reached over the pylon with one hand but the ball was knocked out, sending the play to review for a touchdown; and at 7:48 p.m. Skattebo pushed through for the score, his fifth rushing touchdown of the season, putting Arizona State ahead 14-7 with 6:38 left in the half.

Colorado answered before the break. At 8:06 p.m. Arizona State head coach Kenny Dillingham was unhappy with the referees, who ruled Bourguet's run just short of a first down, leaving the Sun Devils on fourth down. At 8:09 p.m. Sanders found Jimmy Horn Jr. for 24 yards to reach the Arizona State 39 with two minutes left, and at 8:14 p.m. Sanders kept the ball himself for a 16-yard touchdown to tie the game at 14-14 with less than a minute to halftime. Longhetto then nailed a 33-yard field goal at 8:20 p.m. to end the half, leaving Arizona State narrowly ahead 17-14.
