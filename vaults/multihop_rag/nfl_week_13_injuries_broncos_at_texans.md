# NFL Week 13 Injuries Broncos At Texans

In the Week 13 final injury reports published by CBSSports.com on Dec. 1, 2023, with the Texans listed at -3 against the Broncos, Denver's lone player with an injury designation was Mathis, who did not practice Friday after being limited on Thursday.

Outside of him, the Broncos were healthy heading into the matchup with Houston: wideouts Jerry Jeudy (groin) and Marvin Mims Jr. (hamstring) were both full participants on Friday after being limited on Thursday. On the Houston side, Dell logged back-to-back days as a limited participant in practice to end the week and was officially questionable, and Brown was also questionable but did not practice Friday due to a knee injury.
