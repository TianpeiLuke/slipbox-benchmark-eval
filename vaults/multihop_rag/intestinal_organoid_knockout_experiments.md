# Intestinal Organoid Knockout Experiments

Knocking out either Rnf43 or Daam1 caused intestinal organoids to grow into tumour-like structures that kept growing even when the growth factors they usually depend on, such as R-spondin, were withdrawn. "We found that when we knock-out Rnf43 or Daam1, the organoids grow into tumor-like structures. These tumor-like organoids keep on growing, even if we withdraw the growth factors they usually depend on, such as R-spondin," Gabriele Colozza notes.

The experimental system was chosen to probe the influence of Daam1 on Rnf43 and the tissue systems they influence. Intestinal organoids are three-dimensional cultures derived from adult stem cells that replicate the environment of the intestinal lining, and for Colozza they were a window into how Rnf43 and Daam1 influence the intricate interplay between the renewal and differentiation of stem cells in the gut.
