# PCR Testing In Daye's STI Screening

Daye's STI test uses PCR — the same principle as the gold standard detection test for coronavirus that became familiar during the COVID-19 pandemic — but here it looks for trace, or more, amounts of genetic material from pathogens that cause STIs.

The properties Daye claims for the method are specificity and multiplexing: "The specificity of PCRs ensures that false-positive results are reduced, providing a more reliable diagnosis," it suggests, also noting that the test can simultaneously detect multiple pathogens in a single sample. Full marks for convenience, in TechCrunch's assessment.
