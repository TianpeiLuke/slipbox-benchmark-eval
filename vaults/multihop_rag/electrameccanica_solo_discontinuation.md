# The Discontinuation Of The ElectraMeccanica Solo

ElectraMeccanica recalled every Solo in April 2023 over power failure issues, and said at the time that it was pivoting to four-wheeled vehicles. The company soon entered into a plan to merge with electric truck maker Tevva, but in October 2023 it inexplicably pulled the plug on that exit.

The Solo itself was legally a motorcycle: ElectraMeccanica's tiny EV featured one seat, 100 miles of range and an $18,500 price tag, and the automaker pitched the three-wheeled vehicle to city dwellers and delivery fleet managers alike. The TechCrunch writer never got to drive a Solo but sat inside one the previous year and found it claustrophobic with the doors shut.
