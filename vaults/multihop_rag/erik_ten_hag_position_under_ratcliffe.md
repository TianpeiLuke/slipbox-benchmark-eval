# Erik Ten Hag's Position Under Ratcliffe

Erik ten Hag's team are treading water at precisely the wrong moment, The Guardian reported on 25 December 2023: Sir Jim Ratcliffe is taking charge of Manchester United's sporting department before making sweeping changes to the posts that will directly affect the manager.

Two lines of authority above Ten Hag are in play. Richard Arnold has left, despite officially staying until the end of the year, and into his role may come Jean-Claude Blanc, the CEO of Ratcliffe's Ineos Sport, as the man Ten Hag reports to. Ten Hag's line manager may change too, as Ratcliffe considers whether a new football director is required — a position held by John Murtough.

The report suggested an early tell of Ten Hag's standing in Ratcliffe's new empire: whether he is sounded out for his view on a prime factor in his ability to coach and manage the side to success, namely United's transfer policy.
