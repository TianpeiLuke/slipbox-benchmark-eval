# Amnesia: The Bunker

Amnesia: The Bunker, playable on PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, Windows PC, Xbox One, and Xbox Series X, is ranked 36 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31. Polygon's critic says if only one developer could be said to have a master's grasp on interactive horror, it would be Frictional Games, and calls this one of the studio's best.

The Amnesia series has always been a thrill ride of terrifying chases and the quiet, a little too quiet, moments that build up tension between, and The Bunker is no different.

Set in a seemingly abandoned bunker sealed by explosions during World War I, the player's simple yet difficult task is to find an exit. Being a horror game, you also have to collect fuel for the bunker's generator — a veritable beating heart — and scrutinize maps on safe room walls before venturing into the structure's labyrinthine bowels. There is also a monster hunting you: it can ambush you from wall vents, it is attracted to even the slightest bit of sound, and whether you are juicing your hand-cranked flashlight, triggering long-forgotten tripwires, or just opening a heavy door into another concrete-encased corridor, you are going to have to make noise at some point. Critic M. Mahardy calls it as potent in its terror as any horror video game out there.
