# Sony WH-1000XM4 Noise-Canceling Headphones

The Sony WH-1000XM4 was back down to $248 from $348 — a $100 saving, which is $20 more than its all-time low but roughly $80 below its average street price.

This is Sony's last-generation pair of wireless noise-cancelers, but it still delivers powerful ANC, around 30 hours of battery life and a similarly rich feature set to the newer WH-1000XM5, which was not on sale at the time of writing. Unlike its successor it can also fold up for easier storage.

The tradeoffs against the newer model: it does not have the XM5's adaptive ANC system, its mic quality is not as clear, it is slightly heavier, and it sounds a bit boomier out of the box. But for less than $250 it remains a great value. Engadget gave the XM4 a review score of 94 back in 2020.
