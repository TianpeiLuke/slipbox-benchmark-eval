# Surveillance As A Restriction On Palestinian Freedom Of Movement

Amnesty International researcher Matthew Mahmoudi argues that the surveillance technologies deployed in the occupied territories are not merely observational but restrictive: "These technologies are deployed to restrict the freedom of movement of Palestinians," he said.

His grounds are the everyday capacities that surveillance curtails: "In particular, your ability to move, to access work, to access education, to access medical facilities is stifled based on the usage of AI-based surveillance like facial recognition."

The warrant joining the technology to political effect is deterrence of dissent. "We see how the technology ends up being used to deter Palestinians in places like Silwan in East Jerusalem from resisting and dissenting the expanding illegal settlements," Mahmoudi said, adding: "Even their family life and the sort of very mundane everyday actions suddenly become acts of resistance in the face of this ubiquitous surveillance apparatus that underpins much of how apartheid is exacted on Palestinians."
