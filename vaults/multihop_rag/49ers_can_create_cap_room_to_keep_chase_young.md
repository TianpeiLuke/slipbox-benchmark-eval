# The 49ers Can Create The Cap Room To Keep Chase Young

The Sporting News argues that although on the surface it may look hard for the 49ers to re-sign Chase Young long-term, the team has more flexibility to do so than it would initially appear — the outcome depending on how San Francisco manages its salary cap in 2024.

The grounds are the mechanics available to them. If any of Trent Williams, Arik Armstead, Deebo Samuel, George Kittle or Fred Warner were to restructure their contracts, or if a player like Brandon Aiyuk got an extension, that would push some of the 49ers' cap hits into future years. The warrant is that deferred cap cost buys present roster quality: that could prove a worthwhile move if it allows the team to keep Young and keep its Super Bowl window open with the ultra-cheap Brock Purdy at quarterback.

The argument also marks off what will not happen. The team could release some players, like Randy Gregory, to open space, but it doesn't seem likely that any of the highest-paid players listed would be cut or traded, as all are owed hefty guarantees and come with big dead-cap hits; instead the 49ers could trim from the margins of the roster if they need just a bit more space. The conclusion is hedged accordingly: a restructuring of contracts will almost certainly happen in San Francisco during the offseason — it's just a matter of which and how many players get that treatment, and whether those funds are allocated to Young or other portions of the roster.
