# Beeper Mini As An iMessage Client For Android

Beeper Mini is an Android app that lets users send and receive end-to-end encrypted iMessage chats for $1.99 per month, made possible because the team behind it reverse-engineered the iMessage protocol. Beeper, founded by former Pebble smartwatch founder Eric Migicovsky, had originally been working on an app that aggregated all of a user's chat apps into a single interface, but upon discovering new technology that allowed it to reverse-engineer iMessage it set out to build a new app focused solely on bringing iMessage chats to Android users.

What distinguishes Beeper Mini from earlier attempts is that it is a native implementation rather than a relay. "That's the big breakthrough," Migicovsky explained. "We're not actually a middleman anymore. The research that we've done is actually reverse-engineering the iMessage protocol, down to the lowest layer of the protocol. So Beeper Mini doesn't use a Mac server as a relay like all the other apps — they have a Mac Mini in a data center somewhere. And when you send a message, you're actually sending a message to the Mac Mini, which then forwards it to iMessage. Beeper Mini is a native implementation of the iMessage protocol."

Its feature coverage is correspondingly broad: support for high-resolution photos and videos, threads, replies, read receipts, direct messages and group chats, tapback emoji reactions, editing and unsending messages, plus stickers, GIFs and voice notes; the 11 December coverage lists high-quality photos and videos, tapback reactions, typing indicators and read receipts among them. Not yet supported at launch were live location sharing, message effects and FaceTime audio and video calls, and users did not need an Apple ID to use it. "It's a full-blown iMessage app," Migicovsky says. "For all intents and purposes, Beeper Mini looks like an iPhone [sent it]."

The app quickly climbed into the top 20 in the Play Store's charts and saw over 100,000 downloads in its first 48 hours, the company claims.
