# 10 Minute School (10MS)

10 Minute School, shortened to 10MS, is a Bangladeshi edtech company that began as a YouTube channel and now says it is the largest edtech company in Bangladesh; its defining aim is to democratize access to supplementary education for K12 students throughout the country. On 2023-10-12 it announced a pre-Series A of $5.5 million, the highest seed-stage funding so far for a Bangladeshi startup, bringing its total raised to $7.5 million.

What distinguishes 10MS is the breadth of what it delivers through a single smartphone app: live-streamed classes for K12 students, pre-recorded lessons, university admission test prep and personalized quizzes, among other content. Its catalogue at the time of the announcement held 35,000 recorded video lessons and over 82,000 quizzes, plus resources such as model tests, ebooks and lecture notes covering the entire national curriculum. Its reach is skewed away from the capital and toward under-served groups: 52% of its students are from rural and semi-urban areas outside of Dhaka, and 34% are girls, reflecting one of 10MS's stated goals of addressing gender inequalities in education.
