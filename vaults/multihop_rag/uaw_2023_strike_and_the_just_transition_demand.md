# The UAW 2023 Strike And The "Just Transition" Demand

The United Auto Workers of America strike had roiled Detroit and the surrounding area for two weeks as of late September 2023, sending candidates from both parties scrambling to shore up support among workers.

The Verge called the strike a watershed moment for the auto industry, with workers pushing for higher wages, improved job security, and more manageable hours. EVs were also on the table: UAW President Shawn Fain called for a "just transition" to electric cars.
