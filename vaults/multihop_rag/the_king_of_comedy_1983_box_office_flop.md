# The King Of Comedy (1983) As A Box Office Flop

Illustrating the slang term "slept on" during her TikTok quiz, Francesca Scorsese told her father: "King of Comedy was slept on," referring to his 1983 film starring Robert De Niro. The King of Comedy received mostly positive reviews from critics on release but was a flop at the box office.

Scorsese corrected the framing from his own memory of the reception: "People hated it when it came out," he said, before turning towards the camera. "It was the flop of the year, that's what it was called on Entertainment Tonight, New Year's Eve '83 to '84. It's okay, it's alright."
