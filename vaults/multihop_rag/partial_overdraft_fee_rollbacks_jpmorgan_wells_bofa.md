# Partial Overdraft Fee Rollbacks At JPMorgan, Wells Fargo And Bank of America

JPMorgan Chase, Wells Fargo and Bank of America have each eased their overdraft fees without doing away with them completely, unlike Citigroup.

In 2021, JPMorgan Chase eliminated fees for overdrafts that were under $50; according to Bloomberg the new policy still led to an increase in revenue from overdraft fees, which rose 3% in 2022, a year after the policy was implemented. Wells Fargo also loosened its rules without getting rid of the fees entirely, affording customers a 24-hour grace period to pay back any overdrafted accounts. Bank of America cut its overdraft fees from $35 to $10.
