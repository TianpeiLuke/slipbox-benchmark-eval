# NYT Connections (Word Puzzle)

Connections, developed by The New York Times for Android, browser, and iOS, is ranked 31 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31, and Polygon calls it one of the year's best games.

Polygon's context is institutional: after the acquisition of viral hit Wordle, the NYT Crossword ecosystem leveled up to a full-fledged attention competitor — the paper is a media company with a gaming platform — and its staff of puzzle writers and editors are not stooping to mind-numbing mobile content to keep the expansion going.

The game offers a grid of 16 words and a mission: detect the common threads between four different sets of words without embarrassing yourself. The grouping logic ranges from simple ("animals") to silly ("synonyms for farting") to sneaky ("countries when the letter 'A' is added"). Critic Matt Patches answers gruff game show watchers who claim the puzzle is just a clone of BBC's long-running Only Connect, saying they miss the personality within: Connections writer Wyna Liu brings tremendous wit to each day's puzzle, constructing thematic grids and throwing synonym curveballs. Like Wordle, there is a sense of accomplishment when you land all four sets.
