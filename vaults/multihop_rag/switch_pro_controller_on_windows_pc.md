# Using A Switch Pro Controller On A Windows PC

The Switch Pro controller can be used to play PC games, but like the DualSense it is not a plug-and-play affair unless you literally plug it in over USB. Three routes get it working wirelessly.

The first is Steam, which officially supports the Switch Pro controller; adding non-Steam games to your Steam library is a clever way to ensure compatibility for games that support controllers, done by clicking the "Add a Game" option in the bottom left corner of the Steam client. The second, for those who do not want to use Steam to play their games, is DS4Windows and its bevy of drivers — a free yet slightly advanced way to make Windows recognize the wireless Switch Pro controller. The third is hardware: the $19.99 8BitDo USB Adapter 2 comes in handy here too, and after plugging it into your PC and tethering the controller wirelessly to it, you are good to go with no fussing with apps required.
