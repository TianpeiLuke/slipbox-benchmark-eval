# The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes

The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes is Francis Lawrence's Hunger Games prequel starring Rachel Zegler, Tom Blyth, and Peter Dinklage, which Polygon calls a rare meaningful prequel that bolsters the existing narrative. It ranked 46 on the best movies of 2023 list of 2023-10-07 and was in theaters.

Polygon's Petrana Radulovic frames it against the series as a whole: The Hunger Games is one of the few series where the movies are often just as good as the books, and the new prequel — which focuses on a young President Snow, played excellently by Tom Blyth — is no exception.

What distinguishes it is a particular attention to detail and some masterful songs that really color the world.
