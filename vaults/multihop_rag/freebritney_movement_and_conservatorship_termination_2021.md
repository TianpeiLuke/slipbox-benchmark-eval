# The Fan Movement And The 2021 End Of Britney Spears' Conservatorship

According to The Guardian's 24 October 2023 account of *The Woman in Me*, Spears first learned of the blossoming fan movement questioning her conservatorship from a nurse, called 911 in June 2021 to report her father for conservatorship abuse days before a probate court hearing, and was granted her freedom in November 2021.

A nurse showed her the fan movement that was spreading awareness of its suspicions that all was not right with the arrangement. Of speaking publicly at that point she writes: "My voice had been used for me and against me so many times that I was afraid nobody would recognise it now if I spoke freely."

When freedom came in November 2021, she says she felt shock, relief, elation, sadness and joy.
