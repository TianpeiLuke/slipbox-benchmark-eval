# Bridgestone's Wireless In-Wheel EV Charging

At this year's Japan Mobility Show, Bridgestone showed off what it calls a "Wireless In-Wheel Tire," a concept that is less about increasing the sustainability of tires and more about increasing the viability of EVs: turn every tire into an inductive charging pad for your EV.

The design responds to a specific failure of earlier attempts. Previous wireless, in-motion charging concepts for EVs struggled to maintain strong rates because the car kept bouncing up and down as it moved, and wireless charging works best when there is minimal distance between the charger and the device being charged. So in this case the tires themselves contain the receiving coils, since they are the only part of the car that actually touches the ground.

Solutions like this seem fanciful, but they at least show that there is still a lot of room to rethink what a tire can and should be.
