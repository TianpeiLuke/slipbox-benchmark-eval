# Week 12 Running Back Injury Report

Sporting News' Nov. 21, 2023 rundown of the biggest Week 12 running back injuries covered Kenneth Walker III, Aaron Jones and Emanuel Wilson, De'Von Achane and Salvon Ahmed, D'Onta Foreman, and Dameon Pierce.

Second-year Seahawks back Kenneth Walker III suffered an oblique injury in Week 11 that Pete Carroll referred to as "legit," and he had a better chance of missing multiple weeks than of suiting up for Seattle's Thanksgiving Day game against Fred Warner and the 49ers — making Zach Charbonnet a priority waiver claim for anyone with the FAAB or a high waiver priority. Packers back Aaron Jones suffered a knee injury but seemed to have caught a break in that he did not tear his ACL; even a moderate MCL sprain could mean a multi-week absence for the injury-prone veteran, especially in a lost season for the 4-6 Packers, and with rookie Emanuel Wilson also hurting his shoulder in Week 11, AJ Dillon could be getting the keys to Green Bay's backfield.

Dolphins rookie De'Von Achane, who looked like the runaway Offensive Rookie of the Year earlier in the season, appeared to have aggravated his MCL injury on his Week 11 return against the Raiders; he had a chance to return for Miami's Friday Week 12 game with the Jets, but Sporting News would not have been surprised if the 7-3 Dolphins played it safe until he was back to 100 percent, and with backup Salvon Ahmed placed on IR with a foot injury, Jeff Wilson Jr. could be back in play as a handcuff stash. Bears veteran D'Onta Foreman injured his ankle in Week 11 and could miss time now that Khalil Herbert and Roschon Johnson are both back for Chicago — though the best runner on the Bears is quarterback Justin Fields when he is active. Texans back Dameon Pierce returned to practice late the previous week but remained out with his ankle injury against the Cardinals; he could return that weekend, but was being kept off the rankings pending more positive news, and either way Devin Singletary appeared to be the new RB1 in that breakout offense. The rankings were to be updated all week for the latest player movement.
