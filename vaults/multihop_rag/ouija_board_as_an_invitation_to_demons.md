# The Ouija Board as an Invitation to Demons

Fr. Dan Reehil argues that attempting occult practices such as purportedly trying to contact the dead via an Ouija board is a way to open oneself up to demonic activity inadvertently — and that intention does not shield the user.

The ground is a claim about what actually answers. "Demons lie and impersonate dead people," said Reehil. "When asking a board for information about a deceased person, or a life decision, they are all too happy to embed themselves into your life. Using an Ouija board is inviting a demon into your life, whether the person has that intention or not."

He rejects the framing that would defuse the practice. While the Ouija board is marketed as a toy or board game, Reehil disagrees with this characterisation: "Ouija boards should not be viewed in the same way as a typical board game. Although it is advertised as a game, it is far from it. Rather, it is a form of divination." Divination and other occult practices are condemned by the Bible numerous times, he noted. The warrant is that the act works independently of the user's understanding of it: "The very action of using [the Ouija board] has profound spiritual consequences [that are] beyond our control," he said. His conclusion is directional as well as prohibitive — "Whenever we seek to engage demons, we move away from the One True God" — and so: "Stay away from all forms of occult practices — and stay close to God."
