# Prime Day Smart Lighting, Lock And Thermostat Deals, October 2023

The Verge's October 2023 fall Prime Day smart home roundup listed discounts across lighting, locks, thermostats, and outdoor controls. In lighting, the TP-Link Tapo L930-5 smart light strip was $35, down from $50, a 30 percent discount — an RGBWIC gradient strip with color and tunable white light, dimming, and music sync that works over Wi-Fi with no hub and supports every smart home platform including Apple Home. A starter kit of Philips Hue color and white ambiance light bulbs (60W) with a Hue Bridge was $55 off, down to $75.99 at Amazon, and the Hue Bridge can be updated to support Matter. The Yeelight Cube smart lamp was 40 percent off at $54 at Amazon with an on-page coupon, with $15 off additional cubes (regularly $60.99) to complete the modular RGB lamp; the Matter-compatible Cube can double as a digital clock face, ambient light, or RGB color bonanza using its interchangeable panel, dot matrix, or spotlight cubes.

For locks and climate, the SwitchBot smart lock was $78.99 on Amazon, a 20 percent saving, adding smarts to any existing door lock without pulling out a screwdriver and working with Matter when paired with the SwitchBot Hub 2. The newer Google Nest Thermostat was $40 off at $89.99 at Amazon; it lacks the advanced learning features of the original Nest but gets the basics right and is the only thermostat that works with Matter, making it compatible with every major smart home platform including Apple Home. The Ecobee Smart Thermostat Premium was $210, down from $250, a 16 percent discount — besides adjusting HVAC intelligently based on whether you are home or away and where you are in the home, it is an indoor air quality monitor, a smart speaker for Alexa or Siri, and a hub for Ecobee's smart security system.

Outdoors and elsewhere, the Rachio Smart Sprinkler Controller was $116, down from $150, a 23 percent discount and one of its lowest-ever prices, stopping irrigation from running before, during, or after a rainstorm. The Wi-Fi Kasa smart outdoor plug, good for automating holiday lights, was $16 on Amazon, a 36 percent saving, with two waterproof outlets that work with Alexa and Google Home; for Apple Home support, the two-socket Meross Outdoor Smart Plug was $21.75 at Amazon. The Meross smart garage door controller was down to $39.99 on Amazon, working with Alexa, Google Home, and Apple Home, and with Liftmaster and Chamberlain doors via a simple add-on accessory, while the MyQ Chamberlain Smart Garage Control was $22, down from $30, a 27 percent discount. Aqara also had a slew of deals on smart switches, plugs, motion sensors, and light strips, most of which work with Matter through an Aqara hub and some at their lowest price ever, including the powerful yet complicated FP2 presence sensor at $64.99 with an on-page coupon.
