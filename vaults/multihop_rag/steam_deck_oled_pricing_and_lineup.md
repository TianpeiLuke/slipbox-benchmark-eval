# Steam Deck OLED Pricing And Lineup

The Steam Deck OLED goes on sale on November 16th at 1pm ET, with Valve holding units ready to ship that same day, and it starts at $549, replacing all but the entry-level model in the range. Valve says the lineup moving forward is: 256GB Steam Deck LCD at $399, 512GB Steam Deck OLED at $549, and 1TB Steam Deck OLED at $649. There is also a limited edition 1TB OLED version — priced at $680 in the review and $679 in the launch coverage — that comes with a special carrying case, a translucent gray body and orange accents.

Valve has lowered the price of its LCD lineup and will sell through its backstock of 64GB and 512GB models until they're all gone: the 64GB LCD Steam Deck, previously sold at $399, drops to $349, and the 512GB LCD, previously $649, drops to $499, after which both lines will be discontinued — though Valve says it will continue to support all LCD models with software updates. So the long-term lineup starts at the same $399 price, which gets the old LCD Steam Deck but with 256GB of storage instead of 64GB; the new step-up model costs $20 more at $549 but comes with an OLED display, the hardware improvements and double the storage; and the range still tops out at $649, now with 1TB of storage instead of 512GB, an improved carry case that can pop out into a slimmer shell that's smaller and easier to fit in a bag, and the same anti-glare etched glass as the outgoing 512GB LCD model. The updated pricing on the existing LCD models is already in effect.
