# Joseph Parker's Upset Win Over Deontay Wilder

Kiwi heavyweight Joseph Parker, a big underdog according to FanDuel Sportsbook, beat former champion Deontay Wilder in Riyadh on the card reported by The Roar on 23 December 2023, ruining Wilder's chances of facing Anthony Joshua.

The 31-year-old Parker looked the better fighter throughout while the 38-year-old Wilder looked his age in a match-up of former heavyweight champions. Parker, moving to 34-3-0, dominated from the start, landing powerful punches with his right hand while keeping Wilder on his heels with a smart and methodical approach; there was only one round in which Wilder got the better of him.

Parker described the plan afterwards: "Dangerous fight, tough fight, we trained very hard for this. We had great momentum coming into this fight. The strategy was to stay calm, stay relaxed, stay focused … every minute of every round. This is a great finish to the year." Wilder's account was that "My timing was off a little bit. He did a great job of avoiding a lot of my punches. I did feel like I had the upper hand, but things happen. We move on to the next step."
