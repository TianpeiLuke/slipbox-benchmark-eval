# A Patent Redesign Must Be More Than A Colorable Difference

Andrei Iancu argued that a redesign route out of the import ban is not a free option: "From a legal perspective, whatever change Apple makes has to be significant enough to avoid the patent, and it cannot be what's called a 'colorable' difference. Minor changes will not be sufficient."

Whether the route succeeds, Iancu said, depends on the specific patent, the ITC ruling, and exactly what Apple has planned for redesigning the product. The practical consequence is that Apple can't simply make a small tweak to the code and call it a day. Masimo claims the patents are hardware related, so even completely disabling the sensor may not work depending on how the patent is written. Apple could remove the sensor entirely from the design, but that would require mucking around with Apple's meticulous supply chain and could take some time.
