# The Swift Effect On NFL Ratings And Kelce Jersey Sales

The NFL's focus on the Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce dating rumours paid off in ratings even as it annoyed some sports fans. Per a Deadline report cited by The Independent on 4 October 2023, NBC accumulated an average of 27m viewers for the broadcast, with a total of 29m tuning in during the second quarter. On the New Heights podcast, Jason Kelce told his younger brother: "That's gotta be an NFL record right there," noting that about two million viewers were estimated to be female and joking, "I think a few of them might have been there for Deadpool and, uh, Taylor."

Merchandise moved as well: jersey sales for the Chiefs tight end reportedly spiked by 400 per cent, according to a report from the NFL's e-commerce partner, Fanatics, since Swift began attending her rumoured boyfriend's games.
