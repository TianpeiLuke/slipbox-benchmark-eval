# Noora Niasari's Childhood Shelter And The Origin Of Shayda's Script

When Noora Niasari was five years old she lived in a women's shelter with her Iranian mother, fleeing family violence in a country that was not entirely familiar and trying to make a new life — the experience Shayda dramatises, The Guardian reported on 1 October 2023.

Shayda is a dramatisation of Niasari's early life, set in the Iranian diaspora community of suburban Melbourne, which the paper calls a sensational reception for a first film given the specificity of its story. "It was something I had experienced, but I hadn't really seen on screen before," Niasari says of the movie she started thinking about straight after finishing film school. "But I first had to ask my mum for her permission and participation, because I had such a blurry memory of that time."

She asked her mother to write her memoirs, which took six months, and that writing formed the basis of the first incarnation of Shayda's script. The film evolved over time and is not always a direct mirror of what happened to them both, but "it is very emotionally true to our experience".
