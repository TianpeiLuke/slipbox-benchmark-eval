# Inter Miami-FC Cincinnati Second Half: Messi Comes On, Barreal Wins It

Gerardo Martino kept Lionel Messi on the bench to start the second half of the Oct. 7, 2023 Inter Miami-FC Cincinnati match, and Alvaro Barreal's 78th-minute goal decided it 1-0. There were no Inter Miami changes at the restart, which Sporting News called a huge call with playoff hopes squarely on the line and one that could speak to how fit Messi truly was; FC Cincinnati made one change, Aaron Boupendza replacing Sergio Santos up front.

The visitors created the better early chances. Luciano Acosta won a free-kick on the edge of the attacking third in the 47th minute and Barreal's floated delivery was cleared after a nervy moment, while Messi began warming up on the touchline. In the 50th minute Drake Callender made a massive double save with FC Cincinnati right on the doorstep, Boupendza most aggrieved after being stymied by Callender's outstretched right foot from directly in front of goal. Jean Mota shot just wide from distance in the 57th. Messi's influence was immediate after he came on: in the 58th minute he earned a dangerous free-kick just outside the area from a Matt Miazga shirt pull — he appeared to be struggling for footing on the field — but put the effort way over the crossbar, looking a bit rusty.

FC Cincinnati kept threatening on the counter. In the 64th minute an Acosta switch across the top of the area found Barreal in space but his hit was blocked, and Pat Noonan made his second change, Yuya Kubo on for Junior Moreno. Brandon Vazquez headed a corner off the post in the 65th — the visitors' turn to strike the woodwork — and in the 67th Callender made another bonkers save when Boupendza crossed to Vazquez clean through on goal; the offside flag went up, but the replay looked incredibly close, so Callender's stop meant VAR had no chance to intervene. Inter Miami made two more changes in the 76th, Josef Martinez curiously withdrawn for Nick Stefanelli and Robert Taylor on for DeAndre Yedlin, with the hosts needing to win to stay alive.

The goal came in the 79th minute: Callender parried Yuya Kubo's effort but drove it right into Barreal's path, and Barreal buried the follow-up low into the far corner. Roman Celentano took a yellow card for time wasting over a goal kick in the 84th, to the home crowd's delight. In the 88th FC Cincinnati brought on Dominique Badji for Acosta — ending his night early despite trailing by a goal in the Golden Boot race — and Malik Pinto for Vazquez, with Acosta and Boupendza jawing at each other as the change was made, an alarming sign with the playoffs on the horizon. In the 89th Messi drew a very contentious foul from Kubo whose tackle from behind looked clean; his floated delivery was headed out by Miazga for a corner, the short set-piece came back to Messi, whose deep delivery was nervously tapped wide by Celentano, and a Messi foul ended the chance with Santiago Arias left rolling on the ground. With five minutes of added time shown, Messi drew another, more obvious foul just outside the area in a central position at 90+3 and clipped the very top of the crossbar — officially the fourth time the woodwork was struck, though less dramatically.
