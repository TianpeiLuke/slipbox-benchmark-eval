# Ray-Ban Meta Smart Glasses: Verdict And Specs

The $299 Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses earned a 7 out of 10 Verge Score in an October 20th, 2023 review by a self-described smart glasses skeptic who had tested several pairs and made a two-part mini-documentary on the category, and who concluded that whatever you think of Meta, these are a turning point for smart glasses. The scorecard listed as good: improved photo, video, and audio quality; sounding good on calls; stylish, with many options for frames and lenses; easy importing of photos and videos; and a much better charging case design. The bad: a voice assistant that is a little slow, cool AI features not yet available, an LED privacy indicator that is still too subtle, and battery life that improved but could be better. They were listed at $299 at Amazon and $299 at Best Buy.

The discriminating features are in the hardware. They officially come in 150 style variations across two frame styles, seven colors, and multiple lens options including color, prescription, and transitions. The camera has been upgraded from 5MP to 12MP; photo resolution has improved to 3024 x 4032 pixels, while videos are now 1080p at 30 frames per second. There are now five mics instead of one, so the wearer sounds better on calls, and speaker quality has also improved — louder, with more bass, less audio leakage, and spatial audio support. Meta stuffed some AI in there, and the glasses can livestream to Facebook or Instagram.

The boundary of the claim is that none of this is revolutionary. The Google Glass Explorer Edition first introduced modern-day smart glasses in 2013, and several other companies big and small have since jumped on the bandwagon, including Snap, Bose, Razer, Epson, Amazon, and the now-defunct Focals by North; most were underwhelming, with potato cameras, washed-out displays, useless voice assistants, and middling mics, and the reviewer had a hard time imagining the average person liking them enough to own a pair. What changed is execution: the updates are significant, and she knows multiple people who have already bought these.
