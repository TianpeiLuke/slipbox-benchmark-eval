# GM And Honda End Their Affordable EV Program

General Motors and long-time partner Honda have ended plans to build millions of affordable and smaller electric vehicles, TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-30. The reasons given are that the automakers are coming to terms with high interest rates and battery costs coupled with softening EV demand.

TechCrunch places the cancellation in a run of similar decisions that week, asking: "Anyone spotting a trend here?"
