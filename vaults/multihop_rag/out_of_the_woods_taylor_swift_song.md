# Out Of The Woods And The Snowmobile Crash Behind It

Taylor Swift confirmed in interviews that Out Of The Woods was based on a true story, and in her Rolling Stone cover story she said the crash it describes actually occurred on a snowmobile, "with an ex who lost control and wrecked it so badly that she saw her life flash before her eyes". The song's "twenty stitches in a hospital room" arrives after a middle 8 in which the couple seem to have made a clean getaway, and then car brakes are slammed.

Musically the track exemplifies one of 1989's strengths — how the music reflects the emotional tenor of the words, here frantic anxiety. As Taylor and her partner run from forces that seek to destroy them, they are pursued by pounding drums and rushing synths, and there is a desperation to her voice, as though she is repeating "everything's fine, everything's fine, everything's fine" against evidence to the contrary.

Swift framed the revelation as a message to the press: "It was almost like this very strange, subtle clue to the media that they don't know everything that happened in that relationship, and I can have something really major and traumatic happen to me and they don't know about it," she told NPR. The disclosure prompted speculation that the track was also about Harry Styles, since the couple had gone skiing with Justin Bieber and Selena Gomez in late 2012; the lyrics also reference their identical aeroplane necklaces and a December break-up, which ties in with the "Sad Taylor on a boat" meme. She has nonetheless steadfastly refused to name names: "The fact that I've never confirmed who those songs are about makes me feel like there is still one card I'm holding," she told GQ.
