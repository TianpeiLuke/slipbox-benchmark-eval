# The Metcalf-Geno Smith Seahawks Showdown Stack

Sporting News' Week 15 2023 Eagles-Seahawks DraftKings Showdown lineup made DK Metcalf ($14,400) the Captain at 1.5x points and 1.5x salary and paired him with Geno Smith ($9,400) in the FLEX, stacking Seattle's QB1 and WR1 from the home side.

The contest constraints were a $50,000 budget with at least one player required from each team. The case for Metcalf was that the Eagles' secondary issues were well-documented and would be made even worse by the absence of CB Darius Slay (knee); Metcalf had four touchdowns in his last two games, including a three-touchdown game against a stout Cowboys defense. The column flagged the risk that Metcalf had been prone to being undisciplined on the field, resulting in an ejection against the 49ers the previous week — always a concern — but took the good with the bad since the ceiling was so high, judging that no matter who started at quarterback the receiver was in line for another dominant showing against one of the league's most generous secondaries. Smith made the lineup because Metcalf would not be throwing the ball to himself and is not one to get opportunities in the running game, and Smith appeared ready to play; in the event he did not, the column would turn to Drew Lock ($9,200) in his place, reasoning that either way the Seattle quarterbacks were set up for success and should be able to light up the skies.
