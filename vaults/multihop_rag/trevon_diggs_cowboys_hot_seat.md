# Trevon Diggs Cowboys Hot Seat

Yardbarker's Nov. 30, 2023 NFC hot-seat roundup named cornerback Trevon Diggs as the Dallas Cowboys' hottest seat, arguing that the heat would be on him in 2024 as a probable replacement for Stephon Gilmore at left cornerback.

The grounds are that in September Diggs suffered a season-ending ACL injury, and his replacement, DaRon Bland, wowed with an NFL-record five pick-sixes. The warrant joining them is roster logic: there is no way Bland can go back to slot corner in 2024, so Dallas will have to find a boundary role for him — which puts pressure on Diggs to justify holding the other outside spot.
