# STEVE Sightings, November 2023

Two photographers recorded STEVE during the November 2023 storm, both describing it as a first or a career-best: Steven Lomas photographed STEVE above Dunstanburgh Castle in Northumberland, UK, and Martin McKenna (Nightskyhunter) posted STEVE photographs from Swatragh.

"It was the strongest display of the aurora that I have seen in 8 years of photography," Lomas told Space.com. "The highlight was seeing the corona and also STEVE!" He also captured a mythical scene with vibrant green and red auroras dancing over Dunstanburgh Castle. McKenna described a spectacular sight over Swatragh in the form of STEVE (proton arc) extending across the entire sky from west to east and flaring near Jupiter, writing on X: "I've been observing aurora since 1998 with 180 displays and this is my first sighting, what a show!"
