# Britney Spears And Justin Timberlake's Relationship

Britney Spears' first highly public relationship was with Justin Timberlake, her co-star from the Mickey Mouse Club. The pair made many appearances together while dating, including the moment — described by The Independent as iconic — when they wore matching all-denim outfits to the American Music Awards in 2001.

They broke up in 2002, shortly after Timberlake and his boy band NSYNC released their hit "Cry Me a River." The song's music video, which featured a woman with blonde hair, sparked rumours that the couple had split because Spears had allegedly cheated on him.
