# ABC's Broadcast Of The Colorado-UCLA Primetime Pac-12 Game

The Oct. 28, 2023 Colorado-UCLA primetime Pac-12 game was televised on ABC with the network's top team of Chris Fowler and Kirk Herbstreit in the booth.

For cord-cutters, live streaming options were the ESPN app or Fubo, which offers a free trial.
