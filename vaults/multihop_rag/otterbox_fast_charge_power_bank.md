# OtterBox Fast Charge Power Bank

The OtterBox Fast Charge Power Bank was $36 for the 15,000mAh capacity model, down from $45 — a $9 saving.

That is the version Engadget tested and recommends in its portable battery guide.

The pack looks sharp and delivers a fast and reliable charge via USB-C or USB-A.
