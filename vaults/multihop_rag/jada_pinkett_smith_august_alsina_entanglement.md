# Jada Pinkett Smith's "Entanglement" With August Alsina

Jada Pinkett Smith confirmed in 2020 that she had had a relationship with the singer August Alsina during a period when she and Will Smith were separated, describing it on her Facebook Watch series Red Table Talk as an "entanglement." Alsina and Pinkett Smith were introduced in 2015, but it was only in 2020 that he claimed publicly that the two had dated while she was married and that the Smiths' marriage was open at the time. Alsina said Will Smith had given the pair his "blessing," explaining: "I actually sat down with Will and had a conversation due to the transformation from their marriage to life partnership that they've spoken on several times and it not involving romanticism." He added that he "totally gave myself to that relationship for years of my life and I truly and really, really deeply loved and have a ton of love for her."

Pinkett Smith's representatives called the claims "absolutely not true" on the day they surfaced. Less than two weeks later, she and Will Smith appeared together on Red Table Talk, where she confirmed the relationship but placed it inside an amicable separation that had not been disclosed to the press at the time: "We decided that we were going to separate for a period of time and you go figure out how to make yourself happy and I'll figure out how to make myself happy. From there, as time went on, I got into a different kind of entanglement with August... Yes, it was a relationship absolutely." Prompted by her husband, she confirmed it "was a relationship, absolutely." She also rejected Alsina's framing of permission, saying Will had not given it because the only person who could give her permission was herself. The episode is one of the grey areas in the couple's account of the terms of their separation.
