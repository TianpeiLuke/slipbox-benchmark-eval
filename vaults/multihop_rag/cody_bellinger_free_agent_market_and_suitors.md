# Cody Bellinger's Market And Suitors After Ohtani Signed

With Ohtani signed to Los Angeles, Cody Bellinger was the top free-agent bat left on the market in December 2023, seeking a deal worth well over $200 million and drawing the Cubs and Blue Jays as reported favorites.

On Wednesday, Dec. 13, the New York Post's Jon Heyman reported that the Blue Jays and Cubs — both previously believed to be among the favorites to land Ohtani — were now believed to be the favorites for Bellinger with the two-way star off to Los Angeles. Signing with the Cubs would be a return to the team with which Bellinger rebuilt his value after a down year with the Dodgers; signing with the Blue Jays would bring Toronto the powerful left-handed bat it had sought when it pursued Ohtani. On Tuesday, Dec. 18, ESPN's Jesse Rogers reported that sources indicated Bellinger's agent, Scott Boras, was seeking a deal worth well over $200 million, with one source saying he was "not budging." Rogers added that some believed the Cubs would not be willing to pay a player that kind of money until he was 40 years old, while a team like the Blue Jays might, given their desperation to round out the roster.
