# Swamp Thing (DC Studios Film)

DC Studios says its Swamp Thing movie "will investigate the dark origins of Swamp Thing," an undated project in the slate Polygon covered on 2023-10-24.

Swamp Thing is a plant-elemental hero who — depending on which comics you are reading — might or might not be a mutated version of scientist Alec Holland. The character dates back to 1971 and was successfully rebooted by Watchmen author Alan Moore in 1984. He has had several previous screen incarnations, including Wes Craven's 1982 movie Swamp Thing, a three-season USA Network Swamp Thing series starting in 1990, and a little-watched, quietly dropped one-season series in 2019.

Polygon situates the project in a broader trend, noting that the recent appearance of Man-Thing in Marvel's black-and-white special Werewolf by Night suggested there can be space for mainstream superhero comics' occasional forays into EC Comics-style "weird horror" territory — but that it will be a while before audiences see what that looks like on the DC side.
