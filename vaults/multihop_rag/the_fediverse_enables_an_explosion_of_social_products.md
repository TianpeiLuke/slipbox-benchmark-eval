# The Fediverse Enables An Explosion Of Social Products

The Verge's central claim for the fediverse is that it will actually enable an explosion of better social products — for lots of reasons, but one in particular: it allows for so many more of them.

The grounds set aside the protocol advocacy: forget the hand-wavy protocol stuff for a second, the argument runs, and the best thing about embracing ActivityPub is structural. It sticks a crowbar into a single Voltron-ic product like Facebook or Twitter or Snapchat and pries it apart into its component pieces. The warrant is what that decomposition does to the opportunity space: each component piece is then ripe for innovation and new ideas, so the number of products that can exist rises rather than the quality of any single one being decreed.
