# EU AI Act Copyright Obligations For GPAI Makers

GPAI model makers must commit to respecting EU copyright rules, including complying with an existing machine readable opt-out from text and data mining contained in the EU Copyright Directive.

The boundary that matters here is the open source carve-out. The Act's transparency requirements are relaxed for open source GPAIs, but that carve-out does not extend to cutting them loose from the copyright obligations: the Commission confirmed that the Copyright Directive will still apply to open source GPAIs.
