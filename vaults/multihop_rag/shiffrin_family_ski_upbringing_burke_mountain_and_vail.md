# The Shiffrin Family's Ski Upbringing: Burke Mountain And Vail

Jeff and Eileen Shiffrin, both avid and passionate skiers from a tender age, introduced their children to the sport from early childhood, moving between Vail and New England as the family's skiing and careers required, as reported by Essentially Sports on 3 November 2023.

The chronology given is precise. In 1986, a year after they first met, the couple married. Five years later, realising the connection they felt to the snowy trails of Aspen, they moved to the world-class skiing city of Vail. A year after moving they welcomed Taylor, Mikaela's brother, in 1992, and Mikaela arrived three years later in 1995. The couple would take little Mikaela and Taylor on family outings at Vail and teach them the art of gliding over the snow, and when Taylor was 9 and Mikaela was 7 their parents enrolled them in Ski Club Vail. According to Aspen Sojourner, SCV's Rika Moore began to coach the kids and the two rapidly progressed: when most coaches would not even let other kids try the slalom gates, Mikaela cleared them with sheer talent. The family later moved to New England due to Jeff's career, and the kids found their safe space at Burke Mountain Academy. The family moved to Vail in 2009, but Mikaela went back to Burke, and her mother decided to move with her, home-schooling her while monitoring her training.

The results and the parenting are given together. Over the years the fire and sheer talent within Mikaela couldn't be tamed — she won the prestigious Topolino races in Italy, sweeping most of the wins, when she was 14 — while her parents tried their best to keep the soaring youngling grounded; her dad's rules for her were "Be nice. Think first. Have fun," which Shiffrin has affixed to the back of her helmet. Taylor also made immense progress and went on to win two NCAA team titles with the University of Denver. In 2011 Eileen became Mikaela's full-time travelling companion and handled her schoolwork, while Jeff handled the logistics and used his paid time off to visit them with Taylor. The passion for the sport that had reignited in Jeff and Eileen's relationship saw its fruition in their history-making daughter.
