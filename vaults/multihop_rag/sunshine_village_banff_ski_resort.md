# Sunshine Village: Banff Snow Quality And A Season To Late May

Sunshine Village is one of three ski resorts in Banff National Park, lying around 20 minutes from the town of Banff, with over 3,000 acres of ski area famed for its snow quality and reliable coverage — reflected in a long season that typically runs from mid-November to late May. It caters almost equally to every level of skier, starting at the greens on the slopes of Mount Standish; intermediates are at home in the areas served by the Continental Divide lift and the Tee Pee Town Express, while the more advanced should make a beeline for Goats Eye Mountain, and the off-piste at Delirium Dive is also noteworthy.

The trade-off is where you sleep. Banff is the place to stay for livelier evenings and decent accessibility, though because of the town's distance from the ski area there is less of an 'apres' culture on the slopes. Inghams offers a package at the newest hotel in Banff, the four-star Moose Hotel & Suites in the downtown area, with comfortable rooms — balcony or patio areas giving great views of the Rockies — and a spa featuring two heated rooftop pools that remain open in winter; you are a short shuttle from Sunshine Village, or a 40-minute journey to neighbouring Lake Louise. From £1,482pp including seven nights' room-only accommodation, return flights from London Heathrow to Calgary and transfers, departing 27 January 2024.
