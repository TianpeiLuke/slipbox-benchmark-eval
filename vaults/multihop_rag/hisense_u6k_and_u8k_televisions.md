# Hisense U6K And U8K Televisions

The Hisense U6K is one of the more affordable TVs, with the 55- and 65-inch models down to $400 and $548 respectively during October Prime Day and still at those prices after the event. Engadget has seen these deals before, but each matches an all-time low.

The U6K has received generally positive reviews around the web, as it is one of the few budget-level sets with quantum dots, full-array local dimming and a mini-LED backlight — all of which should help it deliver better contrast and color volume than most TVs in its price range. It is not ideal for gaming, being limited to a 60Hz refresh rate and lacking HDMI 2.1 ports, and in general it cannot match the brightness or viewing angles of a more expensive set, but it looks to be a good buy on a tighter budget.

For a well-reviewed midrange model, the Hisense U8K provides much better contrast and peak brightness, plus more gaming-friendly features like the ability to play 4K content at up to a 144Hz refresh rate. That one was down to $748 for a 55-inch model and $998 for a 65-inch set.
