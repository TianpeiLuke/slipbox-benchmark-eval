# Tesla Cybertruck Production Outlook

Tesla gave updates about its long-delayed Cybertruck alongside its Q3 2023 earnings: initial deliveries are set for an event at Giga Texas on November 30, and Elon Musk noted that scaling the Cybertruck will be hard and that it will take 18 months before the pickup is profitable. "I mean, we dug our own grave with Cybertruck," said Musk.

Musk also said Giga Texas will be able to produce about 250,000 Cybertrucks a year starting in 2025. TechCrunch attaches a caveat to that projection on 2023-10-22: Musk isn't great at making predictions — he initially said the Cybertruck would be on the market by 2021 — so some of these numbers should be expected to be pushed out too.
