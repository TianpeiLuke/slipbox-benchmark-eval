# Waller: The Amanda Waller Spinoff Series

Waller is the previously untitled Peacemaker spinoff centered on Viola Davis as no-nonsense supervillain resource broker Amanda Waller, reported by Polygon on 2023-10-24 as one of the projects James Gunn is concentrating on.

Its scheduling comes at Peacemaker's expense. Gunn's hilarious, heartfelt Suicide Squad spinoff series Peacemaker was greenlit for a second season in February 2022, but Gunn has since said he will have to put that project on hold for a bit to concentrate on other things, Waller among them.

The character has a long history across media. Amanda Waller was introduced to DC's movie continuity in David Ayer's little-loved 2016 version of Suicide Squad, though she has been part of the comics continuity since 1986, and she is one of the few elements of Ayer's movie held over in Gunn's 2021 reboot/reshuffle movie, The Suicide Squad. She also turned up for a quick cameo in Black Adam. The new show "features Team Peacemaker," so Polygon suggests it may feel like a stealth season 2, depending on who from the team winds up on the series.
