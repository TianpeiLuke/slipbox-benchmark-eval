# Argentina's First-Half Dominance Against Paraguay

Argentina led Paraguay 1-0 at halftime on Oct. 12, 2023 despite completely and utterly dominating the first 45 minutes, with Nicolas Otamendi's fourth-minute goal somehow remaining the only successful strike — the hosts pummelled Carlos Coronel's net, and while the Red Bulls goalkeeper made a few saves, it was mostly Argentina's wastefulness that stopped them following up the early opener.

Opta's numbers capture the imbalance: in the first half Argentina beat Paraguay in possession (74% to 26%), total shots (10 to 2), shots on target (4 to 0), touches in the opposition box (17 to 1) and completed passes (354 to 101). Paraguay got their first attacking move in the ninth minute by playing long to the striker, but after a few nervy moments the Argentina defence recovered, and by the 41st minute Paraguay were extremely frustrated with their lack of attacking possession — reaching the final third for the first time in ages only for Antonio Bareiro to blast a hopeless effort from a long way out, blocked by a defender who was all over the lone striker.

The wasted chances came in sequence. In the 13th minute Nico Gonzalez broke down the left with plenty of room and produced a shot so bad it was hard to tell if he was trying to hit the target or square it, with nobody near the far post. In the 16th Lautaro Martinez was a split-second late onto a vertical flick from Enzo Fernandez and slid into Coronel's ribs — painful for the goalkeeper, who continued, and surprising that Lautaro escaped a booking. Junior Alonso received the game's first yellow card in the 21st for flying into Alexis Mac Allister and catching him high on the ankle, a very bad tackle not quite worthy of a red. In the 25th Julian Alvarez had a shot saved from a very tight angle after Lautaro threaded him in, and the resulting corner was looped off target by Cristian Romero's head. In the 30th Lautaro had a vicious shot from the penalty spot, after a cutback from Alvarez, clipped by a defender and looped inches over the crossbar, with Argentina pummelling the Paraguay back line with silky move after silky move and their one-touch passing through midfield essentially unplayable, winning the ball back within five seconds whenever they lost it.

The closing stretch of the half brought three more. In the 34th Alvaro Campuzano was whistled for hip-checking Nicolas Tagliafico to the ground — sending both Campuzano and Paraguay manager Daniel Garnero into a frenzy, though the Sporting News called it the right call for overly aggressive body positioning — and the free kick was wasted. In the 36th the referee dramatically waved away a penalty shout after Mac Allister dribbled through multiple defenders at the top of the box and was bumped to the ground, with replay showing a clear grievance and VAR declining to intervene. In the 38th Coronel reacted quickly to stick out his left foot and save a leaping Nico Gonzalez header at the far post from a tight angle. In the 42nd Rodrigo de Paul cut onto his left, brought it back onto his right and unleashed a curler that danced past a diving Coronel and struck the inside of the post before careening along the face of goal and away. And in the 45th, with Enzo Fernandez in acres of space at the top of the box, he squared left to Gonzalez, who from a tight angle dragged his shot wide. Lionel Scaloni would be pleased with the performance but the match hung in the balance on the scoresheet, and Paraguay needed vast improvement after being meticulously carved to pieces.
