# Vermont Sportsbook Bonuses: The Pros And Cons Side By Side

Sporting News' summary judgment on Vermont sportsbook bonuses, published 2023-09-26, sets three advantages directly against three disadvantages. On the pro side: thousands of dollars in value will be up for grabs; bonuses can be claimed easily via links or promo codes; and bonuses can be used to bet on almost any sport.

On the con side, each of those advantages is qualified. Refunds are site credit or bonus bets, not cash. Playthrough requirements are attached to site credit and bonus bets. And you won't earn back the stake on winning bonus bets or bets with site credit.
