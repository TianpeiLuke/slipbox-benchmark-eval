# Fetch My Blade

Fetch My Blade, a solo journaling role-playing game created by Kelly Tran and Ethan Yen, is recommended in Polygon's 2023 roundup on the grounds that you can play it whenever you want, without scheduling nightmares or unfeasible time commitments.

You role-play as the friendly or fierce dog of a retired legendary sword master. Character creation means choosing one of six dog breeds — shih tzu, corgi, mastiff, border collie, husky, or chihuahua — or selecting your own, then determining three truths about your master and aiding them when a mysterious stranger challenges them to a duel; it falls to you to go on a perilous quest to retrieve your master's fabled weapon in time for that final duel. Throughout the quest you use three six-sided dice to resolve mechanics for racing against time, overcoming treacherous encounters, and uncovering new truths about your master's shrouded past. The write-up notes it is a 17-page game.
