# The Manchester Derby's 191st Edition In Context

The Manchester derby of Sunday 29 October 2023 was the 191st edition of the fixture, a local squabble that would attract a global audience of millions, and André Onana was a derby debutant against City despite recent experience of facing Pep Guardiola's side.

Onana's last game for Internazionale had been the 1-0 defeat by Manchester City in Istanbul, in which United's crosstown rivals claimed a first Champions League and so clinched the treble. United went into the derby in form, having claimed victories over Sheffield United, Brentford and FC Copenhagen in their last three matches, but Ten Hag's "depends on the opposition" and "how good is the team around him" comments sounded like hints: in contrast to Inter's Simone Inzaghi, the United manager might harbour doubts about deploying the 27-year-old Onana high up the pitch against City.

The previous season's derby at Old Trafford ended in a 2-1 win for United, with Bruno Fernandes's equaliser controversial because Marcus Rashford was offside — though not so controversial that the goal was chalked off, since he had not touched the ball. So far that term the captain had scored two goals in 12 United appearances, and Ten Hag had no doubt he could be a major factor in the showdown. As The Guardian put it, at 3.30pm on Sunday they would find out just how focused United's players were.
