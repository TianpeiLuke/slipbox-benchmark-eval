# Ohtani Free Agency: Market Expectations And Decision Timeline

By Dec. 3, 2023, the reported market for Shohei Ohtani had produced several offers topping $500 million and an expectation that he would decide on a team within about a week, with the winter meetings of Dec. 3-6 seen as the likely window for a signing.

Sporting News' rumors tracker recorded that all eyes had been on Ohtani since the start of the offseason and that, for the most part, there had not been much to see; the two-way phenom was described as the most sought-after free agent in MLB history, his combination of talent and marketability making him a franchise-changing player. Multiple reports indicated Ohtani could sign at some point during the winter meetings. On Dec. 3, the New York Post's Jon Heyman reported Ohtani had already received several deals that topped $500 million, which could mean he wound up being the first $600 million player in MLB history, and added that five teams were believed to be in the running. Also on Dec. 3, MLB Network's Jon Morosi reported, citing a source, that the superstar was "likely to decide on a team within the next week" as the winter meetings were set to begin in Nashville. Jeff Passan additionally reported Ohtani was expecting a contract somewhere between $550 and $600 million, which would be the largest guaranteed contract in North American sports.
