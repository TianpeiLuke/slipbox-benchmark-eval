# Kevin Durant Most Disrespected Superstar Claim

Essentially Sports argued on Dec. 1, 2023 that Kevin Durant stands as perhaps the most disrespected icon of the sport, on the grounds that his acknowledged talent and accolades are out of proportion to how he is regarded.

The grounds start with where he sits in the league's history. The NBA is home to the world's best basketball players and teams, and throughout its history iconic figures like Magic Johnson, Larry Bird, Kareem Abdul-Jabbar and Michael Jordan have left an indelible mark; but for the better part of the past two decades, players like Stephen Curry, LeBron James and Kevin Durant have dominated the NBA landscape. While LeBron James and Stephen Curry may have accumulated more championships and accolades, Durant is universally acknowledged as the best pure talent of his generation. The dispute the argument answers is that Durant divides opinions: some view him as the most skilled basketball player of his generation, others as a diva who rode the coattails of Stephen Curry to secure his two NBA championships. The warrant is that a careful consideration of Durant's game-changing impact and his multitude of accolades reveals a glaring gap between what he has done and how he is treated.
