# Digital Twin Market Size and Adoption 2023

IoT Analytics projects that the global digital twin market could grow at a compound annual growth rate of approximately 30% between 2023 and 2027, according to its Digital Twin Market Report 2023-2027 as summarised by IoT Business News on 18 November 2023.

Adoption data underpins that projection. 29% of global manufacturing companies have either fully or partially implemented their digital twin strategies, a noticeable increase from 20% in 2020, and the proportion of companies not contemplating implementation appears to have fallen to 9% in 2023, down from 33.6% in 2020. Supporting the trend, an analysis of job postings on SimplyHired suggests growing demand for digital twin expertise: postings mentioning "digital twin" increased by 11% in October 2023 compared with just two years prior, while openings for most other tech topics declined over the same timeframe. Among the 60 tech-related skill sets IoT Analytics tracked, digital twin-related skills experienced one of the most significant growths.

Against this backdrop the report notes eight notable trends helping to advance and promote digital twins, of which the article discusses four in detail: digital twins deployed to meet sustainability goals; digital twins employed as virtual sensors in complex conditions; partnerships between cloud hyperscalers and OT and simulation specialists; and initiatives promoting interoperability of digital twins across multifaceted systems. These trends are described as shaping the future of the market and influencing investment priorities for companies across various industries.
