# Street Fighter 6

Street Fighter 6, developed by Capcom for PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, Windows PC, and Xbox Series X, is ranked 7 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31. After getting knocked down — by a self-inflicted punch, no less — with the feeble Street Fighter 5, Capcom has hit the gym and returned stronger than before; Polygon's review called this the "ultimate fighting game toolbox," and from a robust single-player mode to solid online modes to a cast of memorable new and returning World Warriors, it is Capcom at its most confident.

For newcomers and lapsed fans wary of jumping into online play, Capcom delivered World Tour mode, a robust single-player RPG-lite beat-'em-up in the vein of Sega's Yakuza games: players hit the streets of Metro City where, hilariously, everyone in town not only knows how to fight but relishes impromptu fisticuffs with strangers as a matter of local pride. The streets are part training grounds, part beginning of a silly, epic worldwide adventure where you learn the basics, and untethered from any serious narrative canon the mode lets players of all skill levels have fun.

The approachability extends to an innovative new control scheme called Modern Controls. The six-button layout from the very first Street Fighter is still available, but after multiple attempts at giving players a simplified control scheme, critic Michael McWhertor writes, Capcom has finally cracked it: Modern Controls are comparatively easy to grasp and pretty viable competitively.

What gives the game its longevity is the finely honed one-on-one fighting mechanics, governed by a streamlined set of meters and flashy new moves. Thanks to a well-populated social space called the Battle Hub and a rock-solid online infrastructure there is a robust community to battle daily, and with a compelling roster led by classics like Ryu, Zangief, and Chun-Li plus dazzling newcomers Marisa, Manon, and Kimberly, McWhertor calls it one of the best games of 2023 regardless of genre.
