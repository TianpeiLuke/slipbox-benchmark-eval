# Camden Yards: Baltimore Orioles Postseason Atmosphere

Oriole Park at Camden Yards ranked No. 3 among the eight remaining 2023 playoff teams' home-field environments in The New York Times' 8 October 2023 ranking, on the principle that the park's quality tracks the team's. It opened in 1992, has a capacity of 44,970 and drew an average attendance of 23,911 in 2023.

Its memorable postseason moment is Delmon Young's three-run double in the eighth inning of Game 2 of the 2014 ALDS, completing the Orioles' comeback from three runs down in the eighth to beat the Tigers; Zack Britton pitched a perfect ninth, and the Orioles went on to beat the Tigers in Game 3 in Detroit to sweep the series before losing to the Royals in the ALCS.

The conditional judgement is explicit. When the Orioles are bad, their ballpark is bad — beautiful and unmistakable, but bad because of the empty seats and because it is basically a second home ballpark for every East Coast team that takes the train into Baltimore and overwhelms the place with their own variation of the "Let's go, Yankees" chant; it is a ballpark that can be, and has been, taken over by opposing fans. When the Orioles are good, Camden Yards is one of the best ballparks in the country, starting pregame with every rendition of the national anthem that includes the entire ballpark screaming "O!" in unison, so loud that it drowns out the word "say." The Orioles were awfully good that year — second-most wins in baseball, with home attendance up more than half a million from the previous season — so the expectation was that the park would follow their lead: a young roster might be at risk of being shell-shocked on the road, but at home this team is going to be treated as heroes from the start. Kevin Brown's call as the Orioles clinched the American League East and the No. 1 seed in the American League — "Go crazy, Baltimore. You are the champions of the American League East." — was posted by Awful Announcing on September 29, 2023. The parting caveat: let's just hope that, when they go on the road, manager Brandon Hyde doesn't forget to use his best reliever in a must-win game.
