# Going Infinite: Michael Lewis On Sam Bankman-Fried

Michael Lewis's October 2023 book is an account of Sam Bankman-Fried, published by Allen Lane at $55 on 3 October, The Sydney Morning Herald listed on 1 October 2023.

Its subject is defined by the arc: Bankman-Fried set up his cryptocurrency exchange, FTX, in 2019 and three years later was rolling in money, but when the crypto world went pear-shaped his business hit an irreversible tailspin, and he now faces multiple charges and was due in court on 3 October.

The reviewer's warrant for reading it is the author: "If anyone can be relied on to tell this story of a financial Icarus with verve and narrative flair, it's Michael Lewis, author of Liar's Poker, Moneyball, and The Big Short." A caveat is attached — "No doubt the updated edition will have the verdict on SBF."
