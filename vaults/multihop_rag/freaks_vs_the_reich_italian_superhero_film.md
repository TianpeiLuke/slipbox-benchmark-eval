# Freaks Vs. The Reich

Freaks vs. the Reich became available to stream on Prime Video in early November 2023. Polygon classes it as a superhero and circus war movie, running 2 hours 21 minutes, directed by Gabriele Mainetti and starring Claudio Santamaria, Aurora Giovinazzo, and Pietro Castellitto.

The offbeat Italian film follows a group of circus performers in World War II who are sought after by the Nazis and team up to stop them. The Polygon writer had not yet seen it but reported hearing it is funny, sweet, and has strong action, putting it on his weekend watchlist.
