# Boxing Marination And Upset Risk

CBSSports.com argued on Dec. 23, 2023 that the main problem with marination in boxing over the past few decades is that placing big-name fighters into bouts they could lose has proven to be a major mistake because of upsets — and that was the case for both Joshua and Wilder, despite each being sizable betting favorites.

The warrant that limits the danger in this specific instance is who holds the belts and who is paying: neither Joshua nor Wilder were champions, so it was possible the fights against each other would still take place regardless of defeat, simply due to the sizable investment from Saudi Arabia. In other words, the usual cost of an upset — the superfight evaporating — is blunted here because the money, not a title, is what binds the matchup.
