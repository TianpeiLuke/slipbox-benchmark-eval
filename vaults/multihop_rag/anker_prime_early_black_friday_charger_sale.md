# Anker's Early Black Friday Charger Sale

Anker is kicking off its Black Friday sale early with discounts and a buy-one-get-another-50-percent-off promotion, and buyers must use the listed code to get the discounted price for each product.

WIRED generally likes most Anker gear, and its standouts in this sale are the Anker Prime power bank and the Anker Nano Power Bank; Anker's tiny 511 30-watt charger is also excellent.
