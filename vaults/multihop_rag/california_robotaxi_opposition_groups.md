# California Robotaxi Opposition Groups

Earning back public trust may be a hefty challenge for Cruise, especially in California. As the Cruise drama unfolded in late October 2023, opposition against robotaxis grew in cities like Los Angeles.

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-30 that two of the biggest groups to oppose robotaxi expansion in California are now formally working together.
