# Apple iPad Ninth Generation Deal Listings

The ninth-generation iPad appears in three 2023 deal roundups: The Verge's kids gift guide at $249+ (Best Buy, Walmart, Target), and both of WIRED's Prime Day lists, where it is the best iPad for most people (8/10, WIRED Recommends).

The Verge notes the 2021 iPad isn't just the final holdout in Apple's tablet lineup to have a 3.5mm headphone jack — it's also a great device despite its age, with a sharp 10.2-inch screen, a 12MP camera, and the ability to smoothly run all kinds of apps, unlike cheaper tablets often relegated to Netflix and little else. WIRED's general roundup calls it its favorite iPad for most people even though it's one generation behind: aside from costing less than the current 10th-gen model, it remains compatible with the same accessories as the first-gen Apple Pencil and retains the physical Home button with Touch ID at the bottom of the screen. WIRED's Apple roundup adds that it is affordable even without the discount and packs a bunch of great features: a 12-megapixel rear camera with Center Stage support to keep you in frame during video calls, 64 GB of storage on the base model, a True Tone display that adjusts color temperature to the ambient lighting, and a physical Touch ID-enabled home button; being the same shape and size as its predecessors, all current accessories work with it, including the first-generation Apple Pencil and Apple's Smart Keyboard. For cellular connectivity, the 4G LTE model is also on sale at Amazon for $379 ($21 off).
