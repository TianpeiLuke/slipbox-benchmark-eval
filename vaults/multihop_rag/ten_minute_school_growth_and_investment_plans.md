# 10 Minute School's Post-Round Investment Plans

Having raised its pre-Series A, 10MS plans to invest in its tech capabilities, content and operations, according to co-founder and COO Mirza Salman Hossain Beg. He said 10MS's goal is to provide personalized learning experiences for students by using AI, and that the company will bring in more engineers and product managers to support that tech development.

A second major area of investment is content matched to Bangladesh's evolving K12 curriculum. "The government in Bangladesh right now is going through a lot of massive changes in the national curriculum, and as a result of that, there will be a lot of new content to be created to support the new curriculum," Beg says. Beyond the app itself, 10MS is also exploring a hybrid online/offline model with centers for after school education, and is creating a new vertical for its English material by building a separate sales team.
