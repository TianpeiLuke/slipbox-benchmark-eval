# Fourth Quarter Sequence Of Packers-Chiefs Week 13

The fourth quarter of the Week 13 Packers-Chiefs game ended at 11:28 p.m. with "Game. Set. Match." — Mahomes' Hail Mary falling harmlessly to the turf and giving the Packers a much-needed win, per Sporting News' live log of Dec. 3, 2023.

The quarter's scoring and turning points ran as follows. At 10:28 p.m. Mahomes found Noah Gray with a peel route from 2 yards out, Gray wide open in the end zone, bringing the Packers' lead back down to two at 21-19. Green Bay's offense then stalled: at 10:39 p.m. the Packers failed to conjure up any magic for the first time in ages and the Chiefs got the ball back with a chance to take the lead, and at 10:43 p.m. the ball was punted following a three-and-out for the first time that day after the Green Bay defense held firm. Green Bay found rhythm again — an inch-perfect throw from Love to Romeo Doubs at 10:46 p.m. that gained much more than the 16 yards needed for the first down, and Malik Heath dropping a defender after fielding a curl at 10:50 p.m. — before Mike Danna sacked Love on third down at 10:52 p.m., forcing the field goal team out. Anders Carlson sank a 40-yard attempt at 10:53 p.m. for 24-19.

At 10:59 p.m. Mahomes tossed a pass toward Packers corner Keisean Nixon, who pulled down an over-the-shoulder interception, the noted return man breaking Missourians' hearts just as the Chiefs looked on the brink of another lengthy drive. At 11:11 p.m. Watson went down with yet another injury, one that looked like a hamstring niggle, an unfortunate end for the second-year wideout who was enjoying one of the best games of his young career. At 11:12 p.m. Love took a sack 10 yards behind the line of scrimmage, setting up a difficult 48-yard fourth-down attempt, and at 11:14 p.m. Carlson drilled it right down the middle to grow the lead back to eight at 27-19. At 11:22 p.m. there was a whole lot of laundry left on the field: Rashee Rice looked to have fumbled while scampering downfield but was ruled down by contact, at which point Pacheco threw a punch at Nixon, prompting an ejection.
