# Tom Cole's Nomination Case For Jim Jordan

In nominating Jim Jordan, veteran Republican Rep. Tom Cole of Oklahoma argued that it was time to end the upheaval he had warned against when Kevin McCarthy was suddenly ousted: "We have a chance today to end that chaos, end that uncertainty," Cole said.

The warrant connecting that goal to Jordan specifically was a claim about his temperament and capacity to lead: Cole said Jordan was not a "shrinking violet" but someone who could lead the House.
