# Britney Spears' Confinement In A Solitary Rehab Facility

The passage The Guardian singles out on 24 October 2023 as the most upsetting in *The Woman in Me* is Spears' account of being locked up in a rehabilitation facility, put on lithium and subjected to relentless testing, where she "began to feel like I was being ritually tortured".

While confined she could not see her children or her dog, could not bathe or dress in private or shut the door to her room, and had set bed and waking times, alongside mandatory therapy. For blood tests, "the tech drawing my blood would be flanked by the nurse, a security guard and my assistant. Was I a cannibal? Was I a bank robber? Was I a wild animal? Why was I treated as though I were about to burn the place down and murder them all?" She wondered whether her family was trying to kill her, and, made slow and sclerotic, felt estranged from her own body: "If the idea of my being in that place was to heal, that was not the effect."

She writes that she is not scared of anything after that experience, "but it doesn't make me feel strong; it makes me sad. I shouldn't be this strong."
