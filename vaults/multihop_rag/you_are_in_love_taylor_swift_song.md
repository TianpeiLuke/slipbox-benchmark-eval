# You Are In Love: Written From Watching Jack Antonoff And Lena Dunham

You Are In Love is a gently-sung romance inspired by Taylor Swift observing her producer Jack Antonoff and his then-girlfriend, Lena Dunham. The song's defining quality is that it describes love from the outside rather than from her own experience. "I remember thinking [the backing track] sounded so much like the sound of actual love, true love. Like, through thick and thin, sickness and health love," she told MTV.

Drawing directly on conversations she'd had with Lena, the song blends scenes of everyday life — burnt toast, borrowed shirts — with moments of incandescent tenderness such as kisses on sidewalks and late night heart-to-hearts. In an interview with Elle, Swift talked wistfully about the couple's ease around each other: "I've never had that. [But] now I have more of a grasp on the fact that when you're in a state of infatuation and you think everything that person does is perfect, it then - if you're lucky - morphs into a real relationship when you see that that person is not in fact perfect, but you still want to see them every day."
