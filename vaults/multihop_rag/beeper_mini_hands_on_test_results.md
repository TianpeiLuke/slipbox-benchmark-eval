# Beeper Mini Hands-On Test Results

In TechCrunch's tests Beeper Mini worked as described: it sent iMessage texts from an older Android phone on Google Fi's wireless network, using its own phone number not associated with an Apple ID, to an iPhone 15 Pro Max with a different phone number that is associated with an Apple ID.

Full-res photos and other features like tapbacks and typing indicators also worked. One failure mode showed up: when the Android phone's battery died, the texts reverted to green bubbles and did not make it to Beeper's app — they went to Google Messages instead.
