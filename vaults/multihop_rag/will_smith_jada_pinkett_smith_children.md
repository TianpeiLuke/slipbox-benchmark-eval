# The Smith Children: Jaden, Willow And Trey

Will Smith and Jada Pinkett Smith, married since 1997, share two children together — son Jaden, 25, and daughter Willow, 22 — while Will Smith also has a third child, son Trey, 30, from his first marriage to Sheree Zampino. The distinction matters for how the family is described: Jaden and Willow are the couple's own children, whereas Trey belongs to the earlier marriage and is Will's alone. Jaden, the couple's first child together, was born in July 1998; Willow was born in 2000.

Pinkett Smith credits both of her children with her own development, calling them "little gurus" who have taught her "a deep sense of self-acceptance." She made the remark in the October 2023 interviews surrounding her memoir, in which she also disclosed the couple's long separation. Speaking about that period, she insisted the marriage has never been an open one, saying that she and Will had "eliminated the chance of betrayal" while going on to live their "separate lives."
