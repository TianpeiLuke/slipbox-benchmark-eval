# England's 2023 World Cup Title Defence All But Dead

England's hopes of defending their World Cup crown were in tatters barely halfway through the 2023 tournament, with the 2019 champions sitting second-last of ten on the table on Oct. 26, 2023 and only mathematically able to scrape through to the semi-finals.

The trigger was a fourth abject loss in barely three weeks: an eight-wicket defeat to Sri Lanka in Bengaluru with nearly 25 overs to spare, after England were bowled out for a miserable 156. To reach the knockout stage they needed not only to turn their horrendous form around and win all four of their remaining matches, including an upcoming date with table-toppers and tournament favourites India, but also to hope for numerous other results, including Australia losing at least two and probably three of its own four games.

The Roar noted a hard deadline on that arithmetic: England's tournament might only stay mathematically alive until Sunday evening (AEST), when they faced the daunting challenge of tournament hosts India in Lucknow, with defeat certain to end their semi-final chances for good.
