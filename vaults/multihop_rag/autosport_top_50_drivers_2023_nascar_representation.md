# Autosport Top 50 Drivers 2023 NASCAR Representation

Autosport's annual Top 50 drivers list is a ranking of the 50 standout performers in motorsports for the year, a tradition the renowned motorsports publishing brand has continued since 2002, whose announcement every year creates some suspense within the racing community — and in 2023, as Essentially Sports reported on Dec. 21, it included only three NASCAR drivers.

William Byron led the list of NASCAR drivers with a 12th-place ranking, with Kyle Larson and 2023 Cup Series champion Ryan Blaney joining him. The scarcity of NASCAR drivers in the rankings raised some eyebrows, since the year produced numerous cases of NASCAR drivers standing out with their consistency and skills behind the steering wheel, which is what prompted the publication's review of the major exclusions. The boundary condition the piece itself acknowledges: motorsports is a vast field with elite competitions taking place the world over, so some fan favorites are bound to be left out.
