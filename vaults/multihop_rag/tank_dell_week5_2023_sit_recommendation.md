# Tank Dell As A Week 5 2023 Sit At Atlanta

CBS Sports listed Tank Dell under "Sit Him" for Week 5 of the 2023 season at Atlanta: the claim is that Dell's upside for one huge catch keeps him in play as a middle-of-the-pack flex but that other receivers offer safer floors and nearly as much upside, and the warrant is that defenses have started deliberately bracketing him deep, removing the single play his fantasy value depends on.

The grounds are the previous week's coverage plan and Atlanta's ability to copy it. Houston didn't need Dell in Week 4 — the Texans built a nice lead thanks to their defense and held on without having to throw much. On film the Steelers really emphasised not getting beaten deep by Dell, often using a safety over the top to his side of the field to deter C.J. Stroud from even thinking about throwing at him; it worked for Pittsburgh because Dell had zero deep targets, and it worked for Houston because Nico Collins beat soft coverage repeatedly, including on a 24-yard deep ball while Dell drew triple coverage and on his long touchdown, where Dell pulled a safety over to his side. The Falcons should be able to deploy the same type of coverage with a safety cheating toward Dell, and could also stick outside cornerback A.J. Terrell on Collins to make things harder on Houston; Terrell shadowed a receiver for the first time this season in Week 4, contributing to Calvin Ridley's modest day, and that would ultimately mean multiple bodies on Dell.

The scope conditions are spelled out. If the game is low-scoring, or a one-sided win for Houston — which the column notes they had just done the previous week — Dell's ceiling drops further. The breakdown lists Houston at Atlanta, the Falcons favoured by 2, an over/under of 41.5, an opponent ranked 8th versus wide receivers, 11 projected points, a 27th receiver ranking, and year-to-date totals of 16 receptions on 24 targets for 267 yards, two touchdowns and 14 fantasy points per game. The verdict prefers Romeo Doubs, Christian Watson and Adam Thielen to Dell.
