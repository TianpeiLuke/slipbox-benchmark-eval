# Britney Spears On Music Making From Baby One More Time To Blackout

Per The Guardian's 24 October 2023 account of the memoir, Spears says the period around Baby, One More Time was "probably the moment in my life when I had the most passion for music", and the only album she discusses in any detail afterwards is 2007's Blackout.

Early on she loved making music: she told producer Max Martin she wanted to sound more R&B than straight pop, listened to Tainted Love the night before recording Baby, One More Time and stayed up late so she would sound as gravelly and "fried" as Marc Almond. She was a studio perfectionist who would stay in the booth for hours, and the school setting and uniforms of the Baby video were her idea. "I was unknown and I had nothing to lose if I messed up," she writes.

After that, her music barely features in the book — there is a nod to her breaking records, and the era-defining Toxic is dispensed with in a sentence. Blackout, the cult favourite made at the height of her public struggles yet full of potential, is the exception: the studio was a refuge from the paparazzi hounding her outside, and while the messy DIY video for the single Gimme More may be "by far the worst video I've ever shot in my life", its scrappiness meant "more interesting people started noticing and wanting to work with me". There is some later love for 2016's Glory, and she writes proudly about her 2022 collaboration with Elton John, but says she has no plans to make music at the moment.
