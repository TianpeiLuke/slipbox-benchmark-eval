# Dolphins-Eagles Pregame: Kelly Green Day, AFC East Stakes, And A Tua-Hurts Reunion

The Sporting News pregame log for the Oct. 22, 2023 Dolphins-Eagles "Sunday Night Football" game led with Philadelphia's Kelly Green throwbacks: at 7:29 p.m. it flagged that the Eagles would be back in Kelly Green for what promised to be a fascinating uniform matchup, and at 8:03 p.m. it noted Lincoln Financial Field had a new look for Kelly Green day, including a new logo and wordmark for the stadium.

The blog also framed the standings stakes and the quarterback subplot. At 8:10 p.m. it noted the Dolphins could open a two-game AFC East lead on the Bills with a win, Buffalo having been upset by the Patriots earlier that day. At 8:18 p.m. it pointed out that Tua Tagovailoa and Jalen Hurts were once teammates and competitors at Alabama, and that both had something to prove given Hurts had not looked like himself for much of the season.

Other notes covered personnel and conditions. At 7:55 p.m. the blog remarked that Julio Jones was back in the NFL, with the Eagles. At 7:41 p.m. it noted Tyreek Hill had a chance to break 1,000 yards that night — he would need 186, which seemed at least within reach given his season. At 7:20 p.m. it reported 55 degrees and dropping in Philadelphia, not unusual for the Eagles, and asked whether Tagovailoa could rise to the occasion in cooler weather.
