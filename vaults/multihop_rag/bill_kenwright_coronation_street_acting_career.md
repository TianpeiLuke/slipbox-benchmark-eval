# Kenwright's Coronation Street Role As Gordon Clegg

The Liverpudlian Bill Kenwright, besides being one of the UK's most successful theatre producers, played Gordon Clegg in Coronation Street between 1968 and 2012. He started his career as an actor and landed his breakthrough role in the ITV soap opera in 1968 as Gordon, the son of long-serving Rovers Return barmaid Betty.

He left Weatherfield the following year, but appeared back on screen with occasional visits over the subsequent decades — which is how a single soap role spans more than four decades in his credits.
