# Nico Collins As A Week 6 2023 PPR Flex Starter Against New Orleans

CBS Sports listed Nico Collins as a "Flex Starter In PPR" for Week 6 of the 2023 season against New Orleans: the claim is that neither of his matchups is particularly favourable but he could see a lot of short targets, which alone makes him an acceptable PPR flex, and the warrant is that the Saints are unlikely to abandon aggressive man-to-man coverage even though C.J. Stroud's splits say they should.

The coverage grounds carry the case. New Orleans has played the fifth-fewest snaps of zone coverage this season and has ignored the tendencies some quarterbacks have shown early in the year — it's just what they like to do. Stud cornerback Marshon Lattimore has not followed receivers much, playing 87% of his snaps at right cornerback, to the left of the quarterback, while the other primary outside corner, Paulson Adebo, has allowed a 50% catch rate with minimal yards after catch. That should suit Collins on the margins: he sees a higher target-per-route-run rate against man coverage, 25% versus 22.2% against zone, with a higher catch rate (87.5% against man versus 65.4% against zone) and a higher receiving average (23.3 versus 17.8) — though he has run nearly four times as many routes against zone as against man. Stroud has been outstanding against man all year, completing 67.6% for 11.1 yards per attempt versus 7.6 against zone, but is nowhere near as impressive when pressured, completing 48.1% for a weak 4.3 yards per attempt.

The cautions are that Collins' big games came against soft opponents and that his last game produced nothing. Both of his really big games came in Week 2 against Indianapolis and Week 4 against Pittsburgh, teams that rank bottom-10 in fantasy points allowed to wide receivers, while the Saints are in the top half. In Week 5, even in a game where Houston lost Tank Dell to a concussion before halftime, Collins couldn't get much going, seeing one target in the second half and failing to catch it; he definitely wasn't shadowed and was almost never double-teamed, though a safety was in proximity on a couple of routes, and the column suggests it had more to do with Houston attacking the middle of the field against Atlanta's zone coverage. The breakdown lists Houston at home versus New Orleans, the Saints favoured by 1.5, an over/under of 42.5, an opponent ranked 14th versus wide receivers, 12.8 projected points, a 23rd receiver ranking, and year-to-date totals of 25 receptions on 36 targets for 467 yards, three touchdowns and 17.9 fantasy points per game. The verdict places Collins as a PPR flex ahead of Tyler Lockett and Chris Godwin but behind Jakobi Meyers, Jaylen Waddle and Gabe Davis.
