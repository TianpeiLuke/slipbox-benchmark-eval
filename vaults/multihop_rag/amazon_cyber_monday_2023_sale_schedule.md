# Amazon's Cyber Monday 2023 Sale Schedule

Amazon dragged the year's biggest shopping holidays into 11 days of deals in 2023, beginning its Black Friday sale in the early morning of Friday 17 November — a week ahead of schedule — and switching to Cyber Monday language in the wee hours of Saturday 25 November, Mashable reported in a post updated 27 November 2023 at 5:00 a.m. EST.

Official Cyber Monday mode was running through Monday 27 November and included both a ton of deals carried over from Black Friday plus some new ones.

Mashable said it was curating a running list of Amazon's best Cyber Weekend deals, spotlighting favourites and noting when good deals inevitably sell out, and would be updating the story incessantly as the sale continued, alongside an even bigger list of Cyber Monday deals across more retailers.
