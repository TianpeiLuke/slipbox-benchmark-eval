# NIO November 2023 Vehicle Deliveries

NIO delivered 15,959 vehicles in November 2023 — up 12.6% year over year but down roughly 1% month over month — an announcement the EV maker made on the Friday before Seeking Alpha's December 3, 2023 week-ahead preview.

The two comparisons point in opposite directions and are the whole substance of the datapoint: measured against November 2022 the figure is growth, while measured against the immediately preceding month it is a slight decline. The month-on-month dip is small, at about one percent, so the number reads as flat sequentially rather than as a fall.
