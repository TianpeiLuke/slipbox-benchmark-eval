# Connor Bedard's NHL Debut: Blackhawks Beat Penguins 4-2

Connor Bedard's NHL debut on Tuesday, Oct. 10, 2023 delivered first game, first point and first win, as the Chicago rookie helped the Blackhawks to a 4-2 opening-night victory over the Penguins in Pittsburgh.

Chicago rallied from a 2-0 deficit with four unanswered goals. Bryan Rust opened the scoring for the Penguins in the first period and a Sidney Crosby goal in the second doubled the Pittsburgh lead, but Bedard helped set up a Ryan Donato goal in the middle frame to get the Blackhawks on the board, picking up his first NHL point in the process. In the third period Cole Guttman potted the equalizer early in the frame, and with less than five minutes left in regulation Jason Dickinson fired home a shot from the slot to give Chicago its first lead of the night, before a Nick Foligno empty-net goal sealed the deal.

The line score was Blackhawks 0-1-3 for 4 and Penguins 1-1-0 for 2, final: Blackhawks 4, Penguins 2.
