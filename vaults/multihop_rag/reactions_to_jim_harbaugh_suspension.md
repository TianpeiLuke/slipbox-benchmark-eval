# Reactions To Jim Harbaugh's Suspension

The suspension of Jim Harbaugh drew a wave of public responses from players, the university president, a former assistant and the school itself in the days around Nov. 10-17, 2023. Michigan released a statement expressing frustration with the Big Ten's process and said it intended to seek a court order preventing the disciplinary action from taking effect, as reported by Matt Fortuna on Nov. 10, 2023.

Players answered on social media. J.J. McCarthy and a handful of other Michigan players tweeted "Bet" at the same time on Friday night, hours after Harbaugh was suspended for the remainder of the regular season; McCarthy had earlier worn a shirt reading "Free Harbaugh" while the 59-year-old served a separate suspension at the start of the season.

Former Wolverines assistant and Harbaugh confidant Biff Poggi, in his first year as Charlotte's head coach, offered an open invitation on Friday for Harbaugh to join him on the 49ers' sidelines for their games against Rice on Saturday and at South Florida on Nov. 25, writing that it would be his honor to stand next to Harbaugh again as they had for three years.

University of Michigan President Santa Ono posted on social media the day after the Penn State win, praising the team's ability to "respond to any challenge head on with a conviction to do better and to emerge even stronger" and thanking the countless members of the Michigan family who had reached out over the weekend. The post drew immediate and extensive blowback on social media and beyond, as observers questioned Ono's tone with Michigan in the midst of a self-inflicted controversy.
