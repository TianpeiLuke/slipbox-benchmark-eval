# MasterClass Black Friday Deal

MasterClass's Black Friday 2023 deal gives two memberships for the price of one, starting at $120 per year, which provides two individual memberships; paying a total of $180 gets access for two devices simultaneously, content downloads and a Family membership that covers six devices.

Engadget suggests MasterClass could be a good gift option for people who love to learn or collect hobbies, as it provides access to hundreds of hours of video of masters of their craft explaining how they do their work, and some classes have exercises to complete as well, making it feel more like a real class. The deal was available until the end of the day on Black Friday proper.
