# West Bank And Gaza Casualties, October-November 2023

The Palestinian health ministry has said that more than 170 Palestinians were killed in the West Bank in the month before the Guardian's 11 November 2023 report.

The intensity of that violence in the West Bank has forced the displacement of at least 11 Palestinian communities, whose residents have historically refused to leave despite the difficulties posed by both settlers and the Israeli Defence Forces.

Over the same month in Gaza, according to the Gaza health ministry, the Israeli army's bombardment killed more than 10,000 Palestinians, and United Nations experts have warned that "the Palestinian people are at grave risk of genocide."
