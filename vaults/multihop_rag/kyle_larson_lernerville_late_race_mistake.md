# Kyle Larson's Late-Race Mistake At Lernerville

With just nine laps to the checkered flag at Lernerville, Kyle Larson was leading the race and seemed set to take the victory and earn himself $50,000 in his own competition — but, much like what had happened in Texas, he lost control of his car while trying to defend the lead from co-owner Brad Sweet.

Unlike Texas, he did not DNF and managed to get his car back on track, but his chance to win had gone, and the Cup Series Hendrick Motorsports driver could only finish in eighth place. Larson had taken part in the Lernerville race on the Tuesday, only a couple of days after a hectic race in Texas, and it seems he brought some of that form into the dirt track race. A racer as competitive as Larson would have liked a win, especially after what had happened the Sunday before, but it was a good day for him as the owner as well.
