# Patrick Kane's 2023 Free Agency Situation

Patrick Kane's 2023-24 free agency is the NHL's version of "The Decision": the winger had been on the market since the turn of the new season and, as of Nov. 27, 2023, was closing in on signing with a team. Sporting News allowed that it is not nearly as big as LeBron James' NBA free agency choice in 2010 — close, kind of, maybe ten years ago when Kane was in the middle of his prime.

The path there ran through Chicago and New York. The three-time Stanley Cup champion and longtime Blackhawks star was traded at the previous year's deadline because he wanted another shot at winning a Cup and was not interested in sticking around for Chicago's rebuild; he went to the Rangers, but New York was knocked out in the first round of the playoffs, leaving Kane to hit free agency for the first time in his career. It was announced during the summer that he would undergo hip surgery and likely would not decide until he was ready to return, and that is how it played out — the season began with Kane not on an NHL roster, still rehabbing.

The caveats about his form are explicit. Kane is no longer the offensive dynamo he was in his prime; he has always been a defensive liability, and that has stood out more in recent years with his production declining. It is hard to say how much the hip injury affected his short time with the Rangers, but he was far from impressive in the Big Apple. Regardless, the 35-year-old was ready to get back on the ice with no shortage of interested suitors: despite his camp trying to keep everything hush-hush, rumors and reports had been flying since the start of the campaign, and although it was reported he could decide before Thanksgiving, the holiday came and went without a deal, which was still expected soon.
