# Prime Day Tabletop Game Deals, October 2023

The Verge's October 10th, 2023 Prime Day gaming roundup included three tabletop discounts. Catan — the iconic board game classic in which players strategically compete and trade for resources — was $27 at Amazon and $29 at Target, down from $49, a 45 percent discount.

Happy Salmon was $8.63 at Amazon, $4 off; the kid-friendly party card game by the Exploding Kittens creators can be played to completion in just a few minutes and is a great ice breaker for adults and children alike, getting everyone out of their seats and quickly causing a raucous — with the writer's warning not to make those salmon so happy you end up with bruised forearms.

Splendor, a resource management game, was $19.97 at Amazon, $25 off, which is one of its lowest prices. It is a competitive game set in Renaissance Europe where you compete with up to three other players to build the best jewelry business, and a modern tabletop classic.
