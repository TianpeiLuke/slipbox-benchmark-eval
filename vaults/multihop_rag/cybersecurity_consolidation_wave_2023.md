# The 2023 Cybersecurity Consolidation Wave

Cybersecurity remains a moving target, and that dynamic drives a repeating acquisition cycle: malicious hackers are turning to technologies like AI to break into networks, so as smaller startups come up with new cyber techniques themselves, they become acquisition targets for larger companies looking to stay ahead of the curve.

The pattern shows up in a chain of reactive deals. CrowdStrike acquired the security startup Bionic for $350 million, and IBM bought Polar for $60 million — a deal IBM made, TechCrunch understands, partly in response to Palo Alto buying Cider Security in 2022. There are mega deals in the same trend, such as Cisco's plan to buy Splunk for $28 billion.

The competitive relation runs in two directions at once: for security companies it becomes a question of competitive edge both against malicious hackers and against other security companies. "Palo Alto is buying partly in reaction to these deals," one source said, and some Palo Alto competitors like Wiz, now valued at $10 billion, are also part of that competitive threat.
