# Student Loan Borrowers Curtailing Holiday Gift Spending

Fortune reported on 2023-12-23 that student loan payments were forcing borrowers to cut back on Christmas gift spending, illustrating the squeeze through two named borrowers.

Nicole Plauché, a 23-year-old tech sales manager from Dallas, Texas, said her main struggle was student loan payments: "It just doesn't leave much room for anything outside of just basic necessities." For her family, Christmas is the time of year when they can use their preferred love language, gift giving, and this year she was worried about how much she could afford to spend. Marisa Johnson, 26, a non-profit worker in Citrus Heights, California, has approximately $145,000 in student loan debt from both her bachelor's and master's degrees; since the COVID-era payment pause ended in October she has been paying back $300 a month while she waits to see if she will be enrolled in a new income-driven repayment plan. Because all of her money goes to necessities and loan repayment, she has not been able to spend as much on gifts as in previous years: "I've mostly been trying to think of affordable gifts or combining gifts with my older sister."
