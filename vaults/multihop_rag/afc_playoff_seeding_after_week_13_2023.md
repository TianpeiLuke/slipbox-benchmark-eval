# AFC Playoff Seeding After Week 13 Of The 2023 Season

The Miami Dolphins held the AFC's No. 1 seed after Week 13 of the 2023 season, ahead of the Baltimore Ravens, Kansas City Chiefs, Jacksonville Jaguars, Pittsburgh Steelers, Indianapolis Colts and Cleveland Browns, per Sporting News' Dec. 3, 2023 playoff picture.

At No. 1 were the Dolphins (9-3), first place in the AFC East, who matched the Ravens' record in their dominant road victory at the Commanders; for the moment they had the conference record tiebreaker to jump into the spot, and beating Washington opened up a three-game division lead on the idle Bills. At No. 2 were the Ravens (9-3), first place in the AFC North, who went into their bye with a half-game lead for home-field advantage and the lone playoff bye over three teams right behind them — though the tiebreakers would shake things up depending on which of two favored teams, the Chiefs and Jaguars, joined the Dolphins in winning in separate matchups. The Ravens also led the Steelers by 1.5 games in the division. At No. 3 were the Chiefs (8-4), first place in the AFC West, holding wins over the Jaguars and Dolphins to help them in the tiebreakers; they would win the division again and push for No. 1, but losing to the Packers on the road in Week 13 kept them at No. 3 as the Jaguars lost on Monday night to stay behind them. At No. 4 were the Jaguars (8-4), first place in the AFC South, who had a chance to match the Dolphins and Ravens on Monday night but whose home overtime loss to the Bengals dropped them behind the also-losing Chiefs, to whom they had lost; with the upset Jacksonville also saw its division lead drop to one over Indianapolis and Houston.

At No. 5 were the Steelers (7-5), second place in the AFC North, who lost Kenny Pickett and all their offensive promise in one fell swoop against the Cardinals, also hurt by the weather in the shocking upset at home; they remained in place because of the Browns also losing. At No. 6 were the Colts (7-5), second place in the AFC South, who kept up their surprise playoff momentum with a win over the Titans in Tennessee in Week 13 and still held a head-to-head tiebreaker ahead of the Texans, with no rematch against Houston until Week 18 in Indianapolis. At No. 7 were the Browns (7-5), third place in the AFC North, who lost to a lesser overall NFC West foe in the Rams, only on the road; Joe Flacco tried to get their offense going but the concern had become the defense falling apart and regressing at the wrong time.
