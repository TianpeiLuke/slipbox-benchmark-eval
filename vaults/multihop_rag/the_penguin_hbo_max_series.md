# The Penguin (HBO Max Series)

The Penguin is HBO Max's TV spinoff of Matt Reeves' The Batman, meant to bridge the gap between that movie and Reeves' sequel. Colin Farrell's Penguin solidifies power in the Gotham underworld immediately after the death of Carmine Falcone, played by John Turturro, in the movie.

The series runs eight episodes, is produced by Reeves, and is overseen by Agents of SHIELD writer-producer Lauren LeFranc.

Its release position follows from its bridging role: because it is meant as a lead-in for The Batman Part II, Polygon says that even though it was undated as of 2023-10-24, readers should expect it before the movie hits.
