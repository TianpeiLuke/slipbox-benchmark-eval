# Jets-Raiders Broadcast And Kickoff Details

The Jets at Raiders "Sunday Night Football" game on Sunday, Nov. 12 started at 8:20 p.m. ET from Allegiant Stadium in Las Vegas and was carried on NBC.

Streaming options were Peacock, NBCSports.com and Fubo in the U.S., with DAZN in Canada. Viewers hoping to stream could find it on Peacock or NBCSports.com with a cable subscription; Fubo, which offers a free trial, carried NBC's broadcast; and DAZN carries all NFL games in Canada.

On the call, Mike Tirico handled play-by-play with Cris Collinsworth providing commentary, and Melissa Stark reported from the sidelines.
