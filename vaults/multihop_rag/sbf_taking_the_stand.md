# SBF Taking The Stand As A "Hail Mary"

SBF taking to the stand in his own trial is a "Hail Mary" ahead of a verdict and potential sentencing for seven counts tied to fraud and money laundering. That is the claim former federal prosecutor Josh Naftalis made to TechCrunch in coverage published 2023-10-06.

The grounds Naftalis gives are that testifying is irreversible once it starts: "Once it goes to cross[-examination], he doesn't get to say, 'I'm done,'" he said. "He can't just walk out if he doesn't like how it's going." The warrant joining those grounds to the claim is that a defendant who takes the stand hands control of the questioning to the other side and cannot take it back — which is what makes the move a last-resort gamble rather than an ordinary defense choice.

Naftalis spoke at length about what comes next in the trial, and addressed common misconceptions about the prosecution and the defense, in the latest episode of TechCrunch's Chain Reaction podcast, which can be listened to on Apple Podcasts or Spotify.
