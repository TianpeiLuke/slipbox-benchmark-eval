# Streamers Who Cannot Afford To Leave Kick

Some Kick streamers argue they cannot leave the platform because there is nowhere safe to go that also pays: the alternative, Twitch, is not viable for them either financially or in terms of harassment.

Bree put the case directly on X: "So many people will say 'oh go back to twitch'… I can't. That platform let creators openly harass, bully, abuse, oppress and threaten my life multiple times. I don't feel safe there and I never will. I'm stuck rn. This is my livelihood and how I keep a roof over my head." She also argued that a ban was not straightforward: "Kick's decision not to ban him does hurt, but I do think that from a business standpoint with contracts involved you can't just ban someone. I feel like a lot of people are focusing on this while our time could be better spent working together to make our platform better."

The warrant joining those grounds to the conclusion is economic dependence: unlike the streamers who left, others cannot afford to take the hit, and cannot rely on Twitch alone to pay their bills.
