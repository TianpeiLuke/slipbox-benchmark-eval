# Google Pixel Watch First-Gen Deal

Verge readers could save an extra $80 on the already discounted first-generation Google Pixel Watch by using promo code VERGEPIX80 at Wellbots, dropping the checkout price to $199.99 — down from $350, a 43 percent discount — as reported on October 28th, 2023. That is not bad, the writer noted, considering Google's first-gen smartwatch retailed for $349.99 when it launched a little over a year earlier. The 41mm wearable has a beautiful domed display and native Fitbit integration for health tracking, and comes with six months of Fitbit Premium and three months of YouTube Music.

The first-gen Pixel Watch may not be as capable as the new Google Pixel Watch 2, but the two share strengths: both offer health and fitness-tracking features powered by Fitbit, allowing special features like FDA-cleared EKGs, and both support Google services like Google Assistant and Google Wallet. Thanks to the new Wear OS 4 update, the older model also gets access to the Google Calendar app, Gmail, and the new Safety Check feature introduced with the newer wearable.

There are trade-offs. The Pixel Watch 2 offers superior battery life, whereas the last-gen model struggles to last a full day, and the newer wearable adds Sleep Profiles, abnormal heart rate tracking, and nightly SpO2 features. If you are comfortable with these omissions, The Verge concluded, the first-gen model is still a good wearable to buy at this price.
