# The Robotics Supply Chain As An Underappreciated Trend

Asked which robotics story is not getting enough coverage, Boston Dynamics CTO Aaron Saunders names the supply chain of underlying components rather than AI. There is a lot of enthusiasm around AI and its potential to change all industries including robotics, and although it has a clear role and may unlock domains that have been relatively static for decades, there is a lot more to a good robotic product than 1's and 0's.

His grounds are the requirements of physical embodiment: for AI to achieve the physical embodiment needed to interact with the world, we need to track progress in key technologies like computers, perception sensors, power sources and all the other bits that make up a full robotic system. The recent pivot in automotive towards electrification and Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS) is quickly transforming a massive supply chain, and progress in graphics cards, computers and increasingly sophisticated AI-enabled consumer electronics continues to drive value into adjacent supply chains.

The reason this matters, on his account, is leverage for newcomers: this massive snowball of technology, rarely in the spotlight, is one of the most exciting trends in robotics because it enables small innovative companies to stand on the backs of giants to create new and exciting products.
