# Andrew Redmayne Substitution In Peru Playoff

Graham Arnold's record-breaking achievement is conceivably down to one big ballsy call in the playoff against Peru, The Roar wrote on Nov. 13, 2023: with moments left in extra time against the South Americans, Arnold dragged Mat Ryan and replaced him with Andrew Redmayne, who saved the decisive spot kick — and also threw his opponent's water bottle, with cheat sheet attached, away from the goal.

While national team coaches make important calls every game, this one carried the coach's career: Ryan might well have gotten the Socceroos to Qatar, but if Australia had not advanced there is little chance Arnold would still be their coach today.
