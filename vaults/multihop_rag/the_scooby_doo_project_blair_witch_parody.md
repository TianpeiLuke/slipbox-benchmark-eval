# The Scooby-Doo Project (Blair Witch Parody)

The Scooby-Doo Project is a parody of The Blair Witch Project starring the Mystery Inc. gang, and Polygon calls it among the most interesting hidden gems of the series. It was the Oct. 9 pick in the 2023-10-10 Halloween Countdown, on YouTube.

Polygon notes that Scooby-Doo got extremely dark for a few years in the early 2000s, which also happened to be the best years of the series since its 1970s heyday, with movies like The Witch's Ghost and Zombie Island holding up as excellent. In this short, Scooby, Shaggy, Fred, Velma, and Daphne set off in the Mystery Machine to the remote woods of Casper County, filmed in the same documentary style The Blair Witch Project made iconic and mostly inserting the gang into live-action locations such as real woods or a real town where they interview locals.

It was originally created as a bumper to bookend segments of a 1999 Scooby-Doo marathon, just a few months after the movie it parodied was released, but after Cartoon Network saw what the creators put together it wisely decided to re-air the short segments end-to-end as a movie.

Critic AG argues it stands alongside the original Blair Witch not only as an impressive and loving parody but as a similarly unnerving found-footage horror movie: the gang standing among live-action backgrounds is creepy enough on its own, and hearing beloved characters be accosted by unseen forces as they sprint through the woods is downright scary, particularly for a kid watching in 1999 before seeing the original. The best part comes at the end when, much like the other Scooby-Doo movies of the time, there is a soft implication that the supernatural elements were actually real and the horrors will not stop when the villain's mask comes off — a downright haunting ending.
