# Which Vampire Authors Were Vampires?

Polygon's comic premise is that scholars have largely turned a blind eye to one lingering question about vampire fiction: which of these authors were themselves nosferatu? Was night-stalking Lord Byron secretly a daywalker? Could Anne Rice's innate understanding of deathless sexiness itself result from her fellowship in the camp of the undead?

The stated warrant for pursuing the question is that until we understand which writers have been secretly propagandizing for Dracular overlords, our understanding of the place of vampires in anglophone culture will never be fully complete. On that basis — in the interest of bettering the cause of human understanding and the avoidance of unwilling resurrection — the piece presents a field guide to secretly vampiric authors, past and present, delivering a verdict on each.
