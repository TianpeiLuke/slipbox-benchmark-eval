# The Commission's Earlier Requests For Information To X

Before opening formal DSA proceedings, the European Commission had been probing X for months via a flurry of requests for information, with its earlier actions focused on concerns about the spread of illegal content and disinformation related to the Israel-Hamas war.

In October 2023 the Commission sent Musk's company an "urgent" formal request for information about how it was responding to information risks arising out of the conflict. The EU said at the time that it was also looking at X's compliance across a number of areas, including policies and practices regarding notices on illegal content, complaint handling, risk assessment and measures to mitigate the risks identified, and it gave X until the end of October to respond.

The formal proceeding also came hard on the heels of a complaint against X's adtech by the privacy rights group noyb, although TechCrunch judged the formal proceeding unlikely to be directly linked to that complaint.
