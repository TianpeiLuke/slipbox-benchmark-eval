# The Original 1989: Grammy Wins, Chart Longevity And A $250m Tour

The original 1989 became Taylor Swift's biggest-selling album to date despite being seen as a risk by her record label, won three Grammy Awards including Album of the Year, and has spent 325 weeks in the UK charts. Its supporting tour sold 2.2 million tickets and earned more than $250m at the box office, with almost the entire album played every night.

Featuring hits like Shake It Off, Blank Space and Style, the album was originally written during the 2013-14 Red Tour, with demos stored on Swift's phone in a folder named "Sailor Twips". Deluxe editions came with collectible Polaroids featuring Taylor's favourite lyrics. In 2015, she was one of the main stage headliners at Radio 1's Big Weekend.
