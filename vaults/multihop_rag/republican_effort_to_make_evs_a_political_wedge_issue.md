# The Republican Effort To Make EVs A Political Wedge Issue

The Verge argued that Trump's Detroit comments were the latest attempt to make electric vehicles a major wedge issue in American politics — and that, like most other recent Republican efforts to politicize EVs, the attempt is largely incoherent.

The grounds for the motive are political: President Joe Biden has made EVs a major part of his environmental legacy, and Trump senses the issue could be used to leach support from working-class voters. The grounds for the charge of incoherence are empirical: the attack ignores the nuanced reality of how most autoworkers actually feel about EVs.
