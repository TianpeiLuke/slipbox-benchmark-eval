# Earnings Week Ahead: December 4-8, 2023

The first full week of December 2023 carried a heavy corporate reporting calendar, with several significant companies slated to announce quarterly results between December 4 and December 8 — releases Seeking Alpha framed on December 3, 2023 as holding considerable implications for energy, software, retail, autos and semiconductors.

The companies scheduled to release financial figures that week included NIO (NYSE:NIO), GameStop (NYSE:GME), DocuSign (NASDAQ:DOCU), ChargePoint (NYSE:CHPT), Dollar General (NYSE:DG), C3.ai (NYSE:AI) and Broadcom (NASDAQ:AVGO). Reports were additionally expected from Toll Brothers (NYSE:TOL), Campbell Soup (NYSE:CPB), AutoZone (NYSE:AZO), Lululemon (NASDAQ:LULU), MongoDB (NASDAQ:MDB), Signet (NYSE:SIG) and Chewy (NYSE:CHWY). The article introduced this roster as a curated list of earnings due in the weeks of December 4 to December 8, spanning both NYSE- and Nasdaq-listed issuers.
