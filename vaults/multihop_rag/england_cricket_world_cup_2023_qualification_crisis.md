# England's 2023 Cricket World Cup Qualification Crisis

England's Cricket World Cup hopes hung by a thread after a shock defeat to Afghanistan, leaving their chances of finishing in the top four and progressing to the semi-finals looking dubious as they prepared to face South Africa in Mumbai from 9:30am on 21 October 2023, talkSPORT reported.

Having also lost to New Zealand in their opener, victory for England could scarcely be more crucial. They were boosted by the news that Ben Stokes was fit for the match after picking up a hip injury five days before the tournament.

There was one encouraging sign in the opposition's form: South Africa, despite winning their first two games, suffered a shock defeat to the Netherlands in their most recent match — an indication for England that the Proteas can be beaten. talkSPORT would bring updates from the game in India.
