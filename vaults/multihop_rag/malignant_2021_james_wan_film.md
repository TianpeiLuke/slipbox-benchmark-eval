# Malignant: James Wan's Giallo Turducken Nobody Saw Coming

Malignant, directed by James Wan and starring Annabelle Wallis, Maddie Hasson and George Young, is available on Max. Polygon's point is that there was just no way to see it coming: after the Conjuring and Insidious franchises, plus blockbuster turns with Furious 7 and Aquaman, Wan could have cashed in chips to make another moody franchise-starter to stretch his jump-scare muscles, and instead made a high-emotion giallo stuffed into dingy '90s direct-to-video pastiche like some kind of horror-movie turducken.

Wan pulls back the layers in an almost tedious fashion: the pregnant Madison (Annabelle Wallis) is first the victim of domestic abuse, then she encounters another killer, then she starts dealing with psychotic episodes tied to her childhood imaginary friend Gabriel, and then comes the reveal, which the reviewer refuses to spoil. Strung together with a melodramatic cover of The Pixies' "Where Is My Mind" and revelling in horror tropes to the point of parody, the final twists are some of the most gratifying lunacy of the year, with acrobatic actor Marina Mazepa bringing it all home in a display of gruesome ballet.
