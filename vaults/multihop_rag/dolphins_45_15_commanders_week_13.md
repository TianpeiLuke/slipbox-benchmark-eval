# Dolphins 45-15 Commanders, Week 13 2023

Tyreek Hill had two touchdowns among his 157 receiving yards as the Miami Dolphins routed the Washington Commanders 45-15 in Week 13 of the 2023 season, moving the AFC East leaders to 9-3 for the first time since 2001.

Tua Tagovailoa was turnover-free after giving the ball away four times over the previous two games. De'Von Achane ran for two touchdowns in his return after missing five of the past six games with a knee injury. The Commanders (4-9) lost their fourth in a row, and Washington's Sam Howell threw a pick-six for a third consecutive game.
