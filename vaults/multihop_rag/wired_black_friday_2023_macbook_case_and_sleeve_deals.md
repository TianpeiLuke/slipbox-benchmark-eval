# WIRED Black Friday 2023 MacBook Case And Sleeve Deals

WIRED's Apple Black Friday guide of 25 November 2023 covered three discounted MacBook protection options: Baggu's laptop sleeve, Speck's Smartshell hardshell case and the hardshell WIRED calls its current favourite MacBook case.

For the Baggu sleeve, the discount shows at checkout. WIRED tested the 16-inch version in Sherbert Cherry and reports it looks just as cute in person as online, with puffiness that provides plenty of protection and a Velcro closure strong enough that you need not worry about the laptop sliding out; WIRED tested it with the 15-inch MacBook Air and had space left to squeeze in a keyboard and mouse, and notes there is also a 13-inch/14-inch version for smaller MacBooks or for iPads.

Speck's Smartshell case has proper cutouts for ports and rubberised feet to keep the machine in place, so you can throw the laptop in a backpack, tote or duffle bag without worrying about damage; WIRED tested it with the 14-inch MacBook Pro and says it is available for a wide range of MacBook models, but adds an explicit caveat that the hardshell is not scratch-resistant despite Speck's claims — WIRED has noticed a few scrapes on its own, though not too noticeable. The third case is WIRED's current favourite, tested with the 16-inch MacBook Pro (2021) and available for a few MacBook models, with rubberised feet that prevent sliding, precise cutouts for all ports and ventilation to keep the MacBook from overheating.
