# Gogoro's India Station Rollout Targets

Gogoro plans to simulate its Taiwan growth in India by launching 30 stations in Delhi by the first quarter of 2024. The company began its journey in Taipei with the same number of stations but has since expanded to 12,500 stations serving around 600,000 vehicles throughout Taiwan, and it is open to making many more investments in India to reach that level and even grow bigger over time.

CEO Horace Luke put a figure on the eventual commitment: "If you think about Pan India, we are talking about several billion dollars easily by 2030–2032."
