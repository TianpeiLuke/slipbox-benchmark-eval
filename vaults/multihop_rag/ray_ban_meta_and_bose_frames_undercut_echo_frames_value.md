# Ray-Ban Meta And Bose Frames Undercut The Echo Frames' Value

The Verge's value objection to the Carrera Cruiser is that, whatever its looks, "this is not nearly $400 worth of tech and style" once you compare what rivals give you for similar or less money.

The Ray-Ban Meta Smart Glasses start at $299 — a mere $30 more than the cheapest Echo Frames — and get you better build quality, a surprisingly good hands-free camera for photos and video, the ability to livestream to Instagram, better audio quality, and a much better mic, plus the option of transition lenses; the reviewer allowed that the third-gen Echo Frames are at least on a similar playing field when it comes to style. The Bose Frames Tempo and Soprano retail at $249 and can be found on sale for around $200, with similar battery life and better sound quality. The one buyer the reviewer would exempt is the heavy Alexa user, who could reasonably pay $270 for the base model and maybe use FSA or HSA funds if they need prescription lenses.
