# GLP-1 Receptor Agonists: The Drug Class And Its US Usage Scale

GLP-1 receptor agonists are a class of medications that includes the semaglutides, and the gastrointestinal risk reported in this coverage was linked to all semaglutides in the class.

The named members of the class span both indications for which the drugs are prescribed: Ozempic, prescribed for diabetes management; Wegovy, prescribed for weight loss; Rybelsus, for type 2 diabetes; and Saxenda, for weight loss.

The scale of use is what makes the class's side-effect profile consequential. The number of people in the U.S. using GLP-1 agonists for either diabetes or obesity reached 40 million in 2022, the researchers noted.
