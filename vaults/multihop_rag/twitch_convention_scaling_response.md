# Twitch's Convention Scaling Response

Twitch managed to mitigate crowding issues during this year's convention, a success that chief marketing officer Rachel Delphin credits to the company learning quickly, TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-26.

Delphin explained what had overwhelmed the previous event: "It was a huge demand to come back. I think fandoms have grown in that time period, just the fervor around specific creators that was totally and wholly different than we'd seen before. And the creators themselves had also absolutely exploded. It used to be that you'd come to TwitchCon, be the biggest creator, and you could walk around pretty freely. Now it's huge, it's a whole different kind of apparatus to manage."
