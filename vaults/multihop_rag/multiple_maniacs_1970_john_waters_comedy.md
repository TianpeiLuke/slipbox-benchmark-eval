# Multiple Maniacs (1970): John Waters' Transgressive Comedy

Multiple Maniacs is John Waters' 1970 transgressive comedy, his second film, running 1h 31m and starring Divine, David Lochary and Mary Vivian Pearce. Polygon describes it as "just batshit nutso," constructing a flimsy scenario in which Lady Divine — Waters' go-to collaborator — spirals out of control on a murder spree while her ex-lover (Lochary) plots to kill her first, with other members of Waters' Dreamlander acting troupe.

The distinguishing feature is that the plot exists as an excuse for the film's provocations: bodily fluid expulsion, flamboyant performance, and a moment of backdoor penetration involving a sacred religious object. Polygon frames the film as a historical boundary case — Waters' early transgressive comedies have been anointed by Criterion as art, and one can only imagine what Waters circa 1970 would make of that; today, with scrutiny from every side of the ideological spectrum, it would be almost impossible to do what Waters pulled off back in the '70s, so Criterion knew what it was doing in preserving these jaw-dropping memories. It is available to stream on HBO Max and Criterion Channel, free with ads on Tubi, or for digital rental or purchase at Amazon and Google Play.
