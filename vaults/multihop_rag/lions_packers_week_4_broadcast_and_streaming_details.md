# Lions-Packers Week 4: Kickoff, Channels And Streaming

The Thursday Night Football game between the Detroit Lions and Green Bay Packers was played on Thursday, Sept. 28, with a start time of 8:15 p.m. ET in the primetime slot, Sporting News listed on 2023-09-28. TV channel in Detroit: WJBK (Channel 2). TV channel in Green Bay: WITI (Channel 6) or WGBA (Channel 26). Live stream: Amazon Prime Video, or DAZN in Canada.
