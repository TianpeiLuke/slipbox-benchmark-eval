# Alone: A Taut 98-Minute Woman-On-The-Run Thriller

Alone is a taut spine-chiller from John Hyams (Universal Soldier: Day of Reckoning), starring Jules Willcox, Marc Menchaca and Anthony Heald, and available on Hulu and Kanopy. Polygon describes it as a classic woman-on-the-run thriller.

Jessica (Willcox), a recent widow, is in the midst of moving; on top of that stress, a creepy man (Menchaca) appears to be following her on the road. After he slashes her tires she crashes and wakes up in his basement. What follows is a tightly crafted thriller with great performances, outstanding direction, and enough tension to keep your heart pounding throughout the 98-minute running time.
