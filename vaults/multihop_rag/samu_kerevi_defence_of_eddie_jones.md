# Samu Kerevi's Defence Of Eddie Jones

Samu Kerevi rejected the premise that the Wallabies' failure at the 2023 Rugby World Cup was Eddie Jones' fault, telling Wide World Of Sports on 7 October 2023: "we didn't get the results, you know, we didn't perform the way we wanted to as a team and it's not on Eddie - it's on all of us."

The premise he attacks is that outsiders can judge the coach from the results alone, because they do not see the work. An unabashed Jones fan, Kerevi said: "He's up at 4am working hard. We catch him in the gym, he's working hard and I swear he doesn't even sleep. Seriously, the amount of time he puts into effort, into his staff, into the players, always has time for us ... and I think fans don't see that." He continued: "So I understand what the fans feel and they're just passionate and it's awesome to have that. But no-one realises what goes on inside. No-one realises the things that we're working on tirelessly, to work hard, but especially him and the staff, f--ing work so hard, bro. They're away from families, they're sacrificing as well and … I don't read into the comment stuff, but the young boys, I hear a lot that's going on, social media and stuff. But I don't think people really understand demons, everyone's fights in between."

He conceded the standard by which coaches are judged while denying the conclusion drawn from it: "Eddie's a human as well and at the end of the day it's a results-driven sport, you know, we need results to get that. But he's committed to us and we love that about him. He hasn't thought about going home, he's thought about how we can contribute best and that's what we've done these last three days. I feel for him, you know, like as a head coach, you maybe can't show that side of himself. But he's been so strong for us and gives us strength to just be positive. And again, we put ourselves in this situation."
