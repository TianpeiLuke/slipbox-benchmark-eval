# Wall Street December 2023 Mixed Session

Most US stocks slipped in the overnight Wall Street session reported on 5 December 2023, though the market stayed near its highest level in 20 months after a mixed set of reports that kept alive questions about whether the world's largest economy can pull off a perfect landing in which it kills high inflation but avoids a recession. The S&P 500 edged down 0.1 per cent for its first back-to-back loss since October, the Dow Jones slipped 0.2 per cent, and the Nasdaq composite rose 0.3 per cent.

Stocks fell more sharply in Asia amid worries about the health of China's economy, the world's second largest. US stocks and Treasury yields wavered after reports showed that US employers advertised far fewer jobs at the end of October than expected, while growth for services businesses accelerated more in the prior month than expected.

The underlying hope on Wall Street at the time was that the US economy was slowing from its recent hot pace by just the right amount: too much strength would give inflation more fuel, but too little would mean a recession. The Australian market's gains that day came after this mixed US session.
