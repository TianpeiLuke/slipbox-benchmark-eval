# BG3 Sorcerer: The Social Caster

Polygon names Sorcerer the best class for social interactions in Baldur's Gate 3. Sorcerers get their magic from within themselves and are the most flexible and varied in the spells they can use.

Their primary Ability is Charisma, which also makes them good at dialogue-heavy encounters out of combat. Sorcerers get no armor, so you have to keep them out of melee combat most of the time. The Sorcerer subclass is picked at character creation from Wild Magic, Draconic Bloodline, and Storm Sorcery. Wild Magic leans into the chaotic and untamed nature of magic; Draconic Bloodline gives a dragon-based flavour to your magic; Storm Sorcery lets you Fly whenever you cast a spell. Draconic Bloodline is the best subclass for a Sorcerer, with the reasoning and the skill and feat picks left to Polygon's separate Sorcerer build guide.
