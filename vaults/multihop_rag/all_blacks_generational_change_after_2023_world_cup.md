# The All Blacks' Generational Change After The 2023 World Cup

The 2023 World Cup final marked the end of an All Blacks era on and off the field: Ian Foster's tenure as coach came to an end with Scott Robertson taking over, and multiple generational players — Dane Coles, Sam Whitelock, Brodie Retallick and Aaron Smith — were to call time on their international careers, with others including Ardie Savea, Richie Mo'unga, Beauden Barrett and Shannon Frizell heading to Japan.

Writing before the final on Oct. 26, The Roar reported that the All Blacks would experience a changing of the guard the next season, and noted that the team's own social media channels described the World Cup final selection as the "Last Dance". Dane Coles, a 2015 World Cup winner, would call time on his career, while his tight-five teammates Retallick and Whitelock — the latter bidding to become the first player to win the Webb Ellis Cup for a third time — would head overseas, and fellow World Cup winners Aaron Smith and Beauden Barrett would also head to Japan.

Coles, labelled by Samisoni Taukei'aho as a "Grandad" type figure in the All Blacks, was even denied the chance of a farewell send-off after missing selection for the final. Foster called the conversation with the 36-year-old the hardest of his coaching life: "Yeah, tough. We don't want to talk too much about post-game stuff, but that was a tough one, probably the toughest I have had as a coach. He took it the way I expected him to. He's a champion." After the final, The Roar added that Robertson would have an almighty task on his hands given the scale of the departures.
