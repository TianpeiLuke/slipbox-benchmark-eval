# MLB Props And Futures: World Series, MVP, Home Run Leader And Win Totals

MLB props and futures let you bet on team and player performance irrespective of individual game outcomes. Popular examples include the World Series, where you bet on which team will become the next champion — futures odds appear nearly as soon as the previous World Series ends and change based on offseason moves, team news, team performance and more.

The other named markets are MVP, where you wager on the player you believe will take home the Most Valuable Player award for the season, described as a fun way to keep tabs on certain players all year; home run leader, on which player will crank out the most homers during the regular season; and regular-season win totals, an over/under wager on whether a team will finish over or under the number of wins a sportsbook sets for them.

Every sportsbook offers its own selection of futures and props bets, so shop around to find the books that best fit your needs.
