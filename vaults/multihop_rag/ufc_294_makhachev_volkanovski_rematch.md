# UFC 294: The Makhachev-Volkanovski Rematch

Islam Makhachev was set to defend his UFC lightweight belt against Alexander Volkanovski at UFC 294 on 21 October 2023, a rematch of the February bout in which Makhachev retained his title by unanimous decision, talkSPORT previewed on 20 October 2023.

Makhachev carried a favourable 24-1-0 record into the fight, but Volkanovski would be desperate to add the lightweight belt to his featherweight title. Also on the card, Kamaru Usman was to fight Khamzat Chimaev in a middleweight title eliminator. The event closed out a Saturday of sport that talkSPORT said could end with a bang.
