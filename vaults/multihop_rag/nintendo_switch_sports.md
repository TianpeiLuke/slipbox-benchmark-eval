# Nintendo Switch Sports

Nintendo Switch Sports gracefully takes up the mantle of Wii Sports, whose legacy — and banging soundtrack — Polygon says will never be forgotten. It appears in the outlet's 2023-10-20 list of the 35 best couch co-op games for Nintendo Switch.

It carries over many of the same classic sports, like bowling, golf, and tennis.

It also adds some new ones to the mix, like volleyball and chambara, which Polygon calls a great complement to Wii Sports' boxing — with the practical warning to make sure you have enough space around you so you do not accidentally hit your television.
