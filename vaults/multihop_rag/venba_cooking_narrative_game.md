# Venba (Cooking Narrative Game)

Venba, developed by Visai Games for Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, Windows PC, Xbox One, and Xbox Series X, is ranked 26 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31.

Critic N. Carpenter opens with a confession: she does not love to cook, treating it as more a necessity than anything else, so it is not often that a piece of media leaves her with the feeling that she needs to make something, to spend time in the kitchen reveling in the tedium of chopping and the sizzle of onions frying. Venba elicited that urge, reminding her that food is not just something to keep her alive but something to be cherished.

The game focuses on an immigrant family that has moved from India to Canada. Food transcends the story by way of simple cooking minigames as the player moves through the chapters of main character Venba's life, in moments that switch between painful and heartwarming. Carpenter says it packs as much heart in its one-hour playtime as games 30 times its size.
