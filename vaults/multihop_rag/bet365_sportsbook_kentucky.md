# Bet365 Sportsbook In Kentucky

Bet365 is one of the largest betting operators in Europe, well known for sports betting in the UK, and it is distinguished in the Kentucky market by what the report calls possibly the most generous signup offer of any competitor — bet $1, get $365 — as described by CBS Sports on 13 October 2023.

Its other distinguishing strength is coverage: the wagering menu, specifically rare leagues and sports, is covered by bet365, and the app offers a wide range of in-play and pre-match sports including football, baseball and basketball, with a personalized display to help users navigate to the sports they bet on. It is rolling out in selected states to gain market share in the U.S.

The boundary case named is same game parlays: bet365's SGP offerings aren't as extensive as other sportsbooks'.
