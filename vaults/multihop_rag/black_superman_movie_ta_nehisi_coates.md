# The 'Black Superman' Movie (Ta-Nehisi Coates)

DC announced in early 2021 that writer Ta-Nehisi Coates would reboot Superman with a movie putting a Black man in the title role. The film was meant to be produced by J.J. Abrams under his Bad Robot imprint.

Details have been thin about whether the film would tie into an existing DC comics story — for instance, whether it might be a movie outing for temporary Superman successor Steel or a complete reimagining of the character.

As of Polygon's 2023-10-24 report, the project was reportedly still going forward in the wake of Gunn and Safran's new slate, but no release date had been announced.
