# AFC 2026 World Cup Qualifying Format

The Asian confederation's road to the 2026 World Cup runs in stages, The Roar set out on Nov. 13, 2023: at this stage of qualifiers there are nine groups of four teams; the top two from each group advance into three groups of six for the next stage; and the top two from those games — played from September 2024 to June 2025 — directly advance to the play offs, with another qualifying round to determine the seventh and eighth direct qualifiers.

The precondition that eases the path is expansion: with FIFA expanding the tournament from 36 to 48 teams, eight direct Asian confederation qualifiers will proceed directly instead of four, meaning Arnold had virtually no chance of enduring a similar level of stress through to the next finals. Australia started the road to North America in Melbourne at AAMI Park on the Thursday night, before travelling to the Middle East to play Palestine the following Wednesday at 1.00am AEDT; Lebanon was the other team in Group I.
