# Bedard's Debut Numbers And The Pregame Scenes

Connor Bedard was as advertised in his NHL debut on Oct. 10, 2023: he led all players on his club in shots on goal with five, from 11 shot attempts, and his 21:29 time on ice was second only to Seth Jones, as Chicago head coach Luke Richardson did not hold the 18-year-old back.

The one weakness was in the faceoff dot, where he won just two of 13 draws, but the Sporting News still judged it a strong first impression at the NHL level. Bedard and the Blackhawks were back in action on national TV the next night, as Chicago headed to Boston for a Wednesday date with the Bruins.

The build-up produced its own scenes. At 6:30 p.m. Bedard was in the house for what the blog called the most anticipated first game for a rookie since Auston Matthews' debut in 2016. At 7:33 p.m. came a reminder that a stick may be important when playing hockey — a note to self not to forget your stick ahead of your NHL debut. At 7:36 p.m. he went without a bucket in warmups, rocking the no-helmet look, which the blog read as confidence. The opening faceoff pitted Sidney Crosby against Bedard, which Sportsnet called "what a moment".
