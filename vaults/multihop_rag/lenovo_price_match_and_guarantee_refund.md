# Lenovo Price Match And Guarantee Refund

Lenovo matches pricing on comparable PCs from Lenovo, HP, or Dell with the same key specs, as long as they are sold through a list of recognized retailers. The preconditions are that the comparable PC must be in stock and readily available from both Lenovo and the competing retailer, and you have to contact a Lenovo sales rep in order to facilitate the match. You cannot take advantage of coupons or special discounts while receiving a price match, the policy does not apply to membership stores like Costco, and it does not apply to "Marketplace" items where third parties fulfill the order — so double-check that you bought the product directly from the retailer.

Lenovo also offers a separate price guarantee refund for laptops and personal desktop computers bought from Lenovo.com within 30 days of purchase. To receive a refund you must contact Customer Care and show the lower price of the identical product offered on Lenovo.com. This policy does not apply to doorbuster or clearance products. The Verge published this summary on October 25th, 2023.
