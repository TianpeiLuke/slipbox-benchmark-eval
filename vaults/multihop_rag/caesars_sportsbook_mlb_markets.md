# Caesars Sportsbook: MLB Odds, Futures And Early Cash-Out

Caesars offers a comprehensive suite of MLB odds together with a cash-out option that lets bettors settle bets early to guarantee a payout, though at a reduced amount.

Its baseball markets include run lines, predicting the margin of victory; totals, where bettors forecast the combined scores; and player props focused on individual milestones such as hits, home runs or RBIs. Beyond these, game props allow bets on specific game events, while player futures bets provide the chance to predict longer-term outcomes like season MVPs or rookie of the year honors.
