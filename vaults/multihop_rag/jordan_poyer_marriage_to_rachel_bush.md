# Jordan Poyer's Marriage To Rachel Bush

Jordan Poyer met Rachel Bush in his third season, when he was an emerging starter with the Cleveland Browns, by DMing an Instagram model after she liked one of his posts on Twitter; they married in 2018 and she credits ayahuasca with transforming him, The New York Times reported on 15 December 2023.

She was a freshman at Florida Atlantic, a small-town girl from the Adirondacks who knew nothing about football but thought Poyer was cute. He travelled to Florida to meet her, and before the weekend was over declared, "I'm going to marry this girl." He made numerous trips to Florida that offseason, and that spring, as Rachel was finishing her freshman year of college, she learned she was pregnant. After their daughter, Aliyah, was born, Poyer signed with the Bills and the family moved to Buffalo.

Rachel has no interest in taking ayahuasca herself, but she is grateful her husband discovered it. "After he did ayahuasca, he became like my dream husband," she says. "I was nervous about what it would do to him, but he came back a totally new man, so appreciative of me and our family." She adds: "His attention now is 100 percent on me, his family and his career." Rachel has more than 4 million Instagram followers, an OnlyFans page and a line of skincare products.
