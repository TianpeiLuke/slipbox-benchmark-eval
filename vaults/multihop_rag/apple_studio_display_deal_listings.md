# Apple Studio Display Deal Listings

Both of WIRED's October 10, 2023 Prime Day roundups list the Apple Studio Display (9/10, WIRED Recommends) at the lowest price tracked yet.

Its spacious 27-inch display is perfect for juggling multiple apps at the same time, and its 5K resolution is stunningly sharp — though there's no HDR, so colors look slightly more contrasty and saturated compared with the neutral tones on Apple's Pro Display XDR. Although pricey, WIRED calls it an excellent monitor for anyone who wants a really accurate and sharp screen, and its 12-megapixel camera plus built-in mics and speakers make it ideal for video calls too.
