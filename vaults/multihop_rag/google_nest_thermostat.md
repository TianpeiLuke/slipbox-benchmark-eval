# Google Nest Thermostat Deal Prices

The Google Nest Thermostat was $90 — $40 off Google's MSRP of $130 — in both Engadget's October 2023 Prime Day coverage and its Black Friday roundup, close to a record low.

Engadget's 12 October 2023 piece said $90 is only about $10 more than the lowest price it had tracked, describing it as Google's entry-level smart thermostat that still lets you control heating and cooling from anywhere, suggests tweaks to help you save money, and in some cases can entitle you to rebates from your utility provider; it is all relatively easy to program and works with Google's Nest Hub and other smart displays like the Echo Show.

Engadget's 19 November 2023 Black Friday roundup repeated the $90 price, calling it $40 off and close to a record low. It is a pared-down version of the Nest Learning Thermostat but has plenty of smart features and should help save on energy costs over time; the model is Energy Star-certified, will automatically turn temperatures down in your home when you leave the house, and lets you adjust heating and cooling schedules from your phone.
