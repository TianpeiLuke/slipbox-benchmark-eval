# WIRED Black Friday 2023 iPad Model Deals

WIRED's Apple Black Friday guide of 25 November 2023 covered four discounted iPads: the model it calls the best iPad for most people, a newer redesigned iPad it ranks below that one, the M1 iPad Air and the iPad Mini.

The top pick (8/10, WIRED Recommends) is the best iPad for most people in WIRED's Best iPads guide: it is affordable and packs a True Tone display that adjusts colour temperature to match ambient lighting, a 12-megapixel rear camera with Center Stage support to keep you in frame on video calls, 64 gigabytes of storage on the base model and a Touch ID-enabled home button, and it works with the first-generation Apple Pencil and Apple's Smart Keyboard. The other, newer iPad (7/10, WIRED Review) is not WIRED's top choice — it recommends the ninth-generation model over this one largely on price — but it does bring notable changes: no Home button and slimmer bezels for a modern design, an upgraded rear camera, a selfie camera moved to the centre of the iPad for video calls, and Apple's A14 Bionic chip (the same as in the iPhone 12), making it more powerful than the last-generation model; WIRED notes it was on sale at this same price last Cyber Monday.

For more power than the ninth-generation model, WIRED recommends the iPad Air (8/10, WIRED Recommends), with a 10.9-inch LCD screen, slim bezels, a USB-C port, Touch ID built into the top power button and the same M1 processor as the 2020 MacBook Air, plus support for the Smart Keyboard Cover, the Magic Keyboard Cover and the second-generation and USB-C Apple Pencils. The iPad Mini (8/10, WIRED Recommends) is WIRED's favourite travel tablet — small enough to slip into a tote bag and to fit an airplane tray table — with an 8.3-inch display good for ebooks, movies, sketching and note-taking (compatible with the second-generation and USB-C Apple Pencils) and an A15 Bionic processor, the same one found in the iPhone 13, that handles taxing apps and games. WIRED attaches a timing caveat to both: Apple might launch a new iPad Air next year and is rumoured to be launching a next-generation Mini with a faster processor, but it considers both still strong buys if you do not need the latest and greatest.
