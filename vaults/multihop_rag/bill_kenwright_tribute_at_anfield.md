# Bill Kenwright Tribute At Anfield

Liverpool paid a warm tribute before kick-off at Anfield on 26 October 2023 to Bill Kenwright, the Everton chairman who had died that Monday — rivalry forgotten and friendship remembered.

The Hollies' "He Ain't Heavy, He's My Brother" — the song Kenwright chose for a Hillsborough tribute at Goodison Park, which became a fund-raising single for the justice campaign — was played to mass applause from the Liverpool fans inside the ground. The Guardian noted that Kenwright's powerful speech at the Hillsborough memorial service in 2013 and his lasting support for the bereaved families would not be forgotten across Stanley Park.
