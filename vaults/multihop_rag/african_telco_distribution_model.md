# StarNews' African Telco Distribution Model

StarNews Mobile's growth model places mobile operators at its centre: operators are pivotal in propelling content within Africa's media industry, with unmatched distribution capabilities, so collaborating closely with them is imperative for establishing a solid presence in the market. To draw a parallel for how the operator landscape is structured, Guy Kamgaing described Orange as the "Apple" of Africa's telecommunications world and MTN as the "Google."

The relation runs in both directions. Over the past few years operators have been instrumental in driving data usage across the continent, and they simultaneously upsell their customers with in-house content or with content from partners like StarNews. On the strength of that arrangement StarNews carries an active subscriber base of over 4 million through its partnerships with MTN and Orange across six markets, alongside a community of more than 120 content creators. The model has a documented boundary: StarNews exited South Africa after a misalignment with MTN's strategy for engaging content creators, so the operator partnership holds only where the operator's own creator strategy aligns with the platform's.

This approach let StarNews expand its user base organically while securing nearly $8 million in funding — a $900,000 friends and family round, a $2 million seed investment and a $1.8 million extension — from an array of U.S., European and African VCs including Investisseurs & Partenaires (I&P), Snap via its Yellow Accelerator program, LoftyInc Capital, Graph Ventures and Expert Dojo.

Beneath the operator layer, the platform is structured around channels, with each creator having a dedicated channel. Kamgaing says the company works closely with creators by providing financial support and assisting in content production, and that it guides creators in their digital journey with support in digital acquisition. A significant portion of the company's user acquisition comes through the telecom operators themselves, because it is a seamless process for an operator such as MTN to send messages encouraging its customers to sign up to StarNews to watch social media personalities, including music artists, comedians and influencers. Sign-up is streamlined for convenience and is followed by a straightforward payment mechanism that allows users to pay with their airtime; this works across six different networks, each offering various bundles, weekly subscriptions and daily plans.

The model also determines what content travels well over it. Alongside creator-produced material, StarNews features deeply hyperlocal content spanning topics such as motorbike taxis in Cameroon or food recipes in Ivory Coast, plus genre-specific shows including the footballing competition African Cup of Nations and a program modeled after a singing reality competition, The Voice. StarNews leverages its operator partnerships to raise awareness and significantly boost user activations for these shows: in the 2021 edition of the African Cup of Nations (AFCON) the platform produced exclusive content straight from the event and acquired 600,000 new users, and the company's concert sponsorships at one point attracted 500,000 subscribers by offering them the chance to attend a Fally Ipupa concert.
