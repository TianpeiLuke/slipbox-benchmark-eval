# Digital Twins Deployed for Sustainability Goals

The first trend IoT Analytics highlights is that companies are deploying digital twins to meet sustainability goals, with the pursuit of those goals acting as a tailwind macro factor for the digital twin market. Sustainability was discussed in approximately 21% of recent CEO earnings calls and has remained a consistent topic throughout 2023.

The mechanism is that digital twins simulate real-world conditions and deliver real-time information, so organisations can optimise resource usage, reducing carbon emissions and improving supply and transportation networks. Survey evidence supports the link: according to Capgemini Research Institute's 2022 digital twins report, 57% of organisations agreed that one of the key drivers for their digital twin investments was improving their sustainability, and 51% agreed that digital twins would help achieve their organisation's environmental sustainability goals.

A former VP at an industrial automation vendor quoted in the report frames the scope over which the relation holds: "Digital solutions provide the visibility, analysis, and insight needed to address the challenges inherent in sustainability goals. A digital twin strategy as part of an overall digitalization plan can be a crucial capability for asset-intensive industries and needs to encompass the entire asset lifecycle, process, and value chain from design and operations through maintenance and strategic business planning." The worked example is Aden, a Chinese integrated facility service provider, which created a digital twin for one of its commercial centres in Chengdu, China, designed to help facility managers inspect, maintain and repair building assets; its 3D simulations assist managers in visualising, predicting and optimising energy consumption, with expected benefits including lower annual energy consumption, water usage and waste.
