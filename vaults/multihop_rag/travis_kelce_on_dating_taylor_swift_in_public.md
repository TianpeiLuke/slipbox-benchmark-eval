# Travis Kelce On Dating Taylor Swift In Public

In an interview with WSJ Magazine published on 20 November 2023, Travis Kelce spoke about his relationship with Taylor Swift, saying he admires her as a musician: "I've never been a man of words. Being around her, seeing how smart Taylor is, has been f***ing mind-blowing. I'm learning every day."

He also acknowledged the intense public attention his private life now attracts, noting he had "never dated anyone with that kind of aura around them" — but said he was not letting the media affect the relationship, and took his cue from how Swift handles it. "But at the same time, I'm not running away from any of it," he said. "The scrutiny she gets, how much she has a magnifying glass on her, every single day, paparazzi outside her house, outside every restaurant she goes to, after every flight she gets off, and she's just living, enjoying life. When she acts like that I better not be the one acting all strange."
