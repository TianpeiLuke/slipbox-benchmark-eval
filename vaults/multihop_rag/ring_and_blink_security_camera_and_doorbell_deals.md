# Ring And Blink Security Camera And Doorbell Deals

Amazon's Ring and Blink home-security devices were discounted across the anti-Prime Big Deal Day sales at Best Buy, headlined by the Ring Floodlight Cam Wired Pro at $200, down from $250 — 20% off.

The Ring Floodlight Cam Wired Pro delivers high-quality video with adjustable, accurate motion detection, 2,000 lumens of light and a good digital zoom; it offers smart alerts for people or motion and works with the Ring or Alexa apps but not any other smart home platform, and was $200 at both Amazon and Best Buy. Blink's Video Doorbell was half off at Best Buy at just $29.99; the budget-friendly doorbell only features 1080p video but offers all the basics, including motion-activated recording and alerts, night vision and two-way audio. The Blink Mini indoor camera was on sale for $19.99, $15 off, and mounts easily to a wall, though cloud storage costs extra without a Blink subscription. The new Blink Outdoor 4 was $71.99, $48 off; the battery-powered 1080p camera offers new features like person detection as well as a wider field of view. Alternatively the third-gen Blink Outdoor with a Sync Module 2 started at $44.99, $45 off; the older camera does not offer person detection but can still capture 1080p video and detect motion. The battery-powered Ring Stick Up Cam, usable both indoors and outside, was on sale for $59.99, $40 off; it captures 1080p video and supports both motion detection and color night vision.
