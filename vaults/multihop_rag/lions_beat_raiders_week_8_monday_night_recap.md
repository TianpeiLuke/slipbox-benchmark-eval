# Lions Beat Raiders 26-14 Behind Jahmyr Gibbs' Breakout Monday Night

Detroit beat Las Vegas 26-14 on "Monday Night Football" on Monday, Oct. 30, 2023 at Ford Field, bouncing back from a blowout loss in Baltimore in a game that felt more one-sided than the score indicates. The Lions out-gained the Raiders by 329 yards, but critical mistakes left points on the board: the offense struggled to convert red zone chances into touchdowns in the first half, and two second-half turnovers deep in Raiders territory — including a Jared Goff pick-six — kept Las Vegas in the game.

The story of the night was Lions rookie Jahmyr Gibbs, who finally broke through after entering the season with sky-high expectations. Tasked with leading the backfield in David Montgomery's absence, Gibbs exploded for 189 scrimmage yards and a touchdown, the first 100-yard rushing performance of his career. Amon-Ra St. Brown notched his third consecutive 100-yard game despite entering Monday questionable with an illness. Detroit's defense dominated: the Raiders were abysmal offensively outside a second-quarter touchdown drive led by Josh Jacobs, and Jimmy Garoppolo completed just 10 passes, badly missing a wide-open Davante Adams on multiple occasions. Adams was seen slamming his helmet down on the sideline in the fourth quarter after an 11-yard performance that better quarterback play could have made exponentially better. The quarter-by-quarter line was Raiders 0-7-7-0 for 14 and Lions 3-13-7-3 for 26.

Kickoff was 8:15 p.m. ET. Detroit entered its bye in sole possession of the No. 2 seed in the NFC, while Las Vegas dropped a second consecutive game against an NFC North opponent and faced what might be a must-win game against the Giants in Week 9 to linger in the AFC playoff race. The Lions had won four of their last five matchups with the Raiders, though Las Vegas won the previous meeting in 2019.
