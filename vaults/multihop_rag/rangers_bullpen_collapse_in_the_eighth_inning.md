# The Rangers' Bullpen Collapse In The Eighth Inning

The Texas bullpen turned a 4-1 deficit into 7-1 in the top of the eighth inning of World Series Game 2 on 29 October 2023, as the Diamondbacks teed off on Chris Stratton and Martín Pérez to put the game out of reach.

Stratton came in with no room for error, given the difference between a three-run and a four-run deficit, and Tommy Pham put him to work by running up a full count on eight pitches before singling to start the inning, going 4-for-4 on the day. Lourdes Gurriel Jr. bunted Pham to second with one out. Stratton, who did not seem sharp, got ahead of Alek Thomas 0-2 but could not put him away until pitch number nine, a curve on the corner for strike three — the first time an Arizona hitter had struck out all game, which the announcers noted was maybe the number one reason the Diamondbacks were in front.

Pérez then replaced Stratton with pinch-hitter Pavin Smith due up, but Emmanuel Rivera came in to hit instead and drew a walk on a full count, putting runners on first and second with two outs. Pérez walked Geraldo Perdomo on a 3-2 count to load the bases after a borderline pitch the crowd booed, showing little command. Ketel Marte then knocked in a two-run, two-out single for a 6-1 lead, and Corbin Carroll followed with an RBI single scoring Perdomo for 7-1. Gabriel Moreno struck out on three pitches to finally end the inning, but the damage was done and the game felt all but out of reach for Texas.
