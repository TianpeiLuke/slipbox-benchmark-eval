# The Rain Delay And DLS Position In The First T20I

Rain stopped play in the first T20I with West Indies on 108-4 after 12.4 overs and requiring 64 runs off 44 balls — crucially, six runs ahead of the Duckworth Lewis Stern rate, so that if the rain did not abate the home side would take the spoils.

The heavens cracked out of nowhere and the players scurried off as the covers were hauled on, with Hope on 27 and Powell on 6. The rain then began tailing off and a rope was dragged around the outfield to take off excess water. The Guardian's man in Barbados, Simon Burnton, reported a 9.35 p.m. restart with no overs lost, putting play about eight minutes away; the live blog understood 10.10 p.m. local time — 2.10 a.m. in London — to be the cut off for the match.
