# Georgiana Laudi Recommends "Loved"

Georgiana (Gia) Laudi recommends "Loved: How to Rethink Marketing for Tech Products," by Martina Lauchengco, calling it exactly the comprehensive guide the startup scene so desperately needed. Laudi helps SaaS businesses grow through her agency, Forget the Funnel, which is also the title of the book she co-wrote with her co-founder Claire Suellentrop, whose premise is to present readers with "a customer-led approach for driving predictable, recurring revenue."

Her grounds begin with the problem the book addresses: product marketing, particularly in tech, is one of the most foundational and yet somehow wildly misunderstood disciplines. Lauchengco manages to strike the perfect blend of accessible and comprehensive, so that everyone from founders to practitioners understands the critical role product marketing plays in successful companies and what good looks like at every stage of growth.

What Laudi appreciates most is the ordering of priorities: when it comes to marketing and driving product adoption, even though Lauchengco introduces four fundamentals of product marketing, the first and most critical to get right is customer insight — almost unheard of given that the target market for this book is an industry where engineering and scale reign supreme.
