# James Conner Week 13: Sit Him, Because Kyler Murray's Return Changed The Math

CBS Sports' Week 13 verdict on James Conner is Sit Him, and the stated reason is that Kyler Murray's return has changed the math on Conner's expectations. The grounds: in three games since Murray came back, Arizona has become pass-heavy at a 63.7% pass rate — a stark contrast from Conner's five games before injury at a 55.6% pass rate. While Conner has had some red-zone carries in those games with Murray, his offensive line hasn't kept him clean, especially near the goal line, and meanwhile Murray has a rushing touchdown in three straight games, siphoning the scoring chances.

The matchup adds a second layer. Conner will face his former team, Pittsburgh, which has held all but four running backs to less than 15 PPR points this year, and the ones who notched 15-plus scored a touchdown. If that does not make you feel good about Conner, the article adds that he has averaged 0.42 yards before contact on his runs since Murray's return — 44th out of 47 running backs, with only Breece Hall and Derrick Henry worse — and that Pittsburgh's unit held the Bengals and Browns in consecutive weeks to 0.5 yards before contact or less. The conclusion: if you start Conner, don't expect an efficient day and pray to Ye Olde Fantasy Gods for a garbage-time touchdown; the author would rather trust Jaylen Warren, Chuba Hubbard and A.J. Dillon. Scope: Conner is an Arizona running back, #6, age 28 with 7 years of experience; the weekly breakdown lists the matchup at Pittsburgh with Pittsburgh favored by 5.5 and an over/under of 41, the opponent 8th versus running backs, projected points of 10.1 and an RB rank of 27th; year-to-date he has 526 rushing yards, 14 receptions, 36 receiving yards, 2 touchdowns and 10.3 Fantasy points per game, with projections powered by SportsLine.
