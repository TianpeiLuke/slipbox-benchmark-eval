# Cruise's California Permit Suspension

The week reported in TechCrunch's 2023-10-30 roundup was an absolutely wild one on the robotaxi front, and more specifically for GM's self-driving car subsidiary Cruise. It started with the California Department of Motor Vehicles suspending Cruise's driverless and deployment permits, with the California Public Utilities Commission following shortly after.

The effect was to end the company's robotaxi operations in San Francisco — just months after Cruise received the last necessary permit to commercialize its operations.
