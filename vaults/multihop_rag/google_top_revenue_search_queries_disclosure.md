# Google's Most Lucrative Search Terms, Unsealed At Trial

Judge Amit Mehta ruled to make public a list that provides a glimpse of which search terms make Google the most money: 20 terms that were lucrative for the week of September 22, 2018. Revenue per search term, how many queries each of those terms got, and a separate list of popular search terms ordered by queries rather than revenue were all redacted, so the disclosure is a bare ranking without magnitudes.

The visible list, in order, is: iPhone 8; iPhone 8 plus; Auto insurance; Car insurance; Cheap flights; Car insurance quotes; DirecTV; Online colleges; AT&T; Hulu; iPhone; Uber; Spectrum; Comcast; Xfinity; Insurance quotes; Free credit report; Cheap car insurance; AARP; LifeLock.

TechCrunch read little surprise into it. Google and Apple have a long and mutually beneficial relationship even while competing, so three Apple search-related queries bringing in the big bucks is unsurprising — not least since September 22, 2017 was the official release date of the iPhone 8. Queries like "car insurance," "cheap flights" and "credit report" are perennial favorites and speak to how much Google dominates vertical search, that is, search in very specific market categories. As for LifeLock, the big Equifax data breach of 2017 was a hot topic in September 2017 and LifeLock was making a big push to win business with people who wanted to buy identity theft protection.
