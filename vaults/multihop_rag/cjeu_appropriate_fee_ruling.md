# The CJEU "Appropriate Fee" Ruling

The "appropriate fee" is a line in a ruling handed down by the bloc's top court, the CJEU, earlier this year, on which Meta is relying to justify charging for an ad-free service. In it, the judges allowed the possibility — caveated with "if necessary" — of another caveat, an "appropriate fee," being charged for an equivalent alternative service, that is, one that lacks tracking and profiling.

Both caveats define the concept's boundaries, and they are where the dispute sits: the legal fight against Meta's continued tracking and profiling of users will hinge on what's necessary and appropriate in this context.
