# Tech Layoffs In June 2023

June 2023's largest tech layoff was at Grab, which announced in the week of June 23 that it had laid off more than a thousand people, or 11% of its staff.

The month opened with Spotify announcing on June 5 that it would cut 200 jobs in its podcast unit, a workforce reduction of 2%, coming just a few months after the company announced a significant wave of layoffs. On June 7 Better.com announced it had laid off its real estate team, though it is not clear how many people were impacted, and at least 14 people were laid off from the rocket engine startup Ursa Major; Ursa Major did not respond to TechCrunch's inquiry into the layoffs or the number of people affected, and LinkedIn lists 292 people as currently working at the company. On June 15 Tada — operated by VCNC, a subsidiary of the Korean ride-sharing company Socar — announced it planned to let go of at least 50% of its staff, about 45 people. Mojocare announced on June 18 that it would lay off some 150 employees, a majority of its workforce. Olx Group announced on June 20 that it had cut around 800 jobs globally, as the company started to close operations of its automotive business unit Olx Autos in some markets. On June 29 Plex announced it had laid off approximately 20% of its staff, or 37 employees, and Niantic announced it had laid off 230 employees, one year after laying off around 90.
