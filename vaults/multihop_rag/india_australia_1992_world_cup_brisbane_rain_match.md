# India vs Australia At The 1992 World Cup: The Rain-Affected One-Run Defeat

In the 1992 World Cup meeting, rain spoiled not just the match but India's winning chances, and India lost by just one run on a revised target. Australia set a target of 237 runs in 50 overs, losing nine wickets in the process, with Dean Jones scoring 90 runs off 108 balls; Kapil Dev and Manoj Prabhakar picked up three wickets each for India.

In the second innings rain interrupted play after 16.2 overs and the revised target was recalculated to 236 off 47 overs. India needed 5 runs off 4 balls, but were bowled out due to two run-outs on the very last ball. Mohammad Azharuddin scored a brilliant 93 runs off 102 balls for India.
