# The Sandwich Generation: Parenting Your Own Parents

The "sandwich generation" is The Guardian's 10 October 2023 name for today's midlifers who find themselves simultaneously parenting their children and their own parents — "In one breath we'll be begging our children to swap French fries for vegetables, and in the next breath we'll be urging our parents to exchange cake for sardines."

The defining feature is that parenting no longer starts and stops with our children, nor is it confined to those who have children: in a time of unrelenting change and ever-extending life, most of us will at some stage find ourselves "parenting" our own parents, and many — particularly those who had families later — will be doing both at once.

The condition that sharpens it is technological. The relentless pace of technology, accelerated by the Covid-19 pandemic, has made everything infinitely more complicated, leaving many older (and not-so-old) people baffled and frustrated; the author reports witnessing friends and family dashing to help parents with poorly designed online banking systems, unruly fridge alarms, "upgraded" software that has changed beyond recognition, apps and phones that do not function as expected, and numerous other technical challenges.
