# Polina Marinova Pompliano Recommends "It's What I Do"

Polina Marinova Pompliano recommends "It's What I Do: A Photographer's Life of Love and War," by Lynsey Addario, as a memoir that documents the human cost of war in the chaotic times we are living through. It was her newsletter The Profile that led Pompliano to become an author: "After years of profiling hundreds of fascinating people, I asked myself: What distinguishes the great from the truly exceptional?" — which led to her first book, "Hidden Genius: The Secret Ways of Thinking That Power the World's Most Successful People."

Her grounds are Addario's record and the transmission of feeling through her work. War photojournalist Lynsey Addario has covered every major conflict and humanitarian crisis on the planet, and as she sees the destruction and pain through the lens of her camera, her images translate that intense emotion to people across the globe; her memoir is the story of how she risks her life to tell the stories of ordinary people living in extraordinarily dangerous places.

What Pompliano finds decisive is that despite witnessing so much tragedy and brutality, Addario has never lost her ability to see the good in humanity. Her favorite quote from the book is: "I choose to live in peace and witness war—to experience the worst in people but to remember the beauty."
