# The Paper Tigers (2020): A Middle-Aged Kung Fu Comedy

The Paper Tigers is Bao Tran's (Tran Quoc Bao's) 2020 kung fu action comedy, running 1h 48m, starring Alain Uy, Ron Yuan (Mulan) and Mykel Shannon Jenkins as the eponymous trio: three former martial arts prodigies who, after a lifetime of strenuous training and hard fighting, have grown into beleaguered middle-aged nobodies. When their master is murdered, the three swear an oath to avenge his memory and bring his killer to justice.

Polygon adds a genre caution to keep the premise from misleading: if that sounds serious, the film in fact falls into the Apatowian camp of Dumb Man comedy. It is available to stream free with ads on Tubi, free with a library card on Hoopla and Kanopy, or for digital rental or purchase at Amazon, Apple and Google Play.
