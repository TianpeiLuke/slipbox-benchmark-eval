# OpenAI Employee Revolt And Sutskever's Reversal

Nearly 500 of OpenAI's roughly 770 employees signed a letter saying they might quit unless the startup's board resigned and reappointed the ousted Sam Altman — a number that climbed to over 650 later that Monday — and, remarkably, the signatories included Ilya Sutskever, the chief scientist who had taken part in the removal.

Sutskever publicly reversed himself, publishing a post on X suggesting that he regretted his decision to remove Altman and that he would do everything in his power to reinstate Altman as CEO: "I deeply regret my participation in the board's actions. I never intended to harm OpenAI. I love everything we've built together and I will do everything I can to reunite the company." This was a reversal of the position he had taken days earlier. At an OpenAI all-hands meeting on the Friday afternoon, Sutskever had defended Altman's ouster, dismissing suggestions that pushing Altman out amounted to a "hostile takeover" and claiming that it was necessary to protect OpenAI's mission of "making AI beneficial to humanity."
