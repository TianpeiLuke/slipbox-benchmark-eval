# Ukraine 2023 Counteroffensive Fell Short of Expectations

Ukrainian military officials have conceded that hopes and expectations of a great breakthrough in the 2023 counteroffensive were not met. A year earlier, Ukraine's international military support was solid, with NATO pledging to support Kyiv "as long as it takes" as it defended itself against Russia's invasion launched in February 2022.

Over the summer of 2023, however, the challenge facing Ukraine's forces became glaringly obvious as they struggled to break through heavily fortified Russian positions and lines of defences along a swathe of the 600-mile-long front line across the south and east of the country. After liberating a handful of villages in the summer, Ukrainian and Russian forces were caught in largely attritional battles, with neither side making significant gains.

Ukraine's leadership nonetheless says steep losses have been inflicted on Russian forces and that its forces have made vital progress in other areas, such as the Black Sea: Ukraine's audacious attacks on Russian bases and assets in Crimea that summer prompted the Russian navy to withdraw a number of warships from Sevastopol, handing Kyiv a victory in the battle of the Black Sea.
