# Holiday 2023 Shipping Deadline Planning

The Verge's December 18, 2023 guide collects the upcoming domestic shipping deadlines for various retailers and shipment carriers, so readers sending gifts via traditional mail can ensure packages arrive before Christmas, Kwanzaa, or whatever holiday they celebrate this time of year.

Its framing is the annual pattern the deadlines are meant to defuse: the same thing happens year after year, as things get busy with work, school, and life in general until you're suddenly up against the clock racing your gifts to the local post office, FedEx store, or UPS location. The stated purpose is to help with planning your next move as you begin sending out holiday packages and, hopefully, to avoid some holiday stress.
