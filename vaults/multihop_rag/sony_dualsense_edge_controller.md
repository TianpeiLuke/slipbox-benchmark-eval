# Sony DualSense Edge Controller

Just as Microsoft does, Sony makes its own pro-grade controller: the $199.99 DualSense Edge, which is compatible with the PS5. Polygon notes the plentiful similarities to the Xbox Elite Series 2 but argues it is more helpful to speak about how the Edge differs.

Two differences stand out. Its stick modules can be replaced entirely, which makes the DualSense Edge a little more user-repairable, and the PS5's interface for the Edge is intuitive, making remapping controls and setting schemes the breeze it should be. The scope condition on PC is important: while the Edge is compatible with PC, Sony and Steam have not developed drivers that let gamers take full advantage of its back paddles and swappable control schemes.
