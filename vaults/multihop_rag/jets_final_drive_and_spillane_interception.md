# Jets' Final Drive And The Spillane Interception

The Jets' late comeback bid against the Raiders on Nov. 12, 2023 died on a Robert Spillane interception at 11:16 p.m., and their last-gasp drive ended at 11:26 p.m. when Zach Wilson's final pass, thrown after he was chased out of the pocket, was batted down and the Raiders held on to win 16-12.

The sequence began with a turnover in New York's favor. At 11:03 p.m., just as the Raiders seemed to be inching toward putting the game away, Josh Jacobs lost a fumble at the Jets' 25 — forced by Jamien Sherwood and recovered by Ashtyn Davis — and the turnover went to review as a potential massive swing. At 11:06 p.m. the call on the field stood and Zach Wilson opened the drive with a throw across the middle to Lazard for 17 yards to the Jets 41. At 11:07 p.m. a three-yard catch by Garrett Wilson was followed by a quick pass to Breece Hall, who made defenders miss for 10 yards to the Raiders 46. At 11:08 p.m. Uzomah stayed up after a big hit for six yards, play stopped for an injury to Jakorian Bennett, and Wilson's deep shot to a double-covered Garrett Wilson was broken up. At 11:10 p.m. a dump-off to Tyler Conklin kept the drive alive with a first down at the 36, and at 11:11 p.m., after missing Lazard and a low Conklin catch two yards shy, Wilson connected with Garrett Wilson for six up to the 22 and a fresh set of downs at the two-minute warning.

Then came the pick. At 11:16 p.m. Hall could get only two yards on a check down to the right out to the 20, and on the next play Wilson tried to get the ball to Lazard and was intercepted by Robert Spillane, who returned it to the Raiders' 40 — with 1:14 left and the Jets holding three timeouts, the game was all but over. New York used those timeouts: at 11:17 p.m. Jacobs gained five and the Jets called a quick timeout; at 11:18 p.m. Jacobs was stuffed for no gain and the Jets used their second timeout with 1:05 left, leaving the Raiders a pivotal third-and-5. At 11:19 p.m. Las Vegas trusted O'Connell to put the game away with a pass, but his attempt to Mayer was batted down by Jermaine Johnson, saving the Jets a timeout and forcing a punt, before the Raiders took a delay of game penalty. At 11:20 p.m., even after backing up five yards, Cole still booted it too far and it bounced into the end zone for a touchback, giving the Jets the ball at the 20 with 53 seconds left. At 11:22 p.m. Wilson spiked the first pass with no one open, then hit Conklin on a dump-off for nine yards and again for an impressive leaping grab at the Raiders' 45, with the Jets using their last timeout at 12 seconds. At 11:24 p.m. the Hail Marys began, the first to Gipson sailing out of bounds, and with five seconds left the Raiders called their second timeout.
