# Capitol's Hip-Hop And R&B Executive Build-Out

Michelle Jubelirer says that to make significant inroads with hip-hop and R&B company-wide, CMG needed experts in those areas and fresh perspective on the executive team, and names the hires that followed. EVP of Capitol Music Group Orlando Wharton relaunched Priority Records and signed Kodak Black; Motown SVP of A&R Kenoe Jordan has been working closely with YoungBoy NBA; and Gordan Dillard brought management and artist development experience from his role at SALXCO. CMG also promoted Black executives from within, particularly in the areas of A&R and marketing.

One position remains open: the company is still searching for the right leader for Motown and will not stop until it finds one befitting the stature and legacy of such an important label. Jubelirer adds a boundary on how the build-out should be read: ultimately CMG does not separate music based on genre, but it does have a number of labels, Blue Note, Motown, Priority, Astralwerks, Capitol Christian and Tamla, so artists have real options there.
