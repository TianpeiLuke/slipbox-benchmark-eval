# Flipboard's ActivityPub Federation Launch

On Monday 18 December 2023, the social magazine app Flipboard announced it had integrated with ActivityPub, joining the decentralized social web that includes Twitter/X rival Mastodon and other apps — a web Instagram Threads had also recently joined via the same networking protocol. In its initial phase, select Flipboard accounts will be discoverable and can be followed by the millions of users of decentralized social apps including Mastodon, and over time all profiles on Flipboard will be available in the fediverse, as this network of decentralized social apps is known.

Flipboard is initially testing the integration with select accounts, including publishers like Semafor, Pitchfork, Fast Company, Medium, LGBTQ Nation, Refinery 29, Digiday, Polygon, SPIN, Kotaku, Frommer's, The Verge, Smithsonian Magazine, The Root, ScienceAlert and AFAR Media. While many are focused on news, the debut list also includes nonprofits such as The News Literacy Project and the education-focused news site The 74.

CEO Mike McCue framed the announcement as the start of a process rather than its completion: "What we're announcing on Monday is basically our roadmap for how we will be rolling out ActivityPub, and effectively tearing down the walls around our own walled garden."
