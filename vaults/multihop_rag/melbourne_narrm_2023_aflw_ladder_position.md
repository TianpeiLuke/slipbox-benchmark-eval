# Melbourne (Narrm)'s 2023 AFLW Ladder Position

Reigning premiers Narrm reclaimed top spot on the AFLW ladder — and their position as flag favourites — by defeating third-placed North Melbourne by 23 points, 5.7 (37) to 2.2 (14), at Ikon Park on the Saturday night, The Age reported on 20 October 2023.

It was a hard-fought, low-scoring battle between two powerhouse sides that play a similar game style: each set up well, are fast and like a good dose of contested marking. Narrm controlled more of the game by tightening around the contest and being cleaner going forward, and defended extremely well by suffocating the opportunities North created. The Demons scored just one goal a quarter in the second half — Tyla Hanks in the third and Eden Zanker in the fourth — while the Kangaroos were goalless for three terms after leading by five points at the first change, their opening goals coming from Niamh Martin and Tess Craven. A crafty goal from Demons skipper Kate Hore to open the second term put her side up, and Melbourne never gave up the lead.

Hanks was pivotal for the Demons with 26 disposals, seven tackles and one goal, while her midfield partner Olivia Purcell had 28 touches, five clearances and five tackles. For the Roos, Ash Riddell led the disposal count with 31, and skipper Emma Kearney was crucial in defence. Hanks said that with the state of the competition they just had to take it week by week, but it was good to get the win: "We knew we were up against the best tonight. It's a nice feeling tonight."
