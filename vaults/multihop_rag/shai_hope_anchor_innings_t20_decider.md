# Shai Hope's Anchor Innings In The T20 Decider

Shai Hope's 43 off 43 balls steered West Indies to their target in the deciding T20I, an exemplary anchoring innings that the live blog identified as the wicket England really needed.

At 16 overs West Indies were 112-4 with Hope on 29, and he opened the face to steer Rehan Ahmed for his first boundary; Rehan had struggled as the series progressed, and later in the over Powell muscled him over long-on for six, leaving West Indies 21 runs from a hat-trick of T20 series wins that year — South Africa away, India and England at home. In the 17th over, with West Indies 114-5, Hope was quietly guiding his side towards the finish line. The Guardian's report noted West Indies were 62 for three halfway through their innings, and if England had had 15 more runs at the same point they had also made a complete hash of the rest of it — a trap West Indies avoided thanks to Hope's 43-ball 43 as he again steered his side to their target. The batting display may have lacked their usual flamboyance, but until the final ball it was entirely appropriate for the occasion.
