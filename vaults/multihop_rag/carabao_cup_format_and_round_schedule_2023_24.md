# Carabao Cup 2023/24 Format, Entry Points And Round Schedule

The EFL Cup, called the Carabao Cup for sponsorship reasons, features all 92 participating clubs from the top four tiers of English football — Premier League (20 clubs), Championship (24), League One (24) and League Two (24) — entering in stages, per Sporting News on Dec. 19, 2023.

The entry preconditions are that the 12 Premier League clubs not involved in European cup competitions joined in the second round, and the remaining eight sides joined in the third round. The round schedule ran: First Round, Aug. 8-9 and Aug. 16; Second Round, Aug. 29-30; Third Round (Round of 32), Sept. 27-28; Fourth Round (Round of 16), Oct. 31 - Nov. 1; Quarterfinals, Dec. 20-21; Semifinals, Jan. 9-10 for leg one and Jan. 23-24 for leg two; Final, Sunday, Feb. 25.

Every round is single-elimination except for the semifinals, which are played over two legs. There is no extra time up to and including the quarterfinals — page 580 of the regulations — with matches going straight to penalties if the score is level after 90 minutes. Extra time will, however, be in effect for the semifinal second legs, with no away goals tiebreaker, and for the final, if necessary.
