# The Ireland Series Loss Backlash As All Blacks Motivation

The Roar argued on Oct. 26, 2023 that where the Springboks play for their country, the All Blacks were playing for themselves as much as anyone — and for the generation of players and leaders who would call time on their international careers at week's end — which is why the World Cup final was personal for the men in black.

The framing question is how New Zealand could withstand and overcome the hitherto unshakable wave of momentum and spirit the Springboks play with under Rassie Erasmus in that weekend's final. The grounds offered for the All Blacks' personal motivation are the backlash and toxicity that followed their historic home series loss to Ireland, described as a humbling and public reminder of the expectation of being an All Black — All Blacks social media managers were forced to delete a thousand comments an hour following the humbling series loss in Wellington.

The warrant is that the hurt persisted and became fuel: the pain of the days and weeks after that dark July, as well as the bumps and bruises along the way — including as recently as August when they suffered their biggest defeat to the Boks to lose the inaugural and prestigious Qatar Cup — still ran deep.
