# Fifth Third 1.67% Card Cardholder Benefits

The Fifth Third 1.67% Card's ongoing benefits aren't jaw-dropping, but few no-annual-fee cash back credit card benefits are. Four are listed. Cell phone protection: if your cell phone is lost or stolen you'll receive up to $800 per claim, with a maximum of two claims or $1,000 per 12-month period and a $50 deductible, provided you use the card to pay your monthly phone bill — described as excellent for a no-annual-fee credit card.

Contactless payments: you can use the card touch-free with eligible credit card terminals, and add it to Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Samsung Pay. Card locking: the Fifth Third mobile app lets you effectively activate and deactivate the credit card at will, similar to flipping a switch, so if the card goes missing you can immediately lock it until you find it. Mastercard ID Theft Protection: Mastercard monitors the internet, including the dark and deep web, to find any of your sensitive information, including compromised credentials — a benefit all Mastercard credit card customers have.
