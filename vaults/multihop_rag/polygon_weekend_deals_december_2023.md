# Polygon's Weekend Deals Roundup, December 9 2023

Coming up for air from its coverage of The Game Awards, Polygon published its weekend deals roundup on 2023-12-09, pointing readers to discounts on a variety of 2023 Game Awards-winning titles on Steam.

Other highlights it flagged that weekend were a sitewide sale on Blu-Rays from The Criterion Collection, and the Xbox Series X matching its Black Friday price at Walmart and Amazon. Polygon notes the format is a standing one: as it does every weekend, it rounded up a collection of favourite deals from the worlds of gaming and entertainment, along with some of the best-selling products that had recently appeared on its site.
