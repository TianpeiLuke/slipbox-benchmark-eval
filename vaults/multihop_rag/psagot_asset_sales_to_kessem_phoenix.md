# Psagot's Asset Sales To Kessem And The Phoenix Holdings Group

Psagot recently completed the sale of one third of its mutual funds business and its entire portfolio management business to Kessem, of The Phoenix Holdings group, for NIS 200 million.

The sale of the mutual funds will yield a gain to Psagot of NIS 90 million, to be posted in its fourth quarter financials. That amount is equivalent to the entire company's current market cap — which, if you will, means the rest of its business is valued at zero.
