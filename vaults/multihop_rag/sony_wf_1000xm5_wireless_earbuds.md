# Sony WF-1000XM5 Wireless Earbuds

Sony's WF-1000XM5 wireless earbuds went on sale for $278 during October Prime Day, down from $300 — a $22 discount and their best price yet — and were still going for that price two days later, after the event officially ended.

Engadget calls them the best wireless earbuds on the market right now: Sony improved upon its already remarkable buds with a more comfortable fit, better sound quality and features like adaptive sound and Speak-to-Chat.

The headphone roundup adds detail and a slightly different framing of the size of the cut: $278 is only about $20 off, but it marks the first discount seen for the top pick in Engadget's wireless earbuds buying guide. The pair scored 87 earlier this year — like Sony's over-ear models it comes with a range of useful features, powerful noise cancellation and a warm yet detailed default sound, and the design is much more agreeable than previous Sony earphones.
