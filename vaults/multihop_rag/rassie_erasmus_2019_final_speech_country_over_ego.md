# Erasmus' 2019 Final Speech: Country Over Ego

Rassie Erasmus argued before the 2019 World Cup final that worrying about your own mistakes is a symptom of ego, and that a Springbok's job on the day is to represent South Africa rather than himself — one of the great rugby speeches, delivered before the Springboks sealed a stunning 32-12 victory over England to claim their third William Webb Ellis Cup.

The claim and its warrant are in his own words: "You don't have the right today to worry about your mistakes. If you worry about your mistakes today, you've got ego problems." The grounds are what the players are actually playing for: "You are not representing yourself today. You are fighting for the things that happen in South Africa. You are tackling for the boy who didn't get the same opportunities that you got eventually."

Writing four years later on Oct. 26, 2023, The Roar observed that the "ego" Erasmus spoke about is what is driving the standards and expectations around the Springboks' bid to go back-to-back.
