# Hagler KO 11 Mugabi: Showtime Boxing's Maiden Broadcast

Marvin Hagler stopped John Mugabi in the eleventh round on March 10, 1986 at Caesars Palace, Las Vegas, in an undisputed middleweight title fight that was Showtime Boxing's maiden broadcast — the series hitting the ground running with one of the greatest middleweight championship fights of the modern era.

Hagler came in off his stunning third-round knockout of Thomas Hearns in April 1985, which had made him the best fighter in the world and, at long last, an authentic superstar. After 11 months off — by far his longest period of inactivity — "The Marvelous One" had a taste for blood and returned with a vengeance. John "The Best" Mugabi had earned his ring moniker: the Uganda-born power-puncher had a perfect record of 26 fights, 26 wins and 26 knockouts, with 10 of those stoppages coming in the first round, and he was moving up from super welterweight to middleweight having carried his power with him.

Mugabi got off to a great start, blasting Hagler with horrific power shots in the early rounds. The more experienced Hagler made the necessary adjustments and began punishing his man heavily in the sixth. Mugabi gave everything he had, but Hagler ended matters in the eleventh with a succession of crunching rights.
