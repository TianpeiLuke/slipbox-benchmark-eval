# Xbox Series S Game Pass Bundle Deal

A starter bundle that includes a 512GB capacity Xbox Series S and a three-month subscription to Game Pass Ultimate was $240 at Microsoft for Black Friday 2023, Engadget reported on 2023-11-19.

The console by itself has a list price of $300, and three months of access to Xbox's service will set you back $51 after this year's price hike — though the first month is often just $1 for new members.
