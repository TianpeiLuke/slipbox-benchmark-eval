# The "Near Me" Business Naming Trend

Thai Food Near Me is not the first business to adopt the Google-first naming convention: reminders of Google's kingmaker status in online discoverability are everywhere in cities across the country. Among the businesses The Verge's reporter was able to find were a chain of half a dozen Affordable Dentist Near Me's in Texas; an Antiques Near Me two hours outside New York City; seven Plumber Near Me businesses; a Phone Repair Near Me in Cape Cod, Massachusetts; a Psychic Near Me in Chicago; and more than 20 iterations of "Notary Near Me" across the US.

The scheme spreads by imitation. Felix Silva, who named his own shop Barber Shop Near Me, says an acquaintance selling Christmas trees rebranded his business to Christmas Trees Near Me; Silva's is also not the only Barber Shop Near Me, with shops of the same name in Oak Park, Illinois; Queens, New York; and Muskogee, Oklahoma, according to Google Maps. "It was a compliment. It was nice to see that in some way this inspires some people," Silva says of the Christmas tree rebrand.

The boundary condition is saturation. Silva is always thinking about what is next and what could give his business the edge to keep growing, and sometimes wonders whether an influx of "near me" businesses could water down the impact. But Google is Google, he says, and "near me" is wherever a customer is.
