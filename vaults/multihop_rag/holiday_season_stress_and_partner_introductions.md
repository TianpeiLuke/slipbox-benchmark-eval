# Holiday Season Stress And The Question Of Partner Introductions

The holiday season is often perceived as the most wonderful time of year, but survey data says otherwise for a large minority: in a recent poll by the American Psychological Association, cited by The Independent in December 2023, 41 per cent of US adults said their stress levels increased from November to January compared with other months of the year, and nearly 50 per cent of Americans described their stress level during the season as "moderate."

That backdrop is what makes introducing a new partner to your family over the holidays a genuine question rather than an obvious opportunity. People approaching Christmas, Hanukkah or New Year carry different concerns — from family drama at the dinner table to small spats with a mother about decorations — which raises the question of whether you really want to bring a partner into the mix. The Independent's framing is that while it is important to introduce your loved ones to your significant other eventually, relationship experts believe there are discussions to have beforehand, especially during the holidays.
