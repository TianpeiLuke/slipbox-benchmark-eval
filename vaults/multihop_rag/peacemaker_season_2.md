# Peacemaker Season 2

James Gunn has not forgotten Peacemaker alongside everything else in the DC universe with his name on it: according to the director, Christopher Smith will be making his return for season 2, sometime after Gunn wraps production on Superman: Legacy.

That sequencing sets the timing. Polygon's reading, as of 2023-10-24, is that it likely means season 2 will not come along until sometime in 2025 or even later.

The reassurance the outlet offers is minimal but real — at least audiences know it is out there.
