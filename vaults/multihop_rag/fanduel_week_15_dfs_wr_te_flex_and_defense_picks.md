# FanDuel Week 15 DFS Receiver, Tight End, Flex And Defense Picks

Sporting News filled out its Week 15 FanDuel DFS lineup with receivers Deebo Samuel ($8,400), Puka Nacua ($8,000) and Rashee Rice ($6,500), tight end Chig Okonkwo ($5,000), flex receiver Jayden Reed ($6,100) and the New York Giants defense ($3,500), publishing on Dec. 13, 2023.

Samuel (49ers @ Cardinals) was kept because he was not giving any reason to reconsider: he had back-to-back games with 30 fantasy points and had been a man on a mission since Thanksgiving, and should have no problems continuing that run against the Cardinals. Nacua (Rams vs. Commanders) was preferred partly on price: usually one of Nacua or Kupp has a big day, but this week it could be both; the rookie had been by far Los Angeles' most consistent weapon all season and Stafford continued to trust him even with Kupp back, and while he was not seeing 15-20 targets a game as earlier in the year, he was making the most of seven-plus opportunities. Against a terrible defense both receivers should see plenty of chances, and Nacua checked in at $200 less while being arguably better than Kupp in fantasy at that stage — in a game where every dollar counts, pocketing that extra $200 can really pay off at another roster spot. Rice (Chiefs @ Patriots) was the exception to the Chiefs' receiver issues: the rookie had been very impressive through the first 14 weeks of his NFL career, hauled in another touchdown on Sunday, and had been targeted 29 times over the past three games with two scores; New England would try to take away Travis Kelce, meaning opportunities could come even more often for Rice.

Okonkwo (Titans vs. Texans) had emerged as a nice weapon for rookie quarterback Will Levis with his target share ticking up as the season progressed, turning in 45 and 62 yards in Weeks 12 and 13 on 11 targets in that span, with only a touchdown missing; in a brutal week for main-slate tight end matchups, Sporting News liked him to outperform his listed salary against a Texans team that had struggled defending the position recently. Reed (Packers vs. Buccaneers) was taken at flex with the status of Christian Watson (hamstring) hanging over the Packers: Reed was more than capable of a big day whether Watson played or not, since Watson's presence had capped Reed's ceiling recently but Tampa's secondary was still having major issues, and with Jordan Love appearing to have turned a corner, Reed would be the primary beneficiary. The Giants defense (@ Saints) was chosen because there is a lot of bad offense across the NFL and, despite signing Derek Carr, the Saints were among it: the quarterback refused to sit out and deal with a laundry list of injuries clearly affecting him, teammates appeared fed up, and the Saints were upside-down on the deal with no end in sight — the Giants are not good, but have enough defensive pop to contain an offense that keeps spinning its tires.
