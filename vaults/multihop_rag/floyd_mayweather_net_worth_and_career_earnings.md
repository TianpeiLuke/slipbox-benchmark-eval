# Floyd Mayweather's $450 Million Net Worth And Billion-Dollar Career Earnings

Floyd Mayweather is the richest boxer in the world, Essentially Sports reported on 2023-10-18: the legendary undefeated boxer boasts a staggering net worth of $450 million, making the retired fighter the richest personality in boxing to ever exist. Despite retiring from the sport he occasionally fights in exhibition matches, generating millions each time, and across the span of his career he has made over a billion dollars.

The article also notes that Canelo's and McGregor's careers are intertwined with Mayweather. 'Money' faced both fighters at one point or another, leaving each with a boxing lesson: when Mayweather defeated Canelo it pushed the Mexican to hone his skills while starting a lifelong rivalry, and McGregor made his boxing debut against Mayweather, losing the fight but leaving with a fat stack. An accompanying image credit places Floyd Mayweather Jr. at a game between the New York Liberty and the Las Vegas Aces at Michelob Ultra Arena on June 29, 2023 in Las Vegas, Nevada, a game the Aces won 98-81.
