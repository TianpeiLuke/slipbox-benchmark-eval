# Building A BG3 Warlock: Race, Ability Scores, Pact

Polygon's recommended build order for a Baldur's Gate 3 Warlock is: pick a race for its weapon and armor proficiencies, set Charisma highest and Constitution second, take a subclass at level 1 without agonising over it, and prioritise the Eldritch Invocation at level 2.

Step one is race. Races in Baldur's Gate 3 do not really have any direct impact on your class, but certain races and subraces come with features that play well with certain classes, such as extra movement or proficiency with certain weapons — and for Warlocks it is best to focus on weapon and armor proficiencies. Drow Weapon Training adds weapons like rapiers and hand crossbows; the Civil Militia feature of Humans and Half-Elves adds spears, pikes, halberds, and glaives along with shields; Githyanki Martial Prodigy gives medium armor along with longswords and greatswords; and Dwarven Combat Training gets you battleaxes, handaxes, light hammers, and warhammers, with the Shield Dwarf subrace also granting Medium Armor. Step two is ability scores: when creating and levelling up a Warlock, Charisma is your most important stat because it drives spellcasting, and while the second stat depends somewhat on how you play outside combat, Constitution is a good option for the hit points, since Warlocks — like Sorcerers — have a tendency to be glass cannons. Make Charisma your highest stat and Constitution your second highest. Step three is the pact: you pick your subclass at level 1, but the pact chosen there is actually less important than the Eldritch Invocation you get at level 2; that said, go with the Great Old One for the most broadly useful features. The Eldritch Invocation powers up Eldritch Blast, the bread-and-butter spell for Warlocks, turning it into an Agonizing Blast.
