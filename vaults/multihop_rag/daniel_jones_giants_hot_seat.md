# Daniel Jones Giants Hot Seat

Yardbarker's Nov. 30, 2023 NFC hot-seat roundup named quarterback Daniel Jones as the New York Giants' hottest seat, on the claim that he has no way to dissuade Giants ownership from taking a quarterback high in the QB-rich 2024 NFL Draft.

The grounds are that Jones was out for the season with an ACL injury, so he could not build a case on the field, and that New York can — and will — get out of his $160M contract in 2025. The warrant is that a quarterback who cannot play and whose contract carries no binding future cost gives the front office every reason to draft his replacement.
