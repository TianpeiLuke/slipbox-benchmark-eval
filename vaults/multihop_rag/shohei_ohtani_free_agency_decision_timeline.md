# How Reporting On Ohtani's Decision Timeline Shifted During The Winter Meetings

Reporting on when Shohei Ohtani would choose a team moved back over the course of the 2023 winter meetings, ending with MLB Network's Jon Morosi reporting on Wednesday, Dec. 6 that Ohtani was expected to make his pick before the end of the upcoming weekend, per Sporting News' tracker.

On Sunday, Dec. 3, Morosi reported Ohtani was "likely to decide on a team within the next week," according to a source; Sporting News noted nothing gets the hot-stove crowd excited like a big move during the winter meetings, and that the biggest move of the offseason could come that week in Nashville or soon after. On Monday, Dec. 4, Jon Heyman of The New York Post reported that neither Shohei Ohtani nor Yoshinobu Yamamoto were expected to get a deal done before the final meetings on Wednesday — the baseball markets were waiting for the first major domino to fall in free agency, and it did not appear that would happen before the winter meetings concluded.

By Wednesday, Dec. 6, MLB free agency remained largely on hold as teams waited for Ohtani to sign; though it had at one point been reported he could sign during the winter meetings, there appeared to be a new timeline, with Morosi reporting the decision would come before the end of the upcoming weekend. The tracker noted the winter meetings end on Thursday.
