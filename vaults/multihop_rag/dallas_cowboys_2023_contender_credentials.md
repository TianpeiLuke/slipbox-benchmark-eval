# The Dallas Cowboys' 2023 Contender Credentials

The New York Times argued on 16 October 2023 that it does not see where Dallas is better or going to get considerably better, especially on offense, though it conceded this was a tough time to evaluate the Cowboys fairly because they were so bad against San Francisco on a big stage in their most recent game.

The additional ground offered is cultural rather than statistical: every time the Cowboys enjoy success, they seem to enjoy it a little too much, which the writer treats as a reflection of their owner.

An executive located the problem in identity and consistency: "Run defense and consistency on defense, and then offensively, what is their identity? They just are not very good. Their best games are when they play great defense and get turnovers, but that is not going to happen enough, especially against the good teams." The warrant on both sides is that a contender needs a repeatable formula, and Dallas's best path — great defense plus turnovers — is not repeatable often enough against strong opposition.
