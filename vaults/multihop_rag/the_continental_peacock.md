# The Continental: From the World of John Wick (Peacock)

The Continental, created by Greg Coolidge, Kirk Ward, and Shawn Simmons and starring Colin Woodell, Mishel Prada, and Hubert Point-Du Jour, is Polygon's Peacock pick in its 2023-10-25 roundup of the best new TV series on every streaming service.

Critic PV entered warily: the world of John Wick did not exactly need a prequel series, and it was hard to imagine a television show coming anywhere close to matching the exhilarating highs of the movies' action sequences. One thing gave hope early on — the hiring of action legend Larnell Stovall to lead the show's fight sequences.

Stovall and his team delivered. From the show's opening heist sequence to the incredible rooftop fight in the third episode, The Continental delivers reliable bangers throughout its three-episode run. It smartly avoids becoming too loyal to the movies' visual style, something the miniseries could never accurately mimic on a more limited budget; instead it leans into action appropriate for the era it is set in, and builds surprisingly strong characters and relationships in the brief time at the hotel.
