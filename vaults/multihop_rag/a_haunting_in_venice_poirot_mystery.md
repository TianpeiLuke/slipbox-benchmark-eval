# A Haunting In Venice

A Haunting in Venice became available to stream on Hulu in early November 2023. The horror mystery runs 1 hour 43 minutes, is directed by Kenneth Branagh, and stars Branagh alongside Michelle Yeoh and Jamie Dornan.

Polygon's assessment places it within Branagh's Poirot cycle: his adaptations have generally been a fun time even when they have problems — Death on the Nile is singled out — and A Haunting in Venice is his best yet, as his confidence as director and performer in this mode continues to grow. The verdict: it is perfect fall viewing.
