# Frank Reich's Head Coaching Record

As of Nov. 6, 2023, Frank Reich had posted a winning record of 41-40-1 across his 81 career regular-season games as a head coach, despite the Panthers being just 1-7 under his watch that season. The reason is a solid four-plus-year stint with the Colts, where he had three winning seasons in five campaigns and totaled 40-33-1 despite dealing with numerous changes at the quarterback position.

The year-by-year breakdown runs: 2018 Colts 10-6; 2019 Colts 7-9; 2020 Colts 11-5; 2021 Colts 9-8; 2022 Colts 3-5-1, after which he was fired following Week 9 of the season; and 2023 Panthers 1-7 — a combined Colts/Panthers total of 41-40-1.

Reich made the postseason in two of his first three seasons with the Colts, and the team posted a record of 1-2 in three contests across those two appearances. Sporting News also noted that Reich had a different Week 1 starter at quarterback in each of his first six seasons as a head coach; he was hoping to break that streak with Young in Carolina, but would first need to make it to a seventh season with the club.
