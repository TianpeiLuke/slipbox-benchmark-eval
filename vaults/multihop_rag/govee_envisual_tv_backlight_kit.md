# Govee Envisual TV Backlight Kit

The Govee Envisual TV backlight kit adds drama and atmosphere to nightly viewing by using a mountable camera to track what is on your screen, then projecting a matching light show behind the TV — almost as if the colors were bleeding off the screen.

Setup means attaching the camera to either the top or bottom of your TV. After that you can manage the LED lights with the Govee Home app, or trigger them by voice, thanks to Alexa and Google Assistant support.
