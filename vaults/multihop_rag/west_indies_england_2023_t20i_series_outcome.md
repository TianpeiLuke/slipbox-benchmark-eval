# West Indies Win The 2023 T20I Series 3-2

West Indies beat England by four wickets in the fifth and deciding T20 international on Dec. 21, 2023, taking the series 3-2 when Shai Hope pinged Chris Woakes over extra cover for six at 19.2 overs, with West Indies 133-6.

It was the 120th six of the series, and Hope played a gem of an innings, calmly anchoring West Indies in an increasingly nervous chase with a run-a-ball 43; England fought admirably to defend a below-par total and, but for Hope, would probably have done it. Cricket on TNT Sports posted that the win claimed the series 3-2 and a white-ball clean sweep. The Guardian's report framed the finish as the sequel to a very different match: Tuesday's ludicrous, record-smashing, high-scoring entertainment had been followed on the same surface by thrilling low-scoring entertainment and a different result claimed in very different style, eventually won with four balls to spare and in the most unfitting of ways — with a massive six — by Hope, again proving himself the right man for a run chase. A series marked by stunning power hitting, roaring run rates and an unexpected midway transformation in England's performances and prospects concluded with a 3-2 triumph for West Indies, who extended their supremacy over England at home to four series in three formats across nearly five years.
