# Match Group's Settlement And The Coalition For App Fairness

Asked whether he had anything to say to Match Group, the former co-plaintiff that settled and left Epic's case at the last minute, Tim Sweeney said there are absolutely no hard feelings: Match has been an awesome partner and a fellow member of the Coalition for App Fairness, and he hopes it got out of its settlement what it needed for its business.

His stated reason for the generosity is asymmetry of resources — very few companies have the resources Epic has to fight multinational litigation against the world's two most powerful companies — and he said Epic is grateful Match joined the case because it helped in critical ways. Sweeney closed by restating Epic's posture going forward: Epic will continue to fight for all developers, seeking remedies, and while he doesn't know whether Sundar will be calling him, if he does, all of their discussions will be directed toward solving the problem for everyone.
