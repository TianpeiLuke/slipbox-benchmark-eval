# Anchor Pro Under-Desk Headphone Hanger

The Anchor Pro under-desk headphone hanger was $12, down from $15 — 20 percent off at Amazon. It is an under-desk dual headphone hanger that uses 3M adhesive for mounting and an included Velcro strap to anchor a headphone cable, and it is audience manager Kaitlin Hatton's pick.

She gave it a try for one of The Verge's TikTok videos and it has not disappointed her yet: she uses it to hold her Razer Kraken headset and some extra cords.

It is small enough to remain out of the way but large enough to hold more than the headset itself, and it has a pretty strong hold that does not give even as she raises her standing desk up and down several times a day. It is not the prettiest accessory one can attach to a desk, but it is highly functional.
