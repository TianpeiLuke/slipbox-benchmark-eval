# Chris Partridge's Firing And The Evidence-Destruction Allegation

Michigan fired linebackers coach Chris Partridge on a Friday amid an allegation that he destroyed evidence on a computer pertaining to the sign-stealing allegations. Yahoo Sports' Ross Dellenger and Dan Wetzel reported that the NCAA's investigation into the sign-stealing and advanced scouting operation had found that Connor Stalions was at least partially funded by a Michigan booster named as "Uncle T," and that Partridge is alleged to have destroyed the computer evidence.

The report stated that Partridge is not believed to have had prior knowledge of the sign-stealing operation but later worked to cover it up, and that the NCAA's investigation was still in its early stage.

Michigan's statement on the firing appeared to indicate a connection between Partridge and the NCAA's investigation: "From the outset, our focus has been on seeking due process and allowing the NCAA to conduct a fair and deliberate investigation. Although the Big Ten has closed its investigation, we are continuing to cooperate with the NCAA as it moves forward with its ongoing investigation. Consistent with our commitment to integrity, we will continue to take the appropriate actions, including disciplinary measures, based on information we obtain. Earlier today, Michigan Athletics relieved Chris Partridge of his duties as a member of the Michigan Football staff. Due to employee privacy laws, we are unable to comment further."
