# The Unauthorized Bash Brothers Experience (2019)

The Unauthorized Bash Brothers Experience is a 30-minute 2019 musical movie by The Lonely Island, directed by Mike Diva and Akiva Schaffer and starring Andy Samberg and Schaffer. It is a spoof of Beyoncé's Lemonade focused on Jose Canseco and Mark McGwire's notorious 1980s home run streak, and it was dropped out of nowhere in 2019; Polygon's verdict is that it deserves more love.

In line with the group's previous efforts such as Tour de Pharmacy and 7 Days in Hell (co-starring Kit Harington), it explores the shared psyche of Canseco and McGwire through poetry, abstract imagery and profane lyrics, with appearances from Alana Haim, Maya Rudolph, Hannah Simone, Jenny Slate, Jim O'Heir and Sterling K. Brown as Sia. What the reviewer finds surprising is that Samberg and Schaffer do not skimp on the darkness of the Bash Brothers: with lyrics like "Stab that needle in my ass until I am rich / Make me a god with the chemical sciences," the film eventually finds McGwire begging a vision of his father to save his life while Canseco raps about how therapy is for the weak. It is available to stream on Netflix.
