# The Cancellation Of The GM–Honda Affordable EV Partnership

General Motors and Honda said in April 2022 that they would co-develop millions of small and affordable EVs, teasing sub-$30,000 electrics for North America and aiming to deliver them in 2027.

By October 2023 they canned the collaboration, vaguely citing "extensive studies and analysis" to explain the move. The announcement came as GM slowed its transition to EVs and Honda teased a "new global EV series" of its own.
