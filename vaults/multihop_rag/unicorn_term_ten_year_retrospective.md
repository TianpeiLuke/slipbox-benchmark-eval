# The Unicorn Term At Ten Years

It has been 10 years since Cowboy Ventures' founder Aileen Lee coined an incredibly catchy nickname for what were very rare startups at the time: unicorns.

TechCrunch+ spoke with Lee about how she feels about the term 10 years later, now that her venture firm is also a decade old.

TechCrunch's framing of the anniversary: the unicorn's legacy is not over.
