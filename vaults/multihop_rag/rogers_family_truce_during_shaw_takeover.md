# The Rogers Family Truce During The Shaw Takeover

In early 2022, Rogers family members agreed to temporarily shelve their disagreements, according to the October 2023 court filing, to avoid negative publicity as the company sought regulators' approval for the takeover of Shaw Communications Inc. The C$20 billion transaction was the company's biggest deal ever and among the largest corporate transactions in Canadian history. As part of that truce, Melinda Rogers-Hixon and Martha Rogers agreed not to participate in board discussions about the Shaw deal or receive materials on it, because of an argument about Rogers-Hixon's personal lawyer, whose firm also does work for Telus Corp., a competitor to Rogers and Shaw.

The Shaw takeover finally closed on April 3, 2023, and the Rogers siblings then engaged in talks "intended to resolve all issues between us," Rogers-Hixon stated in an affidavit, adding "I was hopeful all issues could be resolved without negative publicity." Those negotiations collapsed in September 2023. It isn't clear from the court filings whether the discussions included the possibility that any Rogers family members would sell their stake in Rogers Communications, leave their roles at the company, or exit the family trust that controls almost all of the voting shares of the public company.
