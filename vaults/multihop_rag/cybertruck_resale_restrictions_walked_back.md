# Cybertruck Resale Restrictions Walked Back

Tesla threatened to punish early Cybertruck resellers, but it seemed to walk back its previously published rules in a November 2023 update to its U.S. order agreement page.

The rules once stated that Cybertruck customers could not sell their vehicles during their first year of ownership without permission from Tesla, and that the company would seek $50,000 in damages from early resellers. That language is gone from that particular page, at least for now.
