# Aurora Sightings Beyond Usual Latitudes: Greece, Turkey and Australia

The November 2023 storm pushed auroras far outside their usual latitudes, with bright red auroras seen over Greece, northern lights reported in Turkey, and the aurora australis lighting up the skies over Busselton, Western Australia.

Many skywatchers took to X (formerly known as Twitter) to share their encounters. Space weather physicist Tamitha Skov was at a loss for words as she retweeted photographs of the Greek auroras, writing: "Even though this #solarstorm peaked at a G3, such a view is indeed special. Wonder if the changing magnetic field of Earth and growing equatorial #auroral shows are playing a role here?" A post from Turkey reported polar lights caused by the geomagnetic storm seen in Hayrabolu, Tekirdağ on Nov. 5, 2023. Travel and landscape photographer Paul Pichugin captured a timelapse of the aurora australis over Busselton, Western Australia, filmed late on the night of Nov. 4 into the early morning of Nov. 5.
