# The Fourth Quarter Of Broncos 21, Vikings 20

Denver turned an eight-point fourth-quarter deficit into a 21-20 win on Nov. 19, 2023, on a late Russell Wilson drive finished by Courtland Sutton. Early in the quarter the Broncos' own drive stalled on penalties: Wilson picked up a first down on a sneak on 3rd & 1, but an illegal formation penalty set Denver back four yards and Vikings LB D.J. Wonnum sacked Wilson on the next third down, bringing on Will Lutz, who buried his fourth field goal of the day from 37 yards, going a perfect 4-for-4 to account for all of Denver's points, for Vikings 17, Broncos 12.

The Broncos then took the ball away and cut the lead further. At 10:38 p.m. LB Josey Jewell brought pressure, Joshua Dobbs was hit as he threw and Broncos CB Ja'Quan McMillan intercepted, returning it nine yards, and Denver took over at the Vikings' 9-yard line. A 15-yard facemask penalty on Broncos WR Marvin Mims Jr. backed the offense up and the Vikings' defense held, so Lutz kicked again — a 28-yarder, his fifth make of the day, his side's lone source of offense, bringing Denver within one score at Vikings 17, Broncos 15 with 10:37 to play.

Minnesota answered with a long clock-eating drive kept alive by two gambles. What looked like a three-and-out was rescued by a fake punt on a gutsy call from head coach Kevin O'Connell, with Ty Chandler picking up 31 yards into Denver territory. Chandler then made a big gain on 3rd & 20 that appeared to be a first down; the spot was reviewed and overturned, ruling him just short and setting up 4th & inches, on which the Vikings went for it and Dobbs converted on a QB sneak. The 14-play, seven-minute-and-20-second drive stalled at Denver's 12, and Greg Joseph made it 20-15 with a 30-yard field goal.

The game-winning drive came next. Sutton kept it alive with an insane catch on 4th & 3 from Denver's 45 with 2:19 remaining; Samaje Perine took a Wilson pass 10 yards for a first down into the two-minute warning at Minnesota's 45, and finished the drive with four catches for 40 yards and three first downs. At 11:14 p.m. Sutton hauled in a monster 15-yard touchdown grab over Vikings CB Mekhi Blackmon, and after an unsuccessful two-point try Denver led 21-20 with 1:03 to play.

Minnesota's last possession died in three plays. Dobbs converted 4th & inches on a QB sneak with 41 seconds left, and the Vikings took a timeout at their own 36 with one timeout left, needing only field-goal range. The Broncos' pass rush was all over Dobbs on the drive and forced an intentional grounding penalty, costing Minnesota its final timeout and leaving 4th & 25 from its own 21 with 23 seconds remaining. At 11:25 p.m. Dobbs stepped into a big throw across the middle with no one home — a turnover on downs, and the Broncos' comeback victory.
