# NASCAR 2024 Offseason Preparation Window

The NASCAR offseason, as characterized by CBSSports.com on Dec. 7, 2023, is the wintertime respite from what is otherwise a demanding, year-long grind of racing — welcome but very short, and decisive out of proportion to its length.

What distinguishes it is what gets settled in it: come 2024 the grind would soon begin again, and exactly what it looked like would be based in large part on these months, when driver lineups, crew lineups and rules packages are set. With much activity going on within the confines of race shops across NASCAR, the piece rounded up the major storylines for the week of Dec. 4 as the offseason continued.
