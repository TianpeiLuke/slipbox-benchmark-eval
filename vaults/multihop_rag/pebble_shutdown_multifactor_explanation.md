# Pebble's Multifactor Shutdown Explanation

TechCrunch argues on 2023-10-24 that, like most startup shutdowns, the answer as to "why" is not any one thing but a combination of factors — and that this is certainly true for Pebble.

The grounds are the five contributing causes named together: a perfect storm of competition, X's continued traction, the lack of a native app, a brand that didn't resonate, and a space that was maybe a little too safe to be as addictive and as fun as the original. The warrant is that no single one of these was decisive; the failure is the compound of them.
