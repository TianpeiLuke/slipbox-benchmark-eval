# Prime Day 2023 Smartphone Deals

WIRED's Prime Big Deal Days phone picks include Samsung's Galaxy Z Flip5, the Motorola Razr+ at $800 ($100 off), a budget Android pick, WIRED's favorite phone for most people at its lowest tracked price, a cheap Motorola, and the OnePlus 11.

The Galaxy Z Flip5 (7/10, WIRED Recommends) delivers a larger cover screen, so you can do more without opening the phone — an answer for anyone who complains about how big phones are these days — with the Motorola Razr+ offered as a different style of flip phone. The budget pick (8/10, WIRED Recommends) is one of the best you'll find for the price: it lacks wireless charging, for which WIRED points to the Pixel 7A, but has an AMOLED screen with a 120-Hz refresh rate, decent performance, reliable cameras, and battery that lasts more than a day. WIRED's favorite smartphone for most people (8/10, WIRED Recommends) is at an incredible price, the lowest tracked, with smooth performance, a nice bright screen, excellent cameras, and wireless charging, though battery life is just OK. The cheap Motorola performs well for the money: only one OS update, to Android 14, but three years of security updates, plus an NFC sensor for contactless payments, a headphone jack, and a microSD card slot. The OnePlus 11 (7/10, WIRED Recommends) is fast in performance, recharging, and even looks, with a bright 120-Hz AMOLED screen, great speakers, and surprisingly good battery life; WIRED's main gripe is its IP64 water- and dust-resistance rating, not nearly as good as other flagship phones.
