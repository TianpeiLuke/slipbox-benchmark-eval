# Google's Rebuttal To The Publisher Antitrust Suit

Google rejects the premise that it deprives publishers of readers and traffic. A Google spokesperson called the Helena World Chronicle lawsuit "meritless," and attacked the claim of foreclosure directly: "People have many ways to access information and news content today – through publishers' websites, dedicated apps, social media platforms, print papers and more. Google links people to publishers' websites more than 24 billion times each month – at no cost to them."

The rebuttal therefore contests two of the suit's premises at once: that Google is a bottleneck between readers and publishers, and that publishers are uncompensated, by pointing to the volume of free referrals Google sends.
