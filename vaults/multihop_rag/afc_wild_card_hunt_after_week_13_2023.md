# AFC Teams Outside The Playoff Field After Week 13 2023

The Houston Texans (7-5) sat eighth in the AFC after Week 13 of the 2023 season, first among the teams outside the seven-team playoff field, per Sporting News on Dec. 3, 2023.

The order below the cut ran: 8. Houston Texans (7-5); 9. Denver Broncos (6-6); 10. Buffalo Bills (6-6); 11. Cincinnati Bengals (6-6); 12. Los Angeles Chargers (5-7); 13. Las Vegas Raiders (5-7); 14. New York Jets (4-8); 15. Tennessee Titans (4-8).

Sporting News noted that the Texans beat the Broncos in a key wild-card determining matchup in Week 13; that the Bills and Raiders were off to regroup for a run; that the Bengals were staying resilient without Joe Burrow; that the Chargers won on the road at the Patriots to maintain a playoff pulse; and that the Titans and Jets lost close games at home.
