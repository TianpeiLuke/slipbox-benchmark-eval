# The Benavidez-Andrade Undercard And Jermall Charlo's Return

The other fight of note on the Benavidez-Andrade card, per CBS Sports on 2023-11-25, marked the return of WBC middleweight champion Jermall Charlo, who took on Jose Benavidez Jr., the older brother of David Benavidez. Charlo had been out of action since June 2021 as he dealt with out-of-ring issues involving his family and his own mental health, and was looking to knock off the ring rust in a 10-round non-title bout. Benavidez Jr. had picked up his first win in five years in August.

The listed undercard odds were Jermall Charlo -700 versus Jose Benavidez Jr. +500 at middleweight; Subriel Matias -350 versus Shohjahon Ergashev +275 at super lightweight; and Hector Luis Garcia -290 versus Lamont Roach +235 at super featherweight.
