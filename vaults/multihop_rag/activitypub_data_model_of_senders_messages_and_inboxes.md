# The ActivityPub Data Model Of Senders, Messages And Inboxes

In the world of ActivityPub, every post everywhere is made up of a sender, a message, and a URL, and every user has an inbox and an outbox for those messages — that is the whole protocol in a nutshell.

The simplicity is the point: ActivityPub is not a product but a data format like PDF or JPG, so what you do with those messages, URLs, inboxes and outboxes is entirely up to you. The Verge spells out the range this covers. You could have a Twitter-like app that emphasizes text, or an Instagram-like one whose UI shows photos first; your federated YouTube could be full of everybody's videos, or you could make TikTok by filtering only for short and vertical ones; you could use a WhatsApp-style messaging app that only cares about messages sent directly to someone's inbox. Or you could do something nobody's ever been able to do before: a news reader that only includes posts with links to news sites and automatically loads those links in a nice reading interface; a content moderation tool any fediverse app could use to filter and manage content on their platform; the perfect algorithm that only up-ranks shitposts and good jokes, licensed to any app that wants an "Epic Posts Only" mode; an app that's just an endless feed of great stuff for NBA fans; one that's just for crypto true believers; or one that lets you swipe from one to the other depending on your mood.
