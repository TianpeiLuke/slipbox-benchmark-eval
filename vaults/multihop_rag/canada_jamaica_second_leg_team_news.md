# Team News For Canada vs Jamaica, Second Leg

Canada lined up 4-4-2 and Jamaica 4-2-3-1 for the second leg in Toronto on Nov. 21, 2023. Canada: Borjan — Laryea, Miller, Cornelius, Johnston (Millar 84') — Davies, Eustaquio (Russell-Rowe 89'), Kone (Kaye 74'), Buchanan (Hoilett 74') — Larin (Osorio 74'), David. Jamaica: Blake — Leigh (Gray 94'), Pinnock, Lowe, Lembikisa — Johnson, Latibeaudiere (Hector 46') — Bailey (Bernard 90'), Nicholson, De Cordova-Reid — Gray. Jamaica named Demarai Gray as striker in place of the injured Michail Antonio, with Leon Bailey and Bobby De Cordova-Reid on the wings; Alphonso Davies and Jonathan David started for the home side.

The pre-match projections did not fully match those lineups. Sporting News expected Canada interim head coach Mauro Biello, who had deployed a 4-4-2 in the first leg, to bring back the more-utilized three-defender system, which would let him rotate out Kamal Miller — booked in the first leg — to keep him safe for the semifinals, with Alistair Johnston and Derek Cornelius stepping in alongside a retained Steven Vitoria; the projected XI was a 3-5-2 of Borjan — Cornelius, Vitoria, Johnston — Laryea, Eustaquio, Kone, Kaye, Davies — David, Larin. Canada's only injury absence was striker Lucas Cavallini, who withdrew from the squad before the two matches and was replaced by Jacen Russell-Rowe.

Jamaica's absences were more serious. Star striker Michail Antonio suffered an ACL injury in the first leg and began a lengthy rehab process, with West Ham reportedly furious about how it was handled — angered both by the conditions of the pitch in Jamaica and by the fact that he played another 10 minutes after sustaining the injury. Antonio had been replaced by Salernitana striker Trivante Stewart, who struggled and was himself withdrawn in the 81st minute; Demarai Gray was an option despite struggling for fitness and not being part of the first-leg matchday squad, as was Colorado Switchbacks forward Romario Williams, who scored 15 goals in 29 USL Championship matches that season. Cory Burke and Amari'i Bell also missed the international window. Jamaica's projected 4-3-3 was Blake — Leigh, Bernard, Pinnock, Lembikisa — Latibeaudiere, D. Johnson, Bailey — Reid, Nicholson, R. Williams.
