# Super Smash Bros. Ultimate

Super Smash Bros. Ultimate is described by Polygon as another Switch take on a stone-cold classic — Nintendo's fight-to-the-death brawler, stocked with all your favorite characters. It appears in the outlet's 2023-10-20 list of the 35 best couch co-op games for Nintendo Switch.

You can play with up to seven other people.

The goal is simply to survive and be the last one standing.
