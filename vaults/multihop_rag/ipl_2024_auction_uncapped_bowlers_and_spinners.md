# IPL 2024 Auction: The Uncapped Fast Bowlers And Spinners

The uncapped fast bowlers came up at 6:11 PM IST beginning with Yash Dayal, who breached the 4 Cr mark before RCB got him for Rs 5 Cr. At 6:15 PM IST Sushant Mishra drew a bidding war between MI and GT, and at 6:17 PM IST Gujarat Titans bagged Suashant Mishra for Rs 2.20 Cr. At 6:19 PM IST Akash Singh went to SRH for Rs 20 L, and Kartik Tyagi drew a three-way bidding war between GT, KKR and LSG, with GT winning at Rs 60 L. At 6:21 PM IST Ishan Porel came up and went unsold, and at 6:24 PM IST Rasikh Dar went to DC for Rs 20 L.

The uncapped spinner category followed, with a lucky fan, Karan Bahrat Tanna, helping the auctioneer Mallika Sagar through it, as noted at 6:25 PM IST. The same minute Manav Suthar went to GT for 20 L. At 6:28 PM IST M Siddharth drew a bidding war between RCB and LSG; RCB pulled out and LSG bagged Siddharth for 2.4 Cr, while Murugan Ashwin went unsold. At 6:33 PM IST Shreyas Gopal went to MI for Rs 20 L.
