# The Xbox Controller Market In Late 2023

The Verge's claim is that we are living in a golden age of gaming controllers: the gamepads on the market now are higher quality, more versatile, and more customizable than anything from just a few console generations ago.

The grounds are the breadth of the field. If you are gaming on an Xbox Series X or Series S — or a Windows PC — you now have a plethora of great third-party options from the likes of PowerA, Scuf, Nacon, and Turtle Beach, as well as high-quality first-party controllers. The days of the cheap "little sibling" controller that looked cool but barely worked are over.

The recommendation that follows from extensive testing across first-person shooters, fighters, third-person action-adventure, racing and indie roguelikes is that the standard Xbox Wireless Controller is the best Xbox controller for most, and it makes a great PC controller too. But while the de facto Xbox gamepad strikes the right balance of quality, comfort, versatility, and price, several alternatives are worth cross-shopping for specific needs — ranging from high-end options like the Xbox Elite Series 2 and Scuf Instinct Pro to quality budget picks like the PowerA Enhanced Wired Controller.
