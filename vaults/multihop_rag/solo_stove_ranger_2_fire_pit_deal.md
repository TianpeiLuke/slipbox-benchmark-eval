# Solo Stove Ranger 2.0 Fire Pit Black Friday Deal

The Solo Stove Ranger 2.0 bundle in Walmart's Black Friday 2023 sale is "an even better deal than it appears to be", because it actually retails for $264.99 on the brand's website while Walmart's list price is slightly deflated, Mashable reported on 25 November 2023.

The Ranger 2.0 features a removable ashtray and a lightweight stainless steel design plus its own carrying case, and is a smaller version of Mashable's favourite smokeless fire pit — "and yes, it really is virtually smokeless".

The bundle throws in a stand that allows it to safely burn on decks or green grass.
