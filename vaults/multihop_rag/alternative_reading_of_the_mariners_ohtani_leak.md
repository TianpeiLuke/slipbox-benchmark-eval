# An Alternative Reading Of The Mariners Ohtani Leak

Against the inference that the Mariners are genuinely out of the Shohei Ohtani chase, Sporting News offered on Nov. 27, 2023 a competing explanation of why such a report would surface at all: "On the other hand, maybe the M's know all about Ohtani's desire to keep the rumor mill quiet, and leaking 'news' like this would please Ohtani and his camp."

The premise attacked is that the leak is a straightforward disclosure of Seattle's intentions. If the club is aware of Ohtani's preference for silence, then a report distancing themselves from the pursuit could be strategic rather than sincere — pleasing the player's camp instead of revealing the front office's real agenda.
