# Kindle 2022 Base Model Deals

Amazon announced a new entry-level Kindle in 2022, designed to replace the 2019 model; starting at $99.99, it puts Amazon's base e-reader more in line with the most recent Kindle Paperwhite, with longer battery life, a 300ppi screen, USB-C charging instead of Micro USB, 16GB of storage by default, and a new "denim" color resembling the soft blue you might associate with jeans.

As of The Verge's October 16th, 2023 roundup, the ad-supported model had recently dropped to $79.99, $20 off, with three months of Kindle Unlimited included — but at the time of writing it could only be bought at Amazon, Best Buy, and Target for its full retail price. A bundle was still discounted: at Amazon, the Kindle with ads, a power adapter, and a fabric cover started at $134.97, $15 off. Regardless of whether you choose the standalone model or the bundle, The Verge wrote, the new Kindle remains a worthwhile option if you are looking to pick up an e-reader for less than the latest Paperwhite.
