# Tech Layoffs In July 2023

July 2023 was a comparatively quiet month for tech layoffs, but it included a further round at Microsoft: the company announced on July 10 that it was eliminating additional jobs a week after the start of its 2023 fiscal year, on top of the 10,000 layoffs it had announced in January.

The other cuts were mostly at startups. ClickUp announced on July 4 that it had laid off 10% of its workforce, about 90 people. Crunchbase announced on LinkedIn on July 9 that it was laying off 52 employees. The Indian startup Skill-Lync announced on July 13 that it had laid off 20% of its workforce, around 225 employees. Cameo announced on July 18 that it intended to lay off 80 workers. Dunzo announced on July 19 that it was postponing employee salaries for a month and planned a new wave of layoffs as soon as July 20; executives informed employees they would likely be cutting over 200 jobs, in what would be Dunzo's third layoff that year, the company having eliminated about 400 jobs so far in 2023.
