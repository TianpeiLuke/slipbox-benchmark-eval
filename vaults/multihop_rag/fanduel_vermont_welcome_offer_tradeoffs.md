# Why FanDuel's Vermont Welcome Offer Is Special — And Where It Falls Short

Sporting News argues on 2023-09-26 that the FanDuel Sportsbook welcome offer is great for many reasons: it is simple and easy to claim and use, you don't have to make a large wager up front to receive the bonus, and it doesn't matter if your first bet wins or loses since you get the bonus either way. $200 in bonus bets is quite generous, and if you make some winning bets you'll be able to have a nice bankroll right away without any significant expense — with the $100 in Bonus Bets for those signing up by Jan. 10, the offer is even more generous.

The stated advantages, point by point: unlike other offers, you don't have to risk much of your own money to receive the bonus, just $5 to get $200 in Bonus Bets; also unlike other offers, it doesn't matter if your initial wager wins or loses, you get the bonus either way; and you don't have to use your Bonus Bets all at once, since you can divide up how you use them any way you like. The drawbacks are equally explicit: you can't just withdraw the Bonus Bets, you have to wager them; you only have a week to use all $200 of your Bonus Bets; and if you win with Bonus Bets you only get the winnings and not the wager amounts back.
