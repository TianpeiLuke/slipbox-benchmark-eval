# The Rugby Australia High-Performance Director Role And Its Remit

The Rugby Australia director of high-performance is the position at the top of the governing body's high-performance structure, described by chief executive Phil Waugh in December 2023 as the biggest job on his leadership team in terms of its impact on the general sentiment around the game in Australia.

Three duties define the role as described. First, the director oversees all of Australia's men's and women's high-performance programs. Second, the holder is tasked with "integrating" those programs, as Rugby Australia tries to centralise all of Super Rugby's high-performance programs. Third — and perhaps most pressing at the time — the director must build a list of coaching candidates to take over from Eddie Jones, with Rugby Australia wanting the new Wallabies coach set in stone by the start of Super Rugby.

The scope conditions attached are timing and seniority. Rugby Australia always said it wanted the role filled by Christmas, and Waugh's framing sets the standard for the appointment: "It's a big job. It's the biggest job on my leadership team in terms of the impact it has on the general sentiment around the game."
