# Ability Scores Link BG3 Classes To Out-Of-Combat Skills

In Baldur's Gate 3, class determines combat behaviour, but the Ability Score the class relies on determines the out-of-combat skills a character is good at — so choosing a class also chooses a social and exploratory profile.

The mechanism runs in a chain: Baldur's Gate 3 assigns Ability Scores automatically at creation (though they can be tweaked in the character creator), so each class's primary ability ends up as that character's highest score, and the highest score in turn drives the skills tied to it. Two worked cases show the range over which the relation holds. Bards and Sorcerers are both Charisma-based, so their high Charisma also makes them good at Persuasion, Deception, and Intimidation; out of combat those classes are better at talking their way out of — and into — trouble, and at lying. Wisdom-based classes such as Clerics and Druids come out more insightful and more perceptive of their surroundings instead.
