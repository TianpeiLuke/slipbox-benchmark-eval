# Tech Layoffs In August 2023

August 2023's tech layoffs fell heavily on cybersecurity and gaming companies, with Mobile Premier League cutting approximately 50% of its employees — 350 people — announced on August 8, the deepest proportional cut of the month.

The month began with Planet announcing on August 1 that it was laying off 117 employees, or 10% of staff. On August 2, HackerOne announced it was laying off 12% of its workforce, approximately 50 employees, and Inc42 reported that Tekion had laid off around 300 employees, or 10% of its workforce, of whom about 200 were from Tekion's India office. Discord announced on August 3 that it had let go of nearly 40 employees, 4% of its workforce, and Astra announced on August 4 that it had laid off 25% of its workforce since the beginning of the quarter. Rapid7 announced on August 9 that it planned to lay off 18% of its workforce, affecting more than 400 employees globally. NCC Group confirmed on August 10 that it was making more layoffs, its second round in just six months, declining to say how many employees were affected; NCC Group had cut 125 employees in February. On August 14, CoinDesk announced it was cutting 16% of staff, and SecureWorks announced it would let go of 15% of its workforce, impacting about 300 employees, in the company's second round of layoffs that year. Twiga announced on August 21 that it was laying off 283 employees, or 33% of its workforce, and Malwarebytes announced on August 31 that it had laid off 100 employees, mainly impacting corporate employees.
