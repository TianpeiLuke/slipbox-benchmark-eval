# Sevilla's Pressure And Missed Chances At The Sánchez-Pizjuán

Sevilla created and squandered chances at both ends of their 2-1 home defeat by Arsenal on 24 October 2023, throwing what they could at the visitors after pulling a goal back but never finding a way through.

Early on, Arsenal had stepped high and dominated the opening period, and when Sevilla's first opportunity was passed up it did not seem to matter much — at that point it appeared Arsenal would open Sevilla again and often. Yet Diego Alonso's side settled, even if too often their moves ended at the feet of Dodi Lukebakio; it was Lukebakio who missed their best early opportunity, failing to control by the penalty spot after the kind of loose David Raya pass that can only fuel the goalkeeping debate had gifted Sevilla possession in a dangerous position. The next chance also came Sevilla's way, Sergio Ramos stepping out from the back to find Ocampos, who nudged it into the path of Youssef En-Nesyri, whose shot faded past the far post beyond the defence.

Arsenal started the second period as if determined to end the game, and nine minutes in it seemed they might have — but it did not play out that way. A Nemanja Gudelj header from a corner put Sevilla back in it, then substitute Mariano Díaz brought the ball down on his chest and smashed a shot against the bar, and next came an overhead kick from Ocampos going close. These were the moments Arteta had meant, stressing the importance of knowing they will always come and having the capacity to withstand them: "These games demand you play lots of different games," he said. "We controlled it and created chances but at 2-0 we did not kill them off. We conceded from a corner and then we lacked control and had to defend our area, but we are very happy because we found those different things that are very important, and in a stadium where not many teams have won." The fans had issued their own warning with a huge banner at the start declaring "Europe knows what we are capable of"; on 90 minutes En-Nesyri leapt to head, Raya had to push away a wayward cross that almost sneaked in, and Nyland was sent up for a corner until, in the very last second, the ball sat up before Ocampos. Sevilla could not find a way through, and Arsenal, top of Group B, now should.
