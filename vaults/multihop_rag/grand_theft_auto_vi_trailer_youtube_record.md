# The Grand Theft Auto VI Trailer's YouTube Record

In just 22 hours, the first trailer for Grand Theft Auto VI racked up 85 million views, breaking a MrBeast video's record for most YouTube views in 24 hours, TechCrunch reported on 9 December 2023.

The excitement for Grand Theft Auto VI is a decade in the making: the previous entry in Rockstar Games' long-running franchise, Grand Theft Auto V, remains the second-best-selling video game of all time, falling short only of Minecraft.
