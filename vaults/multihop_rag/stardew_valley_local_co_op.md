# Stardew Valley (Local Co-Op)

Stardew Valley is a modern-classic farming sim that takes its cues from the Harvest Moon series, and Polygon says running a farm with your friends only adds to the fun. It appears in the outlet's 2023-10-20 list of the 35 best couch co-op games for Nintendo Switch.

Players can work together to plant and harvest crops or spend their days doing different activities, like fishing or mining.

Local co-op is available for two players, with a practical caveat — do not forget to build an extra cabin on your farm — and up to four can play with online multiplayer.
