# Cynodonts, the Surviving Mammal Ancestors

Only a handful of proto-mammals survived the Great Dying, and one group that did was Cynodontia ("dog teeth") — the ancestors of humans and of all mammals.

At first they probably looked somewhat like a huge, scaly rat, growing to as much as six feet (1.8 m) in length — truly a Rodent of Unusual Size, as Mann puts it. But by the end of the Triassic they had shrunk to the size of modern-day field mice, hiding behind rocks from their reptilian predators.
