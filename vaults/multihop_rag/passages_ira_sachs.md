# Passages (Ira Sachs)

Passages is Ira Sachs' film starring Franz Rogowski, Ben Whishaw, and Adèle Exarchopoulos, which Polygon calls perhaps the platonic ideal of an arthouse film. It ranked 48 on the best movies of 2023 list of 2023-10-07 and was available on Mubi or for digital rental/purchase.

The ingredients Polygon names are a messy love triangle, set in Paris, shot through with explicit sex and arresting fashion choices.

Critic Oli Welsh's defense of the film is unfussy: sometimes you just want to watch complicated hot people — Adèle Exarchopoulos, Ben Whishaw, and Franz Rogowski in this case — messing with each other's heads in tops their characters could not possibly afford, and there is nothing wrong with that.
