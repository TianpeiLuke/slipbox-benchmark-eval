# Thanksgiving 2023 Online Sales Figures

Adobe Analytics said people in the U.S. spent $5.6 billion online on Thanksgiving Thursday 2023, which it calculates as an increase of just 5.5% on the previous year — a message that consumers are holding steady, but that no growth boom should be expected.

Salesforce, which calculates sales based on data collected by its Commerce Cloud division, noted that globally online sales reached $31.7 billion with its U.S. tally at $7.5 billion — both up only 1%. Its calculations of average order value were equally modest: globally, average orders were up just 2% to $103 per "basket," while in the U.S. they were up a paltry 1% to $119.

For context, last year's published figures from Adobe were $5.29 billion, which actually represents an increase of just under 4% — it is likely Adobe Analytics readjusted its final figures for last year, which is why a higher percentage of growth appears. The 5.5% rate is definitely an improvement on last year's 2.9%, but it is nothing compared to the years preceding COVID-19, such as 2017, when growth of 18% or more was being seen. Thanksgiving Thursday, when stores in the U.S. are closed and many people are spending time off work, has become the de facto start of the holiday shopping season, both for those looking to get bargains online and for online retailers kicking off holiday sales deals to meet that demand.
