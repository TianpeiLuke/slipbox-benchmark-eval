# Roblox Aging Up: An Older User Base

Roblox's fastest-growing segment is users aged 17 to 24, growing north of 33 percent year on year, according to what CEO David Baszucki said the company shared in its most recent earnings report — enough that Roblox has stopped using the term "aging up" and now describes itself as having a very substantial over-17 user base. Earlier in 2023 the company had announced grand plans to build an audience of adults, with exclusive experiences and identity verification by photo ID; The Verge had reported in 2020 that half of all US kids under 16 had played it.

Alex Heath pressed on whether the content had caught up. He had made a new Roblox account from scratch about two hours before the interview and found the front page, after entering his age, was still a lot of kids' games he would not play, suggesting Roblox was lagging on content that would draw an adult. Baszucki called that a leading indicator, saying the vision from four to seven years ago — an immersive platform connecting people around the world to socialize and do things together — always assumed the platform would be in every country and for all ages, "a platform for six-year-olds and 60-year-olds." What Heath experienced, he said, is that Roblox has started allowing creators, once a user is validated with photo ID as 17, to ease into more mature experiences. Heath mentioned a trailer shown at RDC a couple of weeks earlier that looked like Grand Theft Auto in Roblox with very impressive graphics, not the blocky thing people associate with the platform.

Asked what the fastest-growing demographic actually does on the platform, Baszucki said it is surprising how many older people play a wide range of experiences and come to socialize. His anecdote: at a family reunion in Canada he met a third cousin, a 24-year-old railroad engineer, who told him all the mechanics at CN Railroad — Canadian National Railway — were playing Roblox, specifically Jailbreak and similar experiences. The level of social connection, immersion, and being together with friends no matter where they are, he said, is somewhat universal, and it is doing surprisingly well for the company.
