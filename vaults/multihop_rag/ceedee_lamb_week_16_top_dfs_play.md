# CeeDee Lamb Week 16 Top DFS Play

CBSSports.com's Dec. 19, 2023 Week 16 wide receiver preview named CeeDee Lamb, WR, Dallas, No. 88, its top DFS play, with projections powered by SportsLine.

The choice between Lamb and Tyreek Hill would not be an easy one; the tiebreaker for the writer was that Lamb was $900 cheaper and Hill might still not be 100% by the time the Cowboys and Dolphins kicked off. Lamb's form supports the pick: the previous week was the first time since Week 9 that he failed to reach the end zone, and the second time since Week 5 that he did not score at least 17 PPR fantasy points. His weekly breakdown at age 24, four years' experience: at MIA, MIA -1.5, O/U 51, opponent vs. WR 15th, projected 19.4 points, WR rank 2nd; year-to-date 103 receptions, 141 targets, 1,306 receiving yards, 10 TD, 21.6 fantasy points per game.
