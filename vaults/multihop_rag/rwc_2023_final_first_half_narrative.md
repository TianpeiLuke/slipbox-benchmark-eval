# The First Half Of The 2023 Rugby World Cup Final

South Africa led New Zealand 12-6 approaching half-time in the 2023 Rugby World Cup final, with all the points coming from penalties after controversy broke out from the outset.

The opening incident set the tone: Shannon Frizell was shown a yellow card for an illegal cleanout on Springboks hooker Bongi Mbonambi — the hooker, who had been cleared earlier in the week by World Rugby following an alleged racial slur on England back-rower Tom Curry, was replaced by Deon Fourie in the third minute. The penalty allowed Handre Pollard to step up and bang over three points. The All Blacks were on the back foot for much of the opening quarter and, after Ethan de Groot was pinged for not rolling away, the Springboks moved further ahead after 12 minutes.

New Zealand's chances came and went. A wicked bounce following a lovely Jordie Barrett chip kick denied Ardie Savea a try soon after, but the All Blacks got on the scoreboard via Richie Mo'unga moments later as referee Wayne Barnes was playing advantage. The Springboks restored their six-point lead soon after when Savea was pinged for not releasing — a call Barnes later apologised for, the No. 8 having had a split-second separation.

The margin peaked at nine. South Africa took a 12-3 lead after 34 minutes when the All Blacks once again failed to release on their goal line, with Pollard licking his lips and saying "thanks", and the All Blacks cut the deficit to 12-6 after 37 minutes when Eben Etzebeth was caught offside and denied his opponents quick ball. Ian Foster's men breathed a sigh of relief in the 42nd minute when Siya Kolisi blew a chance out wide, tucking the ball under his arms and going himself after Beauden Barrett failed to collect a high ball.
