# Hasbro's Marketing of the Ouija Board

Today the Ouija board is produced by Hasbro, which markets the product as a way to communicate with the "spirit world". Fox News Digital reached out to Hasbro for comment but did not hear back by publication time.

The Hasbro website's copy makes the framing explicit: "Enter the world of the mysterious and mystifying with the Ouija board! You've got questions and the spirit world has answers — and the uncanny Ouija board is your way to get them."

It also gives instructions for use and an instruction about attitude: "What do you want to know? Ask your question with a friend using the planchette that comes with the board, but be patient and concentrate because the spirits can't be rushed. Handle the Ouija board with respect and it won't disappoint you," said the website.
