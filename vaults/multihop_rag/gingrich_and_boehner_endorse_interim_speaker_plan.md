# Gingrich And Boehner Endorse The Interim Speaker Plan

The novel concept of boosting the interim speaker's role was gaining favor with a pair of high-profile Republicans: former GOP speakers Newt Gingrich and John Boehner. Gingrich's ground for backing it is a vote count, not a dislike of the nominee — he said that while he likes Jim Jordan, he has "no faith" the nominee can get much beyond the 200 votes he won in the first vote.

The warrant Gingrich offered is the cost of continued paralysis: "We can't sit around and suck our thumbs and hope the world will wait until the House Republicans get their act together," he told Fox News' Sean Hannity on his show. Boehner reposted Gingrich's views saying, "I agree," on social media. Both men have deep experience with the subject — both were chased to early retirement.
