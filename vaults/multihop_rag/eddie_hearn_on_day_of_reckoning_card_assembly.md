# Eddie Hearn On Day Of Reckoning Card Assembly

Eddie Hearn of Matchroom Sport, Joshua's long-time promoter, argued in a "Morning Kombat" interview reported by CBSSports.com on Dec. 23, 2023 that the whole reason the two heavyweights were fighting on the same card, and the reason everyone was going to Saudi Arabia, was to make Joshua vs Wilder in Saudi Arabia.

He cut right to the chase about how hastily the card was assembled after months of rumors: "I think we had six weeks and the card was put together quickly. By the way, I think Otto Wallin is a very dangerous fight six weeks out. [Joshua] is in there with a southpaw who is just coming off a victory over [former cruiserweight titleholder Murat] Gassiev. And Joseph Parker is full of confidence and Wilder has been out for a long time." The conclusion he draws from that admitted risk: "But, look, the reason they are fighting on the same card and the reason we are all going to Saudi Arabia is that we want to make Joshua and Wilder in Saudi Arabia. But everybody wants that fight to happen and if they both win on [Saturday] there is a great chance that you see what I think is the biggest fight in boxing." The claim is conditional on both men winning.
