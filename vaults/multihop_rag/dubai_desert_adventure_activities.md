# Dubai's Desert Adventures, Best In November And December

November and December are the best months to head for Dubai and get stuck into outdoor activities, if you are the kind of person who would rather spend their days exploring than soaking up the sun on the beach: the temperature, although still hot with clear blue skies, is a lot cooler than the sweltering summer months.

Dubai's desert offers a wealth of heart-pumping Arabian activities, from dune buggies to quad biking and desert safaris. Whizz through the desert on a dune drive, keeping your eyes peeled for local wildlife along the way — the desert is home to hopping gazelles, several species of reptile and the Arabian Oryx, a species that was saved from the brink of extinction. Choosing a desert safari adds traditional activities like henna tattooing and dance shows, and many safaris culminate in an evening BBQ dinner.
