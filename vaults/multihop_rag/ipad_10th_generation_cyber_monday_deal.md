# iPad 10th Generation Cyber Monday Deal

Mashable's favourite iPad deal at Amazon for Cyber Monday 2023 was the 10th generation 64GB model at $349, after the 64GB 9th generation iPad went in and out of stock (sometimes findable on sale for $229.99), the 27 November 2023 roundup said.

Compared with the 9th gen, the 10th gen classic iPad has a slightly bigger screen that is now also a Liquid Retina display — 10.9 inches versus 10.2 inches.

It also has a faster A14 Bionic chip for smoother multitasking, and USB-C charging.
