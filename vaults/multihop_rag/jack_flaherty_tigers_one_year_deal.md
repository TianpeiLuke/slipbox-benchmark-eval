# Jack Flaherty's One-Year Deal With The Tigers

Right-hander Jack Flaherty signed a one-year, $14 million deal with the Detroit Tigers, ESPN's Jeff Passan reported on Thursday, Dec. 14, 2023.

Flaherty had undergone shoulder surgery in 2022 and was traded from the Cardinals to the Orioles at the 2023 deadline. In total he made 27 starts in 2023, posting a 4.99 ERA with 148 strikeouts in 144.1 innings.
