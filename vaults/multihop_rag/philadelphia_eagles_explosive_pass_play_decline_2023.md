# The Philadelphia Eagles' Explosive Pass-Play Decline, 2023

The New York Times argued on 16 October 2023 that the Eagles' explosive passing has fallen sharply but that the production should return, while flagging a lingering fear that defenses have caught up to aspects of their scheme.

The grounds are the rate itself, set in league context: explosive passing is down through most of the league, including for the Eagles, who are gaining more than 15 yards on just 11 percent of pass plays, down from 21 percent through six games the previous season and 19 percent for 2022 overall. The warrant for expecting recovery is personnel continuity — Jalen Hurts, A.J. Brown, DeVonta Smith and Dallas Goedert are still on the team — which inclines the writer to trust the production will return. The open question is whether Philly has a counterpunch if defenses have solved the scheme.

An executive framed the same trend in terms of how the Eagles now win: "People are going to crush Hurts and the offense. They are just churning the clock so much. It just feels like they are imposing their will on teams through the run game and through their short passing and it just hasn't taken off from an explosive passing standpoint, so when they play a team that can move the ball on them through the air, it presents some challenges."
