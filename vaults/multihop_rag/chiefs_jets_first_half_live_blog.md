# Chiefs-Jets First Half: A 17-0 Start Trimmed To 20-12 At Halftime

Kansas City raced to a 17-0 first-quarter lead over the New York Jets on Oct. 1, 2023 but went to the locker room ahead only 20-12, with Zach Wilson outplaying Patrick Mahomes over the opening two quarters. At halftime Wilson was 18-for-26 for 141 yards with one passing touchdown, zero interceptions and a 95.2 QBR, while Mahomes was 8-for-16 for 135 yards with one passing touchdown, two interceptions and a 60.2 QBR.

The Chiefs received to start the game (8:23 p.m.) and scored on an eight-play, 57-yard drive that stalled in the red zone, with Harrison Butker converting a 37-yarder at 8:28 p.m.; Mahomes had already found Travis Kelce twice for 29 yards. New York went three-and-out at 8:34 p.m. as Wilson connected on only one of three attempts to Garrett Wilson, and Kansas City took over at its own 31 with 10:35 left in the first. At 8:40 p.m. Isiah Pacheco went untouched on a 48-yard touchdown carry, sprung by a block from pulling guard Trey Smith. The Jets punted again at 8:47 p.m. after Breece Hall's 11-yard run and an incompletion to Ty Conklin on 3rd & 1, and at 8:54 p.m. Mahomes hit tight end Noah Gray — left open because of the attention on Kelce — for a 34-yard touchdown and a 17-0 lead. The quarter ended 17-0 with the Jets facing 3rd & 16 from their own 43 after Wilson's 10-yard completion to Garrett Wilson, a 10-yard sack and a four-yard gain.

New York clawed back in the second quarter. After a punt pinned Kansas City inside its own 10 (9:05 p.m.), the Jets scored a safety at 9:09 p.m. on a face mask penalty against Chiefs right tackle Jawaan Taylor in the end zone. Wilson then hit Garrett Wilson for 14 and Allen Lazard for 39 (9:12 p.m.), and Greg Zuerlein made a 31-yard field goal at 9:15 p.m. with 9:50 left in the half after a low throw to an open Ty Conklin was dropped in the end zone. At 9:20 p.m. Jets strong safety Ashton Davis intercepted Mahomes on the first play of a drive, giving New York the ball at the KC 41, and at 9:25 p.m. Wilson threw a one-yard touchdown to tight end CJ Uzomah after converting 3rd & 5 to Randall Cobb, cutting it to 17-12. Kansas City answered with a drive paced by 34 rushing yards from Pacheco and a 3rd & 6 completion to Kadarius Toney, but the Jets' pass rush forced a red-zone stop and Butker made another 37-yarder at 9:36 p.m. with 2:15 left. Wilson led an eight-play, 41-yard two-minute drill to the KC 34 (9:43 p.m.), but Zuerlein's 52-yard attempt hit the upright at 9:46 p.m. On the next drive's first play (9:49 p.m.), Jets linebacker CJ Mosley intercepted an underthrown 35-yard Mahomes pass intended for Kelce, and New York's Hail Mary attempt to end the half ended with Wilson sacked by Chiefs defensive end Chris Jones.
