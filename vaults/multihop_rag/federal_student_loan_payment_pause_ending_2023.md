# The End Of The Federal Student Loan Payment Pause

The three-year federal student loan payment pause was ended by the Biden administration in 2023, forcing millions of people to start paying back student loans again — and, because the resumption landed just before the holidays, adding a significant new line item to household holiday budgets.

Plauché and Johnson, the two borrowers Fortune profiled on 2023-12-23, are just two of those millions. Courtney Alev, consumer financial advocate at Credit Karma, framed the timing problem: "We know that the holidays are often a very joyful time of the year and an expensive one every year. With federal student loan payments just resuming, it's going to add another often very significant line item into holiday budgets."
