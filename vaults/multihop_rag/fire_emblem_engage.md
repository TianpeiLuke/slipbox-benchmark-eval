# Fire Emblem Engage

Fire Emblem Engage, developed by Intelligent Systems for Nintendo Switch, is ranked 39 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31. Polygon's critic says it was designed for a very specific kind of sicko: one not particularly interested in the origin stories of a horde of teenagers, or the politics of a bourgeois academy, or what kind of tea a teacher prefers, but instead obsessed with the endless minutiae of combat stats, weapon loadouts, and team composition.

Critic M. Mahardy states the general principle behind the praise: he prefers strategy games that can get out of their own way, and loves when strategy developers can put their pens down, throw their hands up, and admit that the stories unfolding in the player's head will almost always be more powerful than anything they could write.

Fire Emblem Engage is one of the foremost proponents of that idea, hurling an excess of characters, weapons, battle scenarios, and stat-boosting abilities at you and leaving the door open to observe character interactions on the battlefield and create the resulting fanfiction in your head. Its actual script is a quagmire of nonsensical JRPG tropes and each cutscene is more skippable than the next, but as an excellent turn-based tactics game that gets out of the player's way, you can do a whole lot worse.
