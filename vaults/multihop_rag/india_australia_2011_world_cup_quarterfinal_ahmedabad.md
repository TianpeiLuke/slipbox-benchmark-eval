# India vs Australia In The 2011 World Cup Quarter-Final

After so much Australian dominance in World Cup meetings with India, India finally got their revenge in the 2011 World Cup, defeating Australia by five wickets. Australia batted first and posted a target of 261 runs, courtesy of a 104-run knock off 118 balls by captain Ricky Ponting, losing six wickets in the process.

In the second innings Sachin Tendulkar, Yuvraj Singh and Gautam Gambhir scored three half-centuries as India chased down the target within 47.4 overs, losing 5 wickets. India went all the way to lift their second ODI World Cup trophy, defeating Sri Lanka in the final.
