# PlayStation Store Gift Card Promotion

A simple Prime Day offer: buy a $100 PlayStation Store digital gift card and use the code PSN100 at checkout, and you get an extra $10 Amazon credit to use on future purchases.

The value depends on your existing habits.

If you often use Amazon and plan on picking up new PS5 games, renewing a PlayStation Plus subscription or buying any other content through the PlayStation Store anyway, it is effectively a bit of free money.
