# Dolphins-Eagles Second Half: A Pick-Six Tie, Then Philadelphia Pulls Away

Miami tied the Oct. 22, 2023 "Sunday Night Football" game at 17 with a third-quarter pick-six, but Philadelphia answered immediately and won 31-17. The Dolphins, trailing 17-10 at the break, opened the second half with a 17-yard Tyreek Hill catch (10:00 p.m.) and a seven-yard Raheem Mostert run into Eagles territory (10:01 p.m.), then faced 3rd & 18 largely because of a holding penalty (10:04 p.m.) and punted from just past midfield (10:05 p.m.). Philadelphia managed one first down before Zach Sieler sacked Hurts for a three-yard loss (10:10 p.m.) and the Eagles punted for the first time on the night (10:11 p.m.).

Miami then drew level. Mostert ripped off back-to-back runs of 21 and 15 yards (10:16 p.m.); Tyreek Hill could not hang on to a would-be big catch, setting up a fourth down (10:19 p.m.); and on 4th & 3 James Bradberry appeared to grab Cedrick Wilson's face mask with no flag, so the Dolphins turned it over on downs (10:20 p.m.). On the next Eagles play Kader Kohou deflected a Hurts pass and Jerome Baker returned it for a touchdown at 10:23 p.m., tying the game 17-17 right after the no-call. Jevon Holland and David Long were both injured on the ensuing kickoff and walked off after a short delay (10:30 p.m.).

Philadelphia answered on the spot. A 25-yard DeVonta Smith catch moved the Eagles into Dolphins territory (10:33 p.m.), a Dallas Goedert catch extended the drive into the red zone (10:34 p.m.), and A.J. Brown broke a tackle for a touchdown at 10:35 p.m. to make it 24-17. Jaylen Waddle returned to the game and opened Miami's next drive with a first-down catch (10:39 p.m.), and the third quarter ended 24-17. Waddle rescued the drive again with a first-down grab on 3rd & 12 (10:44 p.m.), but at 10:47 p.m. Darius Slay intercepted a Tagovailoa pass intended for Mostert. The Eagles then ate the clock: on 4th & 1 deep in their own territory they went for it (10:52 p.m.) and Hurts' sneak succeeded (10:53 p.m.); the Brotherly Shove worked again, not even close (10:55 p.m.); Hurts hit Brown for a 42-yard gain (10:58 p.m.); and Kenneth Gainwell scored with a spin move at 11:00 p.m. for 31-17 with just under five minutes left. Tagovailoa was sacked on back-to-back plays to face 4th & 10 (11:07 p.m.), the Eagles took over on downs (11:09 p.m.), D'Andre Swift gained 22 yards for a first down (11:12 p.m.), and Philadelphia kneeled out the 31-17 win (11:16 p.m.).
