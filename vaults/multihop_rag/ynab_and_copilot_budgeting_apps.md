# YNAB And Copilot, The Two Go-To Budgeting Apps

YNAB — which stands for You Need A Budget — was the overwhelming most popular recommendation Installer received. What readers liked: you can sync all your accounts and cards; it is an independent app you pay for instead of being bombarded by ads and credit card deals; and it has a specific set of principles and systems you can just pick up and start using.

Copilot is the other go-to, and it differs on polish rather than principle. Copilot is much more polished than YNAB, and people love the app's design, its dataviz tools, and just how easy it is to interact with. It is only for iOS and Mac, which is unfortunate, but as finance apps go it seems to be unbeatably fun to use.

Other apps readers recommended, in rough order of popularity: Qapital, Buddy, Empower, HomeBank, Expenses, Quicken, EveryDollar, Nudget, Ledger, and Dime.

The writer's own choice, after reading all the notes and doing some research, is to give Copilot a run: he has tried YNAB in the past and it is great, but he just did a bad job keeping up with it. He is also going to make an epic 2024 budget spreadsheet and see how far that gets him.
