# The US Housing Inventory Shortage Of 2023

Declining housing inventory levels contributed to the drop in housing transactions in 2023: inventory was down 8.1% from a year earlier, according to the National Association of Realtors. More recent inventory data makes the issue starker still — between September 2018 and September 2023 the average number of homes on the market dropped a whopping 60%, to fewer than 700,000 active listings, according to Realtor.com.

Set against this shortage, some real-estate experts and economists argue that housing affordability is even worse now than during 2008, when a damaging downturn in the U.S. housing market set off the dominoes that became the Great Financial Crisis — a crisis that defined a whole decade economically and, in some ways, shaped the current predicament.
