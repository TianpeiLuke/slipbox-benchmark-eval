# Gleyber Torres Yankees Trade Candidacy

Gleyber Torres was ranked No. 10 on Yardbarker's Nov. 13, 2023 list of the top 25 MLB offseason trade candidates: another player one year from free agency, he was projected for a $15.3M salary in his last arbitration season — below his market value but a notable figure for a bat-first second baseman, a general profile the league had devalued in recent years.

Torres was New York's second-best offensive player in 2023, hitting .273/.347/.453 with 25 homers through 672 trips to the plate. Even without interest in an extension, New York could hold him for his final season of club control: the Yankees were already light on consistent hitting beyond Aaron Judge, so on the surface trading their second-best hitter seemed counterintuitive.

The big question was whether the front office believed Oswald Peraza was capable of stepping into an everyday second base role in 2024. If they felt the 23-year-old was ready, trading Torres to clear spending room while bringing back outfield or pitching help made sense.
