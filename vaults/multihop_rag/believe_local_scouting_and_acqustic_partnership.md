# Believe's Local Scouting Model And The Acqustic Partnership

Believe's strength, in Alejandra Olea's account, is its presence across 50 countries around the world: by deploying local teams on the ground the company is fully part of local music scenes and able to identify the labels and artists with the most potential, so the local team's role is to select local artists and labels to partner with and accompany them in their development. The approach is deliberately selective, because the goal is to provide partners, regardless of their level of development, with the highest level of quality of service in the market.

The Iñigo Quintero case runs through that model: Believe identified Acqustic as a promising new label in 2020, with a great team, a strong A&R vision and a great track record; in March 2023 Acqustic signed Iñigo, and "Si No Estás" became the success it is. The relation extends past the hit, since it is now about building on that success and accompanying the artist and the serviced labels into their next phase of growth, which is made possible by Believe's ability to adapt the level of service to the needs of the artist and label: the more successful they become, the more their needs evolve, and the more Believe can provide the best support to supercharge that growth.
