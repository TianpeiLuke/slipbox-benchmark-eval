# The Stepfather's Objection to the Aisle Decision

The stepfather's objection attacks not the daughter's wish but the mother's decision procedure: he argued she should have consulted him and sought a compromise before accepting, and that she failed to weigh what he had contributed to the children's lives.

According to the Reddit user, her husband did not take the news as expected. He asked her a series of questions, the first being whether she had asked Calla if both her mother and stepfather could walk her down the aisle; the mother noted that she had not asked her daughter that. "Then he asked if she ever considered asking him, and I said I could not answer that for her. He asked me if I thought of him when I said yes," she continued. "He asked whether I gave any thought to all he has done for Calla, for both kids, and the fact he's still not looked upon as a fatherly figure all these years later. I told him I did not think of him when asked because I was overjoyed. He told me I should have given him a lot more consideration and I should have tried to compromise with my daughter."

The position was not a momentary reaction. She said that three weeks after the incident her husband had not changed his stance and still felt his wife "was wrong", and that he told her he "felt he deserved more from all" of the family, and that his wife should be "making sure he is respected and honoured for his contribution to the kids' lives".
