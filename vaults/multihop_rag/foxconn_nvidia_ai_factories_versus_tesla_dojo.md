# Foxconn And Nvidia AI Factories Versus Tesla Dojo

Foxconn and Nvidia are building "AI factories" to help accelerate AVs, robotics and other smart applications, TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-22.

The AI factories position the two against Tesla, which is building the Dojo supercomputer to do more or less the same thing — take in vast amounts of data, train it, tweak code and send it back out to self-driving cars.
