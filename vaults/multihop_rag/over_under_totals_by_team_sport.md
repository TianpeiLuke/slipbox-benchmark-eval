# Over/Under Totals By Team Sport

Over/under betting is a major draw across many popular sports, and the only thing that changes from sport to sport is the scale of the number: NFL and NBA totals are set in points, MLB in runs, NHL and soccer in goals. Sporting News walks through one worked line in each.

For the NFL, in a Dallas Cowboys-Philadelphia Eagles game the bookmaker sets the over/under total at 47.5 points; a bettor expecting a high-scoring game bets the "over," and with the Cowboys scoring 28 and the Eagles 24 for a combined 52, the "over" wins because 52 exceeds 47.5. For the NBA, in a Charlotte Hornets-Houston Rockets game the total is 215.5 points; a bettor expecting a defensive showdown bets the "under," and with the Hornets scoring 105 and the Rockets 108 for a combined 213, the "under" wins because 213 is below 215.5.

For MLB, in a New York Yankees-Boston Red Sox game the total is set at 8.5 runs; a bettor expecting an offensively charged game with both teams' best hitters in the lineup bets the "over," and with the Yankees scoring 5 and the Red Sox 6 for a combined 11 runs, the "over" wins. For the NHL, in a Toronto Maple Leafs-Montreal Canadiens game the total is 5.5 goals; a bettor anticipating a game dominated by goalkeepers and defense bets the "under," and with the Maple Leafs netting 3 and the Canadiens 2 for a combined 5, the "under" wins because 5 is below 5.5. For Premier League soccer, in a Manchester United-Chelsea match the total is 2.5 goals; a bettor who feels both teams have been on a scoring spree bets the "over," and with each side scoring 2 for a combined 4 goals, the "over" wins because 4 surpasses 2.5.
