# Kick Streamer Exodus Over The Sex Worker Stream

Streamers began leaving Kick en masse in protest of the platform's lack of safety guidelines, after a prominent creator streamed an encounter with a sex worker without informing her that there were other people present.

The woman was briefly prevented from leaving after she expressed discomfort, while Kick's CEO posted laughing emotes in the stream chat. "It sucks to see a platform stand behind someone who clearly doesn't respect sex workers or their safety," Rachel, a streamer known as TheFoodieWaifu, told TechCrunch. "This was a job for her and to them a game. This woman clearly was afraid and was just trying to work and be safe."
