# Zuckerberg On Bringing Threads To The Fediverse

Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg seems to earnestly believe that bringing Threads to the fediverse is a good idea, both for Meta's product and for its business, telling The Verge's Alex Heath earlier in the fall: "Not everyone wants to use one product, and I think making it so that they can use an alternative but can still interact with people on the network will make it so that that product also is more valuable."

The Verge's writer treated the remark as striking evidence for the fediverse case — "Mark Zuckerberg! Said that!" — reading it as an acknowledgment that people want to feel like their social connections and content belong to them, not to a company that can disappear or pivot or change its ways. His conclusion from Meta's own stance: if Meta can't build and maintain the One True Social Network For Everybody, nobody can, and it's well past time to try something else.
