# Will Smith's Baltimore Book-Event Remarks On A "Brutiful" Marriage

Speaking to a crowd at the Enoch Pratt Free Library in Baltimore, Maryland on 18 October 2023, Will Smith described his relationship with Jada Pinkett Smith as "very, very long and tumultuous," adding: "We call it 'brutiful.' It was brutal and beautiful at the same time." The 55-year-old actor appeared at the press event for his wife's book, Worthy, alongside their two children, Jaden, 25, and Willow, 22, days after she disclosed in the memoir that the couple had been separated for seven years. He opened by telling the crowd, as reported by The Baltimore Sun: "I just really wanted to come out and just be here and hold it down for you the way you have held it down for me."

At another point he called his union with Pinkett Smith, whom he married in 1997, a "sloppy public experiment in unconditional love," according to the Baltimore Banner, and said he is "happier than [he's] ever been in [his] entire life." He credited his wife's sacrifices for his career: "I have achieved every single thing I have ever wanted in my entire life. There is not one thing left that I desire in this world — all of the awards, all of the money, the family — everything I've ever dreamed. And those dreams were largely built on the foundation of Jada's sacrifices. There were many times when Jada put her career aside so I could follow the dreams of mine." He also thanked her for putting him "before herself" and for maintaining a good relationship with his ex-wife Sheree Zampino, with whom he shares 30-year-old son Trey Smith — who was also present at the event — saying that relationship had allowed all three of his children to grow up together. He closed with a question and a pledge: "Can you show up and love somebody for the rest of your life, even when you don't agree with them? Jada is the best friend I have ever had on this planet and I am going to show up for her and support her for the rest of my life."
