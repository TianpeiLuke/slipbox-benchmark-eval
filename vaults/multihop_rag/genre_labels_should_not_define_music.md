# Genre Labels Should Not Define Music

Michelle Jubelirer argues that music is music and genres should not be defined by colour, and that the business needs to change its definition of "pop" music. Her ground is that there seems to be real confusion in the current usage, and her proposed replacement rule is that any music that streams in volume should be called pop music.

The operating philosophy that follows is that great ideas transcend genre: CMG has experts in every "genre" but they all share, collaborate and respectfully challenge each other. Jubelirer goes further than rejecting particular genre boundaries, saying she does not even like the word "genre" and that it should be changed.
