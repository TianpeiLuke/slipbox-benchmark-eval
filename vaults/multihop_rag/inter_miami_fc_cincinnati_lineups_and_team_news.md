# Inter Miami-FC Cincinnati Lineups: Messi On The Bench, Aviles Back From Suspension

For the Oct. 7, 2023 meeting at DRV PNK Stadium, Sporting News listed Inter Miami (5-3-2, right to left) as 1. Callender (GK) — 2. Yedlin (Taylor, 76'), 6. Aviles (Messi, 55'), 27. Kryvtsov, 31. Miller, 32. Allen — 41. Ruiz (Mota, 55'), 5. Busquets, 30. Cremaschi — 11. Farias, 17. Martinez (Stefanelli, 76'), with a 12-man bench listing of Dos Santos (GK), Sailor, Taylor, Mota, Arroyo, Ulloa, Robinson, Messi and Stefanelli. FC Cincinnati (5-4-1, right to left) were 18. Celentano (GK) — 13. Arias, 4. Hagglund, 21. Miazga, 32. Murphy, 31. Barreal (Halsey, 81') — 5. Nwobodo, 10. Acosta (Badji, 88'), 93. Moreno (Kubo, 65') — 19. Vazquez (Pinto, 88'), 17. Santos (Boupendza, 46'), with a nine-man bench of Kann (GK), Kubo, Mosquera, Angulo, Badji, Pinto, Halsey, Valenzuela and Boupendza; the report also gave FC Cincinnati's shape as 5-3-2 in its lineup summary.

Inter Miami's absentees: Jordi Alba remained sidelined with a hamstring injury, having gone down just minutes before Messi and with no publicly known return timeline, and striker Leonardo Campana was also missing after sitting out the midweek defeat to the Chicago Fire with a muscle injury, though Gerardo Martino described his issue as "minor." Important midfielder Gregore, who had missed most of the season with a foot injury, was back in training and should return at some point, though when was not known. Defender Tomas Aviles was back from suspension and started in the three center-back system.

FC Cincinnati's absentees: defender Alvas Powell was suspended after being sent off against the New York Red Bulls in their previous match, with Santiago Arias stepping in at right-back. Otherwise the club was light on availability concerns, with only a leg injury to Arquimides Ordonez and a long-term injury to young midfielder Stiven Jimenez to worry about.
