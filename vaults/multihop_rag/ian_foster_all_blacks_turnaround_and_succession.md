# Ian Foster's All Blacks Turnaround And Succession

Ian Foster survived as All Blacks head coach through home defeats to Ireland, South Africa and Argentina in the summer of 2022, and then turned the team around: after the 2023 quarter-final win over Ireland they were one very small step away from another World Cup final. The All Blacks looked lost at times during this World Cup cycle, and the report calls it still faintly remarkable that Foster survived last summer as those home defeats piled up.

Yet somehow he hung on and turned things round. He holds the unwanted title of being the first New Zealand coach to lose a World Cup pool match, but a semi-final awaited the following Friday evening against an Argentina side that, frankly, were a tier below them. Forget the hand-wringing ahead of the tournament.

Foster will leave his role after the World Cup, to be replaced by all-conquering Crusaders boss Scott Robertson, but he can still exit with the greatest prize of all — and frankly, who would bet against the All Blacks now?
