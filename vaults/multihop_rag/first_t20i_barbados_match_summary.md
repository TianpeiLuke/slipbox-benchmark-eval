# First T20I In Barbados: Match Summary

West Indies beat England in the first T20 international in Barbados on Dec. 12, 2023, chasing down 171 with eleven balls remaining after England collapsed from 112-2 at the ten-over mark.

The Guardian's James Wallace called it an intriguing game that set up the T20I series nicely, and a real statement laid down by Rovman Powell and his men. West Indies were up against it early, with England crunching their way to 112-2 off the first ten overs, but Jos Buttler's side managed only 59 runs off the next ten, losing eight wickets along the way. A total of 171 was thirty or more shy of where England would have liked to end up, and so it proved as West Indies showed how muscular their own batting card is — the home side cleared the ropes fourteen times compared with England's six. Andre Russell and Rovman Powell found the stands with ease at the business end of the match to get West Indies over the line. The second match of the series was to be played in Grenada on the Thursday.
