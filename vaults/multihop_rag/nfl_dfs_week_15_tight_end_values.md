# NFL DFS Week 15 Tight End Values

Sporting News' headline Week 15 2023 tight end DFS play was David Njoku of the Browns against Chicago (DK $4,700, FD $6,200, SD 1.3x).

Njoku had gone nuclear the previous weekend, catching six of his eight targets from Joe Flacco for 91 yards and two touchdowns, and now got a chance for an encore against a Bears team that had surrendered the 10th-most PPR points to tight ends that season. More solid Week 15 tight end values listed were Logan Thomas, Commanders at Rams (DK $3,700, FD $5,100, SD 1.5x); Cade Otton, Buccaneers at Packers (DK $3,200, FD $5,200, SD 1.65x); and Tucker Kraft, Packers vs. Buccaneers (DK $3,000, FD $4,700, SD 1.8x).
