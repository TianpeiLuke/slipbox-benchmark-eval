# The Day Of Reckoning Card And The Heavyweight Unification Path

The Middle East card dubbed the "Day of Reckoning," staged at Kingdom Arena in Riyadh, featured heavyweight stars Anthony Joshua, Deontay Wilder and Joseph Parker plus light heavyweight champion Dmitrii Bivol, as reported by The Roar on 23 December 2023.

The night rearranged the division's plans. Parker's victory over Wilder came before Joshua stopped Otto Wallin with a fifth-round TKO in a heavyweight double-header. The bouts were expected to set up a March bout between Wilder and Joshua; instead, it appears Parker is in a position to step up in March.

The stakes beyond that are stated precisely: the winner of that March fight hopes to get a shot at the undisputed championship, which will be decided on 17 February when Tyson Fury and Oleksandr Usyk meet in Saudi Arabia. The heavyweight division hasn't been unified this century.
