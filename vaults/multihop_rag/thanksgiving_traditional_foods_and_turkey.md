# Thanksgiving Traditional Foods and the Turkey

In America turkeys are an integral part of Thanksgiving Day, with most dinners including the bird — an estimated 46 million turkeys are killed annually for the holiday, though two turkeys are pardoned each year by the president.

The turkey is not a requirement of the meal. Americans who do not eat meat or who follow a vegan lifestyle do not have to worry about missing out, as there are numerous vegan and vegetarian Thanksgiving options.

Beyond the bird, Americans also indulge in Thanksgiving favourites such as yams topped with marshmallows, stuffing, cranberry sauce, cornbread, and pumpkin pie.
