# Georgia's Third-Quarter Field Goal And Lovett's Gain

The third quarter of Georgia's win over Ole Miss on Nov. 11, 2023 ended Ole Miss 14, Georgia 38, with the Bulldogs' last points of the period coming on Peyton Woodring's 27-yard field goal at 9:32 p.m., his first attempt of the night.

The kick followed a 43-yard gain by Dominic Lovett at 9:29 p.m. that the live blog suggested might just seal the deal for Georgia: his catch, together with a personal foul against Ole Miss, set the Bulldogs up inside the Rebels' red zone with the score Ole Miss 14, Georgia 35.
