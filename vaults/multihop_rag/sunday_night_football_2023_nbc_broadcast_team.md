# NBC's 2023 Sunday Night Football Broadcast And Streaming Package

"Sunday Night Football" in the 2023 NFL season aired on NBC, which has long held the broadcast rights to the package, with Mike Tirico on play-by-play, Cris Collinsworth as color analyst and Melissa Stark as sideline reporter. Sporting News described the same three-person crew for the Chiefs-Jets game on Oct. 1 from MetLife Stadium, the Cowboys-49ers game on Oct. 8 from Levi's Stadium, and the Dolphins-Eagles game on Oct. 22.

The crew was in its second season together. Tirico had replaced play-by-play legend Al Michaels — in 2022, per the Oct. 22 listing — and Stark had taken over for Michele Tafoya.

The streaming options were consistent across those games. In the United States, fans could stream via Peacock or NBCSports.com with a subscription, or via Fubo, which offers a free trial. Viewers in Canada could stream every NFL game via DAZN.
