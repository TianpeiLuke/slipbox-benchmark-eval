# Alanna Rizzo Apologized On Air To Jake Mintz And The BBWAA

Following the backlash to her comments, Alanna Rizzo apologized on "High Heat" to Jake Mintz and the BBWAA, calling her remarks "completely inappropriate, it was inaccurate and it was beyond unprofessional. I got caught up in the heat of the moment."

"What I said was completely off base and I've had an interesting 24 hours and I've learned a lot more and I apologize to Jake. I talked to him already, I apologized to him privately and I also apologize to him publicly," Rizzo said. "They do a good job in the clubhouse and I was completely inaccurate in my assessment of what went down there." Awful Announcing described it on October 13, 2023 as "a very heartfelt apology to Jake Mintz and the entire BBWAA."
