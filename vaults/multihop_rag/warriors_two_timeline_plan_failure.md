# Warriors Two-Timeline Plan Failure

Yardbarker set out on Nov. 29, 2023 how Golden State's two-timeline approach — meant to infuse the team with youth and athleticism alongside the veteran core — produced the opposite result: a franchise built through the draft has had very little success selecting players over the past decade, and the Warriors have had a hard time reinventing themselves.

The mechanism runs from failed picks to compensating trades to an older roster. Instead of youth, Kuminga and Moody could not crack the rotation some nights, and 2020's No. 2 overall pick James Wiseman was by then playing in Detroit. If anything, the Warriors got older: over the previous nine months they traded 24-year-old Jordan Poole to the Wizards in a deal that netted 38-year-old Chris Paul, unloaded Wiseman to bring back 30-year-old Gary Payton II, and signed 29-year-old Dario Saric to help anchor the second unit. Behind the Bucks and Clippers, the Warriors were the league's third-oldest team.
