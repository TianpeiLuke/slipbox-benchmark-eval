# Epoch Times Covid And "CCP Virus" Coverage

The Epoch Times was early to lay blame on China for Covid, labelling it the "CCP Virus" in its coverage — a claim the article marks as unproven, noting that the origin of the outbreak is unknown and that the best evidence still points to natural transmission from an animal market.

What lent those unproven claims credibility, according to the article, was China's own documented handling of the virus: withholding information from researchers, a crackdown on whistleblowers, and an authoritarian public health response.

The consequence was a new audience. "The dynamic for The Epoch Times changed in 2020, partly because of their criticism of China around Covid," said A.J. Bauer, an assistant journalism professor at the University of Alabama who studies conservative media. "An entire new kind of ecosystem of Covid deniers and skeptics overlapped with right-wing media and were citing and drawing heavily upon The Epoch Times."
