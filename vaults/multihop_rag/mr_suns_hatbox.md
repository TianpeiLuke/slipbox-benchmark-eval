# Mr. Sun's Hatbox

Mr. Sun's Hatbox, developed by Kenny Sun for Nintendo Switch and Windows PC, is ranked 50 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31. It is about a hat delivery person — or maybe just a blob with legs — who takes their job way too seriously.

At the beginning of the game a customer's package gets stolen and whisked away to a nearby towering castle, and despite the client's apathy toward a single missing hat, the delivery company, named Amazin, proceeds to set up an entire subterranean paramilitary operation beneath the poor customer's home.

As its premise suggests, this pixelated 2D roguelite leans into the absurd. Critic Ana Diaz describes it as part Metal Gear Solid 5, part Spelunky: you undertake missions where you blast away enemies and kidnap them for your own operation while slapstick action unfolds, with anything from a desk lamp to daggers fair game for a weapon; between fights you expand your base and manage a staff of brainwashed blob-people. She calls it fast, frenetic fun, and especially enjoyable to share with friends in co-op.
