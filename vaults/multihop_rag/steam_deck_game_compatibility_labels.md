# Steam Deck Game Compatibility Labels

Valve's compatibility labels are markers on Steam games signifying how well they'll work on the handheld, with four levels: unknown, unsupported, playable and verified.

The discriminating line runs between the top two: playable games generally come with a small caveat, like having to deploy the on-screen keyboard, while verified games have been fully optimized for portable play. Currently, more than 10,000 titles on Steam fall under the playable or verified labels, with more added every day.
