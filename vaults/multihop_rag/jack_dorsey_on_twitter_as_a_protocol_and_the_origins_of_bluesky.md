# Jack Dorsey On Twitter As A Protocol And The Origins Of Bluesky

The Verge noted that even the most successful centralized platforms have long understood that a protocol-driven social web is a good idea: Jack Dorsey used to say that Twitter was better as a protocol than a platform, and he started the project that became Bluesky before also helping get Nostr going.

The article treats this as evidence that the case for the fediverse is not a particularly hot take. It also draws a lesson from what followed at Twitter without retelling the story: the speed with which that platform changed made a lot of people acutely aware that we need a social ecosystem that can resist the whims of a single company or CEO.
