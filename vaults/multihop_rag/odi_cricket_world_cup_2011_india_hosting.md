# The 2011 World Cup: India's Last Hosting Before 2023

The last time India welcomed the ODI Cricket World Cup before 2023 was in 2011, when they were co-hosts with Sri Lanka and Bangladesh. Pakistan were barred from hosting due to terrorist attacks on the Sri Lankan team when touring the country a couple of years prior to the World Cup.

The 2011 World Cup was a tournament of many firsts. Australia's streak of remaining unbeaten in 35 World Cup matches was broken when they were defeated by Pakistan, having not lost a game since the 1999 tournament. It was also the first time two Asian teams were in the finals, as India became the first country to win a World Cup at home by defeating Sri Lanka in the decider.

Writing on Oct. 1, 2023, the Sporting News added that after a prolonged drought of ICC trophies, India were favourites to bring the trophy home again in 2023.
