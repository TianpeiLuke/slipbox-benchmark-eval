# Technology Neutrality: How The Online Safety Act Covers AI Content

The Online Safety Act attempts to address online harms in a "technology neutral" way, Whitehead says, regardless of how they have been created — so although artificial intelligence is not emphasized in Ofcom's consultation, that does not mean AI-generated content falls outside the rules.

The discriminating test is the harm, not the tool. AI-generated CSAM would be in scope by virtue of it being CSAM, and a deepfake used to conduct fraud would be in scope by virtue of the fraud. As Whitehead puts it: "We're not regulating the technology, we're regulating the context."
