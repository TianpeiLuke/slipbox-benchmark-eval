# EU AI Act Scope Exclusions For National Security And Research

The AI Act does not apply to areas outside the scope of EU law, and the Council's press release on the deal emphasizes that the provisional agreement "clarifies that the regulation does not apply to areas outside the scope of EU law and should not, in any case, affect member states' competences in national security or any entity entrusted with tasks in this area."

The Council also confirmed that the AI Act will not apply to systems exclusively for military or defence purposes. A further exclusion covers early-stage and non-commercial use: "Similarly, the agreement provides that the regulation would not apply to AI systems used for the sole purpose of research and innovation, or for people using AI for non-professional reasons," the Council added.
