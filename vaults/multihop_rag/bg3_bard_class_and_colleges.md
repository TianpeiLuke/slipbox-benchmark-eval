# BG3 Bard: The Support Class And Its Colleges

Polygon names Bard the best support class in Baldur's Gate 3. Bards are a specialized kind of caster that focus more on performance and charm than on a weighty list of spells.

Bards only get Light Armor and focus on Charisma as their primary stat, so they rarely belong on the front line of a fight. Where they come into their own is Bardic Inspiration — a way to boost the rolls of their allies — and a spell list that is a nice mix of utility and offensive magic. Bards get three subclasses at level 3: College of Lore, College of Valor, and College of Swords. College of Lore Bards gain many useful out-of-combat skills and can spend Bardic Inspiration to make enemies weaker; College of Valor Bards help their allies hit even harder; College of Swords Bards focus on a swashbuckling style of combat. College of Valor is the best subclass for a Bard, with the reasoning and the skill and feat picks left to Polygon's separate Bard build guide.
