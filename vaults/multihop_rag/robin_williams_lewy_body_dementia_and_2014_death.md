# Robin Williams' Lewy Body Dementia And 2014 Death

Robin Williams was unaware in the years before his death that he was dealing with Lewy body dementia; he died by suicide on 11 August 2014 and was diagnosed with the neurological disorder posthumously after an autopsy, FOX News Entertainment reported on 19 October 2023.

Before he died he reportedly appeared tired while on set and did not seem to be his usual self. Lewy body dementia is the second most common type of dementia after Alzheimer's, and the disease affects brain regions involved in thinking, memory and movement, according to the Mayo Clinic. The autopsy found no illegal drugs or traces of alcohol in Williams' system.

Comedian and friend Steven Pearl described the change during the Vice documentary: "The last time I saw Robin Williams, I hadn't seen him for a few months, and he was thin, and he didn't recognize my wife. He hardly said a word. It took him a second to recognize me. I go, 'Whoa, something's wrong here.' I didn't know what. Nobody would really tell me. And then I never saw him again after that."
