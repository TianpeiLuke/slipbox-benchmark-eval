# Lumesner Carry-On Backpack

Audience manager Kaitlin Hatton committed to traveling more earlier this year and took a look at the gear she had that could be replaced after years of trekking the globe; her ratty old secondhand carry-on bag was the first thing to go, and after several days of weighing the pros and cons of various travel bags she stumbled on this Lumesner carry-on backpack on Amazon, which fit all of her needs.

It can carry a laptop, several days' worth of clothes, her 40oz Hydro Flask bottle, and more, and it even includes some packing cubes. It is very comfortable, and the weight is well distributed when it is completely full. It is also an inexpensive alternative to many name-brand carry-on bags.

So far she has used it on a handful of trips and the quality has held up. One drawback: it holds onto pet fur, so she had to add a small lint roller to her travel necessities — but that is just life while traveling with a dog anyway.
