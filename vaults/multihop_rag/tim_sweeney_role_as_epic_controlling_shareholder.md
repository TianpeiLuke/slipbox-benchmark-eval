# Tim Sweeney's Role As Epic's Controlling Shareholder

Tim Sweeney is Epic's CEO, co-founder, and — importantly — its controlling shareholder, which is what makes him the person behind Epic's lawsuits: challenging these companies in court was his idea, and it has been his fight from the very beginning.

That personal ownership of the fight extended to the courtroom itself. Sweeney watched almost the entire trial in person from the best seat in the house, with a clear view of the jury, the judge, each witness, and the faces of Google's lawyers.
