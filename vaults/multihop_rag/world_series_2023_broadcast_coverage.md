# 2023 World Series Broadcast Coverage

Fox carried the full 2023 World Series between the Rangers and Diamondbacks in the United States, with Sportsnet holding Canadian rights; live streams ran on the Fox Sports app and Fubo in the U.S. and on Sportsnet NOW in Canada.

The commentary team is the listing's other defining feature: Joe Davis handled play-by-play, John Smoltz provided commentary, and Ken Rosenthal and Tom Verducci reported from the dugouts. This lineup applied to the series as a whole, not to any single game.

The cord-cutting boundary is stated explicitly: for those who have cut the cord on cable, fans could find the game on the Fox Sports app or with Fubo, which offers a free trial, while Canadian viewers could find it on Sportsnet or on Sportsnet NOW. The same channel and stream details were published for both Game 3 on Oct. 30 and Game 4 on Oct. 31.
