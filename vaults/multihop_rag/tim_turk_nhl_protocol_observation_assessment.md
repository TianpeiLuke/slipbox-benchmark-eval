# Tim Turk's NHL Protocol Observation Assessment

Tim Turk's evaluation method, which he calls an "NHL protocol observation assessment," runs new clients through specific drills that allow him to visualise their shooting actions from a technical standpoint — he calls each player's shot "like a fingerprint" — as described in The New York Times on 17 November 2023.

The opening step is stationary. Turk throws some pucks down in the slot and simply asks the player to shoot them stationary, so he can watch their body formation, hip and shoulder positions, spine angles, and puck preparation and positioning, paying particular attention to where the puck starts in their stance versus where it finishes.

The precondition implied by the name is that this is what he does with new clients rather than players he already knows, and the aim is observation and visualisation of an existing technique rather than correction of it.
