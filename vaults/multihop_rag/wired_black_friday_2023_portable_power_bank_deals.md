# WIRED Black Friday 2023 Portable Power Bank Deals

WIRED's Apple Black Friday guide of 25 November 2023 listed five discounted portable power banks, ranging from a 24,000-mAh laptop-capable brick to the Zendure SuperTank Pro, a cable-free 5,000-mAh stick, a Baseus high-capacity bank and Satechi's wireless-charging Duo.

The upgrade pick has an absolutely gargantuan 24,000-mAh capacity, can be charged up in an hour, and outputs up to 140W, making it powerful enough to charge laptops and tablets as well as charging a phone very quickly. The Zendure SuperTank Pro is described as the largest portable battery you can get away with taking onto an airplane, which is why it is WIRED's favourite for air travel: 26,800-mAh capacity, four USB ports and up to 100 watts in or out make it the only power bank needed for every device on even extremely long flights, and it kind of looks like a mini carry-on suitcase — but you must click the coupon button to get the full deal. The cable-free option packs a built-in rotating USB-C or Lightning connector (MFi certified) that slots into the bottom of your phone, plus 5,000-mAh capacity and a USB-C port for recharging with a short cable included; WIRED does not recommend it if your phone has a thick case.

The Baseus charger, whose discount also requires clicking the coupon button, is WIRED's recommendation for a high-capacity option: with 10,000-mAh capacity, WIRED contributor Simon Hill says it charged his iPhone 14 Pro once and then delivered an additional 64 percent the second time before tapping out, and it comes with a USB-C cable; the 6,000-mAh version is also on sale for $30 ($20 off). WIRED flags an eligibility condition on those Baseus deals: they are only applicable to Amazon Prime members. The Satechi Duo, again with a coupon button to click, combines wireless charging with power-bank portability, offering 10,000-mAh capacity with LEDs showing remaining juice, wireless charging for both a phone and a Qi-certified wireless earbuds case, and a USB-C port for a third device; WIRED's only gripe is that it is slow, delivering 10 watts of wireless power for phones (7.5 watts for iPhones), 5 watts for earbuds and 10 watts over USB-C.
