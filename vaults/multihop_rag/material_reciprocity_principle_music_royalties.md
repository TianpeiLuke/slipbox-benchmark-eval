# The Material Reciprocity Principle In Music Royalties

Because of the EU-US asymmetry, European countries largely followed the "reciprocity principle" written into international copyright treaties such as the WIPO Performances and Phonograms Treaty of 1996: in those countries where artists and labels are paid performance rights, there was no obligation to pay US artists and labels, since European artists did not receive those payments from airplay in the US.

The artists' groups' statement defends the principle in general terms, saying it "has proven vital in raising the level of copyright protection across the globe for decades."
