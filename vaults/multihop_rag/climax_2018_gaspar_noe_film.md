# Climax: Gaspar Noé's Spiked-Sangria Dance Party In Long Takes

Climax, directed by Gaspar Noé and starring Sofia Boutella, Romain Guillermic and Souheila Yacoub, is available on Showtime — and Polygon warns it isn't for the faint of heart, saying so in the context of a horror movies list. The film is set at an all-night dance party inside a gymnasium, which turns sour after someone spikes the sangria with a little too much LSD.

Its formal signature is the long take: Climax is told in beautifully disorienting long takes that go from dozens of minutes of uninterrupted and propulsive dance sequences to hazy walks through hallways as the camera mimics the dizzy stumbling of the characters. As the psychedelics kick in, so too do some of the attendees' long-held feuds, leading to disastrous and horrifying consequences. The reviewer's boundary condition on the recommendation is explicit — it is rare that a movie truly defies description, but if you have a strong stomach and a will to see something you haven't before, Climax is the perfect movie for you.
