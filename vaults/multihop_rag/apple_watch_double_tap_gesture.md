# The Apple Watch Double Tap Gesture

Double tap is an Apple Watch Series 9 and Ultra 2 feature that lets you interact with the watch without ever needing to use the touchscreen: with a quick pinching motion you can scroll through the new smart stack of widgets in watchOS 10, pause or end timers, skip music tracks, and answer phone calls. It was not available when the watches launched in September 2023 and finally arrived on October 25th, 2023 via the watchOS 10.1 update. Double tap activates the primary action of an app.

Its discriminating feature is that it solves a real problem rather than showing off. Controlling gadgets with gestures is often gimmicky — tech that seeks to show off rather than solve a problem — but The Verge's reviewer argued double tap solves a genuine problem and is born from accessible design that serves real needs. The canonical scenario is the one that turns a scoff into a use: unloading groceries from your car with your hands full when an important call comes through on your watch.

The boundary is how often that scenario arises for a given person. Depending on who you are it might happen multiple times a day or once in a blue moon; across a few weeks with the watchOS 10.1 beta the reviewer said some days she completely forgot double tap existed, because either her hands were free or muscle memory kicked in and she used the watch as she always had, while on other days, when her to-do list felt as long as a CVS receipt, she morphed into a double tap fiend — snapping away, she wrote, like a flamenco dancer with castanets.
