# Alliance DAO Accelerator Program

Alliance DAO is a web3 accelerator that runs a three-month-long program, bringing in web3 founders for at least 10 hours per week twice a year, and it invests $250,000 in the startups that take part. Its All11 program participants were showcased at a demo day that TechCrunch+ covered exclusively on 2023-10-18.

The defining payoff the accelerator advertises is investor access rather than curriculum alone. According to Alliance's website, "Each startup in our latest cohort received an average of over 50 intro requests after demo day," from major VC firms like Paradigm and Multicoin.
