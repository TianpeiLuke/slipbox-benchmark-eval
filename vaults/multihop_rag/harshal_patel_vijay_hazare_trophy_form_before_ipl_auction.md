# Harshal Patel's Vijay Hazare Trophy Form Before The IPL Auction

Harshal Patel went into the IPL 2024 auction having been released by RCB but with strong Vijay Hazare Trophy numbers behind him: 18 wickets in 10 matches, plus 183 runs, meaning he would be considered for an all-rounder role. The observation was posted at 12.04 PM on auction day.

Three minutes earlier, at 12.01 PM, the live blog framed the open questions: would the Vijay Hazare Trophy performances have an impact on the bidding, would Harshal Patel get a bid, and would he be trusted with bowling in the death overs again.
