# Jordan Poyer's Reconciliation With His Biological Father

Jordan Poyer says the ayahuasca experience enabled him to let go of the hate and resentment he had held toward Louis Dunbar, the man who called him when he was 12 and stunned him with the revelation that he was his biological father, The New York Times reported on 15 December 2023.

Dunbar has done multiple stints in prison for violent crimes. In conversations after that first call he often said he wanted to see Poyer or come to one of his games; he never has, and Poyer does not know where he is.

"I always had this hate and resentment toward him I was holding," says Poyer. "This experience enabled me to let that go. I realize I wouldn't have anything — my daughter and my wife — if not for him. Even if it was just the seed he gave my mom. So someday I'll meet him, give him a hug and tell him I'm sorry." Healing, the article notes, takes many forms.
