# The 50-30-20 Budgeting Method

The 50-30-20 budgeting method, championed by Credit Karma consumer financial advocate Courtney Alev, allocates 50% of your income to necessities like food and rent, 30% to things you want, and 20% to savings and debt repayment.

To find space in your budget for gifts, Alev recommends that you plan ahead as much as possible and only take money from the 30% portion of the budget. The method is not mandatory: if 50-30-20 is not the right format for you, there are plenty of other budgeting methods available — recording your spending on an Excel spreadsheet, using an app, or writing it down in a notebook. The scope condition Alev attaches is that keeping a budget is most effective when you do it in a format that works for you. For those who want a starting point, Microsoft Office offers a holiday spending-focused budget template.
