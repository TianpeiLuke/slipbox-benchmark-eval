# FanDuel NFL DFS Contest Format And Scoring Rules

FanDuel NFL daily fantasy contests are defined by two notable scoring rules — four-point passing touchdowns and half-point PPR — played on the Sunday main slate with a $60,000 budget, per Sporting News' Week 14 and Week 15 picks articles of Dec. 7 and Dec. 13, 2023.

Those two rules are what distinguish FanDuel's format from other operators' for lineup-building purposes: passing touchdowns are worth four points rather than six, and receptions are worth half a point each rather than a full point or none. The contest structure named in both articles is the Sunday main slate under a $60,000 salary budget.
