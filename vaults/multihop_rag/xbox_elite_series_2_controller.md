# Xbox Elite Series 2 Controller

Microsoft's Xbox Elite Series 2 builds on the excellent foundation of Microsoft's Xbox controller with features that enthusiast gamers may appreciate, at a $178.99 price and with compatibility limited to Xbox and PC.

Its distinguishing features are swappable stick caps and directional pads, adjustable tension for its analog sticks, and customizable back paddles, and it ships in a nice zip-up case. Polygon's verdict is conditional: if you want more buttons and tweakable settings in a controller, this might be worth its price. For buyers who do not need all of the extra accessories, Microsoft makes a cheaper Elite Series 2 Core, and if you later decide you want the paddles, zip-up case, and swappable sticks, you can buy a third-party kit on Amazon for $22.99.
