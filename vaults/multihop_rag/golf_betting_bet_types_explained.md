# The Bet Types Available In Golf Betting

Golf bets are typically placed on professional tournaments, including PGA and DP World Tour events, and the popular options are a distinct set of market types. Bet to Win means picking a specific golfer to win the entire tournament. Betting Against the Field means wagering on a golfer to outperform the rest of the players. Head-to-Head Bets involve choosing between two golfers on which will finish with a superior score. Each-Way Bets are dual bets where one part is on the golfer to win outright and the other on them finishing within a set range of places, often top 5 or top 10. First-Round Leader bets on who will lead after the first round.

The remaining markets extend the same logic. Top 10/Top 20 Bets wager on a golfer to finish within the top 10 or 20 players. Win/Place Bets are similar to each-way bets but might cover more places. Matchup Bets pick which golfer from a specific pair will have the better tournament performance, while Group Matchups bet on which golfer from a pre-selected group will fare best. Nationality Bets wager on a winning golfer hailing from a certain country. Prop Bets are special bets such as predicting whether a golfer will score a hole-in-one.

Golf betting has surged in popularity, especially live golf betting, which allows fans to bet on every shot and hole for PGA Tour events, The Match, the Ryder Cup and more; sports betting apps and live streaming have made it easier and more exciting, letting you bet on golfers live as the round unfolds with just a few taps.
