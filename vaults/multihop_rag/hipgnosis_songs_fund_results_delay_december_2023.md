# Hipgnosis Songs Fund's Results Delay, December 2023

On the morning of 19 December 2023, the board of Hipgnosis Songs Fund, now led by Chairman Rob Naylor, issued a statement announcing that HSF would no longer be issuing its latest financial results that day as previously expected; the results, covering the six months to the end of September 2023, were instead expected to be published sometime before the close of 31 December.

The stated reason was a recently-updated company valuation from HSF's official independent valuer, the US-headquartered Citrin Cooperman. The UK-listed company's board said this valuation was "materially higher than the valuation implied by proposed and recent transactions in the sector", and pointed to two transactions or would-be transactions as evidence: the proposed sale earlier that year of assets to Blackstone-backed Hipgnosis Songs Capital for USD $440 million pre-costs, or $417.5 million post-costs, a proposal ultimately rejected by HSF shareholders; and the sale of 20,000 "non-core" songs from HSF's portfolio to an unnamed buyer, possibly Kobalt Music Group, for $23.1 million, announced on 11 December, a price which according to the board represented a 14.2% discount on Citrin Cooperman's valuation of that portfolio as of 30 September.
