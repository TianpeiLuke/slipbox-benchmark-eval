# Randy Arozarena Rays Trade Candidacy

Randy Arozarena was ranked No. 15 on Yardbarker's Nov. 13, 2023 list of the top 25 MLB offseason trade candidates, with the argument that although he had not been the subject of the same level of trade speculation as Tyler Glasnow, the Rays should be more willing to move the All-Star outfielder if they were looking to create payroll room.

Tampa Bay had greater depth in the outfield than on its injury-riddled pitching staff, with Josh Lowe, Jose Siri, Harold Ramírez, Luke Raley and Manuel Margot all on hand — Margot having come up in trade rumors as well. None of those players was as good as Arozarena, but trading star players for younger talent was familiar territory for the Tampa Bay front office. Arozarena was projected for a $9M arbitration salary and controllable for three seasons, so he would have significant trade value if the Rays were to consider moving him.
