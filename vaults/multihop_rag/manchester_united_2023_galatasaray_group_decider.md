# Manchester United 2023 Galatasaray Group Decider

United's match against Galatasaray on the Wednesday was not a knockout game, but in truth the scenario was not much different, The Guardian reported on Nov. 27, 2023: defeat would again end United's Champions League campaign prematurely, while a win — assuming Copenhagen lost against Bayern Munich — would put qualifying for the latter stages back in their hands.

United would face the same atmosphere as they had 30 years earlier, because the home fans knew victory would turn their subsequent trip to Denmark into a potential shootout for who goes through with Bayern, with the achievement of knocking out a club with the storied history of United only adding to the pleasure. Galatasaray had already beaten United 3-2 at Old Trafford in early October, Mauro Icardi scoring the winner.
