# Week 10 Fantasy Pass-Catcher Injury Overview

Heading into Week 10 of the 2023 NFL season, Sporting News reported on Nov. 11 that Tee Higgins and Nico Collins were already OUT, while Justin Jefferson, Ja'Marr Chase and T.J. Hockenson were among the other star pass-catchers beaten up going into the week — leaving open the question of whether they would be on the field when the action kicked off Sunday.

The framing was seasonal. After the clocks turned back the previous week it now got darker earlier for the foreseeable future, and that darkness allows the stars to be seen on a clear night — so it was natural to wonder whether the pass-catching stars would shine bright in Week 10.
