# FedEx 2023 Holiday Shipping Deadlines

FedEx's 2023 holiday cutoffs run: December 19th for FedEx Express Saver, December 20th for FedEx 2Day and 2Day A.M., and December 22nd for FedEx SameDay.

FedEx's same-day shipping can get a package where it needs to be by Friday, December 22nd — the last day FedEx can guarantee arrival by Christmas. Shipping by Wednesday, December 20th, allows the two-day method, and getting the package to FedEx by Tuesday, December 19th, allows the Express Saver route and, hopefully, a reasonable shipping fee.
