# Projecting Chase Young's 2024 Contract Value

Spotrac.com projected that Chase Young should make about $17.8 million annually on a four-year deal, but the Sporting News judged that figure a bit light, since it includes a couple of interior defensive linemen — Dre'Mont Jones and Zach Allen — among his closest comparisons.

Assessing how San Francisco can try to keep Young also includes looking at what his value might be on the open market: it is likely the 24-year-old will get a large payday as a free agent, and it's just a matter of how big. The Sporting News expects him to be paid to his potential, but notes he missed a lot of time in Years 2 and 3 with injuries, so he may end up highly paid in terms of average annual value while seeing lower-end guarantees.

The closest template offered is the extension the Packers gave Rashan Gary: four years, $96 million total, an AAV of $24 million, and only $34.6 million guaranteed — the modest guarantee reflecting that Gary was inconsistent to start his career and dealt with a torn ACL in 2022. Young could hypothetically crack $100 million if he's allowed to hit the open market, but the Gary deal projection seemed like a sensible ballpark at the time of writing.
