# Steam Deck OLED Size And Ergonomics

The bad news for the tiny hand gang: the Steam Deck OLED is the same monstrous size as the original — 11.7 inches long, 4.5 inches tall and nearly 2 inches thick — and the thumbsticks on the new model are actually 1mm taller than the first version. This was one of the reviewer's main complaints when she first reviewed the Steam Deck: having smaller-than-average hands, she found it looks and feels absolutely ridiculous when held, and it's just massive no matter how long her manicure gets.

The OLED model does feel more balanced than the original: it's less top-heavy and technically weighs less, and these small adjustments have made a noticeable difference during playtime, so the device feels slightly more manageable as a handheld — or, as she puts it, maybe she's just too distracted by all of its bright, pretty colors to care about the cramping in her palms. The thumbsticks have an improved texture that shouldn't turn gray after a few months' use and feel perfectly serviceable, the OLED touchscreen responds immediately to input, and the haptic feedback on the built-in trackpads feels even more precise, populating in reactive pin-pricks under the textured plastic. After a few cozy nights on the couch swapping between the LCD and OLED Steam Decks, she says she'll probably retire her original model: the Steam Deck OLED is sharper and more responsive, and indie games, AAA games and plain old Steam menus look so much better on the new hardware.
