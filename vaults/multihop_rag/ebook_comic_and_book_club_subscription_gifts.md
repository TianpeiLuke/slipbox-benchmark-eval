# Ebook, Comic And Book Club Subscription Gifts

For a bibliophile whose tastes you don't know — or when you simply want to give more options than a single book — The Verge suggests a gift card to their favourite bookstore or a subscription like Kindle Unlimited, which grants members access to millions of ebooks and even select audiobooks.

Among the alternatives it rounds up: a Comixology Unlimited subscription for fans of Marvel and DC comics as well as manga like Fairy Tale, giving access to over 45,000 comics and graphic novels and more than 2,400 manga titles from a phone or tablet for $5.99 a month, with subscriber discounts on select digital books. A Book of the Month membership currently starts at $59.99 for a three-book plan, with the company curating five to seven bestsellers and classics for members each month so it's easy to choose something to read quickly. For readers who prefer brick-and-mortar shops, a Barnes & Noble gift card (Barnes & Noble, Amazon, or Best Buy) works, and for those who buy from local bookstores there's a Bookshop.org gift card. For those who prefer traditional newspapers and magazines, you can gift a subscription to The New York Times or The Washington Post, or to publications catering to specific interests like Cosmopolitan and National Geographic.
