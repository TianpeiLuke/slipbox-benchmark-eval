# Sleep School's Impartial Position On The Clock Change's Impact

Against the claim that the clock change is a major disruptor of sleep, a spokesperson for Sleep School — a science-based sleep education and therapy service — told The Independent that the organisation is "impartial" on the extent to which the clocks changing impacts sleep.

The premise it declines to grant is that the timing of the shift is what matters: Sleep School argues instead that the important factors are the duration of time asleep and a consistent schedule.
