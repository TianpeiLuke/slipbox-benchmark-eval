# Healy's Criticism Of A One-Off Test Outside A Multi-Format Series

Australia's captain Alyssa Healy insisted the Mumbai Test should have formed part of a multi-format series, in the same way most top-tier women's tours are now conducted: the claim is that the match felt like a novelty and, in a sense, meaningless, and the warrant is that without overall series points for a trophy the contest carried nothing beyond itself.

The structural facts the report gives are that while one Test, three ODIs and three T20s will be played, each is an individual contest rather than an overarching series in the way the Ashes is decided — and that Australia had taken the trophy used for the multi-format 2021-22 series against India to be contested, only to be told by authorities that system would not be used.

Healy separated her disappointment at the result from her disappointment at the format: "I sit here disappointed that we couldn't win the Test match," she said. "But at the end of the day, we weren't necessarily playing for points, we weren't playing for overall series points for a trophy. It does make it a little bit of a novelty in a sense. The fact that it sort of feels meaningless to a sense is really disappointing to me. But in saying that we are disappointed that we couldn't get ourselves over the line."
