# Hostile Interview Questions Put To Israeli Spokespeople

Asked whether he is asked many questions that smack of antisemitism or a lack of understanding and support for Israel's position, Eylon Levy answers "how long have you got" and lists examples from his interviews. The BBC asked him in a radio interview whether there is CCTV evidence that the hostages are in Shifa Hospital and what medical treatment they were receiving. Sky News asked why Yahya Sinwar was releasing hostages even though Israel is bombing Gaza, and he had to explain that it was because Israel is bombing Gaza.

He was asked twice, in Ireland and by the Voice of America, why Hamas had been begging for five weeks to return hostages and Israel was only now bowing to US pressure to take back its children. He was also interviewed by Piers Morgan in London, whom he describes as very confrontational, who spoke about pictures from Gaza and said "this is the most shocking thing I have ever seen"; Levy told him that "the most shocking thing we have seen in our lives was the slaughter on October 7", and Morgan answered "you are right".
