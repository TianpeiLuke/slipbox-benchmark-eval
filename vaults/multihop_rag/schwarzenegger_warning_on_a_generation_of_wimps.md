# Schwarzenegger's Warning On "A Generation Of Wimps"

Arnold Schwarzenegger warned on "The Howard Stern Show" that if young people are over-pampered, the nation may be "creating a generation of wimps and weak people", FOX News Entertainment reported on 10 October 2023.

His grounds are a claim about how character forms: "You can only strengthen your character and become a really strong person inside if you have resistance, if you fail, if you get up again and if you work hard."

The warrant joining the two is that pampering removes exactly the resistance, failure and effort he identifies as the mechanism of strength — so protecting children from hardship produces weakness rather than preventing it. The former California governor issued the warning recently, in the context of his own disciplinarian parenting.
