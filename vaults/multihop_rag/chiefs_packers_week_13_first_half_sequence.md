# First Half Sequence Of Packers-Chiefs Week 13

Green Bay led Kansas City 14-6 at halftime of the Week 13 "Sunday Night Football" game, having led 7-3 after the first quarter, per Sporting News' live log of Dec. 3, 2023.

The Packers received and scored. At 8:23 p.m. the ball tumbled into the end zone and Love and the Packers spilled onto the field to lead off the matchup in the cold. A.J. Dillon burst through the mire for a first down on third-and-short at 8:28 p.m., Love and one of his favourite targets Christian Watson connected for a 20-yard gain at 8:26 p.m. that took Green Bay into Kansas City territory, and Dontayvion Wicks made a catch through traffic at 8:35 p.m. to bring Green Bay to the cusp of the end zone. At 8:39 p.m. Love hit Ben Sims for his first career touchdown — a first NFL touchdown and a first Lambeau Leap for Sims — for 7-0.

Kansas City responded with a field goal. Patrick Mahomes led the Chiefs out for their first possession at 8:42 p.m. and it got off to a good start with two plays and two first downs. Clyde Edwards-Helaire kept the chains moving on third down at 8:45 p.m. to get KC into Green Bay territory, Mahomes and Marquez Valdes-Scantling combined with great composure at 8:47 p.m., and Isiah Pacheco galloped on the outside at 8:48 p.m. to bring Kansas City to the brink of a score. At 8:50 p.m. Harrison Butler sank a field goal after a pair of sacks relegated Kansas City to bringing its special teams onto the field, making it 7-3. Green Bay's second drive ended much like the first: at 9:00 p.m. Love fired an arrow to Watson for 14-3. At 9:10 p.m. Kansas City's explosive offense, held in check thus far, was held to another field goal despite showing flashes of what it could accomplish, closing the half at 14-6.
