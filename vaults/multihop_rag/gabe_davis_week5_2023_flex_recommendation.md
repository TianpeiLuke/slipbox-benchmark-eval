# Gabe Davis As A Week 5 2023 Flex Starter Against Jacksonville

CBS Sports listed Gabe Davis as a "Flex Starter" for Week 5 of the 2023 season against Jacksonville: the claim is that the Jaguars have done well against deep-ball receivers but not enough to make benching Davis a good idea, and the warrant is that Davis' value is a fixed boom-or-bust shape that a merely good coverage matchup cannot erase.

The grounds define that shape. Until his role changes, Davis will be the same kind of fantasy option every week — one big play and he's a stud, one or two misses and he's a dud. He kept playing up to that boom/bust ceiling the previous week by catching a long touchdown, his third straight game with a score and also his third game in four with four or fewer targets. The underlying split is stark: Davis has a massive average depth of target of 16.8 yards, top-10 among the 96 receivers with at least 10 targets this season, but a minuscule 13.6% target share and a 13.4% target-per-route-run rate, 86th of those 96 — clearly a role player with a big-time skill set that isn't leaned on too much. The breakdown lists Buffalo at home versus Jacksonville, the Bills favoured by 5.5, an over/under of 48.5, an opponent ranked 11th versus wide receivers, 12.6 projected points, a 31st receiver ranking, and year-to-date totals of 12 receptions on 18 targets for 220 yards, three touchdowns and 13 fantasy points per game.

The matchup cuts both ways, which is why the verdict lands on flex rather than start or sit. Statistically Jacksonville is top-three in catch rate allowed to wideouts on throws of 15-plus air yards at 29.4%, with seven passes defensed, having done a nice job staying close in coverage on those throws — but they are dead-last in yards per catch (43.8) and yards after catch per reception (15.0) on the ones that are completed, which is exactly the outcome Davis needs. Jacksonville also ranks fifth in zone coverage rate at 78.5% and figures to play a lot of it to keep eyes on Josh Allen and deter him from throwing deep, as they did to Patrick Mahomes in Week 2. The verdict places Davis toward the top of the flex mix, ahead of Tank Dell, Tutu Atwell, Christian Watson and Jerry Jeudy.
