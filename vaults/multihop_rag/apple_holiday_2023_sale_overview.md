# Apple Holiday 2023 Sale Overview

Most of Apple's current-generation devices went on sale for Black Friday 2023, and most of those deals looked like they would carry over through the weekend and into Cyber Monday, Engadget reported on 2023-11-24 — meaning shoppers otherwise occupied on Friday could still save on iPads, Apple Watches, MacBooks, AirPods and more. Some of the prices were new lows and many others matched record prices seen previously, available from retailers like Amazon, Target, Best Buy and B&H Photo.

By Cyber Monday on 2023-11-27, Engadget reported there were still a handful more hours to shop: many of these deals started well before Black Friday and all but a few were still live, covering iPads for couchside entertainment or productivity and AirPods Pro as gifts. Surprisingly, Apple's latest products like the M3 MacBook Pro and the Apple Watch Series 9 were included in the discounts, and Engadget advised clicking "add to cart" on anything that caught the eye, expecting many of the deals to expire before the next day.
