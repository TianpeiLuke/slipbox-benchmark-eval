# Notre Dame's Fourth Quarter Against USC, Oct. 14 2023

Notre Dame outscored USC 17-7 in the fourth quarter of their Oct. 14, 2023 meeting to close out a 48-20 win, with the quarter's decisive plays a 99-yard Jadarian Price touchdown run, a Spencer Shrader field goal and Xavier Watts' fumble-return touchdown. The final line by quarter was USC 3-3-7-7 for 20 and Notre Dame 7-17-7-17 for 48.

The quarter opened much as the second half had gone, with back-to-back Audric Estime runs for five yards each setting up a third-and-5, which Sam Hartman converted with a quick strike to Holden Staes for seven yards up to the Notre Dame 33. That drive stalled — Notre Dame picked up a yard, Price was tackled for no gain and Hartman threw incomplete — and the Fighting Irish punted. Zachariah Branch then produced an explosive punt return, dancing around the field and burning down the left sideline before the punter stopped him at the Notre Dame 18 with 11:13 left and Notre Dame up 18. USC leaned on its running game, with Jones picking up eight yards on two carries and then three more up the middle for first-and-goal at the 7, and at 10:17 p.m. Williams recorded his first passing touchdown of the game, dancing in the pocket before finding Rice in the end zone to make it 31-20 with 9:04 left. Notre Dame answered almost immediately at 10:21 p.m. when Price went 99 yards, racing past the Trojans through the middle and cutting left for the score: Notre Dame 38, USC 20.

USC's next possession was undone by penalties. Branch was tackled at the USC 20 on the return and a holding call backed the Trojans up to their own 10; after a rush for a loss of one and a false start pushed USC inside its own 5, Williams picked up six and then connected with Jackson for 10 yards to keep the drive alive with just under seven minutes to play. Williams was then sacked for a loss of two and threw an incompletion, a second false start followed, and although Williams found Jackson for eight it left a fourth-and-9 with the offense staying on the field and Notre Dame calling timeout. Jaylen Sneed sacked Williams for a loss of eight back to the USC 14 with 4:49 left and Notre Dame leading by 18. Estime then carried repeatedly as Notre Dame wound the clock — gains of seven and more forcing USC's final timeout with 3:54 left, then a stop a yard shy of the marker bringing up fourth-and-1 from the USC 5 and another Trojan timeout — before Shrader kicked a 23-yard field goal at 10:39 p.m. to push the lead to 21 with 3:40 left: Notre Dame 41, USC 20.

The closing sequence produced two more turnovers. Notre Dame's special teams again stopped Branch early on the return at the USC 17, and at 10:44 p.m. Mario Williams lost a fumble after a three-yard reception, with Watts — who had already intercepted two passes — scooping the ball up and taking it into the end zone for 48-20. With the Trojans down 28 and 3:27 left, Williams was done for the day and Miller Moss entered; USC started at its own 25, Quinten Joyner ran for 10 yards and then fumbled, and Notre Dame's Javontae Jean-Baptiste recovered at the USC 36. Estime picked up a first down on back-to-back runs with two minutes left, leaving only kneel-downs, and at 10:51 p.m. the game ended with No. 21 Notre Dame demolishing No. 10 USC 48-20.
