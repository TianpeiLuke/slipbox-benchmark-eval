# Reece James' Elbow On Udogie: No Red Card

Reece James avoided a red card just before halftime at Tottenham on Nov. 6, 2023 despite catching Destiny Udogie in the face with a trailing elbow while challenging for a bouncing ball in the air, escaping with only a yellow.

Sporting News framed it as Chelsea's turn to benefit: while two Tottenham defenders had escaped an early shower earlier in the match for incidents that could potentially have seen them off, Chelsea also had a player avoid further punishment. Given that referees have been strict with incidents such as these, it was surprising to see James get away with just a yellow card. The incident was indeed reviewed, but the VAR official did not ultimately advise a check for Michael Oliver.
