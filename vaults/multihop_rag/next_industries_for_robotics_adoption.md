# The Next Industries For Robotics Adoption

Asked what follows manufacturing and warehouses as the next major category for robotics, Boston Dynamics CTO Aaron Saunders answers construction and healthcare — after broad adoption is achieved in automation-friendly industries like manufacturing and logistics, which still stand out when you match customer needs against the state of the art.

The organising principle is a gradient of uncertainty: as robotics fans out, Saunders thinks it will move slowly from environments that have determinism to those with higher levels of uncertainty.

Construction and healthcare are compelling opportunities on two grounds. They have large workforces and high demand for skilled labor, but the supply is not meeting the need; and their work environments sit between the highly structured industrial setting and the totally unstructured consumer market, which could represent a natural next step along the path to general purpose.
