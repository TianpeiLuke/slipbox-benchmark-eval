# Four Sportsbooks For NBA MVP Futures Betting

The Sporting News names four favourite sportsbooks for placing MVP futures bets — DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM and Caesars — distinguished mainly by usability and the breadth of their markets.

The DraftKings app is one of the most prominent in the US, offering bets for just about any market including the NBA MVP award and known for its ease of use, with top features including live betting options and a diverse prop betting selection. FanDuel is touted as one of the industry's most user-friendly and best sports betting apps and has built a massive following; beyond a packed futures menu that includes NBA MVP, it has a noteworthy selection of player props and a same-game parlay feature.

BetMGM trades ease of use for depth: the app may take a little getting used to, but the sheer number of betting opportunities is fantastic, and popular features such as "edit my bet" and "easy parlay" have helped make it a leading sportsbook in many states where sports betting is legal. Caesars sits at the opposite end — the betting app may be basic, but the simplicity is part of the draw for customers, along with an outstanding selection of competitive odds, and Caesars also provides NBA betting promos and more sports betting bonuses and promotions for existing customers.
