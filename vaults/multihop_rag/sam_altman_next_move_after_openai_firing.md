# Sam Altman's Next Move After The OpenAI Firing

In the days after his firing, Sam Altman's destination was genuinely unsettled: on November 20, 2023 he and Greg Brockman announced they would join Microsoft to lead a new AI research team, yet on the same day both were reported to be still open to returning to OpenAI, and Altman was separately telling investors he planned to launch a new venture.

On November 20 Altman, Brockman and colleagues announced they would join Microsoft to lead a new AI research team, with Satya Nadella leaving the door open to other OpenAI staffers and saying they would be given the resources they needed should they choose to join. As reported by The Verge, however, Altman's move to Microsoft was not a done deal — both Altman and Brockman remained open to returning to OpenAI if the remaining board members who initially fired him stepped aside. At the same time, according to The Information, Altman had been telling investors he was planning to launch a new venture, with Brockman expected to join the effort in whatever form it took — possibly an AI chip startup. Altman also posted on X on November 18: "i love you all. today was a weird experience in many ways. but one unexpected one is that it has been sorta like reading your own eulogy while you're still alive. the outpouring of love is awesome. one takeaway: go tell your friends how great you think they are."

Nadella himself hedged. In a series of interviews he stepped back from earlier reporting that Altman and Brockman were headed to Microsoft, telling CNBC: "Obviously, we want Sam and Greg to have a fantastic home if they're not going to be in OpenAI," and saying he was "open" to them settling at Microsoft or returning to OpenAI should the board and employees support the move.
