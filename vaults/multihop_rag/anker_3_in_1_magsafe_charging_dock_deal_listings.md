# Anker 3-In-1 MagSafe Charging Dock Deal Listings

Both of WIRED's October 10, 2023 Prime Day roundups list Anker's super compact 3-in-1 wireless charger, with the general roundup noting that Anker makes WIRED's favorite lists often and the Apple roundup calling it another excellent and compact 3-in-1 charger.

Its layout is a triangle: a MagSafe pad charges iPhones at up to 15 watts at a slanted angle, a standard Apple Watch puck sits on the side with no fast-charging support, and the space inside the triangle is where you place AirPods Pro — or any other wireless earbuds case — to top them up.
