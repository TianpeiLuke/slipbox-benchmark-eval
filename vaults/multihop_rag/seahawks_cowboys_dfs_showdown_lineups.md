# Seahawks-Cowboys DFS Showdown Lineups

Sporting News' Nov. 30, 2023 single-game DFS builds for Seahawks vs. Cowboys both centre on Cowboys RB Tony Pollard in the multiplier slot, differing mainly in whether the Dallas defense is stacked alongside him.

The DraftKings Showdown lineup is: Captain (1.5X) Cowboys RB Tony Pollard $14,700; FLEX Cowboys QB Dak Prescott $11,000; FLEX Seahawks WR Tyler Lockett $7,200; FLEX Cowboys TE Jake Ferguson $6,200; FLEX Cowboys D/ST $5,600; and FLEX Seahawks WR Jaxon Smith-Njigba $5,200. The reasoning is that, based on the analysis, it is fine to invest plenty in the Cowboys' running and intermediate passing games; Pollard would be a good correlated stack with the Cowboys' defense, and the hope is that Lockett and Smith-Njigba will be the busiest Seahawks in second-half catchup mode.

The FanDuel single-game lineup is: MVP (1.5X) Cowboys RB Tony Pollard $14,500; AnyFLEX Cowboys QB Dak Prescott $17,500; AnyFLEX Seahawks WR Tyler Lockett $11,000; AnyFLEX Cowboys TE Jake Ferguson $9,000; and AnyFLEX Seahawks WR Jaxon Smith-Njigba $8,000. This construction maxes out the salary on FanDuel and is very similar to the DraftKings philosophy of focusing on Pollard, this time without stacking the defense.
