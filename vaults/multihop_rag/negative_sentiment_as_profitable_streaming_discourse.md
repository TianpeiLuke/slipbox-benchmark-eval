# Negative Sentiment as Profitable Streaming Discourse

"Negative sentiment is very profitable," the anonymous AAA game developer told Polygon for its 2023-10-19 piece, noting that social media platforms are built to highlight popular — and sometimes caustic — content.

The relation this sets up is an inherent tension between game developers and hardcore players like Twitch streamers, who are not the main audience of most games but are often some of the most visible players.

The loop closes because Twitch is both cause and measure: the misinformation will vary, but Twitch shapes that discourse while also becoming the very metric people use to make sense of a game's situation. It has gotten so bad, Polygon writes, that people scrutinize Twitch longevity for single-player games, not just live-service or multiplayer ones.

"There is inherently [a] vicious and negative cycle for everyone involved, for how live service interacts with Twitch as a medium," says the developer, who also spent years as a serious Twitch streamer and says he is still recovering from the burnout of livestreaming all the time.
