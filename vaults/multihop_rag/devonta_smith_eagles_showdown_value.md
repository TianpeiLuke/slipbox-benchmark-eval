# DeVonta Smith's Value In The Eagles-Seahawks Showdown

Sporting News took DeVonta Smith ($9,000) in the FLEX of its Week 15 2023 Eagles-Seahawks Showdown lineup, arguing that he had quietly been the more productive Eagles receiver over the previous three weeks with 62.5 fantasy points to A.J. Brown's 54.5.

Most of the attention would continue to be focused on Brown, but Seattle's secondary had been torched by San Francisco the previous week, allowing both Brandon Aiyuk and Deebo Samuel to go over the century mark. And for all the credit the Eagles get for having a great team, they are a fairly top-heavy bunch: there was no clear WR3 behind Brown and Smith, meaning opportunities would be plentiful, so the column expected the big workload to continue for a receiver sometimes forgotten in Philly.
