# Adobe's Firefly Generative AI Tools And Project Stardust

In the week reported by TechCrunch on 2023-10-13, Adobe doubled down on its Firefly generative AI models: the software can now generate more realistic images and can help artists create vector graphics in Illustrator, as covered that week by TechCrunch's Frederic.

Adobe's push extends past image generation into image editing. The company continues to push for easier image editing, and in the same week showed off Project Stardust as a sneak preview of its next-generation AI photo editing engine. Project Stardust is described at this point as a preview rather than a shipped product, so the observation covers what Adobe demonstrated in that week, not what was generally available.
