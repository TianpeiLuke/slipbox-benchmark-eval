# Amazon Echo Show Black Friday Sale

Most of Amazon's Echo Show smart displays were discounted for Black Friday 2023: the recently updated Echo Show 8 was on sale for a new low of $105, while the smaller 5.5-inch Echo Show 5 was back down to an all-time low of $40; those two typically retail for $150 and $70 respectively, and both came bundled with a free LED smart bulb. An Echo Show 5 with a Ring Video Doorbell was also available for $65.

The Echo Show 8 is the top Alexa pick in Engadget's guide to the best smart displays: compared to the Echo Show 5 it packs a better screen, richer speakers and a much sharper camera for video calls, though for something closer to a smart alarm clock for a nightstand the smaller display still delivers most of the same Alexa smarts. For a larger panel, the rotating 10.1-inch Echo Show 10 was $90 off at $160; Engadget gave that one a score of 83 back in 2021, though its review found the Echo Show 8 to be a better value for most.
