# The Eras Tour's Scale And Taylor Swift's Preparation Regimen

Each Eras Tour concert spans over 180 minutes, with more than 40 songs drawn from at least nine albums and 16 costume changes, and in 2023 alone the career-spanning tour broke records and is estimated to have grossed over $1.4bn. The accompanying concert film, the Eras Tour movie, surpassed $250m at the global box office after its October opening weekend, and Swift's rumoured relationship with Kansas City Chiefs tight end Travis Kelce caused a 400 per cent spike in NFL jersey sales.

Swift told Time what preparing for a show of that length required. "Every day I would run on the treadmill, singing the entire set list out loud," she said. "Fast for fast songs, and a jog or a fast walk for slow songs." She also stopped drinking and underwent three months of dance training, which she admitted is "not my strong suit." After a run of shows she takes a day to rest and recover: "I do not leave my bed except to get food and take it back to my bed and eat it there. It's a dream scenario."
