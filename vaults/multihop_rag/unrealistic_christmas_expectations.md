# Unrealistic Christmas Expectations

Hannah Keeley's claim is that parents who make a "must-do" Christmas list that is a "mile long" at the "first sniff of peppermint mochas" are perhaps setting themselves up for failure, and that doing the most could lead to a season of disappointment.

Keeley said she empathised with the instinct, and conceded a case on the other side from her own life: "Aside from the time I asked my husband to turn our bathroom into a Hallmark movie and he actually pulled it off, unrealistic expectations around Christmas usually just set up moms for inevitable disappointment," she said.

The warrant is that the value of the season does not depend on the volume of activities: "Beautiful family Christmas memories can be made without the drive-in 'Grinch' movie, the tacky light tour and the neighborhood Christmas cookie bake-off all in the same weekend." The practical conclusion is that parents should "take it easy and leave plenty of room to relax".
