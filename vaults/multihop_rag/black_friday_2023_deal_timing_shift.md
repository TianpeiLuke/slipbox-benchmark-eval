# Black Friday 2023 Deals Started A Week Early

The best deals of the year were once known to occur during Black Friday and Cyber Monday, which in 2023 fell on Friday, November 24 and Monday, November 27 — but Polygon reported on 2023-11-12 that this is no longer the case: in 2023 the best Black Friday deals kicked off on November 17 at Amazon, Best Buy, Walmart, and other retailers.

The explanation the piece offers is retailer strategy: instead of hosting all of the doorbuster deals on one day right after Thanksgiving, retailers see the value in maximizing the amount of time you have to shop for products before Black Friday. The writer adds a personal observation that it is quite nice not to feel rushed while shopping. The surrounding coverage the shift was reported alongside included the best board game deals, price drops on gaming monitors, and deals on SSDs and microSD cards.
