# Amazon Proteus, Cardinal And Sequoia Deployments

Asked whether a lab visit would show these experiments in action, Tye Brady pointed instead to live sites. If you were to go to outside of Nashville today, you would see Proteus working with Amazon's Cardinal arm, and get to see the interoperability, with the Proteus drives moving carts to the outbound docks.

The second site is in Texas: if you were to go down to Hou 6 just outside of Houston, you would see Sequoia fulfilling orders today, right in time for holiday shopping — placing the system in production rather than in trial as of the 2023-10-28 report.
