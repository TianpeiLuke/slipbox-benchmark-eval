# Ayahuasca, The Psychedelic Brew

Ayahuasca is a psychedelic drug made from the leaves of the Psychotria viridis shrub and the stalks of the Banisteriopsis caapi vine, as described by The New York Times on 15 December 2023.

Its distinguishing features are its composition, its history of use and its effects. It has been used for more than a thousand years by Amazonian tribes, and ingesting it can induce a dreamlike feeling, an altered state of consciousness, mystical experiences and euphoria.

The name carries the meaning: in the Quechua language, ayahuasca — pronounced "eye-ah-WAH-ska" — means "vine of the soul."
