# UAW Strike Week Six Tentative Deals

Progress was made as the United Autoworkers strike wrapped up its sixth week, TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-30. The UAW struck tentative deals with Ford and Stellantis that past week; workers still have to ratify the deal, but with UAW president Shawn Fain's support that outcome is likely.

At GM, however, negotiations appeared to be moving in the opposite direction: the UAW called for a surprise walkout at GM's Spring Hill, Tennessee factory, a plant where 4,000 workers assemble engines and three Cadillac models.
