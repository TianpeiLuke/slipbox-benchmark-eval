# The Apple-Google Information Services Agreement

The Information Services Agreement, or ISA, is the deal that makes Google's search engine the default on Apple's products — and it is the reason Apple was in court in US v. Google at all.

The ISA has been in place since 2002, but Eddy Cue was responsible for negotiating its current iteration with Google CEO Sundar Pichai in 2016. In testimony on the day The Verge reported, the Justice Department grilled Cue about the specifics of the deal.
