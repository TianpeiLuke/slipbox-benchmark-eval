# Samsung T9 Portable SSD Deal

The latest Samsung T9 portable SSD was on sale for $110 for a 1TB drive during Black Friday 2023 — the best price it had been since it came out the previous month — available from Amazon or Samsung directly.

The T9 is the newest iteration of Samsung's popular portable drive that Engadget has long been a fan of, and it supports read and write speeds of up to 2,000 MB/s. It also has dynamic thermal guard to prevent overheating, and comes with USB-C to C and USB-C to A cords so it can be used with a variety of devices.
