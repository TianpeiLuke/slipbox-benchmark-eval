# Product Design As A Decision-Making Process

Product design is a process, not a discipline or a deliverable, and its core is decision-making with an astute instinct for making the best decisions at the most opportune time. So understood, product design helps reduce risk and leads to more effective innovation through quality decision-making, and the process establishes a guide rail throughout any innovation initiative.

The boundary case the definition rules out is the narrow one. It is understandably easy to limit the scope of product design to color choices, content layout and aesthetics, and too often design is minimized to only the act of making user interfaces pretty; but product design is much deeper and broader in scope than visual design assets — it can give direction and focus to a business strategy, a user experience strategy, or technology explorations.
