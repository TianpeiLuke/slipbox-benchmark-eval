# Netflix's November 2023 Slate: Overview

Polygon's November 2023 Netflix guide opens with the problem it exists to solve: even with Netflix's recommendation algorithm serving you new movies, new TV shows, and original programming tailored to your viewing habits, the streaming service's firehose of content makes what is coming and going difficult to parse.

Its summary of the month is that it is a great month for adult animation, with both the Scott Pilgrim animated show and Blue Eye Samurai hitting Netflix; that there is the Squid Game reality show — "because right, that's a thing" — and a new season of The Crown. The editorial highlight of the month is The Social Network, chosen with another new David Fincher movie on the way to Netflix, though Polygon says not for the reasons you probably think.
