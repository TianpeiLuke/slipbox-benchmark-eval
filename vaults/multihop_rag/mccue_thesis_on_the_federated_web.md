# McCue's Thesis On The Federated Web

Flipboard CEO Mike McCue argues that federated social media may only be the beginning of what is to come for the web overall, and the company is betting on it. "I saw what was happening with ActivityPub and it became very clear to me that this is the future of the web, period," McCue said.

His ground is a claim about the structure of the social web: "The social web is people linking to pages and people linking to people. So it's a much more intricate web."

The warrant connecting that structure to Flipboard's own opportunity is discovery: he sees Flipboard as part of that opportunity, saying "there needs to be a way to do discovery and search and have it be beautiful and simple and easy to use. That is what we're focused on."
