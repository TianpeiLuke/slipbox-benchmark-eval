# Hi-Fi Rush Wins Audio Design, 2023

The hyper-stylized, rhythm-based action game Hi-Fi Rush was nominated in a number of categories at the 2023 Game Awards and ultimately took home the prize for audio design.

Polygon endorses the result: it calls Hi-Fi Rush a witty and wild game that features an excellent original soundtrack in addition to licensed songs from artists including Nine Inch Nails, The Flaming Lips, and The Prodigy.
