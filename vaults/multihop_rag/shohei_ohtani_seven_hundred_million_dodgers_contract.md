# Shohei Ohtani's $700 Million Dodgers Contract

Shohei Ohtani agreed on Saturday 9 December 2023 to a 10-year, $700 million contract with the Los Angeles Dodgers, the largest contract in the annals of major North American team sports, his agency CAA announced and The New York Times reported.

Ohtani announced his decision on Instagram, ending years of feverish speculation about his future. The 29-year-old two-way sensation has captivated the industry since he left Japan for Major League Baseball heading into the 2018 season, doing things that appeared impossible in the modern era, feats that harkened back to Babe Ruth. As he travelled the country with the Los Angeles Angels that past summer, fans serenaded him with recruiting pitches, and when he entered free agency a dozen teams lined up, curious to see if they could meet his eye. Only one team could secure his services, and he will now be compensated for both his immense talent and his unparalleled star power.

The deal eclipsed the $360 million record for free agents set the previous winter by New York Yankees slugger Aaron Judge, and surpassed the record-setting $426.5 million extension of his former Angels teammate Mike Trout. It exceeded even contracts outside baseball, topping the $450 million deal inked by Kansas City Chiefs quarterback Patrick Mahomes, and outpaced soccer star Lionel Messi's $674 million contract, signed in 2017 when he was with FC Barcelona. Dodgers officials declined comment when asked if Ohtani had taken a physical, the final step before a deal can become official.
