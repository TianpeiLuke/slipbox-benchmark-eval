# The Three Leading 2023-24 Rookie Of The Year Contenders

Sporting News profiled three leading Rookie of the Year contenders for 2023-24: Victor Wembanyama, Scoot Henderson and Chet Holmgren.

Victor Wembanyama is described as a generational talent and a strong contender, a gem for Gregg Popovich's Spurs whose undeniable impact is evident even with a young roster and potential load management. His elite defense, highlighted by his 7-foot-4 height and 8-foot wingspan, lets him dominate where others cannot, and offensively his agility and skill set are unparalleled for his size; if he refines his shooting and continues adapting to the Spurs' style, as seen in his impressive NBA preseason performance, he will be a force to reckon with.

Scoot Henderson has been placed as Damian Lillard's successor by a restructured Portland Trail Blazers side, supported by talents such as Anfernee Simons and Jerami Grant, with much of the Blazers' fortunes hinging on him; after a stellar season with the G League Ignite, expectations for his rookie year are soaring, and he is known for strength, agility and promising defense, set to become a fan favorite. Chet Holmgren underpins Oklahoma City's anticipation of a strong season: with a 7-foot frame and notable skills, impressive stats and team impact, his wingspan and defensive prowess will challenge opponents, while offensively he capitalizes on mismatches with diverse scoring from the paint to the 3-point line, and his underrated passing syncs well with OKC's pace and style.
