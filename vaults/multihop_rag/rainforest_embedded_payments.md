# Rainforest's Embedded Payments For Software Platforms

Rainforest is an Atlanta-based startup that works with software companies to help them embed financial services and payments into their platforms, taking on incumbents such as Fiserv and FIS while also trying to take market share from other fintechs such as Stripe. Founded in 2022, it defines itself by a single restriction: it serves software platforms only.

The discriminating feature is that exclusive focus. CEO and co-founder Joshua Silver believes it gives the company an edge, because the alternative providers were built for a different customer: "None of the modern processors were built specifically for software platforms. Most of them were built directly for merchants, and they've all had to retrofit their platforms even to accommodate basic payment processing and reporting functions for software companies," he told TechCrunch. Rainforest accordingly captures volume as software platforms migrate off legacy processors such as Fiserv and FIS, and in doing so competes against companies like Stripe and its Connect product.

The boundary case that shapes the concept is how far a software company actually wants to go into finance. In an interview with TechCrunch, Silver respectfully disagreed with a16z general partner Angela Strange's 2019 statement that every company would become a fintech. In his view, most software companies do not actually want to be fintechs and deal with all the regulatory and compliance issues that go with that; they really just want to be able to accept payments and generate more revenue from being able to do so. Embedded payments in this sense is the narrow slice of fintech a software platform will adopt, not a wholesale transformation into one.

As of this October 2023 account, the startup had shown impressive growth in a short period, securing client commitments representing more than $500 million in processing, with much of the volume guaranteed.
