# Shipping Price War Risk And The Case For Carrier Restraint

Some shipping executives are urging against price wars, on the ground that such wars contributed to a wave of consolidation and at least one major bankruptcy in the years leading up to the pandemic. "Each actor will have to be responsible to ensure that the market remains reasonable amid rates that are relatively low," CMA CGM Chief Financial Officer Ramon Fernandez told reporters on Friday, adding the warrant plainly: "Price wars after a while hurt not only those who start them but everyone."

Stephane Kovatchev said what's emerging instead is a standoff between the strong and the weak. "The bigger companies such as Maersk and Hapag-Lloyd have the cash to wait and focus on cost-cutting, as opposed to aggressive capacity reductions — for now," he said. "It all boils down to supply, demand and who will blink first."
