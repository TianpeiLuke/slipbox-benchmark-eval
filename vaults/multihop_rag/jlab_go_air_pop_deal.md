# JLab Go Air Pop Deal

The JLab Go Air Pop was available for $17 at Amazon for Black Friday 2023, which isn't an all-time low — the pair was briefly down to just $10 at Walmart earlier that week — but still takes about $6 off its usual going rate.

Engadget highlights the Go Air Pop as an honorable mention in its budget wireless earbuds guide: its IPX4-rated design is light and generally comfortable, its battery lasts around eight or nine hours per charge and, unlike many cheap earphones, its touch controls actually work. Its boomy sound profile won't win any awards, predictably, but it's well beyond serviceable for less than $20, and JLab even bakes three different EQ presets directly into the earphones. For a cheap backup to a main pair of wireless earphones, this is a great value.
