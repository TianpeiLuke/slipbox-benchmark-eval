# George's Claim Of Epstein's Political Influence

Denise George later testified that she thought Epstein was "flexing his political influence over or with the governor" rather than going through the usual channels — her inference from the route by which the waiver request reached her.

Given Epstein's apparent influence in the Virgin Islands, the obvious question was who would want to rock the boat, and George says she was willing to try. Around St. Thomas she has a reputation as a straightshooter, and the former public-corruption attorney stops short of pointing to official wrongdoing; but she makes clear that she, for one, wouldn't tolerate it. "If I have to lose my job to avoid being part of a conspiracy to cover something up, I would do it," George says. "My bar license, my integrity were more important to me. I wasn't going to compromise that."
