# Aron Baynes' Five-Game Suspension At The Brisbane Bullets

Brisbane coach Justin Schueller said he always knew losing superstar centre Aron Baynes to a five-game suspension was going to be challenging, and that difficulty was magnified in the Bullets' round 4 loss reported on Oct. 19, 2023.

Schueller's immediate concern was the games still to come without him: "We've got to have solutions right now for two more games without Banger (Baynes) and that's the thing that's sitting right in the front of my head, but scoring 73 points isn't enough to get it done in this league."

By round 5, reported Oct. 26, the Bullets had adapted: despite being without Baynes (suspended) and import guard Shannon Scott (hamstring), they soared to a second straight victory on the Sunday and snapped a quirky streak in the process.
