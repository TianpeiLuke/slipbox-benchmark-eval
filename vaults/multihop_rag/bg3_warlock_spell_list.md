# BG3 Warlock Spell List

A Baldur's Gate 3 Warlock starts with two spells from the Warlock spell list, and two of the entries on that list are exclusive to the subclass you pick.

The general options are Armor of Agathys, which grants 5 temporary hit points and deals 5 Cold damage to any creature that hits you in melee; Arms of Hadar, which prevents targets from using reactions; Charm Person, which charms a humanoid so it will not attack you and grants Advantage on Charisma Checks in dialogue; Expeditious Retreat, which grants Dash immediately and as a bonus action each turn until the spell ends; Hellish Rebuke, a reaction that answers your next attacker with flames for 2-20 Fire damage; Hex, which adds 1-6 Necrotic damage to your attacks against the target and gives it Disadvantage on an Ability of your choosing; Protection from Evil and Good, which shields an ally against aberrations, celestials, elementals, fey, fiends, and undead; and Witch Bolt, which links you to a target with lightning for an additional 1-12 Lightning damage each turn it is activated. The subclass-exclusive spells are Burning Hands (3-18 Fire damage to each flammable target) and Command, both exclusive to The Fiend; Dissonant Whispers (frightens a creature so it is easier to hit and cannot move) and Tasha's Hideous Laughter (leaves a creature Prone with laughter and unable to get up), both exclusive to The Great Old One; and Faerie Fire (targets in the light turn visible and Attack Rolls against them have advantage) and Sleep (magical slumber for targets up to a combined 24 hit points), both exclusive to The Archfey.
