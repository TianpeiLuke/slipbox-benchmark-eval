# ESR MagSafe Travel Charging Kit Deal Listings

Both of WIRED's October 10, 2023 Prime Day roundups list this ESR travel kit, which works well if you want something that can prop an iPhone in portrait or landscape orientation.

It can also display an Apple Watch in Nightstand mode, so you can peek at how long before you must get out of bed, and the main body folds open with a MagSafe charging pad for the iPhone and a slot behind for AirPods. The Apple roundup adds the drawback: it doesn't support fast charging and is capped at 7.5 watts for the iPhone and 5 watts for the Apple Watch — but it's still a good, affordable charger. On price, the general roundup notes it has dipped a little lower before, and the Apple roundup specifies it fell to $40 back in July, while both call the current price a solid deal.
