# Wallbox Acquires ABL's Charging Business

EV charging company Wallbox has acquired the operations and assets of German-based EV charging solutions company ABL for €15 million, TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-22. Together the plan is to deploy more than 1 million chargers globally.
