# Starfield (Bethesda Game Studios)

Starfield, developed by Bethesda Game Studios for Windows PC and Xbox Series X, is ranked 34 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31. Polygon's claim is that the game had so much to prove that it is easy to lose sight of what it accomplished: top-tier world-building, a fantastically customizable ship builder, and Bethesda's most engaging combat to date.

Galaxy-spanning faction quests give players plenty to do, but it is the side quests that really make you feel like the captain in something Star Trek adjacent — generation ships and superhero hideouts and colonies of clones. Critic Clayton Ashley says there is good sci-fi here for those willing to make the journey.

Ashley also names the game's marketing problem: it oversold the idea of a galaxy with over 1,000 planets, a number that pales in comparison to its most obvious competitor, No Man's Sky. He calls it a shame this became so much of the focus, because that focus obscured the absolutely fascinating new game plus mode at the game's heart — his framing being that No Man's Sky realizes the universe's infinite number of planets, while Starfield realizes the universe's infinite number of possibilities.
