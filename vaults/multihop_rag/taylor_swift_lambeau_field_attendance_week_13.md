# Taylor Swift Attended The Week 13 Chiefs-Packers Game At Lambeau Field

Taylor Swift was in attendance at Lambeau Field for the Week 13 "Sunday Night Football" game between the Chiefs and Packers on Dec. 3, 2023, as noted in Sporting News' pregame live log at 7:20 p.m., which hoped she would be greeted with an enthralling affair.

The log recorded her arrival and its reception. Sporting News reported that Taylor Swift and Brittany Mahomes were cheering on the Chiefs in Green Bay, and at 7:35 p.m. that Taylor's fans were in euphoria at the sight of her at the game — Sunday Night Football on NBC (@SNFonNBC) posted on December 4, 2023 that "The Swifties were ready for Taylor Swift's arrival!" At 7:40 p.m. the log noted the Chiefs were rocking their all-white kits, perfect for a winter wonderland like Lambeau Field. At 8:00 p.m. it asked whether Travis Kelce would put on a showcase in front of his beau, observing that Kelce was taking the field for another prime time game with Taylor Swift.
