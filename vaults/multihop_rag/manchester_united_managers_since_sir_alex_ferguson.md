# Manchester United's Managers Since Sir Alex Ferguson

If Manchester United were to appoint a new manager in December 2023, that person would be the ninth coach to take charge of a United team following Sir Alex Ferguson's retirement in May 2013, including caretaker and interim managers Ryan Giggs, Ralf Rangnick and Michael Carrick, per Sporting News on Dec. 13, 2023.

The list of managers, their honours and games in charge ran: David Moyes, Community Shield, 51 games; Ryan Giggs (caretaker), no honours, 4 games; Louis van Gaal, FA Cup, 103 games; Jose Mourinho, Community Shield, Europa League and League Cup, 144 games; Ole Gunnar Solskjaer, no honours, 168 games; Michael Carrick (caretaker), no honours, 3 games; Ralf Rangnick (interim), no honours, 29 games; and Erik ten Hag, League Cup, with his games total open because he was still manager of the club.
