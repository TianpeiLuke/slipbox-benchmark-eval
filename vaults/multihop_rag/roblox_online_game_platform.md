# Roblox As An Online Game Platform

Roblox is a widely popular online game platform that "lets you play, create, and be anything you can imagine," according to its description on the Microsoft Store. Its defining features are that players customise characters and play minigames within the game, and that while the platform is free to download on a number of platforms — including Android and iOS smartphones, Xbox game consoles and Windows computers — players can also spend money on in-game items and customisations.

Its user base is young. According to TechCrunch, Roblox's millions of users range between the ages of eight and 18, with its key demographic between nine and 15 years old — the fact that makes parental controls and spending safeguards the recurring point of contention around the platform.
