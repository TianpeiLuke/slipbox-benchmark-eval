# Moonstruck (1987): Norman Jewison's Romantic Comedy

Moonstruck is the 1987 romantic comedy directed by Norman Jewison, running 1h 41m, starring Cher, Nicolas Cage and Vincent Gardenia. Its plot follows a widow (Cher) who is convinced her ill-fated first marriage was doomed by bad luck after a hasty engagement and wedding; when a suitor (Danny Aiello) proposes she accepts, but ends up falling for his estranged brother (Cage) instead.

Polygon locates the film's appeal in its smaller moments rather than its plot: a mother cooking an egg-in-a-hole for her daughter, an older couple trading barbs before the conversation shifts on a dime to expressions of eternal love, an elderly man basking in the moonlight with his five adorable dogs. Its distinguishing features are warm, lived-in sets, loving depictions of food — the egg-in-a-hole has since been colloquially dubbed "Moonstruck Eggs" — hilarious family conversations ("Old man, you give another plate of my food to those dogs, I'm going to kick you till you're dead!"), and complementary lead performances by an assured Cher and an intense Cage, making it a touching, uproarious comedy about superstition, love and family. It streams free with ads on Pluto TV, Tubi and The Roku Channel, with a library card on Hoopla, or is available for digital rental or purchase at Amazon, Apple and Google Play.
