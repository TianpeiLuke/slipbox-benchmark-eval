# Prisco's Week 12 Thanksgiving And Black Friday Picks

Pete Prisco's Week 12 picks for the three Thanksgiving games and the Black Friday game, published by CBS Sports on 2023-11-23, each pair a stated reason with a projected score, and three of the four are annotated with the actual result. For the Thursday 12:30 p.m. ET game on Fox and fubo, he called it a big divisional game for both teams — a survival test for Green Bay and a big one for the Lions if they want home-field advantage in the conference. His reasoning: the Lions have had defensive issues as of late and Jordan Love has played well the past two weeks, which will keep the Packers in the game, but the Lions win it. Pick: Lions 31, Packers 27, with the result recorded as Packers 29, Lions 22.

For the Thursday 4:30 p.m. ET game on CBS and Paramount+, he noted the Commanders are coming off a dud against the Giants while Dallas is flying high and playing as well as any team in the league; Washington's offensive line is an issue, which will lead to the Cowboys having a field day with their defensive front, and Dak Prescott plays well again. Pick: Cowboys 32, Commanders 16, with the result recorded as Cowboys 45-10. For the Thursday 8:20 p.m. ET game on NBC and fubo, he framed a huge division game between two teams with a good rivalry over the past decade: the Seahawks were coming off a bad loss to the Rams while the 49ers were coming off two dominant victories, Geno Smith was banged up so his health was key, and the 49ers have the look of a Super team again. Pick: 49ers 28, Seahawks 19, with the result recorded as 49ers 31-13. For the Friday 3 p.m. ET game on Amazon Prime Video, he wrote that the day-after-Thanksgiving game wasn't what it was expected to be with the Jets being an offensive disaster; the Jets defense was coming off a bad showing against Buffalo and would be challenged by the Dolphins, and Tim Boyle would start at quarterback for the Jets, though he asked whether it would matter — even so, he thought the Jets keep it close with their defense. Pick: Dolphins 21, Jets 16, with the result recorded as Dolphins 34-13.
