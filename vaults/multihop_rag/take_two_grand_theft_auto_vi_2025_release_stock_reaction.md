# Take-Two Grand Theft Auto VI 2025 Release Stock Reaction

Take-Two Interactive was on the losing end of Wall Street in the session reported on 5 December 2023, slipping 0.5 per cent after a trailer for its highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto VI video game said the title is coming in 2025 — later than some analysts had expected.

The same market wrap carried an Australian corporate quote of the day: IAG chief executive Nick Hawkins said the behaviour of Peter Horton, the insurer's group general counsel and company secretary, did not meet the expectations set out in its employee code of ethics and conduct. "This includes the importance of being inclusive and respectful, and we will hold people to account if they fail to meet these expectations," Hawkins said.
