# Buffalo's AFC Wild Card Position Going Into Week 16

Going into Week 16 of the 2023 season, Buffalo was in the thick of the AFC wild card hunt at 8-6, trailing three other 8-6 teams — the Bengals, Colts and Texans — by tiebreakers.

The Bills were also riding high on the heels of their dominant 31-10 Week 15 home win over the Cowboys, led by running back James Cook's 25-carry, 179-yard, one-touchdown rushing bonanza. While Buffalo had had its ups and downs that year, the assessment was that if they made the postseason with their rushing game producing like this, they would be an extremely tough out.
