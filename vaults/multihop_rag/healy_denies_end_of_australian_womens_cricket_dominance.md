# Healy Denies The End Of Australian Women's Cricket Dominance

Alyssa Healy rejected the suggestion that the Test defeat to India showed Australia's unprecedented period of dominance was ending: her counter attacks the inference from a single result, arguing the loss is "probably not even a blip" because it came in a format and conditions the team is not familiar with.

Quizzed on the point after the loss, Healy was defiant. "It's probably not even a blip," she said. "It (Test cricket) is a format where we're not overly familiar with, in conditions that we're not familiar with. I see more positives out of this and than any sort of negativity."

She then challenged the standard being applied to the team, arguing that outside expectations have not kept pace with a squad in transition: "There is always a lot of expectation and external noise about our team and how we're performing. They are so used to seeing us winning, that I think, at times, they forget that we've actually seen quite a lot of change over the last 12 to 18 months. That kind of goes unnoticed external to our group."
