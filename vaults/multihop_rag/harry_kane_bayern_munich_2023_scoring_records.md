# Harry Kane's 2023 Goal Tally And Bayern Munich Scoring Records

Harry Kane scored 52 goals in the 2023 calendar year for Bayern Munich, Tottenham and England, tied for second on TalkSport's list of the year's top scorers. A stunning 2022/23 season saw Bayern splash out over £100m to sign the Spurs legend in August, and Kane repaid the Bundesliga club's faith by breaking a number of records with his 25 goals in all competitions so far in the season.

The leading scorer in the German top flight with 22 goals, the Three Lions skipper set a new Bundesliga record the previous month by finding the back of the net 17 times in his opening 11 outings. Kane, who scored nine goals for England in 2023, is also the first player to register a hat-trick in their first outing against Borussia Dortmund. He is on course to score the most goals in a debut Bundesliga campaign as he hunts down Uwe Seeler's record of 30 for Hamburg in the 1963/64 season, and is on track to break Robert Lewandowski's record of 41 goals in a single season. He is adding assists too, as evidenced by his "filthy" effort against Man United in the Champions League. Like Mbappe, Kane could not overtake Ronaldo's calendar-year tally because of the German winter break.

Earlier in the season, in Bayern's 2-2 draw at RB Leipzig, Bayern earned a 57th-minute penalty against the run of play for a handball and Kane cut the deficit with his eighth league goal. Only Manchester City's Erling Haaland, when he played for Borussia Dortmund, had scored more often in his first six Bundesliga games, with nine goals.
