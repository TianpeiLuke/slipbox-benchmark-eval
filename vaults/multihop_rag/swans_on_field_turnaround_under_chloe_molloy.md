# The Swans' On-Field Turnaround Under Chloe Molloy

After a winless campaign in 2022, the Sydney Swans — led by ex-Collingwood player Chloe Molloy — were competitive in the 2023 AFLW season, winning their first match in round one and sitting within a game of the top eight with two rounds to play as of 26 October 2023.

The Swans recorded their highest total the previous round in a 14-12-96 to 6-3-39 defeat of the Western Bulldogs, the league's winless bottom side, with Molloy recording 17 score involvements to set a new AFLW mark. The Guardian noted the turnaround mattered because fans of Australian rules football in the Harbour City have a reputation for being fickle.
