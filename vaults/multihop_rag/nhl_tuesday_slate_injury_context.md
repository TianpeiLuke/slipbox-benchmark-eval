# Injury Context For The Tuesday NHL National Slate

Tuesday night's NHL national games on Nov. 7, 2023 featured an Original Six matchup at Madison Square Garden plus a meeting between the team that left Colorado and the team that took its place. The Rangers and Red Wings met at the world's most famous arena with both squads off to hot starts, but Dylan Larkin was a game-time decision for Detroit while injuries to the Blueshirts' Adam Fox, Filip Chytil and Igor Shesterkin ensured some of the matchup's biggest stars would not be in uniform. Sporting News still expected plenty of star power on Broadway and plenty of offense.

In the late game the Devils returned to Colorado — the place they left to move to New Jersey — to face the Avalanche, with injuries again central to the storyline as Jack Hughes and Nico Hischier were out until further notice. The outlet's view was that even if injuries took some juice out of the games, SuperDraft was offering snazzy props worth caring about.
