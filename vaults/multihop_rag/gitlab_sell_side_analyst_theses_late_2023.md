# GitLab Sell-Side Analyst Theses, Late 2023

Two sell-side firms held opposite views on GitLab as of Seeking Alpha's December 3, 2023 week-ahead, while reasoning from overlapping premises: Raymond James had recently rated the stock Market Perform, whereas Cantor Fitzgerald started coverage at Overweight with a $55 price target.

Raymond James's Market Perform call rested on a two-sided ground: it cited potential market exuberance on one hand, and growth potential in fiscal 2025 on the other, with that growth potential attributed to a large total addressable market, healthy customers, and a value proposition favouring consolidation over fragmentation. The warrant joining those grounds to a neutral rating is that a genuinely attractive growth story can still be fully — or excessively — reflected in the price, so strong fundamentals do not by themselves justify a buy recommendation.

Cantor Fitzgerald's initiation ran the consolidation premise the other way. It anticipated that GitLab would benefit from product consolidation and from the generative artificial intelligence wave, and treated those two tailwinds as sufficient to underwrite an Overweight rating and a $55 target. Both descriptions are as of the December 3, 2023 preview; the shared premise is that consolidation favours GitLab, and the firms diverge on whether that advantage is already priced in.
