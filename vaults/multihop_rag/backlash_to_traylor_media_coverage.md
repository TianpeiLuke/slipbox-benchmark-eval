# The Backlash: Fans Who Say The Swift-Kelce Coverage Is Too Much

The counter-position to the Swift-Kelce media wave attacks its premise that the story is genuine and welcome, and it comes from within the fanbase and from commentators. Sporting News noted on 2023-09-28 that Travis Kelce had been catching a lot more than footballs lately and that fans everywhere had been swift to hop on the Taylor Swift bandwagon; the celebrity couple had been in the news for well over a week, and despite fatigue from many — Chris 'Mad Dog' Russo's rant being among the most vocal — companies like FanDuel just couldn't help but lean into a profitable storyline. The unresolved question the article poses is whether they are for real or whether this "Love Story" is just some extravagant publicity stunt.

Essentially Sports reported on 2023-10-25 that hardcore football fans were pretty much done with the NFL harping about Kelce and Swift. Reality TV star Rachel Lindsay, a longtime NFL fan, had recently slammed the "obnoxious" coverage, highlighting the "exhausting" uptick in Swifties obsessing over football — and that is offered as just one example of how fans were finding the fixation a little too disrespectful towards the sport.
