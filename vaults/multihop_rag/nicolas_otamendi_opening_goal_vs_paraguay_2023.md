# Nicolas Otamendi's Fourth-Minute Goal Against Paraguay

Nicolas Otamendi scored what the Sporting News called a golazo in the fourth minute of Argentina's Oct. 12, 2023 World Cup qualifier against Paraguay, the only goal of the match.

It came from the first corner of the game. Rodrigo de Paul floated it deep and over the pack to the Argentina defender, who produced an absolutely stunning whipped volley, striking the ball cleanly by chopping his leg down on the bouncing ball and blasting it through the trees into the back of the net.

The context at kickoff was that Messi started on the bench for Argentina but was expected to come into the match early in the second half, with Lautaro Martinez and Julian Alvarez paired up front against a Paraguay side under new management.
