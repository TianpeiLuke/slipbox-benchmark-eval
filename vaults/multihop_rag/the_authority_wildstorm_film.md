# The Authority (WildStorm Film)

The Authority will be the first appearance of WildStorm canon in live-action film, part of DC Studios' slate as reported by Polygon on 2023-10-24.

The WildStorm imprint was founded as an independent company in 1992 and bought up by DC in 1999, and it maintained its own superhero universe separate from DC's until DC shut it down in preparation for its characters to join the mainline DC Universe in the company's 2011 reboot. In its day, WildStorm published independent and creator-owned comics alongside superhero series.

The Authority itself is Warren Ellis and Bryan Hitch's series about a group of seven heroes with high-concept superpowers — such as the Engineer, a woman with nanotech blood, and Jack Hawksmoor, a man who psychically bonds to cities.
