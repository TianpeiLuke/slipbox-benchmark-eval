# How An X Feud Turned Conor McGregor vs Canelo Alvarez Into A Crossover Boxing Prospect

Crossover boxing matches are all the rage these days, Essentially Sports wrote on 2023-10-18, and until a few days before that piece the phenomenon was limited to influencers and MMA stars. That changed after a recent quarrel on X, formerly Twitter, between Conor McGregor and Canelo Alvarez, which left the boxing world buzzing with excitement over a potential crossover match — one that, if it became reality, could help 'Cinnamon' get closer to his former rival Floyd Mayweather in terms of net worth.

The exchange began with a tweet that launched a fiery exchange of words between the two mammoths of combat sports. The brash and confident 'Notorious' McGregor turned to X praising Mayweather's 2013 performance against Canelo, but also boasted about landing more punches on 'Money' Mayweather than the Mexican superstar had. Canelo quickly retorted, asserting he would need only one hand to make quick work of McGregor. The banter escalated from there, bringing a potential fight into the picture.
