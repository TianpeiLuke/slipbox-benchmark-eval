# Cranberries And New England Regional Identity

Cranberry bogs remain a signature of the landscape in the land of the Pilgrims, and Marcus Urann framed his work as service to the region: "I felt I could do something for New England," he told the Associated Press in a story that ran around the nation on Thanksgiving in 1959, as reported by FOX News Lifestyle on 23 November 2023.

"For Massachusetts and for this region, they're so much a part of our history and our culture," said Brian Wick of Massachusetts Cranberries. "They've been part of a fabric of our region from Indigenous people until today."

Urann saw what cranberries could be far beyond the land of the Pilgrims, and closed his 1959 remarks with a creed: "You know, everything in life is what you do for others."
