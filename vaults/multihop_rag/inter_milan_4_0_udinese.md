# Inter Milan 4-0 Udinese

Internazionale beat lowly visitors Udinese 4-0 on Saturday, Dec. 9, 2023 to regain top spot in Serie A, moving two points clear of second-placed Juventus.

Inter took the lead with a penalty by Hakan Calhanoglu in the 36th minute after a foul by defender Nehuén Pérez on Lautaro Martínez that was reviewed by VAR. Five minutes later Federico Dimarco fired an unstoppable left-foot shot into the corner of the net after an assist from Calhanoglu, before Marcus Thuram netted with a superb finish two minutes later from Henrikh Mkhitaryan's cross into the box. Martínez's stunning long-range effort in the 85th minute wrapped up a convincing win. Juventus, in second, had beaten champions Napoli 1-0 at home on the Friday.
