# Cheika's Underdog Framing Before The 2023 Semi-Final

Michael Cheika embraced underdog status before Argentina's 2023 World Cup semi-final while pointedly reminding the All Blacks who the favourites were: "The other team are heavy favourites, everyone is expecting a New Zealand and South Africa final. We just have to focus on what is required."

His grounds are a reading of what New Zealand demand of an opponent: "New Zealand has always been an example in rugby, a benchmark. They make you think of high-level skills in a really open game but there are always threats in the lineout, mauls and rucks. You have to take the moments you are in the game and see them coming at you at different times." Cheika, whose Wallabies lost the 2015 final, was readying for another clash against the All Blacks and a second semi-final.

He also refused to over-read Argentina's 2020 win as evidence: "In the game in 2020 there were things you could pinpoint. You can't say that one game means we can do it as there are a lot of games where we haven't. That moment has to be just a part of it. What we have done since then, all the success and failures [matters in our preparation]." The same week he said he hoped Los Pumas "inspired" Argentina "not by our results but by our ambition".
