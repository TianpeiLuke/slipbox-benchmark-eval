# Steam Deck OLED Models And Pricing

Valve kept Steam Deck pricing the same as the original line while increasing storage at every tier, and set the Steam Deck OLED on sale from November 16, 2023 through the Steam storefront.

The three models are $649 for the 1 TB OLED, up from 512 GB at that price; $529 for the 512 GB OLED, up from 256 GB; and $399 for a 256 GB non-OLED Steam Deck, up from 64 GB. For a limited time the company also offered a special edition 1 TB Steam Deck OLED with a translucent shell for $679, sold only in the U.S. and Canada — a limited run Polygon's reviewer predicted would sell like hotcakes, prompting Valve to reconsider the definition of "limited supply." Polygon discloses that the hardware was reviewed with a unit provided by Valve, and that Vox Media has affiliate partnerships which do not influence editorial content though the company may earn commissions on products purchased via affiliate links.
