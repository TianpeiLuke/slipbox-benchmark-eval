# Taylor Swift's Subsequent NFL Appearances And SNL Cameo

After her first Chiefs game, Taylor Swift kept appearing at Kansas City fixtures and made a Saturday Night Live cameo with Travis Kelce, according to CBS Sports' timeline published 12 October 2023.

On 1 October she went to her second consecutive Chiefs game, against the Jets — who, the timeline notes, technically play in New Jersey — showing up with famous friends Blake Lively, Ryan Reynolds and Hugh Jackman after a week of reports that she would attend; the Chiefs won 23-20. On 12 October, one day after her movie premiere, Swift was in the house for the Chiefs' "Thursday Night Football" divisional matchup against the Broncos at Arrowhead Stadium, where she has performed herself and attended a game before; according to Al Michaels the game would have minimal Swift coverage, though there had already been a mention of the superstar.

On 15 October the two were spotted out in New York City together and then made a surprise appearance on "Saturday Night Live," with Kelce appearing in a skit about how much coverage Swift was getting during games while the singer introduced the musical act of the night, Ice Spice. On 22 October Swift attended her fifth Chiefs game, having now attended games broadcast across all five networks — CBS, NBC, Fox, ESPN and Amazon — watching the Chiefs face the Chargers on CBS in a Chiefs sweatshirt, seated next to Patrick Mahomes' wife, Brittany.
