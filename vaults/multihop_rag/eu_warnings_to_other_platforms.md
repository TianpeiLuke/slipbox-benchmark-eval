# EU Warnings To Other Platforms

X was not the only platform the European Union warned over Israel-Hamas war content in October 2023: TikTok and Meta were warned too, but X drew markedly greater attention from the bloc. Both TechCrunch reports carrying these details are dated 13 October 2023, and the warnings they describe were issued "in recent days" and "earlier this week" relative to that date.

Meta, the subject of one of the two reports, is described as "not alone in being warned by the bloc over content concerns attached to the Israel-Hamas war." Elon Musk's X "has been singled out for even greater attention here" — the bloc issued an "urgent" warning earlier that week and then followed it with a formal request for information about X's compliance approach. TikTok also received a warning from the EU, framed in one report as being about DSA content risks related to the conflict and in the other as being about disinformation related to the war.

The EU's sharper focus on X has a documented antecedent in the same reporting: back in September, a study commissioned via the EU's Code of Practice suggested X is the worst of the major platforms when it comes to spreading disinformation.
