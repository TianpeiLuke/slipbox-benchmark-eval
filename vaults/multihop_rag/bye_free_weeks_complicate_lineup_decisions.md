# Bye-Free Weeks Complicate Lineup Decisions

Sporting News argued on Nov. 21, 2023 that a bye-free week at that stage of the season presents more challenges than ever for fantasy managers, not fewer.

The grounds are what byes do to the decision set: byes make some start 'em, sit 'em decisions no-brainers, because the unavailable player simply cannot be played. The warrant is that removing those forced choices restores discretion across the whole roster, so a 16-game week makes it a lot easier to get lineup decisions wrong — more startable options means more chances to start the wrong one. The column's response to its own premise is editorial: it promises to steer readers in the right direction and keep them apprised of all the information they will need throughout the week.
