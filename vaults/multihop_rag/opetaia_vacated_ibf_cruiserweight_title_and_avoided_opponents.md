# Opetaia's Vacated IBF Cruiserweight Title And Avoided Opponents

Jai Opetaia broke his jaw twice on the way to upsetting Mairis Briedis to win the IBF and Ring belts the previous year, then endured a frustrating wait of 14 months to defend them, as reported by The Roar on 23 December 2023.

Two obstacles are recorded. Multiple opponents have dodged the 28-year-old; and the IBF demanded he fight Briedis next despite the injured Latvian being happy to delay their bout until he is fit early the following year.

Asked whether he had vented his frustrations in the ring, Opetaia acknowledged the frustration but set it aside: "I did, but look at this place, beautiful, I'm happy to be here. I'm pumped; first round knockout, I'm ready for the next fight. The last 18 months is just part of the journey. I'm here in the ring now and that's all that matters. These dudes (promoters) put the people in front of me and I just knock them out."
