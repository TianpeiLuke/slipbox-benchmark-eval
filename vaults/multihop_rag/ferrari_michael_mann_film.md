# Ferrari (Michael Mann)

Ferrari is Michael Mann's Enzo Ferrari biopic starring Adam Driver, Penélope Cruz, and Shailene Woodley, ranked 16 on Polygon's best movies of 2023 list of 2023-10-07 and due in theaters Dec. 25. Polygon quips that between Ferrari and Oppenheimer it has been a fabulous year for biopics about bastards doing tremendously dangerous things.

The standout sequences are unquestionably the races, which Mann infuses with tension, speed, and horror: the movie goes to great lengths to show how dangerous auto racing is, and every time someone gets into a car Mann translates that danger into palpable tension.

What critic AG finds most surprising is that the film's most thrilling sequences are those that clear the way for Adam Driver's performance as Enzo. The movie is an incredible portrait of a man who was a perfect concoction of some of Mann's favorite things — obsessive, brilliant, awful, detached, and a winner through and through — and the film and Driver's exceptional performance make Ferrari's ambition and passion deeply clear, with the painful determination driving him absolutely electric to watch, even more exciting than the races.
