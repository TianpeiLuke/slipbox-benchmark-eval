# Eagles Beat Dolphins 31-17 In Kelly Green On Week 7 Sunday Night Football

Philadelphia rolled to a 31-17 win over Miami on "Sunday Night Football" on Sunday, Oct. 22, 2023 at Lincoln Financial Field, shutting the Dolphins' offense out in the second half and playing smart football down the stretch. The Eagles wore Kelly Green jerseys and helmets for the game, a look Sporting News framed with Deion Sanders' line, "If you look good, you feel good, and if you feel good, you play good."

Miami had quickly erased a 17-3 deficit, scoring a touchdown just before halftime and finding the end zone again in the third quarter when Jerome Baker returned a deflected Jalen Hurts pass for a pick-six. The defining series came right after that pick-six: Hurts shrugged off the turnover and led a surgical touchdown drive. After Philadelphia's defense clamped down on Tua Tagovailoa and the Dolphins, the offense assembled a touchdown drive spanning nearly seven minutes to ice the game. A.J. Brown overcame a slow start to post 137 yards and a touchdown, his fifth consecutive game with at least 125 yards, putting him on course for a career-best season. The quarter-by-quarter line was Dolphins 3-7-7-0 for 17 and Eagles 3-14-7-7 for 31.

Kickoff was 8:20 p.m. ET. The win moved Philadelphia to 6-1 and served as a statement against one of the AFC's best teams, while Miami missed a chance to open a two-game lead in the AFC East and fell to 5-2 ahead of a matchup with the Patriots. The Eagles had not beaten the Dolphins since 2011, when Miami's loss led to coach Tony Sparano's firing the following day; Miami won the 2015 and 2019 meetings, though none of those games carried this one's anticipation.
