# NFL Week 13 Injuries Chargers At Patriots

In the Week 13 final injury reports published by CBSSports.com on Dec. 1, 2023, with the Chargers listed at -5.5 at the Patriots, New England was set to be down a couple of wide receivers while the Chargers carried one questionable player.

Allen made his return to practice on a limited basis on Friday and was officially questionable for Sunday's matchup in New England due to a quad injury; Pipkins was also limited on Friday. On the New England side, Douglas and Boutte were ruled out after not practicing on Friday: Douglas missed the entire week due to a concussion he suffered against the Giants, while Boutte was downgraded on the injury report on Thursday and missed back-to-back days due to an illness on top of his shoulder injury.
