# Wall Street Movers At The End Of September 2023

Blue Apron soared 134.5 per cent after the meal kit company said it was being bought by Wonder Group for $US13 per share in cash in a deal valued at $US103 million ($160.2 million), while energy producers were on the losing end, hurt by the slide in oil's price.

Energy stocks in the S&P 500 fell 2 per cent as a group, more than double the loss of any of the other 10 sectors that make up the index; ExxonMobil fell 1.6 per cent and Schlumberger dropped 4.3 per cent — though energy stocks remain the market's standout performers since the summer. Shares of Ford and General Motors slipped after the United Auto Workers said it would expand its limited strike to include another facility for each, with Ford down 1.1 per cent and GM down 0.6 per cent. All told, the S&P 500 slipped 11.65 points to 4288.05, the Dow dropped 158.84 to 33,507.50, and the Nasdaq added 18.05 to 13,219.32. In sharemarkets abroad, indexes were modestly higher in Europe after exchanges were closed across much of Asia.
