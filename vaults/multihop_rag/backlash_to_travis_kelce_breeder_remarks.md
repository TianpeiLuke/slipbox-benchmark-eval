# The Backlash To Travis Kelce's "Breeder" Remarks

On X, formerly known as Twitter, fans attacked Travis Kelce's "breeder" remarks as sexist, rejecting the idea that women could be described that way. "Forever grateful that Joe Alwyn kept to himself and is private," one wrote, referring to Taylor Swift's ex-boyfriend of six years. "It's what makes Travis Kelce's statements from earlier this year about 'finding a breeder' cringe. You are free to say what you want but are not free from the consequences of doing so. Women are not objects." Another added: "'Start breeding,' oh this is just f***ing disgusting and it's setting us back so much. Women aren't baby-making machines, women are more than the man they're dating, we are more than marriage." A third wrote: "Ain't a Swiftie but don't have to be one to be weirded out about Travis Kelce calling women 'breeders.' That ain't it dude."

A second line of criticism attacked the factual premise of the story that revived the clip — the claim that Swift and Kelce would have children soon — on the grounds that her schedule rules it out, since she was about to return to the Eras Tour until November 2024. "Well I know for DAMN SURE that s*** about 'having kids within the next year' is NOT coming from Taylor's side," one claimed. "She's got the Eras Tour booked all through next year, and she'd rather die than cancel a show. But we do know who's been public about looking for a 'BREEDER.'"
