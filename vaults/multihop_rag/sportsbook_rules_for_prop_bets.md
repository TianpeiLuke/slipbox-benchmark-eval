# Checking Sportsbook Rules Before Placing Prop Bets

Before placing any kind of bet, read the sportsbook's terms and conditions, where you will find all the rules for the various bets you will make. Most sportsbooks' rules are similar, but there are always small variations in how they handle certain situations that could affect your bets, so the check has to be done book by book.

One rule most sportsbooks typically apply concerns player props: if you bet on a player and they do not play in the game, most sportsbooks void the bet and refund your wager — but if that player is involved in the game even for a single play, the bet stands. Reading the T&Cs before placing any wagers is what keeps an unexpected event from catching you by surprise.
