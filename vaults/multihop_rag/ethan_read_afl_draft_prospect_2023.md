# Ethan Read AFL Draft Prospect 2023

Ethan Read, profiled in Kevin Sheehan's top 40 for the 2023 AFL Draft as reported by The Roar on Nov. 15, 2023, is a mobile and skillful ruckman whose work rate and ball use are outstanding features of his game, along with his aggressive work at stoppages. State: Queensland. State League Club: Gold Coast Suns Academy. Community Club: Palm Beach Currumbin. Date of Birth: 07/07/05. Height: 202cm.

A member of the Gold Coast Suns Academy, he was a dominant force for the Allies at the National Championships and earned All Australian honours, having a massive impact on all four games with averages of 21.5 disposals at 76 percent efficiency, 7.5 marks and 13 hitouts to play an important role in the Allies' first title win. His time of 5:56 for the 2km time trial at the national Draft Combine was sensational for any player, much more so for one of his height. He won the Hunter Harrison Medal for his performances with the Gold Coast Suns Academy — a medal presented to the best performed player from the non-Victorian Academy teams who compete in a selection of matches in the Coates Talent League. Weapon: Running ability and ball use. AFL player comparison: Mark Blicavs (Geelong Cats).
