# Subpar Pool

Subpar Pool, developed by grapefrukt games for Android, iOS, Nintendo Switch, and Windows PC, is ranked 37 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31. Polygon describes it as a delightful physics puzzler — best described as dynamic minigolf on a pool table — and says that while you can play on Switch or Steam, it is most at home on your phone.

Critic OW calls it an absolutely ideal mobile game: a reasonably priced paid app with no ads, in-app purchases, or subscription, playable in a spare three minutes.

Play consists of pocketing adorable, smiling pool balls on tables adorned with conveyor belts, portals, and moving pockets, while challenging yourself with a host of mix-and-match rulesets — balls that crack, split, or home in on you, a locked starting position, more balls, no guideline for bounces. Mobile gamers of taste will recognize the work of grapefrukt, aka Martin Jonasson, the Swedish developer of such elegant classics as Holedown, Twofold Inc., and Rymdkapsel.
