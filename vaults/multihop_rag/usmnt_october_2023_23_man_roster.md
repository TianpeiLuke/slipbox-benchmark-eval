# The USMNT's 23-Man Roster For The October 2023 Friendlies

Gregg Berhalter called in 23 players for the October 2023 friendlies against Germany and Ghana — one fewer than the squad brought in for the previous month's friendlies against Uzbekistan and Oman. The squad was announced on Thursday, October 5. PSV midfielder Malik Tillman was forced to withdraw due to injury, with Alex Zendejas of Club America named to the roster in his place.

The official 23-man roster, by position with club, caps and goals, was: goalkeepers Ethan Horvath (Nottingham Forest, 9 caps, 0 goals) and Matt Turner (Nottingham Forest, 33, 0); defenders Cameron Carter-Vickers (Celtic, 12, 0), Sergino Dest (PSV Eindhoven, 28, 2), DeJuan Jones (New England Revolution, 7, 0), Kristoffer Lund (Palermo, 2, 0), Kevin Paredes (Wolfsburg, 1, 0), Tim Ream (Fulham, 52, 1), Chris Richards (Crystal Palace, 12, 1), Miles Robinson (Atlanta United, 27, 3) and Joe Scally (Gladbach, 6, 0); midfielders Johnny Cardoso (Internacional, 7, 0), Luca de la Torre (Celta Vigo, 17, 0), Lennard Maloney (Heidenheim, 0, 0), Weston McKennie (Juventus, 46, 11), Yunus Musah (AC Milan, 29, 0), Gio Reyna (Borussia Dortmund, 20, 4) and Malik Tillman (PSV Eindhoven, 6, 0); and forwards Brenden Aaronson (Union Berlin, 34, 8), Folarin Balogun (Arsenal, 4, 2), Ricardo Pepi (PSV Eindhoven, 18, 9), Christian Pulisic (AC Milan, 62, 26), Tim Weah (Juventus, 33, 5) and Alex Zendejas (Club America, 7, 1).
