# What A British Airways Holidays Dubai Booking Includes

Booking a Dubai trip with British Airways Holidays is presented as a stress-free start to an Arabian adventure, offering great value breaks with the quality and peace of mind expected from British Airways. The package is ATOL-protected, keeping you and your money safe.

Its defining components are financial flexibility and included extras: flexible payment options with deposits from just £75 per person, with no balance due until four weeks before travel for short haul or seven weeks for long haul; the option for customers to use Avios to pay for flights and part-pay holidays; a generous baggage allowance of 23kg per person included, so you won't encounter any add-on costs; and 24/7 support throughout your trip via a dedicated holiday helpline for any issues while you are away. The upshot, per the guide, is that whatever time of year you travel, from the day you book you can put all your focus on planning an unforgettable trip.
