# The ChatGPT "Winter Break" Laziness Hypothesis

The "winter break hypothesis" proposes that ChatGPT gets worse in December because the model "learned" to do less work over the holidays — an explanation offered by users for a drop in output quality they began noticing in November 2023.

Starting in November, ChatGPT users noticed that the chatbot felt "lazier" than normal, citing instances of simpler answers and of the model refusing to complete requested tasks. Some users think this plays into the winter break hypothesis; others wonder whether the chatbot is simulating seasonal depression. OpenAI confirmed it was aware of the issue but was not sure why it was happening.

What would bear on the hypothesis is whether the model itself changed. OpenAI's ChatGPT account posted on December 8, 2023: "we've heard all your feedback about GPT4 getting lazier! we haven't updated the model since Nov 11th, and this certainly isn't intentional. model behavior can be unpredictable, and we're looking into fixing it." That the weights were unchanged since November 11 and that the behavior is unintentional are the facts against which any seasonal explanation has to be tested.
