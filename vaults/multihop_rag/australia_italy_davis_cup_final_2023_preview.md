# Australia vs Italy: 2023 Davis Cup Final Preview

Australia reached the 2023 Davis Cup final in Malaga against Italy after a 2-0 semi-final win over Finland executed without a dropped set, with the final due to start at 2am AEDT on the Monday and to be broadcast on 9GEM, The Age reported on 24 November 2023.

Lleyton Hewitt declared his close-knit tennis band of brothers would give it "a hell of a shake" to bring the Davis Cup back to Australia, and said he could not be prouder of their achievement in making back-to-back finals after the singles wins for Alexei Popyrin and Alex de Minaur. Australia, who lost the previous year's final to Canada, had enjoyed a soft run on paper to the decider, eliminating the Czech Republic and Finland — neither country boasting a top-30 singles player — but will have earned their 29th Davis Cup title if they account for Italy. Canada had defeated Italy in the Davis Cup round-robin matches in September but bowed out to Finland in the knockout stage without Felix Auger-Aliassime and Denis Shapovalov, who have both battled injury and form.

While the Italy tie did not finish until 8.15pm after a noon start, de Minaur and co should be fresh for combat in Sunday afternoon's (Monday AEDT) final as they seek to lift the Davis Cup for the first time in 20 years. Popyrin described what the semi-final had meant: "It's nerves, it's pressure – you're playing for everybody back home. It's something that nothing will ever compare to ... it really was the biggest moment of my career to get the first live rubber win, to get one foot into the final. It was something that I'll never forget."
