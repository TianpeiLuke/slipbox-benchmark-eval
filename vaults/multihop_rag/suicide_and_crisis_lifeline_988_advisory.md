# The 988 Suicide And Crisis Lifeline Advisory

The Suicide and Crisis Lifeline can be reached at 988 or at 1-800-273-TALK (8255) by anyone having thoughts of suicide, or by anyone who knows someone who is.

The advisory is attached as a standing preface to reporting that discusses suicide: "This story discusses suicide. If you or someone you know is having thoughts of suicide, please contact the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline at 988 or 1-800-273-TALK (8255)."
