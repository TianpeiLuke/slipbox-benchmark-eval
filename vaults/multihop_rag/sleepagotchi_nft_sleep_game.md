# Sleepagotchi, NFT-Powered Sleep Game

Sleepagotchi is a seed-stage mobile app game founded by Anton Kraminkin that aims to help people develop healthy sleep habits by gamifying sleep with NFTs; it appeared in Alliance DAO's All11 cohort at the 2023-10-18 demo day. The application is currently available on Apple's App Store, and its game rewards users daily for hitting sleep goals while also letting them collect items as motivation to sleep better.

Kraminkin reported during demo day that the game has 14,000 monthly active users and a 41% one-year retention rate, and according to its website 60.2% of users reported improved sleep since using the game. In January it raised $3.5 million from investors including 6th Man Ventures, Sfermion, 1kx and Shima Capital, and it is currently raising from strategic investors.
