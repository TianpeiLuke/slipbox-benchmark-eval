# Projected Publisher Traffic Losses From AI Search

Publishers believe they will lose somewhere between 20% and 40% of their website traffic once Google's AI products fully roll out, according to a Wall Street Journal report noted by TechCrunch on 15 December 2023. That report also led with a modelling result from the online magazine The Atlantic: when The Atlantic modelled what would happen if Google integrated AI into search, it found that 75% of the time the AI would answer the user's query without requiring a click-through to its website, losing it traffic.

The stakes are set by how much publisher traffic Google currently supplies. According to data from Similarweb cited in the same coverage, Google today drives nearly 40% of publishers' traffic, so a shift to AI-answered queries could have a major impact on publishers' traffic going forward. The suit filed by Helena World Chronicle takes up precisely this question of how AI will impact publishers' businesses.

Some publishers are trying to get ahead of the problem: Axel Springer inked a deal with OpenAI in the same week to license its news for AI model training.
