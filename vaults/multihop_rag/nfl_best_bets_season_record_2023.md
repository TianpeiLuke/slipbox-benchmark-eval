# The 2023 NFL Best Bets Season Record

Through eight weeks of the 2023 NFL season, Sporting News' best bets stood at 65-46-1 — a winning percentage of over .590, a number the column noted many professional bettors wish they were maintaining during a rollercoaster season. That was despite falling three bets under .500 over the previous two weeks, going 5-6 in Week 7 and 6-8 in Week 8, after starting the first six weeks with a best bets winning percentage well over .700.

The column publishes every Friday afternoon, unveiling favorite moneyline, spread, over/under and player prop wagers for the upcoming weekend after handpicking the best value opportunities on Caesars and SuperDraft and explaining how and why to attack the books.

The breakdown of the previous week was mixed rather than uniformly bad: the against-the-spread picks went 1-3 and player props a pitiful 0-3, but the moneyline and over/under picks went 5-2 — so, as the Nov. 3, 2023 column put it, like the Chiefs after a pathetic loss to the Broncos (one of the incorrect picks), it was moving on to Week 9 with its head held high.
