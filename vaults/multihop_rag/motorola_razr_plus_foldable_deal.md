# Motorola Razr Plus Foldable Deal

The Moto razr+ was 30 percent off and down to a record low of $700 for Black Friday 2023.

It earned a spot in Engadget's best foldable phones guide thanks to a handy exterior display that's actually easier to use than the one on the Samsung Galaxy Z Flip 5, and its ability to fold in half and get super compact and pocket-friendly. Its cameras are not on par with those on Samsung's foldables, but it's a generally solid flip phone with a ton of personality.
