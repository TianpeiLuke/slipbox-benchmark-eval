# Cybertruck Variants And Specifications

The Cybertruck is a steel-clad electric pickup truck made by Tesla; according to Musk it measures fewer than 19 feet long, features a bed longer than six feet, and has four doors and room for six adults. Tesla is offering three variants, but the prices, range and other specs have changed since the specs it shared in 2019.

The cheapest Cybertruck is a single-motor rear-wheel version with 250 miles of range and a 6.9-second zero to 60 miles per hour acceleration rate, which will not be available until 2025. The all-wheel drive variant has an estimated 340-mile range and top speed of 112 mph. The third variant, called the Cyberbeast, has three motors delivering 845 horsepower, a 2.6-second 0 to 60 mph acceleration rate, a top speed of 130 mph, an estimated 320-mile range and a $99,990 price tag. Both the all-wheel drive and Cyberbeast versions have a claimed towing capacity of 11,000 pounds.

For context on how far the specs have moved: Tesla's 2019 promises included a $39,900 single-motor version with rear-wheel drive and a 250-mile range, but that was eons ago figuratively speaking, and at least one variant is reported to weigh about as much as a Hummer. Tesla will also offer a range extender that pushes the all-wheel drive version to an estimated 470 miles and the Cyberbeast to more than 440 miles. After the event Musk gave more details on X, saying the range extender will be an "optional pack that fits in about 1/3 of the truck bed. Still room for plenty of cargo. It's meant for very long trips or towing heavy things up mountains."
