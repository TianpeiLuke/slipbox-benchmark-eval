# Defence Of Reich's 59-Yard Field Goal Call

Against the second-guessing of Frank Reich's decision to kick from 59 yards out in the Panthers' Nov. 9, 2023 loss to the Bears, Sporting News offered a defence that attacks the premise that the offense was the better option: Carolina's offense didn't give Reich much reason to believe.

The grounds are the offense's showing on the night. Bryce Young struggled to develop any kind of rhythm through the first three quarters, and even the Panthers' most successful drives were laborious, consistently requiring conversions on third and fourth down. The counter-argument therefore does not dispute that the kick was long or that it failed; it denies that trusting the offense to gain the yardage was a demonstrably better alternative given how the unit had performed.
