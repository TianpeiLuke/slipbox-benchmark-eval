# The Legend Of Zelda: Breath Of The Wild

Polygon picks Breath of the Wild rather than its 2023 sequel as the Zelda starting point for a new Switch owner, because it is where this iteration of Link's story kicks off and because it is extremely accessible to newcomers.

Two standout Zelda games for the Switch have gained the status of modern classic: Breath of the Wild and The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom, the sequel released in 2023. Both are described as masterful accomplishments that put Link in an open world and reimagine the scope of what is possible in exploration and puzzling. Breath of the Wild has all of the joy of exploration on a vast map, with Link able to climb and glide all over, without the mechanics introduced in the sequel, which Polygon calls both expansive and potentially overwhelming.
