# TuSimple Exits The US Market

TuSimple said in a regulatory filing on Monday 4 December 2023 that it is laying off the majority of its U.S. workforce and selling assets in the country as it exits for Asia.

When TuSimple went public in 2021 it was flying high as the leading self-driving trucks developer in the U.S. Its retreat follows a string of internal controversies and the loss of a critical partnership with truck manufacturer Navistar.
