# BEUC: The Choice And Its Implementation Cannot Be Separated

Asked whether the issue for consumer protection authorities is the pay-or-consent choice Meta is offering or how it has gone about implementing it, BEUC's spokesman said the questions are hard to separate as they are "closely interlinked" — so BEUC declines to concede that a better-implemented version of the same choice would be lawful.

The grounds are two, and they run in different directions. On the choice itself, the argument routes through data protection law: "Under consumer law, you need an informed and fair choice to purchase such a subscription. The first question is also dependent on compliance with data protection law. If the practice infringes the GDPR, the fact that it infringes a law which aims to protect fundamental rights should in our opinion be considered unfair and illegal under consumer law too," he told TechCrunch. The warrant there is that breach of a fundamental-rights law carries over into consumer law as unfairness.

On implementation, the objection stands independently of that: "In any case, the choice is designed in a way that is unfair, aggressive and misleading."
