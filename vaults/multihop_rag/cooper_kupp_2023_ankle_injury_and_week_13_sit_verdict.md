# Cooper Kupp: A Week 11 Ankle Sprain And The Week 13 Case For Benching A Stud

CBS Sports' Week 13 verdict on Cooper Kupp is Sit Him, framed as a deliberate exception to a Fantasy rule: you're never supposed to sit your studs, but what if your stud is playing through something that made him look like a dud last week? That was Kupp, who in Week 12 ran a ton of short routes, couldn't separate and didn't have much sudden movement. The argument's grounds are both usage and matchup. On usage, if he is going to come through this week it will likely come on a ton of short targets and/or some targets inside the 10-yard line, but the fact that he has 13 total targets in his past three games combined makes it tough to buy into him suddenly getting 10-plus targets. On matchup, the Browns have the defensive backs to hang tight with a slower-than-normal Kupp in man-to-man coverage, which they play a lot of, and they also have the pass rush to force Matthew Stafford to get the ball out quickly and without much distance. The conclusion: the author is not that nervous to bench Kupp for Rashee Rice, Courtland Sutton and both of the top Seattle wide receivers. Scope: Kupp is an L.A. Rams wide receiver, #10, age 30 with 7 years of experience; the weekly breakdown lists the matchup versus Cleveland with the Rams favored by 3.5 and an over/under of 40, the opponent 14th versus wide receivers, projected points of 13.8 and a WR rank of 25th; year-to-date he has 27 receptions on 51 targets for 393 receiving yards, 1 touchdown and 10.6 Fantasy points per game, with projections powered by SportsLine.

The physical condition behind that verdict was reported earlier, in a Week 11 injury roundup dated 2023-11-19. Kupp had just a single catch against the Seahawks before suffering an ankle injury while blocking. The former All-Pro, who had battled a lingering hamstring injury earlier that year, received attention on the sidelines after limping off, then left the game for further evaluation in the locker room and was ruled questionable to return. NFL Media reported on the Monday that Kupp suffered a low ankle sprain and would be evaluated going forward.
