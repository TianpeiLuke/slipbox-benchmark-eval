# UK Support Organisations For Older Adults

The Guardian's 10 October 2023 piece lists five organisations older adults and their families can turn to, each defined by a distinct function.

Age UK has a database of social activities and groups for older folk. The Walking Football Association keeps keen amateur footballers playing long into their 50s and 60s. Action for Elders offers social and exercise programmes.

The Centre for Ageing Better provides information on working and living as an older person, and u3a, a charity collective, provides local opportunities for people no longer in work to acquire new skills and knowledge.
