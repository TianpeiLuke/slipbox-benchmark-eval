# USD Funds In China Turn To Overseas Deals

On a weekday afternoon at Red Rock Coffee, the café known for spotting venture capitalists in Silicon Valley, one is likely to overhear a few conversations in Mandarin. With China reopening its borders in spring 2023 following three years of COVID-19 restrictions, managers of U.S. funds in the country have been flocking to the Bay Area.

The trips themselves are not new; their purpose is. While these trips were routine before the pandemic, they have now taken on a fresh purpose of discovering deals beyond China.

As China's domestic investment appeal wanes, some investors start to look for opportunities beyond its borders. Rather than a complete departure, they are merely following the footsteps of Chinese talent who have already embarked on global expansion.
