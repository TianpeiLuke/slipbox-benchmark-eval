# Gemini Pro's News Summarization Refusals And Stale Answers

Asked to summarise topical news in early December 2023, Gemini Pro often would not do it, despite having Google Search and Google News at its disposal. It seemed loath to comment on potentially controversial news topics, instead telling users to Google it themselves.

The TechCrunch reporter tried the same prompt and got a very similar response. ChatGPT, by contrast, gave a bullet-list summary with citations to news articles.

There was one partial exception, and it exposed a second problem: Gemini Pro did provide a summary of updates on the war in Ukraine when the reporter asked for one, but the information was over a month out of date.
