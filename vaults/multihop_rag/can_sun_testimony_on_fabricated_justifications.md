# Can Sun's Testimony On Fabricated Justifications

Former FTX lawyer Can Sun testified that during the days in November when the exchange imploded, Bankman-Fried — then trying to raise money from the private equity group Apollo, which wanted an explanation for what had happened — came to him and asked him to come up with justifications for the missing funds. That request, Sun said, "basically confirmed my suspicions that had been rising all day" that Bankman-Fried had purloined the funds.

Sun ran Bankman-Fried through possible explanations, including a margin loan program, and explained that none of them could fit the actual situation — for instance, Alameda had borrowed more from FTX than had ever been in the margin loan program. He said Bankman-Fried acknowledged their conversation with a "yup, yup": "I was expecting a bigger response, but it was very muted." After that, Sun testified, Singh spilled the beans that Alameda had withdrawn assets that included the customer funds. Sun quit the next day, and, like Yedidia, testified with immunity.

The jury then saw the fabrication deployed in public: a Good Morning America appearance by Bankman-Fried on December 1st, 2022, in which he tried to justify the missing funds through the margin loan program. The Verge's reporter called it such a flimsy excuse that even George Stephanopoulos could see through it, and described Sun's testimony as brief and explosive.
