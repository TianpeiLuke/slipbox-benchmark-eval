# Rams 36-19 Browns, Week 13 2023

Matthew Stafford threw three touchdown passes and Puka Nacua became the first Rams rookie wide receiver to reach 1,000 yards in a season as Los Angeles beat the Cleveland Browns 36-19 in Week 13 of the 2023 season.

The Rams extended their winning streak to three games, their first such run since winning the Super Bowl after the 2021 season; at 6-6 they were back in the playoff race after dropping three straight before their bye week. Joe Flacco was 23 of 44 for 254 yards with two touchdowns and an interception in his Browns debut. Cleveland fell to 7-5 and occupied the final playoff spot in the AFC.
