# Walmart Cyber Monday 2023 Vacuum And Air Purifier Deals

WIRED's Walmart Cyber Monday roundup of 27 November 2023 recommended four floor- and air-care machines: the Dyson V12 Detect Slim stick vacuum, a cordless vacuum WIRED calls its favourite, the Dyson Pure Hot + Cool, and a compact carpet cleaner discounted to a price WIRED had seen before only at its lowest.

The V12 Detect Slim (8/10, WIRED Recommends) sits mid-level in Dyson's lineup and adds an LCD screen for selecting one of three power levels and for showing remaining battery, filter and blockage information among other things; like all of Dyson's stick vacuums it is lightweight and easy to manoeuvre around tight homes. The cordless pick is WIRED's favourite in that category: besides reliable suction power even on corrugated surfaces, it stands on its own — a feature WIRED calls surprisingly rare among cordless vacuums — and the Extreme Max converts to a hand vac in a few moments and ships with three attachments.

The Dyson Pure Hot + Cool is described as Dyson wedging function into beautiful form: in testing, WIRED was struck by how well it integrates a fan, an electric heater and an air purifier into one unit, it filters out 99.97 percent of particles 0.3 microns in size, and its built-in air quality monitor displays indoor VOC and particulate levels on a bright, easy-to-read display. The last pick, the "little green machine" carpet cleaner, is at a price matching the lowest WIRED tends to see; it made both the Best Carpet Cleaners and the Best Viral TikTok Gadgets lists and is described as great for cleaning furniture, stairs and vehicles.
