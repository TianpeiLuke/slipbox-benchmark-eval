# Alex Verdugo Red Sox Trade Candidacy

Alex Verdugo was ranked No. 7 on Yardbarker's Nov. 13, 2023 list of the top 25 MLB offseason trade candidates: his name had been floated in trade rumors for a few seasons and the Red Sox had held onto him thus far, but they had a new baseball operations leader in Craig Breslow, who had acknowledged the team had gotten early calls on the left-handed hitting outfielder.

Jarren Duran and Masataka Yoshida also hit from the left side, so dealing Verdugo could free some at-bats for a right-handed addition while perhaps bringing in immediate help for the pitching staff or at second base. MLBTR projected Verdugo for a $9.2M salary in his final season of arbitration. He was coming off another league-average offensive showing, hitting .264/.324/.421 with 13 home runs through 602 plate appearances.

Verdugo had not become the All-Star player the Sox envisioned when building the Mookie Betts return around him. He was a solid regular, a high-contact hitter who rated as a decent defender in right field.
