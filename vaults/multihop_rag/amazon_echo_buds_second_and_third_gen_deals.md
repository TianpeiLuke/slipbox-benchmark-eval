# Amazon Echo Buds Second And Third Gen Deals

The second-gen Amazon Echo Buds are on sale with the wired charging case for $64.99 ($54 off) at Amazon and Best Buy, and with a wireless charging case for $85.99 ($55 off) at Amazon, while the third-generation Echo Buds are $34.99 ($15 off) from Amazon — matching their all-time low.

The 2021 edition of the Echo Buds is a great value: Amazon's second-generation true wireless earbuds offer improved comfort and better noise cancellation than their predecessor, plus a terrific passthrough mode ensuring you can always hear what's going on around you. The $119.99 earbuds also feature hands-free Alexa support and remarkably satisfying sound, with more bass than the AirPods Pro and an appropriate amount of restraint in the high end. Earlier in 2023 Amazon introduced the third-generation Echo Buds at just $44.99: they lack noise cancellation but have a lightweight, open-ear design that should feel more comfortable. The third-gen listing shows $35 against $50, 30 percent off, noting they lack ANC but have a semi-open design making it easier to stay aware of your surroundings, integrate with Alexa, and support multipoint pairing.
