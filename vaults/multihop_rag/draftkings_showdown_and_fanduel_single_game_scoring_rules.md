# DraftKings Showdown And FanDuel Single-Game Scoring Rules

DraftKings Showdown contests use full-point PPR with three-point bonuses for 300 passing yards, 100 rushing yards and 100 receiving yards, while FanDuel contests use four-point passing touchdowns and half-point PPR.

The distinguishing structural feature of DraftKings Showdown is the Captain slot: the Captain pick costs 1.5 times a player's original price, but he also gets his point total multiplied by 1.5. Those were the key scoring settings Sporting News laid out before its lineup and strategy sections — for the DraftKings Showdown format on Dec. 18, 2023 and for the FanDuel single-game format on Dec. 7, 2023.
