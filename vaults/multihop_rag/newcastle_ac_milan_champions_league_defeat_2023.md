# Newcastle's Champions League Defeat To AC Milan

Newcastle lost their final 2023-24 Champions League group game to AC Milan after leading through Joelinton, with Christian Pulisic equalising and Samuel Chukwueze scoring the winner, The Independent reported on 13 December 2023; Newcastle had 17 shots and eight on target but beat Mike Maignan only once, while Milan had two shots on target and scored twice past Martin Dubravka.

The turning point was Pulisic's strike, which came around the time Warren Zaire-Emery drew Paris Saint-Germain level against Borussia Dortmund. Newcastle failed to clear as the ball ricocheted around their box before Olivier Giroud found Pulisic to poke in a shot — the American had been a Newcastle target the previous January, and they must now regret that he joined Milan in the summer. Then, with 11 minutes remaining, Rafael Leao sprinted clear and shot against the post; if this had threatened to be the derby of the depleted, Milan had Leao back for the first time in a month, while Newcastle had Callum Wilson able to start.

Goalkeeping and goal-line defending shaped the night. At 0-0, Fikayo Tomori made a magnificent goal-line tackle to stop Miguel Almiron applying the finishing touch to Joelinton's low cross — a remarkable rescue act, but pressure told when the Brazilian struck. At 1-1 Maignan tipped Bruno Guimaraes' 20-yard shot onto the bar; as Nick Pope had denied Milan victory in San Siro with a string of saves, Stefano Pioli's team could testify to the difference brilliant goalkeeping can make, and now they benefited from it. Newcastle's determination to win rather than settle for the point and the Europa League place left them short-staffed at the back, because centre-back Fabian Schar had charged into the Milan box in open play; in a frantic finale they almost conceded a third, with Dubravka out of his goal, Theo Hernandez almost scoring from the halfway line and Tomori hitting the post.
