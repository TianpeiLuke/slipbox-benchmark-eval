# The US Four-Factor Fair Use Test

Fair use is the carve-out to copyright law that allows copyrighted materials to be used without permission in certain limited circumstances, such as for educational purposes or for reporting news, and US courts determine whether a use qualifies using a test with four factors.

The four factors are: the purpose and character of the use — is the use of the copyrighted work for educational purposes or for commercial purposes; the nature of the copyrighted work — whether or not the work is particularly creative and original; the amount and substantiality of the portion taken — just how much of a copyrighted work was used without permission; and the effect of the use on the potential market for, or value of, the copyrighted work.
