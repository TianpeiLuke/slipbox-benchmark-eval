# Rogers Communications Company Profile

Rogers Communications is Canada's largest wireless provider and the owner of a cable television empire, the Toronto Blue Jays baseball team, and other media and sports assets. The Rogers family's controlling stake in the public company is worth close to C$8 billion ($5.9 billion), according to calculations based on company filings for its 2023 annual meeting.

The company is inseparable from the prominent Toronto family behind it, and the court documents of October 2023 show that the fractures inside that family have never healed.
