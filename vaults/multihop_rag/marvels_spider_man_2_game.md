# Marvel's Spider-Man 2 (Insomniac Games)

Marvel's Spider-Man 2, developed by Insomniac Games for PlayStation 5, is ranked 8 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31, and builds on the successes of the first game both narratively and in its immensely satisfying gameplay. It was released Oct. 20 on PlayStation 5, and Polygon reviewed it using a pre-release download code provided by Sony Interactive Entertainment.

The story takes a familiar Spider-Man narrative — friends turned foes — and adds new twists, building out compelling and demanding villains in the process. The game also sheds its predecessor's odious Spider-Cop bits in favor of establishing a deeper connection between the Spider-Mans and their city, giving it room to breathe while further immersing players in its heroes' worlds and struggles. Critic PV writes that few experiences in gaming are more enjoyable than swinging around New York City with Spider-Man, and with a bigger map to explore and wings to fly there is basically no limit to the fun; the deployment of two protagonists is seamless, since Peter and Miles play differently and bring their own stories and desires, and switching between them is effortless. It is also the rare open-world game that does not feel bloated with missions and side quests.

Polygon's 2023-10-16 piece adds detail on that structure and on speed. Mainstream Spider-Man stories are increasingly Spider-Men stories, and this third Spidey game from Insomniac is no different: you play as both the yassified Peter Parker from Spider-Man Remastered and Miles Morales from his eponymous 2020 debut, switching between the two at your leisure with all the speed afforded by the PlayStation 5's SSD. New to the series are web wings, which let the Spider-Mans fly through the city, gliding over rooftops and gaining speed from wind tunnels without relying on buildings, bridges, or other anchors — especially useful when crossing the East River into the newly added Brooklyn and Queens. Web-swinging is faster too, enhancing the ineffable feeling of being Spider-Man, and traversing the three available boroughs is such a joy that the critic used fast travel less often than in the previous two games. Of the two Spider-Mans, it is Miles who feels more alive with the possibility to break free of expectation and canon.
