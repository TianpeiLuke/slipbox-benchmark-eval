# The Cybertruck May Have An Unseen Shock-Absorbing Mechanism

Against the conclusion that the Cybertruck's apparent lack of crumple zones makes it lethal, The Verge attacks the evidentiary premise: all we have is a couple of videos, and what we need is data, independently verified, before anyone can say definitively that this truck will cause mass death and destruction.

The specific possibility the videos cannot rule out is raised by safety experts quoted by Reuters, who acknowledge there may be some shock-absorbent mechanism that makes up for the apparent lack of crumple zones. "We just don't know yet."
