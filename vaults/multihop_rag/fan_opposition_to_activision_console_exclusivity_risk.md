# Fan Opposition To Activision Console Exclusivity Risk

Not everyone greeted the Microsoft-Activision deal positively, and the objection targets the assumption that current cross-platform availability will hold: some fans do not want to see a permanent switch to Microsoft somewhere down the line.

The precedent they invoke cuts the other way from Sony's current position. Once upon a time, the Activision games Crash Bandicoot and Spyro the Dragon were PlayStation exclusives, which shows how a publisher's titles can be tied to a single console.

That worry has taken organised form: it has even led some fans to make petitions calling for Microsoft to keep the games playable on all consoles in perpetuity.
