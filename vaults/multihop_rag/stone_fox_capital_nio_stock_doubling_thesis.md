# Stone Fox Capital's NIO Stock Doubling Thesis

Stone Fox Capital, an SA Investing Group leader, argued in early December 2023 that NIO shares — then trading at their 2023 lows, having fallen below $8 — had the potential to double to $16.

The grounds are mixed rather than uniformly bullish. Stone Fox Capital attributed the fall below $8 to fluctuating monthly delivery numbers and inconsistent EV deliveries, and flagged that despite an electric vehicle delivery record in Q3, a recent 10% workforce reduction raises concerns for Q4. The warrant joining those observations to a $16 target is the depressed starting point: the same delivery inconsistency that drove the decline has left the stock at its 2023 lows, which is what makes a doubling the analyst's stated upside case.

The scope conditions matter. This is one Seeking Alpha Investing Group author's view as relayed in a December 3, 2023 preview, the $16 figure is framed as potential rather than a formal price target, and the author's own Q4 concern about the workforce cut sits unresolved inside the thesis.
