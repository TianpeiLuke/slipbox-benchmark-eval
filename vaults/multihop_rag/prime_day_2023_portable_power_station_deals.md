# Prime Day 2023 Portable Power Station Deals

Two power stations feature in WIRED's roundup: an EcoFlow unit with a 768 watt-hour LiFePO4 battery, and the 1000 Plus with a 1264Wh battery.

WIRED grants it's debatable whether some of the Best Portable Power Stations are truly all that portable, but the EcoFlow is easy to carry thanks to a large handle along the back, and its LiFePO4 battery is perfect for keeping gadgets charged on camping trips — the main downside being fan noise. The 1000 Plus is worth a look because its smaller version recently earned a spot in WIRED's Best Portable Chargers guide and the larger model is its top pick among Best Portable Power Stations: it packs loads of ports, three AC outlets, and is rated at 2,000 watts with 4,000-watt peak power capability, so you can plug in electric grills and other small appliances without worrying.
