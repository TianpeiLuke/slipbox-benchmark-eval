# Echelon Connect Sport Exercise Bike Black Friday Deal

The Walmart-exclusive Echelon Connect Sport was over $400 off for Black Friday 2023, knocking the price down to $297, Mashable reported on 25 November 2023 — "a solid Peloton alternative for a retail price that won't make you cringe".

Being wallet-friendly does not mean the specs disappoint: the Connect Sport features 32 levels of silent magnetic resistance, aero handlebars and adjustable pedals, and Bluetooth technology that lets you connect any smart device to the Echelon Fit app.

That app gives access to 40+ daily live and 15,000+ on-demand connected fitness classes, and the $297 Black Friday cost even includes a 30-day free trial of Echelon Premier membership to get you started.
