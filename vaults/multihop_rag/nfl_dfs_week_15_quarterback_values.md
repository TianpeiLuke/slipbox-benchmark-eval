# NFL DFS Week 15 Quarterback Values

Sporting News' headline Week 15 2023 quarterback DFS plays were Brock Purdy of the 49ers at Arizona (DK $6,800, FD $8,400, SD 1.2x) and Jordan Love of the Packers against Tampa Bay (DK $6,200, FD $8,000, SD 1.25x).

The column noted it usually does not feature players with salaries higher than $6,200 on DraftKings and $8,000 on FanDuel, but that $6,800 seemed a massive value for the MVP frontrunner: Purdy had been scorching, with 57.9 DK points over San Francisco's past two games and at least 23.8 in five of the Niners' past six, and the Cardinals ranked among the 10 most generous defenses to quarterbacks on DraftKings. For Love, the argument was that he and the Packers' pass-catchers had emerged as legit offensive threats over the past month, finally easing the Cheeseheads' anguish over the departure of Aaron Rodgers; cult hero Tommy DeVito had hit the pause button on Green Bay's good times, but it should start rolling again at home against a miserable Tampa Bay secondary that had surrendered the fourth-most FanDuel points and fifth-most DraftKings points to quarterbacks that season, with Plodmaster General AJ Dillon not about to crack the Bucs' strong front seven. Other solid Week 15 values listed were Matthew Stafford, Rams vs. Commanders (DK $6,000, FD $7,200, SD 1.3x); Derek Carr, Saints vs. Giants (DK $5,800, FD $6,500, SD 1.35x); Joe Flacco, Browns vs. Bears (DK $5,500, FD $7,100, SD 1.35x); and Will Levis, Titans vs. Texans (DK $5,400, FD $6,800, SD 1.35x).
