# The Verge's Score And Verdict For The Echo Frames Carrera Cruiser

The Verge gave the Amazon Echo Frames Carrera Cruiser a Verge Score of 6, listing the price as $314.99 and linking to it at $315 at Amazon.

Under The Good, the review credits the frames as more stylish and offering more options, with improved battery life and audio, and the ability to use them with multiple devices. Under The Bad: bass still isn't great and audio leaks; they do less or sound not as good as others in this category; Alexa still isn't the best assistant when you're on the go; and they are pricey for the category. The scorecard is accompanied by The Verge's standard "How we rate and review products" link.
