# Prime Day 2023 Connected Home And Personal Gadget Deals

This section of WIRED's roundup covers Monster's digital frame, Skylight's 15-inch frame, Tile Stickers, the Niu KQi3 electric scooter, Withings' Body+ and Body Cardio scales, an Epicka travel adapter, and the Google Nest Hub Max.

Monster's digital frame isn't as good as options from Aura or Nixplay, which top WIRED's digital frames guide, but it's better than most other cheap frames: a 1280p screen gives crisp photos and it works with Google Assistant or Alexa, making it a reasonable choice if you can't imagine spending $150 or so. If you can spend more, WIRED prefers its top choices, but Skylight's 15-inch frame looks nice on a shelf or wall-mounted, with a smaller 10-inch version for less. Tile's Bluetooth tracker Stickers can be stuck onto remotes, e-readers, or anything else to keep track of within a 150-foot range, at the best price seen since last year. The KQi3 is WIRED's favorite electric scooter for most people and goes on sale regularly, so never pay full price: great range of 18 to 20 miles for most people, a 20 mph top speed, 9.5-inch tubeless tires for a comfy ride, and disc brakes that reliably bring it to a quick stop. Even after trying the newer Withings Body Comp, the Body+ remains in WIRED's sweet spot, tracking body fat, muscle mass, and total body water and charting progress over time; the Body Cardio is an upgraded model adding heart-health features including a built-in heart monitor that analyzes cardiovascular health using vascular age data — features WIRED found not totally worth the extra price on the pricier Body Comp, but the Body Cardio is cheaper normally and even better on this sale. The Epicka travel adapter is the budget pick in WIRED's Best Travel Adapters guide, working in more than 150 countries with four USB-A ports on the bottom and a 15-watt USB-C port on the side; it has dropped this low before, but not for a while. The Google Nest Hub Max has a nice big screen and slim, powerful speakers, and is WIRED's favorite smart display for reasons including how nicely it doubles as a photo frame and how great it is as a kitchen assistant.
