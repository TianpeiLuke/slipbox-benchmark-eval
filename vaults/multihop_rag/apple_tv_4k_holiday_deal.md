# Apple TV 4K Holiday Deal

The Apple TV 4K is Engadget's favorite premium streaming device and was $125 at B&H Photo over the 2023 holiday sales. That's not a huge discount, but the streaming box rarely goes on sale.

Engadget gave it a 94 in its review because it's fast, has an excellent Siri remote and integrates seamlessly with Apple's overall ecosystem; navigation is clean and easy to use, and the device can stream in 4K, HDR and Dolby Vision.
