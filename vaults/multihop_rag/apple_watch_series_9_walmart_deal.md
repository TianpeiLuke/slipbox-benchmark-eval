# Apple Watch Series 9 Walmart Black Friday Deal

At $359 for the 45mm GPS version, the Apple Watch Series 9 was sitting at an all-time low cost at Walmart during Black Friday 2023, Mashable reported on 25 November 2023.

The Series 9 is "a robust and elite wearable device – the best Apple has ever made", according to Mashable Senior Editor Stan Schroeder. While it looks almost identical to its predecessor, it is equipped with a new s9 chip that makes it about 30 percent snappier than before, and it boasts double the storage, double the peak brightness, and a fun new Double Tap feature.

It is not an essential upgrade if you already have a Series 7 or 8, the roundup says, "but it's certainly a step in the right direction for those with older watches".
