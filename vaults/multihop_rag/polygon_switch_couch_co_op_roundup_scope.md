# Couch Co-Op on the Nintendo Switch as a Roundup Category

Couch co-op, as Polygon uses the term in its 2023-10-20 list of the 35 best couch co-op games for Nintendo Switch, means local multiplayer — old-fashioned local play on a single console, as opposed to online multiplayer. The category is defined by the abundance of Switch titles with local multiplayer features, which the writer argues makes the Switch a great console for game night with friends or a casual play session with a roommate.

The discriminating features the article offers are experiential rather than technical. Local multiplayer makes ass-kicking games much more approachable, thanks to friends respawning your character; it supports building something together in real time, like a farm in Stardew Valley; and it enables communal chaos, as with Untitled Goose Game's treacherous honking. The writer concedes a boundary case in the same breath — couch co-op can also mean getting revived just to get ragdolled across spikes in Spelunky 2 — but holds that it still beats dying alone. The writer also notes this is their preferred play style, as the last few years have given them more excuses to stay in and try new games.

The roundup is scoped to the Switch specifically, and Polygon distinguishes it from adjacent lists: readers wanting platform-agnostic recommendations are pointed to the outlet's favorite local multiplayer games, games to play with a Valentine's Day beau, and a Dear Polygon column about couch co-op. The list is maintained rather than fixed; the last update at the time added Escape Academy.
