# Apple Blocks Beeper Mini's iMessage Access

On Friday night, 8 December 2023, Apple took action against Beeper, having found a way to stop Beeper Mini's messages from being passed from Android phones to its servers — only days after the app launched. By Monday 11 December the app, which brings blue bubble iMessage texts to Android users, was back in operation, with the startup saying it had resumed functioning but not disclosing how that had been made possible; Beeper Mini will also be free for the time being given the unstable nature of its status and Apple's attempts at shutting it down.

Apple justified the block on security grounds, explaining that it needed to shut down the app because it "posed significant risks to user security and privacy, including the potential for metadata exposure and enabling unwanted messages, spam, and phishing attacks." In essence, Apple said it could not verify that Beeper's claims of end-to-end encryption were true, and therefore needed to take "steps to protect our users by blocking techniques that exploit fake credentials in order to gain access to iMessage."

Apple had not yet gotten in touch directly with Beeper, having only released a statement to the press, and it was unclear at the time whether, how or when it would be able to disable Beeper's updated build.
