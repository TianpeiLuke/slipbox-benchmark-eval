# Fair Use, Explained Plainly

"Fair use" is, as it sounds, the argument that there are cases where copyrighted material can be used and even replicated that are fair enough.

Music Business Worldwide's 2023-11-23 piece offers a deliberately clumsy example: I read a great book, then I meet you for breakfast, where I tell you about the story and quote a couple of my favorite passages to you. To suggest that this is copyright infringement would not only be silly — it would mean that copyright protections were getting in the way of the commercial furtherment of the author or rightsholder, since you might go and buy the book on the recommendation.
