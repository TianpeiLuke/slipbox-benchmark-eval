# Exquisite Biome

Exquisite Biome, from game designer Caro Asercion and illustrator Si Sweetman, sits in the world-building TTRPG tradition but focuses specifically on ecological biomes and the creatures that populate them. Game and software developer Everest Pipkin, recommending it in Polygon's 2023 roundup, passes on a pitch she has heard for it: "Spore for the kitchen table."

Play works through short generative prompts tied to a deck of playing cards, with which you build and witness the lives of animals far beyond the edge of our own world — an experience Pipkin says feels a little bit like being David Attenborough on an alien planet. With beautiful, evocative art and short, easy-to-parse rules, she calls it her go-to "what if we made up some weird little guys and set them in motion for an hour" game.
