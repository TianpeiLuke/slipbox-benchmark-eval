# Canada's Path To Copa America 2024 Qualification

Advancement to the CONCACAF Nations League semifinals is enough to book a place at the 2024 Copa America, so the winner of the Jamaica-Canada quarterfinal qualifies for the tournament in the US starting in June, alongside the likes of reigning champions Argentina, and also advances to the Nations League semifinals against one of the other quarterfinalists — the USA, Trinidad and Tobago, Honduras, Mexico, Costa Rica or Panama. After the 2-1 first-leg win, Canada's task was ostensibly straightforward: avoid defeat against Jamaica in the return leg.

The losing side is not eliminated from Copa America contention outright. The ousted Nations League quarterfinalists compete for two more qualification spots in playoffs that take place in March, and the calibre of teams in those playoffs is likely to be weaker, with the USA looking almost certain not to need that route after a 3-0 first-leg win over Trinidad and Tobago. Still, that is a tense and difficult prospect, and both sides would wish to avoid such a precarious situation, since missing out on the Copa America would be a monumental disappointment.

Two facts framed the second leg. Jamaica have never won in Canada, though they showed enough in Kingston — in sunshine, mercifully, after the postponement of the game in torrential conditions the previous night — to suggest they could spring an upset; and their task would be made harder by the probable absence of Michail Antonio, the West Ham United forward who signalled for treatment and limped down the tunnel with a possible hamstring injury during the first half, while Leon Bailey could not match speed and timing with accurate finishes when presented with good early chances.
