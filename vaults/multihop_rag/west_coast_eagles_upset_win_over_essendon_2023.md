# West Coast Eagles' Upset Win Over Essendon, 2023

West Coast withstood a late surge from Essendon to claim their second win of the 2023 AFLW season, 3.5 (23) to 2.7 (19), in a slugfest at the appropriately named Windy Hill on the Sunday, The Age reported on 20 October 2023.

The win came in a difficult week for the club. Eagles coach Michael Prior had caused controversy with comments following the previous week's 70-point loss to Melbourne, blasting the decision to pit strugglers West Coast against the defending premiers: "How we play that team is beyond me. That is what you get when you get fixturing like that." He later backtracked, but his comments put added pressure and focus on his team. "We've had a horror week back at home, and it's been really bloody tough on all of us, and Mick as well. I'm just really proud of the girls," West Coast captain Emma Swanson said post-game on the broadcast. "I can name the whole team. Everyone had their second in the last couple minutes. That was such a team effort, everyone had a moment there when they had to put their head over the footy and lay a tackle and not let it slip."

Aisling McCarthy and Amy Franklin drew first blood for West Coast within the opening five minutes while the hosts were kept goalless; the Bombers added only three behinds in the second term and the Eagles failed to score as the blustery conditions took their toll. Kellie Gibson broke the goal drought with the only major of the third quarter, before the home side came to life with the wind at their backs in the final term, skipper Bonnie Toogood and Amber Clarke reducing the margin to four points. Toogood continued to pepper the scoreboard, but the Eagles held on. Swanson (25 disposals, eight clearances) stood out, while Bombers workhorse Maddy Prespakis (30 disposals, nine clearances) was typically prominent alongside Georgia Nanscawen (21, six). With two rounds left, West Coast had a chance to build a winning streak against bottom-of-the-table Western Bulldogs; Essendon, slipping one place to sixth, had must-win matches against Carlton and Gold Coast on the road to the finals, and would be without Steph Cain, sidelined with a concussion after an elbow to the head from Swanson.
