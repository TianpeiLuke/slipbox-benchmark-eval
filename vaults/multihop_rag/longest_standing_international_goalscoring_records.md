# The Longest-Standing International Goalscoring Records

Only six countries' international goalscoring records have stood for more than 50 years, including Libya, Sudan and Guinea, and if only nations that have qualified for the World Cup are counted, just Denmark, Norway and Hungary have records lasting more than half a century, as reported by The New York Times on 17 November 2023.

Hungary's is perhaps the most impressive of them, considering Ferenc Puskas scored 84 goals in just 85 games and had his international career brought to a premature end at the age of 29 because of the Hungarian Revolution; he later represented Spain at the 1962 World Cup, having gone half a decade without playing international football.

The striking finding in the other direction is how many goalscoring records have been set recently. Sixty-four of the 211 nations' record goalscorers have appeared for them in 2023, and in terms of time since they were set the median goalscoring mark has stood for just seven years, a group that includes the Republic of Ireland's Robbie Keane and Paraguay's Roque Santa Cruz. Increased longevity due to superior fitness levels in the modern game is clearly a major factor, as is the number of relatively new nations on the FIFA list.
