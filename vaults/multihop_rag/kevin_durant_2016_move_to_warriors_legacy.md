# Kevin Durant 2016 Move To Warriors Legacy

In the 2016 Western Conference finals, the Kevin Durant and Russell Westbrook-led OKC Thunder faced the 73-9 Warriors and, after swiftly securing a 3-1 series lead, seemed poised for an NBA Finals appearance — but the Warriors orchestrated a remarkable comeback to win the series in seven games, meaning Durant would go trophy-less for another season, Essentially Sports recounted on Dec. 1, 2023.

In the 2016 offseason, Durant made the pivotal decision to join the very team that had defeated him. The move had profound consequences, leading to a shift in public opinion, with many labeling him a "snake" and "spineless." While Durant achieved the success he craved in the Bay Area, his decision to join the Warriors has permanently cast a shadow on his legacy.
