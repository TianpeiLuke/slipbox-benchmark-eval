# K.O. II Design Lineage From The Pocket Operator Series

The K.O. II name signals that Teenage Engineering considers the EP-133 something of a sequel to the original PO-33 K.O., and while it clearly isn't a Pocket Operator, its retro desk calculator aesthetic takes subtle design cues from that series.

The boundary between the two lines is size and seriousness. At 12 inches diagonally, the K.O. II is in iPad territory, where the Pocket Operators are pocket-sized; it does nod to the PO series by running on AAA batteries or USB power. Engadget's reviewer couldn't say how long it runs on batteries — it will vary from brand to brand — but had been using some cheap rechargeables for over a week and found them still going strong. The reviewer, never particularly enamoured with the Pocket Operators, much prefers the K.O. II's form factor: still very portable but feeling a bit more "serious," and very nice to look at, which is something Teenage Engineering is quite good at. The model number, EP-133, suggests others may follow in the line, prompting hopes for giant calculator versions of other instruments.
