# Where CONMEBOL 2026 Qualifiers Could And Could Not Be Watched

Broadcast rights for Argentina's home World Cup qualifiers in the United States were secured by Telemundo and Univision following an agreement at the 11th hour of the September 2023 international break, so US viewers could watch the Oct. 12 Argentina-Paraguay match on Universo, with streaming on Fubo as well as the company's premium subscription services — listed as Fubo, Telemundo Deportes en Vivo and Universo NOW.

The match was not set to be available globally in regions including Australia, Canada and the United Kingdom, because CONMEBOL had yet to sell broadcast rights to a provider in those territories; it was, however, available free on FIFA+ in select territories. The territory table showed no channel or stream for the UK, Australia, Canada, India, New Zealand, Singapore or Hong Kong.

Other territories with broadcast options for the match were Malaysia (Astro Supersport, with streaming on Astro Go and sooka), Argentina (TyC Sports), Brazil (SporTV 2), France (L'Equipe Web), Spain (Movistar) and Saudi Arabia (SSC).
