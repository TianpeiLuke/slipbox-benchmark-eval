# The Astros' Manager Search After Dusty Baker's Retirement

Dusty Baker announced his retirement after his fourth season with the Astros and his 26th big league managerial season, Essentially Sports reported on 2023-10-30. Houston won the 2022 World Series and two pennants while the 74-year-old was in charge, though despite the starry possibilities of winning, the Texas Rangers defeated the Astros in the ALCS in 2023.

With that seat waiting to be warmed up, the future manager would inherit a seasoned roster that appears ready to make another push at winning a World Series after winning six of the previous seven AL West championships. The caveat is the rotation: given Luis Garcia's Tommy John surgery and Lance McCullers Jr.'s forearm surgery, the starting pitchers are expected to be shaky. On candidates, one of the most sensible picks for a smooth transition would be Joe Espada, age 48, the Astros' bench coach since 2018, on whom Baker relied in the dugout throughout his productive tenure. Apart from him, Buck Showalter, the former New York Mets manager, Don Mattingly, the Toronto Blue Jays bench coach, and A.J. Hinch, the Detroit Tigers manager, could be other possibilities.
