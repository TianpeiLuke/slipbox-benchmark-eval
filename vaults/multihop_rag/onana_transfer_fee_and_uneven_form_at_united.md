# Onana's Transfer Fee And Uneven Form At United

André Onana was bought by Manchester United for £47.2m and arrived on a ticket of being a high-grade shot-stopper who was technically impressive with the ball at his feet, but until impressing against Brentford and Copenhagen he had bombed, costing United goals and points, as The Guardian reported on 28 October 2023.

That uneven form was a reason Erik ten Hag, still building his team, might be wary of his new keeper. Asked why the No 1 was taking time to settle in England, Ten Hag pointed to the demands of the league: "Certain things [here] are different. OK it is 11-v-11, and it is about attacking, defending, transition. But there is more: the approach to the game, the speed of the game, the intensity. The varieties in styles. So there is absolutely a difference and players need to adjust, need time to do so."
