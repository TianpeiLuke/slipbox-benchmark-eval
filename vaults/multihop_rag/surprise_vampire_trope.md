# The Surprise Vampire Trope

The surprise vampire is a story that is not a vampire story but that turns out to have vampires in it — a device Polygon rates cooler than a vampire story proper.

It is a rich tradition, unfolding across many different media intended for all kinds of audiences. Sometimes the surprise vampire is a prominent part of a story that is made richer for the unexpected presence of a bloodsucker. Sometimes it is subtle, an easily missable detail that rewards only the most observant. In the best version, Polygon says, the vampires are always there, in the background, waiting for you to discover them — just like in real life.
