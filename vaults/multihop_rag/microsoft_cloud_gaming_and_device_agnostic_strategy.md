# Microsoft's Cloud Gaming And Device-Agnostic Strategy

With its studios at its back, Microsoft is leaning hard into cloud gaming while attempting to build a device-agnostic ecosystem powered by the Xbox brand.

Engadget reads the purpose of those moves as unlocking the mobile market even more, putting Xbox games on all devices, everywhere, all the time.
