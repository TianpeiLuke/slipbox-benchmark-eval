# Ange Postecoglou's Reaction And The End Of His 52-Match Unbeaten Home Run

Tottenham manager Ange Postecoglou suffered his first home defeat as a manager since he began a 52-match unbeaten home run in November 2020, a run that spanned his time at Yokohama F. Marinos, a two-year spell at Celtic and now Tottenham. He said he was proud of his players despite the loss.

Speaking to BBC post-match, Postecoglou said: "There was a fair bit going on, a lot of VAR intervention, a hard game for me to analyse but I am very proud of the players. Right to the end they were trying to create something for us." He added: "The players couldn't give any more than they did, part of this game is will, effort and determination and they showed that."
