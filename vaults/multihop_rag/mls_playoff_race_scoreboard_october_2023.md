# Around The League: The Eastern Conference Scoreboard That Squeezed Inter Miami

On the two October 2023 nights that decided Inter Miami's season, results elsewhere in the Eastern Conference ran against them. On Oct. 4, with 25 minutes to kickoff at Soldier Field, Sporting News noted the Chicago Fire were also fighting for a playoff spot, sitting 11th and able to jump into a postseason position with a victory — or with a draw plus outside help. Ten minutes before kickoff the other scoreboards were not going according to plan: Charlotte FC, 14th coming into the night, led Toronto FC 2-0, which for the moment jumped them above Inter Miami, and the New York Red Bulls were beating Eastern Conference leaders FC Cincinnati 2-0, a shocking result that momentarily lifted the Red Bulls above Chicago into 11th on 37 points.

Those matches finished badly for the chasing pack. The Red Bulls took a massive three points off FC Cincinnati with a 2-1 win, CF Montreal grabbed a last-gasp equalizer against the Houston Dynamo, and Charlotte FC pummeled Toronto FC 3-0 — not an ideal trio of results for others chasing a playoff spot.

Three days later, on Oct. 7, the most painful scoreline was again Montreal's. Inter Miami were nervous about results around the league, and CF Montreal's 2-0 lead over Portland meant they would be eliminated with anything less than a win; by the time Montreal led 3-0, Inter Miami had to win or they were officially out of the playoff race.
