# Jesper Bratt UNDER 0.5 Assists

Sporting News' third pick for the Nov. 7, 2023 NHL slate was Jesper Bratt, LW, Devils @ Avalanche — UNDER 0.5 assists, despite Bratt entering with seven goals and 11 assists in 11 games. The winger came firing out of the gate, but the outlook changed after New Jersey lost superstar center Jack Hughes to injury when he slammed into the boards against the Blues, forcing the Devils to change things on the fly.

Those changes hit Bratt directly, since he had been featured on the top line alongside Hughes. With fellow center Nico Hischier also out injured, the Devils were left fairly weak down the middle and trusting Michael McLeod to hold the fort between Bratt and Tyler Toffoli.

The recent evidence matches the structural argument: Bratt had been held without an assist in each of the last two games for a team missing a key contributor on offense, and the team would need him to look to score rather than play the role of playmaker. Until the Devils figure things out, the conclusion runs, this team cannot be trusted to do anything positive in the offensive zone.
