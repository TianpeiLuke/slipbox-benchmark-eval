# Winter 2023 Front Line Conditions and Russian Offensives

Weather conditions were deteriorating in Ukraine in December 2023, with mud, freezing rain, snow and ice making offensive and reconnaissance operations challenging. Intense fighting continued nonetheless, particularly around Bakhmut and Avdiivka in eastern Ukraine, where Russian forces were conducting offensive operations and had made some recent, confirmed advances.

Analysts at the Institute for the Study of War noted in the week before this 25 December report that Russian forces have likely committed to offensive operations in multiple sectors of the front, during a period of the most challenging weather of the fall-winter season, "in an effort to seize and retain the initiative" before the Russian presidential elections in March 2024. In the meantime, the ISW noted, "Ukrainian forces establish and consolidate defensive positions to conserve manpower and resources for future offensive efforts."

Ukrainian forces have adopted a more defensive stance as circumstances dictate, and a senior army general warned that week that front-line Ukrainian troops face artillery shortages and have scaled back some military operations because of a shortfall of foreign assistance.
