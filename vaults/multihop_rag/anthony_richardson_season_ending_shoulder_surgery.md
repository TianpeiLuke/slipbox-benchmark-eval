# Anthony Richardson's Season-Ending Shoulder Surgery

News that Indianapolis Colts rookie quarterback Anthony Richardson might undergo season-ending shoulder surgery could carry a silver lining, The New York Times reported on 16 October 2023.

The reasoning is that Richardson got to play enough for the Colts to evaluate where he stands, so if he shuts it down for the season, neither he nor the team would have to suffer through a full season of growing pains.

The counter-evidence is Gardner Minshew's three-pick game against Jacksonville that Sunday, which works against the idea that Indy is better positioned to compete with the journeyman in the lineup — though most coaches and execs the writer had spoken with believed that to be the case in the short term.
