# Pizza Tower

Pizza Tower, developed by Tour De Pizza for Windows PC, is ranked 38 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31. Polygon calls it a perfect object, fully committed to its vision.

You play as Peppino Spaghetti, a chef who must race up the pizza tower to defeat the existential threat posed by Pizzaface, an enormous floating pizza that also happens to be sentient. To get there you platform through a series of levels, picking up speed as you zoom through enemies and obstacles, and critic N. Clark says it is easy to get into a flow state.

The game also beautifully captures the essence of the Wario Land series. It is delightful to look at, with an irreverent art style referential to late-'90s and early-2000s cartoons and absurd enemies and animations, and its levels are chock-full of secret rooms, passageways, and treasures, making it hard to put down and fun to replay.
