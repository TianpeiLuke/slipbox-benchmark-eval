# Blackhawks And Penguins Opening Night Lineups, October 2023

Chicago's opening-night lineup against the Penguins, posted at 7:58 p.m. on Oct. 10, 2023, put Connor Bedard on a line with Taylor Hall and Ryan Donato, with Petr Mrazek in goal.

The full Blackhawks lineup ran Hall-Bedard-Donato; T. Johnson-Reichel-Raddysh; Katchouk-Guttman-Athanasiou; and Perry-Dickinson-Foligno up front, with defence pairs Vlasic-Jones, Korchinski-Murphy and Tinordi-Kaiser, goaltenders Mrazek and Söderblom, and extras Entwistle, R. Johnson and Zaitsev.

Bedard was not the only rookie debuting: at 7:50 p.m. the blog noted Blackhawks defenceman Kevin Korchinski was playing his first NHL game, having been the No. 7 overall pick in the 2022 draft — one of three first-round selections by Chicago that year — after a junior career with the Seattle Thunderbirds of the WHL. On the Pittsburgh side, at 6:45 p.m., the blog flagged a new Penguin: Erik Karlsson, who won his third Norris Trophy last season, was brought in that summer in a trade with the Sharks, giving Pittsburgh a talented blue-line duo of Kris Letang and Karlsson, although the two likely would not be paired together.
