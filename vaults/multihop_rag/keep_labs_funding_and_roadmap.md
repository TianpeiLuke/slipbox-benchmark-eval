# Keep Labs' Funding And Second-Gen Roadmap

With its first product shipping to consumers, Keep Labs was exploring raising capital at the end of 2023 or early 2024, having raised $2.18 million to that point from venture capital sources including StartUp Health's Transformer Fund, Plug and Play Tech Center, Onbelay Capital and 001 Ventures. Angels have helped Keep Labs along the way too, and the company's advisory board includes former IBM Watson head Michael Rhodin. TechCrunch reported this on 4 October 2023.

The near-term roadmap has two named items. The first is commercial: "We've got a lot of contracts we'll be announcing over the next several months, which is super exciting," Wilkins said. The second is a product refresh — "And then after that, it's the launch of our second-gen product, which is the same as the original, except it's got a cooling device."

The rationale offered for adding cooling is the composition of the drug pipeline plus direct demand. Wilkins explained that nearly 50% of the drugs in the FDA pipeline require cooling: "Ozempic is a great example, and people are keeping it in their fridge, next to their butter, and we've heard from customers and enterprise partners that [adding cooling] could be a huge win." Until the second generation arrives, the first-generation product is available from Keep Labs' website, comes in black and white, and costs $299.
