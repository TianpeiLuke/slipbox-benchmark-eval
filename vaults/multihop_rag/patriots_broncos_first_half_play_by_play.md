# Patriots-Broncos First Half Play By Play

Denver led New England 7-3 at halftime of the Christmas Eve 2023 game, a low-scoring first half filled with missed opportunities for both teams.

The live blog logged the half in order: at 8:17 p.m. Bailey Zappe pump faked and fumbled the ball, immediately giving the Broncos offense a chance; at 8:19 p.m. the two teams nearly traded turnovers as Russell Wilson appeared to be intercepted in the end zone, but it was ruled an incomplete pass; at 8:21 p.m. the Broncos went for it on fourth down and came up short, so Zappe's fumble proved inconsequential; at 8:36 p.m. Marvin Mims Jr., a huge playmaker that season, proved it again with a 52-yard return; at 8:40 p.m. the Broncos posted the first score of the night with a 3-yard rush by Javonte Williams; at 8:54 p.m. the Patriots put their first points on the board with a 33-yard field goal from Chad Ryland; at 9:12 p.m. McLaughlin took it to the Denver 37 for a gain of 7 yards as the Patriots looked to get within scoring range for the second time of the evening; at 9:25 p.m. Broncos wide receiver Courtland Sutton was evaluated for a possible concussion, which the team officially announced as being evaluated for a "possible head injury"; at 9:30 p.m. Ryland's field goal try from 47 yards sailed wide right; and at 9:35 p.m. Lutz's 57-yard field goal attempt came up short for the Broncos, ending the half.
