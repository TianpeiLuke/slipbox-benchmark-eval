# Synapse Laid Off 86 People, About 40% Of The Company

Banking-as-a-service startup Synapse confirmed on Friday that it had laid off 86 people, or about 40% of the company.

The San Francisco–based company operates a platform enabling banks and fintech companies to easily develop financial services, and it has been open about past layoffs. In June, CEO Sankaet Pathak wrote in a blog post that the company had let go of 18% of its workforce, because "the current macroeconomic conditions" had begun to impact its clients and platforms, affecting its anticipated growth.
