# Against Messi's MLS MVP And Newcomer Nominations: The League Minutes Do Not Support Them

Sporting News argued on Oct. 7, 2023 that Lionel Messi's nominations for both 2023 MLS Newcomer of the Year and 2023 MLS MVP were unwarranted, attacking the premise that his impact had come in the league at all. Reported 75 minutes before kickoff, the nominations were called a blatant PR move that "laughable doesn't even begin to describe."

The grounds for the objection are the split between competitions: most of the damage Messi had done for Inter Miami came in Cup competitions, and in actual MLS competition he had one goal and two assists in 247 minutes of play. The conclusion drawn was that Messi had been fun, but that the league should not make a mockery of itself by actually giving him either honor.
