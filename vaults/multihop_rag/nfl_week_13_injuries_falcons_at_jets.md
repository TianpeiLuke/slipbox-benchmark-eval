# NFL Week 13 Injuries Falcons At Jets

In the Week 13 final injury reports published by CBSSports.com on Dec. 1, 2023, with the Falcons listed at -2 at the Jets, Atlanta was relatively healthy coming into the matchup while New York would again be without Aaron Rodgers.

Hollins, who injured his ankle in Week 9, participated on a limited basis every day that week, which was encouraging for his prospects to play on Sunday; Hughes practiced fully all week after suffering a hand injury the previous weekend against the Saints; and linebacker Nate Landman (thigh) missed practice on Wednesday but would play vs. New York. Rodgers was once again limited in practice but, as expected, would not be activated that week and would be sidelined as he continued his recovery from a torn Achilles suffered just a few months earlier. Cook popped up on the injury report due to a shoulder injury, but head coach Robert Saleh told reporters Friday that the back should be fine.
