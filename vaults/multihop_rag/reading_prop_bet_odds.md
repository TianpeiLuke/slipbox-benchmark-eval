# Reading Prop Bet Odds

Prop bets at US sportsbooks use the same odds as other major bet types — moneylines, point spreads and totals — so reading them is the same job as reading American odds, which use negative and positive numbers to show how much you stand to win. First, check the sign. Second, if the number is positive, read it as how much you stand to win for every $100 wagered: wager on a quarterback to have over 2.5 touchdowns at +145 and he throws three, and a $100 stake pays $245 — your $100 back plus $145 in winnings. Third, if the number is negative, read it as how much you need to wager to win $100: bet a player to score under 15.5 points in a basketball game at -135 and you would need to bet $135 to win $100.

The $100 figure is a reference unit, not a requirement. You do not have to wager that high or that low, because your payout is calculated proportionally to your wager size when you finalize your bet slip.
