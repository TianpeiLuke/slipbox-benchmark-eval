# Diablo 4 Season of Blood Release Date

All of Diablo 4's season 2 changes, as well as the content for Season of Blood, come to the game on Oct. 17 — just a few weeks before fans expect Blizzard to announce Diablo 4's first expansion at BlizzCon 2023. Polygon reported this on 2023-10-04.

Game director Joe Shely and associate game director Joe Piepiora told Polygon the focus on quality-of-life upgrades was largely due to season 2 being the first season where they and the rest of the Diablo 4 team could really listen to feedback.

The reason is timing: because Season of the Malignant launched just over a month after the release of Diablo 4, that season's content was largely finished before players had really gotten to dive into the action-RPG. With season 2, Blizzard has been able to react more to player feedback and finally create some sweeping, specific changes that players had been requesting.
