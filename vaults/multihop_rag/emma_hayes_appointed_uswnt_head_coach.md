# Emma Hayes Appointed USWNT Head Coach

U.S. Soccer made Emma Hayes' appointment as head coach of the U.S. women's national team official on Tuesday 14 November 2023, making her the 10th full-time coach of the programme, as reported by The New York Times on 15 November 2023.

The sequence began on 4 November, when Chelsea Women released a statement saying their coach was leaving at the end of the season to "pursue a new opportunity outside of the Women's Super League and club football" — Hayes had just entered her second decade in charge, and few knew where she would land. That same day The Athletic, among others, reported that her next job would be with the U.S. women's national team, leading a four-time World Cup and Olympic gold medal-winning programme into a new era. Hayes, who previously won six WSL titles in England, will not take charge until her final season with Chelsea is complete the following May. Sporting director Matt Crocker made the final decision to hire her after a search process that began in August, following the team's surprising exit in the round of 16 at the World Cup and the subsequent departure of head coach Vlatko Andonovski. Though Hayes is seen as one of the world's top coaches in women's soccer, the article notes the appointment still comes as something of a surprise.

Both sides gave statements. U.S. Soccer president Cindy Parlow Cone said: "She has tremendous energy and an insatiable will to win. Her experience in the USA, her understanding of our soccer landscape and her appreciation of what it means to coach this team makes her a natural fit for this role and we could not be more pleased to have her leading our women's national team forward." Hayes said: "This is a huge honor to be given the opportunity to coach the most incredible team in world football history. The feelings and connection I have for this team and for this country run deep. I've dreamed about coaching the USA for a long time so to get this opportunity is a dream come true. I know there is work to do to achieve our goals of winning consistently at the highest levels. To get there, it will require dedication, devotion and collaboration from the players, staff and everyone at the U.S. Soccer Federation."
