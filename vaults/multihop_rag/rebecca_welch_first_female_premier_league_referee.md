# Rebecca Welch As The First Female Premier League Referee

Rebecca Welch became the first female referee to take charge of an English Premier League match on 23 December 2023, officiating Fulham's home game against Burnley, as reported by The Roar that day.

The match itself ended in a deserved 2-0 victory for Burnley, who took all three points at Fulham. Wilson Odobert and Sander Berge scored the goals, lifting Burnley off the bottom of the table.
