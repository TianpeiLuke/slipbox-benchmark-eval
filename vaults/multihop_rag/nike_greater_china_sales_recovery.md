# Nike Greater China Sales Recovery

Nike's sales in China grew by 5% compared with the year-ago period to $1.7 billion in its fiscal first quarter ended 31 August 2023, falling short of the $1.8 billion analysts had expected, according to StreetAccount.

The comparison base matters: during the previous quarter ended 31 May, Nike saw China sales jump 16% year-on-year, but those numbers were against easy comparisons because the region was still under Covid-related lockdown orders during the prior year. While Nike remains bullish on China, the region's economic recovery has so far been a mixed bag — following a sluggish July, retail sales picked up during August to rise 4.6% compared with the prior year, beating expectations of 3% growth forecast by Reuters.

CEO John Donahoe, who said he had travelled to China twice in the previous four months, expressed confidence: "We feel good about the market there and our position. Frankly, a couple things stand out. One, sport is back in China, you can just feel it, and that gives us great confidence about the future and the Chinese consumer in our segment, regardless of the macroeconomic outlook there."
