# The Woman In Me: Britney Spears' Memoir

The Woman in Me is Britney Spears' memoir, released on 24 October 2023 in both the US and the UK. Its publisher's press release describes it as "a brave and astonishingly moving story about freedom, fame, motherhood, survival, faith, and hope." The book is expected to cover the pivotal moments of Spears' life, including the high-profile legal battle to end her conservatorship, which was dissolved in November 2021 after 13 years.

Ahead of publication, the main point of anticipation was what the book would say about Spears' private life. Because she spent the 1990s and 2000s in heavily publicised romances, fans expected her to detail those relationships in the memoir, and in particular to share new material about her second husband, Kevin Federline.
