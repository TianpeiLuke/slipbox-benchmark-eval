# The Case For Simplicity Over Maximized Rewards

The Fifth Third 1.67% Card is worth it for anyone who values simplicity above all else: it has a solid return rate for all purchases and, because it doesn't incur an annual fee, you're guaranteed to make a profit — as long as you stay away from other fees like APR charges and cash advances.

The argument concedes the comparison it is up against. Many other credit card rewards programs have a higher potential value than this card: earning airline miles, hotel points, or transferable rewards that can be converted to travel loyalty programs can literally be worth exponentially more. The caveat that carries the argument is that those programs take a lot of research and experimentation to maximize.

The card is therefore aimed at those who don't have a master's degree in credit card rewards and aren't interested in putting in the effort. With it you can guarantee a solid return every time you swipe, with no catches — except perhaps the requirement of a Fifth Third checking or savings account to qualify.
