# Gogoro's Indian Commercial Launch And Investments

In early December 2023 Gogoro made its first commercial entry into India, introducing its battery-swapping network and smartscooters after running pilots and investing millions of dollars in the country. Speaking on the sidelines of the company's event, CEO Horace Luke made clear his ambitions stretch beyond this initial debut.

The launch followed a series of earlier India announcements: an early battery-swapping pilot in Delhi; a partnership with Belrise Industries to invest a total of $2.5 billion in Maharashtra to build its battery-swapping infrastructure and network in the western peninsular state; and a $25 million investment in electric fleet management startup Zypp Electric.

Luke said Gogoro is in talks with various local and global players to expand its business and presence in India, without sharing names. The company is already working with domestic manufacturers to produce its components locally, which it currently assembles at a facility in Maharashtra in partnership with Foxconn, and it is also looking to partner with Indian and global automobile manufacturers who can deploy its technology.
