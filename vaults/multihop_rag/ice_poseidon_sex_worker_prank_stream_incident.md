# The Ice Poseidon Sex Worker "Prank" Stream Incident

Paul Denino, a creator also known as Ice Poseidon, streamed an encounter with a sex worker on September 21, 2023, in which she was filmed without being told that other people were hidden in an adjacent room.

The interactions with the sex worker took place in a living room, while Denino and fellow streamer Sam Pepper hid in an adjacent bedroom. The stream shows another man, named Andy, paying the sex worker $500 and asking for her consent to film. When the encounter became physical, Denino and Pepper made noises behind the door, which startled the woman. She tried to leave after receiving a text about the people in the other room, but Andy stopped her. When Andy reminded her that he had already paid her, she said the encounter was "creepy." "Why is it creepy?" Andy said. "It's you and me and no one else."
