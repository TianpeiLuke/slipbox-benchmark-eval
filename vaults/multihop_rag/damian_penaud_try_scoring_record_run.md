# Damian Penaud's Try-Scoring Record Run

Damian Penaud's two tries against Italy on 6 October 2023 took him to second overall on France's men's try-scorers' chart with 35, three behind Serge Blanco, in a run that had seen the wing score in seven straight matches and 14 times that year.

The Guardian's judgment was that if the predatory Penaud's progress is a marker of France's hopes, their fellow title contenders should be worried; as a whole, the French machine was tuning up satisfactorily with top spot in Pool A secured, and a potential last-eight collision with Ireland or South Africa, or indeed Scotland, would be a monumental affair.

Galthié spread the credit: "It's easy to congratulate Damian for his attacking performance. But it's also easy when you have a team like that working for you. Players that become great are served by their teams; there's always a balance." The former France scrum-half added: "We're happy to qualify … We'll recuperate together, celebrate qualification, then turn to the quarter-final. For us, after a World Cup final against New Zealand [on opening night], our game next Sunday will be a second final."
