# Jonathan Smith's Oregon State Tenure And Coaching Path

Jonathan Smith narrowly finished below .500 across six seasons at the helm in Corvallis, Oregon, but the Beavers made clear progress during the back half of his tenure, as CBS Sports recounted on 2023-11-25. Smith guided Oregon State to seven victories in 2021 after the program went 9-22 during his first three years. The 2022 season — the Beavers' first 10-win season since 2006 and their highest AP Top 25 finish since 2000 — resulted in a contract extension for Smith through February 2029 that bumped his annual salary to $4.85 million. Oregon State then started the 2023 season 8-2 before dropping back-to-back games against Washington and Oregon.

His path to that job ran through multiple assistant jobs from 2002-17 before he returned to his alma mater as coach in 2018. Smith spent six seasons as an assistant under former college football coach Chris Petersen, first as Boise State's quarterbacks coach from 2012-13 and then as Washington's quarterbacks coach and offensive coordinator from 2014-17. His time in Seattle included a Pac-12 title and a College Football Playoff berth for Washington during the 2016 season.
