# Hydrogel Zinc-Iodine Battery Cycling Results

Testing their aqueous hydrogel-modified zinc-iodine battery, the team found the decay rate was ultralow, with energy storage capacity retained to an outstanding level over the course of 18,000 discharge and recharge cycles — outstripping the stability of currently available zinc-iodine batteries.

The test battery also delivered some surprising results. "The adhesion between the polyanionic and polycationic hydrogel components is remarkably robust, effectively mitigating the swelling of the hydrogel even in the absence of a cellulose paper scaffold," Hongjin Fan explained. "The unexpectedly strong iodine adsorption capability of the positively charged hydrogel has led to a striking and rapid color transformation, exceeding our initial expectations."
