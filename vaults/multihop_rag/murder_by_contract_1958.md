# Murder By Contract (1958)

Irving Lerner's Murder by Contract is a lean, mean noir that had a big influence on Martin Scorsese, who has listed it among his guilty pleasure movies and called it the film that influenced him the most, especially in Taxi Driver. It streams free with ads on Tubi.

Scorsese's stated reason is economy: "The film puts us all to shame with its economy of style," he wrote, adding that "Murder By Contract was a favorite of neighborhood guys who didn't know anything about movies. They just liked the film because they recognized something unique about it." In the film, Vince Edwards plays a methodical hitman asked to kill a witness in a high-profile case; when he finds out his target is a woman he bucks — not on moral grounds, but because women are harder to kill, being in his view unpredictable. Polygon's Pete Volk credits Lerner with directing masterfully on a minimal budget: a tight 81 minutes, aided by Edwards' fantastic lead performance and a great all-guitar score from Perry Botkin.
