# Fifth Third 1.67% Card Rates And Fees

The Fifth Third 1.67% Card comes with a 20.74%–29.99% variable APR. Its introductory offer is a 0% introductory APR on balance transfers for the first 12 billing cycles after account opening, after which the APR will be 20.74%–29.99% based on creditworthiness. The purchase APR is 20.74%–29.99% variable; the balance transfer APR is 20.74%–29.99% variable plus a fee of $5 or 4% of the transferred amount, whichever is greater; and the cash advance APR is 29.99% plus $10 or 5% of the amount of the advance, whichever is greater.

The fee schedule is otherwise light: a $0 annual fee, a $0 foreign transaction fee, a convenience check fee of either $5 or 4% of the amount of each check (whichever is greater), a late payment fee of up to $40, and a minimum interest charge of $1.50.
