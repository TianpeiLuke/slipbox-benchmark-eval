# Thai Food Near Me's Customer Experience

For a group The Verge's reporter spoke with at Thai Food Near Me on an evening in August, the SEO plan had — at least practically — the intended effect. One of the diners, Travis, found the restaurant on Yelp when searching for nearby dining options after missing an earlier reservation elsewhere; several people in the group had heard about the restaurant when it went viral in the spring but had not realized it was in New York. The group agreed the food was good, even though they had not known what to expect, and said they would definitely consider coming back if they were in the area.

The channel, though, was not the one the owners planned for. Co-owner Robert Sampson says that for the first several months of being open, most people were coming in after seeing Twitter and Reddit posts, not Google results, while others would walk by outside, do a double take at the sign, and come inside. "After you get that initial bunch, you've got people who enjoyed their dining experience here," he says. "They'll tell all their friends and family if they liked it, and then those people come from word of mouth."

Despite the admittedly silly name, the reporter found it clear that co-owners Jirapraphanan and Sampson care about what they are serving: the restaurant is clean and stylish, and the menu, carefully designed by Jirapraphanan, features original dishes she had not seen at other places; her food was tasty, came out promptly, and she said she too would note the spot if she were in the area again and craving Thai food. Jirapraphanan wants people to come back because they actually like the food — the name is part, but not all, of the branding. "I want to be different from other Thai restaurants," he says. "I think it did more than we could have ever imagined it would do," Sampson says. The restaurant's owners are Jirapraphanan, Sampson, Natkitta Kornwaewwongcharoen, and Nat P Akethamwong.
