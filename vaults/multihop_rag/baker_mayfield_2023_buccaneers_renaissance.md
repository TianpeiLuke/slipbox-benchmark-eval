# Baker Mayfield's Buccaneers Renaissance

Baker Mayfield entered the Buccaneers' Week 15 matchup with the Packers on Dec. 17, 2023 with a hair under 3,000 yards and 20 touchdowns to just eight interceptions, having quietly emerged as one of the league's best passers while leading Tampa Bay to the top of the NFC South.

The veteran hurler turned back the clock with ruthless efficiency in a campaign Sporting News called a continuing renaissance. Mayfield had shown off his arm talent in years past — he had hit the 300-yard mark on 12 separate occasions and completed at least 75% of his passes 10 other times — but he had rarely done both together, nor provided such a viable scoring punch in those matchups: that Sunday represented just the fourth time he had ever notched at least four touchdown throws. It was just the sort of act the Buccaneers were craving, and it meant so much more for a player whose time in the spotlight had looked to be waning only one year earlier — not bad for an undersized sidewinder who had looked to be approaching the end of his rope as a starting quarterback a year prior. As the article put it, you cannot get better than perfect, and Mayfield was about as close to it as any quarterback has been in a road matchup.
