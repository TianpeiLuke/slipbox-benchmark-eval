# Current Fediverse Apps Mostly Clone Existing Platforms

As of December 2023, almost everything in the fediverse is a one-to-one competitor to an existing platform, and nearly all of these apps are differentiated only in that they're federated — what The Verge calls the "popular app, but federated" phase of the transition.

The examples: Mastodon deliberately looks and feels like Twitter, as do Firefish, Pleroma, GoToSocial and others, since the fediverse is super into replacing Twitter; Pixelfed is Instagram through and through; Lemmy's features are virtually all ripped straight from Reddit; PeerTube maps to YouTube, Friendica to Facebook, BookWyrm to Goodreads, on and on. The article allows that some of these apps are very good, and that a few platforms built on ActivityPub — apps like Mammoth and Ivory — are showing the potential for different user experiences on top of the same data and infrastructure. It also rates the current phase as definitely better than the "popular app, but blockchain" phase of a couple of years ago, "but not by much." A caption notes that Mastodon is the biggest thing in the fediverse so far, but may not be for long; the image is credited to Mastodon.
