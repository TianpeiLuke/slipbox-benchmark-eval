# Coinbase's SEC Standoff And The Call For Regulatory Clarity

Brian Armstrong, CEO of Coinbase, slammed the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission and said the cryptocurrency exchange is looking to invest more outside of the U.S. A Coinbase spokesperson declined to comment for the CNBC story published 30 November 2023.

Armstrong told CNBC's Joumanna Bercetche earlier that week that the Binance settlement allows the crypto industry to move past a spate of scandals: "The enforcement action against Binance, that's allowing us to kind of turn the page on that and hopefully close that chapter of history," he said, adding, "I think that regulatory clarity is going to help bring in more investment, especially from institutions."
