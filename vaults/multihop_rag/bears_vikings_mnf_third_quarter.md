# The Third Quarter Of Bears 12, Vikings 10

Chicago led 6-3 at the end of the third quarter on Nov. 27, 2023, having added a field goal while Minnesota gave the ball away again. Alexander Mattison, who had drawn the ire of Vikings fans all season, started the second half with a booming 21-yard run, but the Bears answered immediately by sacking Dobbs with Montez Sweat and Jaquan Brisker taking him down. Kevin O'Connell then went for it on 4th & 7 and Hockenson gained only six; the play looked good but the Bears' defense looked better, and Chicago took over with favorable field position.

The Bears converted that into points, though not efficiently. Roschon Johnson, impressive in spurts, picked up a first down to keep the drive alive, Fields ran up the middle for another, and then a questionable third-down call had Fields throw behind the line of scrimmage to D.J. Moore for nothing — leaving Cairo Santos to extend the lead with a field goal at 9:58 p.m. for Bears 6, Vikings 3.

Minnesota's running game worked and its passing game did not: Mattison had 19 yards three plays into the next drive. A hold by Jaylon Johnson gifted the Vikings a first down, but they still could not capitalize, with Ty Chandler unable to make T.J. Edwards miss on third down, sending Minnesota to a fourth-down attempt. At 10:08 p.m. Dobbs threw his third interception of the night, deflected by Jaylon Johnson and caught by Edwards — leaving the Bears, interestingly, with less favorable field position than an incompletion would have given them. Chicago then started to look respectable on third down again, completing to Moore to move deeper into Vikings territory, though it might still need another first down to give Santos a fair shot.
