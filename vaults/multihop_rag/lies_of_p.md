# Lies of P

Lies of P, developed by Neowiz Games for Mac, PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, Windows PC, Xbox One, and Xbox Series X, is ranked 49 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31. Polygon concedes the elevator pitch is questionable — Dark Souls mixed with Pinocchio.

In the years leading up to the game, "Pinocchiosouls" was more of a running joke than anything, the profane idea that you can take any world and slap some Dark Souls into it to get people mildly interested. But once you are in the game, eliminating bosses left and right with your sweet parry moves, critic Ryan Gilliam says you quickly find yourself entirely unbothered by how strange it initially seemed, and start telling your partner things like "I have to go back to Geppetto to upgrade my puppet body" as though it were a perfectly normal task to assign yourself on a Tuesday afternoon.

Gilliam notes it is very rare for a Soulslike to ever feel like anything more than a knockoff, even when they are decent fun like The Surge, and his best compliment is that Lies of P feels like the genuine article — a FromSoftware game developed in an alternate dimension and somehow released in this one by mistake. But it was not a mistake or luck, and it was not FromSoftware either: it was a talented group of developers at Neowiz Games and Round8 Studio who took a tired genre, paired it with a bizarre IP, and knocked it out of the park.
