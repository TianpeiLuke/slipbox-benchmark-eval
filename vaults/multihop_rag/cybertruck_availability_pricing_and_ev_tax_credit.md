# Cybertruck Availability, Pricing And The EV Tax Credit

Cybertruck prices are all considerably higher than advertised back in 2019: the rear-wheel version starts at $60,990 — and is not available until 2025 — the all-wheel drive version starts at $79,990, and the Cyberbeast comes in just shy of $100,000.

Availability is the other constraint. Tesla expects to mass produce Cybertrucks starting in 2024, but unless you are a wealthy early adopter with special Tesla connections, securing one will not be easy early on: you need to get in line behind the people who reserved the truck as far back as 2019. Buyers may be able to jump ahead for a price — Cybertruck reservations have appeared on eBay for $10,000 — but TechCrunch advises caution, since it is unclear how easy it would be to verify whether the offers are legitimate, and Musk has threatened to punish resellers in the past.

Price also determines tax treatment. The federal tax credit for EVs has a number of eligibility requirements including price, and under current rules trucks and SUVs priced above $80,000 do not qualify for the $7,500 credit.
