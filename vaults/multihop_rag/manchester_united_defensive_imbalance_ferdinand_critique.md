# Manchester United's Defensive Imbalance: Ferdinand's Critique

Rio Ferdinand argued that Manchester United's European problems stem from a lack of balance in the team — they can score at will but make defensive mistakes that undo the game plan — and that the fix has to be applied over a period of games, not one or two, talkSPORT reported on 29 November 2023.

The grounds are the numbers and the pattern of the concessions. Eight points have been dropped from winning positions, and no other team has suffered a worse fate that term. United had conceded 14 goals in the Champions League group stages, a new and unwanted record for the club that could get worse when they faced Bayern in the final match, while their 12 goals scored was the fifth-highest tally of any club — so the downfall was defensive. "I think they've got to look at themselves," said Ferdinand, a 2007/08 Champions League winner with United. "You can't go into a Champions League campaign and score nine goals in three away games and have one point at the end of it. I think that shows you that there's a lack of balance in the team at the moment. Yes they can get goals and yes they are attacking at will and creating chances, many of them coming through Bruno. But I think defensively, they just made mistakes, I think the goalkeeper today has to hold his hands up and say, 'Yep, I was at fault for a couple of goals. He should do better."

The warrant is that the tactical work was sound and the errors, not the plan, cost the points — and that repeated collapses leave a psychological residue. "I think the manager will be scratching his head and thinking, 'The game plan first-half worked perfectly,' and then all of a sudden they score a goal, it changes the dynamic of the game. They were 2-0 up, 2-1, and you're thinking, 'Wow, I set the team up perfectly well, they execute it but then a mistake cost us,' and at 3-1, the same again. So I think there's a lot of question marks in the performances and the way they're going about things at the moment but balance for me is one of the key aspects that they've got to really find." He continued: "We mentioned in commentary, scars from throwing away leads, those are hard to get away, you've got to really manage games and he mentioned that himself Bruno, you've got to go out managing games correctly. They've got to do that not over one or two or three games, they've got to do that over a period of games now so they can erase those scars that they've created and they'e grown through bad results and throwing away leads, especially in Europe."
