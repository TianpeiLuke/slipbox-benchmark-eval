# Reading American Odds For NBA Props

To read the positive and negative numbers at US sportsbooks, take the sign first: negative numbers, such as -110, represent how much money you need to wager to win $100, while positive numbers, like +145, show how much you stand to win for every $100 you wager.

Work a negative price like this. If you bet on LeBron James scoring over 15.5 points in a contest at -125 odds, you would need to wager $125 to win $100; if the bet was correct you would take home $225 — your original $125 stake returned plus $100 in profit. In many cases, such as a moneyline bet, the negative number also indicates which side of the bet is the favorite to win.

Work a positive price the same way from the other direction. If you bet $50 on Giannis Antetokounmpo to have over 7.5 rebounds and the odds were +130 when you finalized your bet slip, you would win $65 in profit if the bet wins. Positive numbers also indicate which side of the bet the oddsmakers see as the underdog.

Two conditions bound the calculation: you can bet as little or as much as you want, and your payout is calculated based on your wager amount and the odds at the time you make your bet — not the odds later.
