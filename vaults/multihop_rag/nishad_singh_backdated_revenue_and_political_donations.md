# Nishad Singh's Backdated Revenue And Political Donations

Nishad Singh testified that he spruced up FTX's balance sheet with backdated transactions in December 2021 to push FTX's revenue "over the line" to $1 billion. By the time he found out about the use of customer money, in 2022, he was already compromised.

The falsification was not confined to the books. In Singh's case, The Verge reported on October 26th, 2023, it was also the American political system: by coordinating with Gabe Bankman-Fried, Singh had funneled money to a number of political causes, and that money came from "loans" that were not really loans, since he had not signed any term sheets.

Bankman-Fried's account on the stand distanced him from the backdating. He testified that he definitely did not know that Singh, his employee, had backdated interest payments to get FTX over the line to $1 billion — he had merely asked his employees to check whether there was any source of funds that was missing to get to $1 billion. The Verge's reporter described this testimony as especially rambling.
