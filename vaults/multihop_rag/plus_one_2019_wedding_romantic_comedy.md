# Plus One (2019): The Wedding-Season Romantic Comedy

Plus One is a 2019 romantic comedy directed by Jeff Chan and Andrew Rhymer, running 1h 39m, starring Maya Erskine (Pen15) and Jack Quaid (The Boys) as best friends who have watched all their friends get married and become each other's go-to plus ones for the endless marathon of nuptials. The film comes from the Pen15 writing team and premiered at the Tribeca Film Festival before quietly settling onto streaming, where Polygon says it has been largely overlooked.

The reviewer's argument for it is that Netflix may be cranking out romantic comedies but the best still come from a more personal, filmmaker-driven place: here the formula works to the movie's advantage, finding sweet humour in modern situations and wringing Erskine and Quaid for every drip of charisma they have to offer — a gem that could easily be mistaken for product in the current era of rom-coms. It is available free with a library card on Hoopla, free with ads on Tubi, or for digital rental or purchase at Amazon, Apple and Google Play.
