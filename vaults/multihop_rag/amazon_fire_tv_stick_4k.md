# Amazon Fire TV Stick 4K

The previous generation of Fire TV Stick 4K dropped to $23 for October Prime Day and was one of the few Amazon devices still on sale after the event. It has the same processor, RAM and storage as the standard Fire TV Stick, but it can stream 4K HDR content in all its glory; it also supports Dolby Vision HDR and Dolby Atmos, and a picture-in-picture live view feature can show feeds from compatible security cameras right on your TV screen.

The distinction between models matters. The newly refreshed Fire TV Stick 4K was not on sale in October but has a faster processor and supports Wi-Fi 6.

By Black Friday the new model was discounted too: the Fire TV Stick 4K (2023) was $25, down from $50 — 50 percent off. It streams in 4K resolution, as its name suggests, and features mostly standard specs these days like Wi-Fi 6, Dolby Atmos and Vision, HDR10 Plus, and a 1.7GHz processor.
