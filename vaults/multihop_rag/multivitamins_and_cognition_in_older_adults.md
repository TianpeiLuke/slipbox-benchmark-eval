# Multivitamins And Cognition In Older Adults

A recent Columbia University study involving 2,200 adults aged 60 and over found that an inexpensive multivitamin formulated specifically for older people improved cognition, memory and focus, The Guardian reported on 10 October 2023.

The results were so marked that the researchers said the supplement could slow age-related memory decline by three years.

The article frames this as an option even where parents do not require intensive "parenting": a simple supplement could help.
