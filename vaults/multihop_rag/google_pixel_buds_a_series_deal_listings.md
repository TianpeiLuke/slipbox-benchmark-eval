# Google Pixel Buds A-Series Deal Listings

The Google Pixel Buds A-Series appear in The Verge's December roundup at $83.34 ($17 off) at Amazon — a far cry from their all-time low of $58.98 — and in WIRED's October pre-Black Friday list as its top overall earbuds pick (8/10, WIRED Recommends).

The Verge describes them as Google's cheaper earbuds, offering good sound and voice call quality plus perks like Google Assistant and an IPX4 rating for sweat and water resistance, with the tradeoffs of a cheaper set: no noise cancellation, no wireless charging, and more proneness to the occasional connection hiccup. Its listing shows $83 against $100, 17 percent off, describing good comfort, impressive audio quality, and hands-free Google Assistant voice controls for under $100. WIRED says that for nearly everyone — even some iPhone users — these check every box: up to five hours of battery life plus an extra 19 hours via recharging in the carrying case, IPX4 water resistance for workouts, and nearly instant pairing with Android devices; it calls the October price a solid deal but notes they dip to $59 during major sale events, so it might be worth waiting for Black Friday.
