# Netflix Games, November 2023

Two titles joined Netflix Games in November 2023: Slayaway Camp 2: Netflix & Kill and Dead Cells: Netflix Edition.

Netflix's own descriptions, reproduced by Polygon, run as follows. For Slayaway Camp 2: "Leave no survivors. In this sequel, you're Skullface, a killer stuck inside a retro horror movie catalog — solve puzzles, stalk victims and get stabby." For Dead Cells: Netflix Edition: "You're a pile of undead sludge. Find a body, uncover secrets and defeat bosses to escape a deserted castle. If you die, restart and reignite the fight."
