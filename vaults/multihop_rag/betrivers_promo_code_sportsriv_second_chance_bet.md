# BetRivers Promo Code SPORTSRIV Second Chance Bet

BetRivers promo code SPORTSRIV gives new users a 2nd chance bet of up to $500, with the exact cap depending on the user's state — promoted by Sporting News on Nov. 29, 2023 for college basketball games including Duke vs. Arkansas.

The mechanism is a refund in bonus bets rather than cash: if a user's first bet settles as a loss, BetRivers refunds the account in bonus bets. Sporting News' worked example is a $100 bet on Duke that fails to cover against Arkansas, after which BetRivers credits the account with $100 in bonus bets.
