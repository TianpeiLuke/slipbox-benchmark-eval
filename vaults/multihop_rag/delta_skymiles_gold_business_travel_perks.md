# Delta SkyMiles Gold Business Card Travel Perks

The card's travel perks are a free first checked bag, Main Cabin 1 priority boarding, an annual flight credit, 20% back on in-flight purchases, baggage insurance, and car rental loss and damage insurance — several of which extend to travelling companions.

The free first checked bag covers cardholders and up to nine of their guests; because the standard bag fee is $30, using the perk more than three times saves more in bag fees than the annual fee costs. Main Cabin 1 priority boarding likewise covers cardholders and up to nine guests on the reservation, provided they use their SkyMiles number when booking their Delta flights. The flight credit is conditional on spend: cardholders who make eligible purchases totalling $10,000 or more in a year receive a $100 Delta eCredit usable for Delta flights. The 20% back arrives as a statement credit on eligible pre-purchased meals, in-flight food purchases, alcoholic beverages, and audio headsets on Delta flights. Baggage insurance covers cardholders who purchase plane, train, ship or bus ride tickets with the card, kicking in if baggage is stolen, lost, or damaged. Car Rental Loss and Damage Insurance requires the cardholder to book the rental with the card and decline the collision damage waiver at the agency. Terms apply to American Express benefits and offers, enrollment may be required for select benefits, eligibility and benefit level vary by card, and the insurance is underwritten by Amex Assurance Company.
