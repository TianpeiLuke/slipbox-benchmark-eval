# UTSA vs Marshall In The 2023 Scooter's Coffee Frisco Bowl

The UTSA Roadrunners (8-4) took on the Marshall Thundering Herd (6-6) on Tuesday night in the Scooter's Coffee Frisco Bowl, kicking off at 9 p.m. ET on ESPN, with the Roadrunners installed as large betting favorites on odds boards, per Sporting News on Dec. 18, 2023.

The two programs came in with contrasting bowl histories. The Roadrunners were in their 13th season as a program but had yet to win a bowl game; it was the fourth bowl game they had played in five years. Marshall was competing in a bowl game for the seventh straight year.

The teams had also been on different streaks as the year progressed: UTSA had won seven of its last eight games, while Marshall had lost six of its last eight. Sporting News presented live odds, team betting news, player props and a betting prediction for the matchup.
