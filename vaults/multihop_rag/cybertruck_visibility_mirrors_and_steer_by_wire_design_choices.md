# Cybertruck Visibility, Mirrors And Steer-By-Wire Design Choices

Beyond crumple zones, The Verge lists other Cybertruck design choices that make it a troubling addition to the roads: while the hood is sloped, the corner blind spots appear to be dangerous, and although Tesla added cameras to compensate for those blind spots, it's unclear what a driver will actually be able to see.

The steering is digital rather than mechanical — "steering by wire" — sideview mirrors can be removed, and there is no rearview mirror at all, so the only way to see what's beside or behind the truck is via the center console screen that also serves as the instrument panel, GPS mapping, Bluetooth controls, and radio. The exterior is "bullet-proof" steel with hardened Gorilla glass windows.
