# CFP Twelve-Team Expansion And The Sanctity Of The Regular Season

The New York Times argued on 3 December 2023 that the objection to expanding the College Football Playoff to 12 teams — that expansion would damage the sanctity of the regular season — is undercut by the 2023 selection itself: if regular-season games aren't going to matter when it comes to picking the final four, then there are no consequences for expanding to 12.

The grounds are what the selection revealed about the four-team system. The regular season didn't decide who made it that year — thirteen people did. The games mattered; the results didn't. That, the argument holds, is not what sports are supposed to be about.

The consequence the writer draws is about legacy: with the four-team CFP era ending after that season, fans will view it as a broken system that needed to be changed rather than the first frontier of the modernization of the sport.
