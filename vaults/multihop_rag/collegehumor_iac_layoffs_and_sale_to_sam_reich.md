# CollegeHumor's IAC Layoffs And Sale To Sam Reich

The creatives behind CollegeHumor announced a new subscription service in late 2018, made a few ambitious seasons of comedy and live-play Dungeons & Dragons, and then got walloped when their corporate owner, IAC, stopped funding CH Media, resulting in layoffs of nearly the entire staff in January 2020 — a month Polygon notes was a totally chill time to lose your job, with absolutely no major upheavals in television production on the horizon.

But CollegeHumor didn't drop off: IAC sold the brand to executive producer Sam Reich, allowing shows like Dimension 20, Um, Actually, and Game Changer to keep doing the work and spinning off their most successful formats on Dropout. As of Dropout's fifth anniversary in the week of this 26 September 2023 report, CollegeHumor is "the most healthy and sustainable [it's] ever been as a company," says Dimension 20 host Brennan Lee Mulligan — and for the anniversary Reich announced in a new video that the company will cease to be CollegeHumor at all.
