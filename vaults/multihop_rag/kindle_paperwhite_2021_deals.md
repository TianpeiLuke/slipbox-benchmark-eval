# Kindle Paperwhite 2021 Deals

Amazon's latest Kindle Paperwhite is its 11th-gen model, which comes with USB-C support, longer battery life, and a larger 6.8-inch display. The e-reader launched more than two years before The Verge's October 16th, 2023 roundup and often receives steep discounts at retailers like Amazon and Best Buy, particularly around Black Friday and throughout the holiday season; the last-gen model from 2018 can still occasionally be picked up at a discount. All 2021 models are similar to the 2018 model in featuring a waterproof design and Audible audiobook support, and the Kindle Paperwhite Signature Edition is identical to the standard model but adds wireless charging and a sensor that automatically adjusts the backlight.

Amazon has also rolled out a Kindle Paperwhite Kids Edition for $169.99, bundled with a kid-friendly cover, a two-year extended replacement guarantee, and a year of Amazon Kids Plus, much like the Kindle Kids. At the time of writing, Amazon and Best Buy were both selling the Kids Edition for $144.99, $25 off — a relatively minor discount, but likely the only one The Verge expected before the holiday.
