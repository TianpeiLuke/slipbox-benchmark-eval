# Britney Spears And David Lucado

Two months after ending her engagement to Jason Trawick, Britney Spears began dating David Lucado, who worked at a legal firm; she announced their split in August 2014 by tweeting "Ahhhh the single life!", and her representative confirmed to People: "Yes, she's single." The breakup news came as TMZ alleged that Lucado had cheated on Spears and claimed that her father had purchased a video of the infidelity incident. The publication later reported that Lucado denied the cheating allegations, claiming he had been seeing the other woman while he and Spears were broken up.

Despite that history, Lucado defended his ex in a 2016 interview with the New York Times about her conservatorship. He told the paper: "If anyone knew the real Britney, they would know that she would rather be remembered for being the great mother she is rather than the artist she is." The publication noted that he again denied the infidelity claims made by Spears' father, and he added: "And if anyone could see her interactions with her kids, they would know that there is no need for a conservatorship over Britney's personal life."
