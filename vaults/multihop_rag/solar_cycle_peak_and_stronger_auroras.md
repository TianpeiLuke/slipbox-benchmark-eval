# The Solar Cycle Peak and Stronger Auroras

The Northern Lights, or Aurora Borealis, were expected to be even stronger and brighter in the winter of 2023-24 than they have been in the past because of the sun's recent activity.

The evidence cited is sunspot observation. "Recent observations of sunspots, a measure of how active the sun is, show a dramatic increase from this time last year, and we are approaching the expected peak of the 11-year solar cycle in 2024 or 2025," New Scientist reported. "This means that over the next few months in the Northern Hemisphere, the Northern Lights will probably be stronger than they have been for at least a decade."

The practical implication drawn in this October 2023 account is a travel one: head north with a warm hat and mittens to watch the display vividly light up the sky, and now may be the time to pack your bags and get a close look at the dancing waves of light — the remaining question being where to go to capture the magic.
