# Canada Beat Jamaica 2-1 In The Nations League Quarterfinal First Leg

Canada beat Jamaica 2-1 in the CONCACAF Nations League quarterfinal first leg, with Stephen Eustaquio's 85th-minute strike delivering a first win in Jamaica for 35 years and a major step towards Copa America 2024 qualification. Jonathan David scored in first-half added time (45+2) and Eustaquio at 85; Shamar Nicholson equalised for Jamaica at 58. The match had been rearranged for Saturday after heavy rain flooded parts of Kingston's Independence Park on Friday, and Concacaf confirmed the full-time result on Nov. 18, 2023.

The goals came from moves down the flanks and a quick free kick. David capitalised on a skilful turn and cross from Cyle Larin to score his 26th international goal as part of a dominant first half in which Canada had four of the five attempts on target; Lille's striker had two promising early chances as well, poking a shot into Milan Borjan's arms under pressure from two markers and looping a header from an Alphonso Davies cross that forced the goalkeeper to backpedal and claim. Jamaica had gone closest before that, Aston Villa attacker Leon Bailey hitting the post from Bobby Decordova-Reid's corner. Nicholson, the Spartak Moscow forward, raced onto Daniel Johnson's quickly taken free kick and converted coolly 13 minutes after the break, and was aghast when the finish was initially ruled out for offside — a VAR intervention and a pitchside monitor check by referee Mary Penso justified Jamaica's celebrations in their technical area and kept alive their hope of extending an unbeaten run to six matches. Eustaquio's winner, his first international goal since 2021, came as he slotted in from Richie Laryea's surging run and low delivery, ending Canada's run of two consecutive defeats and giving them a second win in seven matches.

Canada's players framed it as a step, not an arrival. "It feels great to get our first win here in 35 years. It wasn't easy, far from it. Still a lot to be done but we're one step closer to where we want to go," one said via Canada Soccer, while another added: "It feels amazing. What the boys did today, in this heat and on this pitch, is unbelievable. We need to come out again and show the brotherhood in Toronto. We need a full stadium there to give us that support." Canada would seal their place at the Copa America if they avoided defeat in the second leg at Ontario's BMO Field on the Tuesday.
