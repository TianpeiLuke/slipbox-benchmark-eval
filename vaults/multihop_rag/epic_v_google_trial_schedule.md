# The Epic v. Google Trial Schedule

Judge Donato told jurors they were looking at roughly five weeks of trial starting November 6th and extending through early December.

The schedule suggests the court will take Thanksgiving off, finish looking at all the evidence by December 4th, take a couple more days off before closing arguments, and then let jurors come to a decision. "I fully expect we'll have plenty of time to reach a verdict well before the December holiday period," said Judge Donato.
