# Godzilla Minus One (Takashi Yamazaki)

Godzilla Minus One is Takashi Yamazaki's film starring Ryunosuke Kamiki, Minami Hamabe, and Yuki Yamada — the first Japanese live-action Godzilla film from Toho since the brilliant, post-Fukushima Shin Godzilla, and one that returns to the original recipe. Polygon ranked it 13 on its best movies of 2023 list of 2023-10-07, when it was in theaters.

Polygon contrasts it with the recent wave of Godzilla media, including the Apple TV series Monarch: those releases, while enjoyable, have taken a distinctly Hollywood approach, building out a mythology in the style of the MCU. Minus One instead is a throwback — to the post-WWII Tokyo setting, to 1950s human melodrama, and to a kaiju singularly focused on the obliteration of large, human-made structures.

Its hero, Koichi Shikishima, is a kamikaze pilot who, in the final days of the war, fakes a technical issue with his plane and takes shelter in a repair facility on a small island that happens to be in the path of a certain teenage lizard monster. Critic CP argues that how Shikishima builds relationships with a wide cast of lovable surrounding characters elevates the film above other giants: where Shin Godzilla chronicled a government response to disaster, Minus One pins its hopes on civilians who, failed by their leaders and the world, must rely on each other.
