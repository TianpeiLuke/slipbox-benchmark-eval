# Browns Survive Bears Game-Ending Hail Mary To Win 20-17

The Bears' prayers were nearly answered on a game-ending Hail Mary against Cleveland, but the ball deflected off Darnell Mooney and into the hands of Browns safety D'Anthony Bell, who made the catch and slid to the ground to end the game and seal Cleveland's 20-17 win, per Sporting News on Dec. 17, 2023.

Justin Fields and the Bears had the ball at the Cleveland 45 trailing by three with five seconds left on the clock. There was no time for a play and a field goal, since Chicago had no timeouts left to try for a shorter kick, so Chicago's only hope of winning the game — and of keeping its surging playoff push trending up — rested on a Hail Mary, a play so dubbed because it takes a miracle to succeed.

The Bears nearly had their miracle. The ball had the distance and was tipped in the air several times before beginning to fall into the arms of Bears receiver Darnell Mooney, who was falling toward the end zone turf; he had his hands on it as it began to fall into his chest. It then deflected off his hands and legs and ultimately into the waiting hands of Bell.
