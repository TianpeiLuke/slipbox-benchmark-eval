# Fallout From John Herdman's Departure From Canada Soccer

Two hours before the rescheduled quarterfinal, with the Canadian federation reeling from the departure of John Herdman, central midfielder Mark-Anthony Kaye said he was happy to see change at the top. Kaye had been left out of multiple squads towards the end of Herdman's tenure and believed his relationship with the former Canada head coach was the reason.

"Me and John had some really tough conversations where he blatantly told me I wasn't good enough to be part of the team. That was hard for me to accept," Kaye told Sportsnet. He had also posted an honest self-reflection on social media a week earlier following his New England Revolution's elimination from the MLS playoffs: "I will commit to being the catalyst that will set the tone for our group for every game, to help restore our club to where it deserves to be, I am confident of that. We will be back and better."
