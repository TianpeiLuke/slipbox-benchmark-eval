# Becker's Claim That Kyrgios Owes His Career To Tennis' Trailblazers

Boris Becker argued in December 2023 that Nick Kyrgios would not be making a living from tennis had it not been for the trail blazed by the stars of Becker's generation: the claim is that today's players owe their livelihoods to past professionals, and the warrant is that without those players the tennis circus would no longer exist at all.

Becker told Eurosport: "We live in a democracy. Everyone can think what he wants. You have to have respect for the past and simply say 'thank you' out loud that these old pros existed, because without them the tennis circus would no longer exist." He then named the generations he had in mind, listing himself among them only in passing: "I did play a bit in the 80s, but there was [Stefan] Edberg, [Mats] Wilander. The 90s were [Andre] Agassi, [Pete] Sampras. All these players make it possible for Nick Kyrgios not to play tennis at all this year and still be able to make a living from tennis."

The grounds are therefore specific rather than general: the evidence Becker offers is Kyrgios' own situation in 2023 — earning from the sport in a year he did not play — which Becker treats as proof that the commercial structure built by earlier generations, not current performance, is what sustains him.
