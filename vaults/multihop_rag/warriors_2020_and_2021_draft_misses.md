# Warriors 2020 And 2021 Draft Misses

Yardbarker argued on Nov. 29, 2023 that using three lottery picks and coming away with maybe one fringe rotation player would doom any franchise that does not have Steph Curry — the Warriors' 2020 and 2021 drafts being the easiest place to point.

The grounds are the specific picks passed over. During a two-year, injury-riddled drought that separated Finals runs, the Warriors in 2021 selected Kuminga one pick ahead of Franz Wagner and Moody over Alperen Sengun and Trey Murphy III; the year before, they drafted Wiseman over LaMelo Ball and Tyrese Haliburton. The piece notes that a certain Warriors beat reporter argued at the time for the better picks, and that there was something to be said for drafting the more NBA-ready players even at the risk of missing out on Wiseman's and Kuminga's ceilings. The concession that bounds the claim: it is hard to nail the draft when you are always selecting at 28 and 30 — the point holds specifically about the lottery picks.
