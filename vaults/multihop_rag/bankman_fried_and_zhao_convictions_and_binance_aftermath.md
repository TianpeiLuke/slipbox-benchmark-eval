# The Bankman-Fried And Zhao Convictions And Binance's Aftermath

Sam Bankman-Fried, who faces potential life behind bars, was scheduled to be sentenced in March, and Changpeng Zhao's sentencing was set for February; while guidelines suggest a sentence of 12 to 18 months, the Justice Department could push for a lengthier punishment for the Binance founder. Unlike FTX, which filed for bankruptcy in late 2022, Binance is still standing, though now without Zhao, who agreed to step down as CEO as part of the plea deal. Even before that the company was seeing a plunge in trading, with volume down by two-thirds between the first and third quarters of the year according to crypto analyst site CoinGecko. With assets of more than $65 billion on the platform Binance remains the world's largest crypto exchange globally, but its market share fell from over 60% in February to under 50% in September, which CoinGecko called "an indication that the exchange may be losing its grip on the industry as regulators continue to pressure it". In the first 24 hours after the Justice Department announced its $4.3 billion settlement with Binance, customers pulled more than $1 billion from the exchange, and liquidity dropped 25% in the immediate aftermath as market makers pulled back their positions, according to data provider Kaiko. A Binance spokesperson told CNBC that Zhao appeared in court "to protect our users and to ensure the longevity of our company", that "Binance's resilience has been tested unlike any other exchange around today", and that it continues to operate the world's largest cryptocurrency exchange by volume and currently sees a climbing percentage of institutional user transactions.

Coinbase is the fourth-biggest global exchange by daily volume according to CoinGecko, the only one publicly traded in the U.S., with a market cap of close to $30 billion. In a report to clients on the Wednesday, analysts at Mizuho noted that Coinbase shares are up about 20% since Zhao's settlement, a rally likely "in anticipation of potential share gains for COIN in wake of outflows from Binance, the industry's largest exchange". Coinbase shares fell 2.4% to $124.72 on the Thursday, wiping out some recent gains, and Mizuho raised its price target on the stock to $35 from $31 while keeping the underperform rating it has maintained since December.
