# Noah Gragson Stewart-Haas No. 10 Seat

The matter of Aric Almirola's replacement in the Stewart-Haas Racing No. 10 had been a rather poorly-kept secret, CBSSports.com reported on Dec. 7, 2023: it was widely expected the team would hire Noah Gragson as its new driver, as Gragson looked to reclaim his standing as a bright young star following a disastrous rookie year in Cup with Legacy Motor Club. Gragson had recently been spotted at Stewart-Haas Racing's shop, and he also drove a Ford in that past weekend's Snowball Derby with Rette Jones Racing.

Gragson did not let on to any 2024 plans in a recent conversation with Matt Weaver of Sportsnaut, instead choosing to discuss his regrets and personal growth from the end of his rookie season. He had resigned as the driver of the No. 42 in August while serving a month-long suspension after he was spotted having liked a racially insensitive meme on his Instagram account. "There have been some challenges this year, but I wouldn't trade them for anything in the world," Gragson said. "I don't know that I would change anything that has happened because I have learned from it and it gives me a chance to be a better person. In life, you either win or you learn, on and off the racetrack. The wins have been great, but you learn through the hardships and defeats. I am grateful to have good people around me, and I'm working really hard every day to hopefully get a second chance and to make the most of it."
