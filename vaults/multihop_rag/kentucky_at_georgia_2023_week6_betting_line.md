# Kentucky At Georgia: The 2023 Week 6 Betting Line

No. 20 Kentucky travelled to No. 1 Georgia with the Bulldogs favoured by 15 points, according to CBS Sports' Week 6 lines published 1 October 2023.

The case for the favourite is qualified. The two-time reigning national champions were undefeated so far, but the Bulldogs had looked human at times: both of Georgia's SEC victories required comeback wins, first at home against South Carolina in Week 3 and then at Auburn in Week 5.

The case for the underdog is Kentucky's running game. Georgia remained the heavy favourite for its first ranked matchup of the season, but coach Kirby Smart and company shouldn't sleep on the Wildcats: Kentucky's win against Florida in Week 5 saw running back Ray Davis take off for 280 yards and three rushing touchdowns, a mark that could have been much more considering Davis eclipsed the 200-yard mark well before halftime.
