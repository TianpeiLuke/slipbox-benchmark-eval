# Patrick Kane's Fit With The Florida Panthers

The Panthers make sense for Kane because they are the defending Eastern Conference champions who carried the momentum of their Cinderella Cup run into 2023-24, sitting tied for third in the conference and second in the Atlantic, so in terms of contenders they are right there in the East — and Kane could have the chance to play with a dynamic center like Aleksander Barkov.

Two secondary arguments support it. Florida has an older group, so Kane's arrival would not mess up the mojo the way it might with the baby Sabres; and although the Panthers do not have a lot of cap room, with roughly $1.7 million available they could make it work. Plus, as Sporting News put it, it's South Beach — who wouldn't want to live in the Miami area and play hockey?

The objection is travel, and it cuts against the premise that Kane should stay East. There had been reports that Kane was not keen on signing in the Western Conference because of the lengthy hours going arena to arena, but in the East nobody has it worse than the Panthers and Lightning given their geography. Sportsnet's Elliotte Friedman reported: "To be perfectly honest, there was one person who said to me, look, if you're worried about the travel in the Western Conference, why would you want to go to Florida when Florida and Tampa Bay have the worst travel in the Eastern Conference? They're remote outposts compared to a lot of the other places."
