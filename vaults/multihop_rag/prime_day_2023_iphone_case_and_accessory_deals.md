# Prime Day 2023 iPhone Case And Accessory Deals

WIRED's iPhone accessory picks include a one-handed phone mount, Zagg's Glass Elite Privacy 360 screen protector, a Casetify storewide sale, OtterBox cases at 20 percent off, a neon blocky case for the iPhone 15 Pro Max, a four-pack of Apple AirTags, and Nomad Lightning cables.

What WIRED likes best about the phone mount is one-handed use: the arms automatically close around the phone when you place it against the trigger button, and release bars free it; a telescopic arm and ball joint let you tweak placement and angle, and a clever magnetic tab attaches the charging cable to the back of the mount so you don't have to fish around for it — and it's super sturdy even on bumpy roads. The Glass Elite Privacy 360 blocks anyone from seeing your screen from the sides, feels great, and is fairly easy to apply, though WIRED's gripe is that Zagg's screen protectors are expensive; this has hit as low as $24, but it's a solid deal if you need one sooner rather than later. Casetify offers an overwhelming variety of case designs that can get pricey, so WIRED suggests taking advantage of the storewide sale: it likes the Impact MagSafe cases, with chunky bumpers and edges to protect the screen and made of plant-based materials as well as upcycled Casetify cases, and the more rugged but quite bulky Bounce case — noting the sale doesn't apply to iPhone 15 products. Many OtterBox cases are 20 percent off, including chargers, cables, and other accessories, with recommendations in WIRED's iPhone 15 and iPhone 14 case guides. The trendy blocky, rectangular case has a bright neon color that makes the phone pop and looks unique, and is fairly well-rounded besides: accurate cutouts, great buttons, and MagSafe support; WIRED tested it with the iPhone 14, but this listing is for the iPhone 15 Pro Max. Apple AirTags are WIRED's favorite Bluetooth trackers for iPhone users, and while this isn't a crazy sale, a bundle of four lets you track every set of keys, luggage, or a kid's backpack at once. Finally, anyone with an iPhone 14 or earlier can never have too many Lightning cables, and Nomad's are durable, available in different lengths, and offered with a choice of USB-C or USB-A on the other end.
