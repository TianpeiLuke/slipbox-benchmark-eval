# James Wise Recommends "The Coming Wave"

James Wise's holiday pick is "The Coming Wave: Technology, Power, and the 21st Century's Greatest Dilemma," by Mustafa Suleyman with Michael Bhaskar. The recommendation is offered on the strength of who is making it rather than an accompanying rationale, which TechCrunch's write-up does not record.

Wise is a partner at VC firm Balderton Capital, and the London-based investor also wrote his first book, "Start-up Century: Why We're All Becoming Entrepreneurs – and How to Make It Work for Everyone," which launched in the U.K. on 23 November 2023 and which TechCrunch suggests should make a great gift for aspiring entrepreneurs.
