# Nintendo Switch Pro Controller

The Switch Pro controller is another Polygon staff favorite, and the outlet calls it the pinnacle if what you want is a great Nintendo Switch and PC controller — in terms of comfort, durability, battery life, and Switch-specific extras.

Oli Welsh is quoted describing it as "a very solid, unassuming but beautifully engineered pad, with Nintendo's typically great ergonomics and switchgear. The D-pad is one of the best out there, and those lovely, fat, responsive face buttons will give you SNES flashbacks in a good way." As a Switch controller it supports, like the Joy-Cons, motion controls, HD rumble for more subtle and accurate jolts of haptic feedback, and amiibo connectivity to redeem in-game perks in several games, including the upcoming The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom. A smaller distinction Polygon singles out: it is one of the few wireless Switch controllers that can wake the console remotely by holding down its home button, where many require you to turn the console on manually.
