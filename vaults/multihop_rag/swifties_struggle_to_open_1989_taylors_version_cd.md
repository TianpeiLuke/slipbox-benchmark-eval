# Young Swifties Struggle To Open The 1989 (Taylor's Version) CD

A group of 11- and 12-year-old "Swifties" went viral after a TikTok video captured their attempts to open a "1989 (Taylor's Version)" compact disc, FOX News Lifestyle reported on 11 November 2023.

The girls came together to give their friend Annika the newly released Taylor Swift CD in hopes of cheering her up after she broke her wrist during soccer practice, mom Shannon Schmidt told Fox News Digital. "The girls knew what a CD was, but we had to borrow Grandma and Grandpa's CD player to let them fully experience it," Schmidt shared. The video captures the comical moment the three sixth and seventh graders attempted to open the CD: the girls — Lilly, Henslee and Annika — can be heard struggling to figure out how to get the CD out without breaking it, or looking for a button to press to release it from the case.

Watching their efforts, Schmidt said, "You guys are making me laugh because this is how we used to listen to music." Seconds later Lilly can be seen pressing the middle of the CD, releasing it from the case, and the girls were amazed when they finally got their hands on the soundtrack. They usually can be found listening to Swift while making her popular friendship bracelets or playing soccer with one another.
