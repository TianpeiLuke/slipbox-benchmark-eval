# NIO Q3 2023 Earnings Preview

NIO (NIO) was set to release its Q3 earnings before the bell on the Tuesday following Seeking Alpha's December 3, 2023 week-ahead preview, with the Wall Street consensus at Buy and Seeking Alpha's Quant Ratings holding at Hold because of concerns about profitability and valuation.

Consensus estimates going into the print were EPS of -$0.23 and revenue of $2.66B. The Earnings Insight attached to the preview is the important qualifier on those numbers: NIO had missed EPS estimates consistently in the past eight quarters and had missed revenue expectations in 50% of those quarters. The Quant system's two stated objections — profitability and valuation — are what separate it from the sell-side's Buy, and both are as of the December 3, 2023 preview date rather than the reported result.
