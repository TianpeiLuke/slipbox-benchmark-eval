# The Republican State-Level TikTok Ban Push

Though opposition to TikTok has been bipartisan, the crusade to ban the platform over national security concerns is primarily led by Republicans at the state level, TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-02. The article treats that position as a likely contributor to the party's social media struggles with young voters.

The observation matters because it sets the party's legislative posture against its outreach problem: the same platform Republicans are campaigning to ban is the one where the youth vote concentrates.
