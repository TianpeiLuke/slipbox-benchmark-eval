# Sony WF-1000XM5 Black Friday Deal

Sony's WF-1000XM5 is the top choice in Engadget's guide to the best wireless earbuds and came down to $248 at Amazon, B&H and other retailers for Black Friday 2023 — a new low, or a match of the lowest price seen, and $52 below Sony's usual $300 list price; several other retailers had it for $2 more.

Like the over-ear XM5, this in-ear model delivers excellent ANC with strong passive isolation, loads of helpful features and a warm sound that's fun out of the box but can be customized if needed, plus a comfortably snug fit that naturally isolates a good chunk of outside noise. Battery life is decent at seven or eight hours per charge and, notably, the design is lighter, smaller and altogether more comfortable than past Sony earbuds. The WF-1000XM5 earned a score of 87 in Engadget's review this past July. As the roundup puts it: yes, Sony's headphone naming scheme is bad.
