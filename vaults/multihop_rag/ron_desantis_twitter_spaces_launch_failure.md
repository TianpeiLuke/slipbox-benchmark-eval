# Ron DeSantis's Twitter Spaces Launch Failure

In May, Florida Gov. Ron DeSantis launched his presidential campaign on Twitter Spaces in an audio conversation with Elon Musk. The conversation started late and was so riddled with technical issues that it ended after just 21 minutes, and critics on both the right and the left described the launch as a disaster.

TechCrunch presents the episode on 2023-10-02 as evidence that Republican politicians have a reputation for botching even the most straightforward digital campaigns — and as context for why, given the resistance to Republican politicians in online spaces that attract young people, candidates may rely on shadowy agencies like Influenceable to do the work for them.
