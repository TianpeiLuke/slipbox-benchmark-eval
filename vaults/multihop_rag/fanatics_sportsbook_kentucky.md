# Fanatics Sportsbook In Kentucky

Fanatics Sportsbook is the betting arm of the fan gear retailer, opened in 2023 and establishing itself as the "most rewarding" sportsbook, distinguished from rival Kentucky books by paying bettors FanCash on their wagers, as described by CBS Sports on 13 October 2023.

The discriminating feature is that rewards currency. Playing with Fanatics earns up to 5% FanCash on bets, which can be spent on team swag from Fanatics or used to place more bets; the rate is 1% on straight bets, 3% on parlay bets and 5% on same game parlays, and FanCash can be redeemed either for sports betting bonuses or for the purchase of sports merchandise on the sister site Fanatics.com. Alongside that, Fanatics offers standard markets and unique sign-up bonuses, plus generous odds boosts, same game parlays and trending bets.

Its second distinguishing feature is presentation: the Discover page on Fanatics Sportsbook gathers marquee games, trending bets, promos and rewards all in one place, as compared with numerous places throughout the website or app on other books.
