# Week 13 Seahawks-Cowboys: Start Dallas, Fade The Seattle Passing Game

CBS Sports argues that in the Week 13 Seahawks-Cowboys game the Dallas side of the ball should be started and the Seattle passing game faded, because the Cowboys defense should keep pressure on Geno Smith and a Seattle offense that has struggled to gain consistency over the last month. The grounds: Smith did OK completing passes when pressured last week (a 60% completion rate) but not at an efficient level (5.4 yards per attempt); only two quarterbacks have exceeded 250 yards against Dallas this year and only four have thrown multiple touchdowns against them. The warrant joining these is that the author does not like Smith's chances to do either, which hurts any upside case for any Seahawks receiver — if the quarterback cannot reach volume or touchdown thresholds, the pass-catchers downstream of him cannot either. A supporting data point on how hard Dallas is to beat through the air: Curtis Samuel was the first receiver to get 100 yards on the Cowboys this year, and it took him 12 targets and 9 catches to get there; no one else has had more than 86.

One scope condition is stated up front: both teams had a week of rest, so this should not be treated like a typical Thursday game. The resulting verdicts are STARTS — Dak Prescott, CeeDee Lamb, Tony Pollard, DK Metcalf (as a low-end WR2), Jake Ferguson and the Cowboys DST; SITS — Tyler Lockett (flex at best), Geno Smith, Jaxon Smith-Njigba, Zach Charbonnet, Michael Gallup and the Seahawks DST; SLEEPERS — Brandin Cooks.
