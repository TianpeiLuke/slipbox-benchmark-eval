# The Sorcerer Class in Diablo 4

Sorcerers are the magic-slinging wizards of Sanctuary in Diablo 4, as Polygon describes them in its 2023-10-18 guide to the best Sorcerer builds and skills.

Their defining capabilities are elemental: Sorcerers can harness lightning to deal with crowds, ice to keep the hordes at bay, and fire to burn the demons of hell. They are also able to enchant their own spells with unique effects, intricately combining abilities for devastating results.

Because each element brings its own unique effect, it can be easy to get overwhelmed when trying to design a Sorcerer build — which is the reason Polygon gathered and simplified the best Sorcerer builds for season 2, linking out to more detailed versions of those builds and to their original creators for readers who need more on item affixes or other complex ideas.
