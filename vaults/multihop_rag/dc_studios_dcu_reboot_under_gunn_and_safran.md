# The DC Studios DCU Reboot Under James Gunn and Peter Safran

DC Studios' plan, as described by Polygon on 2023-10-24, is a two-stage handover: the Snyderverse ends in 2023, and a newly branded DC Studios run by Guardians of the Galaxy and Suicide Squad director James Gunn and producer Peter Safran replaces the DCEU with "the DCU," a mainline continuity that will exist alongside other DC film and TV universes.

The wind-down has a defined ordering. In 2023 the Snyderverse rolls slowly to a halt with Shazam! Fury of the Gods, The Flash, and Blue Beetle, all leading up to the grand finale of Aquaman and the Lost Kingdom. Once the last bits of the DCEU make their way to theaters, it is time to usher in a new era of DC movies.

The incoming structure has two complementary tracks: the new projects on the DC docket are meant to reboot or reshape DC's best-known heroes, while also reaching into some deeper corners of the DC canon for more obscure characters. Gunn and Safran are bringing a long-term plan to DC with them, spanning theaters and TV screens in 2023 and well into the future.

The model has a stated scope limit. Polygon's editorial note says the story covers only Warner Bros.' DC projects and does not include The CW's Arrowverse, which is effectively ending in 2023.
