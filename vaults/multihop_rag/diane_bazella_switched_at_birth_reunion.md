# Diane Bazella's Switched-at-Birth Reunion

Diane Bazella, 64, of Minnetonka, Minnesota, gets to spend holiday time with her birth mother after a nearly 40-year search that revealed she may have been switched at birth — a reunion she described to Fox News Digital in a phone interview published on 15 December 2023.

"Merry Christmas" has a whole new meaning for her. "It means the world for me to be with my mom at the Christmas holiday," Bazella said. "It's all about love. My prayers have been answered, and my heart is full. We both hope we have many more Christmases to spend together." Enjoying Christmastime with her oldest child — and only daughter — has been a long time coming for Sherri Geerts, 81, of Sunnyvale, California. "It's a story with a good ending," Geerts told Fox News Digital.

The search itself was not an ordinary one but, as Bazella put it, an epic journey with numerous twists and turns; the process took grit and determination, research and genealogical skills, and most of all a deep-rooted desire to find out where she belonged. Bazella first found Geerts, her birth mother, in May 2021, and they met in person in 2022. It was only recently that she found the courage to share the story of her decades-long search. "I've spent a lot of time dealing with the healing part of it," Bazella said. "I think it kind of gives people hope that miracles are still going on, and the world needs to hear that right now. So that's part of the reason I've stepped out and told my story now."
