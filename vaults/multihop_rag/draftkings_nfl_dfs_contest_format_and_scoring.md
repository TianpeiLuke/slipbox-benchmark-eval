# DraftKings NFL DFS Contest Format And Scoring Rules

DraftKings NFL daily fantasy contests are defined by full-point PPR, four-point passing touchdowns, and three-point bonuses for 100 rushing yards, 100 receiving yards and 300 passing yards, played on the Sunday main slate with a $50,000 budget, per Sporting News on Dec. 7, 2023.

The discriminating features against other operators are the full-point reception scoring and the yardage bonuses: each catch is worth a full point, and a player who reaches 100 rushing yards, 100 receiving yards or 300 passing yards earns an additional three points. Passing touchdowns are worth four points. The contest structure named is the Sunday main slate under a $50,000 salary budget.
