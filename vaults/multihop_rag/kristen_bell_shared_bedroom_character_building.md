# Kristen Bell On A Shared Bedroom As Character Building

Kristen Bell argues that making her daughters share a bedroom builds character, because "to develop a good character, it's important to always be going through something" — told to Self in 2020 and reported by FOX News Entertainment on 10 October 2023.

The grounds are her expectation about their lives. Bell and Dax Shepard share two daughters, Lincoln, 10, and Delta, 8, and have been married for 10 years; the couple has been honest about the trials of raising their kids, especially keeping them grounded. "It's very important to me that they share a bedroom. I think their lives will be easier than most other people's on the planet," she said — so the difficulty has to be supplied deliberately.

The warrant is that shared constraint teaches negotiation and perspective: "I like the fact that they will have to figure out how to share a bedroom, figure out how to share your closet, figure out how to share your space. If that's the worst thing about your life, that you have to share a bedroom with your sister, you're going to be OK."
