# Travel And Language Learning Gift Cards

For a giftee in dire need of a screen break, The Verge's guide points to travel-oriented gifts ranging from the obvious, like airline gift cards, to national park passes.

A GetYourGuide gift card provides an easy way to take advantage of guided tours and tourist attractions around the world, letting a recipient explore volcanoes like Italy's Mount Etna or swim with sharks in Cape Verde. A Pack & Go gift card (Amazon and Target) covers almost everything needed on vacation: it can be used on a Southwest Airlines flight, to buy gas from Chevron or Texaco on a road trip, or to book an Airbnb or place a Grubhub order. For aspiring polyglots, a Rosetta Stone membership makes it easy to learn everything from French to Arabic to Japanese from home, with extras including speech recognition tech to get the accent right; Rosetta Stone is currently offering up to 50 percent off its plans, with the 12-month single-language tier starting at $126, $42 off. Finally, a gift card to a rideshare service like Uber (Amazon, Best Buy, Target) or Lyft (Amazon, Best Buy, Target) comes in handy for a ride to the airport — or if New Year's Eve plans include popping a few celebratory shots.
