# Singin' In The Rain (1952): The Musical Comedy Classic

Singin' in the Rain is the 1952 musical comedy directed by Gene Kelly and Stanley Donen, running 1h 42m and starring Kelly, Donald O'Connor and Debbie Reynolds. Polygon treats its inclusion as non-negotiable — "If Singin' in the Rain wasn't on this list, we wouldn't be doing our jobs right" — and describes it as one of the most well-loved movies of all time.

The distinguishing note in Polygon's write-up is that the film is stranger than its reputation suggests. It is as joyous and funny as remembered, with Donald O'Connor's "Make 'Em Laugh" bit singled out as leaving viewers in stitches, but alongside the industry jokes and the contemplation of the arrival of sound in movies sits the 13-minute dream sequence "Broadway Melody," which the reviewer calls absolutely hypnotising. It is available to stream on Max, or for digital rental or purchase at Amazon, Apple and Google Play.
