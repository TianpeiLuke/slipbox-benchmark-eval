# Prime Day 2023 Mattress And Bedding Deals

WIRED's bedding picks include the Midnight Luxe mattress with code DEALDAY25, Casper's hybrid bed, an organic kids' mattress and a latex mattress topper both with code DISCOVER, Brooklinen's weighted blanket in terra-cotta, Brooklinen's organic sheets, and a giant 10-foot-by-10-foot blanket.

Entering DEALDAY25 at checkout gets a deal that includes two pillows and matches the best price WIRED has seen its favorite mattress reach: the Midnight Luxe has six layers of foam and individually wrapped inner springs, is medium-firm and comfortable for almost all sleeping positions, with other untested firmness levels in the Luxe collection. Casper's hybrid bed reaches its $1,495 price but is often $1,196 on Amazon; this is still among the best deals WIRED has tracked from the brand that put bed-in-a-box mattresses on the map, and it is a great neutral option striking a good balance between soft and supportive with just enough bounce. The organic kids' pick, WIRED's top organic mattress for children, is made from certified organic cotton, wool, and latex, and while it has dipped slightly lower in the past, the base price has risen since and this price is lower than recent sales. The organic latex topper from My Green Mattress is WIRED's favorite firm topper — 2 inches high, with a zipper to remove the organic cotton cover, staying in place without straps — for anyone not ready for a whole new mattress. Brooklinen's weighted blanket is 12 pounds but feels sufficiently weighty, has held up well through many washes and cat biscuit-making sessions, and is prettier than some options thanks to a textured side. Brooklinen's organic sheet set is WIRED's favorite: soft but not overly silky, unlikely to sleep too hot, made from Global Organic Textile Standard-certified organic cotton and Oeko-Tex certified, meaning tested for and free of known toxic chemicals. The gigantic blanket, which will be the focus of its own story in a few weeks, is loved by reviewer Louryn Strampe: true to its name it measures 10 feet by 10 feet, making it really easy to share even for a blanket hog, at a price matching other shopping holidays.
