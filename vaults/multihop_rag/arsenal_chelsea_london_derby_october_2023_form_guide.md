# Arsenal vs Chelsea: London Derby Form Guide, October 2023

Mikel Arteta's Arsenal went into the 5.30pm London derby at Chelsea on 21 October 2023 full of confidence, having beaten Manchester City in their last match, and with a brilliant recent record against the Blues — six wins from their last seven meetings, talkSPORT reported on 20 October 2023.

Arsenal had triumphed in their last three trips to Stamford Bridge and had not lost in a London derby since May 2022. Chelsea, for their part, went into the match against the Gunners hopeful of a result, having won their last three games in all competitions.
