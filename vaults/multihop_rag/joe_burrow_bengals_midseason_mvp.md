# Joe Burrow Bengals Midseason MVP

Yardbarker's Nov. 9, 2023 AFC midseason team MVP list named quarterback Joe Burrow the Cincinnati Bengals' MVP, on the claim that he appeared fully recovered after a rough start battling a calf injury.

The grounds are the win streak and the numbers inside it: the Bengals, at 5-3, had won four straight, and during that run Burrow had thrown for 1,133 yards, 10 touchdowns and just two interceptions. The warrant is that the turnaround in team results tracks the recovery of his form.
