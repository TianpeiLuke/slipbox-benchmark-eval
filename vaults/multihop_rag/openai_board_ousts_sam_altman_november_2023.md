# The OpenAI Board Ousts Sam Altman, November 2023

As reported on November 18, 2023, the OpenAI board had ejected billionaire and AI evangelist Sam Altman from his CEO role after an apparent vote of no confidence. The board's exact wording in a release issued that afternoon: Altman's "departure follows a deliberative review process by the board, which concluded that he was not consistently candid in his communications with the board, hindering its ability to exercise its responsibilities."

What followed was a whirlwind four days for OpenAI, the generative AI poster child behind the smash hit ChatGPT. The board ousted CEO and co-founder Altman and demoted president and co-founder Greg Brockman, who subsequently resigned, paving the way for what looked like a mutiny by staff insisting the founders be reinstated post-haste. By the time TechCrunch wrote again on November 21, 2023, Microsoft had already hired Altman and Brockman to head up a new internal AI unit — though, as things transpired, nothing had actually been signed yet, with rumors suggesting the ousted leaders might actually return to OpenAI after all, in some capacity at least.

TechCrunch cautioned on November 18 that the full truth would not be known for a long time, as the characters in the drama are likely to be NDA'ed up. Per various whispers and leaks, an all-hands meeting about the situation that afternoon did not produce any revelations beyond banal reassurances that the company is fine and they will get a fresh CEO soon.
