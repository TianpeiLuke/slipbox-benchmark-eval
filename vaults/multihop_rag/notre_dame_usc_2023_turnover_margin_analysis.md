# Turnovers, Not Yardage, Decided Notre Dame's Win Over USC

Notre Dame beat USC on Oct. 14, 2023 despite being out-gained and out-performed on third down, because a 5-0 turnover margin is difficult for any team to overcome — the claim the Sporting News advances is that field position bought by turnovers, plus pressure in the trenches, outweighed USC's statistical edges.

The grounds are the box score. In a season in which much had been made of USC's defensive woes holding back the offense, on this night it was the other way around: USC's defense held Notre Dame to just 251 total yards of offense, 51 fewer than the Trojans gained on offense, and Notre Dame converted only three of 10 third downs against USC's six of 13. The warrant joining those grounds to the conclusion is field position: the 5-0 turnover margin frequently left Notre Dame with only a short field to work with, so that the Fighting Irish started drives on average at their own 47 while the Trojans began on average at their own 28.

A second strand of the argument is that Watts made the headline-grabbing plays but a massive part of Notre Dame's defensive success came in the trenches. Notre Dame's defensive line tallied six sacks of Caleb Williams and three additional hits, frequently pressing the Trojan quarterback, and Williams finished 23-for-37 for 199 passing yards with one passing touchdown and three interceptions.
