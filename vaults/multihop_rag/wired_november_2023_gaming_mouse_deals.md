# WIRED's Pre-Black Friday Gaming Mouse Deals

WIRED lists two gaming mice: its top pick overall, and a more affordable alternative.

The top pick is light, comfortable for a wide variety of hand sizes and shapes, and features a couple of extra buttons without becoming cluttered; designed with esports professionals in mind, it has nice, clicky buttons, hyper-fast response time, and solid wireless connectivity even around 10 feet from the receiver. The second mouse has a comfortable chassis, extra thumb buttons, and — perhaps most importantly — a modest price: even off sale it is one of WIRED's more affordable picks, and this deal makes it even more accessible.
