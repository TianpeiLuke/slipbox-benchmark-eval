# Phone Grips, Skins And Charging Cable Deals

Five small phone accessories were discounted in the under-$25 roundup. Dbrand's leather phone skins were $24, down from $35 — 31 percent off at Dbrand; they add a little grip to the back of a slick phone but are mostly a style choice, high quality and non-damaging.

EWA's MagOne magnetic phone grip was $15.99, $4 off, at Amazon: a grip and kickstand in one, making bigger phones a little more wieldy without getting in the way of MagSafe charging. PopSockets was taking 25 percent off various grips, mounts, cases, and other accessories, including a PopGrip for MagSafe phone handle/stand at $22.50, compatible with iPhone 12 phones and newer.

Uni's 10-foot USB-C to USB-C cable was $7.99, $12 off, at Amazon. The lengthy cable is capable of delivering 100 watts of power to beefy laptops like the 16-inch MacBook Pro, but you can also use it to power up smartphones, tablets, and other gadgets.

And on AirTags: the worst thing about them is that you need something to stick them in, so The Verge flagged a pack of four AirTag keychain holders for $8.91 — silicone, but way more affordable than Apple's official AirTag accessories, just one of which will set you back $35.
