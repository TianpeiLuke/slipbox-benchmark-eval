# The Fiftieth Anniversary Of Equal Prize Money At The US Open

On Thursday, 2023-10-12, Billie Jean King and about 60 athletes were to celebrate the 50th anniversary of equal prize money at the U.S. Open and of the King-Riggs match, at her annual awards dinner for the Women's Sports Foundation in New York.

In August 2023, former President Barack Obama and first lady Michelle Obama attended the U.S. Open at Arthur Ashe Stadium to mark the pay equity milestone. "Let us remember all of this is bigger than a champion's paycheck," Michelle Obama said during the ceremony on opening night. "This is about how women are seen and valued in this world."
