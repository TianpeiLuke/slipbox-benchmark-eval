# Q3 2023 Earnings Week October 23-27 Overview

Earnings season was slated to peak in the week of 23 to 27 October 2023, with a wide range of companies across various sectors reporting results, Seeking Alpha reported on 22 October. The focus would be on mega-cap tech giants, but many other firms would announce results, including those from consumer staples, industrial, automobile, airline and energy segments.

Facebook parent Meta, Amazon, Alphabet and Microsoft were all set to release their quarterly results. The list of companies reporting also included major automakers General Motors and Ford; consumer staples manufacturers Coca-Cola and Colgate-Palmolive; defence leaders Boeing, L3Harris Technologies, Raytheon Technologies, General Dynamics and Northrop Grumman; energy sector giants ExxonMobil and Chevron; payment processors Visa and Mastercard; and major airline Southwest Airlines.
