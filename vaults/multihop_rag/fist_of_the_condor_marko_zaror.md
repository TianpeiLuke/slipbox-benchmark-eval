# Fist of the Condor (Marko Zaror)

Fist of the Condor is a martial arts throwback directed by Ernesto Díaz Espinoza in which Marko Zaror, of John Wick: Chapter Four, plays twin brothers at odds with each other over an ancient text. It also stars Eyal Meyer and Gina Aguad, ranked 47 on Polygon's best movies of 2023 list of 2023-10-07, and was available on Hi-Yah!, free with a library card on Hoopla, and free on Tubi and Plex.

Zaror, who also choreographed the movie's fight scenes, excels: there are high-flying kicks, rapid displays of martial arts forms, and the drama inherent to great cinematic fights.

Polygon's Pete Volk says the movie is at its best when leaning into the action, but that this is not all it has to offer — Díaz Espinoza shoots the landscape of Chile in a way that builds the drama of the fights and adds an element of tranquility to the chaos. For fans of martial arts cinema, it is called a must-watch.
