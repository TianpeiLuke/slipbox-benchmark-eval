# Cassidy In Preacher

Cassidy — a brawling, hard-drinking Irishman who is already a load of fun when you meet him — turns out to be a vampire, and Polygon says he shoots up the power rankings when he sinks his teeth into a poor sap.

The surprise survives the setting. Preacher, the comic book by Garth Ennis and Steve Dillon and its subsequent TV adaptation from Sam Catlin, Evan Goldberg, and Seth Rogen, starts from the basic assumption that God is real and has walked out on the job, which makes a lot of things fair game — namely angels, demons, and the like — yet it is still a shock when you meet Cassidy.
