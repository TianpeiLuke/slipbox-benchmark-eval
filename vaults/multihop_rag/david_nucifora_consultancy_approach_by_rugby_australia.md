# Rugby Australia's Consultancy Approach To David Nucifora

Rugby Australia was trying to get Ireland's long-time director of high-performance David Nucifora on board as a consultant once he wraps up with Ireland, The Roar reported on 11 December 2023, while making clear he was never in the running for the director of high-performance role itself.

The report links that approach to the Schmidt pursuit: should Rugby Australia go down the path of bringing Joe Schmidt into the fold, Schmidt's own relationship with Nucifora could see the Australian reconsider his position with the governing body. In the meantime, Rugby Australia have been making moves to try and twist Nucifora's arm.
