# The Fifth Third Bank Relationship Eligibility Requirement

Eligibility for the Fifth Third 1.67% Cash/Back Card is gated on an existing banking relationship: if you don't have a Fifth Third checking or savings account, you're ineligible to open the card. Only those with a previous relationship with the bank will qualify.

That matters because of the bank's footprint. Fifth Third operates primarily in the Midwest, though it has branches from Illinois to West Virginia to Florida, and if you don't live in an area where it has physical locations you may likely have never heard of it.
