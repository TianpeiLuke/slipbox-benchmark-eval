# Echo Show 5 And Nest Hub Smart Display Deals

Two smart displays were heavily discounted during the anti-Prime Big Deal Day sales: the third-gen Echo Show 5 at $50, down from $90 — 44% off — at Best Buy, and the second-gen Google Nest Hub at $60, down from $100 — 40% off — at Walmart.

The new Echo Show 5 is just as small as its predecessor but features updated mics and a speaker system that delivers double the bass and clearer sound quality. The second-gen Nest Hub is Google's smallest smart display, with a seven-inch touchscreen that can automatically adjust itself to the lighting in the room; what is new in the latest version is its ability to automatically track sleep patterns with its Soli radar chip.
