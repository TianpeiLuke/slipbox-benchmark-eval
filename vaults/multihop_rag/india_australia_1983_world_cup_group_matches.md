# India vs Australia At The 1983 World Cup

India met Australia for the very first time at a World Cup in 1983 and lost that match by 162 runs. Australia posted a target of 321 runs courtesy of a brilliant 110 runs off 131 balls by Trevor Chappell, with Kapil Dev taking a fifer in that innings; India crumbled within 158 runs, with Ken MacLeay picking up six wickets for Australia.

India got their revenge in the very same World Cup, defeating the Australian side by 118 runs. India set a target of 248 runs after being bowled out in 55.5 overs, with Yashpal Sharma scoring 40 off 40 and Jeff Thompson and Rodney Hogg picking up three wickets each for Australia. Australia then crumbled within 129 runs, with Roger Binny and Madan Lal picking up four wickets for India. India went on to win their first-ever ODI World Cup, defeating the West Indies in the final.
