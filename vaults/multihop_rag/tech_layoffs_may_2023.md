# Tech Layoffs In May 2023

May 2023's deepest tech cuts came from Meta, which announced on May 24 that it was laying off about 6,000 people — bringing the total who had lost their jobs at Meta since November to about 21,000 — and from Reliance Retail's JioMart, which laid off over 1,000 employees on May 22 and planned to cut as many as 9,900 more roles over the coming weeks.

The month began with Neato Robotics: on May 1 the Vorwerk-owned company announced it was shutting down, with nearly 100 employees impacted. Bishop Fox announced on May 3 that it had laid off around 50 employees, 13% of its workforce, on May 2. On May 4 Shopify announced it was laying off 20% of its workforce, impacting more than 2,000 people, and also selling its logistics business to Flexport for roughly 13% in stock; Meesho announced it had cut 15% of its workforce, 251 roles, after a first round of layoffs eliminated 150 roles about a year earlier. Rapid, previously known as RapidAPI, announced on May 5 that it had laid off another 70 employees less than two weeks after letting go of 50% of its staff, leaving just 42 people at the company, down from 230 in April — a total drop of 82% in headcount. LinkedIn announced on May 8 that it was cutting 716 jobs, about 3.6% of total employees, and would phase out its local jobs app in China, while also planning to open about 250 new jobs on May 15. Nuro announced on May 12 that it would lay off 30%, about 340 employees across the company. On May 18 TuSimple announced it was laying off about 30% of employees — the company had about 550 employees in the U.S. before the layoff and would have about 220 after the reduction in force — and the consulting firm Krebs Stamos Group announced it had laid off six people; the firm had 18 employees including the founders in April, and on May 18 its site showed only 14 team members. Taxfix announced on May 30 that it had laid off 20% of staff, 120 employees.
