# Why iPhone Discounts Come From Carriers, Not Retailers

WIRED's Apple Black Friday guide of 25 November 2023 sets out a simple structure for how iPhone discounting works: you typically will not see many iPhone deals from retailers, because the sales that do exist usually come from carriers — AT&T, Verizon and T-Mobile — and are attached to conditions rather than offered as straight price cuts.

The relation running through this model is conditional rather than promotional. Carrier iPhone offers carry several requirements, such as being on an unlimited plan or opening a new cell line, so the discount is granted in exchange for a service commitment rather than being available to any buyer at checkout. That is what distinguishes iPhone pricing from the rest of the Apple lineup covered in the same guide, where straightforward retailer discounts are the norm.

The range over which the model holds is narrow and stated: it applies to current iPhones during the November 2023 holiday sales, and WIRED notes the exceptions itself, having laid out a few deals on older unlocked iPhone models alongside some carrier promotions for readers in the market for a new phone.
