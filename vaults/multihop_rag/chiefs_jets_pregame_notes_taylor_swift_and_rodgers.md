# Chiefs-Jets Pregame: Taylor Swift In Donna Kelce's Box, Rodgers Back On The Sideline

Before the Oct. 1, 2023 Chiefs-Jets "Sunday Night Football" game, the Sporting News live blog logged three pregame items in the 6 p.m. hour. At 6:05 p.m. it reported, citing ESPN's Adam Schefter, that Taylor Swift would be joining Travis Kelce's mother, Donna, in a box for the game.

At 6:07 p.m. the blog noted that Aaron Rodgers would be back on the sidelines with the Jets that night for the first time since his season-ending injury. At 6:08 p.m. it observed that it was "all business" for defending NFL MVP Patrick Mahomes.
