# Broadcom Q4 Earnings Preview and VMware Integration

Broadcom (AVGO) was set to announce its Q4 results after Thursday's closing bell in the week following Seeking Alpha's December 3, 2023 preview, going in with shares up more than 67% for the year on the AI wave, a Buy consensus among sell-side analysts, and a Seeking Alpha Quant Rating that had transitioned to Strong Buy from Hold in mid-November.

The California-based semiconductor company's share gain far outpaced the broader market, which the preview read as underscoring its robust position and resilience in the then-current economic climate. Consensus estimates for the quarter were EPS of $10.96 and revenue of $9.28B, against an Earnings Insight record of beating both EPS and revenue estimates in eight consecutive quarters.

Two VMware developments framed the print. On the Friday before the preview, Broadcom announced plans to let go of 1,300 California-based VMware employees following the completion of its multi-billion-dollar acquisition of VMware on November 22 — a step in a $61B cash-and-stock diversification strategy moving the company from semiconductors toward the cloud. On the Wednesday before, a report surfaced that Broadcom was evaluating strategic alternatives for VMware's End-User Computing and Carbon Black cybersecurity units; that is a reported evaluation, not an announced divestiture.

Seeking Alpha author Mike Zaccardi supplied the bull case, contending that Broadcom offers tech and AI exposure at a lower price, with a strong semiconductor product portfolio and solid technical background, and that its earnings beat history, strong expense control and robust profitability make it an attractive investment. The same section of the week-ahead lists other companies reporting alongside Broadcom, including Lululemon Athletica (LULU), Dollar General Corporation (DG), Ciena Corporation (CIEN), Smartsheet (SMAR), Vail Resorts (MTN), Planet Labs PBC (PL), Guidewire Software (GWRE), The Cooper Companies (COO), HashiCorp (HCP), Lakeland Industries (LAKE), Greif (GEF), Liquidity Services (LQDT), GMS (GMS), Methode Electronics (MEI), Hooker Furnishings Corporation (HOFT), DLH Holdings (DLHC) and SecureWorks (SCWX), among others.
