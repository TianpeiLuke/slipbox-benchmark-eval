# ChatGPT Subscription Tiers And Pricing

ChatGPT is sold in a ladder of paid tiers on top of the free chatbot: ChatGPT Plus at $20 per month, a previewed ChatGPT Business tier, and ChatGPT Enterprise — with the tiers distinguished mainly by data-usage policy, control over end users, and access to the newest models.

The ladder was built in stages. OpenAI first said it was "starting to think about how to monetize ChatGPT" in an announcement on the company's official Discord server, posting a waitlist for a version to be called ChatGPT Professional; the waitlist document listed benefits including no "blackout" windows, no throttling and an unlimited number of messages — "at least 2x the regular daily limit." A month later OpenAI launched a pilot subscription plan, ChatGPT Plus, starting at $20 per month. It then previewed ChatGPT Business, described as "for professionals who need more control over their data as well as enterprises seeking to manage their end users"; OpenAI wrote that "ChatGPT Business will follow our API's data usage policies, which means that end users' data won't be used to train our models by default," and planned to make it available in the coming months. ChatGPT Enterprise followed, able to perform the same tasks as ChatGPT — writing emails, drafting essays, debugging computer code — while adding "enterprise-grade" privacy and data analysis capabilities on top of vanilla ChatGPT, along with enhanced performance and customization options.

Capacity became a binding constraint. Following the Dev Day conference, Sam Altman announced the company was pausing new subscriptions for ChatGPT Plus because of a "surge of usage"; the temporary hold on sign-ups, together with demand for new Plus features such as making custom GPTs, led to a slew of resellers on eBay. On December 13, 2023 Altman announced sign-ups had been re-enabled: "we have re-enabled chatgpt plus subscriptions! thanks for your patience while we found more gpus." The Plus subscription includes access to GPT-4 and GPT-4 Turbo.
