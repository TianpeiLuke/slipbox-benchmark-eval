# Mr. Irrelevant: The NFL Draft's Final Pick

"Mr. Irrelevant" is the nickname given to the final pick of the NFL Draft. What distinguishes the title is purely positional: it attaches to whoever is selected last, regardless of the player or the team.

The Mr. Irrelevant title has been a staple of NFL Drafts since the 1976 Draft.
