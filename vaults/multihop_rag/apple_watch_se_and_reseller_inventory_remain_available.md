# Apple Watch SE And Reseller Inventory Remain Available

Even if the import ban goes through — which The Verge said it is looking a lot like it will — that doesn't mean you'll never be able to buy another Apple Watch in the US: the Apple Watch SE remains completely unaffected because it doesn't have a blood oxygen sensor.

In the short term the ban also doesn't forbid third-party resellers from selling their existing inventory of Series 9 or Ultra 2. Only once those resellers run out does it become a problem, if they can't buy more units from Apple. The photo of the unaffected SE is credited to Amelia Holowaty Krales / The Verge.
