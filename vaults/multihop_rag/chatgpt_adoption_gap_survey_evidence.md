# ChatGPT's Adoption Gap: The Survey Evidence

The premise attacked is that ChatGPT's demonstrated capabilities are matched by real-world use: there is mixed evidence as to whether ChatGPT is actually being used in these ways.

A Pew Research survey from August 2023 showed that only 18% of Americans have ever tried ChatGPT, and that most who have tried it use the chatbot for entertainment purposes or answering one-off questions — not for the productivity tasks the capability claims rest on. Teens might not be using ChatGPT all that often either, despite what some alarmist headlines imply, with one poll finding that only two in five teenagers have used the tech in the last six months.

TechCrunch's proposed explanation for the gap is the product itself: ChatGPT's limitations might be to blame.
