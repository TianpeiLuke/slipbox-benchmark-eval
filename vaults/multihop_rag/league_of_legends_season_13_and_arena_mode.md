# League of Legends Season 13 and Arena Mode

League of Legends Season 13, developed by Riot Games for Mac and Windows PC, is ranked 42 on Polygon's best video games of 2023 list of 2023-10-31, and Polygon calls it one of the game's most balanced seasons yet.

Almost every champion has felt viable throughout the year — no small feat for a game with over 140 playable characters — and that has led to great fun and variety on the solo queue ladder and in professional play, where an exciting Worlds had just wrapped up.

The other reason League had an outstanding 2023 is Arena, a new game mode introduced during the game's summer event: a 2v2v2v2 battle mode with fast-paced chaos, using League's roster of champions in a more approachable and containable setting, and with less rage-inducing teammates. Critic Pete Volk notes the mode was removed after the summer event concluded but is reportedly returning soon, and says it cannot come soon enough.
