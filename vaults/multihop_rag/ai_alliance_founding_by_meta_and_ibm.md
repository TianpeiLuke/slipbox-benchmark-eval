# The Founding Of The AI Alliance By Meta And IBM

The AI Alliance is an industry body launched on 5 December 2023 by Meta together with IBM to support "open innovation" and "open science" in AI. Meta, on an open source tear, wants to spread its influence in the ongoing battle for AI mindshare, and pairs itself with IBM, whose audience is decidedly more corporate and enterprise.

Its boundary against an existing body is blurred. TechCrunch asks how the Alliance's work will differ from the quite similar Partnership on AI — similar at least in terms of its overarching mission, members and tenets — given that the Partnership on AI years ago promised to publish research using open source licenses and minutes from its meetings in order to educate the public on pressing AI issues of the day, which is what the AI Alliance purportedly seeks to do.

Confusingly, the Partnership on AI is in fact a member of the AI Alliance. A press release shared with TechCrunch says the Alliance plans to "utilize pre-existing collaborations" — presumably including the Partnership on AI's — to "identify opportunities that develop open AI resources that meet the needs of business and society equally and responsibly."
