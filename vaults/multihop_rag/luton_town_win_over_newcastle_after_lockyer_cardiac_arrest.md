# Luton Town's Win Over Newcastle After Lockyer's Cardiac Arrest

Andros Townsend headed the winner for Luton Town as they beat Newcastle United on 23 December 2023, as reported by The Roar that day.

The circumstance the report attaches to the result is that this was Luton's first match since captain Tom Lockyer suffered a cardiac arrest against Bournemouth a week earlier.
