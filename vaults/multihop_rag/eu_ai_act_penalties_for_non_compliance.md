# EU AI Act Penalties For Non-Compliance

Penalties for non-compliance with the AI Act can lead to fines ranging from €35 million or 7% of global turnover down to €7.5 million or 1.5% of turnover, depending on the infringement and the size of the company, according to the parliament.

The Council's press release further stipulates how the tiers map to conduct: the higher sanction of 7% would apply for violations of the banned AI applications, while penalties of 1.5% would be levied for the supply of incorrect information. Sanctions of 3% could be imposed for violations of other AI Act obligations. The provisional agreement also allows for "more proportionate caps" on administrative fines for SMEs and start-ups in case of infringements, so there appears to be scope for AI startups to face smaller penalties for infringements than AI giants may invite.
