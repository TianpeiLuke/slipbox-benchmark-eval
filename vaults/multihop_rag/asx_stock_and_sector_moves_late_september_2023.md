# ASX Stock And Sector Moves, Late September 2023

REITs, up 0.4 per cent, was the strongest sector on the Australian index in the session reported on 2023-09-26, as Goodman added 0.5 per cent and Dexus climbed 2.8 per cent. In company news, ETF provider Betashares, which manages $30 billion in funds, reached an agreement to acquire Bendigo and Adelaide Bank's superannuation business, its first venture into the superannuation sector and part of what Betashares said was a longer-term strategy to expand into the broader financial sector; shares in Bendigo increased 0.6 per cent on the news.

Among the laggards, casino operator Star Entertainment Group's shares hit an all-time low of 60¢ after it raised $565 million, closing the session 16 per cent weaker at 63¢; Star, which raised $800 million in February, had to return to the market for fresh funding and is hoping to raise $750 million at a share price of 60¢ a share. Healthcare heavyweight CSL shed 1.4 per cent, weighing down the healthcare sector, and insurers IAG (down 2.6 per cent) and Suncorp (down 2 per cent) gave back some of their gains from Tuesday. Gold miners Newcrest (down 2.1 per cent) and Evolution (down 3.5 per cent) were also among the biggest large-cap decliners after the spot gold price dropped 0.9 per cent overnight. Information technology, down 1.1 per cent, was the weakest sector on the local bourse, with WiseTech losing 1.4 per cent.
