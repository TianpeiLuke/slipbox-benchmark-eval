# HyperX Cloud Stinger 2 Deal

For a decent gaming headset at as low a price as possible, the HyperX Cloud Stinger 2 was worth considering at $35 for Black Friday 2023 — only $5 below its usual going rate, but matching the lowest price Engadget has seen.

The Cloud Stinger 2 is the budget pick in Engadget's gaming headset guide: it sounds narrower and less balanced than the Astro A40, but its built-in mic is great for the money and its bass-heavy profile at least makes action scenes feel a little more exciting. It's all comfy too, though it's largely made of plastic.
