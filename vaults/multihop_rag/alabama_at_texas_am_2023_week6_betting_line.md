# Alabama At Texas A&M: The 2023 Week 6 Betting Line

No. 11 Alabama was favoured by 3.5 points at Texas A&M, according to CBS Sports' Week 6 lines published 1 October 2023.

Alabama's form is described as recovering: the Crimson Tide had seemingly started to find a groove after an uninspiring 2-1 start, beating Ole Miss and Mississippi State by a combined 64-27 for a 2-0 start in SEC play.

The counterweight is the recent history of this fixture and what the game means for the Aggies. Texas A&M has caused fits for Alabama in their last two meetings: the Aggies stunned the top-ranked Tide on a walk-off field goal when Alabama last visited Kyle Field in 2021, and in the previous year's meeting at Bryant-Denny Stadium Texas A&M had a chance to win on the final play of regulation before the Tide held on for a 24-20 victory. An upset this weekend would move Texas A&M to 5-1 overall and 3-0 in SEC play. The Aggies have already dealt with the injury bug, losing starting quarterback Conner Weigman for the season, though backup Max Johnson brings no shortage of SEC experience as the former LSU transfer prepares for his second consecutive start.
