# The Risks Of Paying Ransomware Demands

Paying off hackers in the event of a ransomware attack is pretty common practice, but TechCrunch's claim is that it comes with a ton of pitfalls.

The first ground is that the transaction has no enforcement: you are trusting anonymous criminals to do what they promised.

The second is legal exposure independent of whether the criminals deliver: you may potentially be violating U.S. sanctions laws along the way.
