# Super Mario 3D World on Switch

Super Mario 3D World is Polygon's recommendation for anyone searching for that classic Super Mario feeling in a modern 3D world — or anyone who has always wanted to play as Mario in a cat suit. It appears in the outlet's 2023-10-20 list of the 35 best couch co-op games for Nintendo Switch.

Players can choose Mario, Luigi, Peach, or Toad.

Each has a slightly different perk: Luigi can get a bit more air in his jump, while Toad is a bit faster.
