# Week 13 Broncos-Texans: Denver's Defense Is Real, But C.J. Stroud Can Still Be Started

CBS Sports argues that the Broncos defense is a genuine obstacle for quarterbacks in Week 13 yet not one worth fearing enough to bench C.J. Stroud. The grounds for the defensive claim: seven straight quarterbacks to face the Broncos have failed to reach 20 Fantasy points, including Josh Allen once and Patrick Mahomes twice; Denver has forced at least one turnover from a quarterback in eight straight games; and Denver has kept quarterbacks under 7.0 yards per attempt in five straight. The mechanism offered is scheme — Denver seems to be playing a lot of heavy zone coverage, mostly Cover-3, to deter quarterbacks from throwing deep, and has started to blitz more over the last month than it did before.

The warrant that keeps Stroud in lineups is that Stroud's Houdini act has helped him beat most of the defenses he has faced and this one isn't worthy of being feared, though the article concedes a worry that Stroud may not have a monster game without massive yards after the catch from his receivers — a concern partly offset because he ranks ninth-best on the season among qualifying quarterbacks in YAC per completion at 5.37. The verdicts: STARTS — C.J. Stroud, Tank Dell, Nico Collins, Courtland Sutton, Devin Singletary, Javonte Williams and Russell Wilson; SITS — Jerry Jeudy and both defenses, with Dalton Schultz listed as OUT.
