# Old Trafford and Carrington Infrastructure Decay

Rain leaks through the roof of Old Trafford on Manchester's many wet days — the detail The Guardian used on 25 December 2023 as shorthand for the state of Manchester United. The stadium remains a storied venue, but as it continues to crumble the tale of disrepair has become a central narrative in its recent history, and the neglect of a ground dating to 1910 is presented as emblematic of the Glazers' quasi-absentee proprietorship over a decade in which the club has won zero Premier League titles.

Sir Jim Ratcliffe will invest $300m (£237m) into infrastructure, but the report calls this small beer against the multibillion-pound facelift or new venue required. The problem is not confined to the stadium: the Carrington training base is cramped, and as on Sir Matt Busby Way the footprint is large but the configuration and facilities are ageing badly.
