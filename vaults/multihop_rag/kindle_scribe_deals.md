# Kindle Scribe Deals

The ad-free Kindle Scribe is Amazon's biggest e-reader to date and a departure from past Kindle models, packing a 10.2-inch display at 300 dpi along with the same great battery life for which Kindles have become known. What separates it from other models is that it comes with one of two styli, which can be used to annotate books, doodle, or jot down notes; The Verge found the note-taking capabilities lacking in testing, but Amazon has already started to improve the software via free over-the-air updates, helping bring it up to speed with other E Ink competitors.

On pricing as of October 16th, 2023: the model with the Premium Pen, which offers a shortcut button and a built-in eraser, was on sale in the 16GB configuration at Amazon starting at $294.99, $75 off, and the 16GB model with a Premium Pen, a folio cover, and a power adapter started at $334.97, $115 off. The base Kindle Scribe Essentials Bundle — a 16GB Scribe, a Basic Pen stylus, a power adapter, and one of three fabric folio covers — was $310 at Amazon, down from $420, a 26 percent discount.
