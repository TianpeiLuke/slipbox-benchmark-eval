# Prime Day Console And Controller Deals, October 2023

The console highlight of The Verge's October 10th, 2023 Prime Day gaming roundup was the Microsoft Xbox Series X at $400, down from $500 — a 20 percent discount on a refurbished unit at Amazon. The Series X is Microsoft's flagship console, its most powerful and biggest option, focused on fast 4K gameplay, while the Series S is aimed at smooth 1440p performance and takes a disc-less approach. Also listed: Hallmark's Nintendo Entertainment System Console Ornament at $16.16, about $4 off at Amazon — a tiny NES replica holding a little copy of Super Mario Bros. that lights up and plays the classic Mario theme with sound effects when you press its power button.

Controllers made up most of the list. Razer's wired Wolverine V2 Chroma fell to a new low at Amazon at $87.99, $62 off; the Xbox controller has six remappable buttons — more than any other Xbox controller The Verge is aware of — plus clicky buttons and RGB lighting on the grips. The cheaper Razer Wolverine V2 in white, with a built-in USB cable and no RGB lighting, was $45.99 at Amazon, $54 off. The GameSir G7 wired controller for Xbox and PC was $35.99 at Amazon, $9 off, a straightforward third-party gamepad with two programmable rear buttons, clicky face buttons, and a paintable front shell attached with magnets; the GameSir T4 Kaleid was $33.59, $8 off, the first time the transparent wired controller with RGB lighting had gone on sale, with drift-free Hall effect sticks and compatibility with PC, Nintendo Switch, and Android. The 8BitDo Ultimate Bluetooth Controller was $56, down from $70, a 20 percent discount; it comes with its own charging dock, has remappable controls, works with the Nintendo Switch, Steam Deck, and Windows PCs via Bluetooth and 2.4GHz, and, unlike the 2.4GHz-only version, has drift-free Hall effect sticks. The RGB-ified PowerA Spectra Infinity Enhanced Wired Controller for Xbox and PC was $29.99 at Amazon, $15 off, and is one of the cheapest controllers around to sport hair trigger lockouts. The PDP Victrix BFG Wireless Controller for PlayStation 5, PS4, and PC, in its Call of Duty: Modern Warfare II-branded black-and-gold design, was $169.99 at Amazon, $30 off — a very versatile DualSense alternative for FPS and fighting games, with multi-stage hair triggers and sticks and face buttons that can be swapped for an Xbox-style layout or a six-button fightpad.

Steam Deck accessories also featured: the Jsaux Mod Case was $24, down from $30, a 20 percent discount — a protective modular system consisting of a base case with grippy texture, a built-in kickstand, a removable lid, and a rear mounting system that can attach a power bank using an adjustable strap or Jsaux's add-on cooling fan. Jsaux's 45W USB-C charger with a five-foot cable and included right-angle adapter, designed for the Steam Deck but also good for a Nintendo Switch or just about any USB-C phone or tablet, was $15.19 at Amazon, $3 off.
