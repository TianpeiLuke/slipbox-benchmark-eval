# Orlando Arcia's 'Atta Boy Harper' Taunt And How The Phillies Answered It

It was reported on the Monday night of the Braves' Game 2 NLDS win over the Phillies that Atlanta shortstop Orlando Arcia had taunted Bryce Harper in the clubhouse afterward, and that report generated plenty of controversy. Fox Sports' Jake Mintz — one of the two brothers who run the popular Cespedes Family BBQ account — reported that Arcia had celebrated Atlanta's 5-4 win by saying "Atta boy Harper" in the Braves' clubhouse, jeering at Harper having been doubled off on the basepaths after an outstanding catch by Michael Harris in center field and an impressive relay to get Harper at first to end the game.

The comments came back to bite the Braves two days later. Harper homered twice in Game 3 — staring down Arcia after each — as he powered the Phillies to a 10-2 win. When Philadelphia beat Atlanta in Game 4 to eliminate the Braves and advance to the NLCS, players in the Phillies clubhouse celebrated by repeating, "Atta boy Harper."
