# Noah Fant As The Showdown Punt Play

Sporting News rounded out its Week 15 2023 Eagles-Seahawks Showdown lineup with Noah Fant ($2,600) in the FLEX as a sleeper option against a defense that had struggled to contain tight ends at times that season.

The column acknowledged Fant likely needed a touchdown to make a real impact, but penciled him in for around four targets in an offense that seemingly loves to throw touchdowns to random tight ends. Given the state of the budget at that point in the lineup, it was taking a shot on Fant to deliver as a short-yardage option with the Eagles' pass rush threatening to make life difficult.
