# Kravata, LatAm Fiat-Crypto On-Ramp

Kravata is a Colombia-based, seed-stage platform in Alliance DAO's All11 cohort that provides a fiat-to-crypto on and off-ramp for LatAm, founded by Felipe Montes and Juliana Montes. Its purpose is to connect traditional financial infrastructure and fiat currencies with cryptocurrencies for businesses wanting to get into web3 in Latin America.

Its services span more than currency conversion: on- and off-ramps to convert fiat to crypto and vice versa, plus market making and cross-border transfer systems. Its investors include Circle Ventures, Framework Ventures, Alliance DAO and others, and at the time of the 2023-10-18 demo day it was raising a seed round.
