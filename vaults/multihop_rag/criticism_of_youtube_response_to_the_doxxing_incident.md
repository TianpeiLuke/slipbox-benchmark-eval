# Criticism of YouTube's Response to the Doxxing Incident

Polygon's 2023-10-25 piece rejects the adequacy of YouTube's and Shelesh's statements: the response from both failed to address many fans' concerns.

The specific objection to YouTube's framing is its symmetry — the official comment spoke about "both sides," implying responsibility on Douglass' side of the conflict.

The objection to the penalty is its weight: the demonetization feels like a slap on the wrist, which has only inspired further commentary among the community. Polygon's conclusion is that for now YouTube is content to avoid the debate around reaction content and simply profit off of it from afar.
