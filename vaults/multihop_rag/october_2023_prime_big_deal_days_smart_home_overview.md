# October 2023 Prime Big Deal Days: Smart Home Overview

Amazon's fall Prime Day event — Prime Big Deal Days, in Amazon parlance — brought another round of discounts on smart home gear, covering robot helpers to clean your floors, extra sets of eyes to watch over your property, and smart speakers to bark commands at Amazon's Alexa voice assistant. The Verge published its roundup of the best deals on good products on October 10th, 2023, with an update at 2PM ET on October 11th adding additional deals, and said it would keep updating throughout the sale event.

One category the writer singled out was the higher-end Ring smart security cameras, which were getting deep discounts: the Ring Pro 2 video doorbell and Ring Floodlight Cam Wired Pro, which add radar motion detection and better video quality over the base models, are rarely on sale and were seeing up to $100 off.
