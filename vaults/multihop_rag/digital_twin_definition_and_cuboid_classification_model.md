# Digital Twin Definition and Cuboid Classification Model

IoT Analytics defines digital twins, in its March 2023 Decoding Digital Twins article, as a virtual model replicating the behaviour of an existing or a potential real-world asset, system, or multiple systems. That definition captures a macro concept, so the report and the earlier article add a three-dimensional cuboid model to classify digital twins and allow apple-to-apple comparisons.

Each axis of the cuboid represents one dimension. The X-axis is life cycle phase: the six life cycle phases a digital twin is used for, from design to decommissioning. The Y-axis is hierarchical level: a digital twin's five hierarchical levels, from information to multi-system. The Z-axis is use or purpose of implementation: the seven most common uses for digital twins, such as simulation and prediction.

The model yields 210 potential combinations (5 x 6 x 7 = 210). Its limit, stated by IoT Analytics, is that many real digital twin initiatives are tailored to multiple combinations rather than sitting in a single cell.
