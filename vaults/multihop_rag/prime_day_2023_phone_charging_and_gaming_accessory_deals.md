# Prime Day 2023 Phone Charging And Gaming Accessory Deals

Three accessories feature in this section of WIRED's roundup: Ugreen's 145-watt power bank, the Backbone One mobile game controller, and a 20,000-mAh metal power bank for laptops and smaller gadgets.

Ugreen's charger is a 145-watt unit with a 25,000-mAh battery, surprisingly compact for the power it provides though definitely not ultralight at 1.1 pounds, with two USB-C ports and one USB-A port; what sets it apart is that you can actually draw the full 145 watts while charging — one USB-C port at 100W and the other at 45W — a feat very few other batteries WIRED has tested can manage. WIRED is a big fan of the Backbone One (8/10, WIRED recommends), which appears in its Best Mobile Game Controllers guide: it slides open to cradle your phone and is very responsive, with buttons and bumpers that feel nice and clicky, and the USB-C version on sale works with most Android phones and the new iPhone 15 range. The heavy metal brick packs 20,000 mAh and supports a wide variety of fast charging standards, with one USB-C PD port rated at 65 watts, two USB-A QC ports at 30 watts apiece, and a micro-USB input — though USB-C is best for recharging it — and WIRED hasn't seen it go on sale very often.
