# How Shameless Works: Contingency Through Team Growth, Trust Through Escalation

Shameless Media is held together by two structures — a staffing contingency plan that removes the founders as a single point of failure, and a trust relationship with the audience that deepens in stages. The contingency plan was the answer to the parenthood problem: the only way to ensure it would not be an issue was to create one, and in their case that plan involved expanding Shameless Media by growing the team and up-skilling junior employees. Neither Andrews nor McDonald sees kids in her near future, but they found it reassuring to work towards a solution.

The same honesty principle governs the partnership itself. Regardless of the topic, whether personal or work-focused, the two try to be completely honest with each other: "We realised with every honest conversation we had, our relationship was so much better – it was far more intimate," says McDonald.

The audience relationship mirrors the friendship and escalates the same way. It rests on trust — podcast episodes are meticulously well-researched, book recommendations are solid, and the advice given via the Ask Shameless newsletter aims to always hit the mark. It began with a shared interest in celebrity gossip and TV shows, before moving into more personal recommendations of books and podcasts, until eventually reaching the stage where life's most personal questions can be asked and answered. Ask Shameless lands in more than 64,000 subscriber inboxes each Friday and answers reader questions about work, dating, friendship and more; the best of these advice columns have been published in a new book, 4am: Answers to the conundrums that keep you up at night. That second book feels markedly different to their first, The Space Between, published in 2020, which explored the complications of life in one's 20s.
