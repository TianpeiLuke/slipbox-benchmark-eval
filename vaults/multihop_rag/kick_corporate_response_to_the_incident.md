# Kick's Corporate Response To The Incident

Kick addressed the sex worker stream in a public post, affirming that community and public safety "cannot be compromised" in content creation, and removed Paul Denino from the front page and featured category.

"We're continuously learning where this balance sits and are making changes daily," the post continued. "We appreciate our community for the continuous feedback, both the good & the bad. We'll keep you updated on upcoming changes to community guidelines and subsequent enforcement measures." Kick did not respond to TechCrunch's request for comment. In a statement to 404 Media, the company said it "continuously review[s] and refine[s]" its policies but may not disclose specific details "for privacy and confidentiality reasons," adding: "We aim to maintain a fair and consistent approach to content moderation while respecting the privacy of our users and employees."

Bree, another Kick streamer, described the removal of Denino from the front page and featured category as a "start." Returning to Twitch is not an option for her, she said, because of the harassment she faced on that platform.
