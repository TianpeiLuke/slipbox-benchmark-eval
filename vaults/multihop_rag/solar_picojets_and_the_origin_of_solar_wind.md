# Solar Picojets and the Origin of the Solar Wind

In August 2023, scientists got a step closer to uncovering the origin of the large outflows of solar particles, known as solar wind, that shoot out of the sun's corona.

The European Space Agency's Solar Orbiter spotted tiny jets of plasma, known as picojets, shooting from tiny dark spots on the sun known as coronal holes. The mini jets are smaller than other solar jets but still pack a punch, and researchers think that they provide the necessary energy to trigger gusts of solar wind, which streams constantly out of the sun.
