# Creepshow (1982 Horror Anthology)

Creepshow is a horror anthology classic and the Oct. 3 pick in Polygon's 2023-10-10 Halloween Countdown, listed on Kanopy. Polygon's argument is that it is a throwback to a softer, more leisurely style of horror — a middle ground between the plodding tinglers of Poe and contemporary slashers — in contrast with recent horror anthologies used to condense as much terror into a tiny package as possible, like chugging a shot glass of hot sauce.

Chris Plante notes that the new Creepshow home video release, presented in vibrant 4K UHD Blu-ray, is the best way to revisit the film — you can see individual bubbles in the foam of the ocean as it slowly drowns Ted Danson — which is why it made Polygon's list of the best Blu-rays of 2023.

He immediately undercuts that recommendation, though: while he is one of those sickos who obsesses over video resolution and bitrate, you do not need the fanciest version to enjoy the film's comedic chills, and the most aesthetically harmonious format might be a poorly treated VHS tape that recorded the film off TBS in the mid-1990s. His closing pitch is that Stephen King performs a one-person show as a hillbilly, which should be enough.
