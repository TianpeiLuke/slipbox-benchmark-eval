# The Wildsea

The Wildsea, by Felix Isaacs and Mythworks, was picked by a Polygon contributor searching for a system to kickstart a new actual-play campaign against three must-haves: a vivid world, rich character options, and simple but punchy rules.

Players embody wildsailors — swashbuckling adventurers who traverse a strange and vibrant world made of fantastical vegetation. Character generation encourages delightfully bizarre concepts such as pinwolf-taming sharpshooters trapped in amber, omen-speaking moth chefs, or collections of a thousand spiders wearing skin suits. The mechanics are structured around a d6 dice pool system with bands of success that generate interesting outcomes no matter what you roll. The contributor also praises the beautifully illustrated rulebook for not merely allowing but actively endorsing their own twists, with multiple sections devoted to alternate ways of running encounters and ideas for creating your own lore. Their group had a blast playing it both on and off stream, and were stoked but unsurprised when it won an Ennie that year; the recommendation is aimed at anyone looking for a narratively focused game with an imaginative world and mechanics that inspire cinematic play.
