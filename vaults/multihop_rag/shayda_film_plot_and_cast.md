# Shayda: Plot And Cast

Shayda is Noora Niasari's film, executive produced by Cate Blanchett, about an Iranian immigrant in Melbourne who leaves her abusive husband and takes refuge with her daughter in a women's shelter, The Guardian reported on 1 October 2023.

Its defining elements are the four central figures: Shayda (Zar Amir Ebrahimi), the Iranian immigrant who leaves her abusive husband Hossein (Osamah Sami) with her daughter Mona (Selina Zahednia) in tow; and the kindly Joyce (Leah Purcell), who at the shelter protects and guides Shayda through the tough legal process of a custody fight.

The boundary of the story, as described, is therefore the shelter and the custody fight rather than the marriage itself.
