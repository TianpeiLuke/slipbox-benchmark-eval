# How You Get The Girl As A Step-By-Step Reconciliation Tutorial

How You Get The Girl casts Taylor Swift as an agony aunt dispensing advice on how to win back someone's heart, and she framed it explicitly as instructional: "It's kind of a tutorial. All the steps you'd have to do to edge your way back into her life, because she's probably pretty mad at you," she told Radio.com. Her caveat is part of the joke: "If you follow the directions in this song, chances are things will work out… Or you may get a restraining order."

Musically it is one of the few 1989 tracks you can imagine working as a country song if it were stripped of its whip-crack snare drums and buzzing basslines — the acoustic guitar is a surprise on this album. The BBC notes that her prescribed solutions, such as standing outside their house in the rain and bombarding them with cute photos, suggest she hasn't quite weaned herself off Love, Actually.
