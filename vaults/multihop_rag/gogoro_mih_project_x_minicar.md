# Gogoro And MIH's Project X Minicar

Taiwanese battery swapping giant Gogoro came to play at the Japan Mobility Show 2023, showing off how its scooter batteries can also be used to power a tiny car: Project X, a concept built by the Foxconn-led Mobility in Harmony Consortium (MIH). The EV is a three-seater, and the spot in the backseat where you'd normally seat a fourth person is taken up by two Gogoro battery pack slots.

At the event, MIH said it aims to sell 100,000 of the minicars per year in India, Thailand and Japan starting in 2025, initially targeting fleet operators and ride-hailing services rather than individual customers. MIH says Project X supports autonomous driving Levels 2 to 4 depending on the user's needs, and the price isn't yet fixed but should top around $20,000. TechCrunch adds a caveat on 2023-10-30: it's not clear if Gogoro's swappable batteries will be used to power the vehicles going forward, since that would require Gogoro to set up a swapping network in those regions — but if so, it would signal a new revenue stream for the company, which has been struggling to reach profitability amid softening demand and large investments into international expansion.
