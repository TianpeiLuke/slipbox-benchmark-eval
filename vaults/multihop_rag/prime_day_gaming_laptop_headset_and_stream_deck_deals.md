# Prime Day Gaming Laptop, Headset And Stream Deck Deals

Three peripheral and system deals stood out in The Verge's October 10th, 2023 Prime Day gaming roundup. The Asus ROG Flow Z13 was $893 at Amazon, down from $1,300 — a 31 percent discount on a gaming tablet with a detachable keyboard, kickstand, and plenty of chops for QHD gaming.

The Sony InZone H3 was $58 at Amazon and $60 at Best Buy, down from $100, a 42 percent discount; it is the wired (3.5mm / USB-A) model in Sony's latest lineup of gaming headsets, compatible with both PlayStation and PC, and its audio profile can be customized with the InZone companion app and Sony's Spatial Sound Personalizer.

The Elgato Stream Deck Mini was $59, down from $80, a 26 percent discount. It is a smaller, six-key version of Elgato's popular macro pads, with LCD keys that can be customized to offer quick one-button macros during a livestream, or simply used to control shortcuts on a computer and connect to a smart home.
