# Amazon's Delivering The Future 2023 Event

Amazon held its Delivering the Future event in Seattle in the week before TechCrunch's 2023-10-28 report, one of three robotics events that week alongside RoboBusiness in the Bay and ROSCon in New Orleans. TechCrunch chose Amazon's, having gotten quite a bit out of the 2022 version of the event, which was held at a fulfillment center outside of Boston.

This year's event ran two days. The first was held inside the Spheres, the big, glass pair of geodesic domes outside the company's South Lake Union headquarters. The spaces are actually multifloor functional greenhouses, so it's a bit of a temperature adjustment coming in from Seattle October weather — though being inside a muggy glass structure in the rain is an opportunity one gets only 150 or so days a year.
