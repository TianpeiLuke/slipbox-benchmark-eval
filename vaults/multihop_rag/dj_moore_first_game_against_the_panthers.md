# D.J. Moore's First Game Against The Panthers

D.J. Moore faced his former team for the first time on Nov. 9, 2023, eight months after he was traded to the Bears in the deal that sent the No. 1 pick to Carolina, and he made an immediate impact against them.

At 8:19 p.m. Moore instantly made his mark with a 16-yard grab. Later, at 9:37 p.m., he made a 14-yard catch that took the Bears into Carolina territory and gave Chicago a real chance at points with 46 seconds left in the half, prompting Sporting News' remark that the Panthers could use this D.J. Moore guy.
