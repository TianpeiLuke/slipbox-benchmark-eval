# Jon Copley, Ocean Explorer

Jon Copley is a professor of ocean exploration and science communication at the University of Southampton in the U.K. who has completed dozens of dives to the darkest corners of the deep sea, and who dedicates much of his time to addressing the myriad questions and myths surrounding it.

Over the past 25 years Copley has travelled to the world's deepest hydrothermal vents, to Antarctica's icy "midnight zone" and to spectacular undersea mountain chains across the planet, and he says he is still amazed every time he encounters the strange life forms that thrive there. His new book, "Deep Sea: 10 Things You Should Know" (Orion Publishing, 2023), takes a fascinating look at some of the harshest habitats on Earth.
