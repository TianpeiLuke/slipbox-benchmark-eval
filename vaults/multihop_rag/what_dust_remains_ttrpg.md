# What Dust Remains

What Dust Remains, by 2022 Diana Jones Emerging Designer award winner Bianca Canoza — also known as Momatoes — is picked by TTRPG writer, designer and performer Danny Quach of Digital Thiccness as a game that explores themes of melancholy and loss.

Quach frames the choice through taste: despite a generally sunny disposition, big smile, and boisterous laughter, and having been deemed the internet's emotional support himbo, he loves all of his emotions, the good, bad, and ugly — emo, screamo and post-hardcore music and exploring darkness through lyrics; the gothic and macabre, and the comfort of losing something you once had; horror movies and the inherent queerness of being an outcast pushed to their limits. He played What Dust Remains at Big Bad Con 2023, taught by the designer herself, with two strangers who quickly became co-collaborators in a tragic world built in under two hours, telling a story of a legacy that was not quite within reach. Mechanically he describes impassioned and intriguing card-prompted questions alongside focused, intense moments of dice rolls that move the story along, and says the game challenged him to discover, explore, and sit in moments of despair and desperation, leaving the whole table contemplating what parts of themselves they would sacrifice to leave behind a legacy bigger than they could ever be.
