# Deontay Wilder On Pressure Ahead Of Parker Bout

Speaking during Monday's "Morning Kombat" interview ahead of the Dec. 23, 2023 card, Deontay Wilder said a win would look great on paper for both him and Joshua in terms of fighting each other, but insisted there was no added pressure on his end, as reported by CBSSports.com.

"I think a win makes it look great on paper for both of us [in terms of] fighting each other," Wilder said. "But it's no added pressure on my end. Maybe for Joshua, there's more pressure for him than I because I know what I'm coming to do. I'm blessed with God-given power and it's natural. There is nothing I have to inject my body with. There is no type of contaminated meat that I have to eat to make me feel some kind of way. I don't have to alter my gloves in any way, it's all natural to me."
