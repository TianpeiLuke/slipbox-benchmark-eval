# Oregon State Loses Its Coach While The Pac-12 Loses Ten Of Twelve Members

Oregon State began a search for Jonathan Smith's replacement at a moment of acute institutional uncertainty, CBS Sports reported on 2023-11-25. Earlier in that week, Oregon State athletic director Scott Barnes had called it the "No. 1 priority" to reach a contract extension with Smith as speculation of the move began to grow. In a statement on the Saturday after Smith's departure was finalized, Barnes thanked him for his efforts as the Beavers embarked on the search: "Coach Smith and his staff worked tirelessly to rebuild the culture at Oregon State, both on the field and in the classroom. All of Beaver Nation should thank him for the lasting legacy he has left to his alma mater and wish him and his family nothing but the best moving forward."

The departure came as the Beavers faced much uncertainty with the Pac-12 preparing to lose 10 of its 12 members ahead of the 2024 season. Oregon State and Washington State were the only schools in the conference that had not been picked up by other power leagues. Four Pac-12 members — USC, UCLA, Washington and Oregon — would join Smith in the Big Ten the following fall.
