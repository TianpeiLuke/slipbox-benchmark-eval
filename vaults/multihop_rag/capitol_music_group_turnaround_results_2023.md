# Capitol Music Group's Turnaround Results In 2023

Two years into Michelle Jubelirer's tenure, CMG had returned to calmer waters with US market share year-to-date in the high-5% region, alongside glittering successes and a promising array of new talent being primed. Jubelirer said that year-over-year market share is up by 25 per cent and that the company has moved up a notch in the label rankings, while noting she is not one to rattle off a lot of statistics. On TikTok's 2023 wrap-up lists, Capitol was the top-performing label across both the global and US lists.

Asked what she is most proud of in the past 12 months, she called it an incredible year and said that calling it the biggest, the best or the strongest would all be accurate. Her summary claim, made in the interview published 7 December 2023, is that she does not think any label group has been able to turn things around as quickly as she and her team have done in less than two years.
