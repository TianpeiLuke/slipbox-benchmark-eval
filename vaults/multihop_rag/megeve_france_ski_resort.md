# Megève: 400km Of Slopes And Mont Blanc Views

Megève is a popular French resort for cross-country trails that guarantees great skiing on 400km of slopes while exuding luxury in extravagant chalets between adventures on the pistes. Plenty of French bistros, boutiques and Michelin-starred favourites are within walking distance of traditional five-star hotels, and views of Mont Blanc complement al fresco morning coffees — this exclusive Alpine destination twinkles in front of the mountain.

Oxford Ski has a contemporary catered chalet in the heart of Megève, Chalet Ariana, that sleeps up to 10 adults and four children in six en-suite bedrooms. Standout features include an indoor swimming pool, boot warmers and proximity to the Chamois gondola. A seven-night stay, including daily housekeeping and advice from a resort expert, will set groups back a hefty €14,750 (£12,720), departing 3 December 2023. Return flights from London Luton to Geneva Airport start from £51 with Wizz Air in December.
