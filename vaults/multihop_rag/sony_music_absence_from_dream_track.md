# Sony Music's Absence From "Dream Track"

Dennis Kooker's public statements on AI arrived a week after MBW pointed out a notable lack of endorsement from Sony Music Entertainment, and its artists, of YouTube's experimental AI project "Dream Track," which lets creators clone the vocals of successful musicians.

The initial cohort of stars involved in the project included recording artists signed to Warner Music Group and Universal Music Group — or labels within each of those two majors — but no Sony Music artists. In its earlier report MBW suggested that YouTube parent Google's recent submission to the United States Copyright Office, made in response to a request for written submissions as part of a study around copyright law and policy issues raised by AI systems, may have influenced Sony's absence from Dream Track.
