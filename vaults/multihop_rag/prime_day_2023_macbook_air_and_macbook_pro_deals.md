# Prime Day 2023 MacBook Air And MacBook Pro Deals

WIRED's MacBook picks are the M1 MacBook Air, the 13-inch MacBook Pro, and the 15-inch MacBook Air.

The M1-powered MacBook Air (9/10, WIRED Recommends) is WIRED's favorite budget MacBook: a few years old but still one of the most powerful laptops at this price, fast and light, making quick work of simple office tasks, with an improved keyboard — "the one that doesn't break" — and able to handle some light video editing, though anyone needing more power should spring for a newer, beefier option. WIRED really only recommends the 13-inch MacBook Pro (7/10, WIRED Review) if you want the Touch Bar: it has the same M2 chip as the 13-inch MacBook Air, WIRED's top pick in its Best MacBooks guide, but keeps the same 13.3-inch display, 720p webcam, and two Thunderbolt USB-C ports; performance is great and battery life reliable, but it isn't suggested for processor-intensive workloads. The 15-inch MacBook Air (8/10, WIRED Recommends) is the first large-screen option in the Air family, packing the same features as the 13-inch model — redesigned chassis, 1080p webcam, M2 chip — while staying lightweight and thin, an excellent option for people who spend all day working off a laptop and don't want to plug into an external monitor.
