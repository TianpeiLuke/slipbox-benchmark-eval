# Google's News Content Payment Deal With Canada

In November 2023, the month before the Helena World Chronicle suit was filed, Google reached an agreement with the Canadian government under which the search giant would pay Canadian media for use of their content. Under the terms of the deal, Google will provide $73.5 million (100 million Canadian dollars) every year to news organizations in the country, with funds distributed based on the news outlets' headcount.

Negotiations with Meta over the same Canadian law were still unresolved as of mid-December 2023. Meta had begun blocking news in Canada in August, in light of the pressure to pay for content under the new Canadian law.
