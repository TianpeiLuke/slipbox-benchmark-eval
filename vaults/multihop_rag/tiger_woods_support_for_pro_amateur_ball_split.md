# Tiger Woods Support For Pro Amateur Ball Split

Tiger Woods supported the rollback and went further, arguing for an explicit split between a professional ball and an amateur ball, in comments reported by Essentially Sports on Dec. 3, 2023.

The framing matters: it was Woods, whose astounding power behind the shots in the late 1990s and early 2000s started the debate. Surprisingly, the fifteen-time major winner felt a course correction was necessary — "I think this should have happened a long time ago." He then said something that might not sit well with most: "I've been of the position if you play in a pro event or you have a P next to your name, you should be playing a pro ball. If you have an A next to your name and you're playing an amateur event, you should use an amateur ball."
