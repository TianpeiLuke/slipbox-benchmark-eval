# Amazon Q3 2023 Earnings Preview

Ecommerce and cloud giant Amazon was due to release its Q3 earnings on Thursday 26 October 2023 after the market close, with the quarter expected to focus on Amazon's cost-cutting initiatives, supply chain management and AI product deployment as potential positive factors for attracting investment in equities amid current market conditions.

Wall Street analysts held a consensus Strong Buy rating, but Seeking Alpha Quant Ratings assessed the company as a Hold; on a year-to-date basis Amazon shares had expanded by almost 50% in value. SA Investing Group Leader Danie Jones, bullish on the stock, wrote: "AMZN's cost-cutting initiatives and margin improvements make it a solid buying opportunity, despite concerns such as the FTC lawsuit and a weakening economy." Fellow Investing Group Leader Sensor Unlimited expected the stock to continue being range-bound in the near term due to key challenges on operating margins, competition in key growth areas, and valuation risks.

Consensus EPS estimates were $0.60 and consensus revenue estimates $141.621B; Amazon had beaten EPS and revenue expectations in half of the past 8 quarters. Also reporting that day were Kenvue, Intel, Ford, Altria Group, Merck & Co., Bristol-Myers Squibb, Mastercard, Southwest Airlines, Newmont, United Parcel Service, Comcast, Honeywell International, Chipotle Mexican Grill, United States Steel, Royal Caribbean Cruises, Northrop Grumman, International Paper, Boston Scientific, Capital One Financial, L3Harris Technologies, Peabody Energy, Hertz Global Holdings, The Hershey Company, PG&E, Hasbro, FirstEnergy, Harley-Davidson, W.W. Grainger and more.
