# Anduril's Roadrunner Counter-Air Vehicle

Anduril, the controversial defense company co-founded by Oculus founder Palmer Luckey, has developed a new product designed to take on the proliferation of low-cost, high-powered aerial threats, TechCrunch reported on 9 December 2023.

Dubbed Roadrunner, it is a modular, twin-jet-powered autonomous vertical take-off and landing air vehicle, one version of which is capable of carrying a warhead. It can take off, follow and destroy targets or — if there is no need to intercept the target — autonomously maneuver back to base for refueling and reuse.
