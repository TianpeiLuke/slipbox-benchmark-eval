# Logitech G535 Wireless Headset Deal

The Logitech G535 is another honorable mention in Engadget's gaming headsets guide, one that should specifically appeal to those who want a wireless option for less than $100 — and for Black Friday 2023 it was available for just under $80 at Amazon, B&H and Best Buy, about $25 off its usual street price.

The G535's noticeably light frame, relatively balanced sound and Bluetooth support all impress for the price. The caveats: it doesn't work with Xbox, its mic sounds a bit thinner than the wired headsets highlighted in the same guide, and its battery life clocks in at 30 to 35 hours per charge, which is decent but not great.
