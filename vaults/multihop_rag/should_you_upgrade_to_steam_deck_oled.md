# Should You Upgrade To The Steam Deck OLED?

Polygon's reviewer concedes that for an existing Steam Deck owner the OLED model is technically an incremental improvement, but argues that the screen and the battery may still justify the double dip — and that for a heavy user the upgrade is inescapable.

The grounds are the two upgrades the review treats as more than cosmetic: the display and the battery life. The warrant is a usage-dependent one, made explicit through the reviewer's own case — someone who puts hundreds of hours into the Steam Deck each year converts an incremental gain into a large absolute one, so the more you play, the more the incremental case tips over into a purchase. For readers who do not already own a Steam Deck the argument is simpler and unconditional: the reviewer repeats that this is his favorite console ever, and it just got better.
