# TikTok Users' Merch And Live Music Spending

TikTok's impact on music is not limited to streaming services: the Luminate analysis found TikTok users are also larger spenders on music off-platform, and by wide margins more likely to buy merch and attend live shows than the average consumer.

In the US, 45% of TikTok users bought music-related merch over a 12-month period, compared to 35% of overall music listeners, while 38% attended a live music event during the year, compared to 33% of overall music listeners. Overall, US TikTok users spent 22% more on music-related purchases than overall music listeners, and a similar pattern was found in the four other countries the study looked at.
