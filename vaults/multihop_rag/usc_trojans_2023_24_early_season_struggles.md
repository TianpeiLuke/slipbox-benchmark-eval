# USC Had Not Forged An Identity Eight Games Into 2023-24

Although they had played eight games, roughly a quarter of their schedule, the USC Trojans basketball team had not really forged an identity, per Sporting News on Dec. 9, 2023.

Andy Enfield's team had one of the nation's top freshmen in Isaiah Collier and a senior most college basketball fans were familiar with in Boogie Ellis, and had most recently lost to Gonzaga in Las Vegas 89-76 in a statement game — the statement being that they were not as good as they were expected to be. USC posted wins over Kansas State and Seton Hall in November, but lost a tight game to Oklahoma to end the month and then really struggled against the Bulldogs at the MGM Grand Garden Arena on Dec. 2, trailing from start to finish; Gonzaga led by as many as 18 points, overcame Ellis scoring 28, and outscored the Trojans 21-8 on fast breaks.

At that moment USC was not making the most of a talented roster, and Sporting News framed the coming weeks as a test of whether Bronny James could have a positive impact, since he was expected to make his debut on Sunday against Long Beach State.
