# Travis Kelce's Criticism That The NFL Overdid Its Taylor Swift Coverage

Travis Kelce agreed on his podcast that the National Football League was overdoing its coverage of his rumoured girlfriend Taylor Swift, undercutting the league's implicit claim that leaning into the story was simply adding to the broadcast. Speaking after his game against the New York Jets on 1 October 2023, the 33-year-old Kansas City Chiefs tight end discussed the broadcast with his brother Jason, 35, on an episode of New Heights with Jason and Travis Kelce titled "The NFL Needs to Calm Down." The two addressed complaints from longtime football enthusiasts frustrated by constant Swift references during the game at MetLife Stadium: the league had promoted the fixture by speculating about whether the Grammy winner would attend, cameras panned constantly to the star-studded suite where Swift sat with Blake Lively, Ryan Reynolds, Sophie Turner and Hugh Jackman, and the NFL ran a special segment in which Carson Daly of The Voice attempted to explain football to "Swifties."

Asked plainly by Jason whether the NFL was overdoing it, Kelce first said "I think everybody is just, like, overwhelmed," and, pressed to set aside his feelings for Swift, hedged: "I think it brings a little more to the atmosphere, it brings a little bit more to what you're watching. But at the same time, I think..." His brother, the Philadelphia Eagles player, finished the thought — "They're overdoing it" — and Kelce agreed: "They're overdoing it a little bit, for sure. Especially my situation. I think they're just trying to have fun with it." Commenters under the New Heights video echoed the complaint: "I agree the NFL needs to calm down. Pissing off football fans is not how you repay your boi [sic] for all he's done for the sport"; "Pity Taylor. All she ever wanted was to watch the football game. Now she's being blamed and hated left and right"; "The NFL needs to seriously tone it down and let Trav and Tay explore this beautiful thing they have going on in peace."
