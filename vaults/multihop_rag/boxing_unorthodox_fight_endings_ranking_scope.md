# The Scope Of talkSPORT's Craziest Boxing Fight Endings Ranking

talkSPORT's top ten craziest fight endings in boxing history, published 15 November 2023, covers bouts that ended in genuinely outlandish ways — a fighter taken out by the referee, a TV camera, or a furious ringside viewer brandishing a high-heel shoe — rather than by ordinary means.

The premise is that being knocked out by your opponent is always a danger in boxing, but the fighters in this list did not have to imagine stranger fates because they genuinely happened to them.

The boundary of the category is drawn explicitly: fights that finish in a terrible judges' decision, or with Tyson Fury crooning in his surprisingly melodic singing voice, are not counted, because for better or worse those are common events in boxing. These ten fight finales were a little more outlandish.
