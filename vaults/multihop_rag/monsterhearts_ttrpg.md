# Monsterhearts

A Polygon contributor's standout tabletop memory of 2023 was playing Monsterhearts for the first time in three years — one of the first games they ever played, to the point that for ages afterwards they kept calling playbooks in every game "skins."

The contributor explains the odd shape of the pick: choosing what to talk about was trickier than expected, because a lot of what they played that year were playtests or previews of unreleased things, which would feel more like a humblebrag than a recommendation, or were their own games, which would feel self-indulgent to suggest as the "best." The session itself was a one-shot with friends they were reconnecting with, mostly group game mastered. They played a gang of messy screw-ups who did not hugely get along; the contributor's character had traded her old gender to the fae over the summer; and the group tried to investigate a potential monster hunter, an NPC most of their characters found too cool to talk to. A running gag about someone doing a club remix of "Danny Boy" at a memorial service survives in the group chat's name: "Danny Boyz (The Pipes, The Pipes)."
