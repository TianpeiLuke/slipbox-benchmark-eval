# Britney Spears On Body Scrutiny And Diet Control

The Guardian's 24 October 2023 account of *The Woman in Me* reports that Spears' body was treated as public property "from day one" and that, once she was under the conservatorship, her father policed her weight and every calorie she ate was recorded.

Between the videos for her debut single Baby, One More Time and Sometimes, the media noted her changing physique and concluded that the 16-year-old must have had breast implants rather than simply going through puberty. She noticed that talkshow hosts asked her about her chest while her boyfriend Justin Timberlake was asked serious questions about his music. Writing about shaving her head in a salon in 2007, she frames it as a "fuck you" to impossible beauty standards: "I'd smiled politely while TV show hosts leered at my breasts, while American parents said I was destroying their children by wearing a crop top."

That brief moment of freedom did not last: a year later she was ambushed into the conservatorship, which also exercised jurisdiction over her body. "No matter how much I dieted and exercised, my father was always telling me I was fat," she writes; punishing gym routines left her feeling "out of my mind", and her diet was monitored along with every other detail of her life while she went through the motions of her Las Vegas residency. "My body was strong enough to carry two children and agile enough to execute every choreographed move perfectly onstage. And now here I was, having every calorie recorded so people could continue to get rich off my body." On the other side of the conservatorship, she writes, freedom looks like being able to gain weight without anyone "shouting at me", eating chocolate again, and posting selfies — nude or clothed — on Instagram; people who do not understand the last of these, she suggests, have not been "photographed by other people thousands of times, prodded and posed for other people's approval".
