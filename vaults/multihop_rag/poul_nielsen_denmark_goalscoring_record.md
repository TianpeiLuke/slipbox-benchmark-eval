# Poul Nielsen's Denmark Goalscoring Record

Denmark's goalscoring record technically stands for longer than Norway's, but it is no longer held outright: Poul "Tist" Nielsen scored 52 goals in 38 games for Denmark between 1910 and 1925, and his record was equalled by Jon Dahl Tomasson in 2010, as reported by The New York Times on 17 November 2023.

Tomasson — now manager of Blackburn Rovers in the English Championship — elected to retire from international football after that year's World Cup in South Africa rather than seeking to make the record his own. Nielsen's name therefore remains in the record books, although he now holds Denmark's record only jointly.
