# Google One VPN

As of early 2023, Google One subscriptions include access to the company's VPN, which works similarly to other VPNs on the list, hiding your online activity from network operators.

Google One subscribers can access the VPN in 22 countries on Android, iOS, Windows and Mac devices, and they can share VPN access with up to five people who are on their One plans.

Engadget has not fully tested the Google One VPN yet, but says it will add it to the list if it feels it is warranted.
