# The 2016 ISA Renegotiation And Its Revenue-Share Dispute

Eddy Cue testified that when Apple and Google renegotiated the ISA, Apple wanted a higher percentage of the revenue Google made from the Apple users it directed to the search engine, while Sundar Pichai wanted to keep the deal as it was; the two eventually compromised on some other number that was not disclosed in open court, and Google has been paying Apple that amount since.

Discussion of specific numbers was reserved for closed court sessions. Justice Department lawyer Meagan Bellshaw asked Cue whether he would have walked away from the deal if the two sides couldn't agree on a revenue-share figure, and Cue said he had never really considered that an option: "I always felt like it was in Google's best interest, and our best interest, to get a deal done." Cue also argued that the deal was about more than economics, that Apple never seriously considered switching to another provider or building its own search product, and that "certainly there wasn't a valid alternative to Google at the time" — adding that there still isn't one.
