# Kids' Toy And Playset Gifts 2023

The Verge's kids guide rounds up six toys: The Super Mario Bros. Movie Van Playset at $9.99+ (Amazon, Walmart, GameStop), the Pokémon Carry Case Playset at $33.99+ (Walmart, Target, Macy's), Furby at $49 (Amazon, Walmart), the Original Bazooka Bubble Gun at $32.39 (Amazon), the Nerf Perses MXIX-5000 Motorized Blaster at $59.99+ (Amazon, Walmart, Electronics Express), and the frog-green Quick Push electronic sensory fidget toy at $16.99 (Amazon).

It's been a big year for Mario — a new movie and a new Nintendo Switch game in Super Mario Bros. Wonder — so the Van Playset may scratch the itch for tykes who can't get enough of the plumber. The Pokémon Carry Case is a plastic backpack that unfolds to reveal a collapsible climbing wall, a sparring platform, and other arid environments for small two-inch plastic pokémon to play or battle in. The new Furby, a voice-activated animatronic plushie, has all kinds of responses, dances, and songs built in, and — most importantly for the guide — the resurrected '90s toy is a completely offline experience with no "smart" functionality, so a kid won't be pinging a cloud server every time they speak. The Bazooka Bubble Gun is a battery-powered blaster pumping out over 5,000 BPM — bubbles per minute. The Nerf Perses MXIX-5000 shoots soft foam balls as fast as eight pellets per second with a top-loading hopper that's equally fast and frantic to reload, with a warning to outfit kids with proper eye protection. The Quick Push froggy game with lights and sounds feels a bit like popping bubble wrap: recommended for ages three and up, and possibly a great fit for children who enjoy sensory toys.
