# Australian Christmas Retail Spending Outlook For 2023

Australian Retailers Association chief Paul Zahra said that "last year, Christmas spending was bolstered by a record freedom spending phenomenon with delayed overseas travel, whereas this year shoppers are expected to be much more conservative with their budgets", as cost-of-living pressures put a lid on spending, with shoppers tipped to cut back on hospitality, home goods and clothes purchases. The comment was The Age's quote of the day on 4 October 2023.

The same edition's "you may have missed" item recorded that Qantas' freight division has suffered a catastrophic system failure that left dead bodies, live animals and perishable food unable to be collected from airports.
