# Samsung Frame TV And Roku Streambar Deals

Two home-entertainment devices were discounted during the anti-Prime Big Deal Day sales: the 55-inch 2022 Samsung The Frame TV at $1,000, down from $1,500 — 33% off — at Best Buy, and the Roku Streambar at $100, down from $130 — 23% off.

Samsung's art-inspired 4K TV features a QLED display and an anti-glare matte display, which provides artwork and photos with qualities that resemble an actual canvas. The Roku Streambar is a 14-inch soundbar with a 4K/HDR streaming device inside it, so no separate set-top box is needed; it provides audio as well as the content you might want to watch via HDMI to your TV, and was $100 at Amazon, Best Buy and Target.
