# Shinsuke Nakamura As A CM Punk Opponent

Essentially Sports argued on 21 November 2023 that Shinsuke Nakamura is a likely opponent should CM Punk return to WWE at Survivor Series: the claim is that fans might get to see a confrontation between the two, and the warrant is that Nakamura's cryptic promos began right after he used Punk's own finisher, which fans read as a call-out.

The grounds are the promo sequence and the attack that preceded it. Recent cryptic promos by the King of Strong Style left fans scratching their heads; the promos, which started several weeks earlier, show Nakamura calling out to someone he wants to have a duel with, and the mystery opponent has not been revealed — according to reports, talents at WWE are pretty clueless as well, though fans were pretty fast to decode who it might be. At the fallout episode of Raw following Fastlane, Nakamura was particularly frustrated after his loss to Seth Rollins; as Ricochet was walking down the ramp, Nakamura attacked him with CM Punk's finisher Go To Sleep — a move surrounded by a lot of controversy but popularly used by the former WWE star to secure his wins. It was after this that Nakamura's cryptic promos started airing.

The article adds a prediction about the shape such a feud would take: as Nakamura is known to be quite persistent, a long rivalry between the two might be the centre of Punk's return arc.
