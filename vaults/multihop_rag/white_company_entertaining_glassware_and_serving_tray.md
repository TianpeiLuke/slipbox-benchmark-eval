# The White Company's Entertaining Glassware And Serving Tray

The entertaining strand of The White Company's Christmas range is built on the idea that Christmas isn't just about the Big Day but about finding an excuse to celebrate everything, from an impromptu evening of drinks to a family get-together playing games — so the brand's homeware is pitched at being always ready whenever food or drink are called for.

Two products carry the section. The Ferne Twisted Stem Champagne Coupes have hand-twisted stems that twinkle under festive lights, with hand-blown glass providing an extra touch of class to whatever sparkling drinks are served in them. For handing round drinks, canapés or snacks, the guide recommends the Barton Serving Tray: its warm, silver-tone finish has wintery good looks that set off whatever is being served, while the high-quality aluminium, crafted in India, is durable enough to survive parties or energetic family play.
