# Biggest Concert Films: The Box Office Records

"Taylor Swift: The Eras Tour" is poised to become the biggest concert film ever in about two days of release, as of 2023-10-10. Not accounting for inflation, the mark belongs to 2011's "Justin Bieber: Never Say Never," which took $73.1 million across its entire run.

Accounting for inflation, the comparison is much harder: it will be more difficult for "The Eras Tour" to catch "Woodstock," which grossed $50 million in 1970, a total that translates to nearly $400 million today.
