# Ratcliffe-Glazer Ownership Split and Control Risk

The structure The Guardian described on 25 December 2023 is a split in which Sir Jim Ratcliffe, having paid an overpriced £1.3bn for a 25% stake, obtains a major element of control while the Glazers remain majority owners. The 71-year-old oversees football policy.

The relation that makes this unstable is that football policy and commercial policy are not separable: the commercial side affects football decisions and vice versa. Ratcliffe accepts that Manchester United have an impressive revenue-based model that makes the club akin to a cash machine, but he is not enamoured of how the money generated is invested — primarily in the squad.

The report frames the failure mode as a concrete question rather than a certainty: what happens if Ten Hag wants a new midfield ace, Ratcliffe agrees, but the Glazers cite a downturn in income and thus seek to block the signing? The model holds only so long as majority ownership does not exercise its veto over the football authority Ratcliffe has bought.
