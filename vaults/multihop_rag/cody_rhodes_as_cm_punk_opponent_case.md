# Cody Rhodes As A CM Punk Opponent

Essentially Sports argued on 21 November 2023 that a CM Punk return sets up a potential all-time classic rivalry with Cody Rhodes: the claim is that if Punk were to follow Rhodes to WWE and steal his thunder as he did in AEW, it's unlikely the American Nightmare would take kindly to it, and the warrant is Rhodes' own public bitterness about who actually executed the wrestling revolution Punk described.

The historical grounds come first. Both stars were part of All Elite Wrestling but never got a chance to spar there, though Rhodes and Punk had a bout in WWE during their initial days and brawled on a couple of occasions: one was during a tag team match in which Rhodes and his partner Ted DiBiase Jr., then World Tag Team Champions, lost their title to CM Punk and his partner Kofi Kingston, and the two also tussled in front of the 2008 Intercontinental Champion, William Regal. That altercation ended with The American Nightmare as the winner of the fight, and it has been more than a decade since, with Rhodes having grown leaps and bounds.

The attitudinal grounds are Rhodes' own words. In one of his last promos in AEW before departing for WWE, Rhodes said Punk laid the roadmap for a wrestling revolution: "He listed things that for me, were taboo at the time, like going to New Japan, working for Ring of Honor, he talked about teaming with the Young Bucks." He then said it wasn't Punk who executed the revolution: "He was unable to do those things…In his absence, in the void that CM Punk left behind, somebody did do each and every one of those things, I did them! I held every grain of that revolution in my hands and you cheered!" From those words it seems clear Rhodes harbours a degree of bitterness toward Punk's fan welfare, feeling Punk just spoke of a wrestling revolution whereas he was the one to actually execute it. With both being masters on the mic, the article says this has the makings of an all-time classic rivalry, one fans had been clamouring for while the two were signed to AEW.
