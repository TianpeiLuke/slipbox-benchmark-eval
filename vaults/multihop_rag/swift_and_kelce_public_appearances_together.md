# Swift And Kelce's Public Appearances Together

Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce appeared together publicly through the autumn of 2023, at his games, at her concerts, and away from both. On 22 October Swift returned to Arrowhead Stadium to watch the Kansas City Chiefs beat the Los Angeles Chargers — her fourth time attending one of Kelce's games amid ongoing reports that they were dating — and the pair seemingly confirmed the relationship there. Afterwards Chariah Gordon, the partner of Kelce's teammate Mecole Hardman Jr, posted an Instagram picture of Swift standing next to Kelce and reaching to kiss his cheek. Kelce also responded warmly when a fan shouted "Let's go Taylor's boyfriend" while he waited for an elevator, smiling slightly and pumping his fist into the air.

The couple were also photographed together away from stadiums, including a date earlier that month in New York City after their surprise appearances on the season 49 premiere of Saturday Night Live. Sources alleged to Entertainment Tonight that during that date Kelce got too close to Swift's security personnel and told her bodyguard to step aside; on his podcast Kelce explained what he had said and denied pushing anyone: "I placed my hand on the gentleman's back to let him know I was behind him. If I would have pushed him, he probably would have turned around and tased me." Kelce reciprocated the support in November by travelling to Buenos Aires, Argentina, for one of her Eras Tour shows, where Swift changed her "Karma" lyrics to "Karma is the guy on the Chiefs, coming straight home to me," laughing as fans screamed; after the show the "Style" singer was seen running into his arms and kissing him before he whisked her backstage.
