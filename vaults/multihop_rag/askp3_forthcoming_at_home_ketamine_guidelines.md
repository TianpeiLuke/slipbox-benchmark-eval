# ASKP3's Forthcoming Guidelines For At-Home Ketamine Use

ASKP3 announced that it will soon publish guidelines for safe use of the drug.

The organization tied the decision directly to the Perry case and pre-committed to narrow conditions: "In the wake of Matthew Perry's autopsy report, we are committed to creating and publishing guidelines for at-home ketamine use, which should only be practiced with a very specific patient profile and only in the context of a close relationship with a physician and in-office follow-up," the organization said in a released statement.
