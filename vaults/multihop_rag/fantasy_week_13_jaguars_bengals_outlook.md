# Week 13 Jaguars-Bengals: Jacksonville's Defense Is Legit And Should Overwhelm Jake Browning

CBS Sports argues that the Jaguars defense should be accepted as a legit unit and that it will overwhelm a Bengals offense operating with Jake Browning at quarterback. The grounds for treating Jacksonville's defense as real: its ability to slam on the run, pressure C.J. Stroud for four sacks and ultimately hold the Texans to 21 points. The grounds for doubting Cincinnati: the Bengals offense was borderline lucky to even score 10 points last week against the Steelers — two of Ja'Marr Chase's four receptions were tipped by Steelers first, and the Bengals' longest pass play was a designed dump-off to Joe Mixon for 39 yards. On Browning specifically, he had his moments evading the pass rush but his arm strength is just not very good.

The warrant joining the two halves is that a defense that can pressure a quarterback and stop the run, facing a passing attack whose limitation is arm strength, should dictate the game: expect the Jaguars to bring plenty of heat to Browning, then lean into Travis Etienne on the ground to bring home a win.
