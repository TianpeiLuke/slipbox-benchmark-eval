# Prime Day 2023 Smart Lighting And Plug Deals

WIRED's smart lighting picks are a Govee lamp, Kasa mini smart plugs at $6 each (usually around $17), a Philips Hue starter kit, a Govee diffused light strip, a four-pack of WIRED's favorite smart bulbs, and a hexagon light kit.

The Govee lamp was selected as the best lamp in WIRED's Govee Buying Guide, with a modern design, support for Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant, and control via phone or the included remote, at a price matching the lowest seen. A smart plug turns anything plugged into an outlet into a smart device, and WIRED's favorite mini smart plug from Kasa is easy to control and create routines with, working with Google Assistant, Amazon Alexa, and Samsung SmartThings. Philips Hue's smart bulb starter kit isn't a cheap investment and WIRED usually only recommends it as an upgraded kit, but it is a little cheaper right now for three bulbs, a smart switch, and Philips' smart home hub, usable with older Philips bulbs and other third-party accessories — the lowest deal seen on this kit in months. Govee makes some of WIRED's favorite smart lighting, and this diffused light strip is its pick for installation on a wall, stairs, or anywhere in plain view, coming with adhesive brackets so you can make curved shapes like clouds, supporting countless effects in the Govee app and voice commands from Google Assistant or Alexa. The four-pack of smart bulbs is the best price WIRED has seen all year: easy to use, beautifully vibrant, with a variety of preset colors and Alexa and Google Assistant support. The hexagon light kit adds fun ambient lighting to any room, with each hexagon its own light so you can customize designs and color schemes on your wall — just grab a level to put them on straight.
