# Josh Allen Bills Midseason MVP

Yardbarker's Nov. 9, 2023 AFC midseason team MVP list named quarterback Josh Allen the Buffalo Bills' MVP, arguing his value to the Bills was immeasurable even though he probably would not finish in the top three of NFL MVP balloting as he had the previous season.

The grounds are both efficiency and share of the offense: through nine games he was on pace to set a career high in completion rate at 71.3% and topped all quarterbacks in QBR at 75.3, and he had virtually carried the offense by himself, accounting for 24 of the team's 28 offensive touchdowns. The warrant is that team MVP measures indispensability to one's own team rather than standing in a league-wide award race.
