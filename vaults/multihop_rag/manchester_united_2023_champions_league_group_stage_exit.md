# Manchester United's 2023 Champions League Group Stage Exit

Ten Hag's United compiled arguably the worst performance by any English team in a Champions League group stage in 2023, finishing bottom of their group and shipping 15 goals — more than any other English team ever.

The Guardian conceded it was no Blackburn Rovers, but gave extra marks for United's pedigree, for the fact the group really was not much of a group, and for finishing bottom. There were also many moments of farce along the way, a six-part saga of chaos, collapse and laughter in the dark: Onana diving out of the way of the ball in Munich, Onana again diving out of the way of the ball in Istanbul, Casemiro's red card, lost leads in Manchester, and Marcus Rashford unfairly convicted of a venomous stamp in Copenhagen. And yet, with both group games blank at half-time on the final night, United were still just one goal away from going through to the last 16. Bayern, for all their own flaws, were basically a good version of whatever United were attempting to piece together: Kimmich-Goretzka-Musiala an A-grade version of Amrabat-McTominay-Fernandes, Manuel Neuer the thing Onana is trying so earnestly to be, and Bayern actually did go ahead and sign Harry Kane. From there United's season continued to narrow — they could not win the league, the Champions League or the Carabao Cup, leaving the FA Cup and the extended pursuit of fourth or even fifth place, although the state of the coefficient meant the thing that might keep Manchester United out of Europe the next season could be Manchester United being so bad in Europe that season.
