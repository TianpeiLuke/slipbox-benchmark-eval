# Dispute Over Readiness of AI Content Detection Tooling

The disagreement is narrow and it targets timing, not design: whether the tooling needed for a Content ID-style system for AI-generated content already exists. Robert Kyncl cautioned at the Code Conference that the technology to develop such a system is "not yet developed, but… people are working on that."

Music Business Worldwide noted that Believe CEO Denis Ladegallerie might disagree. In comments earlier in 2023, Ladegallerie said the tools to recognise and flag AI-generated content are just about ready to go, and that he expects to see them implemented in the coming few quarters. The premise under attack is therefore Kyncl's readiness assessment — both men accept that fingerprinting-style detection is the answer, but Ladegallerie places its availability within a few quarters rather than in an undeveloped future.
