# Foster Rejects The Favourites Label Against Argentina

Although the All Blacks were 18-point favourites for the opening 2023 World Cup semi-final, Ian Foster rejected the premise that his side sees itself that way: "Firstly, you've never heard us say we're favourites."

His grounds for rejecting the label are recent results and a limit on what the past predicts. Argentina had knocked over the All Blacks the previous year in Christchurch, as well as inflicting a maiden defeat in 2020 in Sydney, and New Zealand had themselves suffered a shock semi-final loss to Eddie Jones' England in 2019. Foster also argued that familiarity does not transfer to this stage: "The past sets us up beautifully for both teams. We do know each other but we don't know each other in terms of a Rugby World Cup, so at this stage in the tournament it's new territory for us and as we've seen World Cups are very different."

He then converted the rejection into a demand on his own team: "We have learnt to greatly respect Argentina. They have a rich history of overachieving at the World Cup and have done a fantastic job to get here at the same level we are, so it is going to be a heck of a game." And: "We know these games are do or die. We're going to have to improve our performance. The stage gets bigger at this time in the tournament and you've got to grow your game. That's our strong focus. The best team on the night wins – Argentina has done that to us."
