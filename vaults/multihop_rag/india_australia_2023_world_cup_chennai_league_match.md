# India vs Australia In The 2023 World Cup League Match

The most recent clash between the two sides before the 2023 final was the league match of that same World Cup, which India won by six wickets. Australia won the toss, chose to bat first and posted a target of 200 runs, being bowled out in 49.3 overs; David Warner scored 41 runs off 52 balls and Steven Smith 46 runs off 71 balls. For India, Ravindra Jadeja picked up three wickets, with Kuldeep Yadav and Jasprit Bumrah picking up two each.

India were in a terrible position at the start of the second innings as both openers got out for a duck and India lost three wickets for just two runs. Two brilliant and sensible innings by KL Rahul and Virat Kohli, who scored 97 runs off 115 balls and 85 runs off 116 balls respectively, took India to shore.
