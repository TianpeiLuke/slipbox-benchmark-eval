# Bryson DeChambeau Opposition To Golf Ball Rollback

Bryson DeChambeau attacked the rollback on the ground that it penalizes earned skill rather than correcting an equipment problem, telling Essentially Sports' Dec. 3, 2023 account: "It's a great handicap for us guys that have worked really hard to learn how to hit it farther."

Echoing sentiments similar to Justin Thomas's, DeChambeau — who joined LIV Golf in 2022 — has a direct stake: the 2020 U.S. Open champion regularly crushes drives to 310-320 yards. He termed the policy "atrocious."
