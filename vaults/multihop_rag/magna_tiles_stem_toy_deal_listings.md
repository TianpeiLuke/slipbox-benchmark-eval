# Magna-Tiles STEM Toy Deal Listings

Magna-Tiles appear in two 2023 roundups: The Verge's kids gift guide lists the Jungle Animals 25-piece set at $39.99 (Amazon, Walmart, Macy's), and WIRED's Prime Day roundup lists a construction set that builds a road and cranes.

The Verge's pitch is that everything is more fun with magnets — just ask any four-year-old — and that the jungle-themed set unites two things most kids love, animals and magnets; how they work, who knows, and nobody is going to care when there's a caiman and a magnetic sloth this adorable involved. WIRED calls Magna-Tiles addicting for just about any kid, in the best kind of way, and a STEM toy it is a major fan of for a variety of ages, with this set a great choice for any construction enthusiast or as an add-on for current Magna-Tiles owners.
