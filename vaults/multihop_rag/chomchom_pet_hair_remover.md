# ChomChom Pet Hair Remover

The ChomChom is a reusable cat and dog hair remover that works great for furniture. It was listed at $20, down from $32 — 38 percent off — in The Verge's under-$25 Black Friday roundup, and at $25, down from $32 — 22 percent off — in the staff favorites roundup the next day, where it is former news writer Mitchell Clark's pick.

It is an extraordinarily simple device — basically a couple of pieces of plastic, fabric, and rubber — but through some dark magic it is better at getting cat hair off his couch, cat tree, and other upholstery than even adhesive-laden lint rollers, though those are still superior if the thing you are trying to de-pet is yourself.

Clark concedes he is stretching the definition of "tech," but had to share the ChomChom because the first time he saw someone use it his jaw dropped, and he hopes it can change your life like it did his. One note: as far as he can tell, the limited-edition cat ChomChom he paid extra for is 0 percent more functional than the regular one — still worth it, though.
