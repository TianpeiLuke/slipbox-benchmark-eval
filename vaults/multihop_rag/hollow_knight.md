# Hollow Knight

In Hollow Knight you are a lonely knight wandering the crumbling halls of an enormous, wending kingdom — home to a defunct civilization of bugs — armed with only a nail, though your arsenal of attacks and powers grows the more you explore and defeat bosses.

Polygon calls it one of the best Metroidvanias, and Souls-likes, on the Switch, crediting its precise platforming mechanics, its memorable cast of characters, and a sprawling map full of secrets. The specific pleasure it names is discovery: there is no better feeling than doing a little digging and then finding an entirely new region on the map — and, the guide adds, you will want to take the game with you.
