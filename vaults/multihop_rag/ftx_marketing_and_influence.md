# FTX's Marketing And Political Influence

FTX bought mainstream recognition through sports branding and paid celebrity partnerships, while Bankman-Fried separately cultivated political access on both sides of American politics — including, according to Lewis, a contemplated $5 billion offer to keep Donald Trump out of the next presidential race. TechCrunch documented the marketing side on 2023-10-01 and the political side on 2023-10-02.

On the branding side, the company gained somewhat mainstream recognition with branding deals and partnerships: in 2021 it bought the naming rights for the Miami Heat's home arena, it got its name branded on Major League Baseball umpires' polos, and it partnered with celebrities including Tom Brady and his ex-wife Gisele Bündchen, as well as Steph Curry, Shaquille O'Neal and Naomi Osaka, among others. The 2023-10-02 account puts figures on two of those friendships and lucrative marketing deals: Tom Brady was paid $55 million and Steph Curry $35 million, to "lend FTX legitimacy and edge." Even Anna Wintour, the fashion doyenne, was in contact — she reached out over a Zoom meeting to ask SBF, whose preferred get-up was wildly curly hair, t-shirts and cargo shorts, to sponsor her Met Costume Ball.

On the political side, Bankman-Fried had close ties to U.S. regulators and government officials, many of whom he donated to. Lewis said that he met with the young crypto king ahead of him seeing Mitch McConnell to discuss how he could fund political candidates who were looking to offset Trump's influence in the Republican Party — a meeting for which the suit SBF brought was tightly balled up under his arm with his dress shoes falling out of the middle of the bundle. Lewis also claimed that SBF had concocted a plan to buy off Trump, to get him not to run again for president. "Like how much would it take to get an answer?" Lewis recounted; "There was a number that was kicking around… this was $5 billion." Lewis said Sam was never sure whether that figure came directly from Trump, and TechCrunch notes it is not known whether anything was ever wired. The giving was not one-sided: SBF also looked to back candidates on the Democratic side of the aisle.
