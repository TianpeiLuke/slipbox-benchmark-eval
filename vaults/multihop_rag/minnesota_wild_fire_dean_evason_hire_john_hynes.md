# Minnesota Wild Fire Dean Evason And Hire John Hynes

The Minnesota Wild relieved head coach Dean Evason and assistant coach Bob Woods of their duties effective immediately, the team said in a statement on Monday evening, with The Athletic's Michael Russo reporting that the Wild would name former Devils and Predators bench boss John Hynes as Evason's successor — reported by Yardbarker on Nov. 27, 2023.

Wild GM Bill Guerin's statement read: "Dean did an excellent job during his tenure with the Minnesota Wild, especially as Head Coach of our team. I am very thankful for his hard work and dedication to our organization. I would also like to thank Bob for his hard work during his time as an Assistant Coach with the Wild. I wish Dean, Bob and their families all the best in the future." Woods, who had worked on the Capitals' bench with Evason briefly as an assistant in the early 2010s, was now on the open market after more than six seasons with the Wild, and it was unclear whether the Wild would opt to fill his vacancy. The Wild would be on the hook for the remainder of Evason's contract, which ran through 2024-25 at just under $2M per season.
