# ChatGPT's Hallucinated Legal Filing Incident

ChatGPT's tendency to hallucinate facts and figures was exposed that month when a lawyer in New York was caught using the generative AI to do "legal research."

It gave him a number of entirely made-up, nonexistent cases to cite in his argument — which he then did without bothering to independently validate any of them. As Engadget put it on 2023-11-30: the judge was not amused.
