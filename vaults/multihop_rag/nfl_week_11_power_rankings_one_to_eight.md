# NFL Week 11 Power Rankings, 1-8

Sporting News' Week 11 power rankings, published Nov. 13, 2023 after a Week 10 in which five teams won on walk-off field goals and many games came down to the wire, put the Philadelphia Eagles first at 8-1 (previous week: 1). The Eagles enjoyed a bye trying to get healthier for a difficult stretch run in a top-heavy NFC, with games at the Chiefs (the Super Bowl 57 rematch), against the Bills, against the 49ers and at the Cowboys in the next four; they still looked like the conference team to beat, having proved they win in many ways around Jalen Hurts.

The Kansas City Chiefs were second at 7-2 (3), having had a good week off as they jumped back into the AFC's top seed and home-field advantage spot with the Ravens losing; already rolling defensively, they were expected to figure out more around Patrick Mahomes after the key Andy Reid bye. The Detroit Lions were third at 7-2 (5), becoming like the Eagles and thus the second-best team in the NFC: able to win shootouts with Jared Goff, Amon-Ra St. Brown and the passing game, to dominate with the running game, and to win with offense or defense, with Dan Campbell named coach of the year. The San Francisco 49ers were fourth at 6-3 (8) after a surprise offensive get-well game in Jacksonville with Brock Purdy leading the way and all his key supporting weapons doing damage even without Christian McCaffrey scoring, the biggest development being the defense going back to dominating a good offense.

The Baltimore Ravens were fifth at 7-3 (2): their offense was fine with Lamar Jackson's running and passing, but he could not afford big mistakes, which he made against the Browns, and the defense needed to pick up the pieces ahead of facing the Bengals on a short week. The Miami Dolphins were sixth at 6-3 (6), taking a much-needed bye to figure out how to beat the better teams, with a defense that should be getting better to support an often explosive offense and a likely Week 11 rebound against the Raiders. The Pittsburgh Steelers were seventh at 6-3 (9), playing the way Mike Tomlin wants with the running game rolling to big totals behind Jaylen Warren and Najee Harris and the defense doing what it needed, more against the pass than the run, in key moments. The Cleveland Browns were eighth at 6-3 (10), having proved they can win with defense in another way — with big plays — while showing more offensive pop when Deshaun Watson plays well and the running game does not slump, their playoff hopes looking much better after a stunning comeback against the Ravens.
