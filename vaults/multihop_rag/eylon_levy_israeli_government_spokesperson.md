# Eylon Levy, Israeli Government Spokesperson

Eylon Levy is a government spokesperson to the international media at the National Public Diplomacy Directorate in the Prime Minister's Office, whose role since 7 October 2023 has been to battle day and night on screens to make the world acknowledge the atrocities and the reality forced on Israel. He is 32 years old, a Jewish kid born and raised in London to parents who had moved there from Israel, who dreamed of serving as a spokesperson for the State of Israel: he would sit watching the then Israeli ambassador to the UK Mark Regev on television and tell himself, "That's what I want to do when I grow up."

He tells Globes that it has always been his dream to act as a spokesperson for the state, that it is a shame it is in such painful circumstances, and that "the legendary Mark Regev and myself have been working shifts on the TV channels worldwide", the same Regev he watched as a child in London. The discriminating feature of his position as of the 3 December 2023 interview is his sudden public recognition: back in October few people had heard of Levy, but sitting in a café in the Azrieli Mall in Tel Aviv for the interview, passersby clearly recognise him and some even stop to take a selfie.
