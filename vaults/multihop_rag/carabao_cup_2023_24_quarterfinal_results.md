# Carabao Cup 2023/24 Quarterfinal Results

Fulham, Chelsea, Middlesbrough and Liverpool won through the 2023/24 Carabao Cup quarterfinals, with two of the four ties settled on penalties, per Sporting News on Dec. 19, 2023.

The quarterfinal draw was held at Old Trafford on Wednesday, November 1, after the conclusion of the final match in round four between Manchester United and Newcastle United, and all matches took place in the week beginning December 18, 2023.

The results were: Tuesday, Dec. 19, Everton 1-1 Fulham, Fulham advancing 7-6 on penalties; Tuesday, Dec. 19, Chelsea 1-1 Newcastle United, Chelsea advancing 4-2 on penalties; Tuesday, Dec. 19, Port Vale 0-3 Middlesbrough; Wednesday, Dec. 20, Liverpool 5-1 West Ham.
