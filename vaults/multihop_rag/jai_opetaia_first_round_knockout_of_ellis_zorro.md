# Jai Opetaia's First-Round Knockout Of Ellis Zorro

Jai Opetaia knocked out Ellis Zorro in the first round in Saudi Arabia early on Sunday morning AEDT, as reported by The Roar on 23 December 2023, moving the unbeaten Australian to 24-0 with 19 stoppages and retaining his Ring Magazine belt.

The finish came with four seconds remaining in the first round, a huge left hand obliterating Zorro; the punch sent the dazed Englishman into the ropes and on to the canvas, and the outclassed Zorro was eventually able to offer a sheepish smile in what was his first loss in 18 professional fights. The bout was reportedly worth $680,000 to the Australian, who was forced to vacate his IBF world title belt to take his place on the mega-card in Riyadh, capping a week of drama in which he was stripped of his IBF status in controversial circumstances.

Opetaia, a former Olympian, said afterwards: "I train for 12 rounds, was prepared for 12 and if the knockout comes, it comes. He was a bit too hesitant and he got clipped, that's the way it goes." Commentator Sergio Mora described the one-punch knockout as "devastating and scary" while fellow pundit Mike Costello added: "At any weight, at any level that's as clinical as it gets in this sport," and "He dismissed Zorro with the minimum of fuss and the maximum of power."
