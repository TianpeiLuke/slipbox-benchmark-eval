# Tottenham And Chelsea's Next Fixtures After Their November 2023 Meeting

After their November 2023 meeting, Tottenham were to continue their push for the Premier League title with a trip to Wolves on Saturday 11 November, with kick-off at Molineux set for 12:30pm GMT, live on TNT Sport 1 in the UK.

Chelsea were to host Spurs' title rivals, Manchester City, on Sunday 12 November at 4:30pm at Stamford Bridge, live on Sky Sports in the UK.
