# Emma Hayes As The Highest-Paid Women's Soccer Coach

The deal agreed between U.S. Soccer and Emma Hayes will make her the highest-paid women's football coach in the world, though her salary is not tied to equal compensation with USMNT coach Gregg Berhalter, as reported by The New York Times on 15 November 2023.

The framing given is market value rather than parity: while her salary is in the same range as the USMNT head coach's, it's thought to be a reflection of the market value for Hayes, with reports that Chelsea was prepared to quadruple her salary to keep her — a detail Hayes herself danced around in her first media availability with the club.

The comparison figures come from U.S. Soccer's annual financial reports, which reveal the salaries of head coaches and other executives. While Berhalter received a new contract that year, his previous deal, running from April 2021 to March 2022, earned him $1.6 million including $300,000 in bonuses; during that same period Vlatko Andonovski earned $446,495, of which only $50,000 was bonus money. With Hayes expected to earn close to $2 million per year, the report expects this to create a knock-on effect for other international women's coaches negotiating their next contracts.
