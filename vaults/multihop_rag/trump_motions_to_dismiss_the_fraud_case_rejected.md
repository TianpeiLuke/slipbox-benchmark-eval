# Trump Motions to Dismiss the Fraud Case Rejected

Trump's lawyers asked the judge to throw out the case, and he denied the request. Their arguments attacked the attorney general's standing and the timing of the claims: they contend that James was not legally allowed to file the lawsuit because there is no evidence that the public was harmed by Trump's actions, and they also argued that many of the allegations in the lawsuit were barred by the statute of limitations.

Engoron, noting that he had "emphatically rejected" those arguments earlier in the case, equated them to the "time-loop in the film 'Groundhog Day.'"
