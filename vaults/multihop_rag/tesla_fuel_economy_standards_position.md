# Tesla's Fuel Economy Standards Position

Tesla has urged the Biden administration to adopt stricter fuel economy standards than the NHTSA has proposed, TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-22. The position stands out because most other automakers have already said the NHTSA's proposal was unfeasible.

TechCrunch reads the move competitively: Tesla's call on regulators to double down seems to be yet another way the EV maker can one-up its competitors.
