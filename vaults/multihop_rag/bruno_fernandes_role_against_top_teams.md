# Bruno Fernandes' Role Against Top Teams

Erik ten Hag argued on 28 October 2023 that Bruno Fernandes' contribution has to change against top opposition: he is at his best with a lot of the ball, so in games where United have less of it his job becomes taking whatever attacking moments arise and otherwise working hard for the team.

"If he does [score] again, I'll be happy," the manager said. "Bruno is an [effective] player when we have a lot of the ball. Then he is at his best. And there are games against top teams when you have less of the ball. So then it is about taking benefit from [any attacking] moments and for the rest you have to work hard for the team. And to get a result because that is the most important, that the team is winning." The warrant is that winning outranks individual effectiveness, so the role adapts to possession rather than the other way round. The Guardian illustrated the point with Fernandes volleying home against Burnley — one of two goals he had scored for the club that season.
