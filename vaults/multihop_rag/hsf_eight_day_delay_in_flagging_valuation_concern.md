# The Eight-Day Delay In Flagging The Valuation Concern

MBW argues that the HSF board had known about Citrin Cooperman's latest valuation, as of 30 September, for at least the past eight days before telling investors it was unhappy with it. The evidence is the board's own earlier disclosure: on 11 December 2023 HSF announced that the $23.1 million price of an agreed "non-core" asset sale represented a 14.2% discount versus Citrin Cooperman's latest valuation of the portfolio, which means the board had by then seen that latest valuation.

The warrant is that a board cannot quote a discount against a valuation it has not received, so the 11 December announcement fixes the earliest date of knowledge. Yet it took until 19 December for HSF to alert its investors to its dissatisfaction with the valuation.
