# DJI Osmo Action 3 Creator Combo Deal

As part of a larger Black Friday 2023 sale on DJI cameras and accessories, the DJI Osmo Action 3 was discounted as a bundle including two batteries, a 32GB microSD card and a case: separately and not on sale the set would run $379, but it came down to $319.

This isn't the newest version of DJI's GoPro alternative camera — the Osmo Action 4 wasn't on sale — and the newer version has a larger sensor and improved dynamic range at a higher price; but for anyone not planning to shoot a lot of low-light footage, the Action 3's performance may satisfy. Engadget put it through its paces in its review and liked the excellent video quality, finding it on par with GoPro's Hero 10, with the magnetic clip mount and long-lasting, fast-charging battery also reasons to buy.
