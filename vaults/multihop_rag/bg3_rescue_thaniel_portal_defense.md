# Rescuing Thaniel And Defending The Portal

With the Battered Lute in hand, walk up to Art Cullagh and accept the prompt to play him a song — proficiency with an instrument is not required, since your character will just dopily pluck away at the strings until Art wakes up. The stage ends with Halsin offering to join your crew.

After Halsin talks to Art, he decides he needs to peer into another dimension to rescue Thaniel. Follow him to the shore and get ready for a fight: let Halsin go into the portal on his own and prepare to defend it, because a massive army of undead creatures appears on the shore and starts trying to reach it. You need to hold them off for multiple rounds of combat to progress the quest. Polygon recommends Shadowheart here, since she can use Turn Undead against all of the creatures, and rates Gale an excellent choice too because his large AoE abilities like Fireball can take out many of the low-health critters at once. Once you have successfully defended the portal for the required time, all of the undead burn up and Halsin returns from the portal carrying Thaniel's body. The Druid asks you to come back to camp and talk; meet him there and he finally offers to join your crew to finish rescuing Thaniel.
