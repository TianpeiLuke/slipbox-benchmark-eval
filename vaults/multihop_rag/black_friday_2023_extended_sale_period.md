# Black Friday 2023's Extended Sale Period

Black Friday was almost here as of 2023-11-19, but Engadget wouldn't blame readers for feeling like it had been here for a while: what was once a one-day event filled with limited-time deals has grown into a month-long event with a plethora of sales to sift through each day. This year, Amazon, Target, Best Buy and Walmart and several other retailers kicked off their Black Friday deals early, with some starting as soon as late October.

To help with the overwhelm, Engadget collected all of the best Black Friday deals available right now, primarily on tech it has tested and reviewed. It notes there's a possibility of slightly lower prices on Black Friday proper — but with each passing day that likelihood becomes less and less as even more early Black Friday deals pop up across the web — and says it will be updating its list regularly in the lead-up to Black Friday.
