# Five MLB Teams Were Hunting For A Manager At The End Of 2023

2023 was a topsy-turvy year for many teams, and amid the heavy restructuring that many MLB clubs had seen of late there were, as Essentially Sports put it on 2023-10-30, pentagonal vacancies for managers. Five teams were in dire need of filling the managerial position as the year ended: the New York Mets, the Los Angeles Angels, the Houston Astros, the Cleveland Guardians and the San Diego Padres. Each was at a crossroads with a common stifling problem.

The searches differed in approach and in obstacles. While some team representatives skimmed through names within the MLB world, some hoped to look for an outside hire — a potentially risky state for all of them. There were many possibilities and roadblocks one might encounter: budgetary issues, past performance, or availability of candidates had left these teams unable to put a pin on one candidate, though the skipper market was abuzz. With the facts broken down, the article judged it a matter of time before the teams narrowed down their best options, with the MLB world welcoming 2024 with high expectations and fans expecting teams to come back with great vigor and ferocity under new management.
