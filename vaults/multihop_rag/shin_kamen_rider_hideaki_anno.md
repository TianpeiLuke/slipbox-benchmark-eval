# Shin Kamen Rider (Hideaki Anno)

Shin Kamen Rider is Hideaki Anno's film starring Sosuke Ikematsu, Minami Hamabe, and Tasuku Emoto, which Polygon calls the best superhero movie of 2023 — one that came from neither Marvel nor DC. It ranked 50 on the outlet's best movies of 2023 list of 2023-10-07 and streams on Prime Video under the title Shin Masked Rider.

Polygon's Pete Volk qualifies the praise: the film might not reach the soaring heights of Shin Godzilla.

What it delivers instead is an incredibly fun time bolstered by terrific costume designs, inventive action sequences, and a delightfully bizarre tone, all while looking gorgeous throughout.
