# Juan Soto Padres Trade Candidacy

Juan Soto, the Padres' left fielder, was ranked No. 1 on Yardbarker's Nov. 13, 2023 list of the top 25 MLB offseason trade candidates: other than Shohei Ohtani's free agency, there may have been no bigger story of the offseason than Soto's future in San Diego, as he entered his final year of club control projected for a $33M salary that would be the highest ever for an arbitration-eligible player.

The Padres were scaling back payroll, perhaps by as much as $50M, and needed multiple starting pitchers while standing to upgrade at first base and/or designated hitter. Trading Soto would let the Friars recoup MLB-ready talent while clearing significant spending room for multiple smaller additions, but it would also be a devastating blow to the lineup: Soto overcame a relatively slow start by his standards to hit .275/.410/.519 with 35 home runs that year. San Diego was still motivated to win in the short term, and barring what would be a stunning run at Ohtani, the club was not going to acquire anyone better than Soto.

President of baseball operations A.J. Preller continued to maintain the Padres would explore a long-term deal, but there was nothing to suggest an extension was particularly likely — Soto had rejected a $440M offer from the Nationals before being traded at the 2022 deadline, and the price would only be higher now that he was a year and a half closer to free agency. In the absence of an extension or a definitive declaration that Soto was off the trade market, speculation would linger; Alden González of ESPN had written the previous week that many throughout the industry believed the three-time All-Star would be available.
