# Richmond Tigers' Comeback Win In Cairns, 2023

A late goal by Emelia Yassir helped Richmond to their first win in four games, an 11-point AFLW victory over Hawthorn, 6.5 (41) to 4.6 (30), at Cazaly's Stadium in Cairns on the Friday night, The Age reported on 20 October 2023.

Separated by one point at half-time and down by nine points entering the last quarter, the Tigers kept their finals hopes alive by limiting Hawthorn to just one point in the decisive final quarter. Midfield duo Monique Conti (35 disposals, seven clearances) and Grace Egan (28, 5) were typically influential for Richmond, while Hawthorn were led by Emily Bates (18, four) and Jasmine Fleming (18, one). "Richmond were just absolute beasts, especially their midfielders. They really worked hard all night," Hawthorn coach Bec Goddard said after the game. "Our work rate really dropped off and we just watched them play some really good footy. If you're a Hawks fan, you would've seen some really exciting moments of footy tonight. Keep getting excited. We are coming, we're growing, we're getting better."

Hawthorn's Irish ace Aine McDonagh kicked the opening goal before Tigers skipper Katie Brennan slotted two unanswered majors to celebrate her 50th game in style, and fellow 50-gamer Greta Bodey snatched her own goal before quarter-time. A skirmish erupted in the second term after Brennan took exception to a tackle by Mattea Breed on Laura McClelland; McClelland was gifted a 50-metre penalty following a bump against Sarah Hosking by Sophie Locke and converted from inside the goal square while players were occupied by the tussle. Bridie Hipwell kicked the only goal of the third quarter to give Hawthorn a game-high nine-point lead, before Richmond rebounded in the last term: Courtney Jones ended Hawthorn's three-goal run with her first touch of the night to cut the margin to three points, and Yassir, dogged by inaccuracy in front of goal, reduced the deficit to a single point before finally breaking through on her third attempt to put the Tigers six points ahead with 11 minutes left, after which Caitlin Greiser sealed the win with a massive goal from 45 metres. Elsewhere in the round, the Lions next play St Kilda before taking on reigning premiers Melbourne in the final round, having staked their claim as premiership contenders again, while Adelaide play top-four side North Melbourne next weekend before 18th-placed Western Bulldogs.
