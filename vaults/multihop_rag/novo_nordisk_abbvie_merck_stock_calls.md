# Stock Calls On Novo Nordisk, AbbVie And Merck

The selection rule is that a good pharma stock should have low impact from expiries and a fairly distributed pipeline, and along those lines Business Line had earlier recommended Novo Nordisk, up 140 per cent since July 2021, and AbbVie, up 27 per cent since October 2021, and on 18 November 2023 reiterated the recommendation on both, now trading at 31 times and 12 times FY24 earnings respectively. Novo Nordisk, with sales ramp-up in weight loss, pill format and weekly injections of GLP-1RA, is well poised to grow even now, and its pipeline will address NASH, an untreated liver disease, plus hematology and other non-diabetic areas. AbbVie, despite Humira's patent loss by end 2023 on $22 billion of 2022 revenue, will continue to be powered by Rinvoq and Skyrizi in autoimmune apart from deep pipeline assets, and the Allergan acquisition, funded out of Humira's cash flows as a replacement for Humira, can cushion the patent loss to an extent.

The Merck call is more qualified. Analyst focus for Merck, at 12 times FY24 EPS, will start shifting to Keytruda's likely patent loss in 2028, but despite the overhang investors can accumulate the stock on dips. The supporting grounds are the deep pipeline powered by Keytruda combinations in immunology, ADC and other cancers, and balance sheet strength; and while biosimilar launches will impact Keytruda's sales in 2028, the rate of decline may be lower owing to strong physician preference for Keytruda.
