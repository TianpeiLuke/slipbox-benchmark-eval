# Anker 3-In-1 MagSafe Charging Cube Deal Listings

Both of WIRED's October 10, 2023 Prime Day roundups list Anker's 2.5-inch charging cube as a great compact charger.

It comes with a MagSafe pad on top that charges at up to 15 watts, a top section that hinges to a 60-degree angle to reveal a charging surface for AirPods, and a shelf on the side with a built-in Apple Watch charger that can comfortably accommodate any Apple Watch including the Ultra. The box also includes a 5-foot cable and a 30-watt charger, and it supports fast charging.
