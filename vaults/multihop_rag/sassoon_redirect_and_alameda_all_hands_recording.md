# Sassoon's Redirect And The Alameda All-Hands Recording

Prosecutor Danielle Sassoon's brief redirect of Caroline Ellison on October 12th, 2023 set a trap that opened the door to the jury hearing a secret recording of an Alameda all-hands meeting in which Ellison confessed to stealing billions of FTX customers' money at Bankman-Fried's direction. On direct examination, near the end, Sassoon had asked about the all-hands meeting without bringing up many specifics; during the cross, Mark Cohen asked Ellison what topics were covered in the meeting while avoiding details — which let Sassoon work the confession in on redirect. The judge then ruled in favour of the jury hearing the recordings, and the court briefly recessed; that was when Bankman-Fried's mother approached the defense lawyers.

The tape was set up by a short afternoon witness. Christian Drappi, a former Alameda software engineer who looked like a handsome funeral director in a black suit and tie, testified that when Binance CEO Changpeng Zhao announced on Twitter that he intended to acquire FTX, Ellison confessed the theft of customer funds to him and a few other employees. The all-hands took place the following day and was secretly recorded by an employee who had joined Alameda three days before. In the recording Ellison did indeed confess to stealing customer funds with Bankman-Fried's approval. Drappi said he resigned less than 24 hours after the meeting; the recording was later sent to him, and he sent it to the government.

The reporter's assessment was that Sassoon demolished any points Cohen had attempted to make and that she did not fully appreciate the cleverness until after Ellison left the stand and the jurors left the room. In the family seats, Joseph Bankman, the defendant's father and a senior advisor to FTX's philanthropic arm, was not in the room for the recording, and Barbara Fried looked unhappy, rubbing her left temple as though she had a tremendous headache.
