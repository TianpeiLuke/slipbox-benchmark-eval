# Reuniting Oliver And Thaniel

Recruiting Halsin does not end the quest: to remove the Shadow Curse you must find Thaniel's other half, Oliver, an obnoxious boy who likes playing hide and seek in the middle of the cursed lands. His little house lies east of Last Light, in an area marked "House in Deep Shadows" on the map.

When you find him, Oliver asks to play hide-and-seek. Oblige him and look around until you perceive his location; he gets mad and starts another game in which he hides again and summons some wraiths to chase you. You can either sneak around and carefully try to catch him again, or get caught and simply kill the shades. Either way, talk to Oliver again — he will not want to play with you, because he is a jerk. Assuming you have already rescued Thaniel, you can tell Oliver that he needs to rejoin his other half, which eventually leads to another minor combat encounter. Oliver teleports you to the Moonrise Towers town square alongside resurrected versions of his Mommy and Daddy Shades, encases himself in a shield, and sics his undead minions on you. Each time you kill one of his enemies his shield also takes damage, and you can target the shield directly as well; break it one way or the other to talk to Oliver again, then persuade or threaten him into rejoining Thaniel. Head back to camp and talk to the now-complete boy. The scope condition Polygon flags is that Thaniel alone will not be able to end the curse on the lands — for that you still need to finish the mainline quest in Moonrise Towers — but Thaniel is whole again, and you have a brand new Druid friend for your adventures.
