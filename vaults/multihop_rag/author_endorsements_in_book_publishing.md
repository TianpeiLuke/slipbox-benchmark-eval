# Author Endorsements In Book Publishing

Book blurbs have become a central part of the publishing industry, on the reasoning that there is no better endorser of a book than other authors and thought leaders. The distinguishing feature of the form is therefore the identity of the recommender: an endorsement carries weight because it comes from someone who writes or thinks professionally in the field.

TechCrunch applied that same spirit editorially for the 2023 holiday season, asking several writers to recommend books that its readers may want to gift.
