# Steam Deck OLED Wireless And Charging Improvements

The Steam Deck OLED has a new Wi-Fi 6E module and improved antennas that should enable faster downloads for anyone with a compatible router. Engadget's reviewer doesn't have a 6E router or multi-gigabit Wi-Fi, but with a regular Wi-Fi 6 router the top download speed she saw on the new Steam Deck was 562mbps — about 10 percent faster than the old Steam Deck, and 10 percent slower than the speeds she gets on her MacBook Pro. The device uses Bluetooth 5.3 and adds a dedicated antenna, which should improve functionality when using Wi-Fi and Bluetooth at the same time.

The launch coverage adds the charging side: there's a reworked power supply — it has a logo now — with a longer 2.5m cable that will charge the OLED model from 20 to 80 percent in "as little as 45 minutes." The new internals also make the Steam Deck OLED marginally lighter and cooler to the touch when playing games, aided by a slightly larger fan; the thumbsticks have been made 1mm taller, and the touch pads' haptics are slightly sharper.
