# Georgia 52-17 Ole Miss: Week 11 Recap

Georgia dominated ninth-ranked Ole Miss 52-17 on Nov. 11, 2023 to earn a date with Alabama in December's SEC Championship Game, with the SEC East champions putting on a show. By quarters the line was Ole Miss 7-7-0-3 for 17 and Georgia 14-14-10-14 for 52.

All-American tight end Brock Bowers returned to the field for his final homestead only 26 days after TightRope surgery, starting against the Rebels with three catches for 34 yards and a fourth-quarter touchdown, having missed just two games after the high ankle sprain suffered against Vanderbilt on Oct. 14. Georgia did not need him, though, because the running backs had a night for both teams: Kendall Milton and Daijun Edwards scored two touchdowns each as the Bulldogs finished with 300 rushing yards, Milton ending with a career-high 127. Carson Beck finished with 306 yards, two touchdowns and one interception — his fourth game that season with over 300 yards and at least two touchdowns.

For Ole Miss, Quinshon Judkins scored the only two touchdowns and finished with 75 yards, while injuries continued to plague the Rebels: top lineman Micah Pettus was ruled out with a broken foot bone sustained in practice, and quarterback Jaxson Dart left at the end of the third quarter after a hit from two Georgia defenders, holding his collarbone as he entered the injury tent before being taken to the locker room and not returning. Dart passed for 112 yards and one interception before his exit; Spencer Sanders finished with 61 yards on four completions. In its trip to the SEC Championship for the third straight year, Georgia would face Alabama, to whom it lost in 2021 — and the dominant win surely inspired confidence with two regular season games left, with the question remaining whether it was enough to overtake Ohio State as the top team in the College Football Playoff rankings.
