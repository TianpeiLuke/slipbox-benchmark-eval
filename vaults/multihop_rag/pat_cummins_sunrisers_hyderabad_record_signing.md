# Pat Cummins' Rs 20.50 Cr Signing By Sunrisers Hyderabad

Sunrisers Hyderabad bought Australia captain Pat Cummins for Rs 20.50 Cr at the IPL 2024 auction, and at 2:12 PM IST he became the most expensive player in IPL history to that point.

The bidding began at 2:05 PM IST as a contest between CSK and MI, with RCB joining the race at 5 Cr and SRH joining at 8 Cr.

Ravi Shastri (@RaviShastriOfc) posted on December 19, 2023: "Patty Cummins. You beauty. You deserve every Penny put out there Champ. Win the Big ones you get International Respect. Take a Bow. #iplauction2024 #IPL".
