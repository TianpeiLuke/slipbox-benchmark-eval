# Displacement And Casualties In Gaza As Of October 12, 2023

The number of people in Gaza displaced from their homes rose quickly over the days preceding Oct. 12, 2023, totaling over 330,000 on that date, with over two-thirds of them staying in UNRWA schools.

Since the war between Israel and Hamas began on Oct. 8, 2023, more than 1,500 Gazans had been killed and more than 5,300 injured, while Hamas attacks had killed more than 1,300 people in Israel and injured about 3,200 others. These are the figures as reported on 2023-10-13.
