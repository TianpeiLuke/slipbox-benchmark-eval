# Hellraiser: Clive Barker's Lament Configuration And The Cenobites

Hellraiser is Clive Barker's 1987 directorial debut, adapting his own 1986 novella The Hellbound Heart, starring Andrew Robinson, Clare Higgins and Ashley Laurence, and available on Prime Video, AMC+, Shudder, Tubi, Pluto and Hoopla. Polygon calls it easily the best and most enduring of the Hellraiser movie series and a must-watch for horror fans.

The core narrative concerns Larry and Julia Cotton, a married couple who move into the home of Larry's recently deceased brother Frank, with whom Julia had a previous affair. After Frank is inadvertently resurrected by a drop of blood spilled by Larry on the floor of the house's attic, he seduces Julia into luring new men to the house so he can drain their life force and fully regain his mortal form.

Surrounding that story is the Lament Configuration, a puzzle box Frank acquired before his untimely death: when solved, it conjures hellish beings known as Cenobites to the mortal plane of existence, which indulge in hellish exercises of sadomasochistic mutilation.
