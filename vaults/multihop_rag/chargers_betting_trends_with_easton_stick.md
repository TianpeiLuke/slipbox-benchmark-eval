# Chargers Betting Trends With Easton Stick At Quarterback

Los Angeles was 4-9-1 against the spread going into Week 16 of the 2023 season, having failed to cover in five of their last six games, and had gone under the total in 10 of their 12 games.

Given how badly the team was shredded on Thursday Night Football the week before, the preview judged it would take some big spreads and some sort of spark from interim head coach Giff Smith for them to cover anything the rest of the season. Justin Herbert's replacement, Easton Stick, amassed an impressive line of 23-for-32 for 257 yards passing and a touchdown in Week 15, but he also had fumble and interception returns against him within about 25 seconds between the end of the third quarter and the start of the fourth. Betting on or against quarterbacks nobody has seen play can be tricky, but focusing on OVER/UNDER trends is not — though the preview flagged that the OVER/UNDER resting at 44 that week seemed tantalizingly low, since Buffalo had averaged 29 points per game in their last four and, with Stick throwing touchdowns but also turning the ball over at a high rate, that figure might be easily surpassed.
