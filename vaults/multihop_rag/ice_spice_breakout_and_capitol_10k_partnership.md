# Ice Spice's Breakout And The Capitol-10K Partnership

Capitol Music Group has been a crucial partner, alongside Elliot Grainge's 10K Projects, in the rise of Ice Spice, a Best New Artist Grammy nominee who has secured no fewer than four Top 10 Billboard Hot 100 singles and over 2 billion global streams off an official debut release that is an EP. Michelle Jubelirer calls her "THE breakout artist of 2023", adding that she is the first artist since Olivia Rodrigo to achieve three Top 5 single debuts in her first four months and that she has just attained another Top 10 with "Barbie World" with Nicki Minaj, and saying they are just at the beginning of what is going to be a tremendous career of artistry and global popularity.

On the signing itself, Jubelirer sets the record straight about the dinner that led to the co-signing of Ice Spice by Capitol and 10K, teasing Grainge about his selective memory: present were Jubelirer, Grainge, Jeremy Vuernick and 10K co-presidents Zach Friedman and Tony Talamo, all of whom won the artist's heart as a team. She says that after decades working with artists it is a rare occasion when you sit with an artist and just know you are in the presence of a future global superstar, and that was and is Ice Spice; her early music was exciting and her explosive growth as an artist and cultural figure has already transcended the world of music, making her a household name, a testament to her talent and drive and to a real team effort between Capitol, 10K and her manager James Rosemond Jr. Jubelirer notes that Ice Spice is a strong woman who has aligned herself with other strong women, the article adding that Ice Spice also signed to Universal Music Publishing Group via UMPG Chairman and CEO Jody Gerson, a close Jubelirer colleague. On the ingredients for widening her audience since "Munch", Jubelirer points to her look, her flow, her lyrics and her collaboration choices, naming Taylor Swift, Nicki Minaj and PinkPantheress, and to the fact that she knows precisely who she is and what is right for her to do and not do, a rare quality at such a young age that lets her make the right choices at warp speed; EVP of Media Ambrosia Healey scored a major coup by getting her the season opener of Saturday Night Live, one of many big media appearances, and every department has played an integral role in the launch and development.
