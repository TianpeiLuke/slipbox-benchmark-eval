# Anker 2-In-1 MagSafe StandBy Charger Deal Listings

Both of WIRED's October 10, 2023 Prime Day roundups list this 2-in-1 charger, featured in WIRED's guide to the Best MagSafe Accessories, at a price matching the lowest tracked.

It doesn't have a built-in Apple Watch charger, but it can top off compatible wireless earbuds while simultaneously charging an iPhone. It charges at the maximum 15-watt rate, and you can turn the iPhone sideways for iOS 17's new StandBy mode, converting it into a bedside alarm clock.
