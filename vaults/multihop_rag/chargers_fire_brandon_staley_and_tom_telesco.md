# The Chargers Fire Brandon Staley And Tom Telesco

Los Angeles fired head coach Brandon Staley and general manager Tom Telesco after a brutal 63-21 Thursday Night Football rout at the hands of the Raiders in Week 15 of the 2023 season.

The Chargers were already twisting in the wind after quarterback Justin Herbert's season-ending hand injury, but the magnitude of the 38-point loss was embarrassing enough to cause the team to clean house midseason. The team was supposed to be good and was not; it had seemed unlikely that Staley was going to be their guy past that year, especially on the heels of their crushing playoff exit in Jacksonville where they blew a 27-point lead and lost in historic fashion. Giving up 63 points to a team that had just been shut out 3-0 four days earlier was enough to cause Los Angeles ownership to hit the shred button.
