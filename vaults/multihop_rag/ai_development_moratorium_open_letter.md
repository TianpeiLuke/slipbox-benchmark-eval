# The AI Development Moratorium Open Letter

Not everybody was enthusiastic about the pace at which generative AI was being adopted. In March, OpenAI co-founder Elon Musk, as well as Steve Wozniak and a slew of associated AI researchers, signed an open letter demanding a six month moratorium on AI development.

The letter attacks the premise underlying the industry's integration wave — that faster deployment is straightforwardly good — by asking for development itself to be paused rather than merely regulated.
