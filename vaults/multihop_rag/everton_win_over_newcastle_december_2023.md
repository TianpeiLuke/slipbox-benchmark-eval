# Everton's Win Over Newcastle, December 2023

Everton trounced Newcastle 3-0 on 7 December 2023 to move clear of the relegation zone despite their ten-point penalty for financial irregularities, as reported by The Roar that day.

The Toffees left it late, with goals from Dwight McNeil, Abdoulaye Doucoure and Brazilian striker Beto enough to down the Magpies. McNeil's opener came from a Kieran Trippier mistake that the Everton man made him pay for, and Goodison Park went wild at what the report calls a massive goal.
