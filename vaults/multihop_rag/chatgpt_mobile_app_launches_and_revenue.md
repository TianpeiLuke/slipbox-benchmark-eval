# ChatGPT Mobile App Launches And Revenue

ChatGPT's mobile apps, launched for iOS in May 2023 and Android in July 2023, topped 110 million combined installs and reached nearly $30 million in consumer spending, according to a market analysis by data.ai.

The rollout was staged by geography. OpenAI launched the ChatGPT app for iOS free to use, free from ads and allowing voice input, but initially limited to U.S. users; the app syncs history across devices so it knows what a user previously searched via the web interface, and it is integrated with Whisper, OpenAI's open source speech recognition system, for voice input. Within six days it topped 500,000 downloads. OpenAI then announced the app was available on iOS in the U.S., Europe, South Korea and New Zealand, and added 11 more countries — Albania, Croatia, France, Germany, Ireland, Jamaica, Korea, New Zealand, Nicaragua, Nigeria and the UK. The app was later expanded to more than 30 further countries: Algeria, Argentina, Azerbaijan, Bolivia, Brazil, Canada, Chile, Costa Rica, Ecuador, Estonia, Ghana, India, Iraq, Israel, Japan, Jordan, Kazakhstan, Kuwait, Lebanon, Lithuania, Mauritania, Mauritius, Mexico, Morocco, Namibia, Nauru, Oman, Pakistan, Peru, Poland, Qatar, Slovenia, Tunisia and the United Arab Emirates. A new version brought native iPad support with full-screen mode optimized for the tablet interface, support for using the chatbot with Siri and Shortcuts, and drag and drop for pulling individual messages from ChatGPT into other apps. On Android, OpenAI first put up a "pre-order" page on Google Play and days later released the app live in the US, India, Bangladesh and Brazil; the Android app looks more or less identical to the iOS one in functionality, getting most if not all of the web version's features and syncing conversations and preferences across devices.

Revenue is large but not dominant, and growth has slowed. In September, OpenAI amassed 15.6 million downloads and nearly $4.6 million in gross revenue across its iOS and Android apps worldwide — a record $4.58 million month for the mobile app — but revenue growth dropped from 30% to 20% in September according to market intelligence firm Appfigures. OpenAI's chatbot app far outpaces all others on mobile devices in terms of downloads, yet it is surprisingly not the top AI app by revenue: several other AI chatbots, such as "Chat & Ask AI" and "ChatOn — AI Chat Bot Assistant," make more money than ChatGPT.
