# Connor Bedard's Rookie Adjustment With The Blackhawks

While goalies were trying to figure Connor Bedard out, he was putting in the time to do the same with them, learning first to use his teammates more, as reported by The New York Times on 17 November 2023.

The early pattern and the change are given together. He accumulated plenty of shots on net early in the season, but they were often from distance and more individualistic chances; then he scored his first NHL goal on a wraparound and his next three goals off passes from teammates. Blackhawks general manager Kyle Davidson framed it: "Look, I'm sure he'll figure out the one-on-one and how to score that way or create chances that way. There is also an element of working off your teammates. You are in the NHL, so everyone is a pretty good player and you're able to create using more than just yourself. I think he's figured that out."

The second stage came on the road, and came from defensive work. Bedard left Chicago with five goals in 11 games and returned from Florida with nine goals in 13 games, scoring twice against the Tampa Bay Lightning and again against the Panthers; those goals were less reliant on teammates and more about the work he's putting in on the other side of the puck. "Even in practice, we've talked to him about maybe tracking harder and attacking pucks on the forecheck and showing him a couple clips," coach Luke Richardson said. "Ten, 12 games in, he's really figuring things out and realizes — why sit back and let things come to him? Go get it." None of this surprised anyone involved: Bedard doesn't show it if he impresses himself, and as grateful as the Blackhawks are to have drafted him, this is what they expected — it's why teams lined up to take losses last season. "It's not the NHL that he was playing in the last few years," Davidson said, "but the level of performance and how he could control a game and dominate a game in junior — it indicated he was going to be able to do that at some point in the NHL. You just never how quickly. It's a big adjustment, especially when you're playing against men and being focused on by the other team every single night. I'm excited for him, but I don't necessarily feel like it's a huge surprise how quickly he's started to really find his groove at the NHL level. But it's exciting to watch."
