# Walmart Cyber Monday 2023 Sonos Amplifier And Subwoofer Deals

WIRED's Walmart Cyber Monday roundup of 27 November 2023 recommended three Sonos components for people extending an existing system: the Sonos Sub (Gen 3), the Sonos Amp and the Sonos Port.

WIRED recommends the Sonos Sub (Gen 3) if your giftee wants to upgrade their bass; the third-generation model packs better processing power and more memory. Its stated compatibility limit is that it is meant to be paired with Sonos' non-portable options — soundbars, speakers and amplifiers — and will not pair with the Move, Roam, Port or Connect. WIRED calls it expensive but top-notch, and notes this is the lowest price it has tracked so far.

The Amp is presented as a great option for powering a turntable, TV, outdoor speakers and more at higher volumes. Its front control panel is touch-sensitive, so you can tap or swipe to pause music and adjust volume; it connects via the Sonos app and supports AirPlay 2. It has no built-in mics, so voice control requires pairing it with another Sonos voice-enabled speaker such as the Sonos One or Sonos Move. The Port differs in role: where the Amp powers speakers directly, the Port streams to an amp that then sends a signal to the speakers. Unlike the Amp it has no control panel, but the Sonos app adjusts treble, bass and volume, it supports AirPlay 2, and as with the Amp it can be paired with other Sonos speakers for voice control.
