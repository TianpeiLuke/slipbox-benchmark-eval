# Britney Spears On Virginity Marketing And Timberlake's Cheating Claim

Spears writes in *The Woman in Me*, as reported by The Guardian on 24 October 2023, that she was actively marketed as a virgin — an "archaic premise" that turned a 16-year-old's sexuality into a timebomb — and that she was relieved rather than wounded when Justin Timberlake told the world they had had a sexual relationship.

The fixation on her "purity" took "the focus off me as a musician and a performer", she writes: "All some reporters could think of to ask me was whether or not my breasts were real (they were, actually) and whether or not my hymen was intact." In fact, she writes happily, she first slept with her older brother's best friend when she was 14. When Timberlake told the world she had cheated on him, she was all but emblazoned with a scarlet letter and knew she was powerless against that narrative — "I don't think Justin realised the power he had in shaming me. I don't think he understands to this day" — but she welcomed his disclosure of their sexual relationship: "To be honest with you, I liked that Justin said that. Why did my managers work so hard to claim I was some kind of young-girl virgin even into my 20s? Whose business was it if I'd had sex or not?"

She could not, however, escape the cycle Timberlake set in motion. She describes her 2004 Onyx Hotel tour in the aftermath as "too sexual, for a start. Justin had embarrassed me publicly, so my rebuttal onstage was to kind of go there a little bit, too. But it was absolutely horrible."
