# Prediction: An AI Compliance Industry And Slow Regulation

TechCrunch predicts that big moves like the EU's AI Act will not visibly change the industry in 2024, because such laws tend to be slow to take effect. That slowness is by design, so companies do not have to adjust to new rules overnight, but it also means we will not see the effect of these big laws for a good while except among those willing to make changes preemptively and voluntarily — so expect a lot of "we are beginning the process of…" talk, and a few quiet lawsuits challenging various parts of laws.

The knock-on prediction is a newly flourishing AI compliance industry, as the billions going into the technology prompt matching investments — at a smaller scale, but still considerable — in making sure the tools and processes meet international and local standards.

For the United States, the forecast is negative and dated: unfortunately for anyone hoping for substantive federal regulation, 2024 is not the year to expect movement on that front. Though it will be a year for AI and everyone will be asking for new laws, the U.S. government and electorate will be too busy with the trash fire that will be the 2024 election.
