# Anker Charger And Power Bank Deals

A slew of Anker chargers, cables, power adapters and other accessories went on sale for Black Friday 2023 at Amazon, with discounts ranging up to 37 percent. The deals extend to Engadget's favorite premium power bank, Anker's Prime 20,000 mAh battery with charging base, which was $130 for the set — its lowest price ever.

One of the company's 67W USB-C chargers was 37 percent off, bringing it to $38 and matching its all-time low. Engadget's top 65W fast wall charger, Anker's 735 GaNPrime, was down to a new low price after a 38 percent discount, and the Anker Nano Power Bank was seeing one of its first discounts since coming on the market a couple of months earlier.
