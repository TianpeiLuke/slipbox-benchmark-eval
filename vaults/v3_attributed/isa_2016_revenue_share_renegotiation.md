# Apple-Google Search Deal Revenue Share Negotiation Details

The Verge reported on 2023-09-26 that under the ISA's current form, negotiated by Eddy Cue with Sundar Pichai in 2016, Google pays Apple "a percentage of the advertising revenue earned from Apple users it directs to Google Search." During the US v. Google antitrust trial, Cue testified that Apple pressed for a higher percentage of this revenue share, while Google favored maintaining the status quo, and they ultimately "settled on an undisclosed figure." When asked if he would have walked away from the deal without agreement, Cue stated, "I always felt like it was in Google's best interest, and our best interest, to get a deal done."
