# Apple's 2016 ISA Negotiation Included Privacy Safeguards and Default Search Rationale

The Verge reported on 2023-09-26 that Apple's Eddy Cue testified Google's Information Services Agreement included provisions requiring Google to let users search without logging in, while Apple separately hardened Safari and its platforms against third-party tracking. Cue stated Apple chose Google as default search engine because "there wasn't a valid alternative" and claimed Google was "the best" product, not just the most lucrative option. During US v. Google antitrust trial testimony, Cue defended the billions-dollar annual deal as serving both companies' best interests.
