# John J. Ray III Leads FTX Liquidation, Calls Failure Worse Than Enron

The Verge reported on 2023-09-28 that John J. Ray III, the bankruptcy attorney who oversaw Enron's liquidation, now leads FTX's remaining operations. Ray has characterized both the exchange's failure as worse than Enron and Sam Bankman-Fried's conduct as "really just old-fashioned embezzlement."
