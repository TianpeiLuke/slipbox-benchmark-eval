# Kelce's Moustache Remark Suggests Prior Contact with Swift

The Age reported on 2023-09-26 that Kelce's comment about his moustache suggested prior contact with Swift. During his August podcast appearance discussing his annual facial hair growth for Coach Andy Reid, Kelce stated, "We're not going to bring up Taylor Swift in this episode, but something tells me she's going to like it." This remark implied he had already made contact with Swift and expected her opinion to matter, since he voiced confidence about the reaction of someone he had not publicly met. The warrant is the unaccounted-for audience he names, and the scope is suggestion rather than proof.
