# Epic's Opening Argument Charts Show Google's Alleged App Store Profit Margins

The Verge reported on 2023-11-07 that Epic presented charts during its opening argument showing Google earns approximately 70 percent profit margins on $12 billion in annual app revenue through Google Play. Epic's lead attorney Gary Bornstein displayed these charts to the jury as evidence of Google's alleged monopolistic practices, claiming that "Google pays actual and potential competitors not to compete."
