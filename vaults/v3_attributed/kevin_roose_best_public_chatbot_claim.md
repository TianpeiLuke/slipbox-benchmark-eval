# Kevin Roose's Endorsement of ChatGPT as Best AI Chatbot

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-30 that New York Times columnist Kevin Roose judged ChatGPT "the best AI chatbot ever released to the general public." This endorsement came from a member of the traditionally sceptical press constituency, marking a significant conversion. Roose's assessment was part of the broader recognition of ChatGPT's impact, as noted in The Atlantic magazine's "Breakthroughs of the Year" for 2022, where Derek Thompson included ChatGPT in "the generative-AI eruption" that "may change our mind about how we work, how we think and what human creativity looks like."
