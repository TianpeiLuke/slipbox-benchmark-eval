# C.J. Stroud as NFL MVP Longshot Candidate

Sporting News reported on 2023-12-05 that C.J. Stroud deserves NFL MVP consideration despite being a longshot bet. The publication noted that "Stroud has taken one of the worst teams in the NFL (11 total wins the last three seasons) and turned it into a playoff contender and potential division champion." Stroud leads the league in passing yards, is one of four quarterbacks who have started the whole season with a passer rating over 100, and maintains the best touchdown to interception ratio in the NFL at 4 to 1. While Brock Purdy, Dak Prescott, and Jalen Hurts are the current MVP favorites, Stroud's transformation of the Houston Texans franchise makes him a compelling longshot candidate.
