# FTX Trial Begins with Unresolved Billions in Crypto Assets

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-01 that Sam Bankman-Fried's criminal trial began with unresolved questions about compensation for investors and creditors, and the fate of billions in crypto assets tied up in legal proceedings. The trial will determine if he is guilty of seven counts of fraud and conspiracy. As of the bankruptcy proceedings, "there were virtually no internal controls and no separateness whatsoever" at FTX according to John J. Ray III, the Enron turnaround veteran who became FTX's CEO after its Chapter 11 filing in mid-November 2022. The outcome remains falsifiable by eventual creditor distributions.
