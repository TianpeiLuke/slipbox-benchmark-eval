# The Verge reporter frames Epic-Google trial as narrative battle between simplicity and complexity

The Verge reported on 2023-11-07 that the Epic v. Google trial may hinge on which side presents the more compelling narrative, with Epic offering a straightforward underdog story while Google focuses on complex business explanations. According to The Verge's analysis, "Epic was able to paint a black-and-white picture of good and evil with itself as the clear underdog" during opening arguments, while Google spent its time "attempting to explain complicated ins and outs of business." The publication notes that Epic's strategy emphasized Google's alleged anti-competitive behavior through charts showing Google's 70% app profit margins on $12 billion annual revenue, whereas Google's defense centered on arguing that its Android app store dominance stems from legitimate competition with Apple's iOS rather than monopolistic practices.
