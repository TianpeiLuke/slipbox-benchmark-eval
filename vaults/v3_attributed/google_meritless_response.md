# Google Denies Antitrust Allegations in Publisher Lawsuit

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-15 that a Arkansas-based publisher filed a class action antitrust lawsuit against Google, accusing the company of siphoning news content and revenue through anticompetitive practices including AI technologies. Google responded through a spokesperson who rejected the suit as "meritless" and pushed back against the central antitrust and AI-training allegations made by Helena World Chronicle.
