# SEO-Driven Business Names Function Primarily as Acquisition Tools

The Verge reported on 2023-10-26 that businesses using SEO-optimized names like "Thai Food Near Me" function primarily as customer acquisition devices rather than retention strategies. Teddy Jirapraphanan, co-owner of Thai Food Near Me, explained that such names help businesses rank higher in Google searches when potential customers search for specific services nearby. The article states: "As with Thai Food Near Me, the most powerful thing an SEO-driven name might be able to do is get customers in the door." However, the source emphasizes that retaining customers depends entirely on the quality of the service or product provided, with successful businesses leveraging positive reviews and active online profiles to maintain their search rankings. This strategy has led to numerous similar business names across various industries, including "Barber Shop Near Me" and "Affordable Dentist Near Me."
