# FTX Collapse Linked to Alameda Research Misuse of Customer Funds

The Verge reported on 2023-10-26 that FTX collapsed "as the result of Bankman-Fried's trading firm Alameda Research purloining its customers' funds." The publication detailed how Sam Bankman-Fried "presided over the various forms of fraud that played out at Alameda Research and FTX, orchestrating some of it and engaging in some of it directly." Former FTX executive Caroline Ellison testified that "Alameda took customer funds; Bankman-Fried knew about it and even invested them against her advice." This misuse of customer deposits, which Bankman-Fried both knew about and participated in despite warnings, directly caused the exchange's catastrophic failure and the trapping of customer money.
