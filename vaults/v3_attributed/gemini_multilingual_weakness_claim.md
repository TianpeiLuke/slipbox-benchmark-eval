# Gemini Pro's French Word Test Failure Supports Multilingual Performance Concerns

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-07 that Gemini Pro struggled with basic French translation tasks, supporting broader concerns about its multilingual capabilities. When prompted to "give me a 6-letters word in French," the model failed to provide an accurate six-letter French word. The author replicated the test and confirmed the poor performance, with Gemini Pro instead providing a seven-letter word. This anecdotal evidence aligns with "reports about Gemini's poor multilingual performance" and third-party observations of weak translation abilities. The failure demonstrates Gemini Pro's difficulty with elementary language tasks despite Google's claims of superior multilingual architecture.
