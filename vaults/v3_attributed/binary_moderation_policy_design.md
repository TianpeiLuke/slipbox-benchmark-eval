# Meta's Black-and-White Moderation Policies Limit Nuanced Content Moderation

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-19 that Meta's "black-and-white policies often preclude it from effectively moderating any nuanced topic, like content that discusses violence without condoning it." The article highlights how Meta's binary approach to content moderation, which relies on strict allow/forbid categories, fails to adequately handle complex speech such as discussions of violence that don't endorse it. This limitation has contributed to accusations of shadowbanning Palestinian content, as the platform struggles to distinguish between legitimate cultural expression and policy violations, particularly affecting Arabic-speaking users given the language's numerous dialects. The company's moderation bias has been documented through years of user complaints and internal reports, including a 2021 third-party study that found Meta's actions had an "adverse human rights impact" on Palestinian users' freedom of expression.
