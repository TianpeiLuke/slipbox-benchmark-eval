# Russell Wilson's Comeback Player of the Year Case

Sporting News reported on 2023-12-05 that Russell Wilson deserves Comeback Player of the Year consideration after his recovery from a "disastrous 2022 season." The article states Wilson has "returned in 2023 to play efficiently, avoid mistakes, and keep his team competitive despite mediocre supporting results." This turnaround under Sean Payton's coaching has made Wilson a longshot candidate for the award, though Demar Hamlin is currently the favorite. Wilson's resurrection from his poor 2022 performance with Denver has been significant enough that Sporting News explicitly argues he "deserves Comeback Player of the Year consideration."
