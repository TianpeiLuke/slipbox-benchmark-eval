# Prosecution's Second Witness Details Bankman-Fried Recruitment and Immunity Agreement

Fortune reported on 2023-10-04 that Adam Yedidia, an MIT graduate and close college friend of Sam Bankman-Fried, testified he was recruited by Bankman-Fried first as a trader at Alameda Research and then as a developer at FTX. "I was giving evidence under legal immunity," Yedidia stated, marking the second prosecution witness to disclose such an agreement following Danielle Sassoon's earlier testimony about witness immunity arrangements.
