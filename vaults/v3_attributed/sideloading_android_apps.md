# Android Sideloading Permitted Unlike iOS

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-06 that Android allows sideloading apps from outside Google Play, which "distinguishes Epic's Google case from its Apple case." Epic's antitrust lawsuit against Google acknowledges that "Android does allow for sideloading apps" unlike Apple's iOS platform. While Epic argues Google makes sideloading "cumbersome," this fundamental difference means Epic cannot allege there's "no other way to load apps onto Android devices, as it could with Apple."
