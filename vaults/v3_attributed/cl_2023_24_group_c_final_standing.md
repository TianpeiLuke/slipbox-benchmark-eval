# Champions League Group C Final Standings 2023/24

Sporting News reported on 2023-12-12 that Group C concluded with Real Madrid finishing first on 18 points (6W 0D 0L, GD +9), Napoli second on 10 points (3W 1D 2L), Braga third on 4 points, and Union Berlin last on 2 points. Real Madrid secured their position with a 3-2 win over Union Berlin, while Napoli's 2-0 victory against Braga eliminated the Portuguese side from Champions League contention.
