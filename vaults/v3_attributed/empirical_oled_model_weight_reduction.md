# Steam Deck OLED Weight Reduction Despite Larger Battery

Engadget reported on 2023-11-09 that the Steam Deck OLED weighs about 30 grams less than the original model. "The device weighs about 30 grams less than the original model, or the equivalent of five quarters." This weight reduction occurs despite the OLED model featuring a larger battery and fan, achieved through a die-shrunk custom AMD APU that improves efficiency while maintaining the same performance targets as the original.
