# OpenAI's Strategic Shift Toward Product Development Post-November 2023 Leadership Changes

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-19 that OpenAI will operate as a product company following the November 2023 leadership shake-up that placed Sam Altman more firmly in control. The publication predicts this change will manifest in a "ship it" mindset, evidenced by the upcoming GPT store platform designed to monetize AI tools directly. According to TechCrunch, "the caution and academic reserve that the previous board exerted gives way to an unseemly lust for markets and customers." This strategic pivot represents a fundamental shift from OpenAI's earlier research-focused approach under its previous board structure.
