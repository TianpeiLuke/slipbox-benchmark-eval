# Alameda Balance Sheet Listed FTX Borrowings, Bankman-Fried Seemed Unclear

The Verge reported on 2023-10-31 that a June 13, 2022 Alameda Research balance sheet shown at trial listed money borrowed from FTX as a line item labeled "ftx borrows." Sam Bankman-Fried appeared unclear about this accounting entry when questioned, despite it representing approximately $8 billion in customer funds that were missing due to software bugs and unauthorized spending. The balance sheet was part of the evidence in Bankman-Fried's wire fraud trial, where he faced questioning about his lack of oversight regarding the commingling of FTX customer deposits with Alameda trading operations.
