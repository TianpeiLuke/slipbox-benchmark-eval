# New Heights Podcast as Venue for Kelce's Swift Statements

CBSSports.com reported on 2023-10-12 that New Heights is the podcast Travis Kelce co-hosts with his brother, Philadelphia Eagles center Jason Kelce. The article identifies this podcast as "the venue where Kelce's own statements about Swift were made and quoted," specifically referencing his July 26 episode where he discussed making a friendship bracelet with his number for Swift and her not meeting him after her concert.
