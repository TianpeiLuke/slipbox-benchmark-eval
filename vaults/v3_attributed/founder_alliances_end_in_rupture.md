# Musk-Page and Musk-Altman Relationship Breakdowns Reveal Ego-Driven AI Leadership Fractures

The Age reported on 2023-12-09 that Elon Musk and Larry Page stopped speaking soon after a 2015 Napa Valley party, and Musk and Sam Altman likewise ceased communication after founding OpenAI together. "There is disagreement, mistrust, egos," Altman said, explaining that competitive dynamics intensify conflicts among aligned leaders. These personal fractures contradict narratives of technical disagreement, revealing instead that interpersonal tensions rooted in ambition and control drive AI leadership divisions rather than purely technological differences.
