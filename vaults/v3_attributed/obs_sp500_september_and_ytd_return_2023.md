# S&P 500 September 2023 Performance

The Sydney Morning Herald reported on 2023-10-01 that the S&P 500 dropped 4.9 per cent in September to drag what had been a big gain for the year down to 11.7 per cent. The decline occurred as Treasury yields climbed to 4.58 per cent, reaching their highest level since 2007, making bonds more attractive than stocks and contributing to investor pessimism about future returns.
