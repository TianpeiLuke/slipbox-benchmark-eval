# Federal Reserve Policy Rate Reaches Highest Level Since 2001

The Sydney Morning Herald reported on 2023-10-01 that the Federal Reserve's main interest rate is at its highest level since 2001, and the central bank indicated last week it may cut interest rates next year by less than it earlier expected. This shift in monetary policy expectation contributed to Treasury yields rising to 4.58 per cent, near their highest level since 2007, as investors adjusted to the prospect of higher-for-longer interest rates.
