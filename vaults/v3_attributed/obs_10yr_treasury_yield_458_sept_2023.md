# US 10-Year Treasury Yield Reaches Highest Levels Since 2007

The Sydney Morning Herald reported on 2023-10-01 that the yield on the 10-year Treasury yield returned to 4.58 per cent, where it was late Thursday, after dipping to 4.52 per cent. It's again near its highest level since 2007. This yield increase reflects market expectations of higher-for-longer interest rates as the Federal Reserve maintains elevated rates to combat inflation, with Treasury bonds offering higher returns and reducing investor appetite for riskier stock investments.
