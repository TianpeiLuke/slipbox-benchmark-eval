# Swift's Attendance Pattern Supports Reciprocal Interest Hypothesis

CBSSports.com reported on 2023-10-12 that Taylor Swift's attendance at five Chiefs games followed Travis Kelce's public invitation, supporting the hypothesis that her appearances reflect mutual interest rather than one-sided publicity. The timeline shows Swift attended games specifically when Kelce was playing, including the Sept. 24 game against the Chicago Bears where she "was especially thrilled to see Kelce get in the end zone." This pattern falsifies the one-sided publicity cycle proposition since her attendance directly correlates with Kelce's involvement and his explicit invitation.
