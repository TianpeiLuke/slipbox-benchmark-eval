# Epic's Antitrust Claims Against Google's Android App Store Restraints

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-06 that Epic Games claims Google's agreements with device makers and developers are anticompetitive restraints under Sections 1 and 2 of the Sherman Act and California law. Epic's core argument is that "Google makes it hard for alternative app stores to succeed and reach consumers" through practices like Anti-Fragmentation Agreements that prevent OEMs from modifying Android to allow frictionless downloading outside Google Play. The company will specifically highlight deals like the cancelled Epic-OnePlus arrangement where OnePlus canceled distributing Epic games directly due to concerns about bypassing Google Play Store. Epic contends these agreements maintain Google's dominance despite Android technically allowing sideloading, arguing the legal "sideloading legality does not neutralize them."
