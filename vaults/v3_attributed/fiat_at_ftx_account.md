# fiat@ftx account definition

The Verge reported on 2023-10-31 that the fiat@ftx account was "the one that tracked how much Alameda owed FTX." This internal FTX account specifically monitored the financial obligations between Alameda Research and the exchange, becoming central to understanding the misappropriation of customer funds that led to FTX's collapse.
