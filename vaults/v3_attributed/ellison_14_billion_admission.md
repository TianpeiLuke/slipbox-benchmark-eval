# Ellison's testimony on Alameda's $14 billion deficit

The Verge reported on 2023-10-10 that Caroline Ellison testified Sam Bankman-Fried directed her to commit crimes including fraud, conspiracy to commit fraud, and money laundering. Ellison stated: "We ultimately took about $14 billion, some of which we were not able to pay back." She described Alameda Research as being in worse financial shape than she had realized when she started in 2018, having been initially funded with loans from acquaintances that were recalled shortly after her arrival.
