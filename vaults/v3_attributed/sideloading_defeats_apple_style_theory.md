# Epic's Weakened Position in Google Antitrust Case Due to Android Sideloading

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-06 that Epic Games faces a key disadvantage in its antitrust lawsuit against Google compared to its Apple case because "this time, Epic can't allege there's no other way to load apps." Unlike Apple's iOS ecosystem which prohibits sideloading, Android permits users to install apps from sources outside the Google Play Store, undermining Epic's previous argument that no alternative installation path exists. While Epic contends that Google makes sideloading cumbersome and uses restrictive agreements with device manufacturers and developers to maintain dominance, the existence of sideloading capability fundamentally weakens its monopolization claims. Epic will focus on arguing that Google's bundling requirements and Anti-Fragmentation Agreements prevent OEMs from easily offering alternative app distribution methods.
