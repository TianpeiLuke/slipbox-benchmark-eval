# Prosecution Frames Bankman-Fried as Adult Criminal, Not Media's 'Boy' Genius

Fortune reported on 2023-10-04 that prosecutor Thane Rehn distinguished the media's characterization of crypto founders as "boys" from his own framing of Sam Bankman-Fried as a "man" who "stole billions of dollars from thousands of victims." Rehn's closing line of the prosecution opening statement directly rejected the sympathetic "boy" archetype, defining Bankman-Fried as an adult criminal actor. The prosecution's narrative contrasted sharply with defense arguments that portrayed him as a well-intentioned startup founder who acted in good faith.
