# Bankman-Fried's Legal Representation and Early Release Request

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-01 that Sam Bankman-Fried is represented by Cohen & Gresser with lead attorney Mark Cohen, a former federal prosecutor who also defended Ghislaine Maxwell in her sex-trafficking trial. "Cohen's request for Bankman-Fried's early release was denied," according to the publication. Bankman-Fried faces seven counts of fraud and conspiracy in his criminal trial.
