# Stanford Study Finds No ChatGPT-Driven Cheating Surge in U.S. High Schools

TechCrunch reported on 2023-09-28 that Stanford researchers found chatbots like ChatGPT did not cause an increase in cheating across U.S. high schools. "New research from Stanford University shows that the popularization of chatbots like ChatGPT have not caused an increase in cheating across U.S. high schools." In a survey of more than 40 U.S. high schools, researchers found that cheating rates remained similar across the board in the year ChatGPT became popular.
