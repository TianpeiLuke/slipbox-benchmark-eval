# Expedia's Antitrust Complaint Against Google Ad Pricing

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-13 that Expedia executives testified Google increased ad prices without improving search results quality. The testimony revealed that Google's price hikes for advertising placements were not accompanied by "any corresponding improvement in search results." This became a key complaint from Expedia during the U.S. v Google antitrust trial, suggesting Google was leveraging its dominant position to raise costs for advertisers while failing to enhance the underlying search experience that justified those higher prices.
