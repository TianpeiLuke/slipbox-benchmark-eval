# Digital Marketers Attend Alligator Party in Florida

The Verge reported on 2023-11-01 that roughly 200 digital marketers, almost all Florida residents, attended an alligator party at a rooftop bar in South Florida. The attendees drank shots while listening to a dancehall reggae band. The event was described as having participants who "dressed like they lived in Florida," with one attendee noted as wearing an orange bandage dress.
