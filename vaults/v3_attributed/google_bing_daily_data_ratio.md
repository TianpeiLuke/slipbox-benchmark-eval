# Google's Daily Search Data Collection Dominance

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-13 that the DOJ has said that Google gets 16 times more data than Bing does everyday. The government's argument centers on how Google uses its platforms and partner deals to block competition in search and advertising, thereby preventing competitors from accessing the data needed to improve their products. This data disparity was highlighted as a key issue in the Justice Department's landmark antitrust case against Google, which could force the company to change its behavior and potentially share its APIs with third-party developers if Judge Amit Mehta rules against it.
