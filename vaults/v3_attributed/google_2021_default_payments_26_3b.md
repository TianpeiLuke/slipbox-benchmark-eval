# Google's $26.3 billion default search payment revealed in antitrust trial

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-31 that Google search head Prabhakar Raghavan testified on October 28, 2023 revealing Google paid "$26.3 billion in 2021 to multiple browsers, phones and platforms, from companies including Apple, Samsung and Mozilla." This payment was specifically for making Google the default search engine across these platforms. About $18 billion of that amount went to Apple alone, highlighting the significant cost Google incurs to maintain default placement and collect user data across different devices.
