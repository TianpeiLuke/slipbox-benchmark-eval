# Ellison's Role as Unpaid Co-CEO Makes Her the Paradigmatically Terrible Ex-Boyfriend Case

The Verge reported on 2023-10-10 that Caroline Ellison's position as named co-CEO of Alameda Research hedge fund while being denied equity compensation exemplifies the paradigmatically terrible ex-boyfriend situation. Ellison, who has already pleaded guilty to criminal charges, testified that Sam Bankman-Fried "directed me to commit these crimes" including fraud, conspiracy to commit fraud, and money laundering. The article describes how Ellison was made co-CEO of the "sin-eater hedge fund" without receiving equity, while Bankman-Fried bragged about presidential ambitions. This arrangement, where she held executive responsibility without ownership stakes, represents what the author terms as the "paradigmatically terrible ex-boyfriend case" - someone who maintains control and authority while denying others fair compensation and accountability.
