# Major AI vendors will integrate AI into existing products in 2024

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-19 that other large companies with AI programs will also shove AI into their existing product lines in 2024, though less aggressively than OpenAI. The article specifically cites Google's Gemini/Bard integration across Google products as an example of this trend. However, the prediction states this integration trend "will be less pronounced there than at OpenAI." The hypothesis would be falsified if major vendors keep AI features separated from core products instead of integrating them.
