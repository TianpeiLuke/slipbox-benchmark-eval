# TechCrunch's Week in Review Newsletter Overview

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-07 that Week in Review (WiR) is the publication's weekly email digest column that highlights the major tech stories of the preceding few days, delivered to subscribers every Saturday. The newsletter opens with a greeting and description, stating it's "a darn useful resource for folks on the go." Key coverage includes Sam Altman backing teenage entrepreneurs' AI startup, Google's Pixel 8 hardware launch, TikTok testing an ad-free tier, and LinkedIn's new AI tools. The article notes that readers can "sign up here to get WiR in your inbox every Saturday."
