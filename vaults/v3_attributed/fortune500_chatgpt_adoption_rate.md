# Fortune 500 ChatGPT Adoption Rate

TechCrunch reported on 2023-09-28 that more than 92% of Fortune 500 companies use ChatGPT for wide-ranging business needs. The article states ChatGPT has "taken the world by storm" and evolved from a productivity tool into a "behemonth used by more than 92% of Fortune 500 companies." This widespread corporate adoption has made OpenAI one of the most-hyped companies despite recent leadership challenges.
