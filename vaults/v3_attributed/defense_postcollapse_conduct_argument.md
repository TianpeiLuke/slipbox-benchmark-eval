# Cohen's Defense Argument on Bankman-Fried's Innocence

Fortune reported on 2023-10-04 that during opening statements in Sam Bankman-Fried's trial, defense attorney Mark Cohen argued that when collapse approached, Bankman-Fried "didn't steal any money" but instead "was willing to give up his personal wealth to make customers whole." Cohen contended that Bankman-Fried behaved as an innocent party during the crisis, concluding that the founder of two billion-dollar businesses acted in good faith despite the catastrophic failure of his crypto empire.
