# Google's 2021 Default Search Engine Payments and Chrome Preinstallation Efforts

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-13 that Google spent $26.3 billion in 2021 to remain the default search engine across platforms and separately sought to have Chrome preinstalled on iPhones. These disclosures emerged during the Justice Department's antitrust case against Google, revealing how the company uses financial agreements to maintain its search dominance. The report indicates that Google's strategy includes Mobile App Distribution Agreements requiring Android manufacturers to pre-load Google apps including Search and Chrome, though these don't have to be default choices. Additionally, Google has revenue share agreements with smartphone makers and wireless carriers to further secure its market position.
