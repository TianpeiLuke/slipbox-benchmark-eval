# Google Search Query Manipulation Allegation

The Age reported on 2023-10-22 that Wired published an opinion piece by lawyer Megan Gray alleging Google manipulates search queries for ad revenue, citing an example where "children's clothing" was replaced with "NIKOLAI-brand kidswear." Google denied altering search terms, calling the article misleading, and Wired subsequently removed it for not meeting standards. Gray, a former DuckDuckGo vice-president, maintained that "Google manipulates Search to maximise ad revenue."
