# Google Ad-Tech Antitrust Case Distinction

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-31 that the Department of Justice filed a separate antitrust suit in January 2023 alongside eight states including New York, California and Colorado, specifically targeting Google's digital advertising dominance. Unlike the 2020 search market antitrust claim, this case aims to "halt Google's anticompetitive scheme, unwind Google's monopolistic grip on the market and restore competition to digital advertising."
