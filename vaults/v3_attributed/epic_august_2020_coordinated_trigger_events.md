# Epic's 2020 Fortnite Hotfix Lawsuits Against Apple and Google

The Verge reported on 2023-11-05 that on August 13, 2020, Epic "deployed a hotfix update to Fortnite without either company's knowledge, letting you purchase V-Bucks directly through its own payment processing option at a discount." Following this unauthorized hotfix enabling discounted direct V-Bucks purchases, both Apple and Google "almost immediately reacted by kicking Fortnite off their app stores for breaking the rules." Epic had anticipated these removals and "was ready and waiting with two lawsuits and an attack ad."
