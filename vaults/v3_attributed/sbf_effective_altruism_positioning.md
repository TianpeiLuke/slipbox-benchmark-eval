# Sam Bankman-Fried's Public Persona as Young Billionaire Savior

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-02 that Sam Bankman-Fried cultivated a public image as a young billionaire who "had nothing" eighteen months earlier but then possessed "$22.5 billion" and positioned himself as someone who "was going to spend it to save humanity from extinction." This characterization, described by Michael Lewis in his book "Going Infinite," presented SBF as "the richest person in the world under 30" with an ambitious vision to protect humanity from existential risks - an image that Lewis found astonishing to encounter.
