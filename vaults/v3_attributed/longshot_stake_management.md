# NFL Award Longshot Betting Strategy

Sporting News reported on 2023-12-05 that when betting on NFL award longshots, "So, keep your bets small, or use a bonus bet (if you have one available), on longshots. If they do go on to win, smile all the way to the bank while you tell everyone, 'I told you so.'" This strategy limits financial risk while maintaining potential upside, particularly with five weeks remaining in the regular season when longshots offer higher payouts than waiting until contenders emerge.
