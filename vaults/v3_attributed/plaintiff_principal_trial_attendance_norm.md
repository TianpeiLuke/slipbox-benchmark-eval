# Sweeney's Daily Trial Attendance Based on Respect for Legal Process

The Verge reported on 2023-12-12 that Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeney attended nearly every day of the Google antitrust trial because "Epic is asking a lot of the court system and the jury here, spending four weeks on a major antitrust trial full of complicated facts and evidence. It wouldn't be right to start something like this and not show up." Sweeney cited the precedent of Apple's Phil Schiller sitting through the entire Epic v. Apple trial as justification for his daily presence, stating it was "necessary to show respect for the legal process."
