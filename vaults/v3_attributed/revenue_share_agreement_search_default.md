# Google's Revenue Share Agreements Condition Payments on Default App Settings

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-13 that Google uses revenue share agreements (RSAs) as payment arrangements with Android OEMs and wireless carriers, conditioning Google's money on partners setting Google Search and Chrome browser as device defaults. Jamie Rosenberg, a Google employee focusing on Android and Google Play, testified that these RSAs specifically require partners like Verizon to make Google apps the default choices on devices in exchange for financial compensation.
