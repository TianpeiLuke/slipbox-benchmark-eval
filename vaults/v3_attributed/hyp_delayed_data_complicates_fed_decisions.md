# Fed's Data-Dependent Rate Approach May Be Compromised by Report Delays

The Sydney Morning Herald reported on 2023-10-01 that postponements of scheduled US economic reports could impair the Federal Reserve's stated data-dependent approach to interest rate decisions. The article states that "Postponements of such reports could complicate things for the Fed, which has insisted it will make upcoming decisions on interest rates based on what incoming data say about the economy." This hypothesis would be falsified if the Fed's October-November guidance continues to track published data cleanly despite any delays.
