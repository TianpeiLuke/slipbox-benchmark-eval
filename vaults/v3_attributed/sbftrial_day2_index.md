# Sam Bankman-Fried Trial Day 2: Jury Selection and Opening Statements

Fortune reported on 2023-10-04 that the second day of Sam Bankman-Fried's trial featured jury selection and opening statements from both sides. Prosecution attorney Thane Rehn described Bankman-Fried as a "man" who "stole billions of dollars from thousands of victims," alleging he used Alameda Research to secretly access customer funds. Defense attorney Mark Cohen countered that Bankman-Fried acted in "good faith" and didn't defraud anyone. The government's first witness was Marc-Antoine Julliard, a Paris-born crypto investor who lost nearly $100,000 when FTX collapsed.
