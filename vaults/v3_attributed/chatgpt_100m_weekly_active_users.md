# ChatGPT Reaches 100 Million Weekly Active Users

TechCrunch reported on 2023-09-28 that "Since its initial launch nearly a year ago, ChatGPT has hit 100 million weekly active users." The AI-powered chatbot developed by OpenAI has experienced rapid adoption since its November 2022 release, reaching this milestone approximately one year after launching. This growth has established ChatGPT as a dominant force in the AI landscape, with OpenAI continuing to invest heavily in the platform's development.
