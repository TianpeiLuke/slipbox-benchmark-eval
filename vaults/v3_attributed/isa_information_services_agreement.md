# Information Services Agreement (ISA) Definition and Function

The Verge reported on 2023-09-26 that the Information Services Agreement (ISA) is a contractual arrangement that has been in place since 2002, making Google's search engine the default on Apple's devices. The ISA involves yearly payments from Google to Apple in exchange for this default positioning. Apple's Eddy Cue, who negotiated the current iteration in 2016, testified that "there wasn't a valid alternative to Google at the time" and stated that Apple picks "the best one and let users easily change it." The agreement prevents Apple from offering users a choice of search engines during device setup.
