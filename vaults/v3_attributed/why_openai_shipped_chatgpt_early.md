# OpenAI's ChatGPT Launch Rationale and Anthropic Rivalry

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-30 that OpenAI released ChatGPT as a "low-key research preview" with the primary goal of harvesting real user interaction data to guide future model development, a decision that was reportedly accelerated by intense rivalry with Anthropic. The publication states that "the goal, OpenAI leadership told the OpenAI rank-and-file at the time, was to gather more data on how people use and interact with generative AI to inform the development of OpenAI's future models." This strategic move transformed ChatGPT from a basic free web interface into OpenAI's most popular product and the fastest-growing consumer application in history.
