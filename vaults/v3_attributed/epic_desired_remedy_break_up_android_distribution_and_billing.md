# Epic's Antitrust Goal Against Google Monopolies

The Verge reported on 2023-11-05 that Epic Games seeks to break up Google's alleged monopolies over Android app distribution and in-app payment processing. Epic's core argument is that "developers aren't stuck paying the 'Google Tax' or passing that fee along to you." The company claims Google's control over app distribution and payment methods creates an illegal monopoly that drives up app prices, forcing developers to pay high fees or pass costs to consumers. Epic previously challenged Apple's App Store in a similar antitrust case, but its lawsuit against Google had been delayed for nearly three years before finally going to trial.
