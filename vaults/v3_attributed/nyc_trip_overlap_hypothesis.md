# Kelce's NYC Visit Hypothesis During Swift's 1989 Re-Recording Period

The Age reported on 2023-09-26 that Travis Kelce took a week off training to visit New York City in August 2023, which "happened to be while Swift was re-recording her 1989 tracks in the city." This temporal overlap suggests potential private meetings between Kelce and Swift during her recording sessions there. The hypothesis that they spent private time together is falsifiable through verified schedules showing no overlap, but the concurrent timing of their presence in NYC supports the possibility of their interaction beyond public knowledge at that time.
