# Google's User Choice Billing Pilot Program

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-06 that Google introduced User Choice Billing in November 2022 as a pilot program allowing Play developers to process sales with their own payment systems. Google "charges app developers a 15% to 30% commission on the sales it processes" but reduces this commission by 4% for companies who choose to use their own payment processing solution instead of Google's first-party billing system.
