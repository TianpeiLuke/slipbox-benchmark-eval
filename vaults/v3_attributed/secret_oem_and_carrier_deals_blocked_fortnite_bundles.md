# Epic's Bundling Attempt Blocked by Google's Secret Deals

The Verge reported on 2023-12-12 that Epic Games discovered Google had secretly paid carriers and OEMs not to carry competing app stores, corroborating CEO Tim Sweeney's suspicions about anti-competitive practices. "When we tried to bundle Fortnite with other smartphone manufacturers like OnePlus and carriers of all sorts, they told us they couldn't do a deal because Google had done a secret deal with them."
