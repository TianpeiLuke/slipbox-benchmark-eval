# Project Liberty was Epic's secret plan to bypass app store fees and sue Google

The Verge reported on 2023-11-07 that Epic Games had a covert plan called "Project Liberty" to modify Fortnite with code that would bypass app-store payment fees, leading to the game's removal from Apple's and Google's stores, and subsequent lawsuits. Epic's lead attorney Gary Bornstein argued that Google maintained its dominance by paying competitors not to compete, stating "Google pays actual and potential competitors not to compete. Literally gives them money and other things of value." The strategy aimed to portray Google as a monopolistic bully while positioning Epic as the underdog fighting for consumer benefit through increased competition.
