# Prosecution's Alleged Theft Destinations

Fortune reported on 2023-10-04 that prosecutor Thane Rehn outlined three destinations of alleged stolen customer funds in his opening statement: Bankman-Fried's jet-setting lifestyle, millions donated to political candidates, and risky bets through his hedge fund. "All of that, all of it, was built on lies," Rehn declared to the jury.
