# FTX Trading Volume Reached $15 Billion Daily at Its Peak

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-02 that trading volume on FTX climbed to about $15 billion per day at its peak. This massive daily trading activity preceded the exchange's dramatic collapse and is cited as the scale of operations that ultimately led to Sam Bankman-Fried's fraud charges. The article references Michael Lewis's upcoming book "Going Infinite," which chronicles SBF's rise and fall based on over 100 interviews conducted between 2021-2023.
