# Travis Kelce's NFL Credentials and Pop Culture Crossover Potential

The Age reported on 2023-09-26 that Travis Kelce, the Kansas City Chiefs tight end wearing number 87, "helped bring his team to Super Bowl victory in both 2020 and 2023." Kelce co-hosts the podcast New Heights with his brother, establishing his media presence beyond football. These biographical identifiers position him as the NFL-side party in potential celebrity crossovers, as evidenced by his documented interactions with pop star Taylor Swift beginning in 2016.
