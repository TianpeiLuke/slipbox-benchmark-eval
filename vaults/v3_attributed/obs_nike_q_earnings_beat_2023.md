# Nike's Stock Jumped 6.7% Following Strong Quarterly Profit Report

The Sydney Morning Herald reported on 2023-10-01 that Nike's shares rose 6.7 per cent on 29 September 2023 after the company reported better profit for the latest quarter than analysts expected. "Strength overseas helped it make up for some declines in North America," according to the publication. This performance occurred during Wall Street's worst month and quarter of the year, as the S&P 500 slipped 0.3 per cent and the Dow Jones fell 0.5 per cent.
