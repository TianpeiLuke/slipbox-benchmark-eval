# Origin of Michael Lewis's SBF Book Through Character Evaluation Request

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-02 that Michael Lewis didn't initially set out to write about Sam Bankman-Fried, but was introduced to him in early 2021 by a Berkeley friend who was considering backing FTX and asked Lewis to "evaluate his character." Lewis was so impressed by SBF's apparent brilliance and rapid ascent from having nothing to $22.5 billion that he decided to write about him, leading to over 100 meetings between them over the following two years. "My jaw was on the floor," Lewis said about SBF's transformation into the richest person in the world under 30.
