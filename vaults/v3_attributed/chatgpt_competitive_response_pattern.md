# Competition-Driven AI Chatbot Development Post-ChatGPT

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-30 that the competitive AI marketplace shifted focus at other firms after ChatGPT's success. "Google scrambled to launch a response to ChatGPT, eventually releasing Bard, a more or less comparable AI chatbot, in February." Following this release, Amazon introduced Q, described as "a more business-oriented take on ChatGPT." Additionally, Google's DeepMind was anticipated to debut its next-generation chatbot Gemini before year's end.
