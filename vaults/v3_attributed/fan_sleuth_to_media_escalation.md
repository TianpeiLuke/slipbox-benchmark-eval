# Swift–Kelce Cultural Pipeline

The Age reported on 2023-09-26 that the Swift–Kelce story moved through a two-stage pipeline: "So, it was only a matter of time before it entered the cultural zeitgeist." First, social-media fan detection produced claims about their connection, then legacy media confirmation converted these into cultural relevance. Only the second step carried institutional warrant, transforming fan speculation into mainstream narrative.
