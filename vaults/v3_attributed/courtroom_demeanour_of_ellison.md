# Caroline Ellison identified Sam Bankman-Fried in court by his suit

The Verge reported on 2023-10-10 that Caroline Ellison "gazed around the courtroom for more than 20 seconds" trying to identify Sam Bankman-Fried, and "after she did spot him, she was asked to identify him, which she did by identifying him as wearing a suit." This moment drew chuckles from the defense table.
