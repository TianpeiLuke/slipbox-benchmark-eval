# Sportsbook Profit Model Through Balanced Betting

Sporting News reported on 2023-11-06 that "Every sportsbook's goal is to even out the betting on both sides of every bet." This balancing act allows sportsbooks to capture the built-in profit from the vigorish (vig) regardless of the outcome. Sportsbooks achieve this by adjusting odds and lines based on betting patterns, moving lines to encourage equal action on both sides. When one side receives heavy betting, the sportsbook will shift the line or odds to make the opposite side more attractive, ensuring they can secure their profit margin without risking their capital on the game's result.
