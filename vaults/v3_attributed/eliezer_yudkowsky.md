# Eliezer Yudkowsky's Role in AI Development and Singularity Community

The Age reported on 2023-12-09 that Eliezer Yudkowsky is "an internet philosopher and self-taught AI researcher" who influenced the Rationalist and effective altruist communities concerned with AI safety. Yudkowsky warned that AI systems might act contrary to human intentions, potentially turning against their creators. The article notes his indirect influence on DeepMind's founding, as Thiel-funded researchers connected to Yudkowsky's ideas sought to build "artificial general intelligence." Yudkowsky's community centered around concerns about the technological singularity, where AI surpasses human intelligence.
