# Prosecutors Use Bankman-Fried's Public Statements as Evidence of Intent

The Verge reported on 2023-09-28 that prosecutors in the Sam Bankman-Fried trial plan to use his public communications as evidence. The indictment calls his November 2022 tweets "false and misleading," alleging that his public statements on Twitter and in interviews will demonstrate a divergence between what he said and what actually happened. This strategy aims to establish intent by showing that Bankman-Fried's public claims about FTX's operations and customer fund segregation were deliberate deceptions while he was simultaneously directing the misappropriation of billions of dollars in customer deposits for personal investments and political contributions.
