# Google's Gemini AI Model Launch Strategy

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-09 that Google unveiled its new flagship AI model family called Gemini during the week of December 8, 2023. However, the company only released a reduced "lite" version named Gemini Pro, while withholding the full-capacity Gemini Ultra model. According to TechCrunch, "Google touted Gemini's coding capabilities and multimodal prowess, claiming that the model can understand images, audio and videos just as well as text," but the released Gemini Pro version was limited to text-in, text-out functionality and proved to be mistake-prone.
