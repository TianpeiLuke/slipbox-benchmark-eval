# Anecdotal Gemini Pro failures don't establish overall model quality

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-07 that anecdotal evidence does not establish Gemini Pro's overall quality, since these reports are uncontrolled single-prompt observations. The article notes that while users reported failures in fact recall, translation, and coding tasks, "Perhaps it's genuinely improved in some areas — posts on X suggest as much." Regarding coding specifically, the article acknowledges mixed evidence: while some users reported failures in basic programming tasks, other posts on X indicate both success and failure cases, making the overall assessment inconclusive.
