# ASX Large-Cap Decliners on 2023-10-01

The Sydney Morning Herald reported on 2023-10-01 that Pilbara Minerals (down 2.1 per cent) and Qantas (down 1.5 per cent) were also among the biggest large-cap decliners. The report noted that Treasury Wine Estates (down 1.9 per cent) and Woolworths (down 1.2 per cent) dragged the consumer staples sector (down 1.1 per cent) lower. Despite stronger iron ore prices boosting some stocks, most sectors traded lower amid expectations of higher-for-longer interest rates ahead of the Reserve Bank's meeting.
