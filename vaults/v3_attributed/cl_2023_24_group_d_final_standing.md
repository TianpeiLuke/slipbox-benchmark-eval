# Champions League Group D Final Standings 2023/24

Sporting News reported on 2023-12-12 that Group D concluded with Real Sociedad finishing first on 12 points and +5 goal difference, Inter Milan also on 12 points but with +3 goal difference, while Benfica and RB Salzburg both ended level on 4 points and -4 goal difference. "Real Sociedad and Inter Milan dominated Group D and could not be separated as they shared a 0-0 draw at San Siro."
