# FTX paid millions to sports stars for legitimacy

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-02 that FTX paid high-profile sports endorsers substantial sums to lend the exchange legitimacy and competitive edge, with Tom Brady receiving "$55 million" and Steph Curry "$35 million" for their marketing deals.
