# CeeDee Lamb's OPOY chances hinge on Tyreek Hill's schedule difficulty

Sporting News reported on 2023-12-05 that CeeDee Lamb could overtake Tyreek Hill for Offensive Player of the Year if Hill's production declines against tough defenses while Lamb maintains his current elevated pace. "Based on his current season average, Lamb is on track for close to 1,600 yards, but based on his production over the last seven games, he could finish with closer to 1,800 yards," according to the analysis. The key factor is that four of Miami's five remaining opponents have pass defenses ranked in the league's top eight, which could slow Hill's historic 2,000-yard pursuit. Meanwhile, Lamb has shown consistent production and would need Hill to "fade down the stretch" to have a realistic shot at stealing the award.
