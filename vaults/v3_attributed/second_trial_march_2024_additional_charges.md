# Second Bankman-Fried Trial Scheduled for March 2024 with Additional Charges

The Verge reported on 2023-09-28 that Sam Bankman-Fried's second trial is scheduled for March 2024, covering additional charges beyond the seven counts being tried in the first proceeding. The first trial involves two counts of wire fraud and five conspiracy charges related to the FTX cryptocurrency exchange fraud. Prosecutors allege Bankman-Fried diverted billions of dollars in customer funds to Alameda Research and used misappropriated deposits for personal investments and political contributions. According to court filings, Bankman-Fried directed special code features that gave Alameda unlimited access to FTX funds while publicly denying such privileged access existed.
