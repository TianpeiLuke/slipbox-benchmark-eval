# NFL Award Longshots Definition and Rationale

Sporting News reported on 2023-12-05 that NFL award longshots are players or coaches with very long betting odds who are performing at high levels but lack proportional public or voter recognition due to more prominent players dominating narratives. These candidates "have not gotten the credit they deserve" despite strong performances, with examples including Jared Goff and C.J. Stroud for MVP, CeeDee Lamb for OPOY, Danielle Hunter for DPOY, Sean Payton for Coach of the Year, and Russell Wilson for Comeback POY. The article explains that longshots often have legitimate statistical cases but are overlooked because "other players have commanded the spotlight."
