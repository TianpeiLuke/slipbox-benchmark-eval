# Cue counters Bellshaw's profit argument by emphasizing platform value over raw revenue

The Verge reported on 2023-09-26 that during the US v. Google antitrust trial, Justice Department lawyer Meagan Bellshaw questioned Apple executive Eddy Cue about whether the Information Services Agreement (ISA) accounted for a significant portion of Apple's profits. Cue responded that Apple doesn't view the money that way because the figure "omits the work Apple did to make its platform attractive enough for the deal to perform." This reframed the discussion from raw profit margins to the broader ecosystem value that enables such partnerships.
