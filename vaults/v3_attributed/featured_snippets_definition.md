# Featured Snippets as Traffic-Diverting Mechanism

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-15 that Featured Snippets are Google's algorithmically extracted answer summaries pulled directly from webpages and surfaced above organic results, which the complaint cites as another traffic-diverting mechanism. The lawsuit filed by Helena World Chronicle argues that Google's "Featured Snippets" technology shifts traffic away from publishers' websites by displaying algorithmically extracted answers from webpages directly in search results. According to the complaint, this practice represents one of the ways Google "siphons off" news publishers' content, readers and ad revenue through anticompetitive means.
