# Peter Thiel's AI Investment Origins

The Age reported on 2023-12-09 that Peter Thiel's wealth came from "an early investment in Facebook and through his work with Musk in the early days of PayPal." The publication noted that Thiel "had developed a fascination with the singularity, a trope of science fiction that describes the moment when intelligent technology can no longer be controlled." This positioned him as a key figure in funding singularity-focused AI research, particularly evidenced by his hosting Demis Hassabis at his San Francisco home in 2010 to discuss artificial general intelligence development.
