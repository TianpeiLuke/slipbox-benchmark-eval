# Anti-Fragmentation Agreements bar OEMs from frictionless app installation outside Google Play

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-06 that Anti-Fragmentation Agreements (AFAs) are Google-OEM contract terms that "prevent the OEMs from modifying Android to allow for frictionless downloading of apps outside of Google Play." These agreements were highlighted in Epic Games' antitrust case against Google, where Epic referenced a canceled deal with OnePlus that would have distributed Epic games through its own app store. The AFAs effectively bar manufacturers from creating modifications that would enable easy installation of apps from alternative sources, maintaining Google Play's dominant market position where 90% of Android apps are downloaded.
