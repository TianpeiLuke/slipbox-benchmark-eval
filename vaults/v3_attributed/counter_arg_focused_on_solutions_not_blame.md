# Bankman-Fried's Nonchalant Response to $8 Billion Missing Funds

The Verge reported on 2023-10-31 that Sam Bankman-Fried faced questioning about the $8 billion discrepancy between FTX and Alameda Research, to which he responded, "I wasn't trying to build out blame for it," claiming he was "focused on solutions." This explanation was met with skepticism, particularly given that no disciplinary actions or corrective measures followed the discovery. As prosecutor Danielle Sassoon pointed out with notable composure, Bankman-Fried did not fire anyone nor implement any repayment plans or risk management rules after learning of the shortfall. His testimony suggested a lack of urgency or accountability, despite being told by employee Adam Yedidia about the issue as early as June 2022. When asked directly whether he had instructed Alameda not to spend FTX customer deposits, Bankman-Fried answered no, indicating a significant failure in oversight. The absence of concrete action post-discovery undermines his claim of prioritizing solutions over blame.
