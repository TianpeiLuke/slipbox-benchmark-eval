# ChatGPT Plus Subscription Benefits

TechCrunch reported on 2023-09-28 that ChatGPT Plus is OpenAI's paid subscription tier whose "defining differentiator is access to GPT-4 and GPT-4 Turbo." The subscription remained paused until December 13, 2023, when OpenAI re-enabled sign-ups after a November pause due to high usage demand. While GPT-4 is largely exclusive to paying ChatGPT Plus users, it can also be accessed for free through Microsoft's Bing Chat integration.
