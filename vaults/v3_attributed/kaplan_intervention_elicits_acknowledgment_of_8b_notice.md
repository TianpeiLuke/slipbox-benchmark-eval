# Judge Kaplan's Direct Question About Yedidia's Disclosure

The Verge reported on 2023-10-31 that Judge Lewis Kaplan had to directly ask Bankman-Fried whether Yedidia had ever told him about the $8 billion hole "in words or in substance" before Bankman-Fried finally admitted he had been informed. This occurred during Bankman-Fried's testimony when he appeared to be denying a previous conversation with Yedidia about the missing funds.
