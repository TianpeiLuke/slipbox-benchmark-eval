# Hard-Launch Definition in Celebrity Relationships

The Age reported on 2023-09-26 that to "hard-launch" a relationship means deliberately making the first public appearance as a couple, ending prior speculation. The publication used this term when describing how Taylor Swift's attendance at Travis Kelce's NFL game would terminate rumors about their romantic status, distinguishing it from the previous ambiguous staged proximity between them.
