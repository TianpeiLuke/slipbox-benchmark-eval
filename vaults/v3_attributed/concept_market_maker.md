# Market Maker Defense in FTX Trial

Fortune reported on 2023-10-04 that Mark Cohen, defending Sam Bankman-Fried, argued Alameda Research's trading on FTX was legitimate because "Alameda acted legally as a customer, payment processor, and market maker, or financial entity that acts as a trading partner for customers looking to buy and sell cryptocurrencies."
