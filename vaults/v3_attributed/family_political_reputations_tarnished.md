# FTX Trial Reveals Reputational Damage to Bankman-Fried's Associates

The Verge reported on 2023-10-26 that Joe Bankman, Barbara Fried, and Gabe Bankman-Fried have become "undesirable in the political arenas where they previously operated, as reputational damage from FTX's funding of their causes replaced their earlier access." The article details how Sam Bankman-Fried's fraudulent activities at FTX affected those closest to him, including his father Joe Bankman, Fried's political action committee Mind the Gap, and Gabe Bankman-Fried's nonprofit Guarding Against Pandemics, all of whom received FTX customer funds. As former associates like Adam Yedidia, Gary Wang, Nishad Singh, and Caroline Ellison faced criminal charges and publicity, "Bankman, Fried, and Gabe Bankman-Fried's reputations are tarnished; in the political arenas where they once operated, they are suddenly undesirable."
