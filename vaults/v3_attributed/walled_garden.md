# Walled Garden Definition in Epic v. Google Context

The Verge reported on 2023-12-12 that "walled garden" refers to a closed mobile platform where the owner controls both app distribution and in-app payment systems. The publication explicitly applied this term to Apple as Epic's rival, contrasting it with Google's Android setup. According to the article, Apple "almost entirely won a similar case two years ago" while Google was found to have an illegal monopoly over Android app distribution and in-app payment systems.
