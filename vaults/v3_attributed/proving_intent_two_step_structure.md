# Government's Two-Step Burden to Prove Criminal Intent in Bankman-Fried Trial

The Verge reported on 2023-09-28 that the prosecution faces a two-step burden to establish criminal intent in the Sam Bankman-Fried trial. First, they must demonstrate that specific transactions occurred through records from the Southern District of New York. Second, they must connect those transactions to Bankman-Fried by proving he knew his actions were wrong and lied about them anyway. According to the indictment, Bankman-Fried allegedly directed code changes that gave Alameda unlimited access to FTX customer funds while publicly claiming there were no conflicts of interest. The government must show that Bankman-Fried's statements about customer asset segregation were deliberate lies, as alleged in the August 14th superseding indictment.
