# SEO Practitioner Stereotype as Content Goblin Hustler

The Verge reported on 2023-11-01 that the stock SEO practitioner fits a stereotype of being a "hustler" or "content goblin" who "trades rules, morals and good taste for eyeballs," plus acts as "a nihilist prankster pleased at getting away with it." This characterization emerged from journalist Nilay Patel's investigation into the SEO industry, where practitioners exploit human impulses for traffic and profit through manipulative search optimization techniques. The stereotype reflects public frustration with declining Google Search quality, as SEO has spread to degrade authentic content with promotional messaging that prioritizes advertising revenue over genuine information sharing.
