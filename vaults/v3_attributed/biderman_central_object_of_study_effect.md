# ChatGPT's Central Role in AI Research and Development

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-30 that ChatGPT became the "central object of study" for AI researchers and developers after removing the skill threshold needed to get usable output from text-generating models. Stella Biderman, an AI researcher at Booz Allen Hamilton and EleutherAI, stated that ChatGPT's primary impact "encouraging people training AIs to try to mimic it, or encouraging people studying AIs to use it as their central object of study." Previously, users needed some skill to consistently get usable results from text-generating models, but ChatGPT changed this dynamic by making generative AI accessible to a broader audience. This shift redirected significant attention and discussion toward the technology, influencing both OpenAI's internal priorities and competitive responses from other AI firms like Google's Bard and Amazon Q.
