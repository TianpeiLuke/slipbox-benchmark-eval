# Epic observed chat censorship when Google's message retention was revealed

The Verge reported on 2023-12-12 that during the Epic v. Google trial, Epic observed message threads on antitrust-sensitive topics falling silent immediately when participants realized chat history was being recorded. Tim Sweeney stated that Google would then switch off retention and destroy the messages, a practice he said was "called out in detail at trial." This observation was part of Epic's evidence showing Google's attempts to control narrative around antitrust issues, with Sweeney noting the company's bad faith efforts to suppress discussion of their monopolistic practices.
