# Kelce's Explanation for Failed Contact with Swift

The Age reported on 2023-09-26 that Kelce explained his failed attempt to contact Taylor Swift at her concert, attributing it to her show-day protocol. He stated, "I was disappointed that she doesn't talk before or after her shows because she has to save her voice for the 44 songs she sings. She doesn't meet anybody, or at least she didn't want to meet me, so I took it personal." This explanation was given on his podcast New Heights.
