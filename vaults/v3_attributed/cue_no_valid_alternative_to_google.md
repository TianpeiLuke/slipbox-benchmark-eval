# Apple's 2016 Search Deal Rationale

The Verge reported on 2023-09-26 that Eddy Cue testified Apple never seriously considered another search provider or building its own search in 2016 because "certainly there wasn't a valid alternative to Google at the time." Cue stated this claim still holds true today. During US v. Google antitrust trial testimony, Cue explained that Apple's product evaluation served as the warrant for this decision, concluding that Google offered the best search experience despite privacy concerns. The Information Services Agreement renegotiation focused on revenue-sharing terms, with Cue saying he "always felt like it was in Google's best interest, and our best interest, to get a deal done."
