# FTX Trial Exposed How Sam Bankman-Fried Destroyed Lives of Closest Friends

The Verge reported on 2023-10-26 that the FTX trial revealed how Sam Bankman-Fried "blew up the lives of the people who are closest to him" while orchestrating fraud at Alameda Research and FTX. The prosecution's case showed that "everyone who loved Bankman-Fried most in the world is now caught up in the case," including former roommates Adam Yedidia, Gary Wang, and Nishad Singh, along with ex-girlfriend Caroline Ellison and parents Joe Bankman and Barbara Fried. These individuals faced legal consequences, reputational damage, and financial ruin as a result of their association with Bankman-Fried's fraudulent activities.
