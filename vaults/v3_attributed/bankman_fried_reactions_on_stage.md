# Bankman-Fried's visible reactions during trial testimony

The Verge reported on 2023-10-10 that Sam Bankman-Fried was observed exhibiting physical reactions during witness testimonies at his criminal fraud trial. During Gary Wang's testimony, Bankman-Fried was seen "vibrating slightly," and his "bouncing became more noticeable" during Caroline Ellison's testimony. These observations occurred on the same day Ellison testified about her guilty pleas to fraud and money laundering charges related to her role at Alameda Research, where she stated that Bankman-Fried "directed me to commit these crimes."
