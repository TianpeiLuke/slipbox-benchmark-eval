# Bankman-Fried faces seven federal charges in October 2023 trial

CNBC reported on 2023-10-06 that Sam Bankman-Fried, 31, faces seven federal charges including wire fraud, securities fraud and money laundering, an exposure that could put him in prison for the rest of his life. As of the 6 October 2023 trial, Bankman-Fried "faces seven federal charges, including wire fraud, securities fraud and money laundering, that could put him in prison for the rest of his life."
