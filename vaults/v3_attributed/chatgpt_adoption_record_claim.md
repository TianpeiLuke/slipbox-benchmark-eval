# ChatGPT's Record-Breaking Consumer Adoption

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-30 that ChatGPT became "the fastest-growing consumer app in history" and OpenAI's "most popular product… ever" following its release as a basic free web interface on GPT-3.5. The article attributes this rapid growth to ChatGPT's sophisticated conversational abilities that made generative AI accessible to mainstream users, with Sam Altman reflecting on Twitter that "what a year it's been" since the launch.
