# Ellison testified about Alameda's early financial instability

The Verge reported on 2023-10-10 that Caroline Ellison testified about Alameda Research's precarious financial situation shortly after she joined in 2018. "Shortly after I started, I learned the company was in worse shape than I realized," Ellison stated during her testimony. She explained that Alameda's initial funding came from loans "from acquaintances" that were recalled a few weeks after her arrival. This revelation came amid testimony about the broader FTX fraud trial, where Ellison has already pleaded guilty to criminal charges related to her relationship with Sam Bankman-Fried.
