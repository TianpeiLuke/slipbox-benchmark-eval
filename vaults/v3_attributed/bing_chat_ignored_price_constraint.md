# Bing Chat fails budget constraint in kids BMX bike search

The Age reported on 2023-10-22 that Bing Chat failed to honor a stated budget constraint when searching for kids BMX bikes. After initially offering "a comparison of a few different options but they were too expensive," Bing Chat responded to "any under $200?" with "more options that were all over $300." This demonstrates a recorded failure of the AI assistant to respect explicit price limitations in follow-up queries.
