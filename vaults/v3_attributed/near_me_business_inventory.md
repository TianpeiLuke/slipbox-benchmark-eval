# Google-Optimized Business Names Found Across US Cities

The Verge reported on 2023-10-26 that a search for Google-optimized business names revealed specific examples across the United States. "Among the businesses I was able to find: a chain of half a dozen Affordable Dentist Near Me's in Texas; an Antiques Near Me two hours outside of New York City; seven Plumber Near Me businesses; a Phone Repair Near Me in Cape Cod, Massachusetts; a Psychic Near Me in Chicago; and more than 20 iterations of 'Notary Near Me' across the US."
