# US Economic Data Calendar and Fed Meeting Timeline

The Sydney Morning Herald reported on 2023-10-01 that "The latest monthly update on the US jobs market is due next week, with a couple of important reports on inflation coming the following week." The article noted these reports would precede the Federal Reserve's next rate meeting, which was scheduled to conclude on November 1, 2023. This sequence of economic indicators was described as potentially complicating the Fed's interest rate decisions, as the central bank had indicated it would base upcoming policy choices on incoming economic data.
