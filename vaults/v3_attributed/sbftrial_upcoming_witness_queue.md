# Court adjourns with prosecution witness sequence set

Fortune reported on 2023-10-04 that the court adjourned near 4:30 p.m. on 2023-10-04, with the scheduled sequence of further prosecution witnesses being "Adam Yedidia continuing on Thursday, then Matt Huang, a former partner at Sequoia Capital, then Gary Wang, a key Bankman-Fried lieutenant."
