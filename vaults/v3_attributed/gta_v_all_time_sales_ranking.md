# Grand Theft Auto V Sales Standing

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-09 that Rockstar Games' Grand Theft Auto V "remains the second-best-selling video game of all time, falling short only of Minecraft." The article noted this franchise context while covering the viral success of the Grand Theft Auto VI trailer, emphasizing how the previous entry maintains its strong market position a decade after release.
