# Steam Deck OLED Wi-Fi 6E Performance Hypothesis

Engadget reported on 2023-11-09 that the Steam Deck OLED's Wi-Fi 6E module and improved antennas will exceed 562 mbps and show a wider margin over the original Deck only when paired with a 6E router and multi-gigabit internet. "The 10 percent gain on a Wi-Fi 6 router would be the falsifier if a compatible 6E network produced no further improvement." The review noted that while the Steam Deck OLED features improved hardware including a die-shrunk custom AMD APU, the wireless performance specifically depends on the router compatibility and internet speed conditions to demonstrate significant improvements over the original model.
