# Helena World Chronicle Publisher Profile

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-15 that Helena World Chronicle, an Arkansas-based publisher owning two weekly newspapers, filed a class action antitrust lawsuit against Google. The publisher alleges Google "siphons off" news content, readers, and advertising revenue through anticompetitive practices including AI technologies like Search Generative Experience and Bard chatbot. Helena World Chronicle claims Google's actions are "starving the free press" by misappropriating publisher content for its Knowledge Graph database, which grew to 500 billion facts about 5 billion entities by 2020.
