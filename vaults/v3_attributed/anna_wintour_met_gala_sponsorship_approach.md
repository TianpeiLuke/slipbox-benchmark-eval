# Anna Wintour Contacted SBF for Met Gala Sponsorship

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-02 that Anna Wintour contacted Sam Bankman-Fried over a video call to ask him to sponsor the Met Costume Ball. The article describes this as occurring during SBF's period of prominence with FTX, characterizing it as a sign of desperate times given SBF's casual attire preferences. This interaction was recounted by Michael Lewis in his book "Going Infinite," which chronicles SBF's rise and fall.
