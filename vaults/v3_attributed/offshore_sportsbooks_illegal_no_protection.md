# Legal Sportsbook Requirement for NBA ROTY Betting

Sporting News reported on 2023-10-04 that bettors should "ensure you're using only legal online sportsbooks" when wagering on NBA Rookie of the Year odds. The publication specifically warns that "offshore sportsbooks advertise widely on the internet, but they are illegal and offer no protection against fraud and other criminal activities." This creates a counter-argument to shopping for the best ROTY odds anywhere online, since heavily advertised offshore sportsbooks must be excluded from consideration due to their illegal status and lack of consumer protections in the relevant market.
