# Contradictory Claims About X's Daily Post Volume Remain Unresolved

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-07 that X's daily post metrics remain unclear after conflicting statements from Elon Musk and CEO Linda Yaccarino. Musk stated in September that X users generate "100 million to 200 million posts every day, excluding retweets," while Yaccarino claimed at an October event that X sees "500 million posts per day." TechCrunch noted the discrepancy as unresolved, stating "So who's right? Beats us," leaving both figures unverified.
