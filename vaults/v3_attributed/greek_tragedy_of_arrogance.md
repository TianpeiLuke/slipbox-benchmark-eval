# FTX Trial Reveals Greek Tragedy of Betrayed Friends

The Verge reported on 2023-10-26 that the FTX trial revealed how Sam Bankman-Fried's arrogance destroyed those closest to him. "It is a Greek tragedy, precipitated by one man's character flaw: the arrogance of Sam Bankman-Fried." The prosecution's case showed that Bankman-Fried's former friends—roommates Adam Yedidia and Gary Wang, longtime family friend Nishad Singh, and ex-girlfriend Caroline Ellison—all became co-conspirators who testified against him. His parents Joe Bankman and Barbara Fried, along with his brother Gabe Bankman-Fried, were also implicated through their involvement in receiving FTX customer funds for political donations and nonprofits. Several associates now face felony convictions and ruined reputations.
