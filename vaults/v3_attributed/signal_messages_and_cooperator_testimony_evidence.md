# Evidence in the FTX fraud trial may include Signal messages and testimony from cooperating co-conspirators

The Verge reported on 2023-09-28 that evidence in the Sam Bankman-Fried fraud trial "may include Signal messages and testimony from former co-conspirators who have pleaded guilty and now cooperate with the government." The trial involves seven counts of wire fraud and conspiracy charges related to the alleged misappropriation of billions in customer funds from FTX to benefit Alameda Research and Bankman-Fried personally.
