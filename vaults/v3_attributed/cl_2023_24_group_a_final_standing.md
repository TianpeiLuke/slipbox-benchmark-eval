# 2023/24 Champions League Group A Final Standings

Sporting News reported on 2023-12-12 that after six matchdays of the 2023/24 Champions League Group A, Bayern Munich finished with 16 points (5W 0D 0L, GD +6), while FC Copenhagen had 8 points (2W 2D 2L, GD 0). "Bayern Munich coasted into the knockout rounds but Copenhagen, Galatasaray and Manchester United all entered the final matchweek with a chance of joining the Bundesliga giants." Galatasaray ended with 5 points and Manchester United with 4 points, confirming Bayern and Copenhagen as qualifiers.
