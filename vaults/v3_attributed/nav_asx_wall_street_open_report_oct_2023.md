# SMH 1 October 2023 market briefing structure and key market movements

The Sydney Morning Herald reported on 2023-10-01 that the ASX was set to open lower following Wall Street's September losses. The article's navigation structure included market decliners like Treasury Wine Estates (down 1.9%) and Woolworths (down 1.2%), with the consumer staples sector falling 1.1%. Key drivers mentioned were iron ore prices rising 1.4% and RBA rate expectations, as "interest-rate sensitive sectors including healthcare and consumer companies were among the weakest." The piece covered Wall Street's performance with the S&P 500 down 0.3%, Dow Jones falling 0.5%, and Treasury yields returning to 4.58%. Major stock moves included Nike up 6.7% and Blue Apron soaring 134.5% following acquisition news.
