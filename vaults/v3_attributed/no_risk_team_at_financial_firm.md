# Alameda's Lack of Risk Team as Governance Choice

The Verge reported on 2023-10-28 that Sam Bankman-Fried's testimony revealed Alameda Research lacked a risk team, which the article characterizes as a significant governance choice. During courtroom testimony, Bankman-Fried acknowledged that "not having a risk team, when you are any kind of financial anything, is certainly a choice." The absence of such a team at a cryptocurrency trading firm and later exchange operator like Alameda Research represents a deliberate organizational decision rather than a neutral operational detail, according to the reporter's analysis of the proceedings.
