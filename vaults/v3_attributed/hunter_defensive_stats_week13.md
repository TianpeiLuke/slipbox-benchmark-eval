# Danielle Hunter's Defensive Performance Through Week 13, 2023

Sporting News reported on 2023-12-05 that through Week 13 of the 2023 season, Danielle Hunter "leads the league in tackles for a loss and is third in sacks with 13.5 (behind Khalil Mack and Watt)." The article notes that Hunter's high-level production has been "instrumental in the Vikings turning things around after a 1-4 start," and that he has played "as well, if not better" than defensive favorites Micah Parsons, Myles Garrett, and T.J. Watt, making him deserving of consideration for Defensive Player of the Year.
