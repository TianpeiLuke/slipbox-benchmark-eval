# IronNet Cybersecurity Startup Shuts Down After Post-IPO Struggles

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-07 that IronNet, the Virginia-based cybersecurity startup founded in 2014 by retired four-star general and former NSA director Keith Alexander, has shut down and laid off its remaining staff. The company had raised more than $400 million in funding but failed to gain traction after its August 2021 IPO, experiencing a steep stock decline following an initial spike. IronNet's closure marks the end of a cybersecurity venture that once held significant promise in the industry.
