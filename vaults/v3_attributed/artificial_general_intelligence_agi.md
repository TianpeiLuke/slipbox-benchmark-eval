# Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) Definition and DeepMind's 2010 Fundraising Goal

The Age reported on 2023-12-09 that artificial general intelligence (AGI) is defined as "a machine that could do anything the human brain could do." In 2010, Demis Hassabis and two British colleagues sought funding to build this technology, representing AGI as "a machine that could do anything the brain could do." This fundraising effort occurred when few people were interested in AI, five years before DeepMind's cat recognition breakthrough on YouTube, and three years before Elon Musk and Larry Page's pivotal discussion about AI's potential dangers versus benefits.
