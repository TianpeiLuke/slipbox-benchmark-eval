# FTX Funding and Valuation Before Collapse

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-01 that within two years of founding FTX in 2019, more than 80 investors provided about "$2 billion in capital to FTX." Its last public funding round was a "$400 million Series C" in January 2022 that increased valuation to "$32 billion."
