# ChatGPT mobile app revenue estimate

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-30 that analytics firm Data.ai estimated ChatGPT's mobile apps generated nearly $30 million in subscription revenue. This figure is notable because the apps had launched only months earlier, demonstrating rapid monetization success for OpenAI's chatbot platform.
