# DraftKings Offers More Open Betting Markets Than Competitors

Sporting News reported on 2023-11-06 that DraftKings has "more open betting markets than most online sportsbooks, and the lines and odds are always competitive." The article noted that while major sportsbooks like Caesars and BetMGM also offer strong competition, DraftKings distinguishes itself through market availability. This assessment came from their analysis of line shopping strategies, where bettors compare odds across platforms to maximize value.
