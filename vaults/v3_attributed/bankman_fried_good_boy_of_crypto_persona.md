# Bankman-Fried's Pre-Collapse Media Strategy and Persona Building

The Verge reported on 2023-09-28 that before FTX's collapse, Sam Bankman-Fried actively cultivated a public image as crypto's "trustworthy face" through extensive media appearances and interviews. According to the publication, Bankman-Fried strategically positioned himself as an important, boyish figure for the cryptocurrency industry despite FTX being newer than competing exchanges. The article quotes Bankman-Fried as telling a colleague that "I honestly think it's negative EV for me to cut my hair. I think it's important for people to think I look crazy," suggesting he deliberately maintained an unkempt appearance as part of his public persona strategy. This calculated approach to publicity helped rapidly elevate FTX's industry standing, making his subsequent fall from grace particularly damaging to the broader crypto industry's reputation.
