# FTX Executives' Emergency Meeting Decision Against Shutting Down Alameda

CNBC reported on 2023-10-06 that after early-November 2022 media reports on Alameda's practices and its ties to FTX, Sam Bankman-Fried convened an emergency meeting with Gary Wang and Nishad Singh to consider shutting Alameda down. Wang testified that they "ultimately decided against such a move, because he and Bankman-Fried were aware that Alameda had no way to repay the roughly $14 billion hole in its books."
