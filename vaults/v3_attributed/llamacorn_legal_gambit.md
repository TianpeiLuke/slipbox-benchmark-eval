# Epic's "Llamacorn" Legal Gambit Against App Store Monopolies

The Verge reported on 2023-11-05 that Epic Games employed a deliberate litigation strategy called the "Llamacorn gambit" during its antitrust cases against Apple and Google. This strategy involved intentionally breaching developer agreements by bypassing app store payment systems, then immediately filing antitrust lawsuits while publicizing the dispute through provocative marketing. The tactic was first used against Apple on August 13, 2020, when Epic deployed a hotfix allowing direct V-Buck purchases, prompting Apple to remove Fortnite from its App Store. Epic was prepared with "two lawsuits and an attack ad, depicting a Fortnite hero throwing a unicorn-llama hammer into a giant screen reminiscent of Apple's famous '1984' Macintosh ad." The strategy aimed to challenge what Epic called the "Google tax" and "Apple tax" - the standard 15-30% fees charged by app stores for in-app purchases.
