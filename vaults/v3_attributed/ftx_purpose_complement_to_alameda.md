# FTX's Founding Purpose as Alameda Complement

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-01 that Sam Bankman-Fried co-founded FTX in 2019 as a complement to Alameda Research, to help bring in revenue and liquidity for the trading arm. Before FTX, Bankman-Fried co-founded crypto-trading firm Alameda Research in 2017.
