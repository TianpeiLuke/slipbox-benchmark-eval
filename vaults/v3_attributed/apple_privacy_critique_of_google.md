# Apple Exec Criticizes Google Privacy While Defending Search Deal

The Verge reported on 2023-09-26 that emails and slides presented in court show Apple executive Eddy Cue and Apple criticizing Google's privacy policies, with Cue agreeing that Apple has "always thought we had better privacy than Google." This occurred during Cue's testimony in the US v. Google antitrust trial, where he defended Apple's decision to make Google the default search engine despite privacy concerns.
