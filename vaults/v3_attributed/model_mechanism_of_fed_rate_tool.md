# Fed's High Rate Strategy to Combat Inflation

The Sydney Morning Herald reported on 2023-10-01 that the Federal Reserve is trying to push still-high inflation down to its target, and its main tool of high interest rates does that by trying to slow the economy and hurting prices for investments. The Fed's strategy works by making borrowing more expensive, which cools economic activity and reduces demand for assets. This approach aims to bring inflation down from its current elevated levels to the Fed's 2% target. The central bank's main interest rate is at its highest level since 2001, reflecting their commitment to controlling inflation despite potential economic slowdown risks.
