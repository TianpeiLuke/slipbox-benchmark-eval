# Steam Deck OLED Display Refresh Rate Specifications

Engadget reported on 2023-11-09 that the Steam Deck OLED display tops out at 90 Hz compared to 60 Hz on the original LCD model. "The screen on the new model doesn't support variable refresh rates (VRR), but it now tops out at 90Hz, while the original could only hit 60Hz." The OLED screen also features a wider P3 color gamut, pure blacks, and peak brightness of 1,000 nits versus the original's 400 nits maximum.
