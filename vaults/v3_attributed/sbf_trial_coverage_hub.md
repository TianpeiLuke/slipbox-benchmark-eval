# TechCrunch's SBF Trial Coverage Navigation Hub

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-06 that its SBF trial coverage hub contains multiple resources for following the FTX founder's fraud and money laundering trial. The hub includes "everything to know from the FTX courtroom ahead of his testimony" and links to trial coverage by reporter Jacquelyn Melinek. Readers can access the FTX-collapse explainer, TechCrunch+ analysis covering "what to expect from both sides of the SBF case," and the Thursday Chain Reaction newsletter and podcast. The publication provides daily trial coverage despite courtroom restrictions on devices, with additional expert commentary available through TechCrunch+. As former federal prosecutor Josh Naftalis told TechCrunch, SBF taking the stand is a "Hail Mary" before the potential verdict.
