# Google's Homepage Market Share Strategy

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-31 that in a 2007 Google strategy meeting, Nitin argued that focusing on homepage market share was "one of the most effective things we can do to make gains in search market share." This strategy treated browser homepage placement as the key lever for dominating search market share, with data showing that changing browsers to Google increased searches by 15%, while switching away decreased them by 27%.
