# News Publishers Sue Google Over AI Training Data Usage

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-15 that a class action lawsuit was filed against Google by news publishers, alleging anticompetitive behavior and harm from AI technologies. The complaint cites Google's AI systems like Search Generative Experience and Bard as threats to publishers' revenue, claiming these systems were trained on "news, magazine and digital publications." The lawsuit references a Washington Post analysis conducted with the Allen Institute for AI that found "News and Media sites form the third-largest category of material used in AI training datasets."
