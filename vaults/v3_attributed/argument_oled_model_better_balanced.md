# Steam Deck OLED Balance Improvement

Engadget reported on 2023-11-09 that the Steam Deck OLED "feels more balanced than the original" despite having the same "monstrous size." The review states that "the OLED model feels more balanced than the original. It's less top-heavy, and it does technically weigh less. These small adjustments have made a noticeable difference during my playtime, and the device feels slightly more manageable as a handheld." However, the reviewer notes this improvement excludes people with small hands, for whom "the device looks and feels absolutely ridiculous when I'm holding it."
