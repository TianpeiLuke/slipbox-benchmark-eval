# Pichai counters Expedia's antitrust claims by emphasizing Google's travel vertical popularity

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-13 that Sundar Pichai rebutted Expedia's antitrust complaints by asserting Google's travel listings are among the company's most popular features. During the US v Google trial, Pichai argued that the ad-rich vertical search results genuinely serve users rather than simply disadvantage competitors like Expedia. This directly challenges Barry Diller's testimony that Google's travel search practices harm competition in the online travel sector.
