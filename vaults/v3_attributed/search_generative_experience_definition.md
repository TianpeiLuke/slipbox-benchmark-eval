# Google's Search Generative Experience Aggravates Publisher Traffic Losses

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-15 that Google's Search Generative Experience (SGE) is an AI-powered conversational search product that answers queries in natural language inside the results page. The Arkansas-based publisher Helena World Chronicle specifically cited SGE in its antitrust lawsuit against Google, arguing that the technology "aggravating publishers' traffic losses" by keeping users within Google's "walled garden" while "plagiarizing" their content. According to the suit, SGE offers web searchers a conversational way to seek information but ultimately discourages end-users from visiting publishers' websites, with publishers unable to block SGE because it uses the same web crawler as Google's general search service.
