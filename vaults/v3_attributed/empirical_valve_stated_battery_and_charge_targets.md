# Steam Deck OLED Battery Life Improvement

Engadget reported on 2023-11-09 that Valve stated the Steam Deck OLED has 30-50% longer battery life than the original model, which reached at most eight hours under ideal conditions. The company also claimed the device would charge from 20% to 80% in 45 minutes. Testing showed the OLED model lasting four hours and 23 minutes compared to the original's two hours and 42 minutes, representing a 62% improvement.
