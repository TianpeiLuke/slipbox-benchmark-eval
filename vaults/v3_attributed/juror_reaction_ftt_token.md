# Juror observes Ellison's testimony about SBF relationship

The Verge reported on 2023-10-10 that a juror who had been asleep during earlier discussions woke up when Caroline Ellison began testifying about her relationship with Sam Bankman-Fried. Ellison, former head of Alameda Research, described their romantic relationship while testifying in Bankman-Fried's criminal fraud trial, stating he directed her to commit crimes including fraud, conspiracy, and money laundering involving approximately $14 billion.
