# Manchester City's Champions League Title Defence Historical Context

Sporting News reported on 2023-12-12 that Manchester City's attempt to retain the Champions League trophy in 2023/24 would be historically significant, stating they are "potentially becoming the first English club since Nottingham Forest in 1980 to successfully defend the trophy." This assertion is based on the historical record of English clubs' performances in European competition, with Nottingham Forest's consecutive victories in 1979 and 1980 being the last instance of an English team retaining the title. City's 2022/23 triumph positioned them as the first English club since that era to have the opportunity for back-to-back championships.
