# Steam Deck OLED Battery Life Exceeds Valve's Estimates

Engadget reported on 2023-11-09 that Hades ran 4h 23min on the Steam Deck OLED versus 2h 42min on the original LCD, a 62 percent gain above Valve's estimate. "That's a 62 percent improvement, surpassing Valve's own estimates" when tested at 800p with a 60 fps cap and similar brightness settings.
