# Axel Springer Signs AI Content Licensing Deal with OpenAI

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-15 that German publisher Axel Springer "just this week inked a deal with OpenAI to license its news for AI model training." This agreement represents publishers' proactive efforts to address anticipated traffic losses from AI search integration, as Google's AI technologies like Search Generative Experience and Bard are projected to reduce publisher website visits by 20-40% according to industry estimates.
