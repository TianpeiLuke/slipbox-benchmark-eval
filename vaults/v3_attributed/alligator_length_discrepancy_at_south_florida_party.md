# Alligator Party Disappointment

The Verge reported on 2023-11-01 that the alligator released at a South Florida rooftop SEO party "was only five and a half feet long, not 10 feet, as advertised." The event was attended by digital marketers accused of ruining the internet through search engine optimization practices. The author had expected to encounter "hustlers" and "content goblins" but instead found the attendees to be generally sympathetic. The smaller-than-advertised alligator became a metaphor for the disappointment with SEO's impact on internet quality.
