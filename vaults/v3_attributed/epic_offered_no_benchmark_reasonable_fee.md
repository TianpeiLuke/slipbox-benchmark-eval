# Epic's Weakened Position in Google Antitrust Trial

The Verge reported on 2023-11-05 that Epic's antitrust position against Google is weakened because it has never specified what constitutes a reasonable fee for Google's store placement. Instead of proposing a fair rate, Epic CEO Tim Sweeney has implied that developers using their own payment systems should pay Google "nothing at all." This stance undermines Epic's credibility as it appears to advocate for using Google's infrastructure without compensation, making Epic's monopoly-breaking arguments less compelling in the upcoming trial.
