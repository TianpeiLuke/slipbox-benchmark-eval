# Sam Altman backs teen entrepreneurs' AI startup Induced AI

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-07 that Sam Altman is among the backers of Induced AI, an AI startup founded by two teenagers earlier in 2023. The company converts businesses' plain-English back-office task descriptions into pseudo-code in real time to automate workflows in "previously unexplored" ways.
