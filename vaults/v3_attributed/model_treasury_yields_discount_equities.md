# Treasury Yields and Stock Market Decline Mechanism

The Sydney Morning Herald reported on 2023-10-01 that Treasury bonds are seen as some of the safest investments possible, and when they pay higher yields, investors are less likely to pay high prices for stocks and other riskier investments. "Loading Treasury bonds are seen as some of the safest investments possible, and when they pay higher yields, investors are less likely to pay high prices for stocks and other riskier investments." This mechanism was cited as a big reason why the S&P 500 dropped 4.9 per cent in September 2023, as Treasury yields climbed sharply while Wall Street accepted a new normal of higher-for-longer Federal Reserve interest rates aimed at controlling inflation.
