# Travis Kelce makes friendship bracelet for NFL camp

CBSSports.com reported on 2023-10-12 that on June 29, 2023, Travis Kelce made a friendship bracelet to give to newcomers at a camp for NFL tight ends, saying he was "taking a page out of Taylor Swift's book." This gesture occurred at the beginning of their rumored romance timeline, before Swift attended any Chiefs games. The article notes this as a potential origin point for their connection, linking Kelce's action to Swift's concert traditions where fans make friendship bracelets.
