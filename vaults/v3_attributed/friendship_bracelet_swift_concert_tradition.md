# Friendship Bracelet as Fan Tradition at Taylor Swift Concerts

CBSSports.com reported on 2023-10-12 that a friendship bracelet, in the context of Taylor Swift's concerts, is a handmade strand exchanged among concertgoers as a fan tradition. These bracelets are distinguished from ordinary jewelry by their role as gift objects that attendees make for one another. The tradition became notable when Travis Kelce made friendship bracelets for fellow NFL tight ends at a camp, "taking a page out of Taylor Swift's book," and later created one specifically for Swift with his number on it. "He said he was 'taking a page out of Taylor Swift's book.'"
