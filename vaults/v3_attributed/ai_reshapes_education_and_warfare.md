# Silicon Valley's AI Control Competition Carries Global Consequences

The Age reported on 2023-12-09 that a "little-publicised but ferocious competition in Silicon Valley for control of the technology that is now quickly reshaping the world" carried significant global consequences. This competition involves powerful tech leaders including Musk, Page, Zuckerberg, Thiel, Nadella, and Altman, who are simultaneously the most worried about AI's dangers and the most determined to create it. The article states that this technology is "reshaping the world along a broad front – from how children are taught to how wars are fought." Their struggle for control stems from the paradox that those claiming to want to protect humanity are also those most invested in developing and profiting from AI technology.
