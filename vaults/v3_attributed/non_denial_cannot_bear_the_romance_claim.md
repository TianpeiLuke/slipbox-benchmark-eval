# Counter-argument to Swift-Kelce relationship inference from timeline signals

CBSSports.com reported on 2023-10-12 that a timeline of events suggests a rumored romance between Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce, but the evidence consists only of one-way gestures and third-party observations. "The rumors are terrible and cruel, but honey, most of them are true" was cited as a non-denial by Jason Kelce on Sept. 14. However, every documented event shows actions by others: Swift attending games, Kelce making bracelets, commentators making lyrical references. None constitute direct statements or confirmed interactions from Swift herself, making the aggregate timeline an over-interpretation of passive signals rather than proof of an actual relationship.
