# Counter-argument to Google optimization business strategy

The Verge reported on 2023-10-26 that one replier in a viral thread objected to the premise that gaming Google is a sound business strategy. The replier argued that "This is about how your products don't even work anymore," suggesting that restaurant owners wouldn't need to resort to SEO-gaming names like "Thai Food Near Me" if Google's search rankings were fair and functional. This counter-argument implies that the real issue isn't businesses adapting to flawed algorithms, but rather that the search platform itself has become unreliable for users seeking genuine recommendations.
