# Apple received $18 billion of Google's 2021 default payments

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-31 that Google paid $26.3 billion in 2021 to make itself the default search engine across various platforms, with roughly $18 billion going directly to Apple. This payment was part of Google's long-standing deal with Apple since 2003, as revealed during Google CEO Sundar Pichai's testimony in the U.S. government's antitrust trial. "About $18 billion of that total amount went directly to Apple, according to a New York Times report published earlier this month."
