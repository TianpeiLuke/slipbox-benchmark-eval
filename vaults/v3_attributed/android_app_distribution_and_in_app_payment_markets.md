# Epic's Antitrust Claims Against Google Focus on Two Monopoly Markets

The Verge reported on 2023-11-05 that Epic's antitrust lawsuit against Google centers on two alleged monopoly markets: "Android app distribution" and "Android in-app payment processing." Epic argues these markets are monopolized by Google, forcing developers and consumers to pay the so-called "Google tax" with no viable alternatives for app distribution or payment processing on Android devices.
