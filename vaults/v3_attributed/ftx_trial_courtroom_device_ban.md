# SBF Trial Reporting Constraints

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-06 that public access to the SBF trial proceedings is limited because "no devices are allowed; reports are being physically put together within the courtroom (or sent to overflow rooms)." This restriction means reporters must compose their dispatches manually inside the courtroom or in designated overflow areas.
