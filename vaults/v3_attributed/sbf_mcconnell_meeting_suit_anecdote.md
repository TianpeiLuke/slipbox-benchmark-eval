# Lewis describes SBF's disheveled appearance at McConnell meeting

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-02 that Michael Lewis described Sam Bankman-Fried arriving at a meeting with Senate leader Mitch McConnell in a disheveled state, with "his suit balled up under his arm and dress shoes falling out of the bundle." Lewis recounted this encounter as part of SBF's efforts to fund political candidates to offset Trump's influence in the Republican Party. The observation came during Lewis's promotion of his book "Going Infinite," which chronicles SBF's rise and fall.
