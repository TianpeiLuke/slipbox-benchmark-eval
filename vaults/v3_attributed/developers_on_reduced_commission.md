# Google's Commission Structure for Developers

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-06 that Google requires apps to use its first-party billing system and charges developers a 15% to 30% commission on sales it processes. However, Google noted that "99% of its developers qualify for a commission of 15% or less, based on their revenues." The company offers a User Choice Billing program that reduces commissions by 4% for developers who use their own payment processing solutions.
