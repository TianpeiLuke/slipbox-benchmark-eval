# LinkedIn unveils AI features across job-hunting, marketing and sales products

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-07 that LinkedIn unveiled a string of new AI features spanning its job hunting, marketing and sales products. "LinkedIn this week unveiled a string of new AI features spanning its job hunting, marketing and sales products," according to Ingrid's reporting. The updates include a substantially updated AI-assisted Recruiter talent sourcing platform with AI assistance built throughout, an AI-powered LinkedIn Learning coach, and a new AI-powered tool for marketing campaigns.
