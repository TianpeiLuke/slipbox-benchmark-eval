# Bankman-Fried's Physical Appearance and Demeanor During Trial

Fortune reported on 2023-10-04 that Sam Bankman-Fried's "cheekbones were more prominent after spending about seven weeks in a Brooklyn prison," and that he "spent most of the day in court staring into a laptop while seated between his two attorneys." The article described his physical appearance and courtroom demeanor during the second day of his trial, noting the contrast between his current gaunt look and his previous public persona as a successful crypto exchange CEO.
