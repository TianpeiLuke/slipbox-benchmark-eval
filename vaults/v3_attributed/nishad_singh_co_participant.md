# Nishad Singh's Role in FTX Alleged Crimes

CNBC reported on 2023-10-06 that Nishad Singh was FTX's former head of engineering and one of the two former MIT friends of Sam Bankman-Fried who lived with the founder in the Bahamas. Singh is named by Gary Wang as a participant in the alleged financial crimes at FTX, including wire and commodities fraud. Wang testified that these crimes were carried out with the help of Bankman-Fried, Singh, and Caroline Ellison. During testimony, Wang confirmed he committed financial crimes while working at FTX and identified Bankman-Fried as someone he committed those crimes with in the courtroom.
