# Google Search Quality Debate and Default Advantage

The Age reported on 2023-10-22 that Google Search's dominance stems from user preference rather than coercion, according to Google's global affairs president Kent Walker. However, the article argues that claims of Google manipulating search queries for ad revenue, such as replacing "children's clothing" with "NIKOLAI-brand kidswear," have been disputed by Google and removed by Wired for not meeting standards. The debate continues over whether Google's search quality advantage is due to its scale and data collection practices rather than superior technology.
