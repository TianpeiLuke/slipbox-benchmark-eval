# Danielle Hunter's Defensive Player of the Year Case

Sporting News reported on 2023-12-05 that Danielle Hunter merits Defensive Player of the Year consideration despite lacking the name recognition of favorites Micah Parsons, Myles Garrett, and T.J. Watt. "He may not have the name recognition that Parsons, Garrett and Watt have, but he has played as well, if not better, this season." Hunter leads the league in tackles for a loss and ranks third in sacks with 13.5, while his play has been "instrumental in the Vikings turning things around after a 1-4 start." His statistical performance and impact on Minnesota's turnaround make him a legitimate DPOY candidate.
