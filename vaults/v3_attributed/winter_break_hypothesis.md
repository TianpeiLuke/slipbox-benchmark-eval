# Winter Break Hypothesis for ChatGPT's December Performance Decline

TechCrunch reported on 2023-09-28 that ChatGPT users have observed degraded performance starting in November, with the chatbot providing simpler answers and refusing tasks. Some users speculate this relates to the "winter break hypothesis," which suggests that AI models like ChatGPT perform worse in December because they "learned" reduced working patterns from seasonal holiday data. OpenAI acknowledged the issue on December 8, 2023, stating they hadn't updated the model since November 11th and that the behavior was unintentional. The company noted that model behavior can be unpredictable and they were investigating fixes. This phenomenon has led some to wonder if the chatbot is simulating seasonal depression, though OpenAI has not confirmed any specific cause for the performance changes.
