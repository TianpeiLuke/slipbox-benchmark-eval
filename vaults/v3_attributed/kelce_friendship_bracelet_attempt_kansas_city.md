# Kelce's friendship bracelet attempt

The Age reported on 2023-09-26 that Kelce intended to give Swift a friendship bracelet with his number during her Eras Tour concert in Kansas City in July 2023. "Unfortunately, the bracelet never made it to her wrist or his digits to her phone." Kelce expressed disappointment that Swift didn't meet him, saying "She doesn't meet anybody, or at least she didn't want to meet me, so I took it personal."
