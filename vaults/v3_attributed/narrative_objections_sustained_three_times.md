# Bankman-Fried's Sustained Narrative Objections During Testimony

The Verge reported on 2023-10-28 that during Sam Bankman-Fried's direct testimony, the count of sustained "Objection, narrative!" responses to his answers reached three. The reporter observed that Bankman-Fried "really loves the sound of his own voice" and noted his tendency to give narrative responses rather than direct answers to questions posed by his own counsel Mark Cohen.
