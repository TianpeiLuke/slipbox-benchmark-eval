# 60 Minutes behind-the-scenes footage shows SBF's nervous demeanor during interview

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-02 that "60 Minutes" filmed behind-the-scenes footage showing Sam Bankman-Fried shuffling playing cards at his desk while Michael Lewis took notes on a yellow legal pad during their interview sessions. The article notes that "the program shot (and it shows here) one segment with SBF talking as he shuffles cards with his leg twitching nervously behind his desk (why? we don't find this out, either) as Lewis, with a flourish of his pen and a look of concentration on his face, dashes off notes on his yellow legal pad." The reason for SBF's card shuffling behavior was never explained in the reporting.
