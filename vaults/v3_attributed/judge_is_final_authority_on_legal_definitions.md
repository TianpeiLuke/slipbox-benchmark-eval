# Judge Kaplan asserts authority over legal term definitions during Bankman-Fried testimony

The Verge reported on 2023-10-28 that Judge Lewis Kaplan asserted his authority over defining legal terms during Sam Bankman-Fried's testimony. After Bankman-Fried offered his own definition of "market manipulation," Kaplan told the jury that he, Kaplan, is the final authority on that term. This occurred during the trial where Bankman-Fried was testifying about his role in the FTX cryptocurrency exchange fraud case.
