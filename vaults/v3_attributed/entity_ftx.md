# FTX Exchange and Sam Bankman-Fried Trial Opening Statements

Fortune reported on 2023-10-04 that during opening statements in Sam Bankman-Fried's criminal trial, prosecutors characterized FTX as an exchange whose customers were told their money was safe while the defense portrayed it as a legitimate, innovative company. The prosecution alleged Bankman-Fried stole billions from customers through his hedge fund Alameda Research, describing him as a "man" who "stole billions of dollars from thousands of victims." In contrast, the defense argued FTX was "a very innovative, successful company" and not a Ponzi scheme, presenting Bankman-Fried as a well-intentioned startup founder who acted in "good faith."
