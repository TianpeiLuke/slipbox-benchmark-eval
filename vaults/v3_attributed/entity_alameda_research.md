# Alameda Research's Role in FTX Trial Opening Statements

Fortune reported on 2023-10-04 that Alameda Research, the crypto hedge fund also owned by Sam Bankman-Fried, figured prominently in opposing characterizations during opening statements. The prosecution named it "the key instrument of the alleged scheme," claiming it was used to steal customer funds, while the defense described it as "a successful, non-shady fund operating legally as an FTX customer, payment processor and market maker."
