# Daron Babin's Early SEO Career Observation

The Verge reported on 2023-11-01 that Daron Babin's SEO career began in 1994, before Google existed, and he recalled that "the air of manipulation was insane". This early observation from Babin highlights the manipulative environment that characterized pre-Google search engine optimization practices, suggesting that deceptive tactics were already prevalent in the industry even before Google's dominance.
