# ChatGPT's First-Year Feature Expansion

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-30 that ChatGPT evolved significantly in its first year from a basic free web interface into a comprehensive AI platform. Initially launched as a "low-key research preview" with GPT-3.5, ChatGPT "gained paid tiers with additional features, including a plan geared toward enterprise customers." The platform expanded its capabilities to include "web searching, document analyzing and image creating (via DALL-E 3)" plus multimodal functions that let it "hear," "speak," "see" and take actions using in-house developed models. These additions transformed ChatGPT from a simple chatbot into OpenAI's "most popular product ever" and made its capability set "a strict superset of its launch state."
