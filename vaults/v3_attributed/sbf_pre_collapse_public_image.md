# Government's Opening Statement Paints Bankman-Fried as Fraudster

Fortune reported on 2023-10-04 that prosecutor Thane Rehn described Sam Bankman-Fried's public persona one year prior to the October 2023 trial, stating he "oversaw a supposedly thriving crypto exchange, jetted between international locales, and hobnobbed with celebrities like Tom Brady and Larry David. He repeatedly emphasized to customers that their money was safe and secure." Rehn characterized this image as deception, asserting that "all of that, all of it, was built on lies."
