# Sam Bankman-Fried Fraud Trial Details

CNBC reported on 2023-10-06 that the six-week federal criminal proceeding against Sam Bankman-Fried over the FTX collapse is taking place before U.S. District Judge Lewis Kaplan in Manhattan. The trial, which began in early October 2023, is scheduled to resume on Tuesday with further key testimony expected from prosecution witness Caroline Ellison. Bankman-Fried faces seven federal charges including wire fraud, securities fraud and money laundering that could result in life imprisonment.
