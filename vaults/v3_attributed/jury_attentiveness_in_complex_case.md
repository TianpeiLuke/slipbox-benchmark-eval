# Jury's Attentive Conduct and Rapid Verdict Surprised Observers

The Verge reported on 2023-12-12 that both Tim Sweeney and journalists observed the jury's engaged behavior throughout Epic's antitrust trial against Google. "They weren't snoozing off as you might expect with the complexity of these documents and things — everybody was paying attention, they're looking at the question asker and the witness and taking notes and reading documents," Sweeney noted. The jury's quick verdict, delivered after four weeks of complex testimony, surprised legal observers who typically expect lengthy deliberations in such cases. "[E]very journalist in the courtroom agreed the jury was attentive; most were also surprised by the rapid verdict," according to the editor's note.
