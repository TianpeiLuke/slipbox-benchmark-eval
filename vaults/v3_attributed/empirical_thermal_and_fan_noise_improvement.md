# Steam Deck OLED Thermal Performance Comparison

Engadget reported on 2023-11-09 that back-to-back play sessions left the reviewer's palms "noticeably cooler on the OLED model than on the original." The larger fan in the OLED model produced "no additional audible noise" compared to the original Steam Deck. This improvement was attributed to the die-shrunk custom AMD APU that runs more efficiently while maintaining the same performance targets as the original hardware.
