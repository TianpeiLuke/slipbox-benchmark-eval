# Google's User Choice Billing Procedure for Commission Reduction

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-06 that Google offers a 4% commission reduction to Play developers who opt into User Choice Billing and handle their own payment processing in 35 supported markets including the U.S. The program, still in pilot phase, requires developers to "use their own payment processing solution" instead of Google's standard billing system which normally charges 15% to 30% commissions. Spotify, Bumble, and Match have utilized this option, with Google claiming that 99% of developers qualify for 15% or less commission rates. Processed-sales reporting verifies developer participation in the program.
