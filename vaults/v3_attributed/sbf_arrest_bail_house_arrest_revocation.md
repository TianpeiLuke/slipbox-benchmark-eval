# Sam Bankman-Fried's Arrest and Bail Revocation

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-01 that Sam Bankman-Fried was arrested in December 2022 in the Bahamas and extradited to the U.S., where he was released on a $250 million bail bond under house arrest at his parents' Palo Alto home. His bail was revoked in August 2023 after he was accused of intimidating former Alameda CEO Caroline Ellison by leaking her private diary. Bankman-Fried faces seven counts of fraud and conspiracy related to the FTX collapse, with potential sentencing of up to 115 years in prison if convicted.
