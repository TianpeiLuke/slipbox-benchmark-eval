# Bankman-Fried Allegedly Used $100 Million in Customer Funds for Political Contributions

The Verge reported on 2023-09-28 that Sam Bankman-Fried allegedly used more than $100 million of FTX customer funds for political contributions. Prosecutors may introduce evidence of these contributions during his trial, even though this specific use of funds is not among the seven criminal charges he faces, which include wire fraud and conspiracy counts.
