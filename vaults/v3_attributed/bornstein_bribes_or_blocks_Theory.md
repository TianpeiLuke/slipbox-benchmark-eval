# Bornstein Frames Google as Monopoly Bully Who Bribes and Blocks Competition

The Verge reported on 2023-11-07 that Epic Games lead attorney Gary Bornstein characterized Google's conduct as paying actual and potential competitors not to compete with Google Play, or blocking them. Bornstein stated Google "pays actual and potential competitors not to compete. Literally gives them money and other things of value," citing an alleged $360 million payment to prevent Activision from opening a competing app store. This strategy, according to Bornstein, produces the result that nearly all Android installs flow through Google Play, with only a tiny fraction going to Samsung's Galaxy Store. Epic argues this behavior constitutes an unlawful monopoly that harms consumers through higher app prices.
