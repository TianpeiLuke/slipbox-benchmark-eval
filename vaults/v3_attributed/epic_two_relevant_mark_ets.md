# Epic's Antitrust Claims Against Google Span Two Markets

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-06 that Epic Games argues Google restrains competition within two separate markets: "the distribution of apps to Android users and the market for payment processing solutions for content inside Android apps." Epic's antitrust lawsuit, beginning November 6, 2023, centers on Google Play's commission structure and restrictive agreements with device manufacturers. Unlike Apple's closed ecosystem, Android allows sideloading, but Epic contends Google makes this process difficult and instead focuses on what it views as anticompetitive practices related to app distribution and in-app payment processing.
