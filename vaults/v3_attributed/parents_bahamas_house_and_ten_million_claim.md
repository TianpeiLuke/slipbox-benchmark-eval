# Barbara Fried allegedly received $10 million of FTX customer funds

The Verge reported on 2023-10-26 that according to a financial expert called by the prosecution, Joe Bankman and Barbara Fried lived in a Bahamas house purchased with FTX investor funds. "FTX's bankruptcy estate alleges in a lawsuit that the pair were given $10 million of FTX customer funds."
