# Gemini Pro factual errors documented by science fiction author

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-07 that Google's Gemini Pro AI model demonstrated significant factual inaccuracies. Science fiction author Charlie Stross documented multiple confabulations in his testing, including the false claim that "Stross contributed to the Linux kernel" which he never has. The model also incorrectly identified 2023 Oscar winners and struggled with basic French translation requests.
