# Google's Commission Structure Extends Beyond Payment Processing

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-06 that Google will argue its app store commissions "aren't just tied to payment processing" during Epic Games' antitrust trial. Google contends that its 15% to 30% commission rates fund essential security controls, discovery guidance, and developer tooling. The company emphasizes that these fees support cross-platform reach extending to tablets, TVs, and autos, positioning the charges as investments in broader ecosystem services rather than mere transaction processing. This argument contrasts with Epic's antitrust claims that Google's commission structure represents anticompetitive behavior in Android app distribution. Google further notes that 99% of developers qualify for the lower 15% commission rate based on revenue thresholds.
