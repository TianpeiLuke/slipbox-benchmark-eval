# U.S. Attorney's Characterization of Bankman-Fried Fraud Case

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-01 that U.S. Attorney Damian Williams called Sam Bankman-Fried's alleged crimes "one of the biggest financial frauds in American history" at a press conference. This characterization came after FTX's collapse in November 2022, which involved fraudulent misrepresentation of FTT token holdings and massive customer fund misuse. Bankman-Fried faces up to 115 years in prison on charges including wire fraud and conspiracy to commit money laundering.
