# Google's High-Value Search Queries Reveal Vertical Search Dominance

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-13 that a court-ordered list of Google's most lucrative search terms from September 22, 2018 reveals the company's dominance in vertical search categories. The list includes high-revenue queries like "iPhone 8," "auto insurance," "cheap flights," "DirecTV," and "life insurance," which represent categories where advertisers pay premium rates. As TechCrunch noted, "queries like 'car insurance,' 'cheap flights' and 'credit report' are perennial favorites and they speak to how much Google dominates vertical search — that is, search in very specific market categories." The publication observed that three Apple-related search terms appeared on the list, reflecting Google's mutually beneficial relationship with Apple despite their competition. However, this analysis rests on data from just one week in 2018, limiting its temporal scope.
