# Epic's Antitrust Victory Over Google Contrasts With Earlier Loss to Apple

The Verge reported on 2023-12-12 that Epic Games won a major antitrust victory against Google, with a federal jury deciding that Google has an illegal monopoly over Android app distribution and in-app payment systems. This outcome surprised observers because "walled garden" rival Apple had almost entirely won a similar case two years earlier. Epic CEO Tim Sweeney noted the divergence from the Epic v. Apple result, stating that the jury was able to "pull apart what was going on and contrast it with the fiction of the story that Google was trying to tell."
