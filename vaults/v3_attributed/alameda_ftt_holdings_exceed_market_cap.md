# Alameda's FTT Balance Sheet Anomaly

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-01 that Alameda Research's balance sheet listed $3.66 billion of unlocked FTT plus $2.16 billion of FTT collateral, totaling $5.82 billion. This amount was "193% higher than the total FTT market cap, which was about $3 billion at the time," meaning Alameda purportedly held more FTT tokens than existed globally. The discrepancy was exposed in November 2022 when CoinDesk published Alameda's financials, contributing to FTX's subsequent collapse and triggering a bank run when Binance withdrew its holdings.
