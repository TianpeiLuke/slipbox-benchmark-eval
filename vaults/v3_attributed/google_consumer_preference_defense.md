# Google's Antitrust Defense: Paying for Default Placement Isn't Illegal

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-31 that Google defends its $26.3 billion annual payments to partners like Apple to be the default search engine, arguing this practice is legal because partners choose Google voluntarily. Google CEO Sundar Pichai testified that "everybody wants Google as the default engine because it's the best," and that the company pays significant amounts to be ubiquitous so it can collect comprehensive data to maintain its search superiority across different devices. The company's position is that these business arrangements don't constitute illegal monopolistic behavior since consumers benefit from Google's superior search capabilities.
