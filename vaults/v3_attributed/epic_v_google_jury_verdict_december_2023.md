# Epic Games Wins Antitrust Case Against Google on App Distribution Monopoly

The Verge reported on 2023-12-12 that a federal jury found Google illegally monopolized Android app distribution and in-app payment systems. The jury determined that Google "illegally tied its Google Play billing system to its app store," marking a significant antitrust victory for Epic Games after a four-week trial. This ruling challenges Google's control over Android app distribution and represents the first major antitrust verdict against a tech company since the 1990s Microsoft case.
