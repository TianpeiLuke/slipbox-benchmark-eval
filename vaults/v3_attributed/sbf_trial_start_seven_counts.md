# Sam Bankman-Fried's Criminal Trial Begins October 2023

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-01 that Sam Bankman-Fried's criminal trial began on Tuesday, 2023-10-03, to determine guilt on seven counts of fraud and conspiracy. A former federal prosecutor stated "The odds seem to be stacked against him at this point." The trial concerns allegations that Bankman-Fried misused customer funds from FTX, the crypto exchange he founded in 2019 that reached a $32 billion valuation before collapsing in November 2022 following revelations about Alameda Research's balance sheet and a bank run triggered by Binance withdrawing $2.1 billion.
