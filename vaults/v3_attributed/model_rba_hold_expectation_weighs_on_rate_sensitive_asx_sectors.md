# RBA Rate Hold Expectations Weigh on Interest-Sensitive ASX Sectors

The Sydney Morning Herald reported on 2023-10-01 that "Most sectors traded lower but interest-rate sensitive sectors including healthcare and consumer companies were among the weakest amid expectations that the RBA will keep rates on hold in October." Market expectations for the Reserve Bank of Australia maintaining the cash rate at its October meeting, combined with higher-for-longer interest rate pricing, pushed rate-sensitive sectors below the broader ASX average. Consumer staples declined 1.1% with Treasury Wine Estates falling 1.9% and Woolworths dropping 1.2%, while healthcare and other interest-rate sensitive segments also underperformed.
