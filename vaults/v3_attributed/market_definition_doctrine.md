# Market Definition in Antitrust Law

The Verge reported on 2023-11-05 that "this whole line of argument is called 'market definition'" in Epic's antitrust case against Google. Market definition is the antitrust analysis that fixes the boundaries of the market a defendant is accused of monopolising; the same conduct is lawful or unlawful depending on whether the relevant market is drawn narrowly (Android-only) or broadly (all smartphones and their stores). The outcome of Epic v. Google hinges on how the court defines the relevant market for app distribution and payment processing.
