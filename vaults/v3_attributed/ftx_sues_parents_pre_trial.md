# FTX Lawyers Sue Bankman-Fried's Parents Before Trial

The Verge reported on 2023-09-28 that "Just before the trial began, the FTX lawyers filed suit against Bankman-Fried's parents, saying they should give back millions of dollars they got from their kid." This pre-trial civil action sought the return of millions of dollars that Sam Bankman-Fried's parents had received from their son, as FTX's legal team pursued asset recovery ahead of the criminal fraud trial proceedings.
