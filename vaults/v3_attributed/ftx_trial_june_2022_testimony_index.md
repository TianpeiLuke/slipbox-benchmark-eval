# The Verge's October 31, 2023 court report index on Sam Bankman-Fried's June 2022 testimony

The Verge reported on 2023-10-31 that Sam Bankman-Fried's June 2022 court testimony revealed key details about the FTX collapse, including his reaction to discovering an $8 billion software bug that overestimated Alameda's debt to FTX. The report indexes his testimony covering the "fiat@ftx" account tracking Alameda's obligations, staff telling him to "stop asking questions" while fixing the bug, absent financial controls, his denial then admission of being told about the $8 billion shortfall by Adam Yedidia on a padel court, and an Alameda balance sheet labeling FTX funds as "ftx borrows."
