# Google's User Choice Billing Pilot Reduces Commission for Developers

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-06 that Google's User Choice Billing program, currently in pilot testing in 35 markets including the U.S., "reduces the standard commission by 4% for companies who choose to use their own payment processing solution." The program is available to all developers selling apps in these markets, with initial testers including Spotify and Bumble, and is part of Google's response to Epic Games' antitrust lawsuit allegations regarding anticompetitive billing practices.
