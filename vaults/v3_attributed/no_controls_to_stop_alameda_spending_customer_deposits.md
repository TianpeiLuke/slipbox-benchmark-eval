# Bankman-Fried's Congressional Testimony Contradicted by Control Questions

The Verge reported on 2023-10-31 that Sam Bankman-Fried's testimony contradicted itself when faced with simple control questions. Despite testifying to Congress about keeping customer funds safe, Bankman-Fried answered "no" to whether he told Alameda staff not to spend FTX customer deposits, whether he set policies preventing it, and whether he put protective measures in place at Alameda. His responses came during his trial for seven counts of wire fraud and conspiracy, where he claimed ignorance about the $8 billion misappropriation of customer funds.
