# Steam Deck OLED Screen Specifications

Engadget reported on 2023-11-09 that the Steam Deck OLED features a 7.4-inch display with reduced bezels compared to the original 7-inch panel, and includes native HDR support. The OLED screen offers a peak brightness of 1,000 nits and supports a wider P3 color gamut. "The OLED also has smaller bezels, making the display larger at 7.4 inches, compared with the original 7-inch screen."
