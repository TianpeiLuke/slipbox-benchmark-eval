# Shadowbanning Definition and Meta's Alleged Practice

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-19 that shadowbanning is the practice of "limits the visibility of" content without notifying the account holder, making it indistinguishable from ordinary algorithmic fluctuation. The article documents how Instagram users accused Meta of shadowbanning their Stories about Palestine, with Palestinian-American filmmaker Khitam Jabr suspecting her account was restricted due to reports against her Palestine travel videos. Meta maintains its policies are applied equally worldwide and denies deliberately suppressing voices, attributing such issues to technical bugs rather than intentional suppression.
