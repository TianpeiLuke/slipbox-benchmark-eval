# Barbara Fried's PAC Received FTX Customer Funds as Political Donations

The Verge reported on 2023-10-26 that Barbara Fried's political action committee, Mind the Gap, was listed among the entities that received FTX customer money as political donations. According to the article, "It is among the entities that received FTX customer money as political donations." The report also noted that Fried and Bankman lived together in a Bahamas house purchased using FTX investor funds, and that Bankman and Fried were alleged to have been given $10 million of FTX customer funds.
