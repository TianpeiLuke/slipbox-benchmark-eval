# AI Customer Withdrawal Prediction for 2024

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-19 that a considerable withdrawal of customers from AI tools will occur in 2024 as measured benefits fail to justify costs and risks. The publication predicts that marketing claims made for machine learning systems in 2023 will face quarterly and yearly reviews, likely finding them "wanting." This hypothesis is falsified if adoption keeps rising through the year's review cycles. The article suggests that lawsuits and regulatory action may emerge against AI service providers that failed to substantiate their claims, leading to consolidation as weaker AI companies are acquired.
