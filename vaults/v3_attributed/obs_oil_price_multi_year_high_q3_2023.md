# Oil Prices Reach One-Year High Despite Recent Drop

The Sydney Morning Herald reported on 2023-10-01 that oil prices reached their highest level in more than a year. "Oil prices have jumped to their highest level in more than a year," despite a 92-cent fall in US crude on September 29 to settle at $US90.79. This represented a significant increase from approximately $US70 in June 2023, indicating strong upward momentum in energy markets despite temporary fluctuations.
