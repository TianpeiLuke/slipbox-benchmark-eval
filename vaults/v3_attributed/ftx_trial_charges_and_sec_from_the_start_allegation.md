# Sam Bankman-Fried Faces Fraud Trial Over FTX Collapse

The Verge reported on 2023-10-31 that Sam Bankman-Fried, founder of the collapsed cryptocurrency exchange FTX, stood trial on seven counts of wire fraud and conspiracy. The Securities and Exchange Commission alleges that FTX was a fraud "from the start," citing a "multi-billion-dollar deficiency caused by his own misappropriation of customer funds." During testimony, Bankman-Fried claimed he didn't discover what the fiat@ftx account tracked until October 2022, despite being told about a software bug causing an $8 billion discrepancy earlier.
