# Spotify announces 1,500 job cuts amid economic challenges

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-09 that Spotify is cutting about 1,500 roles, roughly 17% of staff, marking its third layoff round of the year. Spotify founder and CEO Daniel Ek cited "slow economic growth and rising capital costs" as the stated causes for these workforce reductions aimed at making the company "both productive and efficient."
