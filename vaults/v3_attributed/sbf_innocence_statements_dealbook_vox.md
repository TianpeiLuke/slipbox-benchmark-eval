# Bankman-Fried's Public Claims of Innocence Post-FTX Collapse

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-01 that after FTX's collapse, Sam Bankman-Fried appeared virtually from the Bahamas at the New York Times DealBook Summit saying "I didn't ever try to commit fraud on anyone; I was shocked by what happened this month," and told a Vox reporter by DM he regretted filing for bankruptcy and that "regulators make everything worse."
