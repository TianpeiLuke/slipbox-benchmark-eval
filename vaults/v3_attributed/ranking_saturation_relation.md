# Jirapraphanan's SEO Restaurant Naming Strategy

The Verge reported on 2023-10-26 that Teddy Jirapraphanan co-founded a restaurant named "Thai Food Near Me" specifically to capitalize on search engine optimization, stating: "When you have a million restaurants close by, you will be in the bottom [of rankings] if it's a random name." The restaurant's name directly matches the Google search phrase Jirapraphanan frequently uses when looking for Thai restaurants, making it highly discoverable to customers searching for that exact term. This strategy proved successful, as customers began recognizing and visiting the establishment after searching for "Thai food near me" online. The approach reflects broader trends among local businesses using SEO-focused names like "Barber Shop Near Me" and "Affordable Dentist Near Me" to improve their visibility in search results.
