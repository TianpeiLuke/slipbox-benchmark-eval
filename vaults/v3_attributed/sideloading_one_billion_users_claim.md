# Google's billion-user sideloading claim clarified

The Verge reported on 2023-11-07 that Google told the jury that over a billion people have gone through Android's sideloading flow after being notified of the risks; Google later clarified to The Verge this counts enabling the setting, not necessarily completing an install. Google attorney Glenn Pomerantz boasted that "over a billion people have gone through the process Epic portrays as needlessly onerous to get apps outside the Play Store." However, Google subsequently clarified to The Verge that this figure represents users who enabled the sideloading setting rather than those who actually completed sideloading installations.
