# Jared Goff's MVP Case Based on Team Success and Easy Schedule

Sporting News reported on 2023-12-05 that Jared Goff belongs in the MVP conversation because his Lions are 9-3, are poised to win their division, are contending for the NFC's No. 1 seed, and face the easiest remaining schedule among the top MVP candidates. The article notes that while Brock Purdy, Dak Prescott, and Jalen Hurts are the current MVP favorites, "by that logic, Jared Goff should be in the conversation" given his team's strong performance and favorable path to finish the season.
