# OpenAI Valuation Reaches $80 Billion in December 2023 Funding Round

The Age reported on 2023-12-09 that OpenAI was valued at more than $US80 billion ($A123 billion) in its latest funding round, according to two people familiar with that round. This valuation came as part of the broader competition among Silicon Valley leaders to control AI development, with OpenAI backed by hundreds of millions of dollars from Elon Musk and other funders. The company's ChatGPT chatbot has fundamentally changed the technology industry and introduced the world to both the risks and potential of artificial intelligence.
