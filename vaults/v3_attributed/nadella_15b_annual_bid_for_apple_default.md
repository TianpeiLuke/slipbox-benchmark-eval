# Nadella's $15B Offer for Bing Default Status

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-31 that Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella testified he was willing to spend "$15 billion per year to get Bing into Apple's default search." This statement occurred during the October 2023 antitrust trial proceedings, where Nadella's testimony highlighted Microsoft's interest in securing default search engine placement on Apple devices as a competitive strategy against Google.
