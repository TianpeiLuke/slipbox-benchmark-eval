# DICOM Standard Security Vulnerabilities Expose Patient Medical Records

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-09 that thousands of exposed servers are leaking medical records and personal health information of millions of patients due to security weaknesses in the Digital Imaging and Communications in Medicine (DICOM) standard. "Security shortcomings in DICOM mean many medical facilities have unintentionally made private data accessible to the open web." DICOM is described as the decades-old internationally recognized format for storing and exchanging medical images across industry systems.
