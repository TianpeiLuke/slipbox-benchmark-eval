# Sam Bankman-Fried's Not Guilty Plea and Potential Sentencing

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-01 that in January 2023, Sam Bankman-Fried pleaded not guilty to all counts, including wire fraud, conspiracy to commit money laundering and conspiracy to misuse customer funds. The article states he "could face up to 115 years in jail if convicted on all charges."
