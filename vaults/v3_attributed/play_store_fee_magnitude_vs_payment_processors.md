# Google's App Store Fees Compared to PayPal/Credit Cards

The Verge reported on 2023-11-05 that Google charges up to ten times more per transaction than PayPal or a credit card does. This comparison was offered as intuitive support for Epic's position regarding Google's app store fees, though it falls outside the formal courtroom record. The article notes this observation while discussing Epic's antitrust lawsuit against Google over alleged monopoly practices in Android app distribution and payment processing.
