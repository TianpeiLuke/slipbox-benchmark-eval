# SEO-Driven 'Near Me' Business Naming Trend in US Local Commerce

The Verge reported on 2023-10-26 that US businesses increasingly adopt SEO-optimized names like "Thai Food Near Me" and "Barber Shop Near Me" to improve Google search visibility. This trend leverages Google's dominance in local discovery, with business owners like Teddy Jirapraphanan and Felix Silva explicitly designing their branding around search algorithms rather than traditional memorability. "Everywhere I go, I'm craving Thai food," Jirapraphanan noted, explaining his restaurant's strategic naming. Critics argue this reflects Google's problematic influence on local commerce, as businesses prioritize algorithmic discoverability over authentic branding to rank higher in search results.
