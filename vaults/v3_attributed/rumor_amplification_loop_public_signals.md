# Swift-Kelce Speculation Feedback Loop

CBSSports.com reported on 2023-10-12 that speculation and public signals surrounding Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce reinforced each other throughout 2023. "The talks only intensified" as each public act became new material that intensified rumors. Starting with Kelce's friendship bracelets in June-July, followed by his celebrity crush naming in August, non-denials of rumors in September, Swift's acceptance of his game invite, and her actual game attendance in September, "each dated act by Kelce or Swift (a bracelet, a non-denial, an on-camera invite, a game appearance) becomes new material that intensifies the rumors, which in turn draw more attention to the next act."
