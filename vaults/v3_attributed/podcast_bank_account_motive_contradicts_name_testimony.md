# Bankman-Fried's Contradictory Testimony on Alameda's Name Choice

The Verge reported on 2023-10-28 that Sam Bankman-Fried's courtroom testimony claiming Alameda Research was named for obscurity was contradicted by his prior podcast statement. During his criminal trial testimony, Bankman-Fried stated that "we wanted to be under the radar at that point in time" and chose the name to avoid drawing attention from competitors. However, this explanation is directly undermined by his own previous public statement that the name actually made it easier to obtain a bank account. The contradiction became particularly damaging because Bankman-Fried made the podcast remarks during the first day of co-defendant Gary Wang's testimony, which both Bankman-Fried and the jury attended. "I would find this much more believable if I hadn't already watched a video of Bankman-Fried explaining on a podcast that the name made it easier to get a bank account," the reporter noted.
