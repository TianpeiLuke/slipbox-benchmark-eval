# Google's Android Licensing Bundles Play Store with Anti-Fragmentation Agreements

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-06 that Google's licensing of Android requires OEMs to bundle Google Play with other Google apps and sign Anti-Fragmentation Agreements that prevent modification of Android to allow frictionless downloading outside Google Play. Epic Games argues these practices limit alternative app stores' consumer reach, citing that 90% of apps are still downloaded through Google Play. The company specifically referenced its canceled deal with OnePlus over AFA concerns and claims Google's agreements make it difficult for alternatives like Samsung's Galaxy Store to compete effectively.
