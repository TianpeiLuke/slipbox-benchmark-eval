# GPT Store Concept for User-Created AI Models

TechCrunch reported on 2023-09-28 that OpenAI announced a GPT store where users can create and monetize their own custom versions of GPT. During OpenAI's first developer conference on November 6, the company revealed this marketplace as part of several updates coming to GPT technology. The GPT store represents OpenAI's expansion beyond basic chatbot functionality into a platform economy where developers and creators can build and profit from their own specialized AI implementations.
