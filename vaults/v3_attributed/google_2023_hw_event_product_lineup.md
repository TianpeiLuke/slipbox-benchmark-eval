# Google's October 2023 Hardware Announcements

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-07 that Google unveiled new hardware at its annual event during the week ending October 7, 2023. "This week was Google's annual hardware event, where the search and consumer tech giant showed off what it's been working on." The announcements covered updates on the Pixel 8 and Pixel 8 Pro, Pixel Fold, Android 14, Pixel Buds, Google Assistant, Bard, Pixel Watch 2 and other products.
