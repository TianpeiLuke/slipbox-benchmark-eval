# Alameda's Special Credit Line Arrangement

The Verge reported on 2023-10-10 that Gary Wang testified about Alameda Research's unique financial arrangement with FTX, stating that "Alameda got access to a special credit line and an option to take its balance into the negative without triggering liquidation — something he alleged other accounts at FTX didn't get." This special credit line was defined as the Alameda-only arrangement permitting a negative overall balance without triggering liquidation, with the boundary being that other FTX accounts allegedly received neither element. Wang's testimony contradicted his earlier statements about market making, though the specifics weren't submitted as evidence.
