# SEO Business Model Exploits Google's Perceived Objectivity

The Verge reported on 2023-11-01 that the SEO business model works by "leveraging Google's supposed objectivity to lure people in and then, usually, show them some kind of advertising." This approach involves telling search algorithms what they need to hear so websites can rank highly in search results. The strategy borrows Google's appearance of neutrality and reliability to attract users, then monetizes that traffic through advertisements. Over time, these techniques have become increasingly sophisticated and pervasive, contributing to what some describe as a "search-optimized hellhole" where authentic content is difficult to distinguish from promotional material.
