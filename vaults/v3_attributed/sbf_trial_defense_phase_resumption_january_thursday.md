# SBF Trial Defense Phase Begins After Prosecution Rests

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-06 that Sam Bankman-Fried's fraud and money-laundering trial resumed with the defense phase after the prosecution rested. SBF himself is scheduled to testify, along with several other witnesses, as the onus shifts toward the defense. The trial had been on pause since October 20. Former federal prosecutor Josh Naftalis characterized SBF's planned testimony as a "Hail Mary" given the risks of cross-examination.
