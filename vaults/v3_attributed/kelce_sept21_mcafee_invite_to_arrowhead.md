# Kelce's September 21 Response to Swift Romance Rumors

CBSSports.com reported on 2023-10-12 that Travis Kelce addressed rumors about his relationship with Taylor Swift on the Pat McAfee show on September 21, 2023. Kelce called the speculation "hilarious" and compared it to the school game of telephone. He disclosed that he had invited Swift to a game, framing it as a contest over who would put on the bigger show at Arrowhead Stadium. "It is hilarious how much traction this has actually got," Kelce said, adding "I threw the ball in her court and I told her you know, I've seen you rock the stage in Arrowhead you might have to come see me rock the stage in Arrowhead and see which one is a little more lit."
