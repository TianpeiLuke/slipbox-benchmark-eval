# Thai Food Near Me's SEO-Driven Naming Strategy

The Verge reported on 2023-10-26 that Thai Food Near Me restaurant owner Teddy Jirapraphanan credits their Google-search-inspired name for immediate customer recognition. "When you have a million restaurants close by, you will be in the bottom [of rankings] if it's a random name," Jirapraphanan explained, noting that customers arriving at the restaurant told him they had searched the exact phrase "Thai Food Near Me" and found it through Google search results.
