# Line Shopping Applies to All Bet Types Across Sportsbooks

Sporting News reported on 2023-11-06 that line shopping applies to any priced market - moneylines, point spreads, totals, and prop bets - with the workflow being pick a side, then use drop-down menus and tabs to reveal that bet's odds and lines across books. The publication states that "shopping around lets you find the best lines and odds for any bet" and emphasizes that this process works for "moneylines, point spreads, totals bets, prop bets, and more." Different sportsbooks may offer varying odds due to their risk management strategies, creating opportunities for bettors to find improved lines. The key is comparing the same bets across multiple platforms to identify the most favorable pricing.
