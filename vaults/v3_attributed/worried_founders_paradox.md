# AI Builders' Paradox: Fear and Ambition Drive Creation

The Age reported on 2023-12-09 that among AI's leading builders, intense public fear about the technology coexists with fierce determination to create it and capture its economic rewards. This contradiction is resolved by their belief that only they themselves possess the capability to prevent AI from endangering Earth. The article documents how Silicon Valley figures like Musk, Page, Zuckerberg, Thiel, Nadella, and Altman have simultaneously expressed grave concerns about AI's potential dangers while competing intensely to develop and profit from the technology, driven by both ego and substantial financial incentives.
