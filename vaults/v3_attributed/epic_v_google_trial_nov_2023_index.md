# Epic v. Google Trial Notes Index - November 6, 2023

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-06 that Epic Games' antitrust trial against Google began November 6, focusing on allegations that Google engages in anticompetitive behavior with Google Play. Epic's case relies on the Sherman Act, Cartwright Act, and California's Unfair Competition Law, arguing Google restrains competition in two markets: Android app distribution and in-app payment processing. Key arguments include Google's 15%-30% commission structure, User Choice Billing program, OEM agreements, Anti-Fragmentation Agreements, and developer contracts like "Project Hug." Google counters that it faces competition from other app stores and that its commissions fund security and discovery features.
