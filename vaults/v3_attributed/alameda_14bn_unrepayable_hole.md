# Wang's Courtroom Testimony on Alameda's $14 Billion Hole

CNBC reported on 2023-10-06 that Gary Wang testified in court that he and Sam Bankman-Fried were aware in November 2022 that Alameda Research had no way to repay a roughly $14 billion hole in its books. Wang stated, "he and Bankman-Fried were aware that Alameda had no way to repay the roughly $14 billion hole in its books." This figure represents Wang's courtroom account rather than an audited measurement, according to the report.
