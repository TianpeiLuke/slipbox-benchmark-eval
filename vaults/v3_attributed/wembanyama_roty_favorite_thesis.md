# Victor Wembanyama's Rookie of the Year Favourite Status

Sporting News reported on 2023-10-04 that Victor Wembanyama entered the 2023-24 season as the early Rookie of the Year favourite. "His elite defense, highlighted by his 7-foot-4 height and 8-foot wingspan, allows him to dominate where others can't." The article notes that Wembanyama's defensive impact is unmatched among rookies, while his offensive agility and skill set are exceptional for his size. His impressive NBA preseason performance suggested continued improvement in shooting and adaptation to the Spurs' system under coach Gregg Popovich.
