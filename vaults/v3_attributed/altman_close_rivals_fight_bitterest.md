# Altman on AI Disputes Among Collaborators

The Age reported on 2023-12-09 that Sam Altman observed how disagreement, mistrust, and ego intensify among those closely aligned in purpose, likening it to conflicts within sects and religious orders. He stated, "The closer people are to being pointed in the same direction, the more contentious the disagreements are. You see this in sects and religious orders. There are bitter fights between the closest people." This insight was illustrated through the fractious relationships among AI pioneers like Musk, Page, Zuckerberg, Thiel, Nadella, and Altman himself, who, despite sharing similar goals regarding AI development, experienced significant internal conflicts—particularly highlighted by the turmoil within OpenAI's leadership.
