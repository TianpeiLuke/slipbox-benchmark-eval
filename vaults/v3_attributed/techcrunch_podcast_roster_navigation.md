# TechCrunch Audio Section Recommends Three Podcasts for New Listeners

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-07 that its Audio section recommends three podcasts for new listeners: "Equity (startups and venture deals), Found (founder profiles), and Chain Reaction (blockchain and crypto)." The section serves as a starting point for discovering TechCrunch's podcast offerings, specifically highlighting these three shows in the week ending October 7, 2023 as recommendations for listeners looking for new content while working out, doing dishes, or enjoying autumn activities.
