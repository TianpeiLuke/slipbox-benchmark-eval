# ChatGPT's Missing Research Paper

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-30 that OpenAI has published dozens of research papers on its models but, as Stella Biderman notes pointedly, never released one describing ChatGPT itself. Biderman, an AI researcher at Booz Allen Hamilton and EleutherAI, told TechCrunch that she doesn't see ChatGPT as an AI breakthrough per se. (OpenAI, which has released dozens of research papers on its models, tellingly never released one on ChatGPT.)
