# RAG's Promise vs. Reality in Language Models

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-07 that users criticized Google's Gemini Pro for failing basic factual questions despite having access to retrieval-augmented generation (RAG). Vitor de Lucca tweeted that questions "shouldn't be hard anymore with RAG" but Gemini incorrectly stated Brendan Gleeson won Best Actor instead of Brendan Fraser. The model also confused Oscar winners for "All the Beauty and the Bloodshed," "All Quiet on the Western Front," "Women Talking," and "Pinocchio." Users argued that RAG should ground responses in external documents to prevent such errors, making lookup questions straightforward.
