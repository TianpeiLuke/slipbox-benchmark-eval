# Confabulation in Gemini Pro's responses

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-07 that Charlie Stross identified numerous instances where Google's Gemini Pro model produced fabricated information, including falsely claiming he contributed to the Linux kernel. The model demonstrated "confabulation" by generating fluent, confident statements that were simply untrue rather than expressing uncertainty or refusing to answer. Users reported Gemini Pro providing incorrect Oscar winners, struggling with basic facts despite access to Google's knowledge sources, and failing to accurately translate or generate simple code examples. Science fiction author Charlie Stross specifically highlighted this pattern of factual invention in his blog post analysis of the model's performance.
