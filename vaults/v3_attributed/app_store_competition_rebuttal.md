# Google's Counterargument on App Store Competition in Epic Games Antitrust Trial

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-06 that Google plans to push back against Epic Games' antitrust claims by arguing it faces genuine competition in the app store market. Google will cite Apple's App Store as competition, which the Ninth Circuit recognized in the Apple case, along with Samsung's Galaxy Store and Amazon's Appstore on Android. Google contends these alternatives demonstrate a competitive landscape that undermines Epic's monopoly assertions. Epic counters that Google's practices make alternative app stores difficult to succeed, noting that 90% of apps are still downloaded through Google Play.
