# SEO vs. Sponsored Advertising Distinction

The Verge reported on 2023-11-01 that SEO differs fundamentally from buying advertising space labeled "Sponsored" above organic search results. While sponsored ads involve paying for explicit placement, SEO "is more like the Wizard of Oz projecting his voice to magnify his authority" - aiming to manipulate Google's algorithm to achieve high organic rankings. The practice involves telling the algorithm "whatever it needs to hear for a site to appear as high up as possible in search results" rather than purchasing designated advertising slots.
