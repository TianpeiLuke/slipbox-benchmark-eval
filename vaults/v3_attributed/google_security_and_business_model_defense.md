# Google's Antitrust Defense Claims Sideloading Risks and Competitive Harm

The Verge reported on 2023-11-05 that Google argues Epic's proposed remedy would dismantle Android's baseline security protections against sideloaded apps, making phones less safe. Google further claims this approach would undercut Android's ability to compete with iPhones by preventing it from running a competitive app store if forced to give away its store for free. This counter-argument frames the antitrust dispute as not just about market competition but also user safety and platform viability.
