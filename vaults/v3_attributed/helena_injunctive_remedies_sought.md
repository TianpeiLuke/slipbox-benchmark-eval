# News publishers seek injunctive relief against Google's AI training practices

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-15 that news publishers are seeking court injunctions requiring Google to obtain publisher consent before using website data to train general-purpose AI products. The lawsuit specifically requests that Google allow publishers who opt out of Search Generative Experience (SGE) to remain visible in ordinary Google search results. According to the complaint, Google's AI technologies are designed to discourage users from visiting publishers' websites, with SGE keeping users within Google's "walled garden" while allegedly plagiarizing publisher content. The suit argues that Google's AI training datasets included "news, magazine and digital publications" without proper consent.
