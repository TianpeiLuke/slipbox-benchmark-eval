# Google's RSA Defense Claims Legitimacy Through App Quality

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-13 that Google's Jamie Rosenberg defended the company's revenue share agreements (RSAs) during the US v Google antitrust trial, arguing that default placement of Search and Chrome on Android devices is legitimate because these apps are "best in class." Rosenberg testified on November 8, 2023 that the payments incentivize partners to build and sell more Android devices. According to Bloomberg's coverage cited by TechCrunch, Rosenberg claimed the competition between Google and Apple is "as intense as it gets."
