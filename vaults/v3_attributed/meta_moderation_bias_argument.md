# Meta's Structural Moderation Bias Against Palestinian Content

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-19 that Meta has a structural moderation bias problem that suppresses Palestinian voices, not merely technical bugs. This bias is evidenced by several structural factors: inadequate Arabic dialect coverage leading to misinterpretation of Palestinian content, binary content policies that fail to handle nuanced political discourse, partnerships with state agencies like Israel's Cyber Unit, reliance on user reports for enforcement, and a 2021 commissioned human rights report that found Meta's actions had "adverse human rights impact" on Palestinian users' freedom of expression. Palestinian users and activists, including filmmaker Khitam Jabr and supermodel Bella Hadid, report consistent shadowbanning and account restrictions for pro-Palestinian content that doesn't violate platform policies. Meta maintains its policies are applied equally worldwide and attributes visibility issues to technical bugs rather than deliberate suppression.
