# Pomerantz's Samsung Homescreen Argument Against Google Monopoly Claim

The Verge reported on 2023-11-07 that Google's lead attorney Glenn Pomerantz argued Google cannot monopolize Android app stores because "every single Samsung phone comes with two app stores right on the homescreen." When presenting charts showing Play Store download dominance, Pomerantz explained that "that's what the Samsung phone users are choosing. They're touching Play. Nothing's keeping them from touching the Galaxy Store; it's just what works for them." This commonsense warrant counters Epic's monopoly claims by emphasizing user choice rather than restrictive practices.
