# Google's Argument That Phone Quality Drives App Store Competition

The Verge reported on 2023-11-07 that Google's lead attorney Glenn Pomerantz argued Google's dominance in Android app stores reflects legitimate competition with Apple's iOS, rather than monopolistic behavior. Pomerantz contended that "you cannot separate the quality of a phone from the quality of the apps in its app store, and that means Google and Apple compete against each other." Google maintains that Epic's Play Store dominance is the outcome of this cross-platform rivalry rather than anti-competitive practices. The argument suggests that consumer preference for Google Play stems from the overall quality proposition of Android devices competing against iPhones, making Google's market position a result of competitive dynamics rather than market manipulation.
