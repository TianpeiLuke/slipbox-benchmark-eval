# FTX and Alameda Bankruptcy and New CEO Appointment

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-01 that "FTX and Alameda filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in the U.S. mid-November 2022. Bankman-Fried resigned, and John J. Ray III, the Enron turnaround veteran, was appointed its new CEO." The bankruptcy followed revelations about Alameda's inflated FTT token holdings and a bank run triggered by Binance's withdrawal of $2.1 billion equivalent of cash in BUSD and FTT.
