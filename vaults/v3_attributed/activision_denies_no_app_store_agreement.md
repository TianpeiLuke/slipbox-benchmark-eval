# Activision Payment Evidence Challenges Epic's Monopoly Claims

The Verge reported on 2023-11-07 that Epic Games' $360 million bribery allegation against Google may not hold up in court. Epic claims Google paid developers like Activision not to create competing app stores, but Activision stated in 2022 that it "never entered into an agreement that Activision would not open its own app store." Google now claims to have evidence supporting this position. Epic's attorney admitted Google "was too clever" to create explicit anti-competition contracts, suggesting the company used more subtle tactics to maintain its Android app store dominance.
