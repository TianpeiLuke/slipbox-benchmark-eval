# Manchester United and Newcastle Eliminated from Champions League 2023/24

Sporting News reported on 2023-12-12 that Manchester United and Newcastle United will play no further part in Europe after being eliminated from the 2023/24 Champions League group stage. Both English clubs finished bottom of their respective groups, with Manchester United ending last in Group A behind Bayern Munich and Copenhagen, while Newcastle finished fourth in their group. The article states that "Manchester United and Newcastle United will play no further part in Europe, though, having been eliminated by finishing bottom of their groups."
