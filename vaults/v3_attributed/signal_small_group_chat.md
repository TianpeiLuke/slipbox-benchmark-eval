# FTX Inner Circle's Secret Communication Channel

The Verge reported on 2023-10-26 that Joe Bankman was among individuals included in the "small group chat," which was a Signal conversation where the FTX inner circle coordinated strategy during the exchange's final days. This private messaging group was distinct from public channels and involved key figures like Sam Bankman-Fried's father Joe Bankman, Nishad Singh, and others in strategic discussions as the exchange collapsed. The article describes how this intimate communication channel was used by Bankman-Fried's closest associates and family members to navigate the crisis while FTX customers' funds were being misappropriated.
