# Equity podcast discusses SBF trial and venture funding trends

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-07 that this week's Equity episode covered several key topics including the ongoing Sam Bankman-Fried trial, investment deals from VR firm Rainforest, At One Ventures, Section 32 and Greylock amid declining venture funding, and the barring of Fearless Fund from awarding grants to Black women founders. "The episode discussed the SBF trial, investment deals from VR firm Rainforest, At One Ventures, Section 32 and Greylock amid declining venture funding, and the barring of Fearless Fund — a firm founded to invest in women of color — from awarding grants to Black women founders."
