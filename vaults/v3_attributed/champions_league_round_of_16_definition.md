# Champions League Round of 16 qualification process

Sporting News reported on 2023-12-12 that the UEFA Champions League Round of 16 represents "the first knockout round of the competition, filled by the sixteen clubs that emerge from the group stage in the qualifying positions." The publication detailed how the "16 precious places in the first knockout round" were claimed after the group stage concluded, with teams like Bayern Munich, Copenhagen, Arsenal, PSV, Real Madrid, Napoli, Real Sociedad, and Inter Milan securing qualification through their group performances. Notably, Manchester United and Newcastle United were eliminated after finishing bottom of their respective groups, while PSG advanced to the Round of 16 despite not winning their group.
