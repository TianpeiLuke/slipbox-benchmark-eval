# Swift-Kelce Relationship Claims Remain Unproven Despite Public Observations

The Age reported on 2023-09-26 that observations of Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce together do not confirm a romantic relationship. "Of course, none of this actually confirms Swift and Kelce are actually dating" since all evidence — including stadium attendance, matching references, and shared departure — remains equally consistent with friendship. The publication notes that while Swift appeared at Arrowhead Stadium to support Kelce's team and was photographed leaving with him, along with other circumstantial indicators like his "1989 bedroom painting set" outfit, these observations cannot definitively establish romance versus friendship.
