# Manual Line Shopping Procedure for Sports Betting

Sporting News reported on 2023-11-06 that manual line shopping involves installing multiple licensed sportsbook apps on a phone to compare lines and odds. The procedure requires tapping between different sportsbook applications to evaluate betting options, allowing bettors to trade slower speed for access to more books or consistently preferred ones. This method enables comparison of moneylines, point spreads, totals bets, and prop bets across different platforms to find better odds and improved lines, though it takes longer than using automated live odds feeds.
