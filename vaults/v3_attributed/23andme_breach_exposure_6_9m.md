# 23andMe breach exposed 6.9 million people's data in October 2023

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-09 that 23andMe's October 2023 breach exposed personal data of 6.9 million people, far exceeding the 14,000 customers (0.1%) initially disclosed on December 1, 2023. "The company didn't initially say how many other users might've been impacted by the breach, which 23andMe first disclosed in October. A lot, as it turns out." The compromised data included names, birth years, relationship labels, shared-DNA percentages, ancestry reports, and self-reported locations.
