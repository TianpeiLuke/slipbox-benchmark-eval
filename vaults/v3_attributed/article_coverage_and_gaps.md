# TechCrunch's 2023-12-19 "8 predictions for AI in 2024" contains six prediction sections, missing the promised US election prediction

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-19 that their article "8 predictions for AI in 2024" contains six prediction sections rather than eight. The article's introduction promises predictions including AI's impact on US primary elections and OpenAI's future, but the US-elections prediction is absent and the "Legal cases build and break" section is truncated after its first clause. The included predictions cover OpenAI becoming more product-focused, agent-based models advancing beyond experimental status, generated video and music finding niches, limitations of monolithic LLMs becoming clearer, marketing hype meeting reality, and Apple entering the AI space. "Expect an AI that focuses on practic" ends the incomplete final section.
