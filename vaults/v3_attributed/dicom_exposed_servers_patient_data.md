# Internet-Exposed DICOM Servers Leak Patient Health Records

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-09 that thousands of internet-exposed DICOM servers are leaking medical records and personal health information of millions of patients due to security weaknesses in the decades-old industry standard. Aplite, a Germany-based cybersecurity consultancy, discovered that many medical facilities have unintentionally made private data accessible to the open web through these Digital Imaging and Communications in Medicine (DICOM) servers.
