# Palestinian filmmaker Khitam Jabr's Instagram account locked after non-political West Bank Reels

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-19 that Palestinian-American filmmaker Khitam Jabr was blocked from responding to comments and using her account for 24 hours after posting Reels about her family trip to the West Bank. The content showed Palestinian culture including meals and dancing at a wedding, and was not political. Jabr stated, "I wasn’t even posting anything about the occupation" yet faced restrictions after anti-Palestine comments appeared on her posts. She suspects other users reporting her content led to the temporary lockout, reflecting broader concerns about Meta's moderation bias against Palestinian voices.
