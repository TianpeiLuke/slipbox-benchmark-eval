# TechCrunch's 7 October 2023 Week in Review covers eight major stories

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-07 that its Week in Review edition indexed eight headline stories. "This week, we cover Sam Altman backing a teen's AI startup, Google's hardware event (and first impressions of the Pixel 8 Pro), Flexport drama, and the ongoing FTX fallout," with additional coverage of Gmail's anti-spam measures, TikTok's ad-free subscription testing, LinkedIn's AI tool expansion, and secondary items including X's conflicting user metrics and IronNet's closure. The "Most read" section ranked these stories in that order.
