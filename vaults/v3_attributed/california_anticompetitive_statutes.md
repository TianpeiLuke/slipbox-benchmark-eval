# Epic's Antitrust Claims Include California's Cartwright Act and Unfair Competition Law

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-06 that Epic Games will bring claims under both federal antitrust law and California's anticompetitive statutes in its trial against Google. "To make its case, Epic plans to bring claims under Sections 1 and 2 of U.S. antitrust law, the Sherman Act, as well as California's anticompetitive laws, the Cartwright Act, and the Unfair Competition Law." The company alleges Google restrains competition in Android app distribution and payment processing markets through anticompetitive agreements with device manufacturers and developers.
