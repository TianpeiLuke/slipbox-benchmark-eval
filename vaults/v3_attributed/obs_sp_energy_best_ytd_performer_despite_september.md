# S&P 500 Energy Stocks Performance Post-September 2023 Decline

The Sydney Morning Herald reported on 2023-10-01 that despite energy stocks in the S&P 500 falling 2 per cent on September 29, 2023, they remained the best-performing sector since summer 2023. "Energy stocks, though, remain the market's standout performers since the summer" despite the recent decline following oil price volatility and broader market pressures.
