# Gary Wang's Role and Background in FTX Fraud Case

CNBC reported on 2023-10-06 that Gary Wang, aged 30 at the time of his testimony, was the lesser-known co-founder and technology chief of FTX. Born in China and emigrating to the United States at age 7, Wang was raised in Minnesota and educated at MIT in math and computer science. He briefly worked at Google and first met Sam Bankman-Fried at a high-school summer camp. During the FTX fraud trial, Wang testified that he committed financial crimes while working at FTX, stating "Yes" when asked if he committed wire and commodities fraud with Bankman-Fried's assistance. Wang described himself as having been integral to FTX's operations before its November 2022 bankruptcy.
