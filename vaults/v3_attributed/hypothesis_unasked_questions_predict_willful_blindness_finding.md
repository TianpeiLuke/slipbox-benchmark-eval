# Jury inference of knowledge from unanswered financial questions

The Verge reported on 2023-10-31 that Sam Bankman-Fried's failure to investigate an $8 billion related-party liability after being told to stop asking questions will support juror inference of knowledge or willful blindness. When Bankman-Fried's employees directed him to stop inquiring about the fiat@ftx account because it was "distracting," he complied rather than escalating to counsel or law enforcement. The publication noted that "simple questions nailed Bankman-Fried to the wall" when he couldn't answer whether he had policies preventing Alameda from spending FTX customer money. Jurors focused on his lack of substantive inquiry despite the massive shortfall, with his testimony revealing he didn't call law enforcement, build blame cases, or fire anyone after discovering the $8 billion discrepancy.
