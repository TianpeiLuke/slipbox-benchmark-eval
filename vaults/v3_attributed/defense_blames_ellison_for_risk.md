# Defence argues Ellison's risk management failures caused Alameda's collapse

Fortune reported on 2023-10-04 that during Sam Bankman-Fried's trial, defense attorney Mark Cohen argued Caroline Ellison, as CEO of Alameda Research, failed to adequately protect the hedge fund from crypto market risks. "When the walls came closing in and the aforementioned plane approached the 'eye of the storm,' Bankman-Fried did not act like someone who was guilty," Cohen stated, shifting blame to Ellison's risk management rather than Bankman-Fried's alleged fraudulent design.
