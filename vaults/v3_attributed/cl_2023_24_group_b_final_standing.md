# Champions League Group B Final Standings 2023/24

Sporting News reported on 2023-12-12 that "Arsenal finished top of the pile and their 6-0 thrashing of Lens on matchday five confirmed them as winners of Group B." The final standings showed Arsenal qualifying with 13 points (4W 1D 1L, GD +12), PSV advancing with 9 points (2W 3D 1L, GD -2), Lens securing Europa League entry with 8 points, and Sevilla finishing last with 2 points.
