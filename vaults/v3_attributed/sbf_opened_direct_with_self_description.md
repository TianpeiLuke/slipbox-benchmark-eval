# Bankman-Fried's self-described introversion and pre-Alameda biography

The Verge reported on 2023-10-28 that Sam Bankman-Fried began his testimony by describing himself as "somewhat introverted, naturally" and provided a lengthy account of his life before Alameda Research. According to the publication, Bankman-Fried "gave us a rather prolonged tour of his pre-Alameda Research life, which I will skip" before reaching the events of 2017 when he started his cryptocurrency trading firm. The article notes that during this direct testimony, Bankman-Fried was repeatedly scolded by Judge Lewis Kaplan for not answering questions directly, with the judge having to instruct him to respond to the questions posed by his own lawyer.
