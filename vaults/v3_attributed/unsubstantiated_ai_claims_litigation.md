# AI Marketing Backlash Lawsuits Predicted for 2024

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-19 that AI service providers who failed to substantiate their marketing claims will face lawsuits and regulatory action during 2024. The publication stated that "the hype built up in 2023 is going to be very hard for companies to follow through on" and predicted that "on the far end of this spectrum, we are likely to see lawsuits and regulatory action with AI service providers that failed to back up their claims." This prediction suggests that companies whose marketing promises exceed their AI capabilities will face legal consequences when their tools fail to deliver promised benefits.
