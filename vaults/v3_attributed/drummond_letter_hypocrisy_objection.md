# Bellshaw Uses Drummond's Letter to Counter Google's Consumer Preference Defense

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-31 that U.S. Justice Department lawyer Meagan Bellshaw undermined Google's consumer-preference defense by citing a 2005 letter from Google's own Kent Drummond which stated the company once opposed default settings because "users don't change defaults." This historical position directly contradicts Google's current argument that consumers prefer Google as the default search engine, demonstrating a strategic shift in the company's stance on default settings to maintain market dominance.
