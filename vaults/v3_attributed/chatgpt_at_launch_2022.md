# ChatGPT's Original Form and Framing at Launch

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-30 that ChatGPT was originally launched on November 30, 2022 as a "low-key research preview" according to OpenAI's internal framing. The initial release was a free, web-based, chat-focused interface built on top of OpenAI's existing GPT-3.5 model. OpenAI's leadership positioned it as a data-gathering experiment to understand how people interact with generative AI, rather than as a commercial product. This basic version preceded the addition of paid tiers, web searching capabilities, document analysis, image creation via DALL-E 3, and multimodal features like speech recognition and synthesis.
