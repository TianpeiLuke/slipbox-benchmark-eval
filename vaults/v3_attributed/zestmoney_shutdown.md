# ZestMoney shuts down after failing to find buyer

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-09 that ZestMoney, the Bengaluru buy-now-pay-later startup, is shutting down after failing to find a buyer. The company, which underwrote small-ticket loans to first-time internet users and counted Goldman Sachs among its backers, had employed about 150 people at peak and raised over $130 million in eight years.
