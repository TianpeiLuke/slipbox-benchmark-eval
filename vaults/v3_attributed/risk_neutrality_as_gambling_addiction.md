# Author disputes Bankman-Fried's risk-neutral self-description

The Verge reported on 2023-10-10 that Sam Bankman-Fried's characterization of himself as "risk-neutral" is disputed by the author, who contends this is merely "fancy vocabulary for gambling addiction." This interpretation aligns with Bankman-Fried's reportedly bragging about having a 5 percent chance of becoming US president. The article describes these claims while covering the ongoing criminal fraud trial involving Bankman-Fried and the testimony of his former partner Caroline Ellison.
