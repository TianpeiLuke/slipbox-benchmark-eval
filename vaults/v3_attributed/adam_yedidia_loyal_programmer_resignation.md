# Adam Yedidia's Immunity Testimony Reveals Fear of Unwitting Fraud Participation

The Verge reported on 2023-10-26 that Adam Yedidia, Sam Bankman-Fried's college roommate, testified under immunity because he feared he had unknowingly written code that aided the fraud. Yedidia had texted Bankman-Fried "I love you Sam. I'm not going anywhere" before resigning once he learned that FTX had stolen customer funds. "He was concerned he may have unknowingly worked on code that contributed to the fraud," according to testimony described in the article about Bankman-Fried's trial and the friends who were affected by his actions.
