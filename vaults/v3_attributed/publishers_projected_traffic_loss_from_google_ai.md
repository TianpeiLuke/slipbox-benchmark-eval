# Publisher Traffic Loss Hypothesis from Google AI Search Rollout

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-15 that publishers believe Google's full AI search product rollout will reduce their website traffic by 20-40%. This hypothesis is falsifiable by comparing per-publisher Google referral volume before and after AI-search availability. The concern stems from The Atlantic's modeling showing that 75% of the time, Google's AI would answer user queries without requiring clicks to publisher websites. Since Google currently drives nearly 40% of publishers' traffic, this represents a significant potential revenue threat. Publishers' belief is based on Google's AI technologies like Search Generative Experience (SGE) and Bard AI chatbot keeping users within Google's ecosystem rather than directing them to original content sources.
