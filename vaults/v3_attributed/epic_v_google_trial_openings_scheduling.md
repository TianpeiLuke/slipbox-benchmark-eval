# Epic v. Google Trial Proceeds to Evidence Phase After Opening Statements

The Verge reported on 2023-11-07 that following opening statements in early November 2023, the Epic v. Google trial moved into its evidence phase where Epic's claims about Google's anti-competitive practices and the contested meaning of Android openness are being tested. Epic's lead attorney Gary Bornstein argued that Google "bribes" or "blocks" competitors by paying developers not to build alternative app stores, citing a "$360 million" payment to prevent Activision from opening its own store. Google's attorney Glenn Pomerantz countered that Google's dominance is simply the result of fierce competition with Apple's iOS, emphasizing that "every single Samsung phone comes with two app stores right on the homescreen."
