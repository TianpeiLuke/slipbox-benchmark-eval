# TikTok Tests Ad-Free Subscription Tier

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-07 that TikTok is testing an ad-free subscription tier priced at $4.99 per month. The service removes ads with no other strings attached. TikTok is currently piloting this feature in a single English-speaking market outside the United States, with no announced timeline for broader rollout. "For $4.99, subscribers get an ad-free experience on TikTok — no other strings attached."
