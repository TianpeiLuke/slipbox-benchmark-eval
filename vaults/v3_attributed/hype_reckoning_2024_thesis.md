# 2024 marks AI hype's collision with reality after rapid mainstream adoption

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-19 that 2024 will be the year when AI hype "runs full-steam into reality as people reckon with the capabilities and limitations of AI at large." The publication predicted this reckoning would occur because AI technology moved "from niche to mainstream about as fast as anything ever has" during 2023. TechCrunch identified this rapid adoption as setting up 2024 to be when users confront what large-scale AI can and cannot actually accomplish, marking a shift from the optimistic expectations built up during the previous year's explosive growth.
