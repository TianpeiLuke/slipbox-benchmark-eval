# Gemini Pro as Lite Tier Model

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-07 that Gemini Pro is the "lite" version of Google's Gemini model family. This variant began rolling out to Bard on 2023-12-06 and represents the model that users were reporting issues with during December 2023. The article notes that "A 'lite' version of Gemini, Gemini Pro, began rolling out to Bard yesterday," distinguishing it from other variants in the Gemini family.
