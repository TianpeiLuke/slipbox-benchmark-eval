# Barry Diller's Antitrust Objection to Google's Ad-Heavy Search Results

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-13 that Expedia and IAC chair Barry Diller objects to Google's increasing advertisement load in search results, arguing it disadvantages organic listings and companies dependent on fair search visibility. During the US v Google antitrust trial, Diller's criticism targeted Google's justification for default settings that favor ads over organic results. His objection stems from a 2019 letter to Google where he previously expressed concerns, and he reiterated these views in testimony on November 1, 2023, positioning Expedia as particularly affected by Google's practices that prioritize paid placements in vertical search categories.
