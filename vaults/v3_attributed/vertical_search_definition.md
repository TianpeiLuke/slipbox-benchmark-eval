# Vertical Search Definition from US v Google Trial Coverage

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-13 that vertical search refers to search confined to one very specific commercial market category, such as car insurance, cheap flights, credit reports or pay-TV, as distinct from general web search. The publication explained that queries like "car insurance," "cheap flights" and "credit report" demonstrate how much Google dominates vertical search - "that is, search in very specific market categories."
