# Tim Sweeney's Role in Epic's Antitrust Lawsuits

The Verge reported on 2023-12-12 that Tim Sweeney is Epic's CEO, co-founder, and controlling shareholder, making him "the one behind these lawsuits" and establishing that "it was his idea to challenge these companies in court." The article quotes Sweeney stating, "It's much needed by the industry which is being strangled by a few gatekeepers imposing insane amounts of control and extracting huge taxes."
