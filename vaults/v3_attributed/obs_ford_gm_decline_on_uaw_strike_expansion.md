# Auto stocks decline as UAW expands strikes to additional facilities

The Sydney Morning Herald reported on 2023-10-01 that shares of Ford and General Motors slipped after the United Auto Workers said it would expand its limited strike to include another facility for each company. "Ford fell 1.1 per cent, and GM dipped 0.6 per cent" following the announcement about expanded strike actions.
