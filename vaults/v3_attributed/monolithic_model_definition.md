# Monolithic LLM Limitations vs. Mixture of Experts Approach

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-19 that large language models will face limitations in 2024 as their "seemingly magical 'emergent' capabilities" become better understood. The article explains that "a single monolithic model is unwieldy and expensive, while a mixture of experts — a collection of smaller, more specific models and likely multimodal ones — may prove almost as effective while being much easier to update piecemeal." This shift comes as researchers observe diminishing returns on parameter counts, where training larger models becomes computationally inefficient compared to using specialized smaller models.
