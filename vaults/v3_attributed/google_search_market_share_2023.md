# Google's 2023 Market Dominance and Criticism

The Verge reported on 2023-11-01 that Google was described as "a trillion-dollar corporation holding roughly 90 percent of the online search market." The article characterized widespread dissatisfaction with Google Search results quality in 2023, with critics blaming both Google's dominance and search engine optimization practices for degrading online information quality.
