# Epic's Antitrust Claims Against Google's Android App Store Monopoly

The Verge reported on 2023-11-05 that Epic claims Google has monopolized Android app distribution by making it so difficult for developers and users to bypass the Play Store and its standard fees that "it's created an illegal monopoly that unjustly enriches Google while artificially driving up app prices because other stores can't compete."
