# Ellison testified about her romantic relationship with Bankman-Fried spanning multiple years

The Verge reported on 2023-10-10 that Caroline Ellison testified about her romantic relationship with Sam Bankman-Fried, stating they "slept together on and off from autumn 2018 while she was a trader and he was CEO," followed by two dating periods: "summer 2020 to summer 2021 kept secret, and autumn 2021 to spring 2022 living together." Ellison described their relationship dynamics while testifying in Bankman-Fried's criminal fraud trial, where she had already pleaded guilty to related charges and was cooperating with prosecutors.
