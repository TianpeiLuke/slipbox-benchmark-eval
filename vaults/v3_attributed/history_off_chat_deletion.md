# Google Chat's History-Off Deletion Policy

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-31 that Google Chat's "history-off" setting automatically deletes messages every 24 hours unless users manually change it. During Sundar Pichai's antitrust testimony, the DOJ highlighted this feature as problematic when Google was under litigation hold, accusing the company of systematically destroying chats. The policy distinguishes Google's external messaging from its internal retention-based messaging systems. Pichai acknowledged taking action in February to disable the history-off default to comply with legal requirements.
