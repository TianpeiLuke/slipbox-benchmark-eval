# ASX Futures Point to Opening Decline on October 1, 2023

The Sydney Morning Herald reported on 2023-10-01 that the Australian sharemarket is set to retreat, with futures pointing to a fall of 38 points, or 0.5 per cent, at the open. This follows Wall Street's worst month and quarter of the year, as Treasury yields climbed back to 4.58 per cent, pressuring stock valuations. Interest-rate sensitive sectors like healthcare and consumer companies were particularly weak amid expectations of higher-for-longer interest rates from the Reserve Bank of Australia.
