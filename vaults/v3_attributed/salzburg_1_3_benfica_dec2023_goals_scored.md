# Benfica Eliminates RB Salzburg in Champions League Group D

Sporting News reported on 2023-12-12 that Benfica defeated RB Salzburg 3-1 in Austria on December 12, 2023, eliminating Salzburg from Champions League contention. The victory was led by Angel Di Maria's inspired performance and sealed by Arthur Cabral's stoppage-time goal. "Salzburg had their Europa League destiny in their own hands but crashed out as an Angel Di Maria-inspired Benfica ran out 3-1 winners." Both teams finished Group D with 4 points and -4 goal difference, but Benfica advanced on goals scored tiebreaker.
