# Swift's Attendance at Five Chiefs Games Sparks Dating Rumors

CBSSports.com reported on 2023-10-12 that Taylor Swift had been photographed at five Kansas City Chiefs games during the 2023 regular season, beginning the previous month. "She was in attendance to watch the Chiefs as they defeated the Denver Broncos in Week 6 on 'Thursday Night Football.'" This game attendance count served as the measurable catalyst intensifying speculation about her rumored romance with tight end Travis Kelce.
