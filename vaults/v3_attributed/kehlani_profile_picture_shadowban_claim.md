# Kehlani shadowbanned claim

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-19 that Instagram users suspect Meta of shadowbanning content about Palestine. Singer Kehlani was allegedly shadowbanned immediately after changing her profile picture to "i stand with Palestine", according to a widely shared post by X user "@whoisitbad" dated 14 October 2023. The post states "As soon as Kehlani got super vocal and changed her profile picture to 'i stand with Palestine' she got shadowbanned." This claim cites precedent from supermodel Bella Hadid's similar experience with Instagram content restrictions.
