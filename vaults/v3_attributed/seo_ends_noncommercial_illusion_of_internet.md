# SEO Practitioners Destroyed Internet Idealism

The Verge reported on 2023-11-01 that SEO practitioners have successfully destroyed the illusion that the internet was ever about anything other than selling stuff. The publication described how search engine optimization techniques have become insidious, spreading to the point where searching for information online feels like navigating a "search-optimized hellhole." According to the article, this degradation of online experience explains the near-universal dislike of the SEO profession, as it revealed the commercial motivations underlying much of internet content. The piece noted that SEO's goal is to tell Google's algorithm whatever it needs to hear to achieve high search rankings, ultimately serving advertising purposes rather than genuine information sharing.
