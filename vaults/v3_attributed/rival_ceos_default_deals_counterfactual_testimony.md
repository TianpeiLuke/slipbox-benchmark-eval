# Microsoft and DuckDuckGo CEOs testify on foreclosed default deals

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-31 that earlier in October 2023, the CEOs of Microsoft and DuckDuckGo testified that their search engines would have been substantially more successful, even competitive with Google, had they secured comparable deals with Apple. Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella stated he was willing to spend $15 billion annually to make Bing the default search engine on Apple devices, highlighting how Google's exclusive agreements foreclosed competition in the search market.
