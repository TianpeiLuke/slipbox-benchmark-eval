# Kelce Invites Swift to Arrowhead Game

The Age reported on 2023-09-26 that Travis Kelce announced on The Pat McAfee Show on September 21, 2023, he had invited Taylor Swift to watch a game, telling her: "I've seen you rock the stage in Arrowhead, you might have to come see me rock a stage at Arrowhead and we can see which one's a little more lit." This invitation marked a significant moment in their developing relationship, following earlier public comments and speculation about their connection. Kelce's comment referenced Swift's previous performances at Arrowhead Stadium during her Eras Tour, while suggesting a reciprocal visit to his football domain.
