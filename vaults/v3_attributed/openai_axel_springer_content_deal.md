# OpenAI-Axel Springer December 2023 Partnership Details

TechCrunch reported on 2023-09-28 that OpenAI partnered with Axel Springer in December 2023 for a "real-time" ChatGPT news deal. The agreement enables OpenAI to train its generative AI models on Axel Springer's journalism content and incorporate recent articles from the publisher into ChatGPT responses. Axel Springer owns Business Insider and Politico. The partnership aims to "help provide people with new ways to access quality, real-time news content through our AI tools," according to OpenAI's announcement.
