# SEO Party Attendees Defy Villain Stereotype

The Verge reported on 2023-11-01 that the author's direct observation at a Florida rooftop party for SEO professionals contradicted the popular stereotype of these "internet ruiners" as morally bankrupt hustlers. Instead of encountering villains, the reporter found the attendees largely sympathetic. The article states: "The second thing that went wrong at the alligator party was that I found almost everyone I met to be sympathetic, or at least nice enough not to want to see them get maimed by a five-and-a-half-foot alligator." The author's harshest criticism was limited to their Florida-appropriate dress code, noting that most partygoers were locals wearing attire suited to the climate.
