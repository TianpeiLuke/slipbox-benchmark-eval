# Alameda Research Founding and Arbitrage Strategy

The Verge reported on 2023-10-28 that Sam Bankman-Fried started Alameda Research in 2017 during a crypto bull run to trade arbitrage, despite testifying that he knew "basically nothing" about cryptocurrency at the time. Bankman-Fried explained that "we wanted to be under the radar at that point in time" and chose the name Alameda Research because it was "a sort of generic word, which filled out the company name." He stated they achieved "50 percent to 100 percent annualized returns" from arbitrage trading before founding FTX.
