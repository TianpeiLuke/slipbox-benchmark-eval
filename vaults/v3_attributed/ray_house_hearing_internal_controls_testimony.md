# FTX CEO John Ray's Congressional Testimony on Internal Controls

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-01 that FTX CEO John J. Ray III testified before the House Financial Services Committee, stating "there were virtually no internal controls and no separateness whatsoever" at the collapsed crypto exchange. Ray, an Enron turnaround veteran appointed after FTX's bankruptcy, further testified that he did not "trust a single piece of paper" in the exchange's organization. His testimony came during questioning about FTX's risk management systems following the exchange's $32 billion collapse in November 2022.
