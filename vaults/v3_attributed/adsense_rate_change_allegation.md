# Publisher Alleges Google's AdSense Rate Changes Harm Publishers Economically

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-15 that the complaint additionally alleges that Google's changes to AdSense rates have harmed publishers economically, positioning the rate changes as one more instance of anticompetitive conduct within the digital news advertising line of commerce. The lawsuit filed by Helena World Chronicle claims these rate changes are part of Google's broader anticompetitive behavior that violates U.S. antitrust law, alongside AI technologies like Search Generative Experience and Bard AI that allegedly divert traffic and revenue away from news publishers.
