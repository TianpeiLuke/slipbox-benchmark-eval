# OpenAI's GPT Store Launch Delayed by Leadership Crisis

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-19 that OpenAI's GPT store was originally planned for December 2023 launch but was "understandably delayed due to the C-suite fracas" involving the November leadership crisis. The publication predicted this "app store for AI" would be pushed hard as OpenAI shifts toward a product-focused company under Sam Altman's renewed leadership.
