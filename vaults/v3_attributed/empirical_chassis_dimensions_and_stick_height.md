# Steam Deck OLED Physical Dimensions and Thumbstick Height

Engadget reported on 2023-11-09 that the Steam Deck OLED maintains identical physical dimensions to the original model at 11.7 by 4.5 by nearly 2 inches, with the thumbsticks measuring "1mm taller than the first version."
