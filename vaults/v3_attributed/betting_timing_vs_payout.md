# NFL Award Longshots Lose Value as Season Progresses

Sporting News reported on 2023-12-05 that NFL award longshots become less valuable betting opportunities as the regular season advances toward its final five weeks. While players like C.J. Stroud, Jared Goff, and Danielle Hunter show promise as MVP, OPOY, and DPOY candidates respectively, "if you wait too long before placing a bet, your payout will be a lot less than if you were to roll the dice on a longshot right now." The article notes there remains sufficient time for these underdogs to emerge as contenders, particularly with favorable schedules ahead for some candidates.
