# MIT Study Shows ChatGPT Boosts Productivity and Quality

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-30 that an MIT study found ChatGPT reduced completion time by 40% and improved output quality by 18% for tasks like drafting cover letters, delicate emails, and cost-benefit analyses. The study measured performance using third-party evaluator assessments of both speed and quality metrics.
