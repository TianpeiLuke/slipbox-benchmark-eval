# Barber Shop Near Me's Pre-Opening Digital Presence

The Verge reported on 2023-10-26 that Felix Silva chose the name "Barber Shop Near Me" for his Coral Springs, Florida business after considering over 20 alternatives. Before opening, the business existed only as a logo on an Instagram account. Silva told The Verge that prospective clients assumed "Google was coming to town" due to the generic nature of the name and minimal digital footprint.
