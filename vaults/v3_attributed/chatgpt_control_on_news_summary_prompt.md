# ChatGPT outperforms Gemini Pro on controversial news summarization

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-07 that when prompted to summarize topical news, Google's Gemini Pro model deflected by telling users to Google it themselves, while ChatGPT "gives a bullet-list summary with citations to news articles." The article notes that Gemini Pro appears "loath to comment on potentially controversial news topics," contrasting with ChatGPT's more direct approach to the same prompt.
