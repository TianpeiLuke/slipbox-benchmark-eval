# Kelce's 1989 Album Reference at Arrowhead

The Age reported on 2023-09-26 that observant fans identified Travis Kelce's blue-and-white post-game outfit at Arrowhead Stadium as the "1989 bedroom painting set," which they interpreted as a reference to Taylor Swift's 2014 album that she was re-releasing in October. The article states that "extra observant fans were able to note that Kelce's blue and white post-game outfit was supposedly labelled the '1989 bedroom painting set', perhaps an intentional reference to Swift's 2014 album."
