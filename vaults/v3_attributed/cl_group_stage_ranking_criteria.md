# Champions League Group D Tie-Breaker Rules Applied

Sporting News reported on 2023-12-12 that in Champions League Group D, Real Sociedad and Inter Milan finished level on points (12 each) and were separated by goal difference, with Real Sociedad advancing due to their +5 GD compared to Inter's +3 GD. When teams are level on points and goal difference, the next criterion is goals scored. Though not needed to separate the top two teams, this rule would have been applied to Benfica and RB Salzburg who were level on points (4 each) and goal difference (-4 each), with Benfica progressing based on more goals scored. The tie-breaking sequence follows standard UEFA rules: "points, then goal difference, then goals scored."
