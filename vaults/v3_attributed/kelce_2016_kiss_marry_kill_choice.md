# Travis Kelce's 2016 "Kiss, Marry, Kill" Choice Revealed Link to Taylor Swift

The Age reported on 2023-09-26 that Travis Kelce's first public connection to Taylor Swift was established in 2016 during an AfterBuzz TV segment. During this appearance, Kelce played "Kiss, Marry, Kill" with the options being Swift, Katy Perry, and Ariana Grande. He stated he would "kill Grande, marry Perry and kiss Swift." This 2016 comment resurfaced in 2023 as fans analyzed the timing of his later public interactions with Swift, including his attempts to meet her during her Eras Tour and their reported meetings in New York City. The revelation became significant context when Swift attended one of his NFL games in September 2023, showing visible support for Kelce and his team.
