# SBF's Direct Examination Focuses on Definitions

The Verge reported on 2023-10-28 that Sam Bankman-Fried's direct examination testimony consumed much of the morning with vocabulary explanations. The reporter noted that explaining technical terms like "Amazon Web Services" and "database" was "a bit too detail-oriented." Bankman-Fried also attempted to define "market manipulation" before the judge instructed the jury that he was the final authority on legal definitions. The reporter observed that "we spent a lot of the morning explaining vocabulary" and that the detailed technical definitions seemed excessive for jury comprehension.
