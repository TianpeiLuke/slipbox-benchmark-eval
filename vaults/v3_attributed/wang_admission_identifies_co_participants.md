# Wang admits to FTX financial crimes with Bankman-Fried, identifies him in court

CNBC reported on 2023-10-06 that Gary Wang admitted to committing financial crimes while working at FTX, specifically wire and commodities fraud carried out "with the help of Bankman-Fried, FTX ex-engineering head Nishad Singh and Caroline Ellison." Under questioning by Assistant U.S. Attorney Nicolas Roos on Thursday, Wang confirmed his crimes were committed with these individuals, then stood and identified Bankman-Fried in the courtroom as one of those people. Wang's sworn guilty plea is part of his cooperation agreement with prosecutors, and he faces up to 50 years in prison at sentencing.
