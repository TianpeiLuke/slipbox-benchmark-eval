# Google faces antitrust suit alleging AI harms to publishers and evidence spoliation

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-15 that a class action lawsuit filed by Arkansas publisher Helena World Chronicle accuses Google of anticompetitive behavior violating U.S. antitrust laws. The suit claims Google "siphons off" publishers' content, readers, and ad revenue through technologies like Search Generative Experience (SGE) and Bard AI chatbot. It alleges that Google's AI integration could reduce publisher website traffic by up to 75%, as users receive answers without clicking through. The complaint also raises concerns about Google's "improper spoliation of evidence" through destruction of chat messages, referencing a similar issue sustained in Epic Games' successful app-store antitrust case against Google. The lawsuit seeks damages and an injunction requiring publisher consent for AI training data usage.
