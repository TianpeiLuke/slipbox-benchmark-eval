# Michael Lewis's "Going Infinite" Book on Sam Bankman-Fried

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-02 that Michael Lewis's book "Going Infinite" chronicles the rise and fall of Sam Bankman-Fried, founder of collapsed cryptocurrency exchange FTX. The book, published to coincide with SBF's criminal trial, reveals how Lewis was initially introduced to SBF in 2021 by a friend seeking to evaluate his character for investment purposes. Lewis describes being captivated by SBF's apparent brilliance and rapid ascent from having nothing to commanding $22.5 billion. The book details SBF's eccentric behaviors, including playing video games during TV interviews, and his attempts to influence politics, such as allegedly offering $5 billion to Donald Trump to not run for president again. Lewis conducted over 100 interviews with SBF over two years while writing the book.
