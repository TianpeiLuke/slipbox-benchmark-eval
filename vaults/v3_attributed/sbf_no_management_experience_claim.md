# Ayelet Noff's Assessment of SBF's Media Performance

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-02 that Ayelet Noff, CEO and founder of PR firm Sliced Brand, attributed Sam Bankman-Fried's poor television performance to his lack of experience with large-scale operations. "He was in over his head," she stated, observing his behavior during the 60 Minutes interview. Her assessment focused on his apparent incompetence rather than legal responsibility for FTX's collapse.
