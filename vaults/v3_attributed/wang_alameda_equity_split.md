# Alameda Research Ownership Testimony

CNBC reported on 2023-10-06 that Gary Wang testified in court that Alameda Research ownership was split 10 percent to himself and 90 percent to his boss Sam Bankman-Fried. Wang stated, "ownership of Alameda Research was split 10 percent to myself and the remaining 90 percent to my boss Sam Bankman-Fried."
