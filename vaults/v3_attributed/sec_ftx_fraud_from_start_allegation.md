# SEC's Allegation of FTX Fraud From Inception

The Verge reported on 2023-09-28 that the Securities and Exchange Commission alleges FTX was a fraud "from the start," with a "multi-billion-dollar deficiency caused by his own misappropriation of customer funds." The SEC characterizes the deficiency as being "caused by Bankman-Fried's own misappropriation of customer funds" for the benefit of Alameda Research, effectively treating FTX as a fraudulent enterprise from its founding in 2019.
