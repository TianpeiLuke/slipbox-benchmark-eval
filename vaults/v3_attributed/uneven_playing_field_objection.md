# Google's Dominance Attributed to Structural Advantages Rather Than Merit Alone

The Age reported on 2023-10-22 that Google's competitors operate from an uneven playing field. Kent Walker argued that users prefer Google because it's superior and switching is easy, but this ignores how Google's scale, longevity, ubiquity, and accumulated user data create insurmountable advantages. The article objects that "these arguments do ignore the fact that Google and its competitors are not on an even playing field," leaving unanswered whether user preference stems from Google's inherent quality or from structural barriers that prevent fair competition.
