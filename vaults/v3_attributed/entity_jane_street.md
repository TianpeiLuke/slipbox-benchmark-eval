# Jane Street as Sam Bankman-Fried's Pre-FTX Employer

Fortune reported on 2023-10-04 that Jane Street was the high-frequency trading firm where Sam Bankman-Fried began his finance career after graduating from MIT. "Jane Street was the high-frequency trading firm where Sam Bankman-Fried began his finance career after graduating from MIT, and from which he left before recruiting friends into Alameda and FTX."
