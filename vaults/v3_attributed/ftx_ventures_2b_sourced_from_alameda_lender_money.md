# Ellison's Testimony on FTX Ventures Funding Source

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-06 that Ellison testified the $2 billion FTX Ventures fund was funded with money that had previously been allocated to Alameda from third-party lenders. This testimony came during the second week of the SBF trial, where Ellison detailed how customer funds were redirected to repay debts and finance ventures.
