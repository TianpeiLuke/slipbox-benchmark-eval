# Bankman-Fried's Risk-Neutral Gambling Definition

The Verge reported on 2023-10-10 that Sam Bankman-Fried defined being "risk-neutral" as willingness to take extreme gambles, exemplified by a coin-flip scenario where tails would "destroy the world" and heads would make it "twice as good," yet he would still choose to flip. This definition came during Caroline Ellison's testimony in his criminal fraud trial, where she described Alameda Research's financial troubles and Bankman-Fried's directive to commit crimes. "Bankman-Fried called this being 'risk-neutral,' which seems like a fancy way of saying he was a gambling addict," according to Ellison's account in court.
