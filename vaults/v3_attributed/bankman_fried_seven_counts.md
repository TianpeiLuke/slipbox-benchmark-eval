# Bankman-Fried faces seven criminal counts in October 2023 trial

The Verge reported on 2023-10-26 that "Bankman-Fried is charged with seven counts: two counts of wire fraud and five conspiracy charges." At the close of the prosecution's case in October 2023, Sam Bankman-Fried faced these seven criminal counts related to the FTX collapse, with the prosecution presenting evidence that he orchestrated fraud at both FTX and Alameda Research.
