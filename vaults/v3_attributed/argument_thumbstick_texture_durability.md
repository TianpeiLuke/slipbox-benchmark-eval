# Steam Deck OLED Thumbstick Texture Improvement

Engadget reported on 2023-11-09 that the Steam Deck OLED's thumbsticks have "an improved texture that shouldn't turn gray after a few months' use." This addresses a degradation issue observed with the original Steam Deck's stick texture, which reviewers could only assess over time rather than verify immediately. The 2023 review noted this improvement based on visual inspection and short-term handling, stating the sticks felt "perfectly serviceable" with their new texture design.
