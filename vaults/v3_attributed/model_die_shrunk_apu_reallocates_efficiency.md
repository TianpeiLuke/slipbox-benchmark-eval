# Steam Deck OLED's Die-Shrunk APU Enables Better Battery and Thermals

Engadget reported on 2023-11-09 that the Steam Deck OLED features "a die-shrunk version of the custom AMD APU found in the original Steam Deck — same CPU and GPU, just more efficient." Instead of using the efficiency gains for performance improvements, Valve opted to "add a bigger battery and larger fan to its existing chassis." This approach maintains the same performance targets for developers while improving battery life by 30-50 percent and reducing device weight by 30 grams.
