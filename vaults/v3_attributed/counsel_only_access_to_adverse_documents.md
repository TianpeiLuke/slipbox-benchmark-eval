# Sweeney's First Encounter with Google's Internal Documents During Trial

The Verge reported on 2023-12-12 that Epic CEO Tim Sweeney, as a party executive rather than a lawyer, could not review Google's internal documents before trial and encountered key evidence simultaneously with the jury. During the Android antitrust trial, Sweeney stated that his understanding of Google's anti-competitive practices—"you're leaking our conversations to reporters... you're paying other developers off... they were going around and paying carriers and OEMs secretly not to carry competing stores"—was confirmed by the internal documents revealed during proceedings, which he described as "really disconcerting to see the extent of bad faith efforts."
