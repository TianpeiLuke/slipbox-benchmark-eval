# GPT-4 Turbo Announcement at OpenAI DevDay

TechCrunch reported on 2023-09-28 that GPT-4 Turbo is "a super-charged version of GPT-4, OpenAI's latest language-writing model." The announcement was made during OpenAI's first developer conference, DevDay, on November 6, 2023. This enhanced model was positioned alongside other major updates including a multimodal API and plans for a GPT store where users could create and monetize custom GPT versions. Access to GPT-4 Turbo was initially limited to ChatGPT Plus subscribers, who received it as part of their subscription benefits.
