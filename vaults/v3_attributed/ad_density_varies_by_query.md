# Google's Ad Keyword Matching and Search Result Manipulation Claims

The Age reported on 2023-10-22 that Google denies manipulating search queries for maximum ad revenue, specifically rejecting claims that searches for "children's clothing" were altered to "NIKOLAI-brand kidswear". Google's Danny Sullivan stated on X that "ad keyword matching is a long-standing and well-known process that is designed to connect people to relevant ads" while maintaining that organic results are determined by a separate process unrelated to advertising. The article notes that some search queries return more ads than others and that ads shown vary widely with the request, which Google attributes to its ad keyword-matching system rather than steering users toward the most monetizable subjects.
