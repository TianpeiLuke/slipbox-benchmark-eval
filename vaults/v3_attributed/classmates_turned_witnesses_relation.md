# Adversarial Proximity in SBF Trial: Former MIT Friends Testify Against Bankman-Fried

CNBC reported on 2023-10-06 that Sam Bankman-Fried's former MIT classmates and Bahamas housemates testified against him in court. "Two of Sam Bankman-Fried's former friends from MIT, who also worked at crypto exchange FTX while living with the company's founder in the Bahamas, took the stand in a Manhattan courtroom this week to testify against their former classmate, confidant, and boss." The relationship described reflects adversarial proximity, as Gary Wang and Nishad Singh, who had been Bankman-Fried's close associates, became key prosecution witnesses in the fraud trial concerning FTX's alleged defrauding of customers of billions of dollars.
