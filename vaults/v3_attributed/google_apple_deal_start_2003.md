# Google-Apple Default Search Agreement Duration

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-31 that the Google-Apple default search agreement had been in place since 2003. "Google has had its deal with Apple in place since 2003," according to the source. This gave the agreement roughly two decades of duration by the time of the 2023 trial, spanning approximately 20 years from its establishment to the court proceedings.
