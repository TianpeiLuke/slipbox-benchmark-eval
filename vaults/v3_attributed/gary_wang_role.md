# Gary Wang's testimony against Bankman-Fried

The Verge reported on 2023-10-10 that Gary Wang, the chief technology officer of FTX and co-owner of both FTX and Alameda, testified against Sam Bankman-Fried during the criminal fraud trial. Wang claimed that Alameda received special privileges including a credit line and ability to avoid liquidation with negative balances. During cross-examination by Bankman-Fried's defense attorney Christian Everdell, Wang's credibility was challenged regarding technical aspects of FTX's operations and his prior statements to investigators. The article notes that "Wang didn't exactly help himself out, either" when his courtroom testimony contradicted earlier interviews with the government about market making practices.
