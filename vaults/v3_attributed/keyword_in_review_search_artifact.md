# Silva's SEO Light Bulb Moment

The Verge reported on 2023-10-26 that Felix Silva, owner of Barber Shop Near Me in Coral Springs, Florida, had a pivotal realization about Google's influence on local business discovery. While searching for "vegan" restaurants, Silva noticed that Google returned results for a non-vegan restaurant simply because a customer review contained the highlighted keyword. "It was a light bulb moment... like, 'Okay, this is the path I need to take. I really need to dial in and focus on the online presence, specifically Google, because that is going to be the foundation of the business,'" Silva explained. This insight drove his decision to optimize his barbershop's digital presence around strategic keyword placement rather than traditional branding approaches.
