# Bankman-Fried's Timeline of Learning About fiat@ftx Account

The Verge reported on 2023-10-31 that Sam Bankman-Fried testified he learned of the fiat@ftx account's existence in June 2022 while senior staff were fixing the software bug, but did not learn what the account was for until October 2022. His employees told him "they were busy and I should stop asking questions because it was distracting" when he inquired about the account.
