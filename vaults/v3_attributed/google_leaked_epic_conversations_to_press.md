# Google Leaked Private Conversations to Generate Negative Press Coverage

The Verge reported on 2023-12-12 that trial evidence recounted by Sweeney showed Google leaking private Epic–Google conversations to reporters to generate negative press coverage of Epic. "You're leaking our conversations to reporters to get negative stories written about us," Sweeney stated, describing practices that surprised even him despite his suspicions since 2018.
