# Objection to Wembanyama's ROTY candidacy based on limited availability

Sporting News reported on 2023-10-04 that Victor Wembanyama faces a significant obstacle to winning Rookie of the Year despite being the early favorite. "Even with a young roster and potential load management, Wembanyama's undeniable impact is evident" - however, this same load management and young Spurs roster could limit his available games. The article acknowledges that his playing time may be restricted, which directly contradicts the season-long accumulation argument that typically supports ROTY winners. This makes his statistical case weaker compared to competitors like Scoot Henderson and Chet Holmgren who may have more consistent playing opportunities on rebuilding teams.
