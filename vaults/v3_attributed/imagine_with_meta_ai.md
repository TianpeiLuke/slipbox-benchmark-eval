# Imagine with Meta AI image generation service

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-09 that Meta launched Imagine with Meta AI, a stand-alone web generative-AI experience in the week of December 8, 2023. The service "allows users to create images by describing them in natural language" using Meta's existing Emu image-generation model. Imagine with Meta AI creates high-resolution images from text prompts and operates "similar to OpenAI's DALL-E, Midjourney and Stable Diffusion."
