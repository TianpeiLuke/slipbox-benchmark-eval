# Locked-in Betting Odds Are Immutable After Wager Placement

Sporting News reported on 2023-11-06 that when you bet, you lock in the odds or line as currently posted. "If they change, for better or worse, after you've placed your bet, those changes do not apply to your ticket." This means bettors cannot benefit from improved odds or lines that occur after their wager is placed.
