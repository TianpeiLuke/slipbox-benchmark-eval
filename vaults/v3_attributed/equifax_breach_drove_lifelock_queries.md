# LifeLock's High-Value Search Ranking Hypothesis

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-13 that LifeLock's appearance among Google's most lucrative search queries resulted from the 2017 Equifax breach combined with LifeLock's marketing efforts. The article states that "the big Equifax data breach of 2017 was a hot topic in September 2017 and LifeLock was making a big push to win business with people who wanted to buy identity theft protection." This hypothesis would be falsified if LifeLock ranked comparably high in weeks preceding that breach, suggesting the ranking was driven by contemporary events rather than sustained search interest.
