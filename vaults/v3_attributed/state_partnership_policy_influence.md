# Meta's Content Moderation Bias Hypothesis

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-19 that advocacy groups have raised concerns that Meta's partnerships with government agencies, such as the Israeli Cyber Unit, politically influence the platform's policy decisions. The hypothesis that these partnerships shift content-policy decisions against Palestinian expression would be falsified if removal and restriction rates for comparable Palestine-related content showed no association with escalation in those agency channels. This claim suggests that Meta's moderation practices may disproportionately affect Palestinian users, particularly during periods of heightened conflict when agency cooperation intensifies.
