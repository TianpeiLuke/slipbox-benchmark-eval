# NAB forecasts RBA cash rate increase to 4.35% in November 2023

The Sydney Morning Herald reported on 2023-10-01 that "The National Australia Bank has forecast another rate hike to 4.35 per cent in November." This forecast creates a falsifiable hypothesis regarding the Reserve Bank of Australia's monetary policy decision. The RBA's actual decision in November 2023 will either validate or invalidate NAB's prediction, making this a testable economic forecast rather than mere speculation.
