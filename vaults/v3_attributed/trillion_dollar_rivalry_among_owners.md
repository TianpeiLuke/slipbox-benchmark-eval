# AI Governance Debate Driven by Trillion-Dollar Power Struggle

The Age reported on 2023-12-09 that the AI governance debate is fundamentally a competition for control among Silicon Valley's wealthiest leaders. Elon Musk, Larry Page, Mark Zuckerberg, Peter Thiel, Satya Nadella, and Sam Altman are engaged in a power struggle over artificial intelligence technology that could be worth trillions of dollars. "The people who say they are most worried about AI are among the most determined to create it and enjoy its riches," according to the publication. This contest involves not just ideological differences about AI's future impact, but represents a fight for ownership stakes in what may become the most valuable technology sector in history, with each leader seeking both financial gain and influence over how AI develops globally.
