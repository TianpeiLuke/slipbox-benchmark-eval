# Meta-commissioned report finds adverse human rights impact on Palestinian users

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-19 that during the 2021 Hamas-Israel escalation, a third-party report commissioned by Meta concluded that Meta's actions had an "adverse human rights impact" on Palestinian users' right to freedom of expression and political participation. The report highlighted how Meta's content moderation practices disproportionately affected Palestinian voices on its platforms during the conflict period.
