# SEO-Driven Business Naming Hypothesis Tested by Thai Food Near Me and Barber Shop Near Me

The Verge reported on 2023-10-26 that businesses named after exact Google search queries may gain measurable advantages in search rankings and customer volume. Teddy Jirapraphanan, co-owner of "Thai Food Near Me" in New York, chose the name because "Everywhere I go, I'm craving Thai food. I have to search 'Thai food near me' all the time." The restaurant leverages SEO optimization for digital platforms rather than traditional branding, with Jirapraphanan stating, "When you have a million restaurants close by, you will be in the bottom [of rankings] if it's a random name." Similarly, Felix Silva named his Florida barbershop "Barber Shop Near Me" after witnessing how Google presence affects business success. Both owners observed that customers searched for their exact business names, suggesting that query-matching names can improve discoverability compared to arbitrary naming conventions.
