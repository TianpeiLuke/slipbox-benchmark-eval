# Google's MADA and RSA Distinction in Antitrust Trial

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-13 that Google uses two different partner instruments to maintain search dominance. Jamie Rosenberg testified on November 8, 2023 that the Mobile App Distribution Agreement (MADA) requires Android manufacturers to pre-load 11 Google apps including Search and Chrome, while revenue share agreements (RSA) with carriers and OEMs specifically require Google Search and Chrome to be set as defaults. "They don't have to be the default choices" under MADA, but RSAs explicitly compel default settings in exchange for payments.
