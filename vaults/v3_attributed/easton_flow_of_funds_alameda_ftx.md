# Easton traces billions from FTX customers to real estate and charities

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-06 that University of Notre Dame professor Peter Easton, who assisted government prosecutions of Enron and WorldCom, testified during week three of the SBF trial about tracing billions of dollars between Alameda Research and FTX. Easton found that "much of the money came from customers," and that user deposits funded real estate purchases, investments, political causes and charities.
