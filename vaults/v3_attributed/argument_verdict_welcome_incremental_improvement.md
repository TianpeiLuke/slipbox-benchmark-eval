# Steam Deck OLED Review Summary

Engadget reported on 2023-11-09 that the Steam Deck OLED "isn't the Steam Deck 2 some are waiting for, it's just a welcome improvement on an already good device." The publication scored it 89/100, praising its "bright and smooth visuals," "improved battery life," "better thermals," and "longer-lasting" performance. The OLED screen features a 7.4-inch display with HDR support, 1,000 nits peak brightness, and 90Hz refresh rate. Battery life improved by 62% in testing, lasting 4 hours 23 minutes versus the original's 2 hours 42 minutes. Priced at $549 on Steam, the device retains the same form factor as its predecessor, maintaining its bulky size while feeling less top-heavy and weighing 30 grams less.
