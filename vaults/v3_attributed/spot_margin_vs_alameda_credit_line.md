# Spot Margin Program vs Alameda Credit Line Distinction

The Verge reported on 2023-10-10 that the spot margin program and Alameda's special credit line were not equivalent arrangements. While spot margin accounts could theoretically go negative in one specific coin, they could not avoid liquidation or maintain an overall negative balance. This distinction undermines Gary Wang's claim that Alameda had unique privileges other FTX accounts lacked. The defense attempted to confuse jurors by conflating these different mechanisms during cross-examination.
