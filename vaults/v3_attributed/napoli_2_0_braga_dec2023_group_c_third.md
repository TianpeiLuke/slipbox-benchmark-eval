# Napoli's victory eliminates Braga from Champions League contention

Sporting News reported on 2023-12-12 that Napoli's 2-0 win over Braga at the Diego Armando Maradona stadium "snuffed out their slim hopes of second place." The victory secured Napoli's position in the Champions League knockout stage while relegating Braga to the Europa League as the group's consolation prize, despite Braga's earlier hopes for a higher finish in Group C.
