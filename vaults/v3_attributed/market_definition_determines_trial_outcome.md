# Epic v. Google Trial Hinges on Market Definition

The Verge reported on 2023-11-05 that Epic's antitrust trial against Google centers on how the court defines the relevant market. Epic argues Google monopolizes "Android app distribution" and "Android in-app payment processing," forcing developers to pay high fees. However, Google contends the broader market includes all phones and app stores, not just Android. "If the court decides the relevant market is phones and app stores, not specifically Android ones, then Google's on the road to victory. If it accepts that Android apps are their own market, Epic will be in better shape." The outcome depends entirely on this market definition choice.
