# Plaintiffs' Counsel Frames Antitrust Suit Against Google Over AI Harms to Publishers

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-15 that plaintiffs' counsel Hausfeld LLP framed a class action antitrust lawsuit against Google as necessary to "restore competition in digital news and reference publishing and to set guardrails for the free marketplace of ideas in the AI era." The lawsuit invokes both the Sherman Act and the Clayton Act for class-wide relief, arguing that Google's AI technologies like Search Generative Experience and Bard are harming publishers' bottom lines by siphoning content, readers, and ad revenue through anticompetitive means.
