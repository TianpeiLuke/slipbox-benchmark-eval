# Google Play Commission Structure as of November 2023

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-06 that Google charges app developers a commission of 15% to 30% on sales processed through Google Play's billing system. Google requires apps to use its first-party billing system, though it offers a reduced commission rate through its User Choice Billing pilot program for developers who use third-party payment processing solutions.
