# Sam Bankman-Fried Faces Seven Federal Criminal Counts in FTX Fraud Trial

The Verge reported on 2023-09-28 that Sam Bankman-Fried, founder of FTX and Alameda Research, is facing seven federal criminal counts: "two counts of wire fraud, and five counts of conspiracy charges." The charges stem from allegations that FTX was a fraud "from the start," with Bankman-Fried misappropriating customer funds for personal investments and political contributions. Prosecutors allege he directed special code features that gave Alameda unlimited access to FTX funds while publicly denying such privileged access existed.
