# Counter-argument to monolithic LLM limitations prediction

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-19 that "The limits of monolithic LLMs become clearer" in 2024, suggesting that "something gives" as these models hit walls despite increased compute. However, this prediction contradicts the article's own acknowledgment that "LLMs have indeed proved more capable than anyone expected, and have grown correspondingly more so as more compute is added." The counter-argument is that LLMs consistently exceeded expectations throughout 2023 and continued scaling with compute additions, indicating that the premise of inherent limitations becoming apparent in 2024 may be premature. Rather than hitting a wall, LLMs might continue demonstrating emergent capabilities that surpass current projections, similar to how they exceeded 2022 expectations.
