# Ripple Effect of SBF's Collapse Attributed to Broader Crypto Market Losses

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-02 that beyond direct victims of Alameda Research and FTX, people without positions in either entity still blame Sam Bankman-Fried for wider cryptocurrency losses because "his collapse set off a ripple effect across the market."
