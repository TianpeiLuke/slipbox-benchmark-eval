# Eddy Cue's 2023-09-26 Court Testimony on Apple-Google Search Deal

The Verge reported on 2023-09-26 that Apple executive Eddy Cue testified in US v. Google before Judge Amit Mehta, defending the Information Services Agreement (ISA) that makes Google the default search engine on Apple devices. Cue stated "there wasn't a valid alternative" to Google in 2016 when he renegotiated the revenue-sharing deal, emphasizing that both companies benefited economically. He argued Apple prioritizes user privacy while maintaining that Google offers the best search product. Cue contended that offering search engine choice during iPhone setup would create poor user experience, saying "we pick the best one and let users easily change it." The testimony addressed the tension between Apple's privacy stance and its lucrative partnership with Google.
