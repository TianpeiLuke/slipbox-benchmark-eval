# Peter Thiel's Fascination with the Singularity Concept

The Age reported on 2023-12-09 that Peter Thiel had developed a fascination with the singularity, "a trope of science fiction that describes the moment when intelligent technology can no longer be controlled by humanity." This fascination influenced Thiel's approach to AI development and investment decisions in Silicon Valley's competitive landscape.
