# Apple-Google ISA Revenue-Share Figures Kept Secret During 2023 Court Testimony

The Verge reported on 2023-09-26 that specific revenue-share percentages from Google's default search engine payments to Apple were discussed only in closed court sessions during Apple's testimony, keeping the negotiated financial terms undisclosed to the public record. "Discussion of specific numbers was reserved for closed court sessions," according to the court proceedings. The article notes that while Apple's Eddy Cue testified about wanting a higher percentage and Google's Sundar Pichai preferring the status quo, they "eventually compromised on some other number we weren't told in court."
