# Caroline Ellison's testimony against Sam Bankman-Fried

The Verge reported on 2023-10-10 that Caroline Ellison, former head of Alameda Research and ex-girlfriend of Sam Bankman-Fried, testified in his criminal fraud trial while wearing a dusty rose dress and gray blazer. Ellison had previously pleaded guilty to fraud, conspiracy to commit fraud, and money laundering charges related to the FTX collapse. During her testimony, she stated that Bankman-Fried "directed me to commit these crimes" and acknowledged that they "ultimately took about $14 billion, some of which we were not able to pay back." She described Alameda as being in financial trouble shortly after her 2018 arrival, funded initially with loans from acquaintances that were recalled weeks later.
