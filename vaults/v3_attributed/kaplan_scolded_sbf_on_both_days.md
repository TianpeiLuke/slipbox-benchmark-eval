# Judge Kaplan scolds Bankman-Fried for not answering questions on two consecutive days

The Verge reported on 2023-10-28 that Judge Lewis Kaplan repeatedly scolded Sam Bankman-Fried for not answering questions during both yesterday's evidentiary hearing and today's direct examination. During prosecutor Danielle Sassoon's cross-examination yesterday and his counsel Mark Cohen's direct examination today, Bankman-Fried was reprimanded for failing to provide answers. "The jury is watching all of this intently," according to the reporter covering the trial.
