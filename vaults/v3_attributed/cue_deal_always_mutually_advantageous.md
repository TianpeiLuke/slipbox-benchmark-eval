# Cue's testimony on walking away from Google deal

The Verge reported on 2023-09-26 that during the US v. Google antitrust trial, Apple executive Eddy Cue testified he never considered walking away from the Google search deal despite disagreements over revenue share. When asked by DOJ lawyer Meagan Bellshaw whether Apple would have abandoned the deal, Cue stated: "I always felt like it was in Google's best interest, and our best interest, to get a deal done." Cue maintained that Google was the best search option and that there wasn't a valid alternative.
