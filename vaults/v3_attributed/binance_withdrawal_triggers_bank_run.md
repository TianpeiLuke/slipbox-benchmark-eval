# Binance's Redemption Triggered FTX Bank Run

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-01 that Binance's withdrawal of its remaining "$2.1 billion equivalent of cash in BUSD and FTT" around the time Alameda's balance sheet was exposed "essentially triggered a bank run on FTX." Binance had held an equity position in FTX from 2019 to 2021. The collapse occurred after CoinDesk published Alameda's balance sheet showing $5.82 billion in FTT tokens, which was 193% higher than the total FTT market cap of about $3 billion, indicating more tokens existed on the balance sheet than actually existed in the world.
