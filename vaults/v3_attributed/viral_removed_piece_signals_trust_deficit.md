# Social Media Response to Retracted Wired Article Reveals Public Suspicion of Google

The Age reported on 2023-10-22 that the extent to which Wired's retracted opinion article was shared on social media and boosted by other outlets demonstrates public willingness to accept allegations of Google's foul play. "The degree to which it was shared on social media and boosted in write-ups at other outlets shows just how willing people are to accept foul play on Google's part." This reaction reflects existing suspicions about Google's privacy invasion and inappropriate data handling practices, particularly regarding search query manipulation for ad revenue maximization.
