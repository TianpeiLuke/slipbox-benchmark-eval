# Google's Tied Product Strategy Found Illegal

The Verge reported on 2023-12-12 that a federal jury decided Google illegally tied its Google Play billing system to its app store, finding that Google has an illegal monopoly over Android app distribution and in-app payment systems. The jury's verdict supports Epic Games' claim that "Google’s Google Play billing system" was unlawfully connected to "its app store," establishing a structural relationship where the store serves as a monopolized product enabling control over the tied billing product. This self-reinforcing relation means that control of distribution converts into control of payment.
