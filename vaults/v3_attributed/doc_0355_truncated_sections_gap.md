# Epic v. Google trial coverage cut short

The Verge reported on 2023-11-05 that Epic Games' antitrust lawsuit against Google would go to trial on November 6th after 1,180 days of delays. However, an editor's note from 2023-12-15 stated "It's basically over now" and directed readers to three linked follow-ups, without providing the promised material on trial revelations or "juicy dirt" about either company. The article's planned coverage of Google's alleged monopoly on Android app distribution and payment processing was ultimately cut short.
