# Apple's 2024 AI Strategy Hypothesis

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-19 that Apple's 2024 AI offering will focus on "practical uses of the user's own data across Apple's ecosystems, with an elegant handling of dangerous prompts, multimodal understanding mainly for images, and no media generation." This approach would differentiate Apple by emphasizing privacy and integration rather than generative capabilities. The prediction specifically states that Apple's AI will not include "generated video and generated music" features that other companies might pursue. If Apple ships generative media tools or a cloud-data-first assistant instead, this hypothesis would be falsified. Apple's typical strategy involves entering markets late but with refined, user-focused solutions that address privacy concerns and technical limitations observed in competitors' offerings.
