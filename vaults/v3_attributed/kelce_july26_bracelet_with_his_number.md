# Kelce's Friendship Bracelet Number Ambiguity

CBSSports.com reported on 2023-10-12 that during a July 26, 2023 New Heights podcast episode, Travis Kelce revealed he made a friendship bracelet bearing "my number" to give to Taylor Swift. When his brother Jason asked whether he meant his jersey number or phone number, Kelce replied "you know which one."
