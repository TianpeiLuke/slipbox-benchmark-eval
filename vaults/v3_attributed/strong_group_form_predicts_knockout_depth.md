# Champions League Dominance Hypothesis

Sporting News reported on 2023-12-12 that "The likes of Bayern Munich, Real Madrid and Barcelona have also enjoyed strong group-stage campaigns and will surely be in the mix towards the sharp end of the competition." This observation forms a testable hypothesis: clubs demonstrating dominant 2023/24 group-stage performances will advance deep into the knockout rounds. The hypothesis can be validated by tracking whether these top performers reach the semifinals or finals, or falsified if any of them exit early in the Round of 16 or quarter-finals, despite their strong group showing.
