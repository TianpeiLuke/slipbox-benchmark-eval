# Helena World Chronicle sues Google for antitrust violations over AI content use

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-15 that Arkansas-based publisher Helena World Chronicle filed a class action lawsuit in the U.S. District Court for D.C. against Google and Alphabet, alleging anticompetitive conduct violating the Sherman Act and other antitrust laws. The complaint claims Google "siphons off" publishers' content, readers, and ad revenue through technologies like Search Generative Experience (SGE), Bard AI chatbot, and Knowledge Graph. Helena World Chronicle argues these practices constitute content misappropriation that harms publishers' bottom lines, with AI potentially reducing publisher website traffic by 20-40% as Google keeps users within its "walled garden" instead of directing them to original sources.
