# Megan Gray's Allegation About Google Search Manipulation

The Age reported on 2023-10-22 that Megan Gray, a former vice-president at DuckDuckGo, alleged Google manipulates search queries to maximize ad revenue. "Grey herself is a former vice-president at DuckDuckGo, a privacy-focused company founded explicitly to counter giants such as Google. She admits that she may have misinterpreted the evidence, but maintains that Google manipulates Search to maximise ad revenue." Despite conceding potential misinterpretation, Gray continued asserting that Google's search manipulation serves advertising purposes, according to the publication.
