# FTX Customers' Irreversible Losses Beyond Financial Restitution

The Verge reported on 2023-10-26 that FTX customers suffered irreversible damages when the exchange collapsed due to Sam Bankman-Fried's misappropriation of funds through Alameda Research. "Even if they are made whole by the bankruptcy proceedings, that's still money that they should have had access to at any time they wanted it," meaning customers lost opportunities for essential needs like housing purchases, medical procedures, and life savings preservation. The article emphasizes that while legal proceedings may provide financial compensation, the immediate access to funds that customers lost cannot be restored through bankruptcy restitution processes.
