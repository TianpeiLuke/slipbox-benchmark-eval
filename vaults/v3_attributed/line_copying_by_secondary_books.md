# Sports Betting Apps Copying Lines

Sporting News reported on 2023-11-06 that "Other sports betting apps simply copy those lines" set by major sportsbooks employing oddsmakers and oddsmaking services. These services use computer models considering analytics, statistics, injury news, and matchup data to develop betting lines. The copied lines remain subject to change based on public betting patterns and new information, with sportsbooks adjusting to balance bets on both sides and secure built-in profits.
