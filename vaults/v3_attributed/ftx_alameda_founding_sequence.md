# Sam Bankman-Fried's Biographical Timeline and FTX Founding

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-01 that Sam Bankman-Fried "co-founded crypto-trading firm Alameda Research in 2017" and then "co-founded FTX in 2019, which became the third-largest crypto exchange with a peak valuation of $32 billion." The article states that "within a few years the once third-largest crypto exchange's valuation hit $32 billion at its peak" after its founding in 2019. Bankman-Fried was 31 years old at the time of his trial.
