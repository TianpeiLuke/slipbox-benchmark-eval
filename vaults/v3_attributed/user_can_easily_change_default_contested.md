# DuckDuckGo CEO disputes Apple's claim about ease of changing default search engine

The Verge reported on 2023-09-26 that Apple executive Eddy Cue testified Google is the default search engine on Apple devices because it's the best product, claiming users can "easily" change it. However, this assertion directly contradicts testimony from DuckDuckGo's CEO in the prior week, who stated that switching away from Google's default position requires "too many steps." The dispute over how easily users can change their default search engine represents a key point of contention in the US v. Google antitrust trial, challenging Apple's defense that users have ready alternatives to Google's search service.
