# Google argues iPhone substitution constrains Android app store monopoly claims

The Verge reported on 2023-11-05 that Google will argue Epic's market definition is too narrow, contending that "the real competition is from Apple, because people can choose to buy an iPhone instead." This substitution effect makes it difficult to establish a monopoly over app stores in general, rather than just Android app stores specifically.
