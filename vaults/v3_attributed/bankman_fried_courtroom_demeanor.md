# Bankman-Fried's courtroom demeanor during Wang's testimony

CNBC reported on 2023-10-06 that during Gary Wang's testimony against him in the FTX fraud trial, Sam Bankman-Fried "remained mostly quiet in court intently listening to witnesses and at times writing notes to his attorneys." However, as Wang testified, Bankman-Fried appeared visibly distressed, shifting his gaze between his former friend and the ground, and at one point burying his head in his hands. The report noted Wang identified Bankman-Fried in the courtroom after testifying about their joint criminal activities at FTX.
