# Google presents Epic's internal messages to frame it as policy-violating bully

The Verge reported on 2023-11-07 that Google presented out-of-context internal Epic messages including "How do we not look like the bad guys?" and "everything we're attempting is technically a violation of Google's policy, right?" during the Epic v. Google trial. Google used these selectively chosen communications to portray Epic as knowingly violating policies while positioning itself as the enforcer of fair practices, according to the publication's trial coverage.
