# Nishad Singh's Guilty Plea in FTX Fraud Case

The Verge reported on 2023-10-26 that Nishad Singh, a longtime family friend of Sam Bankman-Fried, "coped to what sounded an awful lot like classic embezzlement" by acknowledging he backdated transactions on FTX to create doctored balance sheets for investors. Singh pleaded guilty to six charges including wire fraud, money laundering, and conspiracy counts around political donations. His admission came during the FTX trial that revealed how Bankman-Fried's actions destroyed the lives of those closest to him, with Singh being among several inner-circle figures who faced legal consequences.
