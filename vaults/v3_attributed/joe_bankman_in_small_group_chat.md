# Joe Bankman in FTX Inner Circle Chat

The Verge reported on 2023-10-26 that Joe Bankman, Sam Bankman-Fried's father, "is among the people included in the 'small group chat,' the Signal group where the FTX inner circle strategized during the exchange's last days." The article identifies him as part of the inner circle discussing FTX's collapse strategy.
