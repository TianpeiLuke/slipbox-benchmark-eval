# Google's Gemini AI Model Family Launch and Initial Reception

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-07 that Google unveiled Gemini, its new flagship generative AI model family designed to power various Google products and services including Bard, the company's ChatGPT competitor. Google claimed Gemini's architecture and capabilities meet or exceed leading gen AI models like OpenAI's GPT-4. However, early user experiences with Gemini Pro, a "lite" version rolled out to Bard, revealed significant issues including factual inaccuracies about 2023 Oscar winners, poor multilingual performance, outdated news summaries, and struggles with basic coding functions. Users reported Gemini Pro failed to correctly answer simple questions, provided incorrect translations, avoided controversial topics, and couldn't reliably generate bug-free code for games like Snake. Security researchers also found ways to bypass safety filters.
