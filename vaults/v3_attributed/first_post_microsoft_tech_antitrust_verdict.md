# Sweeney argues verdict shows Sherman Act still viable against tech monopolies

The Verge reported on 2023-12-12 that Tim Sweeney viewed the Epic-Google antitrust verdict as proof that the Sherman Act remains enforceable against modern technology monopolies. After four years of litigation, Sweeney stated: "it's a great day for all developers to see that the Sherman Antitrust Act works in the new era of tech monopolies." He emphasized that this represents the first major US antitrust verdict against a technology company since US v. Microsoft in the 1990s that actually means change for the industry, treating the decades-long gap as evidence that the Sherman Act had stopped being enforced against new-era monopolies. The federal jury decided Google has an illegal monopoly over Android app distribution and in-app payment systems.
