# Yedidia's Testimony About $8 Billion Hole

The Verge reported on 2023-10-31 that Adam Yedidia — Bankman-Fried's college friend, Bahamas roommate, and FTX employee — testified that he raised the "$8 billion hole" with Bankman-Fried on a padel tennis court in their luxury complex in June or July 2022. During his testimony, Bankman-Fried appeared to deny that this conversation had ever taken place.
