# Google's Data Collection Advantage in Search Competition

The Age reported on 2023-10-22 that Google collects extensive user data beyond searches, including voice input to smart speakers and every clicked result and ad. This data collection gives Google a personalisation advantage over competitors like DuckDuckGo and Bing, even though DuckDuckGo performed worse in search tests and is harder to read. The article notes that Google's search product wins because of its effectiveness, not just its default status.
