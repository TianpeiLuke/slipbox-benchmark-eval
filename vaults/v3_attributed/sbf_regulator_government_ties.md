# Sam Bankman-Fried's Political Connections

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-01 that Sam Bankman-Fried "had close ties to U.S. regulators and government officials, many of whom he donated to." These political and regulatory connections were part of the broader narrative around the FTX founder's influence and eventual downfall.
