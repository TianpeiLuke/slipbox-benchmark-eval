# Swift's NFL Commentary Crossover

The Age reported on 2023-09-26 that Swift lyrics began appearing in NFL game commentary, creating a cultural crossover between football and pop music. CBS announcer Ian Eagle referenced Swift's "Blank Space" during a September 17 game, saying "Kelce finds a blank space for the score." This marked the beginning of what the article describes as football broadcasts "importing her catalogue for a sports audience" while simultaneously making "Swifties... football experts." The phenomenon reached its peak when Swift physically attended an NFL game at Arrowhead Stadium, supporting Travis Kelce and prompting fans to become "observant" of potential romantic connections between the pop star and the football player.
