# Google Play's First-Party Billing Requirement and Commission Structure

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-06 that Google requires apps to use its own first-party billing system for digital content sales. "Today, Google requires apps to use its own first-party billing system, and charges app developers a 15% to 30% commission on the sales it processes." This billing requirement is distinct from Google's commission structure, though related. The requirement mandates that apps selling digital content must use Google's billing system rather than third-party alternatives, while the commission represents Google's revenue share from those transactions.
