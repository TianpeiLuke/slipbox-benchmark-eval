# Google counters Epic's payment-processing monopoly claim with User Choice Billing adoption evidence

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-06 that Google disputes Epic Games' antitrust claim regarding payment-processing market foreclosure by highlighting that Epic declined to use Google's User Choice Billing program. Google's counterargument emphasizes that alternative billing methods are available rather than foreclosed, citing Spotify, Bumble, and Match as adopters of the User Choice Billing system. The program, introduced in November 2022 and still in pilot testing, "reduces the standard commission by 4% for companies who choose to use their own payment processing solution." Google contends that Epic's refusal to participate in this voluntary program undermines Epic's claim that developers lack viable alternatives to Google's billing system.
