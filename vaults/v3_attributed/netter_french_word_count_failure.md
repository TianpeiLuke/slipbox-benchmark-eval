# Benjamin Netter's Empirical Observation of Gemini Pro's French Word Generation Failure

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-07 that Benjamin Netter posted on X on 2023-12-06 demonstrating Gemini Pro's failure to generate a six-letter French word. Netter tweeted: "FYI, Google Gemini is complete trash." When testing the same prompt ("Can you give me a 6-letters word in French?"), Gemini Pro responded with a seven-letter word instead of the requested five-letter word, supporting reports about Gemini's poor multilingual performance.
