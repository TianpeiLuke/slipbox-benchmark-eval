# Cooperation Agreement Definition and Wang's Testimony

CNBC reported on 2023-10-06 that a cooperation agreement, as defined in the SBF trial, is a written deal with the U.S. attorney's office in Manhattan where a guilty defendant testifies for the government. Gary Wang told jurors his six-page agreement "obliges him to meet with prosecutors, answer their questions truthfully, and turn over evidence." Wang testified that he committed financial crimes at FTX with Bankman-Fried, Singh, and Ellison, and confirmed he signed this cooperation agreement in December 2022 that requires him to assist prosecutors.
