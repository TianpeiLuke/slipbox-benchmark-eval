# FTX Collapse from Peak Valuation to Bankruptcy

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-06 that FTX grew to become the third-largest crypto exchange with a peak valuation of $32 billion before collapsing into bankruptcy. The publication described how the exchange went from its height of success to filing for bankruptcy, detailing the fraudulent activities and mismanagement that led to its downfall. As former federal prosecutor Josh Naftalis explained, the case represents one of the biggest within the crypto space, involving alleged fraud and money laundering by former CEO Sam Bankman-Fried. "The prosecution painted a picture of an empire 'built on lies'" during the trial proceedings, according to TechCrunch's coverage.
