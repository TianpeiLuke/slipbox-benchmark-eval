# Prosecution's Case Against Bankman-Fried

The Verge reported on 2023-10-26 that the prosecution has made a strong case that Bankman-Fried presided over the various forms of fraud that played out at Alameda Research and FTX, orchestrating some of it and engaging in some of it directly. The article describes how former friends and associates like Adam Yedidia, Gary Wang, Nishad Singh, and Caroline Ellison testified about Bankman-Fried's central role in the fraudulent activities, with Ellison stating that Alameda took customer funds and Bankman-Fried knew about it and even invested them against her advice.
