# Blue Apron stock surge following Wonder Group acquisition announcement

The Sydney Morning Herald reported on 2023-10-01 that Blue Apron shares surged 134.5 per cent after the meal-kit company announced a $US13-per-share cash acquisition by Wonder Group valuing the deal at $US103 million ($160.2 million). "Blue Apron soared 134.5 per cent after the meal kit company said it was being bought by Wonder Group for $US13 per share in cash in a deal valued at $US103 million ($160.2 million)."
