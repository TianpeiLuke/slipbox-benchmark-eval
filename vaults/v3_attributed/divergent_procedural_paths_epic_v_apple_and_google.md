# Epic v. Google Trial Delayed Compared to Epic v. Apple

The Verge reported on 2023-11-05 that Epic's antitrust lawsuits against Apple and Google diverged significantly in timeline. While Epic v. Apple was tried in 2021 and decided that September mostly in Apple's favor with Supreme Court review still possible, Epic v. Google faced repeated delays. The Google trial was scheduled for November 6th, "a mere 1,180 days after Epic originally sued." The publication noted that while the Apple lawsuit "got their day in court," the similar Google lawsuit "never did."
