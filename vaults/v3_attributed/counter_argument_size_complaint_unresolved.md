# Steam Deck OLED's Persistent Ergonomic Issues Despite Overall Improvements

Engadget reported on 2023-11-09 that despite significant improvements in the Steam Deck OLED model, "the bad news for my tiny hand gang" is that the device remains "still ridiculously large" at 11.7 by 4.5 by nearly 2 inches. The review explicitly states the form factor hasn't changed from the original model, maintaining the same "monstrous size" that makes it look "absurd in small hands." While the OLED model addresses many technical shortcomings with better battery life, improved thermals, and enhanced display quality, it leaves the primary ergonomic objection completely untouched according to the reviewer's assessment.
