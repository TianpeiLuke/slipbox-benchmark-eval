# Bankman-Fried's Testimony on FTX Fund Misuse and Risk Management

The Verge reported on 2023-10-31 that Sam Bankman-Fried testified he could not recall who decided to spend the $8 billion of customer funds, and said there were no rules for how money borrowed from FTX would be returned or for risk management, apart from being "concerned with overall risk management." During questioning, Bankman-Fried admitted he had not established policies to prevent Alameda employees from spending FTX customer money, had not directed his employees not to spend customer deposits, and had not implemented protective measures for customer funds. When confronted with a software bug that revealed Alameda owed FTX approximately $8 billion, Bankman-Fried claimed his employees told him "they were busy and I should stop asking questions because it was distracting" rather than investigating the discrepancy.
