# Futures Odds Variation Across Sportsbooks

Sporting News reported on 2023-11-06 that "sportsbooks sometimes have higher liabilities for specific teams and offer better odds on others to lay that off" which causes significant variation in futures markets. Whereas most game lines are similar across sportsbooks, futures odds can vary significantly because books carry differing liability on particular teams and price others up to lay it off, so timing matters.
