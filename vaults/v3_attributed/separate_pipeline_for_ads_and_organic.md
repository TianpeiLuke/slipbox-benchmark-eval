# Google's Ad System Separation from Organic Search Results

The Age reported on 2023-10-22 that Google maintains its ad systems are separate from organic search ranking. According to Google's official search liaison Danny Sullivan, "Ad keyword matching is a long-standing and well-known process that is designed to connect people to relevant ads. A separate process, which has nothing to do with ads, is used to match organic results to a query." This statement directly addresses allegations that Google manipulates search queries for maximum ad revenue, with Sullivan emphasizing that organic results are matched through an unrelated process.
