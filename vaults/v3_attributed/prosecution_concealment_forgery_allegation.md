# Government's fraud allegations against Sam Bankman-Fried

Fortune reported on 2023-10-04 that the U.S. government alleges Sam Bankman-Fried directed employees to conceal money flows into FTX and forged financial documents given to lenders and investors. Prosecutor Thane Rehn stated that "the defendant lied to the world" and characterized Bankman-Fried as a "man who stole billions of dollars from thousands of victims," distinguishing him from the media portrayal of a good-faith crypto entrepreneur.
