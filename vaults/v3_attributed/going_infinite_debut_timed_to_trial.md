# Source material index for SBF trial coverage

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-02 that its article on Sam Bankman-Fried's alleged $5 billion offer to Trump drew primarily from two sources: Michael Lewis's book "Going Infinite" and Lewis's 60 Minutes interview broadcast on October 1, 2023. The article explicitly cites Lewis's account of SBF's personality, fundraising activities, and political dealings as its main evidentiary basis for the reported claims about FTX's operations and SBF's conduct.
