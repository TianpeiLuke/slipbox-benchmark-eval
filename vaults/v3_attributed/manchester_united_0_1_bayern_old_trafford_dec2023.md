# Kingsley Coman's goal eliminates Manchester United from Champions League

Sporting News reported on 2023-12-12 that Kingsley Coman's goal gave Bayern Munich a 1-0 win at Old Trafford on 12 December 2023, leaving Manchester United on 4 points and eliminating them from Europe altogether. The goal came during the final matchweek of Champions League Group A, where Bayern Munich had already secured qualification while Manchester United needed a positive result to advance. Coman's winner ensured Thomas Tuchel's side finished bottom of the group after a disappointing campaign that saw them win only one of their six matches.
