# Index for The Verge's Epic v. Google Explainer

The Verge reported on 2023-11-05 that Epic Games' antitrust lawsuit against Google would finally go to trial. The explainer is structured around four main sections: background on the August 13, 2020 trigger events when Epic bypassed Google Play fees, the parties' competing legal claims regarding Google's alleged monopoly, market definition arguments over whether Android app stores constitute a separate market from iPhones, and the Apple-case precedent. Epic argues Google's "tax" creates an illegal monopoly, while Google contends it competes with Apple overall.
