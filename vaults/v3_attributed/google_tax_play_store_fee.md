# Google Tax Definition in Epic v. Google Lawsuit

The Verge reported on 2023-11-05 that critics call the in-app payment fee Google collects "the 'Google tax' or the 'Apple tax,'" specifically referring to the up to 30 percent transaction fee Google charges when purchases made in Play-Store-installed Android apps are processed through Google's billing system. This fee applies to virtual currency purchases like V-Bucks in games such as Fortnite, where players buy in-game items through the app's payment system.
