# Third Point's $60M FTX Investment Based on Lack of Alameda Relationship Knowledge

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-06 that Third Point managing director Robert Boroujerdi testified his firm invested $60 million in FTX without knowing of the special relationship between Alameda Research and FTX. Boroujerdi stated, "Had he known of such a relationship, he testified that Third Point would not have invested in FTX in the first place." The testimony occurred during week three of the SBF trial, where Boroujerdi described the partnership that resulted in significant financial exposure for his hedge fund.
