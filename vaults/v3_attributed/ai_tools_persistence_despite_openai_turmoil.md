# AI Chatbots' Persistence Justified by Adoption Data and Investment Despite Leadership Crisis

TechCrunch reported on 2023-09-28 that AI chatbots like ChatGPT will persist despite organizational upheaval at OpenAI. The article states that "while there is a more…nefarious side to ChatGPT, it's clear that AI tools are not going away anytime soon." This persistence is evidenced by ChatGPT's 100 million weekly active users and OpenAI's continued heavy investment in the technology. Although OpenAI faced significant leadership disruption with CEO Sam Altman's firing and return, the widespread adoption across 92% of Fortune 500 companies demonstrates entrenched market penetration. OpenAI further demonstrated commitment through major announcements at its November 2023 DevDay conference, including GPT-4 Turbo, multimodal API capabilities, and a GPT store for custom model monetization. The company's ongoing infrastructure investments, evidenced by re-enabling ChatGPT Plus subscriptions after GPU shortages, support the conclusion that AI chatbot development remains a strategic priority despite temporary leadership instability.
