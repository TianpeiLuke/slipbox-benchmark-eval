# Caroline Ellison's Role in Sam Bankman-Fried Trial

Fortune reported on 2023-10-04 that Caroline Ellison served as both the chief executive of Alameda Research and Sam Bankman-Fried's on-again off-again girlfriend. The prosecution presented her as a "front" who concealed Bankman-Fried's secret access to customer money, while the defense portrayed her as a failed hedge fund executive who inadequately protected her firm from cryptocurrency market risks. By October 2023, she had become a former lieutenant cooperating with the government as a witness.
