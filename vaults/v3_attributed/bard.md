# Bard as Gemini Pro's Consumer Delivery Surface

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-07 that Bard serves as Google's primary consumer interface for its new Gemini Pro AI model, positioning it as a direct competitor to OpenAI's ChatGPT. "Google took the wraps off of Gemini, its new flagship generative AI model meant to power a range of products and services including Bard, Google's ChatGPT competitor." However, early user experiences with Gemini Pro on Bard revealed significant factual inaccuracies and performance issues across various tasks.
