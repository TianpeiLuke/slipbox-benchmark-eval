# GPT-4's Enhanced Natural Language Capabilities

TechCrunch reported on 2023-09-28 that GPT-4 "can write more naturally and fluently than previous models." The article highlights GPT-4 as OpenAI's latest language-writing model, positioned as a significant advancement over earlier iterations like GPT-3. This enhanced fluency and naturalness represents an unbenchmarked comparative improvement in OpenAI's model generations, though the article does not provide specific benchmarks or quantitative comparisons to substantiate this claim. GPT-4 remains largely exclusive to paying ChatGPT users, with limited free access through Microsoft's Bing Chat integration.
