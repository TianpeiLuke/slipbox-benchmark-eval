# Google Faked Gemini AI Demo with Off-Camera Prompt Editing

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-09 that Google was caught staging a Gemini AI demonstration by editing text prompts off camera while showing static images, rather than allowing the model to process live visual input. The incident occurred during Google's promotion of Gemini's multimodal capabilities, which claimed the model could understand images, audio, and videos as well as text. However, the demonstrated Gemini Pro version is strictly text-in, text-out and has proven mistake-prone. "Google was caught faking a Gemini demo by tuning text prompts with still images off camera."
