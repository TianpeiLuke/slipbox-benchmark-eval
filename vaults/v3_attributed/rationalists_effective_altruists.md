# Eliezer Yudkowsky's Rationalist Community and AI Philosophy

The Age reported on 2023-12-09 that Eliezer Yudkowsky was a leader in a community of people who called themselves Rationalists or, in later years, effective altruists. The article states that "They believed that AI could find a cure for cancer or solve climate change, but they worried that AI bots might do things their creators had not intended." This community was concerned that if machines became more intelligent than humans, "the machines could turn on their creators."
