# Generative music models will enter major productions in 2024, especially games

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-19 that generative music models will likely make it into a few major productions like games in 2024, where professional musicians can leverage the tools to create an unending soundtrack. The publication predicted this as part of their 8 AI predictions for 2024, specifically mentioning that "generative music models will likely make it into a few major productions like games, again where professional musicians can leverage the tools to create an unending soundtrack." This represents a shift from experimental to practical application in the gaming industry.
