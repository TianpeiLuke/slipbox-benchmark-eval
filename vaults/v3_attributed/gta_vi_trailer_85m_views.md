# Grand Theft Auto VI Trailer Breaks YouTube View Record

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-09 that the first Grand Theft Auto VI trailer "racked up 85 million views — breaking a MrBeast video's record for most YouTube views in 24 hours." The trailer achieved this milestone within just 22 hours of its release, demonstrating unprecedented viral momentum for the highly anticipated game that has been a decade in the making since Grand Theft Auto V's release.
