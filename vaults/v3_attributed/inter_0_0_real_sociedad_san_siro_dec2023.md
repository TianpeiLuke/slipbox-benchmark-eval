# Inter Milan and Real Sociedad finish level in Champions League Group D

Sporting News reported on 2023-12-12 that Inter Milan and Real Sociedad drew 0-0 at San Siro on 12 December 2023, finishing level on 12 points and leaving the Basque club top of Group D on goal difference. "Real Sociedad and Inter Milan dominated Group D and could not be separated as they shared a 0-0 draw at San Siro. That left the Basque side top of the pile on goal difference."
