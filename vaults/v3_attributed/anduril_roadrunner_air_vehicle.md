# Anduril's Roadrunner Autonomous Aircraft Concept

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-09 that Anduril has developed Roadrunner, a modular, twin-jet-powered autonomous vertical take-off and landing air vehicle designed to counter low-cost high-powered aerial threats. "Dubbed Roadrunner, the modular, twin-jet-powered autonomous vertical take-off and landing air vehicle — one version of which is capable of carrying a warhead — can take off, follow and destroy targets or, if there's no need to intercept the target, autonomously maneuver back to base for refueling and reuse." The system represents Anduril's approach to autonomous defense systems that can engage threats and then return to base for refueling rather than being expendable.
