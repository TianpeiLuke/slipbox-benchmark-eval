# Fortnite's V-Bucks Revenue Model

The Verge reported on 2023-11-05 that Fortnite is a free-to-play game that generates revenue by selling in-game items with its virtual currency V-Bucks. Epic Games lets players purchase V-Bucks directly through its own payment processing system at a discount, rather than exclusively through official app stores. Before August 13th, 2020, players who installed Fortnite through official app stores had their V-Bucks purchases trigger in-app payment fees to Google or Apple, which Epic opposed by deploying a hotfix update to bypass these "Google tax" or "Apple tax" fees.
