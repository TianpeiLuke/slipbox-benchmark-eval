# Apple will leverage Siri brand for AI rather than launching new service

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-19 that Apple will ship its AI under the existing Siri brand rather than introducing a separate "Apple AI" service. The article states that "Apple has an established pattern of waiting, watching and learning from other companies' failures, then blowing in with a refined and polished take that puts others to shame." This approach leverages the incumbency advantage of existing "hey Siri" user habits, despite Siri's reputation for mediocrity. The hypothesis is falsified if Apple introduces a separate AI brand instead.
