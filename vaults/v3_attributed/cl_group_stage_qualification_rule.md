# Champions League Group Stage Qualification Rules

Sporting News reported on 2023-12-12 that in the UEFA Champions League group stage, "the two highest-placed clubs advance to the Round of 16, the third-placed club is transferred to the Europa League, and the fourth (bottom) club exits European competition entirely." This qualification framework determines that of the six-team Champions League group of six matchdays, only the top two finishers progress directly to the knockout stage, while third-place teams drop into the Europa League competition, and fourth-place teams are eliminated from European competition altogether.
