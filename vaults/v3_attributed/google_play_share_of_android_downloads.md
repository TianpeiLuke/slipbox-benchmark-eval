# Epic's Antitrust Evidence Against Google Includes 90% Google Play Download Statistic

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-06 that Epic Games will present evidence in its antitrust trial against Google, "noting that 90% of apps are still downloaded through Google Play." Epic argues this statistic demonstrates that alternative Android app stores have not gained meaningful reach, supporting its claim that Google engages in anticompetitive behavior by bundling the Play Store with other Google apps and using Anti-Fragmentation Agreements that prevent OEMs from easily allowing downloads outside Google Play.
