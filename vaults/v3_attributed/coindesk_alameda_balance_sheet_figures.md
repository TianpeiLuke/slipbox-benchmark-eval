# CoinDesk's Alameda Balance Sheet Revelation

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-01 that CoinDesk published Alameda Research's balance sheet showing $14.6 billion in assets and $8 billion in liabilities as of June 30, 2022. "CoinDesk published a copy of Alameda's balance sheet, showing the firm held $14.6 billion in assets and $8 billion in liabilities as of June 30, 2022." This disclosure sparked concerns about FTX's liquidity and contributed to the exchange's subsequent collapse.
