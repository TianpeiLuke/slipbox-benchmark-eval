# Bankman-Fried's alleged misuse of FTX customer funds

The Verge reported on 2023-09-28 that Sam Bankman-Fried is accused of using "billions of dollars in misappropriated FTX customer deposits" to purchase over $200 million in real estate for himself, make billions of dollars in personal investments, and repay Alameda's lenders. The indictment alleges that Bankman-Fried directed special code features that allowed Alameda to withdraw unlimited amounts from FTX, effectively exempting his trading firm from standard risk management protocols that other customers faced.
