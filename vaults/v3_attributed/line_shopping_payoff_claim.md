# Line Shopping Worth Time Investment for Sports Bettors

Sporting News reported on 2023-11-06 that line shopping is worth the time investment because improved spreads convert otherwise-losing NFL and NBA bets into winners, and better odds let the bettor stake fewer dollars on losses and earn more on wins. The publication states that "shopping around lets you find the best lines and odds for any bet" and emphasizes that "finding the best line or better odds on any bet is like finding money." While manual comparison across multiple sportsbook apps may take a few minutes, using live odds feeds can accomplish the same goal in just seconds, making the practice highly efficient for bettors seeking optimal returns.
