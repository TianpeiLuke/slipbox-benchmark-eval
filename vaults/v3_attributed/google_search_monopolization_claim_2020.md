# Google's 2020 Search Dominance Antitrust Lawsuit Distinct from Advertising Case

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-31 that the antitrust trial held in October 2023 originated from a 2020 claim specifically targeting Google's dominance in online search markets. The article states that this "lawsuit stems from a 2020 antitrust claim over Google's dominance in the online search market" which is legally separate from a subsequent January 2023 Department of Justice case that focused on Google's digital advertising business practices.
