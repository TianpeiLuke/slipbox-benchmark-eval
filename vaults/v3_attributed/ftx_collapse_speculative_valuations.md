# FTX Collapse Attributed to Speculative Valuation Without Fundamental Underwriting

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-02 that the severe and abrupt decline of FTX stemmed from crypto assets being underwritten primarily by speculation and promises of higher value rather than solid fundamentals. This lack of substantive backing made the eventual crash particularly devastating when market valuations plummeted.
