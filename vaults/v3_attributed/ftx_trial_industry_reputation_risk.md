# FTX Trial Carries Broader Crypto Industry Risks Beyond Bankman-Fried Verdict

The Verge reported on 2023-09-28 that the FTX criminal trial against Sam Bankman-Fried extends beyond personal guilt to pose significant risks for the entire cryptocurrency industry. Law professor Yesha Yadav warned that unpredictable evidentiary disclosures during the trial could inflict further damage on an already weakened crypto sector. "This trial is going to be an excruciating moment for the industry because no one knows what kind of evidence might come out," Yadav stated. The trial involves seven counts including wire fraud and conspiracy charges, with the Securities and Exchange Commission alleging that FTX was a fraud "from the start" involving misappropriation of customer funds directed by Bankman-Fried.
