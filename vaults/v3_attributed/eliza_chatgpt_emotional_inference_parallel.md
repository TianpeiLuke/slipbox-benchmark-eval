# Hyland's ELIZA-ChatGPT Emotional Inference Comparison

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-30 that Adam Hyland, a PhD student studying AI at the University of Washington, compared user interactions with Joseph Weizenbaum's 1966 MIT chatbot ELIZA to ChatGPT. Hyland observed that "Humans interacting with the system inferred emotional content and a narrative through line in chat messages" for both ELIZA and ChatGPT, suggesting the psychological phenomenon is consistent across different eras of chatbot technology. This indicates that the perceived "feel" difference between rudimentary chatbots and sophisticated AI like ChatGPT is merely a matter of degree in the underlying model capabilities, rather than a fundamental shift in human interaction psychology.
