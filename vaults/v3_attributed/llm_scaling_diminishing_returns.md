# Diminishing returns on LLM parameter scaling expected in 2024

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-19 that the limits of monolithic LLMs will become clearer in 2024 as researchers encounter diminishing returns on parameter counts. While training a 500-billion-parameter model may technically produce better results, "the compute required to do so could provably be deployed more effectively." The article explains that a single monolithic model is unwieldy and expensive, while a mixture of experts - smaller, more specific models - may prove almost as effective while being much easier to update piecemeal. This represents a shift from the previous belief that continuously adding compute and parameters would yield proportionally better capabilities.
