# Gabe Bankman-Fried's Nonprofit Received Millions from FTX

The Verge reported on 2023-10-26 that "Gabe Bankman-Fried's nonprofit, Guarding Against Pandemics, which got millions from FTX." The article detailed how Gabe Bankman-Fried, Sam Bankman-Fried's brother, received substantial funding from FTX customer money through his nonprofit organization. According to Nishad Singh's testimony, Gabe was involved in coordinating transfers of FTX and Alameda money to political causes through a Signal group chat called "Donations Processing." Singh stated that Gabe had an assistant fly to the Bahamas with blank checks for him to sign, and that Gabe recommended making some donations "in the dark" to avoid public relations issues.
