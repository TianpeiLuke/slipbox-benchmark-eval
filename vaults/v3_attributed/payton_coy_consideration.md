# Sean Payton's Coach of the Year Case Despite In-Season Turnaround

Sporting News reported on 2023-12-05 that Sean Payton should lead the Coach of the Year conversation because he "turned around a team that started 1-5 in-season and resurrected Russell Wilson's career." The article notes that Payton has taken one of the worst teams and transformed it mid-season, specifically highlighting his revival of Wilson's performance. However, the publication indicates that Payton "likely needs Denver to reach the playoffs to convince voters," suggesting that postseason qualification is the key remaining hurdle for his candidacy. Despite being a longshot at +2200 odds compared to Dan Campbell's +200 favorite status, Payton's in-season turnaround achievement is presented as potentially award-worthy if the team can secure a playoff berth.
