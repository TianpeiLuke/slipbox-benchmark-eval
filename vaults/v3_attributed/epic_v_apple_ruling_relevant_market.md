# Judge Rogers' Market Definition Carried Apple's Victory in Epic Lawsuit

The Verge reported on 2023-11-05 that in the Epic v. Apple ruling, "the judge in the Apple case ended up doing" a key market definition choice. Judge Yvonne Gonzalez Rogers defined the relevant market as "digital mobile gaming transactions" rather than iPhone apps specifically. This definitional choice "carried much of Apple's victory" and is now being cited as precedent for the Google trial. The broader market definition made it harder for Epic to prove Apple's monopolistic behavior.
