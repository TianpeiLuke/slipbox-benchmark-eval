# Google's Antitrust Practices Confirmed in Epic Trial

The Verge reported on 2023-12-12 that Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeney confirmed longstanding suspicions about Google's anticompetitive practices, stating: "you're leaking our conversations to reporters to get negative stories written about us; you're paying other developers off to convince them not to launch their own stores; they were going around and paying carriers and OEMs secretly not to carry competing stores." Sweeney testified that when Epic tried to bundle Fortnite with smartphone manufacturers like OnePlus and various carriers, they were told they couldn't proceed because Google had established secret agreements with these partners. This testimony directly supports the hypothesis that rival app stores failed to reach devices due to Google's financial incentives to third parties, which would be falsified if these parties had declined competition for reasons independent of Google payments.
