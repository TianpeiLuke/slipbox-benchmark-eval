# Defense Contradicts Wang's Market-Making Testimony Without Submitting Prior Statements

The Verge reported on 2023-10-10 that during Sam Bankman-Fried's fraud trial, defense attorney Christian Everdell presented alleged contradictions in Gary Wang's market-making testimony without submitting Wang's prior statements as evidence. "Whatever Wang was shown wasn't submitted as evidence or shown to the court," according to the article. The author infers that the material used to refresh Wang's recollection was likely his own earlier testimony. This occurred during cross-examination of Wang, who was testifying about Alameda's special credit arrangements with FTX.
