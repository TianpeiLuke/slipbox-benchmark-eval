# Alameda Research's Strategic Naming and Internal Codename

The Verge reported on 2023-10-28 that Sam Bankman-Fried testified about Alameda Research's naming strategy, stating: "Effectively, we wanted to be under the radar at that point in time. I didn't want to call it Sam's Crypto Trading Firm or anything like that." He explained that "Research" was chosen as a deliberately generic word to prevent competitors from learning what they were building, since competitors would "race to do it." Bankman-Fried also revealed that the internal name for the company at the time was "Wireless Mouse," which he considered less suitable than the public-facing name.
