# Epic cites $360 million payment to Activision in antitrust trial

The Verge reported on 2023-11-07 that Epic Games' lead attorney Gary Bornstein identified a specific $360 million payment from Google to Activision during the Epic v. Google antitrust trial. Bornstein alleged this payment was made to prevent Activision from opening a competing app store, stating Google "literally gives them money and other things of value" to avoid competition with Google Play. However, Activision had previously told The Verge in 2022 that it "never entered into an agreement that Activision would not open its own app store."
