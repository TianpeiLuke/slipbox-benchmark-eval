# Government's Model of Bankman-Fried Scheme Identifies Alameda as Key

Fortune reported on 2023-10-04 that the government's model of the scheme holds that Sam Bankman-Fried retained "secret access" through Alameda Research to FTX customer money in both cash and crypto. The prosecution identified Alameda Research, a crypto hedge fund he also owned, as "the key to his alleged scheme." Using Caroline Ellison, his on-again off-again girlfriend and Alameda's CEO, as a front, Bankman-Fried allegedly directed employees to conceal money flows and forged financial documents distributed to lenders and investors.
