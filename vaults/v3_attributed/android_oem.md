# Android OEM Definition in Epic-Google Antitrust Context

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-06 that an original equipment manufacturer (OEM) in the Epic-Google antitrust dispute refers to Android device makers like Samsung or OnePlus that must agree to Google's terms to use Android. Epic specifically referenced an agreement with OnePlus to distribute Epic games through its own app store, but OnePlus canceled the deal due to concerns about bypassing Google Play Store requirements.
