# Pichai's Chat Retention Policy Change Testimony

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-31 that Google CEO Sundar Pichai testified he changed the company's chat history-off default policy in February 2023 to comply with the DOJ's litigation hold requirements. According to CNBC's account of the October 30, 2023 hearing, Pichai acknowledged that Google had been systematically destroying internal chat messages through its 24-hour auto-delete feature unless users manually adjusted the setting. "Pichai said that he took action against the history-off default for chat in February to comply with the DOJ's litigation hold," the report stated.
