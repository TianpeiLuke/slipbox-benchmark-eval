# Real Madrid's Perfect Champions League Group Stage Campaign

Sporting News reported on 2023-12-12 that Real Madrid, the 14-time European champions, completed six wins out of six with a "topsy-turvy 3-2 win away to Union Berlin" on 12 December 2023. The victory confirmed their perfect group stage record while eliminating Union Berlin from Europa League contention. Napoli secured second place in Group C after defeating Braga 2-0, leaving Union Berlin without European competition for the remainder of the season.
