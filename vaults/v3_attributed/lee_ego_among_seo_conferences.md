# SEO Community Arrogance Claim

The Verge reported on 2023-11-01 that Cade Lee, speaking from over a decade of experience attending SEO conferences and networking events, observed a distinctive arrogance within the SEO community. Lee stated, "You succeed, and now you're a genius. Now you've outdone Google." This ego-driven mindset, according to Lee, characterizes the industry's approach to search engine manipulation, viewing SEO success as a measure of personal brilliance rather than ethical content creation. The article frames this attitude as part of what makes SEO practitioners widely disliked, suggesting their practices prioritize profit over authentic information sharing.
