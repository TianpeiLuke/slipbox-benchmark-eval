# Bing's Redesigned Interface with AI Augmentation

The Age reported on 2023-10-22 that Bing had overhauled its layout over the past year to be very graphic and augmented by generative AI explanations from its chatbot. The publication noted that while the idea of asking follow-up questions in searches was appealing, the feature was "not really there yet" in terms of practical effectiveness.
