# Sam Bankman-Fried's Relationship with Caroline Ellison

The Verge reported on 2023-10-10 that Caroline Ellison, former head of Alameda Research and ex-girlfriend of Sam Bankman-Fried, testified in Bankman-Fried's criminal fraud trial that "he directed me to commit these crimes." Ellison, who has already pleaded guilty to fraud, conspiracy to commit fraud, and money laundering, stated that Bankman-Fried "was a terrible boyfriend" and described their relationship dynamics during her testimony, including how he refused to give her equity while making her CEO of his hedge fund.
