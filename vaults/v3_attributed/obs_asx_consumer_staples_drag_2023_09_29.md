# ASX Consumer Staples Sector Declines on September 29, 2023

The Sydney Morning Herald reported on 2023-10-01 that on September 29, 2023, the ASX consumer staples sector fell 1.1 per cent. Treasury Wine Estates declined 1.9 per cent and Woolworths dropped 1.2 per cent, leading the sector's decline. Other significant large-cap decliners included Pilbara Minerals (down 2.1 per cent) and Qantas (down 1.5 per cent). The broader market faced pressure from prospects of higher-for-longer interest rates ahead of the Reserve Bank's meeting.
