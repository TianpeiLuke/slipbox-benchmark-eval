# Episodes in The Age's 2023-12-09 Account of AI's Rise

The Age reported on 2023-12-09 that its account of AI's rise contained four key episodes: the 2015 Napa Valley argument between Elon Musk and Larry Page about AI's dangers, the Rosewood hotel dinner in Menlo Park that led to OpenAI's founding, the November 2023 OpenAI board revolt against Sam Altman, and the 2010 cocktail party at Peter Thiel's San Francisco house where Demis Hassabis sought funding for DeepMind. "The closer people are to being pointed in the same direction, the more contentious the disagreements are," Altman observed.
