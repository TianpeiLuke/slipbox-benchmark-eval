# OpenAI Leadership Chaos Raises Industry Concerns

TechCrunch reported on 2023-09-28 that OpenAI CEO and co-founder Sam Altman's firing and subsequent restoration "raised concerns about its direction and opened the door for competitors." The publication noted that this leadership instability occurred as OpenAI was becoming "one of the most-hyped companies in recent memory," with its ChatGPT platform used by over 92% of Fortune 500 companies. The article suggests this turmoil "might empower competitors like Meta and its LLaMA family of large language models."
