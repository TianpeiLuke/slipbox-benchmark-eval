# Steam Deck OLED Battery Performance Exceeds Valve's Estimates

Engadget reported on 2023-11-09 that the Steam Deck OLED's battery life exceeds Valve's stated improvements. "The OLED lasted four hours and 23 minutes before dying. With the same settings (800p with a 60 fps cap and similar brightness) the original model died after two hours and 42 minutes. That's a 62 percent improvement, surpassing Valve's own estimates."
