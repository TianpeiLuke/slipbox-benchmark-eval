# FTX Funding Details from TechCrunch Report

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-02 that FTX raised approximately $2 billion in total funding, including $1 billion in 2021, from investors such as Sequoia, Temasek and Tiger Global among dozens of others. This funding occurred during Sam Bankman-Fried's rapid rise from having "nothing" eighteen months earlier to controlling $22.5 billion.
