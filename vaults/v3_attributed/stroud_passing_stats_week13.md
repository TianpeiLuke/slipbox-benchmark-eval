# C.J. Stroud's NFL MVP Case Through Week 13 of 2023 Season

Sporting News reported on 2023-12-05 that C.J. Stroud led the NFL in passing yards through Week 13 of the 2023 season. The article stated he was "one of four quarterbacks who have started the whole season with a passer rating over 100" and noted that "his touchdown to interception ratio is the best in the NFL (4 to 1)." These statistical achievements positioned Stroud as a notable MVP candidate despite playing for a team that had only 11 total wins over the previous three seasons.
