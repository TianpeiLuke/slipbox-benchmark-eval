# This Sporting News NBA Rookie of the Year Betting Guide Structure

This Sporting News reported on 2023-10-04 that their NBA Rookie of the Year betting guide presents "the latest NBA Rookie of the Year odds as posted at online sportsbooks and how to bet on them." The page organizes coverage into four main sections: current odds listings for players like Wembanyama, Henderson, and Holmgren; favorable cases for each contender; step-by-step betting mechanics; and recommended legal sportsbooks including DraftKings and FanDuel.
