# Google Antitrust Navigation Context: Helena Chronicle Lawsuit and Related Cases

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-15 that the Helena World Chronicle's antitrust lawsuit against Google was filed alongside several related legal actions that created a broader regulatory environment. These included the U.S. Department of Justice's existing digital ad technology monopoly suit, the 2020 DOJ civil suit concerning search and search-advertising monopolization, and Epic Games' successful app-store antitrust case. Additionally, Canada had recently reached a news-content settlement with Google requiring payments of "$73.5 million (100 million Canadian dollars) every year to news organizations." The publisher's lawsuit specifically cited Google's AI technologies like Search Generative Experience and Bard as worsening competitive harm, arguing these tools kept users within Google's "walled garden" while plagiarizing publisher content.
