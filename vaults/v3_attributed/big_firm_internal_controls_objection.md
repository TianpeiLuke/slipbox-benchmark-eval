# Google's Internal Controls Failed Despite Trillion-Dollar Valuation

The Verge reported on 2023-12-12 that Google's rank-and-file behavior demonstrated internal controls were failing rather than effectively restraining misconduct. Tim Sweeney, CEO of Epic Games, observed during his antitrust trial testimony that "you'd think a trillion-dollar company would have respectable processes and leadership checks," but instead found evidence of bad faith efforts including leaking conversations to reporters, paying developers to prevent competing stores, and secret deals with carriers and OEMs to block competition. This contradicts the expectation that large firms necessarily maintain effective governance against wrongdoing.
