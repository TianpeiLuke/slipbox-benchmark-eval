# Illegally Tied Definition in Epic v. Google Antitrust Case

The Verge reported on 2023-12-12 that the federal jury found Google "illegally tied" its Google Play billing system to its app store. Epic CEO Tim Sweeney explained this means Google conditioned access to its monopolized Play Store on use of its own billing system, constituting a statute-based wrong under the Sherman Antitrust Act rather than a contractual or regulatory breach. The jury's verdict determined this tying arrangement was part of Google's illegal monopoly over Android app distribution and in-app payment systems.
