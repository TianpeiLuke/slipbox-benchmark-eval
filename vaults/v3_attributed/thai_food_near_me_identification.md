# Thai Restaurant Named After Google Search Query Opens in Manhattan

The Verge reported on 2023-10-26 that Thai Food Near Me, a small Thai restaurant in Murray Hill, Manhattan, was named after co-owner Teddy Jirapraphanan's frequent Google searches for "Thai food near me." The restaurant represents a broader trend of businesses optimizing their names for search engine optimization (SEO) to improve discoverability. "Everywhere I go, I'm craving Thai food," Jirapraphanan says. "I have to search 'Thai food near me' all the time." The name helped the restaurant gain viral attention on Twitter before opening and attracted customers who were searching for that exact phrase.
