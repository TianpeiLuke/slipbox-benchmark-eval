# FTX Trial's Inner Circle: Seven Key Figures Connected to Sam Bankman-Fried

The Verge reported on 2023-10-26 that Sam Bankman-Fried's trial revealed how the collapse of FTX affected seven people closest to him: "Everyone who loved Bankman-Fried most in the world is now caught up in the case." These figures include Adam Yedidia, his college roommate who testified with immunity about potentially unknowingly contributing to fraud; Gary Wang, co-founder of FTX and Alameda Research who admitted to coding secret privileges for Alameda; Nishad Singh, a family friend who pleaded guilty to backdating transactions and creating fake balance sheets; Caroline Ellison, his ex-girlfriend who admitted to creating seven phony balance sheets while knowing Alameda took customer funds; his father Joe Bankman who was in the inner circle's strategy chat; Barbara Fried whose political action committee received FTX customer money as donations; and his brother Gabe Bankman-Fried whose nonprofit received millions from FTX and who participated in donation coordination chats. The article notes that Wang, Singh, and Ellison are now felons whose lives are "irrevocably changed," while Bankman, Fried, and Gabe's reputations are tarnished.
