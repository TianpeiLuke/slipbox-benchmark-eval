# Tim Sweeney's Verge Interview: Key Themes from Epic v. Google Verdict

The Verge reported on 2023-12-12 that Epic CEO Tim Sweeney described the federal jury's antitrust verdict against Google as "an awesome thing" needed by an industry "being strangled by a few gatekeepers." Sweeney, who attended nearly every day of the four-week trial, revealed courtroom surprises including Google's alleged practices of leaking conversations to reporters, paying developers to avoid launching competing stores, and secretly preventing OEMs from carrying alternative app stores. He stated, "It was really disconcerting to see the extent of bad faith efforts that were going on in a company of Google's size."
