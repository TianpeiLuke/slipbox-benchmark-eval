# Swift attends Chiefs vs Chargers game on publication Sunday

CBSSports.com reported on 2023-10-12 that Taylor Swift was at Arrowhead Stadium on the Sunday of that week to watch the Kansas City Chiefs play the Los Angeles Chargers. This attendance made her presence at "five games so far this season" current as of the article's writing, including the Sunday game against the Chargers which was another AFC West opponent for the Chiefs.
