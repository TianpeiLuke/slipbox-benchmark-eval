# Meta's Content Moderation Policies and Alleged Shadowbanning of Palestinian Content Post-October 2023 Gaza Conflict

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-19 that following the Hamas attack on Israelis on October 7, 2023, Israeli retaliatory airstrikes and a total blockade cutting electricity, water and vital supplies devastated Gaza. Meta stated it monitors for violations and may inadvertently flag content, but forbids content praising or supporting Hamas, which the US and EU designate as a terrorist organization. Users have accused Meta of shadowbanning Palestinian content even when it doesn't support Hamas, with Palestinian-American filmmaker Khitam Jabr reporting restrictions on her West Bank travel videos and supermodel Bella Hadid alleging similar censorship of her Palestine-related posts. Meta attributed some content removals to technical bugs rather than intentional suppression.
