# Google's Canadian News Content Settlement

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-15 that under a November 2023 agreement with the Canadian government, Google will pay $73.5 million (C$100 million) annually to Canadian news organizations. The funds will be distributed according to each outlet's headcount. This settlement comes amid broader concerns from publishers about AI technologies like Google's Search Generative Experience and Bard chatbot reducing website traffic and ad revenue. The agreement follows similar negotiations with Meta, which began blocking Canadian news content in August 2023.
