# Cue's Dual Testimony on Apple's Privacy Stance vs Google Partnership

The Verge reported on 2023-09-26 that Eddy Cue's testimony in the US v. Google antitrust trial presented two seemingly contradictory positions: Apple's commitment to protecting user privacy and its endorsement of Google as the superior search engine. During cross-examination, Cue maintained that "we've always thought it was the best" while also acknowledging Apple's criticism of Google's privacy policies. The trial examined whether these positions could coexist, particularly regarding the Information Services Agreement that makes Google the default search engine on Apple devices. Cue testified that Apple never seriously considered alternatives, stating "there wasn't a valid alternative to Google at the time," and that users can easily change the default search engine, though critics dispute this claim.
