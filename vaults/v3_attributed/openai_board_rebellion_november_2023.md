# OpenAI Board Crisis of November 2023

The Age reported on 2023-12-09 that in late November 2023, rebel OpenAI board members attempted to force out CEO Sam Altman "because, they believed, they could no longer trust him to build AI that would benefit humanity." The article states that "over five chaotic days, OpenAI looked as if it were going to fall apart, until the board – pressured by giant investors and employees who threatened to follow Altman out the door – backed down." This internal crisis revealed the deep divisions among AI leaders about the technology's development and control.
