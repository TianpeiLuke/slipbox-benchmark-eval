# Sam Bankman-Fried Faces Seven Fraud and Money Laundering Counts

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-06 that Sam Bankman-Fried, former CEO and co-founder of FTX, faces seven counts tied to fraud and money laundering committed while leading FTX and its sister trading firm Alameda Research. The trial proceedings began with jury selection on October 3, with Bankman-Fried set to testify in his own defense. Former colleagues like Caroline Ellison and Can Sun have already provided testimony detailing alleged misuse of customer funds and improper financial practices within the companies.
