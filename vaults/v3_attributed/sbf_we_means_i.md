# Bankman-Fried's Testimony Style

The Verge reported on 2023-10-28 that during Sam Bankman-Fried's testimony, he sometimes used the first-person plural "we" to refer only to himself, rather than to a group. This linguistic inconsistency was noted alongside other credibility issues, as Bankman-Fried faced repeated judicial instructions to answer questions directly rather than providing narrative responses. The article quotes Bankman-Fried saying "We — there are a lot of competitors and people who we didn't particularly want to know what we were building out" when explaining Alameda Research's naming strategy, demonstrating this pattern of using "we" singularly.
