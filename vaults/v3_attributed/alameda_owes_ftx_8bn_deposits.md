# Alameda Owed FTX $8 Billion in Customer Deposits, Wang Testified

CNBC reported on 2023-10-06 that Gary Wang testified in Manhattan federal court on October 6, 2023, stating "the privileges granted to Alameda ultimately resulted in the hedge fund owing FTX $8 billion of customer deposits." Wang, FTX's technology chief and co-founder, was testifying as part of the Sam Bankman-Fried fraud trial. His testimony revealed that Alameda Research had no way to repay the roughly $14 billion hole in its books when discussing shutting down the hedge fund. Wang admitted to committing financial crimes including wire and commodities fraud while working at FTX, crimes he carried out with Bankman-Fried, Nishad Singh, and Caroline Ellison.
