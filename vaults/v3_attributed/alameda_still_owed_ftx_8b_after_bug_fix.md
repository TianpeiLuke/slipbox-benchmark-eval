# SBF's Testimony on Unresolved $8 Billion Ledger Gap

The Verge reported on 2023-10-31 that Sam Bankman-Fried's October 31, 2023 SDNY trial testimony revealed Alameda Research still owed FTX about $8 billion even after fixing the software bug. "Nor is it how he reacted to finding out that even after fixing the bug, Alameda still owed FTX about $8 billion." Despite discovering the fiat@ftx account in June 2022, Bankman-Fried claimed he didn't understand its purpose until October and was told by employees to stop asking questions because it was distracting.
