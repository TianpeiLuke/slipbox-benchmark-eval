# GPT-4 Access Model and Bing Chat Integration

TechCrunch reported on 2023-09-28 that GPT-4 "remains largely exclusive to paying ChatGPT users." However, the publication noted that users can access GPT-4 for free through Microsoft's Bing Chat integration, which is available in Microsoft Edge, Google Chrome, and Safari web browsers. This represents OpenAI's strategy of limiting direct access to its most advanced language model while partnering with Microsoft to provide broader distribution through the search giant's platforms.
