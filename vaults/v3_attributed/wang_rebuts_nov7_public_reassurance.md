# Wang contradicts FTX's November 7 reassurances

CNBC reported on 2023-10-06 that Gary Wang testified FTX was not fine during the same period when the company publicly blamed bank hours for withdrawal delays and Sam Bankman-Fried tweeted assurances to customers. Wang stated, "FTX was not fine and assets were not fine," directly contradicting the company's public messaging from November 7, 2022, while facing criminal charges for wire and commodities fraud at FTX.
