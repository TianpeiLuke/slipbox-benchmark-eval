# AI Video Finds Niches Where Photorealism Isn't Essential

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-19 that AI-generated video will find 2024 niches where its photorealism shortcomings remain invisible because skilled creators can leverage the stylized output for interesting creative work. The publication stated that "in the hands of skilled creators, a lack of photorealism isn't a problem, and we'll see AI video used in fun and interesting ways." This hypothesis is falsified if AI video adoption occurs only in contexts where realism is judged against live-action standards.
