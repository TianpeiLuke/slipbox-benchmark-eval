# PSV Secures Champions League Round of 16 Qualification

Sporting News reported on 2023-12-12 that PSV's comeback 3-2 win at Sevilla on 29 November 2023 put them on 9 points, and Lens' defeat in the same matchweek confirmed PSV's Round of 16 place before the final matchday. "PSV's comeback win over Sevilla meant have also qualified thanks to Lens' defeat."
