# Al Saadi's Testimony on Palestinian Media Warfare

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-19 that Palestinian-American activist Al Saadi described Palestinians fighting two wars, stating: "The first is with their legal occupier. The second war is with the entire Western media landscape … I mean social media." This claim was made in the context of her testimony about information warfare and platform restrictions affecting Palestinian voices, particularly citing Meta's alleged shadowbanning of Palestine-related content. Her assertion reflects the broader Palestinian experience of digital suppression during the ongoing conflict, as documented through user reports and advocacy group findings.
