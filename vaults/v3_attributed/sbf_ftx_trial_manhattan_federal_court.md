# Sam Bankman-Fried's Fraud Trial Opens in Manhattan Federal Court

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-02 that Sam Bankman-Fried's fraud trial opened at Manhattan Federal Court on Tuesday, October 3, 2023, following his not-guilty plea to roughly seven charges of fraud and conspiracy over the collapse of FTX. The trial comes amid public scrutiny of SBF's alleged "one of the biggest financial frauds in American history" involving the multibillion-dollar collapse of the crypto exchange he founded.
