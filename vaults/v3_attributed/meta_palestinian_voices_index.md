# Meta's Palestinian Content Moderation Bias

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-19 that Meta's content moderation practices exhibit systemic bias against Palestinian voices, extending beyond mere technical bugs. Palestinian-American filmmaker Khitam Jabr experienced account restrictions after posting West Bank travel vlogs, while users widely reported shadowbanning of Palestine-related content on Instagram Stories. The article documents "inherent bias" in Meta's processes, citing inadequate Arabic language resource diversity, culturally insensitive policies, and problematic partnerships with government agencies like Israel's Cyber Unit. During the 2021 Hamas-Israel conflict, a Meta-commissioned report found "adverse human rights impact" on Palestinian expression rights. Meta maintains its policies are applied globally and denies deliberate suppression, attributing visibility issues to technical glitches and offering appeals processes for disputed content removals.
