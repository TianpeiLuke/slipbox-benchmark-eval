# Atletico Madrid Secures Group E Top Spot with 2-0 Win Over Lazio

Sporting News reported on 2023-12-12 that Atletico Madrid's 2-0 victory over Lazio in Madrid on December 13, 2023, determined first place in Champions League Group E. "Atletico Madrid's 2-0 win over Lazio in the Spanish capital on 13 December 2023, a fixture later than the article's own 12 December 2023 dateline, decided first place in Group E, which both clubs had already secured as qualifiers." Both teams had already qualified for the Round of 16 before this decisive match, with Atletico earning the top seed in the group.
