# Esther Rodriguez-Villegas discusses Acurable's wearable respiratory devices

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-07 that Esther Rodriguez-Villegas of Acurable was featured in their "Found" interview series. Acurable is described as a medical device company creating "patient-friendly wearable devices that diagnose and manage respiratory conditions such as sleep apnea at home." The conversation highlighted her background as a career-long academic.
