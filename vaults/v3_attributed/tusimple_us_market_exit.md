# TuSimple relocates to Asia after U.S. workforce reduction

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-09 that TuSimple informed regulators on Monday 4 December 2023 that it is laying off most of its U.S. workforce and selling its American assets to relocate to Asia, following internal controversies and the loss of its Navistar truck-manufacturing partnership. The autonomous truck startup's exit from the U.S. market represents a significant strategic shift amid ongoing challenges in the American commercial vehicle sector.
