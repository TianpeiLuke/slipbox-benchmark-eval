# Defense Argument: Startup Risk vs Fraudulent Intent

Fortune reported on 2023-10-04 that defense lawyer Mark Cohen argued Sam Bankman-Fried didn't defraud anyone, characterizing him as a "nerdy startup founder who acted in 'good faith'" rather than a criminal. Cohen repeatedly stated that "working at a startup is like building a plane as you're flying it" and that businesses sometimes fail, framing FTX's collapse as startup risk-taking rather than fraudulent behavior. He specifically blamed Caroline Ellison for inadequate risk protection at Alameda Research and argued that Bankman-Fried was willing to sacrifice his personal wealth to make customers whole when the crisis hit.
