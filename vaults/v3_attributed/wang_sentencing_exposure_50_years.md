# Wang Faces Up to 50 Years in Prison After Testifying Against SBF

CNBC reported on 2023-10-06 that Gary Wang, former FTX technology chief and co-founder, faces up to 50 years in prison at sentencing following the trial. Wang testified that he committed financial crimes with Sam Bankman-Fried and others, stating "Yes" when asked if he committed financial crimes while working at FTX. He acknowledged seeing Bankman-Fried in the courtroom during his testimony. Wang, 30, cooperated with prosecutors under a six-page agreement requiring truthful answers and evidence turnover.
