# FTX Fraud Verdict and SEC Allegations

The Verge reported on 2023-10-28 that Sam Bankman-Fried, founder of failed cryptocurrency exchange FTX, was found guilty on seven counts including wire fraud. The Securities and Exchange Commission alleged that FTX was a fraud "from the start" with a "multi-billion-dollar deficiency caused by his own misappropriation of customer funds." During his testimony, Bankman-Fried faced repeated judicial scolding for not answering questions directly, while also failing to establish proper risk management systems despite operating a financial exchange.
