# Agent-Based AI Hypothesis for 2024

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-19 that agent-based AI will not take off at scale in 2024, but will demonstrate noticeably more convincing capability than in 2023 and will land a few clutch use cases in famously tedious processes such as submitting insurance claims. The publication stated that "2023 saw a few tentative attempts at this 'agent' approach, but none really caught on. We don't really expect any to really take off in 2024, either, but agent-based models will show their stuff a little more convincingly than they did last year, and a few clutch use cases will show up for famously tedious processes like submitting insurance claims." This prediction is falsified if agents show no credible workflow wins in 2024.
