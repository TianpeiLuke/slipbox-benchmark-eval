# Kelce's Touchdown Catch Met With Swift Lyric Reference

CBSSports.com reported on 2023-10-12 that during Travis Kelce's touchdown reception from Patrick Mahomes on September 17, 2023, CBS Sports play-by-play announcer Ian Eagle described it as "Kelce finds a blank space for the score." This broadcast call marked the beginning of numerous Taylor Swift lyric references made by commentators during NFL games that season, according to the article's timeline of Kelce's rumored relationship with the pop star.
