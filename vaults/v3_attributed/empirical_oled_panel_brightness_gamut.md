# Steam Deck OLED Display Specifications

Engadget reported on 2023-11-09 that the Steam Deck OLED's main upgrade is its HDR OLED display featuring a peak brightness of 1,000 nits, wider P3 color gamut, and pure blacks that make "game blacks merge into the bezel." The 7.4-inch OLED screen replaces the original 7-inch LCD's 400 nit maximum brightness, with Valve stating the new display supports HDR natively and reaches 90Hz refresh rate compared to the original's 60Hz limit.
