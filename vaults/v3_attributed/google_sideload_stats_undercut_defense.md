# Google's Billion-User Sideloading Figure Weakens Monopoly Defense

The Verge reported on 2023-11-07 that Google attorney Glenn Pomerantz claimed over a billion people have enabled sideloading on Android devices, but this figure reportedly measures users who merely enabled the setting rather than those who actually completed sideloading installations. This clarification undermines Google's argument that Android users have meaningful choice in app distribution, since the statistic does not demonstrate actual usage of alternative app stores. The distinction between enabling a setting and completing an installation weakens Google's evidence regarding consumer behavior and market competition.
