# Wall Street's September 2023 Market Performance

The Sydney Morning Herald reported on 2023-10-01 that Wall Street closed out its worst month and quarter of the year with more losses on Friday. "The S&P 500 slipped 0.3 per cent and the majority of stocks within the index sank. The Dow Jones fell 0.5 per cent and the Nasdaq composite edged 0.1 per cent higher." The article noted that solid gains for stocks early on Friday faded as pressure built from within the bond market, with the 10-year Treasury yield returning to 4.58 per cent. Most sectors traded lower, with interest-rate sensitive sectors including healthcare and consumer companies being among the weakest.
