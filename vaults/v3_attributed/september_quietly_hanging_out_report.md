# Swift and Kelce's Quiet Hangouts Confirmed by Media in September 2023

The Age reported on 2023-09-26 that Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce had been "quietly hanging out" for months, marking the first confirmation from professional media rather than amateur Swiftie sleuths on social platforms. This tentative acknowledgment preceded Swift's public appearance at Arrowhead Stadium where she cheered Kelce's touchdown and was seen leaving the stadium with him afterward.
