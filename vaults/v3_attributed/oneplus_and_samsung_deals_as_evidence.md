# Epic's Antitrust Arguments Against Google Focus on Restrictive Agreements

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-06 that Epic Games is pursuing an antitrust trial against Google, arguing the company engages in anticompetitive behavior regarding Android app distribution. Epic's case centers on agreements between Google and device manufacturers that allegedly prevent alternative app stores from competing effectively. "OnePlus had to cancel the deal over concerns that the arrangement would have it bypassing the Google Play Store," according to Epic's claims. The company will also examine a deal between Google and Samsung that Epic says was "designed to prevent the Galaxy Store from being a competitive threat and require Google Play Billing on apps distributed by Samsung." These agreements, Epic argues, mirror anticompetitive practices similar to Microsoft's historical restrictions on browser competition.
