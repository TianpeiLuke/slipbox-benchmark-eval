# AI's Rapid Mainstream Adoption in 2023

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-19 that AI went from niche to mainstream in 2023 faster than almost any comparable technology. The publication characterized this year as a "banger for AI" where the technology experienced unprecedented rapid adoption. This acceleration marked a significant shift from previous years when AI remained primarily within technical and research circles.
