# Kelce's Asymmetric Attraction to Swift During Summer 2023

The Age reported on 2023-09-26 that Travis Kelce's attraction to Taylor Swift was asymmetric during summer 2023. "At this point, it seems like any attraction between the two was one-sided," as Kelce's attempts to contact Swift at her Eras Tour concert in Kansas City failed because "she doesn't talk before or after her shows because she has to save her voice." His July 2023 effort to give her a friendship bracelet with his number was unsuccessful, and he took it personally when she didn't meet him. The structural constraint of Swift's vocal preservation routine prevented any meaningful contact during this initial phase.
