# OpenAI's GPT Store Strategy for 2024

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-19 that OpenAI will push the GPT store through 2024 as the default "app store for AI" for distributing AI tools, following Apple's store model, at the expense of Hugging Face and open-source models. The article states that the "app store for AI" will be pushed hard as the platform to get your AI toys and tools from, and never mind Hugging Face or any open source models. This represents OpenAI's shift toward a product company mindset following its leadership changes, with the GPT store representing their strategy to dominate AI tool distribution. The prediction is falsified if the store fails to ship or becomes a minor distribution channel.
