# Spanish Clubs Dominate 2023/24 Champions League Round of 16 Representation

Sporting News reported on 2023-12-12 that four Spanish clubs secured spots in the 2023/24 Champions League Round of 16. "Real Madrid and Barcelona have also enjoyed strong group-stage campaigns and will surely be in the mix towards the sharp end of the competition." Atletico Madrid and Real Sociedad joined them to complete an unprecedented Spanish dominance, with Real Madrid winning all six group matches and Barcelona finishing strongly in their respective groups.
