# Google's Gemini Pro Falls Short of Multimodal Claims

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-09 that Google's promotional materials for its new AI model Gemini claimed the system could understand images, audio and videos just as well as text. However, the actually shipped product, Gemini Pro, is strictly text-in, text-out and has proven to be mistake-prone. The publication noted that Google was caught faking a Gemini demo by tuning text prompts with still images off camera, undermining its multimodal assertions.
