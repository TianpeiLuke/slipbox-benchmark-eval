# AI Control Debate Framed as Existential Choice Between Elevation and Destruction

The Age reported on 2023-12-09 that the central controversy over AI "has framed an ongoing debate among Silicon Valley founders, chatbot users, academics, legislators and regulators about whether the technology should be controlled or set free." This debate reflects deeper uncertainty about whether AI will elevate the world or inflict grave damage. The article notes that this fundamental question has created divisions even among tech leaders, with figures like Musk, Page, Zuckerberg, Thiel, Nadella, and Altman competing for influence over AI's development while claiming to act in humanity's best interests. The tension illustrates how those most concerned about AI risks are often the same people pursuing its commercial development.
