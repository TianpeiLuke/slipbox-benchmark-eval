# Verge Live Coverage Hub for Bankman-Fried Trial

The Verge reported on 2023-10-10 that its live coverage hub for the Sam Bankman-Fried criminal trial provides "all the latest news and regular updates from the proceedings." The trial involves seven counts of wire fraud and conspiracy, with the SEC alleging that FTX was "a fraud from the start" due to Bankman-Fried's misappropriation of customer funds. Caroline Ellison, former head of Alameda Research, testified that Bankman-Fried "directed me to commit these crimes," including fraud, conspiracy to commit fraud, and money laundering, stating "we ultimately took about $14 billion, some of which we were not able to pay back."
