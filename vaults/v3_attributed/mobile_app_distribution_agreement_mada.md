# Google's Mobile App Distribution Agreement Requires Pre-loading 11 Apps

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-13 that Google requires Android smartphone makers to sign mobile app distribution agreements (MADAs) obliging them to pre-load a bundle of 11 Google apps including Search, Chrome, and Play. Jamie Rosenberg, a Google employee focusing on Android and Google Play, testified on November 8, 2023 that these agreements do not require the apps to be set as user defaults. The testimony came during the Justice Department's antitrust case against Google, where the government argues that Google uses its platforms and deals to block competition in search and advertising.
