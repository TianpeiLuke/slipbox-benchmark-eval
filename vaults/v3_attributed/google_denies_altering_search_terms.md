# Google Denies Altering User Search Terms in Response to Wired Allegation

The Age reported on 2023-10-22 that Google "rejected this in very strong terms, saying the piece was misleading and inaccurate while denying ever altering search terms." This directly rebuts Megan Gray's Wired article that claimed Google manipulates search queries like changing "children's clothing" to "NIKOLAI-brand kidswear" for ad revenue. Google's Danny Sullivan clarified on X that ad systems don't affect organic search results, stating that query matching for ads is separate from the process that determines organic results.
