# Bankman-Fried's Fraudulent Statements About FTX Customer Asset Segregation

The Verge reported on 2023-09-28 that a superseding indictment filed August 14, 2023, alleges Sam Bankman-Fried made false public statements claiming FTX was "avoiding or managing conflicts of interest" and that "as a general principle FTX segregates customer assets from its own assets across our platforms." The indictment claims these statements were lies, as Bankman-Fried was simultaneously directing FTX to permit Alameda Research to withdraw unlimited amounts of money, effectively exempting his trading firm from standard risk management protocols that other customers faced.
