# Epistemology and Self-Testimony in Fraud Cases

The Verge reported on 2023-10-31 that defendants who understand epistemology should avoid testifying in their own defense. During Sam Bankman-Fried's fraud trial, his overly precise language and philosophical hedging about knowledge attribution undermined his credibility. The article argues that fraud liability depends on what defendants knew and when they asked, making self-testimony that meticulously describes one's own inquiry process counterproductive. Bankman-Fried's testimony about not understanding account meanings despite being told about an "$8 billion hole" exemplified how epistemological awareness can harm rather than help a defense.
