# Gary Wang's Courtroom Testimony Against Sam Bankman-Fried

CNBC reported on 2023-10-06 that Gary Wang, FTX co-founder and former MIT classmate of Sam Bankman-Fried, testified in court that he committed financial crimes while working at FTX. "Yes," Wang responded when asked if he committed wire and commodities fraud with Bankman-Fried, Nishad Singh, and Caroline Ellison. Wang described FTX's final days in November 2022, stating that "FTX was not fine and assets were not fine" despite public assurances. He testified under a cooperation agreement requiring truthful testimony and evidence sharing with prosecutors.
