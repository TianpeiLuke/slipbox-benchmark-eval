# Bella Hadid's 2022 Instagram Shadowban Claim

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-19 that Instagram users suspect Meta of shadowbanning Palestine-related content. Bella Hadid, who is of Palestinian descent, alleged in an Instagram Story from 2022 that Instagram "disabled" her from posting Palestine-based content and that her Story views dropped by "almost 1 million" when she posted about Palestine. She claimed she gets "immediately shadowbanned" when posting Palestine-related content. This aligns with broader user complaints about Instagram restricting Palestinian voices, with Meta attributing some issues to technical bugs rather than deliberate suppression.
