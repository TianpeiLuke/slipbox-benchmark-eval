# US Consumer Spending Growth Cools with Mixed Economic Implications

The Sydney Morning Herald reported on 2023-10-01 that "Friday's economic data shows that not only was inflation a touch cooler than expected in August, so was growth in spending by US consumers." This cooler consumer spending growth presents opposing economic forces: it helps reduce inflationary pressures by decreasing demand-pull on prices, but simultaneously weakens a key driver that has prevented the US economy from entering recession. Brian Jacobsen, chief economist at Annex Wealth Management, noted that higher energy prices, student loan repayments, and declining real disposable incomes since June create unfavorable conditions for sustained consumer spending.
