# Linguistic Under-Coverage in Arabic Moderation

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-19 that Meta struggles to navigate the cultural and linguistic nuances of Arabic, a language with over 25 dialects, and has been criticized for neglecting to adequately diversify its language resources. "Meta struggles to navigate the cultural and linguistic nuances of Arabic, a language with over 25 dialects, and has been criticized for neglecting to adequately diversify its language resources." This creates a moderation bias where Arabic dialectal and cultural nuance falls outside classifier and reviewer competence, contributing to systemic suppression of Palestinian voices on Instagram.
