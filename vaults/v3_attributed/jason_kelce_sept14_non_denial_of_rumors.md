# Jason Kelce's Non-Denial of Swift Relationship Rumors

CBSSports.com reported on 2023-10-12 that on September 14, 2023, Jason Kelce was asked about rumors of him hanging out with Taylor Swift and his brother Travis Kelce, and he "did not confirm them, but he also did not deny them." The article presents this non-denial as the substantive entry point for the alleged relationship timeline, rather than any direct statement from Swift or Travis Kelce themselves.
