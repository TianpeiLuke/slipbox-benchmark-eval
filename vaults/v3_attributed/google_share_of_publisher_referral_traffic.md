# Google's Traffic Share to News Publishers

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-15 that Google drives nearly 40% of referral traffic to news publishers' websites, according to data from Similarweb. This figure was cited in a lawsuit filed by Helena World Chronicle, which claims Google's AI technologies are worsening publishers' traffic and revenue losses. The complaint argues that Google's Search Generative Experience and Bard AI chatbot keep users within Google's ecosystem while "plagiarizing" publisher content.
