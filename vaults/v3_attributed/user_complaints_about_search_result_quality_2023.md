# User complaints about Google search quality in 2023

The Verge reported on 2023-11-01 that users characterized Google search results as "absolutely unusable", "garbage" and "a nightmare" because "a lot of the content doesn't feel authentic." The article documented widespread dissatisfaction with search quality, with critics blaming both Google's algorithm and search engine optimization practices for degrading the authenticity of online information discovery.
