# Caroline Ellison's Role at Alameda Research During Relationship with Sam Bankman-Fried

The Verge reported on 2023-10-10 that "Ellison was the head of Alameda Research, the aforementioned hedge fund, during the implosion of it and FTX." Caroline Ellison served as co-CEO of Alameda Research alongside Sam Trabucco in 2021, during her intermittent romantic relationship with Sam Bankman-Fried, who founded the failed cryptocurrency exchange FTX. At the time of Alameda's collapse and FTX's implosion, Ellison held a leadership position at the hedge fund while maintaining her personal relationship with Bankman-Fried, who later became her boss. She eventually pleaded guilty to criminal charges related to fraud, conspiracy, and money laundering connected to the FTX collapse.
