# Interest-rate-sensitive sectors defined

The Sydney Morning Herald reported on 2023-10-01 that interest-rate sensitive sectors include healthcare and consumer companies, whose valuations move strongly with policy rates due to long-dated cash flows or credit-dependent customers. "Most sectors traded lower but interest-rate sensitive sectors including healthcare and consumer companies were among the weakest amid expectations that the RBA will keep rates on hold in October."
