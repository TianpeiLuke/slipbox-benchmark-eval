# Gemini Pro's News Summarization Deficiency

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-07 that Gemini Pro in Bard refused to summarize controversial news topics, instead deflecting to "Google it yourself" despite having access to Google Search and Google News. The author's repeated attempts yielded similarly evasive responses, demonstrating a consistent pattern of refusal plus deflection when handling sensitive current events. This behavior contrasts sharply with ChatGPT's approach of providing cited summaries for the same topics.
