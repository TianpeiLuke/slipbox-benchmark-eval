# Epic's Antitrust Argument Against Apple Initially Rejected by Judge

The Verge reported on 2023-11-05 that Epic's antitrust argument against Apple resulted in a ruling leaving both parties worse off, with Epic faring worse despite Apple's App Store power. The judge found no unfair monopoly and ruled that Epic breached its developer agreement during the initial lawsuit.
