# Google Maps' Viral Restaurant Response

The Verge reported on 2023-10-26 that the official Google Maps account replied "Very relatable" with a crown emoji to a viral tweet about Thai Food Near Me restaurant. The article states the restaurant's name "is both notable and obvious — if you've spent any amount of time searching for things online, you will understand the reference immediately." However, reactions to Google Maps' response in the comment thread were mixed, with one person replying "This is about how your products don't even work anymore."
