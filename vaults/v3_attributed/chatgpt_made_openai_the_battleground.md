# OpenAI's Central Role in the AI Control vs Freedom Debate

The Age reported on 2023-12-09 that "Thanks to its ChatGPT chatbot, OpenAI has fundamentally changed the technology industry and has introduced the world to the risks and potential of artificial intelligence." This development has made OpenAI the central reference point in the ongoing debate about whether AI should be controlled or set free, involving Silicon Valley leaders like Musk, Page, Zuckerberg, Thiel, Nadella, and Altman. The article notes that OpenAI is now valued at more than $80 billion, despite internal conflicts that nearly tore the company apart, revealing the intense competition among tech's elite to shape AI's future while claiming they alone can prevent it from endangering humanity.
