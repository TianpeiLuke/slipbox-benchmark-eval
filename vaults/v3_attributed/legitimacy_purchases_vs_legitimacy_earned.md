# FTX's $5B Political Offer as Evidence of Underlying Weakness

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-02 that Sam Bankman-Fried allegedly offered $5 billion to Donald Trump to cancel his presidential re-run, according to Michael Lewis's interview on 60 Minutes. This revelation implicitly rebuts the notion that FTX's extensive sponsorships, endorsements, and political donations demonstrated the company's financial strength. Instead of proving soundness, these activities represented "purchases of legitimacy" funded by speculative assets, which ultimately collapsed when the underlying business model failed. The same pattern of using marketing and political influence to mask fundamental weaknesses that characterized FTX's rise became evident in its dramatic downfall.
