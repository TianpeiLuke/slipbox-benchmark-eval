# Bankman-Fried faces trial on seven counts of wire fraud and conspiracy

The Verge reported on 2023-10-10 that Sam Bankman-Fried, founder of collapsed cryptocurrency exchange FTX, is on trial for "seven counts of wire fraud and conspiracy." The Securities and Exchange Commission alleges that FTX was "a fraud from the start," with a "multi-billion-dollar deficiency caused by his own misappropriation of customer funds." Former Alameda Research head Caroline Ellison, who pleaded guilty to related criminal charges, testified that Bankman-Fried "directed me to commit these crimes" involving fraud, conspiracy to commit fraud, and money laundering.
