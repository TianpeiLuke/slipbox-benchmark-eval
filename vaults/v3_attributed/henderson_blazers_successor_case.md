# Scoot Henderson's Rookie of the Year Prospects

Sporting News reported on 2023-10-04 that Scoot Henderson's Rookie of the Year chances depend on Portland's restructuring around him as Damian Lillard's successor. The article states that "after a stellar season with the G League Ignite, expectations for his rookie year are soaring" with supporting cast including Anfernee Simons and Jerami Grant positioned to absorb secondary load as the Blazers' fortunes hinge on Henderson's performance.
