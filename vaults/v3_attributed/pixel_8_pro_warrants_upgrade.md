# Darrell's Hands-On Assessment of Pixel 8 Pro Upgrade Potential

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-07 that Darrell found the Pixel 8 Pro to be "enough of an upgrade over the Pixel 7 Pro to potentially justify buying it." The improvement factors cited were enhanced cameras, a brighter screen, and upgraded AI-powered features. However, this positive assessment was tempered by the device's nearly identical design to its predecessor and an underutilized temperature sensor. Darrell's preliminary evaluation suggests the Pixel 8 Pro offers meaningful upgrades despite these limitations, though he noted that a comprehensive review would provide final determination.
