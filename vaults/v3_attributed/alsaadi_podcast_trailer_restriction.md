# Palestinian Journalist's Account Restricted After Posting Podcast Trailer

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-19 that Palestinian journalist Leen Al Saadi, based in Jordan, had her Instagram account restricted in 2022 after posting a trailer for the debut episode of the 'Preserving Palestine' podcast. The podcast documents Palestinian street art under occupation. This incident reflects broader concerns about Meta's moderation practices affecting Palestinian voices on the platform.
