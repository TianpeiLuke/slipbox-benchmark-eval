# Google's Legal Defense of Default Search Status

The Age reported on 2023-10-22 that Google's president of global affairs Kent Walker defended the company's dominant search position, arguing that Google is preferred by users, browsers, and device makers because its search technology is superior. Walker contended that default status is not determinative since switching search providers is easy, and that the lawsuit ignores how competitive the technology industry currently is. He cited examples where users actively chose Google even when it wasn't the default, such as when Mozilla set Yahoo! as default on Firefox or when Windows users frequently switched back to Google despite Microsoft's efforts to promote Bing. "People don't use Google because they have to, they use it because they want to," Walker stated.
