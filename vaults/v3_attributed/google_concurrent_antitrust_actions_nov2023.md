# Google's Multiple Antitrust Cases in Late 2023

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-13 that Google faced three search- and store-related antitrust matters simultaneously: the DOJ case, a settled suit with Match Group, and the Epic Games trial that began November 6 over Google Play's commission structure. "This isn't Google's only antitrust case in trial right now. The search engine giant last week settled a separate antitrust lawsuit with dating site Match Group. On November 6, Google went to trial with Fortnite maker Epic Games. The latter hopes to prove that Google engages in anticompetitive behavior with regard to its Android app store, Google Play, and its commission structure."
