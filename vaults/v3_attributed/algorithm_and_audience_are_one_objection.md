# Counter-argument to SEO-focused business naming strategy

The Verge reported on 2023-10-26 that the restaurant "Thai Food Near Me" exemplifies businesses optimizing for Google search algorithms. However, this approach faces a fundamental objection: "Google Maps — the very entity the meme-y restaurant name is designed to outsmart — couldn't resist" engaging with and amplifying the restaurant's viral social media presence. This demonstrates that Google, as both algorithm operator and social media participant, becomes part of the intended audience despite being the target of the SEO gaming strategy. The company's public response shows that algorithmic optimization alone cannot dismiss human engagement from the platform's operators.
