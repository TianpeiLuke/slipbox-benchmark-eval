# SEO-Optimized Restaurant Strategy

The Verge reported on 2023-10-26 that Thai Food Near Me is a restaurant "optimized for the digital platforms diners use to find places nearby, not for the person walking past on the street or getting a recommendation from a friend." The establishment leverages search engine optimization by using a name that directly matches common Google search queries, making it highly discoverable online at the expense of traditional word-of-mouth marketing and street-level visibility.
