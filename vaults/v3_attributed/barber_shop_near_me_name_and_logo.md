# Felix Silva's 2019 Naming Decision for Barber Shop Near Me

The Verge reported on 2023-10-26 that Felix Silva chose "Barber Shop Near Me" over more than 20 alternatives for his Coral Springs, Florida store in 2019. The article states that Silva "decided on the name Barber Shop Near Me after considering more than 20 other options for his Coral Springs, Florida, store in 2019." He also created a logo featuring "a red location pin resembling Google's own, with a blue, white, and red barber pole pattern in the middle."
