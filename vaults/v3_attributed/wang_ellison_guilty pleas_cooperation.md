# FTX Co-Founder and Alameda CEO Plead Guilty, Plan to Cooperate

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-01 that "FTX co-founder and former CTO Gary Wang, and Alameda Research's former CEO, Caroline Ellison, both pleaded guilty in December 2022 to federal criminal charges in relation to the FTX collapse." The article states they "plan to cooperate with prosecutors and will be major witnesses in the trial, given their close ties to Bankman-Fried, FTX and Alameda." Both individuals also face civil penalties from the SEC and CFTC alongside the criminal charges.
