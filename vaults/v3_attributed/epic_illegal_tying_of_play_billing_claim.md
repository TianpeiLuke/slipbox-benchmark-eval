# Epic's Antitrust Claim Against Google on Payment Tying

The Verge reported on 2023-11-05 that Epic claims Google illegally ties its Google Play payments platform to the Google Play store, foreclosing rival in-app payment mechanisms from competing. Epic argues this creates an illegal monopoly that unjustly enriches Google while artificially driving up app prices because other stores cannot compete with Google's integrated payment system.
