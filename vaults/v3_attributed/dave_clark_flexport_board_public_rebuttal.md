# Dave Clark's Public Response to Flexport Reporting

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-07 that Dave Clark, the former Amazon executive who was ousted as CEO of Flexport after one year, publicly criticized recent CNBC reporting about his departure as "deeply concerning." Clark made these remarks on Monday via a lengthy social media post on X, responding to details revealed about his final days at the $8 billion freight forwarding and customs brokerage startup. He specifically called out Flexport's founder and board following the media coverage.
