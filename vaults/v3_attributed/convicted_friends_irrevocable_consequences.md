# FTX Trial Defendants Face Lasting Consequences Beyond Prison Time

The Verge reported on 2023-10-26 that Gary Wang, Nishad Singh, and Caroline Ellison are now felons whose lives are permanently altered regardless of whether they serve prison sentences. The article states they face ongoing consequences including "whatever restitution requirements they are subjected to" and are "barred from taking certain kinds of jobs." Additionally, "there is the publicity" that tarnishes their reputations, making them "suddenly undesirable" in political circles where they once operated. These lasting impacts persist independently of any custodial sentence they may receive.
