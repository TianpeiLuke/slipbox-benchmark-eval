# Bankman-Fried's Direct Testimony Coherence Hypothesis

The Verge reported on 2023-10-28 that Sam Bankman-Fried's direct testimony showed improved clarity compared to previous hearings. The article states he was "clearer and much easier to understand" with "a minimum of word salad today." The reporter hypothesized this improvement reflected either Bankman-Fried being "more relaxed, or he'd just been more rehearsed," and explicitly noted an intention to monitor whether his coherence would "suddenly becomes much less coherent when Sassoon gets him for the cross."
