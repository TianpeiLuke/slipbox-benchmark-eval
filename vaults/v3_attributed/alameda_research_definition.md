# Alameda Research Hedge Fund Implosion

The Verge reported on 2023-10-10 that Alameda Research was a hedge fund created by Sam Bankman-Fried which Caroline Ellison headed during its joint implosion with FTX. "Ellison was the head of Alameda Research, the aforementioned hedge fund, during the implosion of it and FTX," according to testimony in Bankman-Fried's criminal fraud trial. The fund was deeply troubled financially from early 2018, initially funded with loans from acquaintances that were recalled shortly after Ellison's arrival. She later pleaded guilty to criminal charges and testified that Bankman-Fried "directed me to commit these crimes" involving fraud, conspiracy, and money laundering, stating "We ultimately took about $14 billion, some of which we were not able to pay back."
