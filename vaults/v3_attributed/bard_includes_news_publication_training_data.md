# Google Bard AI Training Data Allegedly Includes Publisher Content

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-15 that a class action lawsuit filed by Helena World Chronicle alleges Google's Bard chatbot was trained on a dataset that included "news, magazine and digital publications." The complaint cites a 2023 News Media Alliance report and a Washington Post article as sources for this claim, arguing that Google's AI technologies harm publishers' revenues by using their content without consent.
