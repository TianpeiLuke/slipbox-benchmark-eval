# Sam Bankman-Fried Damages His Own Credibility During Direct Examination

The Verge reported on 2023-10-28 that Sam Bankman-Fried "torpedo his own credibility on direct testimony" before cross-examination began, as the judge had to instruct him to answer his lawyer's questions. The jury observed Bankman-Fried repeatedly failing to respond directly to his counsel Mark Cohen's questions, with the judge intervening to enforce proper courtroom conduct. According to the report, "We're not even at the cross yet, and the judge has already instructed him to answer the question he's being asked by his own lawyer." The article noted that Bankman-Fried's tendency to not answer questions directly was evident early in his testimony, establishing a pattern of evasiveness that preceded any cross-examination challenges.
