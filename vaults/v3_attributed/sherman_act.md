# Epic's Antitrust Claims Under Sherman Act Sections 1 and 2 Against Google

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-06 that Epic Games plans to bring claims under Sections 1 and 2 of U.S. antitrust law, the Sherman Act, in its trial against Google. Section 1 concerns restraints of competition, while Section 2 addresses monopolization. Epic's case alleges Google engages in anticompetitive behavior regarding Google Play's commission structure and app distribution restrictions, distinguishing it from Epic's previous Apple lawsuit because Android allows sideloading apps.
