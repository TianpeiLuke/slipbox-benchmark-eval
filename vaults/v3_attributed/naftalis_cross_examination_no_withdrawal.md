# Naftalis's 'Hail Mary' Characterization of SBF Testimony

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-06 that former federal prosecutor Josh Naftalis characterized Sam Bankman-Fried's decision to testify in his own trial as a "Hail Mary" maneuver. Naftalis explained that once cross-examination begins, the defendant loses control over their testimony: "Once it goes to cross[-examination], he doesn't get to say, 'I'm done,'... He can't just walk out if he doesn't like how it's going." This highlights the strategic risk SBF faces, as unlike direct examination where a witness can qualify or withdraw answers, cross-examination forces the defendant to continue regardless of unfavorable questioning direction.
