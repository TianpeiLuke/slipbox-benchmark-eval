# NBA Rookie of the Year Futures Betting Period

Sporting News reported on 2023-10-04 that NBA Rookie of the Year futures betting "can be bet on before the start of the NBA regular season and throughout the year." These futures markets allow bettors to wager on which player will win the award, with odds posted by online sportsbooks and settled at season's end. The betting period extends from pre-season through the regular season, similar to other NBA futures markets.
