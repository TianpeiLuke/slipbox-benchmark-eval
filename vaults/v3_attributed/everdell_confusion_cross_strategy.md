# Defense attorney's spot-margin questioning aimed to discredit Wang's reliability

The Verge reported on 2023-10-10 that defense attorney Christian Everdell's cross-examination of Gary Wang focused on spot margin trading technicalities to make Wang appear unreliable to the jury. Everdell "tried to undermine this claim by talking about the spot margin program, which let users lend each other assets for margin trading" and created confusion about whether accounts could have negative balances. However, this line of questioning served only as a "sideshow" because it could not undo the damaging code-review testimony from the previous week, which had established that Alameda received special treatment unavailable to other FTX customers.
