# NFL MVP Award Trends Favor Quarterbacks from Top-Seeded Teams

Sporting News reported on 2023-12-05 that the NFL MVP award "often ends up being someone, typically the quarterback, from one of the top-seeded teams." This explains why Brock Purdy, Dak Prescott, and Jalen Hurts were leading MVP betting boards after Week 13, as they represent quarterbacks from teams positioned for high seeds. The pattern reflects bettors' emphasis on both individual performance and team success when determining MVP favorites.
