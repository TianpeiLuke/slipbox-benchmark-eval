# Apple's Browser Choice Restriction During Google Deal Period

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-31 that during the Google agreement period, Apple never switched to a Google search competitor and never let iPhone users choose a browser during device setup. "The agreement also meant that Apple never switched to a Google competitor or allowed users to choose their browser when setting up their iPhones." This arrangement prevented users from selecting alternative browsers like Chrome during iOS device initialization, maintaining Google's default position.
