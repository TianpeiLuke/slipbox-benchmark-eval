# FTX Software Bug Overestimated Alameda Debt by $8 Billion

The Verge reported on 2023-10-31 that a software bug at FTX overestimated the amount Alameda Research owed to FTX by roughly $8 billion. The bug was fixed by FTX employee Adam Yedidia in June 2022. According to Yedidia's testimony, he informed Sam Bankman-Fried about the "$8 billion hole" on a padel tennis court in their luxury complex during June or July 2022. The bug related to the "fiat@ftx" account that tracked Alameda's obligations to FTX.
