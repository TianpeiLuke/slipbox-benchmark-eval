# SEO-Driven Business Naming Procedure

The Verge reported on 2023-10-26 that successful SEO-focused business naming follows a specific procedure: establish the literal query phrase customers use, reject arbitrary or brand-only candidates, keep the phrase neutral and memorable, and pair it with search-visible assets such as a listing and a keyword-bearing review. Teddy Jirapraphanan of "Thai Food Near Me" and Felix Silva of "Barber Shop Near Me" demonstrated this method by choosing names that mirror actual Google search queries. "When you have a million restaurants close by, you will be in the bottom [of rankings] if it's a random name," Jirapraphanan explained, noting that their strategic naming approach helped customers discover them through search. Silva similarly observed that businesses with strong Google presence perform better, stating this realization came when he saw Google return non-vegan restaurant results simply because a review contained the keyword "vegan."
