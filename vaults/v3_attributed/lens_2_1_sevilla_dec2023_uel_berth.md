# Lens secure Europa League berth with victory over Sevilla

Sporting News reported on 2023-12-12 that Lens defeated Sevilla 2-1 on December 12, 2023, in the Champions League Group B final matchday, securing third place and a Europa League berth. "Lens beat Sevilla 2-1 on 12 December 2023 to make certain of the Europa League berth in Group B." The victory came while PSV and Arsenal drew 1-1 in the group's other simultaneous match, with Arsenal finishing first and PSV claiming second place.
