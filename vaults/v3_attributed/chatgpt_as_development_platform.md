# ChatGPT as OpenAI's Priority Platform

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-30 that internally at OpenAI, ChatGPT became "priority number one" and evolved from being "simply a one-off product but a development platform to build upon." Originally launched as a basic web interface for GPT-3.5, ChatGPT transformed into OpenAI's most popular product and fastest-growing consumer app, gaining enterprise features, web searching, document analysis, and multimodal capabilities including voice and image processing.
