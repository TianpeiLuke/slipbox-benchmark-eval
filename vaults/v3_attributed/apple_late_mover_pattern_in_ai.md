# Apple's Refined AI Entry Strategy

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-19 that Apple follows a competitive pattern of waiting for rivals to fail before entering markets with polished products that outsell competitors. The article states Apple "has an established pattern of waiting, watching and learning from other companies' failures, then blowing in with a refined and polished take that puts others to shame." This approach is expected to repeat in AI because the technology is now ripe for Apple's refinement, and further delays risk ceding market share to competitors.
