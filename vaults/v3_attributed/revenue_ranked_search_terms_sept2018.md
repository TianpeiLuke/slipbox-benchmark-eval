# Google's Top Revenue-Generating Search Terms Revealed in Antitrust Trial

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-13 that Judge Amit Mehta ordered public a list of the 20 search terms that earned Google the most revenue in one week in late September 2018. The list was led by "iPhone 8," "iPhone 8 Plus," "auto insurance," and "car insurance." However, specific revenue figures and query volumes for these terms remained redacted in the court documents. The disclosed terms also included searches for "cheap flights," "DirecTV," "online colleges," "AT&T," "Hulu," "Uber," "Spectrum," "Comcast," "Xfinity," "insurance quotes," "free credit report," "cheap car insurance," "AARP," and "LifeLock."
