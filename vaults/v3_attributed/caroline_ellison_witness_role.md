# Caroline Ellison's Role in FTX Fraud Case

CNBC reported on 2023-10-06 that Caroline Ellison operated FTX's sister hedge fund Alameda Research, was Sam Bankman-Fried's former girlfriend, and pleaded guilty in December 2022. She is considered the prosecution's star witness with a cooperation arrangement similar to Gary Wang's. "Mr. Wang, do you see any of the people you committed those crimes with in the courtroom today?" prosecutor Nicolas Roos asked, to which Wang confirmed seeing Bankman-Fried and implicitly identified Ellison as another co-conspirator present in the scheme involving wire and commodities fraud.
