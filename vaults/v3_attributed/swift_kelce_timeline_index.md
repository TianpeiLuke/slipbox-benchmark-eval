# Timeline Navigation of Swift-Kelce Crossover Events

The Age reported on 2023-09-26 that Taylor Swift's NFL connection with Travis Kelce unfolded across eight key moments from 2016 to September 2023. The timeline begins with Kelce's 2016 game show comment choosing to "kiss Swift" and progresses through his July 2023 bracelet attempt, August moustache remarks and NYC trip, September reports of "quietly hanging out," CBS's 17th broadcast lyric references, Kelce's 21st public invitation, and Swift's Monday game appearance. "The first link between Swift and Kelce was established in 2016" when he played "Kiss, Marry, Kill" and chose Swift as the kiss option. The chronology culminates with Swift's September 25th stadium sighting supporting Kelce's team despite being an Eagles fan.
