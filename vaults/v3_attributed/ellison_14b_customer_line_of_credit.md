# Caroline Ellison's Testimony on $14 Billion Customer Fund Misuse

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-06 that Alameda Research's former CEO Caroline Ellison testified in the SBF trial that she took "$14 billion from customers to repay debts to lenders, using them as a line of credit under the instruction of SBF." Ellison detailed how customer funds were diverted to cover Alameda's financial obligations, including funding a $2 billion venture fund called FTX Ventures. Her testimony directly implicated SBF in directing the misuse of customer deposits, which were also used to finance political causes, charities, and real estate purchases.
