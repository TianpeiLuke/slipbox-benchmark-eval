# Meta Communications Director Attributes Reduced Story Reach to Technical Bug

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-19 that Meta communications director Andy Stone attributed reduced Story reach to a technical bug affecting all Stories that re-shared Reels and Feed posts. Stone stated the issue "hit accounts equally worldwide, was unrelated to subject matter, and was fixed quickly." This explanation was provided in response to user complaints about shadowbanning of Palestine-related content, with Stone's Threads post saying "We identified a bug impacting all Stories that re-shared Reels and Feed posts, meaning this impacted accounts equally, regardless of geography or topic, and we fixed it quickly."
