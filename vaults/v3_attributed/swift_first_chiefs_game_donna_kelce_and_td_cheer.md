# Swift's First Chiefs Game Attendance Observed

CBSSports.com reported on 2023-10-12 that Taylor Swift attended her first Kansas City Chiefs game approximately one month prior to October 12, 2023, during which she was observed "chatting with the tight end's mom, Donna Kelce, and cheering emphatically when No. 87 scored a touchdown." This empirical observation of Swift's public behavior at the stadium was documented by CBSSports.com reporters present at the event, rather than any statement issued by Swift herself regarding her attendance or interactions.
