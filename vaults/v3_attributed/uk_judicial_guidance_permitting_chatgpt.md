# U.K. Judges Permitted to Use ChatGPT for Legal Rulings

TechCrunch reported on 2023-09-28 that the U.K. Judicial Office issued guidance permitting judges to use ChatGPT and other AI tools in legal rulings. The guidance outlines responsible AI usage in courts, including awareness of potential bias and privacy protection. However, the source text truncates the specific conditions and limitations of this permission. The publication notes that this development reflects the broader integration of AI tools into professional workflows, though concerns remain about AI reliability and appropriate usage boundaries. "The U.K. Judicial Office issued guidance that permits judges to use ChatGPT, along with other AI tools, to write legal rulings and perform court duties."
