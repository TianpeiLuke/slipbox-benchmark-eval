# Android's Openness as a Competitive Boundary

The Verge reported on 2023-11-07 that Android's allowance of alternate app channels fundamentally distinguishes it from iOS's closed ecosystem. Google's attorney Glenn Pomerantz emphasized this "comparatively open" nature during the Epic v. Google trial, arguing that Android permits sideloading and alternative app stores unlike Apple's restrictive approach. "Google points to the simple fact that it allows alternate app channels at all — something Android rival iOS doesn't." The defense highlighted that over a billion people have used these alternative installation methods, suggesting that Android's openness undermines Epic's monopoly claims. This openness represents the core boundary Google uses to differentiate its platform from Apple's walled garden, despite Epic's argument that Google has effectively "closed off each and every other option" through anti-competitive practices.
