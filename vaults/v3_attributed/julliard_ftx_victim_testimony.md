# First Prosecution Witness Describes Failed FTX Withdrawal

Fortune reported on 2023-10-04 that Marc-Antoine Julliard, a Paris-born cocoa trader living in London who chose FTX in 2021 and traded Bitcoin and Dogecoin, testified for the prosecution on 2023-10-04 that on Nov. 8, in the exchange's final days, he tried to withdraw almost $100,000 and was "Never" able to.
