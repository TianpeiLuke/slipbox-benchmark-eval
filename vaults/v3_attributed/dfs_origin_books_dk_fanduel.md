# DraftKings and FanDuel Originated as Daily Fantasy Sports Platforms

Sporting News reported on 2023-10-04 that DraftKings and FanDuel "offer[ed] up a nice variety of futures bets, including the NBA Rookie of the Year award" after transitioning from daily fantasy sports (DFS) operators to legal online sportsbooks. Their DFS origins contributed to "intuitive" apps and "competitive" welcome bonuses.
