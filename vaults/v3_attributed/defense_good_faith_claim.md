# Defence opens with claim that Sam Bankman-Fried acted in good faith

Fortune reported on 2023-10-04 that defence lawyer Mark Cohen opened the Sam Bankman-Fried trial by stating "Sam didn't defraud anyone," presenting the defendant as a nerdy startup founder who acted in good faith rather than the prosecution's portrayal as a "cartoon of a villain." Cohen argued that Bankman-Fried built "two billion-dollar businesses" and didn't steal any money.
