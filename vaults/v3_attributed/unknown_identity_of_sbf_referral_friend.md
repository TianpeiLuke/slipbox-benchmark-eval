# Berkeley Friend's Identity and FTX Investment Remain Unknown

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-02 that Michael Lewis was introduced to Sam Bankman-Fried in early 2021 by a Berkeley friend who wanted to evaluate SBF's character before investing in FTX. "No, we don't find out who that friend is, nor do we find out if that particular investment was ever made," according to the article. While SBF raised $2 billion including $1 billion in 2021 from major investors, the identity of this initial introducer and whether they personally invested remains unresolved in both the 60 Minutes interview and Lewis's book coverage.
