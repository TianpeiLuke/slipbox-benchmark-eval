# Kelce's 2023 Moustache and Swift Connection Hint

The Age reported on 2023-09-26 that in August 2023, Kelce discussed his annual moustache grown in honor of Chiefs head coach Andy Reid on his "New Heights" podcast. When asked about Taylor Swift's potential opinion on his facial hair, Kelce said: "We're not going to bring up Taylor Swift in this episode … But something tells me she's going to like it." This comment suggested he had made contact with Swift and potentially planned to meet her, preceding his later invitation for her to attend a Chiefs game.
