# Kelce's Concert Attendance and Friendship Bracelet Gesture

CBSSports.com reported on 2023-10-12 that Travis Kelce attended Taylor Swift's concert but did not meet her, stating "I was disappointed that she doesn't talk before or after her shows because she has to save her voice for the 44 songs she sings... She doesn't meet anybody, or at least she didn't want to meet me, so I took it personal." Kelce made a friendship bracelet with his number to give to Swift and discussed this on his July 26, 2023 "New Heights" podcast episode.
