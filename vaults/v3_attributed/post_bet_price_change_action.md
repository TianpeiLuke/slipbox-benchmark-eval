# Post-Bet Line Movement Protocol

Sporting News reported on 2023-11-06 that "when you bet, you lock in the odds or line as currently posted. If they change, for better or worse, after you've placed your bet, your wager stands at the original price." This establishes the fundamental procedure that after a bet is placed and locked, the only way to act on a subsequent significant line move is to place additional action at the improved odds or more favourable line - the original wager cannot be repriced.
