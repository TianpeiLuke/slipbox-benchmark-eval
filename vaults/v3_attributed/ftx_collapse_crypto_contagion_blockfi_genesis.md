# FTX Collapse Triggered Broader Crypto Industry Distress

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-01 that FTX's collapse in November 2022 triggered broader crypto industry distress, with BlockFi filing for Chapter 11 in November 2022 and Genesis Global Trading following in January 2023. The exchange's downfall began when CoinDesk published Alameda Research's balance sheet revealing inflated FTT token holdings, leading to a bank run after Binance withdrew $2.1 billion. "There were virtually no internal controls and no separateness whatsoever," said John J. Ray III, FTX's appointed CEO, during a House Financial Services Committee hearing.
