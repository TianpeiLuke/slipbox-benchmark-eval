# Stella Biderman's Definition of AI User Experience Breakthrough

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-30 that Stella Biderman, an AI researcher at Booz Allen Hamilton and EleutherAI, distinguished between AI breakthroughs and user experience breakthroughs, stating that ChatGPT was the latter rather than the former. Biderman explained that ChatGPT's primary impact was "encouraging people training AIs to try to mimic it, or encouraging people studying AIs to use it as their central object of study." She noted that previously, users needed some skill to consistently get usable results from text-generating models, but ChatGPT changed this by bringing generative AI mainstream and attracting significant attention and discussion about the technology.
