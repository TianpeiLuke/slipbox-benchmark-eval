# Epic's Antitrust Hypothesis Against Google's Android App Store Practices

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-06 that Epic Games' central antitrust argument is that Google's control over Android creates a monopoly similar to Microsoft's past dominance, where "though Epic will suggest Google makes that process cumbersome." Epic's testable proposition is that Google makes sideloading and alternative stores cumbersome enough that formal availability is not effective competition; it is falsified if consumers install alternative stores at scale absent Google's agreements. The company argues Google's Anti-Fragmentation Agreements prevent OEMs from easily allowing apps outside Google Play, citing examples like OnePlus canceling a direct Epic Games app deal due to Google's restrictions. Epic claims 90% of Android apps are still downloaded through Google Play despite theoretical alternatives like Samsung's Galaxy Store and Amazon's Appstore being available.
