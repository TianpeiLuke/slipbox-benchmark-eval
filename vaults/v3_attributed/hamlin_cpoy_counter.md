# Hamlin's Comeback Player of the Year candidacy questioned due to limited game-day activity

Sporting News reported on 2023-12-05 that Demar Hamlin's candidacy for Comeback Player of the Year is questionable because "Hamlin has seldom been active on game day and rarely seen the field." The article notes that while coming back from a life-threatening cardiac arrest makes him "a great candidate to win CPOY," voters might look elsewhere since his on-field contribution has been minimal relative to the award's purpose, which typically emphasizes returning to significant playing impact rather than just overcoming adversity.
