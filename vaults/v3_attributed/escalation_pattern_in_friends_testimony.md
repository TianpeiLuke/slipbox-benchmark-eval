# Bankman-Fried's Escalating Misconduct Pattern

The Verge reported on 2023-10-26 that testimony from Sam Bankman-Fried's former friends reveals "things just kept escalating" in terms of his misconduct, rather than a single isolated decision. The article suggests this pattern emerged through testimonies of individuals like Adam Yedidia, Gary Wang, and others who were close to Bankman-Fried but became entangled in fraudulent activities at FTX and Alameda Research. However, the publication notes this generalization remains undeveloped in the text where it breaks off mid-sentence.
