# Naftalis characterizes SBF's late testimony as strategic gamble

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-06 that former federal prosecutor Josh Naftalis characterized Sam Bankman-Fried's decision to testify in his own fraud trial as a "Hail Mary" rather than a sound strategic move. Naftalis explained that once SBF takes the stand and faces cross-examination, "he doesn't get to say, 'I'm done,''' and cannot simply withdraw from testimony if it proceeds unfavorably. The comment came as the trial entered its final phases with SBF facing seven counts of fraud and money laundering related to the collapse of FTX and Alameda Research.
