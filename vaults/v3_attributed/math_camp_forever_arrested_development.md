# Co-conspirators appeared unusually young in court due to arrested development in Bankman-Fried's culture

The Verge reported on 2023-10-26 that the FTX trial revealed how Sam Bankman-Fried's environment stunted the growth of his associates. The author observed that "All the co-conspirators seem oddly younger than their years, as though they got stuck in the 'math camp forever' environment Bankman-Fried created for them and simply failed to grow up." This observation came during the prosecution's case against Bankman-Fried, who is charged with seven counts including wire fraud and conspiracy. The article suggests that the culture Bankman-Fried built around himself and his close associates - including roommates Adam Yedidia and Gary Wang, girlfriend Caroline Ellison, and longtime friend Nishad Singh - created an insular world that prevented normal maturation. These individuals, now facing legal consequences and ruined reputations, were described as having been frozen in a perpetual state of youthful deference to Bankman-Fried's authority and lifestyle.
