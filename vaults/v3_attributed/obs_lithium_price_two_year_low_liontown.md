# Lithium Prices Hit Two-Year Low While Liontown Defies Sector Trend

The Sydney Morning Herald reported on 2023-10-01 that lithium prices fell to their lowest level in two years. Despite this sector-wide downturn, lithium miner Liontown "resisted the downward momentum of lithium prices." The report noted that iron ore heavyweights advanced after iron ore prices increased 1.4 percent overnight, helping to bolster the local bourse.
