# Line Shopping Definition in Sports Betting

Sporting News reported on 2023-11-06 that line shopping is "the process of checking the lines and odds for your prospective bet at multiple online sports betting sites" in order to secure improved odds or a more favourable line. The practice involves comparing betting lines across different sportsbooks to find better prices on wagers, as each sportsbook's odds and lines can vary based on their customer betting patterns and risk management strategies.
