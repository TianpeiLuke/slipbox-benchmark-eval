# SEO's "Almighty Algorithm" Is Technically Many Algorithms

The Verge reported on 2023-11-01 that the "almighty algorithm" which SEO practitioners claim to defeat is actually comprised of many algorithms, though it exists as a singular concept only in popular imagination. As one SEO veteran explained, practitioners view themselves as having "outdone Google" when they succeed in manipulating search rankings. The article notes that "we think of [the algorithm] as a single force" even though it technically "constitutes many algorithms."
