# Bankman-Fried's Response to $8 Billion Shortfall

The Verge reported on 2023-10-31 that instead of escalating concerns or halting operations when an $8 billion shortfall persisted after fixing a software bug, Sam Bankman-Fried directed alleged co-conspirator Caroline Ellison to repay third-party loans and continued making investments. According to the report, Bankman-Fried discovered the fiat@ftx account in June 2022 but did not understand its purpose until October, and when he questioned employees about it, they told him "they were busy and I should stop asking questions because it was distracting."
