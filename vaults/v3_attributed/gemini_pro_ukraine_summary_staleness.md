# Gemini Pro's Outdated Ukraine War Summary

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-07 that Gemini Pro in Bard provided a summary of war-in-Ukraine updates when prompted, but "the information was over a month out of date." The article demonstrated this by showing Gemini Pro's responses contained outdated information despite having access to Google Search and Google News. While the model was willing to discuss the topic unlike other controversial subjects, its recency accuracy fell significantly short of expectations for a current events summary.
