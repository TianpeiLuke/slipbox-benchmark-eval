# OpenAI Leadership Fallout May Benefit Meta and AI Startups

TechCrunch reported on 2023-09-28 that OpenAI's leadership turmoil "might empower competitors like Meta and its LLaMA family of large language models, or help other AI startups get attention and funding as the industry watches OpenAI implode and put itself back together." The article noted that Sam Altman's firing and swift return raised concerns about OpenAI's direction, creating opportunities for competitors as the company navigates its leadership challenges.
