# Viral Twitter Photo Boosts Thai Food Near Me Restaurant

The Verge reported on 2023-10-26 that a photo of Thai Food Near Me's sign went viral on Twitter in March 2023, before the restaurant's opening. The article states that the viral post showed the restaurant's sign with "brown butcher paper still covering the windows" and that this "goofy name was enough to garner coverage on news sites, along with an influx of customers in the first crucial months of being open." This social media attention preceded the restaurant's actual opening and contributed to early customer interest.
