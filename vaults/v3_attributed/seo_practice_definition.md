# Search Engine Optimization as Algorithmic Manipulation

The Verge reported on 2023-11-01 that search engine optimization is the practice of telling a search algorithm whatever it needs to hear so that a site appears as high as possible in organic results. The publication described SEO as "less straightforward than buying the advertising space labeled 'Sponsored' above organic search results; it's more like the Wizard of Oz projecting his voice to magnify his authority." This manipulation leverages Google's supposed objectivity to lure people into viewing advertising content, creating a business model where SEO techniques have become increasingly insidious and widespread.
