# FTX's Mainstream Recognition Through Celebrity Partnerships

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-01 that FTX gained mainstream recognition through strategic branding deals including naming rights to the Miami Heat's arena in 2021, placing its logo on Major League Baseball umpires' polos, and securing celebrity partnerships with Tom Brady, Gisele Bündchen, Steph Curry, Shaquille O'Neal, and Naomi Osaka. The article states FTX "gained somewhat mainstream recognition with branding deals and partnerships" during its rapid growth period before its November 2022 collapse.
