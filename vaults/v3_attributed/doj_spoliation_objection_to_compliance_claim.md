# DOJ Accuses Google of Systematically Destroying Chats Despite Litigation Hold

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-31 that the Department of Justice accused Google in February 2023 of systematically destroying internal chat messages through its "history-off" default setting, which automatically deletes messages every 24 hours unless users manually change the setting. This practice continued despite Google being subject to a litigation hold. During Sundar Pichai's testimony, DOJ lawyer Meagan Bellshaw highlighted a 2021 message exchange where Pichai himself requested history to be turned off in a group chat. The DOJ's accusation directly challenges Pichai's claim that he takes great care to comply with the litigation hold, particularly since he only took action against the history-off default for chat in February 2023 after the DOJ raised concerns.
