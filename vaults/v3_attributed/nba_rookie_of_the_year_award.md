# NBA Rookie of the Year as Futures Betting Market

Sporting News reported on 2023-10-04 that the NBA Rookie of the Year award is available as a futures betting market through online sportsbooks. "Bettors across the country can wager on which player they believe will win the coveted NBA Rookie of the Year award as part of NBA futures betting." Unlike single-game wagers, this market allows betting before the regular season starts and continues throughout the year via platforms like DraftKings and FanDuel.
