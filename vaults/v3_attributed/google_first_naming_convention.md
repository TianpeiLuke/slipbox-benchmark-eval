# Google-first Business Naming Strategy

The Verge reported on 2023-10-26 that businesses are adopting "Google-first" names that match literal search phrases customers type, rather than distinctive brand terms. This SEO-driven approach prioritizes digital discoverability over traditional branding. Examples include "Thai Food Near Me," "Affordable Dentist Near Me," and "Barber Shop Near Me." These names directly reflect common Google searches like "thai food near me" and "barber shop near me." Business owners report that such names help them rank higher in search results and attract customers who are actively searching for their services online. The strategy leverages Google's dominance in local business discovery, with one owner noting that using a recognizable search phrase helped customers find their restaurant when generic names would have ranked poorly.
