# Prosecutor's Opening Argument Claims Bankman-Fried Built Empire on Lies

Fortune reported on 2023-10-04 that prosecutor Thane Rehn told jurors that Sam Bankman-Fried's entire empire "all of it, was built on lies" because "behind the curtains, he was not what he appeared to be." Rehn argued that Bankman-Fried deceived customers about the safety of their funds while secretly diverting customer money through Alameda Research, his hedge fund, using girlfriend Caroline Ellison as a front. The prosecutor claimed Bankman-Fried stole billions from thousands of victims to fund his lavish lifestyle and political donations, stating "you will see the full picture."
