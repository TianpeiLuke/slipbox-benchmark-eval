# Sportsbook Odds Generation Through Computer Models

Sporting News reported on 2023-11-06 that oddsmakers and oddsmaking services employed by the best sportsbooks generate opening lines and odds by running computer models and algorithms over analytics, statistics, injury and roster news, performance, and matchup data. "The oddsmakers and oddsmaking services use computer models and algorithms that consider analytics, statistics, injury and roster news, performance, matchup data, and more to develop the lines and odds."
