# SEO-Driven Business Naming Strategy

The Verge reported on 2023-10-26 that SEO (search engine optimization) is the practice of making a business, website, or piece of content more findable within search engine results. This strategy involves optimizing digital presence to capture customers who search for specific terms online, such as "Thai food near me" or "barber shop near me." Businesses like Thai Food Near Me and Barber Shop Near Me have adopted names that directly match common Google search queries to improve their discoverability. The restaurant owners noted that using SEO-focused names helped customers find them through search engines rather than relying on foot traffic or word-of-mouth recommendations.
