# AI Product Consolidation Hypothesis for 2024

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-19 that most of 2023's AI products will not survive 2024 even as underlying capabilities keep improving, producing a round of industry consolidation in which weaker vendors are absorbed. The publication stated that "the hype built up in 2023 is going to be very hard for companies to follow through on" and predicted that "2023's products will not all survive by a long shot, and there will be a round of consolidation as the wobblier riders of the wave fall and are consumed." This hypothesis will be falsified if the 2023 product cohort persists largely intact.
