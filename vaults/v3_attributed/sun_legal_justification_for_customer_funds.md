# FTX's Former General Counsel Testifies About SBF's Request for Legal Justification

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-06 that FTX's former general counsel Can Sun, who cooperated with authorities, testified that Sam Bankman-Fried (SBF) asked him to produce a theoretical "legal justification" for using billions of dollars of consumer funds just days before FTX filed for bankruptcy. This testimony occurred during the third week of SBF's trial, as the defense prepared to present its case following the prosecution's conclusion of its arguments.
