# Bankman-Fried's Testimony on Separation from Wang's Work

The Verge reported on 2023-10-28 that Sam Bankman-Fried testified to a separation between ownership and oversight at Alameda Research. Although he was Alameda's CEO and majority owner, Bankman-Fried stated that he did not supervise Gary Wang's direct work. "He wanted to be clear: he did not supervise Wang's direct work," according to the publication's account of his testimony.
