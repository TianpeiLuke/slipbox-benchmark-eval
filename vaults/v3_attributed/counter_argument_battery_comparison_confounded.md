# Steam Deck OLED Battery Life Claim Requires Context

Engadget reported on 2023-11-09 that the Steam Deck OLED's 62 percent battery life improvement over the original model may be misleading because it compares against a 2022 LCD model whose battery has likely degraded with use. The publication states "though batteries degrade and I've had my" referring to the original Steam Deck being tested, suggesting the actual improvement percentage would be lower when comparing two fresh units. The review acknowledges this caveat when presenting the battery performance metrics between the newer OLED model and the launch-era LCD version.
