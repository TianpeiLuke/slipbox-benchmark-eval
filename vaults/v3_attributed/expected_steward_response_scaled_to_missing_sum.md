# Fund Owner's Response to Missing Funds Scales with Amount

The Verge reported on 2023-10-31 that a fund owner's response to discovering missing funds scales with the amount lost. Reporter Liz Lopatto described how misplacing $900 million would be "a five-alarm fire even for Citibank," while several billion dollars missing would demand immediate law enforcement notification. She contrasted this expected escalation with Sam Bankman-Fried's muted response to discovering an $8 billion shortfall, comparing his lack of urgency to "kicking over a lantern in Chicago in 1871." The non-escalating reaction itself became evidence about the fund owner's beliefs regarding the situation.
