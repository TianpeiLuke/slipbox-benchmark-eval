# Caroline Ellison's testimony about fraudulent balance sheets and misuse of customer funds

The Verge reported on 2023-10-26 that Caroline Ellison, Sam Bankman-Fried's ex-girlfriend, testified she created "seven phony balance sheets" and that "Alameda took customer funds; Bankman-Fried knew about it and even invested them against her advice." Ellison's testimony directly implicated Bankman-Fried in the fraudulent activities at FTX, stating that he continued investing customer funds despite her warnings against it.
