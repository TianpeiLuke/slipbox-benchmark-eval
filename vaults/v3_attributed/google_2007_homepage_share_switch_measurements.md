# Google's 2007 Homepage Experiment Data

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-31 that U.S. Justice Department lawyer Meagan Bellshaw showed Google CEO Sundar Pichai a 2007 email from a Google product strategy meeting during antitrust trial testimony. The email contained empirical observation data showing that when users changed their browser homepage to Google, they conducted "15% more Google searches." Conversely, when users switched away from Google as their homepage, their Google searches dropped "27%."
