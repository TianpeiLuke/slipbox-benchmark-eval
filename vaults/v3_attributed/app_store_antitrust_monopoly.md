# Epic's Legal Theory of Google's Android Monopoly

The Verge reported on 2023-11-07 that Epic Games attorney Gary Bornstein argued Google maintains an "unlawful monopoly" over Android app distribution by allegedly paying competitors not to compete and blocking alternative app stores. Bornstein contended that Google "bribes" or "blocks" any attempt to compete with Google Play, resulting in the vast majority of Android app installs coming from Google's store. This legal theory focuses on proving Google's market control through anti-competitive practices, rather than simply demonstrating that Google Play is the most-used app store due to consumer preference.
