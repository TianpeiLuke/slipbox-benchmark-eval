# Arsenal mathematically secures Group B victory with 6-0 win over Lens

Sporting News reported on 2023-12-12 that Arsenal's 6-0 thrashing of Lens on matchday five (29 November 2023) "confirmed them as winners of Group B." The victory came after Arsenal had previously defeated Lens 2-1 on October 3 and PSV 4-0 on September 20, with their only loss being a 2-1 defeat to Lens on matchday two. This mathematical confirmation of first place in Group B secured Arsenal's progression to the Champions League Round of 16 as group winners.
