# Thai Restaurant's SEO-Driven Name Reflects Google's Business Impact

The Verge reported on 2023-10-26 that "Thai Food Near Me" represents "a small but powerful symbol of Google's far-reaching impact on businesses over the past two decades and the lengths their owners will go to try to optimize their operations for the company's platforms." The restaurant's owners chose the name strategically to capitalize on search engine optimization, betting that the literal Google search phrase would attract customers who search for "Thai food near me." One owner noted that when customers searched for that exact phrase, they discovered the restaurant. The article explains that "The turn is that 25 years after Google Search first arrived, the name says the quiet part out loud," highlighting how businesses now structure their identities around Google's search algorithms rather than traditional branding approaches.
