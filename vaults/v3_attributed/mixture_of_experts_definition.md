# Mixture-of-Experts Model Architecture Prediction for 2024

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-19 that the limitations of monolithic large language models will become apparent in 2024, leading to diminished returns on parameter scaling. Instead of single large models, "a mixture of experts — a collection of smaller, more specific models and likely multimodal ones — may prove almost as effective while being much easier to update piecemeal." This architectural shift addresses the unwieldiness and computational expense of massive single models while maintaining performance capabilities.
