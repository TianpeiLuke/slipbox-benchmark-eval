# OpenAI re-enables ChatGPT Plus subscriptions after November pause

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-13 that OpenAI re-enabled ChatGPT Plus subscriptions after pausing them in November due to a "surge of usage." The company had temporarily stopped accepting new subscribers because of GPU capacity constraints. OpenAI CEO Sam Altman announced the reopening of subscriptions on Twitter, stating "we have re-enabled chatgpt plus subscriptions! 🎄 thanks for your patience while we found more gpus." ChatGPT Plus provides users with access to advanced models including GPT-4 and GPT-4 Turbo. The pause and subsequent re-enablement occurred during a period of high demand for the AI service.
