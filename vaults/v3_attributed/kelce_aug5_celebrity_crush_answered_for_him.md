# Kelce's Celebrity Crush Revealed Through Prompted Response

CBSSports.com reported on 2023-10-12 that on August 5, 2023, Travis Kelce was asked to name his celebrity crush, but "before he could even answer, the answer was given for him." This incident occurred well before dating rumors emerged between Kelce and Taylor Swift, whose relationship became publicly speculated after she began attending Kansas City Chiefs games starting in September 2023. The article does not specify who provided the prompted answer or the exact wording used.
