# Google Paid Developers to Block Competing App Stores, Sweeney Testifies

The Verge reported on 2023-12-12 that Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeney testified Google engaged in anti-competitive practices by "paying other developers off to convince them not to launch their own stores." This testimony came during Epic's antitrust trial against Google, where Sweeney described how Google allegedly used financial incentives to prevent competitors from creating alternative app distribution channels on Android devices. The jury ultimately sided with Epic, finding Google had an illegal monopoly over Android app distribution.
