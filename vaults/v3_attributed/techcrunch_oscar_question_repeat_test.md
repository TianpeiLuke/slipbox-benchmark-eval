# Gemini Pro's Inconsistent Oscar Winner Responses

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-07 that Gemini Pro, Google's new AI model powering Bard, provided incorrect and inconsistent answers when asked about 2023 Oscar winners. The author's replication of the prompt yielded different wrong answers: "Navalny" instead of "All the Beauty and the Bloodshed" for Best Documentary Feature, "All Quiet on the Western Front" for Best International Film, "Women Talking" for Best Adapted Screenplay, and "Pinocchio" for Best Animated Feature Film.
