# Google Antitrust Remedies Contingent on Liability Ruling

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-13 that if Judge Amit Mehta rules against Google in the US antitrust case, the search giant may face several remedies. "If Judge Amit Mehta rules against Google, the search giant may have to change its behavior and share its APIs with third-party developers. It may also be banned from making anticompetitive and exclusive deals with smartphone and computer manufacturers and wireless carriers. Google might end up having to turn over all or most of the data it has collected to other search engines so they can improve their products and attract more users." The government's case centers on Google's alleged use of platform dominance and partner deals to block competition in search and advertising markets.
