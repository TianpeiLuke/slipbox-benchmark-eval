# Unanswered details in SBF-Trump $5B offer account

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-02 that Michael Lewis's book "Going Infinite" leaves several key questions unresolved regarding Sam Bankman-Fried's alleged $5 billion offer to Donald Trump to withdraw from the 2024 presidential race. The article notes that Lewis does not identify "the friend" who introduced him to SBF in early 2021, does not explain why SBF was "shuffling cards with his leg twitching nervously" during their interview, and does not confirm whether any payment was actually made to "camp Trump." These gaps are presented as notable omissions rather than disputed facts, with the article stating "no, we don't find out" each detail.
