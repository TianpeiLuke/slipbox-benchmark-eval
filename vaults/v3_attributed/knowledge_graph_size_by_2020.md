# Google Knowledge Graph Scale Statistics from Publisher Lawsuit

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-15 that by 2020, the Google Knowledge Graph had grown to roughly 500 billion facts describing about 5 billion entities, according to figures quoted in the Helena World Chronicle complaint. The lawsuit alleges that Google compiled this massive database by extracting information from publishers' websites and "open source and licensed databases," representing content the complaint claims was "misappropriated from Publishers."
