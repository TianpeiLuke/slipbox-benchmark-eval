# Pichai admits Google's 2018 attempt to preload services on iOS devices

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-31 that Google CEO Sundar Pichai admitted during antitrust trial testimony on October 30, 2023, that Google attempted to get Tim Cook to preinstall Google services on every iOS device in 2018. Pichai hoped to make Google and Apple's services "so connected as to be inseparable" in a win-win arrangement. However, Cook declined because "Apple doesn't preload third-party software onto its devices, and it wasn't going to make an exception for Google."
