# Swift's Public Appearance at Chiefs Game

The Age reported on 2023-09-26 that Taylor Swift, the 12-time Grammy winner, attended a Chiefs game at Arrowhead Stadium on the Monday covered by their timeline article. During the game, she was observed cheering for player No. 87 (Travis Kelce), shouting what appeared to be "let's f—ing go" after he scored a touchdown, and physically engaging with other fans by chest-bumping them. This public appearance followed months of media speculation about a potential relationship between Swift and Kelce, after years of indirect connections including Kelce's 2016 comments about wanting to meet her and his recent invitation for her to attend a game. The article noted this sighting as the "fated" moment confirming their connection, particularly significant given Swift's known support for the Philadelphia Eagles rather than Kansas City Chiefs.
