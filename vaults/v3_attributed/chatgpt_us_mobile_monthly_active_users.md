# ChatGPT Mobile App Usage Reaches 4.9 Million Monthly Active Users in US

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-30 that the ChatGPT iOS and Android applications recorded 4.9 million monthly active users in the United States alone, according to web metrics company Similarweb. This significant mobile user base demonstrates ChatGPT's continued popularity and widespread adoption since its initial release as a web-based interface. The apps have also generated nearly $30 million in subscription revenue despite launching only months prior.
