# Apple's 30 Percent Fee Justification Failure in Epic v. Apple

The Verge reported on 2023-11-05 that Apple was unable to explicitly justify its 30 percent app store fee to the judge during the Epic v. Apple trial. This failure represents a significant legal vulnerability for Apple that Epic cannot leverage in its subsequent case against Google, as the Apple trial concluded with a ruling largely favoring Apple despite this apparent weakness in their pricing justification.
