# Sports Betting Lines Move Based on Public Reaction and New Information

Sporting News reported on 2023-11-06 that posted betting lines and odds are not static, moving in response to heavy betting on one side and new information before games. Sportsbooks adjust lines to balance betting action and manage risk, meaning "betting lines and odds are then subject to change based on the betting public's reaction and any additional information that comes to light ahead of the game."
