# Missy Ward's SEO Career Origin and Exit

The Verge reported on 2023-11-01 that Missy Ward began doing SEO in 1998, when the field was, in her words, "five girls and all dudes." She eventually sold her company for $40 million. The article describes her as one of the digital marketers attending a rooftop party in South Florida, where she was characterized as dressing like she lived in Florida and being approachable despite working in a field often criticized for degrading internet search quality.
