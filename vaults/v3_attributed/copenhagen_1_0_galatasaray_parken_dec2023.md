# FC Copenhagen defeats Galatasaray 1-0 on December 12, 2023

Sporting News reported on 2023-12-12 that "Lukas Lerager scored the only goal in a 1-0 win for the hosts" FC Copenhagen against Galatasaray at Parken on December 12, 2023. This result denied Galatasaray the victory needed to advance to the Champions League Round of 16 despite reaching 8 points, eliminating them from European competition for the season.
