# AI Marketing Hype vs. Reality Check in 2024

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-19 that machine-learning features purchased in 2023 to avoid falling behind competitors will likely fail their 2024 quarterly and yearly ROI reviews. The article states that "the hype built up in 2023 is going to be very hard for companies to follow through on" and that marketing claims made for AI systems adopted last year "will receive their quarterly and yearly reviews…and it's very likely they will be found wanting." This prediction suggests that enterprise reassessments will broadly invalidate vendor claims, leading to customer withdrawal from AI tools as benefits fail to justify costs and risks.
