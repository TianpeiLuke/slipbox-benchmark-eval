# Publisher's Antitrust Suit Claims Google AI Harms Revenue

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-15 that Arkansas-based publisher Helena World Chronicle filed a class action antitrust lawsuit against Google, claiming the company is "starving the free press" by redistributing publishers' content inside its own products. The complaint specifically alleges that Google's practices cost publishers "billions of dollars" in lost revenue through anticompetitive means including AI technologies like Search Generative Experience and Bard AI chatbot.
