# Sam Bankman-Fried Trial Begins with Jury Selection

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-06 that "The trial kicked off with jury selection October 3." The trial of Sam Bankman-Fried, former CEO of collapsed crypto exchange FTX, began with jury selection on October 3, 2023, as proceedings resumed in the courtroom drama surrounding alleged fraud and money laundering charges.
