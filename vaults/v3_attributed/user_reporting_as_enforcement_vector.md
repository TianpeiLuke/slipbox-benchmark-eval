# Coordinated User Reporting Can Trigger Account Restrictions Regardless of Policy Violations

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-19 that coordinated user reporting can trigger account restrictions independently of whether the reported content violates policy. Palestinian-American filmmaker Khitam Jabr suspected that Instagram restricted her account for 24 hours due to user reports about her Palestine travel videos, even though her content didn't violate Meta's policies. Users have complained that they've been harassed and reported for posting content about Palestine, regardless of whether or not it violates Meta's policies. This makes enforcement volume a function of opponent mobilization rather than rule breach, suggesting that Meta's moderation practices disproportionately affect Palestinian users through mechanisms like shadowbanning.
