# ChatGPT Users Report Lethargy in December 2023

TechCrunch reported on 2023-09-28 that ChatGPT users began noticing the chatbot providing simpler answers and refusing tasks in November 2023. OpenAI confirmed awareness of this issue on December 8, 2023, stating "we haven't updated the model since Nov 11th, and this certainly isn't intentional." Some users speculated this represented a "winter break hypothesis" or seasonal depression simulation. The phenomenon coincided with OpenAI's re-enabling of ChatGPT Plus subscriptions on December 13, 2023.
