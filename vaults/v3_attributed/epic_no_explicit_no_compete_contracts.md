# Google's Alleged Anti-Competitive Conduct Lacks Explicit Contracts

The Verge reported on 2023-11-07 that during the Epic v. Google trial opening arguments, Epic's attorney Gary Bornstein conceded Google "was too clever" to create explicit contracts preventing developers from competing with the Play Store. Instead, Epic's monopoly allegations rely on Google's conduct, such as allegedly paying competitors not to compete, rather than written contractual terms. Bornstein stated, "Google pays actual and potential competitors not to compete. Literally gives them money and other things of value."
