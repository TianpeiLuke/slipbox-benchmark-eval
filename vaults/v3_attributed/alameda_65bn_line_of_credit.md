# $65 billion line of credit testimony

CNBC reported on 2023-10-06 that Gary Wang testified under oath on October 6, 2023, stating "Alameda was permitted to withdraw and transfer funds on FTX under a $65 billion line of credit." Wang made this statement while testifying in the Sam Bankman-Fried fraud trial, explaining the financial arrangements between FTX and its sister hedge fund Alameda Research prior to FTX's collapse.
