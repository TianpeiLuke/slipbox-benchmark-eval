# Sweeney's Relief Contradicts Quick Verdict Concerns

The Verge reported on 2023-12-12 that Tim Sweeney felt "great relief" when the jury rapidly decided in favor of Epic Games against Google, despite trial lawyers' conventional wisdom that "when there's a rapid jury verdict, it's typically not good for the plaintiffs making a complicated case." Sweeney noted there was "some trepidation going on" given this legal axiom, which suggests that quick decisions often indicate unfavorable outcomes for complex plaintiff arguments. The jury's swift verdict contradicted expectations and demonstrated that the justice system could handle intricate tech antitrust cases effectively.
