# Alameda Research Co-Conspirator Hiring Timeline

The Verge reported on 2023-10-28 that Sam Bankman-Fried's hiring spree at Alameda Research brought on Gary Wang first to program the computers, then Nishad Singh roughly a month after Alameda's founding, then Caroline Ellison. This established the core team allegedly involved in subsequent fraudulent activities.
