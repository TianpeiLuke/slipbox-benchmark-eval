# DOJ Implies Apple's 'Best Product' Claim Is Pretext for Google Deal

The Verge reported on 2023-09-26 that the US Department of Justice's implicit argument in US v. Google is that Apple's stated preference for Google as the "best product" is actually driven by economic incentives. During Eddy Cue's testimony about the Information Services Agreement (ISA), DOJ attorney Meagan Bellshaw questioned whether Apple truly believed Google offered superior search capabilities or simply paid the highest price. The DOJ's position, drawn from Apple's own exhibits and financial filings, suggests that Apple placed Google—whose privacy practices Apple publicly criticized—at the center of its platform because of the substantial ISA payments rather than genuine product superiority. This economics-over-privacy interpretation challenges Apple's dual claims of both protecting user privacy and endorsing Google's search quality.
