# Google's Antitrust Litigation Burden and Market Dominance Scrutiny

The Age reported on 2023-10-22 that the US government faces an uphill battle in proving Google violated antitrust laws to achieve its dominant market position. The publication notes that while Google maintains its search engine leads because it is "simply better," questions persist about whether the company uses its scale and data advantages unfairly. "For the US, which has to prove that Google somehow broke the law to achieve the status quo, this legal case could be an uphill battle. But for the rest of us, it offers a rare opportunity for some insight into what the tech giant does with its enormous market share."
