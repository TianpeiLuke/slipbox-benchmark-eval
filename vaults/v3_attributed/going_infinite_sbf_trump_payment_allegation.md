# SBF Allegedly Attempted to Buy Off Trump to Prevent Presidential Run

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-07 that Michael Lewis's book "Going Infinite," which was published on the first day of Sam Bankman-Fried's trial, contains allegations that SBF tried to buy off Donald Trump to prevent him from running for president again. The book reveals details about SBF's political dealings, including this claim about attempting to influence Trump's presidential ambitions through financial means.
