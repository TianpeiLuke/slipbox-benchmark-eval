# Trial Spectator Gallery Includes Notable Figures

Fortune reported on 2023-10-04 that the spectator gallery on day two of Sam Bankman-Fried's trial included "big names," specifically naming Bankman-Fried's professorial parents and Damian Williams, the U.S. attorney for the Southern District of New York. The article presents this notable audience lineup as evidence of the case's high-profile nature, contrasting the "bad 'man'" versus "good 'boy'" narratives being presented in court.
