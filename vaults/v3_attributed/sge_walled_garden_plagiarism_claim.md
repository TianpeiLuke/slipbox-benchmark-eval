# Publisher Lawsuit Alleges Google's SGE AI Search Keeps Users in Walled Garden

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-15 that a class action lawsuit filed by Arkansas-based publisher Helena World Chronicle accuses Google of anticompetitive behavior, specifically arguing that Google's Search Generative Experience (SGE) is "designed to keep users inside Google's 'walled garden'" while reusing publishers' content. The complaint contends that SGE was implemented "with the goal of discouraging end-users from visiting the websites of Class members who are part of the digital news and publishing line of commerce."
