# Swift's Easter Egg Influence on Kelce's Public Persona

The Age reported on 2023-09-26 that Swift's penchant for Easter eggs must be rubbing off on the tight end player. The article documents how Travis Kelce, initially dismissive after failing to meet Swift at her Kansas City concert, began exhibiting behaviors resembling Swift's famous hidden messaging techniques. Notably, Kelce's blue and white post-game outfit at Arrowhead Stadium was identified by fans as the "1989 bedroom painting set," suggesting deliberate pop-cultural references. This represents "an Easter egg" as a deliberately planted, publicly deniable pop-cultural reference embedded in an artist's visible artefacts — albums, lyrics, clothing — whose decoding by fans constitutes evidence that The Age attributes to Swift and claims is "rubbing off" on Kelce.
