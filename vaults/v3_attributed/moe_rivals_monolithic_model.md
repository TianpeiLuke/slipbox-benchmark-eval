# Mixture of Experts vs Monolithic Models Hypothesis for 2024

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-19 that in 2024 mixtures of experts will prove almost as effective as a single monolithic model while being far cheaper to update. The publication stated that "a single monolithic model is unwieldy and expensive, while a mixture of experts — a collection of smaller, more specific models and likely multimodal ones — may prove almost as effective while being much easier to update piecemeal." This hypothesis will be falsified if monolithic models retain a decisive quality or cost advantage throughout 2024.
