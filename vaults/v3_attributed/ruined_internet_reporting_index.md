# The Verge's 2023-11-01 Report on SEO Practitioners and Internet Search Quality

The Verge reported on 2023-11-01 that SEO practitioners are widely blamed for degrading internet search quality, with Google Search results described as "absolutely unusable" and "garbage." The article profiles the SEO community through interviews and observations, challenging the "hustler" stereotype while exploring how search engine optimization has become "insidious." Writer Nilay Patel attended an SEO conference in Florida expecting to find "mechanical monsters" but instead encountered ordinary people. SEO veteran Missy Ward explained that "Google is very clear about what they want" - high-quality content that keeps users engaged. The piece traces SEO's evolution from technical practice to business model that exploits algorithmic preferences, ultimately questioning whether practitioners or Google itself bear responsibility for search quality decline.
