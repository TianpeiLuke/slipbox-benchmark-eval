# Gabe Bankman-Fried's Donation Processing Chat Reveals Covert Political Funding Scheme

The Verge reported on 2023-10-26 that Nishad Singh identified a Signal chat called "Donations Processing" used to coordinate transfers of FTX and Alameda money to political causes. In this chat, Gabe Bankman-Fried recommended that some donations be made "in the dark" to avoid publicity. Singh also testified that Gabe's assistant flew to the Bahamas with blank checks for him to sign. This coordination occurred while FTX customer funds were being misappropriated, with Singh pleading guilty to wire fraud, money laundering, and conspiracy charges related to these political donations.
