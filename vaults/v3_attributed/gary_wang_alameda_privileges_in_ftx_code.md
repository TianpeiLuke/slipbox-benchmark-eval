# Gary Wang's Trial Testimony About FTX Code Privileges

The Verge reported on 2023-10-26 that Gary Wang, co-founder of both FTX and Alameda Research and a college roommate of Sam Bankman-Fried, testified after Adam Yedidia and "sang like a canary about the secret privileges he'd created for Alameda in the FTX code." This testimony occurred during the FTX trial that revealed how Bankman-Fried's actions affected his inner circle, with Wang being among those whose lives were "irrevocably changed" by the fraud allegations.
