# Iron Ore Price Increase Boosts ASX Heavyweights

The Sydney Morning Herald reported on 2023-10-01 that iron ore heavyweights advanced after the iron ore price increased 1.4 per cent overnight. This demonstrates that a rise in the iron ore price lifts the share prices of ASX iron ore heavyweights, holding other factors constant. The stronger iron ore prices helped bolster the local bourse despite broader market pressures from higher-for-longer interest rate expectations and global economic concerns.
