# Google Antitrust Ruling's Potential Ripple Effect on Other Big Tech Cases

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-13 that the US v Google antitrust case could have a ripple effect on other major Big Tech matters. The publication stated that "The Google outcome could also have a ripple effect on other Big Tech cases" including specific actions against Amazon, Apple, and Facebook. The FTC has sued Amazon for allegedly using anticompetitive strategies to maintain monopoly power, the DOJ has been investigating Apple's third-party app policies and potential self-favoring practices, and the FTC is pursuing a case against Facebook demanding the sale of Instagram and WhatsApp. This hypothesis remains unfalsified if these parallel cases proceed and settle independently of the Google judgment's influence.
