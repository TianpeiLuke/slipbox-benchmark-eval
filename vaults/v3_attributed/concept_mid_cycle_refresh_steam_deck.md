# Steam Deck OLED as Mid-Cycle Refresh

Engadget reported on 2023-11-09 that the Steam Deck OLED is a mid-cycle refresh rather than a successor, maintaining the same product line and form factor while upgrading internal components. The device features improvements including a better OLED screen with HDR support, enhanced battery life, improved thumbsticks, superior haptics, and optimized chip and thermals performance. This follows the Switch OLED pattern of incremental enhancement rather than generational replacement, with Valve describing it as "just a welcome improvement on an already good device."
