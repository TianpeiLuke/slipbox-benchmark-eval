# Swift and Kelce spotted leaving Arrowhead Stadium together

The Age reported on 2023-09-26 that Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce were photographed departing Arrowhead Stadium together in a vehicle described by fans as a "getaway car." The sighting occurred after Swift attended Kelce's game where she was seen celebrating his touchdown performance, with observers noting her enthusiastic support despite being an Eagles fan. Swifties quickly documented the pair's exit, interpreting it as confirmation of their rumored relationship.
