# Google pledges tighter merchandising controls after offensive notebooks at K&I Black Summit

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-09 that attendees of Google's K&I Black Summit in August 2023 received third-party notebooks containing racially insensitive language. The notebooks were printed with the phrase "I was just cotton the moment, but I came back to take your notes." The publication noted this would not have been well received by the mostly Black audience. Following the incident, Google pledged to "avoid similar situations as [it engages] with [merchandise] vendors going forward."
