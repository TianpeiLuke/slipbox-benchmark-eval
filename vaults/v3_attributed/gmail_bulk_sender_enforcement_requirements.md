# Google's 2024 Gmail anti-spam requirements for bulk senders

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-07 that Google announced significant changes to how Gmail handles email from bulk senders, requiring them to authenticate messages, provide easy unsubscribe options, and maintain spam complaint rates below a certain threshold or face stricter email handling starting in 2024. The company says that "starting next year, bulk senders will need to authenticate their emails, offer an easy way to unsubscribe and stay under a reported spam threshold."
