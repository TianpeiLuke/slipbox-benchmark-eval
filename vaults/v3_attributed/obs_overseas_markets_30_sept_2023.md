# European Markets Rise While Asian Exchanges Closed on September 29, 2023

The Sydney Morning Herald reported on 2023-10-01 that "European sharemarket indexes closed modestly higher on 29 September 2023 while exchanges across much of Asia were shut for a public holiday." The article noted that while European markets showed modest gains, Asian exchanges were largely closed, and Wall Street ended September with losses as Treasury yields climbed back to 4.58 per cent, impacting investor sentiment globally.
