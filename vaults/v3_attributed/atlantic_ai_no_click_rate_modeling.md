# The Atlantic's AI Search Impact Model

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-15 that when The Atlantic modeled Google's AI-integrated search experience, it found the AI would answer user queries directly 75% of the time without requiring clicks to The Atlantic's website. This statistic was cited in a lawsuit filed by Helena World Chronicle against Google, highlighting concerns that AI search technologies could severely impact publishers' traffic and revenue by keeping users within Google's ecosystem rather than directing them to original content sources.
