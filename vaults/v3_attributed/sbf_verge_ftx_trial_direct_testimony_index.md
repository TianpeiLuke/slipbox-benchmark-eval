# Sam Bankman-Fried's Direct Testimony Details FTX Founding and Conduct

The Verge reported on 2023-10-28 that Sam Bankman-Fried's direct testimony covered his courtroom behavior, vocabulary explanations, and FTX's origins. During his testimony, Bankman-Fried was repeatedly instructed by Judge Lewis Kaplan to answer questions directly, with "Objection, narrative!" sustained three times. The coverage indexed his explanation of founding Alameda Research in 2017 during a crypto bull run, choosing the name to stay "under the radar" rather than "Sam's Crypto Trading Firm." He described achieving "50 percent to 100 percent annualized returns" through arbitrage before founding FTX, which he estimated had only a 20 percent chance of success. The report noted his claimed lack of supervision over Gary Wang's work despite being CEO and majority owner, and his admission that not creating a risk team was a "big mistake."
