# Steam Deck OLED Input Improvements Feel Noticeably Better

Engadget reported on 2023-11-09 that the Steam Deck OLED's input controls feel improved compared to the original model. The reviewer noted that the touchscreen responds immediately to input, and the haptic feedback on the built-in trackpads feels more precise, creating "reactive pin-pricks under the textured plastic." While these improvements were observed during hands-on testing, the assessment was limited to short-duration subjective evaluation rather than extensive long-term use.
