# Five Key Revelations from Google Antitrust Trial (Oct 30-31, 2023)

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-31 that five major revelations emerged from the Google antitrust trial hearings on October 30-31, 2023. First, Google paid $26.3 billion in 2021 to be the default search engine across platforms, with $18 billion going to Apple alone. Second, Google agreed not to promote Chrome browser to Safari users as part of its Apple deal. Third, Google attempted to get preinstalled placement on iPhones in 2018. Fourth, Google deleted internal chat logs despite litigation holds, with messages automatically deleted every 24 hours. Fifth, a 2005 Google letter revealed the company's early understanding that "focusing on homepage market share is one of the most effective things we can do to make gains in search market share."
