# Thiel's £1.4M AGI Investment Following 2010 Meeting

The Age reported on 2023-12-09 that in 2010, following a kitchen meeting at Peter Thiel's San Francisco town house the day after a cocktail party, Thiel and his venture capital firm agreed to put £1.4 million (A$2.7 million) behind Demis Hassabis's artificial general intelligence venture. The meeting occurred after Hassabis, a 34-year-old neuroscientist at the time, leveraged his chess expertise to connect with Thiel at the cocktail party. This early investment in AGI represented one of the first significant bets on artificial general intelligence when few others showed interest in the technology.
