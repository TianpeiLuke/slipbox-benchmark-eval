# SEC's fraud allegation against FTX centers on Bankman-Fried's misappropriation

The Verge reported on 2023-10-10 that the Securities and Exchange Commission alleges FTX was a fraud "from the start," with the distinguishing factor being a "multi-billion-dollar deficiency caused by his own misappropriation of customer funds." This civil allegation frames the core of the criminal fraud trial against Sam Bankman-Fried, who faces seven counts of wire fraud and conspiracy. The allegation directly ties the exchange's collapse to Bankman-Fried's personal actions rather than broader market conditions or operational failures.
