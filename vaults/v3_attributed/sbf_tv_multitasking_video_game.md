# SBF's First TV Appearance Reveals Gaming Multitasking

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-02 that Michael Lewis recounted Sam Bankman-Fried's first television appearance, saying "If you watch the clip, you'll see his eyes going back and forth. It's because he's trying to win his video game at the same time he's on the air." This observation occurred during SBF's rise as FTX founder before his later fraud charges.
