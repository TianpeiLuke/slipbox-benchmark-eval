# Rationalists' AI Risk Hypothesis

The Age reported on 2023-12-09 that Rationalists and effective altruists believed AI could solve major problems like cancer and climate change, but warned that "if machines become more intelligent than humans, they could turn on their creators by doing things their creators had not intended." This hypothesis is falsified if systems exceeding human capability pursue only their designers' stated aims. The article documents how this concern drove figures like Elon Musk and Sam Altman to create OpenAI, valued at over $80 billion, to develop AI safely while competing for control of the technology that "could be worth trillions of dollars."
