# SBF's Political Influence Strategy Through Cross-Party Candidate Funding

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-02 that Sam Bankman-Fried developed a strategic approach to political influence by funding candidates across party lines. According to Michael Lewis's reporting, SBF sought to "counter Trump's influence" within the Republican Party while also supporting Democratic candidates, creating a network of political relationships that could serve his interests regardless of which party held power. This strategy reflected SBF's recognition that "you never know when you might need a friend" in Washington, as Lewis documented in his interviews with the FTX founder. The cross-party funding approach was part of a broader effort to gain political leverage, alongside his attempt to offer $5 billion to Trump to withdraw from the presidential race.
