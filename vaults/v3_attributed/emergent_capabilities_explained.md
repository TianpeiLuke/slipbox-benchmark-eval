# 2024 Research Will Explain LLM Emergent Capabilities and Limits

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-19 that 2024 will bring better understanding of large language models' "emergent" capabilities and their limitations. The article states that "The seemingly magical 'emergent' capabilities of LLMs will be better studied and understood in 2024, and things like their inability to multiply large numbers will make more sense." This research is expected to clarify why LLMs exhibit irregular performance profiles despite their impressive capabilities. The hypothesis that emergent behavior remains unanalyzed at the frontier would be falsified if such research successfully explains these phenomena.
