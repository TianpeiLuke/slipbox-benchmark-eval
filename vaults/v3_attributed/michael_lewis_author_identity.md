# Michael Lewis's Financial Misadventure Chronicles

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-02 that Michael Lewis, the renowned writer specializing in financial adventure and misadventure, is set to release "Going Infinite," a book chronicling the rise and fall of Sam Bankman-Fried. Lewis's notable works include "Liar's Poker," "Moneyball," and "The Big Short."
