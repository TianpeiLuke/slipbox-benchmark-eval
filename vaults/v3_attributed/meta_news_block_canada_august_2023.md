# Meta blocks Canadian news content amid payment disputes

TechCrunch reported on 2023-12-15 that Meta began blocking news content for Canadian users in August 2023 in response to pressure from Canada's new law requiring payment for publisher content. The article states that "Negotiations with Meta are still unresolved, though Meta began blocking news in Canada in August, in light of the pressure to pay for the content." This action was taken as Canada implemented legislation mandating compensation for news content, with Meta's negotiations with Canadian publishers remaining incomplete as of December 2023, unlike Google which reached a C$100 million annual agreement with the Canadian government.
