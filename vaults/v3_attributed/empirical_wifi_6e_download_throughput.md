# Steam Deck OLED Wi-Fi 6E Performance Test

Engadget reported on 2023-11-09 that the Steam Deck OLED's new Wi-Fi 6E module and improved antennas "peaked at 562 mbps download — about 10 percent faster than the original Deck and about 10 percent slower than a MacBook Pro on the same network" when tested against an ordinary Wi-Fi 6 router.
