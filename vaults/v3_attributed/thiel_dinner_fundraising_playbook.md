# Hassabis's chess hook strategy for Thiel meeting

The Age reported on 2023-12-09 that Demis Hassabis spent a year preparing for a crucial meeting with Peter Thiel by researching his interests and identifying chess as a unique hook. Hassabis, a former second-best-under-14 chess player globally, recognized that Thiel loved chess after spotting a chessboard at a cocktail party in Thiel's San Francisco townhouse in 2010. This strategic approach helped Hassabis secure funding for his artificial general intelligence venture, as he "thought that would be my unique hook in: I knew that he loved chess." The meeting was pivotal for Hassabis's efforts to build artificial general intelligence with his British colleagues who were connected to Eliezer Yudkowsky's Rationalist community.
