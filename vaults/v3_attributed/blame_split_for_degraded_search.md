# Contested responsibility for degraded search results between Google and SEO practitioners

The Verge reported on 2023-11-01 that responsibility for degraded search results is contested between Google and SEO practitioners. Some critics blame Google directly, arguing that the trillion-dollar corporation with 90 percent market share is corrupting access to truth and making search results "absolutely unusable" and "garbage." Others point to SEO specialists who exploit algorithms to boost rankings, with one tech writer describing the internet as a "search-optimized hellhole." The practice has become so pervasive that searching for information feels like wading through sponsored content disguised as organic results, leading to widespread disdain for both Google's dominance and the tactics employed by search engine optimizers seeking profit over authenticity.
