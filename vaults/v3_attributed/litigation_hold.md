# Litigation Hold Violation Exposes Google's Chat Deletion Policy

TechCrunch reported on 2023-10-31 that Google's automatic chat deletion policy became legally problematic because the company was subject to a litigation hold during the antitrust trial. "During Pichai's testimony, the DOJ touched on Google's policy of deleting internal chat messages, despite being subject to a litigation hold." The litigation hold obligated Google to preserve potentially relevant documents and communications once litigation was reasonably anticipated, making the systematic destruction of chats through the history-off option a legal exposure point in the trial.
