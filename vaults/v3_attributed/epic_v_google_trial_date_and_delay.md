# Epic v. Google Trial Begins After 1,180-Day Delay

The Verge reported on 2023-11-05 that Epic Games' antitrust lawsuit against Google finally went to trial on 2023-11-06, exactly 1,180 days after Epic originally filed suit. Unlike the Epic v. Apple case which reached trial in 2021, the Google lawsuit faced repeated delays before beginning. Epic alleges Google's mobile app store constitutes an illegal monopoly that forces developers to pay excessive fees, while Google argues that competition from Apple's iPhone ecosystem prevents any monopoly claims.
