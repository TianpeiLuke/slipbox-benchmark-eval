# Student Loan Repayments May Divert Consumer Spending

The Sydney Morning Herald reported on 2023-10-01 that the resumption of US student-loan repayments "may funnel more dollars away from the spending by consumers that has helped to keep the economy afloat." This hypothesis predicts that household funds redirected toward debt repayment will reduce consumption. The claim can be falsified if post-September retail spending data show no measurable deceleration tied to loan repayments.
