# Google Play's Dominant Position in Android App Distribution

The Verge reported on 2023-11-07 that Google Play serves as the default and dominant distribution channel for Android, through which the vast majority of Android app installs occur. Epic's legal filings in its antitrust lawsuit against Google argue that "the vast, vast majority of Android app installs were from Google Play," with only a tiny fraction coming from alternative app stores like Samsung's Galaxy Store. Google maintains this dominance isn't monopolistic but rather the natural result of competing with Apple's iOS ecosystem.
