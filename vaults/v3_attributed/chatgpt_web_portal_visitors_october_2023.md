# ChatGPT Web Traffic Milestone

TechCrunch reported on 2023-11-30 that Similarweb measured 140.7 million unique visitors to the ChatGPT web portal in October 2023. The article notes this web traffic figure as part of ChatGPT's ongoing popularity one year after its initial launch as a "low-key research preview." According to the report, "According to Similarweb, the web metrics company, OpenAI's ChatGPT web portal saw 140.7 million unique visitors in October."
