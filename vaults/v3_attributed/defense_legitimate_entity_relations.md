# Cohen's Defense Model Contradicts Prosecution's Two-Entity Structure

Fortune reported on 2023-10-04 that Mark Cohen, Sam Bankman-Fried's lawyer, presented a competing model contradicting the prosecution's portrayal of separate entities. "Alameda was not subterranean or shady. It was a successful hedge fund," Cohen stated, arguing that FTX was "a very innovative, successful company" rather than a Ponzi scheme. He maintained that the business practices between FTX and Alameda were reasonable, with Alameda acting legally as an FTX customer, payment processor, and market maker.
