# Epic's Antitrust Trial Against Google Was Four Years in the Making

The Verge reported on 2023-12-12 that Epic Games' antitrust trial against Google "has been four years in the making," originating from a September 2019 email in discovery that outlined a plan to draw Google into a legal battle over antitrust issues. The trial lasted four weeks and ended in December 2023 with a jury verdict siding with Epic, finding that Google had an illegal monopoly over Android app distribution and in-app payment systems. Epic CEO Tim Sweeney described the case as his personal fight from the beginning, attending nearly every day of the trial. The plan document from 2019 represented "the causal origin of the proceeding."
